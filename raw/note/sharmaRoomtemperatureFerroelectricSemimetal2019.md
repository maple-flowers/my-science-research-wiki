>【摘要翻译】WTe2是一种层状晶体材料，既具有铁电性又具有金属性。和，Anderson和Blount在1965年首次提出的可逆极性畸变和金属丰度共存形成铁电金属的观点至今仍不清楚。在块体晶体金属中，电可切换的本征极化以及铁电畴的直接观察尚未实现，尽管在原则上，移动导电电荷的不完全屏蔽是可能的。在这里，我们提供的证据表明，自然金属丰度和铁电性共存于块体晶体范德瓦尔斯WTe2通过电输运、纳米尺度的压电响应测量和第一性原理计算。我们证明，尽管是Weyl半金属2在室温下具有可切换的自发极化和自然的铁电畴结构。这种新型材料在功能纳米电子学方面具有诱人的应用潜力。

> ## 一、引言
> 
> ### 1.1 领域背景：打破常识的“铁电金属”
> 
> 在传统教科书中，**铁电性**和**金属性**被认为是两种互不相容的物理性质。铁电体是绝缘体或半导体，其内部的正负电荷中心即使在无外电场时也不重合，形成自发极化，且这种极化方向可被外电场翻转。而金属则拥有大量自由电子，这些电子会像一层完美的“静电屏蔽罩”，将任何内部偶极子产生的电场完全抵消，从而抑制铁电性的产生。这个由安德森和布朗特在1965年提出的理论预言，即“铁电金属”能否在单一材料中稳定存在，一直是凝聚态物理领域悬而未决的世纪难题。
> 
> ### 1.2 研究问题与文献定位
> 
> 尽管过去几十年里，科学家们通过掺杂传统铁电体、设计氧化物界面等方式，试图让铁电性和金属性共存，但一直未能实现本征的、可在室温下工作的、且具有可翻转极化状态的单一相铁电金属。**本文献（Sharma et al., Sci. Adv. 2019）** 正是在此背景下，首次提供了确凿的实验证据，证明层状范德华材料**二碲化钨（WTe₂）** 在块体晶体形态下，就是一种室温下的**铁电半金属**。
> 
> ### 1.3 核心价值与意义
> 
> 这项工作的核心价值在于：
> 
> 1.  **理论印证**：首次在宏观块体材料中，实验证实了半个多世纪前安德森和布朗特的理论预言。
> 2.  **现象发现**：直接观测到了铁电性的两大标志性特征——**分立的铁电畴**和**电可翻转的自发极化**。
> 3.  **概念突破**：将铁电性与拓扑半金属（WTe₂也是一种外尔半金属）结合，开辟了“铁电拓扑电子学”这一全新的研究范式。
> 4.  **应用潜力**：为开发基于范德华材料的超薄、低功耗、多态存储及逻辑器件提供了全新的材料平台。
>

> ## 二、论文结构与逻辑概览
> 
> 本文采用了“提出假说 → 实验证伪 → 理论解释”的经典科学论证结构，其核心逻辑链如下：
> 
> 1.  **问题提出**：铁电性和金属性在块体晶体中能否共存？
> 2.  **结构确认**：通过XRD确认WTe₂的非中心对称极性结构（空间群Pmn21），这是铁电性的必要条件。
> 3.  **属性确认**：通过电输运测量，确认WTe₂从室温到极低温都表现出金属行为，排除了它是绝缘体的可能性。
> 4.  **铁电性直接证据（核心）**：
>     -   **静态证据（畴结构）**：利用**压电力显微镜（PFM）**，在新鲜解理的WTe₂块体单晶表面，直接观测到纳米级反平行铁电畴。
>     -   **动态证据（可翻转性）**：通过构建金属-绝缘体-金属电容器结构，在抑制电流后，成功实现了极化的电控翻转，获得典型的电滞回线与蝴蝶曲线。
> 5.  **理论解释**：通过**第一性原理DFT计算**，揭示了WTe₂的铁电不稳定性源于其各向异性的晶体结构，并计算了极化翻转的能量势垒，证明其室温下可翻转。
> 
> 整个论证过程环环相扣，从结构到属性，再到铁电性的静态和动态双重证据，最后回归理论，形成了一个完整的闭环。

> ## 四、正文核心内容剖析
> 
> ### 4.1 研究背景与问题
> 
> -   **核心矛盾**：铁电性（需要局域化电荷）与金属性（存在巡游电子屏蔽）的共存。
> -   **历史争论**：从安德森和布朗特1965年的预言，到LiOsO₃、Cd₂Re₂O₇等低温“极性金属”的发现，再到通过掺杂或界面工程引入的争议性结果，室温下的本征铁电金属始终未被证实。
> -   **本文切入点**：选择具有非中心对称极性结构的范德华半金属WTe₂，利用其独特的各向异性结构和低载流子浓度，为不完全屏蔽提供可能。
> 
> ### 4.2 方法、模型与实验设计
> 
> -   **核心方法组合**：
>     1.  **电输运（Electrical Transport）**：确认金属性及半金属基态。
>     2.  **压电力显微镜（PFM）**：在纳米尺度探测铁电畴和极化翻转。这是本文最关键的实验技术。
>     3.  **第一性原理密度泛函理论计算（DFT）**：从原子尺度揭示铁电性的起源和翻转机制。
> -   **精巧实验设计**：
>     -   **表面处理**：在惰性气体中新鲜解理WTe₂单晶，确保表面无氧化的本征态。
>     -   **极化翻转难题**：为防止高电导导致电流流过而无法施加电场，巧妙设计了**电容器结构**，利用WTe₂表面极薄的天然氧化层（~2.5 nm）作为绝缘层，成功实现了对铁电性的电场调控。
> 
> ### 4.3 实验结果与讨论
> 
> -   **铁电畴的直接观测**：PFM图像清晰地展示了随机分布、尺寸约20-50 nm的纳米畴，其振幅和相位对比完美符合铁电畴的特征。
> -   **极化翻转的证实**：通过电容器结构，获得了典型的电滞回线（相位）和蝴蝶曲线（振幅），并通过电压脉冲实现了极化态的可逆写入，直接证实了其铁电翻转行为。
> -   **弱压电响应的解释**：实验测得的压电系数（~0.7 pm/V）远小于理论值（~6.5 pm/V）。作者解释，这是因为PFM信号主要来自材料表面极浅的**德拜屏蔽层**（~1.6 nm），局域电场分布不均且信号被“稀释”，这反而佐证了其金属性。
> 
> ### 4.4 理论解释
> 
> -   **极化起源**：DFT计算表明，总极化P ≈ 0.19 μC/cm²，主要贡献来自离子实和价电子。半金属的低载流子浓度导致其屏蔽效应不完全，不足以完全抵消内部偶极场。
> -   **翻转机理**：极化翻转并非通过硬性的原子反转，而是通过转换到另一种等效的晶格畸变模式，这大大降低了翻转能垒（0.29 eV/f.u.），使室温翻转成为可能。
>

> ## 五、结论与贡献
> 
> ### 5.1 核心发现
> 
> 本研究首次在实验室中，实现了安德森和布朗特半个多世纪前预言的“铁电金属”，具体表现为：
> 
> 1.  **确凿证据**：在块体范德华晶体WTe₂中，同时观测到了室温下的**金属导电性**和**铁电性**（可翻转的自发极化）。
> 2.  **直接可视化**：首次在块体铁电金属中，利用PFM直接观测到了纳米尺度的铁电畴。
> 3.  **机理阐明**：通过DFT计算，揭示了WTe₂的铁电性源于其晶体结构畸变，而半金属的低载流子浓度是屏蔽不完全、铁电性得以幸存的关键。
> 
> ### 5.2 理论与实践贡献
> 
> -   **理论贡献**：将铁电性的研究疆域从绝缘体/半导体拓展到了拓扑半金属，建立了“铁电拓扑电子学”的新概念。
> -   **实践贡献**：提供了一种无需复杂界面工程、本征的室温铁电金属材料平台，为后摩尔时代的低功耗、超快响应、多态存储及逻辑器件开辟了新道路。

> ## 七、学术思考与批判性分析
> 
> 1.  **铁电畴的本征性问题**：尽管作者在N₂环境中用新鲜解理面进行测量，并排除了表面氧化物的影响，但PFM信号的探测深度（德拜屏蔽层）仅为~1.6 nm。一个核心问题是：**我们观测到的铁电畴究竟是仅限于表面的现象，还是贯穿整个块材的体效应？** 作者提出了“铁弹耦合”可能导致畴壁向深处传播的假说，但缺乏直接的实验证据（如三维断层扫描）。这为后续研究留下了悬念。
>     
> 2.  **压电系数差异的深层原因**：理论计算的压电系数d₃₃ (6.5 pm/V) 与实验值 (0.7 pm/V) 差距巨大。作者用德拜屏蔽解释，但这是否完全解释了近一个数量级的差异？是否存在其他因素，如**表面极化弛豫、缺陷钉扎或测量中复杂的静电学效应**，这些因素是否被低估了？
>     
> 3.  **“金属”与“半金属”的界定**：论文标题和核心结论都使用了“铁电半金属”（Ferroelectric Semimetal）一词。半金属的低载流子浓度是其铁电性不被完全屏蔽的关键。那么，**如果载流子浓度显著提高，铁电性是否会如传统掺杂铁电体一样被削弱甚至消失？** 即，WTe₂的铁电性是否严格依赖于其“半金属”而非“良金属”特性？
>     
> 4.  **极化翻转的动力学**：论文展示了准静态的翻转，但未涉及翻转动力学。**在铁电金属中，极化翻转速度是否仍受限于传统铁电体的畴壁运动和成核过程？** 巡游电子是否会对翻转动力学产生新的加速或迟滞效应？这对于其在高频器件中的应用至关重要。
>

> ## 八、用户问题预测与解答
> 
> **问题 1：什么是铁电体？什么是金属？为什么它们通常无法共存？** **答**：**铁电体**是一类具有自发极化，并且这种极化方向可以被外电场翻转的材料。**金属**则是拥有大量自由电子的材料，导电性极好。它们无法共存的原因是：金属中的自由电子会像一群“救火队员”，迅速移动到内部偶极子产生的正负电荷端，将其电场完全抵消，这个现象叫**静电屏蔽**。因此，在典型的良导体中，由离子位移产生的内部电场无法稳定存在，铁电性也就无从谈起。
> 
> **问题 2：压电力显微镜（PFM）是如何“看到”铁电畴的？** **答**：PFM的探针就像一位极其灵敏的“盲人摸象者”。它工作时，会向材料施加一个微小的交流电压。由于**逆压电效应**，铁电材料在电压下会发生微小的机械形变（膨胀或收缩）。极化方向向上的区域和向下的区域，在相同电压下形变方向相反。探针通过探测这种纳米级的形变，就能区分出极化方向不同的区域。振幅图告诉我们形变有多强，而相位图则告诉我们形变的方向，从而“画”出铁电畴的分布图。
> 
> **问题 3：既然WTe₂是金属，如何能用电压去翻转它的极化，而不导致短路？** **答**：这正是本实验设计的精妙之处。研究者不是直接将电压加在块体WTe₂上，而是制备了一个**金属-绝缘体-半导体/金属**的**电容器结构**。他们巧妙地利用了WTe₂在空气中自然形成的、极薄（~2.5 nm）的非导电氧化层作为绝缘层。当施加电压时，这个绝缘层阻断了直流电流，但电场依然可以穿透并作用于WTe₂，从而实现对铁电性的调控。这就像在高压电网下，你虽然不能直接触摸电线，但电场依然存在。
> 
> **问题 4：外尔半金属（Weyl Semimetal）是什么？它与这项发现有什么关系？** **答**：**外尔半金属**是一种新型的拓扑量子态，可以看作是一个“三维的石墨烯”。它的导带和价带在三维空间中的一些离散点（称为外尔点）相接，并且在这些点附近，电子的行为像无质量的粒子（外尔费米子）。WTe₂就是第二种被预言的**II型外尔半金属**。这项发现意味着，WTe₂同时集铁电性和拓扑性于一身，我们未来或许能用**电场**来移动或湮灭其外尔点，从而实现用**静电手段操控拓扑量子态**，这是一个极具想象空间的前沿方向。
> 
> **问题 5：这项发现有什么实际应用价值？** **答**：最直接的应用是**非易失性存储（如铁电存储器）**。当前的研究正努力将器件推向原子级厚度，而二维金属电极是瓶颈之一。WTe₂既是铁电体又是金属，这意味着它可以作为**自带开关功能的电极**。更长远来看，其应用潜力包括：
> 
> -   **多态存储**：同时利用电阻态（金属性）和极化态（铁电性）进行数据存储，实现超高密度。
> -   **低功耗逻辑器件**：利用负电容效应，可能制造出突破玻尔兹曼极限的超低功耗晶体管。
> -   **拓扑电子学器件**：利用电场控制拓扑态，开发新型量子计算和自旋电子学器件。
>

> ## 九、专区：便于 Obsidian Dataview 插件调用
> 
> 领域基础知识:: 铁电性（Ferroelectricity）指材料具有自发且可被外电场翻转的电偶极矩。金属性（Metallicity）指材料拥有大量自由电子，导电性随温度降低而增强。传统理论认为，金属中的自由电子会屏蔽内部电场，因此铁电性和金属性无法在同一材料中共存。安德森和布朗特在1965年提出了“铁电金属”理论预言。
> 
> 研究背景:: 尽管已有低温下极性金属的报道，以及通过掺杂或界面工程实现铁电性与金属性共存的尝试，但在室温下，单一相的块体晶体中，尚未有实验证实存在本征的、具有可翻转极化状态的铁电金属。实现这一物理现象一直是凝聚态物理的长期目标。
> 
> 作者的问题意识:: 作者旨在回答一个核心问题：在具有非中心对称结构的块体半金属WTe₂中，能否在室温下实现并直接观测到本征的、可翻转的铁电性，从而证实安德森和布朗特的理论预言？
> 
> 主要研究对象:: 块体范德华晶体二碲化钨（WTe₂），一种具有正交Td相结构（极性空间群Pmn21）的过渡金属硫族化合物（TMDC），同时也是II型外尔半金属。
> 
> 主要研究方法:: 综合运用了**电输运测量**（确认金属性和半金属基态）、**压电力显微镜（PFM）**（纳米尺度探测铁电畴和极化翻转）以及**第一性原理密度泛函理论计算（DFT）**（揭示铁电性起源与翻转机理）。实验中通过构建电容器结构来解决金属中极化翻转的难题。
> 
> 研究意义:: 在实验上首次证实了室温下的本征铁电半金属，解决了物理界长达半个多世纪的争论。它将铁电学的研究范畴拓展到拓扑半金属，开辟了“铁电拓扑电子学”新范式，并为基于范德华材料的新型超薄、低功耗电子器件提供了全新的材料平台和设计思路。
> 
> 研究结论:: 研究证实，块体WTe₂晶体在室温下同时表现出金属导电性和铁电性。通过PFM直接观测到了纳米尺度的铁电畴，并通过电容器结构实现了可逆的极化翻转。DFT计算表明，其铁电性源于晶体结构畸变，而半金属的低载流子浓度导致屏蔽不完全，使铁电性得以幸存。
>

Conventionally, ferroelectricity has often been associated with and observed in materials that are insulating or semiconducting rather than metallic because conduction electrons in metals screen out the static internal fields arising from a long-range dipolar order. In the 1960s, Anderson and Blount (3) proposed a new class of materials with these seemingly incompatible characteristics, i.e., metals with a polar axis and an inversion asymmetric crystal structure termed “ferroelectric metals.” Since then, an experimental demonstration of this concept for a room-temperature single-phase material has remained elusive. Metallic systems undergoing a centrosymmetric-to-noncentrosymmetric structural transition have been observed recently, e.g., in LiOsO3 at 140 K (4) and Cd2Re2O7 at 200 K (5), hinting at the possibility of sustaining ferroelectricity in a metal at room temperature. More progress has been made lately with a study (6) reporting on the observation of polar domains and strain-induced ferroelastic switching in bulk polar metal Ca3Ru2O7 at room temperature. Another strategy that has also been commonly used, besides investigating metallic systems with a noncentrosymmetric crystal structure (7), is to dope well-established ferroelectric materials, such as BaTiO3-d (8, 9) and Nb-doped PbTiO3 (10). However, the ferroelectric instability weakens with increasing electronic density in these systems and will eventually be destroyed above a certain critical density (11). Furthermore, it is not clear that the ferroelectricity and metallicity coexist in a single phase in these materials (12), as other studies suggest a nanoscale phase separation into separate metallic and ferroelectric phases (13). Another approach to obtaining a ferroelectric metal has been to engineer interface-based polar metals in oxide heterostructures (14–16). Despite these investigations, the experimental realization of a native metal with bistable and electrically switchable spontaneous polarization states, the hallmark of ferroelectricity, is yet to be demonstrated. During the submission process of this manuscript, we became aware of a closely related work (17), in which ferroelectric switching has been demonstrated in device structures through electrical transport measurements for bilayer and trilayer WTe2. Fei et al. (17) quantify and establish the temperature dependence of the polarization. However, a natural question arises as to whether ferroelectricity persists to samples thicker than three layers, or even to bulk crystals, and whether ferroelectric domains can be formed. Here, we observe the coexistence of native metallicity and ferroelectricity in bulk crystalline WTe2 at room temperature. Bulk single crystals in their pristine state show ferroelectric domains that are visualized directly. We demonstrate that the bistable spontaneous ferroelectric polarization state is switchable under an external electrical bias and explain the mechanism for “metallic ferroelectricity” in WTe2 through a systematic study of the crystal structure, electronic transport measurements, and theoretical considerations. Density functional theory (DFT) calculations show that the ferroelectricity is related to the anisotropy of the crystal structure. A van der Waals material that is both metallic and ferroelectric in its bulk crystalline form at room temperature has potential for new nanoelectronics applications.

> > One of the defining aspects of ferroelectrics is the reorientation of polarization by means of an external electric field. However, unlike insulating ferroelectrics, switching the polarization is difficult in WTe2 because of its high conductivity: The applied bias induces electrical current rather than acts on the polar distortion (fig. S1). Nonetheless, if the flow of large electrical currents can be prevented, e.g., by insertion of a dielectric layer between the contacts, then it is possible to apply an electric field to WTe2 and realize ferroelectric switching. To achieve such a configuration and to preclude the possibility of direct charge injection (30) from the tip into the WTe2, we prepared thin film samples in a capacitor geometry (Fig. 3A and see Materials and Methods for details). In these capacitor structures, current flow is inhibited by a dielectric layer at the ferroelectric/metal interface (see also section S3) (38), as the WTe2 sample surface briefly exposed to air before the metal deposition forms a very thin oxide layer (34). Figure 3B shows a metalgated WTe2 flake on the surface of a Ti/Au-coated silicon substrate. The WTe2 flake is 15 nm thick, and the Ti/Au metal electrode on top of it is 9.5 nm thick (Fig. 3B, inset). In this configuration, the electrical current flow across the WTe2 is strongly suppressed (fig. S3), although the WTe2 flake is still metallic (25). To demonstrate switching of the ferroelectric polarization of WTe2, spectroscopic PFM measurements were performed through the top electrode in a capacitor geometry, which has been successfully used before for subelectrode PFM measurements (29, 30). The acquired piezoresponse as a function of applied bias (Fig. 3, C and D) shows switchable hysteretic behavior consistent with that seen in traditional ferroelectrics such as BaTiO3 and Pb(ZrxTi1-x)O3 (32). The PFM amplitude response displays a characteristic “butterfly” curve, while the corresponding phase response exhibits a phase inversion (i.e., a phase change of approximately 180°) at minima of the amplitude response. To directly visualize these switchable polarization states, we applied switching bias pulses with the subsequent acquisition of PFM images to capture the remnant state. The bias-driven, oppositely oriented remnant polarization can be clearly seen in the PFM images of different color contrasts (Fig. 3, E to G). The initial near-uniform bright contrast of the phase image transforms to a predominant dark phase contrast. This biasinduced transformation between antiparallel equivalent polarization states of the WTe2 can be accomplished repeatedly and in a reversible fashion by inverting the polarity of the applied writing bias (fig. S4). These measurements therefore demonstrate that the polarization of WTe2 is switchable under an external bias.

> > ## Theoretical insight into the ferroelectric instability
> > 
> > 对铁电不稳定性的理论洞察

> > 内容段落：
> > 
> > To gain further insight into electronic and structural origins of the ferroelectric instability of WTe2, we performed first-principles DFT calculations. Td-phase WTe2 (Td-WTe2) has a C2v point group symmetry, corresponding to the noncentrosymmetric space group Pmn21. The C2v point group contains mirror ac and bc planes precluding polarization along [100] or [010] direction. The mirror ab plane does not belong to this point group and therefore allows for polarization along the [001] direction. Theoretical predictions also show that polar axis orientation can be correlated with electronic anisotropy in layered materials (7). Figure 4A shows the electronic band structure of Td-WTe2 calculated by DFT (27, 39). The valence band maximum and the conduction band minimum cross the Fermi energy in the G-X direction, resulting in the formation of small hole and electron pockets, respectively. The valence band dispersion in the G-Z direction is very flat, consistent with nonuniform conduction charge densities in the [001] direction in Fig. 4B and the anisotropic transport in bulk WTe2 (40). Therefore, a spontaneous electrical polarization in Td-WTe2 along the [001] direction is feasible. In a ferroelectric material, the total polarization P can be split into contributions from ionic cores (Pion) and electrons below EF (Pelectron). In Td-WTe2, Pelectron can be further split into the contribution of the valence bands (PVB), the contribution of the electron pocket (Pep), and that of the hole pocket (Php). Therefore, the total polarization is P 1⁄4 Pion þ Pelectron 1⁄4 Pion þ PVB–Php þ Pep Pion + PVB dominates the overall effect and is trivial to compute by the Berry phase method (41, 42) with fixed band occupations. We obtain Pion + PVB = 0.19 mC/cm2. On the other hand, calculating Php and Pep is not standard. Here, we take the method suggested by Filippetti et al. (7) to estimate Php and Pep by calculating the dipole associated with the conduction electrons. As shown in Fig. 4B, the planar averaged charge density from the electron pocket for each WTe2 layer is almost symmetric, which does not contribute a dipole. On the other hand, the carriers in the hole pocket contribute a dipole within the layer. However, because of the tiny carrier density, the resulting polarization is negligible (<0.01 mC/cm2). The total estimated polarization P is ~0.19 mC/cm2. Normally, for a ferroelectric, the opposite (i.e., antiparallel) Pup and Pdown polarization states can be transformed into one another by application of an inversion operation. The calculated energy barrier for such a transformation in Td-WTe2 is rather high [0.70 eV/formula unit (f.u.)] (Fig. 4D, top). This is because such a transformation is associated with a large in-plane lattice displacement. However, the Td phase is a distorted form of the hexagonal structure with a threefold rotation symmetry (Fig. 4C). Therefore, there are three equivalent distortion vectors (43). The switching between opposite polarization states with different distortion vectors (i.e., Pup of one distortion vector and Pdown of another distortion vector) should have smaller in-plane atomic displacements on average, leading to a smaller energy barrier. As shown in Fig. 4D (bottom), in this scenario, the energy barrier between opposite polarization states denoted as Pup1 and Pdown2 is reduced to 0.29 eV/f.u. This value is smaller than (but comparable to) the energy barrier of ~0.43 eV/f.u. for BiFeO3 (44), a prototypical ferroelectric with a high Curie temperature (~1000 K). A transition path for polarization switching in WTe2 with an even lower energy barrier has also been proposed recently (45). Hence, polarization switching in WTe2 is achievable under normal experimental conditions from a theoretical point of view, supporting the theory that WTe2 is not only polar but also ferroelectric.

> > 讨论部分开头：

> > Using density functional perturbation theory (46) and the finite differences method (47), we calculated the magnitude of the piezoelectric coefficient of WTe2 (see table S1 and fig. S5 for details). The calculated value of the longitudinal piezoelectric coefficient (d33) is about 6.5 pm/V. We find that this calculated value is about an order of magnitude larger than the experimentally measured value of ~0.7 pm/V (see fig. S6 and corresponding text in section S5). However, the experimentally determined rather low value of the piezoelectric constant is understandable if one were to consider that we likely extract the piezoresponse within the Debye length of WTe2, which is ~1.6 nm (see section S6 for details on the calculation of Debye length). Moreover, the calculation is done for a uniform system, while in the measurements, the electric field distribution is likely highly nonuniform. While the detection of piezoresponse at these small length scales of few nanometers is challenging, it is nevertheless not unrealistic. Furthermore, we are aware of electrically induced extrinsic mechanisms (e.g., electrostatic effects and/or electrochemical processes) that can manifest as a piezoelectric- or a ferroelectric-like effect in PFM studies of nominally nonferroelectric materials. However, the experimental data in the present study of WTe2 cannot be explained by these spurious extrinsic mechanisms (see section S7 for a detailed discussion). We note that the Debye length of ~1.6 nm is relatively short compared to the material thicknesses investigated in our study. Nevertheless, one can obtain depth-sensitive data from electrical measurements for thicknesses larger than the Debye length in WTe2 (48). In our study, we have observed ferroelectric domains in thick bulk crystals and ferroelectric switching in ~15-nm films. The question of the physical extent of these domains is an interesting one. One possibility is that the piezoresponse and ferroelectric domains come only from within a Debye length of the surface. However, it is also possible that the ferroelectric domains extend much deeper into the material because ferroelectricity and ferroelasticity are often coupled in conventional ferroelectrics and because ferroelastic domains have been predicted for bulk WTe2 (43). In this latter scenario, ferroelectric domains could potentially propagate through elastically mediated wall motion, where fluctuation in bond lengths by electric fields initiated on the surface (within the Debye length) generates strain down into the bulk of the material and large domains could propagate through into the depth of the material (6). We hope that our study will stimulate further theoretical and experimental studies to answer this question. In summary, the concept of ferroelectric metals predicted in the 1960s has been realized in a Weyl semimetal, WTe2. A combination of experimental and first-principles DFT techniques demonstrates the coexistence of native metallicity and ferroelectricity at room temperature. Bulk crystalline WTe2 exhibits bistable polarization states that are switchable under an external electric field—thus, ferroelectricity is a bulk property of WTe2—and is not just confined to few-layer samples. The investigation of ferroelectric domain walls (49, 50) in metallic systems constitutes an interesting aspect for future research and potential nanoelectronics applications, following recent findings in conventional ferroelectrics. Our findings also raise prospects for discovering ferroelectricity in other metallic layered materials.

> # A room-temperature ferroelectric semimetal
> 
> 室温铁电半金属 **Ferroelectric**: 铁电体，具有自发极化且极化方向可随外加电场反转的材料。 **Semimetal**: 半金属，导带和价带有小重叠，呈半金属性。

> Pankaj Sharma1,2*†, Fei-Xiang Xiang2,3*†, Ding-Fu Shao4*, Dawei Zhang1, Evgeny Y. Tsymbal4†, Alex R. Hamilton2,3†, Jan Seidel1,2† Pankaj Sharma等人（作者列表）
> 
> Coexistence of reversible polar distortions and metallicity leading to a ferroelectric metal, first suggested by Anderson and Blount in 1965, has so far remained elusive. Electrically switchable intrinsic electric polarization, together with the direct observation of ferroelectric domains, has not yet been realized in a bulk crystalline metal, although incomplete screening by mobile conduction charges should, in principle, be possible. Here, we provide evidence that native metallicity and ferroelectricity coexist in bulk crystalline van der Waals WTe2 by means of electrical transport, nanoscale piezoresponse measurements, and first-principles calculations. We show that, despite being a Weyl semimetal, WTe2 has switchable spontaneous polarization and a natural ferroelectric domain structure at room temperature. This new class of materials has tantalizing potential for functional nanoelectronics applications. 可逆极性畸变与金属性共存导致铁电金属的概念，由Anderson和Blount于1965年首次提出，至今仍难以实现。尽管理论上移动导电电荷的不完全屏蔽应该可能，但在块体晶体金属中，电可切换的固有电极化以及铁电畴的直接观察尚未实现。在此，我们通过电输运、纳米压电响应测量和第一性原理计算，提供证据表明块体晶体范德华WTe2中固有金属性和铁电性共存。我们表明，尽管WTe2是外尔半金属，但它在室温下具有可切换的自发极化和天然的铁电畴结构。这类新材料在功能纳米电子学应用中具有诱人的潜力。 **Ferroelectric metal**: 铁电金属，同时具有铁电性和金属性的材料。 **Weyl semimetal**: 外尔半金属，一种拓扑半金属，其电子能带具有外尔点。 **Van der Waals**: 范德华，指层间由弱范德华力结合的材料。 **Piezoresponse**: 压电响应，材料在电场下产生形变或反之的特性。 **Ferroelectric domains**: 铁电畴，铁电体中自发极化方向一致的区域。

> # INTRODUCTION
> 
> 引言
> 
> Ferroelectric materials have a spontaneous electric dipole moment, i.e., polarization, even in the absence of an external electric field. This spontaneous electric dipole moment can be repeatedly transitioned between two or more energetically equivalent states or directions upon application of an external electric field that breaks the degeneracy and forms the fundamental underpinning of numerous technological applications of ferroelectric materials (1). For piezoelectricity, noncentrosymmetric crystal structure is the only requirement (2). In contrast, for a polar material, not only must the crystal structure be noncentrosymmetric but also there should exist a unique polar axis. For a material to be considered a ferroelectric, it needs both to be polar and to show bistability of the polarization along the polar axis (2). Conventionally, ferroelectricity has often been associated with and observed in materials that are insulating or semiconducting rather than metallic because conduction electrons in metals screen out the static internal fields arising from a long-range dipolar order. In the 1960s, Anderson and Blount (3) proposed a new class of materials with these seemingly incompatible characteristics, i.e., metals with a polar axis and an inversion asymmetric crystal structure termed “ferroelectric metals.” Since then, an experimental demonstration of this concept for a room-temperature single-phase material has remained elusive. Metallic systems undergoing a centrosymmetric-to-noncentrosymmetric structural transition have been observed recently, e.g., in LiOsO3 at 140 K (4) and Cd2Re2O7 at 200 K (5), hinting at the possibility of sustaining ferroelectricity in a metal at room temperature. More progress has been made lately with a study (6) reporting on the observation of polar domains and strain-induced ferroelastic switching in bulk polar metal Ca3Ru2O7 at room temperature. Another strategy that has also been commonly used, besides investigating metallic systems with a noncentrosymmetric crystal structure (7), is to dope well-established ferroelectric materials, such as BaTiO3-d (8, 9) and Nb-doped PbTiO3 (10). However, the ferroelectric instability weakens with increasing electronic density in these systems and will eventually be destroyed above a certain critical density (11). Furthermore, it is not clear that the ferroelectricity and metallicity coexist in a single phase in these materials (12), as other studies suggest a nanoscale phase separation into separate metallic and ferroelectric phases (13). Another approach to obtaining a ferroelectric metal has been to engineer interface-based polar metals in oxide heterostructures (14–16). Despite these investigations, the experimental realization of a native metal with bistable and electrically switchable spontaneous polarization states, the hallmark of ferroelectricity, is yet to be demonstrated. During the submission process of this manuscript, we became aware of a closely related work (17), in which ferroelectric switching has been demonstrated in device structures through electrical transport measurements for bilayer and trilayer WTe2. Fei et al. (17) quantify and establish the temperature dependence of the polarization. However, a natural question arises as to whether ferroelectricity persists to samples thicker than three layers, or even to bulk crystals, and whether ferroelectric domains can be formed. Here, we observe the coexistence of native metallicity and ferroelectricity in bulk crystalline WTe2 at room temperature. Bulk single crystals in their pristine state show ferroelectric domains that are visualized directly. We demonstrate that the bistable spontaneous ferroelectric polarization state is switchable under an external electrical bias and explain the mechanism for “metallic ferroelectricity” in WTe2 through a systematic study of the crystal structure, electronic transport measurements, and theoretical considerations. Density functional theory (DFT) calculations show that the ferroelectricity is related to the anisotropy of the crystal structure. A van der Waals material that is both metallic and ferroelectric in its bulk crystalline form at room temperature has potential for new nanoelectronics applications. 铁电材料具有自发电偶极矩，即极化，即使在没有外部电场的情况下也是如此。这种自发电偶极矩可以在施加外部电场时在两个或多个能量等效的状态或方向之间反复转换，这打破了简并，构成了铁电材料众多技术应用的基础（1）。对于压电性，非中心对称晶体结构是唯一要求（2）。相比之下，对于极性材料，不仅晶体结构必须是非中心对称的，而且必须存在唯一的极性轴。要使材料被认为是铁电体，它需要既具有极性，又表现出沿极性轴的极化双重稳定性（2）。传统上，铁电性通常与绝缘体或半导体而非金属相关联，因为金属中的传导电子会屏蔽由长程偶极序产生的静态内场。在20世纪60年代，Anderson和Blount（3）提出了一类具有这些看似不相容特性的新材料，即具有极性轴和反演不对称晶体结构的金属，称为“铁电金属”。从那时起，室温单相材料的这一概念的实验演示一直未能实现。最近观察到经历中心对称到非中心对称结构转变的金属系统，例如在140 K的LiOsO3（4）和200 K的Cd2Re2O7（5），暗示了在室温下金属中维持铁电性的可能性。最近的一项研究（6）报告了在室温下块体极性金属Ca3Ru2O7中观察到的极性畴和应变诱导的铁弹切换，取得了更多进展。除了研究具有非中心对称晶体结构的金属系统（7）之外，另一种常用的策略是掺杂成熟的铁电材料，如BaTiO3-δ（8, 9）和Nb掺杂的PbTiO3（10）。然而，在这些系统中，铁电不稳定性随着电子密度的增加而减弱，并最终在超过某个临界密度时被破坏（11）。此外，尚不清楚在这些材料中铁电性和金属性是否共存于单相中（12），因为其他研究表明纳米级相分离为分开的金属相和铁电相（13）。获得铁电金属的另一种方法是在氧化物异质结构中设计基于界面的极性金属（14-16）。尽管进行了这些研究，但具有双稳态和电可切换自发极化状态的天然金属的实验实现，即铁电性的标志，仍有待证明。在本手稿提交过程中，我们注意到一项密切相关的工作（17），其中通过双层和三层WTe2的电输运测量在器件结构中演示了铁电切换。Fei等人（17）量化并建立了极化的温度依赖性。然而，一个自然的问题是，铁电性是否存在于厚于三层的样品，甚至块体晶体中，以及铁电畴能否形成。在这里，我们在室温下观察到块体晶体WTe2中固有金属性和铁电性的共存。原始状态的块体单晶显示出可直接可视化的铁电畴。我们证明了双稳态自发铁电极化状态在外部电偏压下是可切换的，并通过对晶体结构、电子输运测量和理论考虑的系统研究，解释了WTe2中“金属铁电性”的机制。密度泛函理论（DFT）计算表明，铁电性与晶体结构的各向异性有关。一种在室温下块体晶型中既具有金属性又具有铁电性的范德华材料，具有新的纳米电子学应用潜力。 **Noncentrosymmetric**: 非中心对称，晶体结构缺乏对称中心。 **Polar axis**: 极性轴，晶体中唯一的对称轴，允许极化方向。 **Bistability**: 双稳态，系统存在两个稳定状态。 **Ferroelastic switching**: 铁弹切换，指铁弹畴在应力作用下的切换。 **Density functional theory (DFT)**: 密度泛函理论，一种研究多电子体系电子结构的量子力学方法。

> ## Noncentrosymmetric crystal structure and semimetallic ground state
> 
> 非中心对称晶体结构和半金属基态
> 
> Tungsten ditelluride, WTe2, belongs to the class of materials known as transition metal dichalcogenides (TMDCs). These materials exhibit many different crystal structures, such as hexagonal (2H), monoclinic (1T), and orthorhombic (Td) (18, 19). WTe2 crystallizes in a layered orthorhombic structure (also known as the Td phase; Fig. 1A), in which the tungsten atoms are octahedrally coordinated by the tellurium atoms, and the successive layers in between are rotated by 180° (20, 21). Because of strong intermetallic bonding, the tungsten atoms form slightly buckled zigzag chains resulting in distortion of the tellurium octahedra (around each tungsten atom) (19). Figure 1B shows a typical x-ray diffraction (XRD) pattern of c axis–oriented WTe2 single crystals investigated in this work. The (00l) diffraction peaks confirm the presence of the single-crystalline Td phase of WTe2 (20). The lack of inversion symmetry in the Td phase of WTe2 (polar space group, Pmn21) has played an important role in its identification as a type II Weyl semimetal (22). Unlike most TMDCs, WTe2 is a semimetal in its native ground state (23, 24), instead of being a semiconductor. We confirmed the semimetallic ground state of WTe2 by electrical transport measurements (Fig. 1C). The resistance of WTe2 decreases with decreasing temperature from room temperature down to 10 K, a behavior typical of metallic systems. Furthermore, magnetoresistance measurements at 30 mK with magnetic fields up to 10 T reveal pronounced Shubnikov–de Haas (SdH) oscillations with four main frequencies (Fig. 1, D and E). These oscillations confirm the existence of four well-defined Fermi surfaces, consisting of two sets of electron and hole pockets (25). The presence of both free electrons and holes is believed to be the origin of the extremely large, nonsaturated magnetoresistance in WTe2 (26–28). Given the existence of metallic conduction down to 30 mK, and the fact that noncentrosymmetry is a necessary but insufficient requirement for a material to be ferroelectric, an intriguing question arises: Is semimetallic WTe2 with a noncentrosymmetric space group (Pmn21) a ferroelectric (7)? 二碲化钨（WTe2）属于过渡金属二硫族化物（TMDCs）类材料。这些材料表现出多种不同的晶体结构，如六方（2H）、单斜（1T）和正交（Td）（18, 19）。WTe2结晶为层状正交结构（也称为Td相；图1A），其中钨原子由碲原子八面体配位，相邻层之间旋转180°（20, 21）。由于强烈的金属间键合，钨原子形成略微弯曲的锯齿形链，导致碲八面体（围绕每个钨原子）畸变（19）。图1B显示了本工作中研究的c轴取向WTe2单晶的典型X射线衍射（XRD）图谱。（00l）衍射峰证实了WTe2的单晶Td相的存在（20）。WTe2的Td相缺乏反演对称性（极性空间群，Pmn21），这在其被鉴定为II型外尔半金属中发挥了重要作用（22）。与大多数TMDCs不同，WTe2在其天然基态下是半金属（23, 24），而不是半导体。我们通过电输运测量证实了WTe2的半金属基态（图1C）。WTe2的电阻从室温到10 K随温度降低而降低，这是金属系统的典型行为。此外，在30 mK和高达10 T的磁场下进行的磁阻测量揭示了明显的舒布尼科夫-德哈斯（SdH）振荡，具有四个主要频率（图1，D和E）。这些振荡证实了四个明确定义的费米面的存在，包括两组电子和空穴口袋（25）。自由电子和空穴的同时存在被认为是WTe2中极大、非饱和磁阻的起源（26-28）。鉴于低至30 mK的金属导电性存在，以及非中心对称性是材料成为铁电体的必要但不充分条件，一个有趣的问题出现了：具有非中心对称空间群（Pmn21）的半金属WTe2是铁电体吗（7）？ **Transition metal dichalcogenides (TMDCs)**: 过渡金属二硫族化物，一类层状材料，通式为MX2，M为过渡金属，X为硫族元素。 **Shubnikov–de Haas (SdH) oscillations**: 舒布尼科夫-德哈斯振荡，金属在低温强磁场下电阻随磁场倒数周期性变化的量子振荡现象。

> ## Ferroelectric domains in single crystals
> 
> 单晶中的铁电畴
> 
> To address this question and probe ferroelectricity in WTe2, we used piezoresponse force microscopy (PFM) to spatially map the polarization. PFM is a standard technique used extensively to investigate domain microstructure and dynamics of polarization in classical ferroelectric materials. It exploits the converse piezoelectric effect and detects lattice deformation due to an applied electric field (29, 30). To ensure pristine surfaces for the investigation, we prepared freshly cleaved WTe2 single-crystal samples (several tens of micrometers thick; Fig. 2, A and B, and see Materials and Methods for details). Measurements were performed in an inert dry N2 environment at room temperature immediately after the crystals were cleaved. In this experiment, the conductive nanoscale tip acts as a movable top electrical contact. Spectroscopic electrical transport measurements performed using conductive atomic force microscopy (c-AFM) reveal the expected metallic behavior of WTe2 and establish the ohmic nature of the electrical contacts (see fig. S1). The measured current-bias curves are linear and show appreciable conduction over a rather small bias range. Subsequently, we performed PFM imaging (see Materials and Methods for details). The PFM images (Fig. 2, C to H) show the presence of antiparallel ferroelectric domains. A few of these domains are visualized in more detail in the high-resolution PFM images (Fig. 2, G and H). The domains usually exhibit a distorted circular profile with an average domain size in the range of ~20 to 50 nm. The domain shape resembles those of well-known ferroelectric materials such as strontium bismuth tantalate (29) and triglycine sulfate (31). The piezoresponse characteristics are textbook (29, 32) examples of ferroelectric behavior (Fig. 2, J and K): (i) Domain walls appear as dark lines (i.e., as a minimum in the PFM amplitude signal) in the amplitude image, and (ii) the PFM phase inverts by ~180° between adjacent domains in the corresponding phase image. The surface of the sample is atomically flat (root mean square roughness, ~0.2 nm) with occasional step edges (Fig. 2C) from the cleaving process. We note that, in some but not all cases, submonolayer surface variations close to the resolution limit of our measurement system were observed near the position of the domains (Fig. 2I). In addition to the small oval domains, micrometersized stripe-like domains (shown by arrows in Fig. 2D) were also resolved. This observation of domains in semimetallic WTe2 single crystals implies that WTe2 not only has a polar axis (c axis) but also has bistable polarization states manifesting as static antiparallel domains. Therefore, the above results strongly support the existence of ferroelectricity in semimetallic WTe2 at room temperature. Another aspect that deserves mention here is the stability of WTe2. It is well known that the surface of WTe2 is quite sensitive and can undergo oxidation in air. Previous works (33–36) have investigated in detail the physical and chemical properties of this surface oxide using a range of techniques including Raman spectroscopy, energy dispersive x-ray spectroscopy, cross-sectional transmission electron microscopy, x-ray photoelectron spectroscopy, and electrical transport studies. The surface oxide forms through evolution of W––O (WOx) and Te––O (TeO2) secondary bonds at the surface of WTe2 in air. The WTe2 oxidation is a self-limiting process (35) and results in an amorphous surface oxide layer, which is about 2 nm thick (34). To independently determine time-dependent thickness evolution of the surface oxide, we performed ellipsometry measurements (see section S2 and fig. S2). From our measurements, we find that the thickness of surface oxide saturates at about 2.5 nm after several hours of atmospheric exposure (fig. S2). These results thus are consistent with earlier published reports (34). The exposure time for surface oxide formation ranges from a few hours to days (37). The bulk crystals and relatively thick WTe2 samples are more stable in air compared to samples with just few layers, especially monolayer and bilayer WTe2 (35, 36). Moreover, there is no polar space group in amorphous materials, and ferroelectricity cannot arise as it exists only in crystalline materials. This is the very reason that, in the literature, there are no publications reporting on the observation of existing ferroelectric domains or domain-like features using PFM in amorphous materials. Nevertheless, we performed the measurements presented in Fig. 2 on a freshly cleaved atomically smooth WTe2 single crystal in a controlled N2 environment (see also fig. > ## Probing ferroelectricity and polarization switching
> 
> 探测铁电性和极化切换
> 
> One of the defining aspects of ferroelectrics is the reorientation of polarization by means of an external electric field. However, unlike insulating ferroelectrics, switching the polarization is difficult in WTe2 because of its high conductivity: The applied bias induces electrical current rather than acts on the polar distortion (fig. S1). Nonetheless, if the flow of large electrical currents can be prevented, e.g., by insertion of a dielectric layer between the contacts, then it is possible to apply an electric field to WTe2 and realize ferroelectric switching. To achieve such a configuration and to preclude the possibility of direct charge injection (30) from the tip into the WTe2, we prepared thin film samples in a capacitor geometry (Fig. 3A and see Materials and Methods for details). In these capacitor structures, current flow is inhibited by a dielectric layer at the ferroelectric/metal interface (see also section S3) (38), as the WTe2 sample surface briefly exposed to air before the metal deposition forms a very thin oxide layer (34). Figure 3B shows a metalgated WTe2 flake on the surface of a Ti/Au-coated silicon substrate. The WTe2 flake is 15 nm thick, and the Ti/Au metal electrode on top of it is 9.5 nm thick (Fig. 3B, inset). In this configuration, the electrical current flow across the WTe2 is strongly suppressed (fig. S3), although the WTe2 flake is still metallic (25). To demonstrate switching of the ferroelectric polarization of WTe2, spectroscopic PFM measurements were performed through the top electrode in a capacitor geometry, which has been successfully used before for subelectrode PFM measurements (29, 30). The acquired piezoresponse as a function of applied bias (Fig. 3, C and D) shows switchable hysteretic behavior consistent with that seen in traditional ferroelectrics such as BaTiO3 and Pb(ZrxTi1-x)O3 (32). The PFM amplitude response displays a characteristic “butterfly” curve, while the corresponding phase response exhibits a phase inversion (i.e., a phase change of approximately 180°) at minima of the amplitude response. To directly visualize these switchable polarization states, we applied switching bias pulses with the subsequent acquisition of PFM images to capture the remnant state. The bias-driven, oppositely oriented remnant polarization can be clearly seen in the PFM images of different color contrasts (Fig. 3, E to G). The initial near-uniform bright contrast of the phase image transforms to a predominant dark phase contrast. This biasinduced transformation between antiparallel equivalent polarization states of the WTe2 can be accomplished repeatedly and in a reversible fashion by inverting the polarity of the applied writing bias (fig. S4). These measurements therefore demonstrate that the polarization of WTe2 is switchable under an external bias. 铁电体的一个决定性方面是通过外部电场重新定向极化。然而，与绝缘铁电体不同，在WTe2中切换极化很困难，因为其高导电性：施加的偏压产生电流，而不是作用于极性畸变（图S1）。尽管如此，如果可以阻止大电流的流动，例如通过在接触之间插入介电层，那么就可以向WTe2施加电场并实现铁电切换。为了实现这样的配置并排除从尖端直接电荷注入WTe2的可能性（30），我们制备了电容器几何形状的薄膜样品（图3A，详见材料与方法）。在这些电容器结构中，电流流动被铁电/金属界面处的介电层抑制（另见第S3节）（38），因为在金属沉积前短暂暴露于空气的WTe2样品表面形成了非常薄的氧化层（34）。图3B显示了在涂有Ti/Au的硅衬底表面的金属栅控WTe2薄片。WTe2薄片厚15 nm，其顶部的Ti/Au金属电极厚9.5 nm（图3B，插图）。在这种配置中，尽管WTe2薄片仍然是金属性的，但穿过WTe2的电流流动被强烈抑制（图S3）（25）。为了演示WTe2的铁电极化切换，通过顶部电极在电容器几何形状中进行了光谱PFM测量，这种方法之前已成功用于亚电极PFM测量（29, 30）。作为施加偏压函数获取的压电响应（图3，C和D）显示出可切换的滞后行为，与在传统铁电体如BaTiO3和Pb(ZrxTi1-x)O3（32）中观察到的行为一致。PFM振幅响应显示出特征性的“蝴蝶”曲线，而相应的相位响应在振幅响应的最小值处表现出相位反转（即约180°的相位变化）。为了直接可视化这些可切换的极化状态，我们施加了切换偏压脉冲，随后获取PFM图像以捕获剩余状态。偏压驱动的、取向相反的剩余极化可以在不同颜色对比度的PFM图像中清晰看到（图3，E至G）。相位图像初始近乎均匀的亮对比度转变为主要的暗相位对比度。这种偏压诱导的WTe2反平行等效极化状态之间的转换，可以通过反转施加的写入偏压的极性，以可逆的方式重复实现（图S4）。因此，这些测量表明，WTe2的极化在外部偏压下是可切换的。 **Hysteretic behavior**: 滞后行为，系统状态依赖于历史路径的现象，铁电体中表现为极化-电场回线。

> ## Theoretical insight into the ferroelectric instability
> 
> 对铁电不稳定性的理论洞察
> 
> To gain further insight into electronic and structural origins of the ferroelectric instability of WTe2, we performed first-principles DFT calculations. Td-phase WTe2 (Td-WTe2) has a C2v point group symmetry, corresponding to the noncentrosymmetric space group Pmn21. The C2v point group contains mirror ac and bc planes precluding polarization along [100] or [010] direction. The mirror ab plane does not belong to this point group and therefore allows for polarization along the [001] direction. Theoretical predictions also show that polar axis orientation can be correlated with electronic anisotropy in layered materials (7). Figure 4A shows the electronic band structure of Td-WTe2 calculated by DFT (27, 39). The valence band maximum and the conduction band minimum cross the Fermi energy in the G-X direction, resulting in the formation of small hole and electron pockets, respectively. The valence band dispersion in the G-Z direction is very flat, consistent with nonuniform conduction charge densities in the [001] direction in Fig. 4B and the anisotropic transport in bulk WTe2 (40). Therefore, a spontaneous electrical polarization in Td-WTe2 along the [001] direction is feasible. In a ferroelectric material, the total polarization P can be split into contributions from ionic cores (Pion) and electrons below EF (Pelectron). In Td-WTe2, Pelectron can be further split into the contribution of the valence bands (PVB), the contribution of the electron pocket (Pep), and that of the hole pocket (Php). Therefore, the total polarization is P 1⁄4 Pion þ Pelectron 1⁄4 Pion þ PVB–Php þ Pep Pion + PVB dominates the overall effect and is trivial to compute by the Berry phase method (41, 42) with fixed band occupations. We obtain Pion + PVB = 0.19 mC/cm2. On the other hand, calculating Php and Pep is not standard. Here, we take the method suggested by Filippetti et al. (7) to estimate Php and Pep by calculating the dipole associated with the conduction electrons. As shown in Fig. 4B, the planar averaged charge density from the electron pocket for each WTe2 layer is almost symmetric, which does not contribute a dipole. On the other hand, the carriers in the hole pocket contribute a dipole within the layer. However, because of the tiny carrier density, the resulting polarization is negligible (<0.01 mC/cm2). The total estimated polarization P is ~0.19 mC/cm2. Normally, for a ferroelectric, the opposite (i.e., antiparallel) Pup and Pdown polarization states can be transformed into one another by application of an inversion operation. The calculated energy barrier for such a transformation in Td-WTe2 is rather high [0.70 eV/formula unit (f.u.)] (Fig. 4D, top). This is because such a transformation is associated with a large in-plane lattice displacement. However, the Td phase is a distorted form of the hexagonal structure with a threefold rotation symmetry (Fig. 4C). Therefore, there are three equivalent distortion vectors (43). The switching between opposite polarization states with different distortion vectors (i.e., Pup of one distortion vector and Pdown of another distortion vector) should have smaller in-plane atomic displacements on average, leading to a smaller energy barrier. As shown in Fig. 4D (bottom), in this scenario, the energy barrier between opposite polarization states denoted as Pup1 and Pdown2 is reduced to 0.29 eV/f.u. This value is smaller than (but comparable to) the energy barrier of ~0.43 eV/f.u. for BiFeO3 (44), a prototypical ferroelectric with a high Curie temperature (~1000 K). A transition path for polarization switching in WTe2 with an even lower energy barrier has also been proposed recently (45). Hence, polarization switching in WTe2 is achievable under normal experimental conditions from a theoretical point of view, supporting the theory that WTe2 is not only polar but also ferroelectric. 为了进一步了解WTe2铁电不稳定性的电子和结构起源，我们进行了第一性原理DFT计算。Td相WTe2（Td-WTe2）具有C2v点群对称性，对应于非中心对称空间群Pmn21。C2v点群包含ac和bc镜面，排除了沿[100]或[010]方向的极化。ab镜面不属于该点群，因此允许沿[001]方向的极化。理论预测还表明，极性轴取向可以与层状材料中的电子各向异性相关联（7）。图4A显示了通过DFT计算的Td-WTe2的电子能带结构（27, 39）。价带顶部和导带底部在G-X方向穿过费米能级，分别形成小的空穴和电子口袋。价带在G-Z方向的色散非常平坦，与图4B中[001]方向的不均匀导电电荷密度以及块体WTe2中的各向异性输运一致（40）。因此，Td-WTe2中沿[001]方向的自发极化是可行的。在铁电材料中，总极化P可以分解为离子实（Pion）和费米能级以下电子（Pelectron）的贡献。在Td-WTe2中，Pelectron可以进一步分解为价带（PVB）的贡献、电子口袋（Pep）的贡献和空穴口袋（Php）的贡献。因此，总极化是 P = Pion + Pelectron = Pion + PVB - Php + Pep Pion + PVB主导了整体效应，并且可以通过固定带占据的Berry相位方法（41, 42）简单计算。我们得到Pion + PVB = 0.19 μC/cm²。另一方面，计算Php和Pep并不标准。在这里，我们采用Filippetti等人（7）建议的方法，通过计算与传导电子相关的偶极子来估计Php和Pep。如图4B所示，每个WTe2层的电子口袋的平面平均电荷密度几乎是对称的，不贡献偶极子。另一方面，空穴口袋中的载流子在层内贡献一个偶极子。然而，由于载流子密度极小，由此产生的极化可忽略不计（<0.01 μC/cm²）。总估计极化P约为0.19 μC/cm²。通常，对于铁电体，相反（即反平行）的Pup和Pdown极化状态可以通过施加反演操作相互转换。在Td-WTe2中，这种转换的计算能量势垒相当高[0.70 eV/化学式单位（f.u.）]（图4D，顶部）。这是因为这种转换涉及大的面内晶格位移。然而，Td相是具有三重旋转对称性的六方结构的畸变形式（图4C）。因此，存在三个等效的畸变矢量（43）。具有不同畸变矢量的相反极化状态之间的切换（即一个畸变矢量的Pup和另一个畸变矢量的Pdown）平均应具有较小的面内原子位移，从而导致较小的能量势垒。如图4D（底部）所示，在这种情况下，表示为Pup1和Pdown2的相反极化状态之间的能量势垒降低到0.29 eV/f.u.。该值小于（但可比拟）BiFeO3（44）约0.43 eV/f.u.的能量势垒，BiFeO3是一种具有高居里温度（~1000 K）的原型铁电体。最近还提出了WTe2中极化切换的更低能量势垒的转变路径（45）。因此，从理论角度来看，在正常实验条件下，WTe2中的极化切换是可以实现的，这支持了WTe2不仅具有极性而且具有铁电性的理论。 **Berry phase method**: Berry相位方法，一种计算晶体宏观极化的量子力学方法。 **Curie temperature**: 居里温度，铁电材料失去铁电性的相变温度。

> # DISCUSSION
> 
> 讨论
> 
> Using density functional perturbation theory (46) and the finite differences method (47), we calculated the magnitude of the piezoelectric coefficient of WTe2 (see table S1 and fig. S5 for details). The calculated value of the longitudinal piezoelectric coefficient (d33) is about 6.5 pm/V. We find that this calculated value is about an order of magnitude larger than the experimentally measured value of ~0.7 pm/V (see fig. S6 and corresponding text in section S5). However, the experimentally determined rather low value of the piezoelectric constant is understandable if one were to consider that we likely extract the piezoresponse within the Debye length of WTe2, which is ~1.6 nm (see section S6 for details on the calculation of Debye length). Moreover, the calculation is done for a uniform system, while in the measurements, the electric field distribution is likely highly nonuniform. While the detection of piezoresponse at these small length scales of few nanometers is challenging, it is nevertheless not unrealistic. Furthermore, we are aware of electrically induced extrinsic mechanisms (e.g., electrostatic effects and/or electrochemical processes) that can manifest as a piezoelectric- or a ferroelectric-like effect in PFM studies of nominally nonferroelectric materials. However, the experimental data in the present study of WTe2 cannot be explained by these spurious extrinsic mechanisms (see section S7 for a detailed discussion). We note that the Debye length of ~1.6 nm is relatively short compared to the material thicknesses investigated in our study. Nevertheless, one can obtain depth-sensitive data from electrical measurements for thicknesses larger than the Debye length in WTe2 (48). In our study, we have observed ferroelectric domains in thick bulk crystals and ferroelectric switching in ~15-nm films. The question of the physical extent of these domains is an interesting one. One possibility is that the piezoresponse and ferroelectric domains come only from within a Debye length of the surface. However, it is also possible that the ferroelectric domains extend much deeper into the material because ferroelectricity and ferroelasticity are often coupled in conventional ferroelectrics and because ferroelastic domains have been predicted for bulk WTe2 (43). In this latter scenario, ferroelectric domains could potentially propagate through elastically mediated wall motion, where fluctuation in bond lengths by electric fields initiated on the surface (within the Debye length) generates strain down into the bulk of the material and large domains could propagate through into the depth of the material (6). We hope that our study will stimulate further theoretical and experimental studies to answer this question. In summary, the concept of ferroelectric metals predicted in the 1960s has been realized in a Weyl semimetal, WTe2. A combination of experimental and first-principles DFT techniques demonstrates the coexistence of native metallicity and ferroelectricity at room temperature. Bulk crystalline WTe2 exhibits bistable polarization states that are switchable under an external electric field—thus, ferroelectricity is a bulk property of WTe2—and is not just confined to few-layer samples. The investigation of ferroelectric domain walls (49, 50) in metallic systems constitutes an interesting aspect for future research and potential nanoelectronics applications, following recent findings in conventional ferroelectrics. Our findings also raise prospects for discovering ferroelectricity in other metallic layered materials. 利用密度泛函微扰理论（46）和有限差分法（47），我们计算了WTe2的压电系数大小（详见表格S1和图S5）。纵向压电系数（d33）的计算值约为6.5 pm/V。我们发现，该计算值比实验测量的约0.7 pm/V值大约一个数量级（见图S6及第S5节相应文本）。然而，如果考虑到我们很可能在WTe2的德拜长度（约1.6 nm）内提取压电响应，实验上确定的相当低的压电常数是可以理解的（关于德拜长度的计算详见第S6节）。此外，计算是针对均匀系统进行的，而在测量中，电场分布可能高度不均匀。虽然在几纳米这样的小尺度上检测压电响应具有挑战性，但并非不现实。此外，我们意识到电诱导的外在机制（例如，静电效应和/或电化学过程）可能在名义上非铁电材料的PFM研究中表现为压电或类铁电效应。然而，当前WTe2研究中的实验数据不能用这些虚假的外在机制来解释（详细讨论见第S7节）。我们注意到，约1.6 nm的德拜长度与我们研究中研究的材料厚度相比相对较短。尽管如此，对于厚度大于WTe2德拜长度的材料，可以通过电学测量获得深度敏感数据（48）。在我们的研究中，我们在厚块体晶体中观察到了铁电畴，在约15 nm薄膜中观察到了铁电切换。这些畴的物理范围是一个有趣的问题。一种可能性是，压电响应和铁电畴仅来自表面德拜长度内。然而，铁电畴也可能延伸到材料更深的地方，因为铁电性和铁弹性在传统铁电体中通常耦合，并且块体WTe2已预测存在铁弹畴（43）。在后一种情况下，铁电畴可能通过弹性介导的畴壁运动传播，其中表面（德拜长度内）启动的电场引起的键长波动产生应变向下传递到材料体内，大畴可以传播到材料深处（6）。我们希望我们的研究将激发进一步的理论和实验研究来回答这个问题。总之，20世纪60年代预测的铁电金属概念已在外尔半金属WTe2中实现。实验和第一性原理DFT技术的结合证明了室温下固有金属性和铁电性的共存。块体晶体WTe2表现出在外部电场下可切换的双稳态极化状态——因此，铁电性是WTe2的体性质——而不仅仅局限于少层样品。金属系统中铁电畴壁（49, 50）的研究构成了未来研究和潜在纳米电子学应用的一个有趣方面，继传统铁电体的最新发现之后。我们的发现也为在其他金属层状材料中发现铁电性提高了前景。 **Density functional perturbation theory**: 密度泛函微扰理论，DFT的扩展，用于计算材料对微扰的响应。 **Piezoelectric coefficient**: 压电系数，衡量材料压电效应强弱的物理量。 **Ferroelasticity**: 铁弹性，材料中自发应变可随外加应力切换的性质。

> # MATERIALS AND METHODS

> 材料与方法

> ## Sample fabrication details
> 
> 样品制备细节
> 
> All WTe2 samples were prepared from bulk crystals purchased from HQ Graphene, The Netherlands (www.hqgraphene.com). For the PFM measurements on bulk samples, the WTe2 single crystals were first mounted on a metal disc using conductive Ag paste. To ensure a pristine surface for the measurements, the WTe2 single crystals were cleaved using the Scotch tape method. For measurements on WTe2 thin samples in a capacitor geometry, we first deposited 15 nm Ti/Au on the SiO2 surface of the doped Si substrates. The WTe2 thin flakes exfoliated from the bulk crystals were then transferred onto the substrates and were covered by poly(methyl methacrylate). Thereafter, electron beam lithography was used to pattern the area for subsequent metal deposition (10 nm Ti/Au). To remove any residual resist and control the surface oxides on the WTe2 sample, the patterned areas were briefly etched with an Ar plasma. The cleaned surface was exposed to air for ~10 min before loading into the vacuum chamber for the metal deposition, so that only a very thin oxide layer was formed at the surface. 所有WTe2样品均由从荷兰HQ Graphene（www.hqgraphene.com）购买的块体晶体制备。对于块体样品的PFM测量，首先使用导电银浆将WTe2单晶固定在金属盘上。为确保测量表面原始，使用Scotch胶带法解理WTe2单晶。对于电容器几何形状的WTe2薄样品，我们首先在掺杂Si衬底的SiO2表面沉积15 nm Ti/Au。然后，将从块体晶体剥离的WTe2薄片转移到衬底上，并用聚甲基丙烯酸甲酯覆盖。之后，使用电子束光刻来图案化后续金属沉积（10 nm Ti/Au）的区域。为了去除任何残留抗蚀剂并控制WTe2样品上的表面氧化物，用Ar等离子体短暂蚀刻图案化区域。在送入真空室进行金属沉积之前，将清洁表面暴露于空气中约10分钟，以便仅在表面形成非常薄的氧化层。

> ## Scanning probe microscopy measurements
> 
> 扫描探针显微镜测量
> 
> The scanning probe microscopy measurements were implemented on a commercial AIST-NT SmartSPM 1000 atomic force microscope at room temperature in an inert atmosphere (i.e., controlled N2 environment). The PFM measurements were performed at an AC imaging bias of 1 V (peak to peak) and a frequency of approximately 700 kHz. The spectroscopic piezoresponse hysteresis loops were acquired at fixed spatial locations by supplying a triangular DC waveform with a superimposed low-amplitude AC modulation. 扫描探针显微镜测量在室温下在惰性气氛（即受控N2环境）中使用商用AIST-NT SmartSPM 1000原子力显微镜进行。PFM测量在1 V（峰峰值）的交流成像偏压和约700 kHz的频率下进行。光谱压电响应滞后回线通过在固定空间位置施加三角直流波形并叠加低振幅交流调制来获取。

> ## First-principles DFT calculations
> 
> 第一性原理DFT计算
> 
> First-principles DFT calculations have been performed using Quantum ESPRESSO (51) and Vienna Ab initio Simulation Package (VASP) (52, 53). In Quantum ESPRESSO, the optimized norm-conserving Vanderbilt pseudopotentials (54, 55) were used. In VASP, the projector augmented wave method (56) was used. The exchange and correlation effects were treated within the generalized gradient approximation (57). The lattice constant and atomic coordinates were relaxed until the force on each atom was less than 0.001 eV/Å. For the calculation of bulk Td-WTe2, we used a 16 × 12 × 8 k-point mesh in the irreducible Brillouin zone. Spin-orbit coupling was included in the electronic structure calculations. For the simulation of Td-WTe2 with different distortion vectors, we used supercells (2 × 2√3 ) of a monolayer and a k-point mesh (12 by 8 by 1). We used the lattice constants and atomic positions of the supercells reported in (43) as the initial input for the structure relaxation. 第一性原理DFT计算使用Quantum ESPRESSO（51）和Vienna Ab initio Simulation Package（VASP）（52, 53）进行。在Quantum ESPRESSO中，使用了优化的模守恒Vanderbilt赝势（54, 55）。在VASP中，使用了投影缀加平面波方法（56）。交换和相关效应在广义梯度近似（57）内处理。弛豫晶格常数和原子坐标，直到每个原子上的力小于0.001 eV/Å。对于块体Td-WTe2的计算，我们使用了16×12×8的k点网格在不可约布里渊区中。电子结构计算中包含了自旋轨道耦合。对于具有不同畸变矢量的Td-WTe2的模拟，我们使用了单层超胞（2×2√3）和k点网格（12×8×1）。我们使用参考文献（43）中报道的超胞晶格常数和原子位置作为结构弛豫的初始输入。 **Pseudopotentials**: 赝势，用于简化电子结构计算的有效势。

> ### 全文通俗翻译与讲解
> 
> 这篇文章的标题是《**一种室温下的铁电半金属**》，讲的是科学家们发现了一种叫“二碲化钨”（WTe2）的新材料，它同时拥有两种以前被认为水火不容的“超能力”。
> 
> **1. 这材料到底有什么稀奇？**
> 
> 您可以把材料想象成一个人。有的材料像是“绝缘体”，比如您手里拿的瓷茶杯，它不导电，脾气很犟，电流怎么推都推不动。有的材料是“金属”，比如家里的铜电线，它很听话，电流一推就往前走，导电性很好。
> 
> 但这篇文章讲的“二碲化钨”是个“双面人”，它**既是金属，又是铁电体**。
> 
> -   **金属性（Metallicity）**：简单说就是**能导电**。文章里讲，这种材料从室温到极低温度（零下260多度）都能很好地导电，这就是典型的金属性子。
> -   **铁电性（Ferroelectricity）**：这是它的另一项特技。您可以把“铁电体”想象成一块**自带正负极，而且正负极方向还能来回翻转的磁铁**，只不过它不是磁的，而是电的。它内部天生就有正电荷和负电荷中心不重合，形成了一个“自发电场”（这叫自发极化）。更神奇的是，加一个外部电场，就能让这个内部电场的方向掉个头，并且掉头之后，不加电也能保持住。这个能掉头、能保持的特性，就是“铁电性”。
> 
> **为什么说这俩“水火不容”呢？** 您想想，金属里面有大量能自由流动的电子，就像一盆水。如果在材料内部建立起一个正负电荷分开的电场，那些自由电子会立刻跑过去，把正电荷包围起来“中和”掉，这个电场瞬间就被屏蔽了，根本建立不起来，更别说翻转和保持了。所以长久以来，科学家们都认为一个材料不能既是纯粹的金属又是铁电体。但这篇文章告诉我们，**在二碲化钨这个材料里，这两件事儿同时发生**了！
> 
> **2. 科学家们是怎么证明的？**
> 
> 这就像侦探破案，要拿出铁证。
> 
> -   **证据一：看到“电畴”（Ferroelectric Domains）** 铁电体内部就像一块有很多小磁铁区域的磁铁，每个小区域的正负极方向一致，但不同区域的正负极方向可能相反。这些区域就叫“电畴”。科学家们用一种极其精密的显微镜，叫“压电力显微镜”（PFM），像一个能探测电场的纳米级手指，轻轻扫过材料表面，**直接看到了这些电畴结构**（图2）。这就像用放大镜看到了磁铁上的N极和S极区域一样，是铁电性存在的直接证据。

> ### 论文要点总结（给太奶奶的复习提纲）
> 
> **题目**：**《一种室温下的铁电半金属》**。一个在室温下，既是金属又有铁电性的新材料。
> 
> **研究问题**：文章的核心就是想回答一个几十年的老问题——**在一个能导电的金属块体材料里，到底能不能实现真正的、能用电场翻转的铁电性？**
> 
> **方法**：
> 
> 1.  **电气运输测量**：测电阻和磁场下的反应，确认它是金属，瞧见自由电子和空穴都存在。
> 2.  **纳米级压电响应测量（PFM）**：核心手段，直接“看”到了铁电畴，并“掰”动了它的极化方向。
> 3.  **第一性原理计算（DFT）**：在计算机上进行理论模拟，从原子层面解释为什么铁电性能在金属中存在，并计算翻转的能量壁垒。
> 
> **结论**： 作者们成功证明了，在**体相单晶二碲化钨（WTe2）**这种材料中，**金属导电性和铁电性在室温下稳定共存**。这种铁电性具有可翻转的、稳定的自发极化，并形成了铁电畴结构，是一种真正的体材料性质，并非局限于几个原子层的薄膜。
> 
> **创新点**：
> 
> 1.  **首次实现**：在**室温**和**体块晶体**中，首次实现了安德森和布朗特在1965年预言的“铁电金属”。
> 2.  **直接可视化**：直接观察到了金属单晶中的铁电畴结构，这是铁电性最直观的证据。
> 3.  **揭示新机制**：它不是靠掺杂或界面工程搞出来的，而是在一种纯净的、本征的半金属里发现的，为寻找同类材料提供了新方向。
> 
> **不足之处**：
> 
> 1.  **极化翻转的深度问题**：由于金属的屏蔽效应，电场可能只能渗透到表面几纳米，实验上观察到的极化翻转，是只发生在表面，还是能带动整个材料深处翻转，这点文章没有完全搞清楚，只说需要进一步研究。这就像只看到池塘表面荡起了波纹，但不确定是不是整个池塘的水都跟着动了。
