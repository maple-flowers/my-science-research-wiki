> [!quote]- <center>Abstract</center>

> Whirling topological textures play a key role in exotic phases of magnetic materials and are promising for logic and memory applications. In antiferromagnets, these textures exhibit enhanced stability and faster dynamics with respect to their ferromagnetic counterparts, but they are also difficult to study due to their vanishing net magnetic moment. One technique that meets the demand of highly sensitive vectorial magnetic field sensing with negligible backaction is diamond quantum magnetometry. Here we show that an archetypal antiferromagnet—haematite—hosts a rich tapestry of monopolar, dipolar and quadrupolar emergent magnetic charge distributions. The direct read-out of the previously inaccessible vorticity of an antiferromagnetic spin texture provides the crucial connection to its magnetic charge through a duality relation. Our work defines a paradigmatic class of magnetic systems to explore two-dimensional monopolar physics, and highlights the transformative role that diamond quantum magnetometry could play in exploring emergent phenomena in quantum materials. Diamond quantum magnetometry is utilized to directly read the vorticity of antiferromagnetic spin textures through coupled multi-polar emergent magnetic charge distributions.
>
>【摘要翻译】旋转拓扑织构在磁性材料的奇异相中起着关键作用，在逻辑和存储领域有着广阔的应用前景。在反铁磁体中，这些织构相对于铁磁织构表现出更强的稳定性和更快的动力学特性，但由于它们的净磁矩消失，因此也难以研究。金刚石量子磁强计是一种能够满足高灵敏度矢量磁场传感的技术。在这里，我们表明，一个原型反铁磁赤铁矿主机丰富的织锦单极，偶极和四极紧急磁电荷分布。反铁磁自旋织构先前无法获得的涡度的直接读出通过对偶关系提供了与其磁荷的关键联系。我们的工作定义了一类典型的磁系统来探索二维单极物理，并强调了金刚石量子磁力学在探索量子材料中的涌现现象中所起的变革作用。利用金刚石量子磁强计，通过耦合多极出射磁荷分布直接读取反铁磁自旋织构的涡度。

> ## 一、引言
> 
> 在凝聚态物理与自旋电子学领域，对磁性材料中拓扑结构的研究，正推动着下一代信息存储与逻辑器件的发展。长久以来，研究焦点集中于铁磁体（FM）中的拓扑自旋织构，如斯格明子（Skyrmions）。然而，近年来，反铁磁体（AFM）因其独特的优势——对杂散场不敏感、稳定性更高及动力学速度更快——而备受瞩目。但反铁磁体因其宏观净磁矩为零，其内部拓扑结构的探测一直是巨大的技术挑战。
> 
> 本研究发表于《Nature Materials》，题为《利用钻石量子磁测量揭示反铁磁体中的涌现磁荷》(Revealing emergent magnetic charge in an antiferromagnet with diamond quantum magnetometry)。该工作填补了一项关键空白：利用**钻石量子磁测量（DQM）**技术，首次实现了对反铁磁体α-Fe₂O₃（赤铁矿）中拓扑自旋织构的矢量磁场成像，并揭示了一个深层次的物理现象——这些拓扑结构竟承载着丰富的、可被直接观测的**涌现磁荷**。本文的核心价值在于，它不仅提供了一种强大的探测新范式，还建立了反铁磁自旋织构与磁荷之间的**对偶性关系**，为探索二维单极子物理开辟了全新的材料平台。
> 
> ## 二、论文结构与逻辑概览
> 
> 本文遵循“提出问题 → 发展方法 → 实验验证 → 建立理论 → 物理讨论”的经典论证逻辑。
> 
> 1.  **背景与挑战**：确立反铁磁拓扑织构的重要性，并指出其探测难点在于零净磁矩。
> 2.  **方法与材料**：引入DQM作为高灵敏度、低背向作用的矢量场探测器，并选择具有弱铁磁性的**斜方反铁磁体α-Fe₂O₃**作为理想模型系统。
> 3.  **核心物理建立**：通过薄膜近似下的理论推导，将测量的磁场（B_z）与**倾斜磁化强度的散度**（∇⃗ ⋅ m⃗），进而与**交错涡度**（V⃗ = ∇⃗ × l⃗）建立起直接联系。这是整篇论文物理洞察的基石。
> 4.  **实验发现与分类**：在不同温度下成像，识别并分类了反铁磁畴壁（ADW）、**梅隆（meron）**、**反梅隆（antimeron）**和**双梅隆（bimeron）**等多种拓扑织构，并解析了它们的磁化分布。
> 5.  **核心概念转化**：利用磁学的高斯定律，将磁化强度的散度∇⃗ ⋅ m⃗等效为**涌现磁荷密度**（σ_m）。通过数据处理，将抽象的磁场图转化为直观的磁荷分布图。
> 6.  **性质探讨与验证**：对不同拓扑织构的磁荷分布（单极子、偶极子、四极子）进行特征化，并定量分析了磁荷（Q_m）的标度行为，揭示了织构间的相互作用。
> 7.  **总结与展望**：强调了DQM在区分拓扑织构手性、涡度方面的独特能力，并将其与自旋冰中的磁单极子进行了区分，展望了其在量子材料探索和自旋电子学中的应用前景。

> ## 四、正文核心内容剖析
> 
> ### 1. 研究背景与问题
> 
> -   **领域背景**：反铁磁自旋电子学（AFM Spintronics）旨在利用反铁磁材料中自旋的有序态进行信息编码和传输，其拓扑织构（如梅隆、斯格明子）是实现高密度、低功耗器件的关键。
> -   **核心难题**：反铁磁体的净磁矩为零，传统磁成像技术（如MFM）难以探测。同步辐射X射线二色性技术虽能成像，但对**交错磁化**的符号不敏感，无法直接读取表征拓扑织构旋转方向的**涡度**（Vorticity）。
> -   **切入角度**：作者利用DQM技术，通过测量极弱的**倾斜磁化**（m）产生的杂散场，巧妙绕过了零净磁矩的限制。选择α-Fe₂O₃作为研究对象，因为它是一种典型的**斜方反铁磁体**，其内部由**Dzyaloshinskii-Moriya相互作用（DMI）**导致亚晶格磁矩轻微倾斜，从而产生一个小的、但可被DQM探测的净磁矩m。
> 
> ### 2. 方法模型与核心物理思想
> 
> -   **DQM原理**：利用钻石中的单个**氮-空位（NV）色心**作为原子尺度的量子传感器。NV色心的基态自旋对外部磁场极为敏感，通过**光探测磁共振（ODMR）**技术，可精确测量由塞曼效应引起的能级移动，从而推算出投影到NV轴上的磁场分量BNV。通过扫描和傅里叶重构，可获得样品表面的三维矢量磁场（Bx, By, Bz）。
> -   **物理模型——对偶性关系的建立**：
>     1.  **薄膜近似**：在薄样品和大探测距离下，B_z与磁化强度的散度（∇⃗ ⋅ m_xy）之间有直接的卷积关系（公式1）。
>     2.  **DMI约束**：α-Fe₂O₃的DMI矢量沿c轴，这导致净磁化m随Néel矢量l的变化而取向，且满足m = Δ(ẑ × l)。这使得m始终在面内，且垂直于l。
>     3.  **涡度联系**：基于上述关系，可推导出∇⃗ ⋅ m_xy = Δ[ẑ ⋅ (∇⃗ × l)]。此式建立了**磁化散度**与**交错涡度**（即Néel矢量的旋度）之间的直接正比关系。这意味着，测得的B_z图像，实际上就是交错涡度的投影图！
>     4.  **磁荷涌现**：根据磁学的高斯定律∇⃗ ⋅ B⃗ = 0，在材料内部，∇⃗ ⋅ H⃗ = -∇⃗ ⋅ m⃗。因此，∇⃗ ⋅ m⃗ ≠ 0 的地方即可视为磁荷ρ_m的来源。定义面磁荷密度σ_m = -t(∇⃗ ⋅ m_xy)。结合上述关系，涌现磁荷的极性与大小，完全由拓扑织构的涡度手性（顺时针或逆时针）和相位（ξ_a）决定。
> 
> ### 3. 实验结果与讨论
> 
> 实验通过在不同温度下成像，完美验证了上述理论。
> 
> -   **低温相（T < TM）**：此时体态m趋于零，仅在畴壁等导致l翻转的区域，由于DMI约束造成m出现，从而产生信号。成功观测到**反相畴壁（ADW）**，其特征是B_z信号过零点，且其强度随ADW的相位（a-Néel vs a-Bloch）而变化。
> -   **高温相（T > TM）**：m在体态即有非零值，形成丰富的拓扑织构。成功识别并分类了：
>     -   **a-Bloch梅隆**：分为顺时针和逆时针两种，其B_z图呈径向对称，分别对应“汇聚”和“发散”的磁场，即**涌现磁单极子**。其涡度V⃗与电荷符号一一对应。
>     -   **反梅隆**：其B_z图呈二重对称，对应**涌现磁四极子**，其总电荷Q_m = 0。
>     -   **双梅隆**：梅隆-反梅隆的束缚对，其磁荷分布是两者的叠加。
> -   **定量分析**：通过对图4的分析，证实了孤立梅隆的磁荷Q_m随半径r线性增长，而反梅隆的Q_m为零，这与理论预测一致。实验数据的微小偏差揭示了织构之间通过磁荷进行的相互作用。这种相互作用意味着，虽然单个梅隆的磁荷不是拓扑量子化的，但整个磁荷“画布”提供了一个描述多织构体系的崭新视角。
>

> ## 七、学术思考与批判性分析
> 
> 1.  **磁荷重构的确定性与唯一性**：从B_z图像反演磁化分布m_xy是一个棘手的反问题。文中提到使用了“系统正则化”（Systematic Regularization）方法。此方法依赖于对磁化源（如孤立梅隆模型）的假设。那么，对于更复杂的、未知的织构，这种重构的唯一性和可信度如何？文中对反梅隆Q_m的分析也表明，相互作用会扭曲其对称性，那么此时基于孤立模型的分类标准是否仍然绝对有效？
>     
> 2.  **时空分辨率的极限**：DQM的空间分辨率受限于NV色心与样品的距离（d_NV）。虽然其磁场灵敏度极高，但如何在不牺牲灵敏度的前提下进一步提升空间分辨率，以观测更小尺度的织构或织构核心处的精细结构？此外，DQM的成像速度较慢，如何将其与超快动力学过程结合，以研究反铁磁织构的实时动力学？
>     
> 3.  **“涌现磁荷”的物理实在性**：文章反复强调，反铁磁体中的“磁单极子”并非类似自旋冰中的量子化拓扑激发，而是对磁化散度源的等效描述。在何种意义上，我们可以将其视为一个具有独立物理意义的准粒子？除了静磁学，这种“磁荷”图像能否预测新的动力学行为，例如，“磁荷”之间的库仑力是否会影响织构的动力学？
>     
> 4.  **与自旋电子学的桥接**：文章展望了“磁荷”在新器件中的应用。关键问题在于，如何用电学手段（如电流）直接操控这种“磁荷”？磁荷的极性和大小与织构的涡度直接相关，那么通过自旋轨道矩（SOT）翻转织构涡度，是否就等价于产生/湮灭了一个磁荷？这种“磁荷”信号的读出，相比于直接读取电阻态，有何优势？
>     
> 5.  **方法的普适性限制**：斜方反铁磁体中的弱铁磁性是DQM成功的关键。对于绝大多数严格补偿的共线反铁磁体（如NiO），体态中不存在净磁矩。虽然文中提到织构本身可能产生局域磁矩，但其信号强度将远小于α-Fe₂O₃。DQM在这类更普遍、更重要的反铁磁材料中的探测能力将面临严峻挑战，其信噪比和图像解释的复杂性都将大大增加。

> ## 八、用户问题预测与解答
> 
> 1.  **问题：什么是“涌现”（Emergent）现象？为什么这里的磁荷被称为“涌现磁荷”？** **解答**：涌现现象指一个复杂系统的整体表现出其组成部分所不具备的全新性质。在α-Fe₂O₃中，其基本组成单元是Fe³⁺离子的局域磁矩，它们本身并不携带磁荷。但当这些自旋通过交换作用形成特定的拓扑结构（如梅隆）时，其宏观磁化矢量场m的散度不为零，这在数学上等价于存在一个电荷分布源。这个“磁荷”并非来自基本粒子，而是由自旋的集体序构“涌现”出来的，故名“涌现磁荷”。
>     
> 2.  **问题：DQM技术如何区分拓扑织构的“手性”和“涡度”，而X射线成像为什么不行？** **解答**：关键在于它们测量的物理量不同。DQM直接测量的是由净磁化m产生的杂散磁场，而m的方向与Néel矢量l的涡度（∇⃗ × l）直接相关。一个顺时针涡旋的梅隆和一个逆时针涡旋的梅隆，其m的方向相反，从而产生相反极性的杂散磁场，DQM可以轻易区分。X射线磁性圆二色性（XMCD）主要探测的是自旋-轨道耦合相关的磁矩，它通常对l的取向敏感，但对l的旋转方向（即涡度的符号）不敏感，因此无法区分手性。
>     
> 3.  **问题：α-Fe₂O₃中的“磁单极子”与我们常听说的“自旋冰”中的磁单极子有何不同？** **解答**：这是该领域非常重要的区分。**自旋冰**中的磁单极子是三维的、点状的、拓扑保护的、带量子化“磁荷”的准粒子激发，它们像基本粒子一样可以独立移动。而本研究中α-Fe₂O₃里的“磁单极子”是二维的、弥散分布在梅隆核心周围的、其总磁荷Q_m随积分面积变化且非量子化。它更像是依附在拓扑织构上的一个“云”，是一种对静态磁化分布的有效描述，不能脱离梅隆结构而独立存在或移动。
>     
> 4.  **问题：什么是“莫林相变”（Morin Transition）？它在实验中扮演什么角色？** **解答**：莫林相变是α-Fe₂O₃在约200 K（TM）时发生的一个自旋重取向相变。在TM以下，由于单离子各向异性，磁矩优先沿晶体c轴（面外）排列，此时净磁矩m几乎为零。在TM以上，各向异性改变，磁矩倾向于在ab面（面内）内排列，此时DMI才能诱导出稳定的面内倾斜，产生净磁矩m。这个相变提供了一个完美的“开关”：在低温下，我们看到了无净磁矩背景下的畴壁信号；在高温下，我们看到了由净磁矩构成的丰富拓扑织构。这种对比有力地验证了物理模型。
>     
> 5.  **问题：这项研究成果对未来的硬盘或内存技术有什么潜在影响？** **解答**：传统硬盘和内存（如MRAM）多基于铁磁体。反铁磁体因其对杂散场不敏感、速度快、尺寸小，被认为是下一代自旋电子学的理想材料。本研究的潜在影响在于：（1）**提供了一种新的“读出”机制**：通过探测织构伴随的“磁荷”产生的杂散场，可以非破坏性地读取反铁磁比特的信息。（2）**增加了操控自由度**：除了操控自旋，也许未来可以操控“磁荷”本身，或者利用磁荷间的相互作用设计新的逻辑门。（3）**发现新的材料平台**：指me了α-Fe₂O₃这类斜方反铁磁体是一个极具潜力的研究与应用平台，其上丰富的拓扑织构种类（梅隆、反梅隆、双梅隆）为多态存储提供了可能。
>

> ## 九、专区：便于 Obsidian Dataview 插件调用
> 
> 领域基础知识:: 本工作属于**凝聚态物理**与**自旋电子学**领域，核心涉及反铁磁体中的拓扑自旋织构及其产生的涌现电磁现象。关键概念包括：**反铁磁体 (AFM)**、**拓扑自旋织构**（如**梅隆 (meron)**、**反梅隆 (antimeron)**、**斯格明子 (skyrmion)**）、**涌现磁荷 (emergent magnetic charge)**、**钻石量子磁测量 (DQM)** 以及**斜方反铁磁性**。
> 
> 研究背景:: 反铁磁拓扑织构因其高稳定性与快动力学被视为下一代自旋电子学的理想信息载体，但其零净磁矩使探测极为困难。传统X射线成像技术无法读取表征织构旋转方向的涡度。因此，亟需一种能对反铁磁织构的微弱磁信号进行高灵敏度矢量成像，并直接揭示其涡度特性的新技术。
> 
> 作者的问题意识:: 作者旨在利用**钻石量子磁测量 (DQM)** 技术，解决反铁磁体中拓扑织构的探测与分类难题，特别是实现对X射线技术无法获取的**交错涡度**的直接读取。更进一步，作者试图通过实验揭示，这些拓扑织构是否能通过磁化散度等效为一种**涌现磁荷**，并建立两者之间的对偶关系。
> 
> 主要研究对象:: 本研究的主要对象是**斜方反铁磁体α-Fe₂O₃（赤铁矿）** 薄膜中，由**Dzyaloshinskii-Moriya相互作用 (DMI)** 诱导产生的多种拓扑自旋织构，包括反相畴壁 (ADW)、顺时针/逆时针a-Bloch梅隆、反梅隆和双梅隆。
> 
> 主要研究方法:: 采用**钻石量子磁测量 (DQM)** 技术。利用钻石探针中单个**氮-空位 (NV) 色心**作为量子传感器，通过**光探测磁共振 (ODMR)** 扫描成像，获取样品表面的杂散磁场矢量分布。再结合理论模型（薄膜近似、对偶性关系推导）和数学反演（向下延拓、正则化重构），将磁场图转化为磁化分布图和磁荷密度分布图。
> 
> 研究意义:: 本研究首次提供了一种可对反铁磁拓扑织构进行矢量磁场成像并直接读取其涡度的桌面级实验平台。它揭示了斜方反铁磁体中一个全新的物理图像：拓扑织构可被等效为具有单极、偶极和四极特性的**涌现磁荷**分布，开辟了探索二维单极子物理的新材料范式，并对发展基于反铁磁体的新型自旋电子学器件的读出与操控方案具有重要指导意义。
> 
> 研究结论:: 实验证明，α-Fe₂O₃中的a-Bloch梅隆对应于**涌现磁单极子**，其总磁荷随积分半径线性增长；反梅隆对应于**涌现磁四极子**，总磁荷为零；而畴壁表现为**磁偶极子**。这些磁荷的极性与分布由织构的涡度手性决定，从而建立了反铁磁涡度与磁荷间的直接对偶关系。这种磁荷是非量子化的，并因织构间的相互作用而改变。
> 
> 对领域的贡献:: 1. **方法学贡献**：确立了DQM作为研究量子材料中涌现电磁现象的通用且强大的桌面工具。2. **核心物理发现**：首次在实验上建立了反铁磁拓扑织构的涡度与涌现磁荷之间的对偶性关系，并实现了对a-Bloch梅隆手性的区分。3. **开辟新方向**：定义了以斜方反铁磁体为代表的二维单极子物理研究新平台，区别于传统的自旋冰体系。
> 
> 未来研究方向提及:: 1. 将DQM成像推广到其他斜方反铁磁体（如正铁氧体）和更广泛的量子材料。2. 探测无体态DMI的补偿型反铁磁体中，由织构本身产生的局域净磁矩。3. 利用DQM研究界面工程在超薄膜中诱导的优先涡度。4. 结合自旋操控技术，实现基于“磁荷”自由度的拓扑织构读出与写入，用于非常规计算。

> ### 2. 方法/模型：金刚石量子磁力计与对偶关系
> 
> -   **探测原理**：金刚石中的**NV色心**是一个单自旋量子系统。当它感受到外界磁场时，其荧光光谱会发生塞曼分裂。通过精确测量这种分裂，就能反推出磁场大小。因为NV色心是纳米尺度的点状传感器，所以它能实现极高的空间分辨率成像。
> -   **核心物理定律——对偶关系**：
>     1.  **磁场与磁化散度**：在薄膜近似下，测量的磁场Bz正比于净磁化强度m的**散度**，即 `Bz ∝ ∇·m`。散度可以理解为“场在这一点上向外流出的净通量”。

>     3.  **结论**：**`Bz ∝ ∇·m ∝ 涡旋性`**。这个等式是本文的基石。它意味着，通过测量一个简单的磁场分量Bz，我们就能直接“读出”反铁磁织构的涡旋方向和强度，这是前所未有的能力。

Properties of α-Fe2O3 and DQM
Haematite α-Fe2O3 is an AFM oxide insulator, which hosts a variety of topological spin textures18,27,28. Figure 1a illustrates the atomic structure of α-Fe2O3. It comprises a stack of anti-parallel ferromagnetic sublat
tices along the c axis, with magnetization textures ⃗M 1 and ⃗M 2 (Fig. 1b). Spin re-orientation occurs at the Morin transition temperature, TM ≈ 200 K (ref. 18) (Supplementary Section 1); below and above TM, the ferromagnetic sublattices lie predominantly out of plane and in
plane, respectively. The Néel vector l ⃗ = ⃗M 1 − ⃗M 2 characterizes the
AFM order, whereas m⃗ = ⃗M 1 + ⃗M 2 is the net magnetization (Fig. 1c,d).
Above TM, m⃗ has a predominantly in-plane orientation with an average
magnitude mΔ ≈ 2 × 103 A m–1. This weak magnetization is due to the slight in-plane canting of Δ ≈ 1.1 mrad (Supplementary Section 1)
between ⃗M 1 and ⃗M 2, as a consequence of the bulk Dzyalonshinskii
Moriya interaction (DMI) vector along the c axis29 (Supplementary
Section 5). Consequently, m⃗ lies in plane and satisfies m⃗ ⋅ l ⃗ = 0 .
Since mΔ is much weaker than |l ⃗|, this weak magnetization has no
discernible effect on the AFM character of α-Fe2O3. Finally, m⃗ vanishes
where l ⃗ turns out of plane below TM or due to the formation of AFM spin textures. We quantify the magnetic field distribution from these spin textures via DQM (Supplementary Section 2). Figure 1e illustrates our diamond probe hosting a single NV centre, which is scanned at a constant height above the sample. The NV centre is a spin defect with a paramagnetic ground-state manifold and state-selective optical transitions. This allows the Zeeman splitting between the ground states |±1〉 and |0〉 to be probed with a microwave frequency fmw sweep and optical excitation via optically detected magnetic resonance (ODMR). In the weak-field approximation30 with negligible strain, we infer the magnetic field projected onto the NV axis (BNV) from the energy difference between |0〉 and |+1〉 given by ∆E+ = h(| f+ − D| − γ̃Bbias) = hγ̃BNV , where h is Planck’s constant, f+ is the resonant frequency corresponding to the transition, D ≈ 2.87 GHz and γ̃ = 28 MHz mT–1. A bias field Bbias ≈ 0.5 mT is applied along the NV axis to enable the extraction of field orientation. Figure 1g,h illustrates the variation in ODMR frequency across a linescan over the α-Fe2O3 surface at T = 4 and 300 K, respectively. The colour plot displays the signal amplitude, whereas the white curves demarcate f+ used to extract BNV. An ODMR raster scan across the sample surface provides a BNV image. We transform this to the laboratory coordinates (Bx,y,z) via the Fourier reconstruction technique31, where z coincides with the c axis of the crystal. Figure 1i,j presents images of Bz collected at 4 and 300 K, respectively. The observed qualitative differences reveal distinct magnetic phases for temperatures below and above TM (ref. 18). The image below TM comprises narrow features in an almost-zero field background, consistent with the absence of net magnetization. By contrast, the image above TM displays larger features within a non-zero field background, expected from a non-zero net magnetization.

> > 单元6: 摘要段落 英文：Whirling topological textures play a key role in exotic phases of magnetic materials and are promising for logic and memory applications. In antiferromagnets, these textures exhibit enhanced stability and faster dynamics with respect to their ferromagnetic counterparts, but they are also difficult to study due to their vanishing net magnetic moment. > > 单元12: 正文段落 英文：Haematite α-Fe2O3 is an AFM oxide insulator, which hosts a variety of topological spin textures18,27,28. Figure 1a illustrates the atomic structure of α-Fe2O3. It comprises a stack of anti-parallel ferromagnetic sublattices along the c axis, with magnetization textures ⃗M 1 and ⃗M 2 (Fig. 1b). Spin re-orientation occurs at the Morin transition temperature, TM ≈ 200 K (ref. 18) (Supplementary Section 1); below and above TM, the ferromagnetic sublattices lie predominantly out of plane and in plane, respectively. The Néel vector l ⃗ = ⃗M 1 − ⃗M 2 characterizes the AFM order, whereas m⃗ = ⃗M 1 + ⃗M 2 is the net magnetization (Fig. 1c,d). Above TM, m⃗ has a predominantly in-plane orientation with an average magnitude mΔ ≈ 2 × 103 A m–1. This weak magnetization is due to the slight in-plane canting of Δ ≈ 1.1 mrad (Supplementary Section 1) between ⃗M 1 and ⃗M 2, as a consequence of the bulk Dzyalonshinskii-Moriya interaction (DMI) vector along the c axis29 (Supplementary Section 5). Consequently, m⃗ lies in plane and satisfies m⃗ ⋅ l ⃗ = 0 . Since mΔ is much weaker than |l ⃗|, this weak magnetization has no discernible effect on the AFM character of α-Fe2O3. Finally, m⃗ vanishes where l ⃗ turns out of plane below TM or due to the formation of AFM spin textures. We quantify the magnetic field distribution from these spin textures via DQM (Supplementary Section 2). Figure 1e illustrates our diamond probe hosting a single NV centre, which is scanned at a constant height above the sample. The NV centre is a spin defect with a paramagnetic ground-state manifold and state-selective optical transitions. This allows the Zeeman splitting between the ground states |±1〉 and |0〉 to be probed with a microwave frequency fmw sweep and optical excitation via optically detected magnetic resonance (ODMR). In the weak-field approximation30 with negligible strain, we infer the magnetic field projected onto the NV axis (BNV) from the energy difference between |0〉 and |+1〉 given by ∆E+ = h(| f+ − D| − γ̃Bbias) = hγ̃BNV , where h is Planck’s constant, f+ is the resonant frequency corresponding to the transition, D ≈ 2.87 GHz and γ̃ = 28 MHz mT–1. A bias field Bbias ≈ 0.5 mT is applied along the NV axis to enable the extraction of field orientation. Figure 1g,h illustrates the variation in ODMR frequency across a linescan over the α-Fe2O3 surface at T = 4 and 300 K, respectively. The colour plot displays the signal amplitude, whereas the white curves demarcate f+ used to extract BNV. An ODMR raster scan across the sample surface provides a BNV image. We transform this to the laboratory coordinates (Bx,y,z) via the Fourier reconstruction technique31, where z coincides with the c axis of the crystal. Figure 1i,j presents images of Bz collected at 4 and 300 K, respectively. The observed qualitative differences reveal distinct magnetic phases for temperatures below and above TM (ref. 18). The image below TM comprises narrow features in an almost-zero field background, consistent with the absence of net magnetization. By contrast, the image above TM displays larger features within a non-zero field background, expected from a non-zero net magnetization. 中文翻译：赤铁矿α-Fe2O3是一种反铁磁氧化物绝缘体，承载多种拓扑自旋织构18,27,28。图1a描绘了α-Fe2O3的原子结构。它由沿c轴交替排列的反平行铁磁亚晶格堆叠而成，磁化织构为M⃗1和M⃗2（图1b）。自旋重取向发生在莫林相变温度TM ≈ 200 K处（参考文献18）（补充章节1）；低于和高于TM时，铁磁亚晶格分别主要沿面外和面内取向。奈尔矢量l⃗ = M⃗1 − M⃗2表征反铁磁序，而净磁化m⃗ = M⃗1 + M⃗2（图1c,d）。在TM以上，m⃗主要具有面内取向，平均大小mΔ ≈ 2 × 10³ A m⁻¹。这种弱磁化归因于块体Dzyaloshinskii-Moriya相互作用（DMI）矢量沿c轴（参考文献29）（补充章节5）导致的M⃗1和M⃗2之间约Δ ≈ 1.1 mrad的微小面内倾斜。因此，m⃗位于面内且满足m⃗·l⃗ = 0。由于mΔ远小于|l⃗|，这种弱磁化对α-Fe2O3的反铁磁特性没有明显影响。最后，当l⃗在TM以下转向面外或由于形成反铁磁自旋织构时，m⃗消失。我们通过DQM量化这些自旋织构产生的磁场分布（补充章节2）。图1e展示了我们的金刚石探针，其中包含单个NV色心，在样品上方恒定高度扫描。NV色心是一种自旋缺陷，具有顺磁基态流形和态选择光学跃迁。这允许通过微波频率fmw扫描和光激励，以光探测磁共振（ODMR）探测基态|±1⟩和|0⟩之间的塞曼分裂。在弱场近似30且应变可忽略的情况下，我们根据|0⟩和|+1⟩之间的能量差ΔE+ = h(|f+ − D| − γ̃Bbias) = hγ̃BNV推断出投影到NV轴上的磁场（BNV），其中h是普朗克常数，f+是对应跃迁的共振频率，D ≈ 2.87 GHz，γ̃ = 28 MHz mT⁻¹。沿NV轴施加约0.5 mT的偏置场Bbias以实现场方向的提取。图1g,h展示了在T = 4 K和300 K下，对α-Fe2O3表面进行线扫描时ODMR频率的变化。彩色图显示信号幅度，白色曲线标出用于提取BNV的f+。对样品表面进行ODMR光栅扫描可得到BNV图像。我们通过傅里叶重构技术将其转换到实验室坐标（Bx, By, Bz），其中z与晶体的c轴重合。图1i,j分别展示了在4 K和300 K收集的Bz图像。观察到的定性差异揭示了低于和高于TM时的不同磁相（参考文献18）。低于TM的图像由几乎为零场背景中的窄特征组成，与净磁化缺失一致。相比之下，高于TM的图像在非零场背景中显示出更大的特征，这与非零净磁化相符。 **Morin transition**: 莫林相变，赤铁矿中自旋取向从面外到面内的转变温度。 **Dzyaloshinskii-Moriya interaction (DMI)**: 德扎洛辛斯基-守谷相互作用，一种反对称交换作用，导致磁矩倾斜。 **Zeeman splitting**: 塞曼分裂，磁场中自旋能级的分裂。 **Optically detected magnetic resonance (ODMR)**: 光探测磁共振，利用光学方法检测自旋共振。 **Fourier reconstruction technique**: 傅里叶重构技术，从单分量磁场测量重建矢量磁场。

> > 单元22: 讨论内容 英文：Our ability to identify the duality between topological AFM textures and magnetic charges is due to the direct read-out of staggered vorticity enabled by DQM. Specifically, the NV centre senses the amplitude of the magnetic field projected onto the NV axis, allowing us to deduce the three field components via their linear dependence. This enables us to independently reconstruct the magnetic charge and local magnetization. As such, we go beyond detecting antimerons and merons, to further distinguish between clockwise and anti-clockwise a-Bloch components, which otherwise appear indistinguishable in X-ray dichroic images. Our imaging approach can be extended to a wider family of topological textures, including skyrmions, a-Néel merons and bimerons, as well as distorted AFM textures that are otherwise divergence-free (Supplementary Section 5.6–5.8), relevant for topological AFM circuitry7,27,38. Although haematite provides favourable conditions for DQM imaging due to spin canting, it is, by no means, unique among AFMs in possessing a weak net magnetization or quasi-isotropic spins in two dimensions. It may be possible to observe topological phenomenology in similar canted AFMs, including orthoferrites, orthochromites and iron borate39–42. In compensated AFMs without bulk DMI, staggered spin textures can also generate a local net magnetization, either statically or dynamically8,43,44. Moreover, DQM can be useful in detecting preferential vorticity in ultrathin films induced by interfacial interactions—a key requirement for applications in topological spintronics27,28. The reported duality between magnetic charges and topological AFM textures sheds light on a new class of materials hosting 2D monopolar physics in contrast with other systems that harbour emergent magnetic monopoles, such as the pyrochlore spin ice26. Although intriguing, monopoles in spin ice are intrinsically distinct, as they have an underlying gauge charge, which is topological and quantized. Conversely, the emergent magnetic charges in haematite are 2D, not quantized and are topological in the sense that they dress topological AFM textures underpinning them. We have demonstrated that haematite supports a rich tapestry of interacting magnetic charge distributions that could open up new and complementary ways to detect, manipulate and functionalize—via their magnetic charge—AFM topological textures. <span class="hljs-section">## Properties of α-Fe2O3 and DQM</span>
α-Fe2O3与DQM的性质

Haematite α-Fe2O3 is an AFM oxide insulator, which hosts a variety of topological spin textures18,27,28. Figure 1a illustrates the atomic structure of α-Fe2O3. It comprises a stack of anti-parallel ferromagnetic sublattices along the c axis, with magnetization textures ⃗M 1 and ⃗M 2 (Fig. 1b). Spin re-orientation occurs at the Morin transition temperature, TM ≈ 200 K (ref. 18) (Supplementary Section 1); below and above TM, the ferromagnetic sublattices lie predominantly out of plane and in plane, respectively. The Néel vector l ⃗ = ⃗M 1 − ⃗M 2 characterizes the AFM order, whereas m⃗ = ⃗M 1 + ⃗M 2 is the net magnetization (Fig. 1c,d). Above TM, m⃗ has a predominantly in-plane orientation with an average magnitude mΔ ≈ 2 × 103 A m–1. This weak magnetization is due to the slight in-plane canting of Δ ≈ 1.1 mrad (Supplementary Section 1) between ⃗M 1 and ⃗M 2, as a consequence of the bulk Dzyalonshinskii-Moriya interaction (DMI) vector along the c axis29 (Supplementary Section 5). Consequently, m⃗ lies in plane and satisfies m⃗ ⋅ l ⃗ = 0 . Since mΔ is much weaker than |l ⃗|, this weak magnetization has no discernible effect on the AFM character of α-Fe2O3. Finally, m⃗ vanishes where l ⃗ turns out of plane below TM or due to the formation of AFM spin textures. We quantify the magnetic field distribution from these spin textures via DQM (Supplementary Section 2). Figure 1e illustrates our diamond probe hosting a single NV centre, which is scanned at a constant height above the sample. The NV centre is a spin defect with a paramagnetic ground-state manifold and state-selective optical transitions. This allows the Zeeman splitting between the ground states |±1〉 and |0〉 to be probed with a microwave frequency fmw sweep and optical excitation via optically detected magnetic resonance (ODMR). In the weak-field approximation30 with negligible strain, we infer the magnetic field projected onto the NV axis (BNV) from the energy difference between |0〉 and |+1〉 given by ∆E+ = h(| f+ − D| − γ̃Bbias) = hγ̃BNV , where h is Planck’s constant, f+ is the resonant frequency corresponding to the transition, D ≈ 2.87 GHz and γ̃ = 28 MHz mT–1. A bias field Bbias ≈ 0.5 mT is applied along the NV axis to enable the extraction of field orientation. Figure 1g,h illustrates the variation in ODMR frequency across a linescan over the α-Fe2O3 surface at T = 4 and 300 K, respectively. The colour plot displays the signal amplitude, whereas the white curves demarcate f+ used to extract BNV. An ODMR raster scan across the sample surface provides a BNV image. We transform this to the laboratory coordinates (Bx,y,z) via the Fourier reconstruction technique31, where z coincides with the c axis of the crystal. Figure 1i,j presents images of Bz collected at 4 and 300 K, respectively. The observed qualitative differences reveal distinct magnetic phases for temperatures below and above TM (ref. 18). The image below TM comprises narrow features in an almost-zero field background, consistent with the absence of net magnetization. By contrast, the image above TM displays larger features within a non-zero field background, expected from a non-zero net magnetization.

> ### Characteristic field signatures and vorticity read-out
> 
> 特征磁场信号与涡度读出
> 
> After establishing the relationship between Bz and m⃗ xy, next we show that the images obtained in Fig. 1 are produced by AFM antiphase domain walls (ADWs), merons, antimerons and bimerons—consistent with recent observations in α-Fe2O3 (ref. 18). Below TM, we model the Bz images with a linear AFM domain wall18, characterized by width w and phase ξa (Supplementary Section 5.1). The phase ξa controls the spatial variation of l ⃗, resulting in an AFM Néel (a-Néel) or an AFM Bloch (a-Bloch) ADW profile for (ξa = 0, π) and (ξa = π/2 , 3π/2), respectively. For a linear ADW profile centred at x = 0 along the x axis, ∇⃗ ⋅ m⃗ xy = mΔ (π/w) sin (πx/w) sin(ξa) for |x| ≤ w/2, and zero elsewhere18 (Supplementary Section 5.1). Hence, we expect ADWs to display a sinusoidal profile in ∇⃗ ⋅ m⃗ xy and in Bz, with zero crossing at the centre, and amplitude and sign modulated by sin(ξa). In particular, an a-Néel ADW will not yield a Bz signal as ∇⃗ ⋅ m⃗ xy = 0, whereas an a-Bloch counterpart will show the maximal signal. Based on equation (1), these characteristics are reflected in the calculated Bz image of an ADW model in Fig. 2a (Supplementary Section 5.4), assuming a phase of ξa = π. The measured Bz image (Fig. 1i) and a close-up image (Fig. 2b) capture the signature zero crossing of an ADW. DQM also reveals variations in Bz along the wall boundary, capturing the spatially varying phase ξa in α-Fe2O3 (ref. 18). This phase dependence of Bz allows the unambiguous identification of the zero-signal sections along the wall with varying ξa as a-Néel ADWs. Figure 2c presents a reconstruction of the m⃗ xy distribution of the multi-chiral ADW (illustrated by arrows), obtained by fitting Bz to the data in Fig. 2b through systematic regularization (Supplementary Section 7). In contrast, DQM at 300 K captures larger spatial features of a strong Bz signal (Fig. 1j). In the above-TM regime, we anticipate finite net magnetization forming whirling topological structures, such as multi-chiral merons and antimerons18, as well as topologically trivial in-plane domain walls. Topological textures can be characterized by the topological charge QQ and topological winding NN. Each AFM texture produces a distinctive Bz signal, allowing us to develop a systematic procedure to differentiate them (Supplementary Section 5). Here we focus on 2D topological textures and model isolated (anti)merons based on a linear ansatz18,33, described by phase ξa and winding number NN (Supplementary Section 5.3). The corresponding divergence in polar coordinates (r, φ) is ∇⃗ ⋅ m⃗ xy = mΔ sin (φ(1 − NN) − ξa) f(r), where f(r) is a radial function dependent on the (anti)meron phase (Supplementary Section 5.3). A meron (NN = +1) produces a radially symmetric Bz distribution about its core with magnitude and polarity controlled by sin(ξa). Analogous to ADWs, a-Néel merons (ξa = 0, π) are divergence-free and exhibit Bz = 0, whereas a-Bloch counterparts (ξa = π/2, 3π/2) show the maximal Bz amplitude. In contrast, for an antimeron (NN = −1), the Bz distribution is two-fold symmetric and ξa controls an azimuthal offset. > ### Emergent magnetic charge
> 
> 涌现磁荷
> 
> The fact that DQM provides a direct measure of ∇⃗ ⋅ m⃗ xy creates a unique opportunity to consider a magnetic analogue of the electric Gauss’s law. Namely, the non-zero divergence of magnetization manifests the existence of an areal magnetic charge density via σm = −t(∇⃗ ⋅ m⃗ xy) . Here m⃗ xy is independent of t—valid in the thin-film limit18. Therefore, AFM textures in α-Fe2O3 have associated emergent magnetic charge distributions, which locally act as sources or sinks of the magnetic field. We can define a formal duality relation that connects the magnetic charge density σm to the staggered vorticity VV⃗ via

Crucially, retrieving the emergent charge density σm only requires the Fourier deconvolution of the measured Bz images from the transfer function αxy (Supplementary Section 4.2). We can also perform a downward (upward) continuation35 (Supplementary Section 4.2) of the planar B⃗ distribution captured in Fig. 2b,e,h,k,n, down to (away from) the sample surface. This allows a three-dimensional visualization of B⃗ (= H⃗ in a vacuum) in the volume above the magnetic charge distribution. Note that this charge and field retrieval process is independent of the m⃗ xy reconstruction and the linear meron model assumption. Figure 3a–d illustrates the field lines of B⃗ above the (anti-)clockwise a-Bloch meron, ADW and antimeron. For the two Bloch merons, B⃗ is consistent with the profile expected for spatially extended sources and sinks of the magnetic field emanating from a monopolar distribution, which we retrieved via equation (2) in Fig. 3e,f. This implies that a-Bloch merons host a class of emergent monopoles piggybacking on the topologically protected AFM textures. Interestingly, such monopolar magnetic distributions are not observed in ferromagnetic materials, as the presence of long-range demagnetizing fields favours divergence-free Bloch textures. In our case, we are freed from this constraint due the presence of a weak demagnetizing contribution relative to the stronger AFM exchange in α-Fe2O3. In contrast to merons, the ADW in Fig. 3c and the antimeron in Fig. 3d are associated with σm distributions that exhibit dipolar and quadrupolar characters, respectively (Fig. 3g,h). Finally, we emphasize that the observation of emergent monopoles is fully consistent with the modelling of AFM topological textures in Fig. 2 and does not violate Maxwell’s equation as they are, in fact, sinks and sources of the H⃗ field. Although ∇⃗ ⋅ H⃗ ≠ 0 in the material, the condition of ∇⃗ ⋅ B⃗ = 0 is still conserved since B⃗ = μ0(H⃗ + m⃗ ) and in the absence of any free electric current, ∇ ⋅ H⃗ = −∇ ⋅ m⃗. Qm itself is not a topological invariant, as a smooth transformation of an a-Bloch meron to an a-Néel meron would tune Qm from non-zero to zero. For an isolated antimeron, the two-fold rotational symmetry ensures Qm = 0 for all r values (Supplementary Section 5.9) and at short distances, the experimental Qm value of an antimeron agrees well with this prediction. Beyond measurement-induced variations, deviation from the strict Qm = 0 condition arises when an antimeron is influenced by neighbouring spin textures. Finally, as r goes to infinity, the integration area would overlap with the surrounding spin textures, leading to a further deviation from the Qm = 0 condition. The assumption that we have a collection of isolated spin textures oversimplifies the reality. Although the AFM topological textures are mesoscopically discernible and thus appear localized, they are, in fact, the constituents of the complex multi-textural ensemble that interacts via a 2D magnetic charge canvas. The magnetic charge per constituent is not just dictated by their nature as merons and antimerons, but modified through their interaction with other constituents. > ## Discussion and outlook
> 
> 讨论与展望
> 
> Our ability to identify the duality between topological AFM textures and magnetic charges is due to the direct read-out of staggered vorticity enabled by DQM. Specifically, the NV centre senses the amplitude of the magnetic field projected onto the NV axis, allowing us to deduce the three field components via their linear dependence. This enables us to independently reconstruct the magnetic charge and local magnetization. As such, we go beyond detecting antimerons and merons, to further distinguish between clockwise and anti-clockwise a-Bloch components, which otherwise appear indistinguishable in X-ray dichroic images. Our imaging approach can be extended to a wider family of topological textures, including skyrmions, a-Néel merons and bimerons, as well as distorted AFM textures that are otherwise divergence-free (Supplementary Section 5.6–5.8), relevant for topological AFM circuitry7,27,38. Although haematite provides favourable conditions for DQM imaging due to spin canting, it is, by no means, unique among AFMs in possessing a weak net magnetization or quasi-isotropic spins in two dimensions. It may be possible to observe topological phenomenology in similar canted AFMs, including orthoferrites, orthochromites and iron borate39–42. In compensated AFMs without bulk DMI, staggered spin textures can also generate a local net magnetization, either statically or dynamically8,43,44. Moreover, DQM can be useful in detecting preferential vorticity in ultrathin films induced by interfacial interactions—a key requirement for applications in topological spintronics27,28. The reported duality between magnetic charges and topological AFM textures sheds light on a new class of materials hosting 2D monopolar physics in contrast with other systems that harbour emergent magnetic monopoles, such as the pyrochlore spin ice26. Although intriguing, monopoles in spin ice are intrinsically distinct, as they have an underlying gauge charge, which is topological and quantized. Conversely, the emergent magnetic charges in haematite are 2D, not quantized and are topological in the sense that they dress topological AFM textures underpinning them. We have demonstrated that haematite supports a rich tapestry of interacting magnetic charge distributions that could open up new and complementary ways to detect, manipulate and functionalize—via their magnetic charge—AFM topological textures. Our capability to classify different AFM spin textures could be combined with conventional spin manipulation techniques such as spin torques, allowing for read-out and write-in schemes. Realizing this in a complex manifold of states endowed with highly nonlinear interactions could be attractive for unconventional computing1,2. Finally, the intriguing physical insights revealed in α-Fe2O3 are a testament to the relevance and potential of DQM as a versatile table-top platform to explore emergent phenomena in AFMs and other quantum materials. 我们能够识别拓扑反铁磁织构与磁荷之间的对偶性，归功于DQM实现的交错涡度直接读取。具体而言，NV色心感知投影到NV轴上的磁场幅度，使我们能够通过它们的线性依赖关系推导出三个场分量。这使我们能够独立重建磁荷和局域磁化。因此，我们超越了探测反半子和半子，进一步区分了顺时针和逆时针a-Bloch分量，而这些在X射线二向色图像中原本是无法区分的。我们的成像方法可扩展到更广泛的拓扑织构家族，包括斯格明子、a-Néel半子和双半子，以及原本无散度的畸变反铁磁织构（补充章节5.6–5.8），这适用于拓扑反铁磁电路7,27,38。尽管赤铁矿由于自旋倾斜为DQM成像提供了有利条件，但它在拥有弱净磁化或二维准各向同性自旋的反铁磁体中绝非独一无二。在类似倾斜反铁磁体中，包括正铁氧体、正铬铁矿和硼酸铁，可能观察到拓扑现象学39–42。在没有块体DMI的补偿反铁磁体中，交错自旋织构也可以静态或动态地产生局域净磁化8,43,44。此外，DQM可用于检测由界面相互作用诱导的超薄膜中的优先涡度——这是拓扑自旋电子学应用的关键要求27,28。所报道的磁荷与拓扑反铁磁织构之间的对偶性揭示了一类承载二维单极物理的新型材料，与容纳涌现磁单极子的其他系统（如烧绿石自旋冰26）形成对比。尽管有趣，自旋冰中的磁单极子具有本质区别，因为它们具有底层的规范电荷，该电荷是拓扑且量子化的。相反，赤铁矿中的涌现磁荷是二维的，非量子化，并且在拓扑意义上是为支撑它们的拓扑反铁磁织构披上外衣。我们已经证明，赤铁矿支持丰富的相互作用磁荷分布，这可以开辟新的互补途径，通过其磁荷来检测、操控和功能化反铁磁拓扑织构。我们对不同反铁磁自旋织构进行分类的能力可以与传统的自旋操控技术（如自旋扭矩）相结合，从而实现读写方案。在具有高度非线性相互作用的复杂态流形中实现这一点，对于非传统计算可能具有吸引力1,2。最后，在α-Fe2O3中揭示的引人入胜的物理见解证明了DQM作为一个多功能桌面平台在探索反铁磁体和其他量子材料中涌现现象的相关性和潜力。 **Skyrmion**: 斯格明子，一种拓扑自旋织构。 **Spin torque**: 自旋扭矩，通过自旋极化电流操控磁化。

> ### 文章内容大白话讲解
> 
> 好了，标题弄懂了，咱们就正式进入故事里。这篇文章讲了一群科学家，他们想看看反铁磁体里那些“小磁针”到底是怎么排列的，结果不仅看到了奇妙的花纹，还发现了一个更深层的秘密。
> 
> **1. 背景：为什么研究这个？** 太奶，您知道现在手机、电脑都越来越小，速度越来越快，这背后就需要更先进的存储和计算技术。科学家们发现，磁性材料里的一些特殊“花纹”，比如叫“斯格明子”的，非常稳定，而且可以被电流推着跑，可以用来做未来的存储器和逻辑器件，又小又省电。 以前大家主要研究铁磁体里的这些花纹，但反铁磁体里的花纹有更大的优点：它们**更稳定、跑得更快，而且彼此之间不容易“打架”（干扰）**。但问题是，反铁磁体整体不显磁性，这些花纹就像藏在一件隐身衣下面，**特别难被发现**。以前最先进的X光技术，虽然能看到花纹，但就像看皮影戏，只能看到个轮廓，分不清花纹是在“顺时针转”还是“逆时针转”。
> 
> **2. 他们的“看家本领”：钻石量子磁力计** 这就要请出咱们的主角——钻石量子磁力计了。科学家们把它当做一个超级灵敏的“探针”，在α-氧化铁（铁锈）薄片的表面，像扫描仪一样，一行一行地扫描。
> 
> -   **关键点：** 反铁磁体虽然整体没磁性，但在这个α-氧化铁里，因为一种特殊的相互作用（叫DMI，您不用管它是啥，就理解为一种“拧劲儿”），导致里面那些交替排列的“小磁针”会**稍微地、整齐地偏转一丁点角度**。这一偏转，就产生了一个**极其微弱的、能被钻石探针捕捉到的净磁场**。这个微弱的磁场，就像是那些看不见的花纹投下的“影子”，通过观察这个“影子”，就能反推花纹的样子。
> 
> **3. 他们看到了什么“花纹”？** 科学家们通过这个探针，在低温（4K，约-269℃）和室温（300K，约27℃）下，都看到了清晰的花纹图样。他们看到的可不是简单的条纹，而是像万花筒一样丰富的图案。
> 
> -   **反相畴壁 (ADW)：** 这是两种不同花纹区域交界的地方，像一堵墙。
> -   **麦纫 (Meron) 和 反麦纫 (Antimeron)：** 这是两种核心的花纹。麦纫就像一个小漩涡，它的“小磁针”排列成一个漩涡状。反麦纫就是漩涡方向相反。它们是一种“半斯格明子”，可以理解为半个完整的拓扑花纹。
> -   **双麦纫 (Bimeron)：** 一个麦纫和一个反麦纫手拉手绑在一起，形成一对。
> -   **最关键的是他们不仅看到了花纹，还能分出种类！** 比如，他们能分清漩涡是“顺时针转”还是“逆时针转”（这叫“a-Bloch型”麦纫），以及有的花纹是“发散型”的，有的不是（“a-Néel型”）。这个本事，是之前X光技术做不到的，因为探针能直接测出磁场的方向和强度，从而反推出花纹的旋转方向。
> 
> **4. 重大发现：冒出来的“磁荷”** 这是整个文章最精彩的部分！科学家们发现，他们测到的那个微弱磁场，其分布规律，可以用一种我们非常熟悉的方式来理解，那就是**高斯定律的磁学版本**。
> 
> -   太奶，您还记得电荷的正负吗？正电荷会“发出”电场线，负电荷会“吸收”电场线。他们发现，那些**麦纫（漩涡）花纹的磁场，就像是从一个点“喷涌”出来（或“吸进去”）的**，表现得**就像一个单独的“磁北极”或“磁南极”**！这就是标题里说的“冒出来的磁荷”。
> -   他们给这些花纹起了更形象的名字：
>     -   **顺时针的麦纫** -> 表现得像个**磁单极子**（Monopolar，只有北极或南极）。
>     -   **逆时针的麦纫** -> 表现得像个**反磁单极子**（Anti-monopolar）。
>     -   **反麦纫** -> 表现得像个**磁四极子**（Quadrupolar，更复杂的分布，正负交替）。
>     -   **反相畴壁** -> 表现得像个**磁偶极子**（Dipolar，就像我们熟悉的条形磁铁，有正负两极）。
> 
> 这个发现的意义在于，他们把**拓扑花纹的“旋转方向”** 和**磁荷的“正负”** 直接联系起来了！就像一个硬币的两面，看到花纹的转法，就知道它带的“磁荷”是正是负。
>

> 
> ### 论文总结
> 
> 太奶，故事讲完了，现在我把您要的总结一条条给您列清楚。
> 
> **1. 论文题目** 用钻石量子磁力计，揭开反铁磁体里“冒出来的”磁荷的秘密
> 
> **2. 摘要** 这篇文章主要讲的是，我们利用一种超灵敏的“钻石探针”技术，在一种常见的反铁磁材料（α-氧化铁）里，发现了一些特殊的磁漩涡花纹。我们不仅看清了这些花纹长什么样，是怎么转的，更重要的是，我们发现这些花纹本身就带着一种像“磁单极子”一样的磁荷分布。这个发现让我们能把拓朴花纹和磁荷联系起来，为以后研究二维世界的磁荷物理，以及探索量子材料中的新奇现象，开辟了一条全新的道路。
> 
> **3. 研究问题**
> 
> -   反铁磁体里的拓扑磁花纹因为不显磁性，很难被研究，特别是怎么看清它们的旋转方向？
> -   这些花纹会不会产生一些特殊的、可以被探测到的物理效应？
> -   能不能用一种新的、不干扰材料的测量技术，来揭开这些秘密？
> 
> **4. 方法**
> 
> -   他们用一种叫**钻石量子磁力计（DQM）** 的设备，核心是一个钻石里的“氮-空位色心”缺陷。
> -   把这个比头发丝还细的探针，放在α-氧化铁（一种带有微弱净磁矩的反铁磁体）薄膜表面，像扫描仪一样逐点测量极其微弱的磁场。
> -   这个磁场是由材料内部的磁漩涡花纹产生的，是他们观察的“影子”。
> -   通过复杂的数学计算和模型，他们从测得的磁场“影子”反推出材料内部真实的花纹（磁化分布）和“磁荷”分布。
> 
> **5. 结论**
> 
> -   **看清了花纹：** 成功在α-氧化铁中观看到了麦纫、反麦纫、双麦纫等多种拓扑花纹，并能区分它们的旋转方向，这是传统方法做不到的。
> -   **发现了磁荷：** 证明了这些拓扑花纹确实带有“冒出来的磁荷”。其中，麦纫表现为单极子，反麦纫表现为四极子。
> -   **建立了联系：** 建立了一个“对偶关系”，把花纹的旋转（涡旋度）和磁荷的正负直接挂钩。
> -   **开辟了新领域：** 这个工作定义了一种新的材料体系，用来研究二维空间的单极子物理，并证明了钻石量子磁力计在研究这类量子材料上的巨大潜力。
> 
> **6. 创新点**
> 
> -   **技术上的创新：** 首次将**钻石量子磁力计**这个“桌面级”的平台，成功应用于探测反铁磁体中的拓扑花纹，并展现了其独一无二的优势——能直接读出花纹的旋转方向。
> -   **概念上的创新：** 首次提出了并实验证实了在反铁磁材料中，**拓扑花纹与“冒出来的磁荷”之间存在对偶关系**，把拓扑学和电磁学这两个看似不相关的领域，在实验上联系了起来。
