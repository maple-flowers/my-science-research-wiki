> Abstract Atomic simulations using an embedded atom method (EAM) potential were performed to study changes of packing patterns in titanium clusters containing tens to thousands of atoms. The findings revealed that the changes came from the movements and rearrangements of surface atoms in a large temperature range, and they were strongly dependent on cluster size and elevated temperatures. Small size clusters with icosahedron (Ih) configurations of geometric shell closures were preferred. As the cluster size increased, most of the atoms in these clusters could hold their HCP packing patterns. With the increasing temperature, the coexistence of different packing patterns including HCP, BCC, and Ih occurred. At a high temperature, the disorderly packing quickly extended into the entire region of a large size cluster. The calculations of specific heat were compared with Dulong-Petit law. The simulations provide the possibility to straddle structural transformations and determine critical size suitable for classical theory under certain thermal conditions.
>
>【摘要翻译】摘要利用嵌入原子法（EAM）势进行原子模拟，研究了含数万到数千个原子的钛团簇中堆积模式的变化。结果表明，这些变化是由表面原子在较大温度范围内的运动和重排引起的，并与团簇大小和温度的升高密切相关。小尺寸集群与二十面体（Ih）配置的几何壳封闭的首选。随着团簇尺寸的增大，这些团簇中的大部分原子都能保持其HCP堆积模式。随着温度的升高，HCP、BCC、Ih等不同的堆积形态共存。在高温下，无序堆积迅速扩展到大尺寸团簇的整个区域。比热的计算与杜龙-佩蒂定律进行了比较。模拟结果为在一定热条件下跨越结构转换和确定适合经典理论的临界尺寸提供了可能。

> ## 一、引言
> 
> 钛及其合金因其卓越的**生物相容性**、高比强度及耐腐蚀性，在**增材制造**和生物医用植入领域备受关注。然而，在增材制造过程中，金属粉末颗粒的尺寸可小至纳米级别，其热力学行为与宏观块体材料存在显著差异。理解钛纳米团簇从微观到宏观转变过程中的结构演化，特别是**尺寸效应**和**温度效应**，是材料科学领域的一项关键挑战。
> 
> 本文献《Atomic simulations of packing patterns and thermal behavior in Ti clusters》通过**分子动力学（Molecular Dynamics, MD）** 模拟，系统研究了钛团簇（包含数十至数千个原子）在不同温度下的原子堆积模式变化。该研究精准定位了团簇结构转变的临界尺寸，并揭示了表面原子重排在这一过程中的核心作用，为理解纳米尺度下的相变行为及经典理论的适用范围提供了关键的微观证据。
> 
> ## 二、论文结构与逻辑概览
> 
> 本文遵循“提出问题 → 构建模型 → 模拟验证 → 分析机理 → 得出结论”的经典计算材料学研究范式，其核心论证链条如下：
> 
> 1.  **问题提出**：增材制造中涉及纳米颗粒的熔化和凝固，其结构与块体材料不同，但尺寸依赖的结构转变机制尚不清楚，特别是温度的影响。
> 2.  **模型与方法**：采用**嵌入原子势（EAM）** 描述原子间相互作用，并创新性地使用温度依赖的**索末菲势（Sommerfeld potential）** 将低温HCP相与高温BCC相统一。构建了从19个原子到2601个原子的系列团簇模型。
> 3.  **核心发现**：
>     -   **表面主导**：通过**表面/体积比**分析，量化了小尺寸团簇表面原子占比极高的特征。
>     -   **能量与形状演化**：通过**势能-温度**和**形状因子-温度**曲线，揭示了不同尺寸团簇在升温过程中的结构稳定性和形状变化。
>     -   **微观结构鉴定**：利用**对分析技术（PA）**，精准识别了升温过程中**HCP**、**BCC**、**二十面体**等不同堆积模式的共存与转化。
>     -   **热力学行为**：通过计算**比热容**，并与**杜隆-珀蒂定律**比较，确定了团簇行为趋近于经典块体理论的临界尺寸。
> 4.  **结论**：表面原子的运动和重排是驱动团簇结构转变的关键。小团簇倾向于形成二十面体结构，大团簇在较高温度下可维持HCP结构直至熔化。确定了杜隆-珀蒂定律对钛团簇适用的临界尺寸约为3.0 nm。
>

> ## 四、正文核心内容剖析
> 
> ### 4.1 研究背景与问题
> 
> -   **研究背景**：钛合金增材制造过程中，激光熔化纳米粉末颗粒是关键步骤。纳米颗粒的性质与块体材料迥异，理解其结构随尺寸和温度的演化是优化工艺的基础。
> -   **核心问题**：钛团簇从原子尺度到纳米尺度的**堆积模式**（Packing patterns）如何随**尺寸**和**温度**变化？其结构转变的微观机制是什么？经典热力学理论（如杜隆-珀蒂定律）对此类微小系统的适用边界在哪里？
> 
> ### 4.2 方法学与模型构建
> 
> -   **核心方法**：**分子动力学（MD）模拟**，结合**嵌入原子势（EAM）**。
> -   **关键创新**：采用**温度依赖的索末菲势**。该势函数通过一个双曲正切函数 `g(T)`，将适用于低温HCP相的势能（Ti2）和适用于高温BCC相的势能（Ti1）进行线性组合，成功构建了一个能够描述整个温度区间相变行为的统一势函数。这是本研究的核心技术亮点。
> -   **模型体系**：构建了29个不同尺寸的团簇，从Ti19（直径0.67 nm）到Ti2601（直径4.40 nm），通过控制变量，系统研究尺寸效应。
> 
> ### 4.3 实验结果与讨论解读
> 
> -   **表面效应定量化**：通过计算表面原子占比（图3），明确指出直径小于2.8 nm的团簇为“表面主导”体系，其高表面能是驱动结构转变的根本热力学原因。
> -   **结构演化的尺寸依赖性**：综合图4-6，可以总结出清晰的图谱：
>     -   **小尺寸（<~150原子）**：倾向于形成**二十面体**结构，因其表面能最低，尽管其内部为非晶态。升温过程中会发生多次结构重构。

> 1.  **问题1: 什么是嵌入原子势(EAM)？为什么它适用于金属体系？**

> 2.  **建立尺寸-温度-结构关联**：系统研究从几十个到几千个原子的团簇，在不同温度下的结构模式，绘制出“结构相图”。
> 3.  **桥接微观与宏观**：通过计算**比热容**并与经典物理定律对比，找到了纳米团簇适用宏观理论的临界尺寸，为增材制造工艺中精确控制热输入提供了理论依据。

> ## 二、论文结构与逻辑概览
> 
> 本文遵循“**提出问题 → 构建模型 → 模拟观测 → 分析机制 → 普适化结论**”的经典计算材料学研究范式。
> 
> ### 论文逻辑框架
> 
> _（可设计为流程图）_
> 
> 1.  **研究动机与问题**
>     
>     -   **背景**：增材制造中纳米钛颗粒的尺寸效应不清楚。
>     -   **问题**：钛团簇的原子堆积模式如何随**尺寸**和**温度**变化？
>     -   **目标**：揭示结构转变机制，并确定适用于经典理论的临界尺寸。
> 2.  **模型与计算方法**
>     
>     -   **核心工具**：**分子动力学**模拟。
>     -   **原子间作用力**：采用**嵌入原子势**，并创新性地通过一个温度依赖的“混合函数”将分别描述低温（HCP）和高温（BCC）相的两种势函数结合起来。
>     -   **分析手段**：
>         -   **惯量矩**：团簇形状变化。
>         -   **对分析技术**：原子堆积模式的精确“指纹”鉴定。
>         -   能量与比热容计算。
> 3.  **核心发现与论证**
>     
>     -   **表象**：小团簇能量和形状变化剧烈，大团簇相对稳定（图4）。
>     -   **机制**：
>         -   **核心驱动力**：高表面原子比例是结构易变的主因（图3）。
>         -   **构型演化**：小团簇倾向形成**二十面体**；大团簇保持**HCP**，但表面会出现**BCC**和**Ih**共存，最终整体无序化（图5，图6）。
>     -   **宏观验证**：通过计算比热容与**杜隆-珀蒂定律**对比，发现直径大于约3.0 nm的团簇，其热行为开始接近经典理论（图7）。
> 4.  **结论与应用价值**
>     
>     -   明确了尺寸和温度是调控钛团簇结构的关键“开关”。
>     -   表面原子运动是结构转变的“引擎”。
>     -   为增材制造工艺提供了关键的热力学参数（临界尺寸），指导激光参数选择。

> ## 三、所有图表深度解析
> 
> ### 图1：不同尺寸团簇在50 K（低温）下的能量弛豫过程
> 
> -   **内容描述**：该图展示了Ti19、Ti57、Ti135、Ti389、Ti727和Ti1099六个团簇在极低温度下，**平均每个原子的势能**（单位：eV/atom）随**模拟时间步长**（单位：step，1 step = 1.6 fs）的变化。
> -   **深度解析**：
>     1.  **初始高能态**：所有团簇的初始能量都非常高，因为它们最初被强制排列成与块体材料相同的HCP晶体结构，这个结构对纳米团簇而言并非最稳定状态。
>     2.  **弛豫过程**：能量随后急剧下降，表明原子迅速调整位置，向更稳定的低能态弛豫。能量下降得越快、越深，说明结构转变越剧烈。Ti19和Ti57下降最为显著，说明其结构转变巨大。
>     3.  **稳定与振荡**：弛豫后，能量进入小幅振荡的平稳状态，原子在各自的平衡位置附近做热振动。大尺寸团簇的能量振荡较小，表明其结构更稳定。
>     4.  **关键启示**：**表面原子比例越高，团簇的结构不稳定性越强，越容易发生自发的结构重组，即使温度很低也不例外。**
> 
> ### 图2：对分析技术常用原子对的示意图
> 
> -   **内容描述**：该图是**对分析**技术中用来识别不同晶体结构的原子对指纹的示意图。图中白色圆圈代表一对成键原子，黑色圆圈代表它们共享的近邻原子。
> -   **深度解析**：这是一种“原子关系”的可视化体现。
>     -   **1421/1422对**：特征为4个共享近邻。1421对的近邻原子间成键平行，1422对则交叉。**等量的1421和1422对是HCP（密排六方）结构的标志**，这是钛在室温下的稳定结构。
>     -   **1551对**：特征为5个共享近邻，形成五重对称性。**这是二十面体（Ih）结构的标志**，一种在宏观晶体中不存在的、因纳米尺寸效应而出现的特殊结构。
>     -   **1441/1661对**：特征为4个或6个共享近邻。**高频出现是BCC（体心立方）结构的标志**，这是钛在高温下的稳定结构。
> 
> ### 图3：表面/体积比与团簇原子数的关系
> 
> -   **内容描述**：该图展示了在50 K下，**表面原子比例**（β_surface，无量纲）随**团簇总原子数**（N）的变化。
> -   **深度解析**：
>     1.  **直观趋势**：随着原子数增加，表面原子比例从接近1（Ti19几乎所有原子都在表面）以振荡衰减的方式下降，最终趋近于约0.3。
>     2.  **振荡的含义**：曲线并非光滑下降，而是出现振荡，某些特定原子数的团簇（如某些“幻数”团簇）具有相对较低的表面原子比例，意味着它们可能具有更稳定的几何结构。
>     3.  **核心结论**：**此图是理解全文所有行为的钥匙。** 它定量地证明，对于直径小于2.8 nm（约700个原子）的团簇，绝大多数原子都暴露在表面。这些表面原子配位数少、约束少、活性高，是驱动整个团簇结构变化的核心“引擎”。
> 
> ### 图4：六个团簇的势能和形状因子随温度变化
> 
> -   **内容描述**：
>     -   **(a) 势能-温度图**：展示了六个团簇的**单个原子平均势能**（Eav，单位：eV/atom）随**温度**（单位：K）的变化。
>     -   **(b) 形状因子-温度图**：展示了这些团簇的**形状因子**（F_shape = I1/I3，无量纲，范围0-1）随温度的变化。F_shape=1代表完美球体，值越小代表形状越偏离球形。
> -   **深度解析**：
>     1.  **能量演化**：
>         -   **小团簇（Ti19， Ti57， Ti135）**：在低温区能量下降，再次证明其结构不稳定，会自发寻找更低的能量态（如二十面体）。在450-700 K区间，所有团簇能量都有一个明显下降，这是由构建势函数时引入的混合函数导致的，模拟了从低温相到高温相的转变趋势。
>         -   **大团簇（Ti389， Ti727， Ti1099）**：能量随温度单调上升，直至某个高温点发生跃升，这标志着**熔化**的发生。尺寸越大，熔化温度越高。
>     2.  **形状演化**：
>         -   **小团簇**：形状因子F_shape剧烈波动，表明其形状极不稳定。Ti19、Ti57、Ti135均在经历剧烈变化后，在高温下变为**拉长的非球形**。
>         -   **大团簇**：Ti1099的形状因子始终接近1，保持球形，直到熔化。Ti389则在高温下出现了明显的拉长。
>     3.  **结构-形状关联**：形状的剧烈变化与小团簇内部结构的多次重构密切相关。而大团簇在熔化前能保持其主体结构，因此形状也相对稳定。
> 
> ### 图5：六个团簇的特征原子对比例随温度变化
> 
> -   **内容描述**：这是全文最核心、信息量最大的一幅图。它展示了六个团簇中，**1421、1422、1441、1551、1661五种特征原子对的比例**（Pair fraction， 无量纲）随**温度**的变化。
> -   **深度解析（按团簇逐一解读）**：
>     -   **Ti19**：在0 K时是等量的1421/1422对（HCP）。在50 K立刻转变为混合态。在>650 K后，1551对占绝对主导，表明其稳定为**二十面体（Ih）** 结构。
>     -   **Ti57**：行为类似，但在650-1200 K范围，1551对比例极高，形成了非常稳定的二十面体结构。高温下二十面体解体，结构多样化。
>     -   **Ti135**：低温下保持HCP，>300 K时，1551对增加，发生**HCP → Ih**的转变，并在450-950 K形成稳定的二十面体。在>1300 K时，1441/1661对激增，转变为**BCC**结构。这是一个完整且清晰的结构转变链。
>     -   **Ti389， Ti727， Ti1099**：在极宽的温度范围内，1421和1422对保持等量且占主导，**HCP结构是主体**。随着温度升高，表面首先出现BCC特征对（1441/1661），呈现**HCP + BCC共存**。在接近熔点时，所有特征对比例趋于无序，标志着液态的形成。
> 
> ### 图6：六个团簇在不同温度下的原子堆积快照
> 
> -   **内容描述**：这是对图5数据最直观的“视觉翻译”。它展示了六个团簇在不同代表性温度下的**原子堆积三维图像**，不同颜色代表不同的局部结构。
> -   **深度解析**：
>     -   **Ti19 （50 K, 650 K）**：从近HCP变为拉长的、由三个小二十面体连接而成的“棒状”结构。
>     -   **Ti57 （50 K, 750 K, 1350 K）**：从HCP晶体碎片变为完美的**二十面体**，再变为拉长的混合结构。
>     -   **Ti135 （50 K, 750 K, 1300 K, 1500 K）**：HCP → 内部Ih/表面混合 → 整个变为BCC结构 → 熔化。这完美地展示了尺寸增大带来的结构复杂性。
>     -   **Ti389 （50 K, 750 K, 1300 K）**：从HCP主体，到表面出现BCC包裹层，再到整体拉长导致BCC化。
>     -   **Ti727， Ti1099 （50 K， 高温）**：始终以HCP（绿色）为主体。Ti1099即使在极高温下，核心仍是HCP，直到接近熔点才整体无序化。
> 
> ### 图7：势能贡献的比热容斜率与团簇直径的关系
> 
> -   **内容描述**：该图展示了在高温范围（800-1350 K），由**势能贡献的比热容斜率**（无量纲，杜隆-珀蒂定律中总斜率为3.0，势能贡献为1.5）与**团簇直径**（单位：nm）的关系。
> -   **深度解析**：
>     1.  **经典标尺**：根据**杜隆-珀蒂定律**，晶体中每个原子的比热容为3kB。在分子动力学模拟中，温度恒定，动能贡献为1.5kB。因此，势能部分的斜率若接近1.5，则符合经典理论。
>     2.  **临界尺寸**：图中可见，当团簇直径小于约**3.0 nm**时，斜率值普遍偏低且离散。这表明，小团簇由于表面原子比例高，其热振动模式受限，能量吸收方式与块体不同，不再遵循经典定律。
>     3.  **宏观符合**：直径大于3.0 nm的团簇，其斜率趋近于1.5，表明其热力学行为开始与宏观块体材料类似。**这个3.0 nm就是适用经典热力学理论的临界尺寸。**

> ## 四、正文核心内容剖析
> 
> ### 4.1 研究背景与动机
> 
> _（对应论文引言部分）_
> 
> 作者首先点明了钛合金在生物医学植入物领域的巨大潜力，并指出**增材制造**是解决其加工难题、实现个性化定制的关键技术。为了优化工艺，理解作为原料的纳米颗粒在激光熔化过程中的行为至关重要。核心的挑战在于，**纳米颗粒的性质随尺寸变化极大**，而连接微观原子和宏观现象的关键，就是理解其内部原子堆积模式随温度和尺寸的演化规律。
> 
> ### 4.2 方法模型：构建一个“温度感知”的虚拟世界
> 
> _（对应论文第2节）_
> 
> 该研究的核心创新在于其势函数模型。无法用单一势函数同时描述低温HCP和高温BCC相。作者采用了一个巧妙的**温度依赖的嵌入式原子势**：
> 
> -   **基本框架**：采用**嵌入式原子势**，它将每个原子的能量视为其与周围原子对的相互作用能和它所处位置电子云密度的嵌入能之和。
> -   **关键创新**：通过引入一个**双曲正切函数** `g(T)`，将分别描述BCC和HCP相的两种势函数线性组合起来。这个函数像一个“智能开关”，在低温下使势函数表现出HCP特性，高温下表现出BCC特性，中间温区则平滑过渡。这为模拟整个温度范围的结构演化提供了物理基础。
> 
> ### 4.3 实验发现与讨论
> 
> _（对应论文第3节）_
> 
> 这是论文的核心，层层递进地揭示了钛团簇的结构演化规律。
> 
> #### 4.3.1 表面原子：结构变化的“引擎”
> 
> _（对应图3的分析）_
> 
> 讨论从**图3**开始，明确指出**表面原子比例**是决定团簇行为的根本原因。对于小团簇，绝大多数原子都在表面，它们配位数不足，受束缚小，只需很少的能量就能运动重组，导致整个团簇结构改变。这是贯穿全文的**核心驱动力**。大团簇由于内部原子众多，主体结构得以保持，变化仅集中在表面。
> 
> #### 4.3.2 尺寸效应：小二十面体 vs. 大HCP
> 
> _（对应图4和图5，图6的分析）_
> 
> 这是论文最精彩的发现，清晰地展示了尺寸如何决定结构：
> 
> -   **小尺寸团簇 （Ti19， Ti57， Ti135）**：它们放弃了块体钛的HCP结构，转而形成**二十面体（Ih）**。这是因为二十面体结构虽然密度略低，但具有更高的表面配位数，能最大化表面原子的键合数量，从而降低整个团簇的能量，使其在纳米尺度下比HCP更稳定。
> -   **大尺寸团簇 （Ti389， Ti727， Ti1099）**：它们的主体能保持**HCP**结构，因为巨大的内部原子提供了类似于宏观块体的环境，使得表面能对总能量的贡献降低。二十面体结构无法在长程保持有序，因此不适用于大尺寸。
> 
> #### 4.3.3 温度效应：从表面熔化到整体熔化
> 
> _（对应图5和图6的分析）_
> 
> 温度像个“调色板”，在同一团簇上描绘出不同的结构图案：
> 
> -   **多相共存**：随着温度升高，大团簇并非直接从HCP变为液态，而是经历一个**HCP + BCC**或**HCP + Ih**的共存阶段。这种转变总是从表面开始，因为表面原子更容易获得克服能垒的能量。
> -   **熔化机制**：大团簇的熔化是一个“表面预熔”然后“整体扩展”的过程。紊乱结构首先在表面形成，达到一定高温后，像一场“原子风暴”一样迅速席卷整个团簇，导致其整体熔化。这与宏观块体的熔化行为相似，但存在一个尺寸依赖的熔点降低。
> 
> #### 4.3.4 回归经典：临界尺寸的确定
> 
> _（对应图7的分析）_
> 
> 通过计算势能对**比热容**的贡献，并与**杜隆-珀蒂定律**（经典热力学中晶态固体比热容的极限定律）进行对比，作者发现了一个“临界尺寸”——**直径约为3.0 nm**。大于这个尺寸的团簇，其热行为开始遵循经典理论。这个发现具有重要的实践意义，它告诉我们，在增材制造的热力学模拟中，对于直径大于3.0 nm的钛颗粒，可以近似使用宏观块体材料的热物理参数。
>

> ## 五、结论与贡献
> 
> ### 5.1 核心结论
> 
> 1.  **尺寸效应**：钛团簇的结构是尺寸的函数。小团簇（<~2.8 nm）倾向于形成**二十面体**，而大团簇则能保持宏观**HCP**结构。
> 2.  **温度效应**：升温导致结构转变。小团簇经历多次重排；大团簇则经历**HCP → HCP + BCC共存 → 熔化的过程**，结构多样化始于表面。
> 3.  **表面驱动机制**：所有结构变化的根源在于**表面原子**的高比例和低配位，它们是结构重排的“活性位点”和主要驱动力。
> 4.  **临界尺寸**：**杜隆-珀蒂定律**适用于直径大于约**3.0 nm**的钛团簇，这为宏观理论在纳米尺度下的应用划定了边界。
> 
> ### 5.2 主要贡献
> 
> 1.  **理论贡献**：系统揭示了钛团簇“尺寸-温度-结构”的完整物理图像，阐明了从原子到块体尺度结构演化的微观机制，特别是表面原子的关键作用。
> 2.  **方法贡献**：成功应用温度依赖的混合**嵌入式原子势**，为模拟具有复杂温度-结构相变关系的材料体系提供了新思路。
> 3.  **实践指导**：确定的3.0 nm临界尺寸，为**增材制造**的工艺模拟和参数优化提供了直接的热力学依据，有助于更精确地控制激光能量，获得理想的微观结构。
>

> ## 六、未来发展方向展望
>

> **问题1：杜隆-珀蒂定律（Dulong-Petit law）是什么？为什么它在本文中很重要？**
> 
> **解答**：杜隆-珀蒂定律是经典热力学中的一个经验定律，它指出，在较高温度下，大多数固态晶体元素的**摩尔热容**（即1摩尔物质升高1度所需的热量）是一个常数，约为**3R**（R为气体常数，约8.314 J/(mol·K)）。换算到单个原子，其比热容为**3kB**（kB为玻尔兹曼常数）。这一定律的物理基础是，每个原子在晶格中做三维振动，每个振动自由度贡献一半动能和一半势能，总和为3kB。在本文中，它是一个**“宏观标准”**。作者通过计算纳米团簇的比热容，看其是否接近3kB，来判断该团簇的热力学行为是否已经像宏观块体材料一样。结果发现，直径大于3.0 nm的团簇才符合该定律，从而确定了**宏观理论适用性的临界尺寸**。这对于简化增材制造等工艺的模拟至关重要。
> 
> **问题2：什么是“二十面体（Icosahedron）”结构？为什么它比我们熟悉的HCP结构在纳米尺度下更稳定？**
> 
> **解答**：二十面体是一种由20个三角面、12个顶点和30条棱组成的多面体，类似于足球的形状（但面是三角形不是五边形/六边形）。它具有五重对称性，这在宏观晶体中是不允许的，因为五重对称无法填满整个空间。 在纳米尺度，它的优势在于：
> 
> 1.  **高表面配位数**：一个完美的13原子二十面体，其中心的原子配位数（近邻原子数）是12，而所有12个表面原子，其配位数是6，高于HCP或FCC小团簇表面原子的平均配位数。这意味着它能形成更多的原子键。
> 2.  **降低表面能**：更多的原子键意味着更低的能量，尤其是**表面能**。对于表面原子比例极高的小团簇，最小化表面能是决定其结构形态的首要因素。因此，尽管二十面体整体密度不如HCP，但它能通过“牺牲”体积来换取更低的表面能，从而成为最稳定的构型。随着团簇增大，内部原子增多，体积能（倾向于密堆积）开始主导，结构就转变回HCP。
> 
> **问题3：文中提到的“对分析技术”听起来很复杂，能用更简单的比喻解释一下它是如何识别不同结构的吗？**
> 
> **解答**：我们可以把原子想象成**“人”**，原子间的键想象成**“握手”**。对分析技术就是观察两个正在握手的人，看看他们有多少个**“共同的朋友”**（即同时与这两个人都握手的人），以及这些共同朋友之间是否也在握手。
> 
> -   **HCP结构**：就像两个朋友，他们通常有**4个共同的朋友**，而且这4个朋友之间，情况很特殊：一半是两两平行握手（1421对），一半是交叉握手（1422对）。这两种情况数量相等是HCP的独特标志。
> -   **二十面体结构**：就像两个朋友，他们有**5个共同的朋友**，形成了非常高的群体性（1551对）。这是非晶态或液态中常见的局部紧密结构。
> -   **BCC结构**：就像两个朋友，他们可能有**4个或6个共同的朋友**（1441和1661对），联系相对松散，不像HCP和二十面体那么紧密。
> 
> 通过统计一个团簇中所有这些“交往模式”的比例，我们就能判断出这个“原子社会”的整体结构类型。

<span class="hljs-strong">**ABSTRACT**</span>
<span class="hljs-strong">**摘要**</span>

Atomic simulations using an embedded atom method (EAM) potential were performed to study changes of packing patterns in titanium clusters containing tens to thousands of atoms. The findings revealed that the changes came from the movements and rearrangements of surface atoms in a large temperature range, and they were strongly dependent on cluster size and elevated temperatures. Small size clusters with icosahedron (Ih) configurations of geometric shell closures were preferred. As the cluster size increased, most of the atoms in these clusters could hold their HCP packing patterns. With the increasing temperature, the coexistence of different packing patterns including HCP, BCC, and Ih occurred. At a high temperature, the disorderly packing quickly extended into the entire region of a large size cluster. The calculations of specific heat were compared with Dulong-Petit law. The simulations provide the possibility to straddle structural transformations and determine critical size suitable for classical theory under certain thermal conditions.

<span class="hljs-section">## 1. Introduction</span>
<span class="hljs-section">## 1. 引言</span>

Titanium and titanium alloys exhibit low density, high biocompatibility, adequate toughness as well as corrosion, wear, and fracture resistances [1,2]. Used as implant materials, they provide better solution for shortening recovery periods, less invasive surgeries, and improvement of patients' life comfort. Because of differences in age, sex, and anatomical sites of patients, the composition and/or the structure of the implant materials should change gradually over the volume to avoid premature implant loosening and implantation failure [3–8]. Considering both mechanical and biological criteria, the design and fabrication of these implant materials is a major challenge. However, titanium production is hampered by the high cost in traditionally manufacturing processes, and poor process-ability for complex shape production because of fewer slip systems in α-Ti [9–11]. Additive manufacturing (AM) technology, integrating materials science, mechanism engineering, and laser technology, is giving greater freedom to designers for geometrically complex parts along with reduced manufacturing time. AM implies layer by layer shaping and consolidation of powder materials to arbitrary configurations under a high vacuum atmosphere. The laser melting in AM uses focused thermal energy to rapidly melt Ti particles, which is supported by continuously improved processing conditions in recent years (e.g. higher laser power, smaller focused spot size, smaller layer thickness, etc.), leading to significantly improved microstructural and mechanical properties [12–21]. Up to date, the nanoparticles have been used in the AM process [22–24]. As the size decreases to nanometer, nanoparticles or nanoclusters will present apparently different features from their bulk counterparts, and the study on the clusters containing tens of atoms toward bulk is of great importance in materials designing. Therefore, it becomes a key target for understanding the size dependence of structural changes in these clusters at elevated temperature. Synchrotron radiation X-ray absorption spectroscopy (XAS) is applied to study size effects of free titanium clusters in the mass range from 15 to 1000 atoms, respectively including 15–55 atoms, 80–150 atoms, 300–500 atoms, and more than 600 atoms. The absorption intensity of these clusters/particles evolves from a multi-peaked complex structure being similar to that of Ti atoms towards spectra characterized by two main absorption features as in bulk titanium [25]. These observations might be interpreted as a signal of a transition between different crystalline structures in different cluster size (number of atoms). The information of structural transition should be provided in terms of the changes of packing patterns in the atoms. However, the characteristics of the transition are not yet fully understood, especially accounting for the temperature effect. Atomic simulations within the framework of embedded atom method (EAM), such as molecular dynamics (MD), are particularly well suited to characterize microscopic details in these metal systems [26–32]. In nanoscale, as the number of atoms in one particle increases, there is an exponential increase in computational cost as well as increasing data complexity. There are great challenges that micro scale simulations are validated by comparison with macroscopic properties including structural evolution and thermal behaviors. For example, in the case of bulk Ti, it has two structures including HCP at low temperatures and BCC at high temperatures [33]. In bulk materials with crystal structures, Dulong Petit law indicates that the specific heat per atom is 3kB in a relatively high temperature range, where kB is the Boltzmann constant. In the present work, molecular dynamics simulations are employed to investigate atom packing changes of Ti clusters at elevated temperature. The structures are simulated by employing two different potentials used for HCP and BCC phases at the low and high temperatures as well as intermediate structures with the linear combination of the two potentials. In the simulations, the shape changes of the clusters are related with the inertia moments. The atom packing changes are demonstrated by the pair analysis technique as well as visually packing evolution being insight into the effect of temperature and size conditions on the changes. Contributions of specific heat from potential energy in these clusters are also used to determine the critical size being subject to classical theory, which helps to estimate the amount of focus heat needed to melt these large size clusters.
钛及钛合金具有低密度、高生物相容性、足够的韧性以及耐腐蚀、耐磨损和抗断裂性能[1,2]。作为植入材料，它们为缩短恢复期、减少侵入性手术和提高患者生活舒适度提供了更好的解决方案。由于患者年龄、性别和解剖部位的差异，植入材料的成分和/或结构应在体积上逐渐变化，以避免过早的植入物松动和植入失败[3–8]。综合考虑力学和生物学标准，设计和制造这些植入材料是一项重大挑战。然而，钛的生产受到传统制造工艺高成本的制约，并且由于α-Ti中滑移系较少，复杂形状的加工性较差[9–11]。增材制造（AM）技术集成了材料科学、机制工程和激光技术，为设计者提供了更大的几何复杂零件设计自由度，同时减少了制造时间。AM意指在高真空气氛下将粉末材料逐层成形和固结为任意构型。AM中的激光熔化使用聚焦热能快速熔化钛颗粒，这得益于近年来不断改进的工艺条件（例如更高的激光功率、更小的聚焦光斑尺寸、更薄的层厚等），从而显著改善了微观结构和力学性能[12–21]。迄今为止，纳米颗粒已用于AM工艺[22–24]。随着尺寸减小到纳米量级，纳米颗粒或纳米团簇将呈现出与其块体对应物明显不同的特征，因此研究从含数十个原子到块体尺度的团簇对于材料设计至关重要。因此，理解这些团簇在高温下结构变化的尺寸依赖性成为一个关键目标。同步辐射X射线吸收光谱（XAS）被用于研究质量范围为15至1000个原子的自由钛团簇的尺寸效应，分别包括15–55个原子、80–150个原子、300–500个原子和超过600个原子。这些团簇/颗粒的吸收强度从类似于钛原子的多峰复杂结构演变为具有块体钛中两个主要吸收特征的光谱[25]。这些观察结果可解释为不同团簇尺寸（原子数）下不同晶体结构之间转变的信号。结构转变的信息应通过原子堆积模式的变化来提供。然而，这种转变的特征尚未完全理解，特别是考虑到温度效应。在嵌入原子方法（EAM）框架下的原子模拟，如分子动力学（MD），特别适合表征这些金属体系中的微观细节[26–32]。在纳米尺度上，随着一个颗粒中原子数的增加，计算成本呈指数增长，数据复杂性也增加。微观模拟通过与包括结构演化和热行为在内的宏观性质进行比较来验证，这面临着巨大挑战。例如，在块体钛的情况下，它具有两种结构：低温下的HCP和高温下的BCC[33]。在具有晶体结构的块体材料中，杜隆-珀蒂定律指出，在相对较高的温度范围内，每原子的比热容为3kB，其中kB是玻尔兹曼常数。在本工作中，采用分子动力学模拟研究高温下钛团簇原子堆积的变化。通过使用描述低温和高温下HCP和BCC相的两种不同势以及这两种势的线性组合的中间结构进行模拟。在模拟中，团簇的形状变化与惯性矩相关。原子堆积的变化通过对分析技术以及视觉堆积演化来展示，以深入了解温度和尺寸条件对变化的影响。这些团簇中势能对比热容的贡献也用于确定经典理论适用的临界尺寸，这有助于估计熔化这些大尺寸团簇所需的热量。
<span class="hljs-strong">**AM**</span>: 增材制造，一种通过逐层添加材料制造三维物体的技术。
<span class="hljs-strong">**XAS**</span>: X射线吸收光谱，用于研究材料中原子局部结构和电子状态的技术。
<span class="hljs-strong">**MD**</span>: 分子动力学，一种通过求解原子运动方程模拟原子尺度系统动力学行为的计算方法。
<span class="hljs-strong">**kB**</span>: 玻尔兹曼常数，将温度与能量联系起来的物理常数。

The temperature is kept constant by rescaling the atomic velocities every time-step. In the EAM framework, when the atom packing changes, the electron charge density and the distance between a pair of atoms change accordingly, resulting in the changes of the embedding energy and potential energy of paired atoms, which contribute to the total potential energy. As illustrated in Fig. 1, for the Ti19, Ti57, Ti135, Ti389, Ti727 and Ti1099 clusters, the changes of the total potential energy per atom present similar behaviors. The energy has very high values at initial steps for each cluster, where the atoms are packed into the same structure with that in the bulk crystalline. Then the atoms adjust their positions leading to the abrupt decrease of the energy. In relaxation processes, the atoms present thermal movements around their equilibrium positions. Therefore, the energy changes in an oscillating model. For the Ti19 or Ti57 cluster, as simulation time steps increase, the apparent decrease of the energy indicates the structural transition from the initial structure with the high energy to the low-energy structures. In each of the two clusters, a very high proportion of the atoms are in the surface, where these surface atoms have fewer coordination atoms compared to the atoms inside the clusters. Even at very low temperatures, some surface atoms can move easily, and there are transformations of multiple packing patterns in the relaxation processes. For the Ti19 cluster, because of the higher proportion of the surface atoms, the change of the packing pattern occurs at more short time than that of the Ti57 cluster. For 1422 pairs, the two bonded pairs of four common neighbor atoms have an intersecting point. Equal quantity of 1421 and 1422 pairs can be found in HCP-like local structures, 1551 pairs in locally icosahedral structures, and 1441 and 1661 pairs be used to identify local BCC structures. > > Fig. 3 illustrates the surface/volume ratio as a function of the diameter for the clusters at 50 K. In the Ti clusters with an HCP or icosahedral configuration, one atom has twelve coordinate atoms. As one atom is exposed to the surface, the number of its coordination atoms is less than 12. In this figure, the ratio βsurface is calculated by βsurface = Nsurface/N (Nsurface is given by counting the atom number with less than 12 coordinates). This figure clarifies that in one small size cluster, more atoms lie in the surface. As the atom number increases, the ratio decreases to 0.3, and quantitatively tells us the effect of cluster size, which coincides with experience but is physically easy to understand through the simulations. As given in this figure, the ratio declines in an oscillatory mode, and the amplitude of oscillation decreases as the atom number increases. It can be found that some clusters have relatively low ratio values compared to their neighboring clusters. These are from the following fact that the statics of surface atoms includes the atoms in the outer-most and sub-outer regions, where these atoms have the coordinators which are less than 12. For the clusters with the size smaller than 2.8 nm or containing less than 700 atoms, quite amount proportions of the atoms are in the surface regions. For example, because most of the atoms in the Ti19 cluster can be viewed as the surface atoms, the energy has the highest value compared to those of the other clusters as given by Fig. 4a. With the increase of the atom number in these clusters, the energy decreases accompanied by the decrease of the ratio. The high ratio of the surface atoms contributes to the apparently higher potential energy per atom in the clusters containing less than 700 atoms than that of those larger size clusters. The surface atoms have important influence on the changes of atom packing patterns, leading to apparently packing differences in these clusters. For small size clusters, these surface atoms only need small amount of energy to drive them rearrange their packing patterns. The rearrangements result in the packing changes in the whole cluster. In the cases of the large size clusters, most of the atoms can hold their HCP packing patterns in a temperature range. Therefore, as illustrated in Fig. 4a, below 450 K, there are the decreases of the potential energy for the Ti19, Ti57, or Ti135 cluster, indicating that some configurations with lower energy can be found in the temperature range, whereas the increases of the energy can be found for the Ti389, Ti727, and Ti1099 clusters. Because of introducing the interpolation tanh function, between the 450 K and 700 K, energy values of the six clusters apparently decrease. In the following high temperature regime, the Ti19 cluster has a stably packing pattern, whereas there are differently packing patterns in the other two small size clusters resulting in oscillations of the energy. The energy of the Ti389 cluster increases in oscillation, and above a high temperature of 1450 K, it has an abrupt increase, suggesting that most of the atoms in this cluster are disorderly packed. The melting implication above a high temperature can be also found in the energy changes of the Ti727 and Ti1099 clusters, and the melting temperature of Ti1099 is apparently higher than that of the Ti727. The changes in these clusters result in differences of the shapes at different temperatures as illustrated in Fig. 4b. For a spherical cluster, it's inertia moments of three principal axes have nearly equal values. Therefore, the shape factor is nearly one. The Ti19 cluster show sharp shape changes. Above 600 K, this cluster becomes elongation, and the elongated shape can be hold until very high temperature. The phenomenon, that the elongation at high temperatures followed the dramatic changes in shape, can be also found in the Ti57, Ti135, and Ti389 clusters. In the case of the Ti1099 cluster, it remains nearly spherical shape before and after melting. The following pair analysis can be used to identify the detailed changes of the packing patterns. Fig. 5 details the dependence of pair fraction on the increasing temperatures for the Ti19, Ti57, Ti135, Ti389, Ti727, and Ti1099 clusters. In this figure, the values at 0 K are from the initially constructed structures of the six clusters, where the 1421 and 1422 fractions are equal. For the two small clusters of Ti19 and Ti57, at 50 K, there are decreases of the two kinds of pairs. Below 500 K, the pairs' changes suggest that the Ti19 cluster has two stably packing patterns. Then, the packing patterns present differences. Above 650 K, this cluster has an icosahedron configuration. For the Ti57 cluster, the pairs' changes suggest that as the temperature increases, the atoms lost their HCP packing patterns, and this cluster has icosahedral configurations. In the temperature range from 650 K to 1200 K, the atoms are packed into a stably icosahedral configuration. With increasing the temperature, as the shape of this cluster becomes elongation, it presents differently packing patterns. In the case of Ti135 cluster, at very low temperatures of 50 K and 100 K, this cluster can hold the same HCP packing pattern with that in the bulk crystal. As the temperature increases, while most of the atoms are packed into HCP patterns, some surface atoms adjust their positions. Above 300 K, there is a transformation from HCP to icosahedron. In the temperature range from 450 K to 950 K, the atoms are packed into stable patterns with one icosahedral configuration. Then, there are movements of a few of atoms in the cluster' surface. Above 1200 K, as this cluster lost its icosahedral configuration, the atoms in this cluster are packed into multiply packing patterns. For the Ti389, Ti727, or Ti1099 cluster, in a relatively large temperature range, most of the atoms are packed into the HCP patterns. Above a temperature, the pair fraction changes suggest that a certain number of atoms in the surface have BCC packing patterns. In the following temperature range, the coexistence of BCC and HCP occurs. As the temperature increase further, icosahedral patterns are formed in some atoms of the Ti389 cluster. Then, there is the coexistence of the BCC and HCP patterns, and icosahedral patterns disappear. For the Ti727 and Ti1099 clusters, in the high temperature range respectively above 1450 K and 1500 K, the atoms are in disordered states. Fig. 6 shows the packing patterns of these six clusters at different temperatures. Compared with initially constructed structures, at 50 K, except for the Ti19 cluster, the atoms in the other five clusters still present the same packing pattern with that in bulk HCP Ti crystalline. The Ti19 cluster has different configurations below 650 K. At high temperatures, this cluster has a rod-like shape with three connected small icosahedral structures. For both of the Ti57 and Ti135 clusters, at relatively low temperatures, there are the position adjustments of a few of atoms in the surface, and differently icosahedral configurations occur in large temperature ranges. Above 1300 K, with the disappearance of those icosahedral configurations, the shape of the two clusters becomes elongation, and atom packing presents diversities. It can be noted that at 1300 K, most of the atoms in the Ti135 cluster are packed into the BCC patterns. Most of the atoms in the Ti389, Ti727, and Ti1099 clusters can hold their HCP packing patterns in large temperature ranges. At one certain temperature, packing patterns of some atoms in the surface are transformed from HCP to BCC, resulting in the coexistence of HCP and BCC. For the Ti389 cluster, there is an apparent elongation of the shape (see Fig. 6). As the energy changes with increasing the temperature, the thermal capacity can be determined by ΔU/ΔT, where ΔU includes the potential energy and kinetic energy. In the bulk, the atoms present thermal movements around their equilibrium positions. Therefore, Dulong-Petit law indicates that the slope of ΔU/ΔT in the high temperature regime should be 3.0, where half comes from kinetic energy and the other half from potential energy. In the present simulations, because the temperature is kept constant, the kinetic energy's contribution to the slope can be viewed as 1.5. For the potential energy contribution as plotted in Fig. 7, in the high temperature regime before taking place the abrupt increase of the energy, the relatively large clusters with the size more than 2.8 nm, have slopes of close to 1.5, where the temperature range is from 800 K to 1350 K. Some of them have a little of high values, whereas the others low values. These suggest that although most of atoms in these clusters can hold stably packing patterns, a certain number of atoms in the surface are packed into different patterns. For these small size clusters, the energy increases in a slope with a small value. > > ## 4. Conclusions
> > 
> > ## 4. 结论
> > 
> > Molecular dynamics simulation using an embedded atom method (EAM) potential shows that the clusters' size and temperature greatly affect the changes of the packing patterns in Ti clusters. The small size clusters prefer to the icosahedron configurations at high temperatures, and there are multi-structures' transitions. Most of the atoms in the large size clusters can hold their HCP packing patterns in a larger temperature range. Movements and structural rearrangements of the atoms in the surface play different roles in these clusters. The coexistence of multi-structures occurs with the increase of the temperature. At a high temperature, the accumulation of structural disorder can quickly extend into the entire region of these large size clusters, which resembles the melting of bulk Ti. By calculating the contribution of the potential energy to the specific heat, the critical size of the applicable range of Dulong Petit law is close to 3.0 nm for the Ti clusters. 使用嵌入原子方法（EAM）势的分子动力学模拟表明，团簇尺寸和温度极大地影响钛团簇中堆积模式的变化。小尺寸团簇在高温下倾向于二十面体构型，并存在多种结构转变。大尺寸团簇中的大多数原子在较宽的温度范围内可以保持其HCP堆积模式。表面原子的移动和结构重排在这些团簇中起着不同的作用。随着温度升高，出现多种结构的共存。在高温下，结构无序的积累可以迅速扩展到大尺寸团簇的整个区域，类似于块体钛的熔化。通过计算势能对比热容的贡献，钛团簇杜隆-珀蒂定律适用范围的临界尺寸接近3.0 nm。

**ABSTRACT**
**摘要**

Atomic simulations using an embedded atom method (EAM) potential were performed to study changes of packing patterns in titanium clusters containing tens to thousands of atoms. The findings revealed that the changes came from the movements and rearrangements of surface atoms in a large temperature range, and they were strongly dependent on cluster size and elevated temperatures. Small size clusters with icosahedron (Ih) configurations of geometric shell closures were preferred. As the cluster size increased, most of the atoms in these clusters could hold their HCP packing patterns. With the increasing temperature, the coexistence of different packing patterns including HCP, BCC, and Ih occurred. At a high temperature, the disorderly packing quickly extended into the entire region of a large size cluster. The calculations of specific heat were compared with Dulong-Petit law. The simulations provide the possibility to straddle structural transformations and determine critical size suitable for classical theory under certain thermal conditions.
使用嵌入原子方法（EAM）势的原子模拟研究了含有数十至数千个原子的钛团簇中堆积模式的变化。研究发现，这些变化源于大温度范围内表面原子的移动和重排，并且强烈依赖于团簇尺寸和升高的温度。具有几何壳层闭合的二十面体（Ih）构型的小尺寸团簇更受青睐。随着团簇尺寸增大，这些团簇中的大多数原子能够保持其HCP堆积模式。随着温度升高，出现了包括HCP、BCC和Ih在内的不同堆积模式的共存。在高温下，无序堆积迅速扩展到大尺寸团簇的整个区域。比热容的计算结果与杜隆-珀蒂定律进行了比较。这些模拟为理解结构转变以及在特定热条件下确定适合经典理论的临界尺寸提供了可能。

## 1. Introduction
## 1. 引言

Titanium and titanium alloys exhibit low density, high biocompatibility, adequate toughness as well as corrosion, wear, and fracture resistances [1,2]. Used as implant materials, they provide better solution for shortening recovery periods, less invasive surgeries, and improvement of patients' life comfort. Because of differences in age, sex, and anatomical sites of patients, the composition and/or the structure of the implant materials should change gradually over the volume to avoid premature implant loosening and implantation failure [3–8]. Considering both mechanical and biological criteria, the design and fabrication of these implant materials is a major challenge. However, titanium production is hampered by the high cost in traditionally manufacturing processes, and poor process-ability for complex shape production because of fewer slip systems in α-Ti [9–11]. Additive manufacturing (AM) technology, integrating materials science, mechanism engineering, and laser technology, is giving greater freedom to designers for geometrically complex parts along with reduced manufacturing time. AM implies layer by layer shaping and consolidation of powder materials to arbitrary configurations under a high vacuum atmosphere. The laser melting in AM uses focused thermal energy to rapidly melt Ti particles, which is supported by continuously improved processing conditions in recent years (e.g. higher laser power, smaller focused spot size, smaller layer thickness, etc.), leading to significantly improved microstructural and mechanical properties [12–21]. Up to date, the nanoparticles have been used in the AM process [22–24]. As the size decreases to nanometer, nanoparticles or nanoclusters will present apparently different features from their bulk counterparts, and the study on the clusters containing tens of atoms toward bulk is of great importance in materials designing. Therefore, it becomes a key target for understanding the size dependence of structural changes in these clusters at elevated temperature. Synchrotron radiation X-ray absorption spectroscopy (XAS) is applied to study size effects of free titanium clusters in the mass range from 15 to 1000 atoms, respectively including 15–55 atoms, 80–150 atoms, 300–500 atoms, and more than 600 atoms. The absorption intensity of these clusters/particles evolves from a multi-peaked complex structure being similar to that of Ti atoms towards spectra characterized by two main absorption features as in bulk titanium [25]. These observations might be interpreted as a signal of a transition between different crystalline structures in different cluster size (number of atoms). The information of structural transition should be provided in terms of the changes of packing patterns in the atoms. However, the characteristics of the transition are not yet fully understood, especially accounting for the temperature effect. Atomic simulations within the framework of embedded atom method (EAM), such as molecular dynamics (MD), are particularly well suited to characterize microscopic details in these metal systems [26–32]. In nanoscale, as the number of atoms in one particle increases, there is an exponential increase in computational cost as well as increasing data complexity. There are great challenges that micro scale simulations are validated by comparison with macroscopic properties including structural evolution and thermal behaviors. For example, in the case of bulk Ti, it has two structures including HCP at low temperatures and BCC at high temperatures [33]. In bulk materials with crystal structures, Dulong Petit law indicates that the specific heat per atom is 3kB in a relatively high temperature range, where kB is the Boltzmann constant. In the present work, molecular dynamics simulations are employed to investigate atom packing changes of Ti clusters at elevated temperature. The structures are simulated by employing two different potentials used for HCP and BCC phases at the low and high temperatures as well as intermediate structures with the linear combination of the two potentials. In the simulations, the shape changes of the clusters are related with the inertia moments. The atom packing changes are demonstrated by the pair analysis technique as well as visually packing evolution being insight into the effect of temperature and size conditions on the changes. Contributions of specific heat from potential energy in these clusters are also used to determine the critical size being subject to classical theory, which helps to estimate the amount of focus heat needed to melt these large size clusters.

> > Fig. 3 illustrates the surface/volume ratio as a function of the diameter for the clusters at 50 K. In the Ti clusters with an HCP or icosahedral configuration, one atom has twelve coordinate atoms. As one atom is exposed to the surface, the number of its coordination atoms is less than 12. In this figure, the ratio βsurface is calculated by βsurface = Nsurface/N (Nsurface is given by counting the atom number with less than 12 coordinates). This figure clarifies that in one small size cluster, more atoms lie in the surface. As the atom number increases, the ratio decreases to 0.3, and quantitatively tells us the effect of cluster size, which coincides with experience but is physically easy to understand through the simulations. As given in this figure, the ratio declines in an oscillatory mode, and the amplitude of oscillation decreases as the atom number increases. It can be found that some clusters have relatively low ratio values compared to their neighboring clusters. These are from the following fact that the statics of surface atoms includes the atoms in the outer-most and sub-outer regions, where these atoms have the coordinators which are less than 12. For the clusters with the size smaller than 2.8 nm or containing less than 700 atoms, quite amount proportions of the atoms are in the surface regions. For example, because most of the atoms in the Ti19 cluster can be viewed as the surface atoms, the energy has the highest value compared to those of the other clusters as given by Fig. 4a. With the increase of the atom number in these clusters, the energy decreases accompanied by the decrease of the ratio. The high ratio of the surface atoms contributes to the apparently higher potential energy per atom in the clusters containing less than 700 atoms than that of those larger size clusters. The surface atoms have important influence on the changes of atom packing patterns, leading to apparently packing differences in these clusters. For small size clusters, these surface atoms only need small amount of energy to drive them rearrange their packing patterns. The rearrangements result in the packing changes in the whole cluster. In the cases of the large size clusters, most of the atoms can hold their HCP packing patterns in a temperature range. Therefore, as illustrated in Fig. 4a, below 450 K, there are the decreases of the potential energy for the Ti19, Ti57, or Ti135 cluster, indicating that some configurations with lower energy can be found in the temperature range, whereas the increases of the energy can be found for the Ti389, Ti727, and Ti1099 clusters. Because of introducing the interpolation tanh function, between the 450 K and 700 K, energy values of the six clusters apparently decrease. In the following high temperature regime, the Ti19 cluster has a stably packing pattern, whereas there are differently packing patterns in the other two small size clusters resulting in oscillations of the energy. The energy of the Ti389 cluster increases in oscillation, and above a high temperature of 1450 K, it has an abrupt increase, suggesting that most of the atoms in this cluster are disorderly packed. > > ## 4. Conclusions
> > 
> > ## 4. 结论
> > 
> > Molecular dynamics simulation using an embedded atom method (EAM) potential shows that the clusters' size and temperature greatly affect the changes of the packing patterns in Ti clusters. The small size clusters prefer to the icosahedron configurations at high temperatures, and there are multi-structures' transitions. Most of the atoms in the large size clusters can hold their HCP packing patterns in a larger temperature range. Movements and structural rearrangements of the atoms in the surface play different roles in these clusters. The coexistence of multi-structures occurs with the increase of the temperature. At a high temperature, the accumulation of structural disorder can quickly extend into the entire region of these large size clusters, which resembles the melting of bulk Ti. By calculating the contribution of the potential energy to the specific heat, the critical size of the applicable range of Dulong Petit law is close to 3.0 nm for the Ti clusters. 使用嵌入原子方法（EAM）势的分子动力学模拟表明，团簇尺寸和温度极大地影响钛团簇中堆积模式的变化。小尺寸团簇在高温下倾向于二十面体构型，并存在多种结构转变。大尺寸团簇中的大多数原子在较宽的温度范围内可以保持其HCP堆积模式。表面原子的移动和结构重排在这些团簇中起着不同的作用。随着温度升高，出现多种结构的共存。在高温下，结构无序的积累可以迅速扩展到大尺寸团簇的整个区域，类似于块体钛的熔化。通过计算势能对比热容的贡献，钛团簇杜隆-珀蒂定律适用范围的临界尺寸接近3.0 nm。

> > ### 咱们先弄懂那些让您头疼的“洋词”和“专业话”
> > 
> > 您可以把这些词想象成咱们生活中的东西，就好理解了：
> > 
> > 1.  **钛（Tai）：** 这是一种银白色的金属，又轻又结实，还特别亲和人，不容易生锈，常用来做飞机零件、人造骨头、人造关节啥的。
> > 2.  **原子（Atom）：** 这是构成物质的最基本小颗粒，小到肉眼根本看不见。您可以把一个原子想象成一颗小弹珠。
> > 3.  **团簇（Cluster）：** 就是几十个、几百个甚至几千个原子“抱团”聚在一起，形成的一个非常非常小的集团。这篇文章研究的就是这些“原子小集团”，从几十个原子到几千个原子都有。
> > 4.  **计算机模拟（Atomic simulations）：** 科学家们没有真的用显微镜去看，而是在电脑里编写程序，设定好原子之间“拉手”和“推挤”的规则，然后让电脑算出来这些小集团在不同温度下会变成什么样。就像玩一个高级的“搭积木”游戏。
> > 5.  **堆积模式（Packing patterns）：** 指的就是原子们是怎么排列、怎么“扎堆”的。就像咱们排队，可以排成方阵，也可以围成圆圈。原子们也有自己偏好的排队方式，比如：
> >     -   **HCP结构：** 一种整整齐齐的排列方式，像用六角形的砖砌墙，很稳定。
> >     -   **BCC结构：** 另一种整齐的排列方式，像一个立方体，中心还有一颗原子。这种结构通常在高温下出现。
> >     -   **正二十面体（Icosahedron, Ih）：** 这是一种非常漂亮的对称结构，像个足球，由20个三角面组成。小团簇的原子特别喜欢围成这种形状，因为这样最“省劲”，最稳定。
> > 6.  **相变（Phase transition）：** 就是事物状态的变化。比如水结冰（液态变固态）、水烧开（液态变气态）。在这里，指的是原子们的排列方式改变了，比如从HCP的排法变成了BCC的排法。
> > 7.  **比热（Specific heat）：** 可以理解为，要把一定量的物质温度升高1度，需要给它加多少“热乎劲”。不同物质需要的热量不一样。
> > 8.  **杜隆-珀蒂定律（Dulong-Petit law）：** 这是一个老定律，说的是在温度比较高的时候，很多固体物质，虽然种类不同，但它们的原子，每升高1度所需要的热量是差不多的，是个常数。
> > 9.  **表面原子（Surface atoms）：** 在一个原子小集团里，有些原子在里面，被四面八方都包裹着；有些原子在最外层，没有被完全包裹，这些就是“表面原子”。小集团越小，表面原子占的比例就越大，就像一小撮盐，绝大多数都在表面。

> > ### 现在，咱们把整篇文章“翻译”成大白话故事
> > 
> > **题目：** 用电脑模拟钛原子小集团的排列方式和它们在不同温度下的表现
> > 
> > **故事背景：** 钛金属做的植入物（比如人造骨头）特别好，因为轻便、结实、人体不排斥。但每个人的骨头情况不一样，如果能做出在人体内慢慢变化、能自我适应的植入物，那就更好了。现在有个新技术叫“3D打印”（文章里叫增材制造），能把钛粉一层层地“打印”成复杂的形状。这个技术用的是非常细小的钛粉，小到纳米级别。这些微小的钛颗粒，它的性质和大块的钛可能完全不同。所以，科学家们就想搞清楚，这些由几十到几千个原子组成的钛小集团，在温度升高的时候，内部结构到底是怎么变的。这就像一个“知己知彼”的基础研究，为以后制造出更好的植入物打基础。
> > 
> > **研究问题：** 科学家们主要想搞清楚两个问题：
> > 
> > 1.  这些钛原子小集团，随着它们包含的原子越来越多（也就是个头越来越大），它们的排列方式会发生什么变化？
> > 2.  在温度不断升高的时候，这些小集团的排列方式又会怎么变？什么时候会乱掉，什么时候会“熔化”？
> > 
> > **研究方法是怎样的？** 科学家们用电脑设定了两套“游戏规则”（就是文章里说的“势”），一套模仿低温时钛原子的排列习惯（HCP），一套模仿高温时的习惯（BCC）。然后，他们用一个小技巧，把这两套规则“揉”在一起，让它能根据温度自动切换。 他们先在电脑里“捏”出大小不一的29个钛原子小集团，最小的只有19个原子，最大的有2601个原子。然后，让电脑模拟从零下223度（50K）一直升温到1277度（1550K），观察每个小集团里原子的排列变化、形状变化和能量变化。
> > 
> > **研究结论：**
> > 
> > 1.  **个头大小（尺寸）的影响：**
> >     
> >     -   **小个头集团（比如19个、57个原子）：** 特别喜欢围成“足球”形状（正二十面体），因为这样最稳定。它们很“善变”，会从一种排列变成另一种。
> >     -   **大个头集团（比如几百到上千个原子）：** 大部分原子能保持它们原本整整齐齐的排列（HCP），像一块有纪律的“小晶体”。但是，表面的那些原子因为“约束”少，会比较活跃，容易乱动。
> > 2.  **温度变化的影响：**
> >     
> >     -   **温度升高时：** 大小集团都开始出现混乱。表面原子先开始“不安分”，它们会重新排列，导致出现多种排列方式（HCP、BCC、足球形）同时存在的情况。
> >     -   **温度特别高时（接近熔化）：** 小集团可能会变得很“苗条”，拉成长条状。大集团呢，表面的混乱会迅速向内部蔓延，最后整个集团都变得乱糟糟的，就像冰块化成了水，这就是“熔化”了。
> > 3.  **关于“热乎劲”（比热）的发现：**
> >     
> >     -   科学家们还发现，只有那些个头大于3纳米（约千来个原子）的钛集团，在高温下，它们原子升温和“热乎劲”的关系，才符合那个经典的“杜隆-珀蒂定律”。这为生产上“需要多少热量才能熔化这些粉末”提供了关键参考数据。
> > 
> > **创新点在哪？** 以前的研究可能只关注了尺寸变化，或者只关注了温度变化。这项研究的创新之处在于，它**把尺寸和温度这两个因素放在一起考虑**，系统地展示了从“小个头”到“大个头”的钛原子集团，在不同温度下的“变形记”。特别是，它揭示了一个**临界尺寸**——3纳米左右，只有大于这个尺寸，这些微小颗粒的行为才和大块材料差不多，才能用经典理论来预测。
> > 
> > **不足之处：** 这篇文章是纯粹的计算机模拟，是一个理论上的研究。虽然它揭示了很多微观细节，但**这些发现还需要通过真实的、高精度的实验来验证**。毕竟，电脑里的世界和现实世界可能会有出入。文章里也提到了一个叫“X射线吸收光谱”的实验技术，看到了类似的变化信号，但还不够清晰，说明把模拟和实验完全对上，还是个挑战。
