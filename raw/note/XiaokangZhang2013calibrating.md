> # Calibrating an optical fiber humidity sensor and applying it in real-time monitoring of relative humidity in fresh concrete
> 
> # 光纤湿度传感器的校准及其在新拌混凝土相对湿度实时监测中的应用
> 
> Xiaokang Zhang (张小康)_, Zhuoyong Deng (邓卓勇), and Hanlang Xu (许瀚朗) 张小康_，邓卓勇，许瀚朗
> 
> Department of Physics, School of Science, South China University of Technology, Guangzhou 510640, China 华南理工大学理学院物理系，广州 510640，中国 *Corresponding author: xkzhang@scut.edu.cn *通信作者: xkzhang@scut.edu.cn
> 
> Received May 3, 2013; accepted June 28, 2013; posted online September 3, 2013 收稿日期: 2013年5月3日; 录用日期: 2013年6月28日; 在线发布日期: 2013年9月3日
> 
> A doubly cladding single-mode fiber humidity sensor is fabricated by agarose. The sensor has an insertion loss of −0.08 dB and a power change of −17.83 dB. The responses of the sensor to a relative humidity (RH) range form 30% to 100% at a temperature range form 25 to 34 ◦C are validated. The experiments demonstrate that the absorbability of agarose gel to moisture decreases with increasing RH in measured gas. We propose a calibration method that uses lookup tables and construct a corresponding calibration matrix. Using the sensor, we conduct real-time monitoring of RH in fresh concrete during its hardening process. 利用琼脂糖制作了一种双包层单模光纤湿度传感器。该传感器具有-0.08 dB的插入损耗和-17.83 dB的功率变化。验证了传感器在25至34°C温度范围内对30%至100%相对湿度(RH)范围的响应。实验表明，琼脂糖凝胶对水分的吸收能力随被测气体中RH的升高而降低。我们提出了一种使用查找表的校准方法，并构建了相应的校准矩阵。利用该传感器，我们对新拌混凝土硬化过程中的RH进行了实时监测。 **Doubly cladding single-mode fiber**: 双包层单模光纤，一种具有内外两层包层的特种光纤结构。 **Agarose**: 琼脂糖，一种从海藻中提取的多糖，常用于制作水凝胶。 **Insertion loss**: 插入损耗，指在传输线路中插入某器件所引起的信号功率损耗。 **Relative Humidity (RH)**: 相对湿度，指空气中水汽压与相同温度下饱和水汽压的百分比。
> 
> OCIS codes: 060.2370, 120.4820. OCIS 代码: 060.2370, 120.4820.
> 
> doi: 10.3788/COL201311.090604. 数字对象标识符: 10.3788/COL201311.090604.
> 
> Humidity has a significant effect on many industries, such as textile manufacture, medical production, and national defense. Optical fiber humidity sensors can overcome many limitations of electrical sensors, such as electromagnetic interference and the inability to measure humidity near 100% relative humidity (RH). Thus, optical fiber systems that monitor humidity present extensive development prospects. Optical fiber approaches for humidity sensing are based primarily on optical power variations[1−5] and wavelength shifts[6−8]. Each method has its own advantages. Wavelength-based sensors have excellent long-term stability, but optical power interrogation is simpler and more economical than complex spectroscopy. Therefore, an optical fiber sensing scheme based on power variations would be of great value in short-term detection applications, such as the monitoring of humidity in fresh concrete during its hardening process. The fashions of power-based sensors include evanescent field sensors and other differentially structured sensors, such as tilted fiber Bragg grating (FBG)[4] and combined FBG and tapered fibers[5]. The structure of evanescent field sensors comprises doubly clad single-mode fibers (DCSMF)[1], tapered fibers[2], and side-polished fibers[3]. Among the power variations obtained in these sensors, those derived in a DCSMF sensor reaches nearly 9 dB at 30% to 100% RH and 25 ◦C, with an insertion loss of less than −0.5 dB[1]. 湿度对许多行业有重大影响，如纺织制造、医药生产和国防。光纤湿度传感器可以克服电子传感器的许多局限性，例如电磁干扰和无法测量接近100%相对湿度(RH)的湿度。因此，监测湿度的光纤系统具有广阔的发展前景。用于湿度传感的光纤方法主要基于光功率变化[1−5]和波长偏移[6−8]。每种方法都有其自身的优点。基于波长的传感器具有出色的长期稳定性，但光功率解调比复杂的光谱分析更简单、更经济。因此，基于功率变化的光纤传感方案在短期检测应用中具有巨大价值，例如监测新拌混凝土硬化过程中的湿度。基于功率的传感器类型包括倏逝场传感器和其他差分结构传感器，如倾斜光纤布拉格光栅(FBG)[4]以及FBG与锥形光纤的组合[5]。倏逝场传感器的结构包括双包层单模光纤(DCSMF)[1]、锥形光纤[2]和侧边抛磨光纤[3]。在这些传感器获得的功率变化中，DCSMF传感器在30%至100% RH和25°C条件下，功率变化可达近9 dB，插入损耗小于-0.5 dB[1]。 **Electromagnetic interference**: 电磁干扰，指电磁场对电子设备或系统性能产生的干扰现象。 **Optical power variation**: 光功率变化，指光信号强度随外界物理量变化而产生的改变。 **Wavelength shift**: 波长偏移，指光信号中心波长随外界物理量变化而产生的移动。 **Spectroscopy**: 光谱学/光谱分析，一种通过分析物质与光相互作用来研究其性质的技术。 **Evanescent field**: 倏逝场，在光纤表面附近存在的、强度随距离指数衰减的电磁场。 **Fiber Bragg Grating (FBG)**: 光纤布拉格光栅，一种在光纤纤芯中引入周期性折射率调制的无源器件。 **Tapered fiber**: 锥形光纤，通过加热拉伸使得光纤直径在某区域逐渐变细的光纤结构。 **Side-polished fiber**: 侧边抛磨光纤，将光纤一侧的包层研磨掉，使其接近纤芯的光纤器件。

> The performance of a humidity sensor at different temperatures should be evaluated because RH is temperature dependent. Kronenberg et al.[6] studied the influence of RH and temperature on an FBG. Despite the contributions of the study, however more complex investigations are required for practical applications. Humidity sensors based on optical power variations have only been studied at a certain temperature. To the best of our knowledge, the behavior of humidity sensors at different temperatures has not been examined, and reports on the application of optical fiber humidity sensors are scarce. 由于RH具有温度依赖性，因此应评估湿度传感器在不同温度下的性能。Kronenberg等人[6]研究了RH和温度对FBG的影响。尽管该研究做出了贡献，但实际应用仍需要更复杂的调查。基于光功率变化的湿度传感器仅在特定温度下进行了研究。据我们所知，湿度传感器在不同温度下的行为尚未得到检验，关于光纤湿度传感器应用的报道也很少。
> 
> In this letter, a DCSMF humidity sensor with large power variations and low insertion loss is fabricated. The responses of the sensor to 30% to 100% RH at 25 to 34 ◦C are verified. An approach to calibrating the optical fiber humidity sensor is also proposed. We use the humidity sensor and a temperature sensor to monitor RH in fresh concrete during its hardening process. 在本快报中，制作了一种具有大功率变化和低插入损耗的DCSMF湿度传感器。验证了传感器在25至34°C下对30%至100% RH的响应。还提出了一种校准光纤湿度传感器的方法。我们使用湿度传感器和温度传感器来监测新拌混凝土硬化过程中的RH。
> 
> The principle and fabrication of DCSMF humidity sensors are described in Ref. [1]. The fiber used in our study is naked Corning SMF-28e, whose total length is around 100 cm. The sensing zone is marked approximately 2 cm from the central portion of the fiber. The original cladding of the fiber in the zone is partly removed by etching. The rest functions as an inner cladding. The etched part of the fiber is fixed onto a plate with dimensions of 7 × 3 × 0.2 (cm), in which the center part is hollowed out and the sensing zone does not come into contact with the plate. Two sections of copper wires (diameter, 0.9 mm; length, 4 cm) are positioned at both sides of the zone to form two enclosures. Hydrogel compounded by agarose granules (dry) mixed with deionized water is coated onto the sensing zone to form an outer cladding. The copper wires protect the zone from damage and enable convenient coating. The optical fiber sensor is shown in Fig. 1. The sensing probe is dried at room condition for a day, and the two ends of the fiber are spliced into two pigtails by fusion. The sensing probe is inserted into a climatic chamber (Binder KBF 115). The light from a stabilized DFB-LD light source (1 550 nm; output power, 0.08 dBm) is injected into the sensor, and the output signal is measured by an optical power meter (both provided by Accelink Technologies Co.). DCSMF湿度传感器的原理和制作方法见参考文献[1]。我们研究中使用的光纤是裸纤Corning SMF-28e，总长度约为100 cm。传感区标记在距光纤中心部分约2 cm处。该区域光纤的原有包层通过蚀刻被部分去除，剩余部分作为内包层。光纤的蚀刻部分固定在尺寸为7 × 3 × 0.2 (cm)的板上，板中心部分被挖空，传感区不与板接触。两段铜线（直径0.9 mm，长度4 cm）放置在区域两侧，形成两个围挡。将由琼脂糖颗粒（干燥）与去离子水混合而成的水凝胶涂覆在传感区上，形成外包层。铜线保护该区域免受损坏，并便于涂覆。光纤传感器如图1所示。传感探头在室温条件下干燥一天，光纤的两端通过熔接接入两根尾纤。将传感探头插入气候试验箱（Binder KBF 115）。来自稳定DFB-LD光源（1550 nm；输出功率0.08 dBm）的光被注入传感器，输出信号由光功率计测量（两者均由光迅科技股份有限公司提供）。 **Cladding**: 包层，指光纤中围绕纤芯的介质层，其折射率低于纤芯。 **Etching**: 蚀刻，通过化学方法选择性地去除材料的一种工艺。 **Hydrogel**: 水凝胶，一种能够在水中溶胀但不溶解的亲水性聚合物网络。 **Deionized water**: 去离子水，去除了大部分离子杂质的高纯度水。 **Pigtail**: 尾纤，一端带有光纤连接器的一小段光纤，用于连接设备。 **Climatic chamber**: 气候试验箱，一种能精确控制温度和湿度环境的设备。 **DFB-LD**: 分布式反馈激光二极管，一种能够产生单纵模、窄线宽激光的半导体光源。 **Optical power meter**: 光功率计，一种测量光信号功率的仪器。
> 
> Fig. 1. Optical fiber humidity sensor. 图1. 光纤湿度传感器。
> 
> The sensing probe is subjected to wet–dry cycles to accelerate aging. The climatic chamber runs cycles at a humidity range of 30% to 90% RH at a fixed temperature of 25 ◦C once a day for five consecutive days. Then, the chamber is operated at a humidity range of 30% to 95% RH with intervals of 5% RH at 25, 28, 31 and 34 ◦C. The 5% RH intervals last 40 min. The responses of the sensor to different RH levels at different temperatures are shown in Fig. 2. The optical power loss of the sensor decreases with increasing RH at constant temperature. Previous studies[1] demonstrated that optical power loss decreases as the water content of agarose coating increases at high RH and so the refractive index of the coating (n) decreases. Figure 2 also shows that the loss decreases with higher temperature at constant RH. This result can be attributed to the strong saturation vapor pressure (100% RH) at high temperature and the high water content of the air in the chamber even at a constant RH. Thus, n decreases with increasing temperature and decreasing loss. Figure 2 shows different changes in loss at the same changes of temperature under different RH conditions. For example, loss changes by 2.8 dB at a fixed humidity of 30% RH and a temperature range form 25 to 34 ◦C. Such change is 0.9 dB at 90% RH. Thus, the temperature responses are 0.3 and 0.1 dB/◦C, respectively. However, the change in saturated vapor pressure, as calculated from Eq. (1), is approximately 2.15 kPa when temperature increases from 25 to 34 ◦C. The changes in absolute vapor pressure are 0.65 kPa at 30% RH and 1.94 kPa at 90% RH. Given that the temperature response at 30% RH is larger than that at 90% RH, we conclude that the absorbability of the agarose coating under moisture in high RH considerably decreases. Thus, the effect of temperature on sensor attenuation also diminishes. 传感探头经过干湿循环以加速老化。气候试验箱在25°C固定温度下，以30%至90% RH的湿度范围每天运行一个循环，连续运行五天。然后，试验箱在25, 28, 31和34°C下，以5% RH的间隔，在30%至95% RH的湿度范围内运行。每个5% RH间隔持续40分钟。传感器在不同温度下对不同RH水平的响应如图2所示。在恒定温度下，传感器的光功率损耗随RH的增加而减小。先前的研究[1]表明，在高RH下，随着琼脂糖涂层含水量的增加，光功率损耗降低，因此涂层的折射率(n)减小。图2还显示，在恒定RH下，损耗随温度升高而降低。此结果可归因于高温下较高的饱和蒸气压（100% RH）以及即使在恒定RH下试验箱内空气的高含水量。因此，n随温度升高而减小，损耗也随之降低。图2显示了在不同RH条件下，相同温度变化下损耗的不同变化。例如，在30% RH的固定湿度和25至34°C的温度范围内，损耗变化了2.8 dB。而在90% RH时，此变化为0.9 dB。因此，温度响应分别为0.3和0.1 dB/°C。然而，根据公式(1)计算，当温度从25°C升高到34°C时，饱和蒸气压的变化约为2.15 kPa。在30% RH时，绝对蒸气压的变化为0.65 kPa，在90% RH时为1.94 kPa。鉴于30% RH时的温度响应大于90% RH时的温度响应，我们得出结论：琼脂糖涂层在高RH下对水分的吸收能力显著降低。因此，温度对传感器衰减的影响也随之减弱。 **Refractive index**: 折射率，描述光在介质中传播速度的物理量，决定了光在界面处的偏折程度。 **Attenuation**: 衰减，指信号在传输过程中的强度减弱。
> 
> Sensor attenuation at 100% RH cannot be directly detected because of the limitations of the climatic chamber. The datum at 100% RH is obtained by dripping sufficient water with corresponding temperatures on the sensing zone. A previous test shows that complete immersion of the sensor in water decreases optical power attenuation to −0.12 dBm in 1 min then the attenuation plateaus at −0.008 dBm. Given that the water content of the agarose coating in the experiment reaches its maximum, the results can be regarded as data obtained at 100% RH. Figure 2 indicates that the attenuations at 100% RH and 25 to 34 ◦C are almost the same; i.e., only −0.08 dBm. Sensor attenuation under saturated moisture absorption reaches a minimum value, in which case temperature minimally affects attenuation. 由于气候试验箱的限制，无法直接检测100% RH时的传感器衰减。100% RH的数据是通过在传感区滴加足量相应温度的水获得的。先前的测试表明，将传感器完全浸入水中，光功率衰减在1分钟内降至-0.12 dBm，然后衰减稳定在-0.008 dBm。鉴于实验中琼脂糖涂层的含水量达到最大值，该结果可视为在100% RH下获得的数据。图2表明，在100% RH和25至34°C下，衰减几乎相同；即仅为-0.08 dBm。传感器在饱和吸湿状态下的衰减达到最小值，在这种情况下，温度对衰减的影响最小。

> In conclusion, we propose a method of RH calibration on an optical fiber humidity sensor. The calibrated sensor is used to monitor RH in fresh concrete during its hardening process, in which the resolution ratio of RH is 0.1% at a range form 30% to 100% and the resolution ratio of temperature is 0.1 ◦C at a range from 25 to 34 ◦C. The humidity sensor reported in this letter has a DCSMF structure. The insertion loss of the sensor is only −0.08 dB and its minimum attenuation occurs at 100% RH, which enables convenient sensor calibration. The responses of the sensor to 30%–100% RH at 25–34 ◦C are investigated. Power changes by −17.83 dB at 30%100% RH and 25 ◦C. The absorbability of the agarose coating under moisture decreases with increasing RH in the measured gas. Thus, the effect of temperature on sensor attenuation decreases at high RH. The calibration method proposed in this letter can be used to realize an optical fiber RH sensing system for real-time monitoring. It presents the advantages of simple calibration and low cost, making it of considerable value in practical application. 总之，我们提出了一种对光纤湿度传感器进行RH校准的方法。校准后的传感器用于监测新拌混凝土硬化过程中的RH，其RH分辨率在30%至100%范围内为0.1%，温度分辨率在25至34°C范围内为0.1°C。本快报报道的湿度传感器具有DCSMF结构。该传感器的插入损耗仅为-0.08 dB，其最小衰减发生在100% RH，这便于传感器的校准。研究了传感器在25–34°C下对30%–100% RH的响应。在30%–100% RH和25°C下，功率变化了-17.83 dB。琼脂糖涂层在水分下的吸收能力随被测气体中RH的升高而降低，因此，温度对传感器衰减的影响在高RH下减弱。本快报提出的校准方法可用于实现实时监测的光纤RH传感系统，该方法具有校准简单、成本低的优点，使其在实际应用中具有重要价值。
