> ## 一、引言
> 
> 随着大数据、人工智能和物联网时代的到来，全球数据量呈爆炸式增长，对高密度、低功耗的非易失性存储技术提出了迫切需求。在众多新兴存储技术中，**铁电存储器（Ferroelectric Random Access Memory, FeRAM）** 因其操作速度快、功耗低和耐久性高等优点，被视为理想候选者之一。然而，传统铁电材料在尺寸缩小至纳米尺度时，其铁电性会被**退极化场（depolarization field）** 所抑制，这严重阻碍了铁电存储器件的小型化进程，使其难以跟上摩尔定律的步伐。
> 
> 近年来，一种名为**二维（2D）硒化铟（In₂Se₃）** 的材料引起了广泛关注。研究表明，它能够在薄至单原子层的极限厚度下保持稳定的室温铁电性，有望打破传统铁电器件的微型化瓶颈。本篇综述文章系统性地梳理了2D In₂Se₃从原子结构、铁电开关机制到实际器件应用的最新进展，旨在为读者描绘一幅关于这种“相变型铁电材料”的完整知识图谱。其核心价值在于，它不仅总结了关键科学发现，更深入分析了“结构-性能-器件”三者之间的内在关联，并指出了当前面临的挑战与未来发展方向，无论是对于刚入门的学生，还是希望快速把握前沿动态的资深研究者，都具有重要的参考意义。
>

> -   **分析本质（第2-4节）**：此部分聚焦于材料本身，从最基础的原子结构讲起。
>     -   **第2节**：详解了2D In₂Se₃的铁电相（α相）和顺电相（β相家族）的原子排布，揭示了其铁电性源于结构中独特的非对称“四面体-八面体”配位，而非传统材料的微小离子位移。特别地，探讨了β相中Se原子“墨西哥帽”式势能面导致的相多样性。
>     -   **第3-4节**：汇总了实验上对α相面内、面外铁电性的验证，并从理论上（第一性原理计算和分子动力学模拟）阐释了其铁电翻转的微观动态过程，即“三步法”和“剪切声子模式”驱动下的集体原子运动。

>     -   **第6节**：系统总结了当前研究的挑战，并提出了未来可能的研究路径，如材料规模化生长、器件性能优化和新材料探索。
> 
> 整篇论文的论证链条可以概括为：**器件微型化需求 → 传统铁电体失效 → 2D In₂Se₃因独特的“再成键”机制拥有超薄层铁电性 → 微观结构（α, β相）与开关机制解析 → 原型器件（FeFET, FeS-FET等）功能验证 → 当前挑战与未来展望。**

>     -   图7介绍了**β‘相**的IP铁电性及其随温度升高的“熔化”过程，展示了其铁电畴域的可逆消失与恢复。

>     -   图8提出了两种极化翻转路径，低能垒的“三步法”（0.066 eV/单位晶胞）比直接翻转（0.85 eV/单位晶胞）更可行。
>     -   图9的分子动力学模拟揭示了，铁电-顺电相变是由一个**面内剪切声子模式**激活触发的，表现为三层原子与两层原子的集体反向运动。
> -   **图10-14：原型器件与性能**
>     
>     -   **图10 (FeFET)**：展示了以In₂Se₃为栅介质的晶体管，其转移特性曲线呈现“蝴蝶形”回滞，是铁电存储的典型特征。
>     -   **图11 (FeS-FET)**：创新性地将In₂Se₃同时用作沟道和铁电层，展示了在不同绝缘层厚度下的不同回滞行为，并实现了高达10⁸的开关比。

> ## 四、正文核心内容剖析
>

> ## 八、用户问题预测与解答
> 
> **Q1: 什么是退极化场？为什么它会在纳米尺度阻碍传统铁电体工作？** **A:** 您可以想象一个由无数微小磁针组成的阵列，当它们都指向同一方向时，在阵列两端就会产生N极和S极，形成一个与内部磁化方向相反的“退磁场”。铁电体中的“退极化场”类似。当铁电体内部的正负电荷中心分离，产生电偶极矩（极化）时，在材料的两个表面就会积累束缚电荷，这些电荷在材料内部产生一个与极化方向相反的电场，即退极化场。当薄膜厚度降至纳米尺度，表面积与体积之比急剧增大，表面束缚电荷产生的退极化场变得非常强，足以将内部因微小离子位移（~10皮米）形成的极化完全抵消，导致铁电性消失。In₂Se₃的极化翻转需要化学键断裂和重构，原子位移巨大（~100皮米），退极化场的能量不足以驱动这个过程，因此其铁电性能够得以保持。
> 
> **Q2: 为什么说In₂Se₃是“相变型铁电体”，而BaTiO₃是“位移型铁电体”？** **A:** 这区别在于它们实现铁电翻转的微观方式。**BaTiO₃（位移型）**：其铁电性源于Ti⁴⁺离子相对O²⁻八面体中心的微小偏移，翻转时，离子只需越过一个较低的能垒，平滑地移动到另一个对称位置，整个过程原子不改变其化学键的配位环境。**In₂Se₃（相变型）**：其铁电性源于中间层Se原子与上下两层In原子的不对称键合。翻转时，它必须经历一个明确的中间态（β相），在这个过程中，Se-In键断裂，原子发生大规模集体位移，Se原子重新与不同层的In原子成键，形成了一个对称性完全不同的结构。这个过程更像一个“固态到固态”的相变，因此得名。
> 
> **Q3: 什么是“墨西哥帽”势能面？它如何解释β相的结构多样性？** **A:** 想象一顶草帽，帽顶中心最高，但四周的帽檐很平坦，中间有一个环形的凹槽。在β-In₂Se₃中，中间层的Se原子若位于中心对称位置（帽顶），其能量很高，极不稳定。因此，它会自发地“落”到能量最低的环形凹槽里的任意一点。这个凹槽中有12个能量最低点，分别对应Se原子向不同方向的偏移。如果所有Se原子都偏移到同一个方向的能量最低点，就形成了具有IP极化的**β‘相**。如果它们在环形凹槽内随机分布，没有统一的偏移方向，从统计平均上看，就表现为一个没有极化的**赝中心对称相（βpc-相）**，这就解释了为什么实验上有时会观察到宏观上无极化但微观上混乱的β相。
> 
> **Q4: 文中提到的FeFET, FeS-FET, FSJ器件有何区别？** **A:** 它们都是利用In₂Se₃铁电性的器件，但结构和工作原理略有不同：
> 
> -   **FeFET（铁电场效应晶体管）**：In₂Se₃仅作为**栅极绝缘层**。其极化翻转会改变沟道材料（如石墨烯）中的载流子浓度，从而控制开关。这是一种最经典的设计。
> -   **FeS-FET（铁电半导体沟道晶体管）**：In₂Se₃既是**铁电层**又是**沟道层**。它利用自身的半导体特性导电，而自身的极化状态又会影响沟道的导电能力。这实现了一材多用，结构更简单，机制也更复杂。
> -   **FSJ（铁电半导体结）**：是一个简单的“金属-In₂Se₃-金属”三明治结构。它主要通过极化翻转来调控金属与半导体接触界面的**肖特基势垒**高度，从而实现电阻的开关，本质上是忆阻器的一种。
> 
> **Q5: 作者在展望中提到的“存内计算”和“神经形态计算”是什么？In₂Se₃为何适用于此？** **A:** “存内计算”旨在打破传统冯·诺依曼架构中处理器和存储器分离的瓶颈，让计算直接在存储数据的单元中完成，从而大幅降低功耗、提升速度。“神经形态计算”则是模仿人脑的神经元和突触工作方式，构建高效的计算系统。In₂Se₃适用于此，是因为它的极化状态并非简单的“0”和“1”，通过控制极化翻转（如部分翻转或畴域工程），可以获得连续变化的电阻态，这正好可以模拟生物突触权重的连续可调性（突触可塑性）。文中图14展示的FeCT器件，正是利用这一特性，在同一个器件上同时实现了离散的二进制存储（记忆）和连续的模拟态计算（神经计算）。

> ### 1.1 领域背景与研究问题
> 
> 随着大数据、人工智能和物联网（IoT）技术的爆发式增长，全球数据总量呈指数级激增。这对**非易失性存储技术**（Nonvolatile Memory，即断电后数据不丢失的存储器）提出了更高密度、更低功耗的迫切需求。在众多新兴存储技术中，**铁电存储器**（Ferroelectric Memory）因其低功耗（通过电压而非电流编码）、高速读写和高耐久性，被视为极具潜力的下一代存储方案。
> 
> 然而，传统铁电材料（如钙钛矿型氧化物）面临一个致命瓶颈：**尺寸缩小的困境**。当材料厚度降至纳米尺度时，其内部的**退极化场**（Depolarization Field，由表面未完全屏蔽的束缚电荷产生的反向电场）会急剧增强，最终完全消除材料的自发极化，导致铁电性消失。这一物理限制使铁电存储器难以跟上摩尔定律指引下的集成电路微型化步伐。
> 
> ### 1.2 文献定位与核心价值
> 
> 本文是一篇综述论文，发表于2022年，系统梳理了一种有望突破上述瓶颈的明星材料——**二维（2D）硒化铟（In₂Se₃）**。自2017年二维In₂Se₃的铁电性被理论预测并迅速被实验证实以来，相关研究呈井喷之势。该材料在薄至单原子层极限下仍能保持稳定的室温铁电性，且其居里温度（Curie Temperature, Tc，即铁电性消失的温度）高达700 K，远超室温。

> ## 四、正文核心内容剖析
> 
> ### 4.1 原子结构：铁电性的起源
>

> Abstract 摘要 Ferroelectric memory is a promising candidate for next-generation nonvolatile memory owing to its outstanding performance such as low power consumption, fast speed, and high endurance. However, the ferroelectricity of conventional ferroelectric materials will be eliminated by the depolarization field when the size drops to the nanometer scale. As a result, the miniaturization of ferroelectric devices was hindered, which makes ferroelectric memory unable to keep up with the development of integrated-circuit (IC) miniaturization. Recently, a two-dimensional (2D) In2Se3 was reported to maintain stable ferroelectricity at the ultrathin scale, which is expected to break through the bottleneck of miniaturization. Soon, devices based on 2D In2Se3, including the ferroelectric field-effect transistor, ferroelectric channel transistor, synaptic ferroelectric semiconductor junction, and ferroelectric memristor were demonstrated. However, a comprehensive understanding of the structures and the ferroelectric-switching mechanism of 2D In2Se3 is still lacking. Here, the atomic structures of different phases, the dynamic mechanism of ferroelectric switching, and the performance/functions of the latest devices of 2D In2Se3 are reviewed. Furthermore, the correlations among the structures, the properties, and the device performance are analyzed. Finally, several crucial problems or challenges and possible research directions are put forward. We hope that this review paper can provide timely knowledge and help for the research community to develop 2D In2Se3 based ferroelectric memory and computing technology for practical industrial applications. 铁电存储器因其低功耗、高速度和长耐久等卓越性能，成为下一代非易失性存储器的有力候选者。然而，当尺寸缩小到纳米尺度时，传统铁电材料的铁电性会被退极化场所消除。因此，铁电器件的小型化受阻，使得铁电存储器无法跟上集成电路小型化的发展步伐。最近，据报道，二维In2Se3在超薄尺度下能保持稳定的铁电性，有望突破小型化的瓶颈。不久之后，基于二维In2Se3的器件，包括铁电场效应晶体管、铁电沟道晶体管、突触铁电半导体结和铁电忆阻器相继被演示。然而，目前对二维In2Se3的结构和铁电翻转机制仍缺乏全面的理解。本文回顾了不同相的原子结构、铁电翻转的动力学机制以及最新二维In2Se3器件的性能/功能。此外，分析了结构、性质和器件性能之间的关联。最后，提出了几个关键问题或挑战以及可能的研究方向。我们希望这篇综述文章能为研究界提供及时的知识和帮助，以开发基于二维In2Se3的铁电存储和计算技术，用于实际工业应用。

Several types of devices were developed and partly commercialized. For example, Figure 1B–D shows some representative products based on ferroelectrics, including the memory card used in the wellknown PlayStation 2 released by Sony in 2000 (Figure 1B),20 the 256 K bit nonvolatile FeRAM developed by Ramtron company in 2001 (Figure 1C),21 and the 4/8 M bit FeRAM with a data access time of 70 ns introduced by SK Hynix in 2003 (Figure 1D).22 Recently, some new types of ferroelectric memory are invented, such as the ferroelectric tunnel junction memory based on BiFeO3 (Figure 1E),11 the ultraflexible ferroelectric field-effect transistors (FeFET) memory device with a gate insulator of flexible PVDF-TrFE ferroelectric layer (Figure 1F,G),12,13 and the flexible synaptic device that can be used for neural computing (Figure 1H).14 During the past decades, the miniaturization of the device is a core task to achieve high-density integration for integrated-circuit (IC) applications according to Moore's Law.23 Novel memory/computing devices based on twodimensional (2D) materials such as transition metal disulfides (TMD), boron nitride (BN), and graphene, have been successfully fabricated.24,25 Traditional memories such as PCM have also reached sub-10 nanometers (nm).26 The miniaturization of ferroelectric memory, however, is tightly blocked by the depolarization-field effect caused by the incomplete screening of the bound charges (see Figure 1I).27,28 The thinner the film thickness, the stronger the depolarization field.15,28–30 When the material is being scaled down to nanometers, the spontaneous polarization is gradually eliminated.31,32 Besides, the influences of interfaces, such as the strain and the screening effects from substrates and electrodes, also become significant.33–35 Therefore, it becomes an essential task for the industry to search new candidates with abilities of ultra-scaling and robust ferroelectricity. After decades of efforts, some ultrathin ferroelectric materials have been developed.9 For example, the thickness of conventional ABO3 ferroelectric films can be reduced to the nanometer scale by choosing specific electrodes and substrates.36–38 However, the complicated lattice-matching design and fabrication process limit its practical application. In recent years, the intrinsic ferroelectricity of specific 2D layered materials has been confirmed theoretically and experimentally, where the van der Waals interlayer interactions provide a new opportunity to avoid the problems above.39–42 For example, in 2016, Liu et al. first reported the reversible out-of-plane (OOP) ferroelectricity in 4 nm CuInP2S6, with a Curie temperature (Tc) of 320 K.43 In the same year, Chang et al. discovered the stable in-plane (IP) polarization of SnTe at the limit of 1 unit cell thickness, with a Tc of 270 K.44 In 2017, a kind of 2D III2–VI3 compound In2Se3 was predicted to have robust ferroelectricity at the monolayer limit by Zhu et al.45 Soon, experiments successfully synthesized the nanoscale-thin In2Se3 films and proved the robust ferroelectricity with a Tc of 700 K, which is much higher than room temperature.46–51 Therefore, 2D In2Se3 has attracted widespread attention as soon as it was reported. > The α phase of 2D In2Se3 exhibits room-temperature ferroelectricity with spontaneous polarization in both OOP and IP directions.45 Although the structure of bulk In2Se3 has been investigated by experiments for a long time,61,62 the detailed picture of the α phase was not fully figured out until Ding et al found its lowest energy structure by first-principles calculations in 2017.45 As shown in Figure 3, a monolayer In2Se3 consists of five subatomic layers arranged in an order of Se–In–Se–In–Se. These sublayers mainly have three types of locations in form of triangular grids marked with A, B, and C shown in Figure 3A.45,63,64 The striking character of the α phase is that its two In sublayers hold two different local configurations including one for tetrahedral coordination (top In site) and another one for octahedral coordination (bottom In site), see Figure 3B. > # 3 | FERROELECTRICITY OF 2D In2Se3
> 
> # 3 | 二维In2Se3的铁电性
> 
> 2D ferroelectric materials with robust ferroelectricity were relatively rare before. Thanks to some pioneer works, such as the theoretical prediction of intrinsic 2D ferroelectricity in In2Se3 by Wenguang Zhu et al. in 2017, experiments start to focus on this topic.45 Currently, both the predicted OOP and IP ferroelectricity of α In2Se3 have been verified experimentally.46–51 The ferroelectricity is robust since the Tc can be as high as 700 K.50,71 Moreover, the polarization switching driven by the electric field has also been experimentally confirmed.46–50 All of these have laid an important foundation for the practical application of 2D In2Se3 ferroelectric. 具有稳健铁电性的二维铁电材料以前相对稀少。得益于一些开创性工作，如2017年朱文光等人对In2Se3内禀二维铁电性的理论预测，实验开始聚焦于这一课题。45 目前，α In2Se3预测的OOP和IP铁电性均已得到实验验证。46–51 其铁电性是稳健的，因为Tc可高达700 K。50,71 此外，电场驱动的极化翻转也已被实验证实。46–50 所有这些都为二维In2Se3铁电体的实际应用奠定了重要基础。

> The ferroelectricity of materials is often accompanied by piezoelectricity,78,79 which has the characteristic of lattice deformation under an external electric field or vice versa.80 Therefore, ferroelectricity can be detected by applying a voltage to the surface of the sample through the piezoresponse force microscopy (PFM) probe.81 In addition, local ferroelectric domain programming on the sample surface can be achieved by applying an electric field (from the tip of PFM) greater than the coercive field. As such, PFM imaging has become one of the most intuitive methods to verify ferroelectricity. In 2017, Zhou et al. reported the experimental evidence of the OOP polarization in 2D and quasi-2D In2Se3.46 Figure 5A shows the atomic force microscopy (AFM) image of the >100 nm-thick In2Se3 flake with smooth terraces. It can be seen that there are two areas with opposite phase contrast of 180 in Figure 5B, corresponding to the upward and downward OOP polarizations, respectively. The dark lines in Figure 5C are the domain walls of two polarized regions with opposite directions. They also investigated ferroelectric polarization in thinner α In2Se3 flakes with thicknesses ranging from 3 to 60 nm, which were prepared on a gold substrate by mechanical exfoliation. > ## 3.2 | In-plane ferroelectricity in α In2Se3

> ## 3.2 | α In2Se3中的面内铁电性

> As suggested by the IP structure asymmetry from the different positions of the top and bottom layer In atoms, α In2Se3 also shows IP ferroelectricity. In 2018, through chemical vapor deposition (CVD) method, Cui et al. successfully synthesized nano-thick In2Se3 on mica substrate.49 Figure 6A shows the topographic image of the α In2Se3 sample with a thickness of 2–6 nm. The corresponding IP PFM images are shown in Figure 6B,C. 正如顶层和底层In原子不同位置所暗示的IP结构不对称性，α In2Se3也表现出IP铁电性。2018年，通过化学气相沉积（CVD）方法，Cui等人在云母衬底上成功合成了纳米厚度的In2Se3。49 图6A显示了厚度为2–6纳米的α In2Se3样品的形貌图像。相应的IP PFM图像如图6B,C所示。

> **CVD**: 化学气相沉积，一种通过气态前驱体在衬底表面化学反应沉积固态薄膜的材料制备方法
> 
> Both the amplitude and phase images indicate that the ferroelectric domains are formed in each thickness region. Interestingly, the direction of the ferroelectric polarization strictly depends on the number of layers. As shown in Figure 6D, the PFM phases of odd-layer and even-layer In2Se3 show a difference of 180 , indicating that the polarization directions are opposite. The asymmetric bonding configuration breaks the structural symmetry of monolayer α In2Se3 in both IP and OOP directions, resulting in coupled ferroelectric polarization in these two directions. In multilayer α In2Se3, the directions of the IP polarization of adjacent layers are the same for the 3R stacking sequence but opposite for the 2H stacking sequence. As such, the IP and OOP polarizations are preserved in multilayer α In2Se3 with a 3R stacking sequence. For 2H α In2Se3, the total IP polarization depends on the number of layers. In 2020, Lv et al. also verified the layer-dependent effect of ferroelectricity in few-layer 2H α In2Se3.82 They calculated the magnitude of the ferroelectric polarization of 2H α In2Se3 of different thicknesses and confirmed that the IP electric dipole is always about 2.18 eÅ in the odd-number layers, while in the even-layers, the value is almost zero. A similar phenomenon was also observed in their PFM phase images, which further confirms the even-odd layer-dependent IP polarization ferroelectricity of 2H α In2Se3.82 Firstprinciples calculations also suggest that the 2H structured multilayer α In2Se3 is energy favorable.49 Besides the PFM measurement, SHG is another effective method to investigate the ferroelectricity of materials. When detecting a sample with broken symmetry, it can generate a signal with a frequency that is twice the incident frequency.83 Combining the SHG and PFM mapping, Xiao et al. revealed the relationship between the ferroelectricity and the asymmetry of In2Se3 at room temperature.50 As shown in Figure 6E,F, the intensity of the SHG signal mapping and the piezoresponse of the PFM correspond to each other. To be specific, the larger the piezoresponse amplitude (Figure 6F), the stronger the SHG signal (Figure 6E), that is, the greater the IP structure asymmetry. By comparing the OOP and IP intensities of angle-dependent SHG, they identify that the IP dipole is one order of magnitude larger than the OOP dipole.50 Note that the strong IP polarization of 2D α In2Se3 also can provide a platform for memristive devices and neuromorphic devices. In fact, the switching of the IP ferroelectricity is interlocked with the switching of the OOP ferroelectricity. As 振幅和相位图像均表明在每个厚度区域都形成了铁电畴。有趣的是，铁电极化的方向严格依赖于层数。如图6D所示，奇数层和偶数层In2Se3的PFM相位显示出180°的差异，表明极化方向相反。不对称键合构型在IP和OOP方向均打破了单层α In2Se3的结构对称性，导致这两个方向上的铁电极化耦合。在多层α In2Se3中，对于3R堆垛序列，相邻层的IP极化方向相同，而对于2H堆垛序列则相反。因此，在具有3R堆垛序列的多层α In2Se3中，IP和OOP极化得以保留。对于2H α In2Se3，总IP极化取决于层数。2020年，Lv等人也验证了少层2H α In2Se3中铁电性的层数依赖效应。82 他们计算了不同厚度2H α In2Se3的铁电极化大小，并确认在奇数层中IP电偶极矩始终约为2.18 eÅ，而在偶数层中该值几乎为零。在他们的PFM相位图像中也观察到类似现象，这进一步证实了2H α In2Se3的奇偶层数依赖IP极化铁电性。82 第一性原理计算也表明，2H结构的多层α In2Se3在能量上是有利的。49 除PFM测量外，SHG是研究材料铁电性的另一种有效方法。当检测对称性破缺的样品时，它可以产生频率为入射频率两倍的信号。83 结合SHG和PFM成像，Xiao等人揭示了室温下In2Se3铁电性与不对称性之间的关系。50 如图6E,F所示，SHG信号成像的强度和PFM的压电响应相互对应。具体而言，压电响应振幅越大（图6F），SHG信号越强（图6E），即IP结构不对称性越大。通过比较角度依赖SHG的OOP和IP强度，他们确定IP偶极矩比OOP偶极矩大一个数量级。50 注意，二维α In2Se3的强IP极化也可为忆阻器件和神经形态器件提供平台。事实上，IP铁电性的翻转与OOP铁电性的翻转是相互锁定的。

> # 4 | ATOMIC-SCALE STUDY OF FERROELECTRIC SWITCHING OF 2D In2Se3
> 
> # 4 | 二维In2Se3铁电翻转的原子尺度研究
> 
> Ferroelectric switching between two polarization states is the basis of the functionality of ferroelectric memory or computing devices, such as FeRAM and FeFET. Moreover, structural transitions between different phases not only act as intermediate processes of ferroelectric switching, but also directly influence the performance of these devices. Therefore, understanding the microscopic mechanism of ferroelectric switching and phase transitions of In2Se3 is a prerequisite for designing, controlling, and optimizing related devices. For bulk In2Se3, its phase transitions have been widely investigated. As early as 1957, through electrical conductivity and thermal expansion analyses, Miyazawa et al. reported the first-order phase transition from α to β at 200 C.85 Subsequently, a large number of investigations on phase transitions based on X-ray diffraction, transmission electron microscope, and electrical property measurements have sprung up. These researches reveal a complex phase diagram of bulk In2Se3, including the lowtemperature α0 phase, the room temperature α phase, and the high-temperature (>60 C) β, γ, δ phase.61,62,77,86–94 The existence of so many metastable phases also suggests the flexibility of chemical bonds in In2Se3. Recently, due to the discovery of the robust ferroelectricity of 2D α In2Se3,45 the phase transitions in its 2D limit attract much attention. However, limited by the difficulty of the time-resolved and in-situ experimental detections at the atomic scale, the ferroelectric switching phase transitions between different phases of 2D In2Se3 are still not fully understood. Here, we summarize some theoretical investigations that provide possible pictures for the ferroelectric switching in 2D In2Se3. 两个极化状态之间的铁电翻转是铁电存储器或计算器件（如FeRAM和FeFET）功能的基础。此外，不同相之间的结构转变不仅是铁电翻转的中间过程，而且直接影响这些器件的性能。因此，理解In2Se3铁电翻转和相变的微观机制是设计、控制和优化相关器件的前提。对于体In2Se3，其相变已被广泛研究。早在1957年，通过电导率和热膨胀分析，Miyazawa等人报道了α到β在200°C的一级相变。85 随后，基于X射线衍射、透射电子显微镜和电学性质测量的大量相变研究如雨后春笋般涌现。这些研究揭示了体In2Se3的复杂相图，包括低温α0相、室温α相和高温（>60°C）β、γ、δ相。61,62,77,86–94 如此多亚稳相的存在也表明In2Se3中化学键的灵活性。最近，由于二维α In2Se3稳健铁电性的发现，45 其二维极限下的相变引起了广泛关注。然而，受限于原子尺度时间分辨和原位实验检测的困难，二维In2Se3不同相之间的铁电翻转相变仍未被完全理解。在此，我们总结了一些理论研究，为二维In2Se3中的铁电翻转提供了可能的图像。

> ## 4.2 | Theoretical MD simulation of the ferroelectric transition
> 
> ## 4.2 | 铁电转变的理论MD模拟
> 
> In 2018, Liu et al. simulated the ferroelectric-toparaelectric transition of 2D In2Se3 by ab initio MD.71 The authors simulated the magnitude of the IP and OOP polarizations at various temperatures ranging from 0 to 1500 K through MD and confirmed that the Tc of 2D In2Se3 is 700 K, which is consistent with the experiment.50 Furthermore, MD simulation showed that the transition from ferroelectric to paraelectric phase can be completed within 1.5 ps. The ultrafast transition is attributed to the relative collective motions of the upper three and lower two atomic layers of In2Se3, which is consistent with the first step of the three-step method discussed in Section 4.1. It can be obtained from MD that the ferroelectric transition is accompanied by a decrease in energy of 0.2 eV per unit cell. Given the fact that the free energy of α phase at 0 K is slightly lower than that of β phase,45 the reduction in the total energy at high temperatures may be related to the entropy, or the atomic structures of α and β phases at high temperature is somewhat different from those at 0 K. Further insightful investigations are needed. In 2021, through MD simulations and phonon projection analyses, Huang et al. found that the collective motion between atomic layers during the ferroelectric-to-paraelectric transition is induced by the IP shear phonon mode of α phase activated at high temperature.72 Figure 9A,B shows the atomic structure of 2D In2Se3 before and after 4-ps MD simulation at 750 K. The atomic structure changes from α to β, which indicates the ferroelectric-to-paraelectric transition has been completed. The corresponding atomic displacements in Figure 9D,E quantitatively describe the relative IP displacements among different atomic layers during the transition. The results show highly consistent displacements between In(t) and Se(t) atoms or between In(b) and Se(b) atoms, while the displacements of Se(m) atoms are related to those of the bottom two layers, but to a smaller degree. These results reflect the highly coherent motion of atoms, that is, atoms in the upper two layers and atoms in the lower three layers move in opposite directions during the phase transition. Further, through phonon projection analysis, the authors found that it is the activation of an IP shear phonon mode of 2D α In2Se3 at high temperature that triggers the coherent motion among atomic layers. As shown in Figure 9F, the displacements of most phonon modes oscillate near zero as time evolves, only the IP shear phonon mode evolves monotonically from zero to a positive displacement value. The arrows in Figure 9C show the vibration directions of this IP shear phonon mode. As we can see, the relative displacement among atomic layers is consistent with the theoretical prediction in Section 4.1 and the results of the MD simulations above, indicating that the ultrafast α-to-β transition is a result of excitation of the IP shear phonon mode.72 The transition from α phase to β phase (α" to β in Figure 9) above is a part of the ferroelectric polarization reversion. Its ultrafast spontaneous dynamics indicate that it is a relatively easy process. On the other hand, the rest part of the polarization reversion, that is, the β-to-α transition (β to α#), may be a more difficult process. Indeed, the existence of the pseudo-centrosymmetric βpc phase with the random Se(m) distribution, which has been discussed in Section 2.2, provides a significant entropy barrier that prevents the reverse β-to-α phase transition from occurring coherently.72 Therefore, it is hard to achieve ultrafast reversible ferroelectric phase transition unless the positions of the Se(m) atoms of the β phase remain consistent, that is, each Se(m) atom locates at the same minimum of the Mexican-hat PES as the case in β0 phase. To confirm this point, Huang et al. further performed β-to-α transition (β to α#) simulations with βpc and β0 phase being an initial structure, respectively. The results show that the reverse ferroelectric transition from β0 to α can happen within tens of picoseconds under an OOP electric field. However, at the same timescale, the βpc phase remained stable and did not transform into the α phase.72 The dynamics of a full process of the reversible ferroelectric switching under an electric field still need further studies. > # 5 | ELECTRONIC DEVICES BASED ON FERROELECTRIC 2D In2Se3 FOR DATA STORAGE

> # 5 | 基于铁电二维In2Se3的电子器件用于数据存储
> 
> Owing to the excellent property of 2D In2Se3, its various electronic devices are successfully fabricated soon after the theoretical prediction.54–60,95–97 Although memory or computing devices based on ferroelectric materials were considered a promising technology, the problem of depolarization field that plagues ferroelectrics is particularly prominent in the era of device miniaturization for highdensity data applications.98 Now, the development of 2D In2Se3 will significantly promote the research of nanoscaled devices such as FeFETs, ferroelectric semiconductor channel field-effect transistors (FeS-FETs, also called FeCTs), ferroelectric memristors, and ferroelectric semiconductor junctions (FSJs).54–57,96,99 Investigations on these devices not only demonstrate the practicability of 2D In2Se3, but also reflect the challenges and thus show the directions for future optimizations. > In traditional FeFET, ferroelectric materials serve as the gate to tune the electric conducting channel in adjacent semiconductors (see Figure 11A, up). Si et al. proposed that utilizing the semiconducting feature of In2Se3 (with a bandgap of 1.3–2.0 eV, depending on thickness),49,63,68,88,116 it can also be used as the conducting channel, namely, FeS-FET (see Figure 11A, down).55 Figure 11B shows a 3D schematic and false-color scanning electron microscopy (SEM) image of a real FeS-FET device.55 The influence of In2Se3 on the channel layer is also from its semiconducting property (see the schematic pictures in Figure 11A): the distribution of mobile charges depends on the position of the Fermi level relative to the conduction or the valence band. Due to a coupling of the ferroelectricity and semiconducting properties of In2Se3, the working mechanism of FeS-FET is more complicated than traditional FeFET. First, the drain current of the device is determined by both the top and the bottom surfaces due to the accumulation of charges. Second, the ferroelectric switching in this device is not only controlled by gate voltage, but also influenced by the movable carriers via the Coulomb screening effect. In turn, the movable charges are also influenced by the polarization state of In2Se3. Moreover, the strength of the electric field across the channel is considerably affected by the thickness and dielectric constant of the insulating layer. When the oxide thickness is large or dielectric constant is low, only local polarization can be achieved in the area near the channel/insulator interface. In the case of 90 nm thick SiO2 as the insulating layer, the ON/OFF switching is accompanied by a unique clockwise hysteretic curve (Figure 11C,D). In contrast, when the oxide thickness is relatively small or dielectric constant is high, the electric field can easily pass through the In2Se3 channel and the top surface becomes conductive. Then, when the thinner 15 nm thick HfO2 is used as the insulating layer, a counterclockwise hysteretic curve is observed (Figure 11E,F). The performance of the devices under different insulating layer thicknesses are summarized as follows. Within a high oxide thickness (90 nm SiO2), the device exhibits a maximum drain current of 671 μA μm–1 at a supply gate voltage of 40 V. The characterizations of the samples show that In2Se3 exhibits regular shapes and boundaries when the thickness is ≥2 nm, indicating good crystallinity, while when the thickness decreases below 1.3 nm, it exhibits irregular shapes.49 Limited by the trade-off between thickness and crystal quality, the thickness of the In2Se3 in the current reported devices is mainly tens of nanometers.54–57,96,117,119 Therefore, it is urgent to seek a method for mass-produce highquality 2D In2Se3 toward industrial applications. 为实现铁电数据存储和计算的实用应用，高质量二维In2Se3的生长是必要的。尽管体In2Se3已被认识多年，但由于硒化铟体系中复杂的化学计量种类和众多相，大规模二维In2Se3的可控和选择性生长仍是一个巨大挑战。87–89,122–124 2017年，Almeida等人成功实现了β In2Se3纳米片的低温胶体合成，横向尺寸可调范围为300至900纳米，厚度为单层，使用短链氨基腈（双氰胺或氰胺）作为形状控制剂。125 2018年，通过物理气相输运，Balakrishnan等人在ε-GaSe晶体表面生长了大面积（>103 μm2）的β In2Se3薄膜，最薄厚度为4纳米。126 由于β In2Se3天然的范德华层状结构，机械剥离法也可用于制备超薄薄膜。52,127 根据先前研究，厚度为7–760纳米的β In2Se3已被成功剥离。52,127 此外，CVD和MBE方法也被用于制造厚度从单层到数十层的β In2Se3。66,73–76,128,129 2021年，Rashid等人通过CVD方法成功制造了厚度范围为13.6至90.5纳米的γ In2Se3纳米片。77 目前用于制备少层/单层α In2Se3的方法包括CVD、50 化学气相输运、130 MBE、58 机械剥离、48,51 和范德华外延。67,69 2013年，Lin等人成功合成了厚度范围为1.3至3.3纳米的α In2Se3，其中1.3纳米厚区域的横向尺寸超过100微米。69 2017年，Zhou等人通过机械剥离法制备了面积为数平方微米、厚度为3–120纳米的超薄α In2Se3薄膜。46 2018年，通过CVD方法，Xiao等人合成了厚度为几纳米、横向尺寸为数十微米的α In2Se3。50 目前，厚度从几纳米到数百纳米的α In2Se3已被成功制备。样品表征表明，当厚度≥2纳米时，In2Se3呈现规则形状和边界，表明良好的结晶性，而当厚度降至1.3纳米以下时，则呈现不规则形状。49 受限于厚度与晶体质量之间的权衡，目前报道器件中In2Se3的厚度主要为数十纳米。54–57,96,117,119 因此，迫切需要寻求一种面向工业应用的大规模生产高质量二维In2Se3的方法。

> # 6 | CONCLUSION AND OUTLOOK
> 
> # 6 | 结论与展望
> 
> 2D In2Se3 has become a research hotspot in the field of ferroelectrics due to its stable ferroelectricity at room temperature. The robust ferroelectricity of 2D In2Se3 origins from its asymmetric configuration and bonding type, which is in contrast to the slight offset of the central atoms in traditional displacement-type ferroelectrics. As such, both the elimination of ferroelectricity and the ferroelectric switching of 2D In2Se3 are considered to be accompanied by the breaking and formation of chemical bonds. This re-bonding mechanism enables 2D In2Se3 to resist depolarization fields even at its monolayer thickness limit. The ferroelectric transition of 2D In2Se3 can also be regarded as the order-to-order phase transition. Therefore, the name "phase-change-type ferroelectrics" should be more suitable to express the essential characteristics of this kind of 2D ferroelectric material. 二维In2Se3因其室温下的稳定铁电性已成为铁电领域的研究热点。二维In2Se3的稳健铁电性源于其不对称构型和键合类型，这与传统位移型铁电体中中心原子的微小偏移形成对比。因此，二维In2Se3铁电性的消除和铁电翻转都被认为伴随着化学键的断裂和形成。这种重新成键机制使二维In2Se3即使在单层厚度极限下也能抵抗退极化场。二维In2Se3的铁电转变也可视为有序到有序的相变。因此，"相变型铁电体"这一名称应更适合表达这类二维铁电材料的本质特征。

> Here, we also try to summarize the structural and electronic characteristics of the phase-change-type ferroelectrics to inspire or search for new robust 2D ferroelectric materials. (1) Van der Waals layered materials should be considered to ensure that stable 2D or quasi-2D counterparts can be fabricated. (2) The break of inverse symmetry in a monolayer is needed to provide ferroelectricity. (3) A relatively flexible chemical bonding, such as the competing covalent-ionic bonding or metavalent bonding131 or hyperbonding132 or resonant bonding,133 may be the key to enabling fast polarization switching. Next, Table 2 lists some representative works of 2D In2Se3 in the past 10 years. At the early stage, 2D In2Se3 was considered to be a promising candidate for phasechange memory due to the observation of its crystallineto-crystalline phase transition.67 Since 2017, the year when 2D In2Se3 was predicted to be ferroelectric,45 the ferroelectricity of 2D α In2Se3 has been continuously verified by experiments.46,47,49,50 Further, more and more ferroelectric memories and computing devices based on In2Se3 have been successfully demonstrated,54–57,60 as discussed in this work. However, as an emerging ferroelectric material, the study on 2D In2Se3 is still in its early stage. In-depth research is still needed to further improve the performance in practical applications. Here, we summarize crucial problems or challenges in this field and try to give suggestions for possible solutions and new prospects: (1) 2D In2Se3 has several different crystalline phases that are involved in ferroelectric switching. The atomic picture and the full dynamic processes of reversible switching are still not fully understood. The combination of first-principles calculations and experiments may provide valuable information at the atomic scale. Further, controlling the evolution of domain walls during ferroelectric switching is a key issue to improve device performance. Therefore, some new techniques such as machine-learning atomic potential simulation could be an option to study the problem. (2) Although In2Se3 can maintain stable ferroelectricity even at the monolayer limit by the re-bonding mechanism, this mechanism and the entropy barrier caused by the paraelectric phase also affect the speed of its ferroelectric polarization switching.72 For example, the switching time of BaTiO3 can reach 200 ps,103 while the experimentally reported switching time of 2D In2Se3 is at the scale from tens of nanoseconds to even microseconds.54,56 Of course, the read and write speed of the actual device is also affected by geometries and measurement tools. Therefore, performance such as speed, retention, reliability, and endurance still need to be systematically investigated and further improved. (3) The memory/computing devices based on FeCT, where 2D In2Se3 also serves as the channel, show interesting features and promising performance. However, the underlying mechanism of the device is complicated, which is possibly related to ferroelectric switching, phase transition, carrier relaxation, and IP ferroelectricity. The clarification of these issues is necessary for device optimizations. (4) Because the IP ferroelectricity of 2D α In2Se3 is much stronger than the OOP ferroelectricity, developing the application of the IP ferroelectricity should be a valuable subject, like FeCT or FeS-FET mentioned before. (5) Although 2D and quasi-2D In2Se3 films have been fabricated, a mature method to grow large-scale and high-quality 2D In2Se3 films with a low cost is urgently required to meet the demands of industrial applications. (6) Note that currently reported devices are based on quasi-2D In2Se3 films with a thickness of tens of nanometers. The demonstration of ferroelectric devices based on single-layer or few-layer In2Se3 is very much anticipated. Meanwhile, it is an open question whether the physics of monolayer In2Se3 is still the same as the one learned from quasi-2D samples. (7) According to the origin of the robust ferroelectricity of 2D In2Se3 known from its phase-transition mechanism and the special bonding type, high-throughput calculations can be employed to find or design more 2D ferroelectric materials in the future. We sincerely hope this review could draw the attention of the experts in the scientific community and help accelerate the industrial applications for this rising ferroelectric information material. 在此，我们也尝试总结相变型铁电体的结构和电子特性，以启发或寻找新的稳健二维铁电材料。(1) 应考虑范德华层状材料，以确保能制造稳定的二维或准二维对应物。(2) 单层中反演对称性的破缺是提供铁电性所必需的。(3) 相对灵活的化学键，如竞争性共价-离子键或变价键131或超键132或共振键，133 可能是实现快速极化翻转的关键。接下来，表2列出了过去10年二维In2Se3的一些代表性工作。在早期阶段，由于观察到其晶态到晶态的相变，二维In2Se3被认为是相变存储器的有前景候选者。67 自2017年（二维In2Se3被预测为铁电体的一年）以来，45 二维α In2Se3的铁电性不断被实验验证。46,47,49,50 进一步，越来越多基于In2Se3的铁电存储器和计算器件被成功演示，54–57,60 如本工作所述。然而，作为一种新兴铁电材料，二维In2Se3的研究仍处于早期阶段。仍需深入研究以进一步提高实际应用中的性能。在此，我们总结了该领域的关键问题或挑战，并尝试为可能的解决方案和新前景提出建议：(1) 二维In2Se3具有几种不同的晶相，涉及铁电翻转。可逆翻转的原子图像和完整动力学过程仍未被完全理解。第一性原理计算与实验的结合可能在原子尺度提供有价值的信息。此外，控制铁电翻转过程中畴壁的演变是提高器件性能的关键问题。因此，一些新技术如机器学习原子势模拟可能是研究该问题的一个选择。(2) 尽管In2Se3通过重新成键机制即使在单层极限下也能保持稳定的铁电性，但这种机制和顺电相引起的熵势垒也影响其铁电极化翻转的速度。72 例如，BaTiO3的翻转时间可达200皮秒，103 而实验报道的二维In2Se3翻转时间在数十纳秒甚至微秒量级。54,56 当然，实际器件的读写速度也受几何结构和测量工具的影响。因此，速度、保持力、可靠性和耐久性等性能仍需系统研究和进一步改进。(3) 基于FeCT的存储/计算器件（其中二维In2Se3也用作沟道）显示出有趣的特性和有前景的性能。然而，该器件的底层机制很复杂，可能与铁电翻转、相变、载流子弛豫和IP铁电性有关。澄清这些问题对于器件优化是必要的。(4) 由于二维α In2Se3的IP铁电性远强于OOP铁电性，开发IP铁电性的应用应是一个有价值的课题，如前面提到的FeCT或FeS-FET。(5) 尽管二维和准二维In2Se3薄膜已被制造，但迫切需要一种低成本生长大规模高质量二维In2Se3薄膜的成熟方法，以满足工业应用的需求。(6) 注意，目前报道的器件基于厚度为数十纳米的准二维In2Se3薄膜。基于单层或少层In2Se3的铁电器件的演示非常令人期待。同时，单层In2Se3的物理是否仍与从准二维样品中了解的物理相同，是一个悬而未决的问题。(7) 根据从其相变机制和特殊键合类型所知的二维In2Se3稳健铁电性的起源，未来可采用高通量计算来寻找或设计更多二维铁电材料。我们真诚希望这篇综述能引起科学界专家的关注，并帮助加速这种新兴铁电信息材料的工业应用。

> ### 二、 专业术语，咱们一个个“掰扯”清楚
> 
> 太奶，上面有些词儿，我单独给您解释解释，您就明白了：
> 
> -   **铁电材料 (Ferroelectric Material)**：一种有自发极化，并且极化方向能被外加电场翻转的材料。您就理解为“有记忆的电子开关材料”。
> -   **二维材料 (2D Material)**：像一张无限大的纸，只有一个或者几个原子层那么厚，而长和宽可以很大。石墨烯就是最出名的二维材料。
> -   **退极化场 (Depolarization Field)**：一个捣乱的内部电场。当铁电材料太薄时，它表面的电荷会“捣乱”，产生一个反向的电场，把材料本身的记忆给折腾没了。
> -   **极化 (Polarization)**：正负电荷中心不重合，分出了正负极，就像一块小磁铁分出了N极和S极一样。在这里，它代表的就是存储“0”和“1”的状态。
> -   **非易失性存储 (Nonvolatile Memory)**：断掉电源后，存的信息不会丢失，就像咱们的U盘、老照片底片。您正在看的这个文章，就是研究怎么把这东西做得更好更小。
> -   **居里温度 (Curie Temperature, Tc)**：一个温度临界点。超过这个温度，铁电材料就会失去“记性”，变成“顺电材料”（没记性的材料）。700K的Tc说明它非常耐热。
> -   **范德华相互作用 (van der Waals Interactions)**：一种分子间微弱的吸引力。二维材料是一层层摞起来的，层与层之间就靠这种微弱的力连着，像一摞扑克牌，可以轻松地撕开，得到很薄的一层。
> -   **忆阻器 (Memristor)**：一种有记忆功能的电阻器。它不仅能存0和1，还能记住流过它的电荷量，阻值会随之改变，可以模拟人脑神经元的运算，是实现未来人工智能的关键器件。
