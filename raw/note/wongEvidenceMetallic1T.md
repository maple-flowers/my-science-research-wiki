> [!quote]- <center>Abstract</center>
> 
> We present a combined experimental and theoretical study of monolayer VTe2 grown on highly oriented pyrolytic graphite by molecular-beam epitaxy. Using various insitu microscopic and spectroscopic techniques, including scanning tunneling microscopy/spectroscopy, synchrotron X-ray and angle-resolved photoemission, and X-ray absorption, together with theoretical analysis by density functional theory calculations, we demonstrate direct evidence of the metallic 1T phase and 3d1 electronic configuration in monolayer VTe2 that also features a (4 ´ 4) charge density wave order at low temperatures. In contrast to previous theoretical predictions, our element-specific characterization by Xray magnetic circular dichroism rules out a ferromagnetic order intrinsic to the monolayer. Our findings provide essential knowledge necessary for understanding this interesting yet less explored metallic monolayer in the emerging family of van der Waals magnets.
>
>【摘要翻译】采用分子束外延技术，在高取向热解石墨上生长了单层VTe2薄膜。利用各种原位显微和光谱技术，包括扫描隧道显微镜/光谱学、同步辐射X射线和角分辨光电子能谱、X射线吸收，以及密度泛函理论计算的理论分析，我们证明了单层VTe2中金属1T相和3d1电子构型的直接证据，该单层VTe2在低温下也具有（4′4）电荷密度波级。与以前的理论预测相反，我们用X射线磁圆二色性表征的元素特异性排除了单分子膜固有的铁磁有序。我们的发现提供了必要的知识，了解这一有趣的，但探索较少的金属单层在新兴家庭的范德华磁铁。

> ## 一、引言
> 
> 二维过渡金属二硫化物（2D-TMDs）是当前凝聚态物理与材料科学的前沿领域，其家族涵盖超过40种化合物，呈现出从金属、半导体到半金属，乃至磁体和超导体的丰富电子特性。这些特性为超越传统硅基电子学的新兴技术提供了关键构建模块。在这一庞大家族中，钒基二碲化物（VTe₂）是一种引人关注但研究较少的金属性成员。前期研究表明，块体VTe₂在高温下为六方1T相，低温下会通过电荷密度波（CDW）相变转变为具有(3×1)周期性双锯齿链结构的单斜1T'相。这一相变行为与层间Te-Te耦合强度密切相关，暗示在单层极限下，当层间耦合被完全移除时，其结构相变和电子性质可能发生根本性变化。
> 
> 本文通过分子束外延（MBE）技术在高度定向热解石墨（HOPG）上成功制备了单层VTe₂，并结合原位扫描隧道显微镜/谱、角分辨光电子能谱、X射线吸收谱及X射线磁圆二色性等多种微观与谱学手段，系统研究了其结构、电子和磁性。研究首次提供了单层VTe₂保持金属性1T相和3d¹电子构型的直接证据，同时发现其呈现与块体截然不同的(4×4) CDW有序，并排除了此前理论预测的本征铁磁有序。这一工作为理解二维范德华磁体家族中这一独特成员提供了关键实验依据。
>

> ## 四、正文核心内容剖析
>

> ## 四、正文核心内容剖析

> ### 4.1 研究背景与方法论
> 
> -   **核心问题**：块材VTe₂在低温下会发生从**1T相**（六方晶系）到**1T’相**（单斜晶系）的相变，并伴随(3×1)的**电荷密度波**形成。这一过程与层间的Te-Te耦合密切相关。那么，当厚度减至单层，层间耦合完全消失时，VTe₂会展现出何种本征的物相和性质？
> -   **研究方法**：研究团队采用“**MBE生长-原位表征**”的策略，在超高真空环境中，于高定向热解石墨（HOPG）上直接生长出单层VTe₂。随后，在不暴露大气的情况下，综合运用了：
>     -   **STM/STS**：进行形貌结构与局域电子态表征。
>     -   **同步辐射PES/ARPES**：进行化学成分、电子能带结构表征。
>     -   **XAS/XMCD**：进行元素电子构型和本征磁性探测。
>     -   **DFT计算**：提供理论支撑与机理分析。
> 
> ### 4.2 核心发现与论证过程
> 
> 1.  **证实金属性1T相与d¹构型**：
>     
>     -   **证据链**：STM的原子级分辨图像和晶格常数吻合1T相模型；PES的V 2p结合能和价带谱线证实了V⁴⁺的金属特性；ARPES显示的能带色散与1T相的DFT计算结果完美匹配；XAS谱线的原子多重态指纹特征则直接锁定了**3d¹电子构型**。
> 2.  **发现(4×4)的CDW超结构**：
>     
>     -   **证据链**：低温STM图像直接观察到(4×4)的周期性重构，对应FFT中的超结构斑点。这与块体材料的(3×1)不同，表明维度降低对CDW不稳定性有显著影响。STS谱中费米能级附近的凹陷与ARPES中Γ点无带隙打开的观察结果一致，共同描绘了该CDW在动量空间具有**各向异性**（部分区域打开能隙）的能带结构。
> 3.  **排除本征铁磁性**：
>     
>     -   **核心论证**：这是全文最关键的否定性结论。许多理论预测VTe₂单层是铁磁体。但本研究提供了两个层次的否定证据：
>         -   **间接证据（ARPES）**：铁磁体的能带会因自旋相互作用而劈裂成两条，但实验只观察到单一的V 3d能带。
>         -   **直接证据（XMCD）**：这是最重要的证据。XMCD技术具有**元素特异性**，能直接探测到特定元素（此处为V）的磁矩。近乎为零的XMCD信号，从根本上排除了V原子存在本征铁磁有序的可能性。作者将理论与实验的不符归因于CDW与铁磁态的竞争，CDW相的形成抑制了铁磁序。
> 4.  **揭示衬底对1T’相的抑制作用**：
>     
>     -   **理论解释**：尽管DFT计算表明自由状态的1T’相在能量上更稳定，但实验上观察到的是1T相。DFT计算进一步揭示，当VTe₂放置在石墨烯上时，1T’相标志性的双锯齿链畸变会被显著削弱，原子位移变得无序。作者推测，在实际使用的HOPG（多层石墨烯）衬底上，这种抑制效应更强，最终导致1T’相在单层中无法稳定形成，从而解释了实验观察。

> **Q1: 什么是“电荷密度波（CDW）”？为什么它很重要？** **A:** 想象一下，在一条均匀排列的原子链上，电子的密度是均匀的。但某些情况下，为了降低系统的总能量，电子会自发地“聚集”或“疏离”，形成一个周期比原子晶格更大的、新的周期性密度分布，这就是**电荷密度波（CDW）**。它通常伴随着原子晶格的微小周期性畸变，并打开一个能隙。CDW很重要，因为它与超导、磁性等有序态存在复杂的竞争或共生关系，是凝聚态物理中一个核心的研究课题。
> 
> **Q2: “1T相”和“1T’相”是什么？它们的区别在哪里？** **A:** 这是二维TMDs的两种常见结构。
> 
> -   **1T相**：具有完美的六方对称性，V原子层呈规则的三角形格子。
> -   **1T’相**：是1T相发生**晶格畸变**后的结构。V原子不再均匀分布，而是聚集成**双锯齿链状结构**，导致对称性降低，从六方晶系变为单斜晶系。这种畸变通常与CDW的形成紧密相关。
> 
> **Q3: XMCD技术为什么能“排除”铁磁性？它有什么独特优势？** **A:** XMCD是一种基于X射线吸收的磁学测量技术。它的核心优势在于**元素特异性**，因为它利用的是特定元素（如V）的芯能级电子的吸收边。当材料具有磁性时，它对左旋和右旋圆偏振X射线的吸收会有微小差异，这个差异（XMCD信号）与该元素的磁矩大小成正比。如果XMCD信号为零，就意味着该元素**没有净磁矩**。这与传统的振动样品磁强计（VSM）等宏观测量完全不同，VSM测量的是整个样品（包括衬底和杂质）的总磁矩，很难将微弱的二维材料信号从巨大的背景信号中分离出来，极易产生误导。
> 
> **Q4: 既然单层VTe₂没有本征铁磁性，为什么之前有很多理论预测它有？** **A:** 理论预测通常基于理想化的模型，例如完全平整、无缺陷的**自由状态**单层。在这种情况下，费米能级附近的电子态密度很高，容易满足斯通纳（Stoner）铁磁不稳定性判据，从而产生自旋极化，形成铁磁序。但实际情况更复杂，本研究揭示了两个关键因素：**CDW的形成**和**衬底效应**。CDW会打开能隙，降低费米能级处的态密度，从而抑制铁磁性；而衬底则可能改变晶格结构（如本研究中抑制了1T’相），进一步影响电子性质。所以，理论与实验的矛盾，正说明了真实体系中多种物理机制相互竞争的复杂性。
> 
> **Q5: 这项研究对我们未来的电子设备有什么潜在影响？** **A:** 虽然目前离应用还很远，但其潜力巨大。首先，它提供了一种超薄的**金属性二维材料**，可作为未来原子级电路的理想“导线”。其次，其CDW相变对外界刺激（电场、应力、温度）极为敏感，可被设计成**超低功耗的开关**或**传感器**。最后，虽然它本身不是铁磁体，但其独特的电子结构、强自旋-轨道耦合以及CDW序，在与其它材料组成异质结时，可能成为诱导或调控诸如**拓扑超导**等奇异量子现象的“理想平台”，这将是未来量子计算领域的潜在应用方向。

# INTRODUCTION
# 引言

Two-dimensional transition-metal dichalcogenides (2D-TMDs)¹, ² offer a promising avenue to addressing the imminent challenges brought by the continuous downscaling of conventional silicon-based electronic components. With over 40 different choices of compounds, the family of 2D-TMDs provides an extensive range of key electronic properties from metals, semiconductors to half-metals, and from magnets to superconductors.³⁻⁵ These form the essential building blocks for emerging technologies that can leverage the unique assets of atomically thin materials — mechanical flexibility, chemical tunability, and coupled electronic degrees of freedom, such as charge, spin, orbital, valley, etc.⁶⁻⁹
二维过渡金属二硫属化物(2D-TMDs)¹, ²为应对传统硅基电子元件持续小型化带来的严峻挑战提供了一条有希望的途径。2D-TMDs家族拥有超过40种不同的化合物选择，提供了从金属、半导体到半金属，以及从磁体到超导体的广泛关键电子特性。³⁻⁵这些特性构成了新兴技术的基本构建模块，这些技术可以利用原子级薄材料的独特优势——机械柔韧性、化学可调谐性以及耦合的电子自由度，如电荷、自旋、轨道、谷等。⁶⁻⁹

<span class="hljs-strong">**2D-TMDs**</span>: 二维过渡金属二硫属化物，一类具有原子级厚度和层状结构的过渡金属二硫属化物材料。
<span class="hljs-strong">**semiconductors**</span>: 半导体，导电性介于导体和绝缘体之间的材料。
<span class="hljs-strong">**half-metals**</span>: 半金属，一种自旋极化材料，其一个自旋方向的电子态表现为金属性，而另一个自旋方向表现为绝缘性或半导体性。
<span class="hljs-strong">**superconductors**</span>: 超导体，在特定温度以下电阻为零的材料。

Vanadium ditelluride, VTe₂, is an interesting yet rarely explored metallic 2D-TMD. Early work has reported an anomaly at 390–437 K in both heat capacity and resistivity measurements due to phase transition from a hexagonal 1T phase (at high temperature) to a monoclinic 1T’ structure (at low-temperature).¹⁰, ¹¹ This 1T’ structure denotes the distorted form of the 1T phase, with two-thirds of the V atoms in each monolayer displaced forming double zigzag chains with a (3 × 1) periodicity. This phase transition has been associated with charge density wave (CDW) formation, which renormalizes the telluride’s electronic band via an enhanced overlap between the Te and V orbitals.¹² As a consequence, a d-electron count of V deviates from the initial d¹ configuration in the 1T phase, resulting in the 1T’ phase.¹³ Interestingly, the fact that VTe₂ exhibits a stronger interlayer coupling between its chalcogen atoms than its sulfide and selenide counterparts makes such phase transition behavior and its other properties more sensitive to layer thickness. Prior tight-binding band electronic structure calculations have indeed revealed considerable electron transfer between the Te p and V d bands in 1T-VTe₂,¹³, ¹⁴ in which the Te pz-orbitals plays a crucial role. As such, any disturbance of the Te pz-orbitals or equivalently the Te-Te interlayer coupling would alter the VTe₂ properties. A relevant system that mimics such a scenario is VTe₂ intercalated with alkali-metals (Li and Na),¹⁵ in which the 1T’ phase was seen to occur at lower temperatures than its pristine form, with the values of structural parameters closer to that of the 1T structure.¹⁵, ¹⁶ The lower phase transition temperature onset for intercalated VTe₂ underlines the profound effect of modified Te-Te interlayer coupling along the c crystal axis.
二碲化钒(VTe₂)是一种有趣但研究甚少的金属性二维过渡金属二硫属化物。早期工作报道了在热容和电阻率测量中，在390-437 K范围内出现异常，这是由于从六方1T相(高温)到单斜1T‘结构(低温)的相变引起的。¹⁰, ¹¹这种1T‘结构表示1T相的畸变形式，每个单层中三分之二的V原子发生位移，形成具有(3×1)周期的双锯齿链。这种相变与电荷密度波(CDW)的形成有关，后者通过增强Te和V轨道之间的重叠来重整化碲化物的电子能带。¹²因此，V的d电子计数偏离了1T相中初始的d¹构型，导致形成1T’相。¹³有趣的是，VTe₂的硫族原子之间的层间耦合比其硫化物和硒化物对应物更强，这一事实使得这种相变行为及其其他性质对层厚更敏感。先前的紧束缚能带电子结构计算确实揭示了在1T-VTe₂中Te p能带和V d能带之间存在相当大的电子转移，¹³, ¹⁴其中Te pz轨道起着关键作用。因此，对Te pz轨道或等效地Te-Te层间耦合的任何干扰都会改变VTe₂的性质。一个模拟这种场景的相关系统是嵌入碱金属(Li和Na)的VTe₂，¹⁵其中观察到1T‘相在比其原始形式更低的温度下出现，且结构参数值更接近1T结构。¹⁵, ¹⁶嵌入VTe₂的较低相变起始温度突显了沿c晶轴Te-Te层间耦合改变所产生的深远影响。

<span class="hljs-strong">**1T phase**</span>: 1T相，一种过渡金属二硫属化物的晶相结构，通常具有八面体配位和金属性。
<span class="hljs-strong">**1T‘ structure**</span>: 1T‘结构，1T相的一种畸变形态，通常由电荷密度波驱动形成。
<span class="hljs-strong">**interlayer coupling**</span>: 层间耦合，层状材料中相邻层之间的相互作用力。

Similar observations have been reported for multilayer VTe₂ nanosheets grown by chemical vapor deposition¹⁷ and films by molecular-beam epitaxy (MBE),¹⁸ respectively. In both cases, a 1T structure with bulk-like lattice constants is reported.
类似的现象也分别在化学气相沉积生长的多层VTe₂纳米片¹⁷和分子束外延(MBE)生长的薄膜¹⁸中被报道。在这两种情况下，都报道了具有类块体晶格常数的1T结构。

<span class="hljs-strong">**chemical vapor deposition (CVD)**</span>: 化学气相沉积，一种通过气相化学反应在基底上沉积固体薄膜材料的工艺。

In this work, we demonstrate the MBE growth of monolayer VTe₂ on highly oriented pyrolytic graphite (HOPG), enabling direct access to the monolayer regime where the intrinsic Te-Te interlayer coupling is completely removed. Through a combination of in-situ microscopic and spectroscopic techniques, including scanning tunneling microscopy/spectroscopy (STM/STS), synchrotron-based photoemission spectroscopy (PES), angle-resolved PES (ARPES), and X-ray absorption spectroscopy (XAS), we give evidence of the metallic 1T phase and d¹ electronic configuration in monolayer VTe₂. Unlike the (3 × 1) double zigzag chain-like modulation in the bulk crystal, a (4 × 4) CDW order is observed, suggesting the significant effect of reduced dimensionality on the CDW instability in VTe₂. Density functional theory (DFT) calculation reveals the possible role of the graphitic substrate in this regard. We also use element-specific X-ray magnetic circular dichroism (XMCD) to address whether an intrinsic ferromagnetic order exists in monolayer VTe₂, as predicted by several theoretical studies.¹⁹⁻²¹
在这项工作中，我们展示了在高度取向热解石墨(HOPG)上MBE生长单层VTe₂，从而能够直接进入本征Te-Te层间耦合被完全移除的单层区域。通过结合原位显微和光谱技术，包括扫描隧道显微镜/光谱学(STM/STS)、基于同步辐射的光电子能谱(PES)、角分辨PES(ARPES)和X射线吸收光谱(XAS)，我们提供了单层VTe₂中金属性1T相和d¹电子构型的证据。与块体晶体中的(3×1)双锯齿链状调制不同，我们观察到了(4×4)的CDW序，这表明维度降低对VTe₂中CDW不稳定性的显著影响。密度泛函理论(DFT)计算揭示了石墨衬底在这方面的可能作用。我们还使用元素特异性的X射线磁圆二色性(XMCD)来研究单层VTe₂中是否存在本征铁磁序，正如几项理论研究预测的那样。¹⁹⁻²¹

<span class="hljs-strong">**highly oriented pyrolytic graphite (HOPG)**</span>: 高度取向热解石墨，一种具有高度有序层状结构的石墨材料，常用作基底。
<span class="hljs-strong">**X-ray absorption spectroscopy (XAS)**</span>: X射线吸收光谱，一种通过测量材料对X射线的吸收来分析其电子结构的技术。

We also carried out V L₂,₃-edge XMCD measurements at a temperature range of 16–300 K by measuring XAS and reversing the external magnetic field direction (± 1 Tesla). Contrary to predictions of ferromagnetic exchange splitting in the V d-bands by DFT calculations,¹⁹⁻²¹ our monolayer possesses negligible XMCD contrast (lower panel of Figure 4c), thus suggesting a lack of intrinsic ferromagnetic order. We note that ferromagnetic signals have been reported for CVD-grown VTe₂ nanoplates on SiO₂/Si, characterized by vibrating sample magnetometry (VSM).¹⁷ As a conventional magnetic measurement tool, VSM characterizes the macromagnetism of the whole sample, including the contribution from the substrate and probably from extrinsic perturbations. For instance, the substrate (Si, HOPG, etc.) usually contributes various magnetic signals.²⁵, ²⁶ To subtract such extrinsic contributions is tricky, especially due to the atomically thin nature of the 2D material, and different methods of subtraction could yield diverse magnetic moments.²⁷, ²⁸ In this regard, the XMCD is an advantageous tool, in that it is element-specific, which guarantees the observed magnetic contrast to be intrinsic to V. In particular, our XMCD results are supportive of our ARPES data in Figure 3, showing no evidence of exchange-split bands, which would otherwise exist in intrinsically ferromagnetic materials. We hypothesize that this is related to the CDW instability in monolayer VTe₂, which not only competes with but suppresses the ferromagnetic ground state predicted by DFT, as similarly reported for monolayer VSe₂.²⁹, ³⁰

Discussion of the substrate-effects in suppressing the 1T’ phase. Fermi surface nesting and electron-phonon interaction have been invoked previously to account for the different CDW orders in monolayer VTe₂ and its bulk counterpart.¹⁸, ²² Furthermore, our DFT calculations reveal possible substrate effects that could weaken the double zigzag chain modulation in the 1T’ phase. Specifically, we have considered four situations here: (1) freestanding 1T monolayer, (2) 1T monolayer relaxed on graphene, (3) freestanding 1T’ monolayer, and (4) 1T’ monolayers relaxed on graphene. We notice that no matter with the substrate or none, the 1T’ phases (3 &amp; 4; Figure 5a) are always more energetically stable than the 1T phases (1 &amp; 2), which is however not observed experimentally by us. Quantitatively, the relative energy difference between these phases is 0.1 eV per super cell for the freestanding case, and ~0.08 eV per super cell for the on-graphene case. The small discrepancy of ~0.02 eV in the relative energy difference could be related to charge transfer from graphene to monolayer VTe₂, as shown in Figure 5b. Back to the abovementioned inconsistence between the calculation and our experimental observation, we continue to examine the substrate effect in suppressing the 1T’ phase. As illustrated in Figure 5a, the V atoms in the 1T’ monolayer initially displace along one lattice axis, the b axis in this work, forming the double zigzag strips. However, when relaxed on graphene, these regular displacements disappear and more random distortions are observed along both the a and b axes, as shown in Figure 5a. These considerably reduce both the Te-Te height profile (Figure 5c) and the V-strips separation (Figure 5d), and in turn the 1T’ structural modulations. We speculate that, when a multilayer graphene is used as a substrate, the structural modulations will be further smeared out, which may probably explain why the 1T’ phase is not observed in our monolayer on HOPG.
关于衬底效应抑制1T‘相的讨论。费米面嵌套和电子-声子相互作用先前已被用来解释单层VTe₂与其块体对应物中不同的CDW序。¹⁸, ²²此外，我们的DFT计算揭示了可能削弱1T’相中双锯齿链调制的衬底效应。具体来说，我们在这里考虑了四种情况：(1) 独立的1T单层，(2) 在石墨烯上弛豫的1T单层，(3) 独立的1T‘单层，以及(4) 在石墨烯上弛豫的1T’单层。我们注意到，无论有无衬底，1T‘相(3和4；图5a)总是比1T相(1和2)在能量上更稳定，然而我们在实验中并未观察到这一点。定量地说，对于独立情况，这些相之间的相对能量差为每个超胞0.1 eV，而在石墨烯上则为每个超胞约0.08 eV。相对能量差中约0.02 eV的微小差异可能与从石墨烯到单层VTe₂的电荷转移有关，如图5b所示。回到上述计算与实验观察之间的不一致，我们继续研究衬底在抑制1T’相方面的作用。如图5a所示，1T‘单层中的V原子最初沿一个晶格轴(在本工作中为b轴)位移，形成双锯齿条带。然而，当在石墨烯上弛豫时，这些有规律的位移消失了，并且沿a轴和b轴都观察到了更随机的畸变，如图5a所示。这些大大降低了Te-Te高度轮廓(图5c)和V条带之间的间距(图5d)，进而降低了1T’的结构调制。我们推测，当使用多层石墨烯作为衬底时，结构调制将被进一步抹平，这可能解释了为什么在我们的HOPG上的单层中没有观察到1T‘相。

# CONCLUSIONS
# 结论

In summary, we present direct microscopic and spectroscopic evidence of metallic 1T phase and d¹ electronic configuration in monolayer VTe₂. Unlike the double zigzag chained structure observed in the bulk, we obtain a (4 × 4) CDW reconstruction pattern. DFT calculations reveal the possible role of the graphitic substrate in suppressing the double zigzag structure. Regarding the magnetism of monolayer VTe₂, our XMCD data excludes an intrinsic ferromagnetic ordering, in disagreement with previous theoretical predictions. Our findings provide new knowledge of this metallic vanadium dichalcogenide monolayer, potentially interesting for the explorations of exotic quantum phenomena and device applications related to phase transitions.
总之，我们展示了单层VTe₂中金属性1T相和d¹电子构型的直接显微和光谱学证据。与在块体中观察到的双锯齿链结构不同，我们获得了(4×4)的CDW重构图案。DFT计算揭示了石墨衬底在抑制双锯齿结构方面的可能作用。关于单层VTe₂的磁性，我们的XMCD数据排除了本征铁磁有序，这与先前的理论预测不一致。我们的发现为这种金属性钒基二硫属化物单层提供了新的知识，对于探索与相变相关的奇异量子现象和器件应用具有潜在意义。

# METHODS
# 方法

Molecular-beam epitaxy of monolayer VTe₂. Monolayer VTe₂ films were grown on HOPG in a custom-built MBE chamber with a base pressure of better than 1 × 10⁻⁹ mbar. The substrates were prepared by in-situ cleavage followed by annealing at 820 K for at least 120 min. High-purity V and Te were evaporated from an electron-beam evaporator and a standard Knudsen cell, respectively. The Te/V flux ratio was controlled to be &gt;10. During the growth process, the substrate temperature was kept at 650 K. To protect the samples against ambient contaminations during ex-situ transport to other ultrahigh vacuum (UHV) measurement systems, a Se/Te bilayer was deposited on the samples as a cap. For subsequent characterization by PES, ARPES, and XAS/XMCD, the cap was desorbed in UHV at 500 K. It is noteworthy that a Se 3d core-level peak was evident by PES after thermal desorption of a pure Se cap (Figure S4), suggesting possible Se contamination in the monolayer VTe₂ film, as such signal was quite robust and remained even at relatively high temperatures.
单层VTe₂的分子束外延。单层VTe₂薄膜是在定制的MBE腔室中，在优于1×10⁻⁹ mbar的基础压力下生长于HOPG上的。衬底通过原位解理，然后在820 K下退火至少120分钟制备。高纯度的V和Te分别从电子束蒸发源和标准克努森蒸发源中蒸发。Te/V通量比控制在&gt;10。在生长过程中，衬底温度保持在650 K。为了在异位传输到其他超高真空(UHV)测量系统期间保护样品免受环境污染，在样品上沉积了一层Se/Te双层作为覆盖层。对于随后的PES、ARPES和XAS/XMCD表征，覆盖层在UHV中于500 K解吸。值得注意的是，在热解吸纯Se覆盖层后，通过PES观察到一个明显的Se 3d芯能级峰(图S4)，表明单层VTe₂薄膜中可能存在Se污染，因为该信号相当强，即使在相对较高的温度下也仍然存在。

<span class="hljs-strong">**Knudsen cell**</span>: 克努森蒸发源，一种用于分子束外延中蒸发材料的容器，通过加热产生可控的原子或分子束流。
<span class="hljs-strong">**ultrahigh vacuum (UHV)**</span>: 超高真空，指压力低于10⁻⁹ mbar的真空环境。

Scanning tunneling microscopy and spectroscopy. STM measurements were carried out in a custom-built multi-chamber UHV system housing an Omicron low temperature-STM interfaced with a Nanonis controller. The base pressure was better than 10⁻¹⁰ mbar. A chemically etched tungsten tip was used, and the sample was kept at 77 K during all the measurements. STM images were recorded in constant current mode. For dI/dV spectra, the tunneling current was obtained using the lock-in technique. Note that the bias voltage is applied on the STM tip; hence, negative values correspond to conduction bands and positive values correspond to valence bands. Each curve was obtained by averaging hundreds of individual spectra acquired at random locations of a specific STM image.
扫描隧道显微镜和光谱学。STM测量是在一个定制的多腔室UHV系统中进行的，该系统装有一个与Nanonis控制器接口的Omicron低温STM。基础压力优于10⁻¹⁰ mbar。使用化学腐蚀的钨针尖，样品在所有测量过程中保持在77 K。STM图像以恒定电流模式记录。对于dI/dV谱，隧穿电流使用锁相技术获得。注意，偏压施加在STM针尖上；因此，负值对应于导带，正值对应于价带。每条曲线是通过对特定STM图像中随机位置获取的数百个单独光谱进行平均得到的。

<span class="hljs-strong">**lock-in technique**</span>: 锁相技术，一种用于从噪声背景中提取微弱信号的测量技术。

Synchrotron-radiation photoemission. PES measurements were performed at 300 K at the SINS beamline of the Singapore Synchrotron Light Source (SSLS), which covers the photon energy range from 50 to 1200 eV. A Scienta SES-200 spectrometer was used to collect the spectroscopic data at normal emission, while the X-ray beam was set with an incident angle of 45° relative to the sample surface. A bias voltage of -7.0 eV was applied to the sample during work function measurement in order to negate the effect of the analyzer work function. The binding energy of the data was calibrated using the 4f core-levels and Fermi edge of a reference Au foil.
同步辐射光电子能谱。PES测量是在300 K下，于新加坡同步辐射光源(SSLS)的SINS光束线站进行的，该线站覆盖的光子能量范围为50至1200 eV。使用Scienta SES-200光谱仪在正出射方向收集光谱数据，同时X射线束的入射角相对于样品表面设置为45°。在功函数测量期间，对样品施加-7.0 eV的偏压，以消除分析器功函数的影响。数据的结合能使用参考金箔的4f芯能级和费米边进行校准。

Angle-resolved photoemission. The ARPES measurements were collected with HeIα (hv = 21.218 eV) radiation source (SCIENTA VUV5k). The photoelectrons were analyzed in the plane of incidence with a high energy and angular resolution SCIENTA DA30L analyzer. The angular detection range spans ±15° with respect to the spectrometer lens axes. Wider angular limits were obtained by rotating the sample with respect to the analyzer lens entrance axes. During ARPES acquisition the total energy resolution was set to 20 meV, with the angular resolution being better than 0.2°. The binding energy scale was referred to the Fermi level (EF) as measured for a clean gold substrate.
角分辨光电子能谱。ARPES测量是使用HeIα (hv = 21.218 eV)辐射源(SCIENTA VUV5k)收集的。光电子在入射平面内用高能量和角分辨率的SCIENTA DA30L分析器进行分析。角探测范围相对于光谱仪透镜轴为±15°。通过相对于分析器透镜入口轴旋转样品来获得更宽的角极限。在ARPES采集期间，总能量分辨率设置为20 meV，角分辨率优于0.2°。结合能标度参考了在清洁金衬底上测量的费米能级(EF)。

X-ray absorption spectroscopy and magnetic circular dichroism. XAS and XMCD measurements were carried out at the beamline 6.3.1 of the Advanced Light Source (ALS). The spectra were collected with sample temperatures ranging from 16 to 300 K in TEY mode, in which the sample drain current was recorded as a function of the photon energy. The angle of incidence of the photon beam was set to 45° relative to the sample surface. XMCD spectra were recorded with a fixed circular polarization of the X-rays and opposite magnetic fields up to ±1 T.
X射线吸收光谱和磁圆二色性。XAS和XMCD测量是在先进光源(ALS)的6.3.1光束线站进行的。光谱是在TEY模式下，在16至300 K的样品温度范围内收集的，其中记录样品漏极电流作为光子能量的函数。光子束的入射角相对于样品表面设置为45°。XMCD光谱是在固定X射线圆偏振和高达±1 T的相反磁场下记录的。

Density-functional theory calculations. The DFT calculations were performed using the VASP package, utilizing the projector augmented phase wave (PAW) method,³¹ and the Perdew-Burke and Ernzerhof (PBE) exchange-correlation functional.³² To better estimate the interlayer dispersion interactions in the interface structure, the dispersion-corrected vdW-optB88 exchange-correlation functional was applied.³³⁻³⁶ A separation of 20 Å between VTe₂ layers was found to be sufficient to represent an isolated monolayer. We employed a kinetic energy cutoff of 500 eV and a Γ-centered 21 × 21 × 1 k-point mesh. The lattice parameters and the atomic positions were optimized until the forces on the atoms were less than 1 meV/Å. The relaxed lattice parameter is 3.61 Å, in good agreement with the experimental value. For the interfaces, we use a 3 × 9, 2 × 6, and 2 × 2 supercell for graphene, T phase and T’ phase VTe₂, respectively, in which the strain is less than 2%. The graphene layer is kept fixed during the structure relaxation.
密度泛函理论计算。DFT计算使用VASP软件包进行，采用投影缀加平面波(PAW)方法³¹以及Perdew-Burke和Ernzerhof(PBE)交换关联泛函。³²为了更好地估计界面结构中的层间色散相互作用，应用了色散校正的vdW-optB88交换关联泛函。³³⁻³⁶发现VTe₂层之间20 Å的间距足以表示一个孤立的单层。我们采用了500 eV的动能截断能和Γ-centered 21×21×1的k点网格。晶格参数和原子位置被优化，直到原子上的力小于1 meV/Å。弛豫后的晶格参数为3.61 Å，与实验值吻合良好。对于界面，我们对石墨烯、T相和T‘相VTe₂分别使用3×9、2×6和2×2的超胞，其中应变小于2%。石墨烯层在结构弛豫过程中保持固定。

<span class="hljs-strong">**projector augmented phase wave (PAW)**</span>: 投影缀加平面波方法，一种用于第一性原理计算中描述电子-离子相互作用的有效方法。
<span class="hljs-strong">**exchange-correlation functional**</span>: 交换关联泛函，在密度泛函理论中用于近似处理电子间交换和关联相互作用的数学表达式。

> **科学家们是怎么做这个研究的？（方法）**

> 1.  **“种”出单层材料**：他们用一种叫“分子束外延”（MBE）的精巧方法，在一个非常平整的石墨（就是铅笔芯的主要成分）表面上，像喷雾一样，把钒原子和碲原子一点点喷上去，让它们自己排列成一层原子厚度的薄膜。这技术就像用原子“喷枪”在石墨板上“喷”出一层砖墙。

>     -   **X射线吸收谱（XAS）和X射线磁圆二色性（XMCD）**：这是专门用来探测材料磁性的一对“组合拳”。XAS看特定原子的电子是怎么吸收光的，可以判断原子的电子排布；XMCD则更厉害，它利用磁场和特殊的光，能专门探出材料里某个特定元素（比如钒）是不是有磁性，就像是给原子做“磁共振”检查。

>     -   **大白话解释**：这说的是钒原子最外层电子的“座位”安排。就像教室里的座位分成几排，电子也喜欢坐在特定的“轨道”上。d¹表示一个特定的轨道区里，只有一个电子。
