> Despite intense efforts on all known quasi-two-dimensional superconductors, the origin and exact boundary of the electronic orderings, particularly charge density waves and superconductivity, are still attractive problems with several open questions. Here, in order to reveal how the superconducting gap evolves, we report on high quality complementary measurements of magneto-optical imaging, specific heat, magnetic susceptibility, resistivity measurements, Andreev spectroscopy, and London penetration depth ${\ensuremath{\lambda}}_{ab}(T)$ measurements supplemented with theoretical calculations for $2\mathrm{H}\text{\ensuremath{-}}\mathrm{Nb}{\mathrm{Se}}_{2}$ and $2\mathrm{H}\text{\ensuremath{-}}\mathrm{Nb}{\mathrm{S}}_{2}$ single crystals. The temperature dependence of ${\ensuremath{\lambda}}_{ab}(T)$ calculated from the lower critical field and Andreev spectroscopy can be well described by using a two-band model with $s$-wave-like gaps. The effect of pressure on the superconducting gap of both systems illustrates that both bands are practically affected. Upon compression, the Fermi surfaces do not change significantly, and the nesting remains almost unaffected compared to that at ambient condition. However, a strong bending in the upper critical fields (${H}_{\mathrm{c}2}$) curves is obtained under pressure and support the presence of a strong Pauli paramagnetic effect. In $\mathrm{Nb}{\mathrm{Se}}_{2}$, using a two-band model with $s$-wave-like gaps, the temperature dependence ${H}_{\mathrm{c}2}(T)$ can be properly described. In contrast to that, the behavior of ${H}_{\mathrm{c}2}$ for $\mathrm{Nb}{\mathrm{S}}_{2}$ is ruled by the spin paramagnetic effect. The estimated values of the penetration depth at $T=0\phantom{\rule{0.16em}{0ex}}\mathrm{K}$ confirm that $\mathrm{Nb}{\mathrm{Se}}_{2}$ and $\mathrm{Nb}{\mathrm{S}}_{2}$ superconductors depart from a Uemura-style relationship between ${T}_{\mathrm{c}}$ with ${\mathbit{\ensuremath{\lambda}}}_{ab}^{\ensuremath{-}2}(T)$, the in-plane superconducting penetration depth.
>

> ## 一、引言
> 
> 在凝聚态物理领域，超导电性与电荷密度波等竞争性电子序的共存与相互作用，是一个长期引人入胜的核心问题。本文研究的对象是过渡金属二硫族化合物家族中的两个标志性成员：**2H-NbSe₂** 和 **2H-NbS₂**。这两种材料具有相同的晶体结构，却展现出截然不同的电子基态：2H-NbSe₂ 在 33 K 时形成电荷密度波，并在 7.2 K 进入超导态；而 2H-NbS₂ 则未观测到电荷密度波，直接以 5.7 K 的超导转变温度为特征。
> 
> 这一对比为我们提供了一个理想的平台，来剥离并理解电荷密度波对超导电性的真正影响。然而，此前的研究对于这两个体系，尤其是其超导配对机制和能隙结构，仍存在显著争议。例如，2H-NbS₂ 的超导态是单能隙还是多能隙？2H-NbSe₂ 中电荷密度波的起源是费米面嵌套，还是更复杂的电子-声子耦合？高压作为一种“干净”的调控手段，如何影响这两个体系的超导能隙和临界场？
> 
> 本文的核心价值在于，它通过一套高度互补的实验技术组合（磁光成像、电阻率、比热、磁化率、安德列夫反射谱、伦敦穿透深度测量）与第一性原理理论计算，对上述问题进行了系统性的解答。研究不仅澄清了两体系中双能隙、s波对称性的超导配对机制，还揭示了在高压下电荷密度波与超导电性的竞争关系，以及上临界场中多带效应与泡利顺磁效应各自的主导作用，为理解该类材料的非常规超导电性提供了关键的实验基准。
> 
> ## 二、论文结构与逻辑概览
> 
> 本文的逻辑结构遵循了从宏观表征到微观机制、从常压到高压、从实验到理论的递进式论证链条。
> 
> -   **宏观表征与品质确认 (第III.A-B节)**：通过磁光成像和电输运测量，确认了单晶样品的高质量，并清晰展示了电荷密度波在NbSe₂中的存在和在NbS₂中的缺失，同时揭示了伴随电荷密度波出现的载流子类型反转现象。
> -   **竞争关系的热力学与相图建立 (第III.C-D节)**：利用高压下的比热、电阻率和磁化率测量，构建了详尽的温度-压力相图。该相图直观地展示了在NbSe₂中，电荷密度波被压力逐渐抑制，而超导临界温度则随之上升，直接证明了二者的竞争关系。
> -   **微观能隙结构的探测 (第III.E-F节)**：首先，采用安德列夫反射谱这一对能隙敏感的技术，在两者中均探测到两个超导能隙（一个大能隙，一个小能隙），并确定其s波对称性。随后，通过第一性原理计算，展示了费米面在压力下拓扑结构不变，排除了费米面嵌套作为电荷密度波主因的可能，并指出大能隙主要源于Nb原子衍生的能带。
> -   **超导配对对称性与凝聚态特性的验证 (第III.G-I节)**：通过分析高压下的上临界场行为，揭示了NbS₂中由泡利顺磁效应主导的配对破坏机制，以及NbSe₂中由多带效应导致的上临界场曲线弯曲。最后，通过伦敦穿透深度的温度依赖关系，排除了d波配对的可能性，印证了双s波能隙模型，并发现这两个体系均偏离了传统的Uemura关系，暗示其超导机理的独特性。
> 
> 整个论证链条环环相扣，从现象观测到机制解释，从外部调控到内部机理，从单一实验到多实验互证，逻辑清晰且严谨。

> ## 四、正文核心内容剖析
> 
> ### 1. 研究背景与问题意识
> 
> 作者开篇即点明核心问题：在准二维材料中，电荷密度波与超导电性这两种电子序的起源和确切边界仍充满争议。尤其以2H-NbSe₂（有电荷密度波）和2H-NbS₂（无电荷密度波）这对同构体为经典案例，二者的对比研究对于揭示电荷密度波与超导电性的相互作用机理至关重要。然而，此前关于超导能隙的对称性、大小、数量以及配对机制，学界并无共识。
> 
> ### 2. 研究方法与模型
> 
> 本文的一大亮点在于其**多手段互补的综合实验方案**，几乎覆盖了超导研究的各个方面：
> 
> -   **宏观电磁特性**：**磁光成像**（直观表征磁通穿透与缺陷）、**电输运测量**（电阻率、霍尔效应，测量 (T_c) 及载流子行为）、**比热测量**（测量体超导转变与电荷密度波）、**直流/交流磁化率**（测量 (T_c) 与抗磁屏蔽）。
> -   **配对机制与能隙**：**安德列夫反射谱**（直接测量超导能隙的大小、数量和对称性）、**伦敦穿透深度**（通过测量下临界场 (H_{c1}) 获得，反映超流密度和能隙结构）。
> -   **外部调控**：**高压技术**（最高达 25 GPa，结合金刚石对顶砧，用于调控电荷密度波和超导电性，构建相图）。
> -   **理论支持**：**第一性原理计算**（基于密度泛函理论的QUANTUM ESPRESSO软件包，计算费米面在压力下的演化）。
> 
> ### 3. 核心实验现象与论证逻辑
> 
> -   **电荷密度波与超导电性的竞争**：通过高压相图，作者直观地展示了二者并非简单的共生关系，而是**竞争关系**。在 NbSe₂ 中，电荷密度波被抑制的初期，(T_c) 显著提升，如同“松开刹车”。一旦电荷密度波完全消失，(T_c) 的增加就变得平缓，这可能源于费米面拓扑结构在压力下不变，限制了电子-声子耦合强度的进一步增长。
> -   **超导能隙结构的确立**：安德列夫反射谱（微观）和伦敦穿透深度（宏观）两个独立实验，共同指向了**双能隙、s波对称**的图像。能隙的 BCS 比值大于标准值，表明至少在一个能带上存在强耦合相互作用。电荷密度波的存在与否，并未改变 NbSe₂ 和 NbS₂ 中这种本征的双能隙结构，说明它主要源于 Nb 原子衍生的电子态。
> -   **配对破坏机制的差异**：高压下的上临界场行为揭示了两种材料截然不同的配对破坏机制。NbS₂ 的饱和行为表明其受限于**泡利顺磁效应**（电子自旋磁矩在强磁场下的极化），而 NbSe₂ 的向上弯曲则归因于**多带效应**。这一区别进一步凸显了即使能隙结构相似，其背后的超导物理细节仍存在显著差异。
> 
> ## 五、结论与贡献
> 
> 本研究得出以下核心结论：
> 
> 1.  **确立了竞争关系**：在2H-NbSe₂ 中，电荷密度波与超导电性是竞争性的，压力的施加通过抑制电荷密度波显著增强了超导电性。
> 2.  **阐明了超导能隙结构**：2H-NbSe₂ 和 2H-NbS₂ 均具有两个**s波对称**的超导能隙，且大能隙的耦合强度超过弱耦合BCS理论极限。电荷密度波的存在不影响这一基本能隙结构。
> 3.  **揭示了不同的配对破坏机制**：在高压下，NbS₂ 的上临界场由泡利顺磁效应主导，而 NbSe₂ 则表现出明显的多带效应。
> 4.  **挑战了传统理论认知**：两者均偏离了超流密度与 (T_c) 的 Uemura 普适关系，费米面嵌套并非电荷密度波的主要成因，这些发现为理解层状TMD材料的复杂电子相图提供了新的约束和视角。
> 
> **核心贡献**在于，通过一套无可比拟的互补实验技术组合，为关于NbSe₂/NbS₂的长期争论提供了决定性的、高置信度的实验证据，建立了电荷密度波-超导相互作用与能隙结构的清晰物理图像，并为未来理论发展设定了基准。
> 
> ## 六、未来发展方向展望
> 
> 作者在文中为未来研究指明了多个方向：
> 
> 1.  **电荷密度波的微观起源**：尽管排除了费米面嵌套，但电荷密度波的确切成因仍需探索，特别是**动量依赖的电子-声子耦合矩阵**的作用，这是一个理论挑战。
> 2.  **超导能隙的精细结构**：目前的实验主要在液氦温度（1.5 K）以上进行。为了更精确地确定能隙结构，特别是小能隙的细节，需要将穿透深度等实验扩展到**更低温度（如毫开尔文量级）**。
> 3.  **涡旋态物理**：在常压下，NbSe₂ 的 (H_{c2}(T)) 曲线上的弯折现象，以及其与电荷密度波被抑制后消失的关联，暗示了存在新的涡旋物质相。这需要结合更微观的成像技术（如扫描隧道显微镜）和理论模型进行深入研究。
> 4.  **偏离Uemura关系的本质**：为何这两种材料会系统性地偏离Uemura关系？这是否意味着其超导凝聚的机理与常规或铜氧化物超导体有根本不同？这为理解超导的普适分类提供了新的课题。
> 5.  **低维体系的拓展**：将这些研究从块材推广到单层或少层 NbSe₂/NbS₂，探索维度效应如何进一步调控电荷密度波与超导电性的竞争，这对于二维材料器件应用至关重要。
> 
> ## 七、学术思考与批判性分析
> 
> 尽管本文非常全面，但仍有几个值得深入思考的问题：
> 
> 1.  **“竞争”是否为唯一互动模式？** 本文将压力下 (T_c) 的上升完全归因于电荷密度波的抑制，即“竞争”关系。但文章中也提到，在电荷密度波被完全抑制后，(T_c) 仍有一个缓慢的上升和下降（形成穹顶状）。是否可能存在更复杂的协同效应？例如，电荷密度波涨落本身可能提供某种配对媒介，其完全消失反而对超导不利。对相图电鼻子区域更精细的光谱学研究，如扫描隧道谱，可能会揭示电荷密度波涨落与超导态的共存细节。
>

> ## 四、正文核心内容剖析
> 
> ### 1. 研究背景与动机
> 
> 作者开篇指出，在准二维超导体中，CDW和超导电性的起源和边界仍是未解之谜。特别是，**2H-NbS₂**是TMDs家族中唯一一个未被观察到CDW的超导体，而**2H-NbSe₂**则是CDW与超导共存的典型。这种“孪生”对比为理解两者相互作用提供了绝佳平台。此前，关于超导能隙的对称性、大小以及配对机制都缺乏共识，这正是本文要解决的问题。
> 
> ### 2. 方法与模型
> 
> 本文的亮点在于其**多工具互补的实验策略**，每一项都针对一个特定的物理问题：
> 
> -   **磁光成像（MOI）**：宏观尺度上验证样品均匀性，排除缺陷干扰。
> -   **电输运与比热**：构建宏观相图，追踪CDW和超导的相变。
> -   **高压技术（DAC）**：作为核心调控手段，连续、可逆地压制CDW，揭示其与超导的竞争。
> -   **安德列夫反射谱（IMARE）**：微观尺度上直接测量超导能隙的个数、大小和对称性。
> -   **伦敦穿透深度（λ）测量**：探测超流密度和能隙结构，是判断超导配对对称性（s波、d波等）的有力工具。
> -   **量化分析模型**：
>     -   **两带s波模型**：用于拟合穿透深度和上临界场数据，成功描述了多带效应。
>     -   **WHH模型**：用于拟合上临界场数据，考虑了轨道和自旋顺磁效应。
>     -   **第一性原理计算**：计算费米面，从理论上支持实验观察。
> 
> ### 3. 实验结果与讨论
> 
> 这部分是论文的核心，作者通过精巧的实验设计，层层递进地构建了证据链。
> 
> -   **第一步：确立竞争关系。** 通过输运和磁化率测量，构建了T-P相图。在NbSe₂中，Tc的升高与CDW的抑制严格对应，直接证明了二者的竞争关系。当CDW完全消失后，Tc的增益也趋于饱和，说明竞争的“红利”已吃完。
>     
> -   **第二步：揭示微观能隙。** 安德列夫谱直接“看”到了两个能隙，且两者都表现为s波对称性。关键的发现是，**大能隙的BCS比值超出弱耦合极限**，表明该能带上的电子配对具有强耦合特性。对比NbS₂和NbSe₂，发现两者的能隙结构非常相似，意味着**CDW的存在并不影响超导能隙的基本结构**。
>     
> -   **第三步：关联宏观与微观。** 伦敦穿透深度的测量从宏观角度完美印证了微观的安德列夫谱结果。温度依赖的穿透深度数据同样只能用两带s波模型拟合，排除了d波配对的可能性。这证实了多带超导是材料的内禀属性。
>     
> -   **第四步：提供理论支撑。** 费米面计算表明，压力下费米面拓扑变化不大，且无嵌套，解释了为何Tc不会发生数量级上的剧变。超导主要由Nb原子的能带贡献，CDW的干扰只是改变了Tc，而未能改变能隙结构。
>     
> -   **第五步：探究配对破坏机制。** 上临界场Hc₂的分析显示，NbSe₂和NbS₂在强磁场下破坏超导的机制不同。NbS₂更接近自旋顺磁效应，而NbSe₂则表现出显著的多带效应。
>

> ## 五、结论与贡献
> 
> ### 核心发现
> 
> 1.  **竞争关系的量化**：在2H-NbSe₂中，压力能迅速抑制CDW，并导致Tc显著升高，两者呈明确的竞争关系。
> 2.  **确立两带超导**：有机结合安德列夫谱和伦敦穿透深度两种互补测量，无可辩驳地证实了2H-NbS₂和2H-NbSe₂均为具有**两个s波对称性能隙**的超导体。
> 3.  **能隙结构的鲁棒性**：CDW的存在与否，并不改变超导能隙的基本结构（即大、小能隙的个数和对称性），暗示超导配对主要发生在Nb原子层，CDW仅仅作为一个竞争有序态去分占费米面上的态密度。
> 4.  **偏离普适规律**：发现这两种材料并不遵循描述非常规超导体的Uemura关系，表明其超导机理可能更接近具有强耦合特性的传统多带超导体，如MgB₂。
> 
> ### 理论与应用贡献
> 
> -   **理论贡献**：为TMDs体系中CDW与超导的相互作用提供了关键实验基准，澄清了多带超导的本质，并对其配对机制（强耦合、s波）给出了强有力的约束。
> -   **应用价值**：深入理解这些层状材料的电子特性，是未来基于它们设计新型量子器件的科学基础。高压调控提供了一种在不引入化学掺杂和缺陷的情况下，连续改变材料性质的方法，对材料工程有指导意义。

> ## 六、未来发展方向展望
> 
> 作者在文中暗示了以下未解决的问题和潜在研究路径：
> 
> 1.  **极低温测量**：文中提到，要更彻底地确定能隙结构，需将伦敦穿透深度的测量扩展到更低温度（如mK级别），以更精确地探测是否有极小的能隙节点或残余态密度。
> 2.  **CDW的微观起源**：作者明确指出，研究已表明费米面嵌套不是CDW的主要驱动力，而**电-声子耦合的动量依赖性**（ q-dependent electron-phonon matrix element）可能是关键。这是未来理论工作的重点。

> 5.  **器件应用探索**：基于这些材料，特别是其多带特征和可调性，可以探索其在超导电子学、量子传感等领域的应用潜力。

> ## 七、学术思考与批判性分析
> 
> 尽管本文工作出色，但仍有一些值得深入思考的问题：
> 
> 1.  **两带模型的简化性**：文中使用简化的两带模型，虽然能很好地拟合数据，但真实的能带结构更为复杂。是否存在三个或更多能隙的贡献？将复杂的能带投影到两个“有效”能隙上，是否会丢失一些如各向异性等关键信息？作者在讨论中也承认了这一点，这是一个合理的简化，但也是一个固有的局限。
>     
> 2.  **“竞争”的图像是否过于简单？** 论文将CDW和超导描述为竞争关系。但竞争的具体物理图像是什么？是两者争夺费米面上的态密度，还是CDW导致的晶格畸变抑制了有利于超导的声子模式？虽然压力实验证明了竞争，但并未揭示其微观动力学机制。是否存在某种条件下的共存与协作？
>     
> 3.  **缺陷与不均匀性的影响**：磁光成像明确显示样品存在缺陷，且磁通优先通过这些缺陷穿透。这些缺陷在多大程度上影响了其他宏观测量（如Hc₁的确定）？以及，它们对微观的安德列夫谱测量是否有潜在影响？作者在Hc₁测量时采用了谨慎的方法，但缺陷的影响始终是凝聚态物理实验中的一个系统性问题。
>     
> 4.  **高压实验的内在挑战**：高压下的输运和磁化测量非常困难，样品的尺寸、压力介质的静水压性、压力标定等都会引入误差。虽然作者使用了成熟技术，但高压下的数据，尤其是定量数据（如上临界场的绝对值），其不确定性通常比常压下要大。文中多组数据的一致性部分缓解了这种担忧。
>     
> 5.  **理论计算的深度**：本文的DFT计算主要停留在费米面拓扑结构的层面，未能深入计算电-声子耦合常数λ以及其在不同压力、不同能带上的分布。若能结合这些计算，将能更直接地与实验得出的“强耦合”结论进行对话，并可能揭示CDW的驱动力。

> ## 八、用户问题预测与解答
> 
> **问题1：什么是“电荷密度波”（CDW）？为什么它常与超导电性竞争？** **解答**：您可以想象，材料中的电子密度并非总是均匀分布的。在某些条件下，电子会自发地形成一种周期性的疏密波，就像湖面上的涟漪，这就是**电荷密度波（CDW）**。这种波的形成会打开一个能隙，消耗掉一部分本可以自由移动形成超导电流的电子（即消耗了费米面上的态密度）。同时，CDW伴随的晶格畸变也可能“软化”某些声子，而对超导配对至关重要的声子模式可能被“硬化”或改变。因此，两者常常在争夺电子和声子这两种“资源”，表现出竞争关系，即一个强了，另一个就弱了。
> 
> **问题2：什么是“两带超导”？它和只有一个能隙的常规超导有什么不同？** **解答**：常规超导体（如铝）就像一个只有一条跑道的高速公路，所有电子配对都在这条跑道上形成，用一个“超导能隙”就能描述。而**两带超导**（如MgB₂）则像拥有两条不同属性跑道的公路，不同跑道上电子配对形成的“车辆”速度（能隙大小）不同。这意味着超导性质是由两个能带的电子共同决定的，会表现出更复杂的温度依赖性和磁场响应。本文通过实验直接“看”到了这两个不同的能隙，因此确认了它们是两带超导体。
> 
> **问题3：什么是“Uemura关系”？为什么本文发现它不适用会是一个重要结论？** **解答**：Uemura关系是一个著名的经验规律，它在铜氧化物等非常规超导体中发现：超导临界温度（Tc）与超流密度（衡量超导电子配对“硬度”的物理量）大致成正比。这暗示了非常规超导体的超导机理可能与常规的BCS理论有根本不同，即Tc不是由配对强度，而是由配对的“刚度”决定。本文发现NbSe₂和NbS₂偏离了这个关系，说明它们的超导机理更接近常规的BCS图像，即Tc主要由配对相互作用的强度（表现为强耦合）决定，而不是超流密度。这为将它们与铜氧化物等非常规超导体区分开来提供了关键证据。
> 
> **问题4：作者如何确定超导能隙是“s波”对称性，而不是“d波”？** **解答**：不同的配对对称性会导致超导能隙在动量空间的分布不同。s波是各向同性的（在所有方向上有间隙），而d波是有节点的（在特定方向上能隙为零）。这种差异会直接反映在穿透深度λ(T)的低温行为上。在低温下，d波超导体由于节点存在，准粒子可以轻易被激发，导致λ(T) ~ T的线性变化。而s波超导体由于全域都有能隙，准粒子激发需要克服能隙，呈指数衰减，即δλ(T) ∝ exp(-Δ/kBT)。本文中，λ(T)的数据完全符合指数衰减，远离d波的线性行为，因此被判定为s波对称性。
> 
> **问题5：高压实验在这里起到了什么关键作用，是其他方法不可替代的？** **解答**：高压是一种“干净”且可逆的调控手段。它通过缩小原子间距来改变电子结构和晶格动力学，而**不引入任何化学掺杂或缺陷**，这是它相较于化学替代的最大优势。在本文中，高压起到了“手术刀”的作用，它选择性地“切除”了NbSe₂中的CDW态，同时保留了完整的晶体结构。这使得研究者可以直接对比“有CDW”和“无CDW”下的同一样品，从而清晰地剥离出CDW对超导电性的影响（如Tc的升高，Hc₂曲线“扭结”的消失）。这种干净的对比，是化学掺杂方法难以实现的，因为掺杂本身会引入无序和结构变化，使问题复杂化。

> ## I. INTRODUCTION
> 
> ## I. 引言
> 
> Coexistence of superconductivity with competing physical phenomena such as magnetic or charge order has been of interest for the condensed matter community for a long time [1,2]. It is commonly accepted that for materials with competing ground states, suppressing the magnetic or charge order helps to stabilize the superconducting (SC) phase. This is the case, for instance, in layered two-dimensional (2D) materials where periodic modulations of the charge carrier density, charge density waves (CDWs), have been attributed to nesting of the Fermi surface (FS) [3–5]. Classic examples are the members of the transition-metal dichalcogenide family (TMDs) MX2, where M = Nb, Ti, Ta, Mo and X = S, Se. TMDs provide an ideal playground for studying semiconductors, metals, and superconductors in 2D using the same structural template [6,7]. 2H-NbSe2 and 2H-NbS2 are special among the TMDs since they exhibit superconductivity (Tc = 7.2 K [8] and 5.7 K [9], respectively) at ambient pressure and without intercalation or chemical substitution. Despite being usually considered two competing phenomena, the CDW state and superconductivity coexist in a few systems, the most emblematic one being the TMD 2H-NbSe2 (TCDW = 33 K [10,11]). However, within the family of SC TMDs, 2H-NbS2 stands out as the only system for which a CDW phase has not been observed [9]. This suggests that a comparative analysis of NbS2 and other SC TMDs, such as NbSe2, may help to clarify the interplay between the superconducting and the CDW instabilities in the entire family. Another interesting and rather unique feature of these compounds, NbS2 and NbSe2, is the absence of nesting between hole and electron pockets of the FS [12,13]. The gap symmetry and structure of the SC order parameter can be used to reveal the pairing mechanism in these TMD compounds. 超导电性与磁有序或电荷有序等竞争物理现象的共存长期以来一直是凝聚态物质领域感兴趣的问题[1,2]。人们普遍认为，对于具有竞争基态的材料，抑制磁有序或电荷有序有助于稳定超导(SC)相。例如，在层状二维(2D)材料中，电荷载流子密度的周期性调制——电荷密度波(CDW)——被归因于费米面(FS)的嵌套[3–5]。经典例子是过渡金属二硫族化物(TMD)家族MX2的成员，其中M = Nb, Ti, Ta, Mo，X = S, Se。TMD为利用相同结构模板研究二维半导体、金属和超导体提供了理想平台[6,7]。2H-NbSe2和2H-NbS2在TMD中尤为特殊，因为它们无需插层或化学替代，在常压下即可表现出超导电性（Tc分别为7.2 K [8]和5.7 K [9]）。尽管通常被视为两种竞争现象，CDW态和超导电性在少数体系中共存，最具代表性的是TMD 2H-NbSe2（TCDW = 33 K [10,11]）。然而，在SC TMD家族中，2H-NbS2是唯一未观察到CDW相的体系[9]。这表明对NbS2与其他SC TMD（如NbSe2）的比较分析可能有助于阐明整个家族中超导和CDW不稳定性之间的相互作用。这些化合物（NbS2和NbSe2）另一个有趣且相当独特的特征是FS的空穴和电子口袋之间缺乏嵌套[12,13]。能隙对称性和SC序参量的结构可用于揭示这些TMD化合物中的配对机制。
> 
> **Transition-metal dichalcogenide (TMD)**: 过渡金属二硫族化物，化学式为MX2的层状材料，其中M为过渡金属，X为硫族元素，具有丰富的电子性质。 **Nesting**: 嵌套，指费米面不同部分之间通过某一波矢平移后相互重叠的几何关系，常与电荷密度波形成相关。
> 
> Scanning tunneling spectroscopy measurements of the NbS2 system revealed features in the local density of states (DOS), similar to that seen in MgB2, providing strong indications of two-gap superconductivity [9]. However, so far theoretical calculations on NbS2 have considered only a single-gap scenario [14]. In addition, it is generally agreed on that more than one energy scale is involved in the SC order parameter of NbSe2. On top of that, ambiguity remains in terms of the magnitude of the SC gaps [15–17], and there is clearly no general consensus on the SC pairing mechanisms in both TMD systems. Further studies are necessary to elucidate this issue. High pressure has been shown to be a clean and powerful means of generating different physical states, having been particularly effective not only in tuning the critical temperatures of superconducting transitions but also in inducing superconductivity in materials with CDW orders as their ground states at ambient pressure. Here, by applying external pressure between 0 and 25 GPa, we observe that the main effect on the in-plane superconducting penetration depth λ−2ab, in the TMD NbSe2 and NbS2 superconductors arises from the energy band(s) where the large SC gap, L develops. Carrying out Andreev reflection spectroscopy on both systems, it is shown that the values of the Bardeen-Cooper-Schrieffer (BCS) ratio for large gaps exceed the value obtained within the weak coupling BCS theory, which indicates a strong intraband interaction. The analysis of λ−2ab, and Andreev spectroscopy can be interpreted using a two-band model with s-wave-like gaps [18]. Our results imply, therefore, that the transition temperature in both systems is entirely determined by the intraband interaction within the band(s) where the dominant gap is opened. NbS2系统的扫描隧道谱测量揭示了局域态密度(DOS)的特征，类似于在MgB2中观察到的特征，为双能隙超导电性提供了有力证据[9]。然而，迄今为止关于NbS2的理论计算仅考虑了单能隙情况[14]。此外，人们普遍认为NbSe2的SC序参量涉及不止一个能量尺度。除此之外，关于SC能隙的大小仍存在不确定性[15–17]，并且对两个TMD体系的SC配对机制显然没有普遍共识。需要进一步研究来阐明这一问题。高压已被证明是产生不同物理状态的一种干净而有力的手段，不仅在调节超导转变的临界温度方面特别有效，而且在常压下以CDW有序为基态的材料中诱导超导电性方面也特别有效。在此，通过施加0至25 GPa的外部压力，我们观察到TMD NbSe2和NbS2超导体中对面内超导穿透深度λ−2ab的主要影响来自于大幅值SC能隙L发展的能带。对两个系统进行安德列夫反射光谱测量表明，大能隙的Bardeen-Cooper-Schrieffer (BCS)比值超过了弱耦合BCS理论获得的值，这表明存在强带内相互作用。λ−2ab和安德列夫光谱的分析可用具有类s波能隙的双带模型解释[18]。因此，我们的结果表明两个系统中的转变温度完全由主导能隙打开的能带内的带内相互作用决定。
> 
> **Local density of states (DOS)**: 局域态密度，描述在空间某点和特定能量下电子态的密度，可通过扫描隧道谱测量。 **BCS theory**: BCS理论，由巴丁、库珀和施里弗提出的超导微观理论，描述电子通过声子形成库珀对的配对机制。

> ## II. MATERIALS AND METHODS
> 
> ## II. 材料与方法
> 
> The thin platelets of high-quality single crystals of NbSe2 and NbS2 with optically flat surfaces on both sides were cut into a bar shape. Tabletlike crystals of NbSe2 and NbS2 grew up to 1 mm in size when using an evaporation method previously demonstrated by Fiechter and Kühne for the crystal growth of RuS2 [19], see Supplemental Material (Fig. S1) [20]. Low-temperature transport and specific heat (down to 400 mK) were measured with a Physical Property Measurement System (PPMS, Quantum Design). Four contacts were used to measure the high-pressure in-plane resistivity with the superconducting NbSe2 and NbS2 set in a diamond anvil cell in the PPMS. A single crystal was loaded into a miniature diamond anvil cell with a sample space of approximately 130-μm diameter. We used an insulating gasket which was made of a mixture of cubic boron nitride and epoxy, and Daphne oil 7373 was used as a pressure medium. Pressure was calibrated using several ruby chips with dimensions of approximately 1 mm, which were placed in the cell along with the sample at room temperature. > The macroscopic response of the NbSe2 and NbS2 samples was characterized by magneto-optical imaging (MOI) [25]. One of the advantages of MOI is that it allows for the visualization of the magnetic flux distribution over large distances of several millimeters with spatial resolution of a few microns, thus providing insights into the macroscopic response of the samples while still capturing details at the local scale. This noninvasive technique requires a Bi-doped yttrium iron garnet placed on top of the sample, and a polarized light beam, whose polarization is rotated in the garnet proportionally to the local magnetic field. The samples were cooled down in a closedcycle 4He cryostat equipped with a copper electromagnet. More details about the setup can be found in Ref. [26]. In Fig. 1(a), we determine the critical temperature, Tc, of the superconducting samples by tracking the light intensity in the images as a function of temperature. A field of μ0H = 1 mT is applied, then the samples are cooled down to T = 4 K (field-cooling procedure). The field is subsequently decreased to H = 0, leaving the samples in the remnant state, magnetic flux trapped by the superconductors. As T is increased, we monitor the intensity, I, in a 45 × 45 μm2(30 × 30 px2) square represented in yellow in the MO images (see insets). In Fig. 1(a), I is normalized by the intensity I0 outside the sample. We find Tc ∼ 7.0 K for the NbSe2 sample (white dots) and Tc ∼ 5.60 K for the NbS2 sample (filled dots). > ### B. Transport properties

> ### B. 输运性质
> 
> Figure 2(a) illustrates the temperature dependence of the in-plane resistivity measurements of NbSe2 and NbS2 single crystals during warming from 0.4 K to room temperature (300 K). In both samples, the normal state exhibits simple metallic behavior during cooling down from room temperature, followed by a sharp superconducting transition at Tc, which agrees with magnetization and specific heat data (described later). The resistivity curve of 2H-NbSe2 presents an anomaly near 33 K, which is attributed to the formation of the CDW. In contrast to that, there are no indications of the formation of the CDW in NbS2 in the measured temperature range. The inset of Fig. 2(a) shows a close-up of the lowtemperature region, highlighting the CDW formation. Figures 2(b) and 2(c) show the temperature dependences of the Hall mobility, μ(T), and carrier concentration deduced from the coefficient measurements, based on considerations of an isotropic Fermi surface μ = RH /ρ and n, p = 1/eRH , respectively. The presence of CDW in NbSe2 leads to a change in the sign of charge carriers at a certain temperature of 27.9 K. In NbS2, due to the absence of CDW, such a sign change from p-type to n-type does not occur, as can be seen from the dependences of μ(T ) and p(T). The close relationship between CDW and RH acts as a generally accepted explanation for the Hall coefficient sign reversal and the appearance of highly mobile electrons, resulting in increase of their mean free path. Indeed, as can be seen from Fig. 2(b), below 27.9 K, the electron mobility for NbSe2 begins to rapidly increase up to 30 cm2 V−1 S−1 at T ≈ 10 K. The hole mobility in NbS2, however, also grows, but not significantly, with a value of 9.9 cm2 V−1 S−1 at T ≈ 6.3 K, right up to the SC transition. Our findings are similar to previously reported data [8,9], however, the effect of controlled point-like disorder as well as the samples quality i.e., different residual resistivity ratio can lead to the suppression of a coherent long-range CDW phase, which ultimately does not demonstrate the RH sign change below TCDW. The difference between the transport behavior in NbSe2 and NbS2 is also evidenced from the dependence of n, p(T) [Fig. 2(b)]. Below TCDW, the hole concentration in NbSe2 shows a sharp increase to p = 1.71 × 1023 cm−3, and then at TCDW it inverts the sign with a value of n = 2.4 × 1023 cm−3 followed by a monotonic decrease. In contrast to the significant changes observed in NbSe2, the holes concentration in NbS2 shows only a slight increase [inset in Fig. 2(c)]. In general, the electronic transport for both compounds is consistent with an increase in the mobility of charge carriers when approaching to the SC transition, regardless of the presence or absence of CDW. A qualitative difference in observed concentration behavior for NbSe2 and NbS2 below TCDW may be due to a different scenario in the opening of the pseudogap caused by different scattering rates. Additionally, the drop in RH in NbSe2 and its sign reversal should also be closely related to the CDW transition and can be explained by the drastic increase of the mean-free-path of electron type charge carrier. These spots were suggested to be strongly coupled by CDW modulation [27]. > ### C. CDW and superconductivity under pressure
> 
> ### C. 压力下的CDW与超导电性
> 
> Low-temperature specific heat, CP, being equal to the temperature derivative of the entropy, probes the gap structure of bulk superconductors. The inset of Fig. 3(a) illustrates the temperature dependence of the specific heat of NbSe2 measured at different applied magnetic fields parallel to the c axis. In zero-field specific-heat measurements, a very sharp anomaly is clearly seen, which is attributed to the superconducting transition at Tc. This specific-heat jump is systematically shifted to lower temperatures upon applying DC magnetic fields of up to 6 T. The main panel of Fig. 3(a) presents a bump near 33 K for NbSe2, which is due to the formation of the CDW, followed by a sharp SC transition at Tc = 7.2 K, agreeing with magnetization and specific heat data previously reported [8,9]. Pressure has long been recognized as a fundamental thermodynamic variable, which is a convenient tool for attaining a deep understanding of various SC characteristics. It is considered as a clean way to tune basic electronic and structural properties without changing the stoichiometry. Figure 3(b) illustrates the T dependence of the in-plane resistivity measurements of NbSe2 single crystals around the CDW transitions under various applied pressures during warming. The CDW transition is monotonically suppressed by increasing pressure up to 7.0 GPa. Interestingly, in NbSe2, the relative weight between the CDW and superconductivity can be tuned by application of high pressure or by lowering the dimensionality of the system [28,29]. These observations triggered intense theoretical efforts to explain the origin of the CDW and superconducting transitions in bulk and few-layers 2H-NbSe2, by accounting for the different roles of the electron-phonon coupling and FS nesting [3,13,30]. With the above understanding of the nature of superconductivity in NbSe2, we now study its magnetic response under ambient pressure and at 7 GPa, close to the SC transition. In Figs. 3(c) and 3(d), we show the temperature dependence of the resistivity under ambient pressure and at 7 GPa respectively, at different magnetic fields. The Tc clearly increases with pressure. At ambient pressure, for zero magnetic field, the superconducting critical temperature is 7.2 K, while the critical temperature at 7 GPa rises to approximately 8.5 K. This is the same pressure range within which the CDW is seen to be suppressed (vide infra). Quite expectedly, for both 0 and 7 GPa, with higher magnetic field, the Tc is shifted to lower values and the transition also becomes broad. At ambient pressure, upon increasing the applied field, a kink appears which could be related to an abrupt change from reversible to irreversible behavior. Further theoretical and experimental studies suggest that this kink could be due to the existence of two solid phases of vortex matter [31]. Although various models have been proposed, there is currently no consensus regarding the underlying nature of the disordered phase and the corresponding order-disorder transition in NbSe2. > ### E. Andreev reflection spectroscopy
> 
> ### E. 安德列夫反射光谱
> 
> Intrinsic multiple Andreev reflections (IMARE) spectroscopy of superconductor - normal metal - superconductor (SnS) junctions was performed using the break-junction technique [34]. IMARE occurs in the ballistic regime of SnS junctions and causes excess current at low bias voltages (which produces a so-called foot) and a subharmonic gap structure. This structure consists of a series of minima in dynamic conductance, each minimum corresponding to voltage values Vn = 2 /en, where is the superconducting gap, e is the elementary charge, and n = 1, 2, . . . is the subharmonic order. We now turn to the evolution of the superconducting gap(s) structure of the superconductors NbS2 and NbSe2 using Andreev reflection (AR) spectra on the superconductor-constriction-superconductor (S-c-S) contacts, obtained by means of break junction technique. This method makes it possible to obtain a pure cryogenic cleavage inside the sample, as well as mechanically rearrange the contact area (the geometry of the contact, its area and its position in the bulk of the sample). As it was shown in Ref. [34], a series of features [the so-called subharmonic gap structure (SGS)] is formed on the IU characteristic of symmetric S-c-S contacts due to the effect of multiple Andreev reflections inside the S-c-S interface. Thus, the dynamic conductance curves exhibit a series of characteristic minima at voltages corresponding to Un = 2 /en, where n is a subharmonic number, which is the number of Andreev reflections the carriers experience when moving within the normal constriction. Higher subharmonic modes have shallower minima. Excess conductance at low biases, described as a ”foot” [Figs. 5(a) and 5(b)], is intrinsic to symmetrical Andreev contacts. In the case of a multigap superconductor each separate gap will have its own independent SGS [35]. Dynamic conductance spectrum for an array of m sequential S-c-S-c ...-S contacts will exhibit a SGS scaled by a factor of m. A series of experiments on AR for the NbS2 and NbSe2 single crystals were conducted at temperatures down to 1.5 K. Figure 5(a) shows normalized (the x axis is divided by the number of contacts in the stack for visual clarity) Andreev spectra of NbS2 for three contacts with a different number m of S-c-S contacts in the stack. We see a series of features at 1.85–1.70 V biases and 0.96–0.75 V biases. We treat both features as different gaps, as they are about the same amplitude and their temperature dependences have slightly different behaviors. A similar set of experiments was carried out for NbSe2 crystals [Figs. 5(d)–5(f)]. Here, one can see characteristic features at bias voltages UL = ±2.44−2.28 V and Us = ±1.40−1.15 V, which we assign to large and small gaps, and the features at lower bias voltage have deeper minima [particularly in the black and green curves of Fig. 5(d)]. The symmetric shapes of the troughs for both gaps in each compound implies an s-wave order parameter. The temperature dependences of these SGSs differ from each other. Figures 5(b) and 5(e) depict the temperature evolution of AR spectra, and Figs. 5(c) and 5(f) show energy gap temperature dependencies, extracted from Figs. 5(b) and 5(e), respectively. For NbS2, we obtain the characteristic BCS ratios L/KBTc = 2.01 ± 0.23, s/KBTc = 1.15 ± 0.24, which are well correlated with the scanning tunneling microscopy and heat capacity data [9], for NbSe2 L/KBTc = 2.1 ± 0.2, s/KBTc = 1.25 ± 0.2, which are in line with the heat capacity data from Ref. [17] and ARPES data (see Fig. 4 in Ref. [36]). The values of the BCS ratio for large gaps exceed the weak coupling BCS theory, which indicates a strong intraband interaction. 使用断裂结技术[34]进行了超导体-正常金属-超导体(SnS)结的本征多重安德列夫反射(IMARE)光谱测量。IMARE发生在SnS结的弹道输运区域，并在低偏压下产生过剩电流（产生所谓的"足"）和次谐波能隙结构。该结构由动态电导中的一系列最小值组成，每个最小值对应于电压值Vn = 2 /en，其中是超导能隙，e是基本电荷，n = 1, 2, . . .是次谐波阶数。现在我们转向使用安德列夫反射(AR)光谱研究超导体NbS2和NbSe2的超导能隙结构演化，该光谱通过断裂结技术在超导体-收缩-超导体(S-c-S)接触上获得。该方法使得在样品内部获得纯低温解理面成为可能，以及机械地重新排列接触区域（接触的几何形状、其面积及其在样品体内的位置）。如参考文献[34]所示，由于S-c-S界面内多重安德列夫反射的效应，在对称S-c-S接触的I-U特性上形成了一系列特征[所谓的次谐波能隙结构(SGS)]。因此，动态电导曲线在对应于Un = 2 /en的电压处展示一系列特征最小值，其中n是次谐波数，是载流子在正常收缩区内移动时经历的安德列夫反射次数。更高次谐波模式具有更浅的最小值。低偏压下的过剩电导，描述为"足"[图5(a)和5(b)]，是对称安德列夫接触的本征特性。在多能隙超导体的情况下，每个单独的能隙将有其自己独立的SGS [35]。m个连续S-c-S-c...-S接触阵列的动态电导谱将展示按因子m缩放的SGS。对NbS2和NbSe2单晶进行了一系列AR实验，温度低至1.5 K。图5(a)显示了NbS2的归一化（为视觉清晰，x轴除以堆栈中接触数）安德列夫光谱，针对堆栈中具有不同S-c-S接触数m的三个接触。我们看到在1.85–1.70 V偏压和0.96–0.75 V偏压处有一系列特征。我们将这两个特征视为不同的能隙，因为它们具有大致相同的幅度，并且它们的温度依赖性具有略微不同的行为。对NbSe2晶体进行了类似的一组实验[图5(d)–5(f)]。这里，可以看到在偏压UL = ±2.44−2.28 V和Us = ±1.40−1.15 V处的特征，我们将其归为大幅值和小幅值能隙，而较低偏压下的特征具有更深的最小值[特别是在图5(d)的黑色和绿色曲线中]。每种化合物中两个能隙的槽的对称形状暗示s波序参量。这些SGS的温度依赖性彼此不同。图5(b)和5(e)描绘了AR光谱的温度演化，图5(c)和5(f)显示了分别从图5(b)和5(e)提取的能隙温度依赖性。对于NbS2，我们获得特征BCS比值L/KBTc = 2.01 ± 0.23，s/KBTc = 1.15 ± 0.24，这与扫描隧道显微镜和热容数据[9]良好相关，对于NbSe2，L/KBTc = 2.1 ± 0.2，s/KBTc = 1.25 ± 0.2，这与参考文献[17]的热容数据和ARPES数据（见参考文献[36]中的图4）一致。大能隙的BCS比值超过弱耦合BCS理论值，表明存在强带内相互作用。

> ### F. Fermi surface calculations
> 
> ### F. 费米面计算
> 
> Initially, it was believed that FS nesting is predominantly responsible for the occurrence of CDW phenomena [37] based on an earlier model of a band structure of NbSe2 [38]. However, Whangbo et al. refuted this claim based on tight-binding FS studies [39]. In agreement with Johannes, the prevalence of charge density waves in 2H-NbSe2 is independent of the FS and any nesting [3]. However, the presence of a CDW was seen to be sensitive to the q-dependent electron phonon matrix and not the FS, which is beyond the scope of this work and paves the way for future work bridging the gap between CDW phenomena and electron-phonon coupling. Using ab initio methods, we have calculated the Fermi surfaces for both bulk 2H-NbSe2 [(Figs. 6(a)–6(d)] and 2H-NbS2 [(Figs. 6(f)–6(i)] for a range of pressure values. For 2H-NbSe2, between 1 and 2 GPa [Figs. 6(a) and 6(b)], the only change is the occurrence of a pancake-type electron pocket at the Ŵ point at the higher pressure. This electron pocket is due to the Se atoms. However, on further compression to 10 GPa [Figs. 6(c) and 6(d)], the FS topology does not change except for the overall size. Between 0 and 4.8 GPa for 2H-NbSe2, according to our experiment, the gradual removal of the CDW enhances the superconducting critical temperature [Fig. 4(c)]. Since, from our ab initio calculations, the pancake-type electron pocket is not seen before 2 GPa, we can possibly infer that the occurrence of this electron pocket at the Ŵ point could be the precursor to the suppression of the CDW. Above 4.8 GPa, the increment in Tc is much slower. This sluggish increase in both Tc and the superconducting gap above 4.8 GPa can be attributed to the following: (i) The absence of CDW suppression, which may impede the rise in Tc. (ii) The FS topology does not change upon compression, which is proposed to be a key ingredient for strong electronphonon coupling and superconductivity [40]. (iii) The absence of any nesting, as established in earlier work, which would affect the superconducting properties significantly. The fact that Tc is mostly affected by the large superconducting gap, which is due to the Nb atoms, is also evident from the FS topology as plotted in Figs. 6(a)–6(d). Unlike the Se-derived small electron pocket (where the small gap appears) at the Ŵ point, the Brillouin zone consists of more Fermi surfaces due to the Nb atoms (hence, the occurrence of large gap). On compression, the Nb-derived FS at the Ŵ point changes in shape upon compression. While it has a circular cross section at ambient pressure, it eventually evolves into having a more warped hexagonal cross section under pressure. In the case of 2H-NbS2, Tc increases very slowly with respect to pressure within the entire pressure range of 0 to 25 GPa [Fig. 4(d)]. It has already been mentioned that in NbS2, unlike NbSe2, a CDW does not occur. This can help explain why the pressure gradient of the superconducting critical temperature is also small compared to that of NbSe2. Similar to the reasons outlined in the preceding paragraph for NbSe2, for NbS2 we notice (i) a lack of significant changes in the FS for the entire studied pressure range [Figs. 6(f)–6(i)] and (ii) the absence of nesting. These are responsible for the small magnitude and modest change in Tc under pressure. Although a pancake type electron pocket appears due to the S atoms at the Ŵ point upon compression, similar to NbSe2, this does not contribute to the Tc as the larger band gap, due to the Nb atoms, plays a more dominant role. 最初，基于NbSe2能带结构的早期模型[38]，人们认为FS嵌套是CDW现象发生的主要原因[37]。然而，Whangbo等人基于紧束缚FS研究驳斥了这一说法[39]。与Johannes一致，2H-NbSe2中电荷密度波的普遍存在独立于FS和任何嵌套[3]。然而，CDW的存在被看到对q依赖的电子-声子矩阵而非FS敏感，这超出了本工作的范围，并为未来弥合CDW现象与电子-声子耦合之间差距的工作铺平了道路。使用从头算方法，我们计算了体材料2H-NbSe2 [图6(a)–6(d)]和2H-NbS2 [图6(f)–6(i)]在一系列压力值下的费米面。对于2H-NbSe2，在1至2 GPa之间[图6(a)和6(b)]，唯一的变化是在更高压力下Ŵ点出现一个薄饼型电子口袋。这个电子口袋归因于Se原子。然而，在进一步压缩至10 GPa时[图6(c)和6(d)]，除了整体尺寸外，FS拓扑没有变化。对于2H-NbSe2，在0至4.8 GPa之间，根据我们的实验，CDW的逐渐消除增强了超导临界温度[图4(c)]。由于从我们的从头算计算来看，2 GPa之前未看到薄饼型电子口袋，我们可能推断Ŵ点处该电子口袋的出现可能是CDW被抑制的前兆。在4.8 GPa以上，Tc的增加慢得多。4.8 GPa以上Tc和超导能隙的这种缓慢增加可归因于以下因素：(i) 缺乏CDW抑制，这可能阻碍Tc的上升。(ii) 压缩下FS拓扑不改变，这被认为是强电子-声子耦合和超导电性的关键因素[40]。(iii) 正如早期工作中所确立的，缺乏任何嵌套，这将显著影响超导性质。Tc主要受大超导能隙影响的事实，这归因于Nb原子，也从图6(a)–6(d)中绘制的FS拓扑中显而易见。与Ŵ点处Se衍生的小电子口袋（小能隙出现处）不同，布里渊区包含更多由于Nb原子导致的费米面（因此，出现大能隙）。在压缩下，Ŵ点处Nb衍生的FS在压缩下形状发生变化。虽然在常压下它具有圆形截面，但在压力下它最终演变为具有更扭曲的六边形截面。在2H-NbS2的情况下，Tc在整个0至25 GPa的压力范围内相对于压力增加非常缓慢[图4(d)]。已经提到，在NbS2中，与NbSe2不同，不发生CDW。这有助于解释为什么超导临界温度的压力梯度与NbSe2相比也较小。与前一段对NbSe2概述的原因类似，对于NbS2我们注意到(i)在整个研究压力范围内FS缺乏显著变化[图6(f)–6(i)]和(ii) 缺乏嵌套。这些是Tc在压力下幅度小且变化适中的原因。尽管在压缩下由于S原子在Ŵ点出现薄饼型电子口袋，类似于NbSe2，但这并不贡献于Tc，因为由于Nb原子导致的更大带隙起更主导的作用。

> ### G. Upper critical field under pressure
> 
> ### G. 压力下的上临界场
> 
> The upper critical field (Hc2) is one of the fundamental parameters in type-II superconductors, which provides valuable information on the microscopic origin of pair breaking and reflects the electronic structure responsible for superconductivity. To the best of our knowledge, there are no high-quality measurements of Hc2(T ) under pressure, down to 300 mK, in the conducting plane of 2H-NbS2 and 2H-NbSe2 neither hints as to whether it would be different from that of the well-studied data of both systems at ambient pressure. On the other hand, another important question is whether multiband superconductivity could exist in crossing of the Hc2 curves or not. Figure 3(d) summarizes the temperature dependence of the resistivity at 7 GPa under various magnetic fields down to 300 mK of NbSe2 system, see Fig. S3 for NbS2 data [20]. The criteria for determining Tc identifies the temperature at which the normal state line intersects with the maximum slope of the resistivity curve. The Hc2(T ) under pressure curves are presented in Fig. 7 for both NbSe2 (main panel) and NbS2 (as an inset). Clearly one can see different behavior of both systems. In NbS2, Hc2 tends to saturate at low temperature, while NbSe2 shows a curvature without saturation. Generally, suppression of superconductivity can be due to (i) Zeeman splitting that introduced to the singlet pairing and affect superconductivity down to particular values knows as Clogston-Chandrasekhar paramagnetic limit [41], and (ii) Cooper pairs orbital pair breaking in the superconducting state. We show that the fit of the Werthamer-Helfand-Hohenberg (WHH) [42] formula that includes the spin-paramagnetic effects (α = 0.75) and negligible spin orbit scattering (λso = 0) can effectively describe the whole measurement range of NbS2 data, see the inset of Fig. 7, and yields a critical field of 1.8 T. However, without considering α and λso the data deviates from a conventional WHH model, see the dashed line at the inset of Fig. 7. In the main panel of Fig. 7, the data of NbSe2 presents a clear curvature without saturation, which can originate from the multiband effects. Thus, we tried to fit the NbSe2 data by following the two-band model [43]. The best fit using the Gurevich model with D1 = 4.73 and η = 0.34 agrees very well with the upper critical field data in the whole temperature range and yields a critical field of 7.2 T. > ### H. Experimental determination of the lower critical field
> 
> ### H. 下临界场的实验确定
> 
> Figures 8(a) and 8(b) present the field dependence of the isothermal magnetization (M) at 2 K and under ambient / external pressure for NbSe2 and NbS2, respectively. Interestingly, one can notice that the isothermal magnetization for both samples are symmetric around M = 0. This illustrates the relatively weak surface barriers and most importantly are indicative of strong bulk pinning. These considerations hold for both systems; thus, the vortex penetration occurs at a field very close to lower critical field. However, to obtain reliable estimations of the thermodynamic lower critical field, as we will discuss below, the latter points are very critical. A more sensitive tool for characterizing the SC condensate and probing the gap structure of bulk superconductors is the London penetration depth, λ. The T dependence of λ is directly determined by the gap function (T ) : λ(T ) = λ(T = 0) + δλ(T ), with δλ(T ) ∝ exp(− /kBT) at low T, reflecting a nodeless superconducting gap with s-wave symmetry. In the d-wave pairing scenario containing line nodes, δλ(T ) ∝ T at low T in the clean limit. The experimental determination of the London penetration depth λ(0) can be done through measurements of the lower critical field Hc1. The most popular method to estimate Hc1 [shown in the insets of Figs. 8(d) and 8(e) and the obtained Hc1 values in Fig. S4 [20]] consists of detecting the transition from a Meissner-like linear M(H) regime to a nonlinear M(H) response, once the vortices penetrate into the sample and build up to a critical state. An additional way to determine the lower critical field is to measure the onset of the trapped moment Mt as described in Refs. [44,45] and shown in Fig. 8(c). In contrast to the first method, where a heavy post-processing of the data is needed, here a careful measurement protocol needs to be followed with little data analysis. Indeed, the trapped flux moment Mt is obtained by (i) warming the sample up to temperatures above Tc, then (ii) cooling the sample at zero field down to the chosen temperature, subsequently (iii) the external magnetic field is increased to a certain maximum value Hm, and (iv) measuring the remnant magnetization Mt after the applied field has been switched off. The field Hm at which Mt deviates from zero determines the value of Hc1 at the desired temperature. When considering the reversible magnetization, the trapped magnetic moment is Mt ∝ (H − Hc1)2. Then the extrapolation √Mt → 0 determines the exact value of Hc1. Once the values of Hc1 have been experimentally determined, we need to correct them by accounting for demagnetization effects. Indeed, the deflection of field lines around the sample leads to a more pronounced Meissner slope given by M/Ha = −1/(1 − N ), where N is the demagnetization factor. Taking into account these effects, the absolute value of Hc1 can be estimated using the relation proposed by Brandt [46]. For our sample we find N ∼ 0.96, and 0.95 for NbSe2 and NbS2, respectively. To shed light on the pairing symmetry in our systems, we estimated the penetration depth using the traditional Ginzburg-Landau (GL) theory, where Hc1 is given by μ0H ||c c1 = (φ0/4π λ2ab) ln κc, where φ0 is the magnetic-flux quantum (φ0 = h/e∗ = 2.07 × 10−7 Oe cm2), and κc = λab/ξab is the GL parameter. From the upper critical field values, we can estimate the GL coherence lengths to be ξab = 27.2(2) nm and 22.4(2) nm for both NbSe2 and NbS2 systems from the expression: H B||c c2 = (φ0/2π ξ 2ab ). > ## IV. DISCUSSION
> 
> ## IV. 讨论
> 
> Our results show that the variation of the FS parameters is monotonic (up to 25 GPa), and there are no maxima nor minima in 2H-NbS2. In 2H-NbSe2, a dome shape of Tc(P) is found with a maximum at approximately 10 GPa and the CDW disappears at 5 GPa. The a/c lattice constant ratio in 2H-NbS2 is smaller than that in 2H-NbS2 [32], so that pressure drives the system farther away from the CDW instability. Additionally, our data show that a maximum in Tc can be reached by going farther away from CDW instability. The values of the superconducting gap properties of NbSe2 and NbS2 are summarized and compared in Table S3. The obtained L values of both systems under pressure are higher than the weak-coupling BCS (1.76kBTc) gap value, which reflects a tendency for strong coupling effects, while the smaller gap, s, has a value lower than the BCS prediction. This is consistent with the theoretical constraints that one gap must be larger than BCS gap and one smaller in a weakly coupled two-band superconductor [48]. This deviation of the large gap from the BCS-limit could be caused by several factors, such as (i) the out-of-plane anisotropy of the order parameter discussed in [48], (ii) a complex and nontrivial in-plane angle distribution of the large gap in the k space, (iii) a possible presence of a large gap splitting, (iv) surface sensitivity of SC properties, (v) a significant contribution of high-energy ω > pairs with Re[ (ω)] > exp (where exp is a gap edge of the Eliashberg function) accounted in bulk probes. As for the small gap, the extracted value lies well below the BCS limit and points to a nonzero interaction between the condensates. It is important to note that the simple α model is not self-consistent, but is often used by experimentalists for fitting their thermodynamic data which deviates from the BCS predictions and for quantifying those deviations [49]. However, it is a matter of concern whether one, two, or three bands can properly describe our experimental data, since in the case of multiband superconductivity low-energy quasiparticle excitations can always be explained by the contribution from an electron group with a small gap. > ## V. CONCLUSIONS
> 
> ## V. 结论
> 
> To summarize, we studied the effect of pressure on the CDW transition and superconductivity in the NbSe2 and NbS2 systems. The rapid destruction of the CDW under pressure in NbSe2 is related to the quantum fluctuations of the lattice renormalized by the anharmonic part of the lattice potential. Our results also show that the superconducting gaps of both systems are hence very similar and, therefore, the CDW does not affect the superconducting gap structure. The estimated values of the penetration depth at T = 0 K confirm that NbSe2 and NbS2 superconductors depart from a Uemura-style relationship between Tc and λ−2ab (0). We have estimated the Hc2 phase diagram under pressure from electrical-transport measurements. The behavior for NbSe2 can be described by an effective two-band model, whereas the NbS2 data follow the WHH model including orbital pair-breaking and spin-paramagnetic effects. Our results highlight a competing nature between CDW and superconductivity, which may offer important clues for discussing the unconventional origins of superconductivity in this class of materials. Although, several scenarios have been put forward on the competing origin between electronic order and superconductivity, our results presented here provide an important benchmark for the future development of devices based on these materials. 总而言之，我们研究了压力对NbSe2和NbS2体系中CDW转变和超导电性的影响。NbSe2中CDW在压力下的快速破坏与由晶格势的非谐部分重整化的晶格量子涨落有关。我们的结果还表明，两个体系的超导能隙因此非常相似，因此CDW不影响超导能隙结构。T = 0 K时穿透深度的估计值证实NbSe2和NbS2超导体偏离了Tc与λ−2ab(0)之间的上村式关系。我们从电输运测量估计了压力下的Hc2相图。NbSe2的行为可由有效的双带模型描述，而NbS2数据遵循包括轨道拆对和自旋顺磁效应的WHH模型。我们的结果突出了CDW和超导电性之间的竞争性质，这可能为讨论这类材料中超导电性的非常规起源提供重要线索。尽管已经提出了几种关于电子有序和超导电性之间竞争起源的情况，但我们在此呈现的结果为基于这些材料的器件的未来发展提供了重要基准。
