> Abstract Density Functional Tight Binding (DFTB) calculations are performed to study atomic arrangements and charge distribution of Si(0 0 1) surfaces with one Ge atom adsorbate. For these Ge/Si(0 0 1) systems, the initial height between the Ge atom and the surface, and the adsorption position being relative to the Si dimers on the surfaces, have influences on the stable structures of the Ge atom on the Si surface. Firstly, two reconstructed Si(0 0 1) surfaces including c(4 × 2) and p(2 × 2) are obtained. Then, the Ge atom is placed on these two reconstructed surfaces. The calculations show the following results. For these dimers formed on the two Si(0 0 1) surfaces, dimer’s bond length, and the buckling angle between the dimer and the surface have apparent differences as well as the charge distribution of these two surfaces. The Ge atom prefers to be located at two positions of the dimer: the outside top position of one atom of the dimer and the bridge position of this dimer. In these systems, the Ge atom always loses charges, and the charge transfer is found between atoms of one Si dimer. The asymmetric deviation of the two atoms in the dimer greatly affects the structure and charge distribution of Ge atom and the dimer.

> ### abstract
> 
> 摘要
> 
> Density Functional Tight Binding (DFTB) calculations are performed to study atomic arrangements and charge distribution of Si(0 0 1) surfaces with one Ge atom adsorbate. For these Ge/Si(0 0 1) systems, the initial height between the Ge atom and the surface, and the adsorption position being relative to the Si dimers on the surfaces, have influences on the stable structures of the Ge atom on the Si surface. 采用密度泛函紧束缚（DFTB）计算研究了单个Ge原子吸附在Si(0 0 1)表面的原子排列和电荷分布。对于这些Ge/Si(0 0 1)体系，Ge原子与表面的初始高度，以及相对于表面Si二聚体的吸附位置，都会影响Ge原子在Si表面的稳定结构。 **Dimer**: 二聚体，指两个原子结合在一起形成的结构单元。
> 
> Firstly, two reconstructed Si(0 0 1) surfaces including c(4 2) and p(2 2) are obtained. Then, the Ge atom is placed on these two reconstructed surfaces. 首先，获得了两种重构的Si(0 0 1)表面，包括 c(4 2) 和 p(2 2) 结构。随后，将Ge原子放置在这两种重构表面上。 **Reconstructed surface**: 重构表面，指固体表面原子为了降低表面能而发生重新排列形成的结构。
> 
> The calculations show the following results. For these dimers formed on the two Si(0 0 1) surfaces, dimer’s bond length, and the buckling angle between the dimer and the surface have apparent differences as well as the charge distribution of these two surfaces. 计算结果显示：在两种Si(0 0 1)表面上形成的二聚体，其键长、二聚体与表面之间的翘曲角以及两表面的电荷分布均存在明显差异。 **Buckling angle**: 翘曲角，指二聚体中两个原子相对于表面平面高度不一致而产生的倾斜角度。
> 
> The Ge atom prefers to be located at two positions of the dimer: the outside top position of one atom of the dimer and the bridge position of this dimer. In these systems, the Ge atom always loses charges, and the charge transfer is found between atoms of one Si dimer. The asymmetric deviation of the two atoms in the dimer greatly affects the structure and charge distribution of Ge atom and the dimer. Ge原子倾向于位于二聚体的两个位置：二聚体中某个原子的外部顶端位置或二聚体的桥接位置。在这些体系中，Ge原子始终失去电荷，且在单个Si二聚体的原子之间发现了电荷转移。二聚体中两个原子的非对称偏差极大地影响了Ge原子及二聚体的结构和电荷分布。
> 
> # 1. Introduction
> 
> 1.  引言
> 
> SiGe/Si heterogeneous materials have been widely used in microelectronics and optoelectronic devices. Preparation of precise and controllable SiGe/Si heterogeneous nanometer materials increases the performance of electronic devices [1–5]. SiGe/Si异质材料已广泛应用于微电子和光电子器件。制备精确且可控的SiGe/Si异质纳米材料能够提升电子器件的性能 [1–5]。
> 
> Li et al.’s studies show that ultra-thin layers or monolayer Si/Ge films can improve the high responsivity of the vertical-illumination Si/Ge uni-traveling carrier (UTC) devices because of nanoscale effect [1]. Li等人的研究表明，由于纳米尺度效应，超薄层或单层Si/Ge薄膜可以提高垂直照明Si/Ge单行载流子（UTC）器件的高响应度 [1]。
> 
> In the process of epitaxial growth to prepare SiGe/Si heterogeneous materials, the adsorption of Ge atoms on the surface of Si substrate is the first step of the growth. When Ge atoms are adsorbed on Si(0 0 1) surface, the changes of the Si(0 0 1) surface can be observed both in experiments [6,7] and theoretical calculations [8–12]. 在制备SiGe/Si异质材料的外延生长过程中，Ge原子在Si衬底表面的吸附是生长的第一步。当Ge原子吸附在Si(0 0 1)表面时，在实验 [6,7] 和理论计算 [8–12] 中均能观察到Si(0 0 1)表面的变化。 **Epitaxial growth**: 外延生长，指在单晶衬底上生长一层具有相同晶体取向的单晶薄膜的过程。
> 
> Krüger et al. found that the Si substrate surface presents metallic properties as the Ge atoms were adsorbed on special positions of the Si substrate [8]. Therefore, one of the key problems is arisen on how to control sedimentary position of Ge atoms on Si substrates in the epitaxial growth process. Krüger等人发现，当Ge原子吸附在Si衬底的特殊位置时，Si衬底表面呈现出金属特性 [8]。因此，如何在外延生长过程中控制Ge原子在Si衬底上的沉积位置成为了关键问题之一。
> 
> In laboratory, atom rearrangements on the Si substrate surface occur, and the reconstructed surface plays an important role in forming SiGe/Si films by epitaxial growth technology [13–15]. Here, the c(4 2) and p(2 2) reconstructed surfaces of the Si(0 0 1) substrates have been observed both in experimental and theoretical studies [16,17]. 在实验室中，Si衬底表面会发生原子重排，重构表面在外延生长技术形成SiGe/Si薄膜的过程中起着重要作用 [13–15]。目前，在实验和理论研究中均观察到了Si(0 0 1)衬底的 c(4 2) 和 p(2 2) 重构表面 [16,17]。
> 
> Therefore, the adsorption positions as well as surface structures of the substrate greatly affect the structures and properties of the substrate surfaces in the process of the heterogeneous epitaxial growth. In this paper, Density Functional Tight Binding (DFTB) simulations are performed to study the effect of some possible adsorption positions on the structure stability and charge distribution for the systems of the adsorbed Ge atom on the Si(0 0 1) surfaces, where the Si(0 0 1) surfaces with asymmetric dimers present the c(4 2) and p(2 2) patterns. 因此，在异质外延生长过程中，吸附位置以及衬底的表面结构极大影响了衬底表面的结构和性质。本文采用密度泛函紧束缚（DFTB）模拟研究了Ge原子吸附在Si(0 0 1)表面体系中，若干可能的吸附位置对结构稳定性和电荷分布的影响，其中具有非对称二聚体的Si(0 0 1)表面呈现 c(4 2) 和 p(2 2) 模式。
> 
> # 2. Computational details
> 
> 2.  计算细节
> 
> The source code and Slater-Koster files including Si-Si, Ge-Ge, Si-Ge, and Ge-Si are provided by Dr. Yi Dong and Professor Michael Springborg of University of Saarland in Germany. These codes and S-K files have been used to describe the infinite, periodic, and crystalline systems. 源代码和包括Si-Si, Ge-Ge, Si-Ge, 和 Ge-Si在内的Slater-Koster文件由德国萨尔大学的Yi Dong博士和Michael Springborg教授提供。这些代码和S-K文件已被用于描述无限、周期性和晶体体系。 **Slater-Koster files**: Slater-Koster文件，包含预计算的二中心积分，是紧束缚方法中用于快速计算哈密顿矩阵的核心参数文件。
> 
> Using these S-K files, the optimized lattice constants of crystalline Si and Ge are 5.46 and 5.71 Å respectively, which are less than 1% of the experimental values [18,19]. In our calculations, the energy threshold value is set to 0.0002 eV. When the difference between the energy values of the present step and the previous step is less than this threshold value, the calculations stop. If this constraint condition is not satisfied, each calculation runs 3000 steps. 利用这些S-K文件，优化后的晶体Si和Ge的晶格常数分别为5.46和5.71 Å，与实验值的误差小于1% [18,19]。在我们的计算中，能量阈值设定为0.0002 eV。当当前步骤与前一步的能量值之差小于该阈值时，计算停止。若不满足此约束条件，则每次计算运行3000步。
> 
> Initially, in order to obtain different surface structures which was observed in experiments, two diamond Si(0 0 1) slabs are periodically built with 3 3 and 6 6 unit cells along [1 0 0] and [0 1 0] directions respectively, and there are 3 atomic layers (about 0.272-nm-thickness) along the [0 0 1] direction, as shown in Fig. 1(a)-(d). 最初，为了获得实验中观察到的不同表面结构，沿 [1 0 0] 和 [0 1 0] 方向分别构建了包含 3×3 和 6×6 晶胞的两个周期性金刚石结构Si(0 0 1)平板（slab），沿 [0 0 1] 方向有3个原子层（厚度约为0.272 nm），如图1(a)-(d)所示。
> 
> A vacuum gap with the width of 265.00 Å is introduced between the slabs. Here, the lattice constant a0 of Si bulk is 5.43 Å. In these figures, green balls represent the bottom layer corresponding to the lower surface, orange pink balls the middle layer, and purple balls the top layer corresponding to the upper surface. 平板之间引入了宽度为 265.00 Å 的真空层。此处，体相Si的晶格常数 a0 为 5.43 Å。在这些图中，绿色球代表对应于底表面的底层，橙粉色球代表中间层，紫色球代表对应于顶表面的顶层。
> 
> Fig. 1(a) and (c) are top views along the [0 0 1] direction of these two Si slabs, and the side views are shown in Fig. 1(b) and (d) along the [0 0 1] and [1 1 0] directions. Then, DFTB algorithm is used to obtain reconstructed surfaces of these built surfaces. After obtaining these surfaces, one Ge atom is placed on these reconstructed Si(0 0 1) surfaces with different positions. 图1(a)和(c)是这两个Si平板沿 [0 0 1] 方向的俯视图，图1(b)和(d)是沿 [0 0 1] 和 [1 1 0] 方向的侧视图。随后，使用DFTB算法获得这些构建表面的重构结构。在获得这些表面后，将一个Ge原子放置在这些重构Si(0 0 1)表面的不同位置。
> 
> When the epitaxial Ge atom adsorbed on the Si(0 0 1) surface, the adsorption energy is: 当外延Ge原子吸附在Si(0 0 1)表面时，吸附能为：
> 
> $$E_{ads}(Ge/Si_{slab}) = E_{tot}(Si_{slab}) + E(Ge) - E_{tot}(Ge/Si_{slab}) \quad (1)$$
> 
> where $E_{tot}$ is the total energy of the calculated system, $E_{ads}$ the adsorption energy, and $E(Ge)$ the energy of Ge single atom, which is the energy sum of s and p orbits provided in the S-K file for Ge-Ge. 其中，$E_{tot}$ 为计算体系的总能量，$E_{ads}$ 为吸附能，$E(Ge)$ 为单个Ge原子的能量（即Ge-Ge S-K文件中提供的s和p轨道的能量总和）。
> 
> The surface energy can be calculated from the following equation, 表面能可以通过以下方程计算：
> 
> $$\gamma = \frac{E_{slab} - N E_{bulk}}{2A} \quad (2)$$
> 
> where $E_{slab}$ is the total energy of surface slab, $N$ is the number of atoms in this slab, $E_{bulk}$ is the energy per atom in bulk phase, and $A$ is the surface area. 其中，$E_{slab}$ 是表面平板的总能量，$N$ 是平板中的原子数，$E_{bulk}$ 是体相中的单原子能量，$A$ 是表面积。
> 
> # 3. Results and discussions
> 
> 3.  结果与讨论
> 
> ## 3.1. Reconstructed Si(0 0 1) surface
> 
> 3.1. 重构的Si(0 0 1)表面
> 
> Figs. 2 and 3 show different patterns of the reconstructed Si(0 0 1) surfaces with 3 3 and 6 6 unit cells. As shown the top views of these two Si slabs along the [0 0 1] direction in Figs. 2 and 3 (a), the atoms in the top layer move close to each other along the [1 1 0] direction, and these atoms alternately form dimers having “Zigzag” patterns. 图2和图3显示了具有 3×3 和 6×6 晶胞的重构Si(0 0 1)表面的不同模式。如图2和图3(a)中这两个Si平板沿 [0 0 1] 方向的俯视图所示，顶层的原子沿 [1 1 0] 方向相互靠近，这些原子交替形成具有“之字形”（Zigzag）模式的二聚体。
> 
> Meanwhile, some of these atoms in these dimers present upward movements along the [0 0 1] direction as shown the side views along the [0 0 1] and [1 1 0] directions, whereas the others downward movements. Figs. 2 and 3(b) show the buckling on the surfaces, where the dimers on the upper surface present different patterns. 同时，如沿 [0 0 1] 和 [1 1 0] 方向的侧视图所示，这些二聚体中的某些原子沿 [0 0 1] 方向向上移动，而其他原子则向下移动。图2和图3(b)显示了表面的翘曲情况，顶表面的二聚体呈现出不同的模式。
> 
> As shown in Figs. 2 and 3(c), along the [ -1 1 0] direction, the two atoms in one dimer are upward and downward respectively from the surface. For the case of the 3 3 unit cells, the plaid purple balls represent the upward atoms from the surface, and the other purple balls the downward atoms from the surface. 如图2和图3(c)所示，沿 [ -1 1 0] 方向，一个二聚体中的两个原子分别相对于表面向上和向下。对于 3×3 晶胞的情况，网格紫色球代表相对于表面向上的原子，其他紫色球代表向下的原子。
> 
> As observed along the [ -1 1 0] direction, for the two interval dimer columns being connected with solid lines, including dimer_1 to dimer_4, their buckling direction is consistent with each other, and the similar picture also appears for the two interval dimer rows connected with solid lines along the [1 1 0] direction. > # 4. Conclusions
> 
> 4.  结论
> 
> In this paper, the behaviors of a Ge atom adsorbed on the Si(0 0 1) surfaces with different initial conditions are studied by using DFTB algorithm. We obtained the two different reconstructed Si (0 0 1) surfaces: p(2 2) and c(4 2) patterns. 本文利用DFTB算法研究了在不同初始条件下吸附在Si(0 0 1)表面的单个Ge原子的行为。我们获得了两种不同的重构 Si(0 0 1) 表面：p(2 2) 和 c(4 2) 模式。
> 
> The bond length, buckling angle and charge distribution of the dimers in the two reconstructed surfaces present significantly differences. The charge distribution of the p(2 2) pattern surface presents interval zonal distribution among the obtaining and losing charge areas, whereas the c(2 4) pattern surface the interval block distribution. 两种重构表面中二聚体的键长、翘曲角和电荷分布呈现出显著差异。p(2 2) 模式表面的电荷分布呈现出获得电荷区与失去电荷区交替带状分布，而 c(2 4) 模式表面则呈现出交替块状分布。
> 
> When the Ge atom is adsorbed on these two surfaces, the initial height and initial position to the dimer have obvious effect on the stable position, adsorption energy and charge distribution of the Ge atom. The adsorbed Ge atom prefers to be located at two positions of the dimer: the outside top position of one atom of the dimer and the bridge position of this dimer. 当Ge原子吸附在两种表面上时，初始高度和相对于二聚体的初始位置对Ge原子的稳定位置、吸附能和电荷分布有明显影响。吸附的Ge原子倾向于位于二聚体的两个位置：二聚体中某个原子的外部顶端位置或该二聚体的桥接位置。
> 
> The upward or downward deviation of the two atoms in one dimer greatly affects the structure and charge distribution of Ge atom and dimer. When the Ge atom is above the outside top of the upward Si atom, the bond length of the dimer is shortened and the buckling angle increases. The Ge atom loses more charges, the two Si atoms in the dimer present that one losses charge, the other obtains charges, and then the charge transfer between the atoms increases. 一个二聚体中两个原子的向上或向下偏差极大地影响了Ge原子和二聚体的结构和电荷分布。当Ge原子位于向上Si原子的外部顶端上方时，二聚体键长缩短且翘曲角增加。Ge原子失去更多电荷，二聚体中的两个Si原子呈现一个失去电荷、另一个获得电荷的状态，从而增加了原子间的电荷转移。
> 
> In the case of the bridge position as Ge atom biased towards the upward atom, the charge distribution also presents similar phenomenon. When the Ge atom is located on the top of the downward Si atom, the Ge atom loses less charge, and both of the two Si atoms in the dimer obtain charge accompanying with the decrease of the buckling angle. 在Ge原子偏向向上原子的桥接位置情况下，电荷分布也呈现类似现象。当Ge原子位于向下Si原子的顶端时，Ge原子失去的电荷较少，且二聚体中的两个Si原子均获得电荷，同时伴随着翘曲角的减小。
> 
> These also occur in the case of the adsorption at both the center bridge position and the decentered bridge position of the dimer, where the decentered bridge position is biased towards the upward atom. 这种情况同样出现在二聚体的中心桥接位置以及偏心桥接位置（偏心桥接位置偏向向上原子）的吸附中。
