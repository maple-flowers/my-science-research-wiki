> ## 一、引言
> 
> 在材料科学领域，通过向晶体中引入“**客体物种（Guest Species）**”——即原子、离子或分子——来调控其性质，是实现“按需设计材料”这一终极目标的核心策略。然而，传统三维晶体紧密的晶格结构限制了这一策略的广泛应用。近年来，以**过渡金属硫族化合物（TMDs）** 为代表的**二维层状材料（2D Layered Materials）** 的兴起，为这一经典方法论注入了全新活力。
> 
> 本综述由斯坦福大学的Harold Y. Hwang教授和Yi Cui教授团队联合撰写，系统性地阐述了两种在不破坏化学键的前提下，对二维材料进行可逆、动态调控的关键技术：**静电栅控（Electrostatic Gating）** 和**插层（Intercalation）**。文章的核心价值在于构建了一个从物理机制、实验方法到材料功能调控的完整知识框架，不仅梳理了该领域的研究进展，更展望了其在设计超越传统方法限制的新型人工材料方面的巨大潜力。对于希望快速把握该领域核心脉络的初学者和研究者而言，这是一份权威且全面的导引。
>

> ## 四、正文核心内容剖析
> 
> ### 1. 研究背景与问题定义
> 
> 材料科学的核心目标之一是“按需设计材料”。传统上，科学家通过掺杂、相控制、应变等手段，向三维母体材料中引入电子或空穴，以调控其电学、光学和结构性质，由此发展出“客体物种改性”的概念。然而，这些方法通常涉及高温或强外力，易引入缺陷，且受限于三维材料紧密的晶格结构。文章开篇即指出，**二维层状材料**的出现彻底改变了这一局面。其层内以强共价键结合，层间则为弱的**范德华（van der Waals）相互作用**。这种各向异性的键合结构，使得客体物种可以轻松地**进入层间间隙（范德华间隙）** 或**吸附于表面**，在不破坏层内共价键的前提下，实现对材料性质的深度、可逆调控。这为材料科学打开了一扇通往全新可能性的大门。
> 
> ### 2. 核心方法解析
> 
> 文章的核心贡献在于，它将纷繁复杂的调控手段，凝练成两条清晰的技术路径，并进行了深度的对比分析。
> 
> #### 路径一：客体物种栅控——物理吸附与静电调控
> 
> 此方法的核心是构建一个**双电层晶体管（EDLT）**。其本质类似于一个超级电容器，而非传统的场效应晶体管。
> 
> -   **物理机制**：**双电层（EDL）** 的形成是核心。当在浸没于电解质（含可移动离子）中的电极与沟道材料之间施加电压时，电解质中的离子会迁移并聚集在电极/电解质界面，形成一个厚度仅为~1纳米（相当于离子半径）的电荷层。由于电荷分离距离极小，这个“双电层”的单位面积电容（C）极高，可达几到几十 μF/cm²，远超传统SiO₂的~0.1 μF/cm²。
> -   **技术对比**：文章详细对比了两种实现形式。
>     -   **液态/凝胶电解质**：如离子液体（DEME-TFSI）或锂盐聚合物（PEO:LiClO₄）。优点是电容极大，调控能力强，能实现10¹⁴ cm⁻²级别的载流子浓度。缺点是低温下易发生玻璃化转变，产生应变，且稳定性、与半导体工艺的兼容性较差。
>     -   **固态陶瓷电解质**：如锂离子导体（LiCGC）、钠离子导体、氟离子导体（LaF₃）。这是近年来的重要突破。优点在于机械刚性（可作为衬底）、无低温应变、与超高真空和表面表征技术（如光电子能谱）兼容。发展前景广阔，但技术尚在完善中。
> 
> #### 路径二：客体物种（脱）插层——化学插入与键合重构
> 
> 当栅极电压过高，离子获得足够能量克服能垒，就会从表面的物理吸附转变为进入晶格内部的化学插层。这是一种更深刻、更不可逆（或在特定条件下可逆）的调控方式。
>

> ## 四、正文核心内容剖析
> 
> ### 1. 研究背景：为什么需要“客人”？
> 
> （对应原文引言部分） 传统材料调制的核心是引入**客体物种**（原子、离子、分子）来改变电子结构。其关键特征在于：(i) 不同元素提供不同的电子自由度；(ii) 客体的动态迁移性允许可逆调节；(iii) 保留结构骨架的**拓扑化学转变**能合成亚稳态新材料。然而，在3D材料中，强行插入客体物种往往会破坏化学键，带来缺陷和结构形变。**二维（2D）材料**的出现彻底改变了这一局面。
> 
> ### 2. 方法/模型：两大核心“武器”
> 
> （对应原文“Paths of guest species modulation”章节） 文章将核心技术分为两大路径，其核心区别在于客体物种的位置：
> 
> -   **路径一：静电门控 —— “表面电荷魔法”**
>     
>     -   **核心模型**：**电双层晶体管（EDLT）**。它模仿传统MOSFET，但将栅极介质从固态氧化物换成了含有可移动离子的**电解质**。
>     -   **工作机制**：当施加栅极电压时，电解质中的离子移动到材料（沟道）表面，形成一个厚度仅约1纳米的**电双层**。这个超薄的电双层相当于一个巨大的电容（C = dσ/dΨ），能以极低的电压（~几伏）在材料表面诱导出极高的电荷密度（高达10¹⁴ cm⁻²）。
>     -   **技术实现**：
>         -   **液态/凝胶/聚合物电解质**：如离子液体、含Li⁺的聚合物（PEO:LiClO₄）。优点是电容大，缺点是低温下可能玻璃化、稳定性差、与半导体工艺不兼容。
>         -   **固态陶瓷电解质**：如LiCGC、Na⁺导体、LaF₃等。这是近年来的重要进展，它解决了液态电解质的诸多缺点，具有机械刚性、支持器件集成化、表面开放（可与光谱学表征联用）、兼容超高真空等巨大优势。
>     -   **强大效应**：在如此高的载流子浓度下，许多新奇的量子现象被激发，如：在MoS₂中诱导出**超导**；在Fe₃GeTe₂中实现**室温铁磁性**；在Cr₂Ge₂Te6中翻转**磁各向异性**；驱动MoTe₂的**结构相变**（半导体-金属）；高效调制WS₂的**光学折射率**。
> -   **路径二：插层 —— “内部化学改造”**
>     
>     -   **核心机制**：利用二维材料层间的弱范德华力，将客体物种插入层间间隙。
>     -   **插层物种**：
>         -   **原子**：零价态（如Cu, Ag, Au），与主体相互作用弱，可实现高浓度插层。
>         -   **离子**：带电（如Li⁺, Na⁺, K⁺, 过渡金属离子），会产生电荷转移，与主体形成强离子键或共价键，显著改变电子结构。

> 1.  **问题：门控和插层到底有什么区别？能打个比方吗？** **解答**：可以想象一个书架上的书（二维材料层）。
>     
>     -   **静电门控**就像你拿着一块强磁铁贴在书脊的表面，它能吸引或排斥书页中的“电子墨水”（电荷），改变书页表面的电学性质，但磁铁本身没有进入书里。

> 2.  **问题：为什么电双层能有那么大的电容，从而诱导出那么高的载流子密度？** **解答**：电容的公式是 C = ε * A / d，其中 A 是面积，d 是距离，ε 是介电常数。在**电双层（EDL）** 中，正负电荷在电极和电解质界面两侧分开，距离 d 大约只有1纳米（离子半径尺度），这比传统SiO₂栅极介质层（几十到几百纳米）薄了近百倍。因此，在面积和介电常数相近的情况下，EDL的电容可以高出1-2个数量级。由公式 Q = C * V（电荷=电容*电压）可知，在相同电压下，电容越大，存储的电荷越多，因此能在材料表面诱导出极高的载流子密度。

> The doping/alteration of crystals with guest species (atoms, ions, and molecules) for desired properties has long been a research frontier in materials science. However, the high dimensionality and closely-packed lattice structure in many crystals have limited the applicability of this strategy. The advent of two-dimensional layered materials has revitalized efforts utilizing this powerful methodology via two important approaches, gating and intercalation, offering a prominent and reversible modulation of the targeted materials without breaking chemical bonds. In addition, these dynamically tunable techniques have enabled the synthesis of many new hybrid materials. Here we review the exciting opportunities offered by the interactions between guest species and the host 2D materials in tuning physics and chemistry of the materials system and discuss their remarkable potential for creating artificial materials and architectures beyond the reach of conventional methods. 利用客体物种（原子、离子和分子）对晶体进行掺杂/改性以获得所需性质，长期以来一直是材料科学的研究前沿。然而，许多晶体的高维度和紧密堆积的晶格结构限制了这一策略的适用性。二维层状材料的出现，通过两种重要方法——门控和插层——重新激发了利用这一强大方法论的努力，在不破坏化学键的情况下，为目标材料提供了显著且可逆的调控。此外，这些动态可调技术使得许多新型杂化材料的合成成为可能。本文回顾了客体物种与主体二维材料之间的相互作用在调控材料体系物理和化学性质方面所提供的令人兴奋的机遇，并讨论了它们在创造超越传统方法所能及的人工材料和结构方面的非凡潜力。
> 
> **Guest species**: 客体物种——指引入主体材料中以改变其性质的原子、离子或分子。 **Gating**: 门控——通过施加电场在材料表面积累电荷以调控其性质的技术。 **Intercalation**: 插层——将客体物种可逆地插入层状材料层间间隙的过程。 **2D materials**: 二维材料——具有原子级厚度、层内以共价键结合、层间以范德华力结合的层状材料。

> # Introduction
> 
> 引言
> 
> The quest for materials by design has long propelled scientists to tailor new materials with desired functionalities. For centuries, a variety of control and modulation tools have been developed to tune the properties of archetypal materials systems, via doping, phase control, defects, and strain, etc.1-5 For the past few decades, the on-demand introduction of electrons (holes) or ions into a host material, for the purpose of engineering its electrical, optical, and structural properties, has been widely employed to enable revolutionary advances in semiconductor technology, consequently developing the concept of guest species (atoms, ions, molecules) modification. 按需设计材料的追求长期以来一直推动着科学家们定制具有所需功能的新材料。几个世纪以来，人们开发了多种控制和调控工具，通过掺杂、相控制、缺陷和应变等方法来调控原型材料体系的性质。1-5 在过去几十年中，为了调控材料的电学、光学和结构性质，按需向主体材料中引入电子（空穴）或离子的方法已被广泛应用，推动了半导体技术的革命性进步，由此发展了客体物种（原子、离子、分子）改性的概念。
> 
> There are several key features pertaining to this modulation method inherent to the properties of these guest species. (i) The electronic-structure degrees of freedom, made available through incorporating different elements, can induce a broad range of doping and distinct types of interactions within the material. (ii) The dynamic and mobile nature of the guest species allows for the flexibility of reversible tuning. (iii) Topotactic transformation, which preserves the materials' structural topology and takes advantage of imbalanced kinetic properties of different ion species, leads to the synthesis of new materials with metastable lattice environments and valence states. More importantly, as will be discussed later in the text, building upon the intersection of chemical composition variations and electrostatic modulation, with ionic tuning as a prominent example, substantially expands the tunable range that is beyond the limits of conventional approaches. 这种调控方法有几个关键特征，与这些客体物种的性质密切相关：(i) 通过引入不同元素所获得的电子结构自由度，可以在材料内部诱导广泛的掺杂和不同类型的相互作用；(ii) 客体物种的动态和可移动特性允许灵活的可逆调控；(iii) 拓扑化学转变保持了材料的结构拓扑，并利用不同离子物种不平衡的动力学性质，从而合成具有亚稳态晶格环境和价态的新材料。更重要的是，正如后文将讨论的，在化学成分变化和静电调控的交叉点上，以离子调控为突出例子，极大地扩展了超越传统方法极限的可调范围。
> 
> **Topotactic transformation**: 拓扑化学转变——一种在保持材料基本结构框架的同时，通过插入或移除特定原子/离子来改变其组成和性质的固相化学反应。 **Topochemical**: 拓扑化学的——描述在保持主体晶体结构基本拓扑特征的同时进行的化学反应。
> 
> Two-dimensional (2D) materials, especially 2D transition metal dichalcogenides (TMDs), has brought new vigor and vitality into this field, presenting a perfect platform with a confined, atomically-thin layered structure.6-8 2D materials are suitable owing to their anisotropic bonding (covalent within the layer and van der Waals between layers). The weak interlayer interaction facilitates the isolation of monolayer flakes, whose properties are extremely sensitive to changes in the surrounding environment.6, 7 In addition, guest species insertion into the layered 2D materials can be achieved by accessing the van der Waals gap without breaking the intralayer covalent bonds, not only maintaining the host structure of 2D materials but also confining the doped species.9 Furthermore, combining the diversity of the rapidly expanding 2D materials family and the flexibility for creating copious artificial materials architectures, guest species tuning demarcates a variable pathway to novel materials systems that are anticipated to have significant potential in quantum devices and energy applications.10-12 Apart from the transition metal dichalcogenides, research on layered transition-metal oxides, another heavily studied family of materials, has been fueled with significant new opportunities with reduced dimension as well. The layered transition metal oxides attract tremendous research interest due to their sophisticated structural chemistry and the wide span of physical properties these phases display.13 They often host "many-body" physics problems owing to the strong electronic correlations of the transition metal d orbitals and the entanglement between charge, spin and lattice degrees of freedom, facilitated by the hybridization between transition metal cations and oxygen anions.14 A defining consequence of these strong interactions is a broad spectrum of functionalities, spanning from ferroic orders15 to superconductivity.16 The different energy landscapes present in the materials systems promote intertwined phases constituting rich phase diagrams with competing orders and ground states, which are extremely sensitive to tuning parameters, from the transition metal oxidation state to the chemical environment.13, 14, 16 In this regard, the interaction of transition metal oxides and guest species has attracted enduring interest in materials synthesis research. 二维材料，特别是二维过渡金属二硫族化物（TMDs），为这一领域注入了新的活力，提供了一个具有限域、原子级薄层状结构的完美平台。6-8 二维材料因其各向异性键合（层内共价键，层间范德华键）而非常适合。弱的层间相互作用有利于单层薄片的剥离，其性质对周围环境的变化极其敏感。6, 7 此外，通过进入范德华间隙而不破坏层内共价键，可以实现客体物种插入层状二维材料，不仅保持了二维材料的主体结构，而且限域了掺杂物种。9 此外，结合快速扩展的二维材料家族的多样性和创造丰富人工材料结构的灵活性，客体物种调控开辟了一条通往新型材料体系的可变途径，这些体系有望在量子器件和能源应用中具有巨大潜力。10-12 除过渡金属二硫族化物外，层状过渡金属氧化物——另一类被广泛研究的材料家族——也因维度降低而获得了重要的新机遇。层状过渡金属氧化物因其复杂的结构化学和这些相所展示的广泛物理性质而吸引了巨大的研究兴趣。13 它们通常承载着"多体"物理问题，这是由于过渡金属d轨道的强电子关联性以及电荷、自旋和晶格自由度之间的纠缠，而过渡金属阳离子与氧阴离子之间的杂化促进了这种纠缠。14 这些强相互作用的一个决定性后果是广泛的功能性，涵盖从铁性序15到超导电性16。材料体系中存在的不同能量景观促进了相互交织的相，构成了具有竞争序和基态的丰富相图，这些相图对调控参数——从过渡金属氧化态到化学环境——极其敏感。13, 14, 16 在这方面，过渡金属氧化物与客体物种的相互作用在材料合成研究中引起了持久的兴趣。
> 
> **Transition metal dichalcogenides (TMDs)**: 过渡金属二硫族化物——一类具有MX₂化学式的层状材料（M为过渡金属，X为硫族元素），具有丰富的电子性质。 **van der Waals gap**: 范德华间隙——层状材料中由范德华力连接的层间空隙区域。 **Many-body physics**: 多体物理——研究大量粒子之间相互作用所导致的集体行为的物理学分支。
> 
> In this review, we focus on how the concept of guest species tuning is applied to 2D layered chalcogenides and oxides, and how the introduction of guest species expands our understanding and the potential applications in these materials. We will first introduce the two major techniques that can be used for guest species tuning. The mechanism and experimental design of each technique will be briefly discussed. Then, we will present the exciting progress enabled by guest species modulation, covering the current research interest in both application-oriented work and new phenomena discovered. Finally, we aim to provide an outlook on the immense possibilities that can be expected with the ionic tuning methodology in these materials systems composed of 2D layered chalcogenides and oxides, with the hope of extension to more diverse material families. 在本综述中，我们重点关注客体物种调控的概念如何应用于二维层状硫族化物和氧化物，以及客体物种的引入如何扩展我们对这些材料的理解和潜在应用。我们将首先介绍可用于客体物种调控的两种主要技术，简要讨论每种技术的机制和实验设计。然后，我们将介绍客体物种调控所实现的令人振奋的进展，涵盖当前在应用导向工作和新发现现象方面的研究兴趣。最后，我们旨在展望在这些由二维层状硫族化物和氧化物组成的材料体系中，离子调控方法论可预期的巨大可能性，并希望扩展到更多样化的材料家族。
> 
> # Paths of guest species modulation
> 
> 客体物种调控的途径
> 
> Traditional guest species tuning of 3D materials is mainly rooted in the chemical reaction between host materials and guest ions, introducing electron (hole) donors (acceptors) in the host materials while forming covalent bonding with surrounding atoms (Fig. 1b). The forcible insertion of guest species into the host materials inevitably introduces defects and structural deformation in the host materials. Instead, the layered and thin nature of the 2D materials allows two new categories of interaction between ions and the 2D materials without disrupting the existing convent bonds: 1) the guest species reside on the atomic surface and polarize the 2D materials (i.e., gating, Fig. 1c); 2) the ions migrate into the interlayer sites and chemically interact with the 2D materials (i.e., intercalation, Fig. 1d). 三维材料的传统客体物种调控主要植根于主体材料与客体离子之间的化学反应，在主体材料中引入电子（空穴）给体（受体），同时与周围原子形成共价键（图1b）。将客体物种强行插入主体材料不可避免地会在主体材料中引入缺陷和结构变形。相反，二维材料的层状和薄层特性允许离子与二维材料之间产生两类新的相互作用，而不破坏现有的共价键：1）客体物种驻留在原子表面并极化二维材料（即门控，图1c）；2）离子迁移到层间位点并与二维材料发生化学相互作用（即插层，图1d）。
> 
> ## Guest species gating
> 
> 客体物种门控
> 
> Guest species gating resembles the tuning in metal-oxide-semiconductor field-effect transistor (MOSFET) devices such that the material properties are tuned by shifting the chemical potential when the material of interest is deposited as the channel in a transistor device.1, 17 A dielectric is required in both cases to provide the capacitance that holds the charges induced by an external voltage source, yet it is also the dielectric component that distinguishes ionic gating from conventional oxide-based transistor gating: in MOSFETs, the dielectric is usually an insulating oxide layer (most commonly, SiO2) with a thickness ranging from tens few to hundreds of nanometers (as shown in Fig. 2a); for guest species gating, a medium composed of mobile ions, or an "electrolyte," serves as the dielectric between the gate electrode and the channel (also known as ionic gating). An electric field can be created at the interface with the electrolyte, and the strength of such electric field can be on the order of tens of MV/cm, much higher than the breakdown field of oxide dielectrics.18 The key to achieving such a high electric field lies in the formation of the electric double layer (EDL). 客体物种门控类似于金属-氧化物-半导体场效应晶体管（MOSFET）器件中的调控，即当目标材料作为沟道沉积在晶体管器件中时，通过移动化学势来调控材料性质。1, 17 两种情况下都需要电介质来提供电容，以保持外部电压源诱导的电荷，但正是电介质组分将离子门控与传统的基于氧化物的晶体管门控区分开来：在MOSFET中，电介质通常是绝缘氧化物层（最常见的是SiO₂），厚度从几十纳米到几百纳米不等（如图2a所示）；对于客体物种门控，由可移动离子组成的介质，或"电解质"，作为栅电极和沟道之间的电介质（也称为离子门控）。在电解质界面处可以产生电场，这种电场的强度可以达到几十MV/cm量级，远高于氧化物电介质的击穿电场。18 实现如此高电场的关键在于电双层（EDL）的形成。
> 
> **MOSFET**: 金属-氧化物-半导体场效应晶体管——一种通过电场效应控制半导体沟道电导率的晶体管器件。 **Capacitance**: 电容——储存电荷的能力，在门控中决定可诱导的载流子密度。 **Electric double layer (EDL)**: 电双层——在电极/电解质界面形成的两层符号相反的电荷层，其厚度约为纳米量级。
> 
> A double layer is a universal phenomenon observed at electrode/electrolyte interfaces and it is manifested as two layers of excess charges with opposite signs; one layer in the electrode and the other in the electrolyte. The double layer forms as a result of the abrupt change in the interatomic force experienced by the ions at the interface between the bulk of the electrolyte and the electrode (or target material), and the requirement of global charge neutrality. In particular, ions in close proximity to the electrode experience very different forces from the electrode and the electrolyte, therefore rearranging accordingly and forming a charged layer (Fig. 2b and c). Due to the Coulomb force exerted by this charged layer in the electrolyte, the mobile electrons in the electrodes or materials of interest are either accumulated or depleted at the interface to balance these excess net charges at the interface. Consequently, a layer of charges with opposite sign is formed. 电双层是在电极/电解质界面观察到的普遍现象，表现为两层符号相反的过量电荷；一层在电极中，另一层在电解质中。电双层的形成是由于离子在电解质本体与电极（或目标材料）之间的界面处所经历的原子间力发生突变，以及全局电荷中性的要求。特别是，靠近电极的离子会受到来自电极和电解质的截然不同的力，因此相应地重新排列并形成带电层（图2b和c）。由于电解质中这层带电层施加的库仑力，电极或目标材料中的可移动电子在界面处要么积累要么耗尽，以平衡界面处这些过量的净电荷。因此，形成了一层符号相反的电荷。
> 
> When the electrode is connected to an external voltage source, the density and the polarity of the charges in the double layer can be conveniently controlled. The double layer is effectively a capacitor with a thickness ~1 nanometer, or less at reasonable electrolyte concentrations, i.e., the ionic radius.19 In this regard, an additional differential capacitance (C = dσ/dΨ) can be used to describe the change of surface charge density (σ) with respect to the electric surface potential (Ψ). 当电极连接到外部电压源时，电双层中电荷的密度和极性可以方便地控制。电双层实质上是一个厚度约为1纳米或更小（在合理的电解质浓度下，即离子半径）的电容器。19 在这方面，可以使用额外的微分电容（C = dσ/dΨ）来描述表面电荷密度（σ）相对于表面电势（Ψ）的变化。
> 
> To adopt this concept in transistor operation, we consider a different case, when a second electrode, which is the channel material that we would like to study, is put into the electrolyte and connected to the power source, completing the external circuit. The external voltage applied at the electrode relative to the channel only drops within the double layers at the electrolyte/electrode and electrolyte/channel interfaces. Since the thickness of the double layer is ~ 1 nanometer, a high 2D carrier density (up to 1014 cm-2) can be induced in the channel material at a reasonably low voltage. Transistors operating based on this principle are normally termed as electric double layer transistors (EDLTs), and are key to ionic gating. Thus far, both liquid and solid-state ionic gating method have been developed.20, 21 为了在晶体管操作中采用这一概念，我们考虑一种不同的情况，即将第二个电极（即我们想要研究的沟道材料）放入电解质中并连接到电源，完成外部电路。在电极上相对于沟道施加的外部电压仅在电解质/电极和电解质/沟道界面的电双层内下降。由于电双层厚度约为1纳米，在相当低的电压下就可以在沟道材料中诱导出高二维载流子密度（高达10¹⁴ cm⁻²）。基于这一原理工作的晶体管通常被称为电双层晶体管（EDLTs），是离子门控的关键。迄今为止，已开发出液态和固态离子门控方法。20, 21
> 
> **EDLTs**: 电双层晶体管——利用电双层电容效应工作的晶体管，可实现极高的载流子密度调控。
> 
> ### Gating methods
> 
> 门控方法
> 
> #### Liquid, gel, and polymer-based electrolytes
> 
> 液态、凝胶和聚合物基电解质
> 
> The commonly used electrolytes are those that have long been utilized in electrochemical devices, including ionic liquids and Li ion-based electrolytes (Fig. 2b). Ionic liquids are a family of salts that have a low melting point and are in liquid form at room temperature.22, 23 These electrolytes exhibit an area capacitance of on the order of several to tens of μF/cm2, and have been employed to induce ambipolar conduction20, 24-26 and superconductivity27-29 in a wide range of 2D materials. Li+-based electrolytes, in particular, are a critical component in rechargeable lithium batteries that involves lithium-containing salts dissolved in solvents. However, for EDLT fabrication and measurements, liquid electrolytes consisting of lithium salts dissolved rather in a polymer matrix, e.g., LiClO4 dissolved in polyethylene oxide (PEO), are more commonly used,25, 30-32 albeit with lower ionic conductivity as compared to that in the liquid lithium-based electrolytes. Another widely used technique, apart from the above discussed liquid and solid gating, is gel gating. Owing to the high viscosity of the gel electrolytes, one primary advantage of using gel gating lies in a combination of the better mechanical integrity and the lower evaporation rate, particularly as compared to the liquid state, making them easier to handle and more compatible with stable measurements in transistor geometries.33, 34 常用的电解质是那些长期在电化学器件中使用的电解质，包括离子液体和锂离子基电解质（图2b）。离子液体是一类具有低熔点且在室温下呈液态的盐。22, 23 这些电解质表现出几到几十μF/cm²量级的面积电容，并已被用于在广泛的二维材料中诱导双极性传导20, 24-26和超导电性27-29。尤其是锂离子基电解质，是可充电锂电池中的关键组分，涉及溶解在溶剂中的含锂盐。然而，对于EDLT的制造和测量，更常用的是由锂盐溶解在聚合物基质中组成的液态电解质，例如溶解在聚环氧乙烷（PEO）中的LiClO₄，25, 30-32 尽管与液态锂基电解质相比，其离子电导率较低。除上述讨论的液态和固态门控外，另一种广泛使用的技术是凝胶门控。由于凝胶电解质的高粘度，使用凝胶门控的一个主要优势在于结合了更好的机械完整性和较低的蒸发速率，特别是与液态相比，使其更易于处理，更兼容晶体管几何结构中的稳定测量。33, 34
> 
> **Ionic liquids**: 离子液体——完全由离子组成、在室温下呈液态的盐类，具有低蒸气压和高离子电导率。 **Ambipolar conduction**: 双极性传导——材料中同时存在电子和空穴两种载流子传导的现象。
> 
> Despite the powerful tuning ability of the liquid and gel electrolytes, there are some intrinsic issues pertaining to their liquid nature. One significant drawback is the unwanted strain created due to the glass transition of the electrolyte when temperature is lowered, which may often be detrimental during low temperature transport measurements.35 Other issues include instability against moisture and side chemical reactions, etc. The former can lead to the degradation of device performance at ambient conditions due to water absorption; while the latter, unfortunately, is un-avoidable at high bias in all EDLTs and analogous to the case of electrical breakdown in oxide-dielectric-based transistors. Nevertheless, a more positive perspective to this specific issue is that those undesirable electrochemical reactions usually only occur after the material properties have already been tuned to a large extent, therefore remain largely unaffected. 尽管液态和凝胶电解质具有强大的调控能力，但存在一些与其液态性质相关的固有问题。一个显著的缺点是在降低温度时，由于电解质的玻璃化转变而产生的不希望的应变，这在低温输运测量中通常可能是有害的。35 其他问题包括对湿气的不稳定性和副化学反应等。前者可能导致在环境条件下因吸水而导致器件性能退化；而后者，不幸的是，在所有EDLT中在高偏压下是不可避免的，类似于基于氧化物电介质的晶体管中的电击穿情况。然而，对这个特定问题的一个更积极的看法是，那些不希望的电化学反应通常只在材料性质已经被调控到很大程度之后才发生，因此基本不受影响。
> 
> **Glass transition**: 玻璃化转变——非晶态材料从硬而脆的玻璃态转变为粘弹性或橡胶态的可逆转变。
> 
> #### Solid ceramic-based electrolytes
> 
> 固态陶瓷基电解质
> 
> As a strong motivation, solid state ionic gating has been developed to tackle some of the aforementioned issues of liquid/gel electrolytes (Fig. 2c). Early developments involve the use of porous oxide dielectrics as a proton-conductive electrolyte.36-38 However, the liquid nature of the electrolyte is susceptible to the humidity of the environment, and can therefore block the integration with current solid-state semiconductor technology. Starting more recently in 2016, ceramic-based electrolytes have been demonstrated to show outstanding EDLT performance comparable to their liquid/gel state counterparts. These electrolytes include the lithium-ion conductive glass ceramic (LiCGC),39, 40 sodium superionic conductor41 and the fluoride-ion superionic conductor (LaF3).21 The key advantages of using these ceramic-based electrolytes for gating include the following aspects: (i) Strain degradation of device integrity can be significantly reduced for low temperature experiments owing to the absence of the glass transitions in these electrolytes. (ii) The mechanical rigidity of the electrolytes enables its use both as the gate dielectric and the substrate, which promises a more integrated device architecture and enables further device processing after the EDLT measurements. (iii) The top surface of the channel material is completely open and free, compatible with surface-sensitive experimental techniques, opening new avenues to combine the unprecedented level of materials control with advanced spectroscopic measurements, particularly for atomically thin materials. For these purposes, the optical absorption spectrum of the electrolyte itself is a critical factor for experimental design. In particular, it is known that the commonly-used ionic liquids show strong absorption in the infrared regime,42 which inhibits the spectroscopic investigations of the intrinsic materials properties in this energy spectrum. By contrast, a back-gate geometry, which is often adopted when solid electrolytes are used, can accommodate these exciting possibilities. (iv) Solid state EDLTs are compatible with ultra-high vacuum measurements since these solid ceramic electrolytes do not outgas. Overall, there is tremendous potential for these devices to be integrated with a wide range of in-situ vacuum assisted materials synthesis and/or synchrotron-based measurements. 作为一个强大的动机，固态离子门控已被开发出来以解决液态/凝胶电解质的一些上述问题（图2c）。早期的发展涉及使用多孔氧化物电介质作为质子导电电解质。36-38 然而，电解质的液态性质易受环境湿度的影响，因此可能阻碍与当前固态半导体技术的集成。从更近期的2016年开始，陶瓷基电解质已被证明表现出与其液态/凝胶态对应物相当的出色EDLT性能。这些电解质包括锂离子导电玻璃陶瓷（LiCGC）、39, 40 钠超离子导体41和氟离子超离子导体（LaF₃）。21 使用这些陶瓷基电解质进行门控的关键优势包括以下几个方面：(i) 由于这些电解质中不存在玻璃化转变，低温实验中器件完整性的应变退化可以显著降低；(ii) 电解质的机械刚性使其既能用作栅介质又能用作衬底，这有望实现更集成的器件架构，并在EDLT测量之后进行进一步的器件加工；(iii) 沟道材料的顶面完全开放和自由，兼容表面敏感的实验技术，为将前所未有的材料控制水平与先进的光谱测量相结合开辟了新途径，特别是对于原子级薄的材料。为此目的，电解质本身的光学吸收光谱是实验设计的关键因素。特别是，已知常用的离子液体在红外区域表现出强吸收，42 这抑制了在该能量谱范围内对材料本征性质的光谱研究。相比之下，当使用固态电解质时通常采用的背栅几何结构可以适应这些令人兴奋的可能性；(iv) 固态EDLT与超高真空测量兼容，因为这些固态陶瓷电解质不会放气。总的来说，这些器件在集成到各种原位真空辅助材料合成和/或基于同步加速器的测量方面具有巨大潜力。
> 
> **Superionic conductor**: 超离子导体——具有异常高离子电导率的固态材料，离子在其中可像在液体中一样快速移动。 **Synchrotron**: 同步加速器——一种产生高强度X射线和其他辐射的粒子加速器，用于材料表征。

> ### Effects of gating
> 
> 门控的效果
> 
> The performance of various gating methods (in forms of the area capacitance and the absolute value of the corresponding maximum gate voltage), using different ionic electrolytes and solid state dielectrics, are compared in Fig. 2d.20, 21, 32, 33, 40, 43 The maximum value of the applied gate voltages in the solid-state dielectric is bounded by the dielectric breakdown voltage;44 while for the ionic electrolyte, this breakdown occurs at the sufficiently high voltage when certain electrochemical reactions (e.g., electrolysis of the electrolyte or intercalation of the ions) can be triggered. The maximum carrier density variation introduced by gating can be calculated as the product of the area capacitance and maximum gate voltage: for instance, the dash lines in Fig. 2d indicate that the corresponding carrier density equals 1012, 1013, and 1014 cm-2 respectively. Usually, 2D materials with a carrier density less than 1011 cm-2 are marked by an insulating behavior, and insulator-metal transition occurs when the carrier density becomes over ~1013 cm-2. At a considerably high carrier density (>1014 cm-2), novel phenomena start to emerge, such as superconductivity, quantum phase transitions, etc. (will be discussed later). High-κ solid-state dielectrics have the capability in achieving a carrier density around 1013 cm-2 with a high ON/OFF ratio (e.g., up to 108 in a HfO2-based transistor) of the current–voltage characteristic, makes them desirable for CMOS-like digital logic device and allowing further reduction of the channel length.43, 45 Furthermore, the screening effect by the high-κ dielectrics can be utilized for tuning novel physical phenomena in 2D materials.46 However, limited by the catastrophic breakdown of dielectrics,44 reaching higher doping concentration at low voltages by electrostatic means using the solid-state dielectrics are still challenging. 使用不同离子电解质和固态电介质的各种门控方法的性能（以面积电容和相应最大栅极电压的绝对值的形式）在图2d中进行了比较。20, 21, 32, 33, 40, 43 固态电介质中施加栅极电压的最大值受电介质击穿电压的限制；44 而对于离子电解质，这种击穿发生在足够高的电压下，当某些电化学反应（例如电解质的电解或离子的插层）可以被触发时。门控引入的最大载流子密度变化可以计算为面积电容和最大栅极电压的乘积：例如，图2d中的虚线表示相应的载流子密度分别等于10¹²、10¹³和10¹⁴ cm⁻²。通常，载流子密度低于10¹¹ cm⁻²的二维材料表现出绝缘行为，而当载流子密度超过约10¹³ cm⁻²时发生绝缘体-金属转变。在相当高的载流子密度（>10¹⁴ cm⁻²）下，新的现象开始出现，如超导电性、量子相变等（将在后面讨论）。高κ固态电介质有能力实现约10¹³ cm⁻²的载流子密度，并具有电流-电压特性的高开/关比（例如，在基于HfO₂的晶体管中高达10⁸），使其适用于类CMOS数字逻辑器件，并允许进一步减小沟道长度。43, 45 此外，高κ电介质的屏蔽效应可用于调控二维材料中的新颖物理现象。46 然而，受限于电介质的灾难性击穿，44 使用固态电介质通过静电手段在低电压下达到更高的掺杂浓度仍然具有挑战性。
> 
> **High-κ dielectrics**: 高κ电介质——具有高介电常数（κ）的绝缘材料，可提供更强的栅极电容耦合。 **Insulator-metal transition**: 绝缘体-金属转变——材料在外界参数（如掺杂、压力）变化时从绝缘态转变为金属态的现象。

> As shown in Fig. 2d, one of the most distinctive aspects of the guest species gating method is the extremely high capacitance, which can even surpass that of the traditional solid-state gate dielectric by one to three orders of magnitude. This can lead to a low threshold voltage and steep subthreshold slope in the current–voltage characteristic for a guest species gating method-based FET device. As a consequence, the 2D materials can be doped to an extreme level (~ 1014 cm-2) (Fig. 2d). The first generation of liquid-ionic-gate-based transistors suffered from issues such as low ON/OFF ratio (~200), which is probably due to an non-zero "OFF" state current passing through the interior of the crystal beneath the channel surface.20 This 'detrimental' ON/OFF ratio has significantly limited the application of such technique in digital circuits. To circumvent this problem, liquid gating technology including dual-gate or suspended configurations have been developed to improve the ON/OFF ratio to over 105. Alternatively, the solid-state ionic conductors, albeit a slightly weaker area capacitance compared to the liquid gate, represent a promising alternative. More efforts are urgently needed for the technical development of solid-state ionic gates. 如图2d所示，客体物种门控方法最独特的方面之一是其极高的电容，甚至可以超过传统固态栅极电介质的一到三个数量级。这可以导致基于客体物种门控方法的FET器件在电流-电压特性中具有低阈值电压和陡峭的亚阈值斜率。因此，二维材料可以被掺杂到极端水平（约10¹⁴ cm⁻²）（图2d）。第一代基于液态离子栅极的晶体管存在诸如低开/关比（约200）的问题，这可能是由于非零的"关"态电流通过沟道表面下方的晶体内部。20 这种"不利的"开/关比严重限制了该技术在数字电路中的应用。为了解决这个问题，已开发出包括双栅极或悬浮配置在内的液态门控技术，以将开/关比提高到10⁵以上。或者，固态离子导体，尽管与液态栅极相比面积电容稍弱，但代表了一种有前景的替代方案。固态离子栅极的技术发展迫切需要更多努力。
> 
> **Threshold voltage**: 阈值电压——使晶体管从关态转变为开态所需的最小栅极电压。 **Subthreshold slope**: 亚阈值斜率——描述晶体管从关态过渡到开态时电流随栅压变化的陡峭程度的参数。
> 
> The extreme doping concentrations have engendered novel phenomena and allowed control of various phases of 2D materials under different doping concentrations, examples of which we briefly review here below. One remarkable recent development is the induced superconductivity and ground-state tuning in TMDs-based band insulators enabled by the guest species.47 For instance, gating suspended MoS2 bilayers using ionic liquid gate electrodes has led to the emergence of superconductivity in atomic sheets and the controllable Josephson coupling between the layers (Fig. 3a).48 Very recently, superconductivity in ZrNCl induced by lithium ion gating and the resulting crossover behavior between the BCS and BEC regimes have been demonstrated.49 In addition, observation of collective many-body states (superconductivity and charge density wave) in TiSe2 by ionic gating has also been reported (Fig. 3b).50 Beyond doping, ionic gating has also be used to electrochemically etch the layered superconductor FeSe, offering exciting new opportunities of revealing and controlling superconductivity in an electrochemical device down to the monolayer level.51 Another thriving field is the research on gate control of 2D magnetism. A room temperature 2D ferromagnet was recently achieved by lithium-ion gating of the trilayer Fe3GeTe2 (Fig. 3c), in which the elevated Curie temperature has been attributed to the substantial shift of the electronic bands of Fe3GeTe2 induced by ionic gating.52 Furthermore, the scale of the magnetization hysteresis of Cr2Ge2Te6 is much larger when using ionic gate than the Si gate.53 More recently, ionic gating tuned magnetic anisotropy in Cr2Ge2Te6, due to double-exchange promoted by heavy doping, was also illustrated (Fig. 3d).54 The semiconductor-to-semimetal phase transitions in Group VIB (e.g., Mo, W) TMDs caused by the change in electron chemical potential at high doping concentration was first predicted by theory55 and later observed in experiments (Fig. 3e).56, 57 Finally, the guest species gating method can also be applied to photonic devices. Recently, in a monolayer WS2-based resonator, the ionic gating induced a large change in the refractive index and a minimal change in the imaginary response, which makes it an efficient phase modulator (Fig. 3f).58 Notably, in some studies, especially using lithium-ion-based ionic gating,30, 49, 52 the doping at high gate voltage is caused by intercalation rather than electrostatic gating (which will be discussed later). 极端的掺杂浓度催生了新颖现象，并允许在不同掺杂浓度下控制二维材料的各种相，下面我们简要回顾其中的一些例子。最近一个显著的发展是在基于TMDs的能带绝缘体中，通过客体物种实现了诱导超导电性和基态调控。47 例如，使用离子液体栅电极门控悬浮的MoS₂双层，已导致原子片中超导电性的出现以及层间可控的约瑟夫森耦合（图3a）。48 最近，通过锂离子门控在ZrNCl中诱导的超导电性以及由此产生的BCS和BEC区域之间的交叉行为已被证明。49 此外，通过离子门控在TiSe₂中观察到的集体多体态（超导电性和电荷密度波）也已被报道（图3b）。50 除了掺杂之外，离子门控还被用于电化学刻蚀层状超导体FeSe，为在电化学器件中揭示和控制低至单层水平的超导电性提供了令人兴奋的新机会。51 另一个蓬勃发展的领域是二维磁性的栅极控制研究。最近，通过对三层Fe₃GeTe₂进行锂离子门控实现了室温二维铁磁体（图3c），其中居里温度的升高归因于离子门控诱导的Fe₃GeTe₂电子能带的显著移动。52 此外，使用离子栅极时，Cr₂Ge₂Te₆的磁化滞后回线的规模比使用硅栅极时大得多。53 最近，还阐明了由重掺杂促进的双交换作用导致的Cr₂Ge₂Te₆中离子门控调控的磁各向异性（图3d）。54 第VIB族（例如Mo、W）TMDs中由高掺杂浓度下电子化学势变化引起的半导体到半金属相变首先由理论预测，55 后来在实验中被观察到（图3e）。56, 57 最后，客体物种门控方法也可应用于光子器件。最近，在基于单层WS₂的谐振器中，离子门控诱导了折射率的大变化和虚部响应的最小变化，这使其成为高效的相位调制器（图3f）。58 值得注意的是，在一些研究中，特别是使用锂离子基离子门控时，30, 49, 52 高栅极电压下的掺杂是由插层而非静电门控引起的（将在后面讨论）。

> **Josephson coupling**: 约瑟夫森耦合——两个超导体通过薄绝缘层连接时发生的量子隧穿效应。 **BCS and BEC regimes**: BCS（Bardeen-Cooper-Schrieffer）和BEC（Bose-Einstein Condensate）区域——超导态中库珀对结合强度不同的两种极限行为。 **Charge density wave**: 电荷密度波——低维材料中电子密度周期性调制的集体量子现象。 **Curie temperature**: 居里温度——铁磁材料失去其铁磁性的临界温度。 **Double-exchange**: 双交换作用——通过中间阴离子在不同价态过渡金属离子之间发生的磁相互作用机制。

> In addition, the ionic conductivity is regarded as another key parameter for ionic gating techniques. The slow movement of the ionic guest species requires low bias scan rates to maintain equilibrium at the gate−channel interface, especially at low temperature.59 Therefore, there is strong interest to develop gating methods with high ionic conductivity for high speed or high frequency response. On the other hand, the ionic-liquid-induced doping can be non-volatile at cryogenic temperatures due to the freezing of guest species.60 By utilizing the temperature-induced transition between ionic insulating and conducting states in AgI, a solid-state non-volatile doping approach for 2D semiconductors operational at room temperature was demonstrated, leading to reversibly programmable devices that can function as transistors or diodes with switchable carrier types or device polarities.61 此外，离子电导率被认为是离子门控技术的另一个关键参数。离子客体物种的缓慢移动需要低偏压扫描速率以维持栅极-沟道界面的平衡，特别是在低温下。59 因此，开发具有高离子电导率的门控方法以用于高速或高频响应具有强烈兴趣。另一方面，由于客体物种的冻结，离子液体诱导的掺杂在低温下可以是非易失性的。60 通过利用AgI中温度诱导的离子绝缘态和导电态之间的转变，展示了一种在室温下可操作的二维半导体固态非易失性掺杂方法，产生了可逆可编程器件，这些器件可以作为晶体管或二极管工作，具有可切换的载流子类型或器件极性。61
> 
> **Non-volatile**: 非易失性——在移除外部激励后仍能保持其状态的特性。

> ## Guest species (de-)intercalation
> 
> 客体物种（脱）插层
> 
> In addition to the gating method where guest species are on the surface of the 2D materials, the weak interlayer interaction of the 2D materials permits the guest species to go into the interlayer spacing with an ordered pattern. Depending on the charge and the size, there are three different guest species that can be intercalated into 2D materials: 除了客体物种位于二维材料表面的门控方法外，二维材料弱的层间相互作用允许客体物种以有序的模式进入层间间距。根据电荷和尺寸，有三种不同的客体物种可以插层到二维材料中：
> 
> **Atom**: the atoms in the layered materials refer to intercalants in their zero-valent states. Transition metals like Cu, Co, Ag, Au, etc. can usually be intercalated into layered materials preserving zero valent state (Fig. 4a).62-64 The resultant zero-valent state usually indicates a low level of electron exchange between the host materials and the intercalant, and a much weaker interlayer interaction. **原子**：层状材料中的原子指处于零价态的插层剂。过渡金属如Cu、Co、Ag、Au等通常可以以保持零价态的方式插层到层状材料中（图4a）。62-64 由此产生的零价态通常表明主体材料和插层剂之间的电子交换水平较低，层间相互作用更弱。
> 
> **Zero-valent state**: 零价态——原子未失去或获得电子、保持中性电荷的状态。
> 
> **Ion**: the ions in the layered materials with net electrical charges (Fig. 4b). Cations, including most of the alkali metals (Li, Na, K...), some transition metals (Cr, Fe, Ni...)65, 66 and rare-earth metals (Sc, Nd, Eu...)67 can also be (de-)interacted (from) at their valence states into the host materials. Non-metal ions like oxygen ions can also be (de-)intercalated,68 especially for 2D layered oxides.69 Ion intercalation/insertion normally triggers changes in coordination number and crystal-field environment. Theoretical calculations show that the bonding between lithium ion and graphite lattice has covalent bonding character.70 The transition metal ions in TMDs are usually covalently bonded with the coordinated host atoms.71 The weak interlayer van der Waals interactions can be replaced by strong Coulomb (alkali metal) or covalent (transition metal) interactions after intercalation of ions.9 **离子**：层状材料中带有净电荷的离子（图4b）。阳离子，包括大多数碱金属（Li、Na、K...）、一些过渡金属（Cr、Fe、Ni...）65, 66 和稀土金属（Sc、Nd、Eu...）67 也可以以其价态（脱）插层到主体材料中。非金属离子如氧离子也可以（脱）插层，68 特别是对于二维层状氧化物。69 离子插层/插入通常会引发配位数和晶体场环境的变化。理论计算表明，锂离子和石墨晶格之间的键合具有共价键特征。70 TMDs中的过渡金属离子通常与配位的主体原子形成共价键。71 离子插层后，弱的层间范德华相互作用可以被强的库仑（碱金属）或共价（过渡金属）相互作用所取代。9
> 
> **Coordination number**: 配位数——晶体中一个原子周围最近邻原子的数量。
> 
> **Molecule**: this includes both organic and inorganic molecules (Fig. 4c), which are often polar. In particular, organic molecules which are mainly nitrogen-containing molecules with various sizes from several angstroms to a few nanometers, can be intercalated.72 Two major inorganic intercalants are metal hydroxides,73 and metal halides.74 **分子**：包括有机和无机分子（图4c），通常具有极性。特别是，有机分子主要是含氮分子，尺寸从几埃到几纳米不等，可以插层。72 两种主要的无机插层剂是金属氢氧化物73和金属卤化物。74
> 
> To insert guest species in a layered 2D material, the intercalated compound must be thermodynamically more stable than the physical mixture of the 2D material and guest species at a given condition; otherwise, deposition will occur rather than intercalation. The kinetics of the intercalation process are subject to the spacing or the van der Waals gap of the 2D materials,75 the existence of surface defects, and the diffusion coefficients of the guest species between sites within the spacing.76 On contrast, the zero-valent intercalants do not require a variation in the oxidation state of the host lattice, thus enabling high concentration of the intercalated species, depending on the precursor salt solution concentration and crystal structure.77 In these intercalated compounds, the stability of these intermediate or end phases is governed by the energetically favorable structures and generally influenced by the type of species inserted and the extent of intercalation.78 为了将客体物种插入层状二维材料中，插层化合物在给定条件下必须在热力学上比二维材料和客体物种的物理混合物更稳定；否则，将发生沉积而非插层。插层过程的动力学受制于二维材料的间距或范德华间隙、75 表面缺陷的存在以及客体物种在间距内位点之间的扩散系数。76 相反，零价插层剂不需要主体晶格氧化态的变化，因此能够实现高浓度的插层物种，具体取决于前驱体盐溶液浓度和晶体结构。77 在这些插层化合物中，这些中间相或最终相的稳定性由能量有利的结构决定，并通常受插入物种类型和插层程度的影响。78
> 
> **Diffusion coefficient**: 扩散系数——衡量原子或离子在材料中迁移速率的参数。
>

> #### Solid-state reactions
> 
> 固态反应
> 
> The solid-state reactions are usually the direct reaction of a mixture 2D materials and intercalants at elevated temperatures (Fig. 4d). In this case, elevated synthetic temperatures are generally required due to the large energetic barriers for the diffusing species. In certain temperature regimes, various competing chemical reactions can be all energetically accessible, resulting in the formation of the thermodynamically stable phases. Such high-temperature reaction method has been widely used for the incorporation of transition metals. Examples include the intercalation of metal ions79 and molecules (organic molecules72 or metal halides74) into group IVB and group VB transition metal dichalcogenides or graphite. This is also particularly true for the synthesis of transition-metal oxides80: in general, different thermodynamically stable phases or mixture of these phases of transition metal oxides can be produced in this manner. To this end, the high-temperature synthetic routes represent "thermodynamic control" and are generally restricted to the formation of only the most thermodynamically stable phase(s) for a specific composition, which precludes the formation of a large number of metastable materials. 固态反应通常是二维材料和插层剂的混合物在高温下的直接反应（图4d）。在这种情况下，由于扩散物种具有较大的能量势垒，通常需要较高的合成温度。在某些温度范围内，各种竞争性化学反应都可能在能量上可行，从而导致热力学稳定相的形成。这种高温反应方法已被广泛用于过渡金属的引入。例子包括金属离子79和分子（有机分子72或金属卤化物74）插层到第IVB族和第VB族过渡金属二硫族化物或石墨中。这对于过渡金属氧化物的合成也特别适用80：一般来说，可以通过这种方式产生不同的热力学稳定相或这些相的混合物。为此，高温合成路线代表"热力学控制"，通常仅限于形成特定组成的最热力学稳定相，这排除了大量亚稳态材料的形成。
> 
> **Metastable phase**: 亚稳态相——在给定条件下不是最稳定但可通过动力学控制获得的材料相。
> 
> #### Electrochemical method
> 
> 电化学方法
> 
> In the electrochemical approach, the intercalation process is facilitated by the energy from a power source, which serves as an external knob to control the reaction (Fig. 4e). The electrochemical intercalation process requires an electrochemical device consisting of three critical components: (i) The targeted host material, which serves as working electrode; (ii) an electrolyte or molten salt containing the ionic intercalants of interest, which has an adequately high ionic conductivity for the ion transport; (iii) a counter electrode at which an electrochemical reaction can be established in order to complete the electrical circuit. The intercalation process can be driven by controlling either the current passing through the circuit or the voltage applied across the two electrodes.81 A crucial prerequisite is that the intercalants, electrolyte, and the host 2D materials are electrochemically stable within the applied voltage window. The electrochemical approach is advantageous in controlling the intercalation rate by following the voltage profile, so that the degree of intercalation, or the number of ions intercalated, can be monitored and extracted.82-84 To this end, the correlation between the intercalation time and the number of the intercalant ions naturally constitutes a reliable route to systematically modulate the carrier density in the host materials.85 Additionally, this process is highly reversible before certain phase transitions are induced. For example, the lithium intercalation and deintercalation in LiCoO2 is highly reversible when the charging voltage is below 4.2 V.86 However, at high charging voltages, an irreversible phase transition occurs due to degradation by oxygen release.87, 88 Within the appropriate voltage window, the development of intercalation and de-intercalation by electrochemical means promises a powerful reversible method for both ionic and electronic tuning of the materials.89 在电化学方法中，插层过程由来自电源的能量促进，该电源作为控制反应的外部旋钮（图4e）。电化学插层过程需要一个电化学器件，由三个关键组件组成：(i) 目标主体材料，作为工作电极；(ii) 含有目标离子插层剂的电解质或熔盐，其具有足够高的离子电导率以进行离子传输；(iii) 对电极，在该电极上可以建立电化学反应以完成电路。插层过程可以通过控制通过电路的电流或施加在两个电极上的电压来驱动。81 一个关键的先决条件是插层剂、电解质和主体二维材料在施加的电压窗口内是电化学稳定的。电化学方法的优势在于通过跟踪电压曲线来控制插层速率，从而可以监测和提取插层程度，即插层离子的数量。82-84 为此，插层时间和插层离子数量之间的相关性自然构成了系统调控主体材料中载流子密度的可靠途径。85 此外，在诱导某些相变之前，该过程是高度可逆的。例如，当充电电压低于4.2 V时，LiCoO₂中的锂插层和脱插层是高度可逆的。86 然而，在高充电电压下，由于氧气释放导致的退化，会发生不可逆的相变。87, 88 在适当的电压窗口内，通过电化学手段进行插层和脱插层的发展为材料的离子和电子调控提供了一种强大的可逆方法。89
> 
> **Working electrode**: 工作电极——电化学电池中发生目标反应（如插层）的电极。 **Counter electrode**: 对电极——电化学电池中用于完成电流回路的辅助电极。

> #### Topochemical reactions
> 
> 拓扑化学反应
> 
> Intercalations can also be topochemically achieved at low temperatures by leveraging large differences in the kinetics of the diffusing ions, especially in complex layered materials, for synthesis of novel metastable phases.90, 91 Similar to the intercalation process discussed above, certain groups of atoms and ions can become significantly more mobile, for a given chemical environment and temperature, than the host phase in which they reside. Using this as the driving force, these atoms or ions can be effectively inserted into, or removed from, the host phases, forming new metastable phases or novel structures, given that these species possess large mobility in the host lattice and/or that the host phases have tendency to be reduced or oxidized (Fig. 4f). By utilizing this structural conservation and carefully choosing the initial phases, topochemical reactions can facilitate the formation of a wide range of end phases, which are usually inaccessible via the aforementioned high temperature synthetic routes. The topochemical method is widely used for the intercalation of atoms and ions, and specifically oxygen de-intercalation in transition metal oxides. 插层也可以通过在扩散离子的动力学上利用巨大差异，特别是在复杂层状材料中，以低温拓扑化学方式实现，用于合成新型亚稳态相。90, 91 与上述讨论的插层过程类似，对于给定的化学环境和温度，某些原子和离子基团可以变得比它们所处的主体相更具移动性。以此作为驱动力，这些原子或离子可以有效地插入或从主体相中移除，形成新的亚稳态相或新颖结构，前提是这些物种在主体晶格中具有大的迁移率和/或主体相有被还原或氧化的倾向（图4f）。通过利用这种结构保持性并仔细选择初始相，拓扑化学反应可以促进形成广泛的最终相，这些相通常无法通过上述高温合成路线获得。拓扑化学方法广泛用于原子和离子的插层，特别是过渡金属氧化物中的氧脱插层。
> 
> **Atom intercalation**: Chemical intercalation of atoms and ions is usually a solution-based process involving spontaneous or low temperature heat-assisted redox reactions. Precursors containing the target intercalants are dissolved in a solvent to solvate the ions. The host material of interest is then immersed in the solution for a period of time determined by the reaction kinetics, and if required, at an elevated temperature, to drive the reaction. Alkali metal ions have been shown to be chemically intercalated into various transition metal based layered materials due to the high reduction potential of the alkali metal precursors like n-Butyllithium.92 In recent years, a solution based zero-valent intercalation method has been also developed.62 The atoms in the solution can enter the 2D materials spontaneously due to a strong thermodynamic driving force, a broad spectrum of charge-neutral metal atoms can be intercalated upon diffusion driven by the local concentration gradient63. There exist many TMDs and oxides that can be intercalated upon the chemical insertion (e.g., TaS2,93 GaSe,94 Bi2Se3,62 etc.), and layered binary and perovskite (derived) oxides like WO3-type,95 MO2 rutile-type,96, 97 and AB2O4 spinel-type oxides,98-100 etc. **原子插层**：原子和离子的化学插层通常是一个基于溶液的过程，涉及自发或低温热辅助的氧化还原反应。含有目标插层剂的前驱体溶解在溶剂中以溶剂化离子。然后将目标主体材料浸入溶液中一段时间，该时间由反应动力学决定，如果需要，在升高的温度下进行以驱动反应。由于碱金属前驱体如正丁基锂的高还原电位，碱金属离子已被证明可以化学插层到各种基于过渡金属的层状材料中。92 近年来，还开发了一种基于溶液的零价插层方法。62 溶液中的原子可以由于强大的热力学驱动力而自发进入二维材料，各种电荷中性的金属原子可在局部浓度梯度驱动的扩散下插层。63 存在许多可以通过化学插入进行插层的TMDs和氧化物（例如TaS₂、93 GaSe、94 Bi₂Se₃、62 等），以及层状二元和钙钛矿（衍生）氧化物，如WO₃型、95 MO₂金红石型、96, 97 和AB₂O₄尖晶石型氧化物、98-100 等。
> 
> **Perovskite**: 钙钛矿——具有ABX₃通式的晶体结构类型，A和B为阳离子，X为阴离子，广泛存在于过渡金属氧化物中。 **Spinel**: 尖晶石——具有AB₂O₄通式的晶体结构类型，其中A为二价阳离子，B为三价阳离子。
> 
> **Ion exchange**: For ion intercalated compounds, selective ion exchange can be promoted in an ionic environment by leveraging the difference in reduction or oxidation potential between the ions inside and outside the host 2D materials. For example, the lithium intercalated MoS2 possesses a strong reduction capability: through an ion exchange process involving lithium intercalated MoS2 and transition metal or rare-earth metal salts, novel intercalation compounds of MoS2 incorporating transition and rare earth metals can be prepared.101 Notably, this method was recently extended to noble metals (e.g., Pt) for synthesis of high-performance catalysts.102, 103 Progress has also been made in the realization of an ultrafast ion diffusion coefficient enabled by exchanged ions in atomically thin micas, with dimensions controlled by the Moirésuperlattice length scales, which were observed by atomic-resolution scanning transmission electron microscopy.104 **离子交换**：对于离子插层化合物，通过利用主体二维材料内外离子之间还原或氧化电位的差异，可以在离子环境中促进选择性离子交换。例如，锂插层的MoS₂具有强还原能力：通过涉及锂插层MoS₂和过渡金属或稀土金属盐的离子交换过程，可以制备出包含过渡金属和稀土金属的MoS₂新型插层化合物。101 值得注意的是，该方法最近被扩展到贵金属（例如Pt），用于合成高性能催化剂。102, 103 在原子级薄云母中通过交换离子实现超快离子扩散系数方面也取得了进展，其尺寸由莫尔超晶格长度尺度控制，这些是通过原子分辨率扫描透射电子显微镜观察到的。104
> 
> **Moiré superlattice**: 莫尔超晶格——当两个具有微小晶格失配或旋转角的晶体叠加时形成的长周期干涉图案。
> 
> **Oxygen de-intercalation**: Unlike the above discussed cation (de-)intercalation, which is usually achieved via electrochemical and solution-based chemical methods,62 oxygen de-intercalation often involves other reducing reagents, such as hydrogen,105-107 metal getters,108 and metal hydrides.109-114 The former two strategies need to operate at moderate temperature as a considerable amount of thermal energy is required to facilitate the oxygen-release process. Consequently, at the required elevated temperatures, the formation of some metastable phases is precluded as the reduction reactions typically lead to decomposition to binary oxide phases, rather than the targeted reduced phases.113 To circumvent this issue, metal-based hydrides have been adopted as solid-state reducing reagents, with which the topochemical reactions can be operated at relatively low temperatures, thus preventing materials decomposition. These metal hydrides include NaH,109 LiH,115 CaH2,110, 111, 114 TiH2,116 etc. **氧脱插层**：与上述讨论的阳离子（脱）插层不同，后者通常通过电化学和基于溶液的化学方法实现，62 氧脱插层通常涉及其他还原剂，如氢气、105-107 金属吸气剂108和金属氢化物。109-114 前两种策略需要在中等温度下操作，因为需要相当数量的热能来促进氧释放过程。因此，在所需的高温下，一些亚稳态相的形成被排除，因为还原反应通常导致分解为二元氧化物相，而不是目标还原相。113 为了解决这个问题，金属基氢化物已被用作固态还原剂，使用这些还原剂，拓扑化学反应可以在相对较低的温度下操作，从而防止材料分解。这些金属氢化物包括NaH、109 LiH、115 CaH₂、110, 111, 114 TiH₂、116 等。
> 
> **Metal hydride**: 金属氢化物——金属与氢的化合物，常作为强还原剂使用。
> 
> A number of complex oxides, especially those with perovskite or perovskite-derived structures,91 are suitable precursor phases for these topochemical transitions using metal hydrides. As a result, novel phases that have unconventional transition metal oxidation states and coordination numbers can be produced.113 Perhaps, among many topochemical transitions, the transition to the infinite layer phase ABO2 with a square planar coordination to the transition metal ions (B) by this soft chemistry approach is marked as one of the most studied and intriguing oxygen de-intercalation process.91 Take SrFeOx as an example: the formation of the SrFeO2 infinite-layer phase can be readily facilitated using a CaH2-based topochemical reduction from a precursor brownmillerite phase SrFeO2.5.112, 117 This new phase cannot be directly synthesized by conventional high temperature synthesis and shows a unique antiferromagnetic ground state, which can be converted into a S = 1 high-spin state with an antiferromagnetic to ferromagnetic transition upon applying pressure.118 In this regard, imposing epitaxial strain in a thin-film geometry as an additional tuning knob can potentially induce phase transitions that are inaccessible in bulk.119, 120 许多复杂氧化物，特别是那些具有钙钛矿或钙钛矿衍生结构的氧化物，91 是使用金属氢化物进行这些拓扑化学转变的合适前驱体相。因此，可以产生具有非常规过渡金属氧化态和配位数的新颖相。113 也许，在众多拓扑化学转变中，通过这种软化学方法转变为具有过渡金属离子（B）方形平面配位的无限层相ABO₂被认为是最受研究和最引人入胜的氧脱插层过程之一。91 以SrFeOₓ为例：使用基于CaH₂的拓扑化学还原，从前驱体钙铁石相SrFeO₂.₅可以容易地促进SrFeO₂无限层相的形成。112, 117 这种新相无法通过传统的高温合成直接合成，并显示出独特的反铁磁基态，在施加压力时可以转变为S = 1的高自旋态，并伴随反铁磁到铁磁的转变。118 在这方面，在薄膜几何结构中施加外延应变作为额外的调控旋钮，可以潜在地诱导在块体中无法实现的相变。119, 120
> 
> **Infinite layer phase**: 无限层相——具有ABO₂化学式的结构，其中过渡金属离子呈方形平面配位，氧离子被移除形成层状结构。 **Brownmillerite**: 钙铁石——具有A₂B₂O₅化学式的结构，包含有序氧空位的钙钛矿衍生相。 **Antiferromagnetic**: 反铁磁——相邻磁矩反平行排列的磁有序状态。 **Epitaxial strain**: 外延应变——在晶格匹配的衬底上生长薄膜时因晶格失配而产生的应变。
> 
> Another prominent materials example of oxygen de-intercalated oxides is the Ruddlesden-Popper nickel oxides (nickelates) with low Ni valence, such as La3Ni2O6,121 and RE4Ni3O8 (RE: La, Pr, Nd),107, 122-125 which can be prepared by oxygen de-intercalation using various reducing reagents. These layered compounds, long-thought to be close nickelate cousins of the copper-oxide high temperature superconductors,126 were found to host intriguing physics, including large orbital polarization, charge (spin) ordering, stripe phases, etc.107, 124, 125 They recently gained significant research interest due to their potential for developing additional nickelate superconductors.126-128 另一个氧脱插层氧化物的突出材料例子是具有低Ni价态的Ruddlesden-Popper镍氧化物（镍酸盐），如La₃Ni₂O₆、121 和RE₄Ni₃O₈（RE：La、Pr、Nd）、107, 122-125 这些可以通过使用各种还原剂进行氧脱插层来制备。这些层状化合物，长期以来被认为是铜氧化物高温超导体的近亲镍酸盐，126 被发现承载着有趣的物理学，包括大的轨道极化、电荷（自旋）有序、条纹相等。107, 124, 125 它们最近因在发展额外镍酸盐超导体方面的潜力而获得了巨大的研究兴趣。126-128
> 
> **Ruddlesden-Popper phase**: Ruddlesden-Popper相——具有Aₙ₊₁BₙO₃ₙ₊₁通式的层状钙钛矿衍生结构，由钙钛矿层和岩盐层交替组成。 **Orbital polarization**: 轨道极化——电子在不同轨道中占据的不均匀分布。
> 
> ### Effects of (de-)intercalation
> 
> （脱）插层的效应
> 
> Two major effects of the intercalation on altering the chemical environment in materials can be identified as being of interest: 1. Doping: Unlike the gate-induced electrostatic doping in 2D materials, the intercalation induced doping effect is rooted in the charge transfer process which requires a difference in the chemical potential of the host 2D materials and the intercalant, as well as the availability of an empty band that accepts electrons or holes (Fig. 5a and b). The lithium ion with very high reduction potential is one of the most effective electron donors, which can donate up to one electron per atom to the host lattice. Amphoteric 2D materials (e.g., graphene) can be either an electron acceptor or a donor during the intercalation process: halogen intercalation in graphene has shown strong hole doping behavior.129, 130 However, most of the transition metal dichalcogenides and oxides can only be an electron acceptor during intercalation. This may be due to the bonding between the transition metal and chalcogens/oxygens that leads to a negatively charged chalcogen/oxygen layer.131 As a consequence, the Coulomb repulsion between the layers and the negatively charged guest species may exceed the energy to be gained by intercalation.79 Electronically, the topology of the Fermi surface of the 2D materials can be largely controlled by the doping effect. 2. Chemical bonding: The unique degree of freedom in bonding configuration of the intercalants can create novel electronic structure of the intercalated compounds. Chemical bonding can be formed when the valence electrons of the intercalant atoms and the host atoms interact with each other:71 the difference in the electronegativity of these atoms affects the overlap of the electron clouds (Fig. 5c). Consequently, the electronic band structure is amenable to modifications by the formation of new chemical bonding or hybridization. Another mode of interaction is that the guest species replace the host atoms, modifying the lattice configuration.132 For an individual intercalant, these effects often co-exist, leading to exotic phases and unusual electronic phenomena, as well as motivating novel applications. 插层对改变材料中化学环境的两个主要效应可以被确定为值得关注的：1. 掺杂：与二维材料中栅极诱导的静电掺杂不同，插层诱导的掺杂效应植根于电荷转移过程，该过程需要主体二维材料和插层剂之间化学势的差异，以及接受电子或空穴的空带的存在（图5a和b）。具有非常高还原电位的锂离子是最有效的电子给体之一，每个原子可以给主体晶格提供多达一个电子。两性二维材料（例如石墨烯）在插层过程中既可以是电子受体也可以是给体：石墨烯中的卤素插层已显示出强空穴掺杂行为。129, 130 然而，大多数过渡金属二硫族化物和氧化物在插层过程中只能是电子受体。这可能是由于过渡金属和硫族/氧之间的键合导致带负电的硫族/氧层。131 因此，层与带负电的客体物种之间的库仑排斥可能超过插层所获得的能量。79 在电子学上，二维材料费米面的拓扑结构可以在很大程度上由掺杂效应控制。2. 化学键合：插层剂键合构型的独特自由度可以创造插层化合物的新颖电子结构。当插层剂原子和主体原子的价电子相互作用时，可以形成化学键：71 这些原子电负性的差异影响电子云的重叠（图5c）。因此，通过形成新的化学键或杂化，电子能带结构可以被修改。另一种相互作用模式是客体物种取代主体原子，改变晶格构型。132 对于单个插层剂，这些效应通常共存，导致奇异相和不同寻常的电子现象，并激发新的应用。
> 
> **Fermi surface**: 费米面——在动量空间中分隔占据态和未占据电子态的边界，决定材料的电子输运性质。 **Electronegativity**: 电负性——原子吸引共享电子对能力的量度。
> 
> **Superconductivity and electronic phase transition**: Topochemical transformation enabled by (de-)intercalations can lead to new electronic parent phases, which, upon doping, can emerge as novel superconductors or reveal additional quantum states. These exotic phases are often rooted at the structural phase transitions as discussed above; doping achieved by intercalation or chemical substitution offers further modification on the band structure and/or low-energy landscapes, marked by free charges introduced into the materials. In this regard, both the bonding and doping effects make significant contribution to the creation of unconventional superconductivity and other related phases in 2D (de-)intercalated compounds. **超导电性和电子相变**：通过（脱）插层实现的拓扑化学转变可以导致新的电子母相，这些母相在掺杂后可以成为新型超导体或揭示额外的量子态。这些奇异相通常植根于上述讨论的结构相变；通过插层或化学取代实现的掺杂对能带结构和/或低能景观提供了进一步修改，其标志是引入材料中的自由电荷。在这方面，键合和掺杂效应都对二维（脱）插层化合物中非常规超导电性和其他相关相的产生做出了重大贡献。
> 
> In this context, the chemistry of cation de-intercalation in transition metal oxides involving monovalent cations (Li, Na, K, etc.) has also been deeply investigated, as this process is generically the common chemical reaction in the charging cycles of lithium-ion batteries. One prominent example is the various phases that can be prepared via de-intercalation of sodium ions from NaCoO2, i.e., NaxCoO2. While NaxCoO2 has many polymorphs133, 134 that possess similar formation energy and thus can be synthesized through chemical and electrochemical oxidation, γ NaxCoO2 phase represents the most studied compound in the family.135 The γ-NaxCoO2 phase has a most stable configuration with x = 0.7136 but can be further de-intercalated to phases with x < 0.5.135, 137, 138 Upon varying x by de-intercalation, NaxCoO2 displays many exotic phases, evolving from a Curie-Weiss metal to a paramagnetic metal, and from a charge density wave metallic state to a charge-ordered insulator, manifested by an intriguing phase diagram.138, 139. The "surprise" emerges when the system undergoes another intercalation process inserting water molecules into the interlayer sites of edge-sharing CoO2 pyramids: superconductivity emerges in the intercalated materials140, and the materials exhibit a superconducting dome upon varying x.141-145 Superconductivity in this layered system sparked immerse interest not only due to a Co3+/Co4+ derived low-spin to high-spin transition,146-148 but also related to the inherent frustrated spin structure in the underlying triangular lattice of Co sites.139 在此背景下，涉及单价阳离子（Li、Na、K等）的过渡金属氧化物中阳离子脱插层的化学也已被深入研究，因为该过程通常是锂离子电池充电循环中的常见化学反应。一个突出的例子是可以通过从NaCoO₂中脱插层钠离子制备的各种相，即NaₓCoO₂。虽然NaₓCoO₂有许多多晶型，133, 134 它们具有相似的形成能，因此可以通过化学和电化学氧化合成，γ NaₓCoO₂相代表了该家族中研究最多的化合物。135 γ-NaₓCoO₂相在x = 0.7时具有最稳定的构型，136 但可以进一步脱插层到x < 0.5的相。135, 137, 138 通过脱插层改变x，NaₓCoO₂显示出许多奇异相，从居里-外斯金属演变为顺磁金属，从电荷密度波金属态演变为电荷有序绝缘体，表现为一个引人入胜的相图。138, 139 当系统经历另一个插层过程，将水分子插入共边CoO₂金字塔的层间位点时，"惊喜"出现了：超导电性在插层材料中出现，140 并且材料在改变x时表现出超导圆顶。141-145 该层状系统中的超导电性激发了巨大兴趣，不仅由于Co³⁺/Co⁴⁺衍生的低自旋到高自旋转变，146-148 而且还与底层Co位点三角晶格中固有的阻挫自旋结构有关。139

> As for the topochemical oxygen de-intercalation process, infinite layer nickelates with chemical formula of RENiO2 (RE: rare-earth elements) stand as another materials paradigm. The implementation of the metal hydride based kinetic soft-chemistry approach enabled the preparation of this metastable nickelate phase with unusual Ni valence in both powder and thin-film forms,109 111, 114, 149, 150 which otherwise cannot be obtained by conventional high-temperature thin-film growth, to mimic the starting electronic structure of the superconducting copper oxides. Such materials exploration harnessing this specific synthetic approach, in combination of chemical substitution (another way of ionic modification), has been further fueled by the recent discovery of the first nickelate superconductor with the infinite-layer structure, thin-film Nd1-xSrxNiO2,127 as shown in Fig. 5d, and its intriguing phase diagram.151, 152 The thin-film Pr1-xSrxNiO2 superconductors subsequently found153 mark the potential of uncovering an extended family of layered nickelate superconductors.154 Furthermore, in particular for the thin-film RENiO2, the underlying substrates provide templates for not only hosting topotactically transformed single crystalline films,151, 152 but also for reorienting the oxygen de-intercalation direction, resulting in films with different crystallographic orientations.155 This further unleashes the potential in the preparation of single-crystalline nickelates with distinct lattice symmetry and coordination configuration, which can lead to a wide range of enriched properties. 至于拓扑化学氧脱插层过程，化学式为RENiO₂（RE：稀土元素）的无限层镍酸盐是另一个材料范例。基于金属氢化物的动力学软化学方法的实施，使得在粉末和薄膜形式中制备这种具有不寻常Ni价态的亚稳态镍酸盐相成为可能，109, 111, 114, 149, 150 否则这些相无法通过传统的高温薄膜生长获得，以模拟超导铜氧化物的起始电子结构。利用这种特定合成方法，结合化学取代（离子改性的另一种方式）的材料探索，因最近发现的第一个具有无限层结构的镍酸盐超导体——薄膜Nd₁₋ₓSrₓNiO₂（如图5d所示）127 及其引人入胜的相图而进一步加速。151, 152 随后发现的薄膜Pr₁₋ₓSrₓNiO₂超导体153 标志着揭示扩展的层状镍酸盐超导体家族的潜力。154 此外，特别是对于薄膜RENiO₂，底层衬底不仅为承载拓扑化学转变的单晶薄膜提供模板，151, 152 而且还为重新定向氧脱插层方向提供模板，从而产生具有不同晶体取向的薄膜。155 这进一步释放了制备具有不同晶格对称性和配位构型的单晶镍酸盐的潜力，这可以导致广泛的丰富性质。

> **Single crystalline**: 单晶——整个样品中原子排列具有连续且无缺陷的周期性结构的材料。

> **Magnetism**: By way of intercalation of transition metals through bonding and doping effects, magnetic 2D materials can be prepared. The covalent bonding formed between the intercalants and host lattice can alter the properties of the d-orbitals of the transition metals through hybridization and charge redistribution, thus affecting the magnetic properties. For example, in Ta intercalated TaS2 bilayers, when each intercalated Ta atom is bonded with six S atoms to form an octahedral superstructure, additional spin-split bands across the Fermi level (which develops a magnetic ground state) can be observed (Fig. 5e).71 Similar phenomena were also discovered in other transition metal intercalated compounds.65, 156-158 Besides intercalation-induced magnetism, intercalation-tunable 2D magnetic properties have also drawn considerable attention. One example is the transition from a ferromagnetic semiconducting to a ferromagnetic metallic phase with an enhanced Curie temperature in Cr2Ge2Te6 via organic molecules intercalation, which can be attributed to the change of magnetic coupling from a weak super-exchange interaction to a strong double-exchange interaction upon doping.54 In addition, interlayer coupling plays an indispensable role in the 2D magnetism as well. Calculations show that Li-ion intercalation in bilayer Fe3GeTe2 provides hopping carriers between the two interfacial Te sublayers, leading to the enhancement of the interlayer ferromagnetic coupling at a relatively low intercalation level.159 Recently, the interlayer magnetic coupling of Fe3GeTe2 was shown to be dramatically enhanced through proton intercalation, evidenced by exchange bias in transport measurements.160 However, an experimental approach to quantify the strength of intercalation-induced interlayer exchange coupling is yet to be fully developed. **磁性**：通过键合和掺杂效应进行过渡金属插层，可以制备磁性二维材料。插层剂和主体晶格之间形成的共价键可以通过杂化和电荷重新分布改变过渡金属d轨道的性质，从而影响磁性质。例如，在Ta插层的TaS₂双层中，当每个插层Ta原子与六个S原子键合形成八面体超结构时，可以观察到穿过费米能级的额外自旋分裂能带（这发展出磁基态）（图5e）。71 在其他过渡金属插层化合物中也发现了类似现象。65, 156-158 除了插层诱导的磁性外，插层可调的二维磁性质也引起了相当大的关注。一个例子是通过有机分子插层，Cr₂Ge₂Te₆中从铁磁半导体相到铁磁金属相的转变，并伴随居里温度的提高，这可以归因于掺杂后磁耦合从弱超交换相互作用变为强双交换相互作用。54 此外，层间耦合在二维磁性中也起着不可或缺的作用。计算表明，双层Fe₃GeTe₂中的锂离子插层在两个界面Te亚层之间提供跳跃载流子，导致在相对较低的插层水平下增强层间铁磁耦合。159 最近，通过质子插层，Fe₃GeTe₂的层间磁耦合被证明显著增强，输运测量中的交换偏置证明了这一点。160 然而，量化插层诱导的层间交换耦合强度的实验方法尚未完全发展。
> 
> **Super-exchange interaction**: 超交换作用——通过中间非磁性阴离子在磁性离子之间发生的间接磁耦合。 **Exchange bias**: 交换偏置——铁磁-反铁磁界面处由于交换耦合导致的磁滞回线偏移现象。

> **Structural phase transitions**: For van der Waals materials, although the (de-)intercalation method is viewed as a mild reaction inside the van der Waals spacing without affecting the host lattice, lattice variation and structural transitions as a result of the presence of guest species can be highly relevant in some circumstances. The in-plane lattice variations are usually driven by disorder, substitutions, and/or structural phase transitions. One such intriguing phase transition is the semiconducting 2H to metallic 1T phase transition in lithium intercalation of MoS2.76, 161-163 The driving mechanism was attributed to the drastic increase in the electronic energy of the semiconducting host due to charge-transfer from the lithium intercalant, which can also be applicable to other group-VIB TMDs phase transition with lithium intercalation. Apart from that, metastable phases such as the 1T' phase has also been observed in lithium intercalated MoS2.164 Recently, electrochemical intercalation of exceptionally few lithium ions into WTe2 showed large anisotropic in-plane strain, which was linked to the formation of a newly discovered phase with an exotic atomic and crystallographic arrangement.165 On the other hand, the substitution in the host 2D materials contributes to the structural transition as well. For example, Cu is easy to intercalate and undergo substitution in Bi2Se3.132, 166 **结构相变**：对于范德华材料，尽管（脱）插层方法被视为范德华间距内的温和反应，不影响主体晶格，但由于客体物种的存在而导致的晶格变化和结构转变在某些情况下可能高度相关。面内晶格变化通常由无序、取代和/或结构相变驱动。其中一个引人入胜的相变是MoS₂锂插层中半导体2H到金属1T的相变。76, 161-163 驱动机制归因于来自锂插层剂的电荷转移导致半导体主体电子能量的急剧增加，这也适用于其他第VIB族TMDs在锂插层时的相变。除此之外，在锂插层MoS₂中也观察到亚稳态相如1T'相。164 最近，极少量锂离子电化学插层到WTe₂中显示出大的各向异性面内应变，这与形成具有奇异原子和晶体排列的新发现相有关。165 另一方面，主体二维材料中的取代也对结构转变有贡献。例如，Cu在Bi₂Se₃中容易插层并发生取代。132, 166
> 
> **2H and 1T phases**: 2H和1T相——TMDs的两种不同多型体，2H为半导体相（三角形棱柱配位），1T为金属相（八面体配位）。

> In addition to the understanding of the intercalation process and the intercalation compounds, copious new properties have spurred extensive research efforts, leading to new device designs based on a variety of intercalated compounds. For example, the modulation of catalytic performance of transition metal dichalcogenides by alkali metal intercalation has been observed;89, 167 the metallic MoS2 of 1T phase has been used as low-resistance contacts for 2H-MoS2 transistors;168 a thermal transistor that has been fabricated through intercalation and de intercalation of lithium of MoS2, where the enhanced phonon scattering, c-axis strain, and stacking disorder, etc., result in a thermal ON/OFF ratio of one order of magnitude.169 除了对插层过程和插层化合物的理解外，众多新性质激发了广泛的研究努力，导致了基于各种插层化合物的新器件设计。例如，已观察到通过碱金属插层调控过渡金属二硫族化物催化性能；89, 167 1T相的金属MoS₂已被用作2H-MoS₂晶体管的低电阻接触；168 通过MoS₂的锂插层和脱插层制造了热晶体管，其中增强的声子散射、c轴应变和堆垛无序等导致一个数量级的热开/关比。169
> 
> **Phonon scattering**: 声子散射——声子（晶格振动的量子）在材料中传播时由于各种机制而偏离其原始路径的过程。

> The c-axis lattice expansion is another direct consequence of intercalation, especially for the case of intercalation of large organic molecules. The weak interlayer van der Waals interaction allows the separation of layers to accommodate the intercalated organic molecules170 through external driving force (e.g., concentration gradient or voltage gradient), achieving a minimization of the system energy. The separation of each layer can contribute a 3D to 2D electronic transition. As an example, pyridine was intercalated into TaS2, increasing the interlayer distance from 6 Å to 12 Å, which leads to 2D superconducting properties in a 3D bulk TaS2, and new types of intercalation compounds denoted as organometallic crystals.72, 170 More recently, semiconducting 2D materials have been intercalated with longer-chain alkyl ammonium bromide to create monolayer atomic crystal molecular superlattices, to obtain single-layer properties in vertically integrated (quasi-)bulk ambient environment-sensitive 2D materials (e.g., black phosphorus) (Fig. 5f).171, 172 These interlayer expansion effects also have remarkable potential in the fields of catalysis and actuators.169, 173, 174 c轴晶格膨胀是插层的另一个直接后果，特别是对于大有机分子插层的情况。弱的层间范德华相互作用允许通过外部驱动力（例如浓度梯度或电压梯度）分离层以容纳插层的有机分子，170 实现系统能量的最小化。每层的分离可以促成三维到二维的电子转变。例如，吡啶被插层到TaS₂中，将层间距离从6 Å增加到12 Å，这导致三维块体TaS₂中的二维超导性质，以及称为有机金属晶体的新型插层化合物。72, 170 最近，半导体二维材料已被插层更长链的烷基溴化铵，以创建单层原子晶体分子超晶格，从而在垂直集成的（准）块体环境敏感二维材料（例如黑磷）中获得单层性质（图5f）。171, 172 这些层间膨胀效应在催化和致动器领域也具有显著潜力。169, 173, 174

> **Organometallic crystals**: 有机金属晶体——在层状材料层间含有有机分子的插层化合物。 **actuator**: 致动器——将能量（如电能）转换为机械运动或位移的器件。

> **Phonon and photon transport**: Apart from the modulation in electronic transport properties, considerable progress has also been achieved in the study of phonon and photon transport, leading to breakthroughs in thermoelectrics, photoelectrics, and optoelectronics. For example, the cross plane thermal conductivity can be reduced by one order of magnitude through the intercalation of lithium, which attributes to the phonon scattering by intercalants and intercalation induced structural changes and disorders.169 In addition, n-type flexible thermoelectric materials was synthesized through electrochemical intercalation of organic molecules into TiS2: the hybrid inorganic/organic superlattice form by intercalation contributes to a high figure of merit.175 As for the photon transport measurements, a number of studies have demonstrated the control of optical properties by intercalation.176, 177 For example, the Cu intercalation in Bi2E3 (E=Se, Te) leads to significant enhancement of light transmission due to the reduction of material absorption after intercalation and nanophotonic effect of zero-wave anti-reflection, which is unique to ultra-small thickness of the nanoplates.178 Among others, an important materials example is SnS2, an intrinsic n-type 2D semiconductor, which shows p-type characteristics upon Co intercalation and metallic behavior upon Cu intercalation, and significantly different relative reflectance was observed after intercalation (Fig. 5g).179 **声子和光子输运**：除了电子输运性质的调控外，在声子和光子输运研究中也取得了相当大的进展，导致了热电、光电和光电子学方面的突破。例如，通过锂插层，横向热导率可以降低一个数量级，这归因于插层剂引起的声子散射以及插层诱导的结构变化和无序。169 此外，通过有机分子电化学插层到TiS₂中合成了n型柔性热电材料：插层形成的杂化无机/有机超晶格有助于获得高的优值。175 至于光子输运测量，许多研究已证明通过插层控制光学性质。176, 177 例如，Bi₂E₃（E=Se，Te）中的Cu插层由于插层后材料吸收的减少和零波抗反射的纳米光子效应，导致光透射显著增强，这对于超薄纳米片是独特的。178 其中，一个重要的材料例子是SnS₂，一种本征n型二维半导体，在Co插层时表现出p型特性，在Cu插层时表现出金属行为，并且插层后观察到显著不同的相对反射率（图5g）。179

> # Perspectives
> 
> 展望
> 
> In recent years, the research on the topic of guest species modulation to 2D materials has witnessed tremendous development. The enriched toolbox of accessible modulation techniques and new 2D systems creates an unparallel new dimension for materials design and device application. 近年来，关于二维材料客体物种调控这一主题的研究见证了巨大的发展。丰富的可及调控技术工具箱和新的二维系统为材料设计和器件应用创造了无与伦比的新维度。
> 
> A rapid surge of the potential device applications has been discussed based upon guest species modulation. The strong tuning of carrier density enabled the early blossoming of nanoscale transistors and optoelectronic devices,180, 181 while in recent years, the guest species tuning of additional materials properties promises new transistor architectures. For instance, ionic,182 catalytic,183 and thermal transistors169 have been fabricated to control an ON/OFF state in different parameter and functionality spaces (ionic diffusivity, catalytic performance, thermal conductivity) by intercalation or gating methods. Despite the exciting illustrations of proof-of-concept devices and great potential for future applications, more works are needed to further expand the options of guest species, and address the challenges of the incorporation of these devices for practical use, which is still active research of strong interest. 基于客体物种调控，潜在器件应用的快速增长已被讨论。载流子密度的强调控使纳米级晶体管和光电子器件的早期繁荣成为可能，180, 181 而近年来，对额外材料性质的客体物种调控预示了新的晶体管架构。例如，离子晶体管、182 催化晶体管183和热晶体管169已被制造出来，通过插层或门控方法在不同参数和功能空间（离子扩散率、催化性能、热导率）中控制开/关态。尽管概念验证器件的激动人心的展示和未来应用的巨大潜力，仍需要更多工作来进一步扩展客体物种的选择，并解决将这些器件纳入实际应用的挑战，这仍然是具有强烈兴趣的活跃研究。
> 
> Utilization of multivalent 3d metal ions as the guest species with various orbitals and electronic configurations is still at its infancy. So far, the gating method is mainly targeted to achieve a high carrier density in the 2D materials; therefore, the choice of gating guest species (or their combinations) is commonly limited to monovalent cations or ions without the degrees of freedom of variations in charge and chemical valence. The introduction of 3d orbital electrons in ionic media may therefore provoke novel phenomena and new quantum phases. For instance, in platinum thin films, by introducing paramagnetic ions (FeCl4-) with unpaired 3d orbitals as ionic gating media, reversible electrical switching of ferromagnetic states was showcased.184 To this end, the utilization of ionic gating with 3d metal ions has not been widely adopted, which otherwise presents an intriguing fertile ground for exploration of 2D magnetism. Benefitting from battery research, alkali metal intercalation has been studied for decades, and well-controlled intercalation methods have been developed successfully. However, the intercalation of multivalent 3d metal ions with various orbitals and electron clouds still needs more effort. Current multivalent 3d metal intercalation is mainly based on solid-state reactions at high temperatures with a fixed concentration of intercalants. A dynamically tunable approach to controlling the intercalation of multivalent 3d metal is urgently needed for the development of novel low-dimensional quantum and energy materials systems. More broadly, apart from the cation (de-)intercalation approaches discussed above, research on anion (de)intercalation still awaits to be fully exploited. Oxidative anion insertion processes will also offer many opportunities for the rational design and selective synthesis of a broad spectrum of novel metastable materials. A good example is the soft fluorination chemistry involving both anion insertion185 and exchange with large oxidizing potential186 at low temperatures, which has been used to tune the electronic behavior of copper oxides to induce high-temperature superconductivity.187-189 As new reagents and related topochemistry are discovered to bring about an ever-larger variety of topotactic transformations, the materials synthetic methodology will witness many new opportunities, offering the prospect of unprecedented capability to prepare metastable complex phases on demand. 利用具有各种轨道和电子构型的多价3d金属离子作为客体物种仍处于起步阶段。到目前为止，门控方法主要旨在二维材料中实现高载流子密度；因此，门控客体物种（或其组合）的选择通常限于单价阳离子或没有电荷和化学价变化自由度的离子。因此，在离子介质中引入3d轨道电子可能会引发新颖现象和新的量子相。例如，在铂薄膜中，通过引入具有未配对3d轨道的顺磁离子（FeCl₄⁻）作为离子门控介质，展示了铁磁态的可逆电切换。184 为此，利用3d金属离子进行离子门控尚未被广泛采用，否则这为探索二维磁性提供了一个引人入胜的肥沃土壤。受益于电池研究，碱金属插层已被研究了几十年，并成功开发了良好控制的插层方法。然而，具有各种轨道和电子云的多价3d金属离子的插层仍需要更多努力。当前的多价3d金属插层主要基于高温下的固态反应，插层剂浓度固定。迫切需要一种动态可调的方法来控制多价3d金属的插层，以开发新型低维量子和能源材料体系。更广泛地说，除了上述讨论的阳离子（脱）插层方法外，阴离子（脱）插层的研究仍有待充分开发。氧化性阴离子插入过程也将为合理设计和选择性合成广泛的新型亚稳态材料提供许多机会。一个很好的例子是软氟化化学，涉及在低温下同时进行阴离子插入185和具有大氧化电位的交换，186 已被用于调控铜氧化物的电子行为以诱导高温超导电性。187-189 随着新试剂和相关拓扑化学的发现带来了越来越多样化的拓扑化学转变，材料合成方法论将见证许多新机遇，提供了按需制备亚稳态复杂相的前所未有的能力前景。
> 
> **Multivalent ions**: 多价离子——具有多个可能氧化态的离子，如3d过渡金属离子（Fe²⁺/Fe³⁺等）。
> 
> In addition to the enrichment of guest species, the choice of host 2D materials are facing new opportunities as well. Artificial structures created by stacking different layers together provide a unique platform for the intercalation reaction, yet many aspects remain unexplored. A recent study has shown a higher capacity and larger negative intercalation potential by intercalating lithium into a series of various van der Waals heterostructures between graphene and MoX2 (X = S, Se).190 Key characteristics of an intercalation reaction are determined by the crystallographic and electronic structures of the host material; thus, by stacking layers with different materials together, the properties can be engineered. Along this path, the twisted 2D materials with Moiré pattern potentially stand out as another candidate, as theoretical studies have indicated the localized energy favorable positions for introduced guest species in the Moiré lattice, which may produce unique band structure and properties.191, 192 Furthermore, the roll-up of 2D materials could extend the guest species modulation from 2D to 1D,193 and provide more opportunities to study the interactions of guest species beyond 2D materials.194 除了客体物种的丰富外，主体二维材料的选择也面临新的机遇。通过将不同层堆叠在一起创建的人工结构为插层反应提供了独特的平台，但许多方面仍有待探索。最近的一项研究表明，通过将锂插层到石墨烯和MoX₂（X = S，Se）之间的一系列各种范德华异质结构中，可以获得更高的容量和更大的负插层电位。190 插层反应的关键特征由主体材料的晶体学和电子结构决定；因此，通过将不同材料的层堆叠在一起，可以设计性质。沿着这条路径，具有莫尔图案的扭曲二维材料潜在地成为另一个候选者，因为理论研究表明，在莫尔晶格中引入的客体物种具有局域化的能量有利位置，这可能产生独特的能带结构和性质。191, 192 此外，二维材料的卷曲可以将客体物种调控从二维扩展到一维，193 并提供更多机会来研究二维材料之外的客体物种相互作用。194
> 
> **Van der Waals heterostructure**: 范德华异质结构——由不同二维材料层通过范德华力堆叠而成的人工结构。
> 
> Furthermore, there are additional intriguing lattice orderings concurrently emerging with these ionic rearrangements in topochemical (de-)intercalations, such as spontaneously formed oxygen vacancy-ordered structures,113, 195 in which orderings of oxygen vacancies and of cationic sites along certain preferred directions can occur simultaneously. This further motivates the ideas of dual-(de-)intercalation procedures that involve both cationic insertion and anion de-intercalation processes, or even sequential topochemical synthetic reactions. In addition, the different coordination preference of the transition metal ions plays a decisively important role in forming the ionic framework in the lattice. To this end, 2D thin films, exemplified by the case of the infinite-layer nickelates, suggests opportunities of stabilizing more unconventional complex materials, where the crystal field environment and lattice symmetry can be further perturbed and controlled by the on-demand choice of substrate and the consequentially induced epitaxial strain, particularly at the heterointerfaces. 此外，在拓扑化学（脱）插层中，这些离子重排的同时还出现了额外的引人入胜的晶格有序化，例如自发形成的氧空位有序结构，113, 195 其中氧空位有序化和阳离子位点沿某些优先方向的有序化可以同时发生。这进一步激发了涉及阳离子插入和阴离子脱插层过程的双重（脱）插层程序，甚至顺序拓扑化学合成反应的想法。此外，过渡金属离子的不同配位偏好在形成晶格中的离子框架中起着决定性的重要作用。为此，以无限层镍酸盐为例的二维薄膜，暗示了稳定更非常规复杂材料的机会，其中晶体场环境和晶格对称性可以通过按需选择衬底和由此诱导的外延应变进一步扰动和控制，特别是在异质界面处。
> 
> **Oxygen vacancy**: 氧空位——氧化物晶体中氧离子缺失的晶格缺陷，对材料电子性质有重要影响。 **Heterointerface**: 异质界面——两种不同材料之间的界面区域。
> 
> In addition to new modulation methods, further developments are highly needed in the scalable integration at the system level. Large-scale modulation methods are essential for mass production. Although recent reports have shown that some intercalation methods have potential to be scaled up,71, 196 more efforts are still required to contribute a generic methodology. Another important aspect is the integration of guest species modulation, for which the liquid nature of most ionic gating media makes it difficult to integrate with the modern on-chip semiconductor technique, partly due to the difficulties in controlling the size and the shape of the ionic liquid gel.197 As a result, the widely implemented ionic gating techniques are largely limited to the single device level, and relatively far from finding their broad applications in integrated circuits. In this regard, the recently developed solid-state ionic gating materials21, 39, 41 is particularly exciting due to their likely compatibility with lithography and deposition processes, going beyond single device applications. Last but not least, the guest species modulation methods may have fundamental issues of stability and durability, particularly in harsh environments.162 Therefore, inventing smart ways of assembly, encapsulation, and packaging for guest species modulation is key to future critical applications. 除了新的调控方法外，在系统级别的可扩展集成方面迫切需要进一步发展。大规模的调控方法对于大规模生产至关重要。尽管最近的报告显示一些插层方法有潜力扩大规模，71, 196 仍需要更多努力来贡献一种通用的方法论。另一个重要方面是客体物种调制的集成，为此，大多数离子门控介质的液态性质使其难以与现代片上半导体技术集成，部分原因是难以控制离子液体凝胶的尺寸和形状。197 因此，广泛实施的离子门控技术主要限于单器件层面，距离在集成电路中找到其广泛应用还相对较远。在这方面，最近开发的固态离子门控材料21, 39, 41 特别令人兴奋，因为它们可能与光刻和沉积工艺兼容，超越了单器件应用。最后但同样重要的是，客体物种调控方法可能存在稳定性和耐久性的基本问题，特别是在恶劣环境中。162 因此，为客体物种调控发明智能的组装、封装和包装方法是未来关键应用的关键。

> 
> -   **二维材料 (2D materials)**： 就像只有一层或几层原子那么薄的“超级薄饼皮”，宽和长挺大，但厚度几乎可以忽略。

> -   **门控 (Gating)**： 相当于“表面按摩”。让带电的“补品”贴在材料表面，通过静电吸引或排斥，来调控材料内部的电子多少，就像拧水龙头控制水流大小一样。

> -   **过渡金属硫族化合物 (TMDs)**： 二维材料里的一个“大家族”，由过渡金属和硫、硒等元素组成，性质多样，是研究的热门。

> -   **共价键 (Covalent bond)**： 原子间一种很强的连接方式，像胳膊挽着胳膊，很牢固。二维材料层内的原子就是这种连接。
> -   **电场效应晶体管 (EDLT)**： 一种利用“静电按摩”原理做成的电子器件，可以用来精确研究材料性质的变化。
> -   **拓扑化学反应 (Topochemical reaction)**： 一种很“温柔”的化学反应，只改变材料晶体结构中的一部分（比如夹层里的东西），而保持整体骨架不变，就像只换掉柜子里的东西，但柜子本身不动。
> -   **亚稳态相 (Metastable phase)**： 一种不是最稳定，但也不会马上变走的材料状态。通过“温柔”的方法，可以得到很多用传统高温方法做不出来的亚稳态新材料。

> 
> -   **核心问题**：我们如何利用“客座物种”（原子、离子、分子）与二维材料之间的相互作用，来精准、可逆地调控这些材料的物理和化学性质，并创造出传统方法无法得到的新材料和新结构？
> 
> **4. 方法 (Methods)**
> 
> -   **静电门控 (Gating)**：利用液体、胶体或固体的离子导体作为“电解质”，贴到二维材料表面。通过施加电压，让电解质中的离子在材料表面排队，形成一个超强的电场，从而把材料里的电子吸过来或赶走，实现高浓度的电子或空穴（正电荷）掺杂。
