> ## 一、引言
> 
> ### 领域背景与问题
> 
> 随着信息技术对器件小型化、低功耗和高集成度的需求日益增长，**二维（2D）材料**因其原子级厚度和独特的物理性质成为研究热点。其中，**二维多铁材料**因其能同时存在两种或两种以上的铁性序（如铁电性、铁磁性），并允许通过电场控制磁矩（或反之），即**磁电耦合效应**，在新一代信息存储与逻辑器件中展现出巨大潜力。然而，当前的研究面临核心挑战：如何设计出同时具有强磁电耦合、高居里温度（铁磁/铁电转变温度）和显著铁电极化的单相二维多铁材料。
> 
> ### 文献定位与核心价值
> 
> 本研究发表于 _npj Computational Materials_，题为“**通过插层实现具有强磁电耦合的二维多铁材料：第一性原理高通量预测**”。作者提出了一种通用的“**超晶格插层策略**”，即向过渡金属硫族化合物（TMD）双层中插入过渡金属离子，以系统地设计二维多铁材料。通过高通量第一性原理计算，从960种候选化合物中筛选出21种性能优异的二维多铁材料，并按磁性起源将其分为三类，揭示了它们各自独特的磁电耦合机制。该研究不仅极大地扩展了二维铁性材料家族，也为设计新型非易失性存储和逻辑器件铺平了道路。
> 
> * * *
> 
> ## 二、论文结构与逻辑概览
> 
> 本文遵循“**设计策略 → 高通量筛选 → 分类与机制分析 → 结论**”的清晰论证链条：
> 
> 1.  **提出策略与构建候选库**：提出“插层”策略，构建960种非中心对称的AM₂X₄化合物库。
> 2.  **多步高通量筛选**：依次通过稳定性（声子谱、形成能）、铁电翻转能垒、铁电基态等筛选，确定40种稳定的铁电候选材料。
> 3.  **磁性与磁电耦合分类分析**：在40种铁电材料中，识别出21种同时具有磁性的多铁材料。根据磁性来源（A原子或M原子），将其分为三类（type-a, type-b, type-c），并深入剖析每一类的磁电耦合机制，特别是type-a中通过极化翻转调控斯格明子的新奇现象。
> 4.  **总结与展望**：总结三类多铁材料的特性与优势，展望其在低功耗自旋电子学器件中的应用前景。
>

> ## 三、所有图表深度解析
> 
> ### 图1：T-AM₂X₄ 和 H-AM₂X₄ 的结构示意图
> 
> _（可设计为对比性结构示意图）_ 该图展示了插层化合物的两种晶体结构类型及其铁电翻转机制。
> 
> -   **(a) T-AM₂X₄**：基于1T相的MX₂双层。插层原子A（紫色）与底层三个X原子（黄色）和顶层一个X原子成键，形成非对称的四面体配位，导致极化（蓝色箭头）。
> -   **(b) H-AM₂X₄**：基于2H相的MX₂双层。同样，A原子与上下层X原子的不等价键合打破了中心反演对称性。
> -   **铁电翻转机理**：A原子从位置A（FE1态）翻转至位置A‘（FE2态），相当于上下层的配位环境互换，导致极化方向反转。这直观地展示了极化翻转源于插层原子在双层间的位移。
> 
> ### 图2：高通量第一性原理计算筛选流程图
> 
> _（可设计为流程图）_ 该流程图清晰地展示了从960个候选材料到21个目标多铁材料的筛选漏斗。
> 
> 1.  **输入**：960种非中心对称的AM₂X₄化合物。
> 2.  **步骤一：结构与稳定性筛选**。进行结构优化，计算声子谱和形成能，筛选出104个动力学稳定的和100个热力学稳定的化合物。
> 3.  **步骤二：铁电性能筛选**。计算铁电翻转能垒，设定阈值< 200 meV/f.u.，排除已报道的体系，筛选出40个低能耗、高极化的铁电体。
> 4.  **步骤三：磁性筛选与分类**。在40个铁电体中，计算其磁基态，识别出21个多铁材料，并最终分为type-a（12种）、type-b（7种）、type-c（2种）三类。
> 
> ### 图3：金属性铁电体 T-PdZr₂Se₄ 和 T-CoTi₂Te₄ 的电子结构
> 
> _（可设计为四象限图，a/b/c和d/e/f）_ 本图解释了金属性与铁电性共存的机制。
> 
> -   **(a, d) 电子能带结构**：显示两种材料均无带隙，费米能级穿过能带，证实其金属性。
> -   **(b, e) 投影态密度（PDOS）**：揭示了不同原子的电子态对费米能级附近导电电子的贡献。如T-PdZr₂Se₄的导电电子主要来自ZrSe₂层，而T-CoTi₂Te₄的导电电子则具有自旋依赖性。
> -   **(c, f) 电子密度沿z轴分布**：这是关键证据。红线（ρP）代表极化电子密度，主要局域在插层原子（Pd或Co）周围；蓝线（ρc）代表导电电子密度，主要分布在MX₂层（如ZrSe₂）。这两种电荷的空间分离，使得导电电子无法完全屏蔽由插层原子位移产生的极化电荷，从而实现了金属性与铁电性的共存。

> ## 四、正文核心内容剖析
> 
> ### 1. 研究背景与动机
> 
> -   **问题**：现有二维多铁材料难以同时满足强磁电耦合、高极化强度和高居里温度的要求。第I类多铁体磁电耦合弱，第II类多铁体极化和转变温度低。
> -   **策略**：利用**插层**这一化学手段，在TMD双层（如MoS₂）的范德华间隙中插入过渡金属离子，打破晶体的中心反演对称性，从而同时引入铁电性和磁性。
> 
> ### 2. 方法与模型
> 
> -   **高通量筛选**：以3d/4d过渡金属离子（A）和2H/1T相的TMD双层（MX₂，M=过渡金属，X=S/Se/Te）为组件，构建了960种AM₂X₄结构。
> -   **关键筛选标准**：
>     -   **稳定性**：声子谱无虚频（动力学稳定），形成能为负（热力学稳定）。
>     -   **铁电性**：**铁电翻转能垒** < 200 meV/f.u.，确保低功耗翻转。
>     -   **多铁性**：确认磁基态（铁磁FM、反铁磁AFM、亚铁磁FiM）。
> -   **计算参数**：使用基于**密度泛函理论（DFT）**的VASP软件包，采用PBE泛函，并用**DFT+U**方法处理过渡金属d电子的强关联效应。
> -   **物性计算**：用**Berry phase法**计算面内极化，**偶极修正法**计算面外极化；用**CI-NEB方法**计算翻转能垒；用**蒙特卡洛（MC）模拟**估算居里温度/奈尔温度。
> 
> ### 3. 实验结果与讨论
> 
> **（1）铁电性来源与金属性共存**
> 
> -   **半导体铁电体**：筛选出4种（如T-CdSc₂Se₄），其面外极化（最高15.22 pC/m）与典型二维铁电体In₂Se₃相当。
> -   **金属/半金属铁电体**：发现16种非磁性铁电金属。以T-PdZr₂Se₄为例，揭示了**极化电子（局域在插层原子周围）与导电电子（分布在MX₂层）的空间分离**是共存的关键。其面外极化高达3.10 pC/m，远超实验观测到的WTe₂双层（0.42 pC/m）。
> 
> **（2）21种多铁材料的分类与磁电耦合**
> 
> -   **Type-a（12种）：磁性源于MX₂层的M原子**
>     -   **代表**：T-CdCr₂Te₄（FM，居里温度260K，铁电转变温度>300K）。
>     -   **磁电耦合机制**：此为论文最大亮点。通过极化翻转，可以改变上下CrTe₂层的**DMI强度**，从而可逆地在一层中产生/湮灭**磁斯格明子（skyrmions）**，并实现其手性反转。这提供了一种电场控制拓扑磁结构的全新方案。
> -   **Type-b（7种）：磁性源于插层A原子**
>     -   **代表**：T-CoZr₂S₄（FM，居里温度70K，铁电转变温度>300K）。
>     -   **磁电耦合机制**：极化翻转过程中，磁基态经历了**FM → AFM → FM**的可逆相变，同时易磁化轴也发生面外-面内-面外的翻转。
> -   **Type-c（2种）：磁性源于M和A原子**
>     -   **代表**：T-CoTi₂Te₄（FiM）。
>     -   **磁电耦合机制**：极化翻转导致**自旋极化电子在空间上的重新分布**，以及易磁化轴的改变，实现了对自旋电流的调控。
>

> ## 五、结论与贡献
> 
> ### 核心结论
> 
> 1.  **验证了插层策略的有效性**：通过向TMD双层中插入过渡金属离子，成功设计出40种稳定的二维铁电体，其中21种为多铁材料。
> 2.  **揭示了金属性与铁电性共存的机制**：极化电子与导电电子的空间分离是关键。
> 3.  **发现并分类了三类多铁材料**：按磁性起源分为type-a、type-b、type-c，并阐明了它们各自独特的磁电耦合行为。
> 4.  **预测了电场调控拓扑磁结构**：在type-a材料T-CdCr₂Te₄中，理论上实现了通过极化翻转来产生、湮灭和反转斯格明子手性，这是本文最重要的发现。
> 
> ### 主要贡献
> 
> -   **理论贡献**：系统地提出了通过插层设计二维多铁体的通用策略，并建立了结构-物性-机制的分类与关联。
> -   **材料贡献**：预测了21种性能优异的二维多铁新材料，极大地丰富了二维多铁材料数据库。
> -   **应用前景**：为设计电场写入、磁场读取的新型低功耗非易失性存储和逻辑器件（特别是基于斯格明子的赛道存储器）开辟了新路径。

> ## 七、学术思考与批判性分析
> 
> 1.  **计算方法的局限性**：本研究采用DFT+U和MC模拟，其结果高度依赖于U值和交换耦合参数J的准确性。DFT+U方法本身是一种近似处理强关联体系的方法，而MC模拟忽略了量子涨落效应，可能导致对居里温度等临界温度的估算存在偏差。更精确的方法（如HSE06杂化泛函、量子蒙特卡洛）得出的结果是否会有所不同？
> 2.  **铁电翻转路径的简化**：在CI-NEB计算中，作者假设了插层原子的集体位移为翻转路径。但在实际翻转过程中，是否存在更复杂的原子协同运动或中间态？这种简化路径计算出的能垒是否是最低能垒？
> 3.  **斯格明子相的稳定性**：研究中预测的斯格明子相是在极低温下（< 40 K）且需要精细调节外磁场才稳定存在的。在室温零场下，斯格明子会消失。这对实际应用是一个巨大挑战。如何通过材料设计（如掺杂、衬底效应）提高斯格明子的稳定温度窗口和实现零场稳定？
> 4.  **筛选标准的权衡**：筛选标准（如能垒< 200 meV/f.u.）是人为设定的阈值。虽然这保证了低功耗翻转，但可能排除了那些能垒稍高但具有其他优异性能（如超高居里温度）的潜在材料。这个阈值的选取是否需要在能耗和性能之间进行更仔细的权衡？
> 5.  **插层浓度的精确控制**：该策略假设插层原子均匀占据所有四面体空位，形成完美的AM₂X₄有序结构。但在实验中，精确控制插层浓度和实现长程有序排列是巨大的挑战。如果插层原子呈无序或部分占据，对铁电性和磁电耦合有何影响？

> ## 八、用户问题预测与解答
> 
> 1.  **问题：什么是“磁电耦合”？为什么它很重要？** **解答**：**磁电耦合**是指材料的磁序（如铁磁性）和电序（如铁电性）之间的相互调控。即，用电场可以改变磁化状态，用磁场可以改变电极化状态。这非常重要，因为它允许我们利用电场（低功耗）来写入信息，而用磁场（非破坏性）来读取信息，克服了传统磁存储高写入能耗的瓶颈，是实现下一代超低功耗、高密度信息存储器件的关键物理基础。
>     
> 2.  **问题：文中提到的“斯格明子”是什么？它有什么应用价值？** **解答**：**磁斯格明子**是一种受拓扑保护的、涡旋状的纳米级自旋结构。你可以把它想象成磁矩在空间中的一个“纽结”。它具有尺寸小、稳定性高、驱动电流密度低等优点。其最重要的应用是作为**赛道存储器**中的信息比特，数据通过电流驱动斯格明子运动来实现读写，具有非易失、高速度、高密度的潜力。本文的核心创新点之一就是发现了用电场（而非电流）来产生和操控斯格明子的新方法。
>     
> 3.  **问题：Type-a, Type-b, Type-c三类多铁材料的主要区别是什么？** **解答**：分类的核心依据是**磁性来源**，这决定了它们不同的磁电耦合方式。
>     
>     -   **Type-a**：磁性源于MX₂层中的M原子。插层A原子主要起打破对称性、引入铁电性的作用。磁电耦合很强，表现为极化翻转可调控MX₂层中的拓扑磁结构（如斯格明子）。
>     -   **Type-b**：磁性源于插层A原子。MX₂层是非磁性的。磁电耦合表现为极化翻转过程中，A原子间距变化导致磁交换作用改变，从而使磁基态（FM/AFM）和易磁化轴发生转变。
>     -   **Type-c**：磁性由M原子和A原子共同提供。磁电耦合表现为极化翻转改变了自旋极化的空间分布，类似于调控了自旋电流的开关。
> 4.  **问题：为什么金属中可以存在铁电性？这似乎违反直觉。** **解答**：传统观念认为，金属中的自由电子会屏蔽任何内部电场，从而消除铁电性。但本文揭示了一种共存机制：**空间分离**。如果产生极化的电荷（极化电子）与起屏蔽作用的自由电子（导电电子）在空间上分布于不同区域，屏蔽效应就不完全，铁电性就能得以保留。在本文的体系中，极化电子局域在插层原子周围，而导电电子主要在MX₂层中，二者空间分离，使得“金属铁电体”成为可能。
>     
> 5.  **问题：这篇文章只是理论预测，距离实际应用有多远？** **解答**：确实，这是一项“第一性原理预测”的理论工作。从理论预测到实际应用还有很长的路，主要挑战包括：
>     
>     -   **合成挑战**：能否在实验上精确控制插层过程，制备出文中预测的高质量单层样品？
>     -   **稳定性挑战**：材料在空气中的稳定性如何？斯格明子等拓扑相能否在室温、零场下稳定存在？这需要进一步的材料优化。
>     -   **器件集成挑战**：如何将这些脆弱的二维材料与现有的半导体工艺集成，制造出可靠的器件？ 尽管如此，这项研究为实验学家指明了方向，提供了极具潜力的候选材料库，并阐述了清晰的物理机制，是推动该领域发展至关重要的一步。

> ### 问题1：为什么插层会打破中心反演对称性，从而产生铁电性？
> 
> **解答**：想象一下，一个三明治结构的MX₂双层，本身是对称的。现在，向中间的生菜（范德华间隙）里挤入一小团酱料（插层原子A）。由于酱料与上下两层面包的相互作用力不完全相同，它会被更牢固地粘在某一层面包上，与那一面的三个X原子结合，形成“1上3下”或“3上1下”的配位，导致整个三明治结构不再对称。这种结构上的不对称（正负电荷中心不再重合）就产生了自发极化。当施加电场将酱料从“粘在下面”翻转到“粘在上面”时，极化方向也随之反转，这就是铁电翻转的微观图像。
> 
> ### 问题2：什么是斯格明子？为什么它的手性反转很重要？
> 
> **解答**：**斯格明子**可以形象地理解为一个“磁漩涡”，是一种拓扑保护的纳米级自旋结构。它的自旋排列像一个刺猬蜷缩起来的样子，从边缘到中心，自旋方向逐渐翻转。这个“漩涡”有绕行方向，即**手性**（顺时针或逆时针）。在数据存储中，可以用斯格明子的“有”和“无”来代表二进制信息“1”和“0”。而手性反转则提供了另一个可编码的自由度，比如“左旋”代表“1”，“右旋”代表“0”，这能实现多态存储，极大提升信息密度。本文发现的电场控制手性反转，意味着仅用电场就能在两种状态间切换，比传统用电流（产生热量）驱动手性反转更节能、更高效。
> 
> ### 问题3：第一类（Type-I）和第二类（Type-II）多铁在本文中对应什么？为什么本文要提出新的分类？
> 
> **解答**：传统分类是基于铁电性的微观起源。**第一类多铁**的铁电性与磁性来源不同，如BaTiO₃，两者耦合弱。**第二类多铁**的铁电性由特殊磁序（如螺旋磁结构）直接诱导，如TbMnO₃，两者耦合强，但极化普遍很小。

> -   **漏选风险**：严格的稳定性标准（如声子谱无虚频）可能排除一些亚稳态但实际可合成的材料。设定的200 meV/f.u.的势垒阈值，也可能漏掉一些势垒稍高但其他性能（如超高极化）极为优异的材料。

> ### 问题5：这篇论文中发现的金属性多铁材料，它们金属性带来的导电性，会不会成为其作为存储器件的致命缺陷？

<span class="hljs-section"># Realization of 2D multiferroic with strong magnetoelectric coupling by intercalation: a first-principles high-throughput prediction</span>
通过插层实现具有强磁电耦合的二维多铁性材料：第一性原理高通量预测
<span class="hljs-strong">**2D**</span>: 二维
<span class="hljs-strong">**multiferroic**</span>: 多铁性材料，同时具有两种或以上铁性序（如铁电性、铁磁性）的材料
<span class="hljs-strong">**magnetoelectric coupling**</span>: 磁电耦合，指磁场与电场之间的相互耦合效应
<span class="hljs-strong">**intercalation**</span>: 插层，指外来物质插入到晶体间隙中形成化合物
<span class="hljs-strong">**first-principles**</span>: 第一性原理，基于量子力学基本方程的计算方法
<span class="hljs-strong">**high-throughput**</span>: 高通量，指大规模自动化计算筛选

The discovery of novel two-dimensional (2D) multiferroic materials is attractive due to their potential for the realization of information storage and logic devices. Although many approaches have been explored to simultaneously introduce ferromagnetic (FM) and ferroelectric (FE) orders into a 2D material, the resulting systems are often plagued by weak magnetoelectric (ME) coupling or limited room-temperature stability. Here, we present a superlattice strategy to construct noncentrosymmetric AM2X4 multiferroic monolayers, i.e., intercalating transition metal ions (A) into the tetragonal-like vacancies of transition metal dichalcogenide bilayers (MX2). Starting from 960 intercalated AM2X4 compounds, our high-throughput calculations have identified 21 multiferroics with robust magnetic order, large FE polarization, low transition barrier, high FE/FM transition temperature, and strong ME coupling. According to the origin of magnetism, we have classified them into twelve type-a, seven type-b, and two type-c multiferroics, which exhibit different ME coupling behavior. During the switching of polarization, the reversal of skyrmions chirality, the transition of the magnetic ground state from FM to antiferromagnetic, and the changes in spin-polarized electron distribution were observed in type-a, type-b, and type-c 2D multiferroic materials, respectively. These results substantially expand the family of 2D ferroic materials and pave an avenue for designing and implementing nonvolatile logic and memory devices.
新型二维（2D）多铁性材料的发现因其在信息存储和逻辑器件中的潜在应用而备受关注。尽管已探索了许多方法在二维材料中同时引入铁磁（FM）和铁电（FE）序，但所得系统往往受限于弱的磁电（ME）耦合或有限的室温稳定性。在此，我们提出了一种超晶格策略，通过将过渡金属离子（A）插入到过渡金属二硫族化合物双层（MX2）的四方空位中，构建非中心对称的AM2X4多铁性单层。从960种插层AM2X4化合物出发，我们的高通量计算鉴定出21种具有稳健磁序、大铁电极化、低转变势垒、高铁电/铁磁转变温度和强磁电耦合的多铁性材料。根据磁性的来源，我们将它们分为十二种a型、七种b型和两种c型多铁性材料，它们表现出不同的磁电耦合行为。在极化翻转过程中，分别在a型、b型和c型二维多铁性材料中观察到斯格明子手性的反转、磁基态从铁磁到反铁磁的转变以及自旋极化电子分布的变化。这些结果显著扩展了二维铁性材料家族，并为设计和实现非易失性逻辑和存储器件开辟了道路。

<span class="hljs-strong">**ferroelectric (FE)**</span>: 铁电性，材料具有自发电极化且可被外电场反转的性质
<span class="hljs-strong">**superlattice**</span>: 超晶格，由两种或多种材料交替生长形成的周期性结构
<span class="hljs-strong">**noncentrosymmetric**</span>: 非中心对称，缺乏反演中心的晶体结构

With the rapid development of information technology and increasing demand for the miniaturization of devices, two-dimensional (2D) materials with low power consumption, high storage density, and integration have become the focus of intensive research1–4. Among them, 2D multiferroics combine the advantages of multiple ferroic orders and hold promise in multi-stage storage and nonvolatile logic devices5–7. They are a special class of materials that simultaneously exhibit two or more primary ferroic orders, such as ferroelectricity, ferromagnetism, ferroelasticity, and ferrovalley, and there exists coupling between them, which can be mutually controlled. In particular, the coupling between ferroelectric (FE) and ferromagnetic (FM) orders leads to the magnetoelectric (ME) coupling effect, which offers a unique opportunity to control the spin by applying an electric field. This kind of ME coupling could be used to develop new-generation memory devices in which data can be written electrically and read magnetically, avoiding the drawback of high writing energy in conventional magnetic random-access memory8–10. Depending on the microscopic FE mechanism, multiferroic materials can be divided into two categories, i.e., type-I and type-II. In type-I multiferroics, the ferroelectricity and magnetism originate from different atoms. The coupling between electric dipoles and magnetic spins is mediated by spin-lattice interaction, usually leading to weak ME coupling, but often exhibits considerable spontaneous polarization and high transition temperature. To date, several type-I multiferroics such as CrN11, MIMIIP2X6 (MI, MII = metal elements, X = O/S/Se/Te)12, and ReWCl613 have been predicted theoretically, while multiferroic has been confirmed experimentally in 2D CuCrP2S614. In type-II multiferroics, FE order generally arises from special magnetic order, and the direct correlation between polarization and magnetization promises strong ME coupling. Generally, special magnetic order, such as spiral or complex collinear magnetic order, could lead to spin and charge frustration, which significantly reduces the intensity of FE polarization and transition temperature. Hf2VC2F215, MnRe2O816, and VX2 (X = Cl/Br/I)17 monolayers are predicted to exhibit 120° non-collinear Ytype antiferromagnetic (AFM) spin spiral orders, which can induce FE polarization perpendicular to the spin spiral plane. The polarization switching can realize the reversal of the spin spiral chirality in MnRe2O8 and VX2 monolayers, demonstrating strong magnetoelectric coupling behavior. In addition, Bao et al. theoretically demonstrated that synthesized FeOCl monolayer has a cycloidal spin spiral with a propagation vector of (0.274, 0.5, 0) located in the ac plane, and the induced out-of-plane ferroelectricity can switch the spin spiral chirality through an electric field18. There was also experimental evidence that the NiI2 monolayer exhibited proper screw state with a given handedness coupled to the charge degrees of freedom, producing a chirality-controlled electric polarization with a transition temperature of 21 K19. Since both type-I and type-II multiferroics have advantages and shortcomings, continuous search for single-phase 2D multiferroic materials with excellent FE–FM multiferroic properties, i.e., strong ME coupling, high Curie temperature, and FE polarization intensity, is still a challenge for this fast-growing field. Intercalation is a chemical process in which foreign species insert into crystal gaps to eventually form hybrid intercalation compounds. In 2D layered materials with van der Waals (vdW) gap, intercalation reaction has been proven as a powerful approach for synthesizing single-phase crystals with the coexistence of multiple order parameters20. A family of 2D MA2Z4 materials was obtained by intercalating a MoS2-type MZ2 monolayer into InSe-type A2Z2 bilayers. Among them, 2D MnBi2Te4 is an AFM axion/Chern insulator with quantized anomalous Hall conductivity21–23, while PdBi2Te4 is a superconducting topological metal24. Nontrivial topological properties, FM semiconductors, Ising superconductivity, and robust electron valleys are also found in SrGa2Te4, NbSi2N4, TiSi2N4, MoSi2P4, MoSi2As4, WSi2P4, and WSi2As421,25. In addition, the Kagome lattice formed by Ta8Se12 (66.7% Ta-intercalated) stabilizes the chargedensity-wave and exhibits FM metallicity20, while Cr-intercalated 2H-TaS2 (Cr1/3TaS2) is a chiral crystal, which has a helical spin texture and continuously transforms into a chiral soliton lattice, eventually reaches a forced FM state under an external magnetic field26. In addition, 2D AgCrS2 composed of Ag intercalated CrS2 bilayers has been experimentally demonstrated to be stable and exhibit superionic and FM behavior at room temperature27,28. Briefly speaking, intercalation breaks the inversion symmetry of the system, changes the bonding order from vdW to ionic or covalent type, and modulates the band structures of the host lattice and intercalated species. Inspired by the exotic properties triggered by unique intercalated architectures, herein we propose a general approach to systematically design 2D multiferroic materials by non-centrosymmetric intercalation of 3d/4d transition metal ions (A) into the 2H/1T phase of TMD bilayers (MX2). By filtering a total of 960 possible combinations, we have reproduced the previously reported CoMo2S429, AgCr2S4, and AgCr2Se428, and further unveiled 40 dynamically and thermodynamically stable 2D AM2X4 ferroelectrics with low FE switching barrier (&lt;200 meV/f.u.). Among them, different magnetic orders such as FM, AFM, and ferrimagnetic (FiM) are identified in the 21 FE systems. Depending on the position of the magnetic ions (intercalated A ions or host M atoms), we classify these 21 multiferroics into three types and investigate their different ME coupling behavior. Our intercalation strategy opens an avenue to develop novel 2D materials with multiferroic properties and explores other fascinating topological phenomena.
随着信息技术的快速发展和器件小型化需求的日益增长，具有低功耗、高存储密度和集成度的二维（2D）材料已成为研究热点1–4。其中，二维多铁性材料结合了多种铁性序的优势，在多位存储和非易失性逻辑器件中具有广阔前景5–7。它们是一类同时表现出两种或以上主铁性序（如铁电性、铁磁性、铁弹性和铁谷性）的特殊材料，且序之间存在可相互控制的耦合。特别是，铁电（FE）序与铁磁（FM）序之间的耦合产生磁电（ME）耦合效应，为通过施加电场控制自旋提供了独特可能。这种磁电耦合可用于开发新型存储器件，其中数据可电写入、磁读取，避免了传统磁性随机存储器写入能量高的缺点8–10。根据微观铁电机制，多铁性材料可分为两类，即I型和II型。在I型多铁性材料中，铁电性和磁性源自不同原子，电偶极子和磁矩之间的耦合由自旋-晶格相互作用介导，通常导致弱磁电耦合，但往往表现出可观的自主极化和高转变温度。迄今，已有多种I型多铁性材料如CrN11、MIMIIP2X6（MI, MII = 金属元素，X = O/S/Se/Te）12和ReWCl613被理论预测，而二维CuCrP2S6中的多铁性已得到实验证实14。在II型多铁性材料中，铁电序通常源自特殊的磁序，极化与磁化的直接关联保证了强磁电耦合。通常，特殊的磁序如螺旋状或复杂共线磁序会导致自旋和电荷阻挫，显著降低铁电极化强度和转变温度。Hf2VC2F215、MnRe2O816和VX2（X = Cl/Br/I）17单层被预测表现出120°非共线Y型反铁磁（AFM）自旋螺旋序，可诱导垂直于自旋螺旋面的铁电极化。在MnRe2O8和VX2单层中，极化翻转可实现自旋螺旋手性的反转，展现出强磁电耦合行为。此外，Bao等人理论上证明，合成的FeOCl单层具有传播矢量为(0.274, 0.5, 0)的摆线型自旋螺旋，位于ac平面内，并可通过电场诱导面外铁电性来翻转自旋螺旋手性18。实验证据也表明，NiI2单层表现出与电荷自由度耦合的特定手性的正当螺旋态，产生手性调控的电极化，转变温度为21 K19。鉴于I型和II型多铁性材料各有优缺点，持续寻找具有优异铁电-铁磁多铁性（即强磁电耦合、高居里温度和高铁电极化强度）的单相二维多铁性材料仍是这一快速发展领域面临的挑战。插层是一种化学过程，外来物种插入晶体间隙，最终形成杂化插层化合物。在具有范德华（vdW）间隙的二维层状材料中，插层反应已被证明是合成具有多种序参量共存的单相晶体的有力方法20。通过将MoS2型MZ2单层插入InSe型A2Z2双层中，获得了一族二维MA2Z4材料。其中，二维MnBi2Te4是一种具有量子化反常霍尔电导率的反铁磁轴子/陈绝缘体21–23，而PdBi2Te4是一种超导拓扑金属24。在SrGa2Te4、NbSi2N4、TiSi2N4、MoSi2P4、MoSi2As4、WSi2P4和WSi2As4中也发现了非平庸拓扑性质、铁磁半导体、伊辛超导和稳健的电子谷21,25。此外，由Ta8Se12（66.7% Ta插层）形成的Kagome晶格稳定了电荷密度波并表现出铁磁金属性20，而Cr插层的2H-TaS2（Cr1/3TaS2）是一种手性晶体，具有螺旋自旋织构，可连续转变为手性孤子晶格，在外磁场下最终达到强制铁磁态26。此外，由Ag插层CrS2双层组成的二维AgCrS2已被实验证明在室温下稳定，并表现出超离子和铁磁行为27,28。简言之，插层打破了系统的反演对称性，将键合顺序从范德华类型转变为离子或共价类型，并调节了宿主晶格和插层物种的能带结构。受独特插层结构所带来的奇异性质启发，我们在此提出一种通用方法，通过将3d/4d过渡金属离子（A）非中心对称地插层到TMD双层（MX2）的2H/1T相中，系统设计二维多铁性材料。通过筛选总共960种可能组合，我们重现了先前报道的CoMo2S429、AgCr2S4和AgCr2Se428，并进一步揭示了40种动力学和热力学稳定的二维AM2X4铁电体，具有低铁电翻转势垒（&lt;200 meV/f.u.）。其中，在21个铁电体系中识别出不同的磁序，如铁磁、反铁磁和亚铁磁（FiM）。根据磁性离子（插层A离子或宿主M原子）的位置，我们将这21种多铁性材料分为三类，并研究了它们不同的磁电耦合行为。我们的插层策略为开发新型具有多铁性性质的二维材料以及探索其他迷人的拓扑现象开辟了道路。

<span class="hljs-section"># Results and discussions</span>

<span class="hljs-section">## Screening of 2D non-centrosymmetric intercalated compounds</span>
二维非中心对称插层化合物的筛选

Transition metal disulfide compounds (TMDs) are a series of layered crystals with a chemical formula MX2 (M = transition metal, X = S/Se/Te), in which the neighboring MX2 layers interact weakly with each other via vdW interaction. Such weak interlayer interaction means that different thicknesses of ultrathin TMD films can be obtained in experiments using exfoliation approaches. In general, MX2 monolayers crystallize into three main phases: 1T (triangular) phase with D3d symmetry, 2H (hexagonal) phase with D3h symmetry, and distorted 1T phase (1T’ phase). Some MX2 with 1T and 2H phases exhibit tunable band gaps, high carrier mobility, and strong intrinsic spin-orbit coupling, making them attractive candidates in electronic, optical, and spintronic devices30.
过渡金属二硫族化合物（TMDs）是一系列层状晶体，化学式为MX2（M = 过渡金属，X = S/Se/Te），相邻MX2层之间通过范德华相互作用弱结合。这种弱的层间相互作用意味着可以通过剥离方法在实验中获得不同厚度的超薄TMD薄膜。通常，MX2单层结晶为三种主要相：具有D3d对称性的1T（三角形）相、具有D3h对称性的2H（六角形）相以及扭曲的1T相（1T’相）。一些具有1T和2H相的MX2表现出可调带隙、高载流子迁移率和强内禀自旋-轨道耦合，使其成为电子、光学和自旋电子学器件中极具吸引力的候选材料30。

Owing to the existence of the vdW gap, the MX2 compounds can be easily intercalated by atoms, molecules, or atomic layers, and their properties can also be modulated via intercalation. For example, previous studies have shown that after the intercalation of Ag ions into a 2H–CrS2 bilayer or of Cu into a 2H-CrX2 (X = S/Se) bilayer, the intercalation compounds AgCrX2 and CuCrX2 transits to multiferroic state due to the breaking of inversion symmetry by Ag/Cu ions28,31; 2D CoMo2S4 also shows multiferroic behavior as the Co ions are intercalated into the 1T-MoS2 bilayer and the two FE ground states are energetically degenerate with opposite chirality of the Dzyaloshinskii Moriya interaction (DMI)29.
由于范德华间隙的存在，MX2化合物可以很容易地被原子、分子或原子层插层，其性质也可以通过插层进行调控。例如，先前研究表明，将Ag离子插层到2H–CrS2双层或Cu插层到2H-CrX2（X = S/Se）双层后，插层化合物AgCrX2和CuCrX2由于Ag/Cu离子打破反演对称性而转变为多铁性态28,31；二维CoMo2S4也表现出多铁性行为，因为Co离子插层到1T-MoS2双层中，且两个铁电基态能量简并，具有相反手性的Dzyaloshinskii-Moriya相互作用（DMI）29。

Inspired by these results, we have constructed a series of hybrid intercalation compounds using 2H or 1T phase MX2 bilayers with AB stacking as host lattice and A atoms as guest species. When the intercalated A atoms uniformly occupy the tetragonal-like vacancies in the interlayer space of bilayer MX2, an A atom coordinates with one X atom from the top layer and three X atoms from the bottom layer. This leads to an unequal distance between A and the two MX2 layers, thereby breaking the central inversion symmetry of the system and generating spontaneous polarization. The tetrahedral crystal field can be switched by a 180° rotation when an A ion bonds with three X atoms in the top layer and one X atom in the bottom layer, leading to polarization reversal. The selection of the MX2 layer and A atoms is summarized in Supplementary Table 1, and a total of 960 noncentrosymmetric intercalation compounds were obtained with all combinations of the MX2 layer and A atom. The representative crystal structures of 2H phase compounds AM2X4 (H-AM2X4) and 1T phase compound AM2X4 (T-AM2X4), as well as the corresponding FE switching mechanism, are shown in Fig. 1.

Thirdly, the smaller the FE switching barrier, the less energy is consumed for polarization reversal. <span class="hljs-section">## The intrinsic FE behaviors of AM2X4 monolayers</span>
AM2X4单层的内禀铁电行为

In-plane polarizations (Pin) along the [110] direction and out-ofplane polarizations (Pout) are observed in the semiconducting T-CdSc2Se4, T-CdRh2S4, T-CoSc2S4, and T-CoY2S4. The Pin of T-CdSc2Se4, T-CdRh2S4, T-CoSc2S4, and T-CoY2S4 calculated with the Berry phase method are 269.28 (2.30), 208.55 (1.36), 306.45 (2.16), and 128.26 (1.13) pC/m (eÅ), respectively, while the Pout evaluated by the dipole correction approach are 15.22 (0.13), 9.32 (0.06), 11.35 (0.08), and 13.62 (0.12) pC/m (eÅ). These values are comparable to the typical 2D FE semiconductor In2Se3 [Pin = 259.08 (2.36) pC/m (eÅ), Pout = 12.08 (0.11) pC/m (eÅ)]32. Similar to In2Se3, the interesting intercorrelated in-plane and out-of-plane ferroelectricity may also occur in these 2D FE semiconductors, where the outof-plane electric field used to reverse the Pout also reverses the Pin, which has great potential for advanced memory devices and multidirectional controlled field-effect transistors33–35.
在半导体T-CdSc2Se4、T-CdRh2S4、T-CoSc2S4和T-CoY2S4中，观察到沿[110]方向的面内极化（Pin）和面外极化（Pout）。用Berry相位方法计算的T-CdSc2Se4、T-CdRh2S4、T-CoSc2S4和T-CoY2S4的Pin分别为269.28 (2.30)、208.55 (1.36)、306.45 (2.16)和128.26 (1.13) pC/m (eÅ)，而用偶极修正方法评估的Pout分别为15.22 (0.13)、9.32 (0.06)、11.35 (0.08)和13.62 (0.12) pC/m (eÅ)。这些值与典型的二维铁电半导体In2Se3 [Pin = 259.08 (2.36) pC/m (eÅ), Pout = 12.08 (0.11) pC/m (eÅ)]32相当。与In2Se3类似，这些二维铁电半导体中也可能出现有趣的面内和面外铁电关联现象，其中用于反转Pout的面外电场也会反转Pin，这在先进存储器件和多方向控制场效应晶体管中具有巨大潜力33–35。

For metals or half-metals, there is only a Pout as the Pin is annihilated by free electrons. Using metallic T-PdZr2Se4 as an example, we discuss the mechanism of coexistence of ferroelectricity and metallicity. The metallic behavior of T-PdZr2Se4 was confirmed by calculating the electronic band structure, as displayed in Fig. 3a, b. The conduction electrons ρcðzÞ calculated by Eq. (3) are mainly distributed in the top and bottom ZrSe2 layers (Fig. 3c), which is consistent with the projected density of states (PDOS) results (Fig. 3b). However, the polarization electrons ρPðzÞ obtained by Eq. (5) are mainly distributed around the intercalated Pd atoms and exhibit an oscillatory behavior (Fig. 3c), which is related to the non-uniform charge density distribution caused by the bonding difference between the Pd and the top and bottom Se atoms. The different distribution between polarization and conduction electrons prevents the annihilation of Pout, where the conduction electrons distributed around the Zr atoms on the bottom layer cannot fully screen the vertical polarization originating from the intercalating Pd atom. Therefore, ferroelectricity and metallic behavior can coexist in T-PdZr2Se4, with a Pout of 3.10 pC/m. This mechanism is also responsible for the Pout of other sixteen non-magnetic FE compounds with metallic or half-metallic behavior, as summarized in Table 1, with values ranging from 0.43 to 9.61 pC/m, larger than the experimentally observed polarization of the metallic WTe2 bilayer (0.42 pC/m)36.

In the following, we focus on the 21 intercalation materials with the coexistence of ferroelectricity and magnetism and perform a detailed analysis of their magnetism and ME coupling effects. For simplicity, we classify the 21 systems into three types according to the origin of magnetism: (i) type-a, in which the magnetism of the system originates from the magnetic atoms M in the top and bottom MX2 layers; (ii) type-b, in which the magnetism originates from the intercalated A atoms; (iii) type-c, in which both the M atoms in the MX2 layers and the intercalated A atoms provide magnetism simultaneously.

Type-a multiferroic materials, including T-PdCr2Te4, T-CuMn2Se4, T-AgMn2S4, T-AgMn2Se4, T-AgTc2S4, T-AgTc2Se4, T-AgCr2Te4, T-ZnCr2Se4, T-CdMn2Se4, T-CdCr2S4, T-CdCr2Se4, and T-CdCr2Te4, are obtained by inserting Pd/Cu/Ag/Zn/Cd atoms into T-CrX2, T-MnX2, or T-TcX2 bilayers. Therefore, the magnetism comes from the M atoms in the MX2 bilayers, and the special bonding of the intercalated A atom results in negligible differences in the magnetic moments of the M atoms in the top and bottom layers (~0.1 μB). Among the considered systems, there are seven type-b multiferroic materials, including four T-AM2X4 (CoSc2S4, CoY2S4, CoZr2S4, CoZr2Se4) and three H-AM2X4 (CrMo2Se4, MnMo2S4, MnMo2Se4). In contrast to the type-a multiferroics, the intercalated species in the type-b multiferroics are transition metal atoms (Cr, Co, and Mn) with partially filled d orbitals, which are mainly responsible for the magnetism. The pristine MX2 (M = Sc, Y, Zr, Mo; X = S, Se) bilayers are non-magnetic, and the intercalation of A (A = Cr, Co, and Mn) atoms causes a transition from non-magnetic to magnetic state in the system. The preferred magnetic order was determined by comparing the energy of different magnetic configurations (Supplementary Table 4), and three parameters for magnetic exchange interactions (J1, J2, and J3) are sufficient to describe the magnetic interactions as the magnetism is provided by the intercalated A atom within one layer. Take T-CoZr2S4 for example, its magnetic ground state is FM with the first nearest neighbor magnetic coupling coefficient J1 &gt; 0. This can be explained by the GKA rules and is approximately 20 times larger than that of J2 and J3, dominating the magnetic interaction between the Co atoms. Meanwhile, the magnetization of T-CoZr2S4 prefers the out-of-plane direction with an MAE of 0.61 meV/Co, and the Curie temperature from MC simulation is about 70 K, as illustrated in Supplementary Fig. 10j. Simultaneously, T-CoZr2S4 remains in a FE state at 300 K without significant structural deformation (Supplementary Fig. 9c), indicating that it is thermodynamically stable at room temperature, and the FE transition temperature is higher than room temperature. Combined with the FM transition temperature, it is inferred that T-CoZr2S4 has room temperature stability and maintains multiferroic of coexisting ferroelectricity and ferromagnetism below 60 K. Moreover, T-CoZr2Se4 also has FM magnetic ground state, whereas T-CoSc2S4, T-CoY2S4, H-CrMo2Se4, H-MnMo2S4, and H-MnMo2Se4 favor AFM order, and T-CoY2S4 has the highest Néel temperature of 50 K (Table 2). Except for H-MnMo2Se4, the majority of type-b multiferroics prefer the out-of-plane direction as the easy magnetization axis, and T-CoY2S4 has a maximum MAE of 0.92 meV/Co.
在所考察的体系中，有七种b型多铁性材料，包括四种T-AM2X4（CoSc2S4、CoY2S4、CoZr2S4、CoZr2Se4）和三种H-AM2X4（CrMo2Se4、MnMo2S4、MnMo2Se4）。与a型多铁性材料相反，b型多铁性材料中的插层物种是具有部分填充d轨道的过渡金属原子（Cr、Co和Mn），主要负责磁性。原始MX2（M = Sc, Y, Zr, Mo; X = S, Se）双层是非磁性的，插层A（A = Cr, Co, Mn）原子导致体系从非磁性态转变为磁性态。通过比较不同磁构型的能量（补充表4），确定了优先磁序，由于磁性由单层内的插层A原子提供，三个磁交换作用参数（J1、J2和J3）足以描述磁相互作用。以T-CoZr2S4为例，其磁基态为FM，第一近邻磁耦合系数J1 &gt; 0。这可用GKA规则解释，且J1约为J2和J3的20倍，主导了Co原子间的磁相互作用。同时，T-CoZr2S4的磁化倾向于面外方向，MAE为0.61 meV/Co，MC模拟的居里温度约为70 K，如补充图10j所示。同时，T-CoZr2S4在300 K时仍保持铁电态，无显著结构变形（补充图9c），表明其在室温下热力学稳定，铁电转变温度高于室温。结合FM转变温度，可推断T-CoZr2S4具有室温稳定性，并在60 K以下维持铁电性和铁磁性共存的多铁性。此外，T-CoZr2Se4也具有FM磁基态，而T-CoSc2S4、T-CoY2S4、H-CrMo2Se4、H-MnMo2S4和H-MnMo2Se4倾向于AFM序，T-CoY2S4具有最高的Néel温度50 K（表2）。除H-MnMo2Se4外，大多数b型多铁性材料倾向于面外方向作为易磁化轴，T-CoY2S4具有最大MAE，为0.92 meV/Co。

The magnetic behavior (magnetic ground state and the direction of the easy magnetization axis) during the FE polarization reversal process was examined to investigate the ME coupling phenomena of T-CoZr2S4 (Fig. 6). T-CoZr2S4 maintains the ground state of FM during the initial phase of polarization reversal because the energies of FM are lower than those of AFM states. Nevertheless, the energies of AFM states decrease as get closer to the PE phase, turning the magnetic ground state into an AFM state. Subsequently, the magnetic ground state reverts to the FM state as the energy difference between the AFM and FM states increases. Interestingly, T-CoZr2S4 favors in-plane MAE with a greater value of −13.34 meV/Co in the PE phase, but in other states of the polarization reversal process, it adopts an out-of-plane easy magnetization axis. The magnetic ground state and MAE values for the other type-b multiferroics are also summarized in Supplementary Table 5. During the process of polarization reversal, T-CoY2S4 and H-MnMo2S4 exhibit a similar ME coupling phenomenon as T-CoZr2S4, while T-CoZr2Se4 and T-CoSc2S4 solely change the magnetic ground state, and H-MnMo2Se4 only shows changes in MAE (Supplementary Fig. 12).
我们检查了铁电极化翻转过程中磁行为（磁基态和易磁化轴方向）的变化，以研究T-CoZr2S4的磁电耦合现象（图6）。在极化翻转的初始阶段，T-CoZr2S4保持FM基态，因为FM态的能量低于AFM态。然而，随着接近顺电（PE）相，AFM态的能量降低，磁基态转变为AFM态。随后，随着AFM和FM态能量差的增大，磁基态恢复为FM态。有趣的是，在PE相中，T-CoZr2S4倾向于面内MAE，值较大，为−13.34 meV/Co，但在极化翻转过程的其他态中，它采用面外易磁化轴。其他b型多铁性材料的磁基态和MAE值也总结于补充表5中。在极化翻转过程中，T-CoY2S4和H-MnMo2S4表现出与T-CoZr2S4类似的磁电耦合现象，而T-CoZr2Se4和T-CoSc2S4仅改变磁基态，H-MnMo2Se4仅表现出MAE的变化（补充图12）。

In type-c multiferroics, pristine TiX2 (X = Se/Te) bilayers are nonmagnetic semiconductors, and the intercalation of magnetic Co atoms produces two stable multiferroic materials with lower barriers, namely, T-CoTi2Se4 and T-CoTi2Te4. In addition to the intercalated Co atoms with intrinsic magnetic moment, induced magnetic moments also exist on the Ti atoms. The special coordination between the intercalated Co atoms and the Te atoms results in distinct magnetic moments between the top (Titop) and bottom (Tibot) layers of Ti. Therefore, one has to consider possible FiM configurations. The magnetic configurations and corresponding energies of FM, AFM, and FiM states for type-c multiferroics are presented in Supplementary Fig. 7c and Supplementary Table 6. Both T-CoTi2Se4 and T-CoTi2Te4 exhibit FiM ground states with total net magnetic moment of 0.24 μB/f.u. and 0.21 μB/f.u., respectively. Using T-CoTi2Te4 as a representative, we have investigated the possible ME coupling phenomena in type-c multiferroic material. The band structures of FE1 and FE2 states exhibit metallic properties (Fig. 7a, b), but at the Fermi level, conduction electrons in the FE1 state are mainly contributed by spin-down electrons, while those in the FE2 state are predominantly dominated by spin-up channel, as illustrated in Fig. 7c, d. Furthermore, Titop and Tibot possess magnetic moments of 0.62 μB and 0.82 μB in the FE1 state, whereas these values become 0.82 μB and 0.62 μB in the FE2 state, indicating that reversing polarization direction may affect the spatial distribution of spin-polarized electrons. The more intuitive spin-polarized charge distribution diagrams can be found in Supplementary Fig. 13, which further demonstrates the influence of polarization on the spin-charge distribution. The CoTi2Te4 maintains a FiM order as its magnetic ground state in both FE1 and FE2 phases, but its easy magnetization axis changes from xdirection to z-direction after polarization reversal, further confirming the characteristics of ME coupling.
在c型多铁性材料中，原始TiX2（X = Se/Te）双层是非磁性半导体，插层磁性Co原子产生两种具有较低势垒的稳定多铁性材料，即T-CoTi2Se4和T-CoTi2Te4。除了具有内禀磁矩的插层Co原子外，Ti原子上也存在诱导磁矩。插层Co原子与Te原子之间的特殊配位导致顶层Ti（Titop）和底层Ti（Tibot）的磁矩不同。因此，必须考虑可能的亚铁磁构型。c型多铁性材料的FM、AFM和FiM态磁构型及相应能量见补充图7c和补充表6。T-CoTi2Se4和T-CoTi2Te4均表现出FiM基态，总净磁矩分别为0.24 μB/f.u.和0.21 μB/f.u.。以T-CoTi2Te4为代表，我们研究了c型多铁性材料中可能的磁电耦合现象。FE1和FE2态的能带结构表现出金属性质（图7a、b），但在费米能级处，FE1态中的传导电子主要由自旋向下电子贡献，而FE2态中则主要由自旋向上通道支配，如图7c、d所示。此外，FE1态中Titop和Tibot的磁矩分别为0.62 μB和0.82 μB，而在FE2态中这些值变为0.82 μB和0.62 μB，表明极化方向反转可能影响自旋极化电子的空间分布。更直观的自旋极化电荷分布图见补充图13，进一步证明了极化对自旋电荷分布的影响。CoTi2Te4在FE1和FE2相中均保持FiM序作为磁基态，但其易磁化轴在极化翻转后从x方向变为z方向，进一步证实了磁电耦合特性。

To summarize, we performed high-throughput first-principles calculations to filter potential 2D multiferroics induced by intercalated ions displacement among TMD intercalation compounds. By intercalating transition metal ions into the tetragonal-like vacancies of MX2 bilayers, we obtained 960 candidate 2D AM2X4 structures and identified 40 systems possessing high structural stability, large FE polarization, low FE switching barrier, and high transition temperature. We carefully discussed the intrinsic stabilization mechanism of polarization in four FE semiconductors and 31 FE metals/half-metals, and the out-plane polarization vary from 0.26 pC/m to 15.22 pC/m, comparable to those in typical 2D FE materials. Among the selected 40 FE intercalation compounds, there are 21 materials exhibit ferroelectricity and magnetism simultaneously. According to the origin of magnetism, we divided them into twelve type-a, seven type-b, and two type-c multiferroic materials, which exhibit different ME coupling behavior. In type-a multiferroic T-CdCr2Te4, the magnetism mainly arises from the CrTe2 bilayer, and skyrmion conversion between its top and bottom layers can be achieved through polarization switching. In type-b multiferroic T-CoZr2S4, the unfilled d orbitals of the intercalated Co atoms contribute to the magnetic moment. During the FE switching process, the magnetic ground state undergoes a transition from FM to AFM to FM, and the easy magnetization axis switches between out-of-plane and in-plane directions. The intercalated Co atoms can introduce magnetism into the non-magnetic TiSe2/TiTe2 bilayers, forming type-c multiferroics. In type-c multiferroic T-CoTi2Te4, both the spin-polarized charge spatial distribution and MAE change during polarization reversal. All these findings significantly expand the family of 2D ferroic materials and open a pathway for the development and implementation of nonvolatile logic and memory devices.
总结而言，我们通过高通量第一性原理计算，筛选了TMD插层化合物中由插层离子位移诱导的潜在二维多铁性材料。通过将过渡金属离子插层到MX2双层的四方空位中，我们获得了960种候选二维AM2X4结构，并鉴定出40种具有高结构稳定性、大铁电极化、低铁电翻转势垒和高转变温度的体系。我们仔细讨论了四种铁电半导体和31种铁电金属/半金属中极化的内禀稳定机制，面外极化在0.26 pC/m至15.22 pC/m之间变化，与典型二维铁电材料相当。在选出的40种铁电插层化合物中，有21种同时表现出铁电性和磁性。根据磁性来源，我们将它们分为十二种a型、七种b型和两种c型多铁性材料，它们表现出不同的磁电耦合行为。在a型多铁性T-CdCr2Te4中，磁性主要来自CrTe2双层，通过极化切换可实现其顶层和底层之间的斯格明子转换。在b型多铁性T-CoZr2S4中，插层Co原子未填满的d轨道贡献磁矩。在铁电翻转过程中，磁基态经历从FM到AFM再到FM的转变，易磁化轴在面外和面内方向之间切换。插层Co原子可将磁性引入非磁性TiSe2/TiTe2双层，形成c型多铁性材料。在c型多铁性T-CoTi2Te4中，极化翻转过程中自旋极化电荷的空间分布和MAE均发生变化。所有这些发现显著扩展了二维铁性材料家族，并为非易失性逻辑和存储器件的发展与实现开辟了道路。

<span class="hljs-section">## Computational details</span>
计算细节

All calculations were performed using the density functional theory (DFT), as implemented in the Vienna ab initio simulation package (VASP)46. The ion-electron interactions were described using the projector-augmented plane wave (PAW) approach47, and the electron exchange-correlation interactions were described within the generalized gradient approximation parameterized by Perdew, Burke, and Ernzerhof (PBE)48. The planewave basis was expanded to 500 eV, with a convergence threshold of 10−7 eV in energy and 10−3 eV/Å in force, respectively. The Brillouin zone was sampled with the Γ-centered Monkhorst-Pack k-point grid49, with uniform spacing of 2π<span class="hljs-emphasis">*0.02 Å−1. A vacuum space of more than 15 Å in z direction was added to avoid interactions between neighboring layers. The dynamic stability of all structures was assessed by their phonon dispersions, which were computed with the finite displacement method implemented in the Phonopy code50. The thermodynamic stability was evaluated by the formation energy, which is defined as Ef = (EAM2X4 − EA − 2 × EM − 4 × EX)/n, where EAM2X4 is the total energy of AM2X4 monolayers, EA, EM, and EX are the energies of the individual A, M, and X atoms in their most stable bulk phase, respectively, and n = 7 is the total number of atoms per AM2X4 formula. The FE energy transition barrier was evaluated using the climbing-image-nudged elastic band (CI-NEB) method51. The out-of-plane polarization was calculated considering the dipole correction52 and the in-plane polarization was estimated using the Berry phase method53. To account for the strong correlation effects involving localized d electrons, the effective Hubbard parameters Ueff = U − J were applied to all 3d and 4d transition metal elements, where U is the Coulomb repulsion, and J is the exchange parameter54–56. The Ueff values used in this work are summarized in Supplementary Table 1. The Curie or Néel temperature was evaluated using the Monte Carlo (MC) method57 with an 80 × 80 lattice. The FE switching temperatures were estimated by the ab initio molecular dynamics (AIMD) simulations within the NVT ensemble, which were performed on a 4 × 4 × 1 supercell for at least 10 ps with a time step of 1 fs58.
