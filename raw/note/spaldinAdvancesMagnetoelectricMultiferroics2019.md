> ## 一、引言
> 
> 本篇综述文章“Advances in magnetoelectric multiferroics”深入探讨了**多铁性材料**领域在基础科学、实验与理论方法以及应用方面的最新进展。**多铁性材料 (Multiferroics)**，顾名思义，是指在同一相中至少展现出两种**铁性序 (ferroic orderings)**的材料，例如**铁电性 (ferroelectricity)**、**铁磁性 (ferromagnetism)**、**铁弹性 (ferroelasticity)**或**铁环性 (ferrotoroidicity)**。其中，将**铁电性**和**磁性 (magnetism)**结合的**磁电材料 (magnetoelectric materials)**因其独特的**磁电耦合 (magnetoelectric coupling)**效应而备受关注。
> 
> 研究这类材料的核心驱动力在于，能够通过一个铁性序的共轭场来操控另一个铁性序，特别是利用**电场 (electric field)**来调控材料的**磁化方向 (magnetization orientation)**。相较于传统的磁场控制磁性，电场控制能耗极低，有望革新当前的微电子器件技术，例如实现超低功耗的逻辑和存储器。然而，在同一材料中同时实现强**铁电性**和强**磁性**，并确保两者之间存在显著的耦合，是一项极具挑战性的任务，需要材料化学工程的创新以及新型计算和表征工具的开发。
> 
> 本综述旨在为读者提供一个全面的视角，概述该领域的最新科学突破，包括新型材料的设计、表征与建模工具的进步，以及在应用方面的探索。文章特别强调了将这些科学发现转化为技术创新的关键开放问题，以期指导未来的研究工作，促进**多铁性材料**的实际应用。
> 
> ## 二、论文结构与逻辑概览
> 
> 本篇综述文章围绕**多铁性材料**的三大核心方面——基础科学、表征与建模、以及应用——展开，呈现出清晰的“总-分-总”逻辑结构。
> 
> -   **引言 (Introduction):** 首先，文章定义了**多铁性材料**，强调了**磁电多铁性材料**通过电场控制磁性的巨大潜力及其面临的挑战，并概述了本综述的重点。
>     
> -   **基础科学 (Fundamentals):** 这一部分是核心，深入探讨了实现**多铁性**的各种机制和新材料。
>     
>     -   **多铁性家谱 (Multiferroic family tree):** 介绍了通过不同**铁电**和**磁性**机制组合构建**多铁性材料**的分类框架。
>     -   **BiFeO3 (铋铁氧体):** 详细论述了这一最受关注的**多铁性材料**的独特性能和最新发现。
>     -   **畴壁功能性 (Domain wall functionality):** 阐述了**多铁性材料**中**铁电畴壁**所展现出的新奇物理现象和潜在功能。
> -   **表征与建模进展 (Advances in characterization and modelling):** 接着，文章转向了支持**多铁性材料**研究的工具进步。
>     
>     -   **实验探针 (Experimental probes):** 介绍了多种先进的实验表征技术，能够以高空间和时间分辨率探测材料的自旋、电荷和轨道自由度。
>     -   **理论处理 (Theoretical treatments):** 回顾了从**第一性原理密度泛函理论 (DFT)**到**第二性原理计算 (second-principles calculations)**，再到新兴的**磁电多极矩 (magnetoelectric multipole)**概念等理论建模方法。
> -   **应用 (Applications):** 这一部分聚焦于**多铁性材料**的实际技术应用。
>     
>     -   **电场控制磁性 (Electric-field control of magnetism):** 重点介绍了如何利用电场实现对磁性的调控，包括磁化方向和磁性状态的控制。
>     -   **射频/高频器件 (Radio- and high-frequency devices):** 探讨了**多铁性材料**在可调谐高频器件中的潜力。
>     -   **超低功耗逻辑-存储器件 (Ultralow power logic-memory devices):** 详细介绍了**磁电自旋轨道耦合 (MESO)**逻辑器件等创新架构。
> -   **挑战与机遇 (Challenges and opportunities):** 最后，文章总结了当前领域面临的科学和技术挑战，并展望了未来的研究方向和发展前景，强调了从基础研究向应用转化的重要性。
>

> ## 三、所有图表深度解析
> 
> ### Figure 1: The multiferroic family tree.
> 
> -   **描述:** 这张图以“家谱”的形式形象地展示了**多铁性材料**的各种形成机制和分类。它从四个核心的“根”机制（即两种**铁电性**机制：d0铁电性、孤对铁电性，以及两种**磁性**机制：f电子磁性、d电子磁性）出发，通过不同的组合和相互作用，形成了多样化的**多铁性材料**分支。
> -   **关键信息:**
>     -   **传统铁电性**包括d0构型（如BaTiO3中的Ti4+）和孤对电子活性（如PbTiO3中的Pb2+）。
>     -   **传统磁性**来自稀土离子的f电子（如Eu2+）或过渡金属的d电子（如Fe3+）。
>     -   “家谱”的分支包括**复合材料 (Composites)**（如BaTiO3/Co），以及多种**单相多铁性材料**，如“孤对铁电性，d电子磁性”分支（代表性材料**BiFeO3**），“d0铁电性，f电子磁性”分支（如(Eu,Ba)TiO3），和“新型铁电性，d电子磁性”分支（包含几何驱动、电荷序、磁序诱导以及规避d0限制等多种机制）。
> -   **单位/量纲:** 这是一个概念性的分类图，不涉及具体的物理单位或量纲。
> -   **解读:** 该图清晰地揭示了**多铁性材料**设计的基本原理和现有材料的来源，也同时指出了两个尚未充分探索的分支，即如何在f电子磁体中引入非传统的**铁电机制**，以及如何在传统**铁电体**中实现非常规磁性，为未来的材料发现提供了方向。
> 
> ### Figure 2: Multiferroic composite architectures.
> 
> -   **描述:** 该图展示了三种不同类型的**多铁性复合材料**架构，这些架构能够实现室温下的较大剩磁和极化，并具备足够强的耦合强度以应用于实际。
> -   **关键信息:**
>     -   **a) 经典水平复合材料 (Classic horizontal composites):** 示意性展示了通过层层生长实现的原子尺度超晶格，如**铁电**LuFeO3与**亚铁磁性 (ferrimagnetic)**LuFe2O4的组合。左侧的箭头指示了**极化 (P)**方向和**磁化 (M)**方向。

> ## 四、正文核心内容剖析
> 
> ### 研究背景与问题引入
> 
> **多铁性材料**因其独特的**磁电耦合**效应而成为材料科学的热点，特别是利用**电场**控制**磁性**的潜力。这种控制方式的能耗远低于传统的磁场控制，有望带来变革性的技术应用，如超低功耗存储器和逻辑器件。然而，实现理想的**多铁性材料**面临核心挑战：如何在同一材料中平衡**铁电性**（通常需要空的d轨道）与**磁性**（通常需要部分填充的d或f轨道）之间的化学“反指示”，并确保它们之间存在强耦合。
> 
> ### 多铁性材料的实现机制与分类
> 
> 文章将**多铁性材料**的实现机制归纳为“**多铁性家谱**”，主要分为**复合材料**和**单相材料**。
> 
> 1.  **复合材料 (Composites):**
>     
>     -   通过将非磁性**铁电体**（如BaTiO3）与非**铁电**磁体（如CoFe2O4）结合。
>     -   近期进展包括原子尺度层层生长的**超晶格 (superlattices)**（如LuFeO3/LuFe2O4）和垂直排列的**纳米复合材料 (nanocomposites)**。这些结构通过三维异质外延增强了耦合和矫顽场。
>     -   开放问题包括实现单向交换偏置、位错影响、以及合成更小尺寸纳米柱的挑战。
> 2.  **单相材料 (Single-phase materials):**
>     
>     -   **孤对铁电性，d电子磁性:**
>         -   最典型的代表是**BiFeO3**，其**铁电极化**源于Bi3+的6s2孤对电子，**反铁磁性**源于Fe的3d电子。
>         -   许多**BiXO3**（X为磁性过渡金属）和高压/薄膜稳定的**PbXO3**钙钛矿已被探索，但兼具高居里温度和可切换极化的材料仍然稀缺。
>         -   **氧氟化物 (oxyfluorides)**（如PbFeO2F）和B位有序双钙钛矿、固溶体等是值得关注的方向。
>     -   **d0铁电性，f电子磁性:**
>         -   磁性来自f电子，**铁电性**来自d0构型。
>         -   主要例子是BaTiO3中Ba2+被磁性f7 Eu2+取代的合金，以及应变下的纯EuTiO3。
>     -   **新型铁电性，d电子磁性:**
>         -   利用传统的d电子磁性，但通过非常规途径打破反演对称性来引入**铁电性**。
>         -   **几何驱动多铁性 (Geometrically driven multiferroics):** 小A位阳离子允许配位多面体旋转，这些旋转本身具**极性 (polar)**或引发次级**极性**畸变（如YMnO3）。近期“**混合非本征铁电体 (hybrid improper ferroelectrics)**”的发现（如层状钙钛矿）是重要进展，但其开关和畴结构复杂。
>         -   **电荷序 (Charge ordering):** 磁性材料中（如Fe3O4**磁铁矿 (magnetite)**）的电荷重新排布形成**极性**，但通常导致小带隙、高电导和差的**铁电滞后 (ferroelectric hysteresis)**。有机**电荷转移盐 (charge-transfer salt)**和**金属有机框架 (metal-organic frameworks)**是新方向。
>         -   **磁序诱导 (Magnetic ordering):** 非中心对称**磁序**（如自旋螺旋）打破反演对称性诱导**极化**。通常居里温度较低，但通过化学无序稳定磁螺旋（如YBaCuFeO5）可提高居里温度。
>         -   **规避d0限制:** 通过应变（如SrMnO3）或化学压力（如BaMnO3）使B位离子偏离中心，即使其d轨道部分填充也能实现**铁电性**。
> 
> ### BiFeO3的深度剖析
> 
> **BiFeO3**是目前研究最广泛的**多铁性材料**，拥有巨大的**铁电极化**（~90 μC cm–2）和高于室温的磁序（TN ≈ 640 K）。
> 
> -   **应变工程:** 在大应变下（压缩或拉伸），**BiFeO3**可稳定为超四方“T-like”结构或正交相，表现出巨大的自发极化和磁性。例如，在LaAlO3衬底上生长的**BiFeO3**可形成应变菱方相和T-like相的混合结构，其中菱方相组分具有增强的磁矩。
> -   **其他功能:** **BiFeO3**还展现出**光伏效应 (photovoltaic effect)**、**光催化 (photocatalysis)**、**光致伸缩 (photostriction)**、**电致变色 (electrochromism)**和**气体传感 (gas-sensing)**等多种新奇行为，这可能与其Fe-3d态和内部电场有关。
> -   **开放问题:** 仍需深入理解**BiFeO3**的表面性质、纳米颗粒的尺寸效应，以及混合相中增强磁性的微观机制。
> 
> ### 畴壁功能性
> 
> **铁电畴壁**在**多铁性材料**中展现出丰富的异质功能。
> 
> -   **导电性:** 最引人注目的是**BiFeO3**中**铁电畴壁**的电导性，这为开发可电写入、擦除和移动的纳米尺度金属传输通道提供了可能。在低温下观察到大**磁阻 (magnetoresistance)**和导电性增加，暗示了金属态。
> -   **其他行为:** **畴壁**还表现出强**忆阻行为**、自旋输运和光伏响应。
> -   **其他材料:** 在**非本征铁电体 (improper ferroelectrics)**如ErMnO3和BaTiO3中也发现了导电**畴壁**。**反铁磁性TbMnO3**的孪生**畴壁**处也观察到二维**铁磁相 (ferromagnetic phase)**。
> -   **未来研究:** 需要通过详细计算和精确表征来阐明这些行为的起源，特别是金属-绝缘体转变的极限和**忆阻行为**的潜力。
> 
> ### 表征和建模工具的进步
> 
> 1.  **实验探针 (Experimental probes):**
>     
>     -   **扫描探针技术 (Scanning probe techniques):** 如压电力显微镜 (PFM)、导电原子力显微镜 (c-AFM)、磁力显微镜 (MFM)，结合了空间分辨率和功能响应，近期发展包括使用含**金刚石氮空位 (diamond nitrogen-vacancy, NV)**中心的尖端探测BiFeO3的磁螺旋矩。
>     -   **时间分辨X射线研究 (Time-resolved X-ray studies):** 超快同步辐射X射线源和飞秒激光探针可揭示自旋和电荷自由度耦合的动力学，例如利用**BiFeO3**薄膜产生的**太赫兹 (THz) 辐射**作为记忆读出机制。
>     -   **电子探针 (Electron probes):** **电子能量损失谱 (EELS)**和像差校正显微镜能提供亚纳米空间分辨率的化学和结构信息，特别是对**钙钛矿**中氧八面体旋转和倾斜的识别。
>     -   **光学二次谐波产生 (Optical second-harmonic generation, SHG):** 用于探测**铁电性**，近期结合原位**脉冲激光沉积 (PLD)**氧化物生长，有助于理解**铁电序**的形成。
> 2.  **理论处理 (Theoretical treatments):**
>     
>     -   **第一性原理密度泛函理论 (DFT):** 仍是理解和预测**绝缘磁有序材料**性质的黄金标准。
>     -   **第二性原理计算 (Second-principles calculations):** 通过拟合DFT数据构建有效模型，适用于更大系统（如异质结构、**畴壁**、缺陷）和更长时间尺度（分子动力学）。已成功应用于描述**BiFeO3**的晶体和磁性结构。
>     -   **Landau-Ginzburg热力学势 (Landau-Ginzburg thermodynamic potential):** 用于重现**BiFeO3**的体行为，并预测薄膜异质结构和纳米结构中的性质。
>     -   **多尺度方法 (Multiscale approaches):** 将电子和晶格自由度同等对待，结合改进的有效势生成工具，有望提高系统尺寸和精度。
>     -   **动力学模拟:** 使**铁电**开关动力学及其对磁序影响的计算成为可能，可计算**太赫兹**区域的**磁电响应 (magnetoelectric responses)**。
>     -   **新理论概念:** 鼓励发展新的理论概念，如**磁电多极矩**，作为打破空间反演和时间反演对称性相变的序参数。
> 
> ### 应用探索
> 
> 1.  **电场控制磁性 (Electric-field control of magnetism):**
>     
>     -   驱动力是电场控制磁性所需的能耗远低于磁场。例如，**MESO逻辑器件**的能耗预计可达~1 aJ (阿焦)，远低于现有自旋转移矩方法的~10 fJ (飞焦)。
>     -   **磁化方向的电场控制:** 重大突破是在CoFe**铁磁体**与**BiFeO3**交换耦合时，实现了CoFe磁化180°的电场旋转。在**La,Sr)MnO3/BiFeO3 (LSMO/BFO)**界面，实现了交换偏置的电场控制（低温下）。**Cr2O3**中**反铁磁畴**取向的电场控制也影响了与**铁磁覆盖层 (ferromagnetic overlayer)**的交换偏置。
>     -   **磁性状态的电场控制:** 通过电场调控磁性状态，例如FeRh异质结构中的**铁磁-反铁磁转变 (ferromagnetic–antiferromagnetic transition)**，伴随电阻的显著变化。Mn–Pt金属间化合物和半掺杂钙钛矿锰氧化物也是有前景的体系。
> 2.  **射频和高频器件 (Radio- and high-frequency devices):**
>     
>     -   利用机械耦合的**铁电-铁磁复合材料**，在可调射频/微波信号处理、磁场传感器、磁电随机存取存储器（MERAM）和电压可调磁阻等领域取得进展。
>     -   **多铁性材料**与**铁磁体**接触可实现**铁磁共振 (ferromagnetic resonance)**的电压调谐，为可调谐射频或微波器件（如滤波器、移相器、天线）提供了机会。
> 3.  **超低功耗逻辑-存储器件 (Ultralow power logic-memory devices):**
>     
>     -   Intel提出的一种结合**逆Rashba-Edelstein效应 (IREE)**和**多铁性材料**电场控制磁性的**MESO逻辑器件**架构。
>     -   **IREE**将自旋转换为电荷（或电压），**多铁性材料**则执行相反的电荷到自旋的转换。
>     -   **技术挑战:**
>         -   将**多铁性材料**的开关电压从~5 V降至~100 mV。这需要通过减小薄膜厚度、降低**极化强度**、探索近相边界材料或无强八面体旋转的材料来降低矫顽场。
>         -   将**IREE**的电压输出从数百μV提高两到三个数量级。
>     -   实现这些突破有望带来每比特1 aJ的变革性能耗效率。
> 
> ## 五、结论与贡献
> 
> ### 核心发现
> 
> 本综述全面揭示了**多铁性材料**领域在过去十年间的显著进步。核心发现包括：
> 
> 1.  **多样化的多铁性机制:** 从**复合材料**到多种**单相材料**（基于孤对电子、d0构型、f电子磁性，以及几何驱动、电荷序、磁序诱导等新型铁电机制），**多铁性**的实现途径日益丰富。
> 2.  **BiFeO3作为典范的持续价值:** **BiFeO3**依然是研究最广泛且具有巨大潜力**多铁性材料**，其丰富的相图、应变调控特性以及在纳米结构中展现的光伏、光催化等多功能性持续吸引着研究。

> ## 八、用户问题预测与解答
> 
> 1.  **Q1: 什么是多铁性材料？它与传统材料有何不同，为何备受关注？**
>     
>     -   **A1:** **多铁性材料 (Multiferroics)**是指在同一相中同时展现出两种或多种**铁性序 (ferroic orderings)**的材料，例如**铁电性 (ferroelectricity)**、**铁磁性 (ferromagnetism)**、**铁弹性 (ferroelasticity)**或**铁环性 (ferrotoroidicity)**。它与传统材料的最大不同在于其内在的多种铁性序可以相互耦合，即可以通过一个外场（如**电场**）来控制另一个铁性序（如**磁性**），反之亦然。这种**磁电耦合 (magnetoelectric coupling)**使其备受关注，因为它可以为开发全新的、能耗更低的器件（如用**电场**控制磁存储）提供基础，有望颠覆传统电子学范式。
> 2.  **Q2: 为什么同时实现铁电性和磁性很困难？有哪些主要的策略来克服这一困难？**
>     
>     -   **A2:** 同时实现**铁电性**和**磁性**很困难，主要是因为这两种性质通常在化学上存在“反指示 (contraindication)”。**铁电性**往往需要空的d轨道（如Ti4+的d0构型）以允许离子偏离中心形成极化；而**磁性**则通常需要部分填充的d或f轨道（如Fe3+的3d电子或稀土离子的f电子）以产生磁矩。
>     -   克服这一困难的主要策略包括：
>         -   **复合材料 (Composites):** 将非磁性**铁电体**与非**铁电**磁体结合，通过界面效应实现耦合，如BaTiO3/CoFe2O4。
>         -   **单相材料中的组合机制:** 利用不同的化学组分，一个提供**铁电性**，另一个提供**磁性**，例如**BiFeO3**利用Bi3+的孤对电子提供**铁电性**，Fe3+的3d电子提供**磁性**。
>         -   **新型铁电机制:** 通过非传统方式打破反演对称性诱导**铁电性**，如**几何驱动 (geometrically driven)**（晶格旋转）、**电荷序 (charge ordering)**（电子重新排布形成极性）、**磁序诱导 (magnetically induced)**（非中心对称磁序，如自旋螺旋）等。
>         -   **规避d0限制:** 通过应变或化学压力等方式，使得具有部分填充d轨道的离子也能偏离中心形成**铁电性**。
> 3.  **Q3: BiFeO3为何被称为“多铁性材料中的明星”，其主要优点和局限性是什么？**
>     
>     -   **A3:** **BiFeO3 (铋铁氧体)**被称为“**多铁性材料**中的明星”，因为它是一种少数在室温以上同时表现出强**铁电性**和**反铁磁性 (antiferromagnetism)**的单相**多铁性材料**。其主要优点包括：
>         -   **大自发极化:** 具有高达约90 μC cm–2 (微库伦每平方厘米) 的巨大**铁电极化强度**。
>         -   **高磁序温度:** 磁性居里温度（Néel温度，TN ≈ 640 K）和铁电居里温度（TC ≈ 1100 K）均远高于室温。
>         -   **丰富的功能性:** 在应变下具有结构和磁性的灵活性，可实现不同的相变。同时，其**畴壁 (domain walls)**表现出导电性、**忆阻性 (memristive behaviour)**等新奇功能。
>     -   然而，**BiFeO3**也存在一些局限性：
>         -   **反铁磁性:** 其固有的反铁磁性限制了在一些需要**铁磁性 (ferromagnetism)**响应的器件中的直接应用。
>         -   **漏电流问题:** 许多应用受到高漏电流的困扰，尤其是在薄膜和纳米结构中。
>         -   **高矫顽场:** **铁电极化**翻转所需电场（矫顽场）较高，不利于低电压器件集成。
>         -   **复杂自旋结构:** 具有G型反铁磁螺旋自旋结构，降低了宏观磁性响应。
> 4.  **Q4: 畴壁在多铁性材料中有何特殊作用？它们如何被利用于未来技术？**
>     
>     -   **A4:** 在**多铁性材料**中，**畴壁 (domain walls)**不仅仅是分隔不同**铁电**或磁性区域的边界，它们本身也可以展现出独特的物理性质和功能，因此被视为“器件中的界面 (interface is the device)”。
>     -   特殊作用：
>         -   **导电性:** 某些**铁电畴壁**（如BiFeO3中）可以局部导电，而在畴区则是绝缘的，这可以实现纳米尺度的导电通道。
>         -   **忆阻行为:** **畴壁**的导电状态可以通过**电场**进行调控和记忆，表现出**忆阻器 (memristor)**的特性。
>         -   **自旋输运与光伏效应:** **畴壁**区域还可能支持特定的自旋输运现象和**光伏响应 (photovoltaic response)**。
>     -   未来应用：
>         -   **纳米电子器件:** 利用**畴壁**的导电性、**忆阻性**，可以开发超高密度、低功耗的**信息存储 (memory)**和**逻辑器件 (logic devices)**。
>         -   **可重构电路:** 通过**电场**写入、擦除和移动**畴壁**，可以实现动态可重构的纳米电路。
>         -   **传感器:** **畴壁**对外界刺激（如应变、温度、光照）的敏感性，使其在新型传感器开发中具有潜力。
> 5.  **Q5: 文章中提及的“超低功耗逻辑-存储器件”MESO逻辑是如何工作的？实现其技术目标面临哪些关键挑战？**
>     
>     -   **A5:** **MESO (Magnetoelectric Spin–Orbit coupled)**逻辑器件是一种结合了**逆Rashba-Edelstein效应 (IREE)**和**多铁性材料**电场控制磁性的新型超低功耗逻辑-存储架构。
>     -   **工作原理:**
>         -   **信息写入/存储:** 利用**多铁性材料**的**磁电耦合**特性，通过施加一个低电压**电场**来翻转**铁电极化 (ferroelectric polarization)**，进而控制一个与其耦合的磁性层的磁化方向，实现信息存储。
>         -   **信息读取:** 利用**IREE**将磁性层的自旋信息转换为电荷电流（或电压）输出，从而读取存储的逻辑状态。
>     -   **关键挑战:**
>         -   **降低开关电压:** 目前**多铁性材料**所需的**铁电开关电压**通常较高（~5 V），需要大幅降低至100 mV以下，这需要材料设计（如减小薄膜厚度、降低**极化强度**、寻找低矫顽场材料）。
>         -   **提高IREE输出:** **逆Rashba-Edelstein效应**产生的电压输出目前较低（数百μV），需要提高两到三个数量级以满足器件需求。
>         -   **室温下稳健耦合:** 需要发现新的、室温下具有强大且稳定的**磁电耦合**的**多铁性材料**。
>         -   **集成与规模化:** 与现有硅基工艺的兼容性、薄膜的精确生长、以及大规模制造能力是实现商业化的重要障碍。
>         -   **材料稳定性:** 10纳米**长度尺度**上**铁电**和磁序参数的长期**热稳定性**。

> Multiferroics exhibit more than one primary ferroic ordering—ferromagnetism, ferroelectricity, ferroelasticity or ferrotoroidicity—in the same phase1. The terminology is usually extended to include non-primary orderings such as antiferromagnetism, as well as composites of individual ferroics, and is most often used today to refer specifically to magnetoelectric2 materials combining ferroelectric and magnetic behaviours. Importantly, the combination of ferroic orders in multiferroics can lead to coupling between them, so that one ferroic property can be manipulated with the conjugate field of the other, with particular excitement generated by the prospect of switching the orientation of magnetization using an electric field3,4. The combining and control of these simultaneous ferroic orderings is challenging, requiring engineering of new material chemistries, and development of new tools to compute and characterize the novel properties associated with the coupled behaviours. In turn, it offers routes to entirely new device architectures. This Review focuses on the recent developments in these three aspects: basic science, experimental and theoretical methods, and applications3. In the fundamentals section, we present a multiferroic ‘family tree’ to illustrate the various mechanistic approaches to achieving multiferroicity and their evolution over recent years, before reviewing progress on the leading multiferroic material, BiFeO3, as well as the recently discovered phenomena associated with multiferroic domain walls. We follow with a discussion of leading new spectroscopy and microscopy techniques, including time-resolved methods, as well as new theoretical tools with a particular focus on the emerging field of second-principles calculations. We review technological applications with a focus on electric-field control of magnetism before concluding with a discussion about future challenges and opportunities. We emphasize that the field of multiferroics is now vast, and this Review by its nature reflects our personal perspectives; we direct the reader to recent comprehensive reviews with different emphases5–9. 多铁性材料在同一相中表现出不止一种初级铁性序——铁磁性、铁电性、铁弹性或铁涡旋性1。该术语通常扩展至包括非初级序如反铁磁性，以及单铁性材料的复合物，且如今最常用于特指结合铁电和磁行为的磁电材料2。重要的是，多铁性材料中铁性序的组合可导致它们之间的耦合，从而一种铁性性质可通过另一种的共轭场进行操控，其中通过电场切换磁化方向的前景引发了特别的兴奋3,4。同时组合和控制这些铁性序具有挑战性，需要设计新的材料化学，并开发新的工具来计算和表征与耦合行为相关的新颖性质。反过来，这为全新的器件架构提供了途径。本综述聚焦于这三个方面的最新进展：基础科学、实验和理论方法以及应用3。在基础部分，我们展示了一棵多铁性“家族树”，以说明实现多铁性的各种机制途径及其近年来的演变，然后回顾了领先的多铁性材料BiFeO3的进展，以及与多铁性畴壁相关的最新发现现象。接着我们讨论了领先的新型光谱学和显微镜技术，包括时间分辨方法，以及新的理论工具，特别关注新兴的第二原理计算领域。我们回顾了以电场控制磁性为重点的技术应用，最后讨论了未来的挑战和机遇。我们强调，多铁性领域现已非常广阔，本综述自然反映了我们的个人观点；我们引导读者参考近期其他侧重点不同的综合综述5–9。 **multiferroics**: 多铁性材料，在同一相中表现出两种或以上初级铁性序（铁磁性、铁电性、铁弹性或铁涡旋性）的材料。 **ferroelectricity**: 铁电性，材料具有自发极化且极化方向可随外加电场反转的性质。 **antiferromagnetism**: 反铁磁性，相邻磁矩反平行排列，宏观上不显示净磁矩的磁性。 **magnetoelectric**: 磁电效应，磁场诱导电极化或电场诱导磁化的现象。

> # Fundamentals
> 
> 基础
> 
> We begin with an overview of the most promising new materials and mechanisms that have been identified for creating new multiferroics, with a seperate subsection on the most widely studied multiferroic, BiFeO3. Perhaps the most exciting recent scientific discoveries in the field have been in the area of multiferroic domain walls; we summarize these at the end of this section. 我们首先概述已确定用于创造新型多铁性材料的最有前景的新材料和新机制，并单独用一小节介绍研究最广泛的多铁性材料BiFeO3。该领域近期最激动人心的科学发现或许在于多铁性畴壁方面；我们在本节末尾总结这些发现。

> ## New materials
> 
> 新型材料
> 
> There are now many established routes to circumventing the classic chemical ‘contraindication’ between ferroelectricity (favoured by transition metals with empty d orbitals) and magnetism (favoured by partially filled d orbitals)10. We summarize these, with a focus on recent developments, in the discussion below and in the multiferroic family tree in Fig. 1. We distinguish two conventional mechanisms for ferroelectricity: ‘d0-ness’ typified by the Ti4+ ion in BaTiO3, and lone pair stereochemical activity—for example, on the Pb2+ ion in PbTiO3 or the Ge2+ ion in GeTe. Likewise, we note two established routes to magnetism: partially filled localized (that is, f-electron) states on rare earth ions, or partially occupied d levels in transition metals. These form the four roots of the multiferroic family tree, which combine and interweave in various ways to make up the branches. The most obvious strategies for engineering new multiferroics involve combining one magnetic and one ferroelectric mechanism from these roots to make new branches in the tree. We discuss these options in the following. 目前已有许多既定途径可以规避铁电性（倾向于具有空d轨道的过渡金属）与磁性（倾向于部分填充d轨道）之间经典的化学“禁忌”10。我们在下文的讨论以及图1的多铁性家族树中总结这些途径，并着重介绍近期进展。我们区分两种传统的铁电性机制：以BaTiO3中Ti4+离子为代表的“d0特性”，以及孤对电子立体化学活性——例如PbTiO3中的Pb2+离子或GeTe中的Ge2+离子。同样，我们注意到两种既定的磁性途径：稀土离子上部分填充的局域化（即f电子）态，或过渡金属中部分占据的d能级。它们构成了多铁性家族树的四条根，这些根以各种方式组合交织形成树枝。设计新型多铁性材料最直接的策略涉及从这些根中组合一个磁性机制和一个铁电机制，从而在树上形成新的树枝。我们将在下文讨论这些选项。 **d0-ness**: d0特性，指过渡金属离子具有空的d轨道，有利于铁电性。 **lone pair**: 孤对电子，不参与成键的价电子对，其立体化学活性可驱动铁电性。

> ### Composites branch
> 
> 复合材料分支
> 
> In composite multiferroics11, a non-magnetic ferroelectric, such as BaTiO3, is combined with a non-ferroelectric magnet, such as CoFe2O4. The recently demonstrated layer-by-layer growth of atomic-scale multiferroic superlattices, such as of ferroelectric and antiferromagnetic LuFeO3, with ferrimagnetic but nonpolar LuFe2O4 (ref. 12; Fig. 2a), offers tremendous opportunity since the combination of available crystal structures and chemistries is very large. In addition, the discovery of electric-field controllable chiral polarization vortices in engineered superlattices of SrTiO3/ PbTiO3 (ref. 13) raises the question of whether such vortices can be induced in a multiferroic material, with the magnetization coupled to the polar vortices. Vertically aligned nanocomposites14 (Fig. 2b) are also attractive since their three-dimensional heteroepitaxy provides enhanced coupling and coercive field, and several new chemistries have been recently synthesized. Open questions include whether a unidirectional (rather than uniaxial) exchange bias could be engineered, the influence of dislocations and antiphase boundaries, which may be sites of magnetic disorder, and how to synthesize smaller (<20 nm lateral dimension) nanopillars. Finally, we mention progress in nanopatterned hybrid composites of, for example, conventional ferromagnetic metals with ferroelectric polymers15,16 in which magnetic-field switching of the electric polarization has been demonstrated at room temperature. > ### Lone pair ferroelectricity, d-electron magnetism branch
> 
> 孤对电子铁电性、d电子磁性分支
> 
> In this case the material is single-phase, containing two cations, one of which provides the ferroelectricity and the other the magnetism. In the case of the prototypical multiferroic BiFeO3, the large polarization is driven by the stereochemical activity of the Bi3+ 6s2 ‘lone pair’ of electrons10, and the robust antiferromagnetism comes from the 3d electrons of Fe. Motivated by the favourable properties of BiFeO3, many other BiXO3 (where X is a magnetic transition metal ion) have been synthesized and characterized17, although most have been found to be antipolar rather than ferroelectric. BiCoO3 is polar, with a high Néel temperature TN = 470 K, but with such a large c/a ratio (where c and a are the tetragonal lattice parameters) that its polarization has not yet been switched; it certainly merits further study. Likewise, many PbXO3 perovskites (all of which require a high-pressure or thin-film stabilization) have been explored and are discussed in an excellent review18. To date, the combination of high ordering temperature and switchable polarization is elusive, although PbMnO3 and PbFeO3 are not yet well characterized and could be candidates. Less well studied are the oxyfluorides19–21, such as PbFeO2F, for which there are reports of polar structures in bulk samples; thin-film syntheses and a comprehensive study of magnetoelectricity would be fruitful. Engineering ferromagnetic ordering via B-site-ordered double perovskites22, and the use of solid solutions to frustrate octahedral rotations and promote ferroelectricity23, together with non-perovskite-based lone pair active systems, are promising directions for further study. 在此情况下，材料为单相，包含两种阳离子，一种提供铁电性，另一种提供磁性。以原型多铁性材料BiFeO3为例，其大的极化由Bi3+ 6s2“孤对电子”的立体化学活性驱动10，而强劲的反铁磁性则来自Fe的3d电子。受BiFeO3优异性能的激励，许多其他BiXO3（X为磁性过渡金属离子）已被合成和表征17，但大多数被发现是反极性而非铁电性。BiCoO3是极性的，具有高奈尔温度TN = 470 K，但其c/a比（c和a为四方晶格参数）如此之大，以至于其极化尚未被切换；它无疑值得进一步研究。同样，许多PbXO3钙钛矿（均需高压或薄膜稳定化）已被探索，并在一篇优秀综述中进行了讨论18。迄今为止，高有序化温度与可切换极化的结合仍难以实现，尽管PbMnO3和PbFeO3尚未被充分表征，可能是候选材料。研究较少的是氟氧化物19–21，如PbFeO2F，已有报道称其在块体样品中具有极性结构；薄膜合成以及磁电性的全面研究将会富有成效。通过B位有序双钙钛矿设计铁磁有序22，利用固溶体抑制八面体旋转以促进铁电性23，以及非钙钛矿基孤对电子活性体系，都是进一步研究的有前景方向。 **Néel temperature**: 奈尔温度，反铁磁性材料转变为顺磁性的临界温度。

> ### d0 ferroelectricity, f-electron magnetism branch
> 
> d0铁电性、f电子磁性分支
> 
> Again, single-component, two-cation materials, but in this case with the magnetism coming from f electrons and the ferroelectricity from d0-ness. The leading example is the substitution of magnetic f7 Eu2+ for Ba2+ in the protoypical ferroelectric BaTiO3. Both (Eu,Ba)TiO3 alloys24 and pure EuTiO3 under strain25 have been shown to be magnetic and ferroelectric. In an interesting twist, f-electron magnetism multiferroicity has also been proposed for strained EuO (ref. 26) and clearly merits further exploration. 同样为单组分、双阳离子材料，但在此情况下磁性来自f电子，铁电性来自d0特性。主要例子是在原型铁电体BaTiO3中用磁性f7 Eu2+替代Ba2+。(Eu,Ba)TiO3合金24和应变下的纯EuTiO3 25均已被证明具有磁性和铁电性。有趣的是，f电子磁性多铁性也被提出用于应变EuO（文献26），显然值得进一步探索。

> ### Novel ferroelectricity, d-electron magnetism branch
> 
> 新型铁电性、d电子磁性分支
> 
> In addition to these macroscopic, nanoscale or atomic combinatorial approaches, a number of alternative multiferroics are now established, all of which rely on conventional d-electron magnetism, but introduce the ferroelectricity through an unconventional route to lifting the inversion centre. These form the right-hand side of the family tree. 除了这些宏观、纳米或原子组合方法外，现已确立了一系列替代性多铁性材料，它们均依赖于常规的d电子磁性，但通过非常规途径打破反演中心引入铁电性。这些构成了家族树的右侧分支。
> 
> #### Geometrically driven multiferroics
> 
> 几何驱动多铁性材料
> 
> In geometrically driven ferroelectrics, a small A-site cation permits rotations of its surrounding coordination polyhedra, which are either polar in their own right (for example, in BaNiF4 (ref. 27)) or couple to a secondary polar distortion (as in YMnO3 (ref. 28,29)). This offers the advantage that the polarization is strongly coupled to any canted magnetism that the rotations also induce. In addition, the mechanism yields polarizations that are both robust and small in magnitude, a requirement for some potential applications. A number of new materials and modified mechanisms have been identified during the past decade. > #### Charge ordering
> 
> 电荷有序化
> 
> While the original report of ferroelectricity due to charge ordering in LuFe2O4 (ref. 35) is now questioned36, a charming development in charge-ordered multiferroics is the establishment of a polar charge-ordered state below ~100 K in the first technologically relevant magnet, ferrimagnetic magnetite37, Fe3O4. Magnetite is unlikely to find application for its multiferroic properties, however, since the charge-ordering mechanism leads to a small bandgap, high conductivity and poor ferroelectric hysteresis38. An intriguing recent finding is the observation of chargeorder driven ferroelectricity in an organic charge-transfer salt that in turn causes magnetic ordering by removing the spin frustration39. A recent review40 describes the origins of ferroelectricity, magnetism and magnetoelectric coupling within several such newly discovered organic charge-transfer complexes41 exhibiting spin-induced electric polarization or ferroelectricity-induced spin alignment. The coexistence of robust ferroelectric polarization and magnetic order at room temperature in organic charge-transfer complexes or in metal–organic frameworks42 will be critical for the development of all-organic multiferroics and should be a focus of future research. 尽管最初关于LuFe2O4中电荷有序化导致铁电性的报道35现在受到质疑36，但电荷有序多铁性材料中一个迷人的进展是，在第一种技术相关的磁体——亚铁磁磁铁矿Fe3O4中，在~100 K以下确立了极性电荷有序态37。然而，磁铁矿不太可能因其多铁性而获得应用，因为电荷有序化机制导致带隙小、电导率高、铁电回滞差38。一项引人入胜的近期发现是，在一种有机电荷转移盐中观察到电荷有序驱动的铁电性，该效应进而通过消除自旋阻挫导致磁有序39。一篇近期综述40描述了若干此类新发现的有机电荷转移复合物41中铁电性、磁性和磁电耦合的起源，这些复合物表现出自旋诱导的电极化或铁电性诱导的自旋排列。在有机电荷转移复合物或金属有机框架42中，室温下稳健铁电极化与磁有序的共存对于全有机多铁性材料的发展至关重要，应成为未来研究的重点。 **charge ordering**: 电荷有序化，一种电子局域化现象，其中不同价态的离子在晶格中规则排列，可导致铁电性。

> #### Magnetic ordering
> 
> 磁有序化
> 
> In magnetically induced ferroelectrics, a noncentrosymmetric magnetic ordering, such as a spin spiral, breaks the inversion symmetry and induces a spontaneous polarization. Since magnetic spirals are often stabilized by competition between weak nearest- and next-nearest neighbour magnetic exchange interactions, however, the onset temperatures for multiferroism via this mechanism are, with some exceptions43, usually low. An important recent development is the identification of a new mechanism for stabilizing a magnetic spiral, based on chemical disorder, in which the multiferroic ordering temperature can be much higher. Such defect-mediated, magnetically induced multiferroism is now established in the layered perovskite-related material YBaCuFeO5 close to room temperature44, and the richness of this crystal chemistry augurs well for the development of above-room-temperature multiferroics within this mechanism. 在磁致铁电体中，非中心对称的磁有序（如自旋螺旋）打破反演对称性，诱导自发极化。然而，由于自旋螺旋通常由弱近邻和次近邻磁交换作用之间的竞争稳定，通过此机制产生多铁性的起始温度通常较低，只有少数例外43。一项重要的近期进展是，识别出一种基于化学无序稳定磁螺旋的新机制，在其中的多铁性有序化温度可以高得多。这种缺陷介导的磁致多铁性现已在层状钙钛矿相关材料YBaCuFeO5中接近室温建立44，这种晶体化学的丰富性为在此机制内开发室温以上多铁性材料预示了良好前景。 **spin spiral**: 自旋螺旋，一种具有空间旋转调制的自旋排列，可打破反演对称性并诱导铁电性。

> #### Circumventing the d0-ness requirement for ferroelectricity
> 
> 规避铁电性对d0特性的要求
> 
> Finally, we mention a number of occurrences in which B-site ions with partially filled d states have been coerced into off-centring, circumventing the restriction usually imposed by the second-order Jahn–Teller effect. Two notable examples are by using strain, which has been shown to make perovskite-structure SrMnO3 become ferroelectric45, and (negative) chemical pressure, in the case of perovskitestructure BaMnO3 (refs. 46,47). In both examples, the instability of the high-symmetry paraelectric structure towards the ferroelectric phase coexists with a proximity to a competing crystallographic phase (the 2H, 4H or 6H phases), leading to strong sensitivity to small structural perturbations. 最后，我们提及若干实例，其中具有部分填充d态的B位离子被迫偏离中心，规避了通常由二阶Jahn-Teller效应施加的限制。两个值得注意的例子是：利用应变，已证明可使钙钛矿结构SrMnO3变为铁电性45；以及利用（负）化学压力，在钙钛矿结构BaMnO3中实现（文献46,47）。在这两个例子中，高对称顺电结构向铁电相的不稳定性与接近竞争晶相（2H、4H或6H相）共存，导致对微小结构扰动的强烈敏感性。
> 
> In spite of these many developments, there is no doubt that for multiferroics to fulfil their technological potential, a more diverse palette of new materials with room-temperature robust and coupled ferromagnetism and ferroelectricity is still urgently needed. A quick glance at the ‘multiferroics family tree’ makes it clear that two branches are largely unexplored. First, there are no materials, to our knowledge, that incorporate unusual ferroelectric mechanisms into f-electron magnets. Second, it could be intriguing to try to replicate some of the recent reports of unconventional (that is, not based on ordered d- or f-electron magnetic moments) magnetism—for example, in diluted magnetic semiconductors, topological materials, and even SrTiO3—in conventional ferroelectrics. 尽管取得了这些进展，但毫无疑问，要充分发挥多铁性材料的技术潜力，仍迫切需要更多样化的、在室温下具有稳健且耦合的铁磁性和铁电性的新型材料。快速浏览“多铁性家族树”即可清楚看到，有两个分支在很大程度上未被探索。第一，据我们所知，还没有材料将非常规铁电机制引入f电子磁体。第二，尝试在常规铁电体中重现近期一些非常规磁性（即不基于有序d或f电子磁矩的磁性）的报道——例如在稀磁半导体、拓扑材料甚至SrTiO3中——可能会非常有趣。

> ## Bismuth ferrite
> 
> 铁酸铋
> 
> Of the known multiferroics, bismuth ferrite, BiFeO3, remains arguably the most important. It is certainly the most widely studied, with around 6,000 papers published over the past ten years. The establishment of its large (~90 μC cm–2) ferroelectric polarization, combined with magnetic ordering persisting well above room temperature48, spawned an intense research effort that continues to unveil fascinating new physics and potential new applications49. We summarize the most compelling recent discoveries here. 在已知的多铁性材料中，铁酸铋BiFeO3无疑仍是最重要的。它当然也是研究最广泛的，过去十年间发表了约6,000篇论文。其大铁电极化（~90 μC cm–2）的建立，加上远高于室温的磁有序48，催生了密集的研究努力，持续揭示出迷人的新物理和潜在新应用49。我们在此总结最引人注目的近期发现。
> 
> Under large (~5%) compressive strain, a super-tetragonal ‘T-like’ structure, with an enhanced c/a ratio and an almost square pyramidal Fe–O coordination, has been shown to be the stable phase50. Both ab initio theory and experiments point to a huge spontaneous polarization of ~150 μC cm–2, oriented along or close to the [001] axis. Intriguingly, when BiFeO3 is grown on LaAlO3, a mixed phase ensemble of the strained rhombohedral ‘R-like’ and the T-like structures is stabilized (Fig. 2c). The R-like component has an enhanced magnetic moment that is not yet understood51; a microscopic understanding of its magnetism would be valuable in further optimizing the magnetic properties of BiFeO3. Under biaxial tensile strain, an orthorhombic phase, with ferroelectric polarization in the in-plane [110] direction, has been stabilized by growing BiFeO3 on a NdScO3 (110) orthorhombic substrate52. This structural and magnetic flexibility of strained BiFeO3 reflects the large number of competing low-energy polymorphs53,54 and points to further exploration of the rich bulk crystal chemical phase space in the Bi–Fe–O system55 (Fig. 3). It is likely that several other phases, with different ratios of the parent Bi2O3 and Fe2O3 oxides, are ‘hidden’ within this phase diagram, and could be accessed, for example, by exploiting as yet unidentified stacking sequences of Fe–O and BiO layers35. With ongoing improvements in layer-by-layer synthesis techniques, we anticipate the identification of additional phases within this rich phase diagram over the next few years. 在大约5%的压缩应变下，一种具有增强的c/a比和几乎四方锥Fe–O配位的超四方“类T”结构已被证明是稳定相50。从头计算理论和实验均指出，其具有约150 μC cm–2的巨大自发极化，沿或接近[001]轴取向。有趣的是，当BiFeO3生长在LaAlO3上时，应变菱方“类R”结构与类T结构的混合相系综被稳定化（图2c）。类R组分具有增强的磁矩，其原因尚不清楚51；对其磁性的微观理解对于进一步优化BiFeO3的磁性能将很有价值。在双轴拉伸应变下，通过将BiFeO3生长在NdScO3 (110)正交衬底上，已稳定了一种具有面内[110]方向铁电极化的正交相52。应变BiFeO3的这种结构和磁性灵活性反映了大量竞争的低能多晶型53,54，并指向对Bi–Fe–O体系丰富的块体晶体化学相空间55（图3）的进一步探索。很可能还有几种其他相，具有不同比例的Bi2O3和Fe2O3母体氧化物，“隐藏”在此相图内，并可通过例如利用尚未识别的Fe–O和BiO层堆垛序列35来获取。随着逐层合成技术的持续改进，我们预计在未来几年内将在此丰富相图中识别出更多相。
> 
> In addition to its structural and magnetoelectric richness, we mention a number of other intriguing and unexpected behaviours, which are both potentially useful and not yet well understood, that have been observed in nanostructured and nanoparticulate BiFeO3. These include a photovoltaic effect56, photocatalysis57, photostriction58, electrochromism59 and gas-sensing behaviour60. While not directly exploiting the multiferroic properties of BiFeO3, it is likely that the presence of the magnetic Fe-3d states in the region of the bandgap, which change both the magnitude of the bandgap and the symmetry of the band edges, combined with the internal electric fields from the ferroelectric polarization, are relevant. Research on these aspects of BiFeO3 will undoubtedly expand rapidly over the coming years. Particularly important will be an improved understanding of the properties of BiFeO3 surfaces, where a surface skin layer with different structure and properties from the bulk has been reported61,62, as well as further studies of the effect of size on nanoparticle properties63. 除了其结构和磁电丰富性，我们还提及在纳米结构和纳米颗粒BiFeO3中观察到的若干其他有趣且意想不到的行为，它们既具有潜在用途，又尚未被充分理解。这些包括光伏效应56、光催化57、光致伸缩58、电致变色59和气敏行为60。虽然并非直接利用BiFeO3的多铁性，但很可能与带隙区域中磁性Fe-3d态的存在（它们改变了带隙大小和带边对称性）以及来自铁电极化的内建电场有关。关于BiFeO3这些方面的研究在未来几年无疑将迅速扩展。特别重要的将是加深对BiFeO3表面性质的理解，已有报道称存在与块体结构和性质不同的表面表层61,62，以及进一步研究尺寸对纳米颗粒性质的影响63。

> ## Domain wall functionality in multiferroics
> 
> 多铁性材料中畴壁的功能性
> 
> The atomically thin nature of ferroelectric domain walls in multiferroics (Fig. 4a), combined with their wealth of exotic functionalities, indicate tremendous potential for discovery and exploitation of new physical behaviours, in the manner of Herbert Kroemer’s famous “The interface is the device” statement. In particular, the remarkable observation of electrical conductivity at the ferroelectric domain walls in BiFeO3 (refs. 64–66; Fig. 4b), opened an entirely new avenue of research into novel functionalities at multiferroic domain walls motivated by the prospect of metallic transport through nanoscale channels that can be electrically written, erased and moved67,68. Temperature- and magnetic-field-dependent studies show large magnetoresistance and increasing conductivity as temperature is decreased below ~50 K in BiFeO3, suggesting the possibility of metallic transport at the domain walls; such a demonstration in an otherwise insulating ferroelectric would of course be a significant breakthrough. Subsequent studies have revealed a wealth of behaviour ranging from strong memristive behaviour (Fig. 4c), magnetoresistvity69 and spin transport70, and photovoltaic response71. In addition, conducting ferroelectric domain walls have been identified in several other materials, including improper ferroelectrics such as ErMnO3 (ref. 72; Fig. 4d,e). Here, head-to-head and tailto-tail domain walls are trapped by the topology of the domains caused by the improper ferroelectric phase transition, and the low spontaneous polarization facilitates compensation of the associated polar discontinuity. Intriguingly, the prototypical non-magnetic ferroelectric, BaTiO3, has also recently been manipulated to accommodate charged domain walls that are conducting73. In a completely different example, a two-dimensional, ferromagnetic phase has been seen at the twin domain walls in antiferromagnetic TbMnO3 (refs. 74,75). Future progress on domain wall functionality will rely on detailed calculations and careful characterization to clarify the origin of the observed behaviours76. Important open questions are the limit of possible electron transport—in particular whether a metal–insulator transition can be achieved and controlled—the potential for memristive behaviour, and the role of proper versus improper ferroelectricity in determining the domain wall properties and switching dynamics. 多铁性材料中铁电畴壁的原子级厚度（图4a），结合其丰富的奇异功能性，预示着发现和利用新物理行为的巨大潜力，正如Herbert Kroemer的名言“界面即器件”所描述的那样。特别是，在BiFeO3铁电畴壁处观察到电导率（文献64–66；图4b），开创了多铁性畴壁处新颖功能性的全新研究方向，其动机在于通过可电写入、擦除和移动的纳米级通道实现金属性输运的前景67,68。温度及磁场依赖性研究表明，BiFeO3中在温度降至~50 K以下时存在大的磁电阻和增大的电导率，暗示了畴壁处金属性输运的可能性；在原本绝缘的铁电体中实现这样的演示，无疑将是一个重大突破。后续研究揭示了丰富的行为，包括强忆阻行为（图4c）、磁电阻69和自旋输运70，以及光伏响应71。此外，已在若干其他材料中识别出导电铁电畴壁，包括非本征铁电体如ErMnO3（文献72；图4d,e）。在此，头对头和尾对尾畴壁被非本征铁电相变导致的畴拓扑结构所捕获，且低自发极化有助于补偿相关的极性不连续性。有趣的是，原型非磁性铁电体BaTiO3最近也被操纵以容纳导电的带电畴壁73。在一个完全不同的例子中，在反铁磁TbMnO3的孪晶畴壁处观察到了二维铁磁相74,75。畴壁功能性方面的未来进展将依赖于详细的计算和仔细的表征，以阐明所观察行为的起源76。重要的未决问题包括：可能的电子输运极限——特别是能否实现并控制金属-绝缘体转变——忆阻行为的潜力，以及本征与非本征铁电性在决定畴壁性质和开关动力学中的作用。 **domain wall**: 畴壁，铁性材料中不同取向区域（畴）之间的边界，可具有不同于块体的独特性质。 **improper ferroelectricity**: 非本征铁电性，铁电性并非由极性不稳定所驱动，而是作为其他序参量（如旋转）的副产物出现。

> # Advances in characterization and modelling
> 
> 表征与建模的进展
> 
> In this second section we review advances in experimental and theoretical methods for measuring and describing multiferroic phenomena. 在第二部分中，我们回顾用于测量和描述多铁性现象的实验和理论方法的进展。
> 
> ## Experimental probes
> 
> 实验探针
> 
> The past years have seen the development and implementation of a variety of new experimental tools that offer the promise of studying the spin, charge and orbital degrees of freedom in multiferroics, with a spatial resolution approaching the unit cell dimensions, and with a sub-femtosecond time resolution approaching exchange interaction time scales77. Such local and time-resolved characterization of the magnetoelectric coupling is already leading to the identification of new physics that could not be revealed by the spatial or temporal ensemble-averaged bulk measurements used to date. Scanning probe techniques, such as piezoforce, (conducting-) atomic and magnetic force microscopies (PFM, (c-)AFM and MFM) are particularly attractive since they combine spatial resolution and functional response. We direct the reader to recent reviews for an in-depth treatment of these techniques78, mentioning here specifically the recent use of tips containing diamond nitrogen–vacancy (NV) centres to probe the spiral magnetic moment in BiFeO3 (ref. 79). Time-resolved X-ray studies can now reveal the dynamics of coupling between spin and charge degrees of freedom, with ultrafast synchrotron X-ray sources80 complementing tabletop femtosecond laser-based probes. In one example, terahertz (THz) radiation emanating from multiferroic BiFeO3 thin films during ultrafast modulation of the spontaneous polarization was probed with femtosecond laser pulses and shown to directly reflect the polarization state, suggesting the possibility of using this as a memory read-out mechanism81. Electron probes can now reveal chemical information, through electron energy-loss spectroscopy (EELS), and structural information, using aberration-corrected microscopes, at sub-nanometre spatial resolution. Light elements such as oxygen, and in turn the oxygen octahedral rotations and tilts in perovskites, can be identified82. These are of particular importance since they determine bandwidths and magnetic exchanges, to which the magnetic and ferroelectric properties are sensitive. In addition, magnetic information can now be extracted from electron circular dichroism measurements, in which zone plates induce helicity in the electron beam83. Optical second-harmonic generation is a well-established tool for probing ferroelectricity due to its sensitivity to inversion-symmetry breaking84. The recent implementation of in situ second harmonic generation during pulsed laser deposition (PLD) oxide growth is particularly promising for understanding the emergence of ferroelectric order in materials such as BiFeO3, in which the growth occurs below the ferroelectric Curie temperature85. 过去几年中，多种新型实验工具的开发和应用，为研究多铁性材料中的自旋、电荷和轨道自由度提供了可能，空间分辨率接近晶胞尺寸，亚飞秒时间分辨率接近交换相互作用时间尺度77。这种对磁电耦合的局域和时间分辨表征，已经开始揭示此前无法通过空间或时间系综平均的块体测量发现的新物理。扫描探针技术，如压电力显微镜(PFM)、导电原子力显微镜(c-AFM)和磁力显微镜(MFM)尤其具有吸引力，因为它们结合了空间分辨率和功能性响应。我们建议读者参阅近期综述以深入了解这些技术78，在此特别提及近期使用含金刚石氮空位(NV)中心的探针来探测BiFeO3中螺旋磁矩的工作79。时间分辨X射线研究现在能够揭示自旋与电荷自由度之间耦合的动力学，超快同步辐射X射线源80与台式飞秒激光探针相辅相成。在一个例子中，利用飞秒激光脉冲探测了多铁性BiFeO3薄膜在自发极化超快调制过程中发射的太赫兹(THz)辐射，并显示其直接反映极化状态，表明将其用作存储器读出机制的可能性81。电子探针现在能够通过电子能量损失谱(EELS)提供化学信息，并利用像差校正显微镜在亚纳米空间分辨率下提供结构信息。轻元素如氧，以及进而钙钛矿中的氧八面体旋转和倾斜，均可被识别82。这些尤为重要，因为它们决定了带宽和磁交换作用，而磁性和铁电性质对此敏感。此外，现在可以从电子圆二色测量中提取磁信息，其中波带片在电子束中诱导螺旋性83。光学二次谐波产生是一种成熟的探测铁电性的工具，因为它对反演对称性破缺敏感84。近期在脉冲激光沉积(PLD)氧化物生长过程中实施原位二次谐波产生，对于理解如BiFeO3等材料中铁电序的出现特别有前景，因为其生长发生在铁电居里温度以下85。 **second harmonic generation**: 二次谐波产生，一种非线性光学过程，其中两个光子转换为一个倍频光子，对反演对称性破缺敏感。

> ## Theoretical treatments
> 
> 理论处理方法
> 
> While first-principles density functional theory (DFT) calculations remain the gold standard for understanding and predicting the properties of insulating magnetically ordered materials such as multiferroics, second-principles calculations are proving increasingly valuable in the study of larger systems—for example, heterostructures, domain walls and defects—as well as longer timescales in molecular dynamics. In second-principles calculations, an effective model is constructed by a judicious choice of the essential physics, and the parameters of the model are extracted by fitting to DFT. Such effective models have been used successfully for many years to describe structural phase transitions of prototypical ferroelectrics86,87 and recent extensions to include additional lattice degrees of freedom88, as well as magnetic interactions89, have extended their applicability to multiferroics. For example, an effective Hamiltonian consisting of a lattice part incorporating ferroelectric distortions, octahedral rotations and strain, a contribution from the interaction of the magnetic moments with each other, and coupling between the magnetic moments and the lattice has been shown to accurately reproduce the crystal and magnetic structures of bulk BiFeO3. One length scale further, a LandauGinzburg thermodynamic potential that includes both polar and antiferrodistortive distortions and their coupling to the magnetism has been successful in reproducing the bulk behaviour of BiFeO3 and offers great promise for predicting properties in thin-film heterostructures and nanostructures90. Going forward, improved multiscale approaches that allow treatment of the electronic and lattice degrees of freedom on the same footing91 could lead to vastly enhanced system size and accuracy when combined with improved tools for generating effective potentials using input from first principles92. Modelling of the dynamics of ferroelectric switching93 and its effect on magnetic order94, both of which are on time- and length-scales that are far outside the ranges accessible using density functional methods, now become feasible. Such models in combination with molecular dynamics start to allow calculation of dynamical magnetoelectric responses in the THz region95, which is particularly timely as it coincides with advances in experimental methods for generating THz radiation already mentioned. > ## Electric-field control of magnetism
> 
> 电场控制磁性
> 
> The driver for studying electric-field control of magnetism is the substantially lower energy requirement for manipulating magnetic states with electric rather than magnetic fields. As an example, in the state-of-theart spin-transfer torque approach to reorienting magnetism, each electron’s 1 Bohr magneton (μB) of spin switches 1 μB of magnetization in a data bit. The required current increases linearly with the size of the moment to be switched, amounting to a current density of ~1011 A m–2 and an energy consumption of ~10 fJ in a typical device. For comparison, the total energy consumed in capacitive multiferroic devices101, for a typical 10 × 10 nm2 lat eral geometry, is ~1 aJ, four orders of magnitude smaller102. > ### Electric-field control of magnetic orientation
> 
> 电场控制磁取向
> 
> Perhaps the most significant breakthrough in recent years is the demonstration that the magnetization in the conventional ferromagnet CoFe can be rotated by 180° by an electric field106,107 when it is exchange coupled to BiFeO3. > ## Radio- and high-frequency devices
> 
> 射频与高频器件
> 
> There has been considerable recent progress, particularly using mechanically coupled ferroelectric–ferromagnetic composites114,115, towards the goal of controllable high-frequency responses of magnetoelectrics116. > ## Ultralow power logic-memory devices
> 
> 超低功耗逻辑-存储器件
> 
> A promising recently developed device architecture from Intel117 combines two key recent discoveries: the inverse Rashba–Edelstein (spin Hall) effect (IREE)109,118–120 and multiferroic electric-field control of magnetism. The resulting magnetoelectric, spin–orbit coupled (MESO) logic device121 (Fig. 6) uses the IREE component to convert spin to charge (or voltage) and the multiferroic to perform the opposite conversion of charge to spin. Success of the device rests on an increase in the IREE voltage output from current values of hundreds of μV to hundreds of mV, as well as a reduction in voltage requirement for the magnetoelectric component from the current ~5 V down to ~100 mV. Such breakthroughs could lead to a transformative, 1 aJ per memory bit or logic element. Switching a ferroelectric state with a voltage as small as 100 mV is a ‘grand challenge’ for the multiferroics field. The ferroelectric switching process in BiFeO3 is believed to be limited by nucleation and growth of reverse domains broadly captured by the Kay–Dunn model101, in which the coercive field scales as t–2/3, where t is the film thickness. Consequently, progressively larger reductions in film thickness are needed to reduce the coercive voltage as it is pushed to smaller values. In BiFeO3, La substitution has been shown122 to reduce the switching energy by reducing the polarization123, although to an insufficient extent to date; pushing BiFeO3 close to a phase boundary between ferroelectric and antiferroelectric states, or identifying materials without the robust octahedral rotations of BiFeO3 could be an alternative pathway to smaller coercive fields. The challenges facing the spintronics community in enhancing the output of the inverse Rashba effect124 by two to three orders of magnitude are equally exciting. 英特尔公司近期开发的一种有前景的器件架构117，结合了两项关键近期发现：逆Rashba-Edelstein（自旋霍尔）效应(IREE)109,118–120和多铁性电场控制磁性。由此产生的磁电自旋轨道耦合(MESO)逻辑器件121（图6）利用IREE组件将自旋转换为电荷（或电压），并利用多铁性组件执行相反的电荷至自旋转换。该器件的成功取决于将IREE电压输出从目前的数百μV提升至数百mV，以及将磁电组件所需的电压从目前的约5 V降低至约100 mV。此类突破可带来变革性的每存储位或逻辑元件1 aJ的能耗。用低至100 mV的电压切换铁电态是多铁性领域的“重大挑战”。BiFeO3中的铁电开关过程被认为受限于反向畴的成核与生长，这大致可由Kay-Dunn模型101描述，其中矫顽场按t–2/3比例变化，t为薄膜厚度。因此，需要逐步加大薄膜厚度的减薄程度，以将矫顽电压推至更小值。在BiFeO3中，La替代已被证明122通过降低极化来减少开关能量123，尽管截至目前程度尚不充分；将BiFeO3推向铁电态与反铁电态之间的相界附近，或识别不具有BiFeO3那种稳健八面体旋转的材料，可能是实现更小矫顽场的替代途径。自旋电子学领域在将逆Rashba效应124的输出提高两到三个数量级方面面临的挑战同样令人兴奋。 **Rashba effect**: Rashba效应，自旋轨道耦合导致能带自旋分裂的现象，可用于自旋-电荷转换。

> # Challenges and opportunities
> 
> 挑战与机遇
> 
> We close with our thoughts on the challenges and opportunities in multiferroics that we suggest should be addressed in the coming years, with our top-ten list captured in Box 1. Tremendous progress has been made in understanding chemistry/structure/property relationships, and in engineering specific atomic architectures, so that an era of ‘multiferroic materials by design’ is within reach. In particular, targeted functionalities, such as large magnetization and polarization as well as exotic polarization topologies, start to be accessible. Electric-field control of magnetism, while demonstrated in multiple implementations, must be optimized so that it can be achieved with smaller voltages, ideally below 100 mV. By far, the most urgent need is a broader set of materials that exhibit multiferroic and magnetoelectric behaviour at room temperature. For multiferroic devices to be technologically competitive will therefore require precise growth of ultrathin films guided by theoretical studies to exactly define the chemical compositions needed to optimize the polarization and coercive field. This will require improved fundamental understanding, which will be facilitated by improved first- and second-principles methods. Even with such a low-fieldswitching breakthrough, scale-up and integration, compatibility with existing silicon processing methods and integration with the appropriate peripheral electronics are particularly key challenges. An oxide-based ferromagnet or ferrimagnet that couples strongly to BiFeO3 and has a Curie temperature well above room temperature would be desirable. We expect dynamical effects in multiferroics to increase in importance over the next years, driven by new experimental capabilities, such as ultrafast X-ray sources like the Linac Coherent Light Source (LCLS) at Stanford University, and closing of the so-called THz gap125. We expect that fundamental limits on the dynamics of spin-charge-lattice coupling phenomena will be established. The theoretical proposals of dynamical multiferroicity, in which magnetism is induced by a time-dependent polarization in the reciprocal way to how polarization is induced by a spin spiral126, should be validated by careful experiments. We suggest more work on antiferromagnetic resonance in multiferroics; while many studies were carried out in the 1960s127 and 1970s on conventional antiferromagnets, activity with modern multiferroics, which typically have higher resonance frequencies (~700 GHz in BiFeO3 (ref. 128), compared with ~350 GHz in other perovskite orthoferrites135), has been scarce. It is clear that the field of multiferroics and magnetoelectrics is poised to make further significant breakthroughs and we hope that this Review motivates additional research on this fascinating class of materials and their applications. While scientific interest in the field is beyond question, we emphasize the need to identify market niches and enable pathways to products, so that multiferroics go beyond being an ‘area to watch’ and start to address contemporary technological challenges. To achieve this, a shift of focus from fundamental materials discoveries to translational research and development will be needed, similar to that which occurred in the field of GaN-based light-emitting diodes two decades ago. The complexity of oxide-based material systems raises particular additional challenges, as we have seen, for example, in the colossal magnetoresistive manganites, making the active engagement of applied physicists and device engineers early in the research and development process even more essential. In this vein, the recent engagement of large microelectronic companies in the field of multiferroics117,121 is particularly encouraging. While basic research in multiferroics is vibrant, the field would benefit from an injection of focused programmes that address the transition to devices, in particular scaleup and integration issues. 我们以对未来几年应应对的多铁性领域挑战与机遇的思考作为结尾，十大挑战清单见Box 1。在理解化学/结构/性质关系以及在工程特定原子架构方面已取得巨大进展，因此“按需设计多铁性材料”的时代已近在咫尺。特别是，目标功能性，如大磁化强度和极化以及奇异的极化拓扑，开始变得可及。电场控制磁性虽已在多种实现方案中演示，但必须优化，以能在更小电压下实现，理想情况下低于100 mV。目前最迫切的需求是更广泛的、在室温下表现出多铁性和磁电行为的材料系列。因此，要使多铁性器件在技术上具有竞争力，将需要精确生长超薄膜，并在理论研究的指导下精确确定优化极化和矫顽场所需的化学成分。这将需要改进的基础理解，而改进的第一性原理和第二原理方法将促进这一理解。即使有了这样的低场开关突破，放大与集成、与现有硅工艺的兼容性以及与适当外围电子器件的集成，都是特别关键的挑战。一种与BiFeO3强耦合且居里温度远高于室温的氧化物基铁磁体或亚铁磁体将是理想的。我们预计，在未来几年，多铁性材料的动力学效应将变得更加重要，这得益于新的实验能力，如斯坦福大学的直线加速器相干光源(LCLS)等超快X射线源，以及所谓的太赫兹差距125的缩小。我们预计，自旋-电荷-晶格耦合现象动力学的基本极限将被确立。动态多铁性的理论提议——即通过依赖时间的极化以与自旋螺旋诱导极化相反的方式诱导磁性126——应通过仔细的实验加以验证。我们建议开展更多关于多铁性材料中反铁磁共振的研究；虽然20世纪60年代127和70年代对常规反铁磁体进行了大量研究，但对现代多铁性材料（通常具有更高的共振频率，BiFeO3中约700 GHz，文献128，而其他钙钛矿正铁氧体中约350 GHz，文献135）的研究活动却很少。显然，多铁性与磁电学领域即将取得进一步重大突破，我们希望本综述能激励对这一迷人材料类别及其应用进行更多研究。尽管对该领域的科学兴趣毋庸置疑，但我们强调需要识别市场利基并开辟通往产品的路径，使多铁性材料超越“值得关注的领域”，开始应对当代技术挑战。要实现这一点，需要将焦点从基础材料发现转向转化研究与开发，类似于二十年前氮化镓基发光二极管领域所发生的那样。如我们所见，氧化物基材料体系的复杂性带来了额外的特殊挑战，例如在巨磁阻锰氧化物中，这使得应用物理学家和器件工程师在研发早期阶段的积极参与变得更加必要。在这方面，近期大型微电子公司参与多铁性领域117,121尤其令人鼓舞。虽然多铁性材料的基础研究充满活力，但该领域将受益于聚焦于向器件过渡的专项计划的注入，特别是放大与集成问题。 **Curie temperature**: 居里温度，铁磁性或铁电性材料转变为顺磁性或顺电性的临界温度。

> Science • Discovery of new, room-temperature multiferroics with robust coupling between magnetism and ferroelectricity, low leakage, and high-remanent magnetic moment should be the highest priority for the community. • Atomic-scale design combined with layer-by-layer growth provides an attractive pathway for discovering and synthesizing new materials to address the need for improved room-temperature multiferroics. • Developing new mechanisms for magnetoelectric coupling and understanding and approaching the limits of the strength of such phenomena would be very valuable. • Understanding, controlling and exploiting dynamics should be the next highest priority, with a particular focus on identifying the limits of dynamics and dynamical switching. • From a broader perspective, exploring the role of multiferroic order in the emergence of unconventional superconductivity, quantum criticality and other emergent phenomena could open entirely new directions in physics. 科学 • 发现具有磁性与铁电性之间稳健耦合、低漏电和高剩余磁矩的新型室温多铁性材料，应是该领域的最高优先事项。 • 原子尺度设计与逐层生长相结合，为发现和合成新材料以应对改进的室温多铁性材料需求提供了有吸引力的途径。 • 开发磁电耦合的新机制，理解并接近此类现象强度的极限，将极具价值。 • 理解、控制和利用动力学应成为次高优先事项，尤其聚焦于识别动力学和动态开关的极限。 • 从更广阔的视角看，探索多铁性序在非常规超导、量子临界性及其他涌现现象出现中的作用，可能开辟物理学全新的方向。
> 
> Technology • Achieving thermal stability of robustly coupled ferroelectric and magnetic order parameters at the 10-nm length-scale at room temperature is a critical requirement for future technologies. This will require detailed measurement of magnetoelectric and multiferroic phenomena at such length scales. • Reducing the voltage required for ferroelectric/magnetoelectric switching to ~100 mV would have immense impact and should be an immediate goal for the community. • A key enabler for ultralow power electronics (for example, an attojoule switch) would be the design of proper ferroelectric multiferroics with small but stable spontaneous polarizations of ~1–5 μC cm–2. • Integration and scale-up of synthetic approaches to enable manufacturing would be valuable. In the same vein, methods for etch processing and integration are needed. • From a longer timescale perspective, reaching the theoretical Landauer limit for switching (kTln2) would be desirable and will require significant effort. 技术 • 在10纳米尺度下实现室温下稳健耦合的铁电与磁序参量的热稳定性，是未来技术的关键要求。这将需要在此类尺度下对磁电和多铁性现象进行详细测量。 • 将铁电/磁电开关所需电压降至约100 mV将产生巨大影响，应成为该领域的一个近期目标。 • 超低功耗电子器件（例如阿焦开关）的一个关键使能因素是设计具有约1–5 μC cm–2小而稳定自发极化的本征铁电性多铁性材料。 • 合成方法的集成与放大以实现制造将极具价值。同样，需要蚀刻加工和集成方法。 • 从更长的时间尺度看，达到开关的理论Landauer极限（kTln2）将是理想目标，并需要巨大的努力。
