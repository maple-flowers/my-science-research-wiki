> ## 一、引言
> 
> 多铁性材料是凝聚态物理和材料科学领域的一个前沿热点。这类材料能在单一相中同时展现出铁电有序和（反）铁磁有序，并且这两种有序的耦合——即**磁电效应（Magnetoelectric Effect）**——赋予了材料全新的物理特性和器件应用潜力。然而，自然界中单相多铁材料极为稀少，这构成了该领域的核心科学难题。
> 
> 本篇由R. Ramesh和Nicola A. Spaldin于2007年发表在《Nature Materials》上的经典综述，正是在该领域经历“复兴”的关键节点上，系统性地回顾了**多铁性薄膜材料**的研究进展。它不仅是该领域入门者的必读文献，也为研究者提供了清晰的路线图。文章的核心价值在于：它从基础化学理论的“禁忌”出发，阐述了为何多铁材料如此稀缺，并以此为基础，构建了三种薄膜架构（单相薄膜、水平异质结、垂直异质结）来突破这一瓶颈，最终展望了基于磁电耦合的新型器件。
>

> 
> 本文采用“总-分-总”的经典结构，逻辑链条清晰，旨在引导读者从理论困境走向工程解决方案，再到未来的应用前景。
> 
> 1.  **提出问题 (What & Why):** 文章首先回顾了**多铁性（Multiferroicity）** 和**磁电效应（Magnetoelectric Effect）** 概念的复兴，指出了其巨大的应用潜力，但同时也明确提出核心矛盾：**磁性（Magnetism）** 与**铁电性（Ferroelectricity）** 在传统机制上存在互斥（contra-indication）。
> 2.  **分析问题 (How - Theoretical):** 深入剖析了这种互斥的化学根源（d轨道填充问题），并总结了四种已知的、能绕过此禁忌实现多铁性的物理机制（如孤对电子、几何铁电等）。
> 3.  **解决问题 (How - Material):** 这是文章的核心，将问题从理论转向材料实现。作者提出了三种关键的薄膜架构作为解决方案：

>     -   **水平异质结 (Horizontal Heterostructures):** 通过原子级层状堆叠，在界面处人工设计和创造多铁性。
>     -   **垂直异质结 (Vertical Heterostructures):** 通过自组装纳米柱结构，实现三维的强耦合，以克服衬底夹持效应。
> 4.  **应用与展望 (How - Application):** 基于上述材料，探讨了在电场调控磁性、自旋电子学、新型存储等领域的器件构想，并指出了未来的关键科学挑战。

> ## 四、正文核心内容剖析
> 
> ### 4.1 研究背景：磁性与铁电性的“禁忌”
> 
> 文章开篇即点出核心问题：为何单相多铁性材料如此稀少？这是由它们各自的化学偏好决定的。传统的铁电性需要**B位阳离子**（通常是过渡金属离子）具有空的**d轨道**来形成与氧的强共价键，从而偏离中心产生电偶极矩。而磁性则恰恰需要部分填充的**d轨道**来提供未配对的电子自旋。这种“空轨道”与“半满轨道”的矛盾，构成了两者共存的根本性化学禁忌，即 **d⁰ vs dⁿ 的互斥**。
> 
> ### 4.2 核心方法：绕过禁忌的四大策略与三种薄膜架构
> 
> 为了克服上述禁忌，作者总结了四种物理机制，将铁电性的来源“转移”给其他结构单元：
> 
> 1.  **孤对电子驱动铁电性:** 利用A位大阳离子（如Bi³⁺）的6s²孤对电子的立体化学活性打破空间反演对称性，产生铁电性，而B位仍由磁性离子占据。**BiFeO₃**是这类材料的典型代表。
> 2.  **几何铁电性:** 在六角锰酸盐**YMnO₃**中，铁电性并非源于离子偏离中心，而是由MnO₅多面体的集体倾斜和形变导致，这种结构畸变可以与磁性共存。
> 3.  **磁致铁电性:** 在TbMnO₃等材料中，特定的螺旋磁序打破了空间反演对称性，从而“诱导”出了铁电性。这种铁电性直接源于磁序，因此预期有极强的磁电耦合。
> 4.  **电荷序诱导铁电性:** 在LuFe₂O₄中，Fe²⁺和Fe³⁺的有序排列打破了空间对称性，产生了电极化。
> 
> 基于这些理论，文章重点介绍了实现高性能多铁性材料的三种薄膜工程架构：
> 
> -   **单相薄膜:** 重点讨论了明星材料BiFeO₃。其薄膜性能（极化强度高达~90 μC/cm²）远超块材，最初归因于外延应变，但也与块材中缺陷和漏电流有关。薄膜还抑制了块材中存在的、会抵消宏观磁矩的螺旋自旋结构，从而观察到了显著的磁化强度和磁电耦合。
> -   **水平异质结:** 通过原子级精度的人工层状堆叠，在界面处创造多铁性。例如，通过打破空间反演对称性的A-B-C-A-B-C...排序，或利用界面处的轨道杂化和电荷转移，实现纯电子机制的磁电耦合。
> -   **垂直异质结:** 以CoFe₂O₄（磁性）纳米柱嵌入BaTiO₃或BiFeO₃（铁电）基体为例，实现了三维异质外延。这种结构不仅增大了界面面积，还减弱了衬底对薄膜的机械夹持效应，从而实现了更强的、由应力介导的磁电耦合，甚至实现了电场对磁化方向的翻转。
> 
> ### 4.3 实验与讨论：表征、耦合与器件前景
> 
> 文章详细讨论了该领域面临的实验挑战和表征方法，并预示了器件化的未来。
> 
> -   **表征挑战:** 由于薄膜磁电系数通常很小且易受漏电流干扰，传统直接测量（∂P/∂H等）极为困难。因此，作者强调了发展高空间分辨率的先进技术，如**X射线磁圆二色性-光电子发射显微镜（XMCD-PEEM）** 和**磁光克尔效应（MOKE）** 等，来直接观测微区磁畴/铁电畴及其耦合。
> -   **耦合机制与器件:** 文章提出了两种极具吸引力的电场控磁路径：
>     1.  **控制自旋倾斜:** 在反铁磁多铁材料中，通过电场翻转铁电极化，同时改变Dzyaloshinskii–Moriya相互作用导致的弱铁磁矩方向。
>     2.  **控制交换偏置:** 将铁电反铁磁体与铁磁体组合，利用电场改变反铁磁序，进而通过界面交换偏置操控铁磁层的磁化方向。这被认为是实现非易失性、超低功耗磁存储器的理想方案。
> 
> ## 五、结论与贡献
> 
> 文章的核心结论是：高品质薄膜制备技术的进步，特别是外延异质结构的构建，已经从根本上解决了多铁性材料在自然界中稀缺的难题，并开辟了在原子尺度上人工设计和增强磁电耦合的新范式。
> 
> 其主要贡献在于：
> 
> 1.  **系统性梳理:** 清晰地从化学禁忌的角度，归纳了实现多铁性的四种物理机制，为该领域提供了坚实的理论框架。
> 2.  **提出工程范式:** 创新性地提出了“单相-水平-垂直”三种薄膜架构的设计方法论，为后续的材料创新指明了方向，尤其是垂直异质结概念的提出极具前瞻性。
> 3.  **定义技术前沿:** 指出未来的关键挑战在于纳米级表征（如原位PEEM）、控制漏电流、理解尺寸效应，并提出了电场控制交换偏置和自旋波等具体器件构想，为后续的研究设定了议程。
> 
> ## 六、未来发展方向展望
> 
> 文章在结尾部分对未来研究进行了展望，主要包括：
> 
> 1.  **表征深化:** 需利用原位、高分辨的手段（如PEEM）直接揭示多铁性耦合的微观机制，特别是磁畴和铁电畴在外场下的协同演化。
> 2.  **材料探索:**
>     -   寻找能增大自旋倾斜角度的材料或机制，以获得更大的宏观磁化强度。
>     -   探索更多基于电荷序、孤对电子等机制的候选材料。
>     -   深入研究尺寸效应，确定多铁性在纳米尺度下的稳定性极限。

> ## 四、正文核心内容剖析
> 
> #### 1. 研究背景：为何多铁性材料如此稀有且重要？
> 
> -   **历史渊源与复兴**：文章开篇即点明，**磁电多铁性材料**的概念虽早，但长期受限于单相材料的匮乏。21世纪初的复兴得益于三大支柱：高压合成等带来的新单晶、**第一性原理计算（First-principles calculations）** 的预测能力、以及薄膜生长技术的突破。
> -   **核心物理矛盾**：文章直接从化学键和电子轨道层面解剖了多铁性稀少的根本原因——**互斥性（Contra-indication）**。
>     -   **传统铁电体**：其位移型相变通常要求B位阳离子有空的d轨道（如Ti⁴⁺的3d⁰），以形成强共价键。
>     -   **磁性**：通常源于部分填充的d轨道上的未成对电子。
>     -   因此，在同一位点上同时实现两者是矛盾的。
> -   **解决方案**：文章系统总结了克服这一矛盾的四种途径：
>     -   **孤对电子机制**：让A位离子（如Bi³⁺）的6s²孤对电子提供铁电性，B位离子（如Fe³⁺）提供磁性。代表材料：**BiFeO3**。
>     -   **几何驱动机制**：铁电性源于晶格结构的几何畸变，而非传统的离子位移。代表材料：六方**YMnO3**。
>     -   **磁致铁电机制**：通过形成特殊的、空间反演对称性破缺的磁结构来诱导铁电性。代表材料：**TbMnO3**。
>     -   **电荷有序机制**：非中心对称的电荷有序排列也能导致铁电性。代表材料：**LuFe2O4**。
> 
> #### 2. 三大薄膜架构：解锁多铁性潜力的钥匙
> 
> 本章是全文的核心，详细阐述了三种薄膜技术路线如何解决上述问题并创造新机遇。
> 
> -   **单相薄膜：应变与尺寸的魔力**
>     
>     -   **BiFeO3案例**：这是本文的重中之重。块材`BiFeO3`虽是铁电/反铁磁体，但存在一个长周期（~620 Å）的自旋螺旋结构，导致宏观磁矩被抵消，磁电耦合效应被掩盖。薄膜技术带来了两大改变：
>         -   **巨大极化增强**：薄膜`BiFeO3`的极化值可达~90 μC/cm²，远超早期块材的测量值（~6.1 μC/cm²）。这归因于薄膜的高质量，避免了块材中的漏电和缺陷问题。计算还预测了存在极化高达~150 μC/cm²的**亚稳态四方相**。
>         -   **自旋螺旋的抑制**：外延应变和尺寸效应能抑制该螺旋结构，释放出潜在的**弱铁磁性（Weak Ferromagnetism）**，并观测到显著的磁电耦合。这为用电场控制其反铁磁轴方向交换偏置提供了可能。
>     -   **应变工程**：文章指出，多铁性材料（特别是孤对电子型）的极化对应变不敏感，因为其铁电态非常稳定。但应变可通过改变晶体对称性（如`BiFeO3`从菱方变为单斜），从而改变磁化的易轴方向，实现对其磁电开关行为的调控。
> -   **水平异质结：界面即器件**
>     
>     -   **人工设计多铁性**：通过原子级精确堆叠，可以创造出自然界不存在的多铁性。例如，将`CaTiO3`、`BaTiO3`、`SrTiO3`按非中心对称顺序堆叠，人为打破空间反演对称性，产生极化。
>     -   **纯电子机制磁电耦合**：理论预测，在`Fe/BaTiO3`这类铁磁/铁电界面，铁电位移会改变界面原子轨道重叠，从而直接调制磁层的磁化强度，这是一种非应变介导的新型磁电耦合。
>     -   **场效应器件**：通过类似于传统场效应晶体管的结构，利用铁电/压电层的极化场来调控相邻磁性半导体（如`Co:TiO2`）或锰氧化物（如`LaCaMnO3`）的载流子浓度，从而改变其磁性。
> -   **垂直异质结：三维耦合与应力释放**
>     
>     -   **结构优势**：如图4所示，`CoFe2O4`纳米柱垂直嵌入`BaTiO3`或`BiFeO3`基体中。这种结构相比水平异质结有两大优势：巨大的界面面积，有利于增强耦合；消除了衬底对薄膜的面内夹持（Clamping），使压电/磁致伸缩效应能更有效地传递。
>     -   **耦合机制**：实验证明，这种结构存在强烈的磁电耦合，主要通过**力学途径**：电场翻转铁电基体的极化，产生应变，应变传递到磁性纳米柱，改变其磁各向异性，进而翻转磁化。但这种耦合需要外加一个微弱磁场来打破时间反演对称性，才能实现确定性翻转。
> 
> #### 3. 器件应用：电控磁的未来
> 
> -   **电控交换偏置**：这是最核心的器件构想。利用多铁性材料（如`BiFeO3`）既是铁电体又是反铁磁体的特性，通过电场翻转其铁电极化，同时改变其反铁磁轴方向。由于该反铁磁层与相邻的铁磁层（如`Co`）之间存在**交换偏置（Exchange Bias）** 耦合，反铁磁轴的变化会进而驱动铁磁层的磁矩翻转，最终实现电场对磁性的非易失性控制。这被认为是自旋电子学（Spintronics）中的革命性技术。
> -   **多铁性隧道结**：用极薄的多铁性薄膜（如`BiMnO3`）作为隧道结的势垒层。由于势垒层的电子态是自旋极化的，它会像一个自旋过滤器，只允许特定自旋方向的电子隧穿。如果该势垒层同时是铁电的，其势垒高度又能被电场调制，从而创造出一种同时具有电控和磁控能力的四态电阻器件。
> -   **电控自旋波器件**：理论预测，在铁电-铁磁系统中，电场可以激发自旋波（磁振子），磁场可以激发铁电振荡（声子）。文章认为这是一个极具前景但尚待开发的方向。

> 1.  **问题一：薄膜与块材物性差异的归因是否过于简化？** 文章将`BiFeO3`薄膜极化增强主要归因于高质量和应变，但块材的低极化值可能源于畴壁密度、缺陷化学（如氧空位）和畴夹持效应等更复杂的因素。薄膜与块材的差异，不仅仅是“质量”的区别，更是“生长热力学与动力学路径”的根本不同，这可能导致完全不同的亚稳态和缺陷结构。

> 2.  **问题二：垂直异质结中的耦合机制是“纯粹”的剪应力耦合吗？** 文章强调其力学耦合机制。但在此类铁磁/铁电氧化物界面，必然存在电荷转移、轨道重构和交换作用等界面电子效应。当时的实验（如MFM）可能难以精确区分力学耦合与电子耦合的贡献。更精确的、能原位探测界面电子态和磁态的技术（如PEEM，图3）正是为此而发展的。

> 3.  **问题三：电控交换偏置概念的普适性挑战是什么？** 该构想极度依赖多铁性材料中反铁磁序对铁电序的强耦合。这在`BiFeO3`中是通过非180°极化翻转实现的，但并非所有多铁性材料都具备此特性。此外，在超薄隧道结中，维持良好的反铁磁序和交换偏置效应本身就是一个巨大挑战，界面处的自旋钉扎问题可能比预想的更复杂。
>     
> 4.  **问题四：高极化值是否必然有利于磁电耦合？** 文章提到`BiFeO3`的极化对应变不敏感，因为其处于一个深的自由能势阱中。这暗示了高极化值（意味着高稳定性）可能与磁电耦合（需要对外界刺激有大的响应）之间存在内在矛盾。一个“更软”的、接近不稳定点的铁电体，其极化值可能较小，但对外场的响应（包括磁电耦合）也许会更强。
>     
> 5.  **问题五：该综述的视野是否存在局限性？** 文章发表于2007年，其视野主要集中于氧化物钙钛矿及其衍生结构。近年来，有机-无机杂化钙钛矿、范德华二维磁性材料等新兴体系中也发现了多铁性和磁电耦合，其物理机制（如自旋-声子耦合、层间滑移铁电性）与本文描述的经典框架截然不同。阅读时需意识到其时代性。

> ## 八、用户问题预测与解答
> 
> 针对初学者或交叉学科读者，我预测以下5个延伸问题并系统解答。
> 
> **Q1：铁电性、铁磁性、反铁磁性和铁弹性，这些“铁”到底是什么意思？它们之间有什么联系？** **A1**：您问到了凝聚态物理的核心概念。这些“铁性”的共性在于，材料在某个序参量上表现出**可翻转的、自发的长程有序态**，并伴随回滞现象。
> 
> -   **铁电性（Ferroelectricity）**：序参量是**自发极化（P）**，可在电场（E）下翻转，P-E曲线为电滞回线。
> -   **铁磁性（Ferromagnetism）**：序参量是**自发磁化（M）**，可在磁场（H）下翻转，M-H曲线为磁滞回线。
> -   **反铁磁性（Antiferromagnetism）**：原子磁矩反平行排列，宏观上净磁矩为零，但存在磁有序，可在强磁场下发生自旋翻转。

> The scarcity of ferromagnetic ferroelectrics7 is now well understood to result from the contra-indication between the conventional mechanism for cation off-centring in ferroelectrics (which requires formally empty d orbitals), and the formation of magnetic moments (which usually results from partially filled d orbitals)3. For ferroelectricity and magnetism to coexist in a single phase, therefore, the atoms that move off centre to form the electric dipole moment should be different from those that carry the magnetic moment. In principle, this could be achieved through either an alternative (non-d-electron) mechanism for magnetism, or through an alternative mechanism for ferroelectricity; in practice only the latter route has been pursued, and the exploration of multiferroics with different forms of magnetism is an open area for future research. In conventional ferroelectrics, strain effects can lead to a substantial increase of the spontaneous polarization and Curie temperature58, and can even drive paraelectric materials such as SrTiO3 into the ferroelectric phase59. Because the mechanism for ferroelectricity in multiferroic materials can differ from that in conventional perovskite ferroelectrics, it is unclear whether similar strain effects should be expected in thin-film multiferroics. Indeed, first-principles density functional calculations for multiferroic BiFeO3 showed36,60 that the strain dependence of the ferroelectric polarization in BiFeO3 is rather weak compared with conventional ferroelectric materials, and that this is due to the high stability of the ferroelectric state in BiFeO3, indicated by the large ionic displacements from the centrosymmetric reference structure and the high ferroelectric Curie temperature. As a result, the changes in relative ionic positions when the lattice is strained are small. Although not directly related to the multiferroicity, a similar weak strain dependence should be expected in other Bi-based multiferroics, in which the stereochemically active Bi3+ lone pairs cause high critical temperatures and large polarizations. This indicates that for strain effects to be used in engineering the polarization of new multiferroics, softer ferroelectrics that are closer to the ferroelectric instability would have to be used. Larger qualitative effects can be expected when heteroepitaxial constraints lead to changes in crystal symmetry, as the symmetry determines the easy axes of magnetization, and the presence or absence of spin canting. For example, BiFeO3 undergoes a reduction from rhombohedral to monoclinic symmetry when grown on a [100] SrTiO3 substrate. This should lead to profound changes in the magnetoelectric switching behaviour61 as follows. In rhombohedral BiFeO3, the spontaneous electric polarization is directed along one of the eight [111] axes of the perovskite structure and is accompanied by a ferroelastic strain resulting from the distortion of the lattice. > Vertical heterostructures such as the nanopillar geometry shown in Fig. 2c offer numerous advantages over the conventional ‘horizontal’ heterostructures discussed above. First, they have a larger interfacial surface area and are intrinsically heteroepitaxial in three dimensions; this should allow for stronger coupling between ferroelectric and magnetic components. In addition, substrate-imposed mechanical clamping, which is known to suppress both the piezoelectric response and the magnetoelectric coupling mediated by lattice deformation in thin-film-on-substrate geometries, should be reduced in the vertical architecture85,86. The prototypical multiferroic vertical nanostructure consists of a magnetic spinel phase which is epitaxially embedded into the ferroelectric matrix. The first example87 contained CoFe2O4 pillars in a BaTiO3 matrix, but many different combinations of perovskites (BaTiO3, PbTiO3, BiFeO3 and SrTiO3) and spinels (CoFe2O4, NiFe2O4 and Fe3O4) have since been grown. Detailed studies in progress suggest that the switching is mediated by strong mechanical coupling between the two lattices, which leads to a timedependent modulation of the magnetic anisotropy in the nanopillar. First we review experimental methods for measuring magnetoelectric coupling in thin films. The classical method for probing magnetoelectric coupling is to measure the magnetoelectric response, ∂P/∂H or ∂M/∂E (where P is electric polarization, H is magnetic field, M is magnetization and E is electric field), directly as a function of temperature; this is analogous to the measurement of dielectric or magnetic susceptibilities. In thin films, however, such measurements are often complicated by leakage in the dielectric and other parasitic effects. Furthermore, the magnetoelectric coefficients are often small, leading to response signals that are only a few nanovolts and therefore require lock-in detection. Because of these difficulties, a variety of approaches has been developed to probe magnetoelectric coupling, many of which offer high spatial resolution; these approaches take advantage of the diversity of interactions between magnetoelectric materials and a broad spectrum of electromagnetic radiation (X-rays, visible optics, infrared optics, microwave and millimetre waves). A particularly important development is the evolution of chemical and magnetic state-specific imaging using photo-excited electrons in photoemission electron microscopy (PEEM). State-of-the-art PEEM systems have spatial resolution of the order of 20–50 nm, and with the PEEM 3 microscope at the Advanced Light Source at Lawrence Berkeley Laboratory, the possibility of 10-nm resolution is imminent. This technique has been used successfully by Scholl and co-workers95,96 to probe the exchange bias coupling between ferromagnetic cobalt and antiferromagnetic LaFeO3. Using a combination of X-ray magnetic circular dichroism PEEM to probe the domain structure of the ferromagnet,andlineardichroismPEEMto probetheantiferromagnet, they have demonstrated the existence of exchange bias coupling in this system. A similar approach in our laboratory will explore the coupling between a range of ferro- and ferrimagnets (Co, CoFe2O4, NiFe2O4) and ferroelectric BiFeO3. Magneto-optic measurements in the visible range using the Kerr effect (MOKE) provide another route to exploring magnetoelectric coupling in thin films. These studies are conceptually similar to conventional MOKE measurements, but the modulation of the MOKE response is through an electric field. In addition, ferro- or antiferromagnetic resonance spectroscopy is a powerful technique for probing coupling effects at microwave or millimetre wave frequencies, and, as mentioned above, presents the most obvious route to practical applications. Next we discuss two routes to controlling the magnetic behaviour using electric fields: through modifying the amount or direction of spin canting, and through influencing the exchange bias coupling. A common feature of antiferromagnetic oxides, including the multiferroic perovskites, is the presence of canted magnetism on the transition metal sublattice. Such a canting arises primarily from the Dzyaloshinskii–Moriya interaction38,39, and its existence and magnitude are determined by the symmetry of the crystal and the strength of the spin–orbit interaction respectively. The concept of exchange bias coupling in magnetic materials is well known, and is extensively used in magnetic sensing and storage applications97. Exchange bias occurs when the strong magnetic exchange interactions at the interface between an antiferromagnet (typically a metal such as Mn or a semiconductor such as MnO) and a ferromagnet pin the orientation of the spins in the ferromagnetic layer (Fig. 5). The availability of ferroelectric antiferromagnets, such as the multiferroic perovskites, presents an exciting opportunity for modulating or controlling the magnetic structure by the application of an electric field98,99 as follows. Coupling between ferroelectricity and antiferromagnetism provides permanent, non-volatile control of the orientation of the antiferromagnetic axis61, then coupling between the antiferromagnetic component and an adjacent ferromagnet should allow switching of the ferromagnetism through the application of an electric field. This promises to be an exciting area of research and suggests the possibility of new device technologies, particularly in the spintronic applications discussed next100. Many laboratories worldwide have taken up the challenge, and although these efforts are in their infancy, it is likely that the topic will be extensively researched in the immediate future. Of course, such effects do not necessarily require a multiferroic; a large magnetoelectric coupling would also be sufficient101. The use of magnetoelectric coupling and multiferroics in spintronics is a rapidly emerging area of research, and a number of possible device architectures have been proposed102. This theme, related to the one above, focuses on controlling and manipulating the spins of carriers in a ferromagnet using an electric field. One specific manifestation is a multiferroic tunnel junction which is used as a spin filter device with the potential to control both electrically and magnetically51,103. Experimentally, this seems to be difficult to accomplish because so many variables affect behaviour at such length scales. First, the multiferroic tunnel barrier must be thin; of the order of 1–2 nm thick. The growth of epitaxial thin-film multiferroics with high structural quality containing only a few unit cells is challenging and will undoubtedly be a focal point in the coming years. In addition, the stability of both magnetic and ferroelectric order parameters must be maintained down to these small thicknesses. As we discussed earlier, the robustness of ferroelectricity in thin films is of tremendous current interest, with state-of-the-art experiments and first-principles computations indicating a critical thickness of a few unit cells, strongly dependent on the screening properties of the electrodes64,67. How the critical behaviour is affected by the simultaneous presence of magnetic ordering in multiferroics is an open question. > > Figure 5 Essence of an electrically tuneable magnetic device. This schematic shows the essential features of such a device consisting of a multiferroic (ferroelectric antiferromagnet) in contact with a ferromagnet. Application of an electric field to the multiferroic causes a switching of the ferroelectric polarization: this in turn, through the internal coupling between ferroelectricity and antiferromagnetism, changes the AFM order which subsequently changes the state of magnetism in the ferromagnet owing to its coupling to the antiferromagnet through exchange bias coupling. This is a conceptual device, and there are several current attempts worldwide to create this or similar devices. 图5 电可调磁性器件的核心要素。此示意图显示了这种器件的核心特征，该器件由一个多铁性材料（铁电反铁磁体）与一个铁磁体接触构成。对多铁性材料施加电场会导致铁电极化翻转：这反过来通过铁电性与反铁磁性之间的内部耦合，改变了反铁磁有序，进而由于铁磁体与反铁磁体之间的交换偏置耦合，改变了铁磁体中的磁性状态。这是一个概念性器件，目前全球范围内正在进行多项尝试，以创建此器件或类似器件。
