> # Abstract
> 摘要
> 
> A cellular automaton model has been employed to investigate the transformation from austenite to ferrite in low carbon steels during continuous cooling. An important aspect of this approach is the implementation of incorporating local concentration changes into a nucleation or growth function, which is utilized by the automaton in a probabilistic fashion. The modeling gives a visual insight into the effect of cooling conditions on this transformation. The final nucleation number, the number of ferrite grains per austenite grain, ferrite grain size and the kinetics of ferrite formation are obtained as a function of the cooling rate or the undercooling temperature. © 2003 Acta Materialia Inc. > # 1. Introduction
> 引言
> 
> The problems dealing with phase transformations in low carbon steels, especially the transformation from austenite (γ) to ferrite (α), have attracted greater attention due to their industrial importance. From a physical point of view, it is of interest to understand how the ferrite microstructure evolves in this transformation during continuous cooling; in fact, researchers have developed different methods to investigate it [1–10]. Nevertheless, the complexity and difficulty of real time observation of the solid-state transformation limit the use of those techniques. Fortunately, rapid progress in theories and computational tools makes it desirable to investigate the microstructural evolution in nucleation and growth of ferrite for this kind of transformation in terms of a reasonable model, which should link up processing conditions with microstructures of processed materials. In recent years, modeling of microstructural formation in material processing has markedly progressed and can be computed fairly readily and with some degree of reliability based on cellular automaton (CA) and Monte Carlo [11–16], for example, the processes of recrystallization and dendritic growth and so on. However, in simulating the microstructural formation during the transformation from austenite to ferrite in low carbon steels, there are a few such calculations. Recently, Kumar et al. [17] used a CA model to simulate the competition between nucleation and early growth of ferrite transformed from austenite. In their simulations, a continuous nucleation model proposed by Rappaz and coworkers was adapted for describing the nucleation of ferrite, where quite a few adjustable parameters were used which made the effect of cooling conditions on this transformation difficult to quantitatively distinguish. In the present work, we employ a two-dimensional CA model to investigate this transformation from austenite to ferrite in a low carbon Fe– XiC (here Xi = Si, Mn, Ni, Cu, Cr) steel during continuous cooling but a few parameters are physically introduced. In modeling this transformation, the calculation of growth of ferrite is calculated within the CA lattice is a crucial aspect. Jacot and Rappaz [22] developed a two-dimensional model for describing diffusion-controled phase transformations. In this modeling, the calculations of diffusion kinetics and growth velocity of ferrite are similar to those of Jacot and Rappaz. In addition, this model takes into account the effect of the solute redistribution on the microstructural evolution, such as nucleation and growth of ferrite, in this transformation. The simulated results are shown in a metallographic form to give a visual insight into the effect of cooling conditions on this transformation, which make it easy to be compared with the experiment [6]. As the results of this modeling, the number of ferrite grains per austenite grain, ferrite grain size, the final nucleation number, ferrite fraction transformed, and nucleation fraction of ferrite are obtained quantitatively as a function of the cooling rate or the undercooling temperature.
> 处理低碳钢中的相变问题，特别是奥氏体（γ）向铁素体（α）的转变，因其工业重要性而备受关注。从物理角度来看，理解铁素体微观结构在连续冷却转变过程中如何演变颇具意义；事实上，研究人员已开发了不同的方法来研究它[1–10]。然而，实时观察固态转变的复杂性和困难限制了这些技术的使用。幸运的是，理论和计算工具的快速进步，使得通过一个合理的模型来研究这类转变中铁素体形核和生长的微观结构演变成为可能，该模型应将加工条件与加工材料的微观结构联系起来。近年来，材料加工中微观结构形成的建模已取得显著进展，并且可以基于元胞自动机（CA）和蒙特卡罗方法[11–16]（例如再结晶和枝晶生长等过程）进行相当容易且具有一定可靠性的计算。然而，在模拟低碳钢奥氏体向铁素体转变过程中的微观结构形成方面，此类计算还很少。最近，Kumar 等人[17]使用 CA 模型模拟了奥氏体转变而来的铁素体的形核和早期生长之间的竞争。在他们的模拟中，采用了 Rappaz 及其同事提出的连续形核模型来描述铁素体的形核，其中使用了相当多的可调参数，这使得冷却条件对该转变的影响难以定量区分。在本工作中，我们采用二维 CA 模型来研究连续冷却过程中低碳 Fe–XᵢC（此处 Xᵢ = Si, Mn, Ni, Cu, Cr）钢中奥氏体向铁素体的这种转变，但引入了一些物理参数。在模拟这种转变时，计算 CA 点阵内铁素体的生长是一个关键方面。Jacot 和 Rappaz [22] 开发了一个用于描述扩散控制相变的二维模型。在本建模中，扩散动力学和铁素体生长速度的计算与 Jacot 和 Rappaz 的类似。此外，该模型考虑了溶质再分配对该转变中微观结构演变（如铁素体形核和生长）的影响。模拟结果以金相形式展示，为冷却条件对该转变的影响提供了直观的见解，使其易于与实验进行比较[6]。作为该建模的结果，每个奥氏体晶粒内的铁素体晶粒数、铁素体晶粒尺寸、最终形核数、已转变的铁素体分数以及铁素体的形核分数均作为冷却速率或过冷温度的函数定量获得。
> **Monte Carlo**: 蒙特卡罗，一种基于随机数的计算方法。
> **Recrystallization**: 再结晶，指变形金属在加热后，畸变组织中重新形成无畸变的等轴晶粒的过程。
> **Dendritic Growth**: 枝晶生长，晶体在过冷熔体中生长时，沿特定方向优先生长形成树枝状晶体的过程。
> **Diffusion**: 扩散，物质因热运动而产生的从高浓度区向低浓度区的质量输运过程。
> **Solute Redistribution**: 溶质再分配，相变或凝固过程中，溶质原子在界面两侧发生重新分布的现象。

> ## 2.1. Description of mathematics model
> 数学模型描述
> 
> ### 2.1.1. Basic assumptions
> 基本假设
> 
> The model involves the following assumptions. First, for an Fe– Xi–C multi-component alloy with a given low carbon concentration c, Fe– Xi could be regarded as a super-element S [18], and the multi-component S–C alloy in computation is similar to a low carbon Fe–C alloy. Second, ferrite grains nucleate at the austenite grain boundaries as observed in the experiments [5,6]. Third, the growth of ferrite is mainly controled by the diffusion of C solute. Fourth, for the Fe–C alloy, the equilibrium temperature to start the transformation from austenite to ferrite, Ae3, for a concentration c is the temperature on the γ phase line of an Fe–C phase diagram corresponding to this concentration as shown in Fig. 1 [22].
> 该模型包含以下假设。第一，对于具有给定低碳浓度c的 Fe–Xᵢ–C 多组分合金，Fe–Xᵢ 可被视为一个超元素 S [18]，计算中的多组分 S–C 合金类似于低碳 Fe–C 合金。第二，如实验[5,6]所观察到的，铁素体晶粒在奥氏体晶界处形核。第三，铁素体的生长主要受 C 溶质的扩散控制。第四，对于 Fe–C 合金，给定浓度c下开始奥氏体向铁素体转变的平衡温度 Ae3，是对应于该浓度的 Fe–C 相图上 γ 相线上的温度，如图 1 所示 [22]。
> **Super-element**: 超元素，在计算模拟中，将一组合金元素视为一个整体元素以简化模型。
> **Grain Boundary**: 晶界，多晶体中不同取向晶粒之间的界面。
> **Ae3**: 奥氏体向铁素体转变的平衡开始温度。
> **Phase Diagram**: 相图，表示物质系统在平衡状态下各相组成与温度、压力、成分之间关系的图形。
> 
> Fig. 1. A schematic phase diagram of an Fe–C alloy, where Ae3 indicates the equilibrium temperature at the start of the transformation from austenite to ferrite for a concentration c.
> 图 1. Fe–C合金的示意相图，其中 Ae3 表示给定浓度c下开始奥氏体向铁素体转变的平衡温度。

> ### 2.1.2. Ferrite nucleation [4,5,18–21,23]
> 铁素体形核 [4,5,18–21,23]
> 
> During continuous cooling from Ae3 to a given temperature T, the number of newly formed ferrite grains n can be given by
> 在从 Ae3 连续冷却到给定温度 T 的过程中，新形成的铁素体晶粒数 n 可由下式给出：
> 
> 
> ```
> 
> n(T) = ∫_T^{Ae3} (I(T)[1 - f] / Q) dT (1)
> 
> n(T) = ∫_T^{Ae3} (I(T)[1 - f] / Q) dT (1)
> 
> I(T) = K1(kT)^{-1/2} D_γ exp ( -K2 / (kT (ΔG_{γ→α}^N)^2 ) ) (2)
> 
> I(T) = K1(kT)^{-1/2} D_γ exp ( -K2 / (kT (ΔG_{γ→α}^N)^2 ) ) (2)
> 
> ΔG_{γ→α}^N = ΔG_{γ→α}^S - RT ln a_γ^S (3)
> 
> ΔG_{γ→α}^N = ΔG_{γ→α}^S - RT ln a_γ^S (3)
> 
> ΔG_{γ→α}^S = Σ x_i (ΔT_i^M - ΔT_i^{NM}) + ΔG_{Fe}^{γ→(Sα)} (4)
> 
> ΔG_{γ→α}^S = Σ x_i (ΔT_i^M - ΔT_i^{NM}) + ΔG_{Fe}^{γ→(Sα)} (4)
> 
> ΔG_{Fe}^{γ→(Sα)} = 20853.06 - 466.35T - 0.046304T^2 + 71.147T ln T (5)
> 
> ΔG_{Fe}^{γ→(Sα)} = 20853.06 - 466.35T - 0.046304T^2 + 71.147T ln T (5)
> 
> ln a_γ^S = {ln[(1 - (14 - 12exp(W_γ / RT )) \cdot x_γ^C) / (1 - x_γ^C)]} / (13 - 12exp(W_γ / RT )) (6)
> 
> ln a_γ^S = {ln[(1 - (14 - 12exp(W_γ / RT )) \cdot x_γ^C) / (1 - x_γ^C)]} / (13 - 12exp(W_γ / RT )) (6)
> 
> ```
> 
> where I(T ) is the ferrite nucleation rate per unit area at an austenite grain boundary at some temperature T , f is the area fraction of the grain boundary occupied by ferrite grains, Q is the cooling rate, K1 (=2.07 × 10^3 J^{1/2} cm^{-4}) [4] is a constant related to the nucleation site density, K2 (=6.33 × 10^{-15} J^3 mol^{-2}) [4] is a constant related to the austenite/ferrite interfacial energy, k is Boltzmann’s constant, ΔG_{γ→α}^N is the driving force of ferrite nucleation, ΔG_{γ→α}^S and ΔG_{Fe}^{γ→(Sα)} are the free energy changes of the super-element S and of Fe in S in the γ→α transformation, x_i is the mole fraction of an alloy element X_i, ΔT_i^M and ΔT_i^{NM} are two Zener parameters for the element i as listed in Table 1 [20,23], a_γ^S is the activity coefficient of the super-element S in austenite, x_γ^C is the initial mole fraction of carbon in austenite, R is gas constant, and W_γ (=1250 J/mol) [21] is a constant representing the exchange energy among carbon atoms of austenite.
> 其中，I(T) 是某温度 T 下奥氏体晶界上单位面积的铁素体形核率，f 是被铁素体晶粒占据的晶界面积分数，Q 是冷却速率，K1 (=2.07 × 10^3 J^{1/2} cm^{-4}) [4] 是与形核点密度相关的常数，K2 (=6.33 × 10^{-15} J^3 mol^{-2}) [4] 是与奥氏体/铁素体界面能相关的常数，k 是玻尔兹曼常数，ΔG_{γ→α}^N 是铁素体形核的驱动力，ΔG_{γ→α}^S 和 ΔG_{Fe}^{γ→(Sα)} 是 γ→α 转变中超元素 S 和 S 中 Fe 的自由能变化，x_i 是合金元素 X_i 的摩尔分数，ΔT_i^M 和 ΔT_i^{NM} 是元素 i 的两个 Zener 参数，如表 1 所列 [20,23]，a_γ^S 是超元素 S 在奥氏体中的活度系数，x_γ^C 是碳在奥氏体中的初始摩尔分数，R 是气体常数，W_γ (=1250 J/mol) [21] 是代表奥氏体中碳原子间交换能的常数。
> **Nucleation Rate**: 形核率，单位面积、单位时间内形成的晶核数量。
> **Boltzmann’s constant**: 玻尔兹曼常数，一个将温度与能量联系起来的物理常数。
> **Driving Force**: 驱动力，导致相变或反应发生的自由能差。
> **Free Energy**: 自由能，系统在恒温恒压下可对外做功的能量。
> **Mole Fraction**: 摩尔分数，某组分物质的量与混合物总物质的量之比。
> **Activity Coefficient**: 活度系数，表示实际溶液中某组分行为偏离理想溶液行为的系数。
> **Gas Constant**: 气体常数，理想气体状态方程中的普适常数。

> ### 2.1.3. Solute diffusion and ferrite growth
> 溶质扩散与铁素体生长
> 
> The solute diffusion and growth kinetics are described as follows:
> 溶质扩散和生长动力学描述如下：
> 
> 
> ```
> 
> ∂c_ν / ∂t = D_ν ∇^2 c_ν (7)
> 
> ∂c_ν / ∂t = D_ν ∇^2 c_ν (7)
> 
> D_γ (∂c_γ / ∂n) |_{α/γ} - D_α (∂c_α / ∂n) |_{α/γ} = v_n (c_α^{α/γ} - c_γ^{α/γ}) (8)
> 
> D_γ (∂c_γ / ∂n) |_{α/γ} - D_α (∂c_α / ∂n) |_{α/γ} = v_n (c_α^{α/γ} - c_γ^{α/γ}) (8)
> 
> ```
> 
> where t is time, c_ν is the carbon concentration in phase ν (α or γ) and D_ν is the associated diffusion coefficient, c_α^{α/γ} and c_γ^{α/γ} are the concentrations in the α and γ domains near an α/γ interface, v_n is the movement velocity of the α/γ interface along its normal n toward the γ domain.
> 其中 t 是时间，c_ν 是相 ν（α 或 γ）中的碳浓度，D_ν 是相关的扩散系数，c_α^{α/γ} 和 c_γ^{α/γ} 是 α/γ 界面附近 α 和 γ 区域内的浓度，v_n 是 α/γ 界面沿其法线 n 向 γ 区域移动的速度。
> **Diffusion Coefficient**: 扩散系数，表示物质扩散能力大小的物理量。
> 
> ## 2.2. Cellular automaton algorithm
> 元胞自动机算法
> 
> For the CA algorithm, it must preliminarily be determined that: (1) the boundary conditions of this chosen CA lattice, (2) the neighborhood of a cell, (3) the states of cells, (4) the initial configuration, (5) the nucleation rules, (6) the growth rules, and (7) the time step. In the present model, the CA lattice is chosen as a two-dimensional 200 × 200 hexagonal one, representing a 125 × 125 μm^2 sample, with the periodic boundary conditions. This kind of lattice could reduce the anisotropy caused by a CA lattice in the modeling. The neighborhood is defined as the nearest neighbors from 1 to 6 as shown in Fig. 2. Each cell in this CA lattice has a state: α, γ, or “α/γ interface”, which is a transition state between α and γ states as shown in Fig. 3 [22]. In calculations, the crystallized orientation of each cell in the CA lattice will be probabilistically established from the probable 100 orientations once “transformation” happens; each orientation is represented by an integer from 1 to 100.
> 对于 CA 算法，必须预先确定：(1) 所选 CA 点阵的边界条件，(2) 元胞的邻域，(3) 元胞的状态，(4) 初始配置，(5) 形核规则，(6) 生长规则，以及 (7) 时间步长。在本模型中，CA 点阵选择为二维 200 × 200 的六边形点阵，代表一个 125 × 125 μm^2 的样品，具有周期性边界条件。这种点阵可以减少建模中由 CA 点阵引起的各向异性。邻域定义为从 1 到 6 的最近邻，如图 2 所示。该 CA 点阵中的每个元胞都有一个状态：α、γ 或“α/γ 界面”，这是 α 和 γ 状态之间的过渡状态，如图 3 所示 [22]。在计算中，一旦“转变”发生，CA 点阵中每个元胞的结晶取向将从可能的 100 个取向中概率性地确定；每个取向由一个从 1 到 100 的整数表示。
> **Boundary Condition**: 边界条件，求解区域边界上物理量所满足的条件。
> **Periodic Boundary Condition**: 周期性边界条件，当元胞离开系统一侧时，会从相对的另一侧重新进入系统。
> **Anisotropy**: 各向异性，物质在不同方向上表现出不同物理性质的现象。
> 
> Fig. 2. One cell with its six nearest neighbors from 1 to 6. LCA is the distance between two adjacent cells.
> 图 2. 一个元胞及其六个最近邻（1到6）。LCA 是两个相邻元胞之间的距离。
> 
> Fig. 3. A schematic diagram of cells, each of them has an α, γ, or “α/γ interface” state in the CA lattice.
> 图 3. CA 点阵中元胞的示意图，每个元胞具有 α、γ 或“α/γ 界面”状态。
> 
> ### 2.2.1. Initialization of the CA lattice
> CA 点阵的初始化
> 
> By using homogeneous constant nucleation-rate recrystallization, an initial microstructure is created [24]. In the microstructure, the cells at grain boundaries become the potential nucleation sites in nucleation calculations, where the boundary cells include cells in both grains along a boundary. In the mean time, each cell is set as a γ phase with the same initial concentration as the alloy composition and the same initial temperature T_ini, a little higher than Ae3_0 (Ae3_0 is the temperature on the γ phase line of the Fe–C phase diagram just corresponding to this alloy composition).
> 通过使用均匀恒形核率再结晶，创建了一个初始微观结构 [24]。在该微观结构中，晶界处的元胞成为形核计算中的潜在形核点，其中晶界元胞包括沿晶界两侧晶粒内的元胞。同时，每个元胞被设置为 γ 相，其初始浓度与合金成分相同，初始温度 T_ini 略高于 Ae3_0（Ae3_0 是 Fe–C 相图上与该合金成分对应的 γ 相线上的温度）。
> 
> ### 2.2.2. Temperature change
> 温度变化
> 
> With the temperature being decreased at a given cooling rate, Q, the temperature change of every cell will be T→T - Q·Δt, where Δt is the time step as mentioned in Section 2.2.6.
> 随着温度以给定的冷却速率 Q 降低，每个元胞的温度变化为 T→T - Q·Δt，其中 Δt 是 2.2.6 节中提到的时间步长。
> 
> ### 2.2.3. Nucleation
> 形核
> 
> The nucleation rules are described as follows. One cell at an austenite grain boundary is undercooled once with an undercooling temperature dT = Ae3 - T. A new nucleus density, δn, in unit area at this undercooling temperature is given, as described in Section 2.1.2, by
> 形核规则描述如下。奥氏体晶界上的一个元胞一旦具有过冷度 dT = Ae3 - T 即处于过冷状态。如 2.1.2 节所述，在此过冷温度下单位面积的新晶核密度 δn 由下式给出：
> 
> 
> ```
> 
> δn = ∫_0^{dT} (I(dT) / Q) · (1 - f) d(dT) (9)
> 
> δn = ∫_0^{dT} (I(dT) / Q) · (1 - f) d(dT) (9)
> 
> ```
> 
> where f takes the following value
> 其中 f 取以下值：
> 
> 
> ```
> 
> f = { 1 - a (c^{γ*} - c) / (c^{γ*} - c^{α*}) α/γ } { 0 γ } (10)
> 
> f = { 1 - a (c^{γ*} - c) / (c^{γ*} - c^{α*}) α/γ } { 0 γ } (10)
> 
> ```
> 
> where c^{γ*} and c^{α*} are the concentrations on the austenite and ferrite phase lines of the phase diagram, respectively, at the temperature T as shown in Fig. 1. The nucleation calculations are implemented on the boundary cells, which can be “ferrite/austenite interface” cells and austenite cells. A cell at austenite grain boundaries can become ferrite due to nucleation or growth of its neighboring ferrite cells. Once such a cell becomes ferrite, it will reject a certain amount of solutes into its neighboring austenite cells. After these neighbor austenite cells received the solute from the ferrite cell, the “ferrite/austenite interface” cells will be produced, which are located between any ferrite and austenite cells. For these “ferrite/austenite” interface cells at austenite grain boundaries, their nucleation possibilities will be affected by f = ((c^{γ*} - c) / (c^{γ*} - c^{α*})). In the limit, f = 1 in Eq. (10) indicates that the boundary cell had become a ferrite cell, and it is not necessary to use Eq. (9) to calculate its nucleation probability. To an austenite cell at austenite grain boundaries, f = 0. The nucleation probability p_nuc of this cell at the austenite grain boundary with a surface area S_γ can be given by
> 其中 c^{γ*} 和 c^{α*} 分别是温度 T 下相图上奥氏体和铁素体相线上的浓度，如图 1 所示。形核计算在边界元胞上实施，这些元胞可以是“铁素体/奥氏体界面”元胞和奥氏体元胞。奥氏体晶界上的元胞可以因其邻近铁素体元胞的形核或生长而转变为铁素体。一旦这样的元胞变成铁素体，它将向邻近的奥氏体元胞排出一定量的溶质。当这些相邻的奥氏体元胞从铁素体元胞接收到溶质后，将产生位于任何铁素体和奥氏体元胞之间的“铁素体/奥氏体界面”元胞。对于奥氏体晶界上的这些“铁素体/奥氏体”界面元胞，它们的形核可能性将受到 f = ((c^{γ*} - c) / (c^{γ*} - c^{α*})) 的影响。在极限情况下，等式 (10) 中的 f = 1 表示该晶界元胞已变为铁素体元胞，无需使用等式 (9) 计算其形核概率。对于奥氏体晶界处的奥氏体元胞，f = 0。该元胞在具有表面积 S_γ 的奥氏体晶界上的形核概率 p_nuc 可由下式给出：
> 
> 
> ```
> 
> p_nuc = δn · S_γ. (11)
> 
> p_nuc = δn · S_γ. (11)
> 
> ```
> 
> In calculations, p_nuc of one cell may be sometimes larger than 1.0, and it means that the nucleation event of this cell will definitely take place. If so, p_nuc = 1. For each cell at the austenite grain boundaries, a random number r_s (0 ≤ r_s ≤ 1) representing the probable nucleation probability of ferrite is generated in advance. If r_s ≤ p_nuc, this cell will nucleate and precipitate a certain amount of solutes c_precipitate (= c - c^{α*}) into its neighboring austenite cells. The solute excess c_precipitate is distributed to all the neighboring austenite cells when the austenite cells transform into ferrite ones. But every neighboring austenite cell receives only a part of the solute excess, c_precipitate / n_ei, where n_ei is the number of the neighboring austenite cells.
> 在计算中，某个元胞的 p_nuc 有时可能大于 1.0，这意味着该元胞的形核事件肯定会发生。如果是这样，则 p_nuc = 1。对于奥氏体晶界上的每个元胞，预先产生一个代表铁素体可能形核概率的随机数 r_s (0 ≤ r_s ≤ 1)。如果 r_s ≤ p_nuc，该元胞将形核并向其邻近的奥氏体元胞中析出一定量的溶质 c_precipitate (= c - c^{α*})。当奥氏体元胞转变为铁素体元胞时，多余的溶质 c_precipitate 被分配到所有邻近的奥氏体元胞中。但每个邻近的奥氏体元胞只接收多余溶质的一部分，即 c_precipitate / n_ei，其中 n_ei 是邻近奥氏体元胞的数量。
> 
> ### 2.2.4. Identification of cell states
> 元胞状态的识别
> 
> In the modeling, at any time step t, the state of each cell should be identified by its carbon concentration, temperature and the state of its neighbor cells. The state of a cell determines whether or not the transformation from austenite to ferrite (nucleation or growth of its neighbor ferrite cells) of this cell will take place.
> 在建模中，在任何时间步 t，每个元胞的状态应通过其碳浓度、温度及其邻居元胞的状态来识别。元胞的状态决定了该元胞是否会发生奥氏体向铁素体的转变（其邻近铁素体元胞的形核或生长）。
> 
> ### 2.2.5. Growth of ferrite
> 铁素体的生长
> 
> The growth rules are described as follows: (1) growth only happens on an “α/γ interface” cell, which is assumed to be located between ferrite and austenite cells; (2) this “α/γ interface” cell should be in undercooling; (3) the capturing probability r_tra of this “α/γ interface” cell by that growing ferrite cell is less than or equal to the “capture” probability p_cap, which can be calculated by
> 生长规则描述如下：(1) 生长仅发生在“α/γ 界面”元胞上，该元胞被认为位于铁素体和奥氏体元胞之间；(2) 该“α/γ 界面”元胞应处于过冷状态；(3) 该“α/γ 界面”元胞被正在生长的铁素体元胞捕获的概率 r_tra 应小于或等于“捕获”概率 p_cap，p_cap 可由下式计算：
> 
> 
> ```
> 
> p_cap = l(t) / L_CA (12)
> 
> p_cap = l(t) / L_CA (12)
> 
> l(t) = ∫_{t_0}^t v dt (13)
> 
> l(t) = ∫_{t_0}^t v dt (13)
> 
> ```
> 
> where t_0 is the time “finding” a neighboring ferrite cell to be just transformed from austenite, v is the growing velocity, which could be obtained by solving Eqs. (7) and (8). The detailed calculation procedures, of solving the diffusion equations within each phase using an explicit finite volume technique formulated for a regular hexagonal grid and accounting for the ferrite/austenite interface movement using special interface elements, could be seen in Ref. [22]. L_CA is the distance between two adjacent cells as shown in Fig. 2. r_tra can be obtained with the same procedure of r_s as described in Section 2.2.3. Once an “α/γ interface” cell is “captured” by the growing ferrite cell to become a ferrite cell, some new “α/γ interface” cells neighboring upon the “captured” cell will be produced.
> 其中 t_0 是“发现”一个邻近铁素体元胞刚从奥氏体转变而来的时间，v 是生长速度，可通过求解方程 (7) 和 (8) 获得。关于使用为规则六边形网格制定的显式有限体积法求解每个相内的扩散方程，并使用特殊的界面单元处理铁素体/奥氏体界面移动的详细计算过程，可见参考文献 [22]。L_CA 是两个相邻元胞之间的距离，如图 2 所示。r_tra 可以通过与 2.2.3 节中描述的 r_s 相同的过程获得。一旦一个“α/γ 界面”元胞被生长的铁素体元胞“捕获”而成为铁素体元胞，就会在“被捕获”元胞附近产生一些新的“α/γ 界面”元胞。
> **Finite Volume Technique**: 有限体积法，一种数值解法，通过将计算域划分为控制体积来求解偏微分方程。
> 
> ### 2.2.6. Calculation of time step
> 时间步长的计算
> 
> The time step Δt is a key coefficient to obtain correct results in this modeling, which can be given by
> 时间步长 Δt 是本建模中获得正确结果的关键系数，可由下式给出：
> 
> 
> ```
> 
> Δt = min (L_CA / v_max, L_CA^2 / D_α, L_CA^2 / D_γ) (14)
> 
> Δt = min (L_CA / v_max, L_CA^2 / D_α, L_CA^2 / D_γ) (14)
> 
> ```
> 
> where v_max is the maximum growth velocity of ferrite cells and “min” means the minimum value among the three values in the round bracket.
> 其中 v_max 是铁素体元胞的最大生长速度，“min”表示圆括号内三个值中的最小值。
> 
> ### 2.2.7. Cementite formation
> 渗碳体的形成
> 
> With the temperature decreasing, more number of ferrite cells form, and carbon solutes will build up in those of “α/γ interface” cells in front of these growing ferrite grains, and make them become undercooled. If they could not be promptly diffused out, they would be helpful to the cementite formation. The formed cementite cells will become obstacles to the growth of ferrite.
> 随着温度降低，形成的铁素体元胞数量增多，碳溶质将在这些生长中的铁素体晶粒前方的“α/γ 界面”元胞中积累，并使它们过冷。如果它们不能及时扩散出去，将有助于渗碳体的形成。形成的渗碳体元胞将成为铁素体生长的障碍。
> **Cementite**: 渗碳体，铁和碳的化合物，化学式为Fe₃C，是一种硬而脆的相。

> # 3. Results and discussion
> 结果与讨论
> 
> Fig. 4(a) shows the simulated initial microstructure of steel A36 (C0.17, Mn0.74, Si0.012, Cu0.016, Ni0.01 and Cr0.019) with an initial austenite grain size d_γ = 18 μm, and Fig. 4(b) its final microstructure after the transformation from austenite to ferrite due to a sufficiently high cooling rate [6]. In these simulated figures, different orientation grains are represented by different colors, which are replaced by different gray scales in black and white pictures, and cementite is represented by dark gray as shown in Fig. 4(b). Fig. 5 shows the calculated variation of ferrite grain size as a function of the cooling rate in the A36 steel. In this figure, ferrite grain size d_α is calculated by d_α = Σ_{i=1}^{N_α} d_i^α / N_α (N_α is the total number of ferrite grains in calculation, and d_i^α is the size of the ith ferrite grain). This figure clarifies that a larger cooling rate is helpful for obtaining finer ferrite grains, and quantitatively tells us how to control grain size by cooling, which coincides with metallurgical experience but is physically easy to understand and explicable through this modeling. It is well known from metallurgical experience that a useful way to obtain fine ferrite grains is to increase the nucleation number and prevent growth of ferrite grains. In this CA model, these two events of nucleation and growth are represented by two probabilities (nucleation probability and “capture” probability as described in Section 2.2). If the initial microstructure of austenite is given, one efficient way for advancing nucleation is to increase the nucleation probability of each cell at austenite grain boundaries. At large cooling rates, there exist large undercooling temperatures of those cells at the austenite grain boundaries. Then, their nucleation probabilities will be high. In other words, the average nucleation number, M, of ferrite grains per austenite grain is increased. Fig. 6 gives a comparison between the calculated and experimental [6] average numbers of ferrite grains per austenite grain as a function of the cooling rate, where M is calculated with a ratio of the ferrite grain number N_α to the austenite one N_γ, M = N_α/N_γ. The good agreement between the calculated and experimental data shows sufficient evidence to support that the basic assumptions in Section 2.1.1 are reasonable and this CA modeling based on them is reliable. Fig. 7 details the dependence of the nucleation cell number, n_nuc, of ferrite on the undercooling temperature ΔT (=Ae3_0 – T) at various cooling rates (11, 41, and 61 °C s⁻¹). The characteristics of these curves are that each curve undergoes a rapid increase of n_nuc with ΔT in zone I, and becomes saturated in zone II. Nevertheless, the saturated value at a lower cooling rate is less than a quicker one, and this value is definite for a given cooling rate (n_nuc = 561 for 11 °C s⁻¹, 1352 for 41 °C s⁻¹ and 1590 for 61 °C s⁻¹). Physically, it means that the final nucleation number is a function of the cooling rate, and the nucleation procedure has already finished in zone I at the early stage of undercooling. The dependence of the ferrite fraction transformed Y(%) on the undercooling temperature ΔT, is shown in Fig. 8. Here Y = (n_nuc + n_grow) / n_cell, where n_grow is the increased ferrite cell number from growth and n_cell is the whole cell number in the CA lattice (in the beginning, the initial austenite cell number). In this figure, the saturated values are almost the same except a slightly less value for higher cooling rates due to the appearance of cementite. The combination of Fig. 7 with Fig. 8 indicates that the lesser the cooling rate, the larger the final average size of ferrite grains. It can be easily understood from this cellular automaton modeling. The lower nucleation number at a lower cooling rate implies that more “α/γ interface” cells, on average, would share the precipitated carbon solutes from a ferrite grain formed by nucleation or growth. It leads to the lower concentration difference of carbon between transformed and untransformed parts in a simulated sample, and on the other hand, the lower cooling rate makes the diffusion of carbon between these two parts progress sufficiently, which is helpful for the equiaxed growth of the ferrite nuclei. Fig. 9 shows the time spent for finishing the transformation from austenite to ferrite for these different cooling rates, indicating that a higher cooling rate makes this transformation complete in a shorter time. In this transformation, there exists competition between nucleation and the growth of ferrite. The competition between nucleation and growth can be seen in Fig. 10, which shows a relation of the ferrite nucleation fraction, Y_nuc(=n_nuc/ (n_nuc+n_grow)), with ΔT. Comparing with the curve at the cooling rate 11 °C s⁻¹, it can be easily found that the severe oscillation of the curve in the zone less than ΔT = 95 °C appears at the cooling rate of 61 °C s⁻¹. This reveals that at the beginning of this transformation, the competition is drastic at a large cooling rate. The higher value of Y_nuc at this zone indicates that in this case, the nucleation procedure is mainly responsible for this transformation from austenite to ferrite. Fig. 11(a),(b) [6] provides a visual comparison between experimental and simulated micrographies at the same cooling rate 1 °C s⁻¹, which are similar. In Fig. 11(b), some cementite regions have been marked by white squares.

> # 4. Conclusion
> 结论
> 
> We have employed a two-dimensional cellular automaton model to investigate the transformation from austenite to ferrite in low carbon steels during continuous cooling. Based on this model, we quantitatively demonstrate how the microstructural evolution depends dynamically on the combined behavior of nucleation and the growth of ferrite in this transformation. Moreover, the way the cooling conditions affect the competition between nucleation and growth of ferrite is also quantified. From the simulated results, it can be found that as a function of the cooling rate, the final nucleation number in this transformation is definite for a given rate, and the nucleation process has already finished at an earlier stage of undercooling. At this stage, the competition between nucleation and growth is more drastic at a larger cooling rate for which the nucleation process is mainly responsible for this transformation. Shapes and sizes of the ferrite grains are controled by the solute diffusion in their growing processes. By these simulations, we believe that this modeling based on CA algorithm could be of great help to effectively control this transformation from austenite to ferrite in low carbon steels.
> 我们采用了一个二维元胞自动机模型来研究连续冷却过程中低碳钢从奥氏体到铁素体的转变。基于此模型，我们定量地展示了微观结构演变如何动态地依赖于该转变中铁素体形核和生长的联合行为。此外，冷却条件影响铁素体形核与生长之间竞争的方式也得到了量化。从模拟结果可以发现，作为冷却速率的函数，该转变中的最终形核数对于给定的速率是确定的，并且形核过程在过冷的早期阶段就已经完成。在此阶段，形核与生长之间的竞争在更大冷却速率下更为剧烈，此时形核过程主要负责该转变。铁素体晶粒的形状和尺寸在其生长过程中受溶质扩散控制。通过这些模拟，我们相信这种基于 CA 算法的建模对于有效控制低碳钢中从奥氏体到铁素体的转变可能有很大帮助。
