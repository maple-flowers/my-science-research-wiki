> ## 一、引言
> 
> ### 领域背景：从传统铁电到二维材料
> 
> **铁电性**（Ferroelectricity）是材料的一种物理特性，指材料内部存在**自发极化**（Spontaneous Polarization），并且这种极化的方向可以被外加电场**可逆翻转**。这一特性是非易失性存储器的物理基础。自1921年在罗谢尔盐中发现铁电性以来，钛酸钡（BaTiO₃）、锆钛酸铅（PZT）等传统三维铁电材料已在电容器、传感器等领域得到广泛应用。
> 
> 然而，随着微电子器件不断微型化，传统铁电薄膜面临一个根本性困境：**退极化场效应**（Depolarization Field Effect）。当薄膜厚度减薄至纳米尺度时，材料表面未补偿的束缚电荷会产生一个与极化方向相反的内部电场，导致铁电性减弱甚至完全消失。这好比一个微型磁铁越小，其磁性越容易因热扰动而消失。传统铌酸锂（LiNbO₃）等薄膜在接近几个纳米厚度时，其铁电转变温度（居里温度，**Curie Temperature, Tc**）会急剧下降，这严重阻碍了器件进一步微型化。
> 
> ### 研究问题与文献定位
> 
> 二维材料的兴起为解决这一难题提供了新思路。**二维材料**（Two-dimensional Materials）是指电子仅可在两个维度上自由运动的材料，其层内由强共价键连接，层间则由弱的**范德华力**（van der Waals, vdW）结合。这种独特的结构允许通过机械剥离获得原子级厚度的薄层。
> 
> 本文是一篇**综述性论文**（Review），并非报告一项新的实验发现，而是系统性地梳理和评述了**二维铁电材料**领域的研究前沿。文章的核心问题是：**如何在原子级厚度下实现稳定的铁电性，并探索其背后的新物理机制与应用潜力？**
> 
> 文章重点聚焦于一种颠覆传统认知的新机制——**滑移铁电性**（Sliding Ferroelectricity）。该机制不依赖于材料本身的结构极性，而是通过两层非极性材料间的相对滑移，打破对称性，从而“凭空”产生面外极化。这为设计新型超薄、低功耗铁电器件开辟了全新的材料体系。
> 
> ### 核心价值
> 
> 本文的核心价值在于为读者绘制了一幅二维铁电材料的“全景地图”，并清晰标出两条通往“原子级厚度铁电性”的主要路径：
> 
> 1.  **传统路径**：基于离子位移的**自发极化铁电体**（如CuInP₂S₆, In₂Se₃），通过材料内部离子移动来维持极化，可以看作是传统铁电体在二维极限下的延伸。
> 2.  **新兴路径**：基于层间电荷重分布的**滑移铁电体**（如hBN, TMDs, 石墨烯体系），通过范德华层间滑移诱导极化，是二维材料领域特有的全新物理机制。
> 
> 通过对这两条路径的系统梳理，文章不仅总结了当前的实验进展与应用探索，还为未来的研究指明了方向，对想要快速进入该领域的研究者具有极高的学习价值。
>

> ## 二、论文结构与逻辑概览
> 
> 本文采用了一种“总-分-总”的经典综述结构，逻辑脉络清晰，可概括为以下核心论证链条：

> 
> **2. 介绍主流路径（第1节）**：首先介绍相对传统的“自发极化二维铁电体”，它们继承了离子位移的经典机制，并通过实验验证了其在单层极限下的室温铁电性。这部分内容为后续引出革命性的“滑移铁电”建立了参照系。
> 
> **3. 引出革命性范式（第2节）**：这是文章的核心。作者在此提出“滑移铁电”这一全新范式，其核心机制不再是离子位移，而是层间滑移导致的对称性破缺和电荷转移。该部分按材料体系（hBN、TMDs、石墨烯）详细阐述了其理论预测和实验验证。
> 
> **4. 展示应用前景（第3节）**：基于上述物理机制，探讨如何调控和利用滑移铁电。内容包括： * **畴工程**：如何通过控制极化畴的结构来优化器件性能。 * **器件应用**：分别在非易失性存储器、光电异质结、自发光伏器件等领域的应用实例。 * **规模化制备**：大面积高质量材料生长的最新进展。 * **前沿探索**：铁电性与拓扑绝缘体结合的新概念。

> **5. 总结与展望（第4节）**：概括领域进展，指出当前挑战（如高温稳定性、规模化制备），并预测未来三大发展方向（理论机制、新材料、应用集成）。

> ## 三、所有图表深度解析
> 
> 本文图表丰富，是理解核心概念的关键。以下对所有图表进行深度解析：
> 
> ### 图1：自发极化材料CuInP₂S₆和In₂Se₃
> 
> -   **(a) CuInP₂S₆原子结构示意图**
>     
>     -   **描述**: 展示了CIPS的层状结构，由硫(S)八面体骨架，内嵌铜(Cu¹⁺)、铟(In³⁺)离子和磷-磷(P-P)原子对，层间由范德华力连接。
>     -   **解读**: 这是理解其铁电性的结构基础。Cu¹⁺离子在S八面体笼中，其位置决定了极化状态。
> -   **(b) CuInP₂S₆四重势阱模型**
>     
>     -   **描述**: 一个示意图，展示了Cu¹⁺离子在硫配位场中有四个能量最低的位置（四重势阱），分别标记为Cu1, Cu1′, Cu3等。
>     -   **解读**: 这是CIPS铁电切换的微观机理。极化翻转对应Cu¹⁺离子在这四个势阱间的跃迁，就像小球在多谷地形的不同山谷间移动。
> -   **(c) 极化切换时Cu¹⁺的移动模拟**
>     
>     -   **描述**: 展示了从低极化态(LP)到高极化态(HP)的转变过程中，Cu¹⁺从一个位置(Cu1)迁移到另一个非等效位置(Cu3)，并触发In³⁺反向位移。
>     -   **解读**: 直观地说明了极化态切换是一个离子协同运动的过程，不仅是Cu¹⁺的移动，还伴随着In³⁺的响应。
> -   **(d) CIPS的PFM相位图**
>     
>     -   **描述**: 展示了厚度为30 nm的CIPS薄片在反向直流偏压下写入的“盒中盒”图案。比例尺为1 μm。
>     -   **解读**: **压电力显微镜**（**Piezoresponse Force Microscope, PFM**）是表征铁电性的核心工具。亮暗不同的区域代表极化方向相反的**铁电畴**（Ferroelectric Domain）。这个“盒中盒”图案是人为翻转局域极化形成的，直接证明了CIPS在室温下的铁电翻转能力。
> -   **(e) CIPS的PFM振幅和相位迟滞回线**
>     
>     -   **描述**: 对应(d)图区域的PFM相位（Phase）和振幅（Amplitude）随电压变化的曲线。相位曲线呈现典型的180度翻转，振幅曲线呈现典型的“蝴蝶”形状。
>     -   **解读**: **电滞回线**（Hysteresis Loop）是铁电性的“指纹”。相位回线表明极化在电压驱动下发生了可逆翻转，而振幅蝴蝶曲线表明应变（振幅）与极化方向无关，只与极化强度大小有关。两者结合，是确认铁电性的金标准。
> -   **(f) In₂Se₃的原子构型及其极化切换模型**
>     
>     -   **描述**: 展示了α-In₂Se₃的五层原子结构（Se-In-Se-In-Se），中间Se原子的上下移动可同时翻转面内和面外极化，形成“偶极锁定”效应。
>     -   **解读**: 这是α-In₂Se₃铁电性的核心机制。“偶极锁定”意味着其面内和面外极化是强耦合的，一者翻转，另一者必然翻转，这为多功能器件设计提供了可能。
> -   **(g) 单层In₂Se₃的PFM形貌图及其压电响应回滞**
>     
>     -   **描述**: 左图为单层α-In₂Se₃的原子力显微镜形貌图。右图为其压电响应的相位和振幅回滞曲线。
>     -   **解读**: 本图是单层极限下室温铁电性的直接证据。它证明了α-In₂Se₃在只有一个原子层厚度时，仍具有可被电场翻转的稳定铁电性。
> -   **(h) β′和β″相In₂Se₃的层内极化示意图**
>     
>     -   **描述**: 展示了两种不同晶相（β′反铁电相和β″铁电相）的原子排列和层内极化方向（用箭头表示）。
>     -   **解读**: 说明了In₂Se₃体系的多晶相特征，不同的相具有不同的极化行为，β′相中相邻行的极化方向相反导致宏观无极化（反铁电），而β″相则为同向排列（铁电）。
> 
> ### 图2：自发极化材料BP-Bi和d1T-MoTe₂
> 
> -   **(a) 单层BP-Bi的晶格结构示意图**

> ### 图3：hBN滑移铁电的实验验证
> 
> -   **(a) 六种高对称堆叠配置的hBN–hBN界面示意图**
>     
>     -   **描述**: 展示了AA′, AB, BA等六种可能的堆叠方式。B和N原子分别用不同颜色表示。
>     -   **解读**: 这是理解滑移铁电的基础。在AA′堆叠中，B原子在N原子正上方，偶极子反平行抵消，无宏观极化。而在AB/BA堆叠中，层间滑移导致B和N原子错位，形成极性界面。
> -   **(b) 转角hBN的dc-EFM图像**
>     
>     -   **描述**: **静电力显微镜**（**Electrostatic Force Microscope, EFM**）相位图，展现出大面积三角形周期性电势调制。
>     -   **解读**: 明暗交替的三角形区域就是**铁电畴**（Ferroelectric domains），分别对应AB和BA堆叠区，其界面电势差约200 mV。这直接证实了滑移铁电形成的周期性极化场。
> -   **(c) 石墨烯/p(t)-hBN器件结构示意图**
>     
>     -   **描述**: 展示了用于电输运测量的器件结构，石墨烯夹在hBN和栅极之间。
>     -   **解读**: 这是一种巧妙的间接探测方法。石墨烯的电阻对外部电场极其敏感，当hBN的铁电极化翻转时，产生的电场会掺杂石墨烯，从而改变其电阻，通过测量电阻变化即可“读出”极化状态。
> -   **(d) t-hBN器件的电阻相图**
>     
>     -   **描述**: 展示了t-hBN器件中，石墨烯电阻随顶栅和背栅电压变化的二维彩色图，电阻峰呈双峰渐进式演化。
>     -   **解读**: 这种双峰渐进式演化是**多畴结构**（multi-domain）的特征。与单畴的陡峭翻转不同，多畴翻转通过畴壁移动逐步进行，这为多阻态存储提供了物理基础。
> -   **(e) p(t)-hBN的电滞回线**
>     
>     -   **描述**: 对比了平行堆叠(p-hBN)和转角堆叠(t-hBN)器件的电阻-电压回滞曲线。
>     -   **解读**: p-hBN显示出陡峭的矩形回线，代表单畴的快速翻转，适合非易失性存储器；t-hBN则显示出渐进式回线，代表多畴的逐步翻转，适合低功耗可编程器件。
> -   **(f) 平行排列t-hBN中SP区域的晶格构型**
>     
>     -   **描述**: 展示了AB/BA畴壁过渡区域（SP区）的原子排列，暗示了面内偶极子的存在。
>     -   **解读**: 这是面内滑移铁电性的理论猜想来源。在AB和BA畴的交界处，原子排列方式可能产生净的面内偶极矩。
> -   **(g) t-hBN中不同区域面内极化强度的理论计算**
>     
>     -   **描述**: 展示了通过第一性原理计算得到的，AB/BA畴和畴壁处面内极化强度的大小和方向。
>     -   **解读**: 理论计算结果证实了(f)图的猜想，即畴壁处确实存在面内极化，使得滑移铁电体系不仅能产生面外极化，还能产生面内极化。
> -   **(h) t-hBN中面内铁电性的空间分布**
>     
>     -   **描述**: 理论模拟图，展示了面内极化在空间的矢量分布，形成了一个复杂的拓扑结构（如半子/斯格明子网络）。
>     -   **解读**: 揭示了滑移铁电畴壁不仅是极化方向的过渡区，其本身还是一个具有丰富物理性质的拓扑极化结构。

> ## 四、正文核心内容剖析
> 
> ### 1. 自发极化的二维铁电材料
> 
> 这部分内容作为“前菜”，介绍了继承传统铁电物理的图像。
> 
> -   **核心机制**：**离子位移**（Ionic Displacement）。即在晶格中，阳离子和阴离子发生相对位移，导致正负电荷中心不重合，产生电偶极矩。
> -   **关键挑战**：如何在二维极限下克服退极化场。
> -   **典型材料**：
>     -   **CuInP₂S₆ (CIPS)**：铁电性源于Cu¹⁺离子在S八面体笼中的多势阱跃迁。这是一种离子导体和铁电体的复合体。其关键优势在于Cu¹⁺的迁移率和可调的极化态（LP/HP），但这也可能带来稳定性问题。
>     -   **α-In₂Se₃**：铁电性源于中间Se原子的双稳态移动，并具有独特的“**偶极锁定**”（Dipole Locking）效应，即面内和面外极化强耦合，一者翻转，另一者必然翻转。这为设计多功能器件提供了可能，但其面内铁电性的起源与晶体对称性理论的矛盾（诺伊曼原理）也引发了深入讨论，并催生了“分数量子铁电性”的新概念。
>     -   **BP-Bi**：作为首个单元素二维铁电体，其铁电性源于sp³-sp²杂化诱导的晶格翘曲（Buckling），打破了空间反演对称性。其模型非常简洁，极化强度与翘曲程度线性相关。
>     -   **d1T-MoTe₂**：铁电性源于复杂的晶格畸变（K3模式的Mo原子三聚化和Γ₂模式的Te原子面外位移），其极化翻转路径仍存在争议，反映出该体系的复杂性。
> 
> ### 2. vdW滑移铁电
> 
> 这是文章的核心和灵魂，代表了二维铁电研究的新范式。
> 
> -   **核心机制**：**层间电荷重分布**（Interlayer Charge Redistribution）。两层非极性材料通过层间滑移，改变原子堆叠方式，打破空间反演对称性，导致电子在层间界面处重新分布，产生净的面外偶极矩。这完全颠覆了传统铁电体的离子位移模型。
> -   **类比理解**：想象两块完全相同的透明塑料板，上面印有规则的六边形网格。当它们完全对准时，整体是对称的。但当你将其中一块板相对于另一块滑动一个微小距离时，从特定角度看，网格的交错会产生一种方向感，就像产生了“极性”。滑移铁电性与此类似，是层间原子“错位”导致的电子云不对称分布。
> -   **理论工具**：
>     -   **贝里相位法**（Berry Phase Method）：用于精确计算材料的宏观极化强度。
>     -   **弹性带方法**（Nudged Elastic Band, NEB）：用于计算极化翻转所需的最小能量路径和能垒。
>     -   **差分电荷密度图**（Differential Charge Density Map）：用于可视化和量化层间滑移导致的电荷转移。
> -   **关键优势**：
>     -   **普适性**：母体材料无需本征极性，大大扩展了候选材料范围。
>     -   **超低能垒**：极化翻转仅需克服层间滑移能垒，远低于离子位移的能垒，意味着超低功耗。
>     -   **高速切换**：层间滑移过程本身可以非常快，有望实现超快存储。
> -   **典型体系**：
>     -   **hBN**：滑移铁电的“明星”体系。通过平行堆叠（p-hBN）或微小转角堆叠（t-hBN），可形成AB/BA铁电畴。其面外极化已被PFM和输运实验完美证实，并且还发现了畴壁处的面内极化。
>     -   **TMDs（如WTe₂, MoS₂, WSe₂）**：Td-WTe₂是首个被实验证实的滑移铁电体，其金属性赋予了它独特的“铁电金属”特性。而2H-TMDs通过R堆叠也可实现铁电性，其半导体属性使其在光电器件中更有优势。通过控制层数，可在3R堆叠的TMDs中实现多极化态，实现多态存储。
>     -   **石墨烯体系**：最令人惊讶的发现。在自身无极性、无电负性差异的双层石墨烯中，通过与BN的莫尔超晶格和电子关联效应耦合，也能产生层间铁电性。其极化强度巨大，来源复杂，甚至可能涉及BN封装的界面效应，是目前研究的热点和前沿。
> 
> ### 3. 调控与应用
> 
> 这部分将基础物理机制与实用技术联系起来。
> 
> -   **畴工程**（Domain Engineering）：这是调控滑移铁电性能的核心手段。文章通过四种畴结构模型，清晰地展示了畴壁形态如何决定极化翻转动力学和电滞回线形状。这为像设计电路一样设计极化翻转行为提供了可能。
> -   **非易失性存储**（Nonvolatile Memory）：滑移铁电体在存储器领域展现出巨大潜力，表现出超快速度（1 ns）、超强耐久性（10¹¹次循环）、高抗疲劳性（10⁶次循环无衰减）和潜在的多态存储能力，性能指标全面对标甚至超越现有技术。
> -   **光电异质结**（Optoelectronic Heterojunctions）：利用铁电畴的周期性势场，可以像“光栅”一样调制功能层（如WSe₂）的激子态、发光和输运行为，为可重构光电器件、激子激光器等提供新思路。
> -   **自发光伏效应**（Spontaneous Photovoltaic Effect）：利用铁电材料的内建电场分离光生载流子，无需外置偏压，即可产生光电流。该效应在3R-MoS₂等体系中实现了高外部量子效率（16%）和超快响应速度（2 ps），为新型高效光能转换器件提供了新路径。
> -   **规模化制备**（Scalable Preparation）：任何材料的应用都离不开高质量制备。近期在rBN（厘米级）和3R-TMDs（晶圆级）单晶薄膜生长上的突破，为二维铁电材料从实验室走向工厂扫清了关键障碍。
> -   **铁电拓扑绝缘体**（Ferroelectric Topological Insulator, FETI）：这是一个极具前瞻性的概念。将铁电性与拓扑绝缘体结合，实现用电场非易失性地调控拓扑量子态（如量子反常霍尔效应），为开发低功耗、高速的拓扑电子学器件开辟了新方向。

> ## 七、学术思考与批判性分析
> 
> 1.  **石墨烯铁电的起源争议**：文章多次提到石墨烯体系中的铁电性起源尚不明确（电子关联 vs. 晶格滑移 vs. BN封装效应）。一个更深层次的问题是：我们如何定义一个材料的“铁电性”？如果极化完全由邻近的BN封装层诱导，那么这究竟是石墨烯的铁电性，还是BN铁电性通过石墨烯的一种“显影”？这涉及到对铁电性定义在复合体系中的边界问题，值得深入思考。
>     
> 2.  **“滑移”与“疲劳”的终极矛盾**：滑移铁电的一大优势是超高抗疲劳性，因为不涉及离子迁移。然而，宏观上可观测的极化翻转，必然伴随着数亿个原子层间的相对滑移。这种毫秒、纳秒级的高速、反复滑移，在微观上是否会引入新的失效模式，如层间磨损、界面褶皱或不可逆的层间错位？这种机械-电学耦合的长期可靠性，可能是一个被忽视的“陷阱”。
>     
> 3.  **表征手段的局限性**：目前确认滑移铁电的主要手段是PFM和电输运测量。PFM测量的是表面的压电响应，而非极化本身，容易受到静电、形貌等其他因素的干扰。电输运测量是间接证明，依赖复杂的界面效应。是否存在一种方法，能直接、定量地“看见”层间那极其微弱的电荷转移量，并将它与宏观极化严格对应起来？这仍是实验上的重大挑战。
>     
> 4.  **从“有意思”到“有用”的鸿沟**：文章展示了大量令人兴奋的物理现象和原型器件，但许多关键性能指标（如10¹¹次循环寿命）是在实验室条件下，针对特定微米级器件取得的。如何保证在晶圆级制备中，所有器件的畴结构、畴壁密度、界面质量都能高度均一且可控？从实验室的“杰出个例”走向工业界的“均一产品”，这条鸿沟可能比想象中更大。
>     
> 5.  **“铁电-拓扑”耦合的强耦合判据**：文章最后提到了铁电拓扑绝缘体，并展示了极化可开关拓扑态的现象。但这是否是真正的“强耦合”？即，铁电翻转是否直接、必然地改变了拓扑能带结构（如陈数的改变），还是仅仅通过改变费米能级位置，在不同的拓扑平庸带之间切换？厘清这一点，对于判断FETI概念是否具有革命性意义至关重要。
>

> ## 八、用户问题预测与解答
> 
> 1.  **问题：滑移铁电和传统铁电体，比如我们常见的压电陶瓷，最根本的区别是什么？** **解答**：最根本的区别在于**极化翻转的微观机制**。传统铁电体（如压电陶瓷PZT）的极化翻转，是晶格内部**阳离子和阴离子在外电场下发生相对位移**，导致正负电荷中心重新定位。这就像一群人从一个房间走到另一个房间。而滑移铁电的极化翻转，是**两层完整的原子层整体发生相对滑移**，改变了层间界面的原子堆叠方式，导致电子云在界面处重新分布，从而产生或翻转极化。这就像两张透明的、印有图案的纸，你移动其中一张，原本重叠的图案发生错位，产生了新的纹理。因此，滑移铁电不要求材料本身有极性，且翻转能垒通常很低，因为它是整个层的“滑动”，而不是单个原子克服晶格阻力去“跳跃”。
>     
> 2.  **问题：文章中反复提到的“铁电畴”到底是什么？为什么它有时候是三角形的？** **解答**：**铁电畴**（Ferroelectric Domain）是指材料内部极化方向一致的区域。你可以把它想象成一块由无数微小的磁铁（这里是电偶极子）组成的材料，但所有微小的“电”磁铁并不都指向同一个方向。它们会分成许多小区域，每个区域内所有“电”磁铁方向相同，这个区域就是一个“畴”。不同畴的极化方向不同，它们之间的边界就是“畴壁”。在转角hBN或TMDs中，畴呈三角形，这是由材料本身的晶体结构决定的。hBN和TMDs的晶格是六方对称的。当两层原子以微小转角堆叠时，由于晶格失配，原子排列会自发弛豫，形成一种周期性结构，叫做**莫尔超晶格**（Moiré Superlattice）。在这个超晶格中，能量最低、最稳定的堆叠方式只有AB和BA两种，它们都是极性的，且极化方向相反。在六方对称性下，这两种堆叠区域会自然地形成正三角形和倒三角形交替排列的图案，就像许多六边形瓷砖拼出的三角形图案一样。
>

> **1. 问：滑移铁电和传统铁电的根本区别是什么？** **答**：核心区别在于**极化起源**。传统铁电体（如BaTiO₃）的极化源于晶胞内正负离子的相对位移，发生在化学键层面。而滑移铁电体的极化源于**范德华层间的整体滑移**，导致界面电荷重新分布，产生宏观极化。它不要求材料本身具有极性，仅通过调控层间堆垛方式即可实现，能垒通常极低。

> 摘要 二维铁电材料展现出区别于传统铁电体的属性——显著减弱的退极化场效应, 这为器件微型化与功能集成 提供了新机遇. 基于自极化机制的二维铁电材料已被实验证实, 并成功实现了单层极限的稳定室温铁电性, 其铁电 起源与传统铁电体的离子位移模型一脉相承. 更有意思的是, 基于二维材料的新型滑移铁电体突破了这一理论框 架: 它不依赖母体的本征极化, 仅需通过调控范德华(van der Waals, vdW)异质结的层间滑移矢量, 即可在六方氮化 硼、过渡金属硫族化合物等非自极化材料中诱导稳定的宏观极化. 这种面外极化源于层间电荷再分布, 其超低能 量势垒赋予极化方向非易失性翻转能力, 在超高密度存储器、光电器件等领域展现出独特应用优势. 本文系统梳 理二维铁电材料的研究进展, 着重阐释各类滑移铁电体的构效机制与实验表征, 探讨栅压调控、光电响应等应用 前景的实验探索, 最后展望该领域在机制研究、动态响应、工业化制备等方面面临的挑战. Abstract Two-dimensional (2D) ferroelectric materials have emerged as a revolutionary platform for next-generation nanoelectronics, offering unique properties distinct from traditional ferroelectrics. A key advantage lies in their significantly weakened depolarization field effects, enabling stable room-temperature ferroelectricity even at the monolayer limit. Conventional 2D ferroelectrics, such as CuInP2S6 and In2Se3, inherit the ionic displacement mechanism of their bulk counterparts, where spontaneous polarization arises from symmetry-breaking lattice distortions. Recent breakthroughs, however, have introduced a paradigm-shifting concept: sliding ferroelectricity. Unlike conventional models, this mechanism does not rely on intrinsic polarization of the parent material but exploits interlayer sliding in van der Waals (vdW) heterostructures (e.g., hexagonal boron nitride (hBN) and transition metal dichalcogenides (TMDs)) to induce macroscopic out-of-plane polarization via charge redistribution. The ultralow energy barrier for polarization switching in sliding ferroelectrics enables nonvolatile memory and low-power logic devices, while their compatibility with diverse material systems (conductors, semiconductors, insulators) expands possibilities for band engineering and multifunctional integration. Experimental advances in sliding ferroelectrics have validated their theoretical foundations. In twisted hBN heterostructures, electrostatic force microscopy and transport measurements revealed triangular ferroelectric domains with switchable polarization states, governed by interlayer sliding between AB/BA stacking configurations. Similarly, 2H-phase TMDs (e.g., MoS2, WSe2) exhibit sliding-induced polarization when reconfigured into rhombohedral (3R) stacking, as demonstrated by piezoresponse force microscopy and hysteretic transport behavior. Remarkably, even nonpolar systems like Bernal-stacked bilayer graphene manifest unconventional ferroelectricity under moiré potentials, driven by correlated electron interactions and interlayer charge transfer. Theoretical frameworks, including Berry phase polarization calculations and nudged elastic band simulations, have quantitatively linked sliding-induced symmetry breaking to macroscopic polarization, while differential charge density analysis elucidates the role of interfacial charge redistribution. These discoveries highlight the versatility of sliding ferroelectrics, offering routes to tailor polarization via stacking angles, layer numbers, and heterostructure design. The application potential of 2D ferroelectrics spans nonvolatile memory, optoelectronics, and neuromorphic computing. Prototype devices, such as sliding ferroelectric-based memories, achieve ultrafast switching speeds (~1 ns) and endurance exceeding 1011 cycles. In optoelectronic heterostructures, ferroelectric polarization modulates exciton localization and photovoltaic responses, enabling gate-tunable photodetectors with high quantum efficiency. Challenges remain, however, in understanding dynamic polarization switching under thermal/mechanical stress, scaling up high-quality material synthesis, and integrating 2D ferroelectrics with silicon-based technologies. Future research directions include exploring the coupling between ferroelectricity and topological states, engineering multistable polarization configurations for neuromorphic applications, and developing wafer-scale growth techniques for industrial adoption. By addressing these challenges, 2D ferroelectrics may redefine the frontiers of low-power electronics and adaptive functional materials. **二维铁电材料**: Two-dimensional ferroelectric materials **退极化场**: Depolarization field **滑移铁电体**: Sliding ferroelectrics **范德华异质结**: Van der Waals (vdW) heterostructures **六方氮化硼**: Hexagonal boron nitride (hBN) **过渡金属硫族化合物**: Transition metal dichalcogenides (TMDs)

> 铁电性定义为材料中自发极化可被外电场可逆翻转的特性. 这一现象的发现可追溯至1921年, Valasek在罗谢尔盐(KNaC4H4O6·4H2O)中观察到特征电滞回线[1], 标志着铁电物理研究的开端. 随后, 体系研究的扩展(如BaTiO3[2]、PbTiO3[3,4]、LiNbO3[5]等)与Landau-Ginzburg-Devonshire等铁电理论的发展, 共同奠定了铁电材料在非易失性存储器、电容器及光电传感器等领域的重要地位. 从晶体学视角, 铁电性产生的必要条件包括非中心对称点群与可反转的对称操作, 这同时赋予材料热释电和压电性能. 随着微电子技术发展对器件微型化的需求, 铁电薄膜厚度减薄所引发的退极化场问题日益凸显[6]: 当厚度低于临界尺寸时(通常在纳米量级), 表面束缚电荷产生的退极化场会导致铁电性完全消失[7]. 典型例证包括Fong等人通过X射线散射实验表明PbTiO3薄膜的铁电性能随着层数降低而减弱, 极限厚度在1.2 nm(3个晶胞)时仅存在微弱铁电性, 此时的居里温度相较于块材显著降低[8]; Junquera和Ghosez的实验表明必须引入金属电极辅助屏蔽退极化场, 才能有效地实现薄层BaTiO3铁电相的稳定(实验的极限厚度为6个晶胞)[9]. Ferroelectricity is defined as the property of a material in which spontaneous polarization can be reversibly flipped by an external electric field. The discovery of this phenomenon dates back to 1921, when Valasek observed a characteristic hysteresis loop in Rochelle salt (KNaC4H4O6·4H2O)[1], marking the beginning of ferroelectric physics research. Subsequently, the expansion of system studies (e.g., BaTiO3[2], PbTiO3[3,4], LiNbO3[5]) and the development of ferroelectric theories such as Landau-Ginzburg-Devonshire theory jointly established the important position of ferroelectric materials in fields such as nonvolatile memory, capacitors, and photoelectric sensors. From a crystallographic perspective, the necessary conditions for ferroelectricity include a non-centrosymmetric point group and reversible symmetry operations, which simultaneously endow the material with pyroelectric and piezoelectric properties. With the demand for device miniaturization driven by the development of microelectronics technology, the issue of the depolarization field caused by the thinning of ferroelectric films has become increasingly prominent[6]: when the thickness falls below a critical dimension (typically on the nanometer scale), the depolarization field generated by surface-bound charges can lead to the complete disappearance of ferroelectricity[7]. Typical examples include Fong et al.'s X-ray scattering experiments showing that the ferroelectric properties of PbTiO3 films weaken as the number of layers decreases, with only weak ferroelectricity existing at a critical thickness of 1.2 nm (3 unit cells), and the Curie temperature is significantly reduced compared to the bulk material[8]; Junquera and Ghosez's experiments demonstrated that metal electrodes must be introduced to assist in screening the depolarization field to effectively stabilize the ferroelectric phase in thin-layer BaTiO3 (the experimental critical thickness is 6 unit cells)[9]. **铁电性**: Ferroelectricity - The property of a material characterized by a spontaneous electric polarization that can be reversed by an external electric field. **电滞回线**: Hysteresis loop - The characteristic loop traced by the polarization (or related quantity) of a ferroelectric material as a function of an applied electric field. **退极化场**: Depolarization field - The electric field generated by bound surface charges opposite to the direction of spontaneous polarization, which tends to destabilize the ferroelectric state. **居里温度**: Curie temperature - The temperature above which a ferroelectric material loses its spontaneous polarization and becomes paraelectric.
> 
> 二维材料因其特有的面内共价键-面外范德华力作用机制, 展现出区别于三维体系的量子限域效应. 自2004年石墨烯成功剥离以来[10], 已有超过千种二维材料被制备和预测[11], 其中包含具有本征铁电性的典型体系: CuInP2S6[12,13]、In2Se3[14,15]等. 这些二维铁电体通过自由载流子有效屏蔽退极化场, 其较低极化强度进一步抑制界面电荷积累, 为解决传统铁电材料微型化困境提供新思路. Two-dimensional materials exhibit quantum confinement effects distinct from three-dimensional systems due to their unique in-plane covalent bonding and out-of-plane van der Waals interaction mechanisms. Since the successful exfoliation of graphene in 2004[10], over a thousand types of 2D materials have been fabricated and predicted[11], including typical systems with intrinsic ferroelectricity: CuInP2S6[12,13], In2Se3[14,15], etc. These 2D ferroelectrics effectively screen the depolarization field through free carriers, and their lower polarization strength further suppresses interfacial charge accumulation, providing a new approach to solving the miniaturization challenges of traditional ferroelectric materials. **本征铁电性**: Intrinsic ferroelectricity - Ferroelectricity that originates from the inherent crystal structure and bonding of the material itself, rather than from external interfaces or fields.
> 
> 更值得关注的是, 通过调控范德华异质结层间平移自由度, 可在非极性二维材料中构筑新型滑移铁电体. 典型材料包括: InSe[16,17]、Td-WTe2[18,19]、3R-WSe2[20]、六方氮化硼(hexagonal boron nitride, hBN)[21~23]、双层石墨烯[24,25]等. 这类滑移铁电的极化特性源于层间电荷重分布, 且通过外场调控滑移动力学过程可实现极化态非易失性切换. More notably, by modulating the interlayer translational degrees of freedom in van der Waals heterostructures, a new type of sliding ferroelectric can be constructed in non-polar two-dimensional materials. Typical materials include: InSe[16,17], Td-WTe2[18,19], 3R-WSe2[20], hexagonal boron nitride (hBN)[21~23], bilayer graphene[24,25], etc. The polarization characteristics of this type of sliding ferroelectricity originate from interlayer charge redistribution, and the polarization state can be nonvolatilely switched by controlling the sliding dynamics through an external field.
> 
> 滑移铁电体系具备三个核心优势: 第一, 母体材料无须固有极性, 导电特性(导体/半导体/绝缘体)的可扩展性赋予其多元能带工程的可能; 第二, 通过调节堆叠转角、层数(同质/异质结)等自由度, 可实现材料的物性调控与设计; 第三, 与硅基工艺兼容的干法转移/直接生长技术, 结合超低翻转势垒, 为其器件集成奠定基础. Sliding ferroelectric systems possess three core advantages: First, the parent material does not require inherent polarity, and the scalability of its conductive properties (conductor/semiconductor/insulator) allows for diverse band engineering possibilities; Second, physical properties of the material can be tuned and designed by adjusting degrees of freedom such as stacking twist angle and number of layers (homo-/heterojunctions); Third, dry transfer/direct growth techniques compatible with silicon-based processes, combined with the ultralow switching barrier, lay the foundation for device integration. **能带工程**: Band engineering - The process of manipulating the electronic band structure of a material to achieve desired electronic or optical properties.
> 
> 本文系统综述二维铁电材料研究进展, 聚焦以下维度: (1) 本征铁电材料的极化特性与实验验证; (2) 滑移铁电机理的理论预测与实验进展; (3) 外场调控策略与原型器件开发; (4) 现存挑战与未来研究方向. 通过多方向介绍二维铁电材料的发展历程, 旨在为新型二维铁电器件的设计提供实验与理论依据. This paper systematically reviews the research progress of two-dimensional ferroelectric materials, focusing on the following dimensions: (1) Polarization characteristics and experimental verification of intrinsic ferroelectric materials; (2) Theoretical prediction and experimental progress of sliding ferroelectric mechanisms; (3) External field modulation strategies and prototype device development; (4) Existing challenges and future research directions. By introducing the development history of 2D ferroelectric materials from multiple perspectives, this review aims to provide experimental and theoretical foundations for the design of novel 2D ferroelectric devices.

> 自发极化铁电体的核心特征在于其内部电偶极子可在无外场时自发定向排列, 并在外场驱动下发生可逆翻转, 实验上表现为典型的电滞回线. 此类极化行为可通过扫描探针显微术(空间域铁电畴成像)或电输运测量(整体极化响应)进行表征, 辅以二次谐波产生技术定量评估铁电极化强度与极化反转时的相变. 本节聚焦本征二维铁电材料体系, 重点解析其晶格对称性破缺诱导的极化机理及研究进展. The core feature of a spontaneously polarized ferroelectric is that its internal electric dipoles can align spontaneously in the absence of an external field and undergo reversible switching under an external field drive, experimentally manifested as a typical hysteresis loop. This polarization behavior can be characterized by scanning probe microscopy (spatial domain imaging of ferroelectric domains) or electrical transport measurements (overall polarization response), supplemented by second harmonic generation (SHG) techniques to quantitatively evaluate the ferroelectric polarization strength and phase transitions during polarization reversal. This section focuses on intrinsically 2D ferroelectric material systems, emphasizing the analysis of their polarization mechanisms induced by lattice symmetry breaking and research progress. **自发极化**: Spontaneous polarization - The electric polarization that a material possesses in the absence of an external electric field. **扫描探针显微术**: Scanning probe microscopy (SPM) - A family of microscopy techniques that form images of surfaces using a physical probe that scans the specimen. **二次谐波产生**: Second harmonic generation (SHG) - A nonlinear optical process in which two photons with the same frequency interact with a nonlinear material, generating a new photon with twice the frequency.

> ## 1.1 CuInP2S6
> 
> CuInP2S6 (CIPS)作为二维铁电-离子导体双功能材料, 以层状八面体S骨架为主体结构, 层内嵌铜离子(Cu1+)、铟离子(In3+)及磷原子对(P-P), 层间由vdW作用耦合, 如图1(a)所示[13]. 其铁电性起源于Cu1+在硫配位场中的四重势阱跃迁行为: 在S的八面体笼结构中, Cu1+有4个能量极小值的位置, 如图1(b)所示[26]. 在高于居里温度Tc(~315 K)时, Cu1+随机占据八面体笼的对称等效位点(Cu1/Cu1′), 呈现低极化态(LP); 低温下, Cu1+在电场调控下从Cu1迁入非等效位点Cu3, Cu1+会与邻近层的S成键, 并触发In3+反向位移, 如图1(c)所示, 呈现高极化态(HP)[27,28]. CuInP2S6 (CIPS), as a two-dimensional ferroelectric-ionic conductor bifunctional material, features a layered octahedral S framework as the main structure, with intercalated copper ions (Cu1+), indium ions (In3+), and phosphorus atom pairs (P-P) within the layers, and the layers are coupled by vdW interactions, as shown in Figure 1(a)[13]. Its ferroelectricity originates from the quadruple-well potential hopping behavior of Cu1+ in the sulfur coordination field: within the S octahedral cage structure, Cu1+ has four energy minimum positions, as shown in Figure 1(b)[26]. Above the Curie temperature Tc (~315 K), Cu1+ randomly occupies symmetric equivalent sites of the octahedral cage (Cu1/Cu1′), exhibiting a low polarization state (LP); at low temperatures, Cu1+ migrates from the Cu1 site to the non-equivalent Cu3 site under electric field control, bonding with S in the adjacent layer and triggering a reverse displacement of In3+, as shown in Figure 1(c), resulting in a high polarization state (HP)[27,28].
> 
> 实验研究方面, Belianinov等人首先验证了CIPS的块体铁电性[12]. 而Liu等人通过机械剥离制备4 nm CIPS薄膜, 结合压电力显微镜(piezoresponse force microscope, PFM)证实其接近二维极限下的室温(~320 K)铁电性[13], 如图1(d)和(e)所示. 该材料还被证实可以通过局域电场调控实现Cu1+迁移及极化反转[26], 在Cu密度不足的区域, 还会形成顺电相In4/3P2S6 (IPS)与铁电相CIPS共存的结构[27,29]. 另外, CIPS也被发现存在反常体光伏效应[30], 光电流密度超传统钙钛矿两个量级, 为光铁电器件开发提供新途径. In terms of experimental research, Belianinov et al. first verified the bulk ferroelectricity of CIPS[12]. Liu et al., by preparing 4 nm CIPS films via mechanical exfoliation and using piezoresponse force microscopy (PFM), confirmed its room-temperature (~320 K) ferroelectricity near the 2D limit[13], as shown in Figures 1(d) and (e). This material has also been demonstrated to enable Cu1+ migration and polarization reversal through local electric field control[26]. In regions with insufficient Cu density, a structure coexisting with the paraelectric phase In4/3P2S6 (IPS) and the ferroelectric phase CIPS can form[27,29]. In addition, CIPS has been found to exhibit an anomalous bulk photovoltaic effect[30], with a photocurrent density two orders of magnitude higher than traditional perovskites, providing a new avenue for the development of photoelectric devices. **压电力显微镜**: Piezoresponse force microscopy (PFM) - A scanning probe microscopy technique that detects and maps the inverse piezoelectric effect (mechanical deformation under an applied electric field) to study ferroelectric domains. **体光伏效应**: Bulk photovoltaic effect (BPVE) - A photocurrent generation mechanism in non-centrosymmetric crystals, independent of semiconductor p-n junctions, arising from the asymmetry of carrier excitation, relaxation, and transport.

> ## 1.2 In2Se3
> 
> 铟(In)硒(Se)化合物作为第III-VI主族的半导体化合物, 已被查明拥有众多相态[34]. 其中, In2Se3存在α/β(层状相)及γ/δ/κ(非层状相)等多型结构. Indium (In) selenium (Se) compounds, as group III-VI semiconductor compounds, have been identified to possess numerous phases[34]. Among them, In2Se3 exists in polytypic structures such as α/β (layered phases) and γ/δ/κ (non-layered phases).
> 
> α相是室温下最稳定的相态, 原子结构呈现Se-In-Se-In-Se五层结构, 其中央Se层的双稳态移动机制可以同步翻转面内/面外极化, 形成“偶极锁定”效应[35,36], 如图1(f)所示[31]. 实验上, 两个团队先后验证了α-In2Se3的室温铁电性[14,31]. 而Xue等人的实验更是制备出单层极限下仍保持强铁电性的α-In2Se3, 如图1(g)所示[32]. 需要强调的是, 图1(f)中关于铁电来源的定性解释并不准确, 没有考虑晶格整体的C3v对称性. 根据诺伊曼原理(晶体物理性质不会随对称性操作而变化), 理论上这种对称性会抑制α-In2Se3中面内铁电性的产生. 然而, 更多的实验却持续给出α-In2Se3存在面内铁电性的证据[37,38]. 2023年, Ji等人提出的分数量子铁电性概念很好解释了面内铁电性的成因[39], 他们指出中间Se原子的分数化位移在其中起到重要作用. 另外, α-In2Se3的面内极化受层数奇偶性影响[40~42]: 在2H堆垛(P63/mmc)中, 偶数层因相邻层极化反向而总极化抵消; 3R堆垛(R3m)则保持层间极化取向一致. The α phase is the most stable phase at room temperature, with an atomic structure presenting a Se-In-Se-In-Se five-layer configuration. The bistable movement mechanism of the central Se layer allows simultaneous flipping of in-plane and out-of-plane polarization, forming a "dipole locking" effect[35,36], as shown in Figure 1(f)[31]. Experimentally, two teams sequentially verified the room-temperature ferroelectricity of α-In2Se3[14,31]. Xue et al.'s experiments further prepared α-In2Se3 that maintained strong ferroelectricity down to the monolayer limit, as shown in Figure 1(g)[32]. It must be emphasized that the qualitative explanation of the ferroelectric origin in Figure 1(f) is not entirely accurate, as it does not consider the overall C3v symmetry of the lattice. According to Neumann's principle (the physical properties of a crystal do not change with symmetry operations), this symmetry would theoretically suppress the generation of in-plane ferroelectricity in α-In2Se3. However, more experiments continue to provide evidence of in-plane ferroelectricity in α-In2Se3[37,38]. In 2023, the concept of fractional quantum ferroelectricity proposed by Ji et al. nicely explained the origin of in-plane ferroelectricity[39], pointing out that the fractional displacement of the central Se atom plays a crucial role. Additionally, the in-plane polarization of α-In2Se3 is influenced by the parity of the number of layers[40~42]: in 2H stacking (P63/mmc), the total polarization cancels in even-numbered layers due to the opposite polarization direction of adjacent layers; while 3R stacking (R3m) maintains consistent interlayer polarization orientation. **诺伊曼原理**: Neumann's principle - The principle stating that the symmetry of any physical property of a crystal must include the symmetry elements of the point group of the crystal. **分数量子铁电性**: Fractional quantum ferroelectricity - A quantum phenomenon where the macroscopic polarization arises from the fractional displacement of atoms, incompatible with the overall lattice symmetry.

> # 2 vdW滑移铁电
> 
> # 2 vdW Sliding Ferroelectricity
> 
> 滑移铁电的突破性概念于2017年由Li和Wu提出[60]: 通过构建特定vdW堆垛结构, 可在非极性二维材料中诱导面外极化. 其核心机理不同于传统铁电体的离子位移模型, 仅需层间滑移打破空间反演对称性, 引发界面电荷重分布并形成垂直偶极阵列, 即可实现宏观的面外铁电性. 该机制特性显著: 一是无需母体本征极性, 赋予更多候选材料; 二是极化翻转能垒极低, 为低功耗存算器件奠定基础. 本节将从滑移铁电的理论预测出发, 介绍转角hBN、堆叠TMDs等已经被广泛研究的滑移铁电材料的实验进展. The groundbreaking concept of sliding ferroelectricity was proposed by Li and Wu in 2017[60]: by constructing specific vdW stacking structures, out-of-plane polarization can be induced in non-polar two-dimensional materials. Its core mechanism differs from the ion displacement model of traditional ferroelectrics; it only requires interlayer sliding to break spatial inversion symmetry, triggering interfacial charge redistribution and forming a vertical dipole array, thereby achieving macroscopic out-of-plane ferroelectricity. This mechanism has significant characteristics: firstly, it does not require intrinsic polarity of the parent material, granting a wider range of candidate materials; secondly, the polarization switching energy barrier is extremely low, laying the foundation for low-power memory and computing devices. This section will start from the theoretical predictions of sliding ferroelectricity and introduce the experimental progress of widely studied sliding ferroelectric materials such as twisted hBN and stacked TMDs.

Their monolayer features an X-M-X sandwich structure, and the multilayer systems can exhibit polymorphic characteristics including semiconducting 2H, metallic 1T/1T′, topological Td, and superconducting 2M phases[70,71].
> 
> WTe2作为其中首个被确认的滑移铁电体, 其天然Td相兼具金属性与铁电性. 2018年, Fei等人通过输运测试发现[18]: 双层与三层WTe2出现了显著的电滞回线, 且具有约350 K的相变温度, 而单层WTe2却无此现象, 如图4(a)~(c)所示[18], 暗示WTe2的铁电性来自多层中的电荷重排列. 同年, Li[72]与Ren[73]团队采用Hirshfeld电荷分析量化两个滑移态中层间近邻的4个Te原子的有效电荷(图4(d)[72]), 通过第一性原理计算揭示了极化的层间电荷转移本质: 双层面外极化反转对应沿b轴0.72 Å(能垒0.6 meV/晶胞)[72]或0.493 Å(能垒0.3 meV/晶胞)[73]的层间滑移. 另外, 奇数层的极化切换需要中间偶数层的滑移, 且会导致极化减弱, 块体则因电子屏蔽效应抑制了铁电性的产生. 后续Xiao等人的研究通过二次谐波(second harmonic generation, SHG)的蝴蝶型滞后曲线及拉曼光谱剪切模消失现象(图4(e))[74], 证实了极化反转伴随Td→1T′→Td相变. WTe2, as the first confirmed sliding ferroelectric among them, exhibits both metallicity and ferroelectricity in its natural Td phase. In 2018, Fei et al. discovered through transport measurements[18] that bilayer and trilayer WTe2 exhibited a significant hysteresis loop with a phase transition temperature of about 350 K, while monolayer WTe2 did not show this phenomenon, as shown in Figures 4(a)~(c)[18], suggesting that the ferroelectricity of WTe2 originates from charge rearrangement in the multilayers. In the same year, the teams of Li[72] and Ren[73] used Hirshfeld charge analysis to quantify the effective charges of the four adjacent Te atoms between the layers in the two sliding states (Figure 4(d)[72]). First-principles calculations revealed the interlayer charge transfer nature of the polarization: the out-of-plane polarization reversal of the bilayer corresponds to an interlayer sliding of 0.72 Å (energy barrier 0.6 meV/unit cell)[72] or 0.493 Å (energy barrier 0.3 meV/unit cell)[73] along the b-axis. Additionally, polarization switching in odd-numbered layers requires the sliding of the middle even-numbered layer, leading to weakened polarization, while the bulk material suppresses the generation of ferroelectricity due to electron screening effects. Subsequent research by Xiao et al. confirmed that polarization reversal is accompanied by a Td→1T′→Td phase transition through the butterfly-shaped hysteresis loop of second harmonic generation (SHG) and the disappearance of the shear mode in Raman spectroscopy (Figure 4(e))[74].
> 
> 然而, 2019年, Sharma等人在块体WTe2中观测到20~50 nm大小的铁电畴及其在PFM调控下的极化翻转现象[19]. 其极化归因于c轴方向上的离子位移与价带电子各向异性分布的协同作用. 极化的切换则来自层内晶格的多方向畸变, 相比单方向路径畸变的能垒从0.7 eV/f.u.降低到了0.29 eV/f.u., 与传统铁电体如BiFeO3可比拟(~0.43 eV/f.u.), 这将允许室温中实现极化的反转. 该研究暗示块体Td-WTe2可能存在与少层不同的铁电机制, 但这仍需后续的研究验证. However, in 2019, Sharma et al. observed 20-50 nm sized ferroelectric domains in bulk WTe2 and their polarization switching under PFM control[19]. The polarization was attributed to the synergistic effect of ion displacement along the c-axis and the anisotropic distribution of valence band electrons. The polarization switching arises from multi-directional lattice distortion within the layer, reducing the energy barrier from 0.7 eV/f.u. for a unidirectional path to 0.29 eV/f.u., comparable to traditional ferroelectrics like BiFeO3 (~0.43 eV/f.u.), which would allow polarization reversal at room temperature. This study suggests that bulk Td-WTe2 may have a different ferroelectric mechanism than few-layer systems, but this still requires further research verification.
> 
> 对于半导体型H相TMDs(如MoS2、MoSe2、WS2、WSe2、MoTe2), 本征2H相为反平行AA′堆叠, 如图4(f)左图所示[75]. 通过同向堆叠形成AB/BA菱方(R)构型(如图4(h)中右两图所示)可诱导面外铁电[75]. 2022年, Jarillo-Herrero团队沿用研究hBN铁电的方法系统展现了H相TMDs的室温铁电构筑与探测[23]: 转角H-TMDs具有与t-hBN类似的铁电行为——平行堆叠产生周期性的三角形极化畴(PFM形貌如图4(g)所示[75]). 在构筑完全平行排列的样品中, 他们发现了其中单畴主导的R堆叠样品引发石墨烯电阻曲线单峰回滞和快速的极化切换, 而多畴R堆叠则导致了双峰渐变的响应模式, 说明极化反转机制类似t-hBN中的畴壁迁移, 两者的电回滞线如图4(h)所示[75]. 实验测得极化对石墨烯的势场调制幅度2ΔVP≈110 meV, 约为hBN体系的一半. 这个结果与第一性原理计算吻合, 与TMDs中原子的电负性、层间距和对称破缺程度有关. For semiconducting H-phase TMDs (e.g., MoS2, MoSe2, WS2, WSe2, MoTe2), the intrinsic 2H phase is antiparallel AA′ stacking, as shown in the left panel of Figure 4(f)[75]. Out-of-plane ferroelectricity can be induced by forming AB/BA rhombohedral (R) configurations through parallel stacking (as shown in the two right panels of Figure 4(h))[75]. In 2022, the Jarillo-Herrero team, using the same methods for studying ferroelectricity in hBN, systematically demonstrated the construction and detection of room-temperature ferroelectricity in H-phase TMDs[23]: twisted H-TMDs exhibited ferroelectric behavior similar to t-hBN—parallel stacking produces periodic triangular polarization domains (PFM morphology shown in Figure 4(g)[75]). In constructing completely parallel-aligned samples, they found that single-domain-dominated R-stacked samples induced a single-peak hysteresis in the graphene resistance curve and rapid polarization switching, while multi-domain R-stacking led to a bimodal gradual response mode, indicating that the polarization reversal mechanism is similar to domain wall migration in t-hBN. The electrical hysteresis loops of both are shown in Figure 4(h)[75]. The measured potential field modulation amplitude of polarization on graphene was 2ΔVP≈110 meV, about half that of the hBN system. This result is consistent with first-principles calculations and is related to the electronegativity of atoms, interlayer spacing, and the degree of symmetry breaking in TMDs.
> 
> 对于TMDs异质结, Rogée团队采用化学气相沉积(chemical vapor deposition, CVD)法在WS2表面外延生长H堆叠与R堆叠MoS2(二者晶格常数相近)[76], 通过PFM证实仅R堆叠MoS2/WS2异质结呈现室温中显著的电压依赖的相位回滞特性, 并结合差分电荷密度图验证其层间极化起源. 而在不同材料的moiré异质结中, Fal'ko团队的实验证实层间转角会显著影响材料的层间激子的局域化和激子能量[77]. 这一结论有望扩展至多层TMDs异质结moiré体系中, Yao团队对此体系做出了理论预测, 计算表明三层转角体系中的中性激子和三重激子存在不同的转角调控响应和空间局域特性[78]. For TMD heterostructures, Rogée's team used chemical vapor deposition (CVD) to epitaxially grow H-stacked and R-stacked MoS2 on WS2 surfaces (with similar lattice constants)[76], and confirmed through PFM that only the R-stacked MoS2/WS2 heterostructure exhibited significant voltage-dependent phase hysteresis characteristics at room temperature. Combined with differential charge density maps, they verified the interlayer polarization origin. In moiré heterostructures of different materials, Fal'ko's team experimentally confirmed that the interlayer twist angle significantly affects the localization and energy of interlayer excitons[77]. This conclusion is expected to extend to multilayer TMD heterostructure moiré systems. Yao's team made theoretical predictions for this system, with calculations showing that neutral excitons and trions in the trilayer twisted system have different twist-angle-dependent responses and spatial localization characteristics[78]. **化学气相沉积**: Chemical vapor deposition (CVD) - A method for producing high-quality, high-performance solid materials, typically under vacuum, by exposing a substrate to volatile precursors that react or decompose on its surface.
> 
> 此外, Sun等人在一维WS2多壁纳米管中发现独特的轴向面内铁电性[79]: 施加轴向电压时, 各层WS2因压电效应产生非均匀形变并滑移不同距离, 正负电荷积累形成初始极化; 撤去电场后滑动摩擦力保持残余极化; 反向电场触发各层形变反转并驱动滑移倒转, 导致极化反转. 光电流-电压的回滞测量印证了该滑移-压电耦合机制. Furthermore, Sun et al. discovered unique axial in-plane ferroelectricity in one-dimensional WS2 multi-walled nanotubes[79]: when an axial voltage is applied, each WS2 layer undergoes non-uniform deformation due to the piezoelectric effect and slides by different distances, accumulating positive and negative charges to form an initial polarization; after removing the electric field, the sliding friction maintains the residual polarization; a reverse electric field triggers the reversal of deformation in each layer and drives the sliding reversal, leading to polarization switching. Photocurrent-voltage hysteresis measurements confirmed this sliding-piezoelectric coupling mechanism.

> ## 2.4 石墨烯中的新奇滑移铁电
> 
> ## 2.4 Novel Sliding Ferroelectricity in Graphene
> 
> 石墨烯作为最简单且研究最深入的二维材料, 近期的一些实验表明, 即使其不存在类似BP-Bi的晶格畸变或是hBN中的两组分电负性原子, 仍旧可以产生面外铁电性. 2020年, Zheng等人发现BN封装的伯纳尔(Bernal)堆垛双层石墨烯(BLG)中存在4 K温度下的非常规铁电性[24]: 通过顶栅电压扫描得到的电阻曲线差异显示出显著电滞回线, 如图5(a)所示(由正向扫描图减反向扫描图所得). 值得注意的是, 在顶栅电压Vt超过一定值时, 电阻峰会突然失去对其的响应, 这个现象被称作“层特异性异常屏蔽(LSAS)”现象. 理论分析表明, 垂直电场会诱导BLG出现层间势差, 导带电子局域于低势层, 价带电子则富集于高势层[80~82], 如图5(b)所示[24]. 当BN封装层与BLG形成30°或0°转角时, 莫尔势导致能带平坦化, 在考虑电子关联效应后, 部分价带电子会跃迁至导带, 驱动电荷的层间转移, 如图5(c)所示. 极化形成后, 反向电场需克服Hubbard排斥能垒, 导致电滞行为; 而当电荷转移量超过莫尔平带容量时, 极化态冻结引发LSAS现象. Graphene, as the simplest and most extensively studied two-dimensional material, has recently shown in experiments that it can still generate out-of-plane ferroelectricity even without lattice distortion similar to BP-Bi or two-component electronegative atoms as in hBN. In 2020, Zheng et al. discovered unconventional ferroelectricity at 4 K in BN-encapsulated Bernal-stacked bilayer graphene (BLG)[24]: the difference in resistance curves obtained from top-gate voltage sweeps showed a significant hysteresis loop, as shown in Figure 5(a) (obtained by subtracting the reverse sweep image from the forward sweep image). Notably, when the top-gate voltage Vt exceeds a certain value, the resistance peak suddenly loses its response to it, a phenomenon termed "layer-specific anomalous screening (LSAS)". Theoretical analysis indicates that a perpendicular electric field induces an interlayer potential difference in BLG, with conduction band electrons localized in the low-potential layer and valence band electrons enriched in the high-potential layer[80~82], as shown in Figure 5(b)[24]. When the BN encapsulation layer forms a 30° or 0° twist angle with BLG, the moiré potential leads to band flattening. Considering electron correlation effects, some valence band electrons will transition to the conduction band, driving interlayer charge transfer, as shown in Figure 5(c). After polarization is formed, a reverse electric field must overcome the Hubbard repulsion energy barrier, leading to hysteretic behavior; and when the amount of charge transfer exceeds the capacity of the moiré flat band, the polarization state freezes, triggering the LSAS phenomenon.
> 
> 2022年, Lu课题组的复现实验发现[25], BN封装(30°转角)的BLG存在多级铁电响应: 除电荷中性点(CNP)对称的主回滞环外, 电子/空穴侧莫尔平带完全填充点(FFP)附近出现两个半环, 如图5(d)所示[25]. 两个半环可由主环在Vb方向上平移获得, 说明LSAS状态仅由顶栅电压Vt调控. 在费米面处于不同位置时的Rxx-Vt/dt曲线表明, 系统几乎以相同的Vt进入了LSAS状态, 说明极化现象不仅出现在莫尔带, 还延伸至带隙及高能分散区, 如图5(e)和(f)所示[25]. 实验测得层间电荷转移密度高达1013 cm–2, 对应极化强度达~5 pC/m, 远超单一莫尔带容量, 暗示除了层间电荷转移机制, 晶格滑移或界面畸变可能贡献了额外极化分量. 变温测量的结果进一步表明了这种双层石墨烯中铁电的热稳定性: 体系可以在100 K以内保持较好的电回滞特性, 高于100 K时铁电性逐渐衰减, 直至300 K时基本消失. In 2022, a replication experiment by Lu's group found[25] that BN-encapsulated (30° twist angle) BLG exhibits a multilevel ferroelectric response: besides the main hysteresis loop symmetric about the charge neutrality point (CNP), two half-loops appeared near the full filling points (FFP) of the moiré flat bands on the electron/hole sides, as shown in Figure 5(d)[25]. The two half-loops can be obtained by translating the main loop in the Vb direction, indicating that the LSAS state is solely modulated by the top-gate voltage Vt. The Rxx-Vt/dt curves when the Fermi level is at different positions show that the system enters the LSAS state at almost the same Vt, indicating that the polarization phenomenon appears not only in the moiré band but also extends to the band gap and high-energy dispersive regions, as shown in Figures 5(e) and (f)[25]. The experimentally measured interlayer charge transfer density is as high as 1013 cm–2, corresponding to a polarization intensity of ~5 pC/m, far exceeding the capacity of a single moiré band, suggesting that besides the interlayer charge transfer mechanism, lattice sliding or interfacial distortion may contribute additional polarization components. The results of temperature-dependent measurements further indicate the thermal stability of ferroelectricity in this bilayer graphene: the system can maintain good electrical hysteresis characteristics within 100 K; above 100 K, the ferroelectricity gradually decays and essentially disappears by 300 K.
> 
> 2023年, Klein等人在魔角双层石墨烯(MATBG)中也观测到在100 mK以下类铁电的双稳态特性[83]. 其非易失性切换可与超导态或关联绝缘态共存, 但这种类铁电双稳态的物理来源仍不明确. 更为有趣的是, Lu课题组在2025年的研究表明, 被WSe2屏蔽BN/石墨烯莫尔势后, 导电层石墨烯仍能出现电回滞现象(图5(g)[84]), 他们认为BLG和MATBG铁电的来源可能是用来封装的BN中存在非中心对称的堆叠层. 几乎同时, Liu课题组的实验发现在上下层BN和单层石墨烯形成ABC堆垛时(图5(h)[85]), 同样展现出了极强的室温铁电性(极化强度~1.76 μC/cm2), 且此方法产生的铁电性可扩展至多层Bernal堆垛石墨烯. 两项工作均说明石墨烯中的铁电性来源可能存在电子关联以外的其他可能, 这仍需进一步地研究与探索. In 2023, Klein et al. also observed a ferroelectric-like bistable characteristic below 100 mK in magic-angle twisted bilayer graphene (MATBG)[83]. Its nonvolatile switching can coexist with superconducting states or correlated insulating states, but the physical origin of this ferroelectric-like bistability remains unclear. More interestingly, research by Lu's group in 2025 showed that after screening the BN/graphene moiré potential with WSe2, the conducting graphene layer still exhibited an electrical hysteresis phenomenon (Figure 5(g)[84]), suggesting that the source of ferroelectricity in BLG and MATBG might be the non-centrosymmetric stacking layers present in the encapsulating BN. Almost simultaneously, experiments by Liu's group found that when the upper and lower BN layers and monolayer graphene form ABC stacking (Figure 5(h)[85]), it also exhibited extremely strong room-temperature ferroelectricity (polarization intensity ~1.76 μC/cm2), and the ferroelectricity generated by this method can be extended to multilayer Bernal-stacked graphene. Both works indicate that the source of ferroelectricity in graphene may have possibilities beyond electronic correlation, which still requires further research and exploration. **魔角双层石墨烯**: Magic-angle twisted bilayer graphene (MATBG) - A system formed by two graphene layers rotated by a specific "magic" angle (~1.1°) relative to each other, exhibiting correlated insulating and superconducting phases.

> # 3 滑移铁电材料调控与应用的研究进展
> 
> # 3 Research Progress on the Modulation and Application of Sliding Ferroelectric Materials
> 
> 滑移铁电材料凭借其独特的层间滑移极化机制, 在微型存储器与低功耗电子器件领域展现出革新潜力. 然而, 其实际应用仍面临多重挑战, 比如极化态与外部电场的非线性响应调控、大面积高质量生长工艺开发等. 本节将聚焦畴工程和器件构筑与开发等方向, 梳理当前滑移铁电材料的研究进展与前景. Sliding ferroelectric materials, leveraging their unique interlayer sliding polarization mechanism, show revolutionary potential in the fields of micro-memory and low-power electronic devices. However, their practical application still faces multiple challenges, such as the nonlinear response regulation between polarization states and external electric fields, and the development of large-area, high-quality growth processes. This section will focus on directions such as domain engineering and device construction and development, summarizing the current research progress and prospects of sliding ferroelectric materials.
> 
> ## 3.1 畴结构与极化切换调控
> 
> ## 3.1 Domain Structure and Polarization Switching Control
> 
> 对外部电场的动态响应研究表明, 滑移铁电极化的物理本质与畴壁运动密切相关. 通过扫描探针技术可实现畴结构与极化动力学的原位观测与控制. 以Jarillo-Herrero团队2022年研究为例[75], 他们在R堆叠多畴MoSe2中观察到: 当施加电压时, 铁电畴通过畴壁的弹性扩张或收缩改变极化强度(图6(a)[75]), 且未发现新畴核成核. 这与图4(j)中单畴/多畴体系输运特性差异的猜想一致, 即单畴因缺乏可移动畴壁而难以被电场调控. Research on dynamic response to external electric fields shows that the physical nature of sliding ferroelectric polarization is closely related to domain wall motion. In-situ observation and control of domain structure and polarization dynamics can be achieved through scanning probe techniques. Taking the 2022 study by the Jarillo-Herrero team as an example[75], they observed in R-stacked multi-domain MoSe2 that when a voltage is applied, the ferroelectric domains change their polarization intensity through elastic expansion or contraction of domain walls (Figure 6(a)[75]), and no new domain nucleation was found. This is consistent with the conjecture about the difference in transport characteristics between single-domain and multi-domain systems in Figure 4(j), i.e., single domains are difficult to control by an electric field due to the lack of movable domain walls.
> 
> 另一类典型体系是莫尔铁电体, Xue[86]与Luican-Mayer[87]两个团队的研究表示: 非极性AA区域会被晶格钉扎, 三角畴壁形态随偏压变化呈现可逆扩张/收缩. 在局部应变积累区域, 相邻畴壁可能合并成一个完全螺旋错位结构[88], 如图6(f)和(g)所示[87]. Another typical system is moiré ferroelectrics. Research by the teams of Xue[86] and Luican-Mayer[87] indicates that non-polar AA regions are pinned by the lattice, and the triangular domain wall morphology exhibits reversible expansion/contraction with changes in bias voltage. In regions of local strain accumulation, adjacent domain walls may merge into a fully screw-dislocated structure[88], as shown in Figures 6(f) and (g)[87].
> 
> 2024年, Gao等人通过构造人工畴结构与多尺度理论模拟, 系统性揭示四类畴形态对铁电性能的影响[89]. In 2024, Gao et al., through constructing artificial domain structures and multi-scale theoretical simulations, systematically revealed the influence of four types of domain morphologies on ferroelectric performance[89].
> 
> (1) 孤立单畴壁(图6(d)[89]): 仅含单个可滑移畴壁, 外电场驱动畴壁整体移动, 触发全域极化反转, 呈现显著的双极电滞回线, 是高性能非易失存储器的理想架构; (1) Isolated Single Domain Wall (Figure 6(d)[89]): Contains only a single slidable domain wall. An external electric field drives the overall movement of the domain wall, triggering global polarization reversal and presenting a significant bipolar hysteresis loop, making it an ideal architecture for high-performance nonvolatile memory;
> 
> (2) 周期三角莫尔畴(图6(e)[89]): 受C3对称性限制, 畴壁节点被钉扎无法移动, 极化切换仅通过畴面积弹性缩放而非整体滑移实现, 表现为弱迟滞及线性多态响应, 适用于多阻态或模拟型器件; (2) Periodic Triangular Moiré Domains (Figure 6(e)[89]): Limited by C3 symmetry, domain wall nodes are pinned and immobile. Polarization switching is achieved only through elastic scaling of the domain area rather than overall sliding, manifesting as weak hysteresis and linear multistate response, suitable for multi-resistance states or analog-type devices;
> 
> (3) 多畴壁交联结构(图6(f)[89]): 由生长缺陷或污染引入额外钉扎点, 畴壁滑移路径受阻, 仅局部区域具有有限的电场响应. 该结构极化反转效率极低, 表明杂质对器件性能的致命影响; (3) Multi-Domain Wall Cross-linked Structure (Figure 6(f)[89]): Additional pinning points are introduced by growth defects or contamination, obstructing the domain wall sliding path, with only local regions showing limited electric field response. This structure has extremely low polarization reversal efficiency, indicating the fatal impact of impurities on device performance;
> 
> (4) 完全位错畴(图6(g)[89]): 形成位错网络完全锁死极化方向, 理论计算表明其翻转需超高电场(>1 V/nm), 远超常规实验条件. 这导致器件无电滞响应, 可应用于极化固定的永久存储场景. (4) Fully Dislocated Domains (Figure 6(g)[89]): A dislocation network is formed that completely locks the polarization direction. Theoretical calculations indicate that its switching requires an ultra-high electric field (>1 V/nm), far exceeding conventional experimental conditions. This results in no hysteretic response in the device, which can be applied to permanent storage scenarios with fixed polarization.
> 
> 研究表明, 在未来应用中需精细化调控畴壁密度、位错分布与钉扎强度. 例如, 通过CVD工艺引入边缘位错或控制生长温度调控层间滑移能垒, 可定制畴结构以适配特定器件需求; 采用选区离子注入或局域应力工程选择性削弱钉扎效应, 可增强畴壁移动性. 上述成果为二维滑移铁电材料的畴工程与器件设计奠定了实验与理论基础. The research indicates that in future applications, it is necessary to finely regulate the domain wall density, dislocation distribution, and pinning strength. For example, by introducing edge dislocations through the CVD process or controlling the growth temperature to regulate the interlayer sliding energy barrier, the domain structure can be customized to meet specific device requirements; using selective area ion implantation or local stress engineering to selectively weaken the pinning effect can enhance domain wall mobility. The above achievements have laid an experimental and theoretical foundation for the domain engineering and device design of two-dimensional sliding ferroelectric materials.

> ## 3.2 非易失性存储器件
> 
> ## 3.2 Nonvolatile Memory Devices
> 
> 滑移铁电体的非易失开关特性在电输运实验中展现出显著潜力. 2022年, Liu团队对不同层数3R-MoS2铁电器件的研究表明[90]: 3R-MoS2的极化状态数与其层数正相关(n层对应n种能量简并态和2n-1种原子构型), 且相邻态切换能垒恒为~15.4 meV/晶胞(与层数无关), 如图7(a)~(c)所示[90]. 这种多层滑移铁电中极化切换路径也在后续的理论和实验研究中进行了进一步探讨[91,92]. 实验证实这种超过2层的器件可长时间稳定维持多种极化态, 通过施加较大或较小电场可驱动态之间转变, 如图7(d)所示[90]. 这表明vdW材料的层数也是定向设计功能铁电器件性能的关键因素. 但我们仍需了解其快速存储时的性能, 包括抗疲劳性、写入速度和维持时间. 2024年他们的后续研究表明, 其抗疲劳机制源于硫空位迁移能垒(层内2.6 eV, 层间4.6 eV)远高于极化切换能垒(~136 meV), 可有效抑制空位聚集与内建屏蔽场形成[93]; 分子动力学模拟显示单个硫空位无法钉扎畴壁, 保障器件在106次循环后性能仍无衰减(图7(e))[93]. 写入速度测试中, 器件可稳定响应53 ns超短脉冲(图7(f))[93], 性能指标已匹配商用非易失存储器. Jarillo-Herrero团队通过p-hBN体系验证了同样的高性能器件: 极化切换速度达1 ns, 且在100 ns脉冲下实现1011次循环后保持稳定[94]. 这些研究发现为二维滑移铁电器件的高性能存储应用奠定了基础. The nonvolatile switching characteristics of sliding ferroelectrics show significant potential in electrical transport experiments. In 2022, research by Liu's team on 3R-MoS2 ferroelectric devices with different numbers of layers indicated[90] that the number of polarization states in 3R-MoS2 is positively correlated with its layer number (n layers correspond to n energy-degenerate states and 2n-1 atomic configurations), and the adjacent state switching energy barrier is constant at ~15.4 meV/unit cell (independent of layer number), as shown in Figures 7(a)~(c)[90]. The polarization switching pathways in this multilayer sliding ferroelectric were further explored in subsequent theoretical and experimental studies[91,92]. Experiments confirmed that devices with more than two layers can stably maintain multiple polarization states over long periods, and transitions between states can be driven by applying larger or smaller electric fields, as shown in Figure 7(d)[90]. This indicates that the number of layers in vdW materials is also a key factor in directionally designing the performance of functional ferroelectric devices. However, we still need to understand their performance during fast storage, including fatigue resistance, writing speed, and retention time. Their subsequent research in 2024 showed that the fatigue resistance mechanism originates from the sulfur vacancy migration energy barrier (2.6 eV intralayer, 4.6 eV interlayer) being much higher than the polarization switching energy barrier (~136 meV), which can effectively inhibit vacancy aggregation and built-in screening field formation[93]; molecular dynamics simulations show that a single sulfur vacancy cannot pin the domain wall, ensuring the device's performance remains undegraded after 106 cycles (Figure 7(e))[93]. In writing speed tests, the device can stably respond to 53 ns ultra-short pulses (Figure 7(f))[93], with performance metrics already matching commercial nonvolatile memory. The Jarillo-Herrero team verified the same high-performance device through the p-hBN system: polarization switching speed reaches 1 ns, and it remains stable after 1011 cycles under 100 ns pulses[94]. These research findings lay the foundation for high-performance storage applications of two-dimensional sliding ferroelectric devices.

> ## 3.3 光电异质结界面构筑
> 
> ## 3.3 Optoelectronic Heterojunction Interface Construction
> 
> 滑移铁电材料通过vdW异质结的可控堆垛展现出远超传统钙钛矿铁电体的界面极化调控自由度, 为可重构光电器件开发提供了新途径. 研究聚焦铁电-功能层异质结对激子态与输运特性的调制效应: 2023~2024年的研究表明[95,98], 封装于hBN/t-hBN中的单层WSe2因AB/BA铁电畴势场诱导局域掺杂(图7(g)[95]), 导致正负三重激子的复合发光分别被增强与抑制, 在光致发光谱(PL)中对应特征峰强度的显著差异, 如图7(h)所示[95]. 栅极扫描测试中, PL激子峰强度随电压变化呈现明显电滞回线[98], 证实极化状态的非易失性. Kim等人在hBN/t-hBN封装的单层MoSe2器件中采用光泵浦探测技术[96], 发现铁电势产生的静电莫尔势可强效限制激子的层内扩散, 空间局域效应直接反映为光生载流子动力学参数的显著改变, 如图7(i)和(j)所示[96]. 这些研究将滑移铁电工程扩展到调制各种电子和光子功能层的特性, 为以后光电探测、存储等开关器件的设计提供了新思路. Sliding ferroelectric materials, through the controllable stacking of vdW heterojunctions, exhibit a degree of interfacial polarization modulation freedom far exceeding that of traditional perovskite ferroelectrics, providing a new avenue for the development of reconfigurable optoelectronic devices. Research focuses on the modulation effect of ferroelectric-functional layer heterojunctions on exciton states and transport characteristics: Studies from 2023-2024 indicate[95,98] that monolayer WSe2 encapsulated in hBN/t-hBN undergoes local doping induced by the potential field of AB/BA ferroelectric domains (Figure 7(g)[95]), leading to the enhancement and suppression of the recombination luminescence of positively and negatively charged trions, respectively, corresponding to significant differences in the characteristic peak intensities in the photoluminescence (PL) spectrum, as shown in Figure 7(h)[95]. In gate sweep tests, the PL exciton peak intensity shows a clear hysteresis loop with voltage changes[98], confirming the nonvolatility of the polarization state. Kim et al. used optical pump-probe techniques in monolayer MoSe2 devices encapsulated in hBN/t-hBN[96] and found that the electrostatic moiré potential generated by the ferroelectric potential can strongly confine the intralayer diffusion of excitons. The spatial localization effect is directly reflected in the significant change of the kinetic parameters of photogenerated carriers, as shown in Figures 7(i) and (j)[96]. These studies extend sliding ferroelectric engineering to modulate the properties of various electronic and photonic functional layers, providing new ideas for the future design of switching devices such as photodetectors and memories.
> 
> 电异质结设计方面, 铁电性与二维材料新奇物性耦合扩展了器件功能: 如上文提到的MATBG中的双稳态极化可与超导态/关联绝缘态共存[83], 为实现极化调控的超导开关器件奠定了基础; Kang等人通过构筑WSe2/铁电WTe2异质结并形成矩形莫尔超晶格(图7(k))[97], 实现了铁电切换的莫尔绝缘态及非线性反常霍尔效应, 如图7(l)所示[97]. 此外, 封装工艺对性能的影响不容忽视: 2025年, Wang等人研究表明, t-hBN铁电器件在不同封装环境下卫星电阻峰受到显著调制, 为器件封装策略提供了具体指导[99]. In terms of electrical heterojunction design, the coupling of ferroelectricity with the novel physical properties of two-dimensional materials expands device functionality: as mentioned above, the bistable polarization in MATBG can coexist with superconducting states/correlated insulating states[83], laying the foundation for realizing polarization-controlled superconducting switching devices; Kang et al., by constructing a WSe2/ferroelectric WTe2 heterojunction and forming a rectangular moiré superlattice (Figure 7(k))[97], realized ferroelectric switching of moiré insulating states and the nonlinear anomalous Hall effect, as shown in Figure 7(l)[97]. In addition, the impact of the encapsulation process on performance cannot be ignored: in 2025, Wang et al. showed that the satellite resistance peaks in t-hBN ferroelectric devices are significantly modulated under different encapsulation environments, providing specific guidance for device encapsulation strategies[99]. **非线性反常霍尔效应**: Nonlinear anomalous Hall effect - A Hall effect response that is nonlinear in the applied electric field, occurring in systems with broken time-reversal symmetry and large Berry curvature dipoles.

> ## 3.4 自发光伏器件
> 
> ## 3.4 Spontaneous Photovoltaic Devices
> 
> 体光伏效应通常在非中心对称结构的材料中观察到(如LiNbO3[100]、BaTiO3[101]), 因其有望超越肖克利-奎塞尔(SQ)极限[102]而引起了广泛关注, 但其应用受限于弱内建电场及高带隙瓶颈. 滑移铁电材料的本征极化打破对称性, 可在原子尺度构建强电场, 为制备高效光能转换器件提供了新的路径. 2021年, Akamatsu等人构筑WSe2/黑磷(BP)面内极化异质结[103], 发现了自发光伏效应(图8(a)和(b)[103])且光电流方向受铁电极化调控, 并在理论上认为是一种量子位移电流机制. 2023年, Yang等人通过石墨烯屏蔽层抑制3R-MoS2的去极化场(图8(c))[104], 实现内建电场强化的自发光伏器件, 其外部量子效率(EQE)高达16%(10层3R-MoS2器件, 如图8(d)所示[104]). 后续Wu等人验证这种石墨烯/3R-MoS2界面耦合器件的光电流的电荷转移速度可快至2 ps(图8(e)和(f))[105]. 此外, 其他实验也证实, 通过边缘嵌入[106]、应变工程[107,108]、接触优化[109]等方案可以有效提升器件的体光伏系数. 未来, 结合超薄铁电体、非线性光学拓扑材料及异质界面设计, 将进一步推动高效光能转换与高速光电探测器的实际应用. The bulk photovoltaic effect (BPVE) is typically observed in materials with non-centrosymmetric structures (e.g., LiNbO3[100], BaTiO3[101]) and has attracted widespread attention due to its potential to surpass the Shockley-Queisser (SQ) limit[102], but its application is limited by weak built-in electric fields and high bandgap bottlenecks. The intrinsic polarization of sliding ferroelectric materials breaks symmetry, enabling the construction of strong electric fields at the atomic scale, providing a new pathway for preparing efficient photoconversion devices. In 2021, Akamatsu et al. constructed a WSe2/black phosphorus (BP) in-plane polarized heterojunction[103], discovering a spontaneous photovoltaic effect (Figures 8(a) and (b)[103]) where the photocurrent direction is controlled by ferroelectric polarization, and theoretically considered it to be a quantum shift current mechanism. In 2023, Yang et al. suppressed the depolarization field of 3R-MoS2 using a graphene screening layer (Figure 8(c))[104], achieving a spontaneous photovoltaic device with enhanced built-in electric field, exhibiting an external quantum efficiency (EQE) as high as 16% (in a 10-layer 3R-MoS2 device, as shown in Figure 8(d)[104]). Subsequently, Wu et al. verified that the charge transfer speed of the photocurrent in this graphene/3R-MoS2 interface-coupled device can be as fast as 2 ps (Figures 8(e) and (f))[105]. In addition, other experiments have also confirmed that the bulk photovoltaic coefficient of the device can be effectively improved through schemes such as edge embedding[106], strain engineering[107,108], and contact optimization[109]. In the future, combining ultra-thin ferroelectrics, nonlinear optical topological materials, and heterointerface design will further promote the practical application of efficient photoconversion and high-speed photodetectors. **肖克利-奎塞尔极限**: Shockley-Queisser (SQ) limit - The maximum theoretical efficiency of a single p-n junction solar cell, calculated based on the balance between absorption and radiative recombination. **外部量子效率**: External quantum efficiency (EQE) - The ratio of the number of charge carriers collected by a solar cell or photodetector to the number of incident photons.

> ## 3.5 铁电器件母体材料的生长
> 
> ## 3.5 Growth of Parent Materials for Ferroelectric Devices
> 
> 铁电器件的规模化制备需攻克大面积单晶母体材料的层控生长难题. 当前主流仍依赖机械剥离结合干法转移的实验室级微制备工艺, 虽然2022年Rogée等人尝试CVD法生长出微米级MoS2/WS2异质结[76], 但仍面临3R与2H相共存的相纯度问题. 近期突破集中在Liu团队的两项研究: 首先采用倾斜FeNi(111)单晶基底调控氮化硼生长模式, 利用原子台阶引导BN晶粒单轴定向排布(图8(g)[110]), 实现ABC堆垛的rBN薄膜(与hBN结构区别如图8(h)所示[110])的厘米级单晶制备, 并通过SHG、X射线衍射(X-ray diffraction, XRD)等测量手段证实, 如图8(i)和(j)所示[110]; 其次开发Ni-M(M=Mo, W)界面外延法(图8(k)和(l)[111]), 通过硫族源扩散合成晶圆级3R-TMDs(如MoS2、WS2)单晶薄膜(层数精准调控1~15000层), SHG、XRD测试如图8(m)和(n)所示[111]. 电学表征显示3R-MoS2双层与三层的室温载流子迁移率达155与190 cm2 V–1 s–1, 远超传统2H相材料(图8(o)[111]), 满足器件制备需求. 这些生长技术的突破为二维铁电材料从实验室微器件向工业级宏量制备的跨越奠定了基础. The large-scale preparation of ferroelectric devices requires overcoming the challenge of layer-controlled growth of large-area single-crystal parent materials. The current mainstream still relies on laboratory-level micro-fabrication processes combining mechanical exfoliation with dry transfer. Although Rogée et al. attempted to grow micron-scale MoS2/WS2 heterojunctions using the CVD method in 2022[76], they still faced the phase purity issue of 3R and 2H phases coexisting. Recent breakthroughs are concentrated in two studies by Liu's team: First, using a tilted FeNi(111) single-crystal substrate to control the growth mode of boron nitride, utilizing atomic steps to guide the uniaxial orientation of BN grains (Figure 8(g)[110]), achieving centimeter-scale single-crystal preparation of ABC-stacked rBN thin films (structural difference from hBN shown in Figure 8(h)[110]), and confirmed by measurement methods such as SHG and X-ray diffraction (XRD), as shown in Figures 8(i) and (j)[110]; Second, developing a Ni-M (M=Mo, W) interfacial epitaxy method (Figures 8(k) and (l)[111]), synthesizing wafer-scale 3R-TMDs (e.g., MoS2, WS2) single-crystal thin films via chalcogen source diffusion (with precise layer number control from 1 to 15,000 layers), with SHG and XRD tests shown in Figures 8(m) and (n)[111]. Electrical characterization shows that the room-temperature carrier mobility of bilayer and trilayer 3R-MoS2 reaches 155 and 190 cm2 V–1 s–1, far exceeding that of traditional 2H-phase materials (Figure 8(o)[111]), meeting the requirements for device fabrication. These breakthroughs in growth technology lay the foundation for the leap of two-dimensional ferroelectric materials from laboratory micro-devices to industrial-scale macro-preparation. **X射线衍射**: X-ray diffraction (XRD) - A technique used to determine the atomic and molecular structure of a crystal by measuring the angles and intensities of X-rays diffracted by the sample.

> ## 3.6 铁电拓扑绝缘体的应用前景
> 
> ## 3.6 Application Prospects of Ferroelectric Topological Insulators
> 
> 由极化反转引发的铁电性和由能带反转引发的拓扑性是两种被深入研究但又完全不同的材料特性. 它们之间并没有本质上的排斥, 因此当两者共存于单一材料中时, 便引出了铁电拓扑绝缘体(ferroelectric topological insulator, FETI)的概念[112]. 铁电性和能带拓扑的结合将实现对拓扑态的非易失性调控, 对要求低能耗和高响应速度的纳米电子学至关重要. 然而, 目前二维FETI材料的研究相当匮乏, 比如图7(k)和(l)展示的工作中通过研究WSe2/铁电WTe2异质结中铁电性对拓扑输运性质的影响[97], 观测到了非线性的反常量子霍尔效应. 但研究中提出的强关联体系中Hubbard带中Berry曲率偶极子的分布问题仍需进一步研究. 理论预测中, 众多研究者发现MnBi2Te4 (MBT)展现出独特的三重序参量耦合特性[113~116], 即层间滑动铁电性、可调控反铁磁序和量子反常霍尔态. MBT可在单相中实现拓扑绝缘态(C=1)与平庸绝缘态(C=0)的可逆切换, 实现铁电-拓扑序的强耦合, 并且可以借助极化翻转直接调控材料的金属绝缘体转变以及反常霍尔效应(AHE)和量子反常霍尔效应(AQHE)的切换. 此外, Bi[117,118]、In2Se3异质结[119,120]和多种滑移铁电材料中的铁电-拓扑态耦合的理论预测也有所进展[121,122], 但仍需实验研究跟进. Ferroelectricity, arising from polarization reversal, and topology, arising from band inversion, are two deeply studied but quite different material properties. There is no fundamental exclusion between them, so when both coexist in a single material, the concept of a ferroelectric topological insulator (FETI) emerges[112]. The combination of ferroelectricity and band topology will enable nonvolatile control of topological states, which is crucial for nanoelectronics requiring low power consumption and high response speed. However, current research on 2D FETI materials is quite scarce. For example, the work shown in Figures 7(k) and (l) observed a nonlinear anomalous Hall effect by studying the influence of ferroelectricity on the topological transport properties in a WSe2/ferroelectric WTe2 heterojunction[97]. However, the distribution of the Berry curvature dipole in the Hubbard bands within the strongly correlated system proposed in the study still needs further investigation. In theoretical predictions, many researchers have found that MnBi2Te4 (MBT) exhibits unique triple-order parameter coupling characteristics[113~116], namely interlayer sliding ferroelectricity, tunable antiferromagnetic order, and the quantum anomalous Hall state. MBT can achieve reversible switching between a topological insulating state (C=1) and a trivial insulating state (C=0) within a single phase, realizing strong coupling of ferroelectric-topological order, and can directly control the metal-insulator transition and the switching of the anomalous Hall effect (AHE) and quantum anomalous Hall effect (QAHE) through polarization reversal. In addition, theoretical predictions of ferroelectric-topological state coupling in Bi[117,118], In2Se3 heterojunctions[119,120], and various sliding ferroelectric materials have also progressed[121,122], but still require follow-up experimental research. **铁电拓扑绝缘体**: Ferroelectric topological insulator (FETI) - A material that simultaneously exhibits ferroelectricity and topological insulating properties, allowing for nonvolatile control of topological states. **量子反常霍尔效应**: Quantum anomalous Hall effect (QAHE) - A quantized version of the anomalous Hall effect, occurring in systems with intrinsic magnetic order and no external magnetic field.

> # 4 总结与展望
> 
> # 4 Summary and Outlook
> 
> 二维铁电材料作为突破传统铁电体微型化瓶颈的重要方向, 近年来取得了显著进展. 以离子位移机制为基础的自发极化铁电体通过界面屏蔽效应解决了退极化场干扰问题, 实现了单层厚度的室温铁电性. 然而, 其极化机制仍受限于晶格畸变与离子迁移, 难以进一步降低极化切换能耗. 在此背景下, 滑移铁电体的出现为二维铁电研究开辟了新范式. 通过范德华层间滑移对称性破缺和电荷再分布, 该类材料无需母体材料本征极性, 仅依赖层间滑移即可产生可逆极化, 并展现出超低能垒、高可调性与异质兼容性等诸多优势. 其中, 转角hBN与3R-TMDs的莫尔超晶格不仅揭示了畴壁运动与极化切换的微观机制, 还为多阻态存储和类脑计算提供了物理基础. 此外, 双层石墨烯的层间电荷转移铁电性, 通过与电子关联效应耦合, 催生出超快与高密度的存储潜力. Two-dimensional ferroelectric materials, as an important direction for breaking through the miniaturization bottleneck of traditional ferroelectrics, have made significant progress in recent years. Spontaneously polarized ferroelectrics based on the ion displacement mechanism have solved the problem of depolarization field interference through interfacial screening effects, achieving room-temperature ferroelectricity at monolayer thickness. However, their polarization mechanism is still limited by lattice distortion and ion migration, making it difficult to further reduce the energy consumption of polarization switching. Against this background, the emergence of sliding ferroelectrics has opened up a new paradigm for two-dimensional ferroelectric research. Through symmetry breaking by van der Waals interlayer sliding and charge redistribution, this type of material does not require intrinsic polarity of the parent material, can generate reversible polarization solely relying on interlayer sliding, and exhibits many advantages such as ultralow energy barriers, high tunability, and hetero-compatibility. Among them, the moiré superlattices of twisted hBN and 3R-TMDs have not only revealed the microscopic mechanism of domain wall motion and polarization switching but also provided a physical basis for multi-resistance state storage and neuromorphic computing. In addition, the interlayer charge transfer ferroelectricity in bilayer graphene, coupled with electronic correlation effects, has spawned the potential for ultrafast and high-density storage.
> 
> 调控与应用的探索展现了滑移铁电的广阔前景. 畴工程通过位错密度和钉扎效应优化, 可实现从陡峭双态到渐进多态的器件设计; 滑移铁电异质结通过界面能带调控, 将极化效应拓展至激子限域、非线性输运等光电领域. 此外, 自发光伏效应的开发以及异质集成工艺的突破, 为光-电-热多场耦合器件奠定了基础. The exploration of modulation and applications demonstrates the broad prospects of sliding ferroelectrics. Domain engineering, through optimization of dislocation density and pinning effects, can achieve device designs ranging from steep bistable states to gradual multistate states; sliding ferroelectric heterojunctions, through interfacial band modulation, extend the polarization effect to optoelectronic fields such as exciton confinement and nonlinear transport. In addition, the development of the spontaneous photovoltaic effect and breakthroughs in heterogeneous integration processes lay the foundation for photo-electro-thermal multi-field coupling devices.
> 
> 然而, 当前研究仍面临一些挑战: 部分滑移铁电的新奇物性(如极化-电子态耦合)的物理机制仍需深入探索; 高温环境下造成的滑移势垒变化、界面热膨胀失配等有关材料稳定性与极化切换动力学的研究较为匮乏; 器件应用中的循环寿命、响应速度等核心性能有待进一步验证与优化; 面向规模化的材料制备与异质集成和转角堆叠等工艺仍需技术突破. However, current research still faces some challenges: the physical mechanisms of some novel properties of sliding ferroelectrics (such as polarization-electronic state coupling) still need in-depth exploration; research on the stability of materials and the dynamics of polarization switching related to changes in sliding barriers under high-temperature environments and interfacial thermal expansion mismatch is relatively scarce; core performance metrics such as cycle life and response speed in device applications need further verification and optimization; and technologies for scalable material preparation, heterogeneous integration, and twist-angle stacking still require breakthroughs.
> 
> 展望未来, 二维铁电材料的研究将呈现以下趋势: (1) 理论层面, 融合量子力学与非平衡态动力学模型, 解析滑移-电子关联-晶格弛豫的多体耦合机制; (2) 材料层面, 探索新型滑移铁电体系, 开发兼具高极化强度与超快响应的功能材料; (3) 应用层面, 集成铁电调控与量子物态, 设计可重构光电器件与神经形态芯片. 随着跨学科研究的深入, 二维滑移铁电体有望成为后摩尔时代低功耗电子与智能感知技术的核心材料平台. Looking ahead, the research on two-dimensional ferroelectric materials will exhibit the following trends: (1) At the theoretical level, integrating quantum mechanics with non-equilibrium dynamics models to analyze the multi-body coupling mechanism of sliding-electron correlation-lattice relaxation; (2) At the material level, exploring new sliding ferroelectric systems and developing functional materials with both high polarization intensity and ultrafast response; (3) At the application level, integrating ferroelectric control with quantum states to design reconfigurable optoelectronic devices and neuromorphic chips. With the deepening of interdisciplinary research, two-dimensional sliding ferroelectrics are expected to become a core material platform for low-power electronics and intelligent sensing technologies in the post-Moore era.

> Two-dimensional (2D) ferroelectric materials have emerged as a revolutionary platform for next-generation nanoelectronics, offering unique properties distinct from traditional ferroelectrics. A key advantage lies in their significantly weakened depolarization field effects, enabling stable room-temperature ferroelectricity even at the monolayer limit. Conventional 2D ferroelectrics, such as CuInP2S6 and In2Se3, inherit the ionic displacement mechanism of their bulk counterparts, where spontaneous polarization arises from symmetry-breaking lattice distortions. Recent breakthroughs, however, have introduced a paradigm-shifting concept: sliding ferroelectricity. Unlike conventional models, this mechanism does not rely on intrinsic polarization of the parent material but exploits interlayer sliding in van der Waals (vdW) heterostructures (e.g., hexagonal boron nitride (hBN) and transition metal dichalcogenides (TMDs)) to induce macroscopic out-of-plane polarization via charge redistribution. The ultralow energy barrier for polarization switching in sliding ferroelectrics enables nonvolatile memory and low-power logic devices, while their compatibility with diverse material systems (conductors, semiconductors, insulators) expands possibilities for band engineering and multifunctional integration. Experimental advances in sliding ferroelectrics have validated their theoretical foundations. In twisted hBN heterostructures, electrostatic force microscopy and transport measurements revealed triangular ferroelectric domains with switchable polarization states, governed by interlayer sliding between AB/BA stacking configurations. Similarly, 2H-phase TMDs (e.g., MoS2, WSe2) exhibit sliding-induced polarization when reconfigured into rhombohedral (3R) stacking, as demonstrated by piezoresponse force microscopy and hysteretic transport behavior. Remarkably, even nonpolar systems like Bernal-stacked bilayer graphene manifest unconventional ferroelectricity under moiré potentials, driven by correlated electron interactions and interlayer charge transfer. Theoretical frameworks, including Berry phase polarization calculations and nudged elastic band simulations, have quantitatively linked sliding-induced symmetry breaking to macroscopic polarization, while differential charge density analysis elucidates the role of interfacial charge redistribution. These discoveries highlight the versatility of sliding ferroelectrics, offering routes to tailor polarization via stacking angles, layer numbers, and heterostructure design. The application potential of 2D ferroelectrics spans nonvolatile memory, optoelectronics, and neuromorphic computing. Prototype devices, such as sliding ferroelectric-based memories, achieve ultrafast switching speeds (~1 ns) and endurance exceeding 1011 cycles. In optoelectronic heterostructures, ferroelectric polarization modulates exciton localization and photovoltaic responses, enabling gate-tunable photodetectors with high quantum efficiency. Challenges remain, however, in understanding dynamic polarization switching under thermal/mechanical stress, scaling up high-quality material synthesis, and integrating 2D ferroelectrics with silicon-based technologies. Future research directions include exploring the coupling between ferroelectricity and topological states, engineering multistable polarization configurations for neuromorphic applications, and developing wafer-scale growth techniques for industrial adoption.
