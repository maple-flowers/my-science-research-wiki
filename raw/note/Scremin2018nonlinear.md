> [!quote]- <center>Abstract</center>
> 
> Nolinear Absorption of CT TFF+ aggregates in PMMA matrix was studied with resonant excitation around 800 nm in the femtoseconds regime with the Z-Scan technique with 100 Hz pulse repetition rate. The saturated absorption profiles were fitted with a prepared formula based on steady state rate equations for a homogeneous ensemble of two levels systems, thin samples and quasi monochromatic field without the usual assumption of low saturation regime. The lifetime of the CT state, with the use of this model was estimated in hundred of fs, of the same order of magnitude of the exciting pulse.
>
>【摘要翻译】采用Z扫描技术，在800 nm共振激发下，研究了CT-TFF+聚集体在PMMA基体中的非线性吸收。在不考虑低饱和区假设的情况下，用基于稳态速率方程的公式拟合了两能级系统、薄样品和准单色场的均匀系综的饱和吸收分布。使用此模型，CT状态的寿命以百fs为单位进行估计，与激励脉冲的数量级相同。

> ## 一、引言
> 
> 本文献《Nonlinear absorption of tetrathiafulvalene radical cation (TTF⁺) based charge transfer (CT) aggregates in PMMA》聚焦于**四硫富瓦烯自由基阳离子（TTF⁺）** 这一重要有机电子材料。TTF及其衍生物因其独特的π电子体系和三种稳定的氧化态（TTF⁰, TTF⁺, TTF²⁺），在超分子化学、传感器、逻辑门和氧化还原开关等领域展现出巨大潜力。
> 
> 本研究的核心问题是：**在飞秒激光脉冲激发下，嵌入聚甲基丙烯酸甲酯（PMMA）薄膜中的TTF⁺电荷转移（CT）聚集体的非线性吸收特性为何？其激发态寿命有多长？**
> 
> 研究的独特价值在于：
> 
> 1.  **首次探索**：据作者所知，这是首次在超快时间尺度上研究此类TTF⁺ CT聚集体的非线性光学性质。
> 2.  **方法创新**：作者开发了一种**无需假设低饱和强度**的拟合公式，用于处理Z-Scan（Z扫描）实验数据，并直接从中提取激发态寿命，这是对传统方法的重要改进。
> 3.  **关键发现**：估算出的CT态寿命在**数百飞秒**量级，与激发脉冲宽度相当，这一发现引发了对稳态模型假设适用性的深刻反思。
> 
> 本报告的定位是为初学者、跨学科研究者及需要快速掌握核心观点的读者，提供一个条理清晰、深入浅出的结构化解读。

> ## 四、正文核心内容剖析
> 
> 本节将正文内容拆解为“研究背景”、“方法与模型”、“实验结果与讨论”三部分，进行更细致的阐述。
> 
> #### 1. 研究背景与挑战
> 
> -   **研究对象**：TTF⁺自由基阳离子形成的**电荷转移（CT）聚集体**。当TTF⁺分子相互靠近时，会发生电荷转移，形成二聚体或多聚体，并在吸收光谱上出现特征的、宽且强的CT吸收带。
> -   **研究难点**：直接研究TTF盐晶体或溶液中的非线性光学性质非常困难。
>     -   **晶体**：光学密度极高，导致透射光信号过弱，无法进行透射式测量。
>     -   **溶液**：TTF⁺在极性溶剂中会发生歧化反应，体系不稳定。
> -   **解决方案**：作者采用**溶剂挥发法**，将TTFClO₄盐分散在PMMA（一种透明的有机玻璃）基质中，制成厚度仅30 μm的薄膜。这不仅解决了透光性问题，还通过PMMA（聚甲基丙烯酸甲酯）的固态环境稳定了TTF⁺的歧化反应平衡，使得研究其CT聚集体的超快光学性质成为可能。
> 
> #### 2. 方法、模型与实验
> 
> -   **核心技术：Z-Scan**：
>     -   **原理**：一种测量材料非线性光学性质（如非线性吸收和非线性折射）的标准技术。样品在聚焦高斯光束的焦点附近沿光轴（Z方向）移动，经历连续变化的光强。通过测量远场透射光功率随位置Z的变化，就可以反推出材料的非线性光学系数。
>     -   **本实验配置**：采用**开孔**方案，即收集所有透射光，因此对**非线性吸收**敏感，对非线性折射不敏感。实验在100 Hz的脉冲重复频率下进行，以避免热效应的累积。
> -   **模型构建：超越低饱和强度近似**

> ### 1. 什么是Z扫描技术？为什么它如此重要？
> 
> **解答**：Z扫描是一种通过测量样品透过率随其位置（相对于激光焦点）的变化，来表征其非线性光学性质的简单而强大的技术。想象一个凸透镜聚焦阳光，焦点的光强极高。将样品放在焦点附近来回移动，光强就会连续变化：在焦点处，光强极大，非线性效应显著；远离焦点，光强减弱，表现为线性行为。记录下透过光功率随样品位置（Z轴）的变化曲线，就能分析出材料的非线性吸收（开孔Z扫描）或非线性折射（闭孔Z扫描）。其重要性在于，它只需单光束，无需复杂的泵浦-探测装置，就能快速、灵敏地测量非线性光学系数。
> 
> ### 2. 文中反复提到的“稳态假设”和“非低饱和强度假设”分别是什么意思？
> 
> **解答**：
> 
> -   **稳态假设**：指在一个激光脉冲作用期间，系统的激发态粒子数能够瞬间达到一个动态平衡，不再随时间变化。这通常要求激发态的寿命远短于激光脉冲宽度，这样系统才能“跟上”光强的变化。
> -   **低饱和强度假设**：指输入激光的强度远小于材料的饱和强度，即I << I_sat。此时，吸收系数可以近似为α ≈ α₀(1 - I/I_sat + ...)，使得数学处理大大简化。本文的模型正是放弃了这个假设，允许I与I_sat可以比拟，因此公式保持了更复杂的原始形式。
> 
> ### 3. 为什么激发态寿命与脉冲宽度相当，稳态模型就失效了？
> 
> **解答**：稳态模型失效的原因在于**时间尺度不匹配**。可以把稳态模型想象成一个金鱼缸，水龙头滴水（激发）的速度和缸底小孔漏水（弛豫）的速度一样快，所以水位（激发态粒子数）能保持恒定。但如果一滴水是一次性泼进去的（超短脉冲），而漏水速度却很慢（长寿命），那么在整个脉冲持续期间，水位会持续上涨，远未达到稳态。本文的情况是，脉冲（125 fs）就像一盆水，而弛豫时间（数百fs）只比它慢一点，水位在脉冲结束时才达到最高，整个过程都是瞬态变化，稳态近似自然就不准了。
> 
> ### 4. 文中模型引入的权重因子 c_r 和 c_t 是做什么用的？
> 
> **解答**：这两个因子是处理复杂积分时的数学“技巧”。当我们要计算一个高斯光束（空间上中间强、边缘弱）和一个高斯脉冲（时间上中间强、前后弱）通过非线性样品后的总能量时，会碰到非常复杂的积分。作者的想法是，用一个“等效均匀强度”来替代这个复杂的高斯分布。c_r ≈ 0.5 意味着空间上的积分效果，近似等于用峰值强度的一半作为平均强度来计算。c_t ≈ 0.7 对时间上也做了类似处理。这虽然不是精确解，但通过数值验证，误差很小（约2%），换来了一个可以用软件直接拟合的简洁公式，极大地简化了数据分析。

> Introduction
> 引言
> 
> TTF is studied even in supramolecular chemistry1 for its p electron system and for the presence of three stable oxidation states that makes it attractive in the perspective of exploitation1 as sensor, in logic gates and in redox switching. Here the nonlinear absorption properties of aggregates of the radical cation TTF+ are studied in ultrafast regime. To our knowledge there are no examples of this kind of study in the literature. TTF+ CT systems were included in PMMA matrix according to a previously described procedure2 via solvent casting. The samples were 30 μm thick, and allowed to measure nonlinear optical properties with the Z-Scan technique3, thus in a transmission configuration. Peculiar of the TTF+ dimers and aggregates is the intense broad CT absorption band around 800 nm (fig. 1).
> TTF因其π电子系统和三种稳定氧化态的存在，在超分子化学领域也得到研究¹，这使其在传感器、逻辑门和氧化还原开关等应用前景中具有吸引力¹。本文研究了TTF⁺自由基阳离子聚集体在超快状态下的非线性吸收特性。据我们所知，文献中尚无此类研究的例子。TTF⁺ CT体系按照先前报道的步骤²，通过溶剂浇铸法包含在PMMA基质中。样品厚度为30 μm，允许使用Z扫描技术³以透射光路配置测量非线性光学特性。TTF⁺二聚体和聚集体的一个独特之处在于其在800 nm附近存在强烈的宽CT吸收带（图1）。

> TTF+ salts form CT dimers in polar solvents4 like DMSO but solutions are unstable2 and crystals have high optical density preventing the measurements of their nonlinear optical properties in transmission configurations and for this reason a strategy of obtaining thins samples including in PMMA TTF+ aggregates (from TTFClO4) was developed in a previous work2. In fig. 1 absorption spectra of TTF+ aggregates are shown for a thin film under vacuum and after some hours of air exposure: it was possible to notice that TTF+ in PMMA formed up to a certain extent with air exposure TTF0 (band around 300 nm) and TTF+ TTF0 based aggregates (band at 1400 nm). The interpretation was that the oxygen bound TTF2+ and formed an S-oxide shifts the dismutation equilibrium of TTF+ with the consequent formation of TTF+ TTF0. This was in line with the previously studied dismutation reaction in solution2 with a difference: in solutions, the dismutation reaction of TTF+ fully evolved toward TTF0 and TTF2+, with the complete disappearance of the CT band of TTF+ dimers and aggregates2 while in PMMA at least the reaction reached an equilibrium in which the CT band was still present. The appearance of a new band around 1400 nm may be due to the formation of mixed valence aggregates, and may be representative of a lower energy charge transfer. In a first approximation, it was assumed to have excited around 800 nm aggregates of TTF+ since this CT band was detected in dimers1 and higher order aggregates2,8 in PMMA.

> Experimental, Modelling and Results
> 实验、建模与结果
> 
> The Z-Scan technique was chosen to measure the absorptive nonlinearity in the open aperture scheme3. Briefly the thin sample was translated along a focused Gaussian beam experiencing in this way a continuous variation if the input intensity. A large area photodiode acquired the signal in the far field. The exciting system was constituted of a mode locked Titanium: Sapphire laser (Tsunami, Spectra Physics) pumped by an Ar+ (Beam Lock, Spectra Physics) coupled to a regenerative amplifier with pulse stretcher and compressor (Spitfire, Spectra Physics) pumped by a solid-state intracavity doubled Nd: YLF laser (Merlin, Spectra Physics). The system delivered hundred fs pulses tuneable around 800 nm and repetition rate accordable between 1 KHz and 1 Hz. The Z-Scan setup consisted of a focusing lens (f=160 mm) aligned with a computer controlled (Lab View, National Instruments) motorized (MM2500 Newport) micrometric delay line (resolution 1 μm) on which the sample is mounted. The output signal was acquired in the far field with a large area silicon photodiode connected to a digital oscilloscope (TDS 520B 500 MHz Tektronix). The pulse temporal width was measured with an autocorrelator (Single Shot Autocorrelator SSA Positive Light), the pulse energy was measured with a pyroelectric detector (J3 Joulemeter Molectron) and the beam spatial profile was monitored with a CCD camera. The experimental result here reported (fig. 2) was obtained with 100 Hz of pulse repetition rate since from the tests it resulted not affected from cumulative or thermal effects on the basis of the nonlinearity response time5. The beam parameters were all experimentally determined allowing performing absolute measurements. The Z-Scan profile was fitted with an ad hoc analytical function formulated on the basis of a homogeneous ensemble of two levels system in steady state6:

> According to the obtained results it was possible to notice that under the errors the lifetime of the excited state was of the same order of magnitude of the exciting pulse width, which made weak the assumption of a steady state used to model the ensemble of two levels system (eq. 1) and to obtain the absorption coefficient (eq. 2). In 2006 a paper Gu et al. 9 developed a theoretical model for open aperture Z-scan based on the application of the Adomian decomposition method10, to avoid the initial assumption of low saturation regime used in the original formulation3, but they used steady state models for the absorption coefficient and calculated from the final data fitting the saturation intensity, which is in reality a function of the excited state lifetime. In the present work the assumption of low saturation regime ( input intensity << saturation intensity) was not necessary, the approach uses approximations to evaluate transcendent forms in integrals and did not use series expansions as in ref. 9; the final formula was quite simple (once the experimental parameters values are introduced), used experimentally determined values for the beam parameters and the sample and could be easily introduced in common data analysis programs to perform the fitting of the experimental Z-scan traces provided that a steady state assumption might subsist. > Conclusions
> 结论
> 
> The lifetime of the CT state for TTF+ aggregates was estimated from Z-Scan experimental profiles. The profile fitting was performed with an ad hoc formulated fitting function based on a homogeneous ensemble of two levels systems in steady state. In this way no a priori hypothesis on the magnitude of the saturation intensity respect to the input intensity had to be assumed, since the conventional formulation is based in the low saturation regime hypothesis and had bad performances in fitting the experimental data, since in the femtosecond regime the intensity is usually quite high and nonlinearity may have contribution from orders higher than the third. Therefore the obtained lifetime, of the same order of magnitude of the pulse may give rise to questions regarding the adequacy of a steady state hypothesis. Moreover pure TTF+ based systems resulted very difficult to handle: in air they evolved up to an equilibrium among TTF+, TTF0, TTF2+. Around 800 nm it was excited a CT transition of TTF+ aggregates/dimers around 800 nm, in such a case it may be adequate the modelling with a two level system. As discussed the lifetime of the excited state extracted from the data fitting was of the same order of magnitude of the exciting pulse duration and the steady state assumption for the calculation of the absorption coefficient could be not robust enough. In the literature the parameter extracted from the fittings is the nonlinear absorption, the fitting performs well, but the timescales are masked since the lifetimes usually are not calculated, then the steady state regime assumption under all the available fitting formulas is not discussed.
> 从Z扫描实验曲线中估算出了TTF⁺聚集体的CT态寿命。曲线拟合是通过一个基于均匀二能级系统稳态模型而专门制定的拟合函数来完成的。通过这种方式，无需对饱和光强相对于输入光强的大小作先验假设，因为传统公式基于低饱和区假设，在拟合实验数据时表现不佳，这是因为在飞秒状态下，光强通常相当高，且非线性可能来自高于三阶的贡献。因此，获得的与脉冲宽度同数量级的寿命，可能会引发对稳态假设充分性的质疑。此外，纯TTF⁺基体系被证明非常难以处理：在空气中，它们会演化至TTF⁺、TTF⁰、TTF²⁺之间的平衡。在800 nm附近，激发的是TTF⁺聚集体/二聚体的CT跃迁，这种情况下，用二能级系统建模可能是合适的。正如所讨论的，从数据拟合中提取的激发态寿命与激发脉冲宽度处于同一数量级，计算吸收系数时采用的稳态假设可能不够稳健。在文献中，从拟合中提取的参数是非线性吸收，拟合表现良好，但时间尺度被掩盖了，因为通常不计算寿命，因此，在所有可用拟合公式下的稳态状态假设并未得到讨论。
