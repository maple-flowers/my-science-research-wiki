> # Switching of charge-density wave by carrier tuning in monolayer $\text{TiTe}_2$
> 
> 单层 $\text{TiTe}_2$ 中通过载流子调谐实现电荷密度波的切换 **Charge-density wave (CDW)**: 电荷密度波，指固体中电子密度的周期性调制。
> 
> * * *
> 
> ## Abstract
> 
> ### 摘要
> 
> How charge-density wave (CDW) is influenced by the change in the dimensionality is a fundamental question in condensed-matter physics. Monolayer titanium ditelluride ($\text{TiTe}_2$) undergoes $2\times2$ CDW despite the absence of CDW in bulk counterpart, whereas the mechanism of CDW is under intensive debate. Here we show that the CDW of monolayer $\text{TiTe}_2$ can be conveniently switched by carrier tuning via controlling the Te ratio during molecular-beam epitaxy or depositing potassium (K) atoms on the surface. This is demonstrated by monitoring the CDW-induced $2\times2$ band folding using angle-resolved photoemission spectroscopy. Remarkably, the CDW appears only when the inner hole pocket at the $\Gamma$ point and the elongated electron pocket at the M point show partial Fermi-surface nesting. The present study suggests that the carrier doping is an effective method to pin down the mechanism of CDW in atomic-layer transition-metal dichalcogenides.
> 
> 电荷密度波 (CDW) 如何受维度变化的影响是凝聚态物理学中的一个基本问题。单层二碲化钛 ($\text{TiTe}_2$) 经历了 $2\times2$ CDW，尽管其体材料对应物中不存在 CDW，而 CDW 的机制目前仍存在激烈争论。在这里，我们表明单层 $\text{TiTe}_2$ 的 CDW 可以通过在分子束外延过程中控制碲 (Te) 的比率或在表面沉积钾 (K) 原子，通过载流子调谐来方便地切换。我们通过利用角分辨光电子能谱监测 CDW 诱导的 $2\times2$ 能带折叠证明了这一点。值得注意的是，只有当 $\Gamma$ 点的内空穴口袋和 M 点的延展电子口袋显示出部分费米面嵌套时，CDW 才会出现。本研究表明，载流子掺杂是确定原子层过渡金属硫族化合物中 CDW 机制的一种有效方法。
> 
> **Molecular-beam epitaxy (MBE)**: 分子束外延，一种在超高真空环境下生长高质量单晶薄膜的技术。 **Angle-resolved photoemission spectroscopy (ARPES)**: 角分辨光电子能谱，用于测量固体材料电子能带结构的实验技术。 **Fermi-surface nesting**: 费米面嵌套，指费米面上的两个区域可以通过一个单一的波向量 $\mathbf{q}$ 相互重叠，是驱动 CDW 的常见机制。 **Transition-metal dichalcogenides (TMDs)**: 过渡金属硫族化合物，一类由过渡金属和硫族元素组成的层状材料。
> 
> * * *
> 
> ## I. INTRODUCTION
> 
> ### I. 引言
> 
> Initiated by the discovery of massless Dirac fermion in graphene and associated quantum phenomena such as half integer quantum Hall effect and valley Hall effect [1, 2], a search for new two-dimensional (2D) materials hosting exotic physical properties is now becoming one of the central topics in material science. Transition-metal dichalcogenides (TMDs) offer an excellent platform to realize new 2D materials, not only because bulk TMDs already show outstanding physical properties such as superconductivity, charge-/spin-density wave (CDW/SDW), magnetic order [3], and exotic topological phases [4–6], but also because its layered structure is suitable to study the evolution of electronic states from the bulk to the thinnest monolayer limit. Perhaps CDW is a most fundamental and longstanding issue in the bulk and ultrathin TMDs, because many TMDs undergo CDW with a variety of lattice periodicities, essentially due to the low-dimensional (2D or quasi-2D) nature of electronic states which enhances the electronic instability toward the CDW transition. Also, since the CDW phase often appears in or close to other quantum phases such as Mott-insulator [7, 8], superconductivity [9], and magnetic phases [10, 11], its interplay (such as competition and coexistence) has been fiercely debated.
> 
> 由在石墨烯中发现无质量狄拉克费米子以及相关的量子现象（如半整数量子霍尔效应和谷霍尔效应）[1, 2] 所引发，寻找具有奇异物理性质的新型二维 (2D) 材料现已成为材料科学中的核心课题之一。过渡金属二硫族化合物 (TMDs) 为实现新型二维材料提供了绝佳平台，这不仅因为体材料 TMDs 已展现出超导性、电荷/自旋密度波 (CDW/SDW)、磁序 [3] 以及奇异拓扑相 [4-6] 等卓越物理性质，还因为其层状结构适合研究电子态从体材料到最薄单层极限的演化。或许 CDW 是体材料及超薄 TMDs 中最基本且长期存在的问题，因为许多 TMDs 会经历具有多种晶格周期性的 CDW，这本质上源于电子态的低维 (2D 或准 2D) 性质，增强了向 CDW 转变的电子不稳定性。此外，由于 CDW 相通常出现在或接近于其他量子相（如莫特绝缘体 [7, 8]、超导性 [9] 和磁相 [10, 11]），其相互作用（如竞争与共存）一直备受激烈争论。
> 
> **Mott-insulator**: 莫特绝缘体，一种由于强电子库仑相互作用而导致原本应为金属的材料变为绝缘体的状态。
> 
> Titanium ditelluride ($\text{TiTe}_2$) with octahedra (1T-) structure is an excellent platform to study the interplay between CDW and dimensionality, because it was reported to show intriguing differences in the CDW properties between bulk and monolayer. Bulk $\text{TiTe}_2$ is a simple semimetal [12] characterized by the compensated quasi-2D hole pockets at the $\Gamma / A$ point and a strongly warped quasi-2D electron pocket at the L point of the bulk Brillouin zone (BZ) [13, 14], and it does not exhibit CDW. On the other hand, the monolayer counterpart [see Fig. 1(a) for the crystal structure] was recently reported to show CDW accompanied by $2\times2$ superstructure. This periodicity is identical to that observed in isostructural monolayer $\text{TiSe}_2$ in which the CDW phase is proposed to be stabilized by the condensation of excitons [15] as in the case of bulk $\text{TiSe}_2$ showing similar $2\times2\times2$ CDW [16–20]. Thus, the contrasting CDW behavior between bulk and monolayer is a peculiar characteristic of $\text{TiTe}_2$. To account for this unusual CDW, previous ARPES studies have proposed several key ingredients such as electron-phonon coupling and orbital selective hybridization [14, 21], although the mechanism of CDW is still unclear. Examination of the Fermi surface (FS) nesting condition via controlling the carrier concentration is useful to pin down the origin of CDW, whereas such studies have not been reported in monolayer $\text{TiTe}_2$.
> 
> 具有八面体 (1T-) 结构的二碲化钛 ($\text{TiTe}_2$) 是研究 CDW 与维度相互作用的绝佳平台，因为它被报道在体材料和单层之间表现出 CDW 特性上的有趣差异。体材料 $\text{TiTe}_2$ 是一种简单的半金属 [12]，其特征是在 $\Gamma/A$ 点存在补偿的准二维空穴口袋，在体布里渊区 (BZ) 的 L 点存在强畸变的准二维电子口袋 [13, 14]，且不表现出 CDW。另一方面，其单层对应物 [晶体结构参见图 1(a)] 最近被报道表现出伴随 $2\times2$ 超结构的 CDW。这种周期性与同构的单层 $\text{TiSe}_2$ 中观察到的相同，在该材料中，CDW 相被认为是由于激子凝聚 [15] 稳定化的，正如体材料 $\text{TiSe}_2$ 表现出类似的 $2\times2\times2$ CDW [16-20] 的情况。因此，体材料和单层之间截然不同的 CDW 行为是 $\text{TiTe}_2$ 的一个特殊特征。为了解释这种不寻常的 CDW，之前的 ARPES 研究提出了几个关键因素，如电子-声子耦合和轨道选择性杂化 [14, 21]，尽管 CDW 的机制仍然不清楚。通过控制载流子浓度来检查费米面 (FS) 的嵌套条件，有助于确定 CDW 的起源，而这类研究在单层 $\text{TiTe}_2$ 中尚未报道。
> 
> **Brillouin zone (BZ)**: 布里渊区，晶格在倒空间中的基本单元。
> 
> * * *
> 
> ## II. EXPERIMENTS AND CALCULATION
> 
> ### II. 实验与计算
> 
> ### A. Sample fabrication
> 
> #### A. 样品制备
> 
> High-quality monolayer $\text{TiTe}_2$ film was grown on bilayer graphene by the MBE method [7, 8, 15]. First, bilayer graphene was fabricated by resistive heating of n-type $\text{4H-SiC(0001)}$ single-crystal wafer at $1100\text{ }^\circ\text{C}$ for 30 min under high vacuum better than $1.0\times10^{-9}\text{ Torr}$. Subsequently, monolayer $\text{TiTe}_2$ film was grown by evaporating Ti atoms on the bilayer graphene substrate kept at $400\sim440\text{ }^\circ\text{C}$ in Te-rich atmosphere, and then the as-grown monolayer $\text{TiTe}_2$ film was annealed for 30 min at $400\text{ }^\circ\text{C}$. The growth process was monitored by the reflection high-energy electron diffraction (RHEED). After the growth, the film was transferred to the ARPES measurement chamber without exposing it to air. As seen in Fig. 1(b), the RHEED pattern for $\text{TiTe}_2$ film shows a sharp $1\times1$ streak pattern, confirming the high crystallinity of film.
> 
> 高质量的单层 $\text{TiTe}_2$ 薄膜是通过 MBE 方法 [7, 8, 15] 在双层石墨烯上生长而得。首先，在优于 $1.0\times10^{-9}\text{ Torr}$ 的高真空下，通过电阻加热 n-型 $\text{4H-SiC(0001)}$ 单晶片 $1100\text{ }^\circ\text{C}$ 持续 30 分钟制备双层石墨烯。随后，在富 Te 气氛中，将温度保持在 $400\sim440\text{ }^\circ\text{C}$ 的双层石墨烯基板上蒸发 Ti 原子，生长单层 $\text{TiTe}_2$ 薄膜，并将生长的单层 $\text{TiTe}_2$ 薄膜在 $400\text{ }^\circ\text{C}$ 退火 30 分钟。生长过程通过反射高能电子衍射 (RHEED) 监测。生长后，薄膜被转移到 ARPES 测量室，且未暴露于空气中。如图 1(b) 所示，$\text{TiTe}_2$ 薄膜的 RHEED 图案显示出锐利的 $1\times1$ 条纹图案，证实了薄膜的高结晶性。
> 
> **Reflection high-energy electron diffraction (RHEED)**: 反射高能电子衍射，一种用于实时监测薄膜生长表面结构和质量的分析技术。
> 
> ### B. ARPES measurements
> 
> #### B. ARPES 测量
> 
> ARPES measurements were carried out using a DA-30 electron energy analyzer with a micro-focused synchrotron-radiation at the beamline BL-28A of Photon Factory (KEK) [22] and a MBS A1 electron energy analyzer at the beamline BL5U in UVSOR. The energy and angular resolutions were set to be $25\text{--}50\text{ meV}$ and $0.2\text{--}0.3^\circ$, respectively. The Fermi level ($E_F$) of the sample was calibrated with a gold film deposited onto the substrate.
> 
> ARPES 测量使用 DA-30 电子能谱仪和微聚焦同步辐射光源在 Photon Factory (KEK) 的 BL-28A 光束线 [22] 以及位于 UVSOR 的 BL5U 光束线中的 MBS A1 电子能谱仪进行。能量和角分辨率分别设置为 $25\text{--}50\text{ meV}$ 和 $0.2\text{--}0.3^\circ$。样品的费米能级 ($E_F$) 使用沉积在衬底上的金膜进行校准。
> 
> ### C. Band-structure calculations
> 
> #### C. 能带结构计算
> 
> First-principles band-structure calculations were carried out by using Quantum-Espresso package [23, 24] with generalized gradient approximation [25]. The plane-wave cutoff energy and uniform k-point mesh were set to be $60\text{ Ry}$ and $12\times12\times1$, respectively. The thickness of inserted vacuum layer was set to be more than $10\text{ \AA}$ to prevent interlayer interaction.
> 
> 采用 Quantum-Espresso 软件包 [23, 24] 结合广义梯度近似 [25] 进行第一性原理能带结构计算。平面波截断能量和均匀 k-点网格分别设置为 $60\text{ Ry}$ 和 $12\times12\times1$。插入的真空层厚度设置为超过 $10\text{ \AA}$ 以防止层间相互作用。
> 
> * * *
> 
> ## III. RESULTS AND DISCUSSION
> 
> ### III. 结果与讨论
> 
> ### A. Electronic structure of monolayer $\text{TiTe}_2$
> 
> #### A. 单层 $\text{TiTe}_2$ 的电子结构
> 
> First we present the electronic states of pristine monolayer $\text{TiTe}_2$. Figure 1(c) shows energy distribution curves (EDCs) in the valence-band region measured along the $\Gamma\text{--}M$ cut of the hexagonal BZ at $T = 180\text{ K}$ above the CDW transition temperature ($T_{\text{CDW}} \sim 90\text{ K}$). One can recognize a couple of hole bands crossing $E_F$ centered at the $\Gamma$ point and a shallow electron band in the vicinity of $E_F$ centered at the M point, which are assigned to the Te 5p and Ti 3d orbitals, respectively [13, 14]. Corresponding ARPES-intensity and second-derivative plots shown in Fig. 1(d) signify an M-shaped band around the $\Gamma$ point at $\sim 1.5\text{ eV}$ besides the $\Gamma$-centered hole and M-centered electron bands, in reasonable agreement with the calculated band structure of monolayer 1T-$\text{TiTe}_2$ shown in Fig. 1(e). These results indicate that pristine monolayer $\text{TiTe}_2$ is a compensated semimetal with a negative band gap, consistent with the previous studies [13, 14]. As shown in the left panel of Fig. 1(f), the overall ARPES intensity at $T = 30\text{ K}$ appears to be similar to that at $T = 180\text{ K}$. On the other hand, there exists an extra intensity below the electron band at the M point at $T = 30\text{ K}$ as indicated by a white arrow. Such an extra intensity is completely absent at $T = 180\text{ K}$. As seen from the second derivative intensity plot in the right panel of Fig. 1(f), this extra feature is the replica of the two hole bands at the $\Gamma$ point which are folded to the M point due to the $2\times2$ periodic potential of CDW [14, 21, 27], as also clearly seen from the momentum distribution curves (MDCs) around the M point (see Fig. S1 in Supplemental Material [26]). Moreover, by following the periodicity of reconstructed BZ [see the original and reconstructed BZs in Fig. 1(g)], the shallow electron band at the M point is folded back to the $\Gamma$ point (see Fig. S2 in Supplemental Material [26]). These results strongly suggest that the electronic structure at $T = 30\text{ K}$ is reconstructed by the formation of $2\times2$ CDW, consistent with the previous reports [14, 21, 27]. We estimated the intensity ratio of folded hole bands around the M point relative to that of the main hole bands around the $\Gamma$ point to be $\sim 10%$, which is distinct from the case of $T = 180\text{ K}$ in which the intensity ratio is $0%$ because of the complete absence of the folded hole bands. It is also noted that the spectral weight of folded bands is much weaker than that of isostructural monolayer $\text{TiSe}_2$ (and also bulk $\text{TiSe}_2$) [15, 18, 19, 28, 29]. We will come back to this point later.
> 
> 首先，我们展示原始单层 $\text{TiTe}_2$ 的电子态。图 1(c) 显示了在高于电荷密度波转变温度 ($T_{\text{CDW}} \sim 90\text{ K}$) 的 $T = 180\text{ K}$ 时，沿六边形布里渊区 $\Gamma\text{--}M$ 切线方向测量的价带区域能量分布曲线 (EDCs)。可以识别出几对穿过 $E_F$ 且中心位于 $\Gamma$ 点的空穴带，以及在 $E_F$ 附近、中心位于 M 点的浅电子带，它们分别归因于 Te 5p 和 Ti 3d 轨道 [13, 14]。图 1(d) 中显示的对应 ARPES 强度和二阶导数图表明，除了 $\Gamma$ 中心空穴带和 M 中心电子带外，在 $\Gamma$ 点附近 $\sim 1.5\text{ eV}$ 处存在一个 M 形能带，这与图 1(e) 中显示的单层 1T-$\text{TiTe}_2$ 的能带结构基本一致。这些结果表明，原始单层 $\text{TiTe}_2$ 是一种补偿型半金属，具有负带隙，与先前的研究 [13, 14] 一致。如图 1(f) 左图所示，在 $T = 30\text{ K}$ 时的整体 ARPES 强度与 $T = 180\text{ K}$ 时相似。另一方面，在 $T = 30\text{ K}$ 时，M 点电子带下方存在一个额外强度，如白色箭头所示。这种额外强度在 $T = 180\text{ K}$ 时完全消失。从图 1(f) 右图中的二阶导数强度图可以看出，这一额外特征是 $\Gamma$ 点两个空穴带的副本，由于 $2\times2$ CDW 的周期性势场而折叠到 M 点 [14, 21, 27]，这一点也清晰地从 M 点附近的动量分布曲线 (MDCs) 中看出（参见补充材料 [26] 中的图 S1）。此外，通过观察重构 BZ 的周期性 [参见图 1(g) 中的原始 BZ 和重构 BZ]，M 点的浅电子带被折叠回 $\Gamma$ 点（参见补充材料 [26] 中的图 S2）。这些结果强烈表明，在 $T = 30\text{ K}$ 时的电子结构是由 $2\times2$ CDW 的形成而重构的，与先前的报道 [14, 21, 27] 一致。我们估计 M 点周围折叠空穴带相对于 $\Gamma$ 点周围主空穴带的强度比约为 $10%$，这与 $T = 180\text{ K}$ 时的情况不同，此时强度比为 $0%$，因为完全不存在折叠空穴带。还应注意，折叠带的谱重远弱于同构单层 $\text{TiSe}_2$（以及体材料 $\text{TiSe}_2$）[15, 18, 19, 28, 29]。我们稍后会回到这一点。

> **Energy distribution curves (EDCs)**: 能量分布曲线，在固定动量 $\mathbf{k}$ 下记录的光电子强度随能量变化的曲线。 **Momentum distribution curves (MDCs)**: 动量分布曲线，在固定能量 $E$ 下记录的光电子强度随动量 $\mathbf{k}$ 变化的曲线。

> ### B. Carrier doping effect on the CDW
> 
> #### B. 载流子掺杂对 CDW 的影响
> 
> Next, we present how the CDW is influenced by the change in the carrier concentration. While the previous studies have focused on the experiments on the pristine sample, we are able to modulate the carrier concentration by adopting two methods, i.e. (i) changing the substrate temperature $T_s$ during the epitaxy to effectively control the stoichiometry of the sample, and (ii) systematically depositing potassium (K) atoms on pristine $\text{TiTe}_2$. Figures 2(a)-2(c) show (top panel) ARPES intensity and (bottom panel) corresponding second-derivative intensity plots along the $\Gamma\text{--}M$ cut, measured at $T = 40\text{ K}$, for three different $T_s$ values of $400, 420,$ and $440\text{ }^\circ\text{C}$, respectively; note that the data for $T_s = 420\text{ }^\circ\text{C}$ were already presented in Fig. 1. One can recognize a systematic change in the band structure upon increasing $T_s$. For example, the bottom of the low electron band at the M point systematically moves downward to cause the expansion of the electron pocket. Also, $k_F$ points of the hole bands move toward the $\Gamma$ point, being associated with the shrinkage of the hole pockets. These changes are more clearly visualized in the ARPES intensity at $E_F$ plotted as a function of 2D wave vector in Figs. 2(d)-2(f). From the size of electron and hole pockets in the FS mapping (see red circles and dashed black curves), we have estimated the electron and hole carrier concentrations for the $T_s = 420\text{ }^\circ\text{C}$ sample to be $\sim 0.08e^-$ and $\sim 0.08h^+$, respectively (for details to estimate the carrier concentration, see Supplemental Material S3 [26]). This suggests the effectively-zero total carriers and the well compensated nature. Thus, one can define this sample as a “pristine” sample. On the other hand, we have estimated the total carrier for the $T_s = 400\text{ }^\circ\text{C}$ and $440\text{ }^\circ\text{C}$ samples to be $\sim 0.06h^+$ and $\sim 0.37e^-$, respectively, indicative of the violation of compensated nature. These results suggest that the tuning of $T_s$ works as an effective method to control the carrier concentration. We speculate that the annealing at slightly higher temperature than the optimal value of $T_s = 420\text{ }^\circ\text{C}$ produces Te deficiencies in the film that work as an electron doner. On the other hand, annealing at slightly lower temperature than the optimal on one create Te adatoms on the film that work as an effective accepter probably due to the slightly larger work function of Te ($4.95\text{ eV}$) [31] than that of $\text{TiTe}_2$ ($4.86\text{ eV}$) [32].
> 
> 接下来，我们展示 CDW 如何受载流子浓度变化的影响。虽然之前的研究主要集中在原始样品的实验上，但我们能够通过两种方法调节载流子浓度，即 (i) 在外延生长过程中改变衬底温度 $T_s$ 以有效控制样品的化学计量数，以及 (ii) 系统地在原始 $\text{TiTe}_2$ 上沉积钾 (K) 原子。图 2(a)-2(c) 展示了在 $T = 40\text{ K}$ 下测量、分别对应三个不同 $T_s$ 值（$400, 420,$ 和 $440\text{ }^\circ\text{C}$）的（顶部面板）ARPES 强度和（底部面板）相应的二阶导数强度沿 $\Gamma\text{--}M$ 切割的曲线；请注意，$T_s = 420\text{ }^\circ\text{C}$ 的数据已在图 1 中呈现。可以识别出，随着 $T_s$ 的增加，能带结构发生系统性变化。例如，M 点低电子带的底部系统性地向下移动，导致电子口袋扩张。此外，空穴带的 $k_F$ 点向 $\Gamma$ 点移动，与空穴口袋的收缩相关。这些变化在图 2(d)-2(f) 中以二维波矢为函数绘制的 $E_F$ 处 ARPES 强度图中更清晰地呈现。根据费米面 (FS) 映射中电子和空穴口袋的大小（见红色圆圈和虚线黑曲线），我们估计 $T_s = 420\text{ }^\circ\text{C}$ 样品的电子和空穴载流子浓度分别约为 $0.08e^-$ 和 $0.08h^+$（载流子浓度估计的详细内容，参见补充材料 S3 [26]）。这表明有效总载流子为零且具有良好的补偿特性。因此，可以将该样品定义为“原始”样品。另一方面，我们估计 $T_s = 400\text{ }^\circ\text{C}$ 和 $440\text{ }^\circ\text{C}$ 样品的总载流子分别为 $\sim 0.06h^+$ 和 $\sim 0.37e^-$，表明补偿特性被破坏。这些结果表明，调节 $T_s$ 是一种有效控制载流子浓度的方法。我们推测，略高于 $T_s = 420\text{ }^\circ\text{C}$ 最佳值的退火会在薄膜中产生 Te 缺陷，这些缺陷作为电子给体。另一方面，略低于最佳值的退火会在薄膜上形成 Te 吸附原子，这些原子作为有效受主，这可能是由于 Te 的功函数 ($4.95\text{ eV}$) [31] 略大于 $\text{TiTe}_2$ ($4.86\text{ eV}$) [32]。

> **Stoichiometry**: 化学计量数，化合物中各元素的原子数量之比。
> 
> Interesting physics manifests itself when we carefully monitor the folded bands at the M point. While the $T_s = 420\text{ }^\circ\text{C}$ sample apparently shows replica hole bands at the M point due to CDW [arrows in the top and bottom panels of Fig. 2(b)], the replica bands are not seen for both the $T_s = 400\text{ }^\circ\text{C}$ [Fig. 2(a)] and $440\text{ }^\circ\text{C}$ [Fig. 2(c)] samples. This suggests that CDW appears only in a narrow carrier concentration range around the zero total carrier in both the hole- and electron-doped regions. To further examine the evolution of band structure upon carrier doping, we have systematically deposited K atoms on the pristine sample ($T_s = 420\text{ }^\circ\text{C}$), and show in Figs. 2(g)-2(i) the ARPES intensity for the K dosing time $t_K$ of 6, 12, and 24 min. It is evident that the dosing of K atoms systematically moves the electron and hole bands downward in energy, indicative of electron doping into the system, similarly to the case of increasing $T_s$. The effective total carrier estimated from the FS volume shown in Figs. 2(j)-2(l) is $\sim 0.07e^-, \sim 0.21e^-,$ and $\sim 0.39e^-$ for $t_K = 6, 12,$ and $24\text{ min}$, respectively. These values correspond to the effective K coverage of 7, 21, and 39 % per $\text{TiTe}_2$ unit cell, under the assumption that at one K atom donates one electron to a $\text{TiTe}_2$ layer. As can be seen from Figs. 2(g)-2(i), folded bands are always absent irrespective of $t_K$. This suggests that CDW appears only at the zero doping where electron and hole carriers are compensated.
> 
> 当我们仔细监测 M 点的折叠能带时，有趣的物理现象会显现出来。虽然 $T_s = 420\text{ }^\circ\text{C}$ 的样品在 M 点由于 CDW 显示出副本空穴带 [图 2(b) 上下面板中的箭头]，但在 $T_s = 400\text{ }^\circ\text{C}$ [图 2(a)] 和 $440\text{ }^\circ\text{C}$ [图 2(c)] 样品中均未观察到副本带。这表明 CDW 仅出现在空穴和电子掺杂区域中总载流子接近零的狭窄载流子浓度范围内。为了进一步研究载流子掺杂对能带结构演化的影响，我们在原始样品 ($T_s = 420\text{ }^\circ\text{C}$) 上系统地沉积 K 原子，并在图 2(g)-2(i) 中展示了 K 沉积时间 $t_K$ 为 6, 12 和 24 分钟的 ARPES 强度。很明显，K 原子的沉积系统地使电子和空穴能带在能量上向下移动，表明电子掺杂进入系统，这与增加 $T_s$ 的情况类似。根据图 2(j)-2(l) 所示的费米面体积估算的有效总载流子分别为 $\sim 0.07e^-, \sim 0.21e^-,$ 和 $\sim 0.39e^-$（对应 $t_K = 6, 12, 24\text{ 分钟}$）。这些值对应于每个 $\text{TiTe}_2$ 单元格 7%, 21% 和 39% 的有效 K 覆盖度（假设每个 K 原子向 $\text{TiTe}_2$ 层提供一个电子）。从图 2(g)-2(i) 可以看出，无论 $t_K$ 如何，折叠能带始终不存在。这表明 CDW 仅在电子和空穴载流子补偿的零掺杂状态下出现。

> ### C. Observation of a CDW gap
> 
> #### C. 观察到电荷密度波能隙
> 
> To clarify the CDW transition temperature ($T_{\text{CDW}}$) in monolayer $\text{TiTe}_2$, we have performed the temperature-dependent ARPES measurements near $E_F$. Figure 2(m) shows the EDCs at the $k_F$ point on the inner hole pocket around the $\Gamma$ point measured at various temperatures ($T = 30\text{--}230\text{ K}$). One can see that the leading-edge midpoint is shifted toward higher binding energy (EB) by $2\text{--}10\text{ meV}$ at $T = 30\text{ K}$, accompanied with a two-peaked structure (black arrows). The peak closer to $E_F$ is associated with the CDW-gap opening, whereas that at higher EB is produced by the electron band folded from the M point due to the $2\times2$ CDW formation [21]. On increasing temperature, the leading-edge midpoint gradually moves toward $E_F$ and finally reaches $E_F$ at $T = 90\text{ K}$, suggesting that the CDW sets in at $\sim 90\text{ K}$. We found that the CDW gap opens also on the electron pocket at the M point, and it is strongly anisotropic. As seen from Fig. 2(o), there is a peak at $70\text{ meV}$ in the EDC at the $k_F$ point along the shorter axis of the ellipsoidal pocket (point 5 in the inset) where the FS of ellipsoidal pocket overlaps well with the folded inner hole pocket. This peak exhibits a finite leading-edge shift toward higher EB (see vertical black line and arrows) due to the CDW-gap opening. On approaching the longer axis of the ellipsoidal pocket (from point 5 to point 1), the peak systematically approaches $E_F$ and the leading-edge shift eventually vanishes. This strong k dependence is better visualized in the symmetrized EDCs in Fig. 2(p) as well as the plot of peak position in the symmetrized EDCs and leading-edge shift in Fig. 2(q). From the analyses of the FS, we found that the inner hole pocket at $\Gamma$ and the elongated electron pocket at M partially overlap by the $2\times2$ q vector ($\mathbf{q}_{\text{CDW}}$) in the pristine sample, as schematically shown in Fig. 2(r).
> 
> 为了阐明单层 $\text{TiTe}_2$ 中的电荷密度波转变温度 ($T_{\text{CDW}}$)，我们在 $E_F$ 附近进行了温度依赖 ARPES 测量。图 2(m) 显示了在不同温度下 ($T = 30\text{--}230\text{ K}$) 测量 $\Gamma$ 点附近内空穴口袋 $k_F$ 点的能量分布曲线 (EDCs)。可以看出，在 $T = 30\text{ K}$ 时，前沿中点向更高的结合能 (EB) 方向偏移了 $2\text{--}10\text{ meV}$，并伴随着双峰结构（黑色箭头）。距离 $E_F$ 更近的峰与 CDW 能隙的开启相关，而位于更高 EB 处的峰是由 $2\times2$ CDW 形成导致从 M 点折叠而来的电子能带产生的 [21]。随着温度升高，前沿中点逐渐移向 $E_F$，并在 $T = 90\text{ K}$ 时最终达到 $E_F$，表明 CDW 在 $\sim 90\text{ K}$ 时开启。我们发现电荷密度波能隙也在 M 点的电子口袋处打开，并且它具有很强的各向异性。从图 2(o) 可以看出，在椭球口袋短轴方向（插图中的点 5）沿 $k_F$ 点的 EDC 在 $70\text{ meV}$ 处有一个峰，此处椭球口袋的费米面与折叠的内空穴口袋很好地重叠。由于 CDW 能隙的开启，该峰表现出向更高 EB 的有限前沿偏移（见垂直黑线和箭头）。当接近椭球口袋的长轴（从点 5 到点 1）时，该峰系统性地接近 $E_F$，前沿偏移最终消失。这种强烈的 k 依赖性在图 2(p) 中的对称化 EDCs 以及图 2(q) 中的对称化 EDCs 峰位和前沿偏移图中得到了更好的可视化。通过费米面分析，我们发现原始样品中位于 $\Gamma$ 的内空穴口袋和位于 M 的延展电子口袋部分地被 $2\times2$ 的 q 向量 ($\mathbf{q}_{\text{CDW}}$) 重叠，如图 2(r) 中示意所示。

> ## IV. CONCLUSION
> 
> ### IV. 结论
> 
> In conclusion, we performed high-resolution ARPES study on monolayer 1T-$\text{TiTe}_2$ grown on bilayer graphene. We have succeeded in controlling the carrier concentration in a wide range, by using two independent methods, controlling the substrate temperature during epitaxy and K dosing on the sample. We found that the $2\times2$ CDW, as characterized by the band folding, is fragile against carrier doping and emerges only at the effectively zero doping level with the electron and hole compensation. The present study points to importance of carrier tuning to clarify the mechanism of CDW in monolayer TMDs.
> 
> 总之，我们对生长在双层石墨烯上的单层 1T-$\text{TiTe}_2$ 进行了高分辨率 ARPES 研究。我们通过两种独立的方法——控制外延过程中的衬底温度和向样品中添加 K——成功地在宽范围内控制了载流子浓度。我们发现，通过能带折叠表征的 $2\times2$ 电荷密度波 (CDW) 对载流子掺杂很敏感，仅在有效零掺杂且电子和空穴补偿时出现。本研究表明，载流子调谐对于阐明单层 TMDs 中 CDW 的机制至关重要。
