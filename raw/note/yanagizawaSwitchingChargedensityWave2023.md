> ## 单层TiTe₂中通过载流子调谐实现电荷密度波（CDW）的切换
> 
> ### 一、引言
> 
> 在凝聚态物理和材料科学的前沿，探索新型二维（2D）材料及其奇异量子现象是核心课题之一。**电荷密度波（Charge-Density Wave, CDW）**，即电子密度在空间中周期性调制并与晶格畸变共存的电子凝聚态，是低维体系中一个基本且长期存在的问题。理解其产生机制，特别是它与维度的关系，以及它与其他量子相（如超导）的相互作用，具有重要的基础研究价值。
> 
> **过渡金属二硫族化合物（Transition-Metal Dichalcogenides, TMDs）** 因其层状结构和丰富的物理性质，成为研究CDW的理想平台。本文聚焦于八面体（1T）结构的**二碲化钛（TiTe₂）**，该材料展现出一种奇特的行为：其体材料是一种简单的半金属，**不表现CDW**；然而，当其减薄至单层时，却表现出**2×2的CDW**。这一显著的维度效应背后的物理机制，引发了学界的激烈争论。此前的研究提出了多种可能的驱动力，如电子-声子耦合、激子凝聚等，但均未达成共识。
> 
> 本文的核心价值在于，通过一种创新的实验策略——**载流子调谐（carrier tuning）**——系统地研究了单层TiTe₂中CDW的起源。研究者利用分子束外延生长过程中的参数控制以及表面沉积钾原子两种方法，精确地调控了单层TiTe₂的载流子浓度，并借助高分辨率的**角分辨光电子能谱（Angle-Resolved Photoemission Spectroscopy, ARPES）** 直接观测了电子结构的变化。研究发现，2×2 CDW仅在电子和空穴载流子精确补偿的极窄浓度范围内出现，当掺杂破坏这一补偿条件时，CDW便消失。这一发现强有力地证明了**费米面嵌套（Fermi Surface Nesting）** 是驱动单层TiTe₂中CDW的主要机制，为解决该领域长期存在的争议提供了关键性实验证据。
> 
> ### 二、论文结构与逻辑概览
> 
> 本文遵循了“现象观察 → 调控实验 → 机制分析 → 理论验证”的经典物理论证逻辑链条。
> 
> 1.  **建立起点：表征原始单层TiTe₂的电子结构**
>     -   首先，作者通过ARPES测量了原始（接近零掺杂）单层TiTe₂在CDW转变温度（TCDW ~ 90 K）上下的电子结构，确认了2×2 CDW的存在，其特征是能带折叠现象。
> 2.  **核心实验：通过载流子调谐控制CDW**
>     -   这是本文的核心环节。作者采用两种独立方法来调控载流子浓度：
>         -   **方法一**：改变分子束外延（MBE）生长过程中的衬底温度（Ts），以控制薄膜的化学计量比（Te空位作为电子给体，Te吸附原子作为受主）。
>         -   **方法二**：在原始样品表面沉积钾（K）原子（K原子作为电子给体）。
>     -   通过对一系列掺杂样品的ARPES测量，作者发现了一个关键规律：表征CDW的能带折叠现象**仅在电子和空穴载流子浓度近乎相等（即有效零掺杂）的样品中存在**。无论是空穴掺杂还是电子掺杂，都会导致CDW消失。
> 3.  **表征CDW：观测能隙及其各向异性**
>     -   作者通过温度依赖的ARPES测量，确定了CDW的转变温度（TCDW ~ 90 K），并观测到了CDW能隙的打开。更重要的是，他们发现电子口袋处的CDW能隙具有**强烈的各向异性**，这与费米面嵌套的几何条件高度吻合。
> 4.  **机制辨析与确认：为何是费米面嵌套？**
>     -   作者将单层TiTe₂的电子结构与同构的TiSe₂（其CDW由激子凝聚驱动）进行对比，排除了激子凝聚机制。
>     -   随后，作者系统性地排除了无序效应、范霍夫奇异点散射、雅恩-泰勒畸变、强电子-声子耦合等可能的CDW驱动力。
>     -   最终，通过分析费米面拓扑结构与嵌套矢量（qCDW）的关系，并结合第一性原理计算的电子磁化率 χ₀(q)，作者确认了费米面嵌套是导致CDW不稳定的最合理机制。
> 
> _图：该论文的逻辑流程图。从原始样品的电子结构出发，通过两种载流子调控方法，最终结合实验与理论，将CDW的起源锁定为费米面嵌套。_
>

> ### 四、正文核心内容剖析
> 
> #### 1. 研究背景与动机
> 
> 研究的缘起是单层TiTe₂中一个令人困惑的现象：它在体材料态没有CDW，却在单层极限下出现2×2 CDW。这与TiSe₂（体材料和单层都有CDW）形成鲜明对比，提示其背后机制可能不同。先前的ARPES研究提出了电子-声子耦合、轨道选择性杂化等假说，但缺乏决定性证据。研究者意识到，**费米面拓扑结构**是决定CDW矢量和稳定性的关键，因此，通过系统地**改变载流子浓度来调控费米面**，并监测CDW的响应，是揭示其起源的直接且有效的方法。
> 
> #### 2. 研究方法与模型
> 
> -   **样品制备**：通过**分子束外延（MBE）** 方法，在双层石墨烯/SiC(0001)衬底上生长高质量单层1T-TiTe₂薄膜。生长过程由RHEED实时监控。
> -   **载流子调控**：采用两种独立且互补的方法：
>     1.  **化学计量比调控**：在MBE过程中改变衬底温度（Ts = 400, 420, 440 °C），利用Te原子的高蒸气压，通过控制Te空位（电子给体）或Te填隙/吸附原子（受主）的浓度来改变载流子类型和浓度。
>     2.  **表面电荷转移掺杂**：在超高真空原位条件下，将碱金属钾（K）原子沉积到原始样品表面。K原子会向TiTe₂层转移电子，实现均匀的电子掺杂。
> -   **电子结构表征**：利用具有高能量和动量分辨率的**同步辐射角分辨光电子能谱（ARPES）**，在不同温度下直接测量样品的单电子谱函数，获取能带结构、费米面拓扑、能隙大小等信息。
> -   **理论计算**：采用基于**密度泛函理论（DFT）** 的第一性原理计算（Quantum Espresso软件包）来模拟能带结构，并计算电子磁化率 χ₀(q)，以评估费米面嵌套的强度。
> 
> #### 3. 实验结果与讨论
> 
> -   **原始样品的电子结构鉴定**：确认了单层TiTe₂是一种补偿型半金属，其费米面由Γ点的空穴口袋和M点的椭球型电子口袋组成。在T < TCDW时，清晰地观察到由2×2周期势引起的能带折叠，证实了CDW的存在。
> -   **载流子调谐的核心发现**：通过调控Ts或沉积K原子，系统性地改变了费米面大小。**无论采用哪种方法，使样品偏离完美的电子-空穴补偿（即有效零掺杂）状态，CDW折叠能带就会消失。** 这表明CDW对载流子浓度极其敏感，且仅在有效零掺杂的补偿区出现。
> -   **CDW能隙的各向异性**：在原始样品中，不仅在空穴口袋，也在电子口袋上观测到了CDW能隙的打开。关键的是，该能隙在M点椭球型电子口袋的短轴方向最大，沿长轴方向逐渐减小并消失。这种**各向异性与费米面嵌套的几何条件完美吻合**：嵌套矢量qCDW将空穴口袋与电子口袋的短轴区域连接起来，该区域嵌套条件最好，因此打开的能隙最大。
> -   **CDW机制的辨析**：
>     -   **排除激子凝聚**：与TiSe₂对比，TiTe₂是存在大量自由载流子的半金属，强屏蔽效应阻止了激子的形成。此外，其折叠能带强度远弱于TiSe₂，进一步支持了不同的驱动机理。
>     -   **排除其他机制**：通过分析，依次排除了无序效应、范霍夫奇异点散射、雅恩-泰勒畸变和强电子-声子耦合作为主要驱动力的可能性。
>     -   **确认费米面嵌套**：实验观测到的嵌套矢量qCDW、CDW仅在良好嵌套条件下出现、以及CDW能隙的各向异性，都强烈指向费米面嵌套机制。理论计算得到的电子磁化率在qCDW处出现峰值，而在电子掺杂时消失，为这一结论提供了强有力的理论支持。
> 
> ### 五、结论与贡献
> 
> 本研究通过系统的ARPES测量和载流子调控实验，明确揭示了单层1T-TiTe₂中2×2电荷密度波（CDW）的起源。
> 
> **核心结论：**
> 
> 1.  单层TiTe₂的2×2 CDW**仅在其电子和空穴载流子接近完美补偿（有效零掺杂）的狭窄区域出现**。
> 2.  该CDW的**驱动力是费米面嵌套**，即布里渊区Γ点处的内空穴口袋与M点处的椭球型电子口袋，通过2×2 CDW矢量（qCDW）实现部分嵌套。
> 3.  通过改变MBE生长温度或沉积K原子来**调控载流子浓度，可以有效地开启或关闭CDW**，为在二维极限下操控量子物态提供了新途径。
> 
> **核心贡献：**
> 
> 1.  **解决了一个关键科学争议**：在单层TiTe₂这个具有代表性的体系中，为CDW的起源提供了决定性的实验证据，结束了关于其机制的争论。
> 2.  **提供了一种有效的研究范式**：证明了“载流子调谐”是研究二维TMDs中CDW机制的一种强大而通用的方法，可以广泛应用于其他体系。
> 3.  **建立了材料调控的新平台**：展示了单层TiTe₂的费米面拓扑和电子基态具有高度可调控性，为在CDW边界附近探索超导等奇异量子相奠定了基础。
> 
> ### 六、未来发展方向展望
> 
> 作者在文中和结尾处暗示了以下几个值得深入探索的方向：
> 
> 1.  **结构畸变的精确测定**：文中提到，虽然雅恩-泰勒畸变不是主要驱动力，但不能完全排除其对电子态的微小影响。未来需要进行高精度的结构实验（如**低能电子衍射(Low-Energy Electron Diffraction, LEED IV-曲线分析**或**扫描透射电子显微镜(Scanning Transmission Electron Microscopy, STEM)**）来量化CDW伴随的晶格畸变大小。

> 3.  **在CDW边界搜索奇异量子相**：这是下一步研究的重中之重。最令人兴奋的前景是利用本研究建立的高可控性平台，通过电学栅压或化学掺杂等手段，将系统精确调谐至CDW相变边界附近，抑制CDW序，以探寻是否存在**超导**、**非费米液体行为**或**拓扑相**等奇异量子态。这是当前凝聚态物理领域的一个核心研究范式。
> 4.  **维度效应的普适性研究**：将本研究的方法拓展到其他在体材料中没有CDW但单层可能出现CDW的TMDs体系，研究维度变化对CDW形成的普遍影响。

> ### 七、学术思考与批判性分析
> 
> 作为深度解读，我们需要对论文的研究进行批判性思考，而不仅仅是复述。
> 
> 1.  **问题：费米面嵌套是“部分”的，这是否足以解释CDW的脆弱性？**
>     
>     -   **分析**：文中明确指出，嵌套只发生在电子口袋的短轴区域，即“部分”费米面嵌套。这解释了为何CDW能隙具有各向异性，也解释了为何CDW态不如激子凝聚驱动的TiSe₂那样稳定（折叠带强度弱，对掺杂敏感）。然而，这种“部分”嵌套的效率如何量化？是否需要一个临界嵌套面积比才能驱动CDW？这需要更精细的费米面拓扑分析和理论计算，本文的电子磁化率计算提供了初步支持，但更深入的模型（如考虑了电子-空穴不对称性的嵌套模型）将有助于理解其脆弱性的根源。
> 2.  **问题：能否彻底排除激子凝聚的贡献？**
>     
>     -   **分析**：作者通过TiTe₂是半金属、存在强屏蔽这一论点排除了传统的激子凝聚图像。论据非常有力。但是，在CDW能隙打开的低温下，部分费米面区域会失去自由载流子，屏蔽效应是否会减弱，从而允许某种“激子不稳定性”作为次要因素存在？虽然实验结果（如折叠带强度弱、无强激子峰）不支持其主要地位，但一个更完整的理论框架可能需要同时包含费米面嵌套和部分激子关联的贡献。
> 3.  **问题：K原子沉积这种掺杂方式是否引入了额外的无序效应？**
>     
>     -   **分析**：作者在分析中（补充材料图S5）通过比较准粒子寿命，排除了K沉积作为主要无序来源的可能性。这是一个重要的对照实验。然而，K原子在表面形成的无序势场，即使不主导散射，也可能对CDW的长程相干性产生微妙影响，这可能部分地贡献了CDW的脆弱性。未来的研究可以采用更“干净”的掺杂方式，如静电栅压，来完全排除化学掺杂带来的潜在无序效应，从而获得本征的掺杂相图。
> 4.  **问题：研究结论的普适性边界在哪里？**
>     
>     -   **分析**：本研究成功地将费米面嵌套确立为单层TiTe₂中CDW的机制。那么，对于其他单层TMDs，这个结论是否适用？这取决于具体材料的费米学。对于具有类似大小空穴和电子口袋的半金属性TMDs，费米面嵌套可能是一个普遍机制。但对于半导体性或只有单一类型载流子的TMDs，其他机制（如激子凝聚、电子-声子耦合）可能仍占主导。本研究提供了一个强大的方法论，但每个体系的机制仍需具体分析。
> 
> ### 八、用户问题预测与解答
> 
> 为了让非专业读者更好地理解，这里预测5个可能的延伸问题并解答。
> 
> 1.  **问题：什么是“费米面嵌套”？为什么它会导致CDW？**
>     
>     -   **解答**：**费米面（Fermi Surface, FS）** 是倒空间中电子占据态和未占据态的分界面。**“嵌套”** 是指费米面的一部分，通过平移某个特定波矢（称为**嵌套矢量q**），能够与费米面的另一部分大致重合。想象两片形状互补的拼图块，将它们平移一段距离就能完美对接。当费米面存在大面积的嵌套时，电子系统对外部扰动会变得不稳定。这时，晶格如果发生一个波长等于2π/q的周期性畸变，就能在费米面附近打开一个能隙，使得嵌套区域的电子能量降低。这个能量增益如果超过晶格畸变所消耗的能量，系统就会自发形成电荷密度波（CDW）。简言之，**费米面嵌套就像为CDW的形成提供了电子层面的“蓝图”和“驱动力”**。
> 2.  **问题：为什么通过改变生长温度就能改变载流子浓度？**
>     
>     -   **解答**：这涉及到材料的化学计量比。在生长TiTe₂薄膜时，**碲（Te）** 原子比**钛（Ti）** 原子更容易挥发。当生长温度（退火温度）**略高于最佳值**时，Te原子会更多地蒸发损失，导致薄膜中形成**Te空位**。在晶体中，缺少一个阴离子（Te）相当于在周围引入正电荷，为了保持电中性，这些Te空位会释放自由电子，形成**电子掺杂**。反之，当生长温度**略低于最佳值**时，多余的Te原子可能附着在薄膜表面形成**Te吸附原子**，它们倾向于接受电子，从而形成**空穴掺杂**。因此，通过精确控制温度，可以像“拧阀门”一样精细地调节Te缺陷的浓度，进而控制载流子类型和浓度。
> 3.  **问题：文中提到的“激子凝聚”机制和“费米面嵌套”机制有什么本质区别？**
>     
>     -   **解答**：这两种机制代表了CDW形成的两种不同物理图像。
>         -   **激子凝聚（Exciton Condensation）**：适用于**半导体或半金属**中电子和空穴间存在强库仑吸引作用的情况。电子和空穴会先配对形成一种中性的束缚态，称为**激子**。当温度降低，大量的激子会发生玻色-爱因斯坦凝聚，形成一个宏观量子态，这个态的空间结构就是CDW。驱动力是**电子-空穴对（激子）的凝聚能**。
>         -   **费米面嵌套（Fermi Surface Nesting）**：如问题1所述，它源于**费米面本身的几何形状**。主要驱动力是**电子系统的能量**，通过晶格畸变在嵌套波矢处打开能隙，使电子能量降低。它不要求电子和空穴形成束缚对，本质上是一种**电子-晶格的集体不稳定性**。 简单来说，激子凝聚像是“异性相吸”形成配对，而费米面嵌套更像是“形状互补”导致的集体行为。
> 4.  **问题：这项基础研究有什么潜在的应用价值？**
>     
>     -   **解答**：虽然这项研究属于前沿基础科学，但它为未来的应用铺平了道路。首先，它证明了一种**在原子级厚度材料中，通过电或化学手段“开关”一个量子物态（CDW）** 的方法。这种可控的电阻态切换，有潜力应用于开发**低功耗、超小型的新型电子开关或存储器**。其次，CDW态通常与超导、拓扑绝缘态等奇异量子态存在竞争或共生关系。通过调控载流子抑制CDW，有可能在相变边界附近诱导出**超导**。这项研究提供了一个精确调控到该边界的理想平台，为发现和设计新型二维超导体或拓扑量子器件打开了大门。
> 5.  **问题：既然单层和体材料结构相似，为什么CDW行为截然不同？**
>     
>     -   **解答**：核心原因在于**维度的变化导致费米面拓扑的重大改变**。
>         -   **体材料TiTe₂**：在三维布里渊区中，其电子口袋位于L点，并且在沿着kz（垂直层方向）上存在**强烈的扭曲（warping）**。这意味着电子口袋的形状是三维且复杂的，很难与空穴口袋形成一个单一的、主导的嵌套矢量，因此费米面嵌套效率极低，无法驱动CDW。
>         -   **单层TiTe₂**：当材料减薄至单层，kz方向的色散完全消失，电子态被限制在二维平面内。此时，电子口袋变成了一个**纯粹的二维椭球面**，其形状与Γ点的空穴口袋在二维平面内形成了良好的部分嵌套条件。这种**维度降低带来的费米面“扁平化”**，极大地增强了电子不稳定性的倾向，使得费米面嵌套得以成为CDW的驱动力。这正是“维度效应”在TiTe₂这个具体材料中的体现。
> 
> ### 九、专区：便于 Obsidian Dataview 插件调用

> 
> How charge-density wave (CDW) is influenced by the change in the dimensionality is a fundamental question in condensed-matter physics. Monolayer titanium ditelluride (TiTe₂) undergoes 2×2 CDW despite the absence of CDW in bulk counterpart, whereas the mechanism of CDW is under intensive debate. Here we show that the CDW of monolayer TiTe₂ can be conveniently switched by carrier tuning via controlling the Te ratio during molecular-beam epitaxy or depositing potassium (K) atoms on the surface. > ## I. INTRODUCTION

> ## I. 引言
> 
> Initiated by the discovery of massless Dirac fermion in graphene and associated quantum phenomena such as half integer quantum Hall effect and valley Hall effect [1, 2], a search for new two-dimensional (2D) materials hosting exotic physical properties is now becoming one of the central topics in material science. Transition-metal dichalcogenides (TMDs) offer an excellent platform to realize new 2D materials, not only because bulk TMDs already show outstanding physical properties such as superconductivity, charge-/spin-density wave (CDW/SDW), magnetic order [3], and exotic topological phases [4–6], but also because its layered structure is suitable to study the evolution of electronic states from the bulk to the thinnest monolayer limit. Perhaps CDW is a most fundamental and longstanding issue in the bulk and ultrathin TMDs, because many TMDs undergo CDW with a variety of lattice periodicities, essentially due to the low-dimensional (2D or quasi-2D) nature of electronic states which enhances the electronic instability toward the CDW transition. Also, since the CDW phase often appears in or close to other quantum phases such as Mott-insulator [7, 8], superconductivity [9], and magnetic phases [10, 11], its interplay (such as competition and coexistence) has been fiercely debated. 由在石墨烯中发现无质量狄拉克费米子以及相关的量子现象(如半整数量子霍尔效应和谷霍尔效应) [1, 2] 所引发,寻找具有奇异物理性质的新型二维 (2D) 材料现已成为材料科学中的核心课题之一。过渡金属二硫族化合物 (TMDs) 为实现新型二维材料提供了绝佳平台,这不仅因为体材料TMDs已展现出如超导性、电荷-/自旋密度波 (CDW/SDW)、磁序 [3] 以及奇异拓扑相 [4–6] 等卓越物理性质,还因为其层状结构适合研究电子态从体材料到最薄单层的演化。或许CDW是体材料及超薄TMDs中最基本且长期存在的问题,因为许多TMDs会经历具有多种晶格周期性的CDW,这本质上源于电子态的低维 (2D或准2D) 性质,增强了电子向CDW转变的不稳定性。此外,由于CDW相通常出现在或接近其他量子相(如莫特绝缘体 [7, 8], 超导性 [9], 和磁相 [10, 11]), 其相互作用(如竞争与共存)一直备受激烈争论。
> 
> **Term**: **Dirac fermion**: 狄拉克费米子, 行为类似于无质量相对论性粒子的电子, 其能量-动量关系呈线性。 **Term**: **Quantum Hall effect**: 量子霍尔效应, 二维电子系统在强磁场下表现出的霍尔电导量子化现象。 **Term**: **Superconductivity**: 超导性, 某些材料在特定温度以下电阻完全消失的现象。 **Term**: **Spin-Density Wave (SDW)**: 自旋密度波, 电子自旋密度周期性调制的状态, 常与反铁磁性相关。 **Term**: **Mott-insulator**: 莫特绝缘体, 由于强电子-电子关联作用而本应为导体的物质呈现绝缘体性质。

> Titanium ditelluride (TiTe₂) with octahedral (1T-) structure is an excellent platform to study the interplay between CDW and dimensionality, because it was reported to show intriguing differences in the CDW properties between bulk and monolayer. Bulk TiTe₂ is a simple semimetal [12] characterized by the compensated quasi-2D hole pockets at the Γ/A point and a strongly warped quasi-2D electron pocket at the L point of the bulk Brillouin zone (BZ) [13, 14], and it does not exhibit CDW. On the other hand, the monolayer counterpart [see Fig. 1(a) for the crystal structure] was recently reported to show CDW accompanied by 2×2 superstructure. This periodicity is identical to that observed in isostructural monolayer TiSe₂ in which the CDW phase is proposed to be stabilized by the condensation of excitons [15] as in the case of bulk TiSe₂ showing similar 2×2×2 CDW [16–20]. Thus, the contrasting CDW behavior between bulk and monolayer is a peculiar characteristic of TiTe₂. To account for this unusual CDW, previous ARPES studies have proposed several key ingredients such as electron-phonon coupling and orbital selective hybridization [14, 21], although the mechanism of CDW is still unclear. Examination of the Fermi surface (FS) nesting condition via controlling the carrier concentration is useful to pin down the origin of CDW, whereas such studies have not been reported in monolayer TiTe₂. 八面体 (1T-) 结构的二碲化钛 (TiTe₂) 是研究电荷密度波 (CDW) 与维度相互作用的一个优秀平台,因为它被报道在体材料和单层之间表现出CDW特性上的有趣差异。体材料TiTe₂ 是一种简单的半金属 [12], 其特征是在 Γ/A 点存在补偿的准二维空穴口袋和在体布里渊区 (BZ) [13, 14] 的 L 点存在强畸变的准二维电子口袋,并且不表现出CDW。另一方面,单层对应物 [晶体结构参见图1(a)] 最近被报道表现出伴随 2×2 超结构的CDW。这种周期性与同构单层TiSe₂ 中观察到的周期性相同,其中CDW相被认为是由激子的凝聚 [15] 稳定化的,正如体材料TiSe₂ 表现出类似的 2×2×2 CDW [16–20] 的情况。因此,体材料和单层之间CDW行为的对比是TiTe₂的一个特殊特征。为了解释这种不寻常的CDW,之前的ARPES研究提出了几个关键因素,如电子-声子耦合和轨道选择性杂化 [14, 21], 尽管CDW的机制仍然不清楚。通过控制载流子浓度来检查费米面 (FS) 的嵌套条件,有助于确定CDW的起源,而这类研究在单层TiTe₂中尚未报道。
> 
> **Term**: **Semimetal**: 半金属, 导带和价带有轻微重叠, 费米能级处存在少量电子和空穴的材料。 **Term**: **Brillouin Zone (BZ)**: 布里渊区, 倒空间中的原胞, 用于描述晶体中电子的动量空间。 **Term**: **Exciton**: 激子, 由库仑力束缚的电子-空穴对。 **Term**: **Electron-phonon coupling**: 电子-声子耦合, 电子与晶格振动之间的相互作用。
> 
> In this article, we report angle-resolved photoemission spectroscopy (ARPES) investigation of monolayer TiTe₂ on bilayer graphene grown by the molecular-beam epitaxy (MBE) method. By controlling the annealing temperature during epitaxy or depositing K atoms on the surface, we have succeeded in systematically controlling the carrier concentration in a wide doping range from slightly hole-doped to heavily electron-doped region. As a consequence, we found that the 2×2 CDW appears only in a very narrow doping range in which the electron and hole carriers are compensated. We discuss the implications of the present results in relation to the fermiology and various CDW mechanisms such as FS nesting and exciton condensation, by also comparing the band structure with isostructural TiSe₂. 在本文中,我们报道了通过分子束外延 (MBE) 方法生长的双层石墨烯上单层TiTe₂ 的角分辨光电子能谱 (ARPES) 研究。通过控制外延过程中的退火温度或在表面沉积K原子,我们成功地在从轻微空穴掺杂到强电子掺杂的宽掺杂范围内系统地控制了载流子浓度。因此,我们发现 2×2 电荷密度波 (CDW) 仅在电子和空穴载流子补偿的非常窄的掺杂范围内出现。我们通过将能带结构与同构的TiSe₂进行比较,讨论了当前结果与费米学以及各种CDW机制(如费米面嵌套和激子凝聚)的相关性。

> ### A. Sample fabrication
> 
> ### A. 样品制备

> High-quality monolayer TiTe₂ film was grown on bilayer graphene by the MBE method [7, 8, 15]. First, bilayer graphene was fabricated by resistive heating of n-type 4H-SiC(0001) single-crystal wafer at 1100 °C for 30 min under high vacuum better than 1.0×10⁻⁹ Torr. Subsequently, monolayer TiTe₂ film was grown by evaporating Ti atoms on the bilayer graphene substrate kept at 400∼440 °C in Te-rich atmosphere, and then the as-grown monolayer TiTe₂ film was annealed for 30 min at 400 °C. The growth process was monitored by the reflection high-energy electron diffraction (RHEED). After the growth, the film was transferred to the ARPES measurement chamber without exposing it to air. As seen in Fig. 1(b), the RHEED pattern for TiTe₂ film shows a sharp 1×1 streak pattern, confirming the high crystallinity of film. 高质量的单层TiTe₂ 薄膜通过MBE方法[7, 8, 15]在双层石墨烯上生长。首先,在优于1.0×10⁻⁹ 托的高真空下,通过在 n-型4H-SiC(0001)单晶片上电阻加热 1100 °C持续30分钟制备双层石墨烯。随后,在富Te气氛中,将温度保持在 400∼440 °C的双层石墨烯基板上蒸发Ti原子,生长单层TiTe₂ 薄膜,并将生长的单层TiTe₂ 薄膜在 400 °C退火30分钟。生长过程通过反射高能电子衍射 (RHEED) 监测。生长后,薄膜被转移到ARPES测量室,且未暴露于空气中。如图1(b)所示,TiTe₂ 薄膜的RHEED图案显示出尖锐的 1×1 条纹图案,证实了薄膜的高结晶性。

> ### A. Electronic structure of monolayer TiTe₂
> 
> ### A. 单层TiTe₂的电子结构
> 
> First we present the electronic states of pristine monolayer TiTe₂. Figure 1(c) shows the energy distribution curves (EDCs) in the valence-band region measured along the ΓM cut of the hexagonal BZ at T = 180 K above the CDW transition temperature (TCDW ∼ 90 K). One can recognize a couple of hole bands crossing EF centered at the Γ point and a shallow electron band in the vicinity of EF centered at the M point, which are assigned to the Te 5p and Ti 3d orbitals, respectively [13, 14]. Corresponding ARPES-intensity and second derivative plots shown in Fig. 1(d) signify an M-shaped band around the Γ point at ∼1.5 eV besides the Γ-centered hole and M-centered electron bands, in reasonable agreement with the calculated band structure of monolayer 1T-TiTe₂ shown in Fig. 1(e). These results indicate that pristine monolayer TiTe₂ is a compensated semimetal with a negative band gap, consistent with the previous studies [13, 14]. As shown in the left panel of Fig. 1(f), the overall ARPES intensity at T = 30 K appears to be similar to that at T = 180 K. On the other hand, there exists an extra intensity below the electron band at the M point at T = 30 K as indicated by a white arrow. Such an extra intensity is completely absent at T = 180 K. As seen from the second derivative intensity plot in the right panel of Fig. 1(f), this extra feature is the replica of the two hole bands at the Γ point which are folded to the M point due to the 2×2 periodic potential of CDW [14, 21, 27], as also clearly seen from the momentum distribution curves (MDCs) around the M point (see Fig. S1 in Supplemental Material [26]). Moreover, by following the periodicity of reconstructed BZ [see the original and reconstructed BZs in Fig. 1(g)], the shallow electron band at the M point is folded back to the Γ point (see Fig. S2 in Supplemental Material [26]). These results strongly suggest that the electronic structure at T = 30 K is reconstructed by the formation of 2×2 CDW, consistent with the previous reports [14, 21, 27]. We estimated the intensity ratio of folded hole bands around the M point relative to that of the main hole bands around the Γ point to be ∼ 10 %, which is distinct from the case of T = 180 K in which the intensity ratio is 0 % because of the complete absence of the folded hole bands. It is also noted that the spectral weight of folded bands is much weaker than that of isostructural monolayer TiSe₂ (and also bulk TiSe₂) [15, 18, 19, 28, 29]. We will come back to this point later. 首先,我们展示了原始单层TiTe₂的电子态。图1(c)显示了在六边形BZ的 ΓM 切线方向、高于电荷密度波 (CDW) 转变温度 (TCDW ∼ 90 K) T = 180K处的价带区域的能量分布曲线 (EDCs)。可以识别出几对穿过 EF 中心位于 Γ 点的空穴带,以及在 EF 点附近、中心位于 M 点的浅电子带,分别归因于Te 5p和Ti 3d 轨道 [13, 14]。图1(d)中显示的对应ARPES强度和二阶导数图表明,在 Γ 点附近存在一个M形带,位于 ∼1.5 eV处,除了以 Γ 为中心的空穴带和以 M为中心的电子带,这与图1(e)中显示的单层1T-TiTe₂ 的能带结构基本一致。这些结果表明,原始单层TiTe₂ 是一种补偿型半金属,具有负带隙,与先前的研究 [13, 14] 一致。如图1(f)的左图所示,在 T = 30K时的整体ARPES强度与在 T = 180 K时的强度相似。另一方面,在 M 点处、T = 30 K时,电子带下方存在一个额外的强度,如白色箭头所示。这种额外强度在 T = 180 K时完全消失。从图1(f)右图中的二阶导数强度图可以看出,这一额外特征是位于 Γ 点的两个空穴带的副本,由于CDW [14, 21, 27] 的 2×2 周期性势场而折叠到 M 点,这一点也清晰地从动量分布曲线 (MDCs) 在 M 点附近(参见补充材料中的图S1 [26]) 可以看出。此外,通过观察重构 BZ [的周期性,可以看到原始和重构BZs在图1(g)], 处的浅电子带在M点折叠回 Γ 点(参见补充材料中的图S2 [26])。这些结果强烈表明,在 T = 30 K时的电子结构是由 2×2 CDW的形成而重构的,与先前的报道 [14, 21, 27] 一致。我们估计了在 M 点周围的折叠空穴带相对于在 Γ 点周围的空穴带的主要强度之比为 ∼ 10 %,这与 T = 180 K的情况不同,在 T = 180 K时强度比为 0%,因为完全不存在折叠空穴带。还应注意,折叠带的谱重远弱于同构单层TiSe₂(以及体材料TiSe₂) [15, 18, 19, 28, 29]。我们稍后会回到这一点。

> ### B. Carrier doping effect on the CDW
> 
> ### B. 载流子掺杂对电荷密度波的影响
> 
> Next, we present how the CDW is influenced by the change in the carrier concentration. While the previous studies have focused on the experiments on the pristine sample, we are able to modulate the carrier concentration by adopting two methods, i.e. (i) changing the substrate temperature Ts during the epitaxy to effectively control the stoichiometry of the sample, and (ii) systematically depositing potassium (K) atoms on pristine TiTe₂. Figures 2(a)-2(c) show (top panel) ARPES intensity and (bottom panel) corresponding second derivative intensity plots along the ΓM cut, measured at T = 40 K, for three different Ts values of 400, 420, and 440 °C, respectively; note that the data for Ts = 420 °C were already presented in Fig. 1. One can recognize a systematic change in the band structure upon increasing Ts. For example, the bottom of the electron band at the M point systematically moves downward to cause the expansion of the electron pocket. Also, kF points of the hole bands move toward the Γ point, being associated with the shrinkage of the hole pockets. These changes are more clearly visualized in the ARPES intensity at EF plotted as a function of 2D wave vector in Figs. 2(d)-2(f). From the size of electron and hole pockets in the FS mapping (see red circles and dashed black curves), we have estimated the electron and hole carrier concentrations for the Ts = 420 °C sample to be ∼0.08e⁻ and ∼0.08h⁺, respectively (for details to estimate the carrier concentration, see Supplemental Material S3 [26]). This suggests the effectively-zero total carriers and the well compensated nature. Thus, one can define this sample as a “pristine” sample. On the other hand, we have estimated the total carrier for the Ts = 400 °C and 440 °C samples to be ∼0.06h⁺ and ∼0.37e⁻, respectively, indicative of the violation of compensated nature. These results suggest that the tuning of Ts works as an effective method to control the carrier concentration. We speculate that the annealing at slightly higher temperature than the optimal value of Ts = 420 °C produces Te deficiencies in the film that work as an electron doner. On the other hand, annealing at slightly lower temperature than the optimal one create Te adatoms on the film that work as an effective accepter probably due to the slightly larger work function of Te (4.95 eV) [31] than that of TiTe₂ (4.86 eV) [32]. 接下来,我们展示了电荷密度波 (CDW) 如何受载流子浓度变化的影响。虽然之前的研究主要集中在原始样品的实验上,但我们们可以通过两种方法调节载流子浓度,即 (i) 在外延生长过程中改变衬底温度 Ts 以有效控制样品的化学计量数,以及 (ii) 系统地沉积钾 (K) 原子在原始TiTe₂上。图2(a)-2(c)展示了(顶部面板)ARPES强度和(底部面板)相应的二阶导数强度图沿 ΓM 切割线,在 T = 40 K下测量,分别对应三个不同的 Ts值400、420和 440 °C;请注意, Ts = 420 °C的数据已在图1中呈现。可以识别出,随着 Ts的增加,能带结构发生系统性变化。例如,电子带底的 M 点系统性地向下移动,导致电子口袋的扩张。此外,空穴带的kF 点向 Γ 点移动,与空穴口袋的收缩相关。这些变化在ARPES强度图 EF 中更清晰地呈现,该图以二维波矢为函数绘制在图2(d)-2(f)中。根据费米面映射(见红色圆圈和虚线黑曲线)中电子和空穴口袋的大小,我们估计 Ts = 420 °C样品的电子和空穴载流子浓度分别为 ∼0.08e⁻ 和 ∼0.08h⁺(载流子浓度估计的详细内容,参见补充材料S3 [26])。这表明有效零总载流子和良好的补偿特性。因此,我们可以将该样品定义为“原始”样品。另一方面,我们估计 Ts = 400 °C和 440 °C样品的总载流子分别为 ∼0.06h⁺ 和 ∼0.37e⁻, 表明补偿特性被破坏。这些结果表明,调节Ts 是一种有效控制载流子浓度的方法。我们推测,略高于 Ts = 420 °C最佳值的退火会在薄膜中产生Te缺陷,这些缺陷作为电子给体。另一方面,略低于最佳值的退火会在薄膜上形成Te吸附原子,这些原子作为有效受主,这可能是由于Te (4.95 eV) [31] 的功函数略大于TiTe₂ (4.86 eV) [32]。
> 
> **Term**: **Work function**: 功函数, 将一个电子从固体内部移至其表面所需的最小能量。
> 
> Interesting physics manifests itself when we carefully monitor the folded bands at the M point. While the Ts = 420 °C sample apparently shows replica hole bands at the M point due to CDW [arrows in the top and bottom panels of Fig. 2(b)], the replica bands are not seen for both the Ts = 400 °C [Fig. 2(a)] and 440 °C [Fig. 2(c)] samples. This suggests that CDW appears only in a narrow carrier concentration range around the zero total carrier in both the hole- and electron-doped regions. To further examine the evolution of band structure upon carrier doping, we have systematically deposited K atoms on the pristine sample (Ts = 420 °C), and show in Figs. 2(g)-2(i) the ARPES intensity for the K dosing time tK of 6, 12, and 24 min. It is evident that the dosing of K atoms systematically moves the electron and hole bands downward indicative of electron doping into the system, similarly to the case of increasing Ts. The effective total carrier estimated from the FS volume shown in Figs. 2(j)-2(l) is ∼0.07e⁻, ∼0.21e⁻, and ∼0.39e⁻ for tK = 6, 12, and 24 min, respectively. These values correspond to the effective K coverage of 7, 21, and 39 % per TiTe₂ unit cell, under the assumption that one K atom donates one electron to a TiTe₂ layer. As can be seen from Figs. 2(g)-2(i), folded bands are always absent irrespective of tK. This suggests that CDW appears only at the zero doping where electron and hole carriers are compensated. 当我们在M 点仔细监测折叠能带时,有趣的物理现象会显现出来。虽然 Ts = 420 °C样品在Fig. 2(b)图的上、下面板中由于CDW [箭头]而在 M点显示出赝能带,但在 Ts = 400 °C [Fig. 2(a)] 和 440 °C [Fig. 2(c)] 样品中却未观察到赝能带。这表明CDW仅出现在空穴和电子掺杂区域中,总载流子接近零的狭窄载流子浓度范围内。为了进一步研究载流子掺杂对能带结构演化的影响,我们在原始样品(Ts = 420 °C)上系统地沉积了K原子,并在Figs. 2(g)-2(i)中展示了K沉积时间为6、12和24分钟的ARPES强度。很明显,K原子的沉积系统地使电子和空穴能带向下移动,表明电子掺杂进入系统,这与增加 Ts的情况类似。根据Figs. 2(j)-2(l)中显示的费米面体积估算的有效总载流子分别为 ∼0.07e⁻, ∼0.21e⁻和 ∼0.39e⁻ 对于 tK = 6、12和24分钟,分别。这些值对应于每个TiTe₂ 单胞的7、21和39%的有效K覆盖度,假设每个K原子向TiTe₂层提供一个电子。从Figs. 2(g)-2(i)可以看出,无论 tK如何,折叠能带始终不存在。这表明CDW仅在电子和空穴载流子补偿的总掺杂量为零时出现。
> 
> ### C. Observation of a CDW gap
> 
> ### C. 观察到电荷密度波能隙
> 
> To clarify the CDW transition temperature (TCDW) in monolayer TiTe₂, we have performed the temperature-dependent ARPES measurements near EF. Figure 2(m) shows the EDCs at the kF point on the inner hole pocket around the Γ point measured at various temperatures (T = 30 - 230 K). One can see that the leading-edge midpoint is shifted to ward higher EB by 2-10 meV at T = 30 K, accompanied with a two-peaked structure (black arrows). The peak closer to EF is associated with the CDW-gap opening, whereas that at higher EB is produced by the electron band folded from the M point due to the 2×2 CDW formation [21]. On increasing temperature, the leading-edge midpoint gradually moves to ward EF and finally reaches EF at T = 90 K, suggesting that the CDW sets in at ∼ 90 K. We found that the CDW gap opens also on the electron pocket at the M point, and it is strongly anisotropic. As seen from Fig. 2(o), there is a peak at 70 meV in the EDC at the kF point along the shorter axis of the ellipsoidal pocket (point 5 in the inset) where the FS of ellipsoidal pocket overlaps well with the folded inner hole pocket. This peak exhibits a finite leading-edge shift toward higher EB (see vertical black line and arrows) due to the CDW-gap opening. On approaching the long axis of the ellipsoidal pocket (from point 5 to point 1), the peak systematically approaches EF and the leading-edge shift eventually vanishes. This strong k dependence is better visualized in the symmetrized EDCs in Fig. 2(p) as well as the plot of peak position in the symmetrized EDCs and leading-edge shift in Fig. 2(q). From the analyses of the FS, we found that the inner hole pocket at Γ and the elongated electron pocket at M partially overlap by the 2×2 q vector (qCDW) in the pristine sample, as schematically shown in Fig. 2(r). We will come back to this point later. 为了阐明单层TiTe₂中的电荷密度波 (CDW) 转变温度 (TCDW), 我们在 EF 附近进行了温度依赖ARPES测量。图2(m)显示了在 Γ 点附近的内空穴口袋处的kF 点的能谱 (EDCs), 测量温度为 (T = 30 - 230 K)。可以看出,在 T = 30 K时,主边中点向更高的 EB 方向移动了2-10 meV,并伴随着双峰结构(黑色箭头所示)。距离 EF 更近的峰与电荷密度波能隙开启相关,而位于更高 EB 处的峰是由来自 M 点的电子能带折叠产生的,这是由于 2×2 电荷密度波形成 [21]。随着温度升高,前沿中点逐渐移向 EF ,并在 T = 90 K 时最终达到 EF ,表明电荷密度波在 ∼ 90 K 时出现。我们发现电荷密度波能隙也在 M 点的电子口袋处打开,并且它具有很强的各向异性。从图 2(o) 可以看出,在椭球口袋短轴方向(插图中的点 5)沿 kF 点的 EDC 在 70 meV 处有一个峰,其中椭球口袋的费米面与折叠的内空穴口袋很好地重叠。由于电荷密度波能隙开启,该峰表现出向更高 EB 的有限前沿偏移(见垂直黑线和箭头)。当接近椭球口袋的长轴(从点 5 到点 1)时,该峰系统性地接近 EF ,前沿偏移最终消失。这种强烈的 k 依赖性在图 2(p) 中的对称化 EDC 以及图 2(q) 中的对称化 EDC 峰位和前沿偏移图中得到了更好的可视化。通过费米面分析,我们发现原始样品中位于 Γ 的内空穴口袋和位于 M 的延展电子口袋部分地被 2×2 q 向量 (qCDW) 重叠,如图 2(r) 中示意所示。我们稍后会回到这一点。
> 
> ### D. Mechanism of CDW
> 
> ### D. 电荷密度波机制
> 
> Now that the band structure of pristine and doped monolayer TiTe₂ is established, an important question is the mechanism of CDW in monolayer TiTe₂ which occurs only in the pristine sample. We compare in Fig. 3 the band structure between monolayer TiTe₂ and isostructural monolayer TiSe₂ which also undergoes a 2×2 CDW below ∼240 K [15, 28–30]. In the CDW phase of monolayer TiSe₂ [Fig. 3(a)], replica hole bands with a sizable intensity at the M point associated with the exciton condensation are seen, in line with its indirect zero-gap semiconducting nature which does not satisfy the FS-nesting condition [15]. The exciton condensation is associated with the strong Coulomb interaction between electron and hole [Fig. 3(b)][16–20], and gives rise to a sizable energy gap (exciton gap) of ∼0.2 eV [18–20]. On the other hand, monolayer TiTe₂ is a semimetal with a negative band gap [Fig. 3(c)] so that it is hard to stabilize excitons because of the strong screening by mobile carriers [Fig. 3(d)]. Thus, the exciton condensation mechanism is unlikely to account for the observed 2×2 CDW in monolayer TiTe₂ as discussed in the previous studies [13, 14]. 现在原始样品和掺杂单层TiTe₂ 的能带结构已经建立,一个重要的问题是单层TiTe₂ 中电荷密度波 (CDW) 的机制,该CDW仅发生在原始样品中。我们在图3中比较了单层TiTe₂ 与同构单层TiSe₂ 的能带结构,后者也在低于 ∼240K时经历 2×2 CDW [15, 28–30]。在单层TiSe₂ [的CDW相中,图3(a)], 显示了在M点与激子凝聚相关的具有可观强度的赝能带,这与它的间接零带隙半导体性质一致,这种性质不满足FS-嵌套条件 [15]。激子凝聚与电子和空穴之间的强库仑相互作用 [图3(b)][16–20] 相关,并产生一个可观能隙(激子能隙) ∼0.2 eV [18–20]。另一方面,单层TiTe₂ 是一种半金属,具有负带隙[图3(c)] ,因此由于移动载流子的强屏蔽作用 [图3(d)], 难以稳定激子。因此,激子凝聚机制不太可能解释先前研究中观察到的单层TiTe₂ 中的电荷密度波 (CDW) [13, 14]。
> 
> There are some other mechanisms to possibly account for the CDW in TiTe₂, such as (i) disorder effect, (ii) electron scattering between van-Hove singularities [33–35], (iii) the Jahn-Teller distortion [36], (iv) strong electron-phonon coupling [37–40], and (v) FS nesting. Scenario (i) is unlikely because we observed that the quasiparticle lifetime remains unchanged even in the K-deposited (tK = 6 min) sample in which the CDW is absent (see Fig. S5 in Supplemental Material [26]). Scenario (ii) is also ruled out because the band structure in the normal state does not contain van-Hove singularities. For scenario (iii), the Jahn-Teller distortion is generally accompanied by the change in the atomic arrangement and the crystal symmetry, leading to the energy shift of bands, band folding, and sometimes lifting of band degeneracy. We found that the energy position of bands (such as the M-shaped band at 1.6-2.0 eV at the Γ point, two hole bands dispersing toward EF around the Γ point, and the electron band at the M point) shows no apparent difference between T = 180 K and 30 K [Figs. 1(d) and 1(f)] except for the energy-gap opening in the vicinity of EF. This implies that the influence of Jahn-Teller distortion to the electronic states (if it exists) may be rather small, while a structural experiment to determine exact amount of distortion needs to be further carried out. For scenario (iv), we found that a kink in the energy dispersion, a hallmark of a strong electron-phonon coupling, is not clearly observed in monolayer, in contrast to the case of bulk TiTe₂ [40] (see Fig. S4 in Supplemental Material [26]). Since the possibility of insufficient energy resolution is not fully excluded at the moment to account for the absence of kink, further examination of this scenario requires higher-resolution ARPES data and the experimental determination of phonon dispersion below and above TCDW. We think that the most plausible scenario is (v) the FS nesting. As shown by the schematic FS topology for three different doping levels (hole-doped, pristine, and electron-doped) in Fig. 2(r), the inner hole and elongated electron pockets are partially nested with each other in the pristine sample. In fact, we identified an energy gap on the hole pocket below ∼90 K associated with the CDW gap, as already demonstrated in Fig. 2(m) (note that the gap opening associated with the overlap between the inner hole pocket and the shorter axis of electron pocket is consistent with the proposed orbital-selective band hybridization [14]). Also, the observation of anisotropic CDW gap on the electron pocket at the M point is well explained in terms of the FS nesting, because the overlap between the folded hole pocket and the ellipsoidal electron pocket becomes gradually small on approaching point 1 from point 5 as seen in Fig. 2(o), in accordance with the closure of the CDW gap. On the other hand, the FS overlap becomes ill-defined in the hole- (top panel) and electron-doped (bottom panel) regimes and no clear signature of the CDW gap was observed in these samples. Specifically, in the hole-doped sample, the electron pocket shrinks and the kF point along its shorter axis becomes smaller than that of the inner hole pocket. In the heavily electron-doped sample, the electron pocket significantly expands and its kF point becomes larger than that of the outer hole pocket. As a result, the FS of pristine sample accidentally shows a relatively good nesting condition compared to the doped samples. One may wonder by comparing Figs. 2(e) and 2(j) why the CDW is absent in the tK = 6 min sample which seems to still satisfy the partial nesting condition. While the reason is unclear at the moment, we speculate that the expansion of the longer axis of the ellipsoidal electron pocket in the tK = 6 min sample effectively reduces the ratio of nested k region with respect to the total FS region, making the CDW relatively unstable. To further examine the scenario of FS-nesting-driven CDW, we calculated the electronic susceptibility χ₀(q) based on the results of our DFT calculations. Specifically, we calculated the q (momentum transfer) dependence of the real part of χ₀(q) (Re[χ₀(q))]), based on the calculated FS for monolayer TiTe₂. As seen in Fig. 4(a), a local maximum of Re[χ₀(q)] is located at q = 1/2G corresponding to the M point (G: reciprocal lattice vector) in pristine non-doped TiTe₂. This feature is associated with the FS nesting between the inner hole pocket and the ellipsoidal electron pocket. On the other hand, in electron-doped TiTe₂ (nearly corresponding to the case of tK = 12 min), the bright spot is absent at q = 1/2G [Fig. 4(b)], consistent with the absence of the 2×2 CDW in the electron-doped samples. Thus, our experimental finding that the FS-nesting-driven CDW is realized only around the non-doped region is supported by the calculated electronic susceptibility. Since appearance/absence of the 2×2 CDW in monolayer TiTe₂ is sensitive to the FS topology, it is inferred that the FS-nesting-driven CDW in monolayer TiTe₂ is more fragile compared to the exciton-condensation-driven CDW in monolayer TiSe₂. The reason why CDW is absent in bulk, but not in monolayer, would be due to the difference in the dimensionality of the FS between bulk and monolayer; i.e. bulk TiTe₂ shows a strong warping effect along kz for the electron pocket at the M/L point which does not favor the FS nesting, instead of the purely 2D pocket in the monolayer case. The high controllability of the FS topology for monolayer TiTe₂ established in this study is thereby useful to switch normal metallic and CDW phases. A next step is to search for exotic quantum phases such as superconductivity in the proximity to the CDW phase by perturbing the system using e.g. electrical gating and/or chemical doping. 存在一些其他机制可能用于解释TiTe₂中的电荷密度波 (CDW), 例如 (i) 无序效应, (ii) 范霍夫奇异点 [33–35] 之间的电子散射, (iii) 雅恩-泰勒畸变 [36], (iv) 强电子-声子耦合 [37–40], 以及 (v) 费米面嵌套。情景 (i) 不太可能,因为我们观察到即使在K沉积 (tK = 6 最小) 的样品中(其中不存在CDW), 准粒子寿命保持不变(参见补充材料 [26] 中的图 S5)。情景 (ii) 也被排除,因为正常状态下的能带结构不包含范霍夫奇异点。对于情景 (iii), 雅恩-泰勒畸变通常伴随着原子排列和晶体对称性的变化,导致能带发生能量偏移、能带折叠,有时还会导致能带简并性的解除。我们发现,能带(例如在 Γ 点处 1.6-2.0 eV 的 M 形能带、在 EF 点附近分散的两个空穴能带以及在 M 点处的电子能带)在 T = 180 K 和 30 K [Figs. 1(d) 和 1(f)] 之间的能量位置没有明显差异,除了在 EF 附近的能隙打开。这意味着雅恩-泰勒畸变对电子态的影响(如果存在的话)可能相当小,而需要进一步进行结构实验以确定畸变的精确量。对于情景 (iv), 我们发现能散中的折点(强电子-声子耦合的标志)在单层中未明显观察到,与体材料 TiTe₂ [40] 的情况形成对比(参见补充材料 [26] 中的图 S4)。由于目前尚不能完全排除能量分辨率不足的可能性来解释折点的缺失,进一步检验此情景需要更高分辨率的ARPES数据以及低于和高于 TCDW 的声子散布的实验测定。我们认为最可能的情景是 (v) 费米面嵌套。如图 2(r) 所示的三个不同掺杂水平(空穴掺杂、原始样品和电子掺杂)的费米面拓扑示意图,在原始样品中,内空穴口袋和延长的电子口袋部分嵌套。事实上,我们识别出在低于 ∼90 K 的空穴口袋上存在一个与CDW能隙相关的能隙,如图 2(m) 中已经展示(注意,与内空穴口袋和电子口袋短轴重叠相关的能隙开启与所提出的轨道选择性能带杂化 [14] 一致)。此外,在 M 点小于内空穴口袋的kF 点。在高度电子掺杂样品中,电子口袋显著扩展,其kF 点大于外空穴口袋的kF 点。因此,原始样品的费米面偶然显示出相对于掺杂样品的相对较好的嵌套条件。通过比较图2(e)和图2(j), 人们可能会疑惑为什么在看似仍然满足部分嵌套条件的 tK = 6 min样品中缺少电荷密度波。虽然目前原因尚不清楚,但我们推测在 tK = 6 min样品中椭球状电子口袋的长轴扩展有效地降低了嵌套 电荷密度波的出现/消失对费米面拓扑敏感,因此推断单层TiTe₂ 中的费米面嵌套驱动的电荷密度波与单层TiSe₂中的激子凝聚驱动的电荷密度波相比更为脆弱。电荷密度波在体材料中缺失但在单层中存在的原因可能是体材料和单层之间费米面的维数差异;即体TiTe₂ 在 M/L 点沿 kz 方向对电子口袋显示出强烈的扭曲效应,这不利于费米面嵌套,而不是单层情况中的纯粹二维口袋。本研究中建立的单层TiTe₂ 的费米面拓扑的高可控性,可用于切换正常金属和电荷密度波相。下一步是通过扰动系统在电荷密度波相邻近区域寻找超导性等奇异量子相,例如电控和/或化学掺杂。

> **Term**: **Van-Hove singularity**: 范霍夫奇异点, 固体能带中电子态密度出现尖峰的能量点, 可增强电子关联效应。 **Term**: **Jahn-Teller distortion**: 雅恩-泰勒畸变, 分子或固体中简并电子基态导致的几何结构畸变, 以降低系统能量。 **Term**: **Electronic susceptibility**: 电子磁化率, 描述电子系统对外加电荷或自旋扰动的响应函数, 其峰值常与电荷/自旋密度波的不稳定性相关。

> ## IV. CONCLUSION
> 
> ## IV. 结论
> 
> In conclusion, we performed high-resolution ARPES study on monolayer 1T-TiTe₂ grown on bilayer graphene. We have succeeded in controlling the carrier concentration in a wide range, by using two independent methods, controlling the substrate temperature during epitaxy and K dosing on the sample. We found that the 2×2 CDW, as characterized by the band folding, is fragile against carrier doping and emerges only at the effectively zero doping level with the electron and hole compensation. The present study points to importance of carrier tuning to clarify the mechanism of CDW in monolayer TMDs. 总之,我们对生长在双层石墨烯上的单层1T-TiTe₂ 进行了高分辨率ARPES研究。我们通过两种独立的方法——控制外延过程中的衬底温度和向样品中添加K——成功地在宽范围内控制了载流子浓度。我们发现, 2×2 电荷密度波 (CDW), 通过能带折叠表征, 对载流子掺杂很敏感,仅在有效零掺杂且电子和空穴补偿时出现。本研究表明,载流子调谐对于阐明单层TMDs中CDW的机制至关重要。
