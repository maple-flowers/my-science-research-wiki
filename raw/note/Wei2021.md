> ## ABSTRACT
> 
> ## 摘要
> 
> Density functional tight binding (DFTB) simulations were performed to study structures, energy, and Mülliken populations for Zigzag type (n,0) (n = 3–14) bamboo-like nitrogen carbon nanotubes with spaced packing patterns. After performing self-consistant-charge DFTB calculations, geometric parameters of bamboo-type nitrogen carbon nanotubes were obtained, including the nearest neighbor length of C–C or N–N atoms, the average C–N length, the diameters of C or N rings, the C-C-N angle and the C-N-N angle of the six-atom ring parallel to the tube axis, the C-C-C angle and the N-N-N angle perpendicular to the tube axis. The intrinsic energy,energy gap, and chemical potential were analyzed, and they present apparent differences compared with their pure carbon counterparts. We have thoroughly analyzed the charges transfer of the C and N atoms for the bamboo-like nitrogen‐carbon nanotubes. Mülliken populations present different prospects on these C or N atoms as the chiral index increases, especially for these relative small indexes. 采用密度泛函紧束缚（DFTB）方法，模拟研究了具有间隔排布模式的锯齿型 (n,0) (n = 3–14) 竹节状氮碳纳米管的结构、能量和Mülliken布居。通过自洽电荷DFTB计算，获得了竹节型氮碳纳米管的几何参数，包括C–C或N–N原子最近邻距离、平均C–N键长、C环或N环的直径、平行于管轴的六原子环的C-C-N角和C-N-N角，以及垂直于管轴的C-C-C角和N-N-N角。分析了本征能量、能隙和化学势，它们与纯碳对应物相比呈现出明显差异。我们深入分析了竹节状氮碳纳米管中C和N原子的电荷转移。随着手性指数增加，Mülliken布居在这些C或N原子上呈现出不同的变化趋势，尤其是对于相对较小的指数。

> ## 1. Introduction
> 
> ## 1. 引言
> 
> Over the past few decades,nanometer materials have obtained significant achievements both in theory and experiments. As one of the most materials on nano-scale, N-doped CNTs have been widely used in catalysis and energy storage [1,2]. Experimentally, N-doped CNTs were prepared by different methods using graphitized nitrogen (N-Q), oxypyridine nitrogen (N-6), pyrrole nitrogen (N-5), oxypyridine (N-x), -NO2 and -NH2 with different content [3,4]. Bao et al. prepared N-doped CNTs by thermochemical vapor deposition in the temperature range of 850–1000 ◦C [5]. Usually, the substitution N-doped tube for grids in body can change morphology and properties of these tubes. For example, because nitrogen atoms provide additional electron carriers for the conduction band, the conductivity of CNTs is enhanced. Elías et al. demonstrated that the changes of the electrical properties for the grown single-walled N-CNTs were related to various concentrations of N liquid source in the feedstock [6]. Additionally, the content of nitrogen in these N-CNTs mainly depends on the solubility of the nitrogen during the synthesis and the concentration of the nitrogen-containing compound in the reaction mixture. These dissolved N atoms would precipitate with the C atoms during the growth of the CNTs, and the CNTs exhibit a bamboo-like structure in which the inside part of the cube is separated into a series of compartments [7]. Park Seung-Keun et al. studied 1D bamboo-like N-doped carbon nanotubes encapsulating Co nanoparticles as efficient cathode hosts for Li–S batteries [8]. L.M. Cao et al. synthetized bamboo-like N-doped nanotubes arrays by radiofrequency magnetron sputtering [9]. Chen et al. synthesized the cobalt‐nickel alloy encapsulated with bamboo nitrogen doped carbon nanotubes as microwave adsorbent by means of cooperative catalytic pyrolysis. The design of bamboo-like nitrogen-doped carbon tubes not only provides a rich conductive cavity wall for the generation of induced current, but also can prevent the metal ions encapsulated in carbon nanotubes from corrosion. Moreover, the bamboo-like tubes can maintain the original magnetism and improve the impedance matching characteristics of these carbon nanotubes under extreme chemical environment [10]. Bamboo-like nitrogen-doped carbon nanotubes (N-CNTs) synthesized by facile pyrolysis method were applied to test fluorescence recovery assay DNA detection [11], and they present a low detection limit of 5 nm. Here, some experiments found that nitrogen atoms were concentrated at nodal regions in these bamboo-like structures, and the concentrations were above 23 at.% [12]. The skeleton composed of bamboo-like NCNTs could not only provide smooth mass transport pathways but also facilitate fast electron transfer [13]. In theoretical research, Cairu Shao et al. used ab initio calculations to show that B–N co-doping zigzag SWCNTs having ultra-small diameter are stable. With increasing the doping concentration, the tubes show obvious semiconductor properties [14]. C. H. San et al. have demonstrated that N-doped CNTs has outperformed both pristine and Pt-doped CNTs on the specific thermal capacity, can have a higher structural stability in the oxygen reduction reaction operation [15]. Therefore, it is an interesting issue for the bamboo-type nitrogen and carbon nanotubes from theoretical approaches. Accounting for the fact that there is an exponential increase in computational cost as well as increasing the number of atoms on the nanoscale [16], sophisticated ab initio approaches become hardly possible for systems containing hundreds of atoms. Atomic simulations based on semi-empirical quantum methods, such as Density Functional Tight Binding (DFTB), are particularly well suited to characterize microscopic details in such systems involving atomic packing and electronic behaviors. Successful examples of DFTB simulations have investigated the structural changes and electronic properties related to alloy clusters, sheets, nanowires, and atomic adsorption on substrates [17–21]. In order to further explore the related properties of bamboo-like Ndoped CNTs,we choose carbon nanotubes with Zigzag (n,0) (n = 3–14) tubes, in which some carbon atoms were replaced with nitrogen atoms with a proportion of 1:1. Density Functional Tight Binding (DFTB) calculations are used to study the structures and properties of these Ndoped CNTs with different tube diameters in the present work. These values were examined including the intrinsic energy per atom, the energy gap, and zero chemical potential values as well as the atomic packing patterns in these bamboo-like nitrogen‐carbon tubes. The Mülliken populations were also analyzed to exam the charge transfer among the atoms. > ## 2. Theoretical method
> 
> ## 2. 理论方法
> 
> The present simulations used DFTB+ software, which is developed by Bremen University in Germany [22]. Within the SCC -DFTB formalism, total energy of the system in the method is given by the following formula [23,24]. 本模拟采用德国不来梅大学开发的DFTB+软件[22]。在SCC-DFTB框架下，该方法的体系总能量由以下公式给出[23,24]。
> 
> ```
> Escc = ∑i ni ⟨φi|Ĥ0|φi⟩ + 1/2 ∑αβ ΔqαΔqβγαβ + Erep (1)
> 
> ```
> 
> 其中，ni 是分子轨道 φi 的占据数，Ĥ0 是Kohn-Sham算符。Mülliken布居差 Δqα 由α原子的Mülliken布居 qα 和中性原子的价电子数 qα0 计算得出 (Δqα=qα-qα0) [25]。Erep 是双体排斥势。γαβ 定义如下： **SCC-DFTB**: 自洽电荷密度泛函紧束缚方法（Self-Consistent-Charge Density Functional Tight Binding），在DFTB基础上引入电荷自洽处理，提高计算精度。 **Mülliken population**: Mülliken布居，一种基于原子轨道线性组合的电子布居分析方法，用于分析分子中原子上的电荷分布。
> 
> ```
> γαβ = (1/rαβ) Sαβ (2)
> 
> ```
> 
> 这里，rαβ 是两原子间距离，Sαβ 是原子核间的短程修正项。Mülliken布居由原子轨道线性组合（LCAO）系数计算得到[25–27]。对于包含N个电子的双原子分子（原子 r 和 s），布居由下式给出：
> 
> ```
> N = Nc_r^2 + 2Nc_rc_sS_rs + Nc_s^2 (3)
> 
> ```
> 
> 其中 Srs 表示重叠积分 ∫∞χrχs dν，χr 和 χs 是原子 k 和 l 的归一化原子轨道，cr 和 cs 是轨道系数。Nc_r^2 和 Nc_s^2 代表原子 r 和 s 上的净原子布居，2Nc_rc_sS_rs 是重叠布居。两个原子上的原子布居 N(r) 和 N(s) 分别表示为：
> 
> ```
> N(r) = N(c_r^2 + c_rc_sS_rs); N(s) = N(c_rc_sS_rs + c_s^2) (4)
> 
> ```
> 
> 对于一个包含 Natom 个原子（A, B... 作为原子符号）和 N 个电子的体系，分子轨道如下：
> 
> ```
> φi = ∑A ∑μ cAμi χAμ (5)
> 
> ```
> 
> 其中 χAμ 是原子A的原子轨道，c 是轨道系数。于是，
> 
> ```
> φi*φi = ∑A ∑B ∑μ cAμi cBλi χAμ χBλ (6)
> 
> ```
> 
> 对等式两边积分，并乘以 ni 可得：
> 
> ```
> ni = ni ∑A ∑μ c^2_{Aμi} + 2ni ∑_{A>B} ∑_μ ∑_λ c_{Aμi} c_{Bλi} S_{Aμ,Bλ} (7)
> 
> ```
> 
> 该方程可用于分析电荷分布。分布在原子轨道上的电荷为：
> 
> ```
> n(i, Aμ) = ni c^2_{Aμi} (8)
> 
> ```
> 
> 对 i 求和，原子轨道 χAμ 上的电荷为：
> 
> ```
> n(Aμ) = ∑_i n(i, Aμ) = ∑_i ni c^2_{Aμi} (9)
> 
> ```
> 
> 对原子A的所有原子轨道求和，得到原子A的电荷：
> 
> ```
> n(A) = ∑_μ n(Aμ) (10)
> 
> ```
> 
> A原子的μ轨道和B原子的λ轨道的总重叠电荷为：
> 
> ```
> n(i, Aμ, Bλ) = 2ni c_{Aμi} c_{Bλi} S_{Aμ,Bλ} (11)
> 
> ```
> 
> 众所周知，CNT由一对描述手性矢量 Ch = n a1 + m a2 的手性指数 (n,m) 定义，其中 a1 和 a2 是二维石墨烯片中的两个晶格矢量。如图1所示，从六角网格中切出的锯齿型 (5,0) GNR 绕 y 轴扭转成单壁碳纳米管 (5,0)。在这些CNTs中，平行于管 y 轴的C–C键长设为1.420 Å，不平行于管轴的C–C键长设为1.422 Å。然后，通过用氮原子层替代碳原子层，构建锯齿型 (5,0) 氮掺杂单壁碳纳米管。这里，灰色球代表碳原子，紫色球代表氮原子。在本工作中，考虑了12种初始N-CNTs构型。为忽略周期性镜像间的相互作用，这些管在 x 和 z 方向分别使用了200 Å和200 Å的真空层。为表征原子间的相互作用，这些管的本征能量按下式计算：
> 
> ```
> Eins(N-CNTs) = [nC E(C) + nN E(N) - Etot(N-CNTs)] / N (12)
> 
> ```
> 
> 这里，Eins(N-CNTs) 是每原子本征能量，nC 是C原子数，nN 是N原子数，N 是总原子数。E(C) 和 E(N) 分别是碳和氮的单原子能量。Etot(N-CNTs) 是N-CNTs的总能量。通常，最高占据分子轨道（HOMO）能量 EHOMO 与最低未占分子轨道（LUMO）能量 ELUMO 之间存在能量差。能隙由 Eg = ELUMO - EHOMO 给出。 **HOMO**: 最高占据分子轨道（Highest Occupied Molecular Orbital），分子中能量最高的被电子占据的轨道。 **LUMO**: 最低未占分子轨道（Lowest Unoccupied Molecular Orbital），分子中能量最低的未被电子占据的轨道。

> ### 3.2. Energy analysis
> 
> ### 3.2. 能量分析
> 
> Fig. 5(a) and (b) shows intrinsic energy per atom for pure CNTs and the bamboo-like N-CNTs. The energy increases quickly as the diameter increases for these pure CNTs having small diameters. In a CNT, one C atom is connected with two C atoms to form a hexagonal network pattern, and these C atoms mainly present sp2 hybridization. However, the hexagonal grids of the CNTs will be bent to a certain extent. In the formed spatial topological structures, there are some sp3 hybrid orbitals. For these CNTs with small diameters, the ratio of sp3 hybridization is large. For the tubes having larger diameters, the increase slope of the energy becomes small owing to the decrease of the sp3 ratio. N has one more electron than C. The N-CNTs are easy to form sp3 bonding. Therefore, the intrinsic energy of the N-CNTs is apparently higher than that of the pure tubes owing to the substitution of the nitrogen atoms, and it can be found that the intrinsic energy increases quickly from the chiral index from 3 to 5. Then, the energy increases slowly in an oscillating mode. 图5(a)和(b)显示了纯CNTs和竹节状N-CNTs的每原子本征能量。对于具有小直径的纯CNTs，能量随直径增大而迅速增加。在CNT中，一个C原子与两个C原子相连形成六边形网络图案，这些C原子主要呈现sp2杂化。然而，CNTs的六边形网格会发生一定程度的弯曲。在形成的空间拓扑结构中，存在一些sp3杂化轨道。对于小直径CNTs，sp3杂化比例较大。对于具有较大直径的管，由于sp3比例降低，能量的增加斜率变小。N比C多一个电子。N-CNTs容易形成sp3键合。因此，由于氮原子的替代，N-CNTs的本征能量明显高于纯管，且可以发现，从手性指数3到5，本征能量迅速增加。然后，能量以振荡模式缓慢增加。

> The coexistence of sp2 and sp3 hybridization also affects energy gaps of these CNTs and N-CNTs, especially for these tubes with small diameters. As illustrated in Fig. 5(c), the energy gaps of pure CNTs and bamboo-like N-CNTs behave quite differently. The energy gap of the (5,0) CNTs tube is 0.38 eV, which is in close to the value of 0.37 eV reported in Ref. [29]. For the three pairs of the (7,0)-(8,0), (10,0)-(11,0), and (13,0)-(14,0), they have apparently higher energy gaps compared with the other tubes. The changes of the energy gaps for these (10–14,0) tubes also can be found in Ref. [30]. For the N-CNTs with the indexes of 3, 6, and 8, they have very small gap values, whereas the tubes with 4, 5, and 7 relatively wide gaps. As the index is higher than 10, the gap values become small again. Small energy gap implies that the tube is metallic. Being compared to the pure CNTs, some N-CNTs with small indexes have relative large energy gap, whereas those with large indexes become metallic. For the N-CNTs having the index ranging from 5 to 10, the energy gap value with the odd index is higher than that with even. Fig. 5(d) illustrates the changes of the chemical potential. The pure CNTs presents increase as the chiral index increases. The electron that the N atom has more than the C atom is mainly distributed near nitrogen atoms, so that the chemical activity of nitrogen doped carbon nanotubes near nitrogen atoms is higher than other parts. Therefore, the potential of the N-CNTs is apparently higher than that of the pure C tubes. When the index is higher than 9, followed quickly decreasing, the potential changes little. For the two kinds of tubes, the small diameter tubes show more obvious change of chemical potential compared with the large diameter ones, and this is especially true for N-CNTs. sp2和sp3杂化的共存也影响这些CNTs和N-CNTs的能隙，尤其是对于小直径管。如图5(c)所示，纯CNTs和竹节状N-CNTs的能隙表现截然不同。(5,0) CNTs管的能隙为0.38 eV，与文献[29]报道的0.37 eV相近。对于(7,0)-(8,0)、(10,0)-(11,0)和(13,0)-(14,0)三对管，它们的能隙明显高于其他管。这些(10–14,0)管能隙的变化也可在文献[30]中找到。对于指数为3、6和8的N-CNTs，它们具有非常小的能隙值，而指数为4、5和7的管则具有相对较宽的能隙。当指数大于10时，能隙值再次变小。小能隙意味着管具有金属性。与纯CNTs相比，某些小指数的N-CNTs具有相对较大的能隙，而大指数管则变为金属性。对于指数在5到10之间的N-CNTs，奇数指数的能隙值高于偶数指数。图5(d)展示了化学势的变化。纯CNTs的化学势随手性指数增加而增加。N原子比C原子多出的电子主要分布在氮原子附近，因此氮掺杂碳纳米管在氮原子附近的化学活性高于其他部分。因此，N-CNTs的化学势明显高于纯C管。当指数大于9后，很快下降，然后化学势变化很小。对于两种类型的管，小直径管比大直径管表现出更明显的化学势变化，N-CNTs尤其如此。

> ### 3.3. Mülliken population

> ### 3.3. Mülliken布居
> 
> As illustrated in Fig. 6, the pure CNTs with small chiral index present apparent charge transfer among the C atoms. The difference of Mülliken Population is the largest for the (3,0) tube. Then, the differences decreases with increasing the index. As the index is higher than 7, charge transfer disappears. However, for these C atoms in the N-CNTs, apparent charge transfer still occurs, though this oscillation is more obvious for the tubes with small indexes than those with large ones. In addition, the charge transfer also does in the N atoms in the most N-CNTs. It can be noted that the charge transfer mainly occurs between C and N atoms for these N-CNTs. In order to visually observe the Mülliken population of each atom in the bamboo-like N-CNTs, we use different colors to characterize the Mülliken population of each atom. Fig. 7 shows Mülliken population of these C and N atoms in one unit including two carbon and nitrogen rings. For one neutral C or N atom, it has four or five electrons. Therefore, there are apparent color differences among C and N atoms. In the case of the (3,0) tube, the three N atoms connected with the C atoms presents a color change from yellow to orange, indicating a significant loss of electrons. Correspondingly, the three connecting C atoms get electrons. Moreover, one of the three carbon atoms connecting N atom gains more charges, and the corresponding N atom loses more charges. For the (4,0) tube, these C atoms lie in the interior apparently get some charges. As shown in Fig. 6, the charge difference between N atoms is almost zero, while that between C atoms is obvious, indicating that the charge mainly occurs between C atoms for this tube. Here, the connected C atoms in parallel with the tube axis get charge, while the carbon atoms being neighbor with N atoms lose charge. The C atoms in the (5,0) tube show similar gain or loss electrons with those in (4,0), where the atoms in the interior pick up more electrons than those connected to the N atoms. There is a small charge difference among the N atoms for this tube. Correspondingly, the C–N bond inclines toward the tube axis along the direction of nitrogen, and the C–C bond length in parallel with the tube axis is stretched. For the (6,0) tube, charge transfer occurs between carbon and nitrogen atoms. The N atoms by N–N bonding in parallel with the tube axis lose more charge than the other nitrogen atoms, and carbon atoms having C–C connection get more charge than other carbon atoms. Therefore the population difference becomes larger as shown in Fig. 6. As the chiral index increases to 7 or 8, the phenomenon can be found again that the N atoms connected by N–N bonding apparently lose more charge than the other N atoms, whereas the C atoms in C–C bonds gain more charge than the other carbon atoms. For the (9,0) case, half of the N atoms apparently lost charges, while the C atoms in the interior apparently pick up charges. The C atoms of the (10,0) tube get charges, but the C atoms connected with the N atoms get fewer charges. In the (11,0) and (12,0) tubes, the C atoms connected with N atoms lose a small amount of charges, whereas the other C atoms get charges. The population of the N atoms in the space arrangement is different, and the difference of the population on the N atoms is larger than that on C atoms. The population difference in the space arrangement becomes apparent for the C atoms in the (13,0) and (14, 0) tubes. In meanwhile, the difference in population also becomes very close for the C–C and N–N atoms as shown in Fig. 6, suggesting that the curvature effect of the tube surface has less influence on gain or loss charges as the chirality index becomes large. 如图6所示，具有小手性指数的纯CNTs中C原子间存在明显的电荷转移。Mülliken布居差在(3,0)管中最大。然后，随着指数增加，差值减小。当指数大于7时，电荷转移消失。然而，对于N-CNTs中的C原子，仍然发生明显的电荷转移，尽管这种振荡在小指数管中比大指数管更明显。此外，大多数N-CNTs中N原子也发生电荷转移。可以注意到，这些N-CNTs的电荷转移主要发生在C和N原子之间。为了直观观察竹节状N-CNTs中每个原子的Mülliken布居，我们使用不同颜色来表征每个原子的Mülliken布居。图7显示了包含两个碳环和氮环的单元中C和N原子的Mülliken布居。对于中性C原子或N原子，它分别有4或5个电子。因此，C和N原子间存在明显的颜色差异。在(3,0)管的情况下，与C原子相连的三个N原子呈现从黄色到橙色的颜色变化，表明显著的电子损失。相应地，三个相连的C原子得到电子。此外，连接N原子的三个碳原子中，有一个获得更多电荷，而对应的N原子失去更多电荷。对于(4,0)管，处于内部的C原子明显获得一些电荷。如图6所示，N原子间的电荷差异几乎为零，而C原子间的电荷差异明显，表明该管中电荷转移主要发生在C原子之间。这里，平行于管轴的连接C原子获得电荷，而与N原子相邻的碳原子失去电荷。(5,0)管中的C原子表现出与(4,0)相似的电子得失，其中内部原子比连接N原子的原子获得更多电子。该管中N原子间存在较小的电荷差异。相应地，C–N键沿氮原子方向向管轴倾斜，平行于管轴的C–C键长被拉伸。对于(6,0)管，电荷转移在碳和氮原子之间发生。通过N–N键平行于管轴的N原子比其他氮原子失去更多电荷，而具有C–C连接的碳原子比其他碳原子获得更多电荷，因此布居差变大，如图6所示。当手性指数增至7或8时，再次发现通过N–N键连接的N原子明显比其他N原子失去更多电荷，而C–C键中的C原子则比其他碳原子获得更多电荷。对于(9,0)情况，一半的N原子明显失去电荷，而内部的C原子明显获得电荷。(10,0)管的C原子获得电荷，但连接N原子的C原子获得较少电荷。在(11,0)和(12,0)管中，连接N原子的C原子失去少量电荷，而其他C原子获得电荷。N原子在空间排布上的布居不同，且N原子上的布居差异大于C原子上的布居差异。对于(13,0)和(14,0)管，C原子在空间排布上的布居差异变得明显。同时，C–C和N–N原子间的布居差异也变得非常接近，如图6所示，表明随着手性指数变大，管表面曲率效应对电荷得失的影响减小。

> ## 4. Conclusions
> 
> ## 4. 结论
> 
> In this paper,self-consistent charge density functional tight-binding approach was used to simulate pure CNTs and bamboo-like N-CNTs. This work constructed the models of N-doped CNT with alternative N-atom layer and C-atom layer for the system containing hundreds of atoms, and provided the geometry structures and electron information of these tubes. The geometric parameters of Zigzag (n = 3–14,0) pure CNTs and bamboo-like N-CNTs were obtained after performing DFTB+ geometric optimization. For the N-CNTs having odd or even chiral indexes, the diameter differences and cross-sectional ratio of the N and C rings presents oscillating behavior with the high and low values. The chiral index greatly affects the bonding angles among the atoms in the tube with small indexes. The N-CNTs present higher intrinsic energy compared with their pure counterparts. Being different from the pure CNTs, the NCNTs with large index present metallic properties, and the energy gaps in the (4,0) and(5,0) bamboo-like nanotubes are higher than those of pure CNTs. Doping N can apparently improve the chemical potential of these tubes. From the Mülliken population difference of bamboo-shaped nitrogen carbon nanotubes, it can be found that the charge difference among the C atoms in most tubes are higher than that among the N atoms. With the increase of the tube diameter, population patterns on these C or N atoms present similar space arrangements. The present simulations provide helpful support for developing novel molecular devices, and demonstrate the potential application of the bamboo-like nitrogen carbon nanotubes in vessels for electro-catalysts and electrochemical sensors. In addition, the tubes may be used in the field of light, corrosion resistance, and efficient microwave absorbents. 本文采用自洽电荷密度泛函紧束缚方法模拟了纯CNTs和竹节状N-CNTs。本工作针对包含数百个原子的体系，构建了N原子层和C原子层交替排列的N掺杂CNT模型，并提供了这些管的几何结构和电子信息。通过DFTB+几何优化，获得了锯齿型 (n = 3–14,0) 纯CNTs和竹节状N-CNTs的几何参数。对于具有奇数或偶数手性指数的N-CNTs，N环和C环的直径差以及横截面积比呈现高低值振荡行为。手性指数极大地影响小指数管中原子的键角。N-CNTs的本征能量高于其纯碳对应物。与纯CNTs不同，大指数的N-CNTs呈现金属性，(4,0)和(5,0)竹节状纳米管的能隙高于纯CNTs。掺杂N可以明显提高这些管的化学势。从竹节状氮碳纳米管的Mülliken布居差可以看出，大多数管中C原子间的电荷差异高于N原子间的电荷差异。随着管径增加，这些C或N原子上的布居模式呈现相似的空间排布。本模拟为开发新型分子器件提供了有益支持，并展示了竹节状氮碳纳米管在电催化剂和电化学传感器载体中的潜在应用。此外，这些管还可用于轻质、耐腐蚀和高效微波吸收剂领域。
