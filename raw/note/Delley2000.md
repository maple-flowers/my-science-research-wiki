> [!quote]- <center>Abstract</center>

> Recent extensions of the DMol3 local orbital density functional method for band structure calculations of insulating and metallic solids are described. Furthermore the method for calculating semilocal pseudopotential matrix elements and basis functions are detailed together with other unpublished parts of the methodology pertaining to gradient functionals and local orbital basis sets. The method is applied to calculations of the enthalpy of formation of a set of molecules and solids. We find that the present numerical localized basis sets yield improved results as compared to previous results for the same functionals. Enthalpies for the formation of H, N, O, F, Cl, and C, Si, S atoms from the thermodynamic reference states are calculated at the same level of theory. It is found that the performance in predicting molecular enthalpies of formation is markedly improved for the Perdew–Burke–Ernzerhof [Phys. Rev. Lett. 77, 3865 (1996)] functional.
>
>【摘要翻译】本文介绍了DMol3局域轨道密度泛函方法在绝缘和金属固体能带结构计算中的最新推广。此外，还详细介绍了计算半局部赝势矩阵元和基函数的方法，以及该方法中有关梯度泛函和局部轨道基集的其他未发表的部分。该方法用于计算一组分子和固体的生成焓。我们发现，对于相同的泛函，与以前的结果相比，本文的数值局部化基集得到了改进的结果。从热力学参考态出发，计算了H，N，O，F，Cl，C，Si，S原子的生成焓。发现Perdew–Burke–Ernzerhof[Phys.Rev.Lett.773865（1996）]泛函在预测分子生成焓方面的性能显著提高。

> ## 一、引言
> 
> 在计算化学和材料科学领域，精确预测分子和固体的性质是核心目标之一。实现这一目标的关键在于求解描述多电子体系的薛定谔方程，而**密度泛函理论 (Density Functional Theory, DFT)** 是当前应用最广泛、最成功的量子力学近似方法之一。DFT 的核心思想是将复杂的多电子波函数问题，简化为仅依赖于三维空间中电子密度分布的问题。
> 
> 然而，DFT 的实际应用依赖于具体的数值实现方法，一个关键选择是用于展开电子波函数的数学基组。本文介绍的 **DMol3 方法**，是一种基于**局域化数值原子轨道 (localized numerical atomic orbitals)** 的 DFT 实现方案。这种方法吸取了早期版本 DMol 在分子计算中的成功经验，并将其关键能力扩展到了对**绝缘体、半导体和金属固体**的精确计算。本文的核心价值在于，它不仅详细阐述了将局域化基组方法从“分子”推广到“固体的技术细节，还通过一系列基准测试，系统地验证了该方法在不同材料体系（如 Cu, Si, 石墨）中的表现，并探讨了不同密度泛函（如 PWC, BP, PBE）对生成焓等关键热力学性质预测精度的影响。这篇论文是理解现代材料模拟软件如何在精度与效率之间取得平衡的奠基性文献之一。
> 
> ## 二、论文结构与逻辑概览
> 
> 这篇论文遵循了从方法构建到应用验证的经典逻辑结构，其核心论证链条非常清晰：
> 
> 1.  **提出问题**：如何将 DMol 这种高精度的分子 DFT 方法有效扩展到凝聚态体系（固体）的计算？
> 2.  **方法构建**：
>     -   **核心技术**：详细阐述了为实现固体计算而发展的几项关键方法学，包括：新型局域化基组、针对重原子的半局域赝势、处理周期性边界条件的布里渊区 k 点积分技术，以及梯度修正泛函的数值实现。
>     -   **性能标定**：通过对 Cu, Si, 石墨等典型体系进行计算，系统地研究了这些方法在不同计算参数（如基组截断半径、k 点网格密度）下的收敛行为。
> 3.  **应用验证与讨论**：
>     -   **基准测试**：将新方法应用于计算一系列分子和固体的**生成焓 (enthalpy of formation)**，并与已知的实验值或高精度计算（如 FLAPW）进行比较。
>     -   **功能性评估**：重点评估了不同密度泛函（特别是 BP 和 PBE）在预测生成焓上的表现，并分析了使用从头计算原子参考态替代实验值对结果一致性的影响。
> 4.  **结论**：总结 DMol3 方法的主要进展和优势，并指出当前计算结果精度的主要瓶颈在于密度泛函本身的局限性，而非数值方法的不足。
>

> ## 四、正文核心内容剖析
> 
> ### 4.1 研究背景与问题定义
> 
> 本文的工作始于一个明确的技术挑战：如何将 DMol 这种在分子计算中已被验证的高精度方法，拓展到对无限周期体系（即固体）的模拟。这需要解决两个核心问题：(1) 如何处理固体中由无限个本征态引发的连续能带问题；(2) 如何在保证计算精度的前提下，保持或提升计算效率，以适应更大的系统。
> 
> ### 4.2 方法模型与创新技术
> 
> 论文的核心贡献在于系统地构建并测试了 DMol3 的几大关键技术模块：
> 
> #### A. 局域化基函数 (Localized basis functions)
> 
> -   **概念**：基函数是构建电子波函数的基本“积木”。DMol3 使用在原子周围空间上局域的函数，对于大体系，其数学矩阵（哈密顿矩阵）会自然稀疏，这是实现线性标度算法的基础。
> -   **创新—短尾基函数**：为了在保持精度的同时加速计算，必须严格限制基函数的空间范围（截断半径，Rc）。论文提出了一种结合两种传统方法优点的新方案：
>     1.  **软约束势 (Soft confining potential)**：类似于谐振势，它使基函数在截断半径处平滑衰减，将对动能项计算的影响降至最低。
>     2.  **硬壁边界条件 (Hard wall boundary condition)**：在截断半径处强制函数值为零，这可能导致一阶导数不连续，但影响可控。 这种组合策略的关键在于可以灵活地使用半径的更高次幂作为约束势，使基函数在更宽的范围内精确地遵循原子的指数衰减行为，从而在较小的截断半径下实现高精度。
> 
> #### B. 半局域赝势 (Semilocal pseudopotential)
> 
> -   **动机**：处理重原子时，内部紧束缚的电子（芯电子）在化学上不活跃，但却极大地增加了计算量。**赝势 (Pseudopotential)** 近似将这些芯电子连同原子核一起，视为一个有效的“离子势”，从而只显式处理参与成键的价电子。这能有效引入**标量相对论效应 (scalar relativistic effects)**，对于重元素至关重要。
> -   **处理方法**：DMol3 采用了 Stuttgart-Dresden 和 AREP 等赝势，并针对 DFT 计算进行了适配。作者发现，对赝势的非局域部分进行完全可分离化处理，在计算效率上并无显著优势，其矩阵元搭建的时间主要由球面上的数值投影这一步骤主导。
> 
> #### C. k 点矩阵元与布里渊区积分
> 
> -   **问题**：在周期性固体中，电子波函数被一个周期性函数和一个平面波因子的乘积所描述，该平面波的波矢 k 是一个连续变量。因此，对固体总能量的计算，需要将孤立分子中占据态的求和，推广为对倒空间第一布里渊区内所有 k 点的积分。
> -   **方法**：
>     1.  **矩阵元计算**：论文比较了两种计算哈密顿矩阵元的方法。一种是在计算时直接乘上 k 相关的相位因子；另一种是先计算实空间中的原子轨道相互作用矩阵并存储，再对 k 空间进行快速傅里叶变换求和。对于需要多个 k 点的小晶胞，后一种方法效率更高。
>     2.  **绝缘体**：使用 Monkhorst-Pack 网格进行积分，收敛性呈指数级，且网格间距是控制精度的核心变量。
>     3.  **金属**：由于费米面的存在，积分变得复杂。论文采用了 **Bloechl 四面体方法 (tetrahedron method with Bloechl corrections)**，将布里渊区划分为四面体，并在其中线性化能带能量和矩阵元，同时对费米面曲率进行二阶修正，大幅提升了金属体系计算的精度和收敛速度。这是处理金属体系的关键所在。
> 
> #### D. 梯度泛函 (Gradient functionals)
> 
> -   **概念**：早期的 **局域密度近似 (Local Density Approximation, LDA)** 仅考虑局域点的电子密度。而 **广义梯度近似 (Generalized Gradient Approximation, GGA)** 泛函则进一步引入了电子密度的梯度信息，以更准确地描述非均匀电子气。
> -   **实现**：DMol3 完全基于三维数值积分来计算梯度修正的交换关联能及其矩阵元，避免了繁琐的解析变分导数推导。但在生成原子基组时，为了获得平滑的基函数，文章提到了一种实用技巧：即使所用泛函（如 B88PW91）的径向势函数在某些原子中会产生“扭结”状特征，导致三维积分收敛变慢，也选择使用能产生平滑势的泛函来生成基组。
> 
> ### 4.3 实验结果与讨论
> 
> -   **收敛性测试**：通过对 Cu 和 Si 的测试，确立了基组截断半径 (Rc)，k 点网格密度等关键参数对总能量、晶格常数、体弹性模量等性质的影响，并给出了合理的默认值（如 Rc>9 a.u., Si 使用 6 阶网格）。
> -   **与基准和实验的对比**：
>     -   **Si**：DMol3 与全电子线性缀加平面波（FLAPW）方法这种高精度计算标准取得了高度一致，其能量、晶格常数和体弹性模量的差异分别仅在 0.1 mHa, 0.1% 和 3% 以内，这强有力地证明了 DMol3 方法本身的准确性。
>     -   **石墨**：计算揭示了泛函选择对弱相互作用体系（层间由范德华力主导）的巨大影响。PWC 泛函预测的层间距 (c/a) 偏小，而 BP 等 GGA 泛函则显著高估。这种高度敏感性表明，当时的 GGA 泛函在描述弱键方面存在固有缺陷。
>     -   **生成焓测试 (G2 集)**：这是论文最精彩的应用部分。BP 泛函表现出色，对 148 个分子的平均绝对偏差约为 6 kcal/mol。当使用 DMol3 自身计算的原子生成焓（而非实验值）作为参考时，BP 的性能几乎不变，而 PBE 泛函的性能则大幅提升，接近 BP 的水平。这表明，使用一套自洽的理论参考态（molecular reference states）可以减少由不同实验数据来源带来的不一致性，这对于评估泛函内在性能是更科学的做法。
> 
> ## 五、结论与贡献
> 
> ### 核心发现
> 
> 1.  DMol3 方法通过结合短尾局域化基组、半局域赝势和先进的布里渊区积分算法，成功地将高精度数值轨道 DFT 方法从分子体系扩展到绝缘体、半导体和金属固体。
> 2.  基准测试结果表明，DMol3 方法的数值精度可与全电子 FLAPW 等方法媲美，计算精度瓶颈主要在于所选密度泛函的固有局限，而非数值方法本身。
> 3.  在分子生成焓的预测中，B88PW91 (BP) 泛函表现最优，而使用自洽计算的理论原子参考态，是提升 PBE 等泛函表现一致性的关键。
> 
> ### 理论与应用贡献
> 
> 1.  **方法论贡献**：首次详细公开了 DMol3 中多项关键技术的实现细节，包括软约束势与硬壁边界条件结合的基组设计、对金属的四面体积分方法等，为其他开发者提供了宝贵参考。
> 2.  **实践贡献**：通过系统性的参数标定（如截断半径、k 点网格），为后续用户使用该软件提供了明确指导。同时，论文对 G2 测试集的全面评估，为计算化学界提供了关于泛函性能和原子参考态一致性的重要基准数据。
> 3.  **工具性贡献**：DMol3 的诞生，为材料科学和化学领域的研究者提供了一个兼具高精度和对大体系（如表面、分子筛）高效处理能力的强大工具。
> 
> ## 六、未来发展方向展望

> 3.  **算法的并行化**：论文提到了线性标度部分和 k 点对角化部分在并行计算机上的实现，预示了未来向更大体系、更高效率扩展的趋势。

> 1.  **纳入更高阶相互作用**：开发能够精确描述范德华相互作用（van der Waals interactions）的非局域泛函，或直接与多体色散方法耦合，以解决 GGA 在石墨等体系中的失败。
> 2.  **激发态与动力学**：将方法从基态计算扩展到激发态性质（如光学吸收谱）和动力学模拟（如声子谱、分子动力学）的精确计算，这文中已涉及零点振动计算，但可以更深入。

> ## 七、学术思考与批判性分析
> 
> 1.  **问题 1：泛函的本质局限**：文中反复强调“误差主要来自泛函”。这是否意味着，尽管 DMol3 已实现了极高的数值精度，但在预测反应能垒、强关联体系（如过渡金属氧化物）的电子结构等 DFT 框架性难题上，其精度天花板完全由所选泛函(如 LDA/GGA)决定？DMol3 的这种高精度数值实现，是否反而更好地暴露了 DFT 近似本身的缺陷，从而为发展超越 DFT 的方法提供了更清晰的指引？
> 2.  **问题 2：基组与赝势的平衡**：论文在石墨的例子中提及，c 轴晶格常数对基组质量和赝势选择极为敏感。这引发了一个深层问题：DMol3 方法的“全电子”版本和“赝势”版本在精度和可移植性上是否存在系统性的权衡？对于涉及芯电子参与成键或需要极高精度电子结构计算的体系（如核磁共振屏蔽常数），二者之间的差异是否足以影响物理结论？
> 3.  **问题 3：方法的普适性验证**：论文的基准测试主要集中于单质和少数简单化合物。对于一个新方法的宣称，其验证集是否足够广泛？例如，离子晶体、含有 d 或 f 轨道的金属氧化物、表面吸附体系等，DMol3 在处理这些更具挑战性的化学键和电子结构时，其优越性是否能得到保持？
> 4.  **问题 4：赝势可移植性问题的深层思考**：论文指出，为 Hartree-Fock 开发的 ECP 赝势在 DFT 中使用时会导致额外的晶格收缩。这不仅是简单的“不匹配”问题，它触及了理论计算中的一个根本矛盾：我们是否在用一个表示薛定谔方程解的赝势，去近似另一个不同的方程（DFT 的 Kohn-Sham 方程）的势？这种“范式移植”在什么条件下是安全的，在什么条件下会引入定性的错误？
> 5.  **问题 5：自洽参考态的价值与挑战**：论文提出用理论计算的原子参考态代替实验值，这提高了理论评估的一致性，但这是否会掩盖泛函的系统性误差？例如，一个泛函可能同时高估了固体结合能和原子化能，导致误差相消，使得生成焓看似准确，但预测其它物理性质（如弹性常数）时却可能失败。这种“自洽性”是否是一把双刃剑？

> ## 八、用户问题预测与解答
> 
> 1.  **问题：什么是“局域数值原子轨道”？它和常见的“高斯轨道”或“平面波”有什么区别？**
>     
>     -   **解答**：想象一下，建造一个分子的电子波函数需要积木块（基函数）。
>         -   **高斯轨道 (Gaussian-Type Orbitals, GTOs)**：就像一个形状固定但可缩放的山峰，数学处理快，但物理上不完全是原子轨道的真实形状，需要多个组合才能准确描述。
>         -   **平面波 (Plane Waves)**：像一张无限延伸的起伏的“地毯”，非常适合描述周期性固体，但描述原子核附近剧烈震荡的电子时，需要大量的“地毯”来叠加，计算很昂贵。
>         -   **局域数值原子轨道 (Numerical Atomic Orbitals, NAOs)**：DMol3 使用的方法。它首先在原子周围精确求解得到一个接近真实原子轨道的形状，然后将其截断在空间的一个小区域内（局域）。这就是一个兼顾了物理精确性和计算效率的“定制积木块”。它的优势在于，用很少的积木块就能获得很高精度，并且积木块在空间中是局域的，计算大体系时矩阵会很稀疏，便于快速计算。
> 2.  **问题：论文中反复提到的“k 点”和“布里渊区积分”是什么意思？为什么它对固体计算如此重要？**
>     
>     -   **解答**：在分子中，电子轨道是分立的（比如 HOMO, LUMO）。但在固体中，由于无数原子周期性排列，电子轨道形成了连续的“能带”。为了计算整个固体的总能量，我们无法对能带上的每一个点都进行计算，而是需要一种聪明的采样方法。
>         -   **k 点**：就像在能带这个函数曲线上取的采样点。“k”就是这个采样点的坐标，它在“倒空间”中。
>         -   **布里渊区 (Brillouin Zone)**：这是倒空间中的一个基本单元，所有能带信息都包含在其中。我们只需要在这个区域内进行采样。
>         -   **布里渊区积分**：就像用一组离散的采样点（k 点）的加权平均，来近似计算对整条能带曲线的积分。积分越精确，计算出的总能量、力等性质就越准确。对于绝缘体，能带变化平缓，用少量 k 点即可；对于金属，由于存在费米面（电子占据与否的分界线），能带在此处发生突变，就需要更密集的 k 点采样，这也是论文中图 1 和图 2 收敛行为差异巨大的原因。
> 3.  **问题：论文中比较了 PWC、BP 和 PBE 等多种泛函，它们到底有什么区别？为什么 BP 在这个工作中表现最好？**
>     
>     -   **解答**：这些名字代表了密度泛函理论中处理交换关联能的“配方”。它们的复杂程度和物理考量不同：
>         -   **PWC (Perdew-Wang C)**：这是一个**局域密度近似 (LDA)** 泛函。它只考虑空间中某一点的电子密度，就像只根据当地的“人口密度”来制定政策，没有考虑“人口流动”（密度梯度）。通常计算键能偏强，晶格常数偏小。
>         -   **BP (B88PW91)**：这是一个**广义梯度近似 (GGA)** 泛函。它由 Becke 1988 交换 (B88) 和 Perdew-Wang 1991 关联 (PW91) 组合而成。它不仅看“人口密度”，还看“人口密度如何变化”（梯度）。这通常能更准确地描述分子中的化学键，尤其是键能。
>         -   **PBE (Perdew-Burke-Ernzerhof)**：这是另一个非常流行的 GGA 泛函，设计上比 BP 更遵循第一性原理的约束，没有拟合参数，普适性更强。
>     -   **为什么 BP 表现最好？** 论文的 G2 测试集主要是分子的生成焓。BP 泛函在拟合原子化能方面表现尤为出色，因此在 2000 年那个时代，对于这类测试集，BP 恰好是“配方”最准的那一个。但作者也指出，当使用一套自洽的理论原子参考态时，PBE 的性能也迅速赶上，说明泛函的排名和表现高度依赖于测试体系和评估标准，没有绝对的“最好”。
> 4.  **问题：什么是“赝势”？为什么研究重元素时要用它？**
>     
>     -   **解答**：原子由原子核和核外电子构成。在化学成键过程中，真正起作用的是最外层的价电子，而内层电子（芯电子）紧紧地束缚在原子核周围，它们的波函数在空间上剧烈震荡，很难用基函数展开，而且它们在化学反应中基本不发生变化。
>     -   **赝势 (Pseudopotential)** 的核心思想，就是“冻结”这些慵懒的芯电子，将它们和原子核一起，用一个较平滑的、仅作用于价电子的有效势场来代替。这样做有两个巨大好处：(1) 极大地减少了需要显式处理的电子数，从而大幅加速计算；(2) 对于重原子，由于芯电子运动速度极快，接近光速，必须考虑相对论效应。在赝势的生成过程中，可以方便地将这些**标量相对论效应**包含进去，从而在一个非相对论的理论框架下，间接地捕捉到重元素的相对论性影响，例如金的颜色、汞的低熔点等。
> 5.  **问题：这篇 2000 年的论文对今天的我们研究还有什么意义？我们还需要读它吗？**
>     
>     -   **解答**：非常有意义。虽然计算机性能和 DFT 泛函已经取得了巨大进步，但这篇论文的价值在于：
>         -   **方法论经典**：它清晰地展示了如何将一个基于局域轨道的方法“工业化”，使其能够处理固体和表面。文中关于基组设计、k 点采样、赝势适配的思考，至今仍是计算材料科学方法开发的核心议题。理解了这些基础，才能更好地理解现代软件（如 VASP, CP2K, 以及 DMol3 本身）的工作原理。
>         -   **理解精度来源**：它通过详尽的数值实验，教会我们如何去“诊断”一个计算，结果中的误差是来自数值近似（如基组不完备、k 点不够密）还是物理模型（泛函本身）的缺陷。这种批判性思维是进行任何高水准计算研究的必备素质。
>         -   **历史视角**：它记录了 21 世纪初 DFT 方法发展的一个关键节点，有助于我们理解当前泛函和软件的发展脉络。例如，文中对 BP 和 PBE 的讨论，直接影响了后续 meta-GGA 和杂化泛函（如 B3LYP, HSE06）的开发。本文将 DMol3 方法平台化，为后续与溶剂化模型（COSMO）、动力学等方法的结合奠定了基础，是计算化学软件史上的一个里程碑。

> ## 九、专区：便于 Obsidian Dataview 插件调用
> 
> 领域基础知识:: 密度泛函理论 (DFT) 是计算化学和材料科学中求解多电子体系电子结构的主流方法。它通过将多体问题简化为单电子在有效势场中运动的问题，极大地降低了计算复杂度。其数值实现通常基于不同的基组展开，如平面波、高斯轨道和数值原子轨道。计算固体时，需处理周期性边界条件，并通过布里渊区积分对电子态求和。
> 
> 研究背景:: 早期 DMol 方法在分子计算中取得成功，但将其扩展到固体时面临挑战，主要是如何处理无限周期体系导致的连续能带，以及在保证精度的同时维持计算效率。这需要发展新的基组策略、赝势处理和 k 点积分技术。
> 
> 作者的问题意识:: 如何将基于局域化数值原子轨道的 DMol 方法进行全面扩展，使其能够以高精度和合理的效率处理包含绝缘体、半导体和金属在内的周期性固体体系，并系统性地验证该方法在不同密度泛函下的性能表现。
>

> ### 1. 研究背景：为什么需要新方法？

> 在DMol3之前，许多DFT方法在处理固体时存在局限。例如，基于平面波的方法在处理分子或低维体系时效率不高，而传统的高斯轨道方法在描述固体的周期性边界条件时不够自然。DMol方法的独特优势在于使用**数值原子轨道**作为基组，这种基组在分离原子极限下是精确的，因而能以较少的基函数获得高精度。核心挑战在于，如何将这种为分子设计的“局域轨道”思想，优雅地扩展到无限延伸的固体体系中。
> 
> ### 2. 方法模型：四大支柱的深度解析
> 
> #### A. 局域化基函数：让原子轨道“有界”
> 
> -   **核心思想**：一个原子周围的电子云主要分布在有限空间内，因此描述它的轨道函数也应在一定距离外衰减为零。这就是“局域化”的来源。
> -   **技术实现**：传统方法可能通过设定一个硬性的截断半径（相当于“硬墙”），或依赖高斯函数的自然衰减。DMol3的创新之处在于，它结合了两种方法的优点：使用一个**软约束势**（类似于谐振子势）来迫使轨道在截断半径处平滑地衰减为零。这好比在原子周围设置了一圈越来越粘稠的“胶水”，而非一堵墙，从而避免了波函数导数不连续带来的数值误差，同时保证了计算矩阵的稀疏性，为处理大体系铺平了道路。
> 
> #### B. 半局域赝势：抓住“价电子”的核心
> 
> -   **核心思想**：在化学成键中，真正起作用的是原子的外层价电子，而内层核心电子紧紧束缚在原子核周围，几乎不参与反应。赝势（ECP）的思想就是用一种有效的“假”势能，来替代原子核和内层电子对价电子的复杂作用。
> -   **技术实现**：DMol3采用了**半局域赝势**和**有效核势（ECP）**。它将对价电子的作用势V(r)分解为一个只与距离相关的局域部分，和一个与角动量l相关的非局域部分。精髓在于计算投影矩阵元（公式2），这是最耗时的步骤，但论文指出，采用更复杂的可分离赝势并不能加速这个过程。更重要的是，通过赝势可以自然地引入**标量相对论效应**，这对于重元素（如Cu, Au）的计算至关重要，因为它们的内部电子速度极快，相对论效应不可忽略。
> 
> #### C. k空间矩阵元：处理无限体系的魔法

> ## 八、用户问题预测与解答

> **Q1：DMol3和Materials Studio中的CASTEP模块有什么区别？我该用哪个？** **A1**：简单来说，DMol3是“局域轨道派”，而CASTEP是“平面波派”。DMol3使用数值原子轨道，基函数数量少，物理图像更接近化学家的“原子轨道”直觉，计算效率高，特别适合处理大分子、团簇和某些表面反应。CASTEP使用平面波基组，基函数数量多，但数学上简单且完备，其精度可以通过提高截断能系统性地收敛，因此在处理晶体、能带结构、光学性质等周期性性质时非常强大。选择哪个，取决于你的体系是更偏向“分子”还是“晶体”，以及你更看重“化学直觉”还是“系统性收敛”。
> 
> **Q2：什么是“k点”，为什么计算固体时需要它？** **A2**：你可以把k点理解为电子波的“晶格动量”或“量子波长”。在晶体中，电子不仅在一个原胞内运动，还在整个晶体中传播。不同的k点代表了电子波不同的传播方向和波长。为了计算整个晶体的平均性质（如总能量、电子密度），我们需要对电子所有可能的传播状态求和，这在倒空间就表现为对第一布里渊区内所有k点的积分。所以，k点越多，采样越精细，计算结果就越接近真实值，直到收敛。
> 
> **Q3：论文中多次提到“自洽场（SCF）”，什么是自洽场？** **A3**：想象一下，一个班级的学生要排座位，每个人的位置取决于其他人坐在哪里，同时每个人的位置又决定了周围的环境。排座位的过程就是自洽场(SCF)的迭代过程。在DFT中，每个电子的运动状态依赖于一个由所有电子密度共同决定的有效势场，而这个有效势场又反过来由所有电子的状态决定。因此，我们只能先猜一个电子密度，构建势场，求解方程得到新的电子密度，再用这个新密度去修正势场，如此循环往复，直到输入的密度和输出的密度完全一致，即达到“自洽”。这个过程就是自洽场(SCF)迭代。
> 
> **Q4：为什么计算结果中，LDA和GGA给出的晶格常数和体模量总是一个偏小/偏硬，一个偏大/偏软？** **A4**：这源于两种泛函对电子交换相关作用的近似方式不同。**LDA**（局域密度近似）假设空间中每一点的电子环境是均匀的电子气，这在真实分子或固体中高估了电子间的交换作用，导致化学键被过度增强（过度键合）。因此，原子被拉得更近，导致晶格常数偏小，而要把它们压得更近需要更大的力，所以体模量偏大、偏硬。**GGA**（广义梯度近似）引入了密度梯度的修正，通常会在一定程度上修正这种过度键合效应，使化学键变弱。因此，原子之间距离增大，导致晶格常数膨胀，材料也更容易被压缩，体模量随之减小、变软。
> 
> **Q5：这篇论文是2000年的，现在来看，其中哪些技术已经过时，哪些仍然是核心基础？** **A5**：这是一个很好的历史视角问题。**核心基础**：使用数值原子轨道作为基组来高效处理大体系，并融合赝势、k点积分等技术，这一整套方法论框架至今仍是DMol3和许多类似程序（如FHI-aims, OpenMX）的基石。**已过时/发展的部分**：1. **泛函**：文中测试的B88PW91和PBE等泛函，如今已远非顶尖。现代的meta-GGA（如TPSS, SCAN）和杂化泛函（如HSE06）已成为主流，能提供更高的精度。2. **范德华作用**：作者对石墨c/a的挣扎，在今天已通过引入显式的色散修正项（如DFT-D3）或非局域范德华泛函（如vdW-DF）得到了很好的解决。3. **赝势**：文中使用的ECP/AREP赝势已逐渐被更现代、更精确的赝势库（如DMol3内置的DNP+基组配合的赝势）或PAW方法所取代。4. **计算能力**：文中担心的并行扩展性问题，在今天高度并行的计算架构下，已经不是瓶颈，使得更大、更复杂的体系计算成为可能。

> # INTRODUCTION
> 
> # 引言
> 
> This article describes the local orbital density functional (DF) method DMol3 which evolved from a gas phase molecular method to a method, which with its recent advances, encompasses possibilities for state of the art calculations on insulating and metallic solids. In this article, solid state calculations are applied to the calculation of enthalpies of atom formation for the elements C, Si, and S from their thermodynamic reference states. On the methodological side, the step from finite molecules and clusters to solids involves the necessity of dealing appropriately with the infinite number of eigenstates of the solid. This is done as usual, by idealizing to solid for perfect translation symmetry with respect to a unit cell. The symmetry representations for the lattice translations require a continuum of labels, the vectors in reciprocal space inside the first Brillouin zone. The molecular sum over orbitals generalizes into an integration over the first Brillouin zone (BZ). The basic description of the DMol method for molecules can be found in Ref. 1. The localized numerical orbitals used as basis sets are designed to give a maximum of accuracy for a given basis set size.1 This is done by treating the separated atom limit exactly with the numerical atomic basis functions. The atomic response to the molecular or solid environment can be handled robustly to an excellent approximation by a relatively small number of additional numerical functions.1 With the increase of compute power available, a regime has been reached where the localized basis sets can be implemented with linear scaling algorithms. This perspective has been addressed by introducing basis functions with a strictly finite range. The pertinent details are given in the subsection on short tail localized basis functions below. > ## Localized basis functions
> 
> ## 局域化基函数
> 
> Localized basis functions almost automatically lead to sparse one-electron Hamiltonian matrices for large systems. It is useful to review briefly some basic alternatives on how the finite range can be exploited in computations. With the commonly used Gaussian basis sets, usually a truncation threshold is set and matrix elements below that threshold are approximated as exactly zero. Outside some radius this basis is dominated by a single Gaussian. The actual truncation radius at a given threshold can be reduced by raising the exponent of the Gaussian and thus reducing its range. If one desires to push the cutoff radius down as much as possible, at a given radius one has to optimize accuracy by balancing the adverse effects of reducing the range of the outermost Gaussian against the error from raising the truncation threshold. Raising the truncation threshold implies a discontinuity of the matrix element as a function of distance. Another possibility is to use numerical atomic functions and enforce a zero boundary condition at some radius. This procedure implies a discontinuity of the derivative of the radial function at the cutoff. The treatment of the second derivative needed for the kinetic energy requires special care. 局域化基函数几乎自动地导致大型系统的单电子哈密顿矩阵稀疏化。简要回顾一些基本的替代方法，以了解如何利用有限范围进行计算，这很有用。使用常见的高斯基组时，通常会设置一个截断阈值，并将低于该阈值的矩阵元近似为精确零。在某个半径之外，该基组主要由一个高斯函数主导。在给定阈值下，实际的截断半径可以通过增加高斯函数的指数从而减小其范围来降低。如果希望尽可能降低截断半径，在给定半径下必须通过权衡减少最外层高斯函数范围的不利影响与提高截断阈值所产生的误差来优化精度。提高截断阈值意味着矩阵元随距离变化时出现不连续性。另一种可能性是使用数值原子函数并在某个半径处强制实施零边界条件。此过程意味着在截断处径向函数的导数出现不连续性。处理动能所需的二阶导数需要特别注意。 **Hamiltonian matrices**: 哈密顿矩阵，在量子力学中，表示系统总能量的算符在基函数下的矩阵表示。 **Gaussian basis sets**: 高斯基组，使用高斯型函数作为基函数的量子化学计算方法。

> Sankey and Niklevski8 chose to treat the kinetic energy term in reciprocal space. Comparing the two methods it appears that the tails of a Gaussian basis are similar to ones obtained from an atomic confinement by a harmonic potential, while the other method uses a hard wall confinement. In the present method, advantages of the two other methods are combined. A soft confining potential similar to the harmonic potential minimizes the effect of any discontinuity at the cutoff, and allows calculation of the kinetic energy part in real space. A hard wall boundary condition at the cutoff radius puts the lowest discontinuity to the first derivative as in the second method. Since the present method uses basis functions from numerical atoms or more general central field systems, there is freedom to replace the harmonic potential by a higher power of the radius, and thus to extend the radial range where the basis functions closely follow the atomic exponential behavior. As the method moves toward smaller cutoff radii, this soft confining potential will need to be optimized. If the usual translation into the first Brillouin zone is disregarded for the moment, the k-vectors of the shifted mesh are k = Σ_{i=1}^{3} G_i (m_i − 1/2) / n_i, where each m_i are numbers running from 0 to n_i−1. The Γ-point approximation remains the fastest method for diagonalization with larger cells, in particular when real diagonalizations are used. The Γ-point method is obtained by the default if all lattice parameters exceed 1.76 nm. Symmetry can be used to avoid calculations at redundant k points. This results then in a special k-points method.14 Automatic space group symmetry recognition is done based on the atomic positions in the cell and the corresponding lattice translations. The current method assumes standard orientation of the lattice. Furthermore, symmetry breaking, which may result from the start spin densities used to start up calculations of magnetic structures, are also taken into account. Automatic selection of a symmetry unique k-point set is part of the present approach. In passing, it should be noted that using symmetry unique k-points requires symmetrization of the density, etc. This is done by symmetrizing the population matrices mentioned before. Figure 1 shows convergence with mesh size in the case of the Si primitive cell. Such exponential convergence is characteristic for insulators. The same levels of convergence map onto lower-order meshes, but with the same mesh spacing, for supercells. The default mesh is the order 6-mesh in the case of the Si primitive cell. The superior efficiency of the higher-order shifted meshes is put in a different and slightly less favorable perspective by considering the associated special (or symmetry unique) k-points: the unshifted mesh of order 14 has 104 special points and the shifted mesh of order 10 has 110 points. Brillouin-zone integrations for metals are complicated by the presence of the Fermi surface, which divides occupied and unoccupied orbitals in k-space for the partially filled bands. Such integrations can be done by dividing up space into tetrahedra, whithin which band energies and matrix elements are linearized. Following Bloechl,15 the tetrahedra are spanned on the full equidistant mesh. Symmetry is used again to identify the symmetry unique tetrahedra. As default, the second-order (Fermi surface curvature) corrections15 are applied. A consequence of the second-order correction is the appearance of negative integration weights at some k-points for a fractionally occupied band. Writing the integration weight at a k-point as a product of a Fourier-mesh weight and a band occupation leads to the appearance of negative band occupation numbers at some k-points. The integral band occupation remains positive definite. Applied to an insulating crystal this tetrahedron method gives identical special points, weights, and fast convergence as the Fourier mesh method when applied to insulators. Figure 2 shows convergence with mesh size for copper metal. A power law is characteristic for integrations involving a Fermi surface. The improvement of accuracy obtained with the Bloechl curvature corrections is very significant. The default mesh for the Cu primitive cell is the mesh of order 8. It may happen that an occupied or fractionally occupied band becomes degenerate with an unoccupied band at some k-point. As a consequence the density associated with either of the orbitals becomes ill-defined. Since the total density of both orbitals is well-defined, we choose to populate both orbitals equally at that k-point to avoid generating spurious oscillations in the SCF process. In metals it is found sometimes that a finer k-mesh will not produce spurious oscillations while a more coarse k-mesh does. Spurious SCF oscillations may alternatively be damped by finite temperature occupation or by Gaussian level broadening. This is also true with a small caveat for the electrostatics.2 The atom cutoff radius r_c impacts with a power of 2d asymptotically, where d is the dimensionality of the lattice. > # RESULTS AND DISCUSSION
> 
> # 结果与讨论
> 
> The convergence of the Cu crystal total energy with respect to the local basis cutoff R_c can be seen from Table I. Already for a cutoff radius of 10 a.u. (≈5.3 Å) the total energy appears to be converged to about 10 mHa. The bonding energy with respect to the atom is affected more by small cutoff radii, because the same cutoff radius was used confining the "free" atom. The calculated equilibrium lattice constant is fixed within 0.1% for R_c > 9 a.u. The bulk modulus calculated by the three point difference scheme from total energy has a numerical uncertainty of order 1–2 GPa. It is interesting to put these results in perspective with other calculations. A calculation using defaults gives essentially an identical result as the R_c > 9 a.u. results. The somewhat too short lattice constant is primarily a property of the PWC density functional. It is consistent with the too short lattice constant that the calculated bulk modulus is higher than the experimental value 137 GPa. The ECP, which was designed from first principles for Hartree–Fock (HF)-based calculations, gives results in fair agreement. However, the 2% smaller lattice constant as compared to the all electron calculation should be noted. This change of the energy surface is due to the modification of the atomic scattering properties by the specific pseudopotential. It was found that the AREP,20 which also was developed for HF-based calculations leads to a very similar extra lattice contraction for Cu. Since the ECP incorporates scalar relativistic effects on bonding, one may ask if this contraction is a genuine relativistic effect or should be attributed to shortcomings of the pseudopotential. To sort this out, further calculations were done with R_c = 10 a.u. cutoff. With the all-electron scalar relativistic corrections a 2.30% shortened lattice constant was found, indicating a relativistic contraction for bulk Cu of 0.76%. The Stuttgart group also has a nonrelativistic version of their ECP; this yields −2.73%, or a relativistic contraction of 0.94%. Such a relativistic contraction is indeed particularly pronounced for group IB elements,21 e.g., for Cu2 contractions from 2 to 6 pm are reported in Ref. 21. A contraction by 2 pm for the longer Cu–Cu bond in the bulk corresponds to 0.8% contraction of the lattice constant. While now 0.76%–0.94% contractions are attributed to a relativistic effect, the remainder about 1.2% has to be attributed to lack of portability and other deficiencies of the ECP and AREP when used with DFT. Use of a gradient functional (BP) leads to a reduction of binding energy, a lattice expansion of order 2%, and a decrease of the bulk modulus below the observed value. This effect is consistent with the trend generally observed on going from local density approximation (LDA) to gradient dependent functionals. It has been noted that the default DND basis set, which includes a d-polarization function for Si, misses up to about 1 kcal/mol per bond for tetrahedrally bonded silicon in compounds like in SiF4 and the Si crystal. This is also the major reason for the difference with respect to the default calculation. With the extended basis set, electrostatics was calculated using up to hexadecapole moments, and the "xfine" mesh was used. Higher l polarization functions and higher multipole moments reduce the calculated value of the Kohn–Sham energy gap. Using g-polarization functions in addition further lowers the gap to near its converged value. This converged value is also obtained from high quality FLAPW calculations.22 (For precise comparison with FLAPW results the Hedin Lundqvist–Janak Moruzzi Williams functional is used, difference with PWC is minor for bulk Si.) It is well-known that the Kohn–Sham (KS) energy gap for most semiconductors is significantly smaller than the optical gap. It is gratifying to see that two different all electron implementations of DFT like DMol3 and FLAPW come to agree to within a fraction of a mHartree for energies, 0.1% for lattice constants, and 3% for the bulk modulus, when using the same density functional. The agreement with experiment is by no means as good as the different density functional results among themselves. The results are put in perspective by using a very well tested functional involving density gradients explicitly. It is significant and typical for the present state of the art with "gradient" functionals, that the lattice constant is larger than measured experimentaly and that the bulk modulus is softened. The gradient functional does not lead to much a larger KS bandgap. The KS eigenvalue gap remains very significantly below the optical gap. Harmonic vibration frequencies in molecules and at the Γ-point in reciprocal space for solids are calculated by two-point numerical differentiation of forces. Symmetry is used to diminish the number of calculations. The zero-point vibrational energy and the thermal corrections in solids involve a Brillouin zone integration for the phonons. This integration can be approximated with increasing accuracy by Γ-point sums for increasingly large supercells. Table III shows the convergence properties in the case of Si. Particularly by sampling the acoustic phonons at Γ in a small cell leads to an underestimation of the zero-point vibrational energy. Already at modest cell size a sufficient level of precision is reached. In the case of thermal corrections, the Γ-point sum approximates the acoustic phonon density of states by a set of "Einstein model" discrete frequencies. Again it turns out that for the purpose of calculating enthalpies of formation accuracy on the order of 0.1 kcal/mol atom is obtained with cell size of 16 atoms. The band structure and density of states of graphite are shown in Fig. 3. The bands become degenerate along the line K–H in the BZ, making graphite an example of a semimetal. The density of states has been calculated with the tetrahedron method using the unshifted 12×12×4 mesh, the BP functional, and the experimental geometry (a = 246 pm, c = 680 pm) from the Cerius2 database. For the k-mesh a multiple of 3 is required in the AB plane to actually have k-mesh points along the K–H line (Fig. 4). Since the degenerate bands at E_F can lead to the spurious occupation problem mentioned above, an 8×8×4 k-mesh, which avoids such problems, was chosen for most of the calculations. The effect on calculated properties is minor except for optical properties. Using the PWC local functional, the lattice constant a = 244.7 pm and c/a = 2.694 is found, while experiment is at a = 246.4 or 247 pm and c/a = 2.724 or 2.806 according to Refs. 23, 24. The best available experimental results involve thus an uncertainty of about 3% for the c-lattice parameter. The calculated bulk modulus is 297 GPa. Gradient-dependent functionals remove some or all of the overbinding found with local functionals. With the GGA functional25,26,19 a = 246.5 pm and a 13.8% enlarged c/a ratio is found. The PBE functional27 yields a = 247.0 and 14.5% larger c/a as compared to experiment. The B88PW91 functional yields a = 246.7 pm and an about 25% enlarged c/a ratio. The striking enhancement of the calculated c-lattice parameter involves only a small energy lowering of the total energy by about 1 mHa with respect to the experimental geometry. The weak graphite interlayer interaction makes the calculated c-parameter very sensitive to the choice of basis set. Better basis sets tend to make calculated c larger. The present result is in contradiction with the GGA result from Ref. 28. However, the author is confident that qualitative confirmations of the present result could be found in a number of waste-paper baskets already. While the present result is not documented in the literature yet, it is not a complete surprise. It is known that gradient dependent functionals lead to dramatic weakening of weak bonds. In particular, the B88 exchange part of the functional, which is known to greatly improve calculated dissociation energies of molecules, has been known for making certain weak bonds turn out unbound.29,30 Despite that weakly bonded systems have been considered by design for the very recent HCTH-147 functional,31 it nevertheless overestimates graphite c/a by ≈20%. Vibrational calculations were done at the experimental lattice parameters using the PWC functional. > The atom calculation for the Hund's rule ground state frequently involves nonspherical densities. This is handled as a single atom calculation in the molecular framework. The Hund's rule level occupations are chosen to maximize S_z, which formally implies a single determinant wave function. The nonspherical spin densities of the Hund's rule atom lead to an atomic ground state energy lower by several kcal/mol than the one from our spin-unrestricted spherical atom. It is apparent that the enthalpy of formation for Si and S is underestimated. This is true for the BP functional. For the PWC local functional the underestimate turns again into an overestimate. Intermediate results in appealing agreement with experiment for the solids are obtained from functionals like GGA or PBE27 which have a smaller enhancement factor f(s) for exchange in the limit of large scaled gradient s. Unfortunately GGA or PBE have much inferior results compared to BP when tested with the G2 neutral molecule enthalpies of formation. On the other hand the enthalpies of formation for the atoms from the solid reference state remain in the range of expectations from G2 with the BP functional, i.e., on the order of 6 kcal/mol average deviation. One may use the calculated enthalpies of formation for the elements H, N, O, F, and Cl from the diatomic reference states and for C, Si, and S from the solid calculations instead of potentially less consistent, experimentally deduced values. C, Si, and S are the most frequently used solid state reference states for the G2 test set. Next in importance is white phosporus. Unfortunately this solid is crystallographically not well defined. Therefore calculation of solid reference states for P and the less important Li, Be, B, Na, Al is disregarded for this work. As it turns out, using calculated reference states deteriorates the BP-G2 performance Table V (C). Especially the spread for the G2-2 subset increases unfavorably. This is mainly due to using the atom formation enthalpy from the F2 molecule. This reference state happens to be maximum deviation for the G2-1 subset Table V (A) and (B). Without fluorine, indeed, there is an improvement seen in the rms deviations, (D). Whether or not calculated reference states are used, the BP functional yields an average deviation of 6 kcal/mol, and maximum deviations exceeding 20 kcal/mol for the G2 test set. This appears to be the present state of the art obtainable with functionals that have only explicit dependence on density and density gradients. The performance of the PBE functional27 using experimental atomic enthalpies of formation is impaired by overestimation of the atomic dissociation energy, Table V (E). When calculated atomic enthalpies of formation are used, Table V (F) results. This is very close in performance to the one by BP. > # CONCLUSIONS
> 
> # 结论
> 
> Recent extensions of the DMol3 density functional method are described. This method is designed to do electronic structure calculations for local and gradient-dependent functionals. It uses numerical solutions of DFT-free atoms as part of its basis set, and thus gives highly accurate DFT solutions for the separated atoms limit. Typically a doubled numerical basis set with d-polarization functions (DND) is used for molecular and solids calculations. Basis sets with shorter tails than previously useable significantly speed up calculations for large molecules and solids. Semilocal pseudopotentials help both to introduce scalar relativistic effects and to speed up calculations. The extension with Brillouin zone integrations is specific for calculations with periodic boundary conditions. The tetrahedron method for Brillouin zone integration deals with the complication arising from the Fermi surface in metals; it is designed to become a spectral method with exponential convergence in the case of semiconductors and insulating compounds. The method is used here for solid state calculations of semiconductor Si and insulator α-Sulfur, the semimetal graphite, and metallic Cu. An upshot of these benchmark calculations is that the larger part of uncertainty in the results is due to limitations in the presently available functionals, and not with limitations of the present method of calculation. Si, S, and graphite are also standard thermodynamic reference states. These were used together with the molecular reference states for H, N, O, F, Cl to reassess the performance of two gradient dependent density functionals for the G2 neutral test set. If experimental atom formation energies are used together with calculated molecular dissociation energies to define the calculated molecular enthalpies of formation, B88P91 turns out as the best performing functional for DMol. While B88P91 performance remains virtually unchanged when using the calculated atom formation energies, it is found that the performance of the PBE functional is much improved, coming close to the B88P91 performance. It is expected that the present method will prove useful and efficient in finding density functional results for molecules, solids, and in particular, surfaces with molecules. 最近对DMol3密度泛函方法的扩展进行了描述。该方法旨在对局部和梯度依赖泛函进行电子结构计算。它使用无密度泛函理论原子的数值解作为其基组的一部分，因此能够为分离原子极限提供高度精确的DFT解。通常，用于分子和固体计算的双数值基组包含d极化函数 (DND)。与之前可用的基组相比，具有更短尾部的基组显著加快了对大分子和固体的计算。半局域赝势既有助 于引入标量相对论效应，又能加快计算速度。布里渊区积分的扩展专门用于具有周期性边界条件的计算。布里渊区积分的四面体方法处理了金属中费米面带来的复杂性；它被设计为在半导体和绝缘化合物的情况下成为具有指数收敛性的谱方法。该方法在此用于半导体硅和绝缘体 α硫的固体状态计算，以及半金属石墨和金属铜。这些基准计算的结果表明，结果中的大部分不确定性源于当前可用泛函的局限性，而非当前计算方法本身的限制。Si、S和石墨也是标准的热力学参考态。这些与H、N、O、F、Cl的分子参考态一起被用于重新评估两种梯度依赖的密度泛函在G2中性测试集中的表现。如果使用实验原子形成能和计算的分子解离能来定义计算的分子生成焓，B88P91在DMol中表现出最佳性能。尽管在使用计算的原子形成能时，B88P91的性能几乎不变，但发现PBE泛函的性能有了显著改善，接近B88P91的性能。预计本方法在寻找分子、固体以及特别是有分子的表面的密度泛函结果时将证明其有用且高效。
