> ## 一、引言
> 
> 您是否曾想过，能否用**电场**而非磁场来控制计算机硬盘的磁性比特，从而实现更快、更节能的数据存储？这正是“**多铁性材料**（Multiferroics）”研究的核心目标。这篇由Manfred Fiebig等人于2016年发表在《自然-综述：材料》（_Nature Reviews Materials_）上的综述文章，回顾了该领域从萌芽到当代的曲折发展历程，并系统梳理了其核心物理机制、前沿应用和未来挑战。
> 
> **关于多铁性材料** 简单来说，多铁性材料是一种同时具有**铁磁性**（如磁铁）和**铁电性**（如压电陶瓷）的“跨界”材料。想象一下，一个材料既是磁铁，又是电容器，并且这两种性质还能相互影响。
> 
> **研究背景** 早在2500多年前，人类就发现了铁磁性。直到一个世纪前，铁电性才被认知。将这两种性质结合在一个材料中，创造“多铁性材料”的尝试始于20世纪50年代的苏联。然而，该领域真正的爆发点是在2000年之后。当时，理论学家Nicola Spaldin发现，在万能且广泛应用的**钙钛矿**（Perovskite）晶体结构中，铁电性和磁性是相互排斥的——一个需要空的电子轨道，另一个则需要部分填充的轨道。
> 
> **本文定位与核心价值** 这一“禁忌”的发现，反而激励了科学家们去寻找绕过这一限制的新机制和新材料。本篇综述正是对这一探索历程的全景式扫描。它并非针对某一特定材料的深入报告，而是作为一份“高级地图”，勾勒出多铁性领域的全貌，清晰地区分了不同类型的多铁性机制，并指出了从基础对称性难题到实用器件架构的未来发展方向。其核心价值在于为读者提供了一个系统性的知识框架，帮助理解这个复杂但充满潜力的领域。
>

> ## 四、正文核心内容剖析
> 
> ### 1. 研究背景：一场“不可能”的挑战
> 
> 文章首先明确了多铁性研究的“圣杯”：**用电场控制磁矩**。这能克服传统电流驱动磁场转换磁矩时的废热和速度瓶颈。然而，尼古拉·希尔（Nicola Spaldin）在2000年的理论工作指出，在典型的钙钛矿氧化物中，形成铁电性所需的空d轨道与形成磁性所需的未满d轨道互相排斥，这解释了为何多铁性材料如此罕见，成为该领域现代研究的逻辑起点。
> 
> ### 2. 核心机制：实现“鱼与熊掌兼得”的四条路径
> 
> 为了绕过“d轨道填充”的矛盾，文章系统梳理了四种主要的铁电性机制，它们构成了多铁性材料的分类基础。
> 
> #### **I类多铁性材料：独立的起源**
> 
> 这类材料中，铁电性和磁性来源不同，独立出现，因此耦合往往较弱，但铁电性更强。
> 
> -   **孤对电子机制（Lone-Pair Mechanism）**
>     
>     -   _核心思想_：某些离子（如Bi³⁺, Pb²⁺）的惰性电子对不参与成键，形成空间不对称的“孤对电子”，产生局部电偶极矩。
>     -   _典型材料_：**BiFeO₃**（铁酸铋）。Bi³⁺的6s²孤对电子提供了巨大的自发极化（~100 μC/cm²），使其成为目前唯一已知的室温单相多铁性材料，兼具高居里温度（TC=1103K）和高奈尔温度（TN=643K），是应用研究的明星。
> -   **几何铁电性（Geometric Ferroelectricity）**
>     
>     -   _核心思想_：由于离子半径不匹配等空间效应，导致晶格结构发生畸变，从而产生极性态，而非源于化学键合。
>     -   _典型材料_：六角稀土锰酸盐（h-RMnO₃）。例如h-YMnO₃，其MnO₅多面体的倾斜和畸变，像“挤牙膏”一样迫使稀土离子偏离中心，产生极化（~5.6 μC/cm²）。其铁电居里温度极高（>1200K），但磁有序温度低于120K。
> -   **电荷有序化（Charge-Ordering）**
>     
>     -   _核心思想_：不同价态的离子在空间上不均匀排列，形成电荷密度波，从而产生宏观极化。
>     -   _典型材料_：LuFe₂O₄。其核心设想是Fe²⁺和Fe³⁺离子形成层状交替排列，产生极化。但文章明确指出，该材料的铁电性至今仍备受争议，尚未成定论。
> 
> #### **II类多铁性材料：耦合的起源**
> 
> 这类材料中，铁电性完全由特定的磁性序“诱导”产生，因此磁电耦合极强，但极化值通常较小。
> 
> -   **自旋驱动机制（Spin-Driven Mechanisms）**
>     -   _核心思想_：打破空间反演对称性的不再是传统离子位移，而是复杂的自旋结构。电子的自旋和电荷通过量子力学效应相互作用，将自旋的非对称性“传递”给电荷，从而产生极化。这是实现“强耦合”的关键。
>     -   _三种主要路径_：
>         1.  **逆DM相互作用**：由非共线的螺旋自旋序（Si × Sj）驱动，极化方向由自旋的叉乘决定。极化P ∝ eᵢⱼ × (Sᵢ × Sⱼ)。实例：o-TbMnO₃。
>         2.  **交换收缩**：由共线且自旋不等长的自旋序（Si · Sj）驱动，极化源于磁致伸缩效应。P ∝ Rᵢⱼ(Sᵢ · Sⱼ)。实例：Ca₃CoMnO₆。其极化值通常大于逆DM机制。
>         3.  **自旋依赖的p-d杂化**：由螺旋自旋序调控金属离子和配体离子的电子云杂化强度，产生极化。P ∝ (Sᵢ · eᵢⱼ)² eᵢⱼ。实例：CuFeO₂。
> 
> ### 3. 实验体系与前沿进展
> 
> #### **薄膜与异质结构（Thin Films & Heterostructures）**
> 
> 这代表了将多铁性材料推向应用的关键一步。文章重点讨论了三种策略：
> 
> -   **交换耦合**：将多铁性材料的反铁磁序，与常规铁磁层的磁矩“钉”在一起，从而通过电场控制多铁性反铁磁畴，实现对铁磁层磁性的间接调控。最终目标是实现室温下可重复的**电场驱动磁化反转**，如BiFeO₃-CoFe异质结中的工作。
> -   **应变工程**：通过选择不同的衬底，对薄膜施加拉伸或压缩应力，从而改变晶格常数，稳定块材中不存在的亚稳相，甚至诱导出本不具备的铁电或铁磁序（如在SrTiO₃中诱导出铁电性，在EuTiO₃中同时诱导出铁电和磁性）。
> -   **界面效应**：多铁性本身可以只存在于两种材料的界面处，这种“二维多铁性”为设计全新功能提供了可能。
> 
> #### **畴与畴壁（Domains & Domain Walls）**
> 
> -   **核心概念**：任何铁性材料都由多个均匀取向的“畴”组成，畴与畴之间的边界就是“畴壁”。
> -   **关键发现**：长期以来，畴壁被视为完美晶体中的缺陷。但本文指出，**畴壁可以展现出块材所不具备的全新功能**。例如，研究发现BiFeO₃中的畴壁具有比块材高得多的**电导率**。在II类多铁性材料中，由于铁电性源自磁性，每个铁电畴壁都必然是磁畴壁，成为一个天然的“多铁性界面”。
> -   **意义**：畴壁工程学（domain-wall engineering）成为一个新兴方向，因为这些纳米尺度的“天然界面”可以在材料生长后，通过电场等方式被创建、移动和擦除，具有极高的灵活性。
> 
> #### **非平衡动力学（Non-equilibrium Dynamics）**
> 
> -   **核心问题**：用电场翻转磁矩到底有多快？其过程是怎样的？这是决定多铁性存储器速度的关键。
> -   **现状**：文章指出，这是一个“被严重低估”的领域。目前的研究仍然零散，甚至存在争议。例如，在BiFeO₃中的超快电场翻转是否存在尚未达成共识，而一些磁致铁电体的翻转速度可能固有其慢。这既是挑战，也是未来研究的关键突破口。
> 
> #### **对称性破缺的深层意义**
> 
> 文章从更根本的对称性角度审视了多铁性。多铁性材料同时打破了**空间反演对称性**（由铁电序导致）和**时间反演对称性**（由磁序导致）。这种双重对称性破缺是许多奇特物理现象的根源，并不仅限于多铁性材料。
> 
> -   **铁涡旋性（Ferrotoroidicity）**：一种同时打破空间和时间反演对称性的基本铁性序，其序参量是由自旋涡旋构成的**磁环矩**（T = Σᵢ rᵢ × Sᵢ）。这完善了初级铁性序的对称性分类。
> -   **磁单极子（Magnetic Monopoles）**：在固体中，可以存在作为准粒子的磁单极子。其与环矩、四极矩共同构成了多极展开中与磁电效应相关的项。
> -   **电磁振子（Electromagnons）**：一种磁自旋波激发，但同时伴随着电偶极矩的振荡，是磁性和铁电性动态耦合的典型表现。
> -   **不可逆定向二向色性（NDD）**：光从相反方向穿过材料时，吸收强度不同，这是一种“单向透明”效应。这是时空反演破缺在光学响应上的直接体现。
> 
> ## 五、结论与贡献
> 
> **核心结论**：多铁性材料的研究已从早期的“可能性探索”阶段，发展到对多种共存机制的系统理解和对新型功能的理性设计阶段。尽管尚未实现完全理想的室温、强耦合、电场可控的磁化反转器件，但已经找到了多条可行路径。
> 
> **主要贡献**：
> 
> 1.  **建立了清晰的材料分类体系**：提出并推广了I类（独立序）和II类（共生序）多铁性材料的分类，为领域研究提供了共同语言和框架。
> 2.  **系统化了多铁性的微观机制**：详细阐述了孤对电子、几何效应、电荷有序和自旋驱动这四大机制，为新材料探索提供了理论指导。
> 3.  **将研究焦点从块材拓展到低维体系**：强调了薄膜、异质结构、界面和畴壁在实现和调控多铁性中的核心地位，引领了应用导向的研究方向。
> 4.  **连接了基础物理与器件应用**：将对称性破缺、非平衡动力学等基础物理问题，与实现低功耗存储器、磁电传感器等具体技术目标紧密联系起来，揭示了多铁性研究的深度和广度。
> 
> ## 六、未来发展方向展望
> 
> 文章在结尾部分明确指出了未来的研究趋势和挑战，可归纳为以下几点：
> 
> 1.  **寻找下一代室温多铁性材料**：首要目标是找到一种在室温下，同时具有大且强耦合的自发磁化和自发极化的材料。目前，BiFeO₃仍是唯一的多铁性明星，但其磁性是反铁磁，宏观磁矩小，限制了直接应用。
> 2.  **深入挖掘和优化现有机制**：特别是自旋驱动机制，仍有巨大的优化空间。通过高压、化学掺杂、应变等手段，有望提高其有序温度和极化值，甚至发现新型自旋结构。
> 3.  **从“界面”走向“畴壁”**：与不可更改的生长界面相比，可以动态创建、移动和擦除的**畴壁**将成为下一个研究热点。利用电场控制畴壁，特别是多铁性畴壁，有望实现赛道存储器等新型器件。
> 4.  **探索新材料体系**：超越传统氧化物，探索**非氧化物**（如氟化物）和**有机材料**中的多铁性，这可能会开辟全新的研究空间。
> 5.  **攻克动力学难题**：系统研究磁电耦合的动态过程，理解和实现超快（皮秒级）、可重复的电场控制磁化翻转，这是实现多铁性存储器的必经之路。
> 6.  **探索新型拓扑自旋结构**：研究多铁性绝缘体中的**斯格明子**（Skyrmions），并探索利用电场而非电流来操控它们，这可能会催生全新的、超低功耗的磁信息存储和处理方式。
> 
> ## 七、学术思考与批判性分析
> 
> 1.  **“多铁性”定义的泛化问题**：文章在Box 1中也指出，“多铁性”一词的定义已从最初的“两种及以上初级铁性序共存”，演变为目前实际使用的“铁电性与（反）铁磁性共存”。这种定义的泛化虽然促进了领域的交流，但也可能导致概念的混淆。例如，一个线性磁电材料（如Cr₂O₃）并非多铁性，但常被放在多铁性背景下讨论。这种概念边界的模糊是否在某种程度上阻碍了更精确的物理理解？
> 2.  **I类和II类分法的局限性**：该分类法清晰但可能过于简化。例如，在I类多铁性材料中，磁电耦合的强弱差异巨大。BiFeO₃的耦合就不像几何铁电性材料那么弱。是否存在一种更精细的分类方法，不仅能描述“起源”，还能描述“耦合强度”和“耦合机制”，从而更好地指导材料设计？
> 3.  **静观与动态的失衡**：文章明确指出，对非平衡动力学的研究严重不足。这确实是一个核心问题。目前绝大多数研究聚焦于静态或准静态的磁电耦合，而对一个注定要用于动态器件（如存储器）的物理现象，其动态过程（如开关速度、能量耗散路径、疲劳机制）却知之甚少。这种“静”与“动”的失衡，是当前领域从基础研究走向技术应用的最大鸿沟。
> 4.  **“畴壁”的独立性与可重复性挑战**：尽管畴壁功能化前景诱人，但我们必须批判性地思考其挑战。首先，畴壁的性质高度依赖于其局域环境（如缺陷、应变），其均匀性和可重复性如何保证？其次，单个畴壁的精确电学接触和信号读出，在纳米尺度上是一个巨大的技术难题。最后，在反复操作下，畴壁的移动和结构稳定性问题（疲劳）尚待解决。
> 5.  **竞争性技术的对比**：文章最后提到，多铁性器件需要与**自旋转移矩**（STT）和**自旋轨道矩**（SOT）等纯电学控磁技术竞争。这是一个非常现实的视角。多铁性的优势在于电压驱动，理论上能实现零静态功耗。但STT/SOT技术发展迅速，已接近商用。多铁性若想突出重围，必须在速度、功耗、可靠性或集成密度上，对SOT等技术展现出决定性的优势，而不仅仅是“也实现了电控磁”。
> 
> ## 八、用户问题预测与解答
> 
> 1.  **问题：多铁性材料的“磁电耦合”到底是什么？可以用一个比喻来解释吗？** **解答**：当然。我们可以把“磁电耦合”想象成一个翻译官。磁铁和驻极体说两种不同的“语言”，一种叫磁性，一种叫电性。在普通材料中，它们互不理解。但在多铁性材料中，这个翻译官就存在了。当你用电场（对它说电性语言）时，它不仅能理解，还能将其翻译成磁性语言，从而改变材料的磁性状态。这就是“用电场控制磁性”的本质。翻译官的能力越强，耦合就越强。
>     
> 2.  **问题：I类和II类多铁性材料，哪个更有应用前景？** **解答**：这个问题没有绝对的答案，取决于具体的应用场景。
>     
>     -   **I类（如BiFeO₃）**：强项在于**铁电性大且工作温度高**。BiFeO₃在室温下就拥有巨大的极化值和磁电耦合，这使其成为室温器件原型验证的首选。缺点是其磁结构是反铁磁，净磁矩很小，难以直接“读取”磁状态，需要通过异质结等复杂结构来“转导”信号。
>     -   **II类（如TbMnO₃）**：强项在于**磁电耦合极强，物理极有趣**。磁和电像一个硬币的两面，控制一个必然控制另一个。缺点是目前发现的材料极化值和有序温度都太低（多在低温下工作），离室温应用还很远。 简而言之，**I类更接近当前应用，II类代表着更强大的耦合潜力和未来的可能性**。
> 3.  **问题：如果用电场就能控制磁性，为什么我们现在还不用多铁性材料来做硬盘？** **解答**：这是一个触及核心困境的好问题。主要有三大障碍：
>     
>     -   **材料性能不足**：目前没有一种材料能完美满足所有要求。BiFeO₃虽好，但它是反铁磁体，很难直接读出控制结果。很多磁电耦合强的材料又只在低温下工作。
>     -   **器件架构复杂**：为了克服上述问题，需要构建复杂的薄膜异质结构，将多铁性层与铁磁层、电极层等精确堆叠，这对纳米制造和集成技术提出了极高要求。
>     -   **动态、可靠性和竞争**：我们还不完全清楚电场翻转磁矩的极限速度、功耗和寿命（疲劳）。同时，它必须与已经非常成熟且快速发展的自旋转移矩等纯电学技术竞争，后者不需要复杂的多铁性材料。
> 4.  **问题：文章谈到的“畴壁”是什么？为什么它比整个材料本身更有趣？** **解答**：想象一块由许多不同颜色独立小方块组成的马赛克画，每个小方块就是一个“畴”，它的颜色代表其电或磁的取向。而方块之间的边界，就是你看到的“畴壁”。 畴壁之所以比材料本身更有趣，是因为：
>     
>     -   **新性质涌现**：在畴壁处，原子的排列、化学键、电子结构被迫发生剧烈变化，导致了块材中不存在的新性质，比如从绝缘体变成导体。
>     -   **天然的多铁性界面**：在II类多铁性材料中，铁电畴壁自动就是磁畴壁，它是一个完美的、天然的磁电耦合场所。
>     -   **可动态操控**：与生长出来就固定不变的异质结界面不同，畴壁可以通过施加电场或磁场来移动、创建和擦除，就像在白板上画出可更改的电路，灵活性极高。
>     -   **尺寸极小**：畴壁本身的宽度只有纳米级别，适合构建超高密度的器件。
> 5.  **问题：我是一个刚进入该领域的学生，这篇综述给我指出的最重要的未来研究方向是什么？** **解答**：这篇综述为你指明了几个层次的研究方向，你可以根据自己的兴趣选择：
>     
>     -   **最应用的方向**：**异质结和器件物理**。重点是设计并制备室温下能稳定、快速、可重复实现电场驱动磁化反转的薄膜异质结，并研究其动态可靠性和集成方法。明星材料是BiFeO₃及其衍生物。
>     -   **最基础和前沿的方向**：**畴壁和斯格明子**。探索这些纳米级拓扑结构的物理性质，比如在畴壁处发现全新的磁电效应，或研究如何用电场而非电流来操控作为未来信息载体的斯格明子。
>     -   **最根本的物理方向**：**非平衡动力学**。利用超快激光和太赫兹光谱等技术，揭示磁电耦合在飞秒到纳秒时间尺度上的动态过程，探索光控磁的全新可能。
>     -   **最需要耐心的方向**：**新材料探索**。在非氧化物、有机材料等更广阔的化学空间中，寻找室温下兼具强磁性和强铁电性的“圣杯”材料。
> 
> ## 九、专区：便于 Obsidian Dataview 插件调用
> 
> 领域基础知识:: 多铁性材料（Multiferroics）是同时存在铁电序（Ferroelectric Order）和磁序（Magnetic Order）的化合物。核心研究目标是利用电场控制磁性，以实现低功耗、高速的信息存储与处理。该领域涉及凝聚态物理、材料科学和器件物理的交叉，其核心挑战在于传统材料中铁电性（需空d轨道）与磁性（需未满d轨道）的相互排斥性。
> 
> 研究背景:: 多铁性研究始于20世纪50年代，但直到2000年Spaldin指出钙钛矿结构中铁电性与磁性的“排斥”禁忌后，才进入现代研究阶段。随后，新型机制（如自旋驱动）和材料（如BiFeO₃, TbMnO₃）的发现引发了研究热潮，目标转向寻找能绕过禁戒规则、实现强磁电耦合的新体系，并从基础物理探索逐步走向面向应用的薄膜和异质结构研究。
>

>     -   **b) 应变耦合**：铁电/压电材料与铁磁/磁致伸缩材料结合。电场产生压电应变_**σ**_，该应变传递到磁致伸缩层，通过逆磁致伸缩效应改变其磁化_**M**_。这种耦合在微波频率下效应很强，但工作条件有限。
> -   **c, d) 2D受限型多铁性 (2D confined multiferroics)**
>     
>     -   **c) 多铁性界面**：两种不同材料（分别具有电容率ε和磁导率μ）的界面处，由于对称性破缺、应变梯度等，可以产生块体材料中不存在的多铁态。这种多铁性仅存在于界面附近的几个原子层。
>     -   **d) 多铁性畴壁**：在一个晶体内部，不同畴（A+和A-）之间的边界（畴壁）是一种天然的界面。如果该晶体本身不是多铁性的，但其畴壁处可能因为对称性不同于畴内而成为多铁性的。这是一个非常前沿的概念。
>

> ## 四、正文核心内容剖析
> 
> ### 4.1 研究背景与新切入点
> 
> 文章开篇追溯到2500多年前人类对磁铁的认识，但直到一个世纪前，铁电性才被发现。人类试图将这两种性质结合的愿望催生了多铁性领域。早期的尝试，如Smolenskii和Ioffe在1958年将磁性离子掺入铁电钙钛矿，但收效甚微。2000年，Nicola Spaldin（当时为Hill）的一篇关键论文解释了原因：在关键的钙钛矿结构中，驱动铁电性的离子位移（**位移型铁电性**）需要空的d轨道，而磁性则需要部分填充的d轨道，两者在化学上“天生不合”。这一认识是里程碑，它迫使研究者们将目光从“如何让它们兼容”转向“寻找全新的、不依赖d轨道排空的铁电机制”。
> 
> ### 4.2 方法/模型/体系核心
> 
> 文章的核心是构建了四种多铁性机制的分类体系，并以此为基础展开讨论。
> 
> 1.  **四种机制与两类材料**：
>     
>     -   **I型多铁性材料**：铁电序和磁序的起源不同，独立产生。包括孤对电子、几何和电荷有序机制。其优点是铁电居里温度高，极化大，但磁电耦合通常较弱。
>     -   **II型多铁性材料**：铁电序由特定的磁序（如螺旋磁序）诱导产生，即**磁致铁电性（Improper ferroelectricity）**。其优点是磁电耦合天然非常强，但极化值通常很小，且磁有序温度低。
> 2.  **超越块体——薄膜与异质结**： 研究重心从三维块体材料转向二维薄膜和异质结，因为：
>     
>     -   **精准控制**：薄膜技术可以原子级精确地生长材料，产生块体中无法实现的新相和应变状态。
>     -   **应变工程**：通过选择不同晶格常数的衬底，可以对薄膜施加拉伸或压缩应变，从而诱导、增强多铁性，甚至创造出块体中没有的多铁性材料（如SrTiO₃, EuTiO₃）。
>     -   **界面效应**：两种材料的界面是全新的“物质态”，可以产生多铁性。图3c的概念——即多铁性仅存在于界面——是革命性的。
>     -   **器件化桥梁**：BiFeO₃–CoFe异质结中，用电场驱动铁磁层磁矩翻转，在室温下可重复进行，这是该领域梦寐以求的“圣杯”，其微观机制被归结为磁畴间的耦合。
> 3.  **核心操控单元——畴与畴壁**：
>     
>     -   **畴**：是序参量均匀的区域。多铁性材料的宏观性能由畴的分布和翻转决定。
>     -   **畴壁**：是畴与畴之间的边界。文章强调，许多新奇效应不在畴内，而在畴壁。例如，BiFeO₃的畴壁被发现具有比畴内更高的导电性。这开启了“畴壁电子学”的可能性，即在一个材料内部，通过创建、移动和擦除畴壁来构建电路。图4清晰地展示了I型和II型多铁材料中畴壁的根本区别，II型材料的畴壁必然是磁电耦合的。
> 4.  **根本物理——对称性破缺**： 文章从现象深入本质，指出多铁性的许多奇特性质源于同时打破**空间反演对称性**（由铁电序导致）和**时间反演对称性**（由磁序导致）。这一双重破缺允许了线性磁电效应的存在。更重要的是，它引申出一些超越传统多铁性的概念：
>     
>     -   **铁涡态**：提出了一种新的基本铁性序，其序参量是**环向矩**，同时打破空间和时间反演对称性，被视为铁磁、铁电和铁弹之后的第四种基本铁性序。
>     -   **磁单极子**：在凝聚态物质中，特定自旋排列可以表现为磁单极子，这是磁电效应的深层物理根源之一。
>     -   **非互易定向二向色性**：光沿着相反方向穿过双重对称性破缺的材料时，吸收率不同，甚至可能实现单向透明。这为新型光学器件提供了可能。
> 
> ### 4.3 实验结果与讨论
> 
> 作为一篇综述，本文汇集了多个里程碑式的实验发现：
> 
> -   **BiFeO₃薄膜的巨大成功**：室温下大极化（~100 μC/cm²）和强磁电耦合，使其成为器件研究的核心。
> -   **BiFeO₃–CoFe异质结中电场翻转磁矩**：这是从基础研究到应用原理验证的里程碑，证明了用电场控制磁存储的可行性，可大幅降低功耗。
> -   **h-YMnO₃中畴壁的磁电耦合**：首次在I型多铁中直接观察到磁畴和电畴在畴壁处的耦合，开启了畴壁研究的新纪元。
> -   **o-TbMnO₃中磁控电极化**：首次在II型多铁中观察到巨大的磁电耦合，即磁场可以翻转电极化，完美验证了自旋驱动机制。
> -   **动态过程的初步探索**：对非平衡态动力学的研究表明，多铁序参量的翻转速度可能从皮秒到宏观慢速不等，这直接关系到未来器件的写入速度，是目前被严重低估的研究方向。

> ## 五、结论与贡献
> 
> 本篇综述的核心结论与贡献可以归纳为以下几点：
> 
> 1.  **建立了清晰的分类框架**：将多铁性材料统一在“I型（独立序）”和“II型（磁致铁电）”的分类下，并以四种核心机制（孤对电子、几何、电荷有序、自旋驱动）进行解释，为全领域提供了共同的“语言”和理解基础。
> 2.  **指明了研究范式的转变**：明确指出该领域已从“寻找块体多铁材料”的早期阶段，转向“设计和调控薄膜异质结、界面和畴壁”的新阶段。多铁性不再仅是材料的本征属性，更是一种可以被“设计”出来的功能。
> 3.  **揭示了深刻的物理根源**：将多铁性现象与时间/空间反演对称性的双重破缺这一根本性物理原理紧密相连，并引出了铁涡态、磁单极子等超越传统多铁性的新概念，极大地拓展了该领域的物理深度和广度。
> 4.  **提出了面向未来的挑战**：系统性地指出了该领域面临的瓶颈和挑战，特别是对非平衡态动力学研究的欠缺，以及对室温、强耦合、可实用化器件的持续追求。

> ## 六、未来发展方向展望
> 
> 文章在最后部分明确指出了未来趋势和挑战，可归纳为以下几点：
> 
> 1.  **新材料探索**：寻找室温下同时具有大极化、强磁化和强耦合的多铁材料仍是首要目标。探索方向包括非氧化物（如氟化物）和有机材料。
> 2.  **器件化推进**：在BiFeO₃异质结电场控磁翻转的原理验证基础上，需要解决器件的**可靠性、疲劳、读写速度**等关键问题，并与**自旋转移矩**、**自旋轨道矩**等竞争技术比较。
> 3.  **畴壁工程**：利用畴壁作为可移动、可擦除的天然界面，探索其在**赛道内存**中的应用。核心是精准控制畴壁的生成、运动和湮灭，并利用其磁电耦合特性。
> 4.  **斯格明子**：关注多铁性绝缘体中的斯格明子——一种拓扑保护的磁涡旋。与金属中由电流驱动的斯格明子不同，多铁性绝缘体中的斯格明子可能通过电场实现超低功耗的操控。
> 5.  **动力学研究**：非平衡态动力学是明显短板。未来的理论与实验需重点关注序参量翻转的**时间尺度、路径和可重复性**，特别是探索**全光控**（All-optical control）实现皮秒级超快翻转的可能性。
> 6.  **可控对称性**：设计多层异质结，通过外场选择性翻转其中某一层的序参量，从而“开关”整个结构的整体对称性，实现一种全新的、可重构的多铁功能。

> ## 七、学术思考与批判性分析
> 
> 基于对本文的深度解读，以下提出3-5个批判性问题，旨在激发更深层次的思考：
> 
> 1.  **“I型”与“II型”分类的边界是否绝对清晰？** 文章将磁电耦合的强弱作为区分I型和II型材料的一个特征。然而，随着研究的深入，在部分I型多铁性材料（如BiFeO₃）的畴壁处也发现了显著的磁电耦合。这是否意味着“I型”和“II型”的分类在描述“畴壁”这种受限空间时变得模糊？是否存在一种统一的、基于对称性破缺的框架，可以同时描述畴内和畴壁的物理，从而超越这种分类？
>     
> 2.  **多铁性界面态的普适性与稳定性问题：** 文章强调界面能产生新的多铁态（图3c），这无疑极具吸引力。但界面态的存在依赖于原子级平整的界面，其稳定性（受温度、缺陷、扩散影响）和均一性（在大面积上保持一致）是巨大的工程挑战。我们是否过度乐观地高估了这种“界面多铁性”的实用化前景？它与传统复合材料中通过应变耦合实现的“3D传递型多铁性”相比，其真正的、可量化的优势在哪里？
>     
> 3.  **电场控磁翻转速度的物理极限与能耗悖论：** 文章指出，电场控磁可能会比电流产生的磁场翻转更快、更节能，但也提到某些多铁性材料的翻转“天生缓慢”。这引出了一个根本性问题：克服反铁磁序的宏观惰性，所需的能量和时间是否有下限？如果为了实现超快翻转（如皮秒级）需要施加极高的电场脉冲，那么脉冲的焦耳热和所需的能量源是否会削弱甚至抵消多铁性器件在“静态”能耗上的优势？我们需要一个全面的能量/时间效率分析模型。
>     
> 4.  **“铁涡态”作为第四种基本铁性序的独立性与实用价值：** 文章将铁涡态（Ferrotoroidicity）作为独立的初级铁性序提出，但其到目前为止的观测依赖于电场和磁场的组合。它的共轭场——“环向场”——在自然界中并不独立存在，人工产生也极为困难。一个无法被其共轭场直接切换的“铁性序”，在多大程度上能被视作一个与铁磁、铁电并肩的、独立的“初级”铁性序？它的应用价值是否从根本上就受限于其相关物理量的非独立性？
>     
> 5.  **多铁性研究对传统铁电学的“反哺”作用是否被充分挖掘？** 文章提到多铁性研究恢复了人们对“非本征铁电性”的兴趣。那么，这种影响是否仅限于此？多铁性研究中发展出的高灵敏度探测技术（如飞米级X射线衍射、非线性光学成像）和对畴壁物理的深刻理解，能不能反过来颠覆我们对传统铁电材料（如Pb(Zr,Ti)O₃，即PZT）中疲劳、印记等老化机制的理解，并启发新的解决方案？这是一个可能被低估的巨大潜在贡献。
>

> ## 八、用户问题预测与解答
> 
> 作为非专业读者或初学者，您可能会有以下疑问，这里为您一一解答：
> 
> **问题1：什么是多铁性材料？它和“多铁”这个词中的“铁”有什么关系？**
> 
> **解答**：**多铁性材料（Multiferroics）** 是指同时具备两种或两种以上基本“铁性”序的材料。这里的“铁”并非指这种材料一定含有铁元素，而是指其物理性质具有类似铁磁性的特征：
> 
> 1.  **铁磁性（Ferromagnetism）**：原子磁矩一致排列，产生宏观磁化，如磁铁。这是“铁性”的原型。
> 2.  **铁电性（Ferroelectricity）**：正负电荷中心分离形成的电偶极子一致排列，产生宏观电极化。这种极化可以在外电场下翻转。
> 3.  **铁弹性（Ferroelasticity）**：晶格自发形变，且形变可在外部应力下翻转。 所以，**多铁性材料**通常指同时具有（反）铁磁性和铁电性的材料，它就像一个同时拥有磁铁和电容双重身份的神奇材料。
> 
> **问题2：为什么说BiFeO₃是“明星材料”，而TbMnO₃是“物理学家眼中的宝贝”？**
> 
> **解答**：这个比喻很贴切。
> 
> -   **BiFeO₃（铁酸铋）是“明星”**：因为它**实用性强**。它在室温以上就能同时表现出大极化和强磁性，可以通过电场影响其磁性，且能用薄膜技术大规模制备，因此最接近实际应用，是研究器件的首选。
> -   **TbMnO₃（锰酸铽）是“物理学家眼中的宝贝”**：因为它**物理机制清晰**。它的铁电性完全由特殊的螺旋磁序诱导产生（II型多铁），是验证“磁致铁电”理论的完美模型。虽然它的多铁性只出现在极低温度（~28 K）且极化很小，毫无应用价值，但它清晰地展示了磁与电如何强耦合，是研究基础物理的理想平台。
> 
> **问题3：用电场控制磁性，为什么能比传统方法更节能？**
> 
> **解答**：想象一下，传统的电磁铁通过电流产生磁场，尤其是在纳米尺度，电流会产生大量焦耳热，能量浪费严重，且磁场难以局域化。而用电场控制磁性，就像用一个**电压信号**而不是一个**电流信号**去开关一个晶体管。
> 
> -   **原理上**：电场直接作用于电荷，可以通过改变电荷密度、离子位移或在多铁材料中直接耦合磁序，这是一个不涉及、或很少涉及宏观电流的过程。
> -   **结果**：理想情况下，维持一个电场或翻转一个极化态，其焦耳热能散可以趋近于零，从而实现了**超低功耗**的信息写入和存储。这对于解决现代数据中心的巨大能耗问题具有革命性意义。
> 
> **问题4：Ferromagnetism和Ferroelectricity中的“Ferro”来源是什么？**
> 
> **解答**：这个前缀“Ferro-”源自拉丁语“ferrum”，意为**铁**。在历史上，铁（Fe）是最早被发现并广泛研究的有宏观磁化的材料，即铁磁性（Ferromagnetism）。后来，当科学家发现某些晶体（如罗谢尔盐）具有类似的自发极化并能被外场翻转的特性时，他们便借用“ferro-”这个前缀，创造了**铁电性（Ferroelectricity）**一词，尽管这些材料本身不含铁。因此，“Ferro-”在材料科学中已经演变为一个通用前缀，用于描述具有自发序参量、并能被外场在至少两个稳定态之间翻转的物理性质。
> 
> **问题5：什么是“畴壁”？它为什么如此重要？**
> 
> **解答**：您可以想象一个拥挤的广场，人群分为两派，一派全部面朝东，另一派全部面朝西。**畴**就是人群中方向一致的那片区域，而**畴壁**就是这两派交界处的那个“过渡带”。 在材料中，畴壁是原子尺度上的过渡区域，其宽度只有几个纳米。它之所以重要，是因为：
> 
> 1.  **新物理的诞生地**：在畴壁处，材料的对称性、晶格常数、化学环境都被打破并重建，因此可以产生块体材料内部完全没有的新性质，如导电性增强、磁性产生等。
> 2.  **器件的功能单元**：磁畴壁的运动和信息存储构成了“赛道内存”等新型存储概念。如果能用电压控制多铁性材料中的畴壁运动，就可能实现比电流驱动更节能的存储器。
> 3.  **多铁性耦合的“放大镜”**：在畴壁这个狭窄区域，磁性和铁电性的耦合效应被放大和集中，更容易被研究和利用。

> # The evolution of multiferroics
> 
> 多铁性材料的发展历程
> 
> The spontaneous, uniform orientation of atomic or molecular magnetic moments to generate what is colloquially called a magnet (more correctly, a ferromagnet) has been explored for more than 2,500 years. Barely a century ago, it was discovered that spontaneous ordering of electric dipole moments can occur as well1. This phenomenon was named ferroelectricity because of the analogies to ferromagnetism, such as the hysteretic switching between two stable states in an external field. Although the technological merits of ferromagnetism and ferroelectricity are quite different, attempts were made to combine them in the same phase of a material to create a socalled multiferroic material (BOX 1). 原子或分子磁矩的自发均匀取向，以产生俗称的磁铁（更准确地说，是铁磁体），这一现象已被探索了2500多年。仅仅在一个世纪前，人们发现电偶极矩的自发有序排列也同样可以发生¹。由于与铁磁性的相似性（例如在外场中两个稳定态之间的滞后切换），这种现象被命名为铁电性。尽管铁磁性和铁电性的技术优势截然不同，人们仍尝试将它们在材料的同一相中结合起来，以创造所谓的多铁性材料（方框1）。 **ferromagnet**: 铁磁体，具有自发磁化强度的材料，其磁矩在无外场时也能保持平行排列。 **ferroelectricity**: 铁电性，材料中电偶极矩自发有序排列并可在电场中切换的现象。 **multiferroic material**: 多铁性材料，在同一相中同时存在两种或两种以上初级铁性序的材料。
> 
> Multiferroic materials are interesting mainly for two reasons. On the one hand, they make it possible to exploit the functionalities of both orders; for example, a magnetic bit may be complemented by an electric bit to establish a fourstate memory element. On the other hand, a coupling between the ferromagnetic and the ferroelectric states might induce novel functionalities not present in either state alone. The control of the magnetic properties by electric fields instead of magnetic fields is an example of the advantages that multiferroic materials can offer. In the reading and writing of a magnetic bit, if a voltage pulse can be used instead of a magneticfield generating electric current, the waste heat and relatively long buildup time associated with electric currents are avoided. Multiferroics may thus lead to faster, smaller, more energyefficient datastorage technologies. 多铁性材料之所以引人关注，主要基于两个原因。一方面，它们使得利用两种序的功能成为可能；例如，一个磁比特可以由一个电比特来补充，以建立一个四态存储元件。另一方面，铁磁态和铁电态之间的耦合可能会诱导出任何一种状态单独存在时所不具备的新功能。通过电场而非磁场来控制磁性能，是多铁性材料所能提供的优势之一。在对磁比特进行读写操作时，如果可以使用电压脉冲来代替产生磁场的电流，就能避免与电流相关的废热和相对较长的建立时间。因此，多铁性材料可能带来更快、更小、更节能的数据存储技术。
> 
> The field of multiferroics covers aspects ranging from technological applications to abstract problems of fundamental research. In addition, the study of multiferroics increasingly influences neighbouring research areas, such as complex magnetism and ferroelectricity, oxide heterostructures and interfaces, and also seemingly remote subjects such as cosmology. In this Review, we give an overview of the twists and turns in the development of the diverse field of multiferroics, and we discuss the trends and challenges that will define its future. Readers looking for a more comprehensive or more technical coverage are referred to more extensive general reviews2,3, or to reviews on particular aspects that are highlighted in further sections. 多铁性领域涵盖的方面从技术应用到基础研究中的抽象问题，范围广泛。此外，多铁性的研究日益影响着邻近的研究领域，例如复杂磁性与铁电性、氧化物异质结构和界面，甚至是看似遥远的宇宙学等领域。在这篇综述中，我们概述了多铁性这个多样化领域发展过程中的曲折历程，并讨论了将决定其未来的趋势和挑战。寻求更全面或更技术性覆盖的读者，可参阅更详尽的综合性综述²'³，或后续章节中重点介绍的特定方面的综述。
> 
> We begin with a brief survey of the early days of multiferroics. The realization that in some important classes of materials, magnetic and electric longrange order compete with each other4 may be regarded as the milestone separating historical from contemporary research in this field. We continue with an overview of mechanisms permitting the coexistence of magnetic and ferroelectric order, and evaluate their potential for inducing pronounced magnetoelectric coupling effects (BOX 1). We then scrutinize heterostructures and, in particular, interfaces that introduce additional functionalities, bringing multiferroics closer to device applications. Domains and domain walls are also discussed: any type of coupling between magnetic and electric longrange order in a multiferroic material has its roots in the coupling between the magnetic and electric domains. We then have a closer look at the nonequilibrium dynamics of multiferroic materials, because, considering that the focus in multiferroics is on the manipulation of the magnetic order by electric fields, it is very important to understand the processes and timescales governing the magnetoelectric coupling. Important progress in the understanding of the coupling of magnetic and electric states has often been introduced by symmetry considerations; for this reason, we pay some attention to fundamental symmetry properties of multiferroic materials. Many of the unusual properties of these materials are linked to the unconventional symmetry resulting from the simultaneous presence of magnetic and electric longrange order. In turn, there are compounds displaying the symmetry of multiferroic materials, and the material properties permitted by this symmetry, but no multiferroic order. These systems are equally worth exploring, which we cover in the section devoted to research areas that were significantly influenced by the existence of multiferroics, even though multiferroicity in itself has a minor role in them. Finally, we identify major questions and challenges that will continue to influence research in multiferroics. > # Abstract | Materials with a coexistence of magnetic and ferroelectric order — multiferroics provide an efficient route for the control of magnetism by electric fields. The study of multiferroics dates back to the 1950s, but in recent years, key discoveries in theory, synthesis and characterization techniques have led to a new surge of interest in these materials. Different mechanisms, such as lone-pair, geometric, charge-ordering and spin-driven effects, can support multiferroicity. The general focus of the field is now shifting into neighbouring research areas, as we discuss in this Review. Multiferroic thin-film heterostructures, device architectures, and domain and interface effects are explored. The violation of spatial and inversion symmetry in multiferroic materials is a key feature because it determines their properties. Other aspects, such as the non-equilibrium dynamics of multiferroics, are underrated and should be included in the topics that will define the future of the field.

> 摘要 | 磁序与铁电序共存的材料——多铁性材料，为通过电场控制磁性提供了一条有效途径。多铁性材料的研究可追溯至20世纪50年代，但近年来，在理论、合成和表征技术方面的关键发现重新引发了人们对这类材料的极大兴趣。不同的机制，如孤对电子效应、几何效应、电荷有序效应和自旋驱动效应，可以支持多铁性。正如我们在本综述中所讨论的，该领域的总体焦点正转向邻近的研究领域。我们探索了多铁性薄膜异质结构、器件架构以及畴和界面效应。多铁性材料中空间对称性和反演对称性的破缺是决定其性质的一个关键特征。其他方面，如多铁性材料的非平衡动力学，则被低估了，应当被纳入将决定该领域未来的主题之中。 **lone-pair**: 孤对电子，指原子中未参与化学键合的价电子对。 **charge-ordering**: 电荷有序，指在混合价态化合物中，不同价态的离子在晶格中规则排列的现象。 **spin-driven effects**: 自旋驱动效应，此处指由特定磁结构（如螺旋磁结构）诱导产生铁电极化的机制。

> # Seeding the field
> 
> # 领域的播种
> 
> Efforts to combine magnetic and ferroelectric order in a single compound were first undertaken in the former Soviet Union. In 1958, Smolenskii and Ioffe5 suggested the introduction of magnetic ions into ferroelectric perovskites to create solid solutions hosting magnetic longrange order without losing the ferroelectric order. However, the most intensely investigated compounds were boracites, such as Ni3B7O13I, in which a pronounced linear magnetoelectric effect with hysteretic switching of multiferroic domains by electric or magnetic fields was observed6. The experimental, theoretical and applied achievements of these early days of the field are summarized in REFS 7,8. Two precursurs of the present boom in multiferroics are noteworthy. First, in 1978, Newnham and coworkers9 reported that a spirallike arrangement of magnetic moments in Cr2BeO4 breaks spatial inversion symmetry, as does the electric polarization that is thereby induced. In their analysis, the authors foreshadowed much of the physics behind a new type of magnetically driven (improper) ferroelectricity that, a generation later, would be exploited to obtain multiferroics with strong magnetoelectric interactions. Second, in 1993, many of the phenomena, systems and concepts at the base of contemporary multiferroics research, including the invention of the term multiferroic itself, were formulated at a conference on magnetolelectric phenomena — even today, its proceedings are a fascinating read10. 将磁序和铁电序结合到单一化合物中的努力最早始于前苏联。1958年，Smolenskii和Ioffe⁵建议将磁性离子引入铁电钙钛矿中，以创建在保持铁电序的同时承载磁长程序的固溶体。然而，研究最深入的化合物是方硼石，如Ni₃B₇O₁₃I，在这种材料中观察到了显著的线性磁电效应，且多铁性畴可通过电场或磁场进行滞后切换⁶。该领域这些早期在实验、理论和应用方面的成就总结于参考文献7和8中。当前多铁性研究热潮的两个先驱工作值得注意。首先，1978年，Newnham及其同事⁹报告了Cr₂BeO₄中磁矩的螺旋状排列会打破空间反演对称性，就如同由此感生的电极化一样。在他们的分析中，作者预示了一种新型磁驱动（非本征）铁电性背后的大部分物理原理，这一原理在一代人之后被用来获取具有强磁电相互作用的多铁性材料。其次，1993年，在磁电现象会议上，当前多铁性研究的许多基础性现象、体系和概念，包括“多铁性”一词本身的发明，都被系统地阐述出来——即使在今天，其会议论文集也依然引人入胜¹⁰。 **perovskites**: 钙钛矿，一类具有特定晶体结构的化合物，通式一般为ABX₃，是功能材料研究中的一大类。 **magnetoelectric effect**: 磁电效应，指磁场诱导电极化或电场诱导磁化的现象。 **improper ferroelectricity**: 非本征铁电性，指铁电序不是由极化本身作为初级序参量驱动，而是由其他序参量（如磁序或晶格畸变）驱动的铁电性。
> 
> In 2000, Spaldin revisited the original idea of Smolenskii and Ioffe and explained the reason why the ferroelectric and magnetic orders obstruct each other in crystals with the versatile perovskite structure that is technologically relevant4. In perovskites, the ferroelectric state emerges because the electron clouds of neighbouring ions hybridize, which supports offcentred ions; this type of ferroelectricity is called displacive ferroelectricity and is particularly energetically favourable when the 3d shell is empty. By contrast, magnetic transitionmetal ordering requires partially filled 3d shells — the contradiction is obvious. This realization triggered an intensive search for materials in which ferroelectricity is driven by other, nondisplacive mechanisms that are compatible with magnetic order or that do not have the perovskite structure. The first breakthrough was the discovery of pronounced magnetoelectric interactions in hexagonal (h) YMnO3 (REF. 11), orthorhombic (o) TbMnO3 (REF. 12) and TbMn2O5 (REF. 13). In these last two materials, the magnetoelectric interaction originates from noncentrosymmetric spin textures that induce a magnetically controllable electric polarization. Contrary to the earlier work on Cr2BeO4 (REF. 9), these discoveries inspired the concerted action of different communities — materials science, condensed matter physics and materials theory — that resulted in an impressive expansion of the field of multiferroics. 2000年，Spaldin重新审视了Smolenskii和Ioffe的原始想法，并解释了为什么在具有技术相关性的多功能钙钛矿结构晶体中，铁电序和磁序会相互阻碍⁴。在钙钛矿中，铁电态的出现是因为相邻离子的电子云杂化，这有利于离子偏离中心位置；这类铁电性被称为位移型铁电性，当3d壳层为空时，这种状态在能量上特别有利。相比之下，磁性过渡金属有序化需要部分填充的3d壳层——矛盾显而易见。这一认识引发了对其他类型材料的深入探索，在这些材料中，铁电性是由与磁序兼容的、非位移型机制驱动的，或者不具有钙钛矿结构。第一个突破是在六方(h) YMnO₃（参考文献11）、正交(o) TbMnO₃（参考文献12）和TbMn₂O₅（参考文献13）中发现了显著的磁电相互作用。在后两种材料中，磁电相互作用源于非中心对称的自旋织构，这些织构诱导了可磁控的电极化。与早期对Cr₂BeO₄的研究不同（参考文献9），这些发现激发了不同研究群体——材料科学、凝聚态物理和材料理论——的协同行动，从而推动了多铁性领域的显著扩展。 **displacive ferroelectricity**: 位移型铁电性，由离子相对于其平衡位置的微小位移导致正负电荷中心不重合而产生的铁电性。 **hexagonal**: 六方的，指晶体结构属于六方晶系。 **orthorhombic**: 正交的，指晶体结构属于正交晶系。

> # Mechanisms supporting multiferroicity
> 
> # 支持多铁性的机制
> 
> Following the aforementioned study on perovskite multiferroic materials, the search for ferroelectric materials of the nondisplacive type that permit the coexistence of ferroelectric and magnetic order became the main focus of the field. We distinguish four main classes of these materials on the basis of the mechanisms inducing the multiferroicity (FIG. 1). Other reviews present detailed surveys of these classes14,15 and their distinction by diffraction techniques16. Ferroelectricity may be driven by electronic lone pairs, geometric effects, charge order or magnetism. In the first three classes, the magnetic and ferroelectric orders occur independently, and the multiferroic material is denoted as type I. In the last class, the ferroelectric and magnetic transitions emerge jointly, in which case the multiferroic material is type II (BOX 1). 继上述关于钙钛矿多铁性材料的研究之后，寻找允许铁电序和磁序共存的非位移型铁电材料成为该领域的主要焦点。我们根据诱导多铁性的机制，将这些材料区分为四大类（图1）。其他综述对这些类别¹⁴'¹⁵及其通过衍射技术的区分¹⁶进行了详细考察。铁电性可以由电子孤对电子、几何效应、电荷有序或磁性驱动。在前三类中，磁序和铁电序独立发生，这类多铁性材料被称为第I类。在最后一类中，铁电相变和磁相变联合出现，在这种情况下，多铁性材料属于第II类（方框1）。 **Type I multiferroic**: 第I类多铁性材料，指磁序和铁电序独立产生、具有不同来源和转变温度的多铁性材料。 **Type II multiferroic**: 第II类多铁性材料，指铁电序由特定磁序（如螺旋磁序）诱导产生、两者具有相同转变温度的多铁性材料。
> 
> ## Lone-pair mechanism.
> 
> ## 孤对电子机制。
> 
> The lone pair mechanism is based on the spatial asymmetry created by the anisotropic distribution of unbonded valence electrons around the host ion (FIG. 1a). This mechanism is responsible for the roomtemperature ferroelectricity observed in BiFeO3. In this material, a pair of Bi3+ valence electrons in the 6s orbital is not involved in sp hybridization and creates a local dipole, yielding a spontaneous polarization17 of ~100 μC cm−2 below the Curie temperature18, TC = 1,103 K. A longrange periodic antiferromagnetic structure arises below the Néel temperature19, TN = 643 K. Among the lonepair systems, BiFeO3 is the only roomtemperature singlephase multiferroic material. It has large and robust electric polarization and pronounced magnetoelectric coupling. 孤对电子机制基于宿主离子周围未键合价电子的各向异性分布所产生的空间不对称性（图1a）。在BiFeO₃中观察到的室温铁电性正是由这种机制引起的。在这种材料中，Bi³⁺的一对6s轨道价电子未参与sp杂化，并产生了一个局部偶极子，在居里温度¹⁸（TC = 1,103 K）以下产生约100 μC cm⁻²的自发极化¹⁷。在奈尔温度¹⁹（TN = 643 K）以下，出现了长程周期性的反铁磁结构。在孤对电子体系中，BiFeO₃是唯一一种室温单相多铁性材料。它具有大而稳定的电极化以及显著的磁电耦合。 **Curie temperature**: 居里温度（或居里点），铁电体或铁磁体发生相变、失去自发极化或磁化强度的临界温度。 **Néel temperature**: 奈尔温度，反铁磁体或亚铁磁体发生相变、失去其特征磁序的临界温度。 **spontaneous polarization**: 自发极化，在没有外电场作用时，铁电体内部存在的单位体积内的电偶极矩。
> 
> ## Geometric ferroelectricity.
> 
> ## 几何铁电性。
> 
> Spacefilling effects and geometric constraints can cause structural instabilities in materials. If such steric effects, rather than bond chemistry, lead to ionic shifts that result in the formation of a polar state, the term geometric ferroelectricity can be used (FIG. 1b). For example, in hRMnO3 (R = Sc, Y, In or Dy–Lu), unitcell tripling drives the emergence of a ferroelectric order20–22 at TC ≥ 1,200 K with a polarization Ps = 5.6 μC cm−2 (REF. 23), followed by magnetic ordering at TN ≤ 120 K (REF. 24). A similar behaviour is observed in hLuFeO3 thin films, which exhibit a larger magnetic moment and roomtemperature magnetic order25, but magnetoelectric coupling remains to be demonstrated in this material. Another example is BaNiF4, in which an asymmetry between Ba2+ and F− sites leads to a spontaneous electric polarization26. Despite its small value (~0.01 μC cm−2), this polarization is of interest because it couples to a weak ferromagnetic moment, which can thus be reversed along with the electric polarization27. Finally, a cooperation between two nonpolar lattice modes drives a ferroelectric polarization in Ca3Mn2O7; this polarization can interact with the canted magnetic moments of the compound28. 空间填充效应和几何约束会导致材料中的结构不稳定性。如果这种空间位阻效应，而非键合化学，导致了离子位移并形成极性态，则可以使用几何铁电性这一术语（图1b）。例如，在h-RMnO₃（R = Sc, Y, In 或 Dy–Lu）中，晶胞的三倍化驱动了铁电序的出现²⁰⁻²²，其TC ≥ 1,200 K，极化强度Ps = 5.6 μC cm⁻²（参考文献23），随后在TN ≤ 120 K时发生磁有序化（参考文献24）。在h-LuFeO₃薄膜中观察到了类似的行为，该薄膜表现出更大的磁矩和室温磁序²⁵，但磁电耦合仍有待在该材料中得到证实。另一个例子是BaNiF₄，其中Ba²⁺和F⁻位点之间的不对称性导致了自发极化²⁶。尽管其值很小（约0.01 μC cm⁻²），但这种极化值得关注，因为它与一个弱铁磁矩耦合，因此该铁磁矩可以随电极化一起反转²⁷。最后，Ca₃Mn₂O₇中两个非极性晶格模式的协同作用驱动了铁电极化；这种极化可以与化合物的倾斜磁矩相互作用²⁸。
> 
> ## Charge ordering.
> 
> ## 电荷有序。
> 
> Valence electrons can be distributed nonuniformly around their host ions in the crystal lattice to form a periodic superstructure. For example, it was suggested that the Fe atoms in LuFe2O4 may form a superlattice with an alternating sequence of Fe2+ and Fe3+ ions29. This kind of charge ordering might be the source of an electric polarization and, hence, ferroelectricity30 (FIG. 1c). LuFe2O4 is a prime candidate for chargeorder driven multiferroicity, but after a decade of research the occurrence of ferroelectricity in this material is still questioned29,31. Mixed manganites, such as Pr1−xCaxMnO3, were also taken into consideration32 but did not attract broader attention. For now, charge orderdriven multiferroicity essentially remains at the stage of an interesting concept. 价电子可以在晶格中的宿主离子周围非均匀分布，形成周期性的超结构。例如，有人认为LuFe₂O₄中的Fe原子可能形成具有Fe²⁺和Fe³⁺离子交替序列的超晶格²⁹。这种电荷有序可能是电极化以及铁电性的来源³⁰（图1c）。LuFe₂O₄是电荷有序驱动多铁性的主要候选者，但经过十年的研究，这种材料中铁电性的存在仍然受到质疑²⁹'³¹。混合锰酸盐，如Pr₁₋ₓCaₓMnO₃，也被考虑过³²，但并未引起广泛关注。目前，电荷有序驱动的多铁性基本上仍停留在一个有趣概念的阶段。
> 
> ## Spin-driven mechanisms.
> 
> ## 自旋驱动机制。
> 
> Magnetic order can break inversion symmetry. The interaction of spins and charges may transfer the noncentrosymmetry from the magnetic to the electric lattice, driving the formation of a polar state. These magnetically induced, socalled improper ferroelectric materials represent the ultimate shift away from displacive ferroelectrics, in which magnetic ordering is inhibited, towards materials in which the electric polarization is induced by the magnetic ordering. So far, three main routes for the creation of this kind of multiferroicity have been established (FIG. 1d), as discussed in specific reviews on the topic33–35. The most intensely discussed mechanism is the socalled inverse Dzyaloshinskii–Moriya (DM) interaction. Whereas in the DM interaction36 a noncentrosymmetric crystallographic environment promotes an antisymmetric magnetic interaction, in the inverse DM interaction an acentric spin structure drives a noncentrosymmetric displacement of charges37,38. Spin–orbit coupling is crucial for both the DM and inverse DM interaction. The polarization resulting from the inverse DM interaction is essentially determined by the optimization of the spin configuration from the point of view of antisymmetric exchange, expressed by the antisymmetric product Si × Sj of neighbouring spins Si,j. It yields a onetoone correlation between (antiferro)magnetic order and electric polarization. Multiferroicity of this type was first found in Cr2BeO4 (REF. 9), oTbMnO3 (REF. 12) and CaMn7O12; this last material arguably39 exhibits the highest polarization achieved so far with this mechanism (0.3 μC cm−2)40. In contrast to the DM interaction, the Heisenberglike exchange striction describes an acentric displacement of charges derived from the optimization of the symmetric spin product Si · Sj (REF. 41). Ferroelectricity generated by this kind of displacement was first observed in TbMn2O5 (REF. 13). The dominance of the nonrelativistic symmetric exchange over the relativistic antisymmetric mechanism (|Si · Sj| > |Si × Sj|) is well represented in oTbMnO3, in which an orderofmagnitude increase in the polarization is obtained when a cycloidal order (parameterized by Si × Sj) transforms into a collinear antiferromagnetic order (parameterized by Si · Sj) under pressure42. In general, inversionsymmetryviolating magnetic order may occur in several ways; thus, the existence of spin distributions that could promote ferroelectricity more effectively than the currently known arrangements is possible. 磁序可以打破反演对称性。自旋和电荷的相互作用可以将这种非中心对称性从磁性格子传递到电性格子，从而驱动极性态的形成。这些磁致、所谓的非本征铁电材料代表了从抑制磁有序化的位移型铁电体，向电极化由磁有序化诱导的材料的最终转变。迄今为止，已经建立了三种创建此类多铁性的主要途径（图1d），相关专题综述对此进行了讨论³³⁻³⁵。讨论最激烈的机制是所谓的逆Dzyaloshinskii–Moriya (DM) 相互作用。在DM相互作用中³⁶，非中心对称的晶体学环境促进了反对称的磁相互作用，而在逆DM相互作用中，无中心的自旋结构驱动了电荷的非中心对称位移³⁷'³⁸。自旋轨道耦合对于DM和逆DM相互作用都至关重要。逆DM相互作用产生的极化，基本上由基于反对称交换的自旋构型优化决定，用相邻自旋Si,j的反对称乘积Si × Sj表示。它在（反铁）磁序和电极化之间产生了直接的关联。这种类型的多铁性首次在Cr₂BeO₄（参考文献9）、o-TbMnO₃（参考文献12）和CaMn₇O₁₂中发现；最后一种材料据称³⁹展现了迄今为止通过这种机制获得的最高极化强度（0.3 μC cm⁻²）⁴⁰。与DM相互作用不同，类海森堡交换伸缩描述了一种源于对称自旋乘积Si · Sj优化的电荷非中心位移（参考文献41）。由这种位移产生的铁电性首次在TbMn₂O₅中观察到（参考文献13）。非相对论对称交换相对于相对论反对称机制的优势（|Si · Sj| > |Si × Sj|）在o-TbMnO₃中得到了很好的体现，在加压下，当回旋序（用Si × Sj参数化）转变为共线反铁磁序（用Si · Sj参数化）时，极化强度获得了数量级的增加⁴²。总的来说，违反反演对称性的磁序可能以多种方式出现；因此，可能存在比目前已知排列更有效地促进铁电性的自旋分布。 **Dzyaloshinskii–Moriya interaction**: Dzyaloshinskii–Moriya相互作用，一种反对称的自旋-自旋相互作用，通常出现在非中心对称的晶体中，倾向于使相邻自旋相互垂直。 **exchange striction**: 交换伸缩，指由对称的自旋-自旋相互作用（如海森堡交换作用）驱动的离子位移，从而导致晶格畸变和可能的电极化。
> 
> Finally, in delafossite systems, such as CuMO2 (M=Fe or Cr), a spontaneous polarization P ≤ 0.03 μC cm−2 is induced by a screwlike spin structure with Si×Sj=0. This polarization is driven by a variation in the metal–ligand hybridization as a function of spin–orbit coupling43. 最后，在铜铁矿体系，如CuMO₂（M=Fe或Cr）中，由Si×Sj=0的螺旋状自旋结构诱导了P ≤ 0.03 μC cm⁻²的自发极化。这种极化是由金属-配体杂化程度随自旋轨道耦合变化而驱动的⁴³。

> ## Comparison between the different mechanisms.
> 
> ## 不同机制之间的比较。
> 
> The polarization values resulting from these four mechanisms are compared in FIG. 2. So far, the lonepair mechanism has been the most successful in yielding multiferroicity with the right characteristics for device applications; however, BiFeO3 is the only known material to be multiferroic because of this mechanism. In contrast, many spin driven multiferroic materials are being discovered, and robust roomtemperature systems appear to be within reach40,42,44. This hope is fuelled by the general nature of the driving mechanism itself, which leaves ample room for improvement, using chemical doping, pressure effects and strain, towards higher ordering temperatures or polarization values. Theory and experiment indicate that there are more mechanisms resulting in multiferroicity than the ones discussed so far. For example, ferroelectrically induced magnetic order was predicted for LiNbO3type structures45. Here, a weak magnetization can occur because a polar state breaks the inversion symmetry between antiferromagnetically aligned spins. > ## Composite multiferroics.
> 
> ## 复合多铁性材料。
> 
> So far, the focus has been on systems permitting the coexistence of ferroelectric and magnetic order in a single material. The alternatives are hybrid systems. A return to the historical idea of alloying ferroelectrics with magnetic ions led to the discovery of new multiferroic materials with a magnetoelectric response at room temperature47, which can become particularly pronounced near phase boundaries48. Another possibility is to use composite multiferroic materials, in which a ferromagnetic magnetostrictive and a ferroelectric piezoelectric constituent are merged in a granular or layered form. In constituent 1, magnetostriction denotes the induction of strain by a magnetic field; this strain is transferred via strain coupling between the constituents to constituent 2, where it is converted into a voltage via the piezoelectric effect49. The resulting magnetoelectric coupling can be 108 times larger than that of singlephase multiferroics50; however, it is observed only at microwave frequencies and in a very limited range of operation conditions. The other possible strategy is to use strain to change the size and isotropy of unit cells to destabilize a centrosymmetric structure in favour of a polar phase that emerges independently of the magnetic order. Multiferroicity in Baalloyed bulk SrMnO3 was obtained this way — the strain was exerted chemically through the large size of Ba3+ ions51. However, precise control of the strain value requires thinfilm architectures, which are discussed below. 到目前为止，焦点一直集中在允许铁电序和磁序在单一材料中共存的体系上。替代方案是混合体系。回归到将铁电体与磁性离子合金化的历史思路，导致了在室温下具有磁电响应的新型多铁性材料的发现⁴⁷，这种响应在相界附近会变得特别显著⁴⁸。另一种可能性是使用复合多铁性材料，其中铁磁磁致伸缩成分和铁电压电成分以颗粒或层状形式结合。在成分1中，磁致伸缩指的是由磁场诱导应变；该应变通过成分间的应变耦合传递到成分2，在成分2中通过压电效应转化为电压⁴⁹。由此产生的磁电耦合可以比单相多铁性材料大10⁸倍⁵⁰；然而，这种耦合仅在微波频率和非常有限的操作条件范围内观察到。另一种可能的策略是利用应变改变晶胞的尺寸和各向同性，以破坏中心对称结构的稳定性，有利于形成独立于磁序出现的极性相。Ba合金化的块材SrMnO₃就是以这种方式获得多铁性的——应变是通过Ba²⁺离子的大尺寸化学施加的⁵¹。然而，对应变值的精确控制需要薄膜架构，这将在下面讨论。 **magnetostriction**: 磁致伸缩，指磁性材料在磁场作用下发生尺寸或形状变化的现象。 **piezoelectric effect**: 压电效应，指某些电介质在受到机械应力作用时产生电极化的现象，反之亦然。

> # Thin films and heterostructures
> 
> # 薄膜与异质结构
> 
> Oxide thin films can be grown layer by layer with atomic scale precision, thus much more accurately and controllably than bulk crystals. At first, the obvious goal was the reproduction of multiferroic bulk phases in thin films and their switching in electric fields — this was accomplished in compounds such as oTbMnO3, hRMnO3 and BiFeO3 (REFS 17,52,53). An advantage of thin films is that battery voltages applied across them can generate the electric fields required for magnetoelectric phase control. New phases that are specific to thin films and heterostructures were later explored. For multiferroics, this approach was spectacularly successful in the case of BiFeO3 thin films and heterostructures, which were grown in a large range of structural configurations that are inaccessible in bulk crystals. These structures have already been reviewed in detail54–56; in this Review, we focus on a general classification of novel material phases and coupling effects that may arise in heterostructures. In particular, the effects of strain, heteroepitaxy and interfaces in thinfilm architectures open unprecedented opportunities for the development of multiferroic heterostructures and interface states with potential for device applications. The interfaces between different phases can either transfer the interaction between the constituents of the system or have an active role in determining the properties of the material. 氧化物薄膜可以以原子级精度逐层生长，因此比块状晶体精确得多，也更具可控性。起初，显而易见的目标是在薄膜中复现多铁性块材相及其在电场中的切换——这在o-TbMnO₃、h-RMnO₃和BiFeO₃等化合物中得以实现（参考文献17, 52, 53）。薄膜的一个优势是，施加在其上的电池电压可以产生磁电相控制所需的电场。随后，人们探索了薄膜和异质结构所特有的新相。对于多铁性材料而言，这种方法在BiFeO₃薄膜和异质结构中取得了巨大成功，这些薄膜和异质结构可以在块状晶体中无法实现的各种结构构型中生长。这些结构已经被详细综述过⁵⁴⁻⁵⁶；在本综述中，我们侧重于对异质结构中可能出现的新型材料相和耦合效应进行一般性分类。特别是，薄膜架构中的应变、异质外延和界面效应，为开发具有器件应用潜力的多铁性异质结构和界面态开辟了前所未有的机遇。不同相之间的界面既可以传递体系中各组分之间的相互作用，也可以主动地决定材料的性质。

> ## Ferromagnetic–multiferroic heterostructures.
> 
> ## 铁磁-多铁性异质结构。
> 
> A major drawback of almost all bulk multiferroics is their antiferromagnetic order. In contrast to ferromagnets, the lack of a macroscopic magnetization makes them technologically difficult to exploit. However, this shortcoming of the antiferromagnetic state may be overcome by coupling it to a ferromagnetic state. One possible solution is to exploit exchange bias — the displacement and hardening of a ferromagnetic hysteresis induced by the presence of an adjacent antiferromagnet. This is the mechanism used in the read–write heads of computer hard disks. Connecting an electrically tuneable antiferromagnet with a ferromagnetic layer could make it possible to manipulate the magnetic exchange bias with an electric voltage and to electrically shift the ferromagnetic hysteresis loop. This mechanism could form the basis of lowenergy magnetoelectric memories57–59. An important step in this direction was the lowtemperature demonstration of electrically tuneable pinning of ferromagnetic domains, which resulted in a voltagecontrolled modification of the exchange bias in hYMnO3–Permalloy60. Following predictions of magnetoelectric coupling61 (and possible weak magnetization) in BiFeO3, a modification of the exchange bias, albeit irreversible, was verified at room temperature in BiFeO3–CoFe (REF. 62). Another possible method to avoid the disadvantages of the antiferromagnetic state is to transduce it into a ferromagnetic state by establishing a rigid coupling between the antiferromagnetic order parameter of the multiferroic constituent and the order parameter of an adjacent ferromagnetic constituent. An important breakthrough was the observation of a voltageinduced rotation of the magnetization in a microscopic BiFeO3–CoFe dot63, which eventually led to the demonstration of repeatable magnetization reversal by an electric field at room temperature — the holy grail of research on magnetoelectric multiferroics64. Aside from their technological appeal, these results are remarkable because they trace the complex magnetoelectric switching behaviour back to the formation of domains. Ultimately, domain formation is the most significant factor for the response and performance of devices. All recent experiments on lowenergy magnetization reversal aim to establish a onetoone correlation between domains in ferromagnetic and multiferroic materials64,65. 几乎所有块材多铁性材料的一个主要缺点是其反铁磁序。与铁磁体相比，缺乏宏观磁化强度使得它们在技术上难以利用。然而，反铁磁态的这一缺点可以通过将其与铁磁态耦合来克服。一种可能的解决方案是利用交换偏置——即由于相邻反铁磁体的存在而导致的铁磁磁滞回线的位移和硬化。这是计算机硬盘读写头中使用的机制。将电可调的反铁磁体与铁磁层连接起来，就可能通过电压来操纵磁交换偏置，并电学地移动铁磁磁滞回线。这种机制可以构成低能耗磁电存储器的基础⁵⁷⁻⁵⁹。朝这个方向迈出的重要一步是在低温下演示了电可调的铁磁畴钉扎效应，这导致了h-YMnO₃–坡莫合金中交换偏置的电压控制修改⁶⁰。根据对BiFeO₃中磁电耦合⁶¹（以及可能的弱磁化强度）的预测，在BiFeO₃–CoFe中于室温下验证了交换偏置的改变，尽管是不可逆的（参考文献62）。另一种避免反铁磁态缺点的方法是，通过在多铁性组分的反铁磁序参量与相邻铁磁组分的序参量之间建立刚性耦合，将其转换为铁磁态。一个重要的突破是在微观BiFeO₃–CoFe点中观察到电压诱导的磁化旋转⁶³，这最终导致了室温下通过电场实现可重复磁化翻转的演示——这是磁电多铁性研究的“圣杯”⁶⁴。除了其技术吸引力之外，这些结果之所以引人注目，是因为它们将复杂的磁电切换行为追溯到了畴的形成。归根结底，畴的形成是决定器件响应和性能的最重要因素。近期所有关于低能耗磁化翻转的实验都旨在铁磁和多铁性材料中的畴之间建立直接关联⁶⁴'⁶⁵。 **exchange bias**: 交换偏置，指当铁磁体和反铁磁体接触并冷却通过反铁磁体的奈尔温度后，铁磁体的磁滞回线沿磁场轴发生偏移的现象。

> ## Strain engineering.
> 
> ## 应变工程。
> 
> Another tool for the engineering of multiferroic thin films is epitaxial strain. By selecting the right substrate, a wide range of tensile and compressive strains can be obtained. The associated modification of the lattice constant can induce a transition to new material phases or at least modify the existing ones. By using a ferroelectric and, hence, piezoelectric material as a substrate, strain can even be reversibly tuned postgrowth within a 0.1% window using an external voltage66. The atomic arrangement and the strain provided by the crystal lattice of the substrate can support the growth of otherwise unstable multiferroic phases67; it is also used to pattern the distribution of ferroelectric domains and domain states of multiferroics59,68. Strain was used to demonstrate the correlation between ferroelectric and magnetic spiral order in BiFeO3 (REF. 69), a result that was sought after for a long time. Strain can even induce ferroic order in otherwise nonferroic compounds: polar order was induced in SrTiO3 (REF. 70) and, coexisting with magnetic order, in EuTiO3 (REF. 71) and SrMnO3 (REF. 72), whereas ferromagnetism was induced in LuMnO3 (REF. 73). Strain also controls the density and distribution of vacancies, which represent a powerful degree of freedom for the modification of material properties72. Finally, strain can be used to couple ferroelectric domains to ferromagnetic domains across an interface through magnetostrictive and magnetoelectric coupling74,75 (FIG. 3b). This effect was demonstrated in experiments on CoFe–BaTiO3, which also revealed that the anisotropy and ordering temperature of interfacial magnetism can be controlled by electric fields76. Note that these experiments are different from experiments involving a magnetoelectric response at microwave frequencies in magnetostrictive–piezoelectric composites49. The first type of experiment depends on epitaxal interfaces as a source of a welldefined local interaction, whereas the second type involves unspecified, rough interfaces that serve as strain mediators. 工程化多铁性薄膜的另一个工具是外延应变。通过选择合适的衬底，可以获得大范围的拉伸和压缩应变。随之而来的晶格常数修改可以诱导向新材料相的转变，或者至少改变现有相。通过使用铁电体，也就是压电材料作为衬底，甚至可以在生长后通过外部电压在0.1%的窗口内可逆地调节应变⁶⁶。衬底晶格提供的原子排列和应变可以支持原本不稳定的多铁性相的生长⁶⁷；它也被用来图案化多铁性材料的铁电畴和畴态的分布⁵⁹'⁶⁸。应变被用来证明BiFeO₃中铁电序和磁螺旋序之间的关联⁶⁹，这是一个长期追求的结果。应变甚至可以在原本非铁性的化合物中诱导出铁性序：在SrTiO₃（参考文献70）中诱导了极性序，并在EuTiO₃（参考文献71）和SrMnO₃（参考文献72）中实现了与磁序共存的极性序，而在LuMnO₃（参考文献73）中诱导了铁磁性。应变还控制着空位的密度和分布，这是修改材料性质的一个强有力的自由度⁷²。最后，应变可用于通过磁致伸缩和磁电耦合⁷⁴'⁷⁵在界面上将铁电畴耦合到铁磁畴（图3b）。这种效应在CoFe–BaTiO₃实验中得到了证明，该实验还揭示了界面磁性的各向异性和有序化温度可以通过电场控制⁷⁶。请注意，这些实验不同于在磁致伸缩-压电复合材料中涉及微波频率下磁电响应的实验⁴⁹。第一类实验依赖于外延界面作为明确定义的局域相互作用的来源，而第二类实验则涉及用作应变介质的非特定、粗糙的界面。

> ## Interfaces.
> 
> ## 界面。
> 
> Multiferroic states can also be realized at the interface between two constituents (FIG. 3c). At the interface, properties not found in the bulk can exist because of low local symmetry, confinement effects, strain gradients and chemical anisotropy. Indeed, it was found that the interface between a ferromagnetic material, Fe or Co, and a ferroelectric material, BaTiO3, can be multiferroic, and that the multiferroicity is retained within several monolayers around the interface77. The possible relevance for applications of these 2D multiferroic interface states still has to be elaborated. Note that interfaces break space but not timeinversion symmetry. As a consequence, the magnetization axis at the interface can be reoriented by an electric field, but the direction along this axis remains ambiguous unless a magnetic bias field is applied. Another way of confining multiferroicity to an interface might be through domain walls; in this case, the interface is represented by the multiferroic wall between different domains within a single material that is in itself not multiferroic (FIG. 3d). 多铁性态也可以在两种组分之间的界面上实现（图3c）。在界面处，由于低局域对称性、限域效应、应变梯度和化学各向异性，可能存在块材中不具备的性质。事实上，人们发现铁磁材料（Fe或Co）和铁电材料（BaTiO₃）之间的界面可以是多铁性的，并且多铁性在界面附近的几个单原子层内得以保持⁷⁷。这些二维多铁性界面态可能的应用相关性仍有待详细阐述。请注意，界面打破空间反演对称性，但不打破时间反演对称性。因此，界面处的磁化轴可以被电场重新定向，但沿该轴的方向仍不明确，除非施加了磁偏置场。另一种将多铁性限制在界面处的方法可能是通过畴壁；在这种情况下，界面由单一材料内部不同畴之间的多铁性壁表示，而该材料本身并非多铁性（图3d）。

> # Domains and domain walls
> 
> # 畴与畴壁
> 
> Domains and domain walls (BOX 1) are crucial for the control of many material properties, such as coercivity, resistance and/or fatigue. The magnetoelectric coupling of a multiferroic material roots in the coupling between its individual magnetic and ferroelectric domains. Although advanced functionalities are often based on complex domain architectures64, early investigations focused on symmetry analysis10 and experiments on a single domain state of a singlecrystal multiferroic material. An example is the study of the reversal of the electric order parameter by a magnetic field (or vice versa) via the linear magnetoelectric effect6. Domain patterns were initially imaged by linear optics, and ferroelectric domains were visualized after chemical surface etching or via optical birefringence in materials with simultaneous elastic deformation. Ferromagnetic domains were resolved by the magnetooptical Faraday or Kerr effect10. Using these techniques, domains were studied in multiferroic boracites78, BiFeO3 (REF. 79) and hYMnO3 (REF. 80). The main limitations of the optical approach are the insensitivity to antiferromagnetic order and the limited resolution of, at most, 1 μm. A seminal advance was the application of nonlinear optics to multiferroics. In addition to providing access to antiferromagnetic domain states, a technique such as second harmonic generation can resolve electric and magnetic domains within the same experiment, making spatial magnetoelectric coupling phenomena directly visible81. This was crucial for resolving the pronounced coupling between ferroelectric and antiferromagnetic domains in hYMnO3 and to reveal that this coupling does not originate in the domains but in the domain walls11. Similar studies on spinspiral multiferroics, such as MnWO4 (REFS 82,83) or oTbMnO3 (REF. 84), revealed the dynamics of multiferroic domains in external fields and on crossing phase transitions. Scanningprobe and electronmicroscopy techniques pushed the resolution of domain imaging to the nanoscale, making even domain walls accessible with atomicscale resolution. These advances were extremely helpful for the understanding of the complex multiferroic domain structures in BiFeO3 thin films85. The study of hRMnO3 revealed a particularly interesting example of domain physics: the trimerizing lattice distortion leads to a protected pattern of improper ferroelectric domains with unique topological properties86,87. More recently, attention is moving away from the domains and towards their boundaries — the domain walls88. Whereas heterointerfaces separate constituents in artificial multilayers, domain walls can be regarded as natural homointerfaces occurring within a material. At the walls, charge or spin phenomena similar to the ones occurring at heterointerfaces can emerge but with the advantage that domain walls can be created, moved and erased postgrowth. As FIG. 4 shows, the domain walls in type I and type II multiferroics are different. In type I multiferroics, magnetic and electric walls can coincide but do not have to because of the independence of magnetic and electric order. In type II multiferroics, the magnetic order induces the electric order; therefore, each ferroelectric domain wall is a magnetic domain wall at the same time and, thus, multiferroic. The groundbreaking discovery that domain walls in BiFeO3 have a larger conductance than the surrounding bulk89 triggered a broad interest in domainwall engineering90. Domain walls with increased, attenuated or anisotropic conductance have been investigated in many materials88. These investigations are mainly targeted at the ferroelectric state, and truly multiferroic domain walls receive much less attention. Examples of the phenomena observed at the domain walls are the magnetoelectric coupling or spontaneous magnetization in the domain walls of multiferroic manganites11,91,92. In spindriven ferroelectrics, examples are the coupled Bloch or Néellike rotation of magnetic chirality and the induced electric polarization83,93. In contrast to the aforementioned multiferroicity emerging at the interfaces between different nonmultiferroic compounds (FIG. 3c), multiferroicity occurring at the domain walls within a single nonmultiferroic compounds (FIG. 3d) has not yet been observed. 畴和畴壁（方框1）对于控制许多材料性质（如矫顽力、电阻和/或疲劳）至关重要。多铁性材料的磁电耦合根植于其各个磁畴和电畴之间的耦合。尽管先进功能通常基于复杂的畴架构⁶⁴，但早期研究侧重于对称性分析¹⁰以及针对单晶多铁性材料单畴态的实验。一个例子是通过线性磁电效应⁶研究磁场导致的电序参量翻转（或反之）。畴图案最初通过线性光学成像，铁电畴则在化学表面刻蚀后或通过同时存在弹性形变材料中的光学双折射来可视化。铁磁畴则通过磁光法拉第或克尔效应来分辨¹⁰。利用这些技术，人们研究了多铁性方硼石⁷⁸、BiFeO₃（参考文献79）和h-YMnO₃（参考文献80）中的畴。光学方法的主要局限性在于对反铁磁序不敏感，以及分辨率最高只能达到1微米。一个开创性的进展是将非线性光学应用于多铁性材料。除了能够探测反铁磁畴态之外，像二次谐波产生这样的技术还能在同一个实验中分辨电畴和磁畴，使得空间磁电耦合现象直接可视化⁸¹。这对于解析h-YMnO₃中铁电畴和反铁磁畴之间显著的耦合，并揭示这种耦合并非起源于畴内而是起源于畴壁，至关重要¹¹。对自旋螺旋多铁性材料（如MnWO₄（参考文献82, 83）或o-TbMnO₃（参考文献84））的类似研究，揭示了在外场下和跨越相变时多铁性畴的动力学。扫描探针和电子显微技术将畴成像的分辨率推进到纳米尺度，甚至使得畴壁也能以原子级分辨率观测。这些进展极大地帮助了理解BiFeO₃薄膜中复杂的多铁性畴结构⁸⁵。对h-RMnO₃的研究揭示了一个特别有趣的畴物理例子：三聚化的晶格畸变导致了一种受保护的非本征铁电畴图案，具有独特的拓扑性质⁸⁶'⁸⁷。最近，注意力正从畴本身转向它们的边界——畴壁⁸⁸。异质界面分隔了人工多层膜中的不同组分，而畴壁则可以被视为材料内部天然的同质界面。在畴壁处，可以出现类似于异质界面处的电荷或自旋现象，但其优势在于畴壁可以在生长后被创建、移动和擦除。如图4所示，第I类和第II类多铁性材料中的畴壁是不同的。在第I类多铁性材料中，由于磁序和电序的独立性，磁畴壁和电畴壁可以重合，但并非必须重合。在第II类多铁性材料中，磁序诱导电序；因此，每个铁电畴壁同时也是磁畴壁，因而具有多铁性。具有开创性的发现是，BiFeO₃中的畴壁比周围块材具有更大的电导率⁸⁹，这引发了人们对畴壁工程⁹⁰的广泛兴趣。在多种材料中研究了具有增强、衰减或各向异性电导率的畴壁⁸⁸。这些研究主要针对铁电态，而真正意义上的多铁性畴壁受到的关注要少得多。在畴壁处观察到的现象的例子包括：多铁性锰酸盐畴壁中的磁电耦合或自发磁化¹¹'⁹¹'⁹²。在自旋驱动的铁电体中，例子包括磁手性的耦合布洛赫型或奈尔型旋转与诱导的电极化⁸³'⁹³。与前述在不同非多铁性化合物之间的界面处出现的多铁性（图3c）相比，在单一非多铁性化合物内部的畴壁处出现的多铁性（图3d）尚未被观察到。 **second harmonic generation**: 二次谐波产生，一种非线性光学过程，两个相同频率的光子与材料相互作用后，产生一个频率加倍的光子；对对称性破缺敏感，常用于探测铁电畴和反铁磁畴。

> # Non-equilibrium dynamics
> 
> # 非平衡动力学
> 
> In the field of multiferroics, the strong interest in the electricfield control of the magnetic state is contrasted by the small number of investigations on the temporal evolution of such magnetoelectric switching. However, understanding the switching dynamics is essential for judging and improving the speed, repeatability and control of voltageinduced magnetization reversal. For example, the switching may be inherently faster or slower than conventional magneticfield switching, a factor that needs to be known before the design of a multiferroic memory element can be envisaged. Early work on nonequilibrium dynamics revealed a fundamentally different temporal evolution for the polar and magnetic order94 in multiferroic GaFeO3. Another study showed that optical excitation of CuO induces a transition from commensurate to incommensurate magnetic order within picoseconds, which probably influences the accompanying ferroelectric polarization95. LuFeO2 shows indications of a dynamic correlation between the magnetic and an assumed polarizationinducing charge order96. The resonant excitation of spinspiral multiferroics was predicted to promote an alloptical reversal of the multiferroic order parameters97; related experiments on oTbMnO3, performed at accessible terahertz amplitudes, identified an ultrafast spin deflection98 of ~4%, far below a 180° reversal. Ultrafast optical pumpprobe spectroscopy was used to reveal the coexistence and coupling of antiferromagnetic or ferroelectric and ferromagnetic order in strained oTbMnO3 films99. The ultrafast reversal of the multiferroic order parameters by an electric field pulse in BiFeO3 is debated100,101; electric field experiments on magnetically driven ferroelectrics even revealed that their order parameter reversal can be inherently slow102. In a composite multiferroic, ultrafast manipulation of the polarization via photoinduced magnetostriction was observed103. As these results show, the field is still at the stage of collecting disconnected bits of information on the nonequilibrium dynamics of multiferroics, and a coherent understanding of them has not yet been reached. Note that these experiments on switching are distinct from those in which ultrafast processes in multiferroics are investigated, but not in connection with the multiferroic order — photostrain effects in BiFeO3 are one example104. 在多铁性领域，对电场控制磁态的强烈兴趣与研究此类磁电切换的时间演化过程的少量研究形成了对比。然而，理解切换动力学对于判断和改进电压诱导磁化翻转的速度、可重复性和可控性至关重要。例如，这种切换可能固有地比传统磁场切换更快或更慢，这是在设想设计多铁性存储元件之前需要了解的一个因素。早期的非平衡动力学工作揭示了多铁性GaFeO₃中极性序和磁序在时间演化上的根本性差异⁹⁴。另一项研究表明，CuO的光学激发在皮秒内诱导了从共格到非共格磁序的转变，这可能影响了伴随的铁电极化⁹⁵。LuFeO₂显示出磁序与假定的极化诱导电荷序之间存在动态关联的迹象⁹⁶。据预测，自旋螺旋多铁性材料的共振激发将促进多铁性序参量的全光翻转⁹⁷；在可达到的太赫兹振幅下对o-TbMnO₃进行的相关实验，识别出约4%的超快自旋偏转⁹⁸，远低于180°翻转。超快光学泵浦探测光谱被用来揭示应变o-TbMnO₃薄膜中反铁磁/铁电序与铁磁序的共存和耦合⁹⁹。BiFeO₃中通过电场脉冲实现多铁性序参量的超快翻转存在争议¹⁰⁰'¹⁰¹；对磁驱动铁电体的电场实验甚至揭示，其序参量翻转可能本质上就很慢¹⁰²。在复合多铁性材料中，观察到了通过光致磁致伸缩对极化进行的超快操控¹⁰³。正如这些结果所示，该领域仍处于收集关于多铁性非平衡动力学的零散信息的阶段，尚未达到对它们的贯通理解。请注意，这些关于切换的实验不同于那些研究多铁性材料中超快过程，但与多铁性序无关的实验——BiFeO₃中的光致应变效应便是一个例子¹⁰⁴。

> # Violation of inversion symmetry
> 
> # 反演对称性的破缺
> 
> The Neumann principle relates the physical properties of a material to its structural symmetry, stating that the symmetry displayed by the material properties includes the symmetry elements characterizing the structural arrangement of its charges and spins. Many of the remarkable physical properties of multiferroics arise from the breaking of space and timeinversion symmetry caused by the simultaneous presence of magnetic and electric longrange order — the linear magnetoelectric effect is a prototypical example. 诺伊曼原理将材料的物理性质与其结构对称性联系起来，指出材料性质所显示的对称性包含了表征其电荷和自旋结构排列的对称元素。多铁性材料的许多非凡物理性质都源于磁性和电性长程序同时存在所导致的空间反演和时间反演对称性的破缺——线性磁电效应就是一个典型的例子。 **Neumann principle**: 诺伊曼原理，晶体物理学的核心原理，指出晶体的任何宏观物理性质的对称性，必须包含晶体所属点群的对称元素。
> 
> The increasing understanding of the role of symmetry led to the realization that some phenomena originally associated with the coexistence of magnetic and electric order are actually a consequence of this twofold symmetry violation and can therefore occur in nonmultiferroics as well. This could considerably expand the range of host systems in which these phenomena are observed. A prime example is Cr2O3, a nonmultiferroic material with broken space and timeinversion symmetry, which displays many of the effects discussed in this Review, such as static and optical magnetoelectric effects, ferrotoroidicity and magnetic quasimonopoles3,105–107. 对对称性作用的日益理解使人们认识到，一些最初与磁序和电序共存相关的现象，实际上是这种双重对称性破缺的结果，因此也可以在非多铁性材料中发生。这可以大大扩展观察到这些现象的主体体系范围。一个典型的例子是Cr₂O₃，一种具有破缺空间和时间反演对称性的非多铁性材料，它显示了本综述中讨论的许多效应，如静态和光学磁电效应、铁磁涡旋性和磁准单极子³'¹⁰⁵⁻¹⁰⁷。
> 
> ## Ferrotoroidicity.
> 
> ## 铁磁涡旋性。
> 
> For a long time, the magnetoelectric coupling in multiferroics has been associated with a violation of timereversal symmetry by the magnetic order and a violation of spatial inversion symmetry by the electric order. This vision changed with the discovery that in spin driven ferroelectrics, it is the magnetic order that breaks inversion symmetry, a realization that intensified the search for a primary ferroic order violating both space and timeinversion symmetry. The discovery of such a state would symmetrize and complete the known forms of primary ferroic order, which at the moment include states violating spatial inversion symmetry (ferroelectricity), temporal inversion symmetry (ferromagnetism) or neither of the two (ferroelasticity)108,109. The multipole expansion of the electrodynamic vector potential A(r) includes a space–timeantisymmetric term, a socalled anapole110 or toroidal moment111. The spontaneous, uniform alignment of toroidal moments would then establish a ferrotoroidic state, with domains that could be hysteretically switched by an adjunct toroidal (that is, space–time antisymmetric) external field. Most investigations consider toroidal spin arrangements in the unit cell where the toroidal moment, T = ∑i ri × Si, represents a magnetic whirl (ri is the position of spin Si, FIG. 5a)112. By contrast, the existence of atomic toroidal moments remains debated113. Compounds exhibiting a toroidal magnetic structure have been identified; however, this does not mean that these compounds are ferrotoroidic. Ferrotoroidicity requires the presence of domains (FIG. 5b) and their poling in a toroidal field. Both conditions were realized114,115 in LiCoPO4, in which the coupling to the toroidal field was established with perpendicular magnetic and electric fields. The direct generation of a toroidal field remains an open challenge. Finally, ferrotoroidic structures can also be regarded as antiferromagnetic, and ferrotoroidicity is a reasonable concept only if the toroidal moment can act as the driving (proper) order parameter. This latter criterion was exemplified116 in the case of LiFeSi2O6. 长期以来，多铁性材料中的磁电耦合一直与磁序破坏时间反演对称性以及电序破坏空间反演对称性相关联。随着发现自旋驱动的铁电体中，正是磁序破坏了反演对称性，这一观念发生了改变，这一认识加强了寻找同时破坏空间和时间反演对称性的初级铁性序的努力。发现这样一种状态将对称化并完善已知的初级铁性序形式，目前这些形式包括破坏空间反演对称性（铁电性）、破坏时间反演对称性（铁磁性）或两者都不破坏（铁弹性）的状态¹⁰⁸'¹⁰⁹。电动力学矢量势A(r)的多极展开包含一个时空反对称项，即所谓的anapole¹¹⁰或环形矩¹¹¹。环形矩的自发、均匀排列将建立铁磁涡旋态，其畴可以通过一个伴随的环形（即时空反对称）外场进行滞后切换。大多数研究考虑晶胞中的环形自旋排列，其中环形矩 T = ∑i ri × Si 代表一个磁涡旋（ri是自旋Si的位置，图5a）¹¹²。相比之下，原子级环形矩的存在仍然存在争议¹¹³。已经鉴定出显示环形磁结构的化合物；然而，这并不意味着这些化合物是铁磁涡旋性的。铁磁涡旋性要求存在畴（图5b）以及它们在环形场中的极化。这两个条件都在LiCoPO₄中实现了¹¹⁴'¹¹⁵，其中与环形场的耦合是通过垂直的磁场和电场建立的。直接产生环形场仍然是一个开放的挑战。最后，铁磁涡旋结构也可以被视为反铁磁性的，并且只有当环形矩可以作为驱动（本征）序参量时，铁磁涡旋性才是一个合理的概念。后一个标准在LiFeSi₂O₆的例子中得到了体现¹¹⁶。 **ferrotoroidicity**: 铁磁涡旋性，一种假设的初级铁性序，对应于环形矩（磁涡旋）的自发排列，同时打破空间反演和时间反演对称性。 **toroidal moment**: 环形矩，一种多极子，由自旋围绕一个中心涡旋排列产生，同时在空间和时间反演下改变符号。

> ## Magnetic monopoles.
> 
> ## 磁单极子。
> 
> Magnetic monopoles are a concept closely related to toroidal moments. Magnetic monopoles are the counterpart of the electric (charge) monopoles, but they are not allowed in free space, as expressed by the Maxwell equation, ∇B = 0. However, in condensedmatter systems, magnetic quasimonopoles with ∇H ≠ 0 or ∇M ≠ 0 can exist in both an excited form (such as spin ice117) or as the ground state. The ground state monopole is derived as the second order term in the multipole expansion of the magnetization density, μ(r), in which the magnetic monopole appears along with the toroidal moment and the magnetic quadrupole in space–timeinversionsymmetry violating systems107. These three terms are associated to three qualitatively different contributions to the linear magnetoelectric effect107,111. Note that the relation between the derivations of the toroidal moment from μ(r) and A(r) still needs to be established. Magnetic monopoles, as toroidal moments, can be studied at the level of atoms or unit cells. The first case is theoretically discussed for the LiMPO4 system107 (M=Mn–Ni). A spin arrangement that may be interpreted as a magnetic monopole is sketched in FIG. 5c and was observed in the P63/cʹmʹ phase of the hRMnO3 system118. The existence of a ferromonopolar state is debated107, but it would depend on the existence of a conjugate scalar monopolar field. A solitary magnetic monopole may be conveniently generated by placing a point charge in front of a magnetoelectric medium, which would convert the electric monopole into a submergedimage magnetic monopole. Until now, this possibility has only been explored for topological insulators exposed to a magnetic field119, but a much stronger response can be expected for conventional bulk magnetoelectrics. 磁单极子是与环形矩密切相关的概念。磁单极子是电（电荷）单极子的对应物，但正如麦克斯韦方程 ∇·B = 0 所表述的，它们在自由空间中是不允许存在的。然而，在凝聚态体系中，∇·H ≠ 0 或 ∇·M ≠ 0 的磁准单极子可以以激发态形式（如自旋冰¹¹⁷）或作为基态存在。基态单极子被推导为磁化密度 μ(r) 多极展开中的二阶项，在时空反演对称性破缺的体系中，磁单极子与环形矩和磁四极子一同出现¹⁰⁷。这三项与线性磁电效应的三种定性不同的贡献相关联¹⁰⁷'¹¹¹。请注意，从μ(r)和A(r)推导环形矩之间的关系仍有待建立。与环形矩一样，磁单极子可以在原子或晶胞层面进行研究。第一种情况在理论上针对LiMPO₄体系（M=Mn–Ni）进行了讨论¹⁰⁷。一种可以被解释为磁单极子的自旋排列在图5c中示意性地画出，并在h-RMnO₃体系的P6₃/cʹmʹ相中观察到¹¹⁸。铁磁单极态的存在存在争议¹⁰⁷，但它将取决于是否存在一个共轭的标量单极子场。一个孤立的磁单极子可以方便地通过将一个点电荷置于磁电介质前产生，这将把电单极子转化为一个隐没的镜像磁单极子。到目前为止，这种可能性仅在受到磁场作用的拓扑绝缘体中进行了探索¹¹⁹，但对于传统的块材磁电体，可以预期会有更强的响应。 **magnetic monopoles**: 磁单极子，一种假想的粒子，是仅带有单一磁荷（N极或S极）的基本粒子；在凝聚态物理中，指准粒子激发，其行为类似于孤立的磁荷。 **spin ice**: 自旋冰，一类具有烧绿石结构的磁性材料，其基态具有大量简并度，其中的激发态表现出类似于磁单极子的行为。
> 
> From a technological point of view, toroidal and monopolar systems are interesting because of the inherent linear magnetoelectric effect they display107. This effect may be substantially enhanced by combining a space–time antisymmetric ferrotoroidic state with a ferroelectric and/or ferromagnetic state, similar to how the combination of space–timeantisymmetric magnetic order with ferroelectric order in spindriven multiferroics can be the source of giant magnetoelectric effects. 从技术角度来看，环形和单极子系统因其展示的固有线性磁电效应而引人关注¹⁰⁷。通过将时空反对称的铁磁涡旋态与铁电态和/或铁磁态相结合，这种效应可能会得到显著增强，类似于自旋驱动的多铁性材料中时空反对称磁序与铁电序的结合可以成为巨磁电效应的来源。
> 
> ## Effects at finite frequencies.
> 
> ## 有限频率下的效应。
> 
> The violation of space and time inversion symmetries is not a static phenomenon; it is also realized at finite frequencies. This fact is exploited in composites in which a ferromagnetic magnetostrictive and a ferroelectric piezoelectric constituent are mixed, so that an oscillating magnetic field induces an oscillating electric polarization via strain coupling49. In singlephase multiferroics, attention is focused on two dynamic effects: electromagnons, a magnetoelectric excitation, and nonreciprocal directional dichroism, a groundstate optical response. Both phenomena are manifestations of the twofold symmetry violation rather than of the multiferroic state. As a consequence, they are also observed in nonmultiferroics. 空间和时间反演对称性的破缺并非静态现象；它也在有限频率下实现。这一事实在复合材料中得到了利用，其中将铁磁磁致伸缩成分和铁电压电成分混合，使得振荡磁场通过应变耦合诱导出振荡电极化⁴⁹。在单相多铁性材料中，注意力集中在两种动态效应上：电磁振子，一种磁电激发；以及非互易定向二向色性，一种基态光学响应。这两种现象都是双重对称性破缺的表现，而非多铁性态的表现。因此，它们也在非多铁性材料中观察到。 **electromagnons**: 电磁振子，一种磁振子（自旋波），其激发伴随着电极化的振荡，是磁电耦合在动态下的体现。 **nonreciprocal directional dichroism**: 非互易定向二向色性，指光在材料中沿相反方向传播时，其吸收或折射率不同的现象，通常发生在同时破缺空间反演和时间反演对称性的体系中。
> 
> ### Electromagnons.
> 
> ### 电磁振子。
> 
> An electromagnon is a magnetic resonance that breaks inversion symmetry, giving rise to an oscillating electric polarization (FIG. 6). Electromagnons were first reported in the multiferroics oTbMnO3 and oGdMnO3 (REF. 120), in which a dielectric resonance influenced by magnetic order and magnetic fields was observed. In oGdMnO3, this resonance appeared outside the multiferroic phase, which showed from the start that electromagnons are a consequence of the system symmetry rather than of multiferroicity. Initially, in oTbMnO3, a spinwave excitation was observed that induces a polarization oscillation via the (symmetric) Heisenberg exchange121 (FIG. 6b). This is remarkable, because the static polarization of oTbMnO3 is driven by the (antisymmetric) inverse DM interaction (FIG. 6c,d). Later, oTbMnO3 also revealed a polarization oscillation mediated by the inverse DM interaction122. Electromagnons have been identified in various compounds, including type I multiferroics123, high temperature multiferroics124 and nonmultiferroics125,126. 电磁振子是一种打破反演对称性的磁共振，引起振荡的电极化（图6）。电磁振子首次在多铁性材料o-TbMnO₃和o-GdMnO₃中被报道（参考文献120），在这些材料中观察到了受磁序和磁场影响的介电共振。在o-GdMnO₃中，这种共振出现在多铁性相之外，这从一开始就表明电磁振子是体系对称性而非多铁性的结果。最初，在o-TbMnO₃中，观察到了一种自旋波激发，它通过（对称的）海森堡交换作用诱导极化振荡¹²¹（图6b）。这很引人注目，因为o-TbMnO₃的静态极化是由（反对称的）逆DM相互作用驱动的（图6c,d）。后来，o-TbMnO₃也揭示了由逆DM相互作用介导的极化振荡¹²²。电磁振子已在多种化合物中被确认，包括第I类多铁性材料¹²³、高温多铁性材料¹²⁴以及非多铁性材料¹²⁵'¹²⁶。
> 
> ### Nonreciprocal directional dichroism.
> 
> ### 非互易定向二向色性。
> 
> Identical light waves travelling in opposite directions through a material in which space and timeinversion symmetry are broken are transmitted with different intensity and polarization, an effect called nonreciprocal directional dichroism (NDD). The reversal of this directional dependence by a magnetic field goes under the name of the optical magnetoelectric effect127,128. Spontaneous NDD induced by multiferroic order has been first studied in GaFeO3, a material exhibiting relative absorption differences Δα/α of up to 1.6 × 10−3 (REFS 129,130). Giant NDD with Δα/α = 1 was reported for multiferroic Ba2CoGe2O7 in the terahertz regime131. The requirement of space–time antisymmetry means that NDD is not limited to multiferroics132; in fact, the observation of the antiferromagnetic analogue of the Kerr and Faraday effect in Cr2O3 constitutes the earliest example of NDD105,133,134. The strongest possible manifestation of NDD transmission of light through a material in one direction only — was also observed in a nonmultiferroic. Experiments on CuB2O4 in magnetic fields of up to 53 T showed that such unidirectional propagation would develop at about 75 T (REF. 135). As discussed, space and timeinversion symmetry are violated separately in the two constituents of a composite multiferroic. Another way to acquire such spatially separated symmetry breaking is to grow trilayer superlattices formed by two dielectrics and one magnetically ordered constituent136. Other systems are patterned media, such as magnetic photonic crystals and toroidal or chiral metamaterials137,138. Experimentally, patterned media are more successful in demonstrating NDD than other composite structures: for example, interfaces139 and magnetized chevrons140 or gratings141. Other ways to obtain pronounced NDD are based on the resonant excitation of magnetoelectrically active states such as magnons142, electromagnons143 or skyrmions144. 相同的光波在空间和时间反演对称性均被打破的材料中沿相反方向传播时，会以不同的强度和偏振透射，这种效应被称为非互易定向二向色性（NDD）。通过磁场反转这种方向依赖性被称为光学磁电效应¹²⁷'¹²⁸。由多铁性序诱导的自发NDD首先在GaFeO₃中被研究，该材料表现出高达1.6 × 10⁻³的相对吸收差异Δα/α（参考文献129, 130）。据报道，在太赫兹波段，多铁性Ba₂CoGe₂O₇中观测到了Δα/α = 1的巨NDD¹³¹。时空反对称性的要求意味着NDD不仅限于多铁性材料¹³²；事实上，在Cr₂O₃中观察到反铁磁类似物克尔和法拉第效应构成了最早的NDD例子¹⁰⁵'¹³³'¹³⁴。NDD的最强可能表现形式——光仅在一个方向上穿透材料——也在非多铁性材料中观察到了。在高达53 T磁场下对CuB₂O₄的实验表明，这种单向传播将在约75 T时发展出来（参考文献135）。如前所述，在复合多铁性材料的两种组分中，空间和时间反演对称性是分别被打破的。获得这种空间分离的对称性破缺的另一种方法是生长由两种电介质和一种磁有序组分形成的三层超晶格¹³⁶。其他体系包括图案化介质，如磁性光子晶体和环形或手性超材料¹³⁷'¹³⁸。实验上，图案化介质在演示NDD方面比其他复合结构更成功：例如，界面¹³⁹和磁化锯齿结构¹⁴⁰或光栅¹⁴¹。获得显著NDD的其他方法基于对磁电活性态（如磁振子¹⁴²、电磁振子¹⁴³或斯格明子¹⁴⁴）的共振激发。 **skyrmions**: 斯格明子，一种拓扑稳定的自旋结构，表现为纳米尺度的磁涡旋，具有粒子特性，可被电流或电场驱动。

> # Impact on other research fields
> 
> # 对其他研究领域的影响
> 
> The field of multiferroics is experiencing a migration into other research disciplines, in which multiferroic materials are studied for properties that are, at best, indirectly related to their multiferroic nature. This trend was already apparent in the discussion on space and timeinversion symmetry violation in the previous section. Multiferroics had a major impact on the field of ferroelectrics. Research in multiferroics restored an awareness of the wealth of mechanisms, in addition to the displacive mechanism, that can promote spontaneous polar order. In particular, the importance of improper ferroelectric materials was recognized, because they allow the formation of domains and domain walls with properties and functionalities that do not occur if the polarization is the primary order parameter83,88,145. An increasing interest in oxide electronics146, in which multiferroic materials and their magnetoelectric interactions are an important topic, stimulated impressive progress in the epitaxial growth of oxide films and heterostructures. This opened a path to various multiferroic functionalities. It allows coupling of the antiferromagnetic state of a multiferroic material to the ferromagnetic state of an adjacent material, a multiferroic state to be induced in the first place, and the creation of a magnetoelectric coupling at or across interfaces. Heterostructures also facilitate connection of the multiferroic system to electronic circuitry. Research in multiferroics motivated the improvement of many characterization techniques. For example, Xray diffraction experiments were improved to detect atomic shifts at the femtometre scale147; pyroelectric measurements were refined to measure spontaneous polarizations down to 0.1 nC cm−2; nonlinear laser spectroscopy was developed into a tool for imaging the coexistence and interaction of magnetic and ferroelectric domains11,82; and terahertz spectroscopy was advanced to study electromagnons120. The field of multiferroics has also entered the realm of highenergy physics. Materials with specific magnetic and electric properties may allow the detection of a permanent electric dipole moment of the electron with unparalleled accuracy. The required properties can be obtained by replacing Eu in multiferroic EuTiO3 with Ba until the magnetic order is suppressed148. The multiferroic hRMnO3 compounds are used to scrutinize scaling laws related to string formation in the post BigBang universe86,87 — upon cooling, both systems undergo topologically similar phase transitions. This similarity is used to relate the distribution of the alleged cosmological strings to the distribution of the lines along which ferroelectric domains meet in hRMnO3. Multiferroic materials are a part of these research areas because of their predisposition to host ferroelectric states of uncommon origin or topology. 多铁性领域正在经历向其他研究学科的迁移，在这些学科中，研究多铁性材料是为了获得与其多铁性本质至多间接相关的性质。这一趋势在上一节关于空间和时间反演对称性破缺的讨论中已经很明显。多铁性对铁电体领域产生了重大影响。多铁性研究恢复了人们对除位移型机制之外，还有大量机制可以促进自发极性序的认识。特别是，非本征铁电材料的重要性得到了认可，因为它们允许形成具有特性和功能的畴和畴壁，而这些特性和功能在以极化作为初级序参量时是不会出现的⁸³'⁸⁸'¹⁴⁵。对氧化物电子学¹⁴⁶日益增长的兴趣，其中多铁性材料及其磁电相互作用是一个重要主题，刺激了氧化物薄膜和异质结构外延生长方面的显著进步。这为各种多铁性功能开辟了道路。它允许多铁性材料的反铁磁态与相邻材料的铁磁态耦合，从一开始就诱导多铁性态，并在界面处或跨界面创建磁电耦合。异质结构也便于将多铁性体系连接到电子电路。多铁性研究推动了许多表征技术的改进。例如，X射线衍射实验得到改进，可以检测飞米尺度的原子位移¹⁴⁷；热释电测量被精炼，可以测量低至0.1 nC cm⁻²的自发极化；非线性激光光谱学发展成为一种用于成像磁畴和电畴共存及相互作用的工具¹¹'⁸²；太赫兹光谱学被推进用于研究电磁振子¹²⁰。多铁性领域也已进入高能物理学的范畴。具有特定磁和电性质的材料可能允许以无与伦比的精度探测电子的永久电偶极矩。通过在多多铁性EuTiO₃中用Ba替换Eu直至磁序被抑制，可以获得所需的性质¹⁴⁸。多铁性h-RMnO₃化合物被用来仔细审视与大爆炸后宇宙中弦形成相关的标度律⁸⁶'⁸⁷——在冷却过程中，两个体系都经历了拓扑相似的相变。这种相似性被用来将所谓的宇宙弦的分布与h-RMnO₃中铁电畴交汇线的分布联系起来。多铁性材料之所以成为这些研究领域的一部分，是因为它们易于承载具有不常见起源或拓扑结构的铁电态。

> # Trends and challenges
> 
> # 趋势与挑战
> 
> Despite the advances in the field of multiferroics, some of the current goals are still the same as in the 1960s, and it is safe to assume that they will continue to keep researchers busy for a while. These objectives include the quest for new materials with a strong coupling of magnetic and electric properties, which could raise the very limited number149 of known roomtemperature multiferroics. In particular, a roomtemperature multiferroic with pronounced and strongly coupled spontaneous magnetization and polarization is not yet known. Novel mechanisms driving multiferroicity may be discovered, but the existing ones are far from being fully exploited. For example, there can be many ways to obtain a magnetic order that can stabilize an improper ferroelectric state. This may lead to inherently higher ordering temperatures and polarizations compared with those of the existing spindriven ferroelectrics — predictions and preliminary discoveries are promising42,44. Other, underrepresented classes of materials that may host multiferroic states are nonoxide compounds150 and organic materials151. A multiferroic device in which the magnetization is controlled by an electric field, preferably at low voltages, at room temperature and with ultrafast switching, remains a prime goal152–155. Major accomplishments obtained so far are the repeatable, roomtemperature magnetization reversal by an electric field demonstrated in BiFeO3–CoFe heterostructures64 and the realization of a multiferroic fourstate memory operated at low temperatures156. These are important steps towards the integration of multiferroics into devices, but crucial aspects such as the dynamics, reliability and fatigue of these device concepts still have to be optimized in order to develop a competitive technology. In addition to this, alternative routes for the control of magnetism that use spin torque157 or spin orbit torque158,159 exerted by a spin polarized electric current have been presented, and any multiferroic device will need to compete with their functionality and performance. 尽管多铁性领域取得了进展，但当前的一些目标仍然与20世纪60年代相同，并且可以稳妥地假设，它们将继续让研究人员忙碌一段时间。这些目标包括寻找具有强磁电耦合的新材料，这可能增加已知室温多铁性材料非常有限的数量¹⁴⁹。特别是，目前尚未知晓具有显著且强耦合的自发磁化和极化的室温多铁性材料。驱动多铁性的新机制可能会被发现，但现有机制远未被充分利用。例如，可能存在多种获得能够稳定非本征铁电态的磁序的方法。与现有的自旋驱动铁电体相比，这可能导致固有地更高的有序化温度和极化强度——预测和初步发现是有希望的⁴²'⁴⁴。其他可能承载多铁性态但代表性不足的材料类别包括非氧化物化合物¹⁵⁰和有机材料¹⁵¹。一种多铁性器件，其中磁化强度由电场控制，最好在低电压、室温下实现超快切换，这仍然是一个主要目标¹⁵²⁻¹⁵⁵。迄今为止取得的主要成就是：在BiFeO₃–CoFe异质结构中演示了可重复的室温电场驱动磁化翻转⁶⁴，以及实现了在低温下工作的多铁性四态存储器¹⁵⁶。这些是多铁性材料集成到器件中的重要步骤，但关键方面，如这些器件概念的动力学、可靠性和疲劳特性，仍需优化，以发展出具有竞争力的技术。除此之外，已经提出了利用自旋极化电流施加的自旋转移矩¹⁵⁷或自旋轨道矩¹⁵⁸'¹⁵⁹来控制磁性的替代方案，任何多铁性器件都需要在功能性和性能上与之竞争。 **spin torque**: 自旋转移矩，指自旋极化电流通过一个磁性层时，其自旋角动量转移给局域磁矩，从而对磁矩施加一个力矩，使其发生翻转或进动的效应。

> Multiferroic thin films and heterostructures hold the greatest potential for device applications59. First, they may lead to new types of systems combining magnetic and electric longrange order. Multiferroicity may originate under strain, confinement or gradient effects within one material, as well as at or across the interface between different materials. All of these possibilities represent degrees of freedom that so far have been only marginally explored. Second, heterostructures with strongly coupled ferroelectric and ferromagnetic layers may be used to build a magnetoelectric memristor. In this kind of device, an electric field sets the ferroelectric layer polarization state, which is then transferred to the magnetic layer, where it defines the memristive state through coupling to an adjacent ferromagnetic reference layer. Third, multilayer heterostructures with an integral symmetry that is different from the symmetry of their individual constituents may be assembled. This integral symmetry may even be switchable by, for example, reversing the order parameter of only one of the layers constituting the heterostructure. This was conceptually demonstrated160 by layer selective polarization orientation of trilayer heterostructures such as PbZr0.2Ti0.8O3–La0.7Sr0.3MnO3PbZr0.2Ti0.8O3. By setting the relative polarization of the outer layers, the integral spaceinversion symmetry of the trilayer structure is activated and deactivated on demand. Accordingly, magneticfield poling of outer magnetically ordered layers could switch time reversal symmetry on or off. These symmetrytunable multilayer assemblies constitute a powerful option to increase the number of systems with interesting and controllable magnetoelectric coupling effects. Fourth, the focus on domain walls will grow stronger, because it is an advantage to work with oxide interfaces that can be created, shifted and annihilated postgrowth (in contrast to conventional interfaces that, once grown, cannot be modified). Controllable domain walls could be the key to electricfieldcontrolled racetrack memories161. An electric field may act on the magnetic domain wall and move it, exploiting the magnetoelectric effect. Alternatively, if the domain wall is multiferroic, the electric field may act directly on the wall polarization. As in the bulk compounds, these concepts may be extended to nonoxide thin films. Another element that might have an important role for future applications of multiferroic materials is skyrmions — magnetic whirls that were first observed in halfmetallic systems162 and that were detected in multiferroic insulators163. In multiferroic insulators, skyrmions are localized and electric currents — the usual tool to manipulate them — cannot flow, but the coexistence with ferroic order and the possibility of establishing magnetoelectric control of the skyrmions are worth further exploration. For example, skyrmions in systems with acentric spinspiral order (instead of an acentric crystal structure) may provide a route to giant, locally controllable magnetoelectric interactions. 多铁性薄膜和异质结构在器件应用方面具有最大的潜力⁵⁹。首先，它们可能催生结合磁性和电性长程序的新型体系。多铁性可能源于单一材料内的应变、限域或梯度效应，也可能源于不同材料之间的界面处或跨界面。所有这些可能性都代表了迄今为止仅被有限探索的自由度。其次，具有强耦合铁电层和铁磁层的异质结构可用于构建磁电忆阻器。在这种器件中，电场设定铁电层的极化态，然后将其传递到磁性层，磁性层通过与相邻铁磁参考层的耦合来定义忆阻态。第三，可以组装具有整体对称性不同于其各组分对称性的多层异质结构。这种整体对称性甚至可能是可切换的，例如，通过翻转构成异质结构的仅一层的序参量。这在概念上得到了演示¹⁶⁰，通过对三层异质结构（如PbZr₀.₂Ti₀.₈O₃–La₀.₇Sr₀.₃MnO₃–PbZr₀.₂Ti₀.₈O₃）进行层选择性极化取向。通过设定外层的相对极化，三层结构的整体空间反演对称性可以按需激活和去激活。相应地，对外部磁有序层进行磁场极化可以开启或关闭时间反演对称性。这些对称性可调的多层组件构成了一个强有力的选项，以增加具有有趣且可控磁电耦合效应的体系数量。第四，对畴壁的关注将更加强烈，因为使用可以在生长后创建、移动和湮灭的氧化物界面是一个优势（与一旦生长后就无法修改的传统界面相反）。可控的畴壁可能是电场控制赛道存储器的关键¹⁶¹。电场可以作用于磁畴壁并利用磁电效应移动它。或者，如果畴壁是多铁性的，电场可以直接作用于畴壁的极化。与块材化合物一样，这些概念可以扩展到非氧化物薄膜。另一个可能在未来多铁性材料应用中发挥重要作用的元素是斯格明子——一种磁涡旋，最初在半金属体系¹⁶²中观察到，后来在多铁性绝缘体¹⁶³中被探测到。在多铁性绝缘体中，斯格明子是局域化的，而电流——操控它们的常用工具——无法流动，但与铁性序共存以及建立对斯格明子的磁电控制的可能性，值得进一步探索。例如，在具有无中心自旋螺旋序（而非无中心晶体结构）的体系中的斯格明子，可能提供一条通向巨、可局域控制的磁电相互作用的途径。 **memristor**: 忆阻器，一种具有记忆功能的非线性二端无源电子元件，其电阻值取决于流过它的电荷量。 **racetrack memories**: 赛道存储器，一种非易失性存储设备，利用电流驱动磁畴壁在纳米线中运动来存储信息。

> The field of multiferroics also has a prominent role in promoting cooperation between formerly disjoint research disciplines — systems with strong magnetoelectric interactions are appealing for different fields. Moreover, bringing together two types of order in one material is an inherently interdisciplinary effort. For example, it is no longer sufficient to track the thickness of a thin film during growth; continuous control of its emerging multiple ferroic properties is also required. The combination of deposition techniques with in situ electro and magnetooptical spectroscopy could be very effective to this end. In multiferroic research, dynamical phenomena are still a highly underrated topic. With magnetoelectric switching as one of the declared goals of the field, more attention must be paid to the temporal evolution of these reorientations. One important aspect is the speed of the reversal of the order parameter, which has to occur within picoseconds if memory applications are envisaged. In this respect, alloptical control164 of a multiferroic state may be a particularly rewarding goal. On the other hand, the reversal needs to be highly reproducible at the level of individual domains if magnetoelectric switches, sensors or transducers are considered. Apart from establishing a onetoone association between the orientation of a magnetic order parameter and the orientation of an electric field, more complex types of magnetoelectric control may be taken into consideration. If, for example, a multiferroic state is characterized by three or more order parameters, it might be possible to invert an entire distribution of magnetic domains; the domain pattern remains unchanged but in each individual domain the order parameter is reversed. The inversion of an inhomogeneous physical state has a great technological relevance; for example, it is at the basis of the spinecho effect in nuclear magnetic resonance tomography165 and of active noise reduction166. In conclusion, even though the field of multiferroics has reached some maturity after more than 50 years of research, it is still far from being full grown. Its twists and turns continuously lead to the exploration of new systems and effects, and it is possible that the most exciting results and discoveries have not yet been realized. 多铁性领域在促进原本不相连的研究学科之间的合作方面也扮演着突出角色——具有强磁电相互作用的体系对不同的领域都具有吸引力。此外，将两种类型的序集合在一种材料中，本身就是一项固有的跨学科努力。例如，在薄膜生长过程中，仅仅追踪薄膜的厚度已不再足够；还需要对其新兴的多重铁性性质进行连续控制。将沉积技术与原位电学和磁光光谱学相结合，对此目标可能非常有效。在多铁性研究中，动力学现象仍然是一个被高度低估的主题。由于磁电切换是该领域宣称的目标之一，必须更多地关注这些重新取向的时间演化。一个重要的方面是序参量翻转的速度，如果设想用于存储应用，这必须在皮秒内发生。在这方面，多铁性态的全光控制¹⁶⁴可能是一个特别有价值的目标。另一方面，如果考虑磁电开关、传感器或换能器，翻转需要在单个畴的层面上具有高度可重复性。除了在磁序参量的取向和电场取向之间建立直接关联之外，还可以考虑更复杂的磁电控制类型。例如，如果一个多铁性态由三个或更多序参量表征，也许有可能反转整个磁畴分布；畴图案保持不变，但在每个单独畴中，序参量反转。非均匀物理态的反转具有重大的技术意义；例如，它是核磁共振断层成像中的自旋回波效应¹⁶⁵和有源降噪¹⁶⁶的基础。总之，尽管多铁性领域在50多年的研究后已达到一定的成熟度，但它远未完全成熟。其曲折历程不断引领着对新体系和新效应的探索，而且最激动人心的结果和发现或许尚未实现。

> # Box 1 | Important terms and definitions in the field of multiferroics
> 
> # 方框1 | 多铁性领域的重要术语和定义
> 
> ## Ferroic
> 
> ## 铁性
> 
> Ferroic materials display long-range order with respect to at least one macroscopic property, and they develop domains that can be switched by a conjugate field108,109, as shown in the figure. Occasionally, the term primary ferroic167 is used to indicate ferromagnetic, ferroelectric, ferroelastic and ferrotoroidic order. 铁性材料至少在一项宏观性质上显示出长程序，并且它们会产生可由共轭场切换的畴¹⁰⁸'¹⁰⁹，如图所示。有时，术语“初级铁性体”¹⁶⁷被用来指代铁磁性、铁电性、铁弹性和铁磁涡旋性序。 **ferroic**: 铁性，材料的一种性质，指在无外场时存在自发有序态，且该有序态可在其共轭外场作用下滞后切换。
> 
> ## Magnetoelectric
> 
> ## 磁电
> 
> Originally, only materials in which a magnetic (electric) field induces a proportional polarization (magnetization) were referred to as linear magnetoelectrics, as seen in the figure (the prefix linear, which is often omitted, distinguishes this effect from higher-order effects with nonlinear relations). Multiferroics with magnetic and electric order do not necessarily permit the linear magnetoelectric effect (for example, hexagonal YMnO3), and not all materials displaying the linear magnetoelectric effect are multiferroic (for example, Cr2O3). Nowadays, the term magnetoelectric usually refers nonspecifically to any type of coupling between magnetic and electric properties. 最初，只有那些磁场（电场）能诱导出成比例的极化（磁化）的材料才被称为线性磁电体，如图所示（前缀“线性”经常被省略，以将此效应与具有非线性关系的高阶效应区分开来）。具有磁序和电序的多铁性材料不一定表现出线性磁电效应（例如，六方YMnO₃），并且并非所有表现出线性磁电效应的材料都是多铁性的（例如，Cr₂O₃）。如今，术语“磁电”通常非特指地指磁性和电性之间任何类型的耦合。
> 
> ## Multiferroic
> 
> ## 多铁性
> 
> When introduced10, the term multiferroic referred to materials with a coexistence of two or more primary ferroic orders in the same phase. Its present use is different; it indicates a coexistence of ferroelectric and ferro-, ferri- or antiferromagnetic order in single- or even multiphase materials. The single-phase compounds are sometimes more specifically referred to as magnetoelectric multiferroics in anticipation of a behaviour as seen in the figure. If the magnetic and ferroelectric orders occur independently, the multiferroic material is denoted as type I. If the ferroelectric and magnetic transitions emerge jointly, the multiferroic is of type II. The two types are depicted in the figure. Note that the term multiferroic prevailed from about the year 2000, whereas terms like ferroelectromagnetic were in use before. 在最初引入时¹⁰，术语“多铁性”指的是在同一相中同时存在两种或两种以上初级铁性序的材料。它目前的用法不同；它指的是在单相甚至多相材料中，铁电序与铁磁序、亚铁磁序或反铁磁序的共存。单相化合物有时更具体地被称为磁电多铁性材料，以预期图中所示的行为。如果磁序和铁电序独立发生，则该多铁性材料被指定为第I类。如果铁电相变和磁相变联合出现，则该多铁性材料属于第II类。这两种类型如图所示。请注意，“多铁性”一词大约从2000年开始流行，而在此之前使用的术语是“铁电磁体”等。 **ferri-**: 亚铁磁，指材料内部磁矩反平行排列但大小不等，导致存在净磁矩的磁性状态。
> 
> ## Magnetodielectric
> 
> ## 磁介电
> 
> This term denominates a material in which the dielectric function, ε, changes in a magnetic field. Because the electrical capacitance, C, is proportional to ε, the term magnetocapacitive is also used. In contrast to the linear magnetoelectric effect, which parameterizes a well-defined relation between magnetic and electric fields, the magnetodielectric effect represents a response function for which this relation is ambiguous. 该术语指代介电函数ε在磁场中发生变化的材料。由于电容C与ε成正比，因此也使用术语“磁电容”。与参数化磁和电场之间明确定义关系的线性磁电效应相比，磁介电效应代表了一种响应函数，这种关系对于该函数是模糊的。
> 
> ## Domains and domain walls
> 
> ## 畴和畴壁
> 
> Domains are regions with a uniform orientation of the relevant order parameter: for example, the polarization or the magnetization. At least two orientations of the order parameter (domain states) are allowed for any ferroic material; thus, a typical ferroic material consists of multiple domains, each representing one of the allowed domain states. The interfaces between domains are called domain walls. They can have a width ranging from less than 1 nm to more than 100 nm. They denote the region across which the order parameter reorients between adjacent domains. 畴是具有相关序参量（例如电极化或磁化强度）均匀取向的区域。对于任何铁性材料，至少允许序参量有两种取向（畴态）；因此，典型的铁性材料由多个畴组成，每个畴代表一种允许的畴态。畴之间的界面称为畴壁。它们的宽度范围可以从小于1纳米到超过100纳米。它们表示序参量在相邻畴之间重新取向的区域。
>

> 
> -   **铁电性（Ferroelectricity）**：刚才说了，就是材料内部正负电荷中心不重合，能自发形成正负极，并且这个正负极的方向还能被电场翻转。
> -   **多铁性材料（Multiferroic）**：能同时拥有铁磁性和铁电性的材料，更宽泛地说，是同时拥有磁性和电性有序状态的材料。
> -   **磁电耦合（Magnetoelectric Coupling）**：这是多铁性材料的核心魔法。就是它的“磁性”特性和“电性”特性能互相影响。你用**电场**能改变它的**磁性**，或者反过来，用**磁场**能改变它的**电性**。文章里主要想实现的就是“用电场控制磁性”。
> -   **畴（Domain）和畴壁（Domain Wall）**：咱们可以想象一个大操场，里面站满了人。拿铁磁体来说，如果所有人都朝一个方向看，那就是一个“单畴”。但通常情况下，材料里会分成很多个“小区域”，每个区域里的人朝一个方向看，但不同区域的方向可能不同。这些个小区域就叫“**畴**”。分隔这些区域的边界，就是“**畴壁**”。多铁性材料里既有磁畴，也有电畴，它们之间的互动是研究的关键。
> -   **钙钛矿（Perovskite）**：一种非常经典和重要的晶体结构，像是一个搭积木的万能基座，很多功能材料都是这个结构。文章里说，最初想在这个结构里把磁性和电性结合起来，结果发现一个大矛盾，导致研究走了弯路。
> -   **第一类多铁和第二类多铁（Type I & Type II Multiferroics）**：这就像两个出身不同的冠军。
>     -   **第一类**：磁性和电性来源不同，各管各的，只是碰巧住在了同一个材料里。虽然住一起，但关系不铁。
>     -   **第二类**：电性的产生，完全是磁性的“功劳”，是由一种特殊的磁性排列“诱导”出来的。它俩是天生的连体婴，关系非常紧密，是科学家们更想要的强力耦合类型。
> -   **自旋（Spin）**：您可以把它想象成电子自身带的一个微小的、自带“N”和“S”极的小磁针。材料的磁性，就来源于这些无数个小磁针的排列方式。
> -   **反铁磁（Antiferromagnetic）**：就是材料里的小磁针，一个朝上，一个朝下，一个朝上，一个朝下，完全抵消了，对外不显磁性。目前发现的很多多铁材料都是反铁磁，这不太利于应用，因为不好读出来。所以这也是一个难题。
> -   **薄膜异质结构（Thin-film Heterostructures）**：这不是一种材料，而是一种“手艺活”。像做三明治一样，一层一层地把不同的材料铺上去，每层可能只有几个原子那么厚。通过这种“组合”和“挤压”，能创造出自然界没有的、性能更好的新材料。
> -   **非平衡态动力学（Non-equilibrium Dynamics）**：研究材料在受到外界刺激（比如一个电脉冲）后，它内部的磁性和电性是怎么随时间变化的，翻转得有多快。这直接关系到未来的存储器件能有多快。
