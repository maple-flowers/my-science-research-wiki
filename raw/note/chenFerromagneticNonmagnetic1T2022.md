^KEYannotati

> <span class="highlight" style="background-color: #5fb236">The charge density wave (CDW) states of two-dimensional transition metal dichalcogenides (TMDs) originate from intrinsic couplings between the electronic structures and lattice distortion, inducing interesting physical and chemical properties. The observed TMDs CDW states are mostly nonmagnetic (NM) but with a few ferromagnetic (FM) cases. Physical mechanisms for the formation of FM CDW remain elusive. In this paper, we used density functional theory calculations to study a set of TMDs with magnetic transition metal elements (e.g., V, Cr, and Mn). We found that the FM state can stem from the direct exchange to superexchange transition (e.g., CrX2) or the M-M (M is the metal atom) dimerization (e.g., MnX2). A crystal structure distortion index is proposed to distinguish the different formation mechanisms of FM CDW states. Interestingly, CrX2 has both NM and FM CDW states, which is not observed in other TMDs materials. We found that charge (electron or hole) doping could modulate the different formation mechanisms and induce a phase transition between these two CDW states in CrS2, leading to significant actuation strain output (i.e., 12.17% and 5.93% along x and y directions, respectively) and drastic change of magnetism, which could enable some multifunctionality applications of TMD materials.</span>
> 
> 二维过渡金属二硫属化物（TMD）的电荷密度波（CDW）态源于电子结构和晶格畸变之间的内在耦合，从而产生有趣的物理和化学性质。观察到的 TMD CDW 状态大部分是非磁性 (NM)，但也有少数铁磁性 (FM) 情况。 FM CDW 形成的物理机制仍然难以捉摸。在本文中，我们使用密度泛函理论计算来研究一组具有磁性过渡金属元素（例如 V、Cr 和 Mn）的 TMD。我们发现FM态可以源于直接交换到超交换转变（例如CrX2）或M-M（M是金属原子）二聚化（例如MnX2）。提出了晶体结构畸变指数来区分 FM CDW 态的不同形成机制。有趣的是，CrX2 同时具有 NM 和 FM CDW 态，这是其他 TMD 材料中未观察到的。我们发现电荷（电子或空穴）掺杂可以调节不同的形成机制并诱导 CrS2 中这两种 CDW 态之间的相变，从而导致显着的驱动应变输出（即沿 x 和 y 方向分别为 12.17% 和 5.93%）和磁性的剧烈变化，这可以实现 TMD 材料的一些多功能应用。【👈 译】 ([p1](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LUBGRKES?page=1&annotation=Q7K82XBP))

> <span class="highlight" style="background-color: #e56eee">The excellent electronic, magnetic, and chemical properties of two-dimensional (2D) transition metal dichalcogenides (TMDs) have attracted extensive research, considering their great potential for next-generation electronic, optoelectronics, and electromechanical devices [1–5]. The various superior physical and chemical properties of the TMD family can be attributed to their chemical diversity and their multiple stable crystal structures. For example, the VI group Mo/W TMDs has the thermodynamically stable 2H phase as semiconductors; meanwhile, these materials show metallic character after transits to 1T or 1T′ phase through strain, charge doping, electron beam radiation, alloying and many other methods [6–12]. These multiple properties in a single piece of TMD monolayer are the foundation for the lateral heterostructure designs in electronic and spintronic devices [13–15].</span>
> 
> 二维（2D）过渡金属二硫属化物（TMD）优异的电子、磁性和化学性质吸引了广泛的研究，考虑到它们在下一代电子、光电子和机电设备方面的巨大潜力[1-5]。 TMD家族的各种优异的物理和化学性质可归因于其化学多样性和多种稳定的晶体结构。例如，VI族Mo/W TMDs具有作为半导体的热力学稳定的2H相；同时，这些材料通过应变、电荷掺杂、电子束辐射、合金化等多种方法转变为1T或1T′相后表现出金属特性[6-12]。单片TMD单层的这些多重特性是电子和自旋电子器件中横向异质结构设计的基础[13-15]。【👈 译】 ([p1](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LUBGRKES?page=1&annotation=Q2UYKBSS))

> <span class="highlight" style="background-color: #aaaaaa">Our DFT calculations were conducted by using the Vienna ab initio simulation package (VASP) [22]. The spin-polarized Perdew-Burke-Ernzerhof (PBE) exchange-correlation functional [23] and projector augmented wave method [24] were adopted. The cutoff energy of 400 eV and the Monkhorst-Pack k-point mesh [25] of 15 × 11 × 1 for 1T′ MX2 primitive unit cell and 1 × √3 × 1 supercell of 1T and 1T′ phase. Atomic positions and lattice constants were fully relaxed until the total energy difference and forces were less than 104 eV and 0.005 eV/Å, respectively. A higher convergence criterion for total energy, 106 eV, was adopted to perform the selfconsistent calculation to obtain electronic structures. Periodic boundary condition was applied in all three directions. In addition, a 20 Å vacuum space was applied along z directions to avoid the interaction between two periodic images. To search the nonmagnetic and ferromagnetic 1T′ CDW phases, we set the initial magnetic moments: either zero or 5 μB, on metal atoms in each structure with small and large lattice constants, respectively. Note that the 1T′ NM and FM states have significantly different lattice constants. After complete relaxation in DFT calculations, the stable 1T′ NM and FM CDW could be obtained. The climbing image nudged elastic band (Cl-NEB) calculations [26] were performed to estimate the energy barriers for NM-FM phase transitions.</span>
> 
> 我们的 DFT 计算是使用 Vienna ab initio 模拟包 (VASP) [22] 进行的。采用自旋偏振Perdew-Burke-Ernzerhof (PBE)交换相关函数[23]和投影增强波方法[24]。 1T′ MX2 原始晶胞和 1T 和 1T′ 相的 1 × √3 × 1 超晶胞的截止能量为 400 eV，Monkhorst-Pack k 点网格 [25] 为 15 × 11 × 1。原子位置和晶格常数完全松弛，直到总能量差和力分别小于 104 eV 和 0.005 eV/Å。采用较高的总能量收敛准则106 eV进行自洽计算以获得电子结构。在所有三个方向上应用周期性边界条件。此外，沿 z 方向应用 20 Å 真空空间，以避免两个周期图像之间的相互作用。为了搜索非磁性和铁磁性 1T' CDW 相，我们分别在具有小晶格常数和大晶格常数的结构中的金属原子上设置初始磁矩：0 或 5 μB。请注意，1T' NM 和 FM 态具有显着不同的晶格常数。在DFT计算完全松弛后，可以获得稳定的1T′NM和FM CDW。进行爬升图像微移弹性带 (Cl-NEB) 计算 [26] 来估计 NM-FM 相变的能垒。【👈 译】 ([p2](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LUBGRKES?page=2&annotation=4TB9BG9M))

> 
> 二维TMDs的1T′相因其丰富的物理性质备受关注。大多数1T′ CDW态是非磁性的，其形成由派尔斯不稳定性驱动的M-M二聚化解释。但少数FM-CDW态的发现提出了一个新的科学问题：**铁磁性是如何在CDW畸变中产生并稳定存在的？** 理解其微观机制对设计新型自旋电子器件至关重要。

> #### B. FM-CDW的形成机制
> 
> 这是本文的核心理论贡献。通过分析态密度、分波电荷密度和原子磁矩，作者阐明了两种截然不同的机制：
> 
> -   **机制一：直接交换→超交换转变（适用于CrX₂，VTe₂）**。在1T相中，磁性由M-M直接交换作用主导，呈现AFM。在1T′ FM相中，M-X-M键角接近90°，且X原子上诱导出与M原子反平行的微小磁矩，这符合GKA规则下超交换作用驱动FM耦合的特征。此时，M-M二聚键消失。
> -   **机制二：M-M二聚化（适用于MnX₂）**。MnX₂的1T′ FM相中，依然可以观察到明显的Mn-Mn二聚键，这与传统NM-CDW的形成机制类似。但由于Mn原子d电子更多，二聚化并未完全消除其磁矩，因此保留了FM态。这一机制对应着更小的畸变指数（d₁/d₂ < 0.7）。

> #### D. 电荷掺杂诱导的可逆相变

> ## 八、用户问题预测与解答
> 
> **问题1：什么是电荷密度波（CDW）？它与1T′相是什么关系？** **答**：CDW是一种量子现象，常发生在低维材料中。指材料中电子的电荷密度在空间中发生周期性调制，呈现波状分布。为了屏蔽这种电荷密度的不均匀性，晶格原子也会发生周期性位移（即晶格畸变）。1T′相的形成就是这种电子-声子耦合的结果：高对称1T相的电子结构不稳定（派尔斯不稳定性），导致晶格发生畸变，同时电荷密度重新分布，形成CDW。因此，1T′相是CDW的衍生结构之一。
> 
> **问题2：直接交换和超交换作用有什么根本区别？** **答**：这两种都是解释固体中磁序的微观机制。
> 
> -   **直接交换**：发生在**直接相邻**的磁性原子之间，电子云直接重叠，交换作用很强。通常，根据原子间距和电子云重叠程度，可以导致铁磁或反铁磁耦合。

> # I. INTRODUCTION
> 
> # 一、引言
> 
> The excellent electronic, magnetic, and chemical properties of two-dimensional (2D) transition metal dichalcogenides (TMDs) have attracted extensive research, considering their great potential for next-generation electronic, optoelectronics, and electromechanical devices [1–5]. The various superior physical and chemical properties of the TMD family can be attributed to their chemical diversity and their multiple stable crystal structures. For example, the VI group Mo/W TMDs has the thermodynamically stable 2H phase as semiconductors; meanwhile, these materials show metallic character after transits to 1T or 1T′ phase through strain, charge doping, electron beam radiation, alloying and many other methods [6–12]. These multiple properties in a single piece of TMD monolayer are the foundation for the lateral heterostructure designs in electronic and spintronic devices [13–15]. The low symmetry 1T′ phase, observed in many TMDs, can be viewed as a distortion from the high-symmetry 1T phase. The periodic lattice distortion modulates the charge density and leads to the charge density wave (CDW) superstructures. These CDW states show fantastic properties in experiments and theoretical studies, which expand the TMD applications to heterogeneous catalysis, superconductor, ferromagnetism, superelasticity and shape memory effect [16–18]. At present, most of the reported 1T′ CDW phases are nonmagnetic (NM). The prototypical examples are the well-known MoX₂ and WX₂ (X is the chalcogenide atoms). The formation of such NM-CDW phases has usually been attributed to the Peierls distortion. The formation of zigzag M-M dimer chains in the 1T′ phase stabilizes the distorted structure [2]. Based on the understanding, it has been demonstrated that charge doping could modulate the Perierls distortion to induce a reversible phase transition between the 1T′ and 1T phase, leading to the superelasticity and shape memory effects [17]. In contrast, the reported 1T′ FM-CDW phases are rare, e.g., 1T′ CrX₂ [19,20]. The coupling of FM and CDW enables exciting properties that are not observed in the NM-CDW counterparts, e.g., the recently proposed strain-controlled 2D TMDs heterostructure-based spin valves [21]. However, the physical mechanisms for the formation remain elusive. Thus, there is a need to explore more 1T′ FM-CDW phases and gain an in-depth understanding of their formation mechanisms to unleash their promising potentials in spintronics applications. In this work, we performed density functional theory (DFT) calculations for a set of TMD MX₂ materials with magnetic transition metal elements (i.e., M = V, Cr, and Mn and X = S, Se, and Te). We systematically study their crystal structures and electronic structures to unravel the formation mechanisms of the 1T′ FM-CDW states. 二维过渡金属二硫族化合物优异的电子、磁性和化学性质因其在下一代电子、光电子和机电设备中的巨大潜力而吸引了广泛的研究[1–5]。TMD家族各种优越的物理和化学性质可归因于其化学多样性和多种稳定的晶体结构。例如，第VI族Mo/W TMDs具有热力学稳定的2H半导体相；同时，这些材料通过应变、电荷掺杂、电子束辐照、合金化及许多其他方法转变为1T或1T′相后表现出金属特性[6–12]。单片TMD单层中的这些多重性质是电子和自旋电子器件中横向异质结构设计的基础[13–15]。在许多TMDs中观察到的低对称性1T′相可视为高对称性1T相的畸变。周期性晶格畸变调制电荷密度并导致电荷密度波超结构。这些CDW态在实验和理论研究中显示出奇妙的性质，将TMD的应用扩展到异相催化、超导体、铁磁性、超弹性和形状记忆效应[16–18]。目前，大多数报道的1T′ CDW相是非磁性的。原型例子是众所周知的MoX₂和WX₂（X为硫族原子）。此类NM-CDW相的形成通常归因于Peierls畸变。1T′相中锯齿形M-M二聚体链的形成稳定了畸变结构[2]。基于这一理解，已有研究表明电荷掺杂可以调控Peierls畸变，诱导1T′和1T相之间的可逆相变，从而产生超弹性和形状记忆效应[17]。相比之下，报道的1T′ FM-CDW相很少，例如1T′ CrX₂ [19,20]。铁磁与CDW的耦合使得在NM-CDW对应物中未观察到的令人兴奋的性质成为可能，例如最近提出的应变控制的基于二维TMDs异质结构的自旋阀[21]。然而，其形成的物理机制仍不清楚。因此，需要探索更多的1T′ FM-CDW相并深入理解其形成机制，以释放其在自旋电子学应用中的潜力。本工作中，我们对一组含磁性过渡金属元素（即M = V、Cr和Mn，X = S、Se和Te）的TMD MX₂材料进行了密度泛函理论计算。我们系统地研究了它们的晶体结构和电子结构，以揭示1T′ FM-CDW态的形成机制。
> 
> **Peierls Distortion**: 佩尔斯畸变，一维金属中电子-声子耦合导致晶格周期性畸变并打开能隙的现象。 **Heterostructure**: 异质结构，由两种或多种不同材料组成的复合结构，在界面处具有独特的电子性质。 **Spin Valve**: 自旋阀，一种利用巨磁阻效应工作的器件，其电阻依赖于两个铁磁层磁化方向的相对取向。
> 
> The transition from direct-exchange interaction in the antiferromagnetic (AFM) state to the superexchange interaction leads to the 1T′ FM-CDW state for 1T′ CrX₂ and VTe₂. However, for MnX₂, its 1T′ FM-CDW states result from the Mn-Mn dimerization. In the end, we will also show that the charge doping can induce a reversible phase transition between FM-CDW and NM-CDW CrS₂, leading to a large activation strain output and magnetic order changing. 从反铁磁态中的直接交换相互作用向超交换相互作用的转变导致了1T′ CrX₂和VTe₂的1T′ FM-CDW态。然而，对于MnX₂，其1T′ FM-CDW态源于Mn-Mn二聚化。最后，我们还将展示电荷掺杂可以诱导FM-CDW和NM-CDW CrS₂之间的可逆相变，导致大的驱动应变输出和磁有序变化。
> 
> **Antiferromagnetic (AFM)**: 反铁磁性，相邻原子磁矩反平行排列，宏观上不表现净磁化的磁有序状态。 **Direct Exchange**: 直接交换，相邻磁性原子之间通过波函数直接重叠产生的磁相互作用。
> 
> # II. COMPUTATIONAL METHODS
> 
> # 二、计算方法
> 
> Our DFT calculations were conducted by using the Vienna ab initio simulation package (VASP) [22]. The spin-polarized Perdew-Burke-Ernzerhof (PBE) exchange-correlation functional [23] and projector augmented wave method [24] were adopted. The cutoff energy of 400 eV and the Monkhorst-Pack k-point mesh [25] of 15 × 11 × 1 for 1T′ MX₂ primitive unit cell and 1 × √3 × 1 supercell of 1T and 1T′ phase. Atomic positions and lattice constants were fully relaxed until the total energy difference and forces were less than 10⁻⁴ eV and 0.005 eV/Å, respectively. A higher convergence criterion for total energy, 10⁻⁶ eV, was adopted to perform the self-consistent calculation to obtain electronic structures. Periodic boundary condition was applied in all three directions. In addition, a 20 Å vacuum space was applied along z directions to avoid the interaction between two periodic images. To search the nonmagnetic and ferromagnetic 1T′ CDW phases, we set the initial magnetic moments: either zero or 5 μB, on metal atoms in each structure with small and large lattice constants, respectively. Note that the 1T′ NM and FM states have significantly different lattice constants. After complete relaxation in DFT calculations, the stable 1T′ NM and FM CDW could be obtained. The climbing image nudged elastic band (Cl-NEB) calculations [26] were performed to estimate the energy barriers for NM-FM phase transitions. 我们的DFT计算使用维也纳从头算模拟软件包进行[22]。采用了自旋极化的Perdew-Burke-Ernzerhof交换关联泛函[23]和投影缀加波方法[24]。截断能设为400 eV，对于1T′ MX₂原胞使用15 × 11 × 1的Monkhorst-Pack k点网格[25]，对于1T和1T′相使用1 × √3 × 1的超胞。原子位置和晶格常数完全弛豫，直到总能量差和力分别小于10⁻⁴ eV和0.005 eV/Å。采用更高的总能量收敛标准10⁻⁶ eV进行自洽计算以获得电子结构。在所有三个方向上施加周期性边界条件。此外，沿z方向施加20 Å的真空层以避免两个周期镜像之间的相互作用。为搜索非磁性和铁磁性1T′ CDW相，我们分别在具有小晶格常数和大晶格常数的每个结构中的金属原子上设置初始磁矩：零或5 μB。注意1T′ NM和FM态具有显著不同的晶格常数。在DFT计算中完全弛豫后，可以获得稳定的1T′ NM和FM CDW。进行了爬坡图像微动弹性带计算[26]来估计NM-FM相变的能量势垒。
> 
> **VASP**: 维也纳从头算模拟软件包，一种广泛使用的基于平面波基组和赝势方法的DFT计算程序。 **PBE Functional**: PBE泛函，Perdew、Burke和Ernzerhof提出的广义梯度近似交换关联泛函。 **Projector Augmented Wave (PAW) Method**: 投影缀加波方法，一种结合赝势和全电子计算优势的方法。 **Climbing Image Nudged Elastic Band (Cl-NEB)**: 爬坡图像微动弹性带方法，用于寻找最小能量路径和鞍点的计算方法。
>

The ratio d₁/d₂ will be termed as structure distortion index in this work. For d₁/d₂ close to 0.8, the 1T′ CDW phases have the FM magnetic order, while for d₁/d₂ close to 0.7, they show the NM order. The lattice constants are also very different. > ## B. The physical mechanisms for the formation of 1T′ FM-CDW state
> 
> ## B. 1T′ FM-CDW态形成的物理机制
> 
> The multiple 1T′ FM-CDW states identified in the previous section, particularly the coexistence of FM and NM CDW states in CrS₂, offer the opportunity to understand the formation mechanism of the 1T′ FM phases. We calculated the band structure and electron local function (ELF) of 1T, 1T′-NM and 1T′-FM state of CrS₂ in Fig. S5 [29]. From its band structure, the 1T CrS₂ shows a metallic character. One band continuously passes the Fermi level at the X point, suggesting the well-known Peierls instability. After the phase transition to 1T′ NM-CDW, a band gap opens at the X point in Fig. S5(b) [29]. 前一节中识别的多种1T′ FM-CDW态，特别是CrS₂中FM和NM CDW态的共存，为理解1T′ FM相的形成机制提供了机会。我们在图S5[29]中计算了CrS₂的1T、1T′-NM和1T′-FM态的能带结构和电子局域函数。从其能带结构来看，1T CrS₂显示金属特征。一条能带在X点连续穿过费米能级，表明众所周知的Peierls不稳定性。在相变为1T′ NM-CDW后，图S5(b)[29]中在X点打开了一个带隙。
> 
> **Electron Local Function (ELF)**: 电子局域函数，一种用于可视化电子局域化程度的实空间函数，有助于识别化学键类型。 **Fermi Level**: 费米能级，在绝对零度下电子占据的最高能级。
> 
> Figure 3(a) depicts the density of state (DOS) of 1T′ NM-CDW CrS₂. The partial charge density of the pronounced DOS peak around the Fermi level at the range of −1.5 ∼ 0 eV is shown in Fig. 3(a). The strong dimer bonds can be observed between the neighboring Cr-Cr atoms along the y direction. In addition, the ELF results in Fig. S5(b) confirm the formation of Cr-Cr dimer bonds. [29]. The dimer bonds can also be observed in the 1T′-NM CrSe₂ and CrTe₂ [Figs. S6(a) and S7(a)]. [29]. These results are consistent with other VI group TMDs, such as the 1T′-MoS₂ and 1T′-MoTe₂ [2,16]. The formation of M-M dimer bonds is the physical mechanism to stabilize the observed 1T′ NM-CDW states in CrX₂, as summarized in Fig. 3(c) (left plot). 图3(a)描绘了1T′ NM-CDW CrS₂的态密度。费米能级附近−1.5 ∼ 0 eV范围内显著DOS峰的部分电荷密度显示于图3(a)中。可以在沿y方向的相邻Cr-Cr原子之间观察到强的二聚体键。此外，图S5(b)中的ELF结果证实了Cr-Cr二聚体键的形成[29]。在1T′-NM CrSe₂和CrTe₂[图S6(a)和S7(a)]中也可以观察到二聚体键[29]。这些结果与其他第VI族TMDs一致，如1T′-MoS₂和1T′-MoTe₂ [2,16]。M-M二聚体键的形成是稳定CrX₂中观测到的1T′ NM-CDW态的物理机制，如图3(c)（左图）所总结。
> 
> **Density of States (DOS)**: 态密度，描述在给定能量区间内电子态的数目，是分析材料电子结构的重要工具。
> 
> The Cr-Cr dimer bonds are not observed in the 1T′-FM CrS₂ phase through the partial charge density within the same energy range in Fig. 3(b) and from the ELF result in Fig. S5(c) [29]. There should be other mechanisms to stabilize the 1T′-FM phase. It is known that the AFM magnetic order of 1T arises from direct exchanging interaction between Cr atoms [21,30]. For the 1T′ FM phase, the Cr-X-Cr bond angle is close to 90° and the number of 3d electrons of the Cr atom is 5. According to the Goodenough-Kanamori-Anderson (GKA) rules [31], the Cr-X-Cr superexchange interaction between the two nearest-neighboring Cr atoms enables the FM states. 通过图3(b)中相同能量范围内的部分电荷密度以及图S5(c)[29]中的ELF结果，在1T′-FM CrS₂相中未观察到Cr-Cr二聚体键。应存在其他机制来稳定1T′-FM相。已知1T的AFM磁有序源于Cr原子之间的直接交换相互作用[21,30]。对于1T′ FM相，Cr-X-Cr键角接近90°，Cr原子的3d电子数为5。根据Goodenough-Kanamori-Anderson规则[31]，两个最近邻Cr原子之间的Cr-X-Cr超交换相互作用使得FM态成为可能。
> 
> **Goodenough-Kanamori-Anderson (GKA) Rules**: GKA规则，一组用于预测过渡金属氧化物中磁交换相互作用符号和大小的半经验规则。
> 
> In Table II, we surmised the magnetic moment of each atom in 1T and 1T′ phases. The magnetic moment of chalcogenide atoms is closed to zero in the 1T-AFM phase, suggesting the direct exchanging interaction between Cr atoms. However, in the 1T′ FM-CDW phase, the magnetic moment of both Cr and chalcogenide atoms increases, despite the opposite spin direction. These results indicate the Cr-X-Cr superexchange interaction in the 1T′ FM phase. We also show DOS and partial charge density results of 1T′ FM-CDW CrX₂ in Figs. 3(b), S6(b), and S7(b) [29]. No dimer bonds could be observed. Therefore the direct exchange to superexchange transition leads to the formation of the 1T′ FM-CDW phase in CrX₂ [Fig. 3(c), right plot]. 在表II中，我们总结了1T和1T′相中每个原子的磁矩。在1T-AFM相中，硫族原子的磁矩接近零，表明Cr原子之间存在直接交换相互作用。然而，在1T′ FM-CDW相中，尽管自旋方向相反，Cr和硫族原子的磁矩均增加。这些结果表明1T′ FM相中存在Cr-X-Cr超交换相互作用。我们还在图3(b)、S6(b)和S7(b)[29]中显示了1T′ FM-CDW CrX₂的DOS和部分电荷密度结果。未观察到二聚体键。因此，直接交换向超交换的转变导致了CrX₂中1T′ FM-CDW相的形成[图3(c)，右图]。
> 
> As for VTe₂, its magnetic moment changes are similar to CrX₂ in Table II. Upon the phase transition from 1T to 1T′, the magnetic moment of V atoms increases from 1.102 μB to 1.315 μB, whereas the magnetic moment of Te atoms increases from 0 to ∼0.08 μB. Analogous to CrX₂, the V-Te-V superexchange could be the physical mechanism to stabilize the 1T′ FM-CDW state. 至于VTe₂，其磁矩变化与表II中的CrX₂相似。在从1T到1T′的相变过程中，V原子的磁矩从1.102 μB增加到1.315 μB，而Te原子的磁矩从0增加到约0.08 μB。与CrX₂类似，V-Te-V超交换可能是稳定1T′ FM-CDW态的物理机制。
> 
> However, the formation mechanisms of 1T′ FM-CDW MnX₂ are different from CrX₂ and VTe₂. The calculated DOS and partial charge density results of 1T′-MnX₂ are shown in Fig. 4. Despite the DOS of spin up and down electrons are still asymmetric, they are close to each other and thus lead to a relatively small magnetic moment (Table II). This character of 1T′ MnX₂ is close to that of 1T′-NM [Fig. 3(a)] other than 1T′-FM in CrX₂ [Fig. 3(b)]. Besides, we can observe the dimer bond between neighboring Mn atoms in corresponding partial charge density. The magnetic moments of Mn atoms decrease from 1T to 1T′ phase (Table II). The formation of the 1T′ FM-CDW phase of MnX₂ should be attributed to the Mn-Mn dimerization other than the Mn-X-Mn superexchange interaction. 然而，1T′ FM-CDW MnX₂的形成机制与CrX₂和VTe₂不同。1T′-MnX₂的计算DOS和部分电荷密度结果显示于图4中。尽管自旋向上和向下电子的DOS仍然不对称，但它们彼此接近，因此导致相对较小的磁矩（表II）。1T′ MnX₂的这一特征与1T′-NM [图3(a)]而非CrX₂中的1T′-FM [图3(b)]接近。此外，我们可以在相应的部分电荷密度中观察到相邻Mn原子之间的二聚体键。Mn原子的磁矩从1T到1T′相减小（表II）。MnX₂的1T′ FM-CDW相的形成应归因于Mn-Mn二聚化而非Mn-X-Mn超交换相互作用。
> 
> It is worth noting that the M-M dimerization is the mechanism for the 1T′ NM-CDW phase in group VI TMD. Mn atoms have more d electrons than the group VI metal elements, such as Cr and Mo. The Mn-Mn dimerization might not completely annihilate the magnetic moments of the Mn atoms and thus the FM states are maintained in the 1T′ phase. In addition, we also notice minor changes of chalcogenide atoms magnetizations from 1T to 1T′ phase. It might also be attributed to the Mn-Mn dimerization because some spin-polarized electrons shared between Mn and chalcogenide atoms in 1T phase could be redistributed to the dimer bonds in 1T′ phase. The corresponding electron transfer could be observed from the Bader analysis of 1T and 1T′ MnX₂ in supplementary Table S1 [29]. 值得注意的是，M-M二聚化是第VI族TMD中1T′ NM-CDW相的机制。Mn原子比第VI族金属元素（如Cr和Mo）具有更多的d电子。Mn-Mn二聚化可能不会完全湮灭Mn原子的磁矩，因此FM态在1T′相中得以保持。此外，我们还注意到硫族原子磁化强度从1T到1T′相的微小变化。这也可能归因于Mn-Mn二聚化，因为1T相中Mn和硫族原子之间共享的一些自旋极化电子可能重新分配到1T′相的二聚体键中。相应的电子转移可从补充表S1[29]中1T和1T′ MnX₂的Bader分析中观察到。
> 
> **Bader Analysis**: Bader电荷分析，一种基于电子密度拓扑分析来划分原子区域并确定原子电荷的方法。
> 
> In Table I, we notice a strong correlation between the d₁/d₂ ratio and the mechanisms for 1T′ FM-CDW phases. For d₁/d₂ close to 0.7 and 0.8, superexchange transition acts as the mechanism to stabilize the 1T′ phase. On the other hand, the smaller d₁/d₂ (<0.7) represents a significant lattice distortion, indicating metal atom dimerization. Therefore the distortion index could serve as a simple indicator to distinguish the different mechanisms for FM states in the 1T′ phase. 在表I中，我们注意到d₁/d₂比值与1T′ FM-CDW相的机制之间存在强相关性。当d₁/d₂接近0.7和0.8时，超交换转变作为稳定1T′相的机制。另一方面，较小的d₁/d₂（<0.7）代表显著的晶格畸变，表明金属原子二聚化。因此，畸变指数可作为区分1T′相中FM态不同机制的简单指标。
> 
> At the end of this section, to obtain a better overview, we summarize all the reported 1T′ FM- or NM- CDW phases of group IV to VII TMD family and their formation mechanisms in Fig. 5 [17,19,20]. The 1T′ NM-CDW phase is stable in the middle of the plot, e.g., mainly in group VI. In contrast, most group IV, V, VI, and VII TMD materials do not have a stable 1T′ NM-CDW phase. A plausible explanation is that the group VI TMD have the right number of valence electrons to trigger the Peierls instability, and thus forming the distorted 1T′ phase. The 1T′ FM-CDW state is on the first row including 3d metal elements like V, Cr, and Mn. This is in line with the islands of stability in the magnetic periodic table. The CrX₂ family is located at the crossover points. It is, therefore, reasonable to observe both 1T′ FM- and NM-CDW states. The two formation mechanisms of 1T′ FM-CDW states are also depicted in Fig. 5, in which the semitransparent blue box represents the M-M dimer formation, and the semitransparent green box represents the direct exchange to superexchange transition. A clear pattern can be observed. 在本节末尾，为获得更好的概览，我们在图5中总结了所有已报道的第IV至VII族TMD家族的1T′ FM-或NM-CDW相及其形成机制[17,19,20]。1T′ NM-CDW相在图的中部稳定，即主要在第VI族。相比之下，大多数第IV、V、VI和VII族TMD材料没有稳定的1T′ NM-CDW相。一个合理的解释是第VI族TMD具有适当数量的价电子来触发Peierls不稳定性，从而形成畸变的1T′相。1T′ FM-CDW态位于第一行，包括3d金属元素如V、Cr和Mn。这与磁性周期表中的稳定岛一致。CrX₂家族位于交叉点。因此，同时观察到1T′ FM-和NM-CDW态是合理的。1T′ FM-CDW态的两种形成机制也描绘于图5中，其中半透明蓝色框代表M-M二聚体形成，半透明绿色框代表直接交换向超交换转变。可以观察到一个清晰的模式。

> ## C. Charge doping induced reversible phase transition in CrS₂
> 
> ## C. 电荷掺杂诱导的CrS₂可逆相变
> 
> Considering the intrinsic coupling between the electronic and crystal structures in CDW, charge doping could be an effective method to modulate NM and FM CDW transition in CrX₂. In this section, the charge doping induced phase transition is investigated for CrS₂. Figures 6(a) and 6(b) summarize the variation of 1T′ crystal structures (lattice constant a and b) upon electron/hole injection. The lattice constants of 1T′-NM are taken as references to calculate the lattice strain variation. Upon electron injection in 1T′-NM, its lattice constants a and b gradually increase. At a critical injection value of −0.16 e per atom, a sudden increase from a = 3.088 Å, b = 5.617 Å to a = 3.464 Å, b = 5.950 Å can be observed, corresponding to the spontaneous transition from 1T′ NM-CDW to 1T′ FM-CDW. Here e is the elementary charge. This transition could be confirmed from Fig. S8 [29], in which the d₁/d₂ ratio jumps from 0.739 to 0.911 and the magnetic moment increases to 4.379 μB. The spontaneous transition induces a large strain output, εx ≈ 12.17% and εy ≈ 5.93%, due to a significant difference of lattice constants between 1T′ NM-CDW metastable state and 1T′ FM-CDW ground state (Table I). 考虑到CDW中电子结构和晶体结构之间的内禀耦合，电荷掺杂可能是调控CrX₂中NM和FM CDW转变的有效方法。本节中，研究了CrS₂中电荷掺杂诱导的相变。图6(a)和6(b)总结了电子/空穴注入时1T′晶体结构（晶格常数a和b）的变化。1T′-NM的晶格常数作为参考来计算晶格应变变化。在1T′-NM中注入电子时，其晶格常数a和b逐渐增加。在临界注入值为每原子−0.16 e时，可以观察到从a = 3.088 Å、b = 5.617 Å突然增加到a = 3.464 Å、b = 5.950 Å，对应于从1T′ NM-CDW到1T′ FM-CDW的自发转变。这里e是元电荷。这一转变可从图S8[29]中得到确认，其中d₁/d₂比值从0.739跳至0.911，磁矩增加至4.379 μB。由于1T′ NM-CDW亚稳态和1T′ FM-CDW基态之间晶格常数的显著差异（表I），自发转变诱导了大的应变输出，εx ≈ 12.17%和εy ≈ 5.93%。
> 
> In the reverse direction (electron extraction), the lattice constants of 1T′ FM-CDW CrS₂ gradually decrease to their ground state without any sudden change. The d₁/d₂ ratio (Fig. S8(a) [29]) also gradually restored to the 1T′ FM-CDW ground state value, 0.724. There should be an energy barrier to prevent the phase transition back to the 1T′ NM-CDW phase upon reducing the injected electrons. 在反向（电子抽取）过程中，1T′ FM-CDW CrS₂的晶格常数逐渐减小至其基态，没有任何突然变化。d₁/d₂比值（图S8(a)[29]）也逐步恢复到1T′ FM-CDW基态值0.724。在减少注入电子时，应存在能量势垒阻止相变回到1T′ NM-CDW相。
> 
> Being subject to hole doping, the lattice constants of 1T′ FM-CDW CrS₂ continuously decrease. It will remain as the FM state until the hole doping level reaches Q = 0.15 e per atom. At this point, the lattice constants exhibit a sudden decrease to a = 2.992 Å and b = 5.181 Å. Figure S8(a) shows that the d₁/d₂ ratio changes back to 1 at this point. [29]. Thus this abrupt change is caused by the transition to 1T phase instead of the transition to 1T′ NM-CDW. The observed 1T′ to 1T transition is similar to a previous study that hole doping will lead 1T′ phase transiting to the high-symmetry 1T phase for MoS₂ [17]. After that, the reduction of injected holes in 1T CrS₂ induces a minor change on the lattice constant until Q = 0.06 e per atom. An abrupt decrease and increase could be observed in lattice constant a and b, respectively. The 1T CrS₂ has been restored to the stable 1T′ NM-CDW ground state. The reversible phase transition under the cyclic electron and hole doping generated a hysteresis in Figs. 6(a) and 6(b). 在空穴掺杂下，1T′ FM-CDW CrS₂的晶格常数持续减小。它将保持FM态直到空穴掺杂水平达到Q = 0.15 e每原子。此时，晶格常数突然减小至a = 2.992 Å和b = 5.181 Å。图S8(a)显示d₁/d₂比值在此点变回1[29]。因此，这一突变是由向1T相的转变而非向1T′ NM-CDW的转变引起的。观察到的1T′到1T转变类似于先前的研究，即空穴掺杂将导致MoS₂的1T′相转变为高对称性1T相[17]。此后，1T CrS₂中注入空穴的减少引起晶格常数的微小变化，直到Q = 0.06 e每原子。在晶格常数a和b中分别可以观察到突然减小和增加。1T CrS₂已恢复到稳定的1T′ NM-CDW基态。循环电子和空穴掺杂下的可逆相变在图6(a)和6(b)中产生了滞后。
> 
> **Hysteresis**: 滞后现象，系统状态依赖于其历史路径的现象，在相变中表现为正向和反向转变发生在不同条件下。
> 
> Figures 6(a) and 6(b) also show that a substantial charge doping, i.e., −0.16 e or +0.15 e, is required to trigger the phase transition in CrS₂. Previous studies suggested that Li ion adsorption could induce similar charging doping levels in 2D TMD materials. For example, 20% Li insertion concentration could induce ∼0.24 e per atom electron concentration doping [7]. A gate electric field to drive Li ions in and out of the layered 1T-TaS₂ has been realized in experiments. This method will introduce higher doping levels (∼0.2 electrons from Li) in each atomic layer [32]. 图6(a)和6(b)还显示，需要大量电荷掺杂，即−0.16 e或+0.15 e，来触发CrS₂中的相变。先前的研究表明，锂离子吸附可以在二维TMD材料中诱导类似的电荷掺杂水平。例如，20%的Li插入浓度可诱导约0.24 e每原子的电子浓度掺杂[7]。实验中已实现通过栅极电场驱动Li离子进出层状1T-TaS₂。这种方法将在每个原子层中引入更高的掺杂水平（来自Li的约0.2个电子）[32]。
> 
> To characterize the dynamic stability and the phase transition possibility, we used the CI-NEB [26] method to calculate the energy barrier between 1T′ NM-CDW and 1T′ FM-CDW under various electron/hole doping. Without charging doping, the energy barrier for 1T′ NM-CDW transition to 1T′ FM-CDW is about 87.4 meV/f.u [Fig. 6(c)]. This barrier is high enough to ensure the stability of 1T′ NM-CDW and 1T′ FM-CDW at room temperature and is consistent with the result in Fig. 2(a). For electron doping higher at −0.02, the total energy of 1T′ FM-CDW CrS₂ becomes lower than that of 1T′ NM-CDW, providing the driving force of transition. The energy barrier is quickly reduced to 59.5, 40.2 and 23.6 meV/f.u. at Q = −0.02, −0.06, and −0.10 e per atom in Fig. S8 [29]. On the other hand, hole doping will increase the total energy of 1T′ FM-CDW relative to 1T′ NM-CDW, promoting the NM to FM transition [Fig. 4(c)]. The energy barrier for 1T′ FM-CDW transition to 1T′ NM-CDW also quickly reduced from 86.0, 53.8 to 25.8 meV/f.u. under hole doping from 0.02, 0.06 to 0.10 e per atom (Fig. S8 [29]). 为表征动力学稳定性和相变可能性，我们使用CI-NEB[26]方法计算了不同电子/空穴掺杂下1T′ NM-CDW和1T′ FM-CDW之间的能量势垒。无电荷掺杂时，1T′ NM-CDW向1T′ FM-CDW转变的能量势垒约为87.4 meV/f.u.[图6(c)]。这一势垒足够高，可确保1T′ NM-CDW和1T′ FM-CDW在室温下的稳定性，与图2(a)中的结果一致。当电子掺杂高于−0.02时，1T′ FM-CDW CrS₂的总能量变得低于1T′ NM-CDW，提供了转变的驱动力。在图S8[29]中，能量势垒在Q = −0.02、−0.06和−0.10 e每原子时迅速降至59.5、40.2和23.6 meV/f.u.。另一方面，空穴掺杂将增加1T′ FM-CDW相对于1T′ NM-CDW的总能量，促进NM到FM的转变[图4(c)]。1T′ FM-CDW向1T′ NM-CDW转变的能量势垒也在空穴掺杂从0.02、0.06到0.10 e每原子时从86.0、53.8迅速降至25.8 meV/f.u.（图S8[29]）。
> 
> At room temperature (thermal energy ∼26 meV/atom), the spontaneous phase transition could occur around electron or hole doping of −0.10 e or +0.10 e, which is likely in experiments based on previous experimental results as discussed above. Our results show that charge doping can effectively modulate the formation mechanisms to stabilize different 1T′ CDW states in CrS₂. As a result, an electromechanical actuation strain can be generated as high as ∼12%. This strain output is significant compared with other typical two-dimensional actuation materials, such as ∼1% to ∼6.3% strain from graphene and graphene oxide [33–35], ∼5.8% strain from MoS₂ [17], and ∼3.2% strain from MoTe₂ [12], which is promising for miniaturized electromechanical actuator applications. In addition, charge doping induced FM/NM transition could offer new approaches/concepts for 2D materials based spintronics and memory devices. 在室温下（热能∼26 meV/原子），自发相变可能发生在约−0.10 e或+0.10 e的电子或空穴掺杂附近，基于上述讨论的先前实验结果，这在实验中是可能的。我们的结果表明，电荷掺杂可以有效地调控形成机制以稳定CrS₂中不同的1T′ CDW态。结果，可以产生高达约12%的机电驱动应变。与其他典型的二维驱动材料相比，这一应变输出是显著的，例如石墨烯和氧化石墨烯的约1%至约6.3%应变[33–35]、MoS₂的约5.8%应变[17]以及MoTe₂的约3.2%应变[12]，这对于小型化机电驱动器应用是有前景的。此外，电荷掺杂诱导的FM/NM转变可为基于二维材料的自旋电子学和存储器件提供新的方法/概念。

> # IV. CONCLUSION
> 
> # 四、结论
> 
> In this work, using DFT calculations, we thoroughly investigate the 1T′ FM-CDW states in TMDs and the underlying formation physical mechanisms. We identified several 1T′ FM-CDW phases in VTe₂, CrX₂, and MnX₂. Notably, the CrX₂ family has a unique feature, i.e., the coexistence of both NM and FM 1T′ phase, which is not observed in other TMD materials. Our results and analysis show that there are two mechanisms for the formation of 1T′ FM-CDW phases. For VTe₂ and CrX₂, the primary formation mechanism of FM state should be attributed to direct exchange to superexchange transition. As for the MnX₂ family, the formation of the Mn-Mn dimer bond is the reason for the observed 1T′ FM-CDW states. In the end, taking advantage of coexisting stable 1T′ FM-CDW and NM-CDW phases in CrS₂, we study the charge doping induced reversible phase transition. The observed phase transition generates significant strain output (εx ∼12.17% and εy ∼ 5.93%) and magnetic state transition, promising in 2D materials based miniaturized actuators, spintronic, and memory devices. 本工作中，我们使用DFT计算深入研究了TMDs中的1T′ FM-CDW态及其潜在的形成物理机制。我们在VTe₂、CrX₂和MnX₂中识别了几个1T′ FM-CDW相。值得注意的是，CrX₂家族具有独特的特征，即NM和FM 1T′相的共存，这在其他TMD材料中尚未观察到。我们的结果和分析表明，1T′ FM-CDW相的形成有两种机制。对于VTe₂和CrX₂，FM态的主要形成机制应归因于直接交换向超交换的转变。至于MnX₂家族，Mn-Mn二聚体键的形成是观察到的1T′ FM-CDW态的原因。最后，利用CrS₂中共存的稳定1T′ FM-CDW和NM-CDW相，我们研究了电荷掺杂诱导的可逆相变。观察到的相变产生显著的应变输出（εx ∼12.17%和εy ∼ 5.93%）和磁态转变，在基于二维材料的小型化驱动器、自旋电子学和存储器件中具有前景。
