> ## ABSTRACT
> 
> 摘要
> 
> **The unusually sensitive solvatochromism of 2,5-dicyano-4-methyl-4’-dimethylaminostilbene (1a) whose emission maximum varies from 445 nm in cyclohexane to 641 nm in DMSO are described. 1a with remarkably large two-photon cross sections exhibits very strong polarity-, viscosity-, and temperature-dependence of fluorescence, and can be used to detect polarities, viscosities, and temperature. The successful application of 1a provides a brilliant prototype for the developments of ideal two-photon fluorescence probes.** 本文描述了 2,5-双氰基-4-甲基-4'-二甲氨基二苯乙烯 (1a) 异常敏感的溶剂显色特性，其发射最大值在环己烷中为 445 nm，而在 DMSO 中变为 641 nm。1a 具有极大的两光子截面，其荧光表现出极强的极性、粘度和温度依赖性，可用于检测极性、粘度和温度。1a 的成功应用为理想两光子荧光探针的开发提供了一个优秀的原型。 **Solvatochromism**: 溶剂显色，指化合物的吸收或发射光谱随溶剂极性变化而发生移动的现象。
> 
> **ISSN: 2978-5324** ISSN: 2978-5324
> 
> * * *
> 
> For the development of TPEF (two-photon excited fluorescence) -optimized molecular probes, it is important to have a significant modulation of the photophysical properties of the chromophore in response to external stimuli, such as polarity, pH, ion concentration, and temperature. 为了开发经过 TPEF（两光子激发荧光）优化的分子探针，使发色团的光物理性质能够响应外部刺激（如极性、pH 值、离子浓度和温度）并产生显著调节至关重要。 **TPEF**: 两光子激发荧光，利用两个低能光子激发分子产生单光子荧光发射的技术。
> 
> At the same time, the chromophore should retain a significant two-photon absorption (TPA) cross section ($\delta_{TPA}$) in a suitable excitation window for biological imaging (700-1000 nm, corresponding to an optimum combination of reduced scattering and absorption in biological samples). 同时，发色团在适用于生物成像的激发窗口（700-1000 nm，对应于生物样本中散射和吸收降低的最佳组合）内应保持显著的两光子吸收 (TPA) 截面 ($\delta_{TPA}$)。
> 
> In contrast to PET-based fluorescence probes, which only show increasing or decreasing emission intensity at fixed wavelength, solvatochromic probes may indicate the presence of an analyte by a significant change of emission wavelength, which is unequivocally detectable [1]. 与仅在固定波长下显示发射强度增加或减少的基于 PET 的荧光探针不同，溶剂显色探针可以通过发射波长的显著变化来指示分析物的存在，这种变化是可以被明确检测到的 [1]。 **PET**: 光诱导电子转移 (Photoinduced Electron Transfer)，一种常见的荧光淬灭机制。
> 
> However, at present, there are only a few examples of two-photon sensors for metal ions, fluoride ions, polarity, and pH that have been studied in organic solvents or model membranes [2,3]. 然而，目前在有机溶剂或模型膜中研究的用于金属离子、氟离子、极性和 pH 的两光子传感器的例子很少 [2,3]。
> 
> Here we report on two dipolar dyes (1a-b) with two strongly electron-accepting cyano groups at their acceptor extremity derived from stilbene that display large changes in both one-(OPA) and TPA characteristics and solvatochromism which can be modulated by the addition of analytes. 在此，我们报道了两种基于二苯乙烯衍生的偶极染料 (1a-b)，在其受体端具有两个强电子受体的氰基，它们在单光子吸收 (OPA) 和两光子吸收 (TPA) 特性以及溶剂显色方面均表现出巨大变化，且可通过添加分析物进行调节。
> 
> Surprisedly, these two probes exhibited reasonably large $\delta_{TPA}$, especially two-photon triple fluorescence. 令人惊讶的是，这两个探针表现出相当大的 $\delta_{TPA}$，尤其是两光子三重荧光。
> 
> The stilbene derivative 1a (1b) was obtained in 65% (62%) yield by the reaction of dimethylaminobenzaldehyde (diphenylaminobenzaldehyde) with (2,5-dicyano-4-methylbenzyl) phosphonic acid diethyl ester in the presence of sodium hydride (NaH). 二苯乙烯衍生物 1a (1b) 是通过在氢化钠 (NaH) 存在下，用二甲氨基苯甲醛（二苯氨基苯甲醛）与 (2,5-双氰基-4-甲基苄基) 膦酸二乙酯反应，以 65% (62%) 的产率获得。
> 
> The absorption properties of 1a are almost independent on the solvent (Table 1). 1a 的吸收性质几乎与溶剂无关（表 1）。
> 
> However, with respect to the emission properties, 1a exhibits an unexpected strong solvatochromism (Figure 1a, Supporting Information (SI)). 然而，就发射性质而言，1a 表现出出乎意料的强溶剂显色效应（图 1a，支持信息 (SI)）。
> 
> Only a few previous examples of solvatochromic behavior for stilbene derivatives which just possess one substituent at most in the single aromatic ring and are not ideal candidates for solvatochromic probes had been reported. 此前仅报道了少数二苯乙烯衍生物具有溶剂显色行为的例子，这些衍生物在单个芳环上最多只有一个取代基，并非溶剂显色探针的理想选择。
> 
> Thus, the emission maximum (EM) for 1a (1b) varies from 445 (452) nm in cyclohexane to 641 (604) nm in DMSO. 因此，1a (1b) 的发射最大值 (EM) 从环己烷中的 445 (452) nm 变为 DMSO 中的 641 (604) nm。
> 
> The reason why the solvatochromic shifts of 1b are somewhat smaller (ca. 156 nm, cf. SI) is that decreasing transition energy and increasing sensitivity to solvent polarity for weak electron donor diphenylamino group is inferior to ones for strong electron donor dimethylamino group. 1b 的溶剂显色偏移较小（约 156 nm，参见 SI）的原因是，弱电子给体二苯氨基在降低跃迁能和提高对溶剂极性敏感性方面，不如强电子给体二甲氨基。

> 
> Protic solvents (Hydrogen-Bonding-Donor, HBD) can interact with the donor (dimethylamino) for the ground state of 1a to stabilize the ground state more than the excited state, and thus, lower energy for the ground state results in a relative blue shift as compared with strong polar aprotic solvents (Non-Hydrogen-Bonding-Donor, NHD). 质子溶剂（氢键给体, HBD）可以与 1a 基态的给体（二甲氨基）相互作用，使其基态比激发态更稳定，因此基态能量降低，与强极性非质子溶剂（非氢键给体, NHD）相比产生相对蓝移。
> 
> The fitting between EM in wavenumbers and the solvent parameters such as the $E_T(30)$ data, the Kosower parameter, Z, the Gibbs energy of activation of the solvolysis ($\delta\Delta G^{\neq}$), the index of solvent dipolarity/polarizability ($\pi^_$), and the Lippert-Mataga scale ($\Delta f$) gave an almost linear relationship ($r^2 = 0.90, 0.90, 0.90, 0.85, 0.82$, respectively) (Figure 1b). 波数形式的发射最大值 (EM) 与溶剂参数（如 $E_T(30)$ 数据、Kosower 参数 Z、溶剂解离的吉布斯活化能 $\delta\Delta G^{\neq}$、溶剂偶极性/极化率指数 $\pi^_$ 以及 Lippert-Mataga 量表 $\Delta f$）之间的拟合呈现出近乎线性的关系（$r^2$ 分别为 0.90, 0.90, 0.90, 0.85, 0.82）（图 1b）。
> 
> In one- and two-photon emissions (OPE and TPE), band A ($^1L_a$) of 1a in glycerol all shift to the red with increasing temperature, and the relative intensities of band B ($^1L_b$) gradually decreases (Figure 2e, f). 在单光子和两光子发射 (OPE 和 TPE) 中，1a 在甘油中的 A 带 ($^1L_a$) 均随温度升高而红移，且 B 带 ($^1L_b$) 的相对强度逐渐降低（图 2e, f）。
> 
> The viscosity dependence of the fluorescence for 1a exhibits differences between A band and B band, and between OPE and TPE (Figure 2c, d). 1a 荧光的粘度依赖性在 A 带和 B 带之间，以及 OPE 和 TPE 之间表现出差异（图 2c, d）。
> 
> The intensities of B band in OPE and TPE steadily increase upon the augment of viscosities, whereas the intensities of A band in TPE at first rapidly decline, and then enhance, and a third band at 542 nm, namely exciplex (E) fluorescence from strong intermolecular donor-acceptor systems finally disappears in pure glycerol (Figure 2d). 在 OPE 和 TPE 中，B 带的强度随粘度的增加而稳步增加；而在 TPE 中，A 带的强度首先迅速下降，然后增强；此外，在 542 nm 处由强分子间给体-受体系统产生的一个第三带，即激基复合物 (E) 荧光，最终在纯甘油中消失（图 2d）。 **Exciplex**: 激基复合物，由激发态分子与基态分子之间通过电荷转移相互作用形成的复合物。
> 
> Never has this property in two-photon been reported. 这种两光子特性此前从未被报道过。
> 
> High viscosity preventing intermolecular charges from transfer should be responsible for the disappearance of the third band whose no emergence in the OPE possibly consists in its low intensity and short lifetime. 高粘度阻止了分子间电荷转移，这应该是导致第三带消失的原因；而该带在 OPE 中没有出现，可能是由于其强度低且寿命短。
> 
> In addition, at the low concentration ($10^{-6}$ M) of 1a, the intensity ratio (IR) $I_a/I_b$ of the emission bands A and B decreases with increasing viscosity, but at the high concentration ($3\times 10^{-6}$ M) the IR $I_a/I_b$ increases, irrespective of OPE and TPE (cf. SI). 此外，在 1a 低浓度 ($10^{-6}$ M) 时，发射带 A 和 B 的强度比 (IR) $I_a/I_b$ 随粘度增加而降低；但在高浓度 ($3\times 10^{-6}$ M) 时，$I_a/I_b$ 随之增加，且与 OPE 或 TPE 无关（参见 SI）。
> 
> Furthermore, concentration dependence is found for the IR $I_a/I_b$ of the emission bands A and B of 1a in alcohol (cf. SI). 此外，在醇类溶剂中，1a 发射带 A 和 B 的 $I_a/I_b$ 表现出浓度依赖性（参见 SI）。
> 
> The above environmental influences upon triple fluorescence bands A, B and E usually indicates three emissive species, i.e., the localized excited state (LE), a twisted intramolecular charge transfer state (TICT) and an intermolecular exciplex. 上述环境对三重荧光带 A、B 和 E 的影响通常表明存在三种发光物种，即局部激发态 (LE)、扭曲分子内电荷转移态 (TICT) 和分子间激基复合物。 **TICT**: 扭曲分子内电荷转移态，分子在激发后通过旋转产生电荷分离，通常会导致荧光淬灭或大红移。
> 
> As expected, $\delta_{TPA}$ of 1a climbs its maximum of 5560 GM in nonpolar cyclohexane, while in strongly polar aprotic DMF it falls to its minimum of 130 GM. 正如预期的那样，1a 的 $\delta_{TPA}$ 在非极性环己烷中达到最大值 5560 GM，而在强极性非质子溶剂 DMF 中下降到最小值 130 GM。
> 
> In general, $\delta_{TPA}$ of 1a are much larger in low polarity aprotic solvents than in high polarity aprotic solvents, which should is attributed to the excited state configuration transformation (Table 1). 通常，1a 在低极性非质子溶剂中的 $\delta_{TPA}$ 远大于在高极性非质子溶剂中，这应归因于激发态构型的转变（表 1）。
> 
> The reduction in emission intensity for 1a in acetone and other more polar solvents together with a near lack of photoreactivity is certainly consistent with decay of the emissive state to one or more of the possible noncoplanar and nonemissive TICT which subsequently decay rapidly to the ground state. 1a 在丙酮和其他极性更强的溶剂中发射强度的降低，以及几乎缺乏光反应性，这与发光态衰减到一种或多种可能的非共面且无发射的 TICT 态，随后迅速衰减回基态的情况是一致的。
>

> 
> As in other solvatochromic systems, an internal charge transfer (ICT) due to a strong donor-acceptor interplay between the dimethylamino and the 2,5-dicyano-4-methyl stilbyl moieties can take place in the excited state of 1a. 与其它溶剂显色系统一样，1a 的激发态中由于二甲氨基与 2,5-双氰基-4-甲基二苯乙烯部分之间强烈的给体-受体相互作用，可以发生分子内电荷转移 (ICT)。 **ICT**: 分子内电荷转移，指分子内电子从电子富集区（给体）向电子贫乏区（受体）的转移。
> 
> On one hand, ortho- and meta-cyano groups of double bonds (C=C) are fairly strong electron acceptor in the ground state, which can remarkably decrease transition energy, stabilize resonance structures and increase both the excited state dipole moment and molecular sensitivity to solvent polarity. 一方面，双键 (C=C) 的邻位和间位氰基在基态中是相当强的电子受体，这可以显著降低跃迁能，稳定共振结构，并增加激发态偶极矩以及分子对溶剂极性的敏感性。
> 
> On the other hand, these two cyano groups can notably extend conjugated system, which consumedly improves two-photon absorption cross section and fluorescence quantum yield. 另一方面，这两个氰基能显著扩展共轭体系，从而提高两光子吸收截面和荧光量子产率。
> 
> Additionally, in the excited state, the adiabatic ICT leads to an intermediate that most likely develops a negative charge for nitrogen atom of ortho-cyano group that is, after solvent relaxation, stabilized not only by polar solvents, especially those with high $\pi^_$, but also by meta-cyano group with strong electron-withdrawing ability for greater diffusion of concentrated negative charge. 此外，在激发态中，绝热 ICT 导致产生一个中间体，该中间体的邻位氰基氮原子极有可能带负电；在溶剂弛豫后，这不仅被极性溶剂（尤其是 $\pi^_$ 较高的溶剂）稳定，还被具有强吸电子能力的间位氰基稳定，从而使集中的负电荷进一步扩散。
> 
> This ICT-solvent relaxation sequence explains the significant red shift of the emission spectra in polar solvents as well as the temperature and viscosity dependence of the emission properties. 这种 ICT-溶剂弛豫序列解释了极性溶剂中发射光谱的显著红移，以及发射性质对温度和粘度的依赖性。
> 
> In conclusion, we have shown that the covalent attachment of two cyano groups in the single aromatic ring and dimethylamino group to stilbene is a considerably ingenious strategy, which makes the molecule a highly sensitive two-photon thermo-solvatochromic probe with remarkably large $\delta_{TPA}$ and very high fluorescence quantum yield capable of being used to detect the analytes with high $\pi^_$, and to detect polarities, viscosities, and temperature. 总之，我们证明了在单个芳环上共价连接两个氰基并将二甲氨基连接到二苯乙烯上是一个相当巧妙的策略，这使得该分子成为一个高度敏感的两光子热-溶剂显色探针，具有极大的 $\delta_{TPA}$ 和极高的荧光量子产率，能够用于检测具有高 $\pi^_$ 的分析物，以及检测极性、粘度和温度。
> 
> The results provide a useful design strategy for synthesis of new two-photon sensors for further applications. 该结果为合成用于进一步应用的新型两光子传感器提供了一种有用的设计策略。
