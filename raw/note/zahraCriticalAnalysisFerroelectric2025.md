> [!quote]- <center>Abstract</center>
> 
> Abstract MXenes, 2D materials with high conductivity, adjustable surface terminations, and high magnetism, have garnered attention for their potential to resolve the energy crisis. The incorporation of doping and composite can induce ferroelectricity and ferromagnetism in their intrinsic state. The etching procedure in synthesis significantly impacts their magnetic and electronic characteristics. This review explores the ferromagnetic, ferroelectric, and multiferroic properties of MXenes, mainly transition metal carbides and nitrides, and discusses fabrication procedures. It also describes the potential applications in electronic devices, emphasizing the significance of MXenes' structural and functional properties for advanced technology.
>
>【摘要翻译】MXenes是一种具有高导电性、可调表面终端和高磁性的二维材料，因其解决能源危机的潜力而备受关注。掺杂和复合物的引入可以诱导铁电性和铁磁性的本征态。合成过程中的刻蚀工艺对其磁学和电学特性有重要影响。本文综述了MXenes（主要是过渡金属碳化物和氮化物）的铁磁性、铁电性和多铁性，并讨论了其制备工艺。介绍了MXenes在电子器件中的潜在应用，强调了MXenes的结构和功能特性对先进技术的重要意义。

> ## 一、引言
> 
> 在当今信息时代，对高性能、小型化电子器件的需求日益增长，促使科研人员不断探索新型先进材料。**二维（2D）材料**，因其原子级厚度所展现出的独特物理、化学和电子特性，成为了材料科学领域的研究热点。在众多二维材料中，**MXene**（发音为“迈克辛”）自2011年被发现以来，凭借其高导电性、可调的表面官能团以及丰富的元素组合，迅速崛起为一颗新星，尤其在能源存储、电磁屏蔽和电子器件等领域展现出巨大潜力。
> 
> 然而，要实现MXene在下一代非易失性存储器、自旋电子学和高灵敏度传感器等尖端应用中的突破，关键在于发掘和调控其本征的**铁电（Ferroelectricity，FE）** 与**铁磁（Ferromagnetism，FM）** 特性。铁电材料具有可被外电场反转的自发极化，铁磁材料则具有可被外磁场反转的自发磁化，两者均是构建逻辑和存储单元的核心物理基础。但MXene大多具有中心对称的晶体结构，这在理论上禁止了铁电性的产生；同时，其内部强的金属-碳/氮共价键也使得本征铁磁性较为罕见。
> 
> 这篇于2025年6月在线发表在《Critical Reviews in Solid State and Materials Sciences》期刊上的综述文章，正是针对上述挑战，系统性地梳理了当前在MXene及其复合材料中诱导、测量和应用铁电与铁磁性能的最新研究进展，并对本征多铁性这一前沿方向进行了评述。本文的价值在于，它不仅为初学者提供了从合成到应用的完整知识图谱，也为研究者提供了关于如何通过缺陷工程、掺杂、复合和应变等手段在二维极限下实现并调控有序态（铁电序、铁磁序）的策略性总结。
> 
> ## 二、论文结构与逻辑概览
> 
> 本综述的核心逻辑链条是：**“材料合成是基础，物性调控是关键，器件应用是导向”**。整篇论文结构清晰，层层递进，旨在回答一个核心问题：**如何在一个原本非铁电、非铁磁的MXene体系中，实现并利用铁电与铁磁这两种协同作用？**
> 
> _以下为论文的逻辑框架图（文字描述）：_
> 
> -   **第一部分：基座构建（第1-2章）**
>     -   **引言**：提出2D材料背景，引出MXene作为新兴材料的重要性及挑战。
>     -   **从MAX到MXene**：阐述了MXene的前驱体MAX相及其晶体结构，并详细总结了多种蚀刻方法（如HF蚀刻、熔盐蚀刻等）及其对表面官能团（Tx）的控制，这是决定MXene性能的“基因”。
> -   **第二部分：物性探索（第3-5章）**
>     -   **铁电性（FE）**：分别从计算预测和实验验证两个维度，介绍了在Sc₂CO₂、Nb₂CF₂、V₂C等多种MXene中诱导铁电性的策略，核心是**打破中心对称性**。
>     -   **铁磁性（FM）**：同样从计算和实验出发，分析了Cr、Mn基MXene的本征铁磁性，以及通过掺杂（如Gd、Nb）和化学还原等手段在Ti₃C₂等非磁性MXene中诱导铁磁性的方法，核心是**调控d轨道电子自旋**。
>     -   **多铁性（MF）**：作为前沿方向，探讨了在单一MXene材料（如Hf₂VC₂F₂）或复合材料中同时存在铁电与铁磁序，甚至实现磁电耦合的可能性。
> -   **第三部分：应用导向（第6-7章）**
>     -   **电子器件应用**：重点介绍了MXene在铁电忆阻器、电池/纳米发电机和自旋电子器件中的应用实例，展示了其性能优势。
>     -   **总结与展望**：归纳了当前挑战，如规模化合成、室温稳定性等，并对未来研究方向进行了展望。

> ## 四、正文核心内容剖析
> 
> ### 4.1 研究背景：从MAX到MXene的化学剪刀
> 
> MXene的合成始于其前驱体**MAX相**，一种三元层状碳化物/氮化物。其化学式为 `M_{n+1}AX_n`，其中M-X键是强共价/离子/金属混合键，而M-A键是较弱的金属键。这种化学键强度的差异，构成了一把“化学剪刀”。通过使用氢氟酸（HF）或熔融盐等蚀刻剂，可以选择性地剪断M-A键，移除A原子层，从而将三维MAX相剥离成二维MXene层。剥离后，M-X层表面会悬挂高活性的化学键，它们会立即与蚀刻液中的离子或分子结合，形成表面官能团 `T_x`（如-O， -OH， -F， -Cl等），因此MXene的化学式写为 `M_{n+1}X_nT_x`。正是这些官能团，极大地影响了MXene的电子结构、亲疏水性以及最终的物理性质，成为调控其铁电、铁磁性的重要“旋钮”。
> 
> ### 4.2 核心方法：如何在MXene中“创造”铁电与铁磁序
> 
> 本综述的核心方法论是如何在原本不具备这些性质的MXene中诱导出铁电性和铁磁性。其策略可归纳为三种思想：
> 
> 1.  **打破对称（对称性破缺）**：这是诱导铁电性的核心思想。MXene本征的中心对称结构被破坏时，正负电荷中心不再重合，从而产生电偶极矩。具体方法包括：
>     
>     -   **表面官能团工程**：在不同侧或不同位点吸附特定官能团，如Sc₂CO₂中，O原子的不对称排列导致了面外铁电性。
>     -   **元素掺杂**：用不同半径或电负性的原子替换M位原子，造成晶格畸变。
>     -   **施加外部应变**：对Hf₂CF₂等MXene施加微小应变，可使其从非极性相转变为极性相。
> 2.  **调控自旋（自旋极化）**：这是诱导铁磁性的核心思想。MXene的磁性主要来源于过渡金属M原子的d轨道电子。通过调控费米能级附近的电子态密度，使自旋向上和自旋向下的电子数不平衡，即可产生净磁矩。具体方法包括：
>     
>     -   **选择本征磁性元素**：选择含有Cr、Mn、Fe等具有未填满d轨道元素的MXene，如Cr₂C、Mn₂C等，它们本身具有较大的磁矩。
>     -   **掺杂磁性原子**：在非磁性MXene（如Ti₃C₂）中掺杂Gd³⁺、Nb等磁性离子，引入局域磁矩。
>     -   **缺陷工程**：制造Ti或C空位，可以产生未配对的局域电子，从而诱导出磁性。
> 3.  **界面耦合（复合效应）**：将MXene与已知的铁电材料（如BaTiO₃, BTO）或铁磁材料（如BiFeO₃, BFO）进行复合。这种方法不仅能直接引入铁电/铁磁相，还能通过界面处的电荷转移、应力传递和交换耦合等效应，增强或调控MXene的性能，甚至实现多铁性。
>     
> 
> ### 4.3 实验与理论讨论：从计算预测到实验验证
> 
> 综述通过大量案例展示了“理论预测→实验验证”的闭环研究范式。
> 
> -   **纯粹计算分析**：例如，通过第一性原理计算，预测了a-MXene（如Nb₂CF₂）是一类罕见的**铁电金属**，并计算了其极化反转势垒和巨隧穿电阻效应，为后续实验提供了指导。
> -   **纯粹实验分析**：例如，通过压电力显微镜（PFM）和振动样品磁强计（VSM）等实验手段，直接观测到了V₂C MXene的铁电畴翻转和Gd掺杂Ti₃C₂的室温铁磁回线，证实了理论预测。
> -   **理论与实验结合**：例如，在Cu/MXene/PZT铁电忆阻器的研究中，实验发现了其低功耗、高开关比的优异性能，而随后的DFT计算则从原子层面解释了MXene如何降低Cu离子的迁移势垒，为性能提升提供了微观机制的解释。

> 本综述通过对大量文献的系统梳理，得出以下核心结论：

> 1.  **MXene是一个功能化的材料平台**：通过前驱体MAX相的多样性和蚀刻方法的精确控制，可以实现对MXene结构和表面官能团的原子级裁剪，这是其性能调控的基石。
> 2.  **铁电与铁磁性是可诱导与调控的**：尽管本征铁电与铁磁性在MXene中并不普遍，但通过**对称性破缺**（如官能团化、应变）和**自旋极化调控**（如掺杂、缺陷）等策略，可以成功地在多种MXene及其复合材料中实现并增强这些性质。

> ## 七、学术思考与批判性分析
> 
> 尽管本综述内容全面，但从批判性思维的角度，我们可以提出以下深度问题：
> 
> 1.  **“诱导”与“本征”的界限模糊**：论文中许多案例通过掺杂或复合来“诱导”铁电/铁磁性。那么，观测到的性能究竟是来自MXene本身被改性，还是来自掺杂/复合的次生相？例如，在MXene/Gd复合体系中，铁磁信号的来源是掺杂的Gd离子团簇还是Gd-MXene的均一相？这个问题关系到我们对MXene本征物理性质的准确理解。
> 2.  **铁电金属的悖论与现实**：论文中提到了“铁电金属”（如a-MXene）这一概念。传统铁电性要求材料是绝缘体，因为自由电子会屏蔽内部电场，使极化无法稳定存在。那么，如何在实验上严格区分“铁电金属”的极化翻转行为与普通的静电吸附或离子迁移现象？这是一个对实验表征要求极高的科学难题。
> 3.  **多铁性的耦合强度与实用性**：论文中展示的多铁性多为I型多铁，即铁电和铁磁起源不同，耦合较弱。这在应用上价值有限。真正的目标是实现II型多铁或强磁电耦合，使一个序参量可以被另一个序参量有效控制。论文中提及的几个案例，其磁电耦合系数是否足够大，达到了实用化水平？未来如何通过界面对称性设计来增强这种耦合？
> 4.  **性能的均匀性与可靠性**：MXene材料的一个显著特点是其表面官能团的随机性和不均匀性。这种原子尺度的化学无序性，对于宏观上需要高度一致性的铁电畴翻转和磁畴运动会产生怎样的影响？这可能是影响器件一致性和耐久性的根本原因，但论文中对此讨论较少。
> 5.  **计算预测的局限性**：论文引用了大量基于理想化结构（如完美晶体、均匀官能团）的DFT计算。然而，真实的MXene充满了缺陷、官能团混合和层间堆叠无序。这些理想化模型的预测结果在多大程度上能指导真实实验？未来的计算模拟是否需要更多地考虑统计性模型和真实环境的影响？

> ## 八、用户问题预测与解答
> 
> **问题1：MXene和石墨烯都是2D材料，它们有什么区别？为什么MXene更适合做铁电/铁磁应用？** **答**：两者都是明星2D材料，但本质不同。石墨烯是单质碳，其出色的性能源于其独特的狄拉克锥电子结构，但它是非磁性的，且没有带隙，难以产生铁电性。而MXene是一大类过渡金属碳/氮化物的总称，其化学组成和结构极其丰富，可以通过选择不同过渡金属（如Cr， Mn）直接引入磁性，也可以通过表面官能团（如-O， -F）破坏对称性来诱导铁电性。这种化学和结构上的高可调性，是MXene在铁电/铁磁领域比石墨烯更具优势的核心原因。
> 
> **问题2：什么是“表面官能团Tx”？它为什么这么重要？** **答**：在MXene合成过程中，通过蚀刻去除A原子层后，暴露出的M原子表面具有很高的活性，会立即与蚀刻液中的离子结合，形成化学基团，这就是表面官能团Tx。常见的Tx包括-O、-OH、-F、-Cl等。它们极其重要，可谓“改头换面”：1. **决定稳定性和分散性**：不同的Tx使MXene在不同溶剂中的分散性不同。2. **调控电子结构**：-O、-OH是电子受体，会降低费米能级，使MXene表现出p型半导体特性；而-F是电子给体，影响相反。3. **诱导功能性**：如前所述，特定官能团的不对称排列可直接导致铁电性。因此，控制Tx就是控制MXene的性能。
> 
> **问题3：铁电体、铁磁体和多铁体，这三者到底是什么关系？** **答**：我们可以用一个简单的类比来理解：
> 
> -   **铁电体**：就像一个“电开关”，它的正负电荷中心可以分离（形成电偶极子），并能被外部电压翻转方向。数据存储靠的是电荷极化方向。
> -   **铁磁体**：就像一个“磁开关”，它内部的微小磁矩能自发排列，并能被外部磁场翻转方向。数据存储靠的是磁化方向。
> -   **多铁体**：同时拥有“电开关”和“磁开关”的材料，相当于一个“多功能开关”。更神奇的是，在一些多铁体中，这两个开关还能联动，你可以用电场控制磁化方向，或用磁场控制电极化方向，这为开发低功耗、高密度的新型存储和逻辑器件提供了可能。
> 
> **问题4：在MXene中实现室温铁磁性，为什么这么难？** **答**：铁磁性源于原子磁矩的平行排列，但这种有序排列很容易被热运动破坏。当温度超过居里温度时，材料就会变成顺磁性。MXene是二维材料，其磁各向异性（即磁矩更容易沿某个方向排列）通常很弱。根据Mermin-Wagner理论，在二维各向同性体系中，热涨落会破坏长程磁有序，因此很难存在本征的室温铁磁序。要实现在MXene中的室温铁磁性，必须通过引入强自旋轨道耦合（如使用重元素）、增大磁各向异性或通过掺杂引入强局域磁矩等方式，来抵抗热涨落，稳定铁磁态。
> 
> **问题5：这篇综述对我一个刚入门的学生有什么实际的帮助？** **答**：这篇综述是你进入MXene功能物性研究领域的绝佳“地图”和“导航”。它的帮助是立体的：

> 1.  **知识体系构建**：它帮你快速梳理了从MAX相、合成蚀刻到MXene结构、性质、应用的全链条知识，省去了你从零散文献中拼凑信息的时间。
> 2.  **前沿方向把握**：它明确指出了当前的研究热点（铁电、铁磁、多铁）和主要挑战，让你知道“大家都在做什么”和“什么还没做”。
> 3.  **研究思路启发**：它总结了“如何诱导铁电/铁磁性”的多种策略（掺杂、官能团化、应变等），相当于给你提供了一个“实验方法工具箱”，你可以从中寻找灵感来设计自己的课题。

> 1.  **改变电子结构**：-O和-F是强电负性基团，会从MXene层中吸引电子，改变其导电性（从金属性变为半导体性）和磁性。
> 2.  **打破对称性**：如果面包两面的酱料不同（比如一面是-O，一面是-F），就会导致正负电荷中心不重合，从而产生本征的**铁电性**。这是诱导非铁电MXene产生铁电性的关键“开关”。
> 
> **Q3：什么是“电滞回线”和“磁滞回线”？为什么说它们是铁电/铁磁体的“身份证”？** **A3**：这两个回线是证明材料具有可翻转记忆效应的核心证据。
> 
> -   **电滞回线 (P-E loop)**：想象一个倔强的弹簧。施加一个正向电场（用力拉），材料内部的正负电荷分离（产生极化，弹簧伸长）。撤掉电场（松手），极化不会归零，而是保留一部分（剩余极化，弹簧不能完全缩回）。要消除这部分极化，必须施加一个反向电场（反向用力推）。这个“极化强度（P）随外加电场（E）变化而滞后”的闭合曲线就是电滞回线。它证明了材料有**两个可以稳定存在的极化状态**，且能被外加电场反复翻转，这是存储“0”和“1”的物理基础。
> -   **磁滞回线 (M-H loop)**：将上面的“电场”换成“磁场”，“电荷极化”换成“电子自旋磁矩”，就是磁滞回线。它证明了材料有**两个稳定的磁矩方向**，可用于磁存储（如硬盘）。
> 
> **Q4：多铁性听起来很完美，为什么在单一材料中这么难实现？** **A4**：这源于一个根本性的物理矛盾，被称为“**d⁰ vs dⁿ 问题**”。
> 
> -   **铁电性**通常需要过渡金属离子具有**空的d轨道（d⁰）**，比如Ti⁴⁺。这样，这些离子可以偏离中心位置，与周围的阴离子形成强共价键，导致正负电荷中心分离，产生极化。
> -   **铁磁性**则需要过渡金属离子具有**部分填充的d轨道（dⁿ）**，比如Cr³⁺，Mn²⁺。这些未成对的电子是产生净磁矩的根源。 你看，一个要空轨道，一个要半满轨道，这本身就是鱼与熊掌，难以兼得。因此，本征的多铁单相材料极为罕见。这也是为什么科学家们转而寻求通过**复合或构建异质结**，将负责铁电性和铁磁性的不同“任务”分给不同的材料或原子层来完成。
>

> 
> 1.  **加速锂离子传输**：这个内建电场可以作为一个额外的驱动力，帮助带正电的锂离子（Li⁺）更快地穿过电极/电解液界面，并进入电极材料内部，从而显著提升**快充能力（倍率性能）**。

> ABSTRACT 摘要
> 
> MXenes, 2D materials with high conductivity, adjustable surface terminations, and high magnetism, have garnered attention for their potential to resolve the energy crisis. The incorporation of doping and composite can induce ferroelectricity and ferromagnetism in their intrinsic state. The etching procedure in synthesis significantly impacts their magnetic and electronic characteristics. This review explores the ferromagnetic, ferroelectric, and multiferroic properties of MXenes, mainly transition metal carbides and nitrides, and discusses fabrication procedures. It also describes the potential applications in electronic devices, emphasizing the significance of MXenes' structural and functional properties for advanced technology. MXenes，一类具有高导电性、可调表面端基和高磁性的二维材料，因其解决能源危机的潜力而备受关注。通过掺杂和复合，可以在其本征态中诱导出铁电性和铁磁性。合成过程中的刻蚀步骤对其磁性和电子特性有显著影响。本综述探讨了MXene（主要是过渡金属碳化物和氮化物）的铁磁、铁电和多铁性能，并讨论了其制备过程。同时，本文也阐述了其在电子器件中的潜在应用，强调了MXene的结构和功能特性对于先进技术的重要性。 **Term**: MXenes: 一类二维过渡金属碳化物、氮化物或碳氮化物，具有独特的层状结构和优异的物理化学性质。 **Term**: 2D materials: 二维材料，指电子仅可在两个维度的纳米尺度上自由运动的材料。 **Term**: surface terminations: 表面端基，指在MXene合成过程中，附着于其表面的化学官能团。 **Term**: ferroelectricity: 铁电性，指材料具有自发极化，且该极化方向可被外加电场改变的特性。 **Term**: ferromagnetism: 铁磁性，指材料具有自发磁化，且该磁化方向可被外加磁场改变的特性。 **Term**: multiferroic: 多铁性，指材料同时具有两种或两种以上铁性有序（如铁电性、铁磁性、铁弹性）的特性。

> ## 1. Introduction
> 
> ## 1. 引言
> 
> Two-dimensional (2D) materials are gaining attention because of their impressive electrical, optical, magnetic, and bio-related applications. The discovery of graphene opened the gateway for the development of numerous 2D materials.[1] 二维(2D)材料因其出色的电学、光学、磁学和生物相关应用而备受关注。石墨烯的发现为众多二维材料的开发打开了大门。[1] **Term**: graphene: 石墨烯，一种由单层碳原子以sp²杂化轨道组成的六角型呈蜂巢晶格的二维碳纳米材料。
> 
> Numerous 2D materials have been introduced in the last twenty years, including single elements and compounds.[2] 在过去的二十年里，已经涌现出大量的二维材料，包括单质和化合物。[2]
> 
> Metal oxides, transition metal dichalcogenides (TMDs),[3] graphitic carbon nitride,[4] and double-layered hydroxides[5,6] are examples of compound 2D materials. 金属氧化物、过渡金属二硫族化物(TMDs)、[3] 石墨相氮化碳、[4] 以及双金属氢氧化物[5,6] 是化合物二维材料的例子。 **Term**: transition metal dichalcogenides (TMDs): 过渡金属二硫族化物，一类由过渡金属原子和硫族元素原子组成的层状二维材料。
> 
> The demand for better and more efficient materials has led to the invention of new advanced 2D materials. These materials have been widely investigated in recent eras, as they have high bandgap and improved optical, electronic, chemical, and electrochemical properties.[7] 对更好、更高效材料的需求促使了新型先进二维材料的发明。近年来，这些材料因其高带隙和改善的光学、电子、化学及电化学性质而被广泛研究。[7]
> 
> Due to this, they can be used in different applications ranging from energy storage to disease treatments.[8] 因此，它们可以应用于从能量存储到疾病治疗等不同领域。[8]
> 
> MAX phases are a group of ternary carbides/nitrides. They are a nanolaminated layered material that possesses interesting and unique properties.[9] MAX相是一组三元碳化物/氮化物。它们是一种纳米层状材料，具有有趣且独特的性质。[9] **Term**: MAX phases: MAX相，一类具有六方层状结构的三元过渡金属碳化物或氮化物，其化学式为 M_{n+1}AX_n。
> 
> The MAX phase was first discovered in 1960 by Helga Rohde and Hans Kudielka, who found Ti2SC and Zr2SC.[10] MAX相最早于1960年由Helga Rohde和Hans Kudielka发现，他们发现了Ti2SC和Zr2SC。[10]
> 
> Afterwards, the International Union of Pure and Applied Chemistry (IUPAC) added more element columns to the periodic table in 1988. At that time researchers discovered Ti3SiC2 and Ti3GeC2 MAX phases, where the value of n ranges from 1 to 2.[11,12] 随后，国际纯粹与应用化学联合会(IUPAC)于1988年在元素周期表中增加了更多元素列。那时，研究人员发现了Ti3SiC2和Ti3GeC2 MAX相，其中n的值范围从1到2。[11,12] **Term**: IUPAC: 国际纯粹与应用化学联合会，一个致力于制定化学命名、术语和标准方法的国际组织。
> 
> In 1994, a student named Schuster contributed to this journey by inventing new types of MAX phase, i.e., Ti3AlC2.[13,14] 1994年，一位名叫Schuster的学生通过发明新型MAX相（即Ti3AlC2）为这一进程做出了贡献。[13,14]
> 
> The value of n reached 3 when the researcher discovered a new MAX phase in 1999, i.e., the Ti4AlN3 phase (where n + 1 = 4).[15] 当研究人员在1999年发现一种新的MAX相，即Ti4AlN3相（其中n+1=4）时，n的值达到了3。[15]
> 
> The MAX phase was spotlighted when Barsoum and El-Raghy introduced the Ti3SiC2 phase with unique properties. After that, they have worked on different types of MAX phases. Their discovery was then used as a basis to explore the MAX phase within the scientific community further. 当Barsoum和El-Raghy介绍了具有独特性质的Ti3SiC2相后，MAX相开始受到关注。此后，他们研究了不同类型的MAX相。他们的发现随后被用作科学界进一步探索MAX相的基础。
> 
> In the MAX phase, the M denotes the early transitional metal (e.g., Ti, Nb, Mo, Cr, V, etc.), A represents the group XIII or XIV elements (such as Al, Si, Ga, etc), while X is carbon and/or nitrogen (Figure 1a).[16] 在MAX相中，M表示早期过渡金属（如Ti, Nb, Mo, Cr, V等），A代表第XIII或XIV族元素（如Al, Si, Ga等），而X是碳和/或氮（图1a）。[16]
> 
> The chemical formula used for the MAX phase is Mn+1AXn. Here, n is a variable that ranges from 1 to 3. MAX phase is an amazing processable material. They possess different applications, some of which are preferably tailorable, while others exhibit good thermal shock resistance and metallic conductivity.[9,17–23] MAX相使用的化学式是M_{n+1}AX_n。这里，n是一个从1到3的变量。MAX相是一种令人惊叹的可加工材料。它们具有不同的应用，其中一些是优选可定制的，而另一些则表现出良好的抗热震性和金属导电性。[9,17–23]
> 
> The properties of the MAX phase depend on its composition and structure. The M layers are closely packed with the layer of X atoms; in between, a layer of A elements fills the octahedral sites. MAX相的性质取决于其组成和结构。M层与X原子层紧密堆积；在两者之间，一层A元素填充了八面体位置。
> 
> When the A element gets removed from the MAX phase, it is known as MXene. These layers are so closely packed that they cannot be removed by mechanical pressure; they can only be removed via a chemical reaction known as etching. 当A元素从MAX相中被移除时，得到的产物被称为MXene。这些层结合得如此紧密，以至于无法通过机械压力将其移除；它们只能通过一种称为刻蚀的化学反应来移除。
> 
> M. Naguib et al. introduced the first MXene, i.e., Ti3C2Tx, in 2011 by removing the Al element from the Ti2AlC2 phase using hydrofluoric (HF) acid as an etchant. Here, Tx replaces the A element.[24] M. Naguib等人于2011年通过使用氢氟酸(HF)作为刻蚀剂，从Ti2AlC2相中移除Al元素，制备了第一个MXene，即Ti3C2Tx。这里，Tx取代了A元素。[24] **Term**: hydrofluoric (HF) acid: 氢氟酸，一种强腐蚀性酸，常用于MXene的刻蚀制备。
> 
> Transition metal carbides and/or nitrides, also known as MXenes, are the newly introduced 2D material with tremendous electrical, optical, and chemical applications. MXene's general chemical formula is Mn+1XnTx.[25] 过渡金属碳化物和/或氮化物，也称为MXenes，是新近引入的二维材料，具有巨大的电学、光学和化学应用潜力。MXene的通用化学式为M_{n+1}X_nT_x。[25]
> 
> Tx represents the surface terminations, a functional group formed during the etching procedure. The type of surface termination groups depends on the concentration and etching method used.[26,27] Tx代表表面端基，即在刻蚀过程中形成的官能团。表面端基的类型取决于所使用的刻蚀剂浓度和刻蚀方法。[26,27]
> 
> For instance, the MXene made via using HF solution for etching was terminated by O, OH, or F elements because of high surface energy. By following another method, MXene can be terminated by more elements like (Cl, S, Br, etc.). The new report showed that these groups can be removed by following some protocols.[28,29] 例如，使用HF溶液刻蚀制备的MXene，由于表面能高，其端基为O、OH或F元素。通过采用其他方法，MXene的端基可以包含更多元素，如Cl, S, Br等。最新报告显示，这些基团可以通过遵循某些方案被移除。[28,29]
> 
> In that case, the chemical formula of MXene becomes Mn+1Xn, which is known as pristine MXene.[26,28,30] 在这种情况下，MXene的化学式变为M_{n+1}X_n，这被称为本征MXene。[26,28,30] **Term**: pristine MXene: 本征MXene，指无表面端基的MXene。
> 
> MAX phases are naturally three-dimensional (3D), whereas after etching the 'A' elements from MAX phases, the resultant MXene has a 2D structure. MAX相本质上是三维(3D)的，而将‘A’元素从MAX相中刻蚀掉后，得到的MXene具有二维结构。
> 
> Due to its 2D structure, MXene possesses an increased reaction rate, enhanced surface area, high electrical conductivity, and mechanical properties.[27] 由于其二维结构，MXene具有更高的反应速率、更大的比表面积、高导电性和良好的机械性能。[27]
> 
> The increased surface area enhances the electric, catalytic, and energy storage applications of MXene. 增大的比表面积增强了MXene在电学、催化和能量存储方面的应用。
> 
> MXene is chemically more stable as compared to the MAX phase because of the presence of weakly bonded A group elements in MAX phases. These reasons combine to make MXene a superior choice in applications compared to the MAX phase. MXene在化学上比MAX相更稳定，因为MAX相中存在弱键合的A族元素。这些原因共同使得MXene在应用中比MAX相更优越。
> 
> X. H. Wang et al. synthesized Ti3C2 by providing heat treatment in a C-rich atmosphere to remove the Si element from the Ti3SiC2 phase.[21] X. H. Wang等人通过在富碳气氛中进行热处理，从Ti3SiC2相中移除Si元素，合成了Ti3C2。[21]
> 
> The M-X bond is a combination of metallic, ionic, and covalent bonds and has a hybrid character, which is strong compared to the M-A metallic bond.[31] Therefore, the M-A bond breaks down during the etching procedure.[32] M-X键是金属键、离子键和共价键的混合体，具有杂化特性，比M-A金属键更强。[31] 因此，在刻蚀过程中M-A键会断裂。[32]
> 
> Due to the various combinations of elements in the MAX phase, the researchers are trying to fabricate new MXenes. Sokol et al. reported about 155 different MAX phases in their publication.[33] This number has doubled in the past four years. Based on these MAX phases, around 55 MXenes have been discovered so far. 由于MAX相中元素组合的多样性，研究人员正试图制备新的MXenes。Sokol等人在其出版物中报道了大约155种不同的MAX相。[33] 这个数字在过去四年里翻了一番。基于这些MAX相，迄今为止已发现约55种MXenes。
> 
> As a unique material with a combination of fascinating properties, MXene exhibits a variety of applications. But they can be divided into five major types: electrical (batteries, supercapacitors),[34] magnetic (electromagnetic interference (EMI) effect),[35] optical (photonics, lasers),[36] mechanical, and layer structure.[37] 作为一种兼具迷人特性的独特材料，MXene展现出多种应用。但这些应用主要可分为五大类：电学（电池、超级电容器）[34]、磁学（电磁干扰(EMI)效应）[35]、光学（光子学、激光器）[36]、力学以及层状结构。[37] **Term**: supercapacitors: 超级电容器，一种介于传统电容器和电池之间的储能装置，具有高功率密度和长循环寿命。 **Term**: electromagnetic interference (EMI): 电磁干扰，指电磁波对电子设备或系统性能产生的干扰现象。
>

This review provides guidance and offers valuable insights for future researchers to better understand the transition metal carbides and/or nitrides in energy storage devices. 此外，还讨论了MXene及MXene基复合材料在储能应用中面临的未来前景和主要挑战。本综述为未来的研究人员更好地理解过渡金属碳化物和/或氮化物在储能器件中的应用提供了指导，并提供了宝贵的见解。

> ## 2. MAX to MXene
> 
> ## 2. 从MAX相到MXene
> 
> The fabrication of MXene can be achieved by following two different routes: the top-down approach and the Bottom-up approach.[38–40] MXene的制备可以通过两种不同的路线实现：自上而下法和自下而上法。[38–40] **Term**: top-down approach: 自上而下法，一种从宏观块体材料出发，通过物理或化学方法剥离、刻蚀得到纳米材料的制备方法。 **Term**: Bottom-up approach: 自下而上法，一种从原子、分子等微观单元出发，通过组装、反应生成纳米材料的制备方法。
> 
> The top-down approach is mostly used as compared to a bottom-up approach. The top-down approach is preferred as it is simple and has little or no effect on the material during the chemical reaction.[41] 与自下而上法相比，自上而下法使用更为广泛。自上而下法更受青睐，因为它简单，且在化学反应过程中对材料影响很小或没有影响。[41]
> 
> In the top-down approach, the first step involves preparing the layered precursors, known as the MAX phase. The structure of MXene's crystal is defined by its MAX phase. Second, the A group elements are exfoliated, due to which weak multilayers of MXene are formed.[38,42] 在自上而下法中，第一步是制备层状前驱体，即MAX相。MXene的晶体结构由其MAX相决定。第二步，剥离A族元素，由此形成弱的MXene多层结构。[38,42]
> 
> During the etching procedure, the surface termination, Tx, gets attached to the M-X bonds from the etchant. Lastly, the delamination of MXene layers is performed to convert the multilayers into single or few-layer nanosheets (Figure 2b).[44,45] 在刻蚀过程中，来自刻蚀剂的表面端基Tx会附着在M-X键上。最后，对MXene层进行分层，以将多层转化为单层或少层纳米片（图2b）。[44,45] **Term**: delamination: 分层，指将多层MXene剥离成单层或少层纳米片的过程。
> 
> Delamination refers to the separation of MXene layers into individual 2D sheets. MXene with single or few layers has higher adhesive energy than multilayer MXene because the Ti3C2Tx multilayer MXene has a stronger dipole moment and higher polarity. 分层指的是将MXene层分离成独立的二维薄片。单层或少层MXene比多层MXene具有更高的粘附能，因为多层Ti3C2Tx MXene具有更强的偶极矩和更高的极性。 **Term**: dipole moment: 偶极矩，衡量分子中极性大小和方向的物理量。
> 
> MXene is divided into two types, i.e., single transition metal (e.g., Ti3C2) and double transition metal (e.g., Mo2TiC2) (Figure 2c). MXene分为两种类型，即单过渡金属MXene（如Ti3C2）和双过渡金属MXene（如Mo2TiC2）（图2c）。
> 
> The general crystal structure of MXene is hexagonally close-packed (HCP). Nevertheless, B. Anasori et al. stated that when the value of n is 1 in MXene, i.e., the M atoms show an HCP pattern (ABABAB), but when n is 2 or 3, the M atoms exhibit FCC pattern (ABCABC).[29] MXene的一般晶体结构是六方密堆积(HCP)。然而，B. Anasori等人指出，当MXene中n的值为1时，即M原子显示HCP排列(ABABAB)，但当n为2或3时，M原子表现出FCC排列(ABCABC)。[29] **Term**: hexagonally close-packed (HCP): 六方密堆积，一种晶体中原子的密堆积方式。 **Term**: FCC: 面心立方 (Face-Centered Cubic) 的缩写，一种晶体结构。
> 
> ### 2.1. Etching techniques of MXene
> 
> ### 2.1. MXene的刻蚀技术
> 
> The etching procedure plays a crucial role in the synthesis of MXene, as it significantly affects its composition and structure. If the applied temperature is high during the reaction, the M-X bonds will break with M-A bonds, whose structure will be 3D rock-like.[26] 刻蚀过程在MXene的合成中起着至关重要的作用，因为它显著影响其组成和结构。如果反应过程中施加的温度过高，M-X键会与M-A键一起断裂，其结构将变为三维岩石状。[26]
> 
> Similarly, if the amount of corrosive chlorine used is high, the M atoms will also be etched out with A elements; that's why these two factors should be checked while choosing an etching procedure for better A element etching. 同样，如果使用的腐蚀性氯气量过多，M原子也会与A元素一起被刻蚀掉；这就是为什么在选择刻蚀方法以更好地刻蚀A元素时，应考虑这两个因素。
> 
> The most commonly used etching method is wet acid etching, which utilizes an HF solution. As we know, HF is a hazardous chemical, so this method is unsafe for researchers and the environment. Another method that has gained attention is Molten salt, as it is non-toxic. Other etching methods include fluoride salt/strong acid etching, NH4HF2 etching, and fluorine-free etching (molten salt etching, electrochemical etching, and chemical vapor deposition (CVD) etching).[46] 最常用的刻蚀方法是湿法酸刻蚀，它使用HF溶液。我们知道，HF是一种危险化学品，因此这种方法对研究人员和环境都不安全。另一种受到关注的方法是熔融盐法，因为它无毒。其他刻蚀方法包括氟化物盐/强酸刻蚀、NH4HF2刻蚀和无氟刻蚀（熔融盐刻蚀、电化学刻蚀和化学气相沉积(CVD)刻蚀）。[46] **Term**: chemical vapor deposition (CVD): 化学气相沉积，一种通过气体反应物在基底表面发生化学反应，生成固态薄膜或涂层的方法。
> 
> Details of the etching procedures are given in Table 1. 刻蚀方法的详细信息见表1。
> 
> The etching procedure plays a crucial role in determining the properties and applications of MXene. The properties of MXene depend on the elements present in the MAX phase and etching procedure. MXene exhibits superior electrical properties compared to metals and traditional semiconductor materials, which is driving daily research on this material. 刻蚀过程在决定MXene的性质和应用方面起着至关重要的作用。MXene的性质取决于MAX相中存在的元素和刻蚀过程。与金属和传统半导体材料相比，MXene表现出更优越的电学性质，这推动了人们对这种材料的日常研究。
> 
> MXene is suitable for energy storage applications due to its high surface area, which provides ample active sites.[41] MXene因其高比表面积而适用于能量存储应用，这提供了充足的活性位点。[41]
> 
> To investigate the hidden properties (i.e., magnetic and electric properties) of MXene in advanced technologies like energy storage and memory storage, experimental and computational work is carried out.[64] 为了探究MXene在诸如能量存储和记忆存储等先进技术中的隐藏特性（即磁学和电学特性），人们开展了实验和计算工作。[64]
> 
> Memory storage devices require a hysteresis loop, which can only be formed in ferroelectric and ferromagnetic materials. These two are the types of electric and magnetic properties. We briefly discuss ferroelectricity and ferromagnetism in materials and their importance in energy storage applications. 记忆存储器件需要回滞回线，这只能在铁电和铁磁材料中形成。这两者属于电学和磁学性质的范畴。我们简要讨论了材料中的铁电性和铁磁性及其在能量存储应用中的重要性。 **Term**: hysteresis loop: 回滞回线，在铁电或铁磁材料中，极化强度或磁化强度随外加电场或磁场变化而形成的闭合曲线，表征了材料的铁电或铁磁性能。
> 
> Chandrasekaran et al. reported the first ever MXene with intrinsic ferroelectricity. According to his analysis, the Sc2CO2 MXene possesses a ferroelectric nature and reversible polarization.[65] Chandrasekaran等人报道了首个具有本征铁电性的MXene。根据他的分析，Sc2CO2 MXene具有铁电性质和可翻转的极化。[65] **Term**: intrinsic ferroelectricity: 本征铁电性，指材料在不依赖外部因素（如掺杂、应变）的条件下，自身结构所产生的铁电性。
> 
> Afterwards, Wijethunge et al. reported that another MXene (i.e., Nb2NF2) has ferroelectric nature, as it exhibits good reversible polarization in the out-of-plane route.[66] 随后，Wijethunge等人报道了另一种MXene（即Nb2NF2）具有铁电性质，因为它在面外方向上表现出良好的可翻转极化。[66]
> 
> Not all MXenes can naturally have ferroelectric (FE) and ferromagnetic (FM) properties. These properties can be induced in MXene through external factors, such as vacant formation, applied electric field, and doping of other molecules on it. 并非所有MXene都能天然具有铁电(FE)和铁磁(FM)性质。这些性质可以通过外部因素在MXene中诱导产生，例如空位形成、施加电场以及在其上掺杂其他分子。
> 
> ### 2.2. Ferroelectricity and ferromagnetism in MXene
> 
> ### 2.2. MXene中的铁电性与铁磁性
> 
> Ferromagnetism belongs to the type of magnetism in which the materials can attain continuous magnetization at the atomic scale, even if the external magnetic field is removed. When placed in an external magnetic field, the ferromagnetic material stores magnetism for some time, and when the applied magnetic field is removed, it forms a hysteresis loop. 铁磁性属于磁性的一种类型，在这种类型中，即使移除外加磁场，材料也能在原子尺度上保持连续的磁化。当置于外加磁场中时，铁磁材料会储存一段时间的磁性，当外加磁场被移除时，它会形成一个回滞回线。
> 
> Ferroelectric materials are named after ferromagnetic materials (FM), as they have a permanent electric dipole movement similar to the permanent magnetic dipoles present in FM materials (e.g., Fe).[67] 铁电材料因铁磁(FM)材料而得名，因为它们具有永久的电偶极矩运动，类似于铁磁材料中存在的永久磁偶极子（例如，铁）。[67]
> 
> The ferroelectric materials (FE) form a hysteresis loop as they have permanent polarization that can be switched in opposite directions when the external field is applied.[68] 铁电(FE)材料形成回滞回线，因为它们具有永久的极化，当施加外场时，该极化可以翻转到相反方向。[68]
> 
> In these materials, the direction of dipoles can change in at least two directions, depending on the orientation of the external electric field. The main difference between ferroelectric and ferromagnets is that the ferroelectrics possesses spontaneous polarization upon the applied external electric field. In contrast, ferromagnets exhibit spontaneous magnetization by an external magnetic field, as shown in Figure 3.[69] 在这些材料中，偶极子的方向至少可以在两个方向上改变，这取决于外加电场的方向。铁电体和铁磁体之间的主要区别在于，铁电体在施加外电场时具有自发极化。相反，铁磁体在施加外磁场时表现出自发磁化，如图3所示。[69] **Term**: spontaneous polarization: 自发极化，指在没有外加电场的情况下，铁电材料内部由于正负电荷中心不重合而产生的极化。
> 
> The switchable polarization nature of ferroelectric materials enables their use in various advanced applications, such as electronic devices and data storage devices, including nonvolatile memories, logic-in-memory devices, and negative capacitance. 铁电材料可翻转极化的特性使其能够用于各种先进应用，例如电子器件和数据存储设备，包括非易失性存储器、存内逻辑器件和负电容。
> 
> Due to their unique performance in nanoelectronics and microelectronics, Ferroelectrics have garnered considerable attention from researchers in recent years. 由于其在纳米电子学和微电子学中的独特性能，铁电体近年来引起了研究人员的极大关注。
> 
> In the beginning, 3D materials were used as ferroelectrics, for example, BaTiO3 (BTO), perovskite oxides PbZr0.2Ti0.8O3 (PZT), Hf1-xZrxO (HZO), etc.[70–72] 最初，三维材料被用作铁电体，例如，BaTiO3 (BTO)、钙钛矿氧化物PbZr0.2Ti0.8O3 (PZT)、Hf1-xZrxO (HZO)等。[70–72] **Term**: perovskite oxides: 钙钛矿氧化物，一类具有与钙钛矿(CaTiO3)相同晶体结构的氧化物材料，通式为ABO3。
> 
> But with the increasing demand for slim electronic devices, the 3D materials became difficult to use in ferroelectrics because of their large thickness and presence of structural and chemical discontinuities on the surface (e.g., defects or dead layer) as well as the influence of depolarization and screening field, causing low stability in its polarization properties (Figure 3a).[72,73] 但随着对轻薄电子器件需求的增加，3D材料因其厚度大、表面存在结构和化学不连续性（如缺陷或死层）以及退极化场和屏蔽场的影响，导致其极化性能稳定性低，因此在铁电体中的应用变得困难（图3a）。[72,73] **Term**: dead layer: 死层，指铁电材料表面由于缺陷、应力等原因导致极化性能退化或消失的薄层。 **Term**: depolarization: 退极化，指铁电体内部产生的与自发极化方向相反的电场，会削弱甚至消除自发极化。
> 
> As shown in Figure 4a, the screening charge of ferroelectric capacitance is caused by the dead layer, while the final screening field (Escreen) can affect the out-of-plane polarization. The influence of the dead layer becomes more prominent as the thickness of the 3D materials is reduced. 如图4a所示，铁电电容的屏蔽电荷由死层引起，而最终的屏蔽场(Escreen)会影响面外极化。随着3D材料厚度的减小，死层的影响变得更加显著。
> 
> Despite this, the 2D materials are more suitable as they have stable out-of-plane and in-plane polarization at the nanoscale to microscale (Figure 4b).[75–77] 尽管如此，二维材料更为合适，因为它们在纳米到微米尺度上具有稳定的面外和面内极化（图4b）。[75–77]
> 
> The absence of a dead layer complements the ferroelectric property in In2Se3 2D materials in Figure 4b.[74] 图4b中In2Se3二维材料中死层的缺失，有助于增强其铁电性能。[74]
> 
> The van der Waals behavior of 2D materials prevents the other materials from incorporating them; it is the main reason behind their outstanding properties. 二维材料的范德华特性阻止了其他材料与之结合；这是其优异性能的主要原因。 **Term**: van der Waals: 范德华力，分子间普遍存在的一种弱相互作用力。
> 
> Although they are amazing materials, very little theoretical and experimental work has been conducted on 2D ferroelectric materials. 尽管它们是令人惊叹的材料，但关于二维铁电材料的理论和实验工作却很少。
> 
> Interestingly, ferromagnetic and ferroelectric properties can be found in one material called "Multiferroic." This type of material combines amazing properties from both FE and FM materials. 有趣的是，铁磁性和铁电性可以在一种被称为“多铁性”的材料中找到。这种材料结合了铁电和铁磁材料的惊人特性。
> 
> There are generally two types of multiferroic materials, i.e., type-I multiferroics and type-II multiferroics.[78] 多铁性材料通常有两种类型，即I型多铁体和II型多铁体。[78]
> 
> Type-I multiferroics are materials in which ferroelectricity and magnetism generate (mostly independently) from different origins; however, there is still a connection between them. This type of multiferroic has high polarization and ferroelectricity (over magnetism) when high temperatures are applied. I型多铁体是指铁电性和磁性源自不同机制（大多相对独立）的材料；然而，它们之间仍然存在联系。这种类型的多铁体在高温下具有高极化强度和铁电性（优于磁性）。
> 
> In contrast, the type-II multiferroics have a stronger coupling between ferroelectricity and magnetism, in which the ferroelectricity is generated from magnetism, though they possess low polarization. This coupling is also known as magnetoelectric (ME) coupling. 相比之下，II型多铁体具有更强的铁电-磁性耦合，其中铁电性源自磁性，尽管它们具有低极化强度。这种耦合也称为磁电(ME)耦合。 **Term**: magnetoelectric (ME) coupling: 磁电耦合，指材料中铁电性和磁性之间存在相互作用的效应，电场可以改变磁化状态，磁场可以改变极化状态。
> 
> These properties in 2D MXene are significant as they can be used in slim electronic devices. Type 1 multiferroics typically exhibit strong ferroelectricity, accompanied by weak ferromagnetism. BiFeO3 is one of the most popular type 1 multiferroic materials. 这些在二维MXene中的特性意义重大，因为它们可用于轻薄电子器件中。I型多铁体通常表现出强铁电性，并伴有弱铁磁性。BiFeO3是最流行的I型多铁材料之一。
> 
> However, type II multiferroics have weaker ferroelectricity than type 1 multiferroic materials, for instance, TbMnO3. 然而，II型多铁体的铁电性比I型多铁材料弱，例如TbMnO3。
> 
> Detailed reviews on this topic are rare, as not much research has been done on the subject. Here, we review the recent research (both theoretical and experimental) on the ferroelectric and ferromagnetic properties of MXene, aiming to encourage scientific work for practical applications in energy storage. 关于此主题的详细综述很少，因为对此课题的研究并不多。在此，我们回顾了关于MXene铁电和铁磁性质的最新研究（理论和实验），旨在鼓励为能量存储的实际应用开展科学工作。

> ## 3. Ferroelectricity in MXene-based nanostructures
> 
> ## 3. MXene基纳米结构中的铁电性
> 
> It is rare to have ferroelectric polarization in 2D materials like MXene because MXene possesses a centrosymmetric structure that prohibits spontaneous polarization. Furthermore, due to surface depolarization, it isn't easy to stabilize the out-of-plane polarization in monolayer materials, which prevents the making of a stable ferroelectric state. 在像MXene这样的二维材料中，铁电极化是罕见的，因为MXene具有中心对称结构，这阻止了自发极化。此外，由于表面退极化效应，在单层材料中难以稳定面外极化，这阻碍了稳定铁电态的形成。
> 
> The first ferroelectricity observed in 2D MXene was in 2017 by a scientist Chandrasekaran. After that, many scientists have reported ferroelectric behavior in different types of MXene, among which some MXenes have intrinsic ferroelectricity, while some don't. 二维MXene中首次观察到铁电性是在2017年，由科学家Chandrasekaran发现。此后，许多科学家报道了不同类型MXene中的铁电行为，其中一些MXene具有本征铁电性，而另一些则没有。
> 
> We have divided this section into two parts: experimental work and computational work conducted on MXene. 我们将本节分为两部分：对MXene进行的实验工作和计算工作。
> 
> ### 3.1. Computational analysis
> 
> ### 3.1. 计算分析
> 
> For those MXenes that don't have intrinsic ferroelectricity, doping or making a composite of them with other ferroelectric materials can induce ferroelectric properties in their nanostructures. 对于那些没有本征铁电性的MXene，对其进行掺杂或与其他铁电材料复合，可以在其纳米结构中诱导铁电性能。
> 
> Using the first principle calculation, Lei Zhang et al.[79] predicted the intrinsic ferroelectricity in three types of MXenes by using density functional theory (DFT). Lei Zhang et al. found that all three MXenes (i.e., Nb2CS2 and Ta2CS2; Sc2CO2 and Y2CO2; Sc2CS2 and Y2CS2) showed high levels of piezoelectric response and ferroelectric polarization. 使用第一性原理计算，Lei Zhang等人[79]利用密度泛函理论(DFT)预测了三种MXene的本征铁电性。Lei Zhang等人发现，所有三种MXene（即Nb2CS2和Ta2CS2；Sc2CO2和Y2CO2；Sc2CS2和Y2CS2）都显示出高水平的压电响应和铁电极化。 **Term**: first principle calculation: 第一性原理计算，一种基于量子力学原理，不依赖经验参数，从最基本的物理定律出发计算材料性质的方法。 **Term**: density functional theory (DFT): 密度泛函理论，一种用于研究多电子体系电子结构的量子力学方法，广泛应用于物理、化学和材料科学领域。
> 
> By doing theoretical calculations, the authors found that the MXene sheets increased the ferroelectric polarization in BaTiO3 nanocrystals. It is because the MXene nanosheets changed the morphology of BaTiO3 nanocrystals and Ti-O bonds (Figure 4b). 通过理论计算，作者发现MXene纳米片增强了BaTiO3纳米晶中的铁电极化。这是因为MXene纳米片改变了BaTiO3纳米晶的形态和Ti-O键（图4b）。
> 
> Finally, a more recent computational study stated that Ti3C2Tx MXene (T = F or O; FMX) was coated with fluorographene (FG) to improve the dielectric constant and ferroelectricity.[80] 最后，一项更近期的计算研究表明，Ti3C2Tx MXene（T = F或O；FMX）被氟化石墨烯(FG)包覆，以提高介电常数和铁电性。[80] **Term**: dielectric constant: 介电常数，衡量材料在电场中储存电能能力的物理量。
> 
> The FMX@FG in ferroelectric polymer composite exhibits an improved dielectric constant and low ferroelectric loss. The reason for this performance is that the FG present in the composite will enhance the crystalline structure with excessive microcapacitor networks, extensive interfacial dipoles, and elevated b-phase crystallinity. 铁电聚合物复合材料中的FMX@FG展现出提高的介电常数和低铁电损耗。这种性能的原因在于，复合材料中的FG将通过过多的微电容器网络、广泛的界面偶极子和提高的b相结晶度来增强晶体结构。
> 
> By applying DFT calculations, the researcher[81] examined the 12 different types of M2CT2 MXenes; they found that three MXenes (i.e., Sc2CS2, Y2CS2, and Y2CO2) have ferroelectricity as the ground state, whereas the other MXenes attained ferroelectric nature when doped with Sc or O and external strains are applied. 通过应用DFT计算，研究人员[81]研究了12种不同类型的M2CT2 MXenes；他们发现三种MXene（即Sc2CS2, Y2CS2, 和Y2CO2）以铁电性为基态，而其他MXene在掺杂Sc或O以及施加外部应变时获得了铁电特性。 **Term**: ground state: 基态，指原子、分子或材料体系能量最低、最稳定的状态。
> 
> Exploring advanced ferroelectric materials, Yu-Jie Zeng et al.[82] revealed ferroelectricity in quin-layer MXene (i.e., M2CT2) via doing first principle calculations. This type of MXene consists of transition metal (M = Ti, Zr, Hf), carbon, and surface terminations (T = O, F, OH). 在探索先进铁电材料的过程中，Yu-Jie Zeng等人[82]通过第一性原理计算揭示了五层MXene（即M2CT2）中的铁电性。这种类型的MXene由过渡金属（M = Ti, Zr, Hf）、碳和表面端基（T = O, F, OH）组成。
> 
> By introducing surface terminations on the surface of MXene, not only disrupts the symmetry of the crystal structure but also deforms the atomic arrangement of the MXene layers, resulting in the asymmetric structure, which is essential for ferroelectric properties. 在MXene表面引入表面端基，不仅破坏了晶体结构的对称性，还使MXene层的原子排列变形，导致不对称结构，这对铁电性能至关重要。
> 
> The calculation results showed the co-existing out-of-plane (OOP) and in-plane (IP) electric polarizations. 计算结果显示，共存在面外(OOP)和面内(IP)电极化。
> 
> The high-order coupling between out-of-plane (OOP) and in-plane (IP) polarization enables more efficient and stable polarization under external electric fields, improving the ferroelectric nature of quin-layer MXene. 面外(OOP)和面内(IP)极化之间的高阶耦合使得在外加电场下能够实现更高效、更稳定的极化，从而改善了五层MXene的铁电特性。
> 
> Similarly, by modifying the structure and surface functionalization of MXene, Yinggan Zhang et al.[83] indicated ferroelectricity in a new family of MXene (i.e., M2CO2). These types of MXene have hexagonal phase structures and are known as a-MXenes because their atomic configurations are similar to a-In2Se3. 同样，通过改变MXene的结构和表面功能化，Yinggan Zhang等人[83]指出了一类新的MXene（即M2CO2）中的铁电性。这类MXene具有六方相结构，被称为a-MXenes，因为它们的原子构型类似于a-In2Se3。
> 
> Among all kinds of a-MXene, the Nb2CF2, Mo2CO2, Ta2CF2, W2CF2, and Mo2CF2 a-MXenes have been declared to be rare ferroelectric metals by the first principle calculations. 在所有种类的a-MXene中，Nb2CF2, Mo2CO2, Ta2CF2, W2CF2和Mo2CF2 a-MXenes被第一性原理计算宣布为罕见的铁电金属。
> 
> The authors changed the structure and surface terminations of MXene, which induces a spontaneous electric polarization, removing the point symmetry, and is important for the ferroelectric effect . 作者改变了MXene的结构和表面端基，这诱导了自发极化，消除了点对称性，对铁电效应至关重要。
> 
> The calculations revealed that a-MXenes demonstrate tunable ferroelectric properties having a polarization reversal energy barrier (Figure 5). 计算表明，a-MXenes展现出可调的铁电性能，并具有极化翻转能量势垒（图5）。
> 
> Furthermore, a-MXene-based ferroelectric tunnel junctions (FTJs) exhibit extraordinarily high tunneling electroresistance ratios (TERs); due to this reason, they are suitable for advanced ferroelectric memory devices. 此外，基于a-MXene的铁电隧道结(FTJs)展现出极高的隧穿电阻比(TERs)；因此，它们适用于先进的铁电存储器件。 **Term**: ferroelectric tunnel junctions (FTJs): 铁电隧道结，一种由两个金属电极夹着一层铁电薄膜组成的器件，其电阻状态可通过铁电层的极化方向进行调控。 **Term**: tunneling electroresistance ratios (TERs): 隧穿电阻比，衡量铁电隧道结高低电阻态比值的关键参数。
> 
> Ferroelectricity in MXene improved its performance in spintronics and photocatalytic applications. The electric field generated by the ferroelectric effect assisted a-MXenes in photocatalytic reaction by utilizing infrared light to control the band gap restriction of 1.23 eV. MXene中的铁电性提高了其在自旋电子学和光催化应用中的性能。铁电效应产生的电场通过利用红外光控制1.23 eV的带隙限制，辅助了a-MXenes在光催化反应中的应用。 **Term**: band gap: 带隙，指导带底与价带顶之间的能量差，决定了材料的光学和电学性质。

Nb2CTx a novel type of MXene, is reported to fabricate KNbO3 [98] and LiNbO3.[99] Standard ferroelectric force microscopy measurements confirmed their ferroelectricity as they showed great coercive field, saturation, and remnant polarization. 如上所述，MXene可以生长出另一种独特的铁电材料。据报道，一种新型MXene Nb2CTx可用于制备KNbO3 [98] 和LiNbO3。[99] 标准的铁电力显微镜测量证实了它们的铁电性，因为它们显示出优异的矫顽场、饱和极化和剩余极化。 **Term**: coercive field: 矫顽场，使铁电材料极化强度降为零所需的反向电场强度。 **Term**: remnant polarization: 剩余极化，撤除外加电场后，铁电材料中仍保留的极化强度值。
> 
> In previous papers, most researchers have discussed the ferroelectric properties in single-transition MXene. But after some time, ferroelectricity was also induced in double-transition MXene by introducing another advanced material. Syed Rizwan et al. first observed the ferroelectric behavior in Mo2TiC2 MXene film, where the researcher first synthesized double transition MXene (DTM) and Ni-doped MXene.[100] 在之前的论文中，大多数研究人员讨论了单过渡金属MXene中的铁电性能。但过了一段时间，通过引入另一种先进材料，双过渡金属MXene中也诱导出了铁电性。Syed Rizwan等人首次在Mo2TiC2 MXene薄膜中观察到了铁电行为，研究人员首先合成了双过渡金属MXene (DTM)和Ni掺杂的MXene。[100]

> ## 4. Ferromagnetism in MXene-based nanostructures
> 
> ## 4. MXene基纳米结构中的铁磁性
> 
> Ferromagnetic materials have a net magnetic moment, unlike antiferromagnetic materials; this feature is beneficial in spintronics applications.[102] 与反铁磁材料不同，铁磁材料具有净磁矩；这一特性在自旋电子学应用中非常有益。[102] **Term**: antiferromagnetic: 反铁磁，指材料中相邻原子或离子的磁矩反平行排列，宏观上不显示净磁矩的特性。
> 
> Most MXenes do not exhibit strong ferromagnetic (FM) properties. The magnetism present in MXene depends on its chemical composition and structure configuration, most importantly on the fulfillment of the d-orbitals.[64] 大多数MXenes不表现出强铁磁(FM)性能。MXene中存在的磁性取决于其化学组成和结构构型，最重要的是取决于d轨道的填充情况。[64]
> 
> It is rare for MXene to have ferromagnetism naturally, as MXene has strong M-T and M-X covalent bonds due to which they do not have instinctive magnetic properties. It has been reported that magnetization can be generated in MXene by doping with other materials, while some MXenes exhibit a natural magnetic behaviour. MXene天然具有铁磁性是罕见的，因为MXene具有强M-T和M-X共价键，因此它们不具备本征磁性。据报道，通过掺杂其他材料可以在MXene中产生磁化，而一些MXenes则表现出天然的磁性行为。
> 
> Nevertheless, the first time ferromagnetism was reported by Dong et al. in the Ti2MnC2Tx type of MXene if one or two layers of MXene are comprised.[103] 尽管如此，Dong等人首次报道了当MXene由一层或两层组成时，Ti2MnC2Tx型MXene中存在铁磁性。[103]
> 
> ### 4.1. Computational-based analysis
> 
> ### 4.1. 基于计算的分析
> 
> Using the first principle calculation, Yong Zhang et al. reported that Cr3C2 MXene is naturally a ferromagnetic material whose three Cr atoms are magnetically coupled.[103,104] 使用第一性原理计算，Yong Zhang等人报道了Cr3C2 MXene天然是一种铁磁材料，其三个Cr原子是磁耦合的。[103,104]
> 
> The MXene, whose early transition metal is Mn or Cr, generally has a spontaneous ferromagnetic moment, owing to half-filled 3-d orbitals. 早期过渡金属为Mn或Cr的MXene，由于3d轨道半充满，通常具有自发铁磁矩。
> 
> Using periodic boundary conditions and density functional theory calculations, the researcher demonstrated that Mn2C exhibits antiferromagnetic behavior and Mn2CO2 displays a ferromagnetic nature. Meanwhile, M2C and M2CO2 (M = Fe, Cr) naturally have a ferromagnetic ground state.[105] 使用周期性边界条件和密度泛函理论计算，研究人员证明了Mn2C表现出反铁磁行为，而Mn2CO2显示出铁磁特性。同时，M2C和M2CO2（M = Fe, Cr）天然具有铁磁基态。[105]
> 
> In contrast, the Cr-based MXene also exhibits ferromagnetism, for example, Cr2NOx,[106] and CrCO2,[107] but Cr2N[108] is an antiferromagnet. 相比之下，Cr基MXene也表现出铁磁性，例如Cr2NOx[106]和CrCO2[107]，但Cr2N[108]是反铁磁体。
> 
> The presence of functional groups (e.g., F, H, OH, or Cl) in Cr2N MXene affects its magnetic behavior in a way that it changes from a ferromagnet to an antiferromagnet.[109] Cr2N MXene中官能团（如F, H, OH或Cl）的存在会影响其磁性行为，使其从铁磁体转变为反铁磁体。[109]
> 
> In addition, the nitride MXene (i.e., X = N) shows intrinsic magnetic behavior.[64] The extra electrons in nitrogen generate magnetism, owing to their uniform spin moment and magnetic anisotropy. 此外，氮化物MXene（即X = N）显示出本征磁性行为。[64] 氮原子中的额外电子，由于其均匀的自旋矩和磁各向异性，产生了磁性。 **Term**: magnetic anisotropy: 磁各向异性，指材料的磁性随方向不同而变化的特性。
> 
> For instance, Hemant Kumar and his team found that nitrogen-based MXene such as Cr2NO2, Ti2NO2, and Mn2NTx (here T can be O, OH, and F) via performing first principle calculation.[110] 例如，Hemant Kumar及其团队通过第一性原理计算发现，氮化物基MXene如Cr2NO2, Ti2NO2, 和Mn2NTx（此处T可以是O, OH和F）具有磁性。[110]
> 
> Similarly, doing first principle calculations,[103] Hf2VC2O2, Hf2MnC2O2, and Ti2MnC2Tx (Tx = O, OH, and F) were also predicted to be ferromagnets considering their high Curie temperature and magnetic moments. 同样，通过第一性原理计算，[103] 考虑到其高居里温度和磁矩，Hf2VC2O2, Hf2MnC2O2, 和Ti2MnC2Tx (Tx = O, OH, and F) 也被预测为铁磁体。 **Term**: Curie temperature: 居里温度，指铁磁或亚铁磁材料转变为顺磁性的临界温度。
> 
> Moreover, Siriwardane et al. improved the magnetic properties of Hf2VC2O2 and Hf2MnC2O2 when biaxial strain is applied via density functional theory.[111] 此外，Siriwardane等人通过密度泛函理论，在施加双轴应变时，改善了Hf2VC2O2和Hf2MnC2O2的磁性能。[111]
> 
> ZrCO2 MXenes naturally do not possess ferromagnetic properties, but Altaf Ur Rahman et al.[112] induced ferromagnetism in ZrCO2 monolayer MXenes by doping Cr atoms via ab initio calculations. ZrCO2 MXenes天然不具备铁磁性能，但Altaf Ur Rahman等人[112]通过从头计算，通过掺杂Cr原子在ZrCO2单层MXenes中诱导了铁磁性。 **Term**: ab initio calculations: 从头计算，即第一性原理计算。
> 
> To understand the effect of the dopant, the authors doped transition metal on different sites of MXene (i.e., Zr-site, C-site, and O-site), as shown in Figure 9. 为了理解掺杂剂的影响，作者将过渡金属掺杂在MXene的不同位点上（即Zr位、C位和O位），如图9所示。
> 
> According to formation energy, the Cr atom is most ideally doped at the Zr site compared to others. 根据形成能，Cr原子最理想地掺杂在Zr位，而不是其他位点。
> 
> All possible sites can be obtained experimentally by exothermic reaction indicated by formation energies. The results from ferromagnetic and antiferromagnetic coupling calculations showed that the Cr atom doped at the Zr site has a ferromagnetic ground state. 所有可能的位点都可以通过由形成能指示的放热反应实验获得。铁磁和反铁磁耦合计算的结果表明，掺杂在Zr位的Cr原子具有铁磁基态。
> 
> The author explained that extrinsic ferromagnetism and half-metallicity can be generated via Cr substitutional doping at all possible sites in Zr2CO2 monolayer MXene. Ferromagnetism at room temperature and half-metallicity are the main features of spintronic device applications. 作者解释说，通过在Zr2CO2单层MXene中所有可能位点进行Cr替位掺杂，可以产生非本征铁磁性和半金属性。室温铁磁性和半金属性是自旋电子学器件应用的主要特征。 **Term**: half-metallicity: 半金属性，指材料中一种自旋方向的电子表现出金属性，而另一种自旋方向的电子表现出半导体性或绝缘性的特性。
> 
> In a recent report, Ping Li et al.[113] demonstrated that Cr2COOH is a ferromagnetic semiconductor by first principle calculations. The monolayer Cr2COOH MXene possesses spontaneous long-range magnetic order in its natural form. 在最近的一份报告中，Ping Li等人[113]通过第一性原理计算证明了Cr2COOH是一种铁磁半导体。单层Cr2COOH MXene在其自然形态下具有自发的长程磁序。
> 
> Each Cr atom possesses vdifferent 3d electron counts, i.e., Cr1 is 3d3 (3 mB) and Cr2 is 3d2 (2 mB), resulting in a total magnetic moment of 5 mB per unit cell, as shown in Figure 10. 每个Cr原子具有不同的3d电子数，即Cr1是3d3 (3 μB)，Cr2是3d2 (2 μB)，导致每个晶胞的总磁矩为5 μB，如图10所示。 **Term**: magnetic moment: 磁矩，描述磁性物体磁性强弱和方向的物理量。

> ### 4.2. Experiment-based analysis
> 
> ### 4.2. 基于实验的分析
> 
> Whereas Yunliang Yue et al. described that Fe2C, a type of pristine MXene, is an intrinsic ferromagnetic material.[114] 而Yunliang Yue等人描述道，Fe2C，一种本征MXene，是一种本征铁磁材料。[114]
> 
> Similarly, Zr2N can be converted into a ferromagnet by applying a certain amount of strain, i.e., higher than 4%,[115] as Zr2N was naturally an antiferromagnet. 同样，通过施加一定量的应变，即高于4%，Zr2N可以从天然的反铁磁体转变为铁磁体。[115]
> 
> In functionalized MXene, the ground state remains retained, which is important for ferromagnetism.[106,109] 在功能化的MXene中，基态得以保留，这对铁磁性至关重要。[106,109]
> 
> Most importantly, the functional group affects the magnetic property in MXene because it comes from the free electron attached to the surface of the transition metal.[116] 最重要的是，官能团影响MXene的磁性，因为它来自附着在过渡金属表面的自由电子。[116]
> 
> Among all types of MXenes, the Ti3C2 is the most researched and popular one, as it was discovered first.[117] We will now discuss some of the work done on the magnetic properties of Ti3C2 MXene. 在所有类型的MXenes中，Ti3C2是研究和应用最广泛的一种，因为它是最早被发现的。[117] 我们现在将讨论一些关于Ti3C2 MXene磁性方面的工作。
> 
> Exploring the ferromagnetic properties of Ti3C2 MXene, Sunaina Rafiq et al. induced strong ferromagnetism in Ti3C2 MXene by doping gadolinium (Gd+3) on it, resulting in a steady FM hysteresis loop (Figure 11).[118] 在探索Ti3C2 MXene的铁磁性能时，Sunaina Rafiq等人通过在Ti3C2 MXene上掺杂钆(Gd+3)离子，在Ti3C2 MXene中诱导出强铁磁性，形成了一个稳定的FM回滞回线（图11）。[118]
> 
> The ferromagnetism increases in MXene because of the free electrons carried by the Gd+3 cation. In MXene, the Ti-3d has a countable spin density at the Fermi level, due to which the ferromagnetism increases when doping the Gd+3 cation, forming a soft ferromagnetic composite. 由于Gd+3阳离子携带的自由电子，MXene中的铁磁性增强。在MXene中，Ti-3d在费米能级具有可数的自旋密度，因此当掺杂Gd+3阳离子时，铁磁性增强，形成软铁磁复合材料。 **Term**: Fermi level: 费米能级，在绝对零度下，电子占据的最高能级。
> 
> Furthermore, Mehroz Iqbal et al. demonstrated the novel idea of the co-existence of ferromagnetic-antiferromagnetic properties in both Ti3C2 and La-doped MXene by doing calculations and experimental work.[119] 此外，Mehroz Iqbal等人通过计算和实验工作，证明了Ti3C2和La掺杂MXene中同时存在铁磁-反铁磁性能的新观点。[119]
> 
> Similar to the previous paper, doping other materials on the surface of MXene can induce ferromagnetism. Jameela Fatheema et al. performed theoretical calculations as well as experimental work and found that doping niobium (Nb) on MXene induced ferromagnetism in the Nb/Ti3C2 composite, Figure 12a,b, considering the stable structure of Nb, which does not require other functional groups for surface terminations.[120] 与之前的论文类似，在MXene表面掺杂其他材料可以诱导铁磁性。Jameela Fatheema等人进行了理论计算和实验工作，发现考虑到Nb的稳定结构（不需要其他官能团进行表面端基化），在MXene上掺杂铌(Nb)在Nb/Ti3C2复合物中诱导了铁磁性，如图12a,b所示。[120]
> 
> In his recent work, Tej B Limbu et al. enhanced the ferromagnetic properties in MXene by performing a chemical reduction reaction.[122] During the response, MXene was chemically reduced by using L-ascorbic acid, so the ferromagnetic transition temperature of Ti3C2Tx MXene shifted from 50k to 150k. 在他最近的工作中，Tej B Limbu等人通过进行化学还原反应增强了MXene中的铁磁性能。[122] 在反应过程中，使用L-抗坏血酸化学还原MXene，使得Ti3C2Tx MXene的铁磁转变温度从50K移到了150K。
> 
> Other examples of dopants that can induce ferromagnetism in MXenes are BiFeO3,[123] nitrogen,[98,124] and SiCNWs@Co/C.[125] 其他可以在MXenes中诱导铁磁性的掺杂剂例子包括BiFeO3, [123] 氮，[98,124] 以及SiCNWs@Co/C。[125]
> 
> On the other hand, V2C is a newly discovered pristine MXene, which has also been reported to have strong ferromagnetic properties by twisting the angle of its nanosheets.[126] 另一方面，V2C是一种新发现的本征MXene，据报道，通过扭转其纳米片的角度，它也具有强铁磁性。[126]
> 
> This twisting breaks the interlayer symmetry of V2C, which rearranges the density of electron configuration around the Fermi level, enhancing the Stoner ferromagnetism of MXene. 这种扭转打破了V2C的层间对称性，重新排列了费米能级附近的电子构型密度，增强了MXene的Stoner铁磁性。 **Term**: Stoner ferromagnetism: Stoner铁磁性，一种基于能带理论解释金属铁磁性起源的模型，认为当特定能带的自旋劈裂导致能量降低时，会产生铁磁性。
> 
> However, some DTM have a higher Curie temperature than Ti3C2; for instance, the TiHfC and TiZrC DTM are reported to be intrinsic ferromagnets. Meanwhile, some DTM, such as TiCrC and TiMoC, are antiferromagnets and nonferromagnets, respectively, which can be converted into a ferromagnet by applying tensile strain greater than 3% and 1%.[127] 然而，一些双过渡金属MXene (DTM)比Ti3C2具有更高的居里温度；例如，据报道TiHfC和TiZrC DTM是本征铁磁体。同时，一些DTM，如TiCrC和TiMoC，分别是反铁磁体和非铁磁体，它们可以通过施加大于3%和1%的拉伸应变转变为铁磁体。[127]
> 
> Carbonitride MXene is a type of MXene in which X is carbon and nitrogen (X = C and N). Kaifeng Wang et al.[121] induced the ferromagnetism in carbonitride MXene, i.e., Ti3CNCl2, by following the Lewis molten salt etching technique. 碳氮化物MXene是X为碳和氮（X = C和N）的一类MXene。Kaifeng Wang等人[121]通过采用Lewis熔融盐刻蚀技术，在碳氮化物MXene（即Ti3CNCl2）中诱导了铁磁性。
> 
> During the Lewis molten salt etching, the Co2+ incorporated with high temperature, due to which the MXene was able to exhibit ferromagnetic properties forming typical "S" shaped M-H curves (Figure 12c). 在Lewis熔融盐刻蚀过程中，Co2+在高温下掺入，因此MXene能够表现出铁磁性能，形成典型的“S”形M-H曲线（图12c）。

> ## 5. Multiferroic properties of MXene
> 
> ## 5. MXene的多铁性
> 
> Material that exhibits both ferroelectric and ferromagnetic properties is known as multiferroic (MF) material. Multiferroic materials are a new class of materials used in nonvolatile memory devices, as they can shift their magnetization, strain, or electrical polarization.[138,139] 同时表现出铁电性和铁磁性的材料被称为多铁(MF)材料。多铁材料是一类用于非易失性存储器件的新型材料，因为它们可以改变其磁化、应变或电极化状态。[138,139]
> 
> These materials are gaining attention as they offer structured writing and inexpensive reading, due to which they can overcome the challenges researchers face in random access memory (RAMS).[140–142] 这些材料因其提供结构化的写入和廉价的读取而备受关注，因此它们可以克服研究人员在随机存取存储器(RAMS)中面临的挑战。[140–142]
> 
> Multiferroic properties are difficult to present in 3D materials as they have bulk sizes. Meanwhile, 2D materials are favorable to obtain multiferroic behavior as they can lose fixed symmetry elements because of the reduction of their dimensions, which results in the induction of ferroelectric property. 由于3D材料具有体块尺寸，其多铁性难以呈现。同时，二维材料因其尺寸减小而可能失去固定的对称元素，从而诱导铁电性，因此有利于获得多铁行为。
> 
> Additionally, 2D materials such as MXene are the best candidates for external strain, surface adsorption, and electric field modulation since they offer a large surface area.[100] 此外，像MXene这样的二维材料，由于其提供大的表面积，是进行外部应变、表面吸附和电场调制的最佳候选者。[100]
> 
> However, the multiferroic behavior in MXene and 2D materials has not been reported extensively. 然而，关于MXene和二维材料中多铁行为的报道并不广泛。
> 
> Doing first-principle calculations, Mingyu Zhao et al.'s study concluded that the 2D MXene derivative, i.e., i-MXene (Ta2/3Fe1/3)2CO2, exhibits type-I multiferroic properties with a high magnetoelectric effect.[143] 通过第一性原理计算，Mingyu Zhao等人的研究得出结论，二维MXene衍生物，即i-MXene (Ta2/3Fe1/3)2CO2，表现出具有高磁电效应的I型多铁性。[143]
> 
> This study provided a strategy for identifying the multiferroic nature in 2D materials. 这项研究为识别二维材料中的多铁性提供了一种策略。
> 
> Meanwhile, the type-II multiferroic property was predicted in the monolayer of Hf2VC2F2 MXene, as the magnetism directly originates from the ferroelectricity.[144] 同时，在单层Hf2VC2F2 MXene中预测了II型多铁性，因为其磁性直接源于铁电性。[144]
> 
> In another report via doing density functional theory (DFT) calculation, the author[141,145] predicted the magnetoelectricity in Mo2NCl2 MXene monolayers. The main reason behind this behavior is that Mo ions have an unequal charge, due to which the out-of-plane polarization will be induced. 在另一份通过密度泛函理论(DFT)计算的报告中，作者[141,145]预测了Mo2NCl2 MXene单层中的磁电性。这种行为背后的主要原因是Mo离子具有不等价电荷，因此将诱导出面外极化。
> 
> Exploring the MF property in MXene, Rabia Tahir et al. examined the ferroelectric and ferromagnetic behavior in Ti3C2Tx film by doing experimental work.[146] 在探索MXene的多铁性时，Rabia Tahir等人通过实验工作研究了Ti3C2Tx薄膜中的铁电和铁磁行为。[146]
> 
> The first paper reported the multiferroic nature of 2D MXene at room temperature, which can be utilised for data storage applications.[147] 第一篇论文报道了二维MXene在室温下的多铁性，可用于数据存储应用。[147]
> 
> Continuing the work on MXene, Rabia Tahir et al. increased the ferroelectricity and induced the multiferroic property in double transition metal (DTM), i.e., Mo2TiC2Tx, by following a simple fabrication procedure (Figure 13).[100] 继续对MXene的研究，Rabia Tahir等人通过简单的制备过程，在双过渡金属MXene (DTM)，即Mo2TiC2Tx中，增强了铁电性并诱导了多铁性（图13）。[100]
> 
> Multiferroic DTM has excellent potential for use in nanodevices in the near future. 多铁性DTM在不久的将来在纳米器件中具有巨大的应用潜力。
> 
> On the other hand, Lazare et al.[148] reported that the magnetoelectric behavior was reported in 2D Ti3C2Tx MXene along with ferroelectric and ferromagnetic behavior by doping it with a nanoparticle, such as lanthanum (La) doped bismuth ferrite (BLFO), then co-doped it with La and selenium (Se) making a BLFSO nanoparticle. 另一方面，Lazare等人[148]报道了通过掺杂纳米颗粒，如镧(La)掺杂的铁酸铋(BLFO)，然后共掺杂La和硒(Se)制成BLFSO纳米颗粒，在二维Ti3C2Tx MXene中观察到了磁电行为以及铁电和铁磁行为。
> 
> Upon doping the BLFO nanoparticle, the prepared composite showed a good polarization response when the stable magnetic field was applied, indicating its magnetoelectric behavior. 掺杂BLFO纳米颗粒后，当施加稳恒磁场时，所制备的复合材料显示出良好的极化响应，表明其具有磁电行为。
> 
> As mentioned above, the magnetoelectric and multiferroic properties are different in terms of the origin of generation. These behaviors present in MXene are useful in various electrical, optical, and magnetic applications. 如上所述，磁电性和多铁性在产生原理上是不同的。MXene中存在的这些行为在各种电学、光学和磁学应用中非常有用。
> 
> Similarly, doping multiferroic material, such as BiFeO3 onto Ti3C2Tx MXene via a hydrothermal method enhances the multiferroic properties in Ti3C2Tx@BiFeO3 nanocomposites.[149] 同样，通过水热法将多铁材料如BiFeO3掺杂到Ti3C2Tx MXene上，增强了Ti3C2Tx@BiFeO3纳米复合材料中的多铁性能。[149]
> 
> Exploring the multiferroic property of BiFeO3, Syed Rizwan et al. claimed that bismuth ferrite co-doped by other ferromagnetic material can induce and enhance the overall performance of MXene nanocomposite.[123] 在探索BiFeO3的多铁性时，Syed Rizwan等人声称，由其他铁磁材料共掺杂的铁酸铋可以诱导并提高MXene纳米复合材料的整体性能。[123]
> 
> The Gd substitution on BiFeO3 (BFO) can be seen in the SEM image (Figure 14). Gd对BiFeO3 (BFO)的取代可以在SEM图像中看到（图14）。
> 
> They first doped bismuth ferrite (BiFeO3) with Gd and Sn, making a nanoparticle, i.e., BGFSO (BiGdFeSnO), and then co-doped this nanoparticle on the Ti3C2Tx MXene, due to which the ferroelectricity and magnetism increased in the MXene composite (Figure 15). 他们首先用Gd和Sn掺杂铁酸铋(BiFeO3)，制成纳米颗粒，即BGFSO (BiGdFeSnO)，然后将这种纳米颗粒共掺杂到Ti3C2Tx MXene上，由此MXene复合物中的铁电性和磁性得到增强（图15）。
> 
> Liming Wang et al.[150] performed the first principle calculation and found that CO2CF2 MXene[151] possesses both FE and FM properties naturally with adjustable ferroelectricity and magnetic skyrmions. Liming Wang等人[150]进行了第一性原理计算，发现CO2CF2 MXene[151]天然同时具有铁电和铁磁性质，并具有可调的铁电性和磁性斯格明子。 **Term**: magnetic skyrmions: 磁性斯格明子，一种具有拓扑保护特性的纳米尺度自旋结构，可看作一种准粒子。
> 
> The multiferroic CO2CF2 MXene can be used as reversible magnetic skyrmion material in spintronic devices. 多铁性CO2CF2 MXene可用作自旋电子学器件中的可逆磁性斯格明子材料。
> 
> Interestingly, a recent report[152] claimed that multiferroic behavior in heterostructures could be induced by combining the ferroelectric MXene (i.e., Sc2CO2) with ferromagnetic MXene (i.e., Hf2MnC2O2). Both MXenes complemented each other to generate the magnetoelectricity. 有趣的是，最近一份报告[152]声称，通过将铁电性MXene（即Sc2CO2）与铁磁性MXene（即Hf2MnC2O2）结合，可以在异质结构中诱导多铁行为。两种MXene相互补充以产生磁电性。
> 
> Moreover, the nitride-based MXene has also proven to be a promising 2D multiferroic material, as indicated by DFT calculations.[153] 此外，DFT计算表明，氮化物基MXene也是一种有前景的二维多铁材料。[153]

> ## 6. Applications of ferroelectric and ferromagnetic behavior of MXene in electronic devices
> 
> ## 6. MXene的铁电与铁磁行为在电子器件中的应用
> 
> Electronic devices containing MXene have garnered considerable recognition since the discovery of MXene, primarily due to their high electrical conductivity and large surface area properties, which enable unique application. 自MXene发现以来，含有MXene的电子器件因其高导电性和大比表面积特性而获得了广泛的认可，这些特性使其能够实现独特的应用。
> 
> The outstanding electronic nature of MXene sets it apart from other 2D materials because of its tunable work function (WF) and other features (i.e., high conductivity, mechanical strength, and chemical stability.[155,155] MXene出色的电子特性使其区别于其他二维材料，这是因为它具有可调的功函数(WF)以及其他特征（如高导电性、机械强度和化学稳定性）。[155,155] **Term**: work function (WF): 功函数，指将一个电子从固体表面最低能态移出到无穷远处所需的最小能量。
> 
> Due to the MXene's has potential to be used in the engineering of metal-semiconductor interfaces (M/S) for advanced electronic and nanoelectronic devices, for example, ferroelectric memristors, supercapacitors, and batteries. Some of the potential applications of MXene-based electronic devices are given below: 由于MXene有潜力用于先进电子和纳米电子器件的金属-半导体界面(M/S)工程，例如铁电忆阻器、超级电容器和电池。以下是一些基于MXene的电子器件的潜在应用：
> 
> ### 6.1. MXene-based nanostructures in ferroelectric memristors
> 
> ### 6.1. MXene基纳米结构在铁电忆阻器中的应用
> 
> Ferroelectric memristors have better performance than ordinary memristors, as they possess some key features like high integration density, low energy consumption, rapid switching, and adjustable resistance levels.[149,156–158] 铁电忆阻器比普通忆阻器具有更好的性能，因为它们具有一些关键特性，如高集成度、低能耗、快速开关和可调的电阻水平。[149,156–158]
> 
> Some common ferroelectric memristors are PZT, BaFe12O19, HfO, and so on. Lead zirconate titanate (PZT) is one of the most researched as it possesses interesting ferroelectric properties.[159] 一些常见的铁电忆阻器有PZT, BaFe12O19, HfO等等。锆钛酸铅(PZT)是研究最多的铁电材料之一，因为它具有有趣的铁电性能。[159]
> 
> The ferroelectric memristors have a lower on/off ratio and operating voltage than the ordinary conductive-bridge memristor. This issue can be treated by adding other materials to them. 铁电忆阻器比普通导电桥忆阻器具有更低的开关比和工作电压。这个问题可以通过向其中添加其他材料来解决。
> 
> For instance, the Cu-doped PZT ferroelectric memristor has been researched widely, but some issues still need to be addressed.[160,161] They possess bad endurance and operation voltage, although their electric performance can be improved by introducing the transition layer to adjust the interfacial barrier. 例如，Cu掺杂的PZT铁电忆阻器已被广泛研究，但仍有一些问题需要解决。[160,161] 尽管可以通过引入过渡层来调节界面势垒来提高其电学性能，但它们的耐久性差，工作电压也不理想。
> 
> As discussed above, 2D materials possess unique electric and magnetic properties that can contribute to enhancing the performance of ferroelectric materials. 如上所述，二维材料具有独特的电学和磁学性质，有助于提高铁电材料的性能。
> 
> In one report, A. Lipatov et al. and H. Lu et al. incorporated graphene with a PZT ferroelectric memristor to enhance the overall performance.[162,163] 在一份报告中，A. Lipatov等人和H. Lu等人将石墨烯与PZT铁电忆阻器结合，以提高整体性能。[162,163]
> 
> However, G/PZT still has problems, like bad stability and operation voltage. Therefore, MXene is the best candidate for ferroelectric memristors, as it possesses remarkable properties such as forming conductive filaments and charge trapping. 然而，G/PZT仍然存在稳定性和工作电压不佳等问题。因此，MXene是铁电忆阻器的最佳候选者，因为它具有形成导电细丝和电荷俘获等显著特性。 **Term**: conductive filaments: 导电细丝，在忆阻器中，指在外加电场作用下，由离子迁移或缺陷重排形成的纳米级导电通道，是电阻开关效应的关键。
> 
> The following are some examples of MXene used in ferroelectric memristors to enhance their properties. Miaocheng et al. introduced a hybrid system for ferroelectric memristors to improve their overall performance.[88] 以下是MXene用于增强铁电忆阻器性能的一些例子。Miaocheng等人为铁电忆阻器引入了一种混合系统，以改善其整体性能。[88]
> 
> The variety of ferroelectric and diffusion of Cu ions are the main cause of switching behavior in MXene/PZT memristor systems. According to the DFT results, the induced MXene is important to stabilize and build conductive filaments quickly in the Cu/PZT system since the MXene/PZT system has a much lesser relocation barrier of Cu ions than PZT (i.e., 4.43 eV). 铁电性的多样性和Cu离子的扩散是MXene/PZT忆阻器系统中开关行为的主要原因。根据DFT结果，引入的MXene对于在Cu/PZT系统中稳定和快速建立导电细丝至关重要，因为MXene/PZT系统的Cu离子迁移势垒比PZT小得多（即4.43 eV）。
> 
> In their experimental work,[90] Miaocheng et al. introduced MXene in a lead zirconate titanate (PZT) system to enhance the resistive-switching nature of PZT-based ferroelectric memristors. Additionally, MXene-PZT-based ferroelectric memristors can attain constant multi-resistance state storage at different applied currents. 在他们的实验工作中，[90] Miaocheng等人将MXene引入锆钛酸铅(PZT)体系中，以增强PZT基铁电忆阻器的电阻开关特性。此外，基于MXene-PZT的铁电忆阻器可以在不同外加电流下实现稳定的多阻态存储。
> 
> Furthermore, to obtain resistive threshold switching, MXene was induced in the BaFe12O19 ferroelectric memristor. As a result, the BFO/MXene system showed better results, characterized by the quick formation and movement of charges.[89] 此外，为了获得电阻阈值开关，在BaFe12O19铁电忆阻器中引入了MXene。结果，BFO/MXene系统显示出更好的结果，其特点是电荷的快速形成和移动。[89]
> 
> Kubra et al. applied MXene as an active middle layer and laser-reduced graphene as the top and bottom electrodes in the trilayer ferroelectric memristor (Figure 16). The ferroelectric nature of Mo2Ti2C3Tx MXene improved the overall performance by demonstrating the controlled conduction filament formation of the memristor, resulting in excellent resistive switching, a sufficient memory window of Roff/Ron = 102, and excellent cycle-to-cycle endurance of up to 103 cycles.[164] Kubra等人将MXene作为中间活性层，将激光还原石墨烯作为顶部和底部电极，应用于三层铁电忆阻器中（图16）。Mo2Ti2C3Tx MXene的铁电特性通过展示忆阻器可控的导电细丝形成，改善了整体性能，实现了优秀的电阻开关特性、足够的存储窗口（Roff/Ron = 10^2）以及高达10^3次循环的优异循环耐久性。[164]
> 
> ### 6.2. MXene-based nanostructures for batteries and nanogenerators
> 
> ### 6.2. MXene基纳米结构在电池和纳米发电机中的应用
> 
> The ferroelectric nature of MXene can also help in energy storage devices as an electrode material because of its high surface area and good pseudocapacitance. For instance, it has been reported[95] that MXene introduced in lithium-ion batteries (LIBs) improved the capacitance of the battery (Figure 17a). MXene的铁电特性也有助于其作为电极材料在储能器件中的应用，因为它具有高比表面积和良好的赝电容。例如，据报道，[95] 在锂离子电池(LIBs)中引入MXene提高了电池的电容（图17a）。 **Term**: pseudocapacitance: 赝电容，一种发生在电极表面的法拉第过程，通过快速可逆的氧化还原反应来存储电荷，其电化学行为类似于电容。
> 
> The BaTiO3 prevents the MXene sheets from stacking together and the reduction of Ti atoms. Meanwhile, more Li+ ions were attracted onto the anode owing to the internal electric field produced by the polarization of BaTiO3 nanoparticles (Figure 17d). BaTiO3可以防止MXene片层堆叠在一起以及Ti原子的还原。同时，由于BaTiO3纳米颗粒极化产生的内建电场，更多的Li+离子被吸引到阳极上（图17d）。
> 
> Consequently, BT/f-Ti3C2Tx composites revealed much better long-term cycle stability and capacitance (Figure 17b,c). 因此，BT/f-Ti3C2Tx复合材料显示出更好的长期循环稳定性和电容（图17b, c）。
> 
> Recently, research on MXene-based nanostructures for nanogenerators has been rising, thanks to their excellent properties, including tunable surface chemistry, conductivity, electronegativity, and mechanical flexibility.[165] 最近，由于MXene基纳米结构具有可调的表面化学、导电性、电负性和机械柔韧性等优异性能，其在纳米发电机中的研究不断增多。[165]
> 
> A recent study claimed that MXene contributed as a ground state in a piezoelectric nanogenerators (PENG), due to which Li-doped ZnO nanowires were polarized proficiently during the poling process.[86] 一项最新研究声称，MXene在压电纳米发电机(PENG)中作为基态做出了贡献，使得Li掺杂的ZnO纳米线在极化过程中被有效地极化。[86]
> 
> In addition, the nanowires (NW)-covered MXene preserved the metallic Ti3C2 flakes from clustering during the composite formation, thereby enhancing the uniformity of the electric field. 此外，纳米线(NW)覆盖的MXene在复合材料形成过程中阻止了金属Ti3C2薄片的聚集，从而增强了电场的均匀性。
> 
> As a result, the overall performance (such as ferroelectricity and output power) of Li: ZnO/Ti3C2 PENG increased by two times as compared to the ordinary PENG composed of Li: ZnO. The detailed schematic is shown in Figure 18. 结果，Li: ZnO/Ti3C2 PENG的整体性能（如铁电性和输出功率）比由Li: ZnO组成的普通PENG提高了两倍。详细的示意图如图18所示。

> Viet Anh Cao et al. presented a new approach to improve the overall performance of triboelectric nanogenerators (TENGs). The author fabricated a composite of ferroelectric polymers with chemically modified Ti3C2 fillers. Viet Anh Cao等人提出了一种提高摩擦纳米发电机(TENGs)整体性能的新方法。作者制备了一种铁电聚合物与化学改性Ti3C2填料的复合材料。
> 
> In this system, Ti3C2 MXene nanoflakes were used as positive and negative filler between the friction layers of triboelectric nanogenerators (TENGs). The functional group attached to MXene determines its output performance. 在该系统中，Ti3C2 MXene纳米片被用作摩擦纳米发电机(TENGs)摩擦层之间的正负填料。附着在MXene上的官能团决定了其输出性能。
> 
> For example, Ti3C2 with -NH2 functionalized groups (NH2-Ti3C2) demonstrates triboelectrically positive properties, while Ti3C2 with N-functionalized groups (N-Ti3C2) shows triboelectrically negative characteristics. 例如，具有-NH2官能化基团的Ti3C2 (NH2-Ti3C2)表现出摩擦电正性，而具有N-官能化基团的Ti3C2 (N-Ti3C2)则显示出摩擦电负性。
> 
> Both the systems achieved a maximum output open-circuit voltage of 250 V, an output power density of 13 mW/cm2 and a short-circuit current density of 280 mA/cm2.[166] 两种系统都实现了最高250 V的开路输出电压，13 mW/cm2的输出功率密度和280 mA/cm2的短路电流密度。[166]

> ### 6.3. MXene-based nanostructures for spintronics devices
> 
> ### 6.3. MXene基纳米结构在自旋电子学器件中的应用
> 
> Materials with stable ferromagnetism at room temperature can be utilized in electronic devices such as spintronics, data storage, and magnetic sensors. 在室温下具有稳定铁磁性的材料可用于电子器件，如自旋电子学、数据存储和磁传感器。
> 
> According to the DFT results,[119] the lanthanum (La)-doped Ti3C2 MXene exhibits increased ferromagnetism and magnetic moment. Because of the induced La, the nanocomposite has the potential to be used in spintronic devices. 根据DFT结果，[119] 镧(La)掺杂的Ti3C2 MXene表现出增强的铁磁性和磁矩。由于诱导的La，该纳米复合材料具有应用于自旋电子学器件的潜力。
> 
> Furthermore, a recent study, Syed Rizwan et al., induced spintronic properties in MXene by doping BFO (co-doped by Gd and Sn).[123] 此外，在最近的一项研究中，Syed Rizwan等人通过掺杂BFO（用Gd和Sn共掺杂）在MXene中诱导了自旋电子学特性。[123]
> 
> Here, the superexchange influence of the Ti3C2 and BGFSO interface presents a clear hysteresis loop with lower saturation magnetization due to the Ti3C2/BGFSO hybrid system. This magnetic effect plays a vital role in spintronic devices. 这里，Ti3C2和BGFSO界面的超交换作用呈现出清晰的回滞回线，由于Ti3C2/BGFSO混合体系，其饱和磁化强度较低。这种磁效应在自旋电子学器件中起着至关重要的作用。 **Term**: superexchange: 超交换作用，一种通过非磁性阴离子介导的磁性离子之间的间接交换相互作用。
> 
> Janus MXene is one of the newly discovered MXene that also has the potential to be used in electronic devices. Swetarekha Ram et al.[167] reported that Janus MXene with an oxygen (-O) functional group has low magnetic anisotropy as compared to the in-plane anisotropy of MXene with F and OH functional groups (Figure 19). Janus MXene是一种新发现的MXene，也具有在电子器件中应用的潜力。Swetarekha Ram等人[167]报道，与具有F和OH官能团的MXene的面内各向异性相比，具有氧(-O)官能团的Janus MXene具有较低的磁各向异性（图19）。 **Term**: Janus MXene: Janus MXene，一种上下表面具有不同官能团的MXene，类似罗马神话中的双面神Janus。
> 
> Moreover, among all Janus MXenes, TiCrCO2 has the highest ferromagnetism and exchange constant. The above factors make the Janus MXene the best candidate for spintronic applications. 此外，在所有Janus MXenes中，TiCrCO2具有最高的铁磁性和交换常数。上述因素使Janus MXene成为自旋电子学应用的最佳候选者。
> 
> Furthermore, double transition metals have high half-metallicity and magnetism compared to the single transition metals. For instance, the first-principles study confirmed that W2CrN2O2 and Mo2Cr2N2O2 MXene exhibit strong ferromagnetic half-metallic behavior, making them the best candidates for spintronic devices.[168] 此外，与单过渡金属相比，双过渡金属具有更高的半金属性和磁性。例如，第一性原理研究证实，W2CrN2O2和Mo2Cr2N2O2 MXene表现出强铁磁半金属行为，使其成为自旋电子学器件的最佳候选者。[168]
> 
> MXene is a next-generation material as it possesses unique properties. For example, MXene exhibits high conductivity and a charged surface, contributing to electrochemical properties that are important in electronic applications. MXene是一种下一代材料，因为它具有独特的性质。例如，MXene表现出高导电性和带电表面，这两者贡献了在电子应用中至关重要的电化学性能。
> 
> In addition, the tunable nature allows us to adjust its electric and magnetic behavior, which are useful in tailored spintronic devices. Due to these outstanding properties, MXene is preferred and researched more for electronic and magnetic applications than other materials. 此外，其可调谐的特性使我们能够调节其电学和磁学行为，这对于定制的自旋电子学器件非常有用。由于这些卓越的性能，MXene在电子和磁学应用方面比其他材料更受青睐，研究也更多。

> ## 7. Summary and future prospects
> 
> ## 7. 总结与未来展望
> 
> In summary, the review paper highlights the potential applications of 2D MXene, as they have unique multifunctional properties, i.e., ferroelectric and/or ferromagnetic. 总之，这篇综述文章强调了二维MXene的潜在应用，因为它们具有独特的多功能特性，即铁电性和/或铁磁性。
> 
> This paper demonstrates the in-depth investigation of fabrication methods, etching techniques, and their effect on the material's performance. The formation of surface termination (Tx) depends on the type of etchant used for the etching method, which later determines the properties of MXene. 本文展示了对其制备方法、刻蚀技术及其对材料性能影响的深入研究。表面端基(Tx)的形成取决于刻蚀方法中使用的刻蚀剂类型，这随后决定了MXene的性质。
> 
> With the increasing demand for slim electronic devices, the main focus of research has shifted from 3D materials to 2D materials. 2D materials, such as MXene, possess a high surface area and high conductivity compared to traditional bulk materials. 随着对轻薄电子器件需求的增加，研究重点已从3D材料转向2D材料。与传统的体块材料相比，MXene等二维材料具有高比表面积和高导电性。
> 
> Ferroelectricity and ferromagnetism are hard to obtain naturally in MXene; that's why scientists have adopted interesting techniques to generate FE and/or FM behavior in MXene. These properties can be induced in MXene by doping, vacancy formation, or making composites with other materials. 铁电性和铁磁性在MXene中难以天然获得；这就是为什么科学家们采用了有趣的技术来在MXene中产生铁电和/或铁磁行为。这些性质可以通过掺杂、空位形成或与其他材料复合而在MXene中诱导产生。
> 
> The ferroelectric and/or ferromagnetic properties present in MXene can be utilized in numerous cutting-edge technologies, such as non-volatile memory devices, ferroelectric memristors, batteries, and spintronics, which enable the development of highly advanced electronic and nanoelectronic devices in the future. MXene中存在的铁电和/或铁磁性质可用于众多尖端技术，如非易失性存储器件、铁电忆阻器、电池和自旋电子学，这将促进未来高度先进的电子和纳米电子器件的发展。
> 
> Despite possessing these remarkable properties, several challenges still limit the full potential of MXene in practical applications. All MXenes were synthesized in the laboratory, but it is challenging to fabricate them on a large scale for industrial applications. 尽管具有这些卓越的性能，但一些挑战仍然限制了MXene在实际应用中的全部潜力。所有MXene都是在实验室中合成的，但要在工业应用中大规模制造它们仍然具有挑战性。
> 
> On the other hand, mostly FE and FM are induced in MXene by doping other materials or surface terminations, which are hard to stabilize at room temperature. 另一方面，MXene中的铁电性和铁磁性大多是通过掺杂其他材料或表面端基来诱导的，这些在室温下难以稳定。
> 
> Although MXene has shown tremendous applications in recent years, further research is needed on MXene to address these challenges. 尽管近年来MXene已显示出巨大的应用潜力，但仍需要进一步研究MXene以应对这些挑战。
> 
> From our perspective, the 2D MXene can address the high demand for advanced and slim electronic devices; however, we need to explore and understand the full underlying potential of MXene in practical and large-scale applications. 从我们的角度来看，二维MXene可以满足对先进和轻薄电子器件的高需求；然而，我们需要探索和理解MXene在实际和大规模应用中的全部潜在价值。
> 
> For this purpose, further research is required to induce FE/FM in MXene via doping or composite formation. The FE/FM behavior in MXene can also be used in optoelectronic and biomedical applications, like photovoltaic devices, photocatalysis, and bone regeneration. 为此，需要进一步研究通过掺杂或复合形成在MXene中诱导铁电/铁磁性。MXene中的铁电/铁磁行为也可用于光电和生物医学应用，如光伏器件、光催化和骨再生。
