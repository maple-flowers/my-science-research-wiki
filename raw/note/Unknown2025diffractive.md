> ## 一、引言
> 
> 在激光材料加工领域，对激光束进行精确的**光束整形**是提升加工质量、效率与灵活性的关键技术。传统的**衍射光学元件**因其强大的光束操控能力而备受青睐，但其制造方法常受限于高成本、低灵活性或缓慢的原型制作速度。
> 
> 本文献《用于材料加工中光束整形的双光子聚合毫米级衍射光学元件》(Millimeter-Scale Diffractive Optical Elements Fabricated by Two-Photon Polymerization for Beam Shaping in Materials Processing) 针对这一痛点，探索了**双光子聚合**这一先进增材制造技术。该技术能够以亚微米级分辨率直接制造任意三维结构，为快速、灵活地定制衍射光学元件提供了全新方案。
> 
> 本研究的核心价值在于，成功制造了一个直径达3.5毫米的毫米级衍射光学元件，用于将高斯光束转化为双环形光束（对应近场的双贝塞尔光束），并验证了其在高达22.8瓦平均功率下的高损伤阈值，证明了2PP技术在制造高功率激光加工用微光学元件方面的巨大潜力。
> 
> ## 二、论文结构与逻辑概览
> 
> 本文遵循“设计-制造-测试-分析”的经典工程研究逻辑，结构清晰。其核心论证链条如下：
> 
> 1.  **问题定义**：材料加工需要灵活的光束整形方案，现有衍射光学元件制造方法受限。
> 2.  **方案提出**：采用双光子聚合技术作为灵活的制造平台。
> 3.  **设计与计算**：
>     -   目标：制造一个能将高斯光束整形为双环形/贝塞尔光束的衍射光学元件。
>     -   方法：通过改进的棱镜与透镜算法，将轴向聚焦项替换为轴棱锥项，计算生成所需相位分布的**计算机生成全息图**。
>     -   转换：将灰度全息图转换为可用于3D打印的STL文件。
> 4.  **制造与优化**：
>     -   **拼接策略**：将大尺寸STL文件分割为多个小方块，以匹配打印物镜的视场，实现毫米级制造。
>     -   **误差补偿**：实施倾斜校正以补偿样品台的非完美垂直度。
>     -   **工艺参数**：设定激光功率、扫描速度、层厚等关键参数，耗时68.5小时完成制造。
> 5.  **性能验证**：
>     -   **光束分析**：用光束分析仪在远场成功观测到预期的双环形光束，验证了整形能力。
>     -   **损伤测试**：通过逐步增加激光功率，测定了衍射光学元件的损伤阈值，验证其在材料加工中的鲁棒性。
> 6.  **结论与展望**：总结成功要素，指出2PP制造衍射光学元件在高功率应用中的可行性，并提出未来改进方向。
>

> ## 四、正文核心内容剖析
> 
> ### 4.1 研究背景与方法模型
> 
> -   **研究背景**：激光材料加工中，**贝塞尔光束**因其无衍射特性而在精密钻孔、透明材料体改性等领域具有优势，但单光束加工速度慢。多光束并行加工是提升效率的关键，而传统轴棱锥与扫描系统的结合实现复杂。因此，一种能生成多贝塞尔光束的紧凑、灵活的光学元件成为迫切需求。
> -   **问题凝练**：如何以一种快速、灵活、低成本的方式，制造出能够承受高功率激光并用于材料加工的大尺寸、多光束衍射光学元件？
> -   **方法模型：计算机生成全息图（CGH）计算**
>     -   **基础算法**：**棱镜与透镜算法**。该算法通过“棱镜项”实现光束的横向位移，通过“透镜项”实现轴向聚焦，从而将一束光分成多束并聚焦到不同位置。
>     -   **核心创新**：文献对算法进行了关键修改，**将透镜项替换为轴棱锥项**。轴棱锥是一种能将高斯光束转化为贝塞尔/环形光束的光学元件。这一替换使得计算出的CGH能够直接生成具有贝塞尔光束特性的光场分布，且可以同时生成两束（双环形光束）。
>     -   **转换流程**：计算出的相位图（灰度图）被转换为三维STL文件。每个像素的灰度值对应一个特定高度（2 μm至6.4 μm），从而形成一个4.4 μm的调制深度，对应6π的相位调制量。此调制深度是衍射效率与制造时间之间的一个折衷选择。
> 
> ### 4.2 实验与数据
> 
> -   **制造工艺（2PP）**：
>     -   **核心挑战**：如何制造直径3.5 mm的元件，远超物镜500 μm × 500 μm的视场（FOV）。
>     -   **解决方案**：将STL模型分割成7×7共49个拼接方块，逐块、逐层打印。这是实现从微米级精度到毫米级尺寸跨越的关键。
>     -   **关键工艺参数**：激光功率25 mW，扫描速度10 mm/s，层厚0.1 μm，填充线间距0.3 μm。总耗时68.5小时。
>     -   **误差补偿**：引入**倾斜校正**。通过测量基板上三个点的起始位置来拟合一个平面，并在打印每个方块时动态调整起始高度，以补偿样品台与物镜的不垂直，避免了数微米的高度误差。
> -   **性能测试**：
>     -   **光束整形验证**：在51 mm远场处，成功捕获到双环形光束，与理论模拟（图1c）一致。但光束并非完美单环，而是由多个间隔约600 μm的细环组成，这是衍射生成环形光束的典型现象。
>     -   **损伤阈值测试**：
>         -   **设备**：高功率飞秒激光器（1030 nm, 191 fs, 100 kHz）。
>         -   **方法**：逐步增加激光功率，每步持续30秒，直到元件损坏。
>         -   **结果**：元件在平均功率22.8 W（脉冲能量228 μJ，峰值功率密度24.8 GW/cm²）下仍未损坏，显示出极高的损伤阈值。
>         -   **分析**：高损伤阈值归因于元件极薄（总厚度6.4 μm），减少了材料对激光的吸收。该结果与同类光刻胶的损伤阈值研究结果在同一量级，验证了2PP元件用于高功率材料加工的可行性。
> 
> ### 4.3 讨论
> 
> -   **制造缺陷与光学性能的关联**：文章将实测光束轮廓（图5）中的伪影与光学显微镜图像（图2）中的缺陷直接关联。例如，拼接方块角落的亮点对应着那里的微孔衍射。这种坦诚的“缺陷-效应”分析增加了研究的严谨性。
> -   **工艺参数的内在权衡**：全文贯穿了一个核心权衡——**制造效率、表面质量与光学性能之间的平衡**。

> 当前，**衍射光学元件**是实现光束整形的主流方案之一，它通过表面的微结构来精确调控光波的相位。然而，传统DOE的制造方法（如光刻、电子束刻蚀）存在成本高、周期长、难以快速原型化的痛点。本研究立足于这一瓶颈，探索了一种名为**双光子聚合**的增材制造技术，用于快速、灵活地制造定制化DOE。论文的核心目标在于验证2PP技术能否制造出不仅尺寸达到毫米级，而且具备足够高激光损伤阈值，从而能真正应用于实际工业级激光加工的DOE。这填补了将2PP技术从实验室微光学原型推向宏观应用的关键空白。
> 
> ### 1.3 核心价值
> 
> 本文最大的价值在于**工程应用的突破**。它不仅仅展示了2PP打印DOE的可行性，更通过一系列工程手段（如拼接算法、倾斜校正）解决了大尺寸与高精度之间的矛盾，并首次在2PP打印的DOE上验证了承受高峰值功率的能力，证明了其作为工业级耐用光学元件的潜力。
>

> ### 4.2 方法/模型：从算法到3D模型
> 
> 这是本研究的核心技术环节，其逻辑链条如下：
> 
> 1.  **设计输入**：一个直径为3.5 mm、波长为800 nm的**高斯光束**。
> 2.  **核心算法**：采用改良的**棱镜与透镜算法**。该算法能将一束光分成多束，并分别控制每束光的横向偏移（棱镜项）和轴向焦点（透镜项）。本研究的关键创新点在于，将“透镜项”替换为**轴锥镜全息图**，从而将高斯光束转化为具有无衍射特性的**贝塞尔光束**。在远场，贝塞尔光束表现为一个环形光束。
> 3.  **相位图生成**：通过算法计算出一个包含5833x5833像素的灰度相位图（图1b），像素尺寸为600 nm。每个像素的灰度值对应一个相位延迟量。
> 4.  **STL模型转换**：将灰度相位图转换为3D打印通用的STL文件。这是一个关键步骤，将抽象的相位信息具象化为物理高度。灰度值越大，打印高度越高。最大高度（白）为6.4 μm，最小高度（黑）为2 μm，形成4.4 μm的**调制深度**。这个调制深度提供了6π的相位变化，这是一个在衍射效率与打印时间之间的折中选择。
>     -   _公式1_：调制深度 (h) 与相位差 (\Delta \phi) 的关系为 (\Delta \phi = \frac{2\pi}{\lambda} \cdot h \cdot (n - n_0))，其中 (n=1.55) 是光刻胶折射率，(n_0 \approx 1) 是空气折射率，(\lambda=800 nm) 是目标波长。
> 
> ### 4.3 实验/制造：挑战与对策
> 
> 制造过程的核心在于克服2PP高精度与大尺寸之间的矛盾。
> 
> -   **设备与材料**：使用飞秒激光器（780 nm, 100 fs）和20倍显微镜物镜（NA=0.8）。该物镜视场为500 μm x 500 μm。材料为FemtoBond 4B光刻胶。
> -   **关键工艺1：大面积拼接**：为制造3.5 mm直径的DOE，需要将STL模型分割成7x7共49个500 μm x 500 μm的方块，然后逐块、逐层打印和拼接。
> -   **关键工艺2：倾斜校正**：在3.5 mm x 3.5 mm的范围内，基片与物镜的微小不垂直度会导致数微米的高度误差，这足以毁掉整个结构。为此，作者通过三点测量计算出倾斜平面，并在打印时对每个方块的起始位置进行Z轴补偿，这是保证大面积结构成功的关键工程细节。
> -   **制造结果**：耗时68.5小时，成功制造出DOE。光学和SEM图像（图2, 3）显示其宏观结构正确，但微观存在阶梯效应和表面粗糙度，这源于NA=0.8物镜相对较低的分辨率。
> 
> ### 4.4 实验结果与讨论
> 
> -   **光束整形效果**：光束分析仪图像（图5）成功捕获到两个相交的环形光束，这是对DOE设计功能的最直接证明。尽管存在一些杂散光（来自未衍射光、拼接缝隙、孔洞缺陷），但主光束清晰可辨，与模拟（图4c）预测一致。
> -   **损伤阈值测试**：这是全文最具应用价值的亮点。测试使用波长1030 nm、重频100 kHz的激光器，逐步提升功率。DOE在承受22.8 W的平均功率（对应脉冲能量228 μJ，峰值功率密度24.8 GW/cm²）后仍未损坏。这一结果与文献中其他光刻胶的损伤阈值处于同一量级，证明了这种薄型DOE（厚度仅6.4 μm）具有出色的抗损伤能力，足以应用于实际材料加工。

> ## 七、学术思考与批判性分析
> 
> 1.  **效率与质量的权衡**：论文在多处体现了工程优化的核心——权衡。例如，调制深度（6π）的选择是在衍射效率与打印时间间的权衡；物镜NA（0.8）的选择是在表面质量（分辨率）与制造速度（视场）间的权衡；层数（45层）的选择是在光束质量与制造时间间的权衡。思考：**是否存在一个更优的全局优化模型，能将这些相互关联的参数整合起来，以最小化时间成本的同时最大化光学性能，而不仅仅是逐项权衡？**
>     
> 2.  **损伤机制的模糊性**：论文将高损伤阈值归因于“DOE的厚度薄（6.4 μm）导致激光吸收减少”，但这只是一个假设性解释。**真正的损伤机制是什么？是光热效应（线性吸收和热积累）、光化学效应（多光子吸收导致的键断裂），还是场致效应（如介质击穿）？** 进行更深入的损伤机理研究（如时间分辨光谱、损伤形貌分析）对于指导未来材料设计和工艺优化至关重要。

> 3.  **“远场”表征的局限性**：论文仅表征了DOE的远场环形光束，并以“近场贝塞尔光束尺寸太小难以测量”为由未进行表征。然而，对于许多应用（如精密钻孔），贝塞尔光束的中心光斑尺寸和焦深才是核心性能指标。**仅仅依赖远场环形光束的均匀性来推断近场贝塞尔光束的质量，其可靠性存疑。** 一个更完整的研究应包含使用更高分辨率系统（如扫描刀口法或纳米探针）对近场贝塞尔光束的定量表征。

> ## 八、用户问题预测与解答
> 
> 1.  **问题：什么是“轴锥镜”，它和普通的透镜有什么不同？**
>     
>     -   **解答**：普通透镜（如凸透镜）将平行光汇聚到一个**点**上，形成一个焦平面。而**轴锥镜**是一种锥形棱镜，它将平行光汇聚到光轴上的一个**线段**上，形成一个细长的焦柱。这个焦柱内的光场分布近似于**贝塞尔函数**，因此被称为**贝塞尔光束**。贝塞尔光束的最大特点是“无衍射”，即在很长的传播距离内，其中心光斑的大小和形状几乎保持不变，这使其在需要长焦深、高精度的应用中（如厚材料钻孔）极具优势。
> 2.  **问题：什么是“衍射效率”，它为什么很重要？**
>     
>     -   **解答**：DOE的工作原理是将入射光能量分配到不同的衍射级次上。**衍射效率**是指目标衍射级次（例如，我们想要的两个环形光束）所获得的能量占总入射能量的比例。这个比例越高，说明DOE的能量利用率越高，杂散光（如那些不需要的更高阶衍射环）越少。本文选择的6π相位调制深度，就是试图在打印时间和尽可能高的衍射效率之间取得平衡，因为理论上调制深度越接近理想值，效率越高。
> 3.  **问题：为什么制造一个3.5 mm的元件需要68.5小时这么久？**
>     
>     -   **解答**：这源于2PP技术的“点对点”或“层对层”的串行写入本质。想象一下，用一支极细的笔（直径约300 nm）去画满一个3.5 mm x 3.5 mm的区域，并且还要画几千层（每层仅100 nm厚），这本身就是一个极其耗时的工作。此外，由于物镜的视场大小只有500 μm，需要将画布分成49个方格，每画完一个方格，需要移动样品台对准下一个方格，加入了这个“拼接”过程的时间开销，自然更久。68.5小时的结果是一个在精度、尺寸和速度之间的折中。
> 4.  **问题：论文中提到的“高阶衍射环”或“杂散光”是什么？是设计的缺陷吗？**
>     
>     -   **解答**：这不完全是设计缺陷，而是物理衍射现象和制造误差共同作用的结果。理想的连续相位结构被2PP制造出的“台阶”所近似，这必然会产生非目标方向的光，即“高阶衍射环”。同时，制造过程中的拼接缝隙、缺陷孔洞等也会产生额外的杂散光。这些杂散光不仅浪费能量，还可能干扰主光束的加工效果。因此，优化工艺（如减少层高）来抑制它们，是提升DOE质量的关键。
> 5.  **问题：既然2PP可以任意制造三维结构，为什么还要用CGH来计算相位图，而不直接设计一个起伏的曲面？**
>     
>     -   **解答**：这是一个非常好的问题，触及了DOE设计的本质。直接设计一个起伏的曲面来达到特定光束整形，理论上可行，但工程上极其困难。CGH提供了一种“逆向设计”的数学框架：我们先确定想要的输出光场（目标），然后反向计算输入光场（已知）需要被施加什么样的相位延迟（即DOE的表面浮雕结构）。这种计算基于傅里叶光学原理，成熟且高效。直接设计曲面则需要解决一个复杂的、非直观的逆散射问题，计算量大且难以保证全局最优解。因此，用CGH计算相位图，再将其转化为物理高度，是目前最主流、最有效的DOE设计方法。

> The ability to shape laser beams is of critical importance for laser-based materials processing technologies. The capacity to modify the spatial intensity profile of a laser beam facilitates the customization of the beam to suit particular processing applications, thereby enhancing beam quality [1, 2], efficiency [3, 4], and flexibility [5]. A variety of optical components have been developed to address this need, with Diffractive Optical Elements (DOEs) emerging as one of the most robust and versatile solutions [6]. DOEs have the capability to encode complex phase functions in a single element, thereby enabling advanced beam manipulation, including splitting, shaping, and focusing. The flexibility of these functions makes them well-suited for the generation of multiple beams, non-Gaussian profiles, and propagation-invariant beams like Bessel beams. However, conventional fabrication methods, including photolithography [7], electron beam lithography [8], nanoimprint lithography [9], ion beam milling [10], and reactive ion etching [11], are frequently constrained by factors such as high cost, inflexibility, or slow prototyping. To overcome these limitations, additive microfabrication techniques have gained attention as an alternative approach for the production of complex freeform optics. Among them, Two-Photon Polymerization (2PP) stands out due to its capability to fabricate arbitrary three-dimensional structures with sub-micrometer resolution and high surface quality [12]. This technique relies on the nonlinear absorption of femtosecond laser pulses to induce localized polymerization within a photoresist, allowing for the direct writing of intricate micro-optical components [13]. Since only a spatially limited part of the focal volume exhibits a sufficient photon density to induce a polymerization reaction inside the material, feature sizes below the diffraction limit can be achieved [14]. Thus, this degree of precision and design freedom offered by 2PP makes it an ideal candidate for the rapid prototyping of custom DOEs tailored to specific beam shaping applications, as it was already shown by multiple research groups [15-18]. In this study, we explore the use of 2PP to fabricate DOEs designed to generate multiple Bessel beams. Bessel beams are of particular interest due to their non-diffracting properties, which make them advantageous in applications such as precision drilling [19, 20], and volumetric modification of transparent materials [21]. However, many Bessel beam-based applications suffer from low process speeds, as the combination of axicons and scanner optics is difficult to implement. Therefore, there is significant interest in multibeam elements, which can be used for parallel processing [20]. Furthermore, axicons are often integrated into compact systems such as endoscopic devices [22, 23], where the combination of axicons with additional optical beam shaping elements is of high interest to maintain the compactness of the system. The phase profiles required to produce these beams were computed using the prism and lens algorithm described by Liesener et al. [24], modified by replacing the axial focusshift term with a holographic axicon component [25] to achieve the desired Bessel beam structure. However, the characterization of Bessel beams in the near field behind an axicon proves to be challenging due to the very small spot dimensions, which are on the order of the pixels of beam profilers. For this reason, in the context of this work, the ring beams in the far field of the DOE were characterized, whose uniformity also provides insights into the beam shaping capabilities of the DOE. The ability of 2PP-printed Fresnel axicons to form a Bessel beam before the ring beam has already been confirmed in a previous work [18]. In this paper we demonstrate the fabrication of such a DOE with a diameter of 3.5 mm and a maximum structure height of 6.4 μm. By fabricating DOEs with these dimensions, a research gap is closed by increasing the lateral extent and thus the maximum usable laser power compared to previous printed micro-optics while simultaneously preserving the high resolution of the 2PP process. The manufacturing of the DOE required approximately 68.5 hours and involved the conversion of the calculated phase input into a three-dimensional STL model. This STL-file was split to match the full field of view of the objective lens used in the printing system. Following fabrication, the beam shaping performance of the DOE was evaluated using a beam profiler. > # 3. 2PP Fabrication of DOE
> 
> **3. DOE的2PP制造**
> 
> The 2PP setup used for the DOE fabrication is described in detail in [28] and consists of an ultrashort pulse Ti:Sa laser (Tsunami, Spectra-Physics Inc.), three linear axes (Wafer Max Z and ANT 130-XY, Aerotech Inc.), and a Galvo scanner (hurrySCAN II 14, SCANLAB GmbH). The utilized laser source operates at a repetition rate of 82 MHz, with a pulse length of 100 fs and a central wavelength of 780 nm. A microscope objective (20x NA=0.8 Plan-Apochromat, Carl Zeiss Microscopy Germany GmbH) with a large field of view (FOV) of 500 μm x 500 μm was utilized to facilitate the fabrication of millimeter-scale optical elements within a reasonable timeframe. Accordingly, the STL file derived from the DOE is divided into 49 squares, with each square measuring 500 μm x 500 μm. The arrangement of these squares in a 7x7 grid of stitching tiles corresponds to the DOE diameter of 3.5 mm. The fabrication of each square is executed in a sequential manner, employing a layer-by-layer approach. The fabrication of each layer is conducted using the Galvo scanner. Subsequent to the fabrication of each layer, the sample is positioned for the subsequent layer by the axis system. After the fabrication of one square, the axis system is employed to position the sample for the subsequent square. Due to the inherent axis tolerances and limitations in the fabrication of the sample holder and glass substrate, the samples can only be positioned in a not perfectly perpendicular orientation relative to the microscope objective. Thus, the initial point of intersection between the photoresist and glass, which serves as the starting point for the print, exhibits variability across the printing area. As small deviations in perpendicularity over an area of 3.5 mm x 3.5 mm showed significant height errors of several micrometers, which is equivalent to the total structure height, a tilt correction was implemented. The starting point was determined at three distinct locations on the glass substrate, and a plane intersecting those three points was subsequently calculated. During the fabrication process, the initial position of each fabricated square was adjusted in relation to the first square, with the adjustment being proportional to the plane. Moreover, the minimum pixel height of the DOE was established at 2 μm, thereby ensuring the presence of a 2 μm thick base below the DOE structure, which serves as an error tolerance. The DOE was fabricated with the photoresist FemtoBond 4B (Laserzentrum Hannover e.V., Germany) and its fabrication required a total of 68.5 hours. All relevant process parameters are listed in Table 1. **用于DOE制造的2PP装置在[28]中有详细描述，包括超短脉冲钛宝石激光器（Tsunami, Spectra-Physics Inc.）、三个线性运动轴（Wafer Max Z和ANT 130-XY, Aerotech Inc.）和一个振镜扫描仪（hurrySCAN II 14, SCANLAB GmbH）。所用激光源以82 MHz的重复频率运行，脉冲长度为100 fs，中心波长为780 nm。利用具有500 μm x 500 μm大视场（FOV）的显微镜物镜（20x NA=0.8 Plan-Apochromat, Carl Zeiss Microscopy Germany GmbH），以在合理的时间范围内实现毫米级光学元件的制造。相应地，从DOE导出的STL文件被分成49个正方形，每个正方形尺寸为500 μm x 500 μm。这些正方形按7x7拼接块的网格排列，对应于DOE直径3.5 mm。每个正方形的制造以顺序方式执行，采用逐层方法。每层的制造使用振镜扫描仪进行。每层制造后，样品由轴系统定位到下一层。一个正方形制造完成后，使用轴系统将样品定位到下一个正方形。由于固有的轴公差以及样品架和玻璃基板制造中的局限性，样品只能以相对于显微镜物镜非完全垂直的取向定位。因此，光刻胶和玻璃之间的初始交点（作为打印的起点）在整个打印区域表现出变化。由于在3.5 mm x 3.5 mm区域上垂直度的微小偏差显示出几微米的显著高度误差，这相当于总结构高度，因此实施了倾斜校正。在玻璃基板上的三个不同位置确定起点，随后计算与这三个点相交的平面。在制造过程中，每个制造正方形的初始位置相对于第一个正方形进行调整，调整量与平面成比例。此外，DOE的最小像素高度设定为2 μm，从而确保DOE结构下方存在2 μm厚的基底，用作误差容限。DOE使用光刻胶FemtoBond 4B（Laserzentrum Hannover e.V., Germany）制造，其制造总共耗时68.5小时。所有相关工艺参数列于表1中。**

> # 5. Damage Test
> 
> **5. 损伤测试**
> 
> The Ti:Sa laser, for which the DOE was designed, did not possess sufficient power to reach the damage threshold of the DOE. By applying 2 W average laser power (corresponds to 400 μJ pulse energy) at 800 nm, no damage was detected on the DOE. Consequently, an ultrashort pulse laser with a higher power (Carbide CB3-40W, Light Conversion, UAB) was utilized, featuring a pulse width of 191 fs, a repetition rate of 100 kHz, and a central wavelength of 1030 nm. The repetition rate was selected to achieve the highest possible pulse energy. The employment of a lens enabled the matching of the raw beam diameter to the DOE diameter of 3.5 mm. Subsequently, the power was increased in 5 % steps of the lasers maximum output power until the DOE was destroyed. Each power step was maintained for a duration of 30 seconds. The DOE was able to withstand an average power of 22.8 W, which equates to a pulse energy of 228 μJ at a repetition frequency of 100 kHz. With regard to the irradiated area of the DOE, which has a diameter of 3.5 mm, a resulting peak power intensity of 24.8 GW/cm² could be calculated. The high damage threshold is potentially attributable to the thin thickness of 6.4 μm of the DOE, which results in a reduction of laser absorption by the material. The determined damage threshold of the DOE is comparable to the damage threshold study by Butkute et al. [33], in which a photoresist (SZ2080) of similar composition was used and a maximum laser fluence of 17 ± 9 mJ/cm² was achieved when focusing a 1030 nm, 100 kHz, and 300 fs laser on bulk material with a height of 30 μm. > # 6. Conclusion and outlook
> 
> **6. 结论与展望**
> 
> The findings of this study demonstrate that 2PP-generated DOEs have the potential to be utilized in high-power applications. In order to achieve this objective, a number of significant factors were taken into consideration. Initially, an algorithm was developed that has the capacity to calculate custom DOEs and transform the phase map into 2PP printable STL files. Secondly, by using a stitching algorithm, the size limitation imposed by the field of view of the microscope objective could be exceeded. Furthermore, the DOE's design, with a thickness of 6.4 μm, enabled its fabrication within a reasonable timeframe of 68.5 hours, which can be accomplished over the course of a weekend. Thirdly, the measurements obtained from the beam profiler demonstrate the suitability of the calculated holograms for the purpose of beam shaping. This is evidenced by the successful generation two ring beams. Fourthly, the conducted damage tests provide evidence that the 2PP structures are suitable for utilization at high average laser powers and pulse energies, which are necessary for micro material processing with ultrashort pulse laser systems. An average power of 22.8 W and a peak power intensity of 24.8 GW/cm² could be used without damaging the DOE. These advances establish the prerequisites for future material processing experiments using 2PP fabricated DOEs. The damage threshold may be increased further by reducing the yellow tint and consequently enhancing the transparency of the photoresist. This objective could be accomplished by incorporating an alternative photoinitiator within the photoresist or by completely removing the photoinitiator [34], although this approach may lead to a reduction in processing speed. Furthermore, the removal of the organic component of the utilized organic-inorganic hybrid photoresist through techniques such as calcination [35] might also result in the achievement of higher damage thresholds. Moreover, the beam shape could be enhanced through the utilization of objectives with a higher numerical aperture, thereby resulting in an improved surface quality. However, these objectives possess a smaller FOV, which would result in a substantial increase in fabrication time. Therefore, it is essential to establish a trade-off between processing time and the quality of the beam shape. Another option for increasing the surface quality would be to implement a post processing step like high temperature annealing [36], or coating by atomic layer deposition [37]. **这项研究的结果表明，2PP生成的DOE具有在高功率应用中使用的潜力。为实现这一目标，考虑了许多重要因素。首先，开发了一种算法，能够计算定制DOE并将相位图转换为2PP可打印的STL文件。其次，通过使用拼接算法，可以超越显微镜物镜视场所施加的尺寸限制。此外，DOE的设计厚度为6.4 μm，使其能够在68.5小时的合理时间范围内制造，这可以在一个周末内完成。第三，从光束分析仪获得的测量结果表明，计算的全息图适用于光束整形目的。成功产生两个环形光束证明了这一点。第四，进行的损伤测试提供了证据，表明2PP结构适用于高平均激光功率和脉冲能量，这对于使用超短脉冲激光系统进行微材料加工是必要的。22.8 W的平均功率和24.8 GW/cm²的峰值功率强度可在不损坏DOE的情况下使用。这些进展为未来使用2PP制造的DOE进行材料加工实验建立了先决条件。通过减少黄色色调从而提高光刻胶的透明度，可进一步提高损伤阈值。这一目标可通过在光刻胶中加入替代光引发剂或完全去除光引发剂[34]来实现，尽管这种方法可能导致加工速度降低。此外，通过煅烧[35]等技术去除所用有机-无机杂化光刻胶的有机组分，也可能实现更高的损伤阈值。此外，可通过使用具有更高数值孔径的物镜来增强光束形状，从而获得改进的表面质量。然而，这些物镜具有较小的FOV，这将导致制造时间大幅增加。因此，必须在加工时间和光束形状质量之间建立权衡。提高表面质量的另一个选择是实施后处理步骤，如高温退火[36]或通过原子层沉积涂层[37]。**
