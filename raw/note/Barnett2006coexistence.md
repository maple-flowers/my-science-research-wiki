> # Coexistence of gapless excitations and commensurate charge-density wave in the 2H-transition metal dichalcogenides

> An unexpected feature common to 2H-transition metal dichalcogenides (2H-TMDs) is revealed with first-principles Wannier functions analysis of the electronic structure of the prototype 2H-TaSe₂: The low-energy Ta “5dz₂” bands governing the physics of charge-density wave (CDW) is dominated by hopping between next-nearest neighbors. With this motivation we develop a minimal effective model for the CDW formation, in which the unusual form of the hopping leads to an approximate decoupling of the three sublattices. In the CDW phase one sublattice remains undistorted, leaving the bands associated with it ungapped everywhere in the Fermi surface, resolving the long-standing puzzle of coexistence of gapless excitations and commensurate CDW in the 2H-TMDs.
> 通过对原型2H-TaSe₂电子结构的第一性原理瓦尼尔函数分析，我们揭示了2H-过渡金属二硫化物（2H-TMDs）的一个意想不到的共同特征：主导电荷密度波（CDW）物理的低能Ta “5dz₂” 能带主要由次近邻之间的跃迁主导。基于此，我们为CDW的形成开发了一个最小有效模型，其中不寻常的跃迁形式导致三个子晶格的近似解耦。在CDW相中，一个子晶格保持未畸变，使得与之相关的能带在费米面上的任何地方都保持无间隙，从而解决了在2H-TMDs中无间隙激发与公度CDW共存的长期难题。
> **First-principles**: 第一性原理，指基于量子力学基本原理，不依赖经验参数的计算方法。
> **Wannier functions**: 瓦尼尔函数，固体物理中一组完备的正交局域函数，用于描述电子的局域化行为。
> **Hopping**: 跃迁，描述电子从一个原子轨道（或瓦尼尔函数中心）移动到另一个的量子力学过程。
> **Sublattice**: 子晶格，一个晶体结构可以被分解为几个相互穿插的等效布拉维格子。
> **Fermi surface**: 费米面，在倒空间中，将占据态和非占据态分开的等能面。

> Charge-density waves (CDWs) in solids has been a topic of central interest in condensed matter physics for many years [1]. Recent scanning tunneling microscopy experiments showing a periodic modulation in the local density of states in cuprate superconductors [2, 3], has reinvigorated such interest. Despite being one of the earliest discovered class of materials which exhibit a CDW at low temperatures [4], many properties of the 2H-transition metal dichalcogenides (2H-TMDs) are still not understood, leading to much recent theoretical [5, 6, 7] and experimental [8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15] research effort (for a review, see Ref. [16]). Two key issues concerning the CDW phase in these materials deserve the most attention. First, controversy exists between different experimental groups on the driving mechanism of CDW originating from quantitative differences between the angle-resolved photoemission spectroscopy (ARPES) data. While some experimental results [9, 10, 11, 12] suggest that the hexagonal Fermi surfaces around the Γ point are consistent with the CDW nesting vector, others [13, 14, 15] indicate that this Fermi surface is too large to give the correct nesting vector. Second, and of a more qualitative nature, ARPES measurements [9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15] find no evidence of a gap opening on the hexagonal Fermi surface, in direct contrast with traditional wisdom of CDW materials.
> 固体中的电荷密度波（CDWs）多年来一直是凝聚态物理中的一个核心课题[1]。近期扫描隧道显微镜实验在铜氧化物超导体中观测到局域态密度的周期性调制[2, 3]，重新激发了这一兴趣。尽管2H-过渡金属二硫化物（2H-TMDs）是最早发现的在低温下表现出CDW的材料之一[4]，但其许多性质仍未被理解，导致近期有大量的理论[5, 6, 7]和实验[8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15]研究努力（综述见参考文献[16]）。关于这些材料中CDW相的两个关键问题最值得关注。首先，由于角分辨光电子能谱数据之间的定量差异，不同实验组对CDW的驱动机制存在争议。一些实验结果[9, 10, 11, 12]表明，Γ点周围的六角形费米面与CDW嵌套矢量一致，而另一些[13, 14, 15]则指出该费米面太大，无法给出正确的嵌套矢量。其次，一个更具定性性质的问题是，ARPES测量[9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15]未发现六角形费米面打开能隙的证据，这与CDW材料的传统认知直接矛盾。
> **Scanning tunneling microscopy (STM)**: 扫描隧道显微镜，一种利用量子隧道效应获得表面原子级图像的仪器。
> **Local density of states (LDOS)**: 局域态密度，描述实空间中特定位置和能量下可用的电子态数目。
> **Angle-resolved photoemission spectroscopy (ARPES)**: 角分辨光电子能谱，一种直接测量固体中电子能带结构的实验技术。
> **Nesting vector**: 嵌套矢量，连接费米面上平行部分的波矢，它会导致体系对特定波长的微扰响应增强，从而引发电荷或自旋密度波。

> In this Letter, we focus on the latter issue and suggest a simple picture for why such gapless excitations are permitted in the CDW phase. Using density-functional theory, the electronic structure of prototype 2H-TMD, 2H-TaSe₂, is analyzed with a newly developed Wannier function (WF) approach [17, 18], and a striking feature is revealed: the low-energy bands near Fermi surfaces, which governs the physics of CDW, is dominated by hopping between second-nearest neighbors. This special nature of hopping, in combination with the triangular lattice vectors, effectively splits the system into three weakly coupled triangular sublattices. Since the CDW state gives distortion of only two of the sublattices, whose stability is illustrated with a simple model, such unique electronic structure naturally leaves the bands associated with the undistorted sublattice ungapped in this CDW phase, resolving the puzzle of the observed gapless excitations along the nested regions of the Fermi surface.
> 在这封信中，我们关注后一个问题，并对为何无间隙激发在CDW相中被允许提出了一个简单的物理解释。利用密度泛函理论，我们使用新开发的瓦尼尔函数（WF）方法[17, 18]分析了原型2H-TMD——2H-TaSe₂的电子结构，并揭示了一个显著特征：主导CDW物理的费米面附近的低能带主要由次近邻之间的跃迁主导。这种特殊的跃迁性质，结合三角晶格矢量，有效地将系统分裂为三个弱耦合的三角子晶格。由于CDW态仅使其中两个子晶格发生畸变（其稳定性通过一个简单模型说明），这种独特的电子结构自然地使得与未畸变子晶格相关的能带在此CDW相中保持无间隙，从而解决了在费米面嵌套区域观测到无间隙激发的难题。
> **Density-functional theory (DFT)**: 密度泛函理论，是一种通过电子密度而非波函数来研究多电子体系电子结构的方法。
> 
> The lattice structure of 2H-TMDs consists of stacked layers of 2D-triangular lattices of transition metals (e.g. Ta or Nb) sandwiched between layers of chalcogen atoms (e.g. S or Se). Rough estimation of the ionization leads to the Ta⁴⁺Se²⁻₂ configuration with one valence 5dz₂ electron left per Ta atom that forms the metallic bands at the Fermi level. Indeed, the band structure from our first-principles calculations [19] (see Fig. 1) shows a strong dz₂ character in the two metallic bands corresponding to two weakly coupled TaSe₂ sandwiches per unit cell. Unexpectedly, little Se p characters are found in these two bands. The calculated low-energy bands agree well with experiments, except that the saddle bands on the ΓK and AH lines are not as flat and close to the Fermi level as reported [9, 11].
> 2H-TMDs的晶格结构由过渡金属（如Ta或Nb）的二维三角晶格层堆叠而成，这些层夹在硫族原子（如S或Se）层之间。粗略的电离估算导致Ta⁴⁺Se²⁻₂构型，每个Ta原子留下一个价态5dz₂电子，在费米能级形成金属带。实际上，我们的第一性原理计算[19]（见图1）得到的能带结构显示，两个金属带具有强烈的dz₂特征，这两个金属带对应于每个晶胞中两个弱耦合的TaSe₂夹层。出乎意料的是，在这两个带中几乎未发现Se p特征。计算得到的低能带与实验吻合良好，除了ΓK和AH线上的鞍点带不如文献[9, 11]报道的那样平坦和接近费米能级。
> 
> FIG. 1: First-principles band structure (dots) with the dz₂ (black circles) and dxy/dx₂-y₂ (blue circles) characters shown. The bands below −0.7 eV are mainly Se p bands. Also shown are the bands constructed from low-energy WFs (green solid lines) and a 2D ‘nesting’ model (red dotted line; see text).
> 图1：第一性原理能带结构（点），显示了dz₂（黑色圆圈）和dxy/dx₂-y₂（蓝色圆圈）特征。−0.7 eV以下的能带主要是Se p带。图中还显示了由低能瓦尼尔函数构建的能带（绿色实线）和一个二维“嵌套”模型（红色虚线；见正文）。

> Based on the first-principles ground state, the low-energy Hilbert space can be accurately extracted via local WFs (see Fig. 2), which we constructed by extending recently developed energy-resolved method [17, 18] to incorporate desired symmetry [21]. As expected, the WF located at each Ta site has strong dz₂ symmetry near the center, before extending its unusual tails of dxy/dx₂-y₂ symmetry to the nearest neighboring Ta sites due to strong hybridization with the dxy/dx₂-y₂ orbitals near the K and H points (see Fig. 1). This particular shape of the WF results in an intriguing feature in the hopping integral (evaluated via t_μν = ⟨μ|h_DFT|ν⟩ with density functional theory Hamiltonian, h_DFT, and Wannier states |μ⟩ and |ν⟩). That is, the second neighbor hopping, t₂ = 115 meV, overwhelms the first neighbor hopping, t₁ = 38 meV, due to remarkable phase cancelation in the latter case (to be discussed in more detail below). In addition, interlayer hoppings are found to be comparable to first-neighbor in-plane hopping with t⊥,1 = 29 meV and t⊥,2 = 23 meV.
> 基于第一性原理基态，低能希尔伯特空间可以通过局域瓦尼尔函数精确提取（见图2），我们通过扩展最近发展的能量分辨方法[17, 18]以纳入所需的对称性[21]来构建瓦尼尔函数。正如预期，位于每个Ta位点的瓦尼尔函数在中心附近具有强烈的dz₂对称性，然后由于其与K和H点附近的dxy/dx₂-y₂轨道的强杂化，将其不寻常的dxy/dx₂-y₂对称性尾部延伸到最近邻Ta位点（见图1）。瓦尼尔函数的这种特殊形状导致跃迁积分（通过t_μν = ⟨μ|h_DFT|ν⟩和密度泛函理论哈密顿量h_DFT以及瓦尼尔态|μ⟩和|ν⟩计算）具有一个有趣的特征。即，由于后者情况下的显著相位相消，次近邻跃迁t₂ = 115 meV超过了最近邻跃迁t₁ = 38 meV（下文将更详细讨论）。此外，发现层间跃迁与最近邻面内跃迁相当，t⊥,1 = 29 meV，t⊥,2 = 23 meV。
> **Hilbert space**: 希尔伯特空间，量子力学中描述量子态所属的完备向量空间。
> **Hybridization**: 杂化，原子轨道线性组合形成新的杂化轨道的过程或电子轨道间的混合。
> **Hamiltonian**: 哈密顿量，描述系统总能量的算符。

> FIG. 2: a) Low-energy WF centered at Ta sites with ag (dz₂) symmetry, colored to show its gradient from positive (red) to negative (blue). Notice the local dxy/dx₂-y₂ symmetry in the hybridization tail (circled) located at neighboring Ta sites. b) Schematics of the WF in the layer of the Ta triangular lattice, with a similar color scheme giving the sign of the WF and three small dots marking the positions of Se atoms in the next layer. c) and d) Schematics of phase interference in hopping to first and second nearest neighbors, respectively.
> 图2：a) 以Ta位点为中心的低能瓦尼尔函数，具有ag（dz₂）对称性，用颜色显示其从正（红色）到负（蓝色）的梯度。注意位于邻近Ta位点的杂化尾部（圆圈内）的局域dxy/dx₂-y₂对称性。b) Ta三角晶格层中瓦尼尔函数的示意图，类似的颜色方案给出了瓦尼尔函数的符号，三个小点标记了下一层中Se原子的位置。c) 和 d) 分别为跃迁到最近邻和次近邻时相位干涉的示意图。

> A simple microscopic picture for the unexpected dominance of second-neighbor hopping can be obtained from the symmetry of the WFs. As shown in Fig. 2(c) and (d), the contributions to the hopping parameters between neighboring WFs come mainly from overlap of their hybridization tails, since the tail-center (dz₂ − dxy/dx₂-y₂) overlap gives negligible contribution due to its odd parity. While the first-neighbor hopping suffers seriously from the phase cancelation (illustrated by the opposite sign in Fig. 2(c)), the second-neighbor hopping benefits greatly from the phase coherence (the same sign in Fig. 2(d)) of the overlap. Such symmetry consideration should hold for all 2H-TMDs of the same class, due to their similar local environment around the transition metal sites. Specifically in 2H-TaSe₂, this unusual electronic structure provides a plausible intuitive resolution to the puzzling experimental observation of gapless excitations in the CDW phase.

> A question that naturally follows is, how robust this result is when the finite first neighbor interaction—which mixes all bands and thus destroys the exact decoupling into the three independent sublattices—is taken into account. We examine this issue, switching on the first-neighbor electron-phonon coupling constant γ₁ in Eq. (4), up to a third of the value of γ₂ as suggested by the first principles results: t₁ ≃ t₂/3. As expected, we find that the degeneracy of the bands originating from the distorted sublattices shown in Fig. 5(b) is lifted. In addition, the triple degeneracy originating from the undistorted sublattice shown in Fig. 5(a) at the Fermi energy is lifted. However, this does not produce a quasiparticle gap at the Fermi energy. More specifically, this triple degeneracy is lifted in such a way that two of the energies are increased to above the Fermi energy and the other one is decreased to below the Fermi energy. It can be seen that this will indeed not gap the Fermi surface, given γ₁ is considerably smaller than γ₂. 一个自然随之而来的问题是，当考虑有限的最近邻相互作用——它混合了所有能带，从而破坏了到三个独立子晶格的精确解耦——时，这一结果有多稳健。我们研究了这个问题，在方程(4)中开启最近邻电子-声子耦合常数γ₁，其值高达第一性原理结果建议的γ₂的三分之一：t₁ ≃ t₂/3。正如预期，我们发现图5(b)中显示的源自畸变子晶格的能带的简并被解除。此外，图5(a)中显示的源自未畸变子晶格在费米能处的三重简并也被解除。然而，这并未在费米能处产生准粒子能隙。更具体地说，这种三重简并是以这样一种方式解除的：三个能量中的两个增加到费米能级以上，另一个降低到费米能级以下。可以看出，鉴于γ₁远小于γ₂，这确实不会使费米面产生能隙。

> In conclusion, we have studied the CDW state in the 2H-transition metal dichalcogenides and found that due to a unique feature in the electronic structure of these materials revealed from first-principles calculations, the triangular lattice can be effectively decoupled into three independent sublattices, with one remaining undistorted in the CDW phase. As illustrated with a model calculation, this leads to the remarkable situation where no regions of the entire Fermi surface become gapped even when these materials exhibit a commensurate CDW. 总之，我们研究了2H-过渡金属二硫化物中的CDW态，并发现，由于第一性原理计算揭示的这些材料电子结构的一个独特特征，三角晶格可以有效地解耦为三个独立的子晶格，其中一个在CDW相中保持未畸变。正如模型计算所说明的，这导致了一个显著的情况：即使这些材料表现出公度CDW，整个费米面上也没有任何区域变得有能隙。
