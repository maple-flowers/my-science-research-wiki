> ## 一、引言
> 
> 在数据爆炸的时代，传统计算机体系结构（冯·诺依曼架构）的“存储墙”和“功耗墙”问题日益凸显，促使研究者们探寻能够模拟人脑高效并行处理与低功耗特性的**神经形态计算**技术。**忆阻器**，作为一种电阻值随外加电压历史而改变的非易失性存储器件，被认为是实现人工神经突触的核心候选者之一。然而，传统硅基忆阻器在微缩化、柔性和成本方面面临挑战，这为二维（2D）材料开辟了新的应用舞台。
> 
> 在此背景下，本研究首次报道了一种基于功能化双过渡金属MXene的柔性忆阻器。**MXene**是一类新型的二维过渡金属碳化物/氮化物，其通式为`M_{n+1}X_nT_x`，因其丰富的表面化学、可调带隙和优异的机械性能而备受关注。本文聚焦于一种特定的双过渡金属MXene——**碳化钼钛（Mo₂Ti₂C₃Tx）**，通过简单的热处理成功诱导出其**铁电性**，并将其作为功能层，结合激光还原石墨烯（LSG）电极，构筑了三明治结构的高性能柔性阻变存储器（RRAM）。
> 
> 该研究的核心价值在于，它不仅为双过渡金属MXene家族开辟了“铁电学”这一新应用方向，也为下一代柔性、可穿戴的非易失性存储及神经形态计算设备提供了全新的材料体系和器件设计思路。
>

> ### 图7：器件结构与基本电学特性
> 
> -   **(a) 柔性器件照片**: 展示了LSG/H-DTM/LSG三明治结构器件具有良好的**柔性**。
> -   **(b) 器件结构示意图**: 直观展示了“LSG顶电极 / 热处理MXene活性层 / LSG底电极”的三层结构。
> -   **(c) I-V特性曲线**: 对比了原始MXene和热处理MXene器件的I-V特性。原始MXene器件表现为线性电阻，无开关效应。而**热处理MXene器件**在±4V扫描电压下，呈现出典型的双极性阻变特性：在+1.8V左右由高阻态（HRS）**SET**到低阻态（LRS），在-1.7V左右由LRS **RESET**回HRS。开关比（Roff/Ron）约为`10²`。

> 
> ### 图8：存储性能
> 
> -   **(a) 半对数I-V曲线**: 更清晰地展示了两种电阻状态之间的稳定切换。
> -   **(b) 循环耐久性**: 器件在`10³`次循环内，HRS和LRS的电阻值保持稳定，未见明显退化。
> -   **(c) 数据保持性**: 在长达`5×10³秒`（约83分钟）的测试中，HRS和LRS的电阻值几乎没有波动，表明数据存储具有良好的稳定性。
> 
> ### 图9：导电细丝机制模型图
> 
> -   直观地描绘了存储器在三个状态下的内部微观过程：
>     1.  **初始态/高阻态**: 氧空位随机分布，未形成贯通路径。
>     2.  **SET过程（低阻态）**: 在正向电压和铁电内电场的共同作用下，氧空位从底电极向上迁移，排列成连接上下电极的**导电细丝**，器件导通。
>     3.  **RESET过程（高阻态）**: 在反向电压下，氧空位被拉回，导电细丝在最细处断裂，器件恢复高阻态。
>

> ## 四、正文核心内容剖析
>

> ## 四、正文核心内容剖析
> 
> ### 1. 研究背景与动机
> 
> -   **核心挑战**：传统硅基忆阻器存在制造成本高、难以柔性化、性能不稳定等问题。新型二维材料，特别是MXene，因其丰富的表面化学、可调带隙和高机械强度成为理想候选。
> -   **研究空白**：双过渡金属MXene（如Mo₂Ti₂C₃Tₓ）在忆阻器领域的应用研究极少，其铁电性的诱导与利用更是空白。
> -   **本文策略**：利用热处理在MXene框架内原位生成TiO₂等铁电相，复合而成具有铁电性的功能层，同时采用激光还原石墨烯这一柔性电极，旨在构建全二维、柔性、高性能忆阻器。
> 
> ### 2. 核心方法解析
> 
> -   **MXene热处理**：这是激活铁电性的关键步骤。在400°C真空加热，促使MXene层内深层的Ti原子与表面官能团反应，原位生成**金红石型TiO₂纳米晶**。由于TiO₂与MXene晶格应变和界面效应，诱导了整体薄膜的宏观铁电性。
> -   **激光还原GO**：通过激光束的高能量，将绝缘的GO中的含氧官能团（如环氧基、羟基）光热解离，将其转化为导电的**还原氧化石墨烯（rGO）**。这种方法无需化学试剂，快速、可控，且能直接图案化，是实现柔性电极的巧妙手段。
> 
> ### 3. 结果与讨论精要
> 
> -   **铁电性的起源**：多种表征手段（XRD、FTIR、Raman、PFM、P-E loop）构成完整证据链，一致指向**热处理诱导的TiO₂（可能还有MoO₃）**是铁电性产生的根本原因。PFM和P-E loop从微观和宏观层面直接证实了铁电畴的翻转。
> -   **电阻转变机制**：作者提出了一个**铁电调控的氧空位导电细丝模型**。
>     -   **氧空位来源**：MXene氧化形成的TiO₂/MoO₃本身富含氧空位。
>     -   **铁电场的调控**：MXene的铁电性产生的内部极化电场，为氧空位的定向迁移提供了“天然导轨”，使其能更快、更有序地形成导电细丝，从而降低了操作的随机性，增强了器件的稳定性和均一性。
>     -   **传导验证**：HRS态的Log I-Log V拟合斜率≈2，符合**空间电荷限制电流**机制，这是导电细丝通道形成前，载流子被陷阱捕获和释放的典型特征。
> 
> ### 4. 关键数据解读
> 
> -   **开关比（RON/ROFF）≈ 10²**：这是一个中等偏上的性能指标，足以满足大多数存储和简单逻辑运算的需求，表明高、低阻态之间区分度明显。
> -   **耐久性 > 10³ 次**：证明器件在多次连续写入/擦除操作后，性能依然稳定，具有实用化潜力。
> -   **保持性 > 5×10³ 秒**：说明存储的信息在断电后能长期保存，确认了其非易失性。

> ## 七、学术思考与批判性分析
> 
> 1.  **铁电性来源的精准归属**：文中将铁电性归因于金红石TiO₂，但金红石TiO₂块体通常被认为是顺电的。该体系的铁电性起源可能更复杂，是否源于**应变诱导的纳米晶金红石TiO₂**、**TiO₂与MXene的界面效应**，或两者协同作用？文中引用的支持文献[61]是关于负压诱导的相变，这暗示了界面的应变可能是关键。未来需要更直接的原子尺度表征来澄清。
>     
> 2.  **MoO₃在器件中的作用**：文中明确了MoO₃的形成，但在机理解释中主要聚焦于TiO₂。MoO₃本身也是一种重要的忆阻材料，其氧空位迁移率很高。那么，MoO₃相在导电细丝的形成和断裂中扮演了何种角色？是辅助、主导，还是仅仅作为旁观者？澄清这一点至关重要。

> 3.  **性能对比的深度**：文末对比表显示，本工作的开关比(10²)和耐久性(10³)并非文献中最优。例如，Ag/Ti₃C₂Tₓ/Pt器件开关比高达10⁶。虽然作者强调了柔性和铁电调控的新颖性，但若能在器件尺寸、操作电压、速度等方面与标杆器件进行更全面的对比，并讨论其性能差距的可能原因，论证将更具说服力。

> 4.  **逻辑链的严谨性**：作者提出“铁电内部电场”调控了氧空位的定向迁移，这是一个非常精彩的推论。但文章并未提供直接证据，例如通过原位PFM测量在电场下观察畴翻转与导电通道形成的关联。目前的证据链是：**A（有铁电）+ B（有导电细丝行为）→ C（铁电调控细丝）**。这更多是相关性分析，而非直接因果关系证明。

> ## 八、用户问题预测与解答
> 
> 1.  **问题：什么是MXene？为什么它适合做忆阻器？** **解答**：MXene是一类新型二维过渡金属碳化物/氮化物陶瓷材料。您可以将其想象成“导电的二维黏土”。它由MAX相（一种层状三元化合物）中刻蚀掉A原子层（通常是Al）而得到，多层原子层堆叠，表面带有丰富的官能团（如-O、-OH、-F）。它之所以适合忆阻器，是因为：(1) 本征的金属导电性为载流子传输提供了通道；(2) 丰富的表面官能团和缺陷是离子迁移（如氧空位）的“高速公路”，有利于形成导电细丝；(3) 二维层状结构使其极易制成柔性薄膜，符合可穿戴设备的需求。
>     
> 2.  **问题：什么是铁电性？它在忆阻器中起什么作用？** **解答**：铁电性是指材料在没有外加电场时，也能保持一种可被外加电场翻转的自发极化状态。就像一个“微小的电子开关”，其指向可以“向上”或“向下”。在本研究中，铁电性起“方向导航”作用。铁电材料的内部极化电场像一个内置的“定向车道”，引导氧空位离子更有序、更快地排列形成导电通道，从而让忆阻器在高低电阻状态间的切换更稳定、更可控，解决了传统忆阻器中导电细丝生长随机导致的性能不稳定问题。
>     
> 3.  **问题：激光还原石墨烯（LSG）和普通的石墨烯有何不同？为什么用它做电极？** **解答**：普通石墨烯通常通过化学气相沉积或机械剥离获得，工艺复杂。LSG是直接用激光照射氧化石墨烯（GO）薄膜，将其原位还原成石墨烯。优势在于：(1) **工艺简单**：无需化学试剂和高温，速度快，可在大气环境中进行；(2) **图案化灵活**：激光可以像“笔”一样直接在GO薄膜上“写”出任意的电极图案；(3) **柔性兼容**：LSG本身是柔性的，与柔性的MXene层完美匹配，共同构成了全柔性器件。
>     
> 4.  **问题：文中提到的“导电细丝”和“空间电荷限制电流”是什么？** **解答**：
>     
>     -   **导电细丝**：您可以把它想象成忆阻器内部一条由氧空位“离子”排队组成的“隐形导线”。当在电极上施加电压时，这些氧空位会定向迁移，最终连接两个电极，瞬间形成一条纳米级的导电通路，使器件从高阻态变为低阻态（开启）。当施加反向电压时，这条“导线”在最薄弱处断裂，器件恢复高阻态（关闭）。
>     -   **空间电荷限制电流**：这是在导电细丝形成前，高阻态下的一种电流传导方式。当电子被注入到材料中，若材料内部存在大量陷阱（缺陷），电子会被捕获，移动缓慢。只有当注入的电子填满了大部分陷阱后，后续电子才能自由移动，形成较大电流。其I-V关系近似为I∝V²，是识别导电细丝机制的一个重要“指纹”。

ABSTRACT
摘要
The emergence of two-dimensional (2D) mixed transition metal MXenes has notably broadened the scope of 2D layered materials towards a wide variety of applications. Here, we report for the first time the induced ferroelectricity in free-standing Mo2Ti2C3Tx MXene film after undergoing simple heat treatment and its utilization as an active functional layer for non-volatile resistive random-access memory. The ferroelectric response, oxide phase formation and memory storage kinetics of the free-standing heated Mo2Ti2C3Tx film outperformed the pristine non-heated Mo2Ti2C3Tx. Ferroelectric behavior was observed solely in heated Mo2Ti2C3Tx owing to the formation of TiO2 with typical butterfly dielectric constant loop. Specifically, by utilizing the adaptive property of graphene oxide to reduce into conductive graphene sheets amid laser scribing, a tri-layer memristor structure Laser reduced graphene/heated Mo2Ti2C3Tx/Laser reduced graphene was fabricated and analyzed against the Laser reduced graphene/non-heated Mo2Ti2C3Tx/Laser reduced graphene device scheme. The former tri-layer free-standing scheme exhibited controlled conduction filament formation showcasing excellent resistive switching, sufficient memory window of Roff/Ron = 102 and excellent cycle-to-cycle endurance of up to 103 cycles. This finding reveals the remarkable potential of double transition MXenes to be used as functional middle layer, which was not yet explored in resistive RAM memory storage elements, hence opening the door for another domain of applications i.e. neuromorphic computing and ferroelectronics based on non-volatile memory.
二维(2D)混合过渡金属MXenes的出现，显著拓宽了二维层状材料面向多种应用的范围。在此，我们首次报道了自支撑Mo2Ti2C3Tx MXene薄膜经过简单热处理后诱导产生的铁电性，并将其用作非易失性阻变随机存取存储器的活性功能层。加热后的自支撑Mo2Ti2C3Tx薄膜在铁电响应、氧化物相形成和存储动力学方面均优于未加热的原始Mo2Ti2C3Tx。仅加热后的Mo2Ti2C3Tx观察到铁电行为，这是由于形成了具有典型蝴蝶状介电常数回线的TiO2。具体而言，通过利用氧化石墨烯在激光划刻过程中还原为导电石墨烯片的适应性特性，我们制备了一种三层忆阻器结构：激光还原石墨烯/加热Mo2Ti2C3Tx/激光还原石墨烯，并对其与激光还原石墨烯/未加热Mo2Ti2C3Tx/激光还原石墨烯器件方案进行了对比分析。前者三层自支撑方案表现出受控的导电细丝形成，展示了优异的电阻开关特性、充足的Roff/Ron = 10²的存储窗口以及高达10³次循环的优异循环耐久性。这一发现揭示了双过渡金属MXenes作为功能性中间层的巨大潜力，这在阻变RAM存储元件中尚未被探索，从而为非易失性存储器在神经形态计算和铁电电子学等另一个应用领域打开了大门。

<span class="hljs-bullet">1.</span> Introduction
<span class="hljs-bullet">1.</span> 引言
In an ever-growing effort to reproduce human brain functionalism through artificial neural networks and synapses, current computing Von Neumann technology needs to be replaced by advanced neuromorphic based computing architectures [1–4]. Memristors and non-volatile resistors with memory function, have potential to appear as bio-synapsis mimicking human brain functions on grounds of highly efficient switching features, memory storage without power and high throughput for brain powered applications [5,6]. In order to meet the rising artificial intelligence requirements, such synaptic non-volatile memory devices are required that have functionalism comparable to the human brain such as neuromorphic transmission characteristics, fast switching speed and information storage and processing [7]. Memristors, for this reason, have captivated greatest academic and industrial attention towards the development of an electronic brain and real-time neuromorphic technology. The two-terminal resistive RAM devices (RRAM) work by switching the resistance states, hence storing the memory information when the voltage stress is turned off while an ultralow operating voltage is provided to the two electrode ends of the device. RRAM devices outclass other memory devices from the same non-volatile category such as Ferroelectric RAM (FeRAM), Magneto-resistive RAM (MRAM) and Phase-change RAM (PRAM) because of its unique combination of easy and cost-effective fabrication, high scalability, excellent endurance and fast switching speed, the other NVM being lacking in one property or the other [8].
在通过人工神经网络和突触再现人脑功能的不断努力中，当前的冯·诺依曼计算技术需要被先进的基于神经形态的计算架构所取代[1–4]。忆阻器和具有记忆功能的非易失性电阻，凭借其高效的开关特性、无电源的存储能力以及适用于脑驱动应用的高吞吐量，有潜力作为模拟人脑功能的生物突触出现[5,6]。为了满足日益增长的人工智能需求，需要具有可比拟人脑功能的突触型非易失性存储器件，例如神经形态传输特性、快速开关速度以及信息存储和处理能力[7]。因此，忆阻器在开发电子大脑和实时神经形态技术方面吸引了学术界和工业界的极大关注。两端阻变RAM器件(RRAM)通过切换电阻状态来工作，从而在关闭电压应力时存储记忆信息，同时为器件的两个电极端提供超低工作电压。RRAM器件在非易失性存储器类别中，相较于其他同类器件，如铁电RAM(FeRAM)、磁阻RAM(MRAM)和相变RAM(PRAM)，因其兼具简便且成本效益高的制造工艺、高可扩展性、优异的耐久性和快速开关速度的独特组合而更胜一筹，其他非易失性存储器(NVM)则总会在某一方面有所欠缺[8]。
<span class="hljs-strong">**Neuromorphic Computing**</span>: 神经形态计算，一种模仿神经生物结构和功能的计算架构。
<span class="hljs-strong">**Non-Volatile Memory (NVM)**</span>: 非易失性存储器，在断电后仍能保持存储数据的存储器。

A variety of advancements have been made for employing non-volatile memory devices and memristors in synaptic applications including materials development such as bioelectronic memories and advanced strategies such as ultrasonic signals modulating the resistance states in artificial synaptic networks [9,10]. Different conduction mechanisms based on electronic and ionic charge transport have been investigated that are responsible for driving the resistance switching (RS) devices including electrochemical metallization (ECM), oxygen vacancy filament, redox reactions and several interface mediated phenomena [11–13]. To meet the need for designing memristor devices, unconventional materials or their counterparts are required as the conventional metal/oxide/metal or semiconductor tri-layer scheme, despite exhibiting fast switching responses, cannot be scaled to meet exceptionally demanding efficiency and density requirements for artificial neural networks [14–16]. For example, common silicon-based memristors experience significant constraints when it comes to RS variability, stored data reliability, information fidelity and unaffordable fabrication costs. Furthermore, substrate-grown memory architectures face limitations of device miniaturization, bending stability and durability. These challenges altogether hinder the performance of conventional memory devices for flexible and wearable electronics [17–19] and future machine learning.
在将非易失性存储器件和忆阻器应用于突触应用方面已取得了多种进展，包括材料开发，如生物电子存储器，以及先进的策略，如利用超声信号调制人工突触网络中的电阻状态[9,10]。研究人员已经研究了基于电子和离子电荷传输的不同导电机制，这些机制负责驱动电阻开关(RS)器件，包括电化学金属化(ECM)、氧空位导电细丝、氧化还原反应和几种界面介导现象[11–13]。为了满足设计忆阻器件的需求，需要非常规材料或其对应物，因为传统的金属/氧化物/金属或半导体三层方案，尽管表现出快速的开关响应，但无法按比例缩放以满足人工神经网络极其苛刻的效率和密度要求[14–16]。例如，常见的硅基忆阻器在RS变异性、存储数据可靠性、信息保真度和高昂的制造成本方面面临显著限制。此外，基于衬底生长的存储器架构面临器件小型化、弯曲稳定性和耐用性的限制。这些挑战共同阻碍了传统存储器件在柔性和可穿戴电子设备[17–19]以及未来机器学习中的性能。
<span class="hljs-strong">**Resistance Switching (RS)**</span>: 电阻开关，指材料在外部电场作用下，电阻值在高阻态和低阻态之间可逆变化的现象。
<span class="hljs-strong">**Electrochemical Metallization (ECM)**</span>: 电化学金属化，一种基于活性金属电极离子迁移和氧化还原反应的阻变机制。

The upsurge in exploring 2D materials has led the academia and industrial researchers to incorporate and investigate 2D layered MXene structures in non-volatile memory architectures [4,5,14,20–22]. The tunable band gap properties, rich surface chemistry along with the excellent mechanical strength and flexible free-standing synthesis of MXene family make them suitable aspirant for a plethora of applications [23–27]. The substrate-free flexible synthesis technique of MXene and their cost-effective fabrication proposition in a memristor device is a step forward towards wearable data-storage devices and outperforms their performance from other memristors. Originated from MAX precursors with the chemical formula Mn+1AXn, these 2D derivatives are left with Mn+1XnTx layered structure with ‘A’, mainly Al or Si, being etched out after chemical etching process. The rest ‘M’ is a family of single or double transition metal or metals, ‘X’ being carbon, nitrogen or the combination of carbon and nitrogen and ‘Tx’ are the surface terminations that get attached as residuals towards the end of synthesis. In a long series of MXene array, thicker MXene structures containing two different transition metals, known as double transition MXenes; general formula being M3C2 and M4C3, are not yet explored to its full potential especially a combination of molybdenum and titanium metals [28–31]. Double Transition-Metal MXenes (DTM) are extended version of layered 2D MXene structures that exhibit excellent mechanical and electrical properties [31–33] owing to the enriched nature of different transition metals, chemically bonded in a single layered structure.
探索二维材料的热潮引领学术界和工业界的研究人员将二维层状MXene结构引入并研究于非易失性存储器架构中[4,5,14,20–22]。MXene家族可调的带隙特性、丰富的表面化学性质，以及优异的机械强度和灵活的自支撑合成能力，使其成为众多应用的合适候选者[23–27]。MXene的无衬底柔性合成技术及其在忆阻器件中具有成本效益的制造方案，是向可穿戴数据存储设备迈出的一步，其性能优于其他忆阻器。这些二维衍生物源自化学式为Mn+1AXn的MAX相前驱体，经过化学刻蚀过程，‘A’（主要是Al或Si）被刻蚀掉后，留下Mn+1XnTx层状结构。剩下的‘M’是单一或双过渡金属族，‘X’是碳、氮或碳氮组合，‘Tx’是在合成结束时作为残余物附着的表面终止基团。在长长的MXene系列中，含有两种不同过渡金属的较厚MXene结构，即双过渡金属MXenes，其通式为M3C2和M4C3，其全部潜力尚未被探索，特别是钼和钛金属的组合[28–31]。双过渡金属MXenes(DTM)是层状二维MXene结构的扩展版本，由于不同过渡金属在单层结构中以化学键合，其性质丰富，因而展现出优异的机械和电学性能[31–33]。
<span class="hljs-strong">**MAX phase**</span>: MAX相，一类层状三元碳化物/氮化物，是合成MXene的前驱体。

Furthermore, 2D ferroelectrics are being searched extensively to improve the device performance in tri-layer memory circuits, and many reports utilizing ferroelectric nature of materials have been published exhibiting functional data storage devices [34–38]. Notably, the recent advancements in inducing ferroelectricity and tailoring the band gap of MXene after it undergoes heat treatment have opened up additional degrees of freedom to control the charge transport in such materials when used for non-volatile memory applications [39]. On the other hand, Graphene, owing to its versatile behavior, has found multiple utilizations in energy storage, data storage and photocatalytic applications. The use of graphene oxide (GO) as an active middle layer or assisted layer to enhance the switching characteristics in a memristor circuit has been reported comprehensively [40–42]. However, laser scribing GO is an efficient and easy way to switch its properties from insulator to metal by removing oxygen functionalities from the GO sheet, hence opening a gate to be used as metallic electrodes in data storage/resistive RAM circuits [43,44]. The utilization of rGO as electrodes addresses the issues of leakage current and diffusion of ions in conventional metal electrodes when being employed in non-volatile memory structures.
此外，人们正在广泛寻找二维铁电体以改善三层存储电路中的器件性能，并且已发表了许多利用材料铁电性质展示功能性数据存储器件的报告[34–38]。值得注意的是，最近在MXene经过热处理后诱导铁电性和调整其带隙方面取得的进展，为将此类材料用于非易失性存储器应用时控制电荷传输开辟了额外的自由度[39]。另一方面，石墨烯由于其多功能性，在能量存储、数据存储和光催化应用中得到了多种利用。使用氧化石墨烯(GO)作为活性中间层或辅助层以增强忆阻器电路中的开关特性已有广泛报道[40–42]。然而，激光划刻GO是一种高效且简便的方法，通过去除GO片上的含氧官能团，将其属性从绝缘体转变为金属，从而为在数据存储/阻变RAM电路中用作金属电极开辟了道路[43,44]。在非易失性存储器结构中使用还原氧化石墨烯(rGO)作为电极，解决了传统金属电极存在的漏电流和离子扩散问题。
<span class="hljs-strong">**rGO**</span>: 还原氧化石墨烯，即Reduced Graphene Oxide。

In this report, comprehensive ferroelectric measurements along with Piezo Force Microscopy (PFM) were carried out for the first time for the double transition MXene molybdenum titanium carbide (Mo2Ti2C3Tx) intrinsically and heated double transition MXene (Ht- Mo2Ti2C3Tx). Along with that, this is the very first study of employing functional Mo2Ti2C3Tx as an active middle layer in a tri-layer memristor scheme utilizing GO in its laser reduced form i.e. laser scribed graphene (LSG) as top and bottom electrodes.
在本报告中，首次对双过渡金属MXene钼钛碳化物(Mo2Ti2C3Tx)本征态和加热后的双过渡金属MXene(Ht-Mo2Ti2C3Tx)进行了全面的铁电测量以及压电响应力显微镜(PFM)分析。此外，这也是首次将功能性Mo2Ti2C3Tx作为活性中间层应用于三层忆阻器方案的研究，该方案利用GO的激光还原形态，即激光划刻石墨烯(LSG)作为顶部和底部电极。
<span class="hljs-strong">**Piezo Force Microscopy (PFM)**</span>: 压电响应力显微镜，一种用于在纳米尺度表征材料压电和铁电特性的扫描探针显微技术。

<span class="hljs-bullet">2.</span> Experimental section
<span class="hljs-bullet">2.</span> 实验部分
The wet chemical etching process was utilized for the synthesis of Mo2Ti2C3Tx from its MAX phase. The MAX phase was pre-washed with HCl to remove the impurities and was then subjected to HF etching. The solution (1g MAX + 10 ml HF) is allowed to homogeneously stir for 96 h at 500 rpm. Centrifugation with DI water was further done to remove acidic impurities from etched powder and maintain the pH &gt; 6 to obtain exfoliated MXene powder. The complete synthesis protocol has already been reported in Ref. [30]. The etched Mo2Ti2C3Tx powder was delaminated with the organic base 25 % TMAOH to obtain free-standing flexible film of Mo2Ti2C3Tx. The etched powder was slowly added to the 6 ml TMAOH +34 ml DI water dispersion solution, dried at 65 ◦ C @ 650 rpm for 5 h, washed and then centrifuged at 2380 rcf for an hour to separate few layers delaminated Mo2Ti2C3Tx. The free-standing MXene film was further heated at 400 ◦ C in a vacuum oven to induce ferroelectric and oxide phases that was used as an active layer in memristor structure. Drop-wise spin coating technique was used to acquire GO free-standing film after making a homogenous solution of GO powder in deionized water. The spinning was performed at 700 rpm until the solution was dried and could be lifted off from the glass substrate. This GO free standing film was passed through the laser scriber, operating at engraving speed of 900 mm/min to remove the oxygen contained species from GO and reduce it to conductive LSG.
采用湿化学刻蚀法从MAX相合成Mo2Ti2C3Tx。MAX相先用HCl预清洗以去除杂质，然后进行HF刻蚀。将溶液(1克MAX + 10毫升HF)在500转/分下均匀搅拌96小时。然后用去离子(DI)水进一步离心，以去除刻蚀粉末中的酸性杂质，并保持pH &gt; 6，以获得剥离的MXene粉末。完整的合成方案已在文献[30]中报道。用有机碱25% TMAOH对刻蚀后的Mo2Ti2C3Tx粉末进行分层处理，以获得自支撑的柔性Mo2Ti2C3Tx薄膜。将刻蚀粉末缓慢加入6毫升TMAOH + 34毫升去离子水的分散液中，在65°C、650转/分下干燥5小时，洗涤，然后在2380 rcf下离心一小时，以分离出少层分层的Mo2Ti2C3Tx。随后将自支撑MXene薄膜在真空烘箱中于400°C下进一步加热，以诱导铁电相和氧化物相，并将其用作忆阻器结构中的活性层。将GO粉末在去离子水中制成均匀溶液后，采用逐滴旋涂技术获得GO自支撑薄膜。旋涂在700转/分下进行，直至溶液干燥并可从玻璃基底上剥离。将此GO自支撑薄膜通过激光划刻机，以900毫米/分钟的雕刻速度操作，以去除GO中的含氧物种，并将其还原为导电的LSG。

2.1. Fabrication of resistive RAM MXene devices
2.1. 阻变RAM MXene器件的制备
In order to fabricate a tri-layer MXene-based memory device, two flexible LSG thin films of 0.5 × 0.5 cm2 area are bind across the DTM Mo2Ti2C3Tx middle layer through Nafion binder, acting as top and bottom electrodes. A very small layer of Nafion is sliced on top of LSG acting as bottom electrode (BE), and middle DTM MXene thin film is placed on the top of it with the help of tweezer to make a strong inter facial connection. DTM is placed in such a way as to leave 0.1 cm2 area on the edge of BE uncovered to provide a contact for the voltage biasing. Another layer of Nafion is similarly sliced again on the DTM MXene following the placement of LSG top electrode (TE). This completes the fabrication of our first tri-layer scheme LSG/DTM/LSG. Second device, in comparison to the first device, is fabricated similarly with the heated DTM Mo2Ti2C3Tx acting as an active layer schemed as LSG/H-DTM/LSG throughout the discussion. Furthermore, the two devices were placed in a vacuum oven at room temperature for 12 h to get rid of any excess moisture created because of the binder. These devices were then subjected to I–V testing. Fig. 1 displays the schematics from the synthesis of MXene to the device fabrication. The environmental stability of our synthesized Mo2Ti2C3Tx free-standing flexible film has also been shown in Fig. S1 through XRD and IV results showing stable MXene peak and current values.
为了制造基于MXene的三层存储器件，将两片0.5 × 0.5 cm²面积的柔性LSG薄膜通过Nafion粘合剂粘合在DTM Mo2Ti2C3Tx中间层的两侧，作为顶部和底部电极。在作为底电极(BE)的LSG上涂覆一层很薄的Nafion，然后用镊子将中间的DTM MXene薄膜放置其上，以形成牢固的界面连接。DTM的放置方式需在BE边缘留出0.1 cm²的区域未被覆盖，以便为电压偏置提供接触点。在DTM MXene上同样再涂覆一层Nafion后，放置LSG顶电极(TE)。这样就完成了我们第一个三层结构LSG/DTM/LSG的制备。与第一个器件相比，第二个器件的制备方法类似，但使用的是加热后的DTM Mo2Ti2C3Tx作为活性层，在整个讨论中将其方案记为LSG/H-DTM/LSG。此外，将两个器件置于室温下的真空烘箱中12小时，以去除因粘合剂产生的多余水分。然后对这些器件进行I-V测试。图1展示了从MXene合成到器件制备的示意图。我们合成的Mo2Ti2C3Tx自支撑柔性薄膜的环境稳定性也通过XRD和IV结果在图S1中展示，显示了稳定的MXene峰和电流值。

To investigate the appearance of additional peaks related to metaloxygen (M O) vibrations, Fourier Transform Infrared Spectroscopy (FT-IR) analysis was performed for the pristine Mo2Ti2C3Tx and Ht Mo2Ti2C3Tx. The region above 1600 cm 1 is characterized by functional groups stretching and bending vibrations and are commonly observed in most of the materials. The absorption bands below 1600 cm 1 wavenumber are the regions which can be attributed to oxygen symmetric and asymmetric vibrations. In Fig. 3a, the structure of the pristine Mo2Ti2C3Tx can be perceived by the absorption peaks at 620, 500 and 432 cm 1, featuring the stretching vibrations of Ti–O, Mo–C and Ti–C bonds [48,49]. Notably, a few additional absorption peaks are also observed in the FT-IR spectrum of Ht - Mo2Ti2C3Tx corresponding to weak peaks of MoO3 and TiO2 affiliated vibrations as was also observed in the XRD spectral range. The peaks at 1425 cm 1 and 618 cm 1 are ascribed to the stretching of Ti–O vibrations [50] whereas the weak intensity absorption peaks at 1164 cm 1 and 867 cm 1 strongly suggest the presence of Mo––O characteristic stretching vibration of the hexagonal phase of MoO3 and stretched O(3) atoms linked to three molybdenum atoms [51,52]. 3.1. Piezoforce microscopy analysis of Mo2Ti2C3Tx MXene film

Piezoresponse Force Microscopy (PFM) is a powerful technique for spotting and confirming ferroelectric domains. The PFM system used was a Bruker IconIR type atomic force microscope. The spring factor of the probe used was 2.8 N/m and the vibration frequency in the free state was 75 kHz. The electric domains are crucial for understanding the effect on material’s overall polarization and the properties associated with it [70]. Fig. 6 shows multiple maps obtained from the PFM scans. From left to right, the maps represent the morphology (surface topology) as shown in Fig. 6a, out of plane (OOP) phase and in-plane (IP) phase. The selected scanning area of these maps covered the region of 1.5 μm. The scan rate, which determined how fast the PFM scans across the Ht - Mo2Ti2C3Tx MXene film, was set to 0.4 Hz while an AC voltage of 2000 mV is applied to the probe of PFM during scanning. In the morphology map, the image revealed the topological variations and surface features of Ht Mo2Ti2C3Tx MXene film. The surface undulation or the extent of height variation is measured to be 240 nm approximately. This means that the highest point is about 240 nm higher than the lowest point on the surface of MXene film. Ht - Mo2Ti2C3Tx MXene film exhibited irregular and small domains in OOP and IP phase maps as shown in Fig. 6(b and c) at different magnifications i.e., 300 nm and 1 μm, respectively. Notably, the distinctive features here are the domain signals that are more pronounced both outside and inside the surface of Ht - Mo2Ti2C3Tx MXene film suggesting more defined variations in the structure [71].

3.2. Ferroelectric analysis of Mo2Ti2C3Tx MXene film
3.2. Mo2Ti2C3Tx MXene薄膜的铁电分析
The ferroelectric properties of Mo2Ti2C3Tx (non-heated and heated) MXene films with approximate thickness of 40 μm and dimensions of 0.2 cm × 0.2 cm were investigated by using Precision Material Analyzer, Radiant Technologies Inc at room-temperature (Fig. S3). In Fig. 4a, the Polarization vs Electric Field (P-E) loops of pristine Mo2Ti2C3Tx showed non-ferroelectric response in contrast to Ht - Mo2Ti2C3Tx MXene which exhibited the ferroelectric response as shown in Fig. 4(b and c). This particular ferroelectric nature emerged in the heated version of Mo2Ti2C3Tx MXene owing to the presence of rutile TiO2 phases present within the MXene sheets after the intrinsic MXene is kept in a heating environment. TiO2 is known to exhibit ferroelectricity in proximity [29] and has also shown similar behavior in Mo2Ti2C3Tx causing the overall electric domains to align under the electric field [60,61]. The 400 ◦C heat treatment is required to allow deep lying Titanium atoms in Mo2Ti2C3Tx layered MXene structure to participate in oxidation and provide sufficient rutile phase of TiO2 responsible for ferroelectric behavior. However, once the TiO2 phase has been induced in flexible Mo2Ti2C3Tx MXene film, this chemical modification in the MXene structure is permanent and stable under low/room-temperature measurements. The PE loops of Ht - Mo2Ti2C3Tx MXene film showed the distinct visual representation of ferroelectric materials as the applied voltage increased the electric domains within the material, orient themselves along the same direction of applied voltage. The orientation of domains continued until the domains approached their state of saturation and hence the remnant polarization (Pr) remained intact even when the applied voltage is reduced to zero. This behavior is the intriguing and unique nature of ferroelectric materials to retain polarization state in the absence of external electric field [62].
使用Radiant Technologies Inc的精密材料分析仪，在室温下研究了Mo2Ti2C3Tx（未加热和加热）MXene薄膜（厚度约40 μm，尺寸0.2 cm × 0.2 cm）的铁电特性（图S3）。在图4a中，原始Mo2Ti2C3Tx的极化-电场(P-E)回线显示出非铁电响应，与Ht-Mo2Ti2C3Tx MXene形成对比，后者显示出铁电响应，如图4(b和c)所示。这种特殊的铁电性质出现在加热后的Mo2Ti2C3Tx MXene中，这是由于在将本征MXene置于加热环境中后，MXene片层内存在的金红石TiO2相所致。已知TiO2在一定条件下会表现出铁电性[29]，并且在Mo2Ti2C3Tx中也显示出类似的行为，导致整体电畴在电场下排列[60,61]。需要进行400°C热处理，以使Mo2Ti2C3Tx层状MXene结构中深处的钛原子参与氧化，并提供足够的金红石TiO2相，从而产生铁电行为。然而，一旦在柔性Mo2Ti2C3Tx MXene薄膜中诱导出TiO2相，这种MXene结构中的化学修饰在低/室温测量下是永久且稳定的。Ht-Mo2Ti2C3Tx MXene薄膜的PE回线显示了铁电材料的独特视觉表现：随着施加电压的增加，材料内部的电畴沿施加电压的相同方向取向。电畴的取向持续进行，直到畴接近其饱和状态，因此即使施加电压降至零，剩余极化(Pr)仍然保持不变。这种行为是铁电材料在无外部电场情况下保持极化状态的有趣且独特的性质[62]。
<span class="hljs-strong">**Polarization**</span>: 极化，指电介质材料中正负电荷中心分离，形成电偶极矩的状态。
<span class="hljs-strong">**Remnant Polarization (Pr)**</span>: 剩余极化，指铁电材料在移除外加电场后，仍能保持的极化强度。

The ferroelectric behavior of Ht - Mo2Ti2C3Tx MXene film was observed at different voltage and frequency ranges. When the applied voltage was increased from 5V to 500V, greater force was experienced by the electric domains of the Ht - Mo2Ti2C3Tx to switch the domains more, causing an increase in Pr. On the other hand, when the frequency of the applied voltage was increased, a notable reduction in Pr was observed. Multiple factors such as presence of mobile charges, relaxation and switching of domain walls etc., greatly influenced the polarization values of Ht Mo2Ti2C3Tx MXene film [63–65]. At lower frequency, higher Pr was observed that may be attributed to the existence of mobile charges and termination groups residing on the surface of Ht - Mo2Ti2C3Tx which influenced the Pr. Conversely, at higher frequency, low value of Pr was observed. This phenomenon could possibly arise because certain domain walls and mobile charges were no longer actively participating in the polarization. These domain walls and mobile charges could possibly have a dampening effect on the overall performance and efficiency of the Ht Mo2Ti2C3Tx MXene film. These findings are consistent with the prior research that emphasized the significance of domain behavior and charge-related effects on PE loops [63–65]. The ferroelectric property of Ht - Mo2Ti2C3Tx MXene film was also verified by dielectric constant measurement. One can readily observe in Fig. 5(a and b) the characteristic dielectric loops resembling the butterfly shape [66,67].
在不同的电压和频率范围下观察了Ht-Mo2Ti2C3Tx MXene薄膜的铁电行为。当施加电压从5V增加到500V时，Ht-Mo2Ti2C3Tx的电畴受到更大的力，从而更多地切换畴，导致Pr增加。另一方面，当施加电压的频率增加时，观察到Pr显著降低。多种因素，如移动电荷的存在、畴壁的弛豫和切换等，极大地影响了Ht-Mo2Ti2C3Tx MXene薄膜的极化值[63–65]。在较低频率下，观察到较高的Pr，这可能归因于存在于Ht-Mo2Ti2C3Tx表面的移动电荷和终止基团影响了Pr。相反，在较高频率下，观察到较低的Pr值。这种现象可能因为某些畴壁和移动电荷不再积极参与极化过程而产生。这些畴壁和移动电荷可能对Ht-Mo2Ti2C3Tx MXene薄膜的整体性能和效率产生阻尼效应。这些发现与先前强调畴行为和电荷相关效应对PE回线重要性的研究一致[63–65]。Ht-Mo2Ti2C3Tx MXene薄膜的铁电特性也通过介电常数测量得到了验证。可以轻易地在图5(a和b)中观察到类似蝴蝶形状的特征介电回线[66,67]。

3.3. Non-volatile memory
3.3. 非易失性存储器
The memory effect for the two devices based on Mo2Ti2C3Tx (DTM) and heated Mo2Ti2C3Tx MXene (H-DTM) sandwiched tri-layer structures along with laser scribed reduced graphene oxide as top and bottom electrode contacts (LSG/DTM/LSG and LSG/H-DTM/LSG) was investigated by current voltage (I–V) cycling measurements at roomtemperature. Fig. 7a demonstrates the images of flexible LSG/H-DTM/ LSG device showing good flexibility whereas Fig. 7b displays probe contacts on the device along with the enlarged atomic structure of the active layer (AL). The current-voltage responses of different AL-based DTM structures under the positive and negative voltage biasing up to ± 4V are displayed in Fig. 7c. The device fabricated with DTM as AL exhibited almost linear response to the external biasing showing no definite switching between different resistance states as shown in the inset of Fig. 7c. When the central functional layer is replaced with the heated and ferroelectric version of the DTM structure while biasing to the same electrodes, the device demonstrated clear distinction between two resistance states converting it to a potential bipolar resistive RAM architecture with resistance switching (RS) characteristics. The LSG/HDTM/LSG device exhibiting RS operated under the voltage window of ± 4V with the SET value of 1.8 V under the positive biasing and RESET of 1.7 V due to the negative biased voltage stress. Long-lasting and repetitive response was observed under the electric field stress for both the SET and RESET states. The device was initially in the zero-conduction state i.e. HRS till 1.8 V, wherein the SET process switched the resistance and put the device into positive-conduction or LRS observing a definite conduction phenomenon. Thereby, the device retained its memory or LRS until the negative voltage stress is applied that shifted the device back to HRS at the RESET value. Exploring further the I–V response of ferroelectric H-DTM memory cell for the conduction mechanism, the abrupt and sharp increase in current states at SET is indicative of conduction filament (CF) mechanism responsible for resistance switching into LRS. The formation of oxygen-based phases of TiO2 and MoO3 as predicted through XRD, RAMAN and FTIR analysis, increases the oxygen concentration and converts the active layer into highly oxidized MXene nanosheets, the factors specific for the growth of oxygen vacancies-based conductive filament in a memristive cell [4,5,21]. Moreover, the internal electric field, induced owing to the ferroelectric polarization-created bound charges in H-DTM AL, drifted these oxygen vacancies Vo in a specified direction hence, controlling the randomness and growth of CF in LSG/H-DTM/LSG cell [72]. Collectively, the Vo’s along with free charge carriers, intrinsic to metallic nature of MXene, continues to grow from the BE as the external voltage is applied and appears as a continuous chain of CF at the SET voltage. This is responsible for the LRS state of the cell displaying the same state when external voltage stress is removed. In the second half of the loop, the migration of these vacancies towards the TE under the influence of negative biasing ruptures the filament, shifts the device back to HRS. The conduction mechanism in our device is strongly influenced by the relative orientation between the internal electric field generated by space charges and ferroelectric polarization [73]. If these fields are aligned or coupled, they can facilitate the trapped charge carriers enhancing conduction (LRS). Conversely, if the fields are oppositely oriented, they may increase trapping by reducing the conduction (HRS). Therefore, RS depends on how these two fields interact.
通过在室温下进行电流-电压(I-V)循环测量，研究了基于Mo2Ti2C3Tx(DTM)和加热Mo2Ti2C3Tx MXene(H-DTM)作为三明治三层结构，并以激光划刻还原氧化石墨烯为顶部和底部电极接触层(LSG/DTM/LSG和LSG/H-DTM/LSG)的两种器件的存储效应。图7a展示了柔性LSG/H-DTM/LSG器件的图像，显示出良好的柔性，而图7b显示了器件上的探针接触点以及活性层(AL)的放大原子结构。图7c显示了不同AL基DTM结构在高达±4V的正负电压偏置下的电流-电压响应。以DTM为AL的器件对外部偏置表现出几乎线性的响应，没有显示出在不同电阻状态之间的明确切换，如图7c插图所示。当中心功能层替换为加热且铁电版本的DTM结构，并在相同电极上施加偏置时，该器件展示出两种电阻状态之间的清晰区分，使其成为一种具有电阻开关(RS)特性的潜在双极阻变RAM架构。表现出RS特性的LSG/H-DTM/LSG器件在±4V的电压窗口下工作，SET值为1.8V（正偏压下），RESET值为1.7V（负偏压应力下）。在SET和RESET状态下，均观察到在电场应力下持久且重复的响应。该器件初始处于零导通状态，即高阻态(HRS)，直到1.8V，此时SET过程切换了电阻，使器件进入正导通或低阻态(LRS)，观察到明确的导通现象。因此，器件保持其存储状态或LRS，直到施加负电压应力，在RESET值处将器件切换回HRS。进一步探究铁电H-DTM存储单元I-V响应的导电机制，SET时电流状态的突然急剧增加表明导电细丝(CF)机制是导致电阻切换到LRS的原因。通过XRD、拉曼和FTIR分析预测的TiO2和MoO3氧化基相的形成，增加了氧浓度，并将活性层转化为高度氧化的MXene纳米片，这些是忆阻单元中基于氧空位的导电细丝生长的特定因素[4,5,21]。此外，由铁电极化产生的束缚电荷在H-DTM AL中诱导的内部电场，使这些氧空位Vo沿特定方向漂移，从而控制了LSG/H-DTM/LSG单元中CF的随机性和生长[72]。总的来说，Vo连同MXene金属性固有的自由电荷载流子，随着外部电压的施加，从BE开始持续生长，并在SET电压处表现为连续的CF链。这导致了器件的LRS状态，在移除外部电压应力时仍显示相同状态。在循环的后半部分，这些空位在负偏压影响下向TE迁移，导致导电细丝断裂，将器件切换回HRS。我们器件中的导电机制受到空间电荷产生的内部电场与铁电极化之间相对取向的强烈影响[73]。如果这些场对齐或耦合，它们可以促进被捕获的电荷载流子，增强导电性(LRS)。相反，如果场方向相反，它们可能通过降低导电性来增加捕获(HRS)。因此，RS取决于这两个场如何相互作用。
<span class="hljs-strong">**Conduction Filament (CF)**</span>: 导电细丝，在阻变存储器中，由氧空位或金属离子迁移形成的纳米级导电通道。
<span class="hljs-strong">**High Resistance State (HRS)**</span>: 高阻态，阻变存储器中的低导电状态。
<span class="hljs-strong">**Low Resistance State (LRS)**</span>: 低阻态，阻变存储器中的高导电状态。
<span class="hljs-strong">**SET**</span>: 置位，阻变存储器从高阻态转变为低阻态的过程。
<span class="hljs-strong">**RESET**</span>: 复位，阻变存储器从低阻态恢复为高阻态的过程。

To further investigate the electronic charge transport inside an active layer responsible for RS in LSG/H-DTM/LSG resistive RAM cell, I–V curve was re-plotted as Log I - Log V scale in Fig. 7(d and e) and the slopes were compared with theoretical relationship of I–V in different conduction models. In LRS state in both the SET and RESET region, slopes ≈ 1 were observed indicating ohmic hopping of electrons through the CF followed by I ∝ V as also measured in most of CF based memristor cells [74,75]. In HRS, slope value ≈ 2 indicates Child’s square law characteristic i.e. space charge limited current SCLC where I ∝ V2 and slope values greater than 2 represent trap free space charge limited current conduction [76].
为了进一步研究LSG/H-DTM/LSG阻变RAM单元中负责RS的活性层内部的电子电荷传输，将I-V曲线重新绘制为Log I - Log V坐标图，如图7(d和e)所示，并将斜率与不同导电模型中I-V的理论关系进行了比较。在SET和RESET区域的LRS状态中，观察到斜率≈1，表明电子通过CF进行欧姆跳跃，遵循I ∝ V关系，这在大多数基于CF的忆阻单元中也被测量到[74,75]。在HRS中，斜率值≈2表明Child平方律特性，即空间电荷限制电流(SCLC)，其中I ∝ V²，而斜率值大于2代表无陷阱空间电荷限制电流传导[76]。
<span class="hljs-strong">**Space Charge Limited Current (SCLC)**</span>: 空间电荷限制电流，固体中一种由注入电荷形成的空间电荷区控制的导电机理。

<span class="hljs-bullet">4.</span> Conclusion
<span class="hljs-bullet">4.</span> 结论
This research study, based on one of the double transitions metals MXene Mo2Ti2C3Tx, stands as the first report carrying out ferroelectric response in the said material free-standing structure. This ferroelectric response was generated when the pristine delaminated Mo2Ti2C3Tx film underwent efficient heat treatment, after which oxide phases i.e. rutile TiO2 and MoO3 were seen to be formed along with the intact MXene structure. This is in contrast with the pristine Mo2Ti2C3Tx results that showed non-ferroelectric behavior. Following that, the electrical characterization i.e. I–V results showed excellent RS feature, excellent cycleto-cycle repeatability, and stable resistive RAM retention performance over time. The formation of oxide phases in heated DTM functional layer helped in creating strong oxygen vacancy-based conduction filament along with the drift of free charge carriers, leading to enhanced memory storage capability for the proposed memory device. The Roff/Ron ratio of 102, endurance of 103 cycles and retention of 5 × 103 s anticipate that the double transition metal MXene structure Mo2Ti2C3Tx can potentially be used in the field of non-volatile memory devices and highly competent synaptic functions.
这项基于双过渡金属MXene Mo2Ti2C3Tx的研究，是首次报道该材料自支撑结构中的铁电响应。这种铁电响应是在原始分层的Mo2Ti2C3Tx薄膜经过有效热处理后产生的，热处理后观察到形成了金红石TiO2和MoO3等氧化物相，同时保持了完整的MXene结构。这与显示非铁电行为的原始Mo2Ti2C3Tx结果形成对比。随后，电学表征，即I-V结果显示，其具有优异的RS特性、优异的循环间重复性以及随时间稳定的阻变RAM保持性能。在加热的DTM功能层中形成的氧化物相有助于产生强大的基于氧空位的导电细丝，以及自由电荷载流子的漂移，从而增强了所提出存储器件的存储能力。Roff/Ron比为10²、10³次循环的耐久性和5 × 10³秒的保持性预示着，双过渡金属MXene结构Mo2Ti2C3Tx有潜力用于非易失性存储器件和高效的突触功能领域。
