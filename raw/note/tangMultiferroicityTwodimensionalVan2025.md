> > ### 4.1 设计策略（对应第2节）

> > #### 4.1.1 在2D磁体中创建铁电序
> > 
> > 由于磁性过渡金属的d电子会破坏传统铁电所需的d0条件，2D磁体通常是非中心对称的缺乏者。作者介绍了三种打破反演对称的方法：
> > 
> > -   **界面工程**：Cr2S3与蓝宝石衬底之间的界面相互作用，使自插层Cr原子发生层间滑移，形成ABA堆叠，打破空间反演对称，产生面外铁电（室温）和铁磁（TC约200K）。VCl3单层生长在NbSe2基底上，界面作用使Cl原子沿y轴位移，产生面内电极化，同时具有双条纹反铁磁序（TN约16K）。
> > -   **化学计量调控**：Fe1+xTe2通过调节Fe含量，非均匀原子插层/空位导致层间滑移和金属离子沿z方向位移，同时出现面外/面内极化和室温铁磁。Fe3−xGeTe2中增加Fe空位可将中心对称P63/mmc空间群变为非中心对称P3m1，产生面外极化，用SHG检测。
> > 
> > 这些工作说明，通过人工设计，2D磁性材料可以同时容纳铁电序。
> > 
> > #### 4.1.2 在2D铁电体中建立磁序
> > 
> > 此类策略是在已知二维铁电材料中引入磁性。
> > 
> > -   **CuCrP2S6 (CCPS)**：单斜低对称结构。Cu+占据S6八面体的偏心位置，形成面外电偶极；Cr3+提供磁矩，层间反铁磁序TN约30K，层内条纹反铁电序TC约145K。在2D极限下，可通过电压偏压将亚稳反铁电态切换为稳定的铁电态，实现电场驱动的Cu+重排，与磁性共存，产生非常规磁电耦合。
> > -   **CuCrSe2**：单层CuCrSe2中，Cu夹在两个CrSe2层之间，Cu与Se的垂直位移产生面外电极化，同时位移导致Cr 3d轨道变化，产生铁磁序（TC约120K）。这体现了轨道工程的作用。
> > -   **Fe掺杂α-In2Se3**：Fe0.16In1.84Se3在铁电半导体中引入磁性，磁性掺杂反而增强了铁电性，类似稀磁半导体。
> > 
> > #### 4.1.3 在2D铁弹体中诱导铁电序
> > 
> > 铁弹序是自发应变，常存在于中心对称结构，如磷烯。在某些低对称2D材料中，铁弹和铁电可以共存。
> > 
> > -   **IV族单硫属化物**：SnS、SnSe等具有褶皱结构，由于电负性差导致原子位移，产生大电极化。实验上，AA堆叠SnSe纳米片表现出滑动诱导的面内和面外极化，以及两种铁弹畴；AB堆叠则中心对称，无铁电性。
> > -   **ReS2/ReSe2**：通过T相（P-3m1）到T'相（P-1）的结构相变，产生两个等价铁弹畴。施加单轴应变可切换畴，光伏效应证实面内铁电性，铁弹切换可重新定向铁电极化。这样实现了铁弹-铁电的耦合。
> > 
> > #### 4.1.4 在2D异质结构中实现多铁性
> > 
> > 多铁异质结构往往比单相材料有更灵活的设计和更大的磁电效应。3D氧化物异质结构已有大量研究，但2D vdW异质结构仍处于早期。
> > 
> > -   实验例：Cr2Ge2Te6（铁磁绝缘体）与P(VDF-TrFE)（有机铁电）机械堆叠，铁电极化切换可逆调控Cr2Ge2Te6的矫顽场和自旋极化，展示了磁电耦合的潜力。
> > -   全vdW异质结构：理论预测Cr2Ge2Te6/In2Se3双层中，In2Se3极化反转可改变Cr2Ge2Te6的磁各向异性（面外到面内）。实验上Fe2.64GeTe2/α-In2Se3异质结，电压导致Fe2.64GeTe2矫顽场明显下降，但不可逆、非循环，归因于In2Se3压电效应引起的界面应变，而非直接可切换磁电耦合。LaCl/α-In2Se3、Fe3GeTe2/α-In2Se3、CrI3/α-In2Se3等也未有直接可切换的实验证据。原因可能包括筛选效应、不利能带排列、界面应变弛豫。
> > 
> > ### 4.2 挑战与机遇（对应第3节）
> > 
> > #### 4.2.1 材料开发
> > 
> > 本征2D多铁稀缺，因为铁电和磁性要求相互排斥。但2D结构更易破缺中心对称，轻元素（S、Se）的位移也可产生铁电。过渡金属化合物是主要候选，铁弹性相关多铁更少，需要开发超低对称结构。制备方法上，机械剥离适合本征材料筛选；CVD或薄膜沉积适合工程化，因为衬底应变、缺陷更易引入。
> > 
> > #### 4.2.2 检测方法
> > 
> > 传统3D表征不适用。需要AFM（原子力显微镜，可探测铁电畴、磁畴、铁弹畴）、光学方法（磁圆二色MCD、磁光克尔效应MOKE、二次谐波SHG）无损检测。结合微纳米器件和源表、PPMS等进行定量测量。但目前缺乏统一的单位和标准计算方法。
> > 
> > #### 4.2.3 提高居里温度与耦合
> > 
> > 大多数2D多铁TC低于室温。需要稳定晶体结构、增强交换作用。掺杂、缺陷、插层可增加自旋密度和不对称性。异质结可组合室温TC的铁磁/铁电层，但界面质量关键。离子门控也可调节电子密度和铁性序。高通量计算和机器学习可用于筛选材料。增强磁电耦合系数的一个直观思路是让所有铁性序同源，如原子插层同时调控态密度和对称性；调节配体/配位环境使铁电和磁性的起源统一。但需要克服d电子对极化的抑制。
> > 
> > #### 4.2.4 应用
> > 
> > 目前尚无确切器件报告。可借鉴3D多铁：电场控制磁性可实现低功耗自旋逻辑门、磁隧道结（铁电势垒调控隧穿磁阻）、忆阻器（耦合极化与磁化实现多态阻变，用于神经形态计算）。器件架构设计至关重要。
> > 
> > ### 4.3 展望（第4节）
> > 
> > 2D vdW多铁研究快速推进。弱静电屏蔽使多铁半导体和金属成为可能，也提供更多集成自由度。未来可能产生超越冯诺依曼和摩尔定律的新器件。

> ## 一、引言
> 
> 本文发表于《Chemistry of Materials》2025年特刊“Chemistry of Emergent 2D Materials”，是一篇**观点（Perspective）**文章，作者来自香港理工大学应用物理系。**多铁材料（multiferroics）**指在同一相中同时具有至少两种铁性序（ferroic orders）的材料，例如**铁电性（ferroelectricity）**、**铁磁性（ferromagnetism）**或**铁弹性（ferroelasticity）**。这些铁性序通常表现为自发极化（P）、自发磁化（M）或自发应变（ε），并可在外部电场、磁场或机械场下滞后切换。多铁材料最吸引人的特性是不同铁性序之间的相互耦合，例如利用电场而非电流控制磁畴，可显著降低焦耳热并提升能效。
> 
> 然而，在单相材料中实现多铁性面临深刻的物理矛盾：**铁电性**往往要求过渡金属离子具有**d⁰电子构型**，而**磁性**则需要部分填充的d轨道，即所谓“**d⁰规则**”。2003年**BiFeO₃**和**TbMnO₃**的发现打破了这一僵局，但仍存在磁电耦合强度有限、与现有器件集成困难等问题。2016年后三维多铁研究热度出现下滑。
> 
> 与此同时，**二维范德瓦尔斯（van der Waals, vdW）材料**为该领域带来全新机遇。二维层状材料具有无悬挂键、层内饱和化学键、可剥离至单层极限且结构稳定等优点；弱层间相互作用允许人为堆叠并构建异质结构，且维度降低增强了电荷、自旋、晶格自由度对外场的响应。特别是**层间滑移（interlayer sliding）**可产生净电极化，**Dzyaloshinskii–Moriya（DM）相互作用**可将非中心对称结构与自旋排列耦合。本文聚焦于“如何在已有二维铁性材料中诱导额外的铁性序”，系统回顾了2D vdW多铁的最新进展、设计策略、现存挑战及应用前景。

> ## 四、正文核心内容剖析
> 
> ### 4.1 设计策略
> 
> #### 4.1.1 在2D磁体中创建铁电序
> 
> 由于磁性过渡金属的d电子会破坏传统铁电所需的d⁰条件，2D磁体通常难以自发产生极化。作者介绍了三种有效途径：
> 
> -   **界面工程**：Cr₂S₃与蓝宝石衬底间的相互作用驱动自插层Cr原子层间滑移，形成独特的ABA堆叠，打破空间反演对称性，实现室温面外铁电，同时保持铁磁序（TC≈200 K）。VCl₃单层生长在NbSe₂衬底上，界面作用使Cl原子沿y方向位移形成两个反平行畴，产生面内电极化，并伴随双条纹反铁磁序（TN≈16 K）。
> -   **化学计量调控**：Fe₁₊ₓTe₂通过非化学计量生长引入原子插层/空位，导致层间滑移和金属离子位移，同时出现面外/面内极化与室温铁磁。Fe₃₋ₓGeTe₂增加Fe空位后空间群从中心对称P6₃/mmc变为非中心对称P3m1，SHG证实面外极化。
> -   **理论认识**：2D体系中轻元素（S、Se）的位移也可产生明显铁电性，这不同于传统3D铁电中金属离子位移为主，为2D多铁提供了额外自由度。
> 
> #### 4.1.2 在2D铁电体中建立磁序
> 
> -   **CuCrP₂S₆（CCPS）**：单斜低对称结构，Cu⁺占据S₆八面体偏心位置形成电偶极，Cr³⁺提供磁矩。块体中层间反铁磁序TN≈30 K，层内条纹反铁电序TC≈145 K。在2D极限下，可通过电压偏压将亚稳反铁电态切换为稳定铁电态，这种电场驱动的Cu⁺重排与磁序共存，产生非常规磁电耦合。
> -   **CuCrSe₂**：单层中Cu夹在两层CrSe₂之间，Cu与Se的垂直位移诱导显著面外极化；该位移同时调制Cr 3d轨道，导致铁磁序（TC≈120 K）。这是“轨道工程”与结构不对称共同作用的范例。
> -   **Fe掺杂α-In₂Se₃**：Fe₀.₁₆In₁.₈₄Se₃在铁电半导体中引入磁性，且磁性掺杂并不破坏铁电性，反而增强了铁电有序，类似稀磁半导体机制。
> 
> #### 4.1.3 在2D铁弹体中诱导铁电序
> 
> 铁弹序通常出现在中心对称晶格中，如磷烯。但在某些低对称2D材料中，铁弹与铁电可以共存：
> 
> -   **IV族单硫属化物**：SnS、SnSe等具有褶皱结构，电负性差异导致显著原子位移和自发极化。实验上AA堆叠的SnSe纳米片展现出滑移诱导的面内与面外极化，同时存在两种铁弹畴；AB堆叠则保持中心对称，无铁电性。
> -   **ReS₂/ReSe₂**：T相（P-3m1）到T'相（P-1）的结构相变产生两个等价铁弹畴。通过聚合物封装施加单轴应变可切换铁弹畴，光伏效应证实面内铁电性，且铁弹切换可重新定向电极化，实现铁弹-铁电机械耦合。
> 
> #### 4.1.4 在2D异质结构中实现多铁性
> 
> 异质结构相比单相材料具有设计灵活、磁电效应显著的优点。三维氧化物异质结构已有大量研究，而2D vdW异质结构仍处于早期阶段。
> 
> -   **代表性实验**：Cr₂Ge₂Te₆（铁磁绝缘体）与P(VDF-TrFE)（有机铁电）机械堆叠，铁电极化切换可逆调控Cr₂Ge₂Te₆的矫顽场和自旋极化，展示可行的磁电耦合。
> -   **全vdW异质结构**：理论预测Cr₂Ge₂Te₆/In₂Se₃双层中In₂Se₃极化反转可改变Cr₂Ge₂Te₆的磁各向异性。实验上Fe₂.₆₄GeTe₂/α-In₂Se₃异质结中，电压虽使矫顽场显著下降，但不可逆、非循环，归因于In₂Se₃压电效应产生的界面应变，而非直接可切换的磁电耦合。
> -   **共性挑战**：LaCl/α-In₂Se₃、Fe₃GeTe₂/α-In₂Se₃、CrI₃/α-In₂Se₃等体系尚未在实验中实现直接且可切换的磁电耦合。可能原因包括静电屏蔽效应、不利能带排列、界面应变弛豫等。界面工程与精准堆叠是未来关键。
> 
> ### 4.2 挑战与机遇
> 
> #### 4.2.1 材料开发
> 
> 本征2D多铁仍然稀缺，因为铁电与磁性在传统框架下互相排斥。但2D结构更易破缺中心对称，轻元素位移可诱导铁电，过渡金属化合物是主要候选材料。铁弹性相关多铁报道更少，需要发展超低对称结构。制备方法上，机械剥离适合基础筛选，CVD或薄膜沉积更适合工程化，因为衬底应变和缺陷可被主动利用。
> 
> #### 4.2.2 检测方法
> 
> 由于样品极薄，传统宏观电极化或磁化测量面临挑战。目前依赖**原子力显微镜（AFM）**探测铁电/铁磁/铁弹畴，**磁圆二色（MCD）**、**磁光克尔效应（MOKE）**、**二次谐波（SHG）**等光学手段无损检测磁性及结构对称性。结合微纳器件与PPMS可实现定量测量，但亟需统一性能单位与计算标准。
> 
> #### 4.2.3 提高居里温度与耦合
> 
> 室温工作是多铁应用的前提，但2D体系热涨落显著，多数材料TC低于室温。作者提出多条路径：通过金属掺杂、缺陷、插层增强未配对自旋密度与结构不对称性；构建室温铁电/铁磁异质结并优化界面；利用离子门控或静电掺杂调节电子密度；借助高通量计算和机器学习筛选材料。
> 
> 提升磁电耦合系数的关键是使不同铁性序“同源”。原子插层可同时调控态密度与对称性；调节配体/配位环境可使铁电与磁性起源统一，但需要解决非满d壳层对极化的抑制。
> 
> #### 4.2.4 应用前景
> 
> 目前尚无成熟的2D多铁器件报告，但可借鉴3D多铁概念：电场控制磁性用于**自旋逻辑门**、**磁隧道结（MTJ）**、**多态忆阻器**及**神经形态计算**。2D材料的原子级厚度和清洁界面有望带来更低的切换电压和更高集成密度。

> ## 八、用户问题预测与解答
> 
> 1.  **什么是多铁材料？为什么铁电与磁性难以共存？**
>     
>     多铁材料指同时具有至少两种“铁性序”（铁电、铁磁、铁弹）的材料。铁电性需要晶格具有非中心对称结构，且过渡金属离子通常为d⁰构型以利于形成位移型极化；而磁性要求过渡金属离子具有部分填充的d轨道，以产生未配对自旋。因此，同一种离子很难同时满足“d⁰”和“d非零”条件，这就是**Hill的d⁰规则**导致多铁材料稀少的原因。
>     
> 2.  **二维范德瓦尔斯材料相比三维多铁有哪些优势？**
>     
>     ①可剥离至单层极限，结构稳定；②层间无悬挂键，易于堆叠构建异质结构；③弱层间相互作用允许通过层间滑移产生极化；④晶体结构柔软，易于通过应变、掺杂、界面工程调控；⑤维度降低使磁各向异性、多铁耦合更丰富。
>     
> 3.  **什么是滑移铁电？**
>     
>     滑移铁电（sliding ferroelectricity）是二维材料特有的一种铁电机制：两个中心对称的层状材料在堆叠时，层间相对水平位移会打破整体反演对称性，从而产生垂直层面的净电极化。比如AA堆叠的SnSe或双层MnSe，通过层间滑移可产生可切换的极化。这种机制不依赖传统离子位移，而是依赖层间电子重分布。
>     
> 4.  **如何检测单层2D多铁的铁电性和磁性？**
>     
>     铁电性可采用压电力显微镜（PFM）观察铁电畴和滞后回线；二次谐波（SHG）对对称性敏感，可探测非中心对称结构和极化方向。磁性可采用磁光克尔效应（MOKE）、磁圆二色（MCD）、超导量子干涉仪（SQUID）或反常霍尔效应等。对于单层，通常需要衬底或封装保护，并结合低温探针台进行测试。
>     
> 5.  **2D多铁有哪些潜在应用？**
>     
>     潜在应用包括：电场控制磁化实现低功耗自旋逻辑和磁存储；磁电耦合实现“电写磁读”的存储器；多态忆阻器用于神经形态计算；磁隧道结中利用铁电势垒调控隧穿磁阻；以及柔性电子、应变传感器等。
>     
> 6.  **为什么目前2D多铁的居里温度普遍较低？**
>     
>     一是低维体系中热涨落增强，长程磁有序更难维持；二是多数二维磁体的本征交换作用较弱，导致TC低于室温；三是铁电序与磁序的起源不同，同时优化两者往往顾此失彼。通过增强交换耦合（如选择Cr、V等强磁性离子）、增加层间耦合、离子掺杂或构建异质结，可提高TC。
>     
> 7.  **文章提到的“Janus材料”是什么？**
>     
>     Janus材料是指两个表面具有不同原子种类或化学环境的二维材料，例如一面是S、另一面是Se的MoSSe。这种不对称结构会打破垂直方向的镜面对称，从而产生垂直电极化，同时可调节电子结构和磁性，是工程化2D多铁的一种策略。
>

> ## 1. INTRODUCTION

> ## 1. 引言
> 
> Multiferroics are materials that exhibit multiple ferroic orders, such as ferroelectricity, ferromagnetism, or ferroelasticity. These ferro orders usually manifest as spontaneous moments, such as spontaneous polarization (P), spontaneous magnetization (M), or spontaneous strain (ε), which can be hysteretically switched under external electric, magnetic, or mechanical fields. 多铁材料是指表现出多种铁序的材料，例如铁电性、铁磁性或铁弹性。这些铁序通常表现为自发矩，如自发极化 (P)、自发磁化 (M) 或自发应变 (ε)，它们在外部电场、磁场或机械场的作用下可以发生滞后切换。 **Ferroelectricity**: 铁电性，材料在无外场时具有自发极化，且极化方向可通过外电场改变。 **Ferromagnetism**: 铁磁性，材料具有自发磁化，且磁化方向可通过外磁场改变。 **Ferroelasticity**: 铁弹性，材料具有自发应变，且应变方向可通过外力场改变。
> 
> The most compelling feature of multiferroics is the mutual coupling between different ferroic orders, which enables the control of one order parameter via the conjugate field of another. For instance, magnetic domains can be manipulated using an electric field instead of an electric current, which significantly reduces Joule heating and enhances energy efficiency. 多铁材料最引人注目的特点是不同铁序之间的相互耦合，这使得通过另一种序的共轭场来控制某个序参数成为可能。例如，可以使用电场而非电流来操纵磁畴，从而显著降低焦耳热并提高能量效率。
> 
> However, achieving the coexistence of multiple ferroic orders in a single-phase material is challenging due to symmetry constraints and the often conflicting electronic requirements, such as the $d^0$ rule for ferroelectricity and partially filled d-orbitals for magnetism. This longstanding challenge was addressed in 2003 with the discovery of two milestone multiferroics: $\text{BiFeO}_3$ and $\text{TbMnO}_3$. 然而，由于对称性限制以及经常冲突的电子要求（例如铁电性的 $d^0$ 规则和磁性的部分填充 d 轨道），在单相材料中实现多种铁序的共存具有挑战性。这一长期挑战在 2003 年随着两个里程碑式多铁材料 $\text{BiFeO}_3$ 和 $\text{TbMnO}_3$ 的发现而得到解决。 **$d^0$ rule**: $d^0$ 规则，传统观点认为铁电性通常出现在过渡金属离子的 d 轨道完全为空的情况下。
> 
> $\text{BiFeO}_3$ features as a type-I multiferroic, in which the magnetism and spontaneous polarization originate from the canted antiferromagnetic ordering of $\text{Fe}^{3+}$ spins and the displacement of $\text{Bi}^{2+}$, respectively. The intrinsic multiferroic property in bulk $\text{BiFeO}_3$ is relatively weak due to the separation of spin orders and electric dipoles. In contrast, type-II $\text{TbMnO}_3$ multiferroics exhibit a strong magnetoelectric coupling, where the electric polarization originates from sinusoidal antiferromagnetic order, resulting in an obvious rotation of electric polarization with changes in the exchange interaction energy under an external magnetic field. $\text{BiFeO}_3$ 是一种 I 型多铁材料，其磁性和自发极化分别源于 $\text{Fe}^{3+}$ 自旋的倾斜反铁磁有序和 $\text{Bi}^{2+}$ 的位移。由于自旋序和电偶极子的分离，块体 $\text{BiFeO}_3$ 的内在多铁特性相对较弱。相比之下，II 型 $\text{TbMnO}_3$ 多铁材料表现出强烈的磁电耦合，其电极化源于正弦反铁磁序，导致电极化随着外部磁场下交换相互作用能量的变化而发生明显的旋转。 **Type-I Multiferroic**: I 型多铁材料，电序和磁序独立产生。 **Type-II Multiferroic**: II 型多铁材料，磁序诱导电序，通常具有强磁电耦合。
> 
> Those findings sparked significant interest and a surge in paper publications with extensive studies on exploring materials, lattice dynamics, chiral textures, electronic structures, domain behaviors, and topological states (Figure 1a). 这些发现引发了浓厚的兴趣和论文发表的热潮，广泛研究了材料探索、晶格动力学、手征结构、电子结构、畴行为和拓扑态（图 1a）。
> 
> However, since 2016, there has been a gradual decline in research activity, possibly due to the challenges in realizing practical applications of 3D multiferroics, such as limited magnetoelectric coupling strength and difficulties in integrating these materials into existing device architectures. Emerging van der Waals (vdW) crystals have recently provided a promising platform for exploring multiferroics and the interaction of ferroic orders, with rapidly growing research interests (Figure 1b). 然而，自 2016 年以来，研究活动逐渐下降，这可能是由于 3D 多铁材料在实际应用中面临挑战，例如磁电耦合强度有限以及将这些材料集成到现有器件架构中的困难。新兴的范德华 (vdW) 晶体最近为探索多铁性及其铁序相互作用提供了一个极具前景的平台，研究兴趣迅速增长（图 1b）。
> 
> In 2D vdW layers, the absence of dangling bonds and the presence of saturated in-plane chemical bonds ensure structural stability even down to the monolayer limit, mitigating concerns related to depolarization or structural distortion with decreasing thickness (Figure 1c,d). 在二维 vdW 层中，悬空键的缺失和饱和的面内化学键确保了即使在单层极限下的结构稳定性，减轻了随着厚度降低而产生的去极化或结构畸变方面的担忧（图 1c,d）。 **Dangling bonds**: 悬空键，原子表面未饱和的化学键。
> 
> Furthermore, the reduced dimensionality enhances the tunability and coupling possibility among charge, spin and lattice degree of freedom in response to the external fields, as well as a range of physical property modulations through chemical doping, mechanical stacking, strain tuning and interfacial engineering (Figure 2). 此外，降低的维度增强了电荷、自旋和晶格自由度在响应外部场时的可调性和耦合可能性，以及通过化学掺杂、机械堆叠、应变调控和界面工程实现的一系列物理性质调制（图 2）。
> 
> Specifically, the vdW layered materials provide more flexible structure variation to shift the neutral charge center, such as interlayer atom displacement or sliding, which brings about a considerable net electric dipole. The resulting noncentrosymmetric structures could also manipulate the long range spin ordering through Dzyaloshinskii−Moriya (DM) interaction, resulting in canted spin structures or magnetic anisotropies. 具体而言，vdW 层状材料提供了更灵活的结构变化以偏移电中性中心，例如层间原子位移或滑动，从而产生显著的净电偶极子。由此产生的非中心对称结构还可以通过 Dzyaloshinskii-Moriya (DM) 相互作用操纵长程自旋有序，导致倾斜的自旋结构或磁各向异性。 **Dzyaloshinskii−Moriya (DM) interaction**: DM 相互作用，一种导致自旋倾斜、产生非共线磁结构的反对称交换相互作用。
> 
> Conversely, the frustrated spin structure or residual strain may also break the inversion symmetry and induce spontaneous polarization through the inverse DM interaction. The 2D materials with naturally flexible properties highly tolerate external mechanical force applied to tune strain polarizations, enabling a wide range of anisotropic modulation of spin orders and electric polarization. 相反，受挫自旋结构或残余应变也可能破坏反演对称性，并通过逆 DM 相互作用诱导自发极化。具有天然灵活特性的二维材料能够高度耐受用于调节应变极化的外部机械力，从而实现自旋序和电极化的广泛各向异性调制。
> 
> Importantly, the weak interlayer interaction in 2D vdW materials relaxes the constraints of lattice mismatching, allowing for deterministic stacking and facile design of artificial multiferroic heterostructures without issues like interfacial atom diffusion or strain. 重要的是，二维 vdW 材料中弱的层间相互作用放宽了晶格失配的限制，允许确定性的堆叠和人工多铁异质结构的简便设计，而不会出现界面原子扩散或应变等问题。
> 
> Typical early theoretical calculations have predicted ferroelasticity-based multiferroics, such as the coexistence of ferroelasticity and ferromagnetism in p-doped $\alpha$-SnO monolayer and in-plane ferroelectricity and ferroelasticity in binary black phosphorus analogues (such as GeS, GeSe, SnS, and SnSe) interlocked through the unique phosphorene-isomorphic puckering square lattice (Figure 3). 早期的典型理论计算预测了基于铁弹性的多铁材料，例如 p 掺杂 $\alpha$-SnO 单层中铁弹性与铁磁性的共存，以及通过独特的磷烯同构褶皱方格晶格互锁的二进制黑磷类似物（如 GeS, GeSe, SnS 和 SnSe）中的面内铁电性和铁弹性（图 3）。
> 
> Subsequent studies on transition metal halides have confirmed multiferroicity and demonstrated electric tuning of magnetic moments. These findings have spurred the identification of various intrinsic and engineered 2D multiferroics, such as metal dichalcogenides, MXene, heterostructure, multielement compounds and doped/sliding materials (Figure 3). 随后对过渡金属卤化物的研究确认了多铁性并演示了磁矩的电调控。这些发现促进了各种内在和工程化二维多铁材料的鉴定，例如金属二硫属化物、MXene、异质结构、多元素化合物以及掺杂/滑动材料（图 3）。 **MXene**: 一类由过渡金属碳化物、氮化物或碳氮化物组成的二维材料。
> 
> The preliminary progress significantly expands the range of materials with potential multiferroicity, indicating an opportunity for 2D multiferroics to complement and build upon 3D multiferroics. Motivated by these developments and inspired by them, we review 2D multiferroics to stay current with the latest progress, with a focus on artificially engineered systems. Subsequently, we also outline the challenges at the current state and offer perspectives on 2D multiferroics for unlocking future opportunities and applications in this burgeoning field. 初步进展显著扩大了具有潜在多铁性的材料范围，表明二维多铁材料有机会补充并基于 3D 多铁材料进行发展。在这些进展的激励和启发下，我们回顾了二维多铁材料以跟进最新进展，重点关注人工工程系统。随后，我们还概述了当前状态下的挑战，并就二维多铁材料提出了观点，以开启该新兴领域未来的机遇和应用。

> ## 2. DESIGN PRINCIPLES AND APPROACHES FOR REALIZING 2D MULTIFERROIC
> 
> ## 2. 实现二维多铁材料的设计原理与方法
> 
> With the ongoing trend toward device miniaturization, significant efforts have been directed toward developing 2D multiferroic materials. In conventional magnetic perovskites like $\text{BiFeO}_3$, lone pairs (two outer 6s electrons) in $\text{Bi}^{3+}$ ions play a crucial role in inducing ferroelectric order. In contrast, 2D materials often exhibit ferroelectricity through alternative mechanisms, such as charge ordering, which is attributed to the contained transition metals with different valences or the distinct stacking configuration. 随着器件小型化的持续趋势，人们投入了大量努力开发二维多铁材料。在像 $\text{BiFeO}_3$ 这样的传统磁性钙钛矿中，$\text{Bi}^{3+}$ 离子中的孤对电子（两个外层 6s 电子）在诱导铁电序方面起着至关重要的作用。相比之下，二维材料通常通过替代机制表现出铁电性，例如电荷有序，这归因于包含不同价态的过渡金属或独特的堆叠配置。 **Lone pairs**: 孤对电子，原子最外层不参与化学键合的电子对。
> 
> Encouraged by these features, numerous strategies have been developed to artificially realize multiple ferroic orders in ultrathin 2D materials. Therefore, this section will introduce various innovative design methodologies for engineering 2D multiferroics. In addition, the specific properties of representative and emerging 2D multiferroics, including the year of discovery, thickness, Curie or Néel transition temperatures ($T_C$ or $T_N$), spontaneous polarization value, design principles, and experimental or theoretical studies, are summarized and comprehensively compared in Table 1. 受这些特性的启发，人们开发了众多策略以在超薄二维材料中人工实现多种铁序。因此，本节将介绍工程化二维多铁材料的各种创新设计方法。此外，表 1 总结并全面比较了具有代表性的和新兴的二维多铁材料的具体性质，包括发现年份、厚度、居里温度或内尔温度 ($T_C$ 或 $T_N$)、自发极化值、设计原理以及实验或理论研究。

> ### 2.1. Creating Ferroelectric Order in the 2D Magnets.
> 
> ### 2.1. 在二维磁体中创建铁电序
> 
> The magnetic properties of 2D materials, such as anti/ ferromagnetism, Curie temperature ($T_C$), coercive field ($H_C$) and perpendicular magnetic anisotropy (PMA), are known to be strongly dependent on both layer thickness and stoichiometry. However, the discovery of electrical polarization in 2D anti/ferromagnets is less reported because the presence of real d electrons in the magnetic transition metal ions prevents ferroelectricity. 二维材料的磁性能，如反铁磁/铁磁性、居里温度 ($T_C$)、矫顽场 ($H_C$) 和垂直磁各向异性 (PMA)，已知强烈依赖于层厚度和化学计量比。然而，二维反铁磁/铁磁体中电极化的发现报道较少，因为磁性过渡金属离子中真实 d 电子的存在阻碍了铁电性的产生。 **Coercive field**: 矫顽场，使磁化强度变为零所需施加的反向磁场强度。 **Perpendicular Magnetic Anisotropy (PMA)**: 垂直磁各向异性，磁化方向倾向于垂直于材料平面的特性。
> 
> Recently, an intriguing design has been developed that utilizes the interfacial interaction between sapphire and the 2D $\text{Cr}_2\text{S}_3$ ferromagnet to modulate the interlayer sliding of self-intercalated Cr atoms, leading to the formation of a unique ABA stacking order that significantly breaks the spatial inversion symmetry. As a result, the $\text{Cr}_2\text{S}_3$ nanoflakes, with a thickness of one-unit-cell thickness, exhibit robust room-temperature out-of-plane ferroelectricity and lowtemperature long-range ferromagnetic order ($T_C \sim 200\text{ K}$). 最近，一种有趣的设计被开发出来，利用蓝宝石与二维 $\text{Cr}_2\text{S}_3$ 铁磁体之间的界面相互作用来调制自插层 Cr 原子的层间滑动，从而形成一个显著破坏空间反演对称性的独特 ABA 堆叠序。结果，厚度为一个单位晶格的 $\text{Cr}_2\text{S}_3$ 纳米片表现出鲁棒的室温面外铁电性和低温长程铁磁序 ($T_C \sim 200\text{ K}$)。
> 
> A similar interface engineering strategy has also been applied to 2D $\text{VCl}_3$ antiferromagnets. $\text{VCl}_3$ monolayer hosts a bistriped antiferromagnetic order with a $T_N$ of approximately $16\text{ K}$ by fabricating the interface between $\text{VCl}_3$ and a $\text{NbSe}_2$ substrate. The directional interfacial interaction can cause the displacement of Cl atoms along the y-axis in two antiparallel domains, which breaks the in-plane inversion symmetry and results in a notable in-plane electrical polarization (Figure 4b). The electronic screening is present in the $\text{VCl}_3$ monolayer but cannot fully compensate for the total electric polarization. 类似的界面工程策略也被应用于二维 $\text{VCl}_3$ 反铁磁体。通过在 $\text{VCl}_3$ 和 $\text{NbSe}_2$ 基底之间构建界面，$\text{VCl}_3$ 单层在约 $16\text{ K}$ 的 $T_N$ 下具有双条纹反铁磁序。方向性界面相互作用可导致 Cl 原子在两个反平行畴中沿 y 轴位移，从而破坏面内反演对称性并导致显著的面内电极化（图 4b）。$\text{VCl}_3$ 单层中存在电子屏蔽，但不能完全补偿总电极化。
> 
> Another intriguing example is engineering the local layered structure by tuning the chemical stoichiometry. By modulating the chemical composition in 2D $\text{Fe}_{1+x}\text{Te}_2$ via the growth parameters, nonuniform atomic intercalation or vacancy is artificially induced, leading to the sliding of interlayers and the metal ions displacement along the z direction (Figure 4a). The 2D $\text{Fe}_{1+x}\text{Te}_2$ flakes thus not only produce spontaneous alignment of electric dipoles along the out-of-plane and inplane directions but also exhibit spontaneous magnetization at room temperature. 另一个有趣例子是通过调节化学计量比来工程化局部层状结构。通过生长参数调制二维 $\text{Fe}_{1+x}\text{Te}_2$ 的化学成分，人工诱导非均匀原子插层或空位，导致层间滑动以及金属离子沿 z 方向的位移（图 4a）。因此，二维 $\text{Fe}_{1+x}\text{Te}_2$ 薄片不仅在面外和面内方向产生电偶极子的自发排列，而且在室温下表现出自发磁化。
> 
> Additionally, increasing Fe deficiency can also create electrical polarization in the 2D $\text{Fe}_{3-x}\text{GeTe}_2$ magnet by breaking the inversion symmetry from the centrosymmetric $P6_3/mmc$ space group (intrinsic $\text{Fe}_3\text{GeTe}_2$) to the non centrosymmetric $P3m1$ space group. Using the second harmonic generation (SHG) technique, the 2D $\text{Fe}_{3-x}\text{GeTe}_2$ magnet shows a distinct 3-fold SHG pattern, confirming the existence of dominant out-of-plane polarization. 此外，增加 Fe 缺失还可以通过将对称中心 $P6_3/mmc$ 空间群（固有 $\text{Fe}_3\text{GeTe}_2$）破坏为非中心对称 $P3m1$ 空间群，在二维 $\text{Fe}_{3-x}\text{GeTe}_2$ 磁体中创建电极化。利用二次谐波产生 (SHG) 技术，二维 $\text{Fe}_{3-x}\text{GeTe}_2$ 磁体显示出明显的 3 倍 SHG 图案，证实了主导面外极化的存在。 **Second Harmonic Generation (SHG)**: 二次谐波产生，一种非线性光学过程，常用于探测材料的非中心对称性（反演对称性破缺）。
> 
> Those reports highlight the potential to artificially break the lattice inversion symmetry and induce ferroelectric ordering in 2D magnets by modulating interlayer configurations and engineering a distorted lattice structure. 这些报道突显了通过调制层间配置和工程化畸变晶格结构，在二维磁体中人工破坏晶格反演对称性并诱导铁电有序的潜力。
> 
> ### 2.2. Establishing the Magnetic Order in 2D Ferroelectrics.
> 
> ### 2.2. 在二维铁电体中建立磁序
> 
> Traditional ferroelectric perovskite thin films are usually insulators with large remanent polarization; however, they suffer from depolarization when reduced in thickness to the few-unit-cell scale. Over the past decade, significant advancements have been made in the development of 2D ferroelectrics with ultrathin thickness and high $T_C$ for memory applications. 传统的铁电钙钛矿薄膜通常是具有大剩余极化的绝缘体；然而，当厚度降低到几个单位晶格量级时，它们会遭受去极化影响。在过去十年中，开发用于存储应用的超薄且具有高 $T_C$ 的二维铁电体取得了显著进展。
> 
> To further enhance multifunctionality, increasing efforts have been devoted to introducing the magnetic order into 2D anti/ferroelectrics, aiming to realize multiferroicity within a single material. The family of 2D multiferroics has since been expanded to include both ferroelectric/ferromagnetic and antiferroelectric/ antiferromagnetic couplings, offering diverse combinations of ferroic orders. 为了进一步增强多功能性，人们投入了越来越多的努力将磁序引入二维反铁电/铁电体中，旨在单种材料中实现多铁性。二维多铁材料家族随后扩展到包括铁电/铁磁和反铁电/反铁磁耦合，提供了多种铁序组合。
> 
> A notable example is the vdW multiferroic $\text{CuCrP}_2\text{S}_6$ (CCPS), which crystallizes in a monoclinic structure of low symmetry (Figure 1c). In the bulk phase, the $\text{Cu}^{1+}$ occupies the off-center positions within $\text{S}_6$ octahedra, giving rise to the out-of-plane electric dipole. As the incorporation of magnetic $\text{Cr}^{3+}$ into the vdW layers, the CCPS exhibits interlayer antiferromagnetic ordering with a $T_N$ of approximately $30\text{ K}$ and intralayer stripe antiferroelectric ordering with a $T_C$ of approximately $145\text{ K}$. 一个显著的例子是 vdW 多铁材料 $\text{CuCrP}_2\text{S}_6$ (CCPS)，它结晶为低对称性的单斜结构（图 1c）。在块体相中，$\text{Cu}^{1+}$ 占据 $\text{S}_6$ 八面体中的离心位置，从而产生面外电偶极子。随着磁性 $\text{Cr}^{3+}$ 进入 vdW 层，CCPS 表现出 $T_N$ 约为 $30\text{ K}$ 的层间反铁磁有序和 $T_C$ 约为 $145\text{ K}$ 的层内条纹反铁电有序。
> 
> Remarkably, at the 2D limit, the metastable antiferroelectric state can be switched into a stable ferroelectric state via the external voltage bias. Although the multiferroicity of CCPS originated from different cations, the unconventional magnetoelectrical coupling emerges through electric-field-driven rearrangement of $\text{Cu}^{1+}$ positions within $\text{CrS}_6\text{−P}_2\text{S}_6$ cages. This process stabilizes the ferroelectric state, coexisting with magnetic order. 值得注意的是，在二维极限下，亚稳的反铁电状态可以通过外部电压偏置切换为稳定的铁电状态。虽然 CCPS 的多铁性源于不同的阳离子，但非常规的磁电耦合是通过电场驱动的 $\text{Cu}^{1+}$ 在 $\text{CrS}_6\text{−P}_2\text{S}_6$ 笼中的位置重新排列而产生的。这一过程稳定了铁电态，并与磁序共存。
> 
> Another compelling case is the monolayer $\text{CuCrSe}_2$, which comprises an intercalated Cu layer sandwiched between two $\text{CrSe}_2$ layers (Figure 4c). The distinct vertical displacement between Cu and Se atoms induces significant out-of-plane electrical polarization. As a result, the displacement leads to shifts of the Cr 3d orbitals, thereby giving rise to ferromagnetic orders with a $T_C$ of approximately $120\text{ K}$. These findings highlight the high potential of orbital engineering and structural asymmetry to achieve multiferroicity in 2D vdW systems. 另一个引人注目的案例是单层 $\text{CuCrSe}_2$，它由夹在两个 $\text{CrSe}_2$ 层之间的插层 Cu 层组成（图 4c）。Cu 和 Se 原子之间明显的垂直位移诱导了显著的面外电极化。结果，这种位移导致 Cr 3d 轨道的偏移，从而产生 $T_C$ 约为 $120\text{ K}$ 的铁磁序。这些发现突显了轨道工程和结构不对称在二维 vdW 系统中实现多铁性的巨大潜力。
> 
> Furthermore, doping strategies have been employed to induce multiferroic behavior in 2D ferroelectric semiconductors. Moreover, it has been predicted that intentionally doping magnetic elements in specific regions of 2D ferroelectric semiconductors could lead to the coexistence of ferroelectric and ferromagnetic orders akin to diluted magnetic semiconductors. 此外，掺杂策略被用于在二维铁电半导体中诱导多铁行为。而且，据预测，在二维铁电半导体的特定区域有意识地掺杂磁性元素，可能会导致类似于稀磁半导体的铁电和铁磁序共存。 **Diluted magnetic semiconductors**: 稀磁半导体，在半导体基体中掺入少量磁性杂质而形成的半导体。
> 
> One of the typical examples is $\text{Fe}$-doped $\alpha\text{-In}_2\text{Se}_3$ ($\text{Fe}_{0.16}\text{In}_{1.84}\text{Se}_3$), which induces magnetic orders in 2D $\alpha\text{-In}_2\text{Se}_3$ ferroelectric semiconductors with $R\text{-}3m$ or $P6_3/mmc$ symmetry groups (Figure 4d). The intrinsic ferroelectricity of $\text{Fe}_{0.16}\text{In}_{1.84}\text{Se}_3$ is also enhanced by magnetic doping, providing a pathway for future exploration of 2D multiferroics with tunable ferromagnetic properties. 一个典型例子是 Fe 掺杂的 $\alpha\text{-In}_2\text{Se}_3$ ($\text{Fe}_{0.16}\text{In}_{1.84}\text{Se}_3$)，它在具有 $R\text{-}3m$ 或 $P6_3/mmc$ 对称群的二维 $\alpha\text{-In}_2\text{Se}_3$ 铁电半导体中诱导了磁序（图 4d）。$\text{Fe}_{0.16}\text{In}_{1.84}\text{Se}_3$ 的固有铁电性也通过磁性掺杂得到了增强，为未来探索具有可调铁磁特性的二维多铁材料提供了途径。
> 
> ### 2.3. Inducing the Ferroelectric Order in the 2D Ferroelastics.
> 
> ### 2.3. 在二维铁弹性材料中诱导铁电序
> 
> Ferroelastic order typically involves the spontaneous relaxation of atoms, generating lattice strain along different directional orientations. This phenomenon can occur in lattices with centrosymmetric point groups, such as in phosphorene, and is not necessarily related to ferroelectricity. However, in certain low-symmetry 2D materials, ferroelastic and ferroelectric orders can coexist due to structural anisotropy and significant atomic displacements. 铁弹性序通常涉及原子的自发弛豫，沿不同方向产生晶格应变。这种现象可以发生在具有中心对称点群的晶格中（如磷烯），且不一定与铁电性相关。然而，在某些低对称性的二维材料中，由于结构各向异性和显著的原子位移，铁弹性序和铁电序可以共存。
> 
> For instance, group-IV monochalcogenides (e.g., $\text{SnS}$, $\text{SnSe}$, $\text{GaS}$, $\text{GaSe}$) with puckered structures have been predicted to exhibit prominent atomic displacements, which are driven by significant differences in electronegativity, leading to large spontaneous electrical polarization. Experimentally, ferroelectric order can be induced by modulating the interlayer stacking configuration in 2D $\text{SnSe}$. 例如，具有褶皱结构的 IV 组单硫属化物（如 $\text{SnS}$, $\text{SnSe}$, $\text{GaS}$, $\text{GaSe}$）被预测将表现出显著的原子位移，这是由电负性的显著差异驱动的，导致较大的自发电极化。在实验上，可以通过调制二维 $\text{SnSe}$ 的层间堆叠配置来诱导铁电序。
> 
> $\text{AA}$-stacked $\text{SnSe}$ nanosheets exhibit sliding-induced in-plane and out-of-plane polarization, along with two types of ferroelastic domains (Figure 4e). Conversely, $\text{AB}$-stacked configurations, which are centrosymmetric, do not display ferroelectricity. $\text{AA}$ 堆叠的 $\text{SnSe}$ 纳米片表现出由滑动诱导的面内和面外极化，以及两种类型的铁弹性畴（图 4e）。相反，中心对称的 $\text{AB}$ 堆叠配置不显示铁电性。
> 
> Engineering the structural phase transition in 2D materials is also an intriguing approach to induce structural distortion or alter the stacking sequence, thereby enabling ferroelectric and ferroelastic order in a single-phase material. For example, in 2D $\text{ReS}_2$ and $\text{ReSe}_2$, two ferroelastic domains with two equivalent orientations are generated through the transition from the $\text{T}$ phase ($P\text{-}3m1$) to the “$\text{T}$” phase ($P\text{-}1$) (Figure 4f). 工程化二维材料的结构相变也是一种诱导结构畸变或改变堆叠顺序的有趣方法，从而在单相材料中实现铁电和铁弹性序。例如，在二维 $\text{ReS}_2$ 和 $\text{ReSe}_2$ 中，通过从 $\text{T}$ 相 ($P\text{-}3m1$) 向 “$\text{T}$” 相 ($P\text{-}1$) 的转变，产生两个具有等效方向的铁弹性畴（图 4f）。
> 
> The domains can be switched by applying uniaxial strain via polymer encapsulation. The observed photovoltaic effect confirms the in-plane ferroelectricity, which can be further reoriented through ferroelastic switching. Ferroelastic domains can influence the ferroelectric order through strain engineering, thereby enhancing the coupling between the multiferroic orders and providing a promising pathway for developing innovative 2D multiferroics. 这些畴可以通过聚合物封装施加单轴应变来切换。观察到的光伏效应证实了面内铁电性，且可通过铁弹性切换进一步重新定向。铁弹性畴可以通过应变工程影响铁电序，从而增强多铁序之间的耦合，为开发创新的二维多铁材料提供了一条极具前景的途径。
> 
> ### 2.4. Achieving Multiferroicity in 2D Heterostructure.
> 
> ### 2.4. 在二维异质结构中实现多铁性
> 
> Compared to single-phase multiferroics, multiferroic heterostructures typically exhibit a more flexible material design and a more substantial magnetoelectric effect. This strategy has been successfully implemented in fabricating oxide thin film or alloy heterostructure multiferroics, such as $\text{BiFeO}_3/\text{CoFe}$, $\text{BiFeO}_3/\text{CoPt}$, $\text{BaTiO}_3/\text{CoFe}$, $\text{La}_{0.7}\text{Sr}_{0.3}\text{MnO}_3/\text{PMN-PT}$ and $\text{EuTiO}_3/\text{Ca-SrTiO}_3$. 与单相多铁材料相比，多铁异质结构通常表现出更灵活的材料设计和更显著的磁电效应。这一策略已成功应用于制造氧化物薄膜或合金异质结构多铁材料，如 $\text{BiFeO}_3/\text{CoFe}$, $\text{BiFeO}_3/\text{CoPt}$, $\text{BaTiO}_3/\text{CoFe}$, $\text{La}_{0.7}\text{Sr}_{0.3}\text{MnO}_3/\text{PMN-PT}$ 和 $\text{EuTiO}_3/\text{Ca-SrTiO}_3$。
> 
> Compared with bulk counterparts, thin films normally exhibit effective interfacial magnetoelectric coupling, which is mainly due to the low local symmetry, strain gradient and chemical anisotropy at the interface. The voltage-induced modulation of the antiferromagnetic orders in the multiferroic layer can change the magnetic moment of the adjacent ferromagnetic layer, thereby establishing magnetoelectric coupling. 与块体材料相比，薄膜通常表现出有效的界面磁电耦合，这主要是由于界面处的低局部对称性、应变梯度和化学各向异性。多铁层中电压诱导的反铁磁序调制可以改变相邻铁磁层的磁矩，从而建立磁电耦合。
> 
> Although intriguing magnetoelectric coupling behaviors have been observed in 3D or thin film heterostructures, the development of 2D vdW heterostructure multiferroics remains limited. Integrating individual ferroelectric and ferromagnetic components into a heterostructure by sequential deposition or mechanical stacking may be a more straightforward fabrication strategy and more likely to be applied in magnetoelectric devices capable of “electric writing” and “magnetic reading”. 尽管在 3D 或薄膜异质结构中观察到了有趣的磁电耦合行为，但二维 vdW 异质结构多铁材料的发展仍然有限。通过顺序沉积或机械堆叠将单个铁电和铁磁组件集成到异质结构中，可能是一种更直接的制备策略，且更有可能应用于能够实现“电写”和“磁读”的磁电器件。
> 
> The key advantage of 2D multiferroics lies in their atomically thin thickness and atomically flat surfaces, which enable clean interfacial contact and compelling device architectures through stacking. For example, the heterostructure multiferroics composed of 2D vdW ferromagnetic $\text{Cr}_2\text{Ge}_2\text{Te}_6$ and the ferroelectric thin film $\text{P}(\text{VDF-TrFE})$ have been achieved by mechanical stacking (Figure 4g). The polarization switching in the ferroelectric thin film enables the reversible control of coercive field and spin polarization of the $\text{Cr}_2\text{Ge}_2\text{Te}_6$ ferromagnetic layer, suggesting the potential feasibility of magnetoelectric coupling toward device applications. 二维多铁材料的关键优势在于其原子级厚度和原子级平整的表面，这使得通过堆叠实现洁净的界面接触和引人注目的器件架构成为可能。例如，由二维 vdW 铁磁体 $\text{Cr}_2\text{Ge}_2\text{Te}_6$ 和铁电薄膜 $\text{P}(\text{VDF-TrFE})$ 组成的异质结构多铁材料已通过机械堆叠实现（图 4g）。铁电薄膜中的极化切换实现了对 $\text{Cr}_2\text{Ge}_2\text{Te}_6$ 铁磁层矫顽场和自旋极化的可逆控制，表明磁电耦合在器件应用中的潜在可行性。
> 
> Regarding all-vdW layered heterostructures, the bilayer $\text{Cr}_2\text{Ge}_2\text{Te}_6/\text{In}_2\text{Se}_3$ multiferroics were theoretically predicted to exhibit strong interlayer magnetoelectric coupling effects. The reversal of the electric polarization of the $\text{In}_2\text{Se}_3$ monolayer can alter the magnetic anisotropy of the $\text{Cr}_2\text{Ge}_2\text{Te}_6$ monolayer between out-of-plane and in-plane orientations. 关于全 vdW 层状异质结构，双层 $\text{Cr}_2\text{Ge}_2\text{Te}_6/\text{In}_2\text{Se}_3$ 多铁材料在理论上被预测具有强层间磁电耦合效应。$\text{In}_2\text{Se}_3$ 单层电极化的反转可以使 $\text{Cr}_2\text{Ge}_2\text{Te}_6$ 单层的磁各向异性在面外和面内方向之间切换。
> 
> A recent demonstration of an all-vdW layered heterostructure has been fabricated by exfoliation and vertically stacking the ferromagnetic $\text{Fe}_{2.64}\text{GeTe}_2$ and ferroelectric $\alpha\text{-In}_2\text{Se}_3$ nanoflakes. In this heterostructure, the magneto-optic microscopy reveals that the coercive field of the $\text{Fe}_{2.64}\text{GeTe}_2$ layer appears to have a prominent decrease upon the application of alternating voltages. Nevertheless, the voltage-dependent change in magnetic property is nonreversible and noncyclic, which is ascribed to the strain variation at the interface of the heterostructure, originating from the piezoelectric $\text{In}_2\text{Se}_3$ layer, rather than a direct and switchable magnetoelectric coupling. 最近通过剥离并垂直堆叠铁磁 $\text{Fe}_{2.64}\text{GeTe}_2$ 和铁电 $\alpha\text{-In}_2\text{Se}_3$ 纳米片，演示了一种全 vdW 层状异质结构。在这种异质结构中，磁光显微镜显示，在施加交变电压时，$\text{Fe}_{2.64}\text{GeTe}_2$ 层的矫顽场似乎显著降低。然而，磁性能随电压的变化是不可逆且非循环的，这被归因于异质结构界面处的应变变化（源自压电 $\text{In}_2\text{Se}_3$ 层），而非直接且可切换的磁电耦合。
> 
> Such a configuration has also been applied to other magnetic 2D materials to construct all-van der Waals (vdW) layered multiferroics, such as $\text{LaCl}/\alpha\text{-In}_2\text{Se}_3$, $\text{Fe}_3\text{GeTe}_2/\alpha\text{-In}_2\text{Se}_3$, and $\text{CrI}_3/\alpha\text{-In}_2\text{Se}_3$. However, most of these heterostructures have yet to demonstrate direct and switchable magnetoelectric coupling in experiments, highlighting a broader challenge even within conventional multiferroic fabrication strategies. The weak or absent interfacial coupling between assembled ferroelectric and ferromagnetic layers may be attributed to factors such as screening effects, unfavorable band alignment, and interfacial strain relaxation. 这种配置也被应用于其他磁性二维材料以构建全范德华 (vdW) 层状多铁材料，如 $\text{LaCl}/\alpha\text{-In}_2\text{Se}_3$, $\text{Fe}_3\text{GeTe}_2/\alpha\text{-In}_2\text{Se}_3$ 和 $\text{CrI}_3/\alpha\text{-In}_2\text{Se}_3$。然而，这些异质结构中的大多数在实验中尚未证明直接且可切换的磁电耦合，这突显了即使在常规多铁制备策略中也面临着更广泛的挑战。组装的铁电层和铁磁层之间微弱或不存在的界面耦合可能归因于屏蔽效应、不利的能带对齐和界面应变弛豫等因素。
> 
> These findings highlight the promise of 2D heterostructure multiferroics for the development of low-power, nonvolatile, and highly tunable memory and logic devices. Moving forward, systematic interface engineering, precise stacking control, and operando characterization techniques are crucial for uncovering the potential fundamental mutual coupling mechanisms and their applications. 这些发现突显了二维异质结构多铁材料在开发低功耗、非易失且高度可调的存储和逻辑器件方面的潜力。展望未来，系统的界面工程、精确的堆叠控制和原位表征技术对于揭示潜在的基础相互耦合机制及其应用至关重要。
> 
> ## 3. OPPORTUNITIES AND CHALLENGES IN 2D MULTIFERROICS
> 
> ## 3. 二维多铁材料的机遇与挑战
> 
> The above advancements confirm the feasibility of designing 2D van der Waals multiferroics, with several successful cases demonstrated. However, future development still faces significant challenges in the development of 2D multiferroics, particularly in material preparation, performance improvement, and potential applications. 上述进展证实了设计二维范德华多铁材料的可行性，并展示了几个成功案例。然而，二维多铁材料的未来发展仍面临重大挑战，特别是在材料制备、性能提升和潜在应用方面。
> 
> ### 3.1. Developing 2D Multiferroics.
> 
> ### 3.1. 二维多铁材料的开发
> 
> Intrinsic 2D multiferroics remain scarce due to the mutual exclusivity of multiple ferroic orders in many materials, whereas the structural flexibility of a 2D configuration can increase the likelihood of achieving multiferroicity. Notably, 2D magnetic materials, predominantly transition metal-based compounds, are more prevalent than their ferroelectric or ferroelastic counterparts. 由于许多材料中多种铁序之间存在互斥性，内在的二维多铁材料仍然稀少，而二维结构的结构灵活性可以增加实现多铁性的可能性。值得注意的是，二维磁性材料（主要是基于过渡金属的化合物）比铁电或铁弹性材料更为普遍。
> 
> Recent reports have shown that breaking centrosymmetry is easier in 2D systems. Some centrosymmetric 2D materials exhibit notable ferroelectricity, which is often enhanced by structural distortions or defects. In particular, light elements, Such as S or Se, can depict displacements and result in obvious ferroelectricity, differing from the metal atom displacements typically observed in 3D ferroelectrics. This deviation from traditional 3D ferroelectric behavior offers avenues for developing 2D multiferroics. 最近的报道表明，在二维系统中破坏中心对称性更容易。一些中心对称的二维材料表现出显著的铁电性，这通常由结构畸变或缺陷增强。特别是 S 或 Se 等轻元素可以产生位移并导致明显的铁电性，这与 3D 铁电体中通常观察到的金属原子位移不同。这种对传统 3D 铁电行为的背离为开发二维多铁材料提供了途径。
> 
> As mentioned above, focusing on transition metal compounds and incorporating structural processing methods presents a feasible approach for preparing 2D multiferroics. Comparatively, ferroelastic-related 2D multiferroics are still less reported, highlighting the importance of developing materials with ultralow symmetry structures. Due to the high crystal quality and ease of availability, mechanical exfoliation remains a preferred method for preparing intrinsic multiferroics, facilitating fundamental property investigations and material screening. However, chemical vapor deposition or thin-film deposition may open up more possibilities in engineering, as substrate-induced strain, defects, or distortions are more likely to occur. 如前所述，专注于过渡金属化合物并结合结构处理方法是制备二维多铁材料的一种可行途径。相比之下，与铁弹性相关的二维多铁材料报道仍然较少，这突显了开发超低对称结构材料的重要性。由于晶体质量高且易于获得，机械剥离仍然是制备内在多铁材料的首选方法，有利于基础性质研究和材料筛选。然而，化学气相沉积或薄膜沉积可能会在工程化方面开辟更多可能性，因为基底诱导的应变、缺陷或畸变更容易发生。
> 
> ### 3.2. Detection of 2D Multiferroicity.
> 
> ### 3.2. 二维多铁性的检测
> 
> Due to the ultrathin nature of 2D multiferroics, traditional characterization methods used for 3D crystals may no longer be applicable. As a result, optical techniques and microprobe microscopes will play an essential role in detecting multiferroicity. Atomic force microscopy (AFM) can image ferroelectricity, ferromagnetism, and ferroelasticity, providing a reliable method for qualitatively determining multiferroicity in 2D materials with a Curie temperature ($T_C$) higher than room temperature. 由于二维多铁材料的超薄特性，用于 3D 晶体的传统表征方法可能不再适用。因此，光学技术和微探针显微镜在检测多铁性方面将发挥关键作用。原子力显微镜 (AFM) 可以对铁电性、铁磁性和铁弹性进行成像，为居里温度 ($T_C$) 高于室温的二维材料定性确定多铁性提供了一种可靠的方法。
> 
> Optical techniques, such as magnetic circular dichroism (MCD), magneto-optical Kerr effect (MOKE), and second-harmonic generation (SHG), can nondestructively detect magnetic characteristics and structural symmetry across a wide temperature range. These methods, assisted by the application of external electric fields, magnetic fields, or mechanical force fields, enable detailed investigations of multiferroicity and the mutual coupling behaviors between different ferroic orders. 光学技术，如磁圆二色性 (MCD)、磁光克尔效应 (MOKE) 和二次谐波产生 (SHG)，可以在广泛的温度范围内无损地检测磁特性和结构对称性。这些方法在外部电场、磁场或机械力场的辅助下，能够对多铁性以及不同铁序之间的相互耦合行为进行详细研究。 **MOKE**: 磁光克尔效应，通过测量反射光的偏振状态变化来探测材料表面的磁化强度。
> 
> Integrating micro- and nanodevices with source meters would be an effective quantitative testing method. Magnetic susceptibility modules or ferroelectric analyzers can be combined with probe station systems or physical property measurement systems (PPMS), providing precise measurements of individual 2D domains. However, defining common performance units and standardized calculation methods is a crucial step toward extensive research on 2D multiferroics. 将微纳米器件与源表集成将是一种有效的定量测试方法。磁化率模块或铁电分析仪可以与探针台系统或物理性质测量系统 (PPMS) 相结合，提供对单个二维畴的精确测量。然而，定义通用的性能单位和标准计算方法是开展广泛二维多铁材料研究的关键一步。
> 
> ### 3.3. Enhancement of Curie Temperature and Coupling in 2D Multiferroics.
> 
> ### 3.3. 二维多铁材料居里温度与耦合的增强
> 
> Maintaining considerable multiferroicity and mutual coupling at room temperature is the key section and challenge for practical applications. In 2D systems, thermal fluctuations are more pronounced, resulting in a low thermal conductivity ($T_C$) of most 2D materials below room temperature. However, some 2D materials exhibit reliable ferroelectric orders at room temperature, such as $\text{In}_2\text{Se}_3$, $\text{CrTe}_2$, and $\text{ReS}_2$, providing promising frameworks for further development. 在室温下维持显著的多铁性和相互耦合是实际应用的关键部分和挑战。在二维系统中，热涨落更为明显，导致大多数二维材料的居里温度 ($T_C$) 低于室温。然而，一些二维材料在室温下表现出可靠的铁电序，如 $\text{In}_2\text{Se}_3$, $\text{CrTe}_2$ 和 $\text{ReS}_2$，为进一步发展提供了有前景的框架。
> 
> To achieve stable room-temperature 2D multiferroics, it is essential to establish a stable crystal structure that prevents symmetry restoration and supports long-range order driven by strong exchange interactions. Metal atom doping, defects, and intercalation (e.g., transition metals like $\text{Co}, \text{Mn}, \text{Cr}, \text{Ni}$, or rare-earth elements) can enhance unpaired spin density and crystal asymmetry. Another approach is to directly combine ferromagnetic and ferroelectric layers with the $T_C$ above RT into heterostructures, which can induce interfacial exchange bias, charge transfer, and orbital reconstruction. 为了实现稳定的室温二维多铁材料，必须建立一个能够防止对称性恢复并支持由强交换相互作用驱动的长程有序的稳定晶体结构。金属原子掺杂、缺陷和插层（例如 $\text{Co}, \text{Mn}, \text{Cr}, \text{Ni}$ 等过渡金属或稀土元素）可以增强未配对自旋密度和晶体不对称性。另一种方法是将 $T_C$ 高于室温的铁磁层和铁电层直接组合成异质结构，这可以诱导界面交换偏置、电荷转移和轨道重构。
> 
> However, the interface quality is crucial in strengthening multiferroic coupling. Ion gating or electrostatic doping can also adjust electron density and alter ferroic orders, providing another avenue for tuning multiferroic behavior. With the increase in reports on 2D intrinsic multiferroics, high-throughput computing and machine learning will play a crucial role in predicting and screening emerging 2D multiferroic materials. 然而，界面质量对于加强多铁耦合至关重要。离子门控或静电掺杂也可以调节电子密度并改变铁序，为调控多铁行为提供了另一种途径。随着二维内在多铁材料报道的增加，高通量计算和机器学习将在预测和筛选新兴二维多铁材料方面发挥至关重要作用。
> 
> Enhancing the coupling coefficient between different ferroic orders is essential for advancing high-performance devices. An intuitive approach for improving the coupling coefficient is manufacturing 2D multiferroics with identical origins for all ferroic orders. Atom intercalation between van der Waals layers presents a feasible method for simultaneously modulating the density of states and symmetry. The quasi-layered structures also provide a ready framework for substituting intercalation atoms, such as $\text{Cu}(\text{CrS}_2)_2$, a multiferroic material where Cu can act as active replacement sites. Additionally, regulating the ligands and coordination environment of magnetic metal atoms in 2D materials also unifies the origins of ferroelectricity and magnetism, further enhancing the overall multiferroic properties.
