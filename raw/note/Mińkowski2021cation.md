> # Abstract
> 
> # 摘要
> 
> Using a recently developed approach to represent ab initio based force fields by a neural network potential, we perform molecular dynamics simulations of lead telluride and cadmium telluride crystals. In particular, we study the diffusion of a single cation interstitial in these two systems. Our simulations indicate that the interstitials migrate via two distinct mechanisms: through hops between interstitial sites and through exchanges with lattice atoms. We extract activation energies for both of these mechanisms and show how the temperature dependence of the total diffusion coefficient deviates from Arrhenius behaviour. The accuracy of the neural network approach is estimated by comparing the results for three different independently trained potentials. 利用一种最新发展的通过神经网络势函数表示基于从头算的力场的方法，我们对碲化铅和碲化镉晶体进行了分子动力学模拟。我们特别研究了单个阳离子间隙在这两种体系中的扩散。我们的模拟表明，间隙原子通过两种不同的机制迁移：通过间隙位置之间的跳跃以及通过与晶格原子的交换。我们提取了这两种机制的活化能，并展示了总扩散系数的温度依赖性如何偏离阿伦尼乌斯行为。通过比较三种不同独立训练的势函数的结果，评估了神经网络方法的准确性。 **Term**: **neural network potential**: 神经网络势函数，一种利用人工神经网络拟合原子间相互作用势能面的方法。 **molecular dynamics**: 分子动力学，一种通过数值求解牛顿运动方程来模拟原子和分子体系随时间演化的计算模拟方法。 **interstitial**: 间隙原子，占据晶体晶格中正常原子位置之间空隙的原子。 **activation energy**: 活化能，启动一个特定过程（如扩散）所需克服的能量势垒。 **diffusion coefficient**: 扩散系数，描述粒子在介质中扩散快慢的物理量。 **Arrhenius behaviour**: 阿伦尼乌斯行为，描述物理量（如扩散系数）随温度呈指数关系变化的规律。

> # 1. Introduction
> 
> # 1. 引言
> 
> Lead telluride (PbTe) and cadmium telluride (CdTe) are semiconductors that combined constitute an immiscible system, which is often employed in the manufacturing of various nanostructures such as two-dimensional nanolayers, onedimensional nanowires and zero-dimensional quantum dots [1, 2]. Experiments show that even at very high temperatures, but below the melting point, these two compounds remain separated and morphological transformations of the shape of the particular phases occur, both, during annealing of an as-grown sample [3] and multilayer crystal growth [4]. 碲化铅 (PbTe) 和碲化镉 (CdTe) 是半导体，它们结合构成一个不混溶体系，该体系常用于制造各种纳米结构，如二维纳米层、一维纳米线和零维量子点 [1, 2]。实验表明，即使在非常高但低于熔点的温度下，这两种化合物仍保持分离，并且在退火处理生长态样品 [3] 和多层晶体生长 [4] 过程中，特定相的形态都会发生转变。 **Term**: **semiconductor**: 半导体，导电性介于导体和绝缘体之间的材料。 **nanostructure**: 纳米结构，至少在一个维度上尺寸在纳米尺度（1-100 nm）的结构。 **quantum dot**: 量子点，在三个维度上均受量子限域效应的纳米尺寸半导体颗粒。
>

>

> 
> By the appropriate choice of the experimental conditions, such as the thickness of the deposited layers, growth rate and temperature, one can design nanoobjects of desired shape and size [4–6]. From the application point of view, the most interesting structures are PbTe quantum dots and quantum wells embedded in CdTe, which can emit and detect light in the mid-infrared range [1, 2, 7]. Despite many experimental studies concerning PbTe/CdTe growth, the theoretical understanding of the dynamics of the underlying processes occurring during the morphological transformations is scarce. The multistage disintegration of a single PbTe layer inserted between two bulk CdTe materials into separate quantum dots during annealing at high temperatures was reproduced by the Cahn–Hilliard model [3]. Furthermore, the growth of PbTe/CdTe multilayers was studied by means of kinetic Monte Carlo [5, 6]. However, the models used in these studies have coarse-grained character without explicitly taking into account the underlying processes on the microscopic level. Hence, the atomistic mechanism of the morphological transformations across the PbTe/CdTe interface is still unknown. On the atomic scale one can expect that the morphological transformations described above are the result of diffusion of atoms across the PbTe/CdTe interface [8]. Point defects such as interstitials or vacancies could play a crucial role. For instance, interstitials from one compound could diffuse across the interface to the other one and participate there in the transformation of the crystal structure. Therefore, the first step in studying the mechanism of the morphological transformations is to focus on diffusion of native defects in PbTe and CdTe, without considering the interface. 通过适当选择实验条件，如沉积层厚度、生长速率和温度，可以设计具有所需形状和尺寸的纳米物体 [4–6]。从应用角度来看，最令人感兴趣的结构是嵌入在CdTe中的PbTe量子点和量子阱，它们可以发射和探测中红外波段的光 [1, 2, 7]。尽管有许多关于PbTe/CdTe生长的实验研究，但对形态转变过程中发生的底层过程动力学的理论理解却很匮乏。Cahn–Hilliard模型 [3] 再现了在高温退火过程中，插入在两个块体CdTe材料之间的单层PbTe层多阶段分解为分离的量子点的过程。此外，通过动力学蒙特卡罗方法 [5, 6] 研究了PbTe/CdTe多层膜的生长。然而，这些研究中使用的模型具有粗粒化特征，没有明确考虑微观层面的底层过程。因此，跨越PbTe/CdTe界面形态转变的原子尺度机制仍然未知。在原子尺度上，可以预期上述形态转变是原子跨越PbTe/CdTe界面扩散的结果 [8]。点缺陷，如间隙原子或空位，可能起着至关重要的作用。例如，来自一种化合物的间隙原子可以跨越界面扩散到另一种化合物，并参与那里的晶体结构转变。因此，研究形态转变机制的第一步是关注PbTe和CdTe中本征缺陷的扩散，而不考虑界面。 **Term**: **quantum well**: 量子阱，一种在一个维度上限制载流子运动的势阱结构。 **Cahn–Hilliard model**: Cahn-Hilliard模型，一种描述相分离过程（如旋节分解）的连续介质模型。 **kinetic Monte Carlo**: 动力学蒙特卡罗，一种模拟系统随时间演化（如原子扩散）的随机方法。 **point defect**: 点缺陷，晶体中只涉及单个原子或少数几个原子位置的缺陷，如空位和间隙原子。 **vacancy**: 空位，晶体晶格中缺少原子的位置。
> 
> Molecular dynamics (MD) simulations are a common method for studying the time evolution of condensed matter systems. In order to perform such simulations one needs to determine the forces acting on the atoms, which are often described by empirical potentials. There exist empirical potentials for PbTe [9, 10], and CdTe [11, 12] crystals. However, the accuracy of empirical potentials is in general not adequate due to their limited flexibility. Alternatively, ab initio MD simulations can be performed, which are, however, computationally expensive and therefore not practical for the analysis of the diffusion of defects, where large systems and long simulation times are required. In order to overcome the difficulties related to both these approaches, we employ the neural network method of Behler and Parrinello [13–15], in which a high-dimensional artificial neural network is trained with energies and forces determined by means of ab initio calculations. It has been shown that such neural networks are capable of accurately reproducing the references data and deliver the accuracy of ab initio methods at a fraction of their cost. To date, this approach has been applied to study properties of various systems [16, 19–21]. 分子动力学 (MD) 模拟是研究凝聚态体系时间演化的常用方法。为了进行此类模拟，需要确定作用在原子上的力，这些力通常由经验势函数描述。存在用于PbTe [9, 10] 和CdTe [11, 12] 晶体的经验势函数。然而，由于其有限的灵活性，经验势函数的准确性通常不足。或者，可以进行从头算分子动力学模拟，但这种方法计算成本高昂，因此对于需要大体系和长时间模拟的缺陷扩散分析来说不切实际。为了克服这两种方法相关的困难，我们采用了Behler和Parrinello的神经网络方法 [13–15]，其中利用从头算确定的能量和力来训练一个高维人工神经网络。已经证明，这种神经网络能够准确地再现参考数据，并以远低于从头算方法的成本提供其精度。迄今为止，该方法已被应用于研究各种体系的性质 [16, 19–21]。 **Term**: **empirical potential**: 经验势函数，基于实验数据或物理直觉构建的简化的原子间相互作用势能函数。 **ab initio MD**: 从头算分子动力学，基于电子结构理论（如密度泛函理论）直接计算原子间力的分子动力学模拟。
> 
> In this work, neural networks for bulk PbTe and CdTe are trained and used to carry out extensive molecular simulations for the diffusion of single cationic interstitials in these materials over a range of temperatures. From the obtained MD trajectories we calculate the diffusion coefficients for the interstitials as a function of temperature and analyse the different diffusion mechanisms occurring in the two systems. For both materials, the same calculations are performed for three independently trained neural network potentials in order to estimate the overall accuracy of the method. 在这项工作中，我们训练了块体PbTe和CdTe的神经网络，并用于开展广泛的分子模拟，研究这些材料中单一阳离子间隙原子在一定温度范围内的扩散。从获得的MD轨迹中，我们计算了间隙原子扩散系数随温度变化的函数，并分析了两种体系中发生的不同扩散机制。对于这两种材料，使用三个独立训练的神经网络势函数进行相同的计算，以评估该方法的整体准确性。
> 
> The remainder of the article is organized as follows. In section 2 we review the main physical properties of PbTe and CdTe crystals. Then, in section 3, we introduce the computational techniques that we employed in our study and in section 4 we present the results of our simulation. Finally, in section 5 we conclude by discussing our findings. 本文的其余部分组织如下：在第2节中，我们回顾PbTe和CdTe晶体的主要物理性质。然后，在第3节中，我们介绍本研究采用的计算技术，并在第4节中展示我们的模拟结果。最后，在第5节中，我们通过讨论我们的发现进行总结。
> 
> # 2. Systems
> 
> # 2. 体系
> 
> PbTe and CdTe are semiconductors belonging to the groups IV–VI and II–VI, respectively. They possess very similar lattice constants, however, their lattice structures are distinct. According to diffraction experiments the lattice constant a of PbTe at room temperature is 6.46 Å [22] and that of CdTe is 6.48 Å [23]. The lattice constant mismatch is therefore very small. However, there exists a lattice-type mismatch. PbTe crystallizes in the rocksalt structure and CdTe in the zinc-blende structure. Their lattices can be in fact represented as two interpenetrating face-centered cubic (fcc) cation and anion sublattices shifted with respect to each other by a/2 for rocksalt and a/4 for zinc-blende. Because of the different crystallographic structures the coordination number for PbTe and CdTe is also different. In PbTe each atom has 6 nearest neighbours, while in CdTe each atom has 4 of them. PbTe和CdTe分别是属于IV–VI族和II–VI族的半导体。它们具有非常相似的晶格常数，然而，它们的晶格结构是不同的。根据衍射实验，PbTe在室温下的晶格常数a为6.46 Å [22]，CdTe为6.48 Å [23]。因此，晶格常数失配非常小。然而，存在晶格类型失配。PbTe结晶为岩盐结构，CdTe结晶为闪锌矿结构。它们的晶格实际上可以表示为两个相互穿透的面心立方 (fcc) 阳离子和阴离子亚晶格，对于岩盐结构，它们彼此相对位移a/2，对于闪锌矿结构，位移a/4。由于晶体学结构不同，PbTe和CdTe的配位数也不同。在PbTe中，每个原子有6个最近邻，而在CdTe中，每个原子有4个最近邻。 **Term**: **lattice constant**: 晶格常数，晶体单胞的物理尺寸。 **rocksalt structure**: 岩盐结构，一种离子晶体结构，阳离子和阴离子均占据面心立方格点，且彼此沿1/2方向位移。 **zinc-blende structure**: 闪锌矿结构，一种共价晶体结构，类似于金刚石结构，但由两种不同原子占据晶格点。 **face-centered cubic**: 面心立方，一种晶体结构，原子位于立方体的顶点和每个面的中心。 **coordination number**: 配位数，晶体中一个原子周围最近邻原子的数目。
> 
> From annealing and growth experiments it is known that systems containing both PbTe and CdTe are immiscible, i.e. instead of mixing they remain separated in thermal equilibrium [3, 4]. Consequently, an interface is created, with which some surface free energy is associated [8]. The ratios of the energies for different interface orientations controls the shape of the nanoobjects that emerge in the experiments. Among these nanoobjects there are nanolayers, nanowires and quantum dots. The equilibrium shape of the PbTe quantum dots embedded in CdTe was predicted using ab initio methods [8] and their electronic properties were also studied [24]. Since PbTe and CdTe share the common anion (Te), the morphological transformations at the atomic scale must occur through the diffusion of cations (Pb and Cd) across the interface and the subsequent reorganization of the local lattice structure. It has been suggested that point defects play an important role in this process of cation exchange [25]. 从退火和生长实验可知，同时含有PbTe和CdTe的体系是不混溶的，即它们在热平衡中保持分离而不是混合 [3, 4]。因此，会形成一个界面，并与之关联一些表面自由能 [8]。不同界面取向的能量比控制着实验中出现的纳米物体的形状。这些纳米物体包括纳米层、纳米线和量子点。嵌入CdTe中的PbTe量子点的平衡形状已通过从头算方法预测 [8]，其电子性质也已被研究 [24]。由于PbTe和CdTe具有共同的阴离子 (Te)，原子尺度上的形态转变必须通过阳离子（Pb和Cd）跨越界面的扩散以及随后局域晶格结构的重组来实现。有人提出点缺陷在这种阳离子交换过程中起重要作用 [25]。
> 
> There are several possible types of point defects in the crystal structure: vacancies, interstitials, Schottky and Frenkel defects. Vacancies and interstitials are the simplest defects, however, the barrier for the diffusion of vacancies in PbTe and CdTe is very high [26]. Therefore, in this work we focus only on interstitials. We consider native interstitials diffusing in the bulk of PbTe and CdTe. Since we study PdTe and CdTe separately, our simulated systems do not contain interfaces between the two materials. 晶体结构中有几种可能的点缺陷类型：空位、间隙原子、肖特基缺陷和弗伦克尔缺陷。空位和间隙原子是最简单的缺陷，然而，在PbTe和CdTe中，空位扩散的势垒非常高 [26]。因此，在这项工作中，我们只关注间隙原子。我们考虑在PbTe和CdTe块体中扩散的本征间隙原子。由于我们分别研究PdTe和CdTe，我们的模拟体系不包含两种材料之间的界面。 **Term**: **Schottky defect**: 肖特基缺陷，离子晶体中产生等量阳离子和阴离子空位的点缺陷。 **Frenkel defect**: 弗伦克尔缺陷，原子或离子离开其正常晶格位置进入间隙位置，同时产生一个空位和一个间隙原子的点缺陷。
> 
> In PbTe crystals all energetic minima for Pb interstitials are equivalent. Each Pb interstitial atom is surrounded by 4 nearest-neighbour Pb and 4 nearest-neighbour Te lattice atoms [25]. In CdTe, on the other hand, there are two nonequivalent energetic minima for Cd interstitials. One of them is a tetrahedrally coordinated site with Te atoms and octahedrally coordinated with Cd atoms, the other one has the reversed configuration of the first one [26]. They are labelled Ta and Tc, respectively. 在PbTe晶体中，Pb间隙原子的所有能量极小值点都是等价的。每个Pb间隙原子被4个最近邻Pb原子和4个最近邻Te晶格原子包围 [25]。另一方面，在CdTe中，Cd间隙原子有两个非等价的能量极小值点。其中一个是由Te原子四面体配位、由Cd原子八面体配位的位置，另一个则具有与第一个相反的配置 [26]。它们分别标记为Ta和Tc。
> 
> # 3. Methods
> 
> # 3. 方法
> 
> In order to study the diffusion of cation interstitials in PbTe and CdTe, the following computational strategy is employed. First, the Vienna ab initio simulation package (VASP) [17] is used to calculate the reference data, i.e. electronic ground state energies and forces for a given set of configurations of PbTe and CdTe with density-functional theory (DFT). These energies and forces are then used to train neural networks by means of the neural network potential package (n2p2) [27]. Finally, the trained neural networks are used for performing MD simulations with Large-scale Atomic/Molecular Massively Parallel Simulator (LAMMPS) [28]. In the post-processing of the trajectories obtained at different temperatures the position of the interstitial is identified at each time step and from the interstitial’s trajectory its diffusion coefficient is extracted. This approach is used iteratively, that is the training set is repeatedly expanded with new configurations, for which new energies and forces are calculated, and the neural network is retrained at each iteration with the expanded set. These retrained neural networks are subsequently used to perform new MD simulations. Below we describe the methods used in this work in more detail. 为了研究PbTe和CdTe中阳离子间隙原子的扩散，采用了以下计算策略。首先，使用维也纳从头算模拟包 (VASP) [17] 计算参考数据，即使用密度泛函理论 (DFT) 计算给定PbTe和CdTe构型集的电子基态能量和力。然后，利用神经网络势函数包 (n2p2) [27] 使用这些能量和力来训练神经网络。最后，使用训练好的神经网络，通过大规模原子/分子大规模并行模拟器 (LAMMPS) [28] 进行MD模拟。在对不同温度下获得的轨迹进行后处理时，识别每个时间步长间隙原子的位置，并从间隙原子的轨迹中提取其扩散系数。这种方法被迭代使用，即使用新的构型反复扩展训练集，计算这些新构型的能量和力，并在每次迭代中用扩展的集合重新训练神经网络。这些重新训练的神经网络随后被用于执行新的MD模拟。下面我们更详细地描述本工作中使用的方法。 **Term**: **density-functional theory**: 密度泛函理论，一种通过电子密度而非波函数来研究多电子体系电子结构的量子力学计算方法。 **VASP**: 维也纳从头算模拟包，一个广泛使用的进行电子结构计算和量子力学-分子动力学模拟的软件包。 **LAMMPS**: 大规模原子/分子大规模并行模拟器，一个经典的分子动力学模拟程序。
> 
> ## 3.1. Density-functional theory
> 
> ## 3.1. 密度泛函理论
> 
> All our DFT calculations are carried out with the exchange–correlation functional PBEsol [18]. The spin–orbit coupling is not considered. As the starting point for generating the set of reference configurations, 4 × 4 × 4 PbTe and CdTe supercells with the experimental lattice constants were used. Subsequent structures were created by increasing and decreasing the lattice constant, and randomly displacing the atoms from their positions in the perfect lattices. As the neural network was trained, configurations obtained from the MD trajectories were also subsequently added. Supercells containing one interstitial atom were also included in the training set such that the total number of atoms in a structure was either 512 or 513. The Brillouin zone integrations for all structures were performed using the Γ-point only. A convergence test for the k-point mesh revealed that the forces obtained with a denser 3 × 3 × 3 k-mesh did not significantly differ from the Γ-point calculations but lasted roughly 10 times longer. 我们所有的DFT计算都使用交换-关联泛函PBEsol [18] 进行。未考虑自旋-轨道耦合。作为生成参考构型集的起点，使用了具有实验晶格常数的4 × 4 × 4 PbTe和CdTe超胞。随后的结构是通过增加和减小晶格常数，以及将原子从完美晶格中的位置随机位移来创建的。随着神经网络的训练，从MD轨迹中获得的构型也被陆续添加。包含一个间隙原子的超胞也包含在训练集中，使得一个结构中的总原子数为512或513。所有结构的布里渊区积分仅使用Γ点进行。对k点网格的收敛性测试表明，使用更密的3 × 3 × 3 k网格得到的力与Γ点计算没有显著差异，但持续时间大约长10倍。 **Term**: **exchange–correlation functional**: 交换-关联泛函，密度泛函理论中用于近似电子交换和关联相互作用的数学表达式。 **PBEsol**: 一种为固体和表面优化的交换-关联泛函。 **Brillouin zone**: 布里渊区，晶体倒空间中的原胞。 **Γ-point**: 布里渊区中心。
> 
> ## 3.2. Neural network potential
> 
> ## 3.2. 神经网络势函数
> 
> The data obtained from VASP then can be used to train a neural network potential, which represents the force field used in the MD simulations. The input of the neural network is an atomic configuration and the output is its electronic ground state energy. Since the neural network is an analytic function of the atomic coordinates, the forces acting on each of the atoms can be determined by simple differentiation. For training the neural network potential and employing it in MD simulations we use the approach introduced by Behler and Parrinello [13]. In this approach, the information about the atomic configurations is encoded in so called symmetry functions [29]. They transform the Cartesian coordinates into values that are invariant with respect to the translation and rotation of the whole system, and to the exchange of two arbitrary atoms of the same type. The values of these symmetry functions serve as input for the neural network. 从VASP获得的数据随后可用于训练神经网络势函数，该势函数代表了MD模拟中使用的力场。神经网络的输入是原子构型，输出是其电子基态能量。由于神经网络是原子坐标的解析函数，因此可以通过简单的微分来确定作用在每个原子上的力。为了训练神经网络势函数并将其用于MD模拟，我们使用了Behler和Parrinello [13] 引入的方法。在这种方法中，关于原子构型的信息被编码在所谓的对称函数中 [29]。它们将笛卡尔坐标转换为对于整个体系的平移和旋转，以及对于任意两个同类原子的交换保持不变的值。这些对称函数的值作为神经网络的输入。 **Term**: **symmetry function**: 对称函数，一种描述原子局域环境的结构指纹，满足平移、旋转和交换不变性。

> ## 3.3. Molecular dynamics
> 
> ## 3.3. 分子动力学
> 
> Once the neural network for a given system is trained, it can be used for performing MD simulations. The program n2p2 provides an interface to LAMMPS [27], which is used for all MD simulations in the present work. In total, the systems consisted of 513 atoms. The simulations are performed with a timestep of 2 fs with a Nose–Hoover thermostat and barostat for temperatures ranging from 700 to 1200 K in intervals of 50 K. > ## 4.1. Neural network training

> ## 4.1. 神经网络训练
> 
> For each of the two investigated materials, PbTe and CdTe, three independent neural networks with different random seeds chosen for the initialization of the values of the network’s parameters were trained. Consequently, the final values of these parameters were different for each network also at the end of the training, even though all three networks represented the same system. The configurations used for the training were created iteratively. First, the lattice constants of the PbTe rocksalt and CdTe zinc blende unit cells were optimized using VASP. The initial set consisted of a few hundreds of 4 × 4 × 4 supercells created with these optimized lattice constants. In addition to the perfect structures, configurations with the atoms displaced randomly from their equilibrium positions were also used. To the subsequent sets configurations with slightly modified lattice constants were added to account for the thermal expansion of crystals at finite temperatures. Moreover, new configurations were also taken from MD trajectories. During the MD simulations, an extrapolation warning was produced each time the value of some symmetry function was out of range for which the neural network was initially trained. The number of such warnings was monitored during the simulations and the configurations with the largest number of extrapolation warnings were added to the set. Additionally, predictions for the same trajectory by two independently trained neural networks were compared and those configurations for which the predictions differed considerably were also added. This procedure was repeated until there were no extrapolations warnings and no significant differences between different neural networks in the relevant range of temperatures. 对于两种被研究的材料PbTe和CdTe，各训练了三个独立的神经网络，它们使用不同的随机种子来初始化网络参数值。因此，即使在训练结束时，所有三个网络代表相同的体系，这些参数的最终值对于每个网络也是不同的。用于训练的构型是迭代创建的。首先，使用VASP优化PbTe岩盐和CdTe闪锌矿单胞的晶格常数。初始集合包含了几百个使用这些优化晶格常数创建的 4 × 4 × 4 超胞。除了完美结构外，还使用了原子从其平衡位置随机位移的构型。在随后的集合中，添加了晶格常数略有修改的构型，以考虑晶体在有限温度下的热膨胀。此外，还从MD轨迹中获取了新的构型。在MD模拟期间，每当某个对称函数的值超出神经网络初始训练的范围时，就会产生外推警告。在模拟过程中监控此类警告的数量，并将具有最多外推警告的构型添加到集合中。此外，比较了两个独立训练的神经网络对同一轨迹的预测，并将那些预测差异显着的构型也添加进去。重复此过程，直到在相关温度范围内没有外推警告，并且不同神经网络之间没有显着差异。

> ## 4.3. Diffusion mechanisms
> 
> ## 4.3. 扩散机制
> 
> As already mentioned, two different mechanisms of interstitial diffusion were observed in the MD simulations of PbTe and CdTe. In one of them the interstitial atom simply jumps between two different energetic minima, in the other one it exchanges its position with one of the lattice atoms. We refer to these mechanisms as hops and exchanges, respectively. The mechanisms of diffusion in PbTe are illustrated in figure 5. Since there is only one equilibrium position for the interstitial atom in PbTe and two in CdTe, the diffusion in the former is simpler. During a hop, the interstitial Pb atom jumps between the nearest-neighbouring minima along the [100] direction. The length of the jump is equal to a/2. On the other hand, during the exchange process the interstitial Pb atom kicks out one of its four neighbouring Pb lattice atoms, which in turn assumes the position in one of the neighbouring minima, either in the [110] or [111] direction relative to the original position of the first interstitial. The effective jump of the interstitial Pb atom is therefore (a/2, a/2, 0) or (a/2, a/2, a/2) and analogously in all the equivalent crystallographic directions. Hence, during an exchange the interstitial moves by a distance of a√2/2 or a√3/2, respectively. 如前所述，在PbTe和CdTe的MD模拟中观察到两种不同的间隙扩散机制。在一种机制中，间隙原子简单地在两个不同的能量极小值之间跳跃，在另一种机制中，它与一个晶格原子交换位置。我们分别称这些机制为跳跃和交换。PbTe中的扩散机制如图 5 所示。由于PbTe中间隙原子只有一个平衡位置，而CdTe中有两个，因此前者的扩散更简单。在跳跃过程中，间隙Pb原子沿 [100] 方向在最近邻极小值之间跳跃。跳跃长度为 a/2。另一方面，在交换过程中，间隙Pb原子踢出其四个邻近Pb晶格原子中的一个，该晶格原子随后占据一个邻近极小值的位置，相对于第一个间隙原子的原始位置，方向为 [110] 或 [111]。因此，间隙Pb原子的有效跳跃为 (a/2, a/2, 0) 或 (a/2, a/2, a/2)，并且在所有等效的晶体学方向上类似。因此，在交换过程中，间隙原子分别移动 a√2/2 或 a√3/2 的距离。

> Analogously, in figure 6 the diffusion mechanisms in CdTe are shown. The diffusion of interstitial Cd atoms is more complex. As mentioned in section 2, for the interstitial Cd atom there are two different equilibrium positions labelled Ta (tetrahedral anion site) and Tc (tetrahedral cation site). Moreover, the Cd interstitial can exist in three charge states [39, 40]: one neutral and two charged ones, Cd+ and Cd2+. The height of the energy barrier between the interstitial sites depends on the particular charge state. In the present case the interstitial atom occupies most of the time the Ta site and only for a very short time it is found in Tc position, which corresponds to the state Cd2+ discussed in references [39, 40]. The diffusion through hops proceeds between Ta and Tc sites along the [110] direction, as described in reference [39]. It consists of jumps of the interstitial Cd atom between these sites in the directions [111] and [11−1]. The length of a single jump is √23 a. Exchanges, on the other hand, occur mostly between two Tc sites, also in the [110] direction. One exchange is therefore effectively equivalent to two successive hops. 类似地，图 6 显示了CdTe中的扩散机制。间隙Cd原子的扩散更复杂。如第 2 节所述，对于间隙Cd原子，有两个不同的平衡位置，标记为 Ta（四面体阴离子位点）和 Tc（四面体阳离子位点）。此外，Cd间隙原子可以存在于三种电荷状态 [39, 40]：一种中性和两种带电状态，Cd+ 和 Cd2+。间隙位点之间的能量势垒高度取决于特定的电荷状态。在当前情况下，间隙原子大部分时间占据 Ta 位点，仅在很短的时间内出现在 Tc 位点，这对应于参考文献 [39, 40] 中讨论的 Cd2+ 状态。如参考文献 [39] 所述，通过跳跃进行的扩散沿 [110] 方向在 Ta 和 Tc 位点之间进行。它包括间隙Cd原子在这些位点之间沿 [111] 和 [11−1] 方向的跳跃。单次跳跃的长度为 √23 a。另一方面，交换主要发生在两个 Tc 位点之间，也沿 [110] 方向。因此，一次交换实际上等效于两次连续的跳跃。

> # 5. Conclusions
> 
> # 5. 结论
> 
> In this work, the diffusion processes of interstitial Pb and Cd atoms have been studied in a supercell of PbTe and CdTe, respectively. Futhermore, a procedure of extracting the value of the diffusion coefficient from the trajectories generated by neural network based MD simulations has been demonstrated. For the training of the neural network potentials ab initio data calculated with the PBEsol functional were used. For both systems the results for the diffusion coefficients for three different independently trained neural network potentials were compared. The corresponding activation energies were extracted and it was found that they differ from each other by no more than 100 meV, which can be viewed as the accuracy of the neural network approach used in this work for the calculation of activation energies. Both in PbTe and CdTe two different mechanisms of interstitial diffusion have been observed, namely hops and exchanges, for which separate activation energies have been extracted. Hops were more frequent in CdTe and exchanges in PbTe. However, in both materials exchanges had longer effective jump lengths. Therefore, interstitial diffusion is dominantly controlled by exchanges in both materials. Taking into account two diffusion mechanisms with different activation energies explains the deviations of the temperature dependence of the diffusion coefficient from the Arrhenius law. 在这项工作中，分别在PbTe和CdTe的超胞中研究了间隙Pb和Cd原子的扩散过程。此外，还展示了一种从基于神经网络的MD模拟生成的轨迹中提取扩散系数值的程序。对于神经网络势函数的训练，使用了用PBEsol泛函计算的从头算数据。对于这两个体系，比较了三个不同独立训练的神经网络势函数的扩散系数结果。提取了相应的活化能，发现它们彼此之间的差异不超过 100 meV，这可以看作是本工作中用于计算活化能的神经网络方法的准确性。在PbTe和CdTe中，都观察到了两种不同的间隙扩散机制，即跳跃和交换，并为此提取了分离的活化能。跳跃在CdTe中更频繁，交换在PbTe中更频繁。然而，在两种材料中，交换具有更长的有效跳跃长度。因此，间隙扩散主要由两种材料中的交换控制。考虑具有不同活化能的两种扩散机制解释了扩散系数的温度依赖性偏离阿伦尼乌斯定律的原因。
> 
> The mechanism of atom exchange is particularly interesting in the context of the morphological transformations observed experimentally in PbTe/CdTe systems. In this work, only selfdiffusion of cations within either PbTe or CdTe has been considered. However, the framework presented here can be also used to study the diffusion of foreign atoms in the crystal. One can expect that Pb cations introduced in CdTe exchange with the host Cd cations and Cd cations in PbTe exchange with the host Pb cations. This could lead to a subsequent rebuild of the local crystal structure, which in turn could be an underlying mechanism for the morphological transformations observed in the PbTe/CdTe growth and annealing experiments.
