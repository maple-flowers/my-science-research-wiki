> # Studying the Polarization Switching in Polycrystalline $\text{BiFeO}_3$ Films by 2D Piezoresponse Force Microscopy
> 
> 通过二维压电响应力显微镜研究多晶 $\text{BiFeO}_3$ 薄膜中的极化翻转 **$\text{BiFeO}_3$ (BFO)**: 铁酸铋，一种在室温下具有单相多铁性的材料。 **Piezoresponse Force Microscopy (PFM)**: 压电响应力显微镜，一种用于表征铁电材料域结构及其动态过程的扫描探针显微技术。
> 
> For rhombohedral multiferroelectrics, non-180° ferroelectric domain switching may induce ferroelastic and/or (anti-)ferromagnetic effect. So the determination and control of ferroelectric domain switching angles is crucial for nonvolatile information storage and exchange-coupled magnetoelectric devices. We try to study the intrinsic characters of polarization switching in $\text{BiFeO}_3$ by introducing a special data processing method to determine the switching angle from 2D PFM images of randomly oriented samples. The response surface of $\text{BiFeO}_3$ is first plotted using the piezoelectric tensor got from first principles calculations. Then from the normalized 2D PFM signals before and after switching, the switching angles of randomly oriented $\text{BiFeO}_3$ grains can be determined through numerical calculations. In the polycrystalline $\text{BiFeO}_3$ films, up to 34% of all switched area is that with original out-of-plane (OP) polarization parallel to the poling field. 71° polarization switching is more favorable, with the area percentages of 71°, 109° and 180° domain switching being about 42%, 29% and 29%, respectively. Our analysis further reveals that IP stress and charge migration have comparable effect on switching, and they are sensitive to the geometric arrangements. This work helps exploring a route to control polarization switching in $\text{BiFeO}_3$, so as to realize desirable magnetoelectric coupling. 对于菱方多铁电体，非180°的铁电畴翻转可能会引起铁弹性及/或（反）铁磁效应。因此，确定和控制铁电畴的翻转角度对于非易失性信息存储和交换耦合磁电器件至关重要。我们尝试通过引入一种特殊的数据处理方法，从随机取向样品的二维PFM图像中确定翻转角度，从而研究 $\text{BiFeO}_3$ 中极化翻转的本质特性。首先利用第一原理计算得到的压电张量绘制 $\text{BiFeO}_3$ 的响应面。然后，通过对翻转前后的归一化二维PFM信号进行数值计算，可以确定随机取向 $\text{BiFeO}_3$ 晶粒的翻转角度。在多晶 $\text{BiFeO}_3$ 薄膜中，高达34%的翻转区域其原始面外（OP）极化方向与极化电场平行。71°极化翻转更为有利，71°、109°和180°畴翻转的面积百分比分别约为42%、29%和29%。我们的分析进一步表明，面内（IP）应力和电荷迁移对翻转的影响相当，且它们对几何排列较为敏感。这项工作有助于探索控制 $\text{BiFeO}_3$ 极化翻转的途径，以实现理想的磁电耦合。 **Multiferroelectrics**: 多铁电体，同时具有两种或多种铁序（如铁电、铁磁）的材料。 **Out-of-plane (OP)**: 面外，指垂直于样品表面的方向。 **In-plane (IP)**: 面内，指平行于样品表面的方向。
> 
> It is known that, non-180° ferroelectric domain switching is significant in rhombohedral ferroelectrics. 180° domain switching doesn’t change the crystal lattice structure of rhombohedral materials, while non-180° ferroelectric domain switching may change the elastic condition and magnetic order parameters. On the one hand, elastic interactions induced by non-180° domain switching tend to destabilize switched domains, and result in the disappearance of non-volatile information storage; On the other hand, non-180° domain switching under electric field can realize magnetoelectric coupling in rhombohedral ferroelectrics. Therefore, the understanding of the intrinsic switching characterization is crucial for further manipulation and application of rhombohedral ferroelectrics. 众所周知，非180°铁电畴翻转在菱方铁电体中具有重要意义。180°畴翻转不会改变菱方材料的晶格结构，而非180°铁电畴翻转可能会改变弹性条件和磁有序参数。一方面，非180°畴翻转引起的弹性相互作用倾向于使翻转后的畴不稳定，导致非易失性信息存储的消失；另一方面，电场驱动的非180°畴翻转可以在菱方铁电体中实现磁电耦合。因此，理解其内在的翻转特性对于进一步操纵和应用菱方铁电体至关重要。
> 
> $\text{BiFeO}_3$ (BFO), the only known single-phase multiferroic material at room temperature till now, has attracted remarkable attention because of its potential applications in eco-friendly multistate memory devices on the basis of electric and magnetic mutual modulation, of which the cornerstone is the polarization switching under electric field. BFO has a rhombohedrally distortion ($R3c$ space group) perovskite structure with rotation of oxygen octahedral and polarization distortion along the pseudocubic [111] direction. Correspondingly, it possesses eight possible polarization directions and three switching angles (71°, 109° and 180°). $\text{BiFeO}_3$ (BFO) 是目前已知唯一的室温单相多铁材料，因其在基于电磁互调的环保多状态存储设备中的潜在应用而受到广泛关注，而其基石正是电场下的极化翻转。BFO 具有菱方畸变的（$R3c$ 空间群）钙钛矿结构，伴随氧八面体旋转以及沿伪立方 [111] 方向的极化畸变。相应地，它具有八个可能的极化方向和三个翻转角度（71°、109° 和 180°）。 **Perovskite structure**: 钙钛矿结构，一种通式为 $ABO_3$ 的晶体结构。 **Pseudocubic**: 伪立方，指虽然晶格略有畸变但近似于立方结构的描述方式。

> ## Results
> 
> ## 结果
> 
> Figure 2b gives the surface morphology of the sample before polar scan, which actually didn’t change after poling. > Using the data processing method described above, polarization switching with different angles (71°, 109°, and 180°) can be observed in the polycrystalline BFO films (Fig. 3c). All the normally switched domains are assigned into 2 groups, that is, with original OP polarization (OPP) parallel ($\theta' < 90^\circ, P_z' > 0$) or antiparallel ($\theta' > 90^\circ, P_z' < 0$) to $z$ axis. 使用上述数据处理方法，在多晶 BFO 薄膜中可以观察到不同角度（71°、109° 和 180°）的极化翻转（图 3c）。所有正常翻转的畴被分为两组，即原始面外极化（OPP）与 $z$ 轴平行 ($\theta' < 90^\circ, P_z' > 0$) 或反平行 ($\theta' > 90^\circ, P_z' < 0$)。
> 
> The understanding of the experimental results bases on the following three points: (a) Electric energy and stress energy are comparable during polarization switching. (b) Electric energy increases with switching angle. (c) Stress energy varies for different switching. 实验结果的理解基于以下三点： (a) 极化翻转过程中电能和应变能处于相当的数量级。 (b) 电能随翻转角度的增大而增加。 (c) 不同翻转方式的应变能不同。
> 
> ## Discussion
> 
> ## 讨论
> 
> In summary, we investigated the polarization switching in polycrystalline BFO films by 2D Piezoresponse Force Microscopy. First, a data processing method is introduced to determine the switching angles in polycrystalline BFO films. Second, BFO polycrystalline sample could demonstrate a variety of switching angles (71°, 109° and 180°) and switching forms, indicating the possible applications of BFO on multistate memories. Finally, although the overall possibility of 71° switching is obviously larger than that of 109° and 180° switching, the possibility of any specific switching could be greatly affected by two comparable factors, charge migration and IP stress. 总之，我们利用二维压电响应力显微镜研究了多晶 BFO 薄膜中的极化翻转。首先，引入了一种确定多晶 BFO 薄膜中翻转角度的数据处理方法。其次，BFO 多晶样品展现了多种翻转角度（71°、109° 和 180°）和翻转形式，表明 BFO 在多状态存储器中的潜在应用。最后，尽管 71° 翻转的整体可能性明显大于 109° 和 180° 翻转，但任何特定翻转的可能性都可能受到电荷迁移和面内（IP）应力这两个相当因素的极大影响。
