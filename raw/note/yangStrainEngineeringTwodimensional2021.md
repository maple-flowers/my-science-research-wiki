> Abstract
>            Two‐dimensional (2D) materials have attracted extensive research interests due to their excellent properties related to unique structure. Strain engineering, as an important strategy for tuning the lattice and electronic structure of 2D materials, has been widely used in the modulation of physical properties, which broadens their applications in flexible nanoelectronic and optoelectronic devices. In this review, we first summarize the methods of inducing strain to 2D materials and discuss the advantages and problems of various methods. We then introduce the strain‐induced effects on optical, electrical, and magnetic properties, together with the phase transition of 2D materials. Finally, we illustrate the potential applications of strained 2D materials and further look forward to their opportunities and challenges in practical applications in the future.

> ## 一、引言
> 
> **领域背景**：自2004年石墨烯被发现以来，**二维（2D）材料**因其独特的原子级厚度和优异的物理性质（如高载流子迁移率、可调带隙等）成为材料科学和凝聚态物理的研究热点。为了进一步拓展其应用，研究者开发了多种性质调控策略，其中**应变工程（Strain Engineering）** 作为一种通过改变材料晶格结构来调控电子结构的有效手段，正受到广泛关注。
> 
> **研究问题**：尽管应变工程在传统半导体工业中已有应用，但体材料可承受的弹性应变极小。二维材料虽然具有超强的变形能力，但如何系统性地施加、调控并利用应变来优化其性能，仍是该领域从基础研究走向实际应用的关键挑战。
> 
> **文献定位与核心价值**：本文是一篇发表于《InfoMat》期刊的综述文章，由北京航空航天大学的杨圣雪教授团队撰写。该文并未提出新的实验数据，而是对这一快速发展领域进行了系统性梳理。其核心价值在于：
> 
> 1.  **方法学分类**：首次从物理机制上对多种应变引入方法进行了清晰分类和比较。
> 2.  **“方法-性能-应用”逻辑链**：完整地构建了从应变引入方法，到光学、电学、磁学性能调控，再到柔性传感器和光探测器等终端应用的知识体系。
> 3.  **前瞻性指引**：明确指出了该领域当前面临的技术瓶颈和未来研究方向，为后续研究者提供了清晰的路线图。
> 
> ## 二、论文结构与逻辑概览
> 
> 本文遵循“总-分-总”的经典综述结构，逻辑链条清晰，旨在为读者建立从技术手段到最终应用的完整认知。
> 
> **核心论证链条**：_原子级厚度赋予二维材料优异的应变耐受性 → 多种物理方法可将应变有效引入二维材料 → 应变显著改变晶格与电子结构 → 实现对光学、电学、磁学等多种物理性质的调控 → 最终应用于柔性电子/光电子器件。_
> 
> **逻辑概览**：
> 
> 1.  **引言**：提出研究背景与应变工程的重要性。
> 2.  **方法篇**：系统阐述六种主要的应变引入方法（_晶格失配、柔性基底、图案化基底、压电基底、AFM针尖、气泡_），并对比其优缺点与应变类型。
> 3.  **性能调控篇**：详述应变对二维材料关键性质的调控作用，包括：
>     -   **声子模式**：拉曼峰位移与分裂。
>     -   **能带结构**：带隙大小改变及间接-直接带隙转变。
>     -   **非线性光学响应**：二次/三次谐波（SHG/THG）强度与偏振变化。
>     -   **电学性质**：载流子迁移率、压阻/压电效应。
>     -   **磁学性质**：非磁性材料的磁性诱导与磁性调控。
>     -   **相变**：半导体相（2H）到金属相（1T'）的转变。
> 4.  **应用篇**：聚焦两大核心应用——**柔性应变传感器**（基于压阻效应）和**柔性光探测器**（基于能带与光吸收调制）。
> 5.  **结论与展望**：总结现有进展，指出界面应变传递效率、理论预测与实验差距等关键挑战。
>

> ### 1. 研究背景
> 
> 文章开篇确立了二维材料在柔性电子学中的核心地位，并指出**应变工程**是调控其物理性质的关键策略，其优势在于二维材料原子级厚度带来的超强弹性变形能力（如石墨烯可承受25%弹性应变）和对应变的高度敏感性。
> 
> ### 2. 应变引入方法（核心贡献）
> 
> 本章是全文的方法论基础，对六种方法进行了系统阐述，其核心在于区分所产生的**应变类型（单轴/双轴，均匀/非均匀，拉伸/压缩）** 和**应变范围**。
> 
> -   **晶格失配**：通过异质结或外延生长时原子间距不匹配，在界面处产生局部、非均匀的应变。
> -   **柔性基底法**：分为三种子类：
>     -   _弹性模量失配_：通过预拉伸释放产生褶皱，形成周期性非均匀应变。
>     -   _热膨胀系数失配_：加热或降温时，基底与材料的形变差异引入均匀双轴应变。
>     -   _机械弯曲_：直接弯曲基底，产生均匀单轴应变，其应变值可由公式 `ε = τ/2R` 精确计算。
> -   **图案化基底**：将二维材料转移到预制的纳米结构（波纹、柱体、孔洞）上，形成共形接触，产生与图案相关的局域化应变。
> -   **压电基底**：通过对压电陶瓷施加电压，使其产生逆压电效应而变形，从而向材料传递可控的双轴应变。
> -   **AFM针尖**：利用原子力显微镜的纳米级探针按压悬空或附着在基底上的二维材料，产生高度局域化的应变，并可实现原位测量。
> -   **气泡法**：利用界面处聚集的污染物或通过压力差，在二维材料中形成鼓包，在鼓包顶部产生平缓的梯度应变。
> 
> ### 3. 应变调控的性能
> 
> -   **光学性质**：应变通过改变声子频率和能带结构，导致拉曼峰的**红移/蓝移与分裂**（声子模式软化/硬化），以及PL峰的**增强和红移**（带隙减小与“漏斗效应”）。二次谐波（SHG）对应变极其敏感，其偏振模式变化可用于**精确映射应变张量**，灵敏度比PL高一个数量级。
> -   **电学性质**：应变可显著提高载流子迁移率（抑制散射），产生**压阻效应**（应变改变带隙和载流子浓度，导致电阻变化，评估参数为应变系数GF）和**压电效应**（单层非中心对称材料中，应变产生极化电荷，可调制肖特基势垒）。
> -   **磁学性质**：应变不仅能在非磁性材料（如NbS₂、ReSe₂）中**诱导铁磁性**，还能调控本征二维磁体的居里温度和磁耦合状态。
> -   **相变**：拉伸应变可驱动MoTe₂等材料从半导体2H相转变为金属1T'相，伴随**电导率数个数量级的突变**，极具应用价值。
> 
> ### 4. 器件应用
> 
> -   **柔性应变传感器**：核心原理是**压阻效应**。性能由**应变系数（GF）** 衡量，GF值越高，灵敏度越高。通过结构设计（如石墨烯滑动变阻器）和栅压调控，可大幅提升GF。基于压电效应的传感器，利用极化电荷调控肖特基势垒，可获得超高GF（高达~10⁵）。
> -   **柔性光探测器**：应变通过两种机制提升性能：一是**增强光吸收**，褶皱结构增加光吸收面积，带隙窄化拓宽吸收光谱；二是**促进载流子分离**，应变诱导的能带倾斜或内置电场（源于压电效应）有效分离光生电子-空穴对，抑制复合，从而提升响应度和光电流。
> 
> ## 五、结论与贡献
> 
> **核心结论**：文章系统论证了应变工程是调控二维材料物理性质、拓展其在柔性电子领域应用的强大且多功能的策略。从精准的局部应变到可控的全局均匀应变，多样化的方法为调控不同的物理性质提供了丰富的工具箱。
> 
> **主要贡献**：
> 
> 1.  **建立了清晰的方法论分类体系**：不再简单罗列技术，而是根据物理机制（晶格失配、弹性模量失配、热膨胀失配等）对应变引入方法进行分类，并清晰对比了每种方法产生的应变类型（单轴/双轴、均匀/非均匀）、范围和优缺点。
> 2.  **构建了完整的“方法-性质-应用”知识体系**：将应变工程技术、物理性质调控机制和终端器件应用三者有机串联，为读者提供了从基础到应用的完整图景。
> 3.  **明确了领域面临的挑战与未来方向**：指出了界面应变传递效率低、理论预测与实验实现差距大、器件集成稳定性不足等关键瓶颈，为后续研究指明了方向。

> ## 七、学术思考与批判性分析
> 
> 以下问题旨在激发更深层次的思考，而非对原文的否定：
> 
> 1.  **对“直接带隙转变”的通用性反思**：文章指出应变能诱导间接带隙到直接带隙的转变，从而增强发光效率。但这一结论是否适用于所有二维半导体？其转变机制（如K点和Σ点能谷的相对移动）是否因材料而异？这种转变是否总是伴随着发光效率的显著提升，还是会因为应变引入的缺陷态猝灭而抵消？矩阵元效应（跃迁概率）在应变下的变化是否被忽略了？
> 2.  **应变传递效率的定量缺失**：文章多次提到“应变传递效率”是核心问题，但未给出一个能够定量描述或预测它的物理模型。不同方法、不同材料、不同界面结合力下的传递效率，其决定因素是什么？是范德华力的强度、界面的粗糙度还是化学键合的存在？未来的研究应致力于建立一个普适的界面应变传递模型。
> 3.  **动态应变与静态应变的差异性**：文中讨论的应用多为静态或准静态应变。然而，许多传感器（如声学传感器、振动监测器）工作在动态应变下。材料的动态应变响应（如粘弹性、阻尼）与静态响应有何不同？动态应变下的载流子弛豫、压电电荷的积累与耗散等动态过程，对器件的高速响应和频率带宽有何影响？这是一个值得探索的新维度。
> 4.  **多场耦合效应的复杂性**：文章主要关注应变单一变量的作用。但在实际器件中，应变常与电场、光照、温度等多物理场共存。例如，压电效应和光电效应的耦合产生了压电光电子学效应。这些多场耦合效应是简单的线性叠加，还是会产生协同或拮抗的非线性新现象？如何解耦并利用这些复杂效应来设计多功能器件，是一个更前沿的挑战。
> 5.  **应变工程的规模化与可重复性**：综述中展示的许多高性能器件（如AFM针尖诱导应变、超高GF传感器）都是在实验室微小样品上实现的。这些方法在推向晶圆级、大批量生产时，面临的根本性挑战是什么？例如，如何在晶圆尺度上实现均匀、可控的应变？如何保证无数次弯曲循环后，褶皱结构和界面不发生疲劳失效？这直接决定了该技术的商业化前景。

> ## 八、用户问题预测与解答
> 
> **问题1：为什么二维材料能承受的弹性应变远大于其块体材料形式？** **解答**：这主要源于**尺寸效应**和**缺陷概率**。宏观块体材料内部不可避免地存在大量微裂纹、位错等缺陷，在应力作用下，这些缺陷处会发生应力集中，导致裂纹扩展并最终断裂，因此其断裂强度远低于理论值。而二维材料，尤其是高质量单晶薄片，其原子级厚度大大降低了其内部存在缺陷的概率，使其更接近“完美晶体”，因而能够承受接近理论极限的极大弹性应变。此外，二维材料的柔韧性使其能通过面外变形（如褶皱）来弛豫应力，而块体材料则难以实现。
> 
> **问题2：论文中提到的“压阻效应”和“压电效应”核心区别是什么？对传感器设计有何不同影响？** **解答**：两者的物理本质和应用模式截然不同。
> 
> -   **压阻效应**：是指材料在**应力作用下电阻发生变化**的现象。其原理是应变改变了材料的能带结构（如带隙、有效质量），从而改变了载流子浓度和迁移率。它存在于所有半导体材料中，是一种**被动**的传感机制，需要外部电源驱动来读取电阻变化。压阻传感器的输出信号通常是连续变化的模拟量，适用于测量静态或动态应变的大小。
> -   **压电效应**：是指**非中心对称晶体**在应力作用下，内部产生电极化，从而在两端面产生符号相反的**极化电荷**的现象。这是一种**主动**的传感机制，它能将机械能直接转换为电能（或反之）。压电传感器在应变瞬间产生一个电压/电流脉冲信号，非常适合用于动态压力、振动和能量收集，并能实现自驱动传感。
> 
> **问题3：为什么拉曼光谱和光致发光谱（PL）能用来监测应变？它们各自有什么侧重？** **解答**：这两种光谱技术都是无接触、无损的应变表征利器，但探测的物理过程不同。
> 
> -   **拉曼光谱**：对应**声子（晶格振动）模式**。应变导致晶格常数和键长改变，直接影响了声子频率，表现为拉曼峰的位移。对于单轴应变，还能引起峰的劈裂。拉曼峰位移与应变大小通常呈线性关系，因此可以**定量**计算应变值。拉曼面扫（Raman mapping）可以给出**空间分辨的应变分布图**。
> -   **光致发光谱（PL）**：对应**电子能带结构**。应变改变了能带宽度和能带极值点位置，导致电子-空穴复合发光（PL）的峰位发生移动（通常是红移）。特别是，它能直观地体现**间接带隙到直接带隙的转变**，这会使PL强度急剧增加。PL的“漏斗效应”是表征非均匀应变场中激子漂移的有力证据。 **总结**：拉曼直接反映了“晶格”的应变，而PL反映了“电子结构”对应变的响应。两者结合，可从晶格和电子两个维度完整描绘应变的影响。
> 
> **问题4：文章提到多种引入应变的方法，对于一个想探索应变对新型二维材料性能影响的研究者，应如何选择最合适的方法？** **解答**：选择方法需根据研究目标进行权衡，是一个多重决策过程：
> 
> -   **若目标是快速、可逆地筛选应变效应**：优先选择**弯曲柔性基底**法。它操作简单，应变大小可连续调节，且能原位进行光谱或电学测量，是基础研究的首选。
> -   **若需要获得均匀、双轴应变**：应选择**热膨胀系数失配**或**压电基底**法。前者利用温度控制，后者利用电压控制，都能产生较高质量的双轴应变场。
> -   **若研究局域应变效应（如诱导磁性、量子点）**：**AFM针尖**和**图案化基底**是理想选择，它们能在纳米尺度上产生高度局域化的应变场。
> -   **若目标是开发高性能柔性器件原型**：**预拉伸释放形成褶皱**或**图案化柔性基底**的方法更贴近实际应用，因为它们能构建出稳定的柔性器件结构。
> -   **需要考虑的关键因素**：应变类型（单轴/双轴）、均匀性、可调性、范围、是否可逆、与微纳加工工艺的兼容性、以及设备成本（如AFM、压电系统）。
> 
> **问题5：应变工程在二维材料领域要实现商业化应用，面临的最大现实挑战是什么？** **解答**：最大的挑战是如何从实验室的原型器件走向**高可靠性、长寿命、低成本、可批量制造的商业产品**。这具体体现在：
> 
> 1.  **制造一致性**：如何在大面积晶圆上实现高度均匀、可控的应变？当前许多方法依赖于微机械剥离和手工转移，随机性大。
> 2.  **器件稳定性**：柔性器件在反复弯折、拉伸、扭转下，材料和界面会疲劳、蠕变或脱落，导致性能漂移甚至失效。封装技术是关键。
> 3.  **信号可靠性**：传感器的输出信号易受温度、湿度等环境因素干扰，如何实现高选择性和高信噪比，并开发有效的校准和补偿算法，是实际应用的前提。
> 4.  **系统集成**：将应变传感器与电源、信号处理、无线传输模块集成在单个柔性芯片上，形成完整的系统，是当前和未来的技术高地。
> 
> ## 九、专区：便于 Obsidian Dataview 插件调用
> 
> 领域基础知识:: 二维（2D）材料，如石墨烯和过渡金属硫族化合物（TMDs），因其原子级厚度而具有优异的力学、光学和电学性能。应变工程（Strain Engineering）是一种通过有意施加机械变形来改变材料晶格结构，进而调控其电子能带和物理性质的核心策略，对发展柔性电子器件至关重要。
> 
> 研究背景:: 传统的半导体应变工程受限于块体材料的脆性，应变幅度小。二维材料具有超强的弹性变形能力，对应变高度敏感，为应变工程开辟了广阔空间。然而，如何系统、可控地对二维材料施加应变，并理解应变与各种物理性质之间的内在关联，是推动其应用的关键。
> 
> 作者的问题意识:: 虽然二维材料的应变工程研究已取得大量成果，但缺乏一个从方法学、性质调控到器件应用的系统性梳理。本文旨在对现有应变引入方法进行分类和比较，系统总结应变对二维材料多种物理性质的调控机制，并展示其在柔性器件中的应用前景，最后指出面临的挑战和未来方向。
> 
> 主要研究对象:: 以石墨烯、MoS₂、WS₂、WSe₂、MoTe₂、黑磷(BP)等为代表的各类二维材料，及其在应变下的物理性质与器件应用。
> 
> 主要研究方法:: 本文为综述，系统归纳了六种主要的应变引入方法：1. 晶格失配；2. 利用柔性基底（弹性模量失配、热膨胀失配、机械弯曲）；3. 采用图案化基底；4. 使用压电基底；5. AFM探针尖加载；6. 气泡法。同时，涵盖了应变监测与表征技术，如拉曼光谱、光致发光(PL)谱、二次/三次谐波产生(SHG/THG)等。
> 
> 研究意义:: 本综述为二维材料应变工程领域提供了一个清晰、全面的知识框架。它不仅帮助研究者快速理解不同应变技术的原理和优劣，也系统梳理了应变调控材料性能的物理机制，为设计新型高性能柔性电子/光电子器件提供了理论指导和实践参考，指明了未来亟待突破的方向。
> 
> 研究结论:: 应变工程是调控二维材料性能的有效手段，可实现对其光学（带隙、光子模式）、电学（载流子迁移率、压阻/压电效应）、磁学及晶体结构相变的全方位调控。基于这些效应，高性能的柔性应变传感器和光探测器已被成功制备。然而，实现界面应变高效传递、缩小理论与实验差距、以及提升器件集成稳定性，仍是该领域应用转化的关键瓶颈。
> 
> 对领域的贡献:: 1. 建立了基于物理机制的应变引入方法分类体系，清晰对比了不同方法的优劣。2. 构建了“方法-性质-应用”的完整逻辑链条，提升了该领域知识的系统性。3. 明确指出界面应变传递效率和器件集成稳定性是未来商业化面临的核心挑战，为后续研究指明了方向。
>

> ## 八、用户问题预测与解答
> 
> **Q1：我是一个材料学新生，什么是“应变工程”？它和“掺杂”有什么区别？** **A1**：你可以把“应变”想象成“拉伸”或“挤压”一个材料，让它的原子间距发生改变。**应变工程**就是通过精确控制这种原子尺度的拉伸或挤压，来改变材料的电子结构，从而调控其性能。而“掺杂”则是通过引入外来原子替换或嵌入原本的晶格中。应变是物理方法，不改变材料化学成分，通常是可逆的；掺杂是化学方法，不可逆。两者都是调控材料性能的核心手段，但思路完全不同。
> 
> **Q2：为什么二维材料特别适合应变工程，而普通块体材料（比如硅）就不行？** **A2**：主要有两个原因。第一，**变形能力**：二维材料只有一个或几个原子层厚，就像一张纸，它可以被大幅度弯曲、拉伸而不断裂（如石墨烯可承受25%的弹性应变）。而硅是块体材料，像一块玻璃，稍微弯曲就会碎裂，通常只能承受不到1%的应变。第二，**灵敏度**：二维材料的所有原子都暴露在表面，其电子结构对应变极为敏感，微小的应变就能产生显著的性质变化。
> 
> **Q3：文章里提到了很多“拉曼谱峰红移”、“G峰分裂”，这些术语听起来很复杂，它们到底在说什么？** **A3**：可以把拉曼光谱想象成材料的“分子指纹”。当激光照射材料时，会与原子振动（声子）相互作用，产生特定能量的散射光信号，这就是拉曼峰。**“红移”** 就是指这个拉曼峰的能量降低，向低频端移动，这通常表示原子间的键被拉伸，结合力变弱。**“峰分裂”** 是指原本的一个峰，因为某个方向的拉伸打破了对称性，导致两个不同方向的振动模式不再相同，从而在光谱上表现为两个峰。通过观察这些峰的变化，我们就能像看地图一样，知道材料中哪里有应变，应变有多大，方向如何。
> 
> **Q4：文章反复提到“直接带隙”和“间接带隙”，它们对制造光电器件有什么影响？** **A4**：**带隙**是电子从束缚态变成自由态所需要的最小能量。**直接带隙**材料在发光时，电子可以直接跃迁回低能态并释放光子，效率很高，像LED和激光器就需要这种材料。**间接带隙**材料的电子跃迁需要额外“拐个弯”（吸收或释放一个声子），不能直接发光，效率极低，硅就是典型的间接带隙材料，因此它不适合做发光器件。这篇综述的关键点在于，**应变可以将一些二维材料（如双层WSe₂）从间接带隙转变为直接带隙**，使其发光效率大幅提升，极大地拓展了它们在光电器件中的应用潜力。
> 
> **Q5：这些柔性应变传感器和光电探测器，未来最可能走进我们生活的应用是什么？** **A5**：最贴近生活的应用场景是**可穿戴健康监测设备**和**电子皮肤**。例如，集成在手表或衣物中的高灵敏度应变传感器，可以实时监测脉搏、呼吸、关节运动等生理信号。而触觉传感器阵列，可以像真正的皮肤一样感知压力分布，让未来的假肢拥有触觉，或使机器人能完成更精细的操作。此外，柔性光电探测器可用于可折叠手机的全屏屏下指纹识别、柔性摄像头等，它们将重塑我们与电子设备的交互方式。

> # Abstract
> 
> 摘要
> 
> Two-dimensional (2D) materials have attracted extensive research interests due to their excellent properties related to unique structure. Strain engineering, as an important strategy for tuning the lattice and electronic structure of 2D materials, has been widely used in the modulation of physical properties, which broadens their applications in flexible nanoelectronic and optoelectronic devices. In this review, we first summarize the methods of inducing strain to 2D materials and discuss the advantages and problems of various methods. We then introduce the strain-induced effects on optical, electrical, and magnetic properties, together with the phase transition of 2D materials. Finally, we illustrate the potential applications of strained 2D materials and further look forward to their opportunities and challenges in practical applications in the future. 二维（2D）材料因其与独特结构相关的优异性能而引起了广泛的研究兴趣。应变工程作为调控二维材料晶格和电子结构的重要策略，已被广泛用于物理性质的调制，从而拓宽了其在柔性纳米电子和光电器件中的应用。在这篇综述中，我们首先总结了向二维材料引入应变的方法，并讨论了各种方法的优点和问题。然后，我们介绍了应变对二维材料光学、电学和磁学性质的影响，以及二维材料的相变。最后，我们说明了应变二维材料的潜在应用，并进一步展望了未来在实际应用中的机遇和挑战。

> # 1 | INTRODUCTION
> 
> 1 | 引言
> 
> Since graphene was successfully exfoliated in 2004,1 two-dimensional (2D) materials have become the hotspots over the past decade due to their excellent optical, electrical, magnetic, mechanical, and thermal properties.2-4 2D materials usually possess ultrahigh carrier mobility and field-effect switching ratio, which make them ideal channel materials for field-effect transistors (FETs) and digital logic transistors.5 In addition, 2D materials with appropriate and tunable direct band gap have achieved extremely efficient photon absorption, photon emission, and photoelectric conversion and then have been widely used in the field of optical and optoelectronic devices.6 Based on the characteristics of flexibility and transparency, 2D materials can be used in the emerging research areas such as transparent display screen and wearable electronics.7 Recently, in order to further meet more demands and expand the application fields of 2D materials, various techniques have been exploited to modulate their properties, including doping,8,9 alloying,10 inducing defect,11 forming van der Waals heterostructure,12,13 electrostatic regulation,14 and strain engineering.15-18 Among them, strain engineering is an effective technique that changes the lattice and electronic structure and thus modulates various properties of 2D materials. Strain engineering has been widely used in the semiconductor industry in the last few decades. For example, when the uniaxial or biaxial tensile strain is applied to the channel of a silicon transistor, the electron mobility (or hole mobility) can be greatly enhanced.19 However, traditional semiconductor bulk single crystals only withstand very limited strain, which largely limit the further application of strain modulation. Compared with bulk materials, 2D materials have stronger deformation capacity and they can withstand greater elastic strain without fracture, which show great potentials in strain engineering. For example, most graphite materials will break when the strain is up to 0.1%, while monolayer graphene can withstand 25% elastic strain.20 In addition, for traditional strain engineering, only during the process of in-plane epitaxial growth, it can obtain less than 1% of residual strain or prestrain. While there are a variety of deformation methods both in plane and out of plane to be designed for 2D materials due to the atomic-scale thickness, which means more various methods to induce strain to 2D materials. Moreover, 2D materials are more sensitive to strain and even small strains can change the lattice structure, which significantly modulate their physical properties. Strain-induced changes of various physical properties have been effectively achieved in 2D materials. In aspect of optical properties, strain can directly change the phonon structure and energy band structure of 2D materials, resulting in the shift of Raman and photoluminescence (PL) peaks.21,22 Especially, strain induces the transition from indirect to direct band gap in 2D materials, which greatly enhances luminous efficiency, making it more suitable for optoelectronic devices. As for electrical properties, proper strain amplitude can improve the performance of FETs by increasing the carrier mobility of 2D materials.23 The piezoresistive and piezoelectric effect appear in strained 2D materials, allowing their potential applications in the sensors, photodetectors, and nanogenerators.24 In addition, strain can not only modulate the magnetism of intrinsic van der Waals magnets but also induce magnetism in nonmagnetic 2D materials.25,26 Therefore, strain engineering of 2D materials greatly expands their application scope in the field of flexible devices, such as strain sensors, optoelectronic devices, and other wearable devices.27-29 In this review, we present how to induce strain to 2D materials from the lattice mismatch—the use of different substrates, such as flexible substrates, patterned substrates, and piezoelectric substrates—to the action of atomic force microscope (AFM) tip and bubbles. Meanwhile, we simply introduce some techniques of monitoring the strain distribution in strained 2D materials, including scanning tunneling microscopy (STM), high-resolution transmission electron microscopy (HRTEM), Raman and PL spectroscopy, as well as second and third harmonic generation. Then, we discuss the modulation of various properties of 2D materials based on the strain engineering, such as phonon mode, energy band, carrier mobility, piezoresistive effect, piezoelectric effect, magnetic behaviors, and phase transition. Finally, we illustrate the potential applications of strained 2D materials, including flexible strain sensors and photodetectors, and further look forward to their opportunities and challenges in practical applications in the future. 自从2004年石墨烯被成功剥离以来，1二维（2D）材料因其优异的电学、光学、磁学、力学和热学性能，在过去十年中已成为研究热点。2-4二维材料通常具有超高的载流子迁移率和场效应开关比，这使其成为场效应晶体管（FET）和数字逻辑晶体管的理想沟道材料。5此外，具有合适且可调谐直接带隙的二维材料实现了极高的光子吸收、光子发射和光电转换效率，因而被广泛应用于光学和光电子器件领域。6基于柔性和透明性的特点，二维材料可用于透明显示屏和可穿戴电子器件等新兴研究领域。7近年来，为了满足更多需求并拓展二维材料的应用领域，人们开发了各种技术来调控其性能，包括掺杂、8,9合金化、10引入缺陷、11形成范德华异质结构、12,13静电调控14以及应变工程。15-18其中，应变工程是一种改变晶格和电子结构从而调控二维材料各种性能的有效技术。应变工程在过去几十年中已广泛应用于半导体工业。例如，当对硅晶体管的沟道施加单轴或双轴拉伸应变时，电子迁移率（或空穴迁移率）可大幅提高。19然而，传统半导体体单晶只能承受非常有限的应变，这在很大程度上限制了应变调制的进一步应用。与体材料相比，二维材料具有更强的变形能力，能够承受更大的弹性应变而不断裂，这在应变工程中显示出巨大潜力。例如，大多数石墨材料在应变达到0.1%时就会断裂，而单层石墨烯可以承受25%的弹性应变。20此外，对于传统应变工程，只有在面内外延生长过程中，才能获得小于1%的残余应变或预应变。而由于二维材料具有原子级厚度，可以设计多种面内和面外的变形方法，这意味着有更多的方法向二维材料引入应变。此外，二维材料对应变更敏感，即使很小的应变也能改变其晶格结构，从而显著调控其物理性质。应变诱导的各种物理性质变化已在二维材料中有效实现。在光学性质方面，应变可直接改变二维材料的声子结构和能带结构，导致拉曼和光致发光（PL）峰位移。21,22特别地，应变可诱导二维材料从间接带隙向直接带隙转变，从而大大提高发光效率，使其更适用于光电子器件。在电学性质方面，适当的应变幅值可通过提高二维材料的载流子迁移率来改善FET性能。23压阻效应和压电效应出现在应变二维材料中，使其在传感器、光电探测器和纳米发电机中具有潜在应用。24此外，应变不仅可以调控本征范德华磁体的磁性，还可以在非磁性二维材料中诱导磁性。25,26因此，二维材料的应变工程极大地扩展了其在柔性器件领域的应用范围，如应变传感器、光电子器件和其他可穿戴器件。27-29在这篇综述中，我们介绍了如何向二维材料引入应变，从晶格失配——使用不同衬底，如柔性衬底、图案化衬底和压电衬底——到原子力显微镜（AFM）针尖和气泡的作用。同时，我们简要介绍了一些监测应变二维材料中应变分布的技术，包括扫描隧道显微镜（STM）、高分辨透射电子显微镜（HRTEM）、拉曼和PL光谱，以及二次和三次谐波产生。然后，我们讨论了基于应变工程对二维材料各种性质的调控，如声子模式、能带、载流子迁移率、压阻效应、压电效应、磁性行为和相变。最后，我们说明了应变二维材料的潜在应用，包括柔性应变传感器和光电探测器，并进一步展望了未来在实际应用中的机遇和挑战。

> # 2 | METHODS OF INDUCING STRAIN
> 
> 2 | 引入应变的方法
> 
> ## 2.1 | Lattice mismatch
> 
> 2.1 | 晶格失配
> 
> In the traditional semiconductor industry, strain is induced in bulk single crystals due to the mismatch of lattice constants between single-crystal materials and substrates in the epitaxial growth. The method of lattice mismatch can also be applied to the strain inducing of 2D materials with atomic thickness. When there is a lattice mismatch between two components of 2D heterojunctions or between 2D materials and substrates, tensile or compressive strain is induced in 2D materials. Recently, various lateral heterojunctions formed by two kinds of 2D materials with similar crystal structures but different lattice constants have been widely demonstrated, emerging large strain at the line interface. Li et al grew a monolayer WSe2–MoS2 lateral heterojunction by the method of two-step epitaxial growth and revealed the existence of both tensile and compressive strain in the epitaxial MoS2 region through the PL measurements, due to a large mismatch of lattice constants between WSe2 and MoS2.30 Moreover, Shih et al imaged the moiré pattern in the MoS2 region of in-plane WSe2–MoS2 heterojunctions by STM to further investigate the strain distributions induced by lattice mismatch.31 By analyzing the moiré pattern, 2D strain tensor of the central lattice point was considered εaa = 1.17%, εbb = −0.26%, and εab = εba = 0.69%, respectively. From the scanning transmission electron microscopy (STEM) images, two types of dislocations were found at the interface of WSe2–MoS2 lateral heterojunctions, causing the partial relief of strain and then the anisotropic strain. Strain induced by lattice mismatch is inhomogeneous, which is the largest at the center of the mismatched interface, while it decreases in the region far away from the interface. Through high-resolution microscopic characterization methods, such as STEM and STM, the lattice distortion is clearly observed, so that the strain distribution can be investigated in detail. In addition, the moiré pattern formed by lattice mismatch not only helps quantitatively describe the strain tensor but also modifies the electronic structure of 2D materials, which has aroused wide interests.32 在传统半导体工业中，由于外延生长过程中单晶材料与衬底之间晶格常数的失配，体单晶中会产生应变。晶格失配的方法也可应用于具有原子级厚度的二维材料的应变引入。当二维异质结两种组分之间或二维材料与衬底之间存在晶格失配时，二维材料中就会产生拉伸或压缩应变。最近，由两种晶格结构相似但晶格常数不同的二维材料形成的各种横向异质结已被广泛报道，在线界面处产生大应变。Li等人通过两步外延生长法生长了单层WSe2–MoS2横向异质结，并通过PL测量揭示了由于WSe2和MoS2之间晶格常数失配较大，外延MoS2区域同时存在拉伸和压缩应变。30此外，Shih等人通过STM对WSe2–MoS2面内异质结MoS2区域的莫尔花纹进行成像，进一步研究了晶格失配引起的应变分布。31通过分析莫尔花纹，中心晶格点的二维应变张量分别为εaa = 1.17%，εbb = −0.26%，εab = εba = 0.69%。从扫描透射电子显微镜（STEM）图像来看，在WSe2–MoS2横向异质结界面处发现了两种类型的位错，导致应变部分释放，从而产生各向异性应变。晶格失配引起的应变是不均匀的，在失配界面中心处最大，而在远离界面处减小。通过高分辨显微表征方法，如STEM和STM，可以清晰地观察到晶格畸变，从而详细研究应变分布。此外，由晶格失配形成的莫尔花纹不仅有助于定量描述应变张量，还能改变二维材料的电子结构，这引起了广泛的研究兴趣。32
> 
> **Lattice mismatch**: 晶格失配，两种材料之间晶格常数的差异。 **Moiré pattern**: 莫尔花纹，由于两个周期性图案叠加而产生的干涉图案。 **Strain tensor**: 应变张量，描述材料中应变状态的数学量。

> ## 2.2 | Flexible substrates
> 
> 2.2 | 柔性衬底
> 
> Due to the atomic-scale thickness, 2D materials tend to deform in the out-of-plane direction after being subjected to the external force. The deformation leads to the generation of strain in 2D materials. By selecting proper flexible substrates, strain can be induced and adjusted in 2D materials by applying the external force to the substrates. There are many kinds of flexible substrates available, such as polydimethylsiloxane (PDMS), polycarbonate (PC), polystyrene (PS), polyethylene terephthalate (PET), polyethylene naphthalate (PEN), and polyimid (PI). 由于具有原子级厚度，二维材料在受到外力作用后容易在面外方向发生变形。这种变形导致二维材料中产生应变。通过选择合适的柔性衬底，可通过对衬底施加外力来在二维材料中引入并调节应变。可用的柔性衬底种类很多，如聚二甲基硅氧烷（PDMS）、聚碳酸酯（PC）、聚苯乙烯（PS）、聚对苯二甲酸乙二醇酯（PET）、聚萘二甲酸乙二醇酯（PEN）和聚酰亚胺（PI）。

> ### 2.2.2 | Mismatch of thermal expansion
> 
> 2.2.2 | 热膨胀失配
> 
> Choosing the substrate with a thermal expansion coefficient being different from 2D materials can induce strain to 2D materials during the synthesis process of chemical vapor deposition (CVD) or epitaxial growth and the condition of local heating. Because the degrees of expansion and contraction between the substrate and 2D materials are different when the temperature changes, there exists the strong interaction among them; subsequently, the deformation of substrate can induce tensile or compressive strain in 2D materials. Recent studies indicate that 2D materials grown on a variety of rigid substrates can generate strain, such as silicon/silicon oxide (Si/SiO2), sapphire, and mica.39-42 However, this strain cannot be effectively transferred to 2D materials due to low Young's modulus of rigid substrates. Therefore, using flexible substrates with high Young's modulus can effectively induce more strain to 2D materials.43 As a result of much higher thermal expansion coefficient, some polymer substrates are prone to be heavily stretched when being heated, thus inducing tensile strain in the 2D materials attached to their surfaces. For example, a PDMS substrate has a relatively large positive thermal expansion coefficient, while the thermal expansion coefficient of MoS2 is negative. When being locally heated using a laser, the stretching of the PDMS substrate will introduce tensile strain to MoS2, as shown in Figure 1(C), (D).44 Then, a biaxial homogenous strain of 0.23% has been achieved by heating up the MoS2/PDMS sample at 150°C. Furthermore, it is important to maintain a balance between thermal expansion coefficient and Young's modulus of flexible substrates. According to the finite element analysis done by Frisenda et al, substrates with both larger thermal expansion coefficient and higher Young's modulus can effectively transform the thermal expansion of substrates into homogenous biaxial strain of 2D materials, and the calculated strain transfer efficiency is larger than 80% when heating or cooling the PC substrate with higher Young's modulus of 2.5 GPa.45 After transferring monolayer MoS2 onto the PC substrate, a variable strain in MoS2 is continuously induced from a compressive strain of −1.48% to a tensile strain of 0.48% by gradually changing the temperature of the PC substrate from −200 to 100°C. Despite the larger thermal expansion, some polymer substrates tend to shrink when being heated because of their unique mechanical properties.46 A PS substrate is a typical thermally induced shrinkable substrate, which is often used to introduce compressive strain to 2D materials. Nam et al realized well-defined 3D texturing of graphene by heating PS substrates above the glass transition temperature and achieved uniaxial, biaxial (uniform), and localized strains by controlling the boundary of PS substrates (Figure 1(E), (F)).47 It was also found that the morphology of graphene crumples was related to the deformation degree of PS substrates, which was easily tuned by changing the processing parameters. Based on the mismatched thermal expansion between the substrate and 2D materials, biaxial and homogenous strains can be induced in a controlled manner by heating or cooling the substrate. While one problem that cannot be ignored is that the induced strain will be released slowly with the decrease of temperature or long-time storage of samples. 选择热膨胀系数与二维材料不同的衬底，可以在化学气相沉积（CVD）或外延生长的合成过程以及局部加热条件下向二维材料引入应变。由于温度变化时衬底与二维材料之间的膨胀和收缩程度不同，它们之间存在强烈的相互作用；随后，衬底的变形会在二维材料中产生拉伸或压缩应变。最近的研究表明，在各种刚性衬底上生长的二维材料可产生应变，如硅/二氧化硅（Si/SiO2）、蓝宝石和云母。39-42然而，由于刚性衬底的杨氏模量较低，这种应变无法有效传递至二维材料。因此，使用高杨氏模量的柔性衬底可有效地向二维材料引入更多应变。43由于具有高得多的热膨胀系数，一些聚合物衬底在受热时容易发生大幅拉伸，从而在附着于其表面的二维材料中引入拉伸应变。例如，PDMS衬底具有相对较大的正热膨胀系数，而MoS2的热膨胀系数为负。当使用激光局部加热时，PDMS衬底的拉伸会向MoS2引入拉伸应变，如图1（C）、（D）所示。44然后，通过将MoS2/PDMS样品加热至150°C，实现了0.23%的双轴均匀应变。此外，保持柔性衬底热膨胀系数与杨氏模量之间的平衡非常重要。根据Frisenda等人进行的有限元分析，具有较大热膨胀系数和较高杨氏模量的衬底可以有效地将衬底的热膨胀转化为二维材料的均匀双轴应变，当加热或冷却具有较高杨氏模量（2.5 GPa）的PC衬底时，计算得到的应变传递效率大于80%。45将单层MoS2转移至PC衬底后，通过将PC衬底的温度从−200°C逐渐改变至100°C，可在MoS2中连续诱导从−1.48%的压缩应变到0.48%的拉伸应变的变化。尽管热膨胀较大，一些聚合物衬底在受热时因其独特的力学性能而倾向于收缩。46PS衬底是一种典型的热致收缩衬底，常用于向二维材料引入压缩应变。Nam等人通过将PS衬底加热至玻璃化转变温度以上，实现了石墨烯的明确三维纹理化，并通过控制PS衬底的边界获得了单轴、双轴（均匀）和局部应变（图1（E）、（F））。47还发现石墨烯起皱的形貌与PS衬底的变形程度相关，可通过改变工艺参数轻松调节。基于衬底与二维材料之间的热膨胀失配，可通过加热或冷却衬底以可控方式诱导双轴均匀应变。但一个不可忽视的问题是，随着温度降低或样品长时间储存，诱导的应变会缓慢释放。

> ## 2.3 | Patterned substrates
> 
> 2.3 | 图案化衬底
> 
> Transferring 2D materials to the patterned substrates is also a common way to induce strain into 2D materials due to the fluctuation of patterned surface. The surface of rigid substrates has been successfully modified through the techniques of ion-beam sputtering, photolithography, electron-beam etching, and nano self-assembly. The obtained substrates form a unique surface with a periodic ripple,53 nanocone,54,55 or nanopillar structure.56-59 By using patterned substrates, strain has been successfully introduced into many 2D materials, including graphene, MoS2, etc. For example, Molle et al used Au as a sacrificial layer to pattern SiO2/Si substrates by ion-beam sputtering and then transferred MoS2 nanosheets to the rippled SiO2/Si substrates to induce local strain in the obtained MoS2 ripples, introducing a one-directional anisotropy. In addition, one-dimensional (1D) semiconductor nanostructure arrays are applied to understand the nanopatterning of substrates, and these substrates induce periodic strain to 2D materials. Simultaneously, nanopatterned substrates and 2D materials construct the mix-dimensional hybrid heterojunctions. It was proved that the stretchable supercapacitors showed stable electrochemical performance even under 100% strain and 5000 cycles. > ## 2.4 | Piezoelectric substrates
> 
> 2.4 | 压电衬底
> 
> Piezoelectric ceramic has the ability of generating mechanical deformation when applying an excited electric field; that is, it has the inverse piezoelectric effect, which can be used as a substrate to induce strain into 2D materials. When the electric field is applied in the direction of electric polarization of piezoelectric substrates, the substrates deform in the plane perpendicular to the electric field and thus induce isotropic strain to the connected 2D materials. The positive or negative voltage determines whether the piezoelectric substrates are stretched or contracted.65 Therefore, whether the induced strain in 2D materials is tensile or compressive depends on the direction of electric field. Louca et al introduced the variable tensile strain to a ribbon-like MoTe2 single crystal by applying an external electric field to a piezoelectric stack, resulting in an anisotropic magnetoresistance being dependent on the relative direction of tensile strain and crystal orientation.66 In order to improve the strain transfer efficiency, the MoTe2 single crystal was glued on the surface of piezoelectric stack, instead of relying on the van der Waals interaction. However, the provided strain was still relatively small, with the maximum value being only 0.05%. Lau et al induced a uniform biaxial compressive strain to trilayer MoS2 by applying a bias voltage to a piezoelectric substrate ([Pb(Mg1/3Nb2/3)O3]0.7-[PbTiO3]0.3, PMN-PT) (Figure 2(H)).67 Due to ultrathin thickness and superelasticity of the graphene top electrode, the polarization induced strain from the PMN-PT substrate was better transferred to trilayer MoS2, and the induced strain was up to 0.2% under the voltage of 500 V. Compared with uniaxial strain induced by bending or prestraining flexible substrates, biaxial strain induced by using piezoelectric substrates can be modulated more precisely by controlling the applied voltage. Although this method of inducing strain is relatively simple and straightforward, there are potential safety problems due to the excessive applied voltage, which does not meet the energy-saving requirements as well. Moreover, it still remains unknown that if the interfacial interaction between piezoelectric substrate and 2D materials is strong enough to completely transfer the polarization-induced strain from the deformed substrate to the target 2D materials. How to obtain larger strain amplitude and higher strain transfer efficiency still remains a problem when inducing strain by piezoelectric substrates. 压电陶瓷具有在施加激励电场时产生机械变形的能力；即，它具有逆压电效应，可用作向二维材料引入应变的衬底。当电场沿压电衬底的电极化方向施加时，衬底在垂直于电场的平面内变形，从而对连接的二维材料引入各向同性应变。正电压或负电压决定了压电衬底是拉伸还是收缩。65因此，二维材料中诱导的应变是拉伸还是压缩取决于电场方向。Louca等人通过向压电堆叠施加外部电场，向带状MoTe2单晶引入了可变拉伸应变，导致各向异性磁阻依赖于拉伸应变与晶向的相对方向。66为了提高应变传递效率，将MoTe2单晶粘在压电堆叠表面，而不是依赖范德华相互作用。然而，提供的应变仍然相对较小，最大值仅为0.05%。Lau等人通过向压电衬底（[Pb(Mg1/3Nb2/3)O3]0.7-[PbTiO3]0.3，PMN-PT）施加偏压，向三层MoS2引入了均匀的双轴压缩应变（图2（H））。67由于石墨烯顶电极的超薄厚度和超弹性，来自PMN-PT衬底的极化诱导应变更好地传递至三层MoS2，在500 V电压下诱导应变高达0.2%。与通过弯曲或预拉伸柔性衬底引入的单轴应变相比，使用压电衬底引入的双轴应变可通过控制施加电压更精确地调控。尽管这种引入应变的方法相对简单直接，但由于施加电压过高，存在潜在的安全问题，同时也不符合节能要求。此外，压电衬底与二维材料之间的界面相互作用是否足够强，能够将极化诱导应变从变形衬底完全传递至目标二维材料，这仍然未知。如何在使用压电衬底引入应变时获得更大的应变幅值和更高的应变传递效率，仍然是一个问题。

> **Inverse piezoelectric effect**: 逆压电效应，施加电场导致压电材料产生机械变形的现象。 **Magnetoresistance**: 磁阻，材料电阻随外加磁场变化的现象。

> ## 3.1 | Phonon mode
> 
> 3.1 | 声子模式
> 
> The phonon structure of 2D materials will be changed due to the distortion of lattice structure caused by uniaxial or biaxial strain. Generally, the tensile strain leads to the softening of phonon mode, while compressive strain is the opposite. Raman spectroscopy is an effective method to investigate the change of phonon mode, which can be shown in the splitting and shifting of Raman peaks. Ferrari et al investigated the optical phonon changes of monolayer graphene under uniaxial strain by Raman spectroscopy and found that uniaxial tensile strain resulted in the red shifts of G and 2D peaks of graphene.50 Furthermore, the G peak split into G+ and G− sub-bands due to the split of doubly degenerate E2g phonons when the applied strain was over 0.6%, which represented the vibration modes perpendicular and parallel to the strain direction, respectively (Figure 4(A)). In other studies, the 2D peak of monolayer graphene also splits into two peaks, 2D+ and 2D−, both of them undergo the red shift with increasing strain, and the red shift value depends on the strain direction.86 The first-principles calculations show that the reason for the splitting of 2D double-resonance Raman scattering peak is the combine effect of distorted Dirac cones and TO phonon dispersion anisotropy caused by uniaxial strain. In addition to graphene, research on the strain-related Raman spectroscopy of other 2D materials such as transition metal sulfides or selenides has also been extensively done. Bolotin et al studied the evolution of Raman peaks of monolayer MoS2 under uniaxial strain and revealed that the A0 peak showed almost no shift in position, while the E0 peak split into E0+ and E0− due to the breaking of crystal symmetry (Figure 4(B)).51 Berry et al carried out the Raman mapping on the wrinkled and flat positions of a MoS2 flake and found that the red shifts of E¹₂g and A1g peaks only appeared in the wrinkled position (Figure 4(C), (D)).87 It was also observed that the A1g mode was less sensitive to the strain than E¹₂g mode, which meant that the Raman peak of out-of-plane vibration mode was less sensitive to the strain response than that of in-plane vibration mode when applying the out-of-plane strain. In addition, for anisotropic 2D materials, uniaxial strains along different crystal orientations have different effects on their Raman peaks. Taking black phosphorus (BP) for example, Lau et al discovered that uniaxial tensile strain along its zigzag (ZZ) direction caused the red shifts of A²g and B2g Raman peaks, but it had no effect on the A¹g peak, yet the A¹g and B2g Raman peaks got redshifted, while the A²g peak barely shifted when the strain was applied along the armchair (AC) direction.88 According to the density functional perturbation theory calculations, the anisotropic Raman responses originated from the changes of both bond lengths and bond angles in the strained BP flakes. This proves that the uniaxial strain can selectively adjust the out-of-plane and in-plane vibration modes of BP, which provides guidance for the application of anisotropic flexible electronic devices.89 Based on the strain-induced phonon mode of 2D materials, Raman spectroscopy has been widely used in monitoring the magnitude and distribution of strain by measuring the strain-sensitive and Raman-active modes of strained 2D materials. The frequency of Raman peaks changes linearly with the strain, which means the magnitude of strain in 2D materials can be quantified through the shifts of Raman peaks of a certain vibration mode. Moreover, Raman mapping offers a straightforward way to monitor the inhomogeneous strain distribution. 由于单轴或双轴应变引起的晶格结构畸变，二维材料的声子结构会发生改变。通常，拉伸应变导致声子模式软化，而压缩应变则相反。拉曼光谱是研究声子模式变化的有效方法，可通过拉曼峰的分裂和位移来体现。Ferrari等人通过拉曼光谱研究了单层石墨烯在单轴应变下的光学声子变化，发现单轴拉伸应变导致石墨烯的G和2D峰发生红移。50此外，当施加应变超过0.6%时，由于双重简并E2g声子的分裂，G峰分裂为G+和G−子带，分别代表垂直于和平行于应变方向的振动模式（图4（A））。在其他研究中，单层石墨烯的2D峰也分裂为两个峰，2D+和2D−，二者均随应变增加而发生红移，且红移值取决于应变方向。86第一性原理计算表明，2D双共振拉曼散射峰分裂的原因是由单轴应变引起的扭曲狄拉克锥和TO声子色散各向异性的共同作用。除了石墨烯，其他二维材料如过渡金属硫化物或硒化物的应变相关拉曼光谱研究也广泛开展。Bolotin等人研究了单层MoS2在单轴应变下拉曼峰的演变，揭示A0峰的位置几乎无位移，而E0峰由于晶体对称性破缺分裂为E0+和E0−（图4（B））。51 Berry等人在MoS2薄片的褶皱和平坦位置进行拉曼成像，发现E¹₂g和A1g峰的红移仅出现在褶皱位置（图4（C）、（D））。87还观察到A1g模式对应变的敏感性低于E¹₂g模式，这意味着当施加面外应变时，面外振动模式的拉曼峰对应变响应的敏感性低于面内振动模式。此外，对于各向异性二维材料，沿不同晶向的单轴应变对其拉曼峰有不同的影响。以黑磷（BP）为例，Lau等人发现沿其锯齿形（ZZ）方向的单轴拉伸应变导致A²g和B2g拉曼峰红移，但对A¹g峰无影响；而当应变沿扶手椅形（AC）方向施加时，A¹g和B2g拉曼峰发生红移，而A²g峰几乎无位移。88根据密度泛函微扰理论计算，各向异性拉曼响应源于应变BP薄片中键长和键角的变化。这证明单轴应变可以选择性地调节BP的面外和面内振动模式，为各向异性柔性电子器件的应用提供了指导。89基于应变诱导的二维材料声子模式，拉曼光谱已广泛用于通过测量应变二维材料中应变敏感和拉曼活性模式来监测应变的幅度和分布。拉曼峰的频率随应变线性变化，这意味着可通过特定振动模式拉曼峰的位移来量化二维材料中的应变幅度。此外，拉曼成像提供了一种直接的方式来监测不均匀应变分布。
> 
> **Raman spectroscopy**: 拉曼光谱，利用单色光照射样品时产生的非弹性散射光来分析物质结构的技术。 **Red shift**: 红移，光谱峰向长波长（低频率）方向移动。 **First-principles calculations**: 第一性原理计算，基于量子力学基本原理，不依赖经验参数的计算方法。 **Density functional perturbation theory**: 密度泛函微扰理论，基于密度泛函理论计算材料对微扰响应的理论。

> ## 3.2 | Energy band
> 
> 3.2 | 能带
> 
> Strain can significantly change the electronic structure of 2D materials and thus increases or decreases the band gap.90,91 Javey et al demonstrated that there was a drastic enhancement of PL intensity and a slight red shift of PL peak position for the bilayer WSe2 under uniaxial homogeneous tensile strain (Figure 4(E)).90 Density functional theory (DFT) calculations showed that the conduction band minima changed from K to Σ points in the Brillouin zone, indicating a transition from an indirect band gap to a direct band gap when applying uniaxial strain, which was consistent with the experimental results. The strain-induced transition between indirect gap and direct gap is also observed in other 2D materials both in experiment and calculation, including MoS2,91 WS2,92 antimonene,93 BP,94 and so on. In addition, for nonhomogeneous strain caused by wrinkles or bubbles, compared with the unstrained region, the PL spectroscopy in the strained region shows increased intensity and red-shifted peak position, which originates from the “funnel effect”.22,34 As shown in Figure 4(F), when the laser irradiates on a wrinkled MoS2 flake, photogenerated excitons drift to the wrinkle region with the maximum strain which has the narrowest band gap and then recombine here, resulting in the red shift of PL peak position.22 Beyond that, the modulation of band gap by strain is related to the layer number of 2D materials.49,51,95 Bolotin et al reported the band gap structure changes of monolayer and bilayer MoS2 under uniaxial tensile strain and found that when applying 1% tensile strain, the band gap of monolayer MoS2 decreased by 45 meV, whereas that of bilayer MoS2 decreased by 120 meV.51 Yan et al studied the evolution of energy band structure of few-layer BP (2–10 layers) with in-plane biaxial strain by infrared (IR) spectroscopy.95 The results showed that the shift of IR peak position was linear with the applied strain, and the rate of shift was obviously exponential and dependent on the number of layers; that is, less layers corresponded to the smaller rate of IR peak shifts, which was completely consistent with the calculation results. This dependence was closely related to the interlayer van der Waals interaction caused by the strain, indicating that the in-plane tensile strain weakened the interlayer interaction due to the in-plane puckered crystal structure of BP. 应变可以显著改变二维材料的电子结构，从而增大或减小带隙。90,91 Javey等人证明，在单轴均匀拉伸应变下，双层WSe2的PL强度显著增强，PL峰位置发生轻微红移（图4（E））。90密度泛函理论（DFT）计算表明，施加单轴应变时，导带最小值从布里渊区的K点变为Σ点，表明从间接带隙转变为直接带隙，这与实验结果一致。应变诱导的间接带隙与直接带隙之间的转变在实验和计算中也在其他二维材料中观察到，包括MoS2、91 WS2、92锑烯、93 BP94等。此外，对于由褶皱或气泡引起的非均匀应变，与未应变区域相比，应变区域的PL光谱显示出增强的强度和红移的峰位置，这源于“漏斗效应”。22,34如图4（F）所示，当激光照射在褶皱的MoS2薄片上时，光生激子漂移到具有最大应变的褶皱区域，该区域带隙最窄，然后在此复合，导致PL峰位置红移。22此外，应变对带隙的调控与二维材料的层数有关。49,51,95 Bolotin等人报道了单轴拉伸应变下单层和双层MoS2的带隙结构变化，发现施加1%拉伸应变时，单层MoS2的带隙减小45 meV，而双层MoS2的带隙减小120 meV。51 Yan等人通过红外（IR）光谱研究了少层BP（2–10层）在面内双轴应变下能带结构的演变。95结果表明，IR峰位置的位移与施加应变呈线性关系，且位移速率明显呈指数关系并依赖于层数；即层数越少，IR峰位移速率越小，这与计算结果完全一致。这种依赖性与应变引起的层间范德华相互作用密切相关，表明面内拉伸应变因BP的面内褶皱晶体结构而削弱了层间相互作用。
> 
> **Density functional theory (DFT)**: 密度泛函理论，一种研究多电子体系电子结构的量子力学方法。 **Exciton**: 激子，半导体中由库仑相互作用束缚在一起的电子-空穴对。 **Funnel effect**: 漏斗效应，由于应变梯度导致激子向带隙最窄区域漂移并复合的现象。

> ## 3.3 | Nonlinear optical response
> 
> 3.3 | 非线性光学响应
> 
> Recently, 2D materials have been proved to exhibit various interesting nonlinear optical properties, including second-harmonic generation (SHG) and third-harmonic generation (THG). SHG is a nonlinear optical process described by the second-order susceptibility tensor, usually observed in materials with broken inversion symmetry, such as monolayer or odd-layer transition metal dichalcogenides (TMDs),96 and CrOCl.97 SHG is sensitive to the lattice symmetry of materials, and the intensity can be greatly influenced by strain because strain will distort the lattice, thus changing the optical susceptibility of 2D materials. Meanwhile, the direction of applied strain can also be indicated by the polarization-resolved SHG. Lu et al transferred monolayer MoS2 to the Si/SiO2 substrate spin-coated with TiO2 nanowire (NW) suspension, thus inducing strain in MoS2 through the formation of wrinkles.98 By analyzing the polarization-resolved SHG signal, it was found that there existed a strong anisotropic enhancement of SHG in the hybrid structure between monolayer MoS2 and 1D TiO2 NW, and the anisotropy mainly depended on the relatively direction between MoS2 and TiO2 NW. When TiO2 NW was along the AC direction of MoS2, the strain-induced deformation of AC axis was larger than that of ZZ axis, which led to the enhancement of SHG perpendicular to NW being less than that parallel to NW, resulting in a large anisotropic SHG signal finally. Liu et al investigated the evolution of SHG intensity of monolayer MoSe2 under different uniaxial tensile strains generated by bending the mica substrate.99 Because monolayer MoSe2 belongs to the D3h point group, it has strong second-order nonlinear optical response. The intensity of SHG signal decreases linearly with the tensile strain, implying that the strain amplitude can be directly monitored by the SHG intensity. Moreover, the relative change of SHG intensity with strain is up to 0.49 ± 0.05/ε, which is nearly one order of magnitude larger than that of PL peak intensity, showing that SHG is a more effective and sensitive technique to monitor the strain in 2D materials. In addition, the strain direction can be displayed through the evolution of polarization-resolved SHG pattern. Without strain, the intensity of SHG shows six same petals when the polarization of SHG is parallel or perpendicular to the incident polarization, thus the total SHG intensity has a circular shape. When the strain is applied, the SHG intensity decreases in each petal, which leads to the asymmetric shape of six petals, resulting in a strong angular dependence of the total SHG intensity, and thus the minor axis of polarization-resolved SHG intensity can indicate the strain direction. 近年来，二维材料已被证明表现出各种有趣的非线性光学性质，包括二次谐波产生（SHG）和三次谐波产生（THG）。SHG是一种由二阶极化率张量描述的非线性光学过程，通常出现在具有破缺反演对称性的材料中，如单层或奇数层过渡金属二硫属化物（TMDs）96和CrOCl。97 SHG对材料的晶格对称性敏感，其强度会受到应变的极大影响，因为应变会扭曲晶格，从而改变二维材料的光学极化率。同时，施加应变的方向也可以通过偏振分辨SHG来指示。Lu等人将单层MoS2转移至旋涂有TiO2纳米线（NW）悬浮液的Si/SiO2衬底上，从而通过形成褶皱在MoS2中引入应变。98通过分析偏振分辨SHG信号，发现单层MoS2与一维TiO2 NW之间的混合结构中存在强烈的SHG各向异性增强，且各向异性主要取决于MoS2与TiO2 NW之间的相对方向。当TiO2 NW沿MoS2的AC方向时，AC轴的应变诱导变形大于ZZ轴，导致垂直于NW的SHG增强小于平行于NW的增强，最终产生大的各向异性SHG信号。Liu等人研究了通过弯曲云母衬底产生的不同单轴拉伸应变下单层MoSe2的SHG强度演变。99由于单层MoSe2属于D3h点群，其具有强的二阶非线性光学响应。SHG信号强度随拉伸应变线性减小，意味着应变幅值可直接通过SHG强度监测。此外，SHG强度随应变的相对变化高达0.49 ± 0.05/ε，几乎比PL峰强度的变化大一个数量级，表明SHG是监测二维材料应变更有效且更灵敏的技术。此外，应变方向可通过偏振分辨SHG图案的演变来显示。无应变时，当SHG偏振平行或垂直于入射偏振时，SHG强度呈现六个相同的花瓣，因此总SHG强度呈圆形。当施加应变时，每个花瓣的SHG强度减弱，导致六个花瓣形状不对称，使得总SHG强度具有强烈的角度依赖性，因此偏振分辨SHG强度的短轴可指示应变方向。
> 
> **Second-harmonic generation (SHG)**: 二次谐波产生，两个光子相互作用产生一个频率加倍的光子的非线性光学过程。 **Third-harmonic generation (THG)**: 三次谐波产生，三个光子相互作用产生一个频率三倍的光子的非线性光学过程。 **Transition metal dichalcogenides (TMDs)**: 过渡金属二硫属化物，化学式为MX₂（M=Mo, W等；X=S, Se, Te）的层状半导体材料。

> **Photoelastic effect**: 光弹效应，材料在应力作用下光学性质发生变化的现象。 **Photoelastic tensor**: 光弹张量，描述光弹效应的张量。

> ## 3.4 | Electrical properties
> 
> 3.4 | 电学性质
> 
> ### 3.4.1 | Carrier mobility
> 
> 3.4.1 | 载流子迁移率
> 
> When 2D materials are used in FETs, two conditions need to be satisfied, that is, proper band gap and high carrier mobility. Theoretical studies have demonstrated that strain in 2D materials can not only influence the band gap but also change the carrier mobility.102,103 In some cases, strain can significantly increase the carrier mobility of 2D materials. Berry et al obtained a few-layer MoS2 flake with wrinkles by micromechanical exfoliation and fabricated the MoS2 FETs in the wrinkled and flat regions, respectively.87 The transfer curves of corresponding devices at different temperatures are shown in Figure 5(A), (B). It is observed that the carrier mobility of the wrinkled device (μw = 5.55 cm² V⁻¹ s⁻¹) is about three times that of the flat device (μf = 1.42 cm² V⁻¹ s⁻¹) at 30 K because the tensile strain in MoS2 wrinkles suppresses the electron–phonon coupling and reduces the lattice scattering and thus increases the carrier mobility. Garaj et al fabricated MoS2 FETs on the Si substrate with a crest-SiNx (c-SiNx) dielectric layer (Figure 5(C)) and found that the mobility of c-SiNx MoS2 FETs was much higher than that of ordinary MoS2 FETs with a SiO2 dielectric layer (Figure 5(D)), reflecting the roughly positive correlation between the carrier mobility and the substrate roughness.104 Theoretical calculations indicated that the strain fluctuations of the rough c-SiNx dielectric layer reduced the band gap and carrier effective mass of MoS2, thus significantly improving the electrical properties and the carrier mobility. However, strain also sometimes has negative impact on the carrier mobility of 2D materials. For example, few-layer SnSe has a large anisotropic ratio of carrier mobility, and monolayer SnSe is a nearly direct band gap semiconductor with high electron mobility of 10³ cm² V⁻¹ s⁻¹ at zero strain.105,106 Under small tensile strain, the mobility of monolayer SnSe reaches up to 10⁴ cm² V⁻¹ s⁻¹, whereas it will reduce to 300 cm² V⁻¹ s⁻¹ once the strain is over 8%. This phenomenon is attributed to the combine effect of strain on the effective mass and the elastic modulus. Small strain effectively decreases the effective mass and increases the elastic modulus, leading to an obvious enhancement of carrier mobility, but the opposite often occurs for the larger strain. In addition, strain can achieve the modulation of anisotropic free-carrier mobility of 2D materials. Yang et al found that the preferred conducting direction of monolayer BP was rotated by 90° under 3–6% uniaxial or biaxial strain according to the first-principles simulations.107 For intrinsic monolayer BP, the electron mobility along the AC direction is as high as 10⁵ cm² V⁻¹ s⁻¹, while that along the ZZ direction is very low. When applying uniaxial strain (5–6%) or biaxial strain (3–4%), the electron mobility along the corresponding direction decreases (AC) or increases (ZZ) dramatically. >     -   **缺点：** 应力分布不均匀，主要集中在两种材料的交界处。
