> ## 一、引言
> 
> 随着人工智能、物联网等数据密集型应用的爆发式增长，传统计算机体系结构（冯·诺依曼架构）中处理器与存储器之间的数据传输瓶颈，即所谓的 **“存储墙”（Memory Wall）** 问题日益严峻。这极大地限制了计算系统的能效和吞吐量。为突破此瓶颈，**神经形态计算（Neuromorphic Computing）** 应运而生，它通过模仿人脑的并行、事件驱动工作模式，有望实现能效的数量级提升。实现这一新型计算范式的核心在于开发能够模拟生物**突触可塑性（Synaptic Plasticity）** 的非易失性存储器技术。
> 
> 传统的钙钛矿型铁电材料（如PZT）虽性能优异，但在与硅基**互补金属氧化物半导体（CMOS）** 工艺兼容以及微缩至纳米尺度时面临严重挑战。2011年，**铪基铁电（Hafnium-Based Ferroelectrics, Hf-FEs）** 材料的发现成为该领域的转折点。其最大的优势在于与CMOS工艺的完美兼容和卓越的微缩能力，为实现后摩尔时代的高性能、低功耗“存算一体”及神经形态芯片提供了革命性的材料基础。
> 
> 本文是一篇全面而深入的综述，旨在系统梳理Hf-FEs从材料物理、器件结构到神经形态系统集成的完整知识链条，为读者理解其颠覆性潜力和面临的关键挑战提供了清晰的路线图。其核心价值在于：它不仅汇总了现有成果，更构建了一个从基础原理到前沿应用的逻辑框架，是初学者和研究者快速把握该领域全貌的绝佳指南。
>

> 2.  **基础物理与器件（第二部分）**：系统阐述Hf-FEs的**材料体系（掺杂工程）**、**器件结构（FeFET、FTJ、FeRAM、Fe-Diode）**、**物理机制（相变与极化翻转）** 及**关键性能指标**。这是整篇论文的理论基石，回答了“是什么”和“为什么好”的问题。

> 
> 本部分将遵循“背景 → 问题 → 方法/模型 → 关键发现”的逻辑链，对原文核心内容进行重组和降维解读。

> ### 4.1 研究背景与动机
> 
> -   **核心问题**：传统冯·诺依曼计算架构的“存储墙”瓶颈导致数据搬运的能耗和延迟远超计算本身，无法满足AI时代对算力和能效的爆炸式需求。
> -   **解决方案**：神经形态计算，通过模仿人脑的存算一体模式，从根本上消除数据搬运。
> -   **关键使能技术**：寻找一种能与CMOS工艺兼容、可高度微缩、且能模拟突触功能的新型非易失性存储器。
> 
> ### 4.2 核心方法/模型——Hf-FEs技术栈
> 
> Hf-FEs之所以能成为上述挑战的完美答案，是因为其背后有一套完整的技术栈。
> 
> #### 4.2.1 材料基础：掺杂工程
> 
> HfO₂本体的稳定相并非铁电相。其铁电性源于一种亚稳态的**极性正交相（o-phase, Pca21）**。为了稳定这一关键相，科研人员采用了**掺杂工程**，其作用机制可类比为_用不同大小的支架撑起一个不稳定的帐篷_。
> 
> -   **锆（Zr）掺杂（HZO体系）**：由于Zr和Hf化学性质极为相似，Zr掺杂成为主流选择。它能在较宽的温度范围（400-600°C）和浓度（可达50%）下稳定o相，工艺窗口宽，适合大规模生产。
> -   **镧（La）掺杂**：La的离子半径大，稳定o相的效果最强，可实现高达**55 μC/cm²的2Pr**，且能耐受800°C高温，适合后端工艺（BEOL）集成。
> -   **铝（Al）掺杂**：Al离子半径小，通过引入压应力来稳定o相，特别适用于超薄薄膜。
> -   **共掺杂策略**：这是更高阶的“材料设计”，通过协同效应实现1+1>2的效果。例如，La-Al共掺杂中，La抑制氧空位迁移提高耐久性，而Al则促进氧空位生成稳定铁电相，两者协同实现了极化强度和耐疲劳性的双重提升。
> 
> #### 4.2.2 器件架构：四大主流
> 
> 基于Hf-FEs薄膜，演化出多种器件结构，各有侧重：
> 
> -   **铁电场效应晶体管（FeFET）**：三端器件，通过在晶体管栅极中集成铁电层，利用极化翻转来调制阈值电压，实现“0”和“1”状态。优点是结构简单、与CMOS工艺高度兼容，可以实现高开关比（如3.5×10⁶）。它像是一个**自带记忆的开关**。
> -   **铁电隧道结（FTJ）**：两端器件，铁电层作为超薄势垒。极化翻转改变势垒形状，从而改变量子隧穿电阻，实现高低阻态。优点是速度快（可达500 ps）、功耗低，但开关比（ON/OFF ratio）通常较低。它像一个**可调节电阻的隧道**。
> -   **铁电随机存取存储器（FeRAM）**：基于**铁电电容（FeCAP）** 的1T-1C（1个晶体管-1个电容）结构。通过电容的极化存储数据，读写速度快，耐久性极高（>10¹²次循环），是一种非常成熟的存储技术。
> -   **铁电二极管（Fe-Diode）**：两端器件，通过极化翻转调制肖特基势垒高度，实现整流方向和电流大小的改变。其最大的优势在于**自选择特性**，能有效抑制交叉阵列中的潜行电流，无需额外的选择器，非常适合超高密度集成。
> 
> #### 4.2.3 物理机制：铁电性的起源与翻转
> 
> -   **起源之争**：铁电性在HfO₂中的起源尚无定论，主流理论包括：
>     1.  **声子-能带驱动机制**：认为晶格动力学中的“软模”导致晶格失稳，是铁电相形成的内在原因。
>     2.  **缺陷介导机制**：强调**氧空位（VO²⁺）** 的关键作用，其聚集和迁移可以稳定极性相并辅助极化翻转。这解释了实验中观察到的“唤醒”和“疲劳”效应。
>     3.  **界面/尺寸约束机制**：认为在纳米尺度下，界面能和表面能主导了相的稳定性，使得在块体材料中不稳定的o相得以稳定存在。
> -   **翻转机制**：极化翻转并非铁电畴的统一运动，而是通过**畴壁（DW）** 的移动和氧离子的迁移实现的。研究发现，HfO₂中存在极低能量势垒（73 meV）的翻转路径，这解释了其超快的开关速度。
> 
> ### 4.3 主要实验结果与讨论
> 
> #### 4.3.1 模拟突触可塑性
> 
> Hf-FEs器件的核心价值在于其多级、非易失的极化状态，可以完美映射为神经网络的**突触权重**。
> 
> -   **长时程增强/抑制（LTP/LTD）**：通过施加连续的正向或负向脉冲，可以渐进式地增加或减小器件的电导，模拟学习过程。Hf-FEs器件表现出高耐久性（>10⁶次更新）和低功耗（如245 fJ/spike）的特点。
> -   **脉冲时序依赖可塑性（STDP）**：当施加给器件的电脉冲模拟前后神经元的尖峰信号时，其电导变化量取决于两个脉冲到达的相对时间差，这是无监督学习的基础。
> 
> #### 4.3.2 构建神经形态系统
> 
> 基于单个突触器件，可以构建大规模的交叉阵列，实现高效的**存内计算（In-Memory Computing）**。
> 
> -   **向量-矩阵乘法（VMM）**：在交叉阵列中，输入电压向量与存储为电导矩阵的权重，通过欧姆定律和基尔霍夫定律，在物理上瞬时完成VMM。这是一种高度并行的模拟计算，能耗极低。
> -   **网络实现**：论文展示了基于Hf-FEs的**单层感知机（SLP）**、**卷积神经网络（CNN）**、**脉冲神经网络（SNN）** 等，在手写数字识别（MNIST）等任务中取得了接近理想软件的准确率，但在能效上提升了数个数量级。
>

> ## 六、未来发展方向展望
> 
> 文章指出，尽管Hf-FEs取得了巨大进展，但仍有诸多挑战需要克服，这些挑战也指明了未来的研究方向：
> 
> 1.  **基础机理的深化研究**：铁电相（o相）的原子级稳定路径和极化翻转的动力学机制仍不明确，需要借助**高分辨透射电镜（TEM）** 和第一性原理计算等手段进行更深入的探索。
> 2.  **超低热预算工艺**：目前HfO₂铁电相的结晶化需要超过450°C的退火，与先进CMOS后端工艺（<400°C）的热预算存在冲突。发展**等离子体增强原子层沉积（PE-ALD）**、超快退火等低温工艺是走向集成的关键。
> 3.  **三维（3D）堆叠集成**：为了进一步提升存储密度，利用Hf-FEs与**原子层沉积（ALD）** 技术的兼容性，实现如3D FeNAND和3D交叉阵列的垂直堆叠是必然趋势，这对保形沉积和界面控制提出了更高要求。
> 4.  **器件可靠性与均匀性**：“唤醒”效应、疲劳、印迹效应以及器件间的差异性，是制约大规模阵列性能的瓶颈。通过**界面工程**、**共掺杂策略**和**电路级补偿**（如CAMR电路）来协同优化是解决之道。
> 5.  **多功能融合与系统协同设计**：Hf-FEs的应用不应局限于单一存储或计算。未来将探索其压电、热释电、磁电耦合等特性，实现“传感-存储-计算”一体化。同时，需要从材料、器件、电路到算法进行跨层次的协同设计，以充分发挥其潜力。

> ## 八、用户问题预测与解答
> 
> 基于以上解读，我们预测以下延伸问题并给出系统性解答：
> 
> **Q1: 为什么说Hf-FEs是“CMOS兼容”的，这一点为何如此重要？** **A1:** “CMOS兼容”意味着Hf-FEs材料（如HfO₂）和制备工艺（如原子层沉积ALD）可以无缝集成到现有的、价值数十亿美元的硅基芯片制造工厂中。这包括：1）HfO₂本身就是现代晶体管中常用的高k栅介质材料；2）制备温度相对可控；3）材料能耐受后续工艺中的高温和氢气氛围。其重要性在于，它极大地降低了新技术从实验室走向大规模量产的“死亡之谷”风险。不像许多新材料需要建立全新的、昂贵的生产线，Hf-FEs可以借用成熟的CMOS平台，从而实现快速、低成本的产业化。
> 
> **Q2: 什么是“唤醒效应”和“疲劳效应”，它们对器件有何影响？** **A2:** 这是铁电器件中常见的可靠性问题。
> 
> -   **唤醒效应（Wake-up Effect）**：指铁电器件在初始的电场循环中，剩余极化强度（Pr）逐渐增大的现象。这通常是因为初始状态下，一些铁电畴被缺陷钉扎，随着电场循环，这些缺陷（主要是氧空位）重新分布，释放了被钉扎的畴，导致可翻转的极化强度增加。这就像_新机器需要磨合才能达到最佳性能_，但对于要求状态恒定的存储器来说，这是一个不稳定的因素。
> -   **疲劳效应（Fatigue Effect）**：指器件在经历大量（如10⁶-10¹²次）读写循环后，可翻转的极化强度（2Pr）逐渐下降，存储窗口缩小，最终导致器件失效。主流解释是，在反复极化翻转过程中，氧空位在电极/铁电界面处积累，形成“死层”或对铁电畴产生更强的钉扎，阻碍了极化翻转。这就像_反复弯折的铁丝最终会因金属疲劳而断裂_。
> 
> **Q3: FeFET和FTJ作为突触器件，各自的优缺点是什么？** **A3:** 两者都是主要的候选器件，但性能侧重点不同：
> 
> -   **FeFET（三端器件）**：优点是**高开关比**，信号读取更容易，不易出错；有独立的读写路径，电路设计更灵活，能效更高。缺点是结构相对复杂，微缩到先进节点时，短沟道效应等挑战更大；其“读”操作本质上是对沟道电阻的读取，存在一定的扰动。
> -   **FTJ（两端器件）**：优点是**结构简单**，可以做成极小的“十字交叉”点，是实现超高密度3D集成（如ReRAM那样）的理想选择；开关速度极快。缺点是**开关比通常较低**，这给外围读出电路的设计带来了巨大挑战，容易造成误读；在无源交叉阵列中，读写容易受到周围未选通单元的“潜行电流”干扰。
> 
> **Q4: 论文中提到的“存内计算”是怎么实现的？它好在哪里？** **A4:** “存内计算”的核心思想是让数据在存储的地方被直接处理，而不是搬来搬去。
> 
> -   **实现方式**：在Hf-FEs交叉阵列中，神经网络的权重被编码为阵列中特定位置器件的电导值。当输入信号（电压）加到阵列的行线上时，根据欧姆定律（I=VG），每条列线上的电流就是流过该列所有器件的电流之和。这个求和过程恰好完成了输入向量（V）和权重矩阵（G）的“乘加运算（MAC）”，而这正是神经网络最核心的计算任务。
> -   **优点**：1）**极高能效**：避免了数据在处理器和存储器之间频繁搬运，而数据搬运的能耗是计算本身的数倍至数千倍。2）**高度并行**：一个交叉阵列可以在一个时钟周期内完成整个矩阵的乘法运算，计算速度极快。
> 
> **Q5: 作为一个初学者，如果我想进入这个领域，应该从哪里开始？** **A5:** 这是一个非常好的问题。建议遵循以下学习路径：
> 
> 1.  **基础概念**：首先，弄懂**铁电性**的基本概念（极化、电滞回线P-E Loop、矫顽场、居里温度）和**MOSFET**（金属-氧化物-半导体场效应晶体管）的基本工作原理。
> 2.  **核心文献**：精读这篇综述（Nano-Micro Lett. 2026）和文中引用的几篇关键里程碑文献，如2011年Böscke等人首次发现HfO₂铁电性的经典论文，以及涉及HfO₂相变动力学的文章。
> 3.  **聚焦关键器件**：从FeFET入手，理解其结构、工作原理、性能指标（记忆窗口、开关比、耐久性、保持性）以及它与传统MOSFET的联系和区别。这是最直观、最容易与已有知识建立连接的结构。
> 4.  **动手实践（如果条件允许）**：尝试使用TCAD等半导体器件仿真工具，仿真一个简单的FeFET，观察极化翻转如何影响其电学特性。
> 5.  **追踪前沿**：关注该领域的顶级会议和期刊，如**IEDM， VLSI Technology**， 《Nature Electronics》等，了解最新的研究动态。

> #### Q1: 什么是“铁电”，它和普通电容器里的“电介质”有什么区别？
> 
> **A:** 简单来说，**铁电材料**是一种具有“记忆效应”的特殊电介质。普通电介质在外加电场下会产生极化，电场一撤，极化就消失了。而铁电材料的极化状态在电场撤去后依然能稳定存在，相当于一个“永电体”。这个特性使得它可以用来存储信息（极化向上=1，向下=0），而普通电容器只能用来暂存电荷。
> 
> #### Q2: 为什么HfO₂的“CMOS兼容性”如此重要？它具体指什么？
> 
> **A:** “CMOS兼容性”意味着这种新材料可以直接在现有的、成熟的芯片制造工厂里使用，而不需要建设昂贵的新生产线。这主要包括两点：1）**材料兼容**：HfO₂和掺杂元素（如Zr、Si）都是半导体工业大量使用的标准材料，不会污染设备。2）**工艺兼容**：它能承受芯片制造后端（BEOL）中高达400°C以上的高温退火工艺，性能不会失效。这一特性使得Hf-FEs的产业化成本极低，是实现商业化的巨大优势。
> 
> #### Q3: 论文中提到的“FeFET”、“FTJ”、“FeRAM”这么多器件，我该怎么简单区分它们？
> 
> **A:** 您可以这样理解：
> 
> -   **FeFET（铁电场效应晶体管）**：像一个**水龙头**。铁电栅极是把手，控制着源极和漏极之间的水流（电流）。把手的位置（极化状态）水龙头关闭（逻辑0）或开启（逻辑1）。
> -   **FTJ（铁电隧道结）**：像一个**可变电阻的隧道**。两个电极是隧道口，铁电层是隧道本身。隧道的“宽窄”由极化方向改变，导致电子通过时的电阻发生巨大变化。
> -   **FeRAM（铁电随机存取存储器）**：最常见的结构是**1T-1C**，就像一个**带开关的电容器**。晶体管是开关，铁电电容器是水桶。水桶可以装水或不装水（存储1/0），开关控制着对水桶的读写操作。
> -   **Fe-Diode（铁电二极管）**：一个**单向阀门**，但它的导通方向可以被铁电极化所改变。这种“自整流”特性对于大规模阵列非常重要。
> 
> #### Q4: 用Hf-FEs模拟“神经突触”听起来很厉害，但它的具体实现原理是什么？
> 
> **A:** 生物突触的强度（权重）可以连续变化，这是学习和记忆的基础。HfO₂铁电材料的一个精妙之处在于，它的极化不仅仅是“向上”或“向下”两种状态。通过施加一个不完全的电压脉冲，可以使铁电畴部分翻转，从而产生介于“0”和“1”之间的多个中间极化状态。这个中间极化状态对应着器件的一个中间电阻值。通过精确控制脉冲的个数、幅度和宽度，我们就可以让器件的电阻值像突触权重一样，连续、渐进地增加（LTP）或降低（LTD），从而在硬件上模拟了学习过程。

ABSTRACT
摘要
Hafnium-based ferroelectric (Hf-FEs) materials overcome the limitations of perovskite ferroelectric materials. Owing to their compatibility with the complementary metal–oxide–semiconductor process and high scalability, Hf-FEs devices have driven the development of non-volatile memory and neuromorphic computing, demonstrating significant potential for application in image processing and in-memory logic operations. First of all, this paper summarizes the material systems, device structure, and physical mechanisms relevant to Hf-FEs devices. Then, for the purpose of achieving efficient neuromorphic computing, the evaluation parameters related to Hf-FEs devices, specifically concerning storage performance and synaptic plasticity, are discussed. Furthermore, the progress of Hf-FEs devices in arrays and hardware integration is systematically reviewed, offering insights for future applications. Finally, this study explores in depth the prospects and challenges of these devices in advanced applications, providing valuable guidance for the development of high-performance neuromorphic computing devices.

<span class="hljs-section">## 1 Introduction</span>
<span class="hljs-section">## 1 引言</span>
With the exponential growth of the Internet of Things, Cloud Computing, and Artificial Intelligence (AI), the demand for high-performance, low-power computing systems has surged. The physical separation of memory and processing units in the traditional von Neumann architecture leads to frequent data movement, which results in significant energy consumption and severely limits system throughput, forming the so-called "memory wall" bottleneck [10–13]. This efficiency bottleneck has prompted researchers worldwide to actively seek new computing paradigms [10, 14–16], leading to the emergence of neuromorphic computing. These systems, inspired by the brain’s neural structure and operational mechanisms, perform computational tasks in a highly parallel and event-driven manner and are expected to improve energy efficiency by several orders of magnitude. The core of this paradigm shift lies in the ability of non-volatile memory technology to mimic synaptic plasticity, which is the biological basis of learning and memory [17–20]. Traditional perovskite ferroelectric materials, such as Pb(Zr, Ti) O3, SrBi2Ta2O9, and BaTiO3, have long been extensively studied for their excellent ferroelectric performance. However, their compatibility with silicon-based complementary metal–oxide–semiconductor (CMOS) processes, structural stability under high-temperature annealing conditions, and the interface dead layer and depolarization effects that arise when the thickness is reduced to below 100 nm pose significant limitations. These limitations make it difficult for them to meet the integration size and reliability requirements of advanced microelectronic nodes [21–23]. Concurrently, 2D semiconductors such as MoS2 have garnered significant attention due to their atomic thinness, superior electrostatic control, and inherent compatibility with CMOS processing. These properties offer a compelling platform for constructing high-performance, reconfigurable logic and memory devices. Through engineered van der Waals heterostructures, diverse physical mechanisms, including charge trapping and ferroelectric polarization, can be precisely modulated, thereby enriching the device toolkit for implementing versatile in-memory computing and neuromorphic hardware. On the other hand, efforts are also ongoing to find new bulk ferroelectric materials that are inherently compatible with CMOS processes, in order to balance integrability and performance stability [24–26].
随着物联网、云计算和人工智能（AI）的指数级增长，对高性能、低功耗计算系统的需求急剧上升。传统冯·诺依曼架构中存储与处理单元的物理分离导致频繁的数据搬运，从而产生显著能耗并严重限制系统吞吐量，形成所谓的“存储墙”瓶颈 [10–13]。这一效率瓶颈促使全球研究人员积极寻求新的计算范式 [10, 14–16]，神经形态计算应运而生。这些系统受大脑神经结构和运行机制启发，以高度并行和事件驱动的方式执行计算任务，有望将能效提升数个数量级。这一范式转变的核心在于非易失性存储器技术能够模拟突触可塑性，而突触可塑性是学习与记忆的生物学基础 [17–20]。传统的钙钛矿铁电材料，如Pb(Zr,Ti)O3、SrBi2Ta2O9和BaTiO3，因其优异的铁电性能而长期被广泛研究。然而，它们与硅基互补金属氧化物半导体（CMOS）工艺的兼容性、高温退火条件下的结构稳定性，以及当厚度减至100 nm以下时出现的界面死层和退极化效应，构成了重大限制。这些限制使其难以满足先进微电子节点对集成尺寸和可靠性的要求 [21–23]。同时，MoS2等二维半导体因其原子级厚度、优异的静电控制和与CMOS工艺的固有兼容性而备受关注。这些特性为构建高性能、可重构逻辑和存储器件提供了引人注目的平台。通过工程化的范德华异质结构，可以精确调控电荷捕获和铁电极化等多种物理机制，从而丰富实现多功能存内计算和神经形态硬件的器件工具包。另一方面，人们也在持续努力寻找与CMOS工艺本质上兼容的新型体铁电材料，以平衡集成性与性能稳定性 [24–26]。

It is against this backdrop of exploration that a turning point occurred in 2011 with the first discovery of stable ferroelectricity in doped hafnium oxide (HfO2) systems [35]. This discovery initiated a surge of research on HfFEs (Fig. 1). Unlike perovskite materials, Hf-FEs exhibit several fundamental advantages, including the retention of ferroelectricity at atomic-level thicknesses, natural compatibility with existing CMOS processes, and good tolerance to hydrogen atmosphere annealing treatment, an essential process in the back-end-of-line (BEOL) [36, 37]. These properties originate from the metastable polar orthorhombic phase, which can be stabilized through doping, strain engineering, and surface energy modulation [38–41]. Leveraging the exceptional performance of Hf-FEs devices, device level innovations have advanced rapidly. Therefore, ferroelectric field-effect transistors (FeFETs), ferroelectric tunnel junctions (FTJs), ferroelectric diodes (Fe-Diodes), and three-dimensional ferroelectric random access memory (FeRAM) arrays based on Hf-FEs have been successfully developed. Hf-FEs demonstrate exceptional performance in image processing, logic operations, and hardware integration, with this rapid progress laying a solid technical foundation for achieving three-dimensional monolithic integration of memory, logic, and neuromorphic functionalities, and the field is developing rapidly (Fig. 2) [42–47]. <span class="hljs-section">### 2.1 材料体系</span>
In hafnium-based materials, HfO2-based materials are known for their stable and controllable properties. The ferroelectricity of these materials originates from the formation of asymmetric centers within the metastable polar orthorhombic phase (o-phase, Pca21), a process induced by mechanical constraints [35]. Unlike traditional perovskite materials, HfO2-based materials are highly compatible with mainstream CMOS-integrated circuit technology. This compatibility greatly enhances their application prospects in the semiconductor industry [48–50]. However, given the metastable nature of the ferroelectric orthorhombic phase in HfO2, suppressing the formation of non-ferroelectric phases requires careful control of various process parameters. Chemical doping, by altering the local bonding environment within the HfO2 film and inducing strain, offers a viable approach to stabilize the ferroelectric orthorhombic phase [3, 51, 52]. Beyond the initially reported silicon (Si) doping, numerous other elements including zirconium (Zr), lanthanum (La), aluminum (Al), and yttrium (Y) have been employed to optimize the ferroelectric properties of HfO2 [37, 53–56]. Figure 3 shows some common doped HfO2 ferroelectric materials. Among various dopants, the Zr dopant is the most widely used in HfO2 due to the very similar physical and chemical properties of Zr and Hf. Fig. 3a shows the crystal structure of the orthorhombic phase in Zr-doped HfO2, which is the key basis for its ferroelectric performance. The microstructure characteristics of the HZO thin film can be clearly observed in Fig. 3b through STEM-HAADF of HZO. For Zr-doped HfO2, the dopant concentration can be stably maintained at 50% when the maximum ferroelectric polarization is achieved, facilitating uniformity and reproducibility in mass production [53, 64]. In addition, HZO thin film can be crystallized within a wider and lower temperature range (400–600 °C) compared to other dopant-doped thin films, which is beneficial to BEOL integration [65]. Fig. 3c shows a device fabrication flow chart based on anti-ferroelectric HZO. Lanthanide elements are also considered promising candidate elements for doping HfO2 [55, 65]. The crystal structure diagram of La-doped HfO2, intuitively reflecting the doping state of La atoms in the HfO2 lattice, is presented in Fig. 3d. And Fig. 3e shows the TEM image of La:HfO2 thin film, revealing the crystal structure details and microscopic morphology of the La:HfO2 thin film. Given the relatively large ionic radius and low electronegativity of La atoms, Jeon et al. demonstrated that La doping in hafnium-based FETs can reduce oxygen vacancy concentration in HfO2 thin films, minimizing defects and improving gate bias stability [66]. Among various dopants, La exhibits the strongest stabilizing effect on HfO2 ferroelectrics, which is beneficial for achieving excellent ferroelectric performance. For example, the La:HfO2 thin films reported by U. Schroeder et al. showed a 2Pr of 55 μC cm−2 after annealing at 800 °C [55]. Fig. 3f shows the process flow of the La:HfO2 FeTFET, further illustrating the application of La-doped HfO2 materials in low-power FeTFET design. Besides Zr and La, some elements commonly used in the semiconductor field, such as Si and Al, are also frequently used as dopants [67, 68]. Figure 3g shows the crystal structure of HfAlOx, revealing the structural characteristics of HfO2 lattice formation resulting from Al doping. The extremely small ionic radius of Al causes Al doping to introduce compressive stress lattice strain, which suppresses the formation of the monoclinic phase during the rapid thermal annealing process. This strain-dominated stabilization mechanism enables strong ferroelectricity in ultra-thin films while maintaining a considerable remnant polarization [68, 69].
在铪基材料中，基于HfO2的材料以其稳定且可控的性质而闻名。这些材料的铁电性源于亚稳态极性正交相（o相，Pca21）中不对称中心的形成，这一过程由机械约束诱导 [35]。与传统钙钛矿材料不同，HfO2基材料与主流CMOS集成电路技术高度兼容。这种兼容性极大增强了其在半导体工业中的应用前景 [48–50]。然而，鉴于HfO2中铁电正交相的亚稳态性质，抑制非铁电相的形成需要精确控制各种工艺参数。化学掺杂通过改变HfO2薄膜内的局部键合环境并诱导应变，为稳定铁电正交相提供了可行途径 [3, 51, 52]。除最初报道的硅（Si）掺杂外，锆（Zr）、镧（La）、铝（Al）和钇（Y）等众多其他元素也被用于优化HfO2的铁电性能 [37, 53–56]。图3展示了一些常见的掺杂HfO2铁电材料。在众多掺杂剂中，Zr掺杂剂因Zr和Hf的物理化学性质非常相似，在HfO2中应用最广。图3a显示了Zr掺杂HfO2中正交相的晶体结构，这是其铁电性能的关键基础。通过HZO的STEM-HAADF像，图3b可清晰观察到HZO薄膜的微观结构特征。对于Zr掺杂的HfO2，当达到最大铁电极化时，掺杂浓度可稳定维持在50%，有利于大规模生产中的均匀性和重复性 [53, 64]。此外，与其他掺杂薄膜相比，HZO薄膜可在更宽且更低的温度范围（400–600 °C）内结晶，这有利于BEOL集成 [65]。图3c展示了基于反铁电HZO的器件制造流程图。镧系元素也被认为是掺杂HfO2的有前景候选元素 [55, 65]。图3d给出了La掺杂HfO2的晶体结构图，直观反映了La原子在HfO2晶格中的掺杂状态。图3e展示了La:HfO2薄膜的TEM图像，揭示了La:HfO2薄膜的晶体结构细节和微观形貌。鉴于La原子相对较大的离子半径和较低的电负性，Jeon等人证明，在铪基FET中掺杂La可降低HfO2薄膜中的氧空位浓度，最小化缺陷并改善栅偏压稳定性 [66]。在众多掺杂剂中，La对HfO2铁电体表现出最强的稳定效应，有利于实现优异的铁电性能。例如，U. In summary, the performance of hafnium-based ferroelectric materials is highly dependent on the selection of doping elements, with different dopants being adapted to differentiated application scenarios by modulating lattice strain, oxygen vacancy concentration, and phase stability. The HZO system formed by Zr doping has become a mainstream choice for high-density FeFET and FeRAM arrays because of its high remanent polarization and good CMOS process compatibility, but its high coercive field may lead to an increase in operating voltage [58]. La doping can significantly suppress oxygen vacancies and improve high-temperature stability, maintaining strong ferroelectricity even after annealing at 800 °C, which is suitable for high-reliability storage and logic devices requiring back-end high-temperature integration [55]. Although Si doping first revealed the ferroelectricity of HfO2 and has a mature process, its poor endurance means that it is mostly used for basic research and prototype verification. Compressive strain introduced by Al doping is conducive to stabilizing the ferroelectric phase in ultra-thin films, which demonstrates potential in ultra-thin FTJs and three-dimensional stacked neuromorphic devices [61]. Consequently, doping engineering has become a critical method for customizing the ferroelectric performance of HfO2, meeting diverse application needs ranging from high-density memory to low-power neuromorphic computing. Co-doping strategies have achieved breakthroughs in the overall performance of HfO2-based ferroelectric materials through synergistic effects between different elements. The core synergistic mechanisms include three key aspects:

<span class="hljs-strong">**coercive field**</span>: 矫顽场，反转铁电极化所需的外加电场；<span class="hljs-strong">**endurance**</span>: 耐久性，器件能够承受的循环擦写次数；<span class="hljs-strong">**oxygen vacancy**</span>: 氧空位，氧化物晶格中缺失的氧离子，对铁电性能有重要影响。

(1) Synergistic regulation of oxygen vacancies: For example, in La–Al co-doping, Al promotes the generation of oxygen vacancies to stabilize the ferroelectric phase, while La suppresses their excessive migration. This synergy enhances polarization strength and ensures high endurance, as evidenced in a 3D ferroelectric memory based on La–Al co-doped HfO2 thin films, which exhibits excellent ferroelectric properties and outstanding memory performance [70] .
(1) 氧空位的协同调控：例如，在La-Al共掺杂中，Al促进氧空位生成以稳定铁电相，而La抑制其过度迁移。这种协同增强了极化强度并确保高耐久性，基于La-Al共掺杂HfO2薄膜的3D铁电存储器即证明了这一点，其展现出优异的铁电性能和出色的存储性能 [70]。
(2) Lattice strain synergistic equilibrium: This can be achieved by optimizing the concentration and spatial distribution of dopants. For instance, tuning process parameters to induce favorable in-plane shear stress promotes the formation of the ferroelectric orthorhombic phase. The study by Liu et al. demonstrates that when Al-doped HZO thin films are prepared with a cycle ratio of 1:48, the ferroelectric performance is significantly improved [71] .
(2) 晶格应变协同平衡：可通过优化掺杂剂的浓度和空间分布来实现。例如，调节工艺参数以诱导有利的面内剪切应力，促进铁电正交相的形成。Liu等人的研究表明，当以1:48的循环比制备Al掺杂HZO薄膜时，铁电性能显著提升 [71]。
(3) Synergistic interface engineering: As shown by Mehrdad Ghiasabadi Farahani et al., constructing multilayer structures by inserting La-doped HfO2 subnanometer layers is an effective strategy for enhancing the performance of HZO-based ferroelectric films. This approach is particularly suitable for high-performance electronic devices such as non-volatile memory, ferroelectric memory, and capacitive memory elements [72] .
(3) 协同界面工程：如Mehrdad Ghiasabadi Farahani等人所示，通过插入La掺杂HfO2亚纳米层构建多层结构是提升HZO基铁电薄膜性能的有效策略。该方法特别适用于非易失性存储器、铁电存储器和电容式存储元件等高性能电子器件 [72]。

<span class="hljs-section">### 2.2 Device Structure</span>
<span class="hljs-section">### 2.2 器件结构</span>
HfO2 possesses unique properties, most notably its CMOS compatibility and scalability, which enable its integration into device architectures for various memory and logic applications. In this section, several structures of Hf-FEs devices will be explored, with a focus on their working principles, advantages, and scaling potential.
HfO2具有独特的性质，最显著的是其CMOS兼容性和可微缩性，使其能够集成到各种存储和逻辑应用的器件架构中。本节将探讨几种Hf-FEs器件结构，重点关注其工作原理、优势及微缩潜力。

<span class="hljs-section">#### 2.2.1 FeFETs</span>
<span class="hljs-section">#### 2.2.1 铁电场效应晶体管</span>
FeFETs, by directly embedding non-volatile memory functions within transistors, enable novel memory computing, neuromorphic computing, and other computing paradigms. Structurally, they are compatible and scalable with CMOS technology. FeFETs switch ferroelectric polarization states via field control. This polarization reversal alters the transistor’s threshold voltage, which is then used to represent "0" or "1" states; this polarization state is maintained even after power is turned off. Compared to perovskite-structured ferroelectric materials, hafnium-based FeFETs exhibit higher coercive fields and greater resistance to the depolarization field, resulting in superior retention characteristics [91, 92]. Fig. 4a illustrates an FeFET employing an MFS stacked structure. The HZO/IGZO-based FeFET exhibits excellent electrical performance, featuring not only a large memory window of 2 V and a near-ideal subthreshold swing of 63mV dec−1, but also achieving a high on/off current ratio of 3.5 × 106 and an ultra-low off-state leakage current down to the pA μm−1 level [4]. Building upon this, Fig. 4b demonstrates an MFMIS-stacked FeFET. This structure successfully mimics biological synaptic function, enabling electrical modulation of synaptic weights and low-voltage operation, which makes it suitable for low-power neuromorphic computing. In addition, integrating such devices into 3 × 3 synaptic arrays effectively verifies the electrically modifiable weighted-sum operation, providing a foundation for the future implementation of neuromorphic computing in AI hardware [80] . The integrated application of FeFET in array architectures has been actively explored by researchers, exemplified by the 16 × 16 FeFET AND array shown in Fig. 4c. This array achieves selective write operations and incorporates a biasing scheme for write inhibition, which is realized solely through bit line voltage. These capabilities highlight the potential of FeFET in high-density, low-power neuromorphic memory applications [81]. Despite the significant progress made in FeFET technology over the past decade, its development remains limited by several key challenges, including unstable memory window behavior, significant device-to-device variation, and the detrimental "wake-up"/"fatigue" effects observed in the ferroelectric layer. Furthermore, scaling effects impose significant limitations when FeFETs are integrated at high density. To address these challenges, future research can be conducted in several directions. These include exploring novel ferroelectric materials and superior interface layers through materials and interface engineering, as well as developing novel device structures such as three-dimensional stacking and multi-gate structures through structural innovation and optimization. Through interdisciplinary collaborative innovation, FeFET technology is expected to overcome current bottlenecks and play a key role in future high-energy-efficiency computing.
FeFETs通过将非易失性存储功能直接嵌入晶体管中，实现了新型存算一体、神经形态计算等计算范式。在结构上，它们与CMOS技术兼容且可微缩。FeFETs通过电场控制切换铁电极化状态。这种极化反转改变晶体管的阈值电压，从而用于表示“0”或“1”状态；即使断电后，该极化状态仍得以保持。与钙钛矿结构铁电材料相比，铪基FeFETs具有更高的矫顽场和更强的抗退极化场能力，从而具备更优的保持特性 [91, 92]。图4a展示了一种采用MFS堆叠结构的FeFET。基于HZO/IGZO的FeFET展现出优异的电学性能，不仅具有2 V的大存储窗口和接近理想的63mV dec−1亚阈值摆幅，还实现了3.5 × 106的高开关比和低至pA μm−1量级的超低关态漏电流 [4]。在此基础上，图4b展示了一种MFMIS堆叠FeFET。该结构成功模拟了生物突触功能，实现了突触权重的电调制和低电压工作，适用于低功耗神经形态计算。此外，将此类器件集成到3 × 3突触阵列中，有效验证了电可调的加权和运算，为未来在AI硬件中实现神经形态计算提供了基础 [80]。研究人员积极探索FeFET在阵列架构中的集成应用，图4c所示的16 × 16 FeFET AND阵列即为一例。该阵列实现了选择性写入操作，并采用仅通过位线电压实现的写抑制偏置方案。这些能力凸显了FeFET在高密度、低功耗神经形态存储应用中的潜力 [81]。尽管过去十年FeFET技术取得了显著进展，但其发展仍受若干关键挑战的限制，包括不稳定的存储窗口行为、显著的器件间差异，以及铁电层中观察到的有害“唤醒”/“疲劳”效应。此外，当FeFETs高密度集成时，微缩效应构成重大限制。为应对这些挑战，未来研究可从多个方向展开。包括通过材料与界面工程探索新型铁电材料和优质界面层，以及通过结构创新与优化开发三维堆叠和多栅结构等新型器件结构。通过跨学科协同创新，FeFET技术有望克服当前瓶颈，在未来高能效计算中发挥关键作用。
<span class="hljs-strong">**MFS**</span>: Metal-Ferroelectric-Semiconductor，金属-铁电体-半导体堆叠结构；<span class="hljs-strong">**subthreshold swing**</span>: 亚阈值摆幅，晶体管从关态转向开态时栅压变化与电流变化之比，理想值为60 mV/dec；<span class="hljs-strong">**MFMIS**</span>: Metal-Ferroelectric-Metal-Insulator-Semiconductor，金属-铁电体-金属-绝缘体-半导体结构；<span class="hljs-strong">**wake-up effect**</span>: 唤醒效应，铁电薄膜在初始电场循环中极化逐渐增强的现象；<span class="hljs-strong">**fatigue**</span>: 疲劳，铁电材料在反复极化切换后极化强度衰减的现象。

<span class="hljs-section">#### 2.2.2 FTJs</span>
<span class="hljs-section">#### 2.2.2 铁电隧道结</span>
Unlike three terminal FeFETs, the FTJs are two terminal devices. Its basic structure comprises two conductive electrodes separated by a nanometer-thick ferroelectric layer, which serves as an energy barrier. This concept was initially proposed by L. Esaki et al. in 1971 and termed "polarity switch" [93]. However, it was not until 2012 that a functional solid-state device utilizing the FTJ principle was demonstrated [94]. In FTJs, charge carriers are transported via quantum mechanical tunneling through the ultra-thin ferroelectric barrier. The polarization state of the ferroelectric material governs the tunneling resistance, leading to two distinct resistance states, which enable the storage of binary or higher order information. FTJs are considered promising candidates for constructing artificial synapses and high-density, ultrafast memories. As shown in Fig. 4d, a Pt/SiO2/HZO/TiN FTJ device achieves sub-nanosecond switching speed of 500 ps at voltages ≤ 5 V. This FTJ exhibits a low write current density of 1.3 × 104 A cm−2, endurance of over 107 cycles under 85 °C, and a large read current density of 88 A cm−2 at voltages below 0.1 V, thus demonstrating its suitability for future FTJ-based neural network computing [82] . Fig. 4e illustrates an FTJ employing an MFIS structure with strong ferroelectric properties. Interestingly, the authors found the MFIS structure to be superior to the MIFS structure with respect to ferroelectric properties, exhibiting a higher remnant polarization of approximately 69 μC cm−2. Furthermore, the MFIS FTJ exhibited reduced leakage current, a higher tunneling electroresistance ratio, and a thinner interfacial dead layer during short-pulse switching, thus providing a promising pathway for future development [83]. Figure 4f illustrates a 3D vertically integrated ferroelectric HZO-based FTJ array designed to emulate the full functionality of biological synapses. The fabricated 3D vertical FTJ synapse features high integration density and superior performance metrics, including analog conductance modulation under training schemes, low-energy consumption per synaptic weight update (1.8 pJ spike−1), and excellent repeatability (&gt; 103 cycles) [8]. FTJs hold great promise for ultra-high-density non-volatile memory applications. However, current FTJ devices based on HfO2 also face some challenges: the underlying tunneling mechanism remains unclear, and a further improvement in the switching ratio is needed to achieve reliable sensing operations. It is expected that the ferroelectric domain switching process can be directly observed at the atomic scale in the future by utilizing in situ electrical testing and advanced microscopic characterization techniques, which will reveal its dominant tunneling mechanism. The intrinsic polarization strength of HfO2 ferroelectric thin films can be precisely controlled through material and band engineering to obtain more stable and superior ferroelectric performance, and to explore novel device architectures. Collaborative innovations in physical mechanisms, material systems, and device structures will pave the way for the next generation of ultra-high-density memory and neuromorphic computing applications.
与三端FeFETs不同，FTJs是两端器件。其基本结构由两个导电电极和中间一层纳米厚的铁电层组成，铁电层充当能量势垒。这一概念最初由L. Esaki等人于1971年提出，并称为“极性开关”[93]。然而，直到2012年才展示了利用FTJ原理的功能性固态器件 [94]。在FTJs中，载流子通过量子力学隧穿穿越超薄铁电势垒。铁电材料的极化状态决定隧穿电阻，产生两种不同的电阻态，从而实现二进制或更高阶信息的存储。FTJs被认为是构建人工突触和高密度、超快存储器的理想候选者。如图4d所示，一个Pt/SiO2/HZO/TiN FTJ器件在≤5 V电压下实现了500 ps的亚纳秒开关速度。该FTJ具有1.3 × 104 A cm−2的低写入电流密度，在85 °C下超过107次循环的耐久性，以及在0.1 V以下电压下88 A cm−2的高读取电流密度，从而证明了其适用于未来基于FTJ的神经网络计算 [82]。图4e展示了一种采用MFIS结构并具有强铁电性能的FTJ。有趣的是，作者发现MFIS结构在铁电性能上优于MIFS结构，展现出约69 μC cm−2的更高剩余极化。此外，MFIS FTJ在短脉冲开关过程中展现出更低的漏电流、更高的隧穿电致电阻比和更薄的界面死层，从而为未来发展提供了有前景的途径 [83]。图4f展示了一个三维垂直集成的铁电HZO基FTJ阵列，旨在模拟生物突触的全部功能。所制备的3D垂直FTJ突触具有高集成密度和优越的性能指标，包括训练方案下的模拟电导调制、每次突触权重更新低能耗（1.8 pJ spike−1）和出色的可重复性（&gt; 103次循环）[8]。FTJs在超高密度非易失性存储应用中具有巨大前景。然而，当前基于HfO2的FTJ器件也面临一些挑战：底层隧穿机制尚不明确，且需要进一步提高开关比以实现可靠的读出操作。预计未来可利用原位电学测试和先进显微表征技术，在原子尺度直接观察铁电畴开关过程，从而揭示其主导隧穿机制。通过材料和能带工程可精确调控HfO2铁电薄膜的本征极化强度，以获得更稳定、更优异的铁电性能，并探索新型器件架构。物理机制、材料体系和器件结构的协同创新将为下一代超高密度存储和神经形态计算应用铺平道路。
<span class="hljs-strong">**MIFS**</span>: Metal-Insulator-Ferroelectric-Semiconductor，金属-绝缘体-铁电体-半导体结构；<span class="hljs-strong">**tunneling electroresistance ratio**</span>: 隧穿电致电阻比，FTJ中不同极化态下电阻的比值；<span class="hljs-strong">**ferroelectric domain**</span>: 铁电畴，铁电材料中自发极化方向一致的区域。

<span class="hljs-section">#### 2.2.3 FeRAMs</span>
<span class="hljs-section">#### 2.2.3 铁电随机存取存储器</span>
The basic concept of FeRAM can be traced back to the 1950s. In this memory architecture, the two terminals of a ferroelectric capacitor (FeCAP) are connected to lines in the vertical direction, forming memory arrays. The one transistor-one capacitor (1T-1C) configuration is the most common FeRAM implementation. In a 1T-1C FeRAM cell, the memory cell is composed of an access transistor integrated with a FeCAP. Binary information is stored via the capacitor’s switchable polarization states (up or down), and the access transistor enables random access read and write operations [95–97]. As shown in Fig. 4g, the core structure of a FeCAP consists of a ferroelectric material thin film sandwiched between top and bottom electrodes. Hafnium-based materials exhibit distinct ferroelectric hysteresis loop characteristics, maintaining two stable polarization states and thereby enabling data storage. Embedded ferroelectric memory was successfully realized by Kasidit et al. using an HZO-based FeCAP with its thickness reduced to 4 nm. Test results indicate that the device exhibits excellent reliability even at a low operating voltage of 1.2 V; it achieves an endurance of at least 1012 cycles at a 200 kHz operating frequency, coupled with a 10 year data retention capability [84] . This achievement demonstrates that reducing the thickness of the HZO thin film effectively lowers the operating voltage and improves endurance. This provides a crucial pathway for developing low-power, high-reliability back-end compatible embedded FeRAM. A single transistor combined with a FeCAP constitutes the most common 1T-1C cell structure in FeRAM. As shown in Fig. 4h, within this cell, the gate of the access transistor is connected to the word line for gating control, the source is connected to the bit line to facilitate data reading and writing, and the other terminal of the FeCAP is connected to the plate line to apply the necessary voltage pulses during operation. In practical applications, millions of 1T-1C cells are integrated and organized into high-density memory arrays. Figure 4i shows a FeCAP-based memory array architecture. To mitigate ferroelectric fatigue in HZO arrays, Huang et al. introduced a design scheme integrating a CAMR recovery circuit, which enables dynamic detection of fatigue states and signal recovery. This technology, through its built-in detection and recovery mechanisms, significantly enhances FeRAM’s reliability and lifetime, offering a practical solution for its application as an on-chip storage element and in next-generation non-volatile memory and "More than Moore" devices, and thereby demonstrating promising development prospects [86] .
FeRAM的基本概念可追溯到20世纪50年代。在这种存储架构中，铁电电容（FeCAP）的两个端子连接到垂直方向的连线，形成存储阵列。一晶体管一电容（1T-1C）配置是最常见的FeRAM实现方式。在1T-1C FeRAM单元中，存储单元由一个存取晶体管与一个FeCAP集成而成。二进制信息通过电容的可切换极化状态（向上或向下）存储，存取晶体管则实现随机访问读写操作 [95–97]。如图4g所示，FeCAP的核心结构由夹在顶电极和底电极之间的铁电材料薄膜组成。铪基材料表现出独特的铁电电滞回线特性，保持两个稳定极化态，从而实现数据存储。Kasidit等人利用厚度减至4 nm的HZO基FeCAP成功实现了嵌入式铁电存储器。测试结果表明，该器件即使在1.2 V的低工作电压下也表现出优异的可靠性；在200 kHz工作频率下，耐久性至少达到1012次循环，并具备10年数据保持能力 [84]。这一成就表明，减小HZO薄膜厚度可有效降低工作电压并提高耐久性。这为开发低功耗、高可靠性的后端兼容嵌入式FeRAM提供了关键途径。单个晶体管与FeCAP组合构成FeRAM中最常见的1T-1C单元结构。如图4h所示，在此单元中，存取晶体管的栅极连接到字线进行选通控制，源极连接到位线以实现数据读写，而FeCAP的另一端连接到板线，以便在工作时施加必要的电压脉冲。在实际应用中，数百万个1T-1C单元被集成并组织成高密度存储阵列。图4i展示了一种基于FeCAP的存储阵列架构。为缓解HZO阵列中的铁电疲劳，Huang等人引入了一种集成CAMR恢复电路的设计方案，该方案能动态检测疲劳状态并进行信号恢复。该技术通过其内置的检测与恢复机制，显著增强了FeRAM的可靠性和使用寿命，为其作为片上存储元件以及在新一代非易失性存储器和“超越摩尔”器件中的应用提供了实用解决方案，展现出光明的发展前景 [86]。
<span class="hljs-strong">**FeCAP**</span>: Ferroelectric Capacitor，铁电电容；<span class="hljs-strong">**1T-1C**</span>: One Transistor-One Capacitor，一晶体管一电容单元结构；<span class="hljs-strong">**hysteresis loop**</span>: 电滞回线，铁电材料极化强度随电场变化的闭合曲线；<span class="hljs-strong">**CAMR**</span>: Continuous-Amplitude Modulated Recovery，连续幅值调制恢复电路。

<span class="hljs-section">#### 2.2.4 Fe‐Diodes</span>
<span class="hljs-section">#### 2.2.4 铁电二极管</span>
Besides three terminal transistors and two terminal junction devices, Fe-diodes, owing to their simple structure and ultra-high scalability, have emerged as promising storage elements, especially suitable for ultra-high-density crossbar array integration. Unlike FTJs that rely on tunneling resistance, their working principle is based on Schottky barrier modulation regulated by polarization reversal, with MFS or MFM structures. The ferroelectric polarization direction can realize current rectification, forming a significant non-volatile on/off ratio. This inherent self-selection characteristic can effectively suppress sneak currents in crossbar arrays, eliminating the need for an additional selector, greatly simplifying circuit design and improving array density [31, 98, 99]. Figure 4j shows a Fe-diode fabricated with a W/HZO/W plug structure. The device exhibits stable polarization characteristics and a good memory window, enabling nondestructive readout at low bias voltages. Based on the switchable bidirectional rectification characteristics of this device, Han et al. constructed a 2-bit multiplication scheme that requires only 11 devices and 16 steps to obtain a 4-bit product output, with a power consumption of approximately 11 fJ throughout the entire operation. This demonstrates the feasibility of applying Fe-diodes to self-selective high-density crossbar arrays [87]. Fe-diodes operating through polarization-driven Schottky barrier modulation often exhibit high leakage currents and unstable switching behavior because of insufficient barrier control and interface defects. By introducing a buffer layer at the two-dimensional MoS2 top electrode/FE interface (Fig. 4k) and increasing the HZO thickness from 3 to 8 nm, Hwang et al. shifted the dominant conduction mechanism from direct tunneling to Schottky emission. This resulted in more robust polarization-induced barrier modulation, a significantly improved on/off ratio, a high current density of up to 50 A cm−2 (read at 3 V), endurance exceeding 1010 cycles, and stable memory retention for 10 years at room temperature. This confirms the exceptional scalability and strong potential of this Fe-diode design in next-generation integrated memory applications [90]. Beyond achieving performance breakthroughs at the individual device level, the potential for applying Fe-diodes in arrays has also been initially validated, paving the way for their use in high-density integrated memory and computing. Huang et al. successfully fabricated a 16-layer stacked Fe-diode array (Fig. 4l). The core innovation lies in unifying the storage and random source generation functions in the same three-dimensional arrays. This "memory-computing-random integrated" architecture achieves an area efficiency of up to 0.06 F2 state−1 and a low programming energy consumption of 25 fJ. More importantly, the inherent rectification characteristics and three-dimensional stacking capability of Fe-diodes provide an ideal device solution for constructing high-density, self-selected, low-crosstalk crossbar arrays. This work demonstrates, from the perspective of array scale, the great potential of Fe-diodes in realizing the next generation of high-energy efficiency and high-area efficiency edge intelligent computing hardware [90]. Recent advances in Fe-diodes based on HfO2 have demonstrated remarkable performance, including nanosecond switching speeds, excellent endurance, and multilevel operation capabilities. However, challenges remain in achieving high uniformity, reliable polarization reversal at low voltages, and complementary diode pairing for practical memory logic applications. As previously mentioned, Hf-FEs devices exhibit superior performance in terms of switching speed, endurance, and power consumption, making them central to building nonvolatile storage and neuromorphic computing hardware. <span class="hljs-section">### 2.3 Physical Mechanism</span>
<span class="hljs-section">### 2.3 物理机制</span>
HfO2 exhibits complex phenomena with the coexistence of multiple phases, and the origin of ferroelectricity in ferroelectric hafnia remains controversial. Using first-principles computing, Lee et al. discovered that flat bands of polar phonons, and the resulting local dipoles, induce scale-free ferroelectric order in HfO2. Therefore, the emergence of these flat phonon bands in HfO2 provides an explanation for the origin of its scale-free ferroelectricity (Fig. 5a) [103]. Under ambient conditions, the monoclinic phase (m-phase, P21/c) is the most stable phase of HfO2. As the temperature increases above 1973 K, the stable phase transforms into the tetragonal phase (t-phase, P42/nmc), and subsequently into the cubic phase (c-phase, Fm3m) at 2773 K. Additionally, phase transitions can be induced by pressure [51]. It is now generally accepted that ferroelectricity in hafnium-based thin films originates from the o-phase. Among numerous phases, the polar phases of HfO2 are metastable compared to the non-polar m-phase. A fundamental challenge in fabricating ferroelectric HfO2-based thin films is suppressing the formation of the non-polar monoclinic phase while promoting the emergence of polar phases. Various extrinsic factors, including temperature, oxygen vacancy concentration, dopants, strain, and surface/interface effects, can influence the phase transition (Fig. 5b). Figure 5c illustrates the formation of the ferroelectric orthorhombic phase in doped HfO2 thin films during rapid thermal processing.
HfO2表现出多相共存的复杂现象，铁电氧化铪中铁电性的起源仍存在争议。通过第一性原理计算，Lee等人发现，极性声子的平带及其产生的局域偶极子在HfO2中诱导了无标度铁电序。因此，HfO2中这些平带声子的出现为其无标度铁电性的起源提供了解释（图5a）[103]。在环境条件下，单斜相（m相，P21/c）是HfO2最稳定的相。当温度升至1973 K以上时，稳定相转变为四方相（t相，P42/nmc），随后在2773 K转变为立方相（c相，Fm3m）。此外，压力也可诱导相变 [51]。目前普遍认为，铪基薄膜的铁电性源于o相。在众多相中，HfO2的极性相相对于非极性m相是亚稳态的。制备铁电HfO2基薄膜的一个根本挑战在于抑制非极性单斜相的形成，同时促进极性相的出现。包括温度、氧空位浓度、掺杂剂、应变和表面/界面效应在内的多种外在因素均可影响相变（图5b）。图5c示意了掺杂HfO2薄膜在快速热处理过程中铁电正交相的形成。

<span class="hljs-section">#### 2.3.1 Ferroelectric Phase Transition Mechanisms</span>
<span class="hljs-section">#### 2.3.1 铁电相变机制</span>
The ferroelectricity in HfO2 was first discovered in Si:HfO2, which immediately aroused great interest in the dopant effect. It is understood that dopants stabilize phases by modulating size and influencing the formation of oxygen vacancies [104, 108]. Rohit Batra et al. employed high-throughput first-principles density functional theory (DFT) computing to investigate the effect of nearly 40 dopants on the phase stability of HfO2. They found that dopants with a larger ionic radius and lower electronegativity most effectively stabilize the P-O1 phase. Their study predicts that lanthanide elements, the lower half of the alkaline earth metal series like Ca, Sr, Ba, and Y, are the most favorable dopants for enhancing ferroelectricity in HfO2 (Fig. 5d) [104]. Epitaxial strain is also considered a key factor in stabilizing the metastable o-phase in HfO2 thin film. It is understood that through in-plane tensile stress, the Poisson effect causes out-of-plane contraction (transverse strain), leading to lattice distortion and driving the t-phase to transform into the o-phase (Fig. 5e) [4]. The effect of biaxial strain on the thermodynamic stability of HfO2 polymorphs has been extensively studied. Fan et al. demonstrated that tensile strains greater than 3% render the Pca2 phase more stable than the antipolar Pbca phase [109]. Furthermore, strain can synergistically interact with other extrinsic factors such as doping, oxygen vacancy, and interfacial effects to enhance the stability of the ferroelectric phase. The variation in oxygen vacancy concentration is a crucial factor in the phase evolution of HfO2. Numerous studies have demonstrated the significant impact of oxygen vacancies on the ferroelectric performance of HfO2-based thin films. Given that oxygen vacancies are intrinsic to HfO2, experimental observations indicate that the migration of these vacancies from the interface to the bulk region triggers a transition from the m-phase to the polar o-phase. Critically, a higher concentration of oxygen vacancies suppresses the monoclinic phase while stabilizing the polar o-phase [110, 111]. Through DFT calculations, He et al. demonstrated that when the concentration of doubly charged oxygen vacancies (VO2+) exceeds 2%, the ferroelectric polar o-phase becomes thermodynamically more stable than the m-phase. This confirms VO2+ as a stabilizer of the ferroelectric phase in HfO2 (Fig. 5f) [105].
HfO2的铁电性首次在Si:HfO2中发现，这立即引发了对掺杂剂效应的浓厚兴趣。人们认识到，掺杂剂通过调节尺寸和影响氧空位的形成来稳定相 [104, 108]。Rohit Batra等人采用高通量第一性原理密度泛函理论（DFT）计算，研究了近40种掺杂剂对HfO2相稳定性的影响。他们发现，离子半径较大、电负性较低的掺杂剂最有效地稳定P-O1相。他们的研究预测，镧系元素以及碱土金属系列下半部分的Ca、Sr、Ba和Y是增强HfO2铁电性的最有利掺杂剂（图5d）[104]。外延应变也被认为是稳定HfO2薄膜中亚稳态o相的关键因素。据信，通过面内拉伸应力，泊松效应引起面外收缩（横向应变），导致晶格畸变并驱动t相转变为o相（图5e）[4]。双轴应变对HfO2多晶型热力学稳定性的影响已得到广泛研究。Fan等人证明，大于3%的拉伸应变使Pca2相比反极性Pbca相更稳定 [109]。此外，应变可与掺杂、氧空位和界面效应等其他外在因素协同作用，增强铁电相的稳定性。氧空位浓度的变化是HfO2相演变的关键因素。大量研究证明了氧空位对HfO2基薄膜铁电性能的显著影响。鉴于氧空位是HfO2的本征缺陷，实验观察表明，这些空位从界面向体区的迁移触发了从m相到极性o相的转变。关键的是，较高的氧空位浓度抑制单斜相，同时稳定极性o相 [110, 111]。通过DFT计算，He等人证明，当双电荷氧空位（VO2+）浓度超过2%时，铁电极性o相在热力学上比m相更稳定。这证实了VO2+是HfO2中铁电相的稳定剂（图5f）[105]。

<span class="hljs-section">#### 2.3.2 Switching Mechanisms</span>
<span class="hljs-section">#### 2.3.2 开关机制</span>
Polarization switching is a fundamental property of ferroelectrics. Understanding the microscopic mechanisms is not only of fundamental scientific importance but also crucial for their application in future electronic devices. Currently, the microscopic mechanisms of polarization switching in fluorite-structured ferroelectrics remain unclear, although several theories, such as domain wall motion and oxygen ion migration, have been proposed. Microscopic mechanism: Domain walls (DWs) are interfaces separating domains with distinct polarization orientations. In HfO2, polarization switching driven by DW motion primarily occurs via low-energy-barrier (2;0)-type DW pathways. Compared to the high-energy barrier (1.36 eV) of conventional (1;1)-type DWs, the (2;0) pathway exhibits a significantly reduced barrier of only 73 meV due to its geometric similarity to the t-phase. This process involves the movement of a DW from a polarization layer number of nu = N to nu = N + 1. The tetragonal transition state can be further stabilized under tensile strain. Applying a 0.4% strain reduces the energy of the pseudo-monoclinic intermediate state, effectively eliminating the transition barrier. Consequently, polarization reversal controlled by electric fields or mechanical stress becomes highly efficient (Fig. 5g) [106]. In Pca21-structured HfO2, ferroelectricity originates from the asymmetric displacement of oxygen ions within the unit cell. These oxygen ions are categorized into two types: polar oxygen ions (Op), which generate the net polarization along the Z-axis, and non-polar oxygen ions (Onp), located in non-polarizing regions. Polarization reversal involves the concerted migration of oxygen ions, primarily through two distinct pathways: The Intralayer Migration Pathway: Op ions displace within their original positions, directly reversing the orientation of the local dipole moment. The Interlayer Migration Pathway: Onp ions migrate across the polar/nonpolar boundary, achieving polarization reversal by inverting the X2− vibrational mode (represented by the gray-colored transition state). During this process, the identities of Onp and Op ions are exchanged, leading to the reversal of the net polarization direction. (Fig. 5h) [107]. Phenomenological models: The aforementioned atomic-scale images reveal the physical origin of polarization reversal; however, the macroscopic switching dynamics and reliability of Hf-FEs thin films in practical device applications are strongly dependent on their polycrystallinity, defect distribution, phase boundary disorder, and spatial inhomogeneity. To quantify these complex behaviors, a series of phenomenological models has been widely adopted, among which the Kolmogorov–Avrami–Ishibashi (KAI) model and the nucleation-limited switching (NLS) model are critical [112, 113]. Studies have shown that the switching of polycrystalline hafnium-based ferroelectric thin films is mainly dominated by the nucleation process, and domain wall motion is limited. The switching image described by the classical KAI model, which involves free lateral expansion after nucleation, is often not applicable here. The experimentally observed non-exponential relaxation, broadened switching time distribution, and partial polarization switching characteristics are highly consistent with the predictions of the NLS model. This model regards the thin film as a collection of independent regions with distributed switching times, and their distribution originates from the fluctuation of the local electric field, which is caused by grain boundaries, defects, and multiphase coexistence. Cristóbal Alessandri et al. successfully extracted the minimum switching time τ∞ (~ 236 ns), the activation field Ea (~ 2.4 MV cm−1), and the statistical distribution f(η) of the local electric field enhancement factor from the experimental characterization of HZO thin films and fitting with the NLS model. This distribution usually conforms to the generalized beta distribution, which directly reflects the inhomogeneity of the thin film [63]. It is worth noting that there are significant differences in the switching dynamics between epitaxial thin films and polycrystalline thin films. Although epitaxial thin films exhibit a higher nucleation density, their switching speed is slower than that of polycrystalline thin films. This highlights the rate-limiting role of nucleation in Hf-FEs switching. However, under high electric fields close to the activation field, the switching dynamics of both thin films show a convergence trend between the NLS and KAI models. This indicates that under high-field driving, the local potential barrier is "smoothed" and the switching approaches a uniform, intrinsic limit [114].
极化开关是铁电体的基本性质。理解其微观机制不仅具有基础科学意义，对其在未来电子器件中的应用也至关重要。目前，萤石结构铁电体中极化开关的微观机制尚不清楚，尽管已提出了畴壁运动和氧离子迁移等几种理论。微观机制：畴壁（DWs）是分隔不同极化取向畴的界面。在HfO2中，由DW运动驱动的极化开关主要通过低能垒的(2;0)型DW路径发生。与传统(1;1)型DW的高能垒（1.36 eV）相比，(2;0)路径因其与t相的几何相似性，能垒显著降低至仅73 meV。这一过程涉及DW从极化层数v = N移动到v = N + 1。四方过渡态在拉伸应变下可进一步稳定。施加0.4%的应变可降低伪单斜中间态的能量，有效消除过渡势垒。因此，由电场或机械应力控制的极化反转变得高效（图5g）[106]。在Pca21结构的HfO2中，铁电性源于晶胞内氧离子的不对称位移。这些氧离子分为两类：极性氧离子（Op），产生沿Z轴的净极化；非极性氧离子（Onp），位于非极化区域。极化反转涉及氧离子的协同迁移，主要通过两种不同路径：层内迁移路径：Op离子在其原位内位移，直接反转局域偶极矩的方向。层间迁移路径：Onp离子跨越极性/非极性边界迁移，通过反转X2−振动模式（由灰色过渡态表示）实现极化反转。在此过程中，Onp和Op离子的身份互换，导致净极化方向反转（图5h）[107]。唯象模型：上述原子尺度图像揭示了极化反转的物理起源；然而，在实际器件应用中，Hf-FEs薄膜的宏观开关动力学和可靠性强烈依赖于其多晶性、缺陷分布、相界无序和空间不均匀性。为量化这些复杂行为，一系列唯象模型被广泛采用，其中Kolmogorov–Avrami–Ishibashi（KAI）模型和成核限制开关（NLS）模型至关重要 [112, 113]。研究表明，多晶铪基铁电薄膜的开关主要由成核过程主导，畴壁运动受限。经典KAI模型描述的成核后自由横向扩展的开关图像在此通常不适用。实验观察到的非指数弛豫、展宽的开关时间分布和部分极化开关特性，与NLS模型的预测高度一致。该模型将薄膜视为具有分布开关时间的独立区域集合，其分布源于由晶界、缺陷和多相共存引起的局域电场涨落。Cristóbal Alessandri等人通过对HZO薄膜的实验表征和NLS模型拟合，成功提取了最小开关时间τ∞（~ 236 ns）、激活场Ea（~ 2.4 MV cm−1）和局域电场增强因子的统计分布f(η)。该分布通常符合广义贝塔分布，直接反映了薄膜的不均匀性 [63]。值得注意的是，外延薄膜与多晶薄膜的开关动力学存在显著差异。尽管外延薄膜表现出更高的成核密度，其开关速度却慢于多晶薄膜。这凸显了成核在Hf-FEs开关中的速率限制作用。然而，在接近激活场的高电场下，两种薄膜的开关动力学表现出NLS和KAI模型的收敛趋势。这表明在高场驱动下，局域势垒被“平滑”，开关趋近于均匀的本征极限 [114]。
<span class="hljs-strong">**domain wall**</span>: 畴壁，铁电体中分隔不同极化取向畴的界面；<span class="hljs-strong">**KAI model**</span>: Kolmogorov–Avrami–Ishibashi模型，描述铁电开关动力学的经典唯象模型；<span class="hljs-strong">**NLS model**</span>: Nucleation-Limited Switching model，成核限制开关模型，强调成核过程主导开关动力学的模型。

Furthermore, the temporal characteristics of hafnium-based ferroelectric devices, such as wake-up and fatigue, are also closely related to oxygen vacancy migration and its pinning/depinning effect on DWs. Wake-up corresponds to the gradual increase of the switchable region during electric field cycling, which is manifested as the evolution of two independent NLS contributions in dynamics. Fatigue, on the other hand, originates from the pinning of DWs due to defect accumulation, resulting in a decrease in switching speed and a reduction in saturated polarization [115]. The dynamics of these cyclic evolutions can also be described and fitted using phenomenological models based on statistical distributions. In summary, the integration of macroscopic phenomenological models with first-principles computing and atomistic simulations constitutes a multi-scale framework for understanding the unique switching behavior of hafnium-based ferroelectrics. This framework not only reveals the correlation between its microscopic origins and macroscopic manifestations but also provides critical theoretical tools and practical guidance for optimizing material microstructures, predicting device endurance, and designing reliable operation schemes.
此外，铪基铁电器件的时间特性，如唤醒和疲劳，也与氧空位迁移及其对DWs的钉扎/脱钉效应密切相关。唤醒对应于电场循环中可开关区域的逐渐增加，在动力学上表现为两个独立NLS贡献的演化。疲劳则源于缺陷积累导致的DWs钉扎，造成开关速度下降和饱和极化减小 [115]。这些循环演化的动力学也可通过基于统计分布的唯象模型进行描述和拟合。总之，宏观唯象模型与第一性原理计算和原子尺度模拟的结合，构成了理解铪基铁电体独特开关行为的多尺度框架。该框架不仅揭示了其微观起源与宏观表现之间的关联，还为优化材料微观结构、预测器件耐久性以及设计可靠的操作方案提供了关键的理论工具和实践指导。

<span class="hljs-section">#### 2.3.3 Mainstream Theories and Controversies</span>
<span class="hljs-section">#### 2.3.3 主流理论与争议</span>
The physical origin of ferroelectricity in HfO2 remains a frontier topic of academic discussion. Currently, several competing theoretical perspectives exist, which collectively constitute a diverse framework for understanding this phenomenon. Phonon-band-driven mechanism: Starting from the microscopic nature of lattice dynamics, this mechanism posits that soft modes exist in the phonon spectrum of certain crystal phases of HfO2, such as the cubic or tetragonal phase. Specifically, the frequency of certain vibrational modes decreases with temperature or stress and tends toward zero, leading to lattice instability and acentric distortion, thereby inducing a ferroelectric phase transition. First-principles computing indicates that doping or applying strain can modulate these soft mode behaviors, enabling the dynamic stabilization of the energetically metastable acentric orthorhombic phase (Pca21) [116]. This mechanism emphasizes the intrinsic origin of ferroelectricity, providing a microscopic theoretical basis for understanding the macroscopic effects of doping and strain. The ferroelectricity observed in the pioneering experiments by Böscke et al. in 2011 can be regarded as a macroscopic manifestation of this mechanism realized in specific materials [35, 117]. Defect-mediated mechanism: Unlike the previous mechanism that focuses on perfect lattices, this mechanism emphasizes the critical role of point defects, especially oxygen vacancies. Oxygen vacancies affect ferroelectricity in two ways: first, as a source of electrostatic fields, their ordering can generate a built-in electric field that induces local polarization; second, as charge traps, their charging and discharging processes can assist or modulate the switching of macroscopic polarization [44, 118]. Experimentally observed "wake-up effect" frequency dependence, and fatigue characteristics strongly point to the dynamic behavior of oxygen vacancies [37]. Computing studies further reveal that the presence of oxygen vacancies can significantly change phase stability and reduce the formation energy of the ferroelectric phase. This mechanism primarily elucidates the dynamic evolution of ferroelectricity, reliability concerns, and its extreme sensitivity to preparation conditions. Interface and size constraint mechanism: This mechanism starts from the macroscopic thermodynamics of the thin film system, pointing out that at the nanometer scale, the contributions of surface energy and interface energy to the total free energy become crucial. Specific top/bottom electrode materials can alter the relative stability of each phase through interfacial stress, chemical bonding, and interfacial charge. This thermodynamically screens and stabilizes metastable ferroelectric o-phases that are difficult to exist in bulk materials [55]. Epitaxial strain experiments have successfully stabilized novel ferroelectric phases [119], providing direct evidence for this. This mechanism mainly explains why ferroelectricity only appears in ultra-thin films and its strong dependence on electrodes and thickness. Currently, a single mechanism is insufficient to explain all experimental phenomena. The gradually forming consensus in the field is that the ferroelectricity of HfO2 originates from a multi-scale and multi-physical field coupling synergistic process. Intrinsic instability driven by the phonon band constitutes the physical basis for the existence of the ferroelectric phase. Interface and size constraints provide a thermodynamic stabilization pathway at the nanometer scale. Oxygen vacancies dominate the polarization reversal behavior, activation process, and reliability at the kinetic level. Recent studies have clearly indicated that its ferroelectricity is the result of the combined action of "metastable phase stabilization", "defect engineering", and "interface modulation" [3, 53].
HfO2中铁电性的物理起源仍是学术讨论的前沿话题。目前存在几种相互竞争的理论观点，共同构成了理解该现象的多元化框架。声子带驱动机制：该机制从晶格动力学的微观本质出发，认为HfO2的某些晶相（如立方相或四方相）的声子谱中存在软模。具体而言，某些振动模式的频率随温度或应力降低并趋于零，导致晶格失稳和偏心畸变，从而诱发铁电相变。第一性原理计算表明，掺杂或施加应变可调控这些软模行为，实现能量上亚稳态的偏心正交相（Pca21）的动态稳定 [116]。该机制强调铁电性的本征起源，为理解掺杂和应变的宏观效应提供了微观理论基础。Böscke等人2011年开创性实验中观察到的铁电性，可视为该机制在特定材料中实现的宏观表现 [35, 117]。缺陷介导机制：与前述关注完美晶格的机制不同，该机制强调点缺陷，特别是氧空位的关键作用。氧空位通过两种方式影响铁电性：首先，作为静电场源，其有序化可产生内建电场，诱导局域极化；其次，作为电荷陷阱，其充放电过程可辅助或调制宏观极化的开关 [44, 118]。实验观察到的“唤醒效应”频率依赖性和疲劳特性，强烈指向氧空位的动态行为 [37]。计算研究进一步揭示，氧空位的存在可显著改变相稳定性并降低铁电相的形成能。该机制主要阐明铁电性的动态演化、可靠性问题及其对制备条件的极端敏感性。界面与尺寸约束机制：该机制从薄膜系统的宏观热力学出发，指出在纳米尺度，表面能和界面能对总自由能的贡献变得至关重要。特定的顶/底电极材料可通过界面应力、化学键合和界面电荷改变各相的相对稳定性。这在热力学上筛选并稳定了难以在体材料中存在的亚稳态铁电o相 [55]。外延应变实验已成功稳定了新型铁电相 [119]，为此提供了直接证据。该机制主要解释为何铁电性仅出现在超薄膜中及其对电极和厚度的强依赖性。目前，单一机制难以解释所有实验现象。领域内逐渐形成的共识是，HfO2的铁电性源于多尺度、多物理场耦合的协同过程。声子带驱动的本征不稳定性构成了铁电相存在的物理基础。界面与尺寸约束在纳米尺度提供了热力学稳定途径。氧空位在动力学层面主导极化反转行为、激活过程和可靠性。近期研究已明确指出，其铁电性是“亚稳态相稳定”、“缺陷工程”和“界面调制”共同作用的结果 [3, 53]。
<span class="hljs-strong">**soft mode**</span>: 软模，频率随温度降低而趋于零的晶格振动模式，常与相变相关。

<span class="hljs-section">### 2.4 Performance</span>
<span class="hljs-section">### 2.4 性能</span>
Hf-FEs thin films achieve high-performance ferroelectric devices compatible with CMOS technology by stabilizing the o-phase. These films offer core advantages, including high remanent polarization, ultrafast polarization switching, and excellent endurance, thus providing a physical basis for novel non-volatile memory technologies. Regarding basic electrical responses, polarization–electric field loops serve as the core basis for characterizing ferroelectricity, directly confirming the presence of strong ferroelectric polarization in the material. As shown in Fig. 6a, under a driving voltage of ± 4 V, the device exhibits a maximum polarization greater than 20 μC cm−2 and a remanent polarization of 10 μC cm−2, with the coercive field being less than 1.5 MV cm−1, thus meeting the requirements for low-power operation [80]. Beyond the static polarization characteristics, the dynamic current–voltage (I–V) response is crucial for understanding the read–write mechanism. The I–V curves reveal transient current peaks associated with polarization switching. As shown in Fig. 6b, the polarization increases with an increasing electric field and decreases with a decreasing electric field, but it does not completely return to its initial state. This characteristic demonstrates the inherent memory function of ferroelectric materials. The results demonstrate that the device exhibits self-rectifying characteristics and resistance switching capabilities, which are extremely beneficial for storage devices in cross arrays by effectively suppressing crosstalk [120]. Capacitance–voltage (C–V) testing is indispensable for further evaluating the dielectric behavior and operating range of the device. The C–V response exhibits a symmetrical "butterfly" shape, with the peak of the dielectric constant corresponding to the critical point of the ferroelectric phase transition, thus providing a basis for designing the operating window of the device. As shown in Fig. 6c, the total capacitance of this superlattice is greater than that of traditional anti-ferroelectric ZrO2 and ferroelectric Zr:HfO2 of the same thickness, further indicating the characteristics of the hybrid ferroelectric-anti-ferroelectric ordering. This observation demonstrates the superior performance of the HfO2-ZrO2 superlattice stack [1]. Leakage current performance is a key indicator for evaluating the reliability of non-volatile memory devices. Pulsed measurements of leakage current density are employed to assess the read/write functionality, which aids in investigating their storage characteristics. The test results under a single read/write cycle (Fig. 6d) reveal that the read current remains stable across all write voltages. This confirms the data nonvolatility and highlights the excellent storage properties of the Hf-FEs devices [121]. In terms of dynamic switching performance, the nanosecond level polarization switching speed is a hallmark advantage of Hf-FEs devices. As reported in Fig. 6e, the HZO device achieved τ1 ≈ 5.3 ns when its area was reduced to 9 μm2. Furthermore, the switching time of HZO thin film is predicted to potentially be shortened to the sub-nanosecond range, exceeding the performance of traditional PZT devices by 1–2 orders of magnitude [122]. Reliability assessment is a key metric for evaluating the performance of Hf-FEs devices. Firstly, thermal stability ensures device reliability under complex environmental conditions, as shown in Fig. 6f, Ce-doped HfO2 thin film at + 10 mm and − 10 mm exhibits exceptional thermal stability. Their ferroelectric performance remains stable even at 150 °C, highlighting their excellent high-temperature tolerance [123]. Secondly, endurance determines the device’s operational lifetime, as illustrated in Fig. 6g, revealing that work function-engineered Au/Ti/HZO/W capacitors possess outstanding stability: negligible degradation of ± Ps (&lt; 5%) and ± Pr (&lt; 7%) is observed after 109 cycles at an electric field of 3 MV cm−1. This performance surpasses that of symmetric W/HZO/W devices (with degradations of 8% and 12%, respectively) [58]. Furthermore, uniformity in device performance is crucial for large-scale array integration. Fig. 6h displays the cumulative probability of on- and off-state currents for 10 cells over 10 cycles. The on/off current ratio distribution was obtained from 10 DC cycles of a single FTJ device. The coefficients of variation for the on and off states are 0.405 and 0.276, respectively. These small variations are beneficial for implementing synaptic devices, ensuring operational consistency [124]. Finally, retention testing is central to verifying data non-volatility, as presented in Fig. 6i, a retention time &gt; 104 s is maintained under a ± 3 V bias voltage, confirming their long-term stability for nonvolatile memory applications [4]. The reliability of Hf-FEs devices is supported by three pillars: thermal stability, endurance, and uniformity. These pillars are deeply interconnected and exert mutual constraints. Thermal stability serves as the physical foundation for device functionality, determining the intrinsic robustness of the ferroelectric orthorhombic phase under thermal stress. Its quality directly restricts data retention capabilities in high-temperature environments. Endurance reflects the device’s ability to survive under dynamic operation. The accumulation and annihilation of oxygen vacancies at the interface during cyclic polarization directly affect the decay rate of polarization strength and the device’s operational lifetime. Uniformity, as a critical bridge from single devices to large-scale arrays, whose coefficient of variation not only determines the circuit’s design margin and energy consumption efficiency but also restricts the overall system’s reliability ceiling through the "weakest unit" effect. Superior thermal stability provides a foundation for high endurance, while sophisticated interface engineering, such as work function layer design, can improve the uniformity of electrical properties in addition to increasing endurance. Therefore, a true breakthrough in reliability depends on the collaborative optimization of material phase stability, interface defect engineering, and process uniformity control. This approach overcomes the limitations of focusing on a single performance metric and achieves a systematic improvement in the overall device reliability.
Hf-FEs薄膜通过稳定o相，实现了与CMOS技术兼容的高性能铁电器件。这些薄膜具有高剩余极化、超快极化开关和优异耐久性等核心优势，为新型非易失性存储技术提供了物理基础。在基本电学响应方面，极化-电场回线是表征铁电性的核心依据，直接证实材料中存在强铁电极化。如图6a所示，在±4 V驱动电压下，器件展现出大于20 μC cm−2的最大极化和10 μC cm−2的剩余极化，矫顽场小于1.5 MV cm−1，满足低功耗工作要求 [80]。除静态极化特性外，动态电流-电压（I-V）响应对理解读写机制至关重要。I-V曲线揭示了与极化开关相关的瞬态电流峰。如图6b所示，极化随电场增加而增大，随电场减小而减小，但不会完全回到初始状态。这一特性证明了铁电材料固有的存储功能。结果表明，该器件具有自整流特性和电阻开关能力，通过有效抑制串扰，对交叉阵列中的存储器件极为有利 [120]。电容-电压（C-V）测试对于进一步评估器件的介电行为和工作范围不可或缺。C-V响应呈现对称的“蝴蝶”形状，介电常数峰值对应铁电相变临界点，从而为设计器件工作窗口提供依据。如图6c所示，该超晶格的总电容大于相同厚度的传统反铁电ZrO2和铁电Zr:HfO2，进一步表明了混合铁电-反铁电有序的特征。这一观察结果证明了HfO2-ZrO2超晶格堆叠的优越性能 [1]。漏电流性能是评估非易失性存储器件可靠性的关键指标。采用漏电流密度脉冲测量评估读写功能，有助于研究其存储特性。单个读写周期下的测试结果（图6d）表明，在所有写入电压下，读取电流保持稳定。这证实了数据非易失性，凸显了Hf-FEs器件优异的存储性能 [121]。在动态开关性能方面，纳秒级极化开关速度是Hf-FEs器件的标志性优势。如图6e报道，当面积缩小至9 μm2时，HZO器件实现了τ1 ≈ 5.3 ns。此外，HZO薄膜的开关时间预计可缩短至亚纳秒范围，超过传统PZT器件1–2个数量级 [122]。可靠性评估是衡量Hf-FEs器件性能的关键指标。首先，热稳定性确保器件在复杂环境条件下的可靠性，如图6f所示，Ce掺杂HfO2薄膜在+10 mm和−10 mm处表现出卓越的热稳定性。即使在150 °C下，其铁电性能仍保持稳定，凸显了其优异的高温耐受性 [123]。其次，耐久性决定器件的运行寿命，如图6g所示，揭示功函数工程化的Au/Ti/HZO/W电容具有出色的稳定性：在3 MV cm−1电场下循环109次后，±Ps（&lt;5%）和±Pr（&lt;7%）的退化可忽略不计。这一性能超过了对称的W/HZO/W器件（退化分别为8%和12%）[58]。此外，器件性能的均匀性对大规模阵列集成至关重要。图6h展示了10个单元在10个周期内开态和关态电流的累积概率。开关电流比分布由单个FTJ器件的10个直流周期获得。开态和关态的变异系数分别为0.405和0.276。这些较小的变异有利于实现突触器件，确保操作一致性 [124]。最后，保持测试是验证数据非易失性的核心，如图6i所示，在±3 V偏压下保持时间&gt; 104 s，证实了其在非易失性存储应用中的长期稳定性 [4]。Hf-FEs器件的可靠性由三大支柱支撑：热稳定性、耐久性和均匀性。这些支柱深度互联，相互制约。热稳定性是器件功能的物理基础，决定铁电正交相在热应力下的本征稳健性。其优劣直接制约高温环境下的数据保持能力。耐久性反映器件在动态操作下的存活能力。循环极化过程中界面氧空位的积累与湮灭，直接影响极化强度的衰减速率和器件的运行寿命。均匀性，作为从单个器件走向大规模阵列的关键桥梁，其变异系数不仅决定电路的设计裕度和能耗效率，还通过“最弱单元”效应制约整个系统的可靠性上限。卓越的热稳定性为高耐久性提供基础，而精细的界面工程，如功函数层设计，除了提高耐久性外，还可改善电学性能的均匀性。因此，可靠性的真正突破取决于材料相稳定性、界面缺陷工程和工艺均匀性控制的协同优化。这种方法克服了单一聚焦性能指标的局限，实现了器件整体可靠性的系统提升。
<span class="hljs-strong">**PZT**</span>: Pb(Zr,Ti)O3，锆钛酸铅，一种传统钙钛矿铁电材料；<span class="hljs-strong">**work function**</span>: 功函数，将电子从固体中移出所需的最小能量，影响金属-半导体接触特性。

<span class="hljs-section">### 2.5 Applications in Memory</span>
<span class="hljs-section">### 2.5 在存储器中的应用</span>
Hf-FEs materials possess compelling characteristics, notably their robust polarization switching at the nanoscale, outstanding endurance exceeding 1010 cycles, compatibility with mainstream CMOS processes, and tunable coercive field, positioning them as strong candidates for next-generation non-volatile memory. Based on these materials, novel memories using Hf-FEs are continuously being explored [125, 126], such as high-density, low-power ternary content addressable memory (TCAM) and advanced memory applications like 3D ferroelectric NAND (FeNAND) flash memory. TCAM is widely regarded as one of the most promising memory technologies, playing a crucial role in network and computing systems by enabling high-speed search operations [131–133]. Ni et al. proposed utilizing TCAM as an attention memory module, which avoids data movement by directly calculating the distance between query vectors and each stored entry within the memory. They designed a TCAM cell based on two FeFETs and constructed a 2× 2 prototype array (Fig. 7a). Tests in one-shot and few-shot learning tasks demonstrated that this ferroelectric TCAM method reduced the power consumption of a single memory search operation by a factor of 60 and decreased latency by a factor of 2700 [127]. In terms of improving energy efficiency, novel storage architectures such as combinational encoding content addressable memory (CECAM) have emerged in recent years. CECAM outperforms traditional TCAM in terms of content storage density. Figure 7b illustrates a CECAM cell based on ferroelectric HZO FeFET. Unlike traditional TCAM, which requires a pair of complementary switches per bit to store ’0’, ’1’, or ’don’t care’ states, this architecture eliminates the complementary pair structure. Instead, it employs 2N independent switches that enable the combinatorial encoding of any combination of N 0s and N 1s, thereby overcoming the limitations of bit-by-bit processing. This design, while enabling efficient parallel search, also boasts negligible standby power consumption and significantly reduced dynamic power consumption—particularly in mismatch scenarios [128].
Hf-FEs材料具有引人注目的特性，尤其是其在纳米尺度上的稳健极化开关、超过1010次循环的优异耐久性、与主流CMOS工艺的兼容性以及可调的矫顽场，使其成为下一代非易失性存储器的强有力候选者。基于这些材料，利用Hf-FEs的新型存储器不断被探索 [125, 126]，例如高密度、低功耗的三元内容可寻址存储器（TCAM）和3D铁电NAND（FeNAND）闪存等先进存储应用。TCAM被广泛认为是最有前途的存储技术之一，通过实现高速搜索操作，在网络和计算系统中发挥着关键作用 [131–133]。Ni等人提出利用TCAM作为注意力存储模块，通过直接计算查询向量与存储器内每个存储条目之间的距离来避免数据移动。他们设计了一种基于两个FeFET的TCAM单元，并构建了2×2原型阵列（图7a）。在单次和少次学习任务中的测试表明，这种铁电TCAM方法将单次存储搜索操作的功耗降低了60倍，延迟降低了2700倍 [127]。在提高能效方面，近年来出现了组合编码内容可寻址存储器（CECAM）等新型存储架构。CECAM在内容存储密度方面优于传统TCAM。图7b展示了一种基于铁电HZO FeFET的CECAM单元。与传统TCAM需要每位一对互补开关来存储“0”、“1”或“无关”状态不同，该架构取消了互补对结构。取而代之，它采用2N个独立开关，实现N个0和N个1任意组合的组合编码，从而克服了逐位处理的限制。该设计在实现高效并行搜索的同时，还具有极低的待机功耗和显著降低的动态功耗——尤其是在失配场景下 [128]。
<span class="hljs-strong">**TCAM**</span>: Ternary Content Addressable Memory，三元内容可寻址存储器，可存储“0”、“1”和“无关”三种状态的CAM；<span class="hljs-strong">**FeNAND**</span>: Ferroelectric NAND，铁电NAND闪存；<span class="hljs-strong">**CECAM**</span>: Combinational Encoding Content Addressable Memory，组合编码内容可寻址存储器。

Despite enhanced TCAM technologies, such as CECAM, showcasing the potential of ferroelectric materials in efficient, high-speed associative computing, the escalating demand for storage density driven by big data and AI applications underscores the need for more scalable solutions. Consequently, NAND flash memory architectures, which offer both ultra-high density and cost-effectiveness, have emerged as a significant candidate solution [134–136]. As illustrated in Fig. 7c, 2D FeNAND arrays represent a crucial step in transforming the FeFET concept into practical NAND memory technology. Its structure is similar to that of charge trapping NAND, where each basic unit comprises multiple series-connected FeFETs. These transistors share common source/drain diffusion regions and are individually controlled by separate word lines [130]. However, despite 2D FeNAND demonstrating promising potential in experimental verification, its planar geometry inherently limits its density when scaling to terabit and beyond storage capacities. To overcome this limitation and fully realize the high-density potential of FeNAND, vertical stacking must be achieved through three-dimensional integration. The excellent conformality of atomic layer deposition (ALD) for key materials, such as doped HfO2 and TiN, coupled with their robust thermal budget tolerance during the stacking process, makes Hf-FEs exceptionally well-suited for transitioning to a three-dimensional FeNAND architecture.
尽管如CECAM等增强型TCAM技术展示了铁电材料在高效、高速关联计算中的潜力，但大数据和AI应用对存储密度日益增长的需求凸显了对更可扩展解决方案的需求。因此，兼具超高密度和成本效益的NAND闪存架构已成为重要的候选方案 [134–136]。如图7c所示，2D FeNAND阵列是将FeFET概念转化为实用NAND存储技术的关键一步。其结构类似于电荷俘获型NAND，每个基本单元由多个串联的FeFET组成。这些晶体管共享公共源/漏扩散区，并由独立的字线控制 [130]。然而，尽管2D FeNAND在实验验证中展现出可喜的潜力，其平面几何结构在向太比特及更高存储容量扩展时本质上限制了密度。为克服这一限制并充分发挥FeNAND的高密度潜力，必须通过三维集成实现垂直堆叠。原子层沉积（ALD）对掺杂HfO2和TiN等关键材料的优异保形性，以及它们在堆叠过程中稳健的热预算耐受性，使Hf-FEs特别适合向三维FeNAND架构过渡。
<span class="hljs-strong">**ALD**</span>: Atomic Layer Deposition，原子层沉积，一种以单原子层逐层生长薄膜的精密沉积技术。

3D FeNAND employs vertically stacked memory holes or trench arrays. In these high aspect ratio structures, gate-all-around or double-gate configurations are commonly adopted, featuring cylindrical or slit-shaped polysilicon channels surrounded by ferroelectric gate stacks. This multilayer design significantly improves the bit density per unit area. Kim et al. demonstrated Hf-FEs transistors using polysilicon gate electrodes, achieving a large memory window of 2.2 V, a fast switching speed of 10 ns, and a high endurance of 107 cycles (Fig. 7d) [129].
3D FeNAND采用垂直堆叠的存储孔或沟槽阵列。在这些高深宽比结构中，通常采用栅极全包围或双栅配置，具有被铁电栅堆叠包围的圆柱形或狭缝形多晶硅沟道。这种多层设计显著提高了单位面积的位密度。Kim等人展示了使用多晶硅栅电极的Hf-FEs晶体管，实现了2.2 V的大存储窗口、10 ns的快速开关速度和107次循环的高耐久性（图7d）[129]。

<span class="hljs-section">## 3 Neuromorphic Computing Based on Hf‐FEs</span>
<span class="hljs-section">## 3 基于Hf-FEs的神经形态计算</span>
<span class="hljs-section">### 3.1 Synaptic Plasticity</span>
<span class="hljs-section">### 3.1 突触可塑性</span>
With the exponential growth of data generation and processing volume, modern computing systems based on the traditional von Neumann architecture have become inadequate. Neuromorphic computing, inspired by the organization and function of the human brain, is widely regarded as a promising next-generation AI technology due to its remarkable biomimetic characteristics in computational parallelism, energy consumption, and system scalability [137]. Given the bio-like device behavior of ferroelectric devices, they are employed to emulate key biological structures in neuromorphic computing, such as synapses and neurons [14, 138–142]. Hf-FEs synaptic devices perfectly meet the core requirements of neuromorphic computing for a hardware platform, owing to their seamless CMOS process integration, emulation of polymorphic behavior, low-power consumption, and non-volatility. Synaptic plasticity describes the ability to emulate biological synaptic functions. Ferroelectric synaptic devices exhibit inherent structural homology with biological synapses (Fig. 8a). Combined with analogous information processing mechanisms, they enable precise emulation of synaptic plasticity—the neurobiological foundation of learning and memory. The core of implementing biological synapse emulation based on Hf-FEs synaptic devices lies in directly utilizing the non-volatile multilevel polarization states of ferroelectric materials to simulate the continuous change of weights. Through precise doping and interface modulation, Hf-FEs can maintain stable ferroelectricity at an ultra-thin scale, and its polarization vector can undergo partial and continuous reversal under the action of an applied electric field. This characteristic is manifested at the device level as the progressive adjustment of the remnant polarization of the ferroelectric layer, which in turn linearly modulates the channel conductance or tunnel junction resistance. Applying a short voltage pulse that simulates an action potential can induce a small change in the polarization states, thereby achieving gradual changes in conductance similar to long-term potentiation/depression (LTP/LTD). Meanwhile, the high coercive field and optimized endurance ensure the non-volatile retention of weights and reliable cycle tolerance, which is the physical basis for constructing learnable, low-power artificial neural network hardware. Hf-FEs synaptic devices exhibit extremely high endurance, meaning that they can withstand highly frequent weight updates without performance degradation. Furthermore, the ultra-thin nature of Hf-FEs materials allows for lower operating voltages in these synaptic devices, thus reducing computing power consumption. Compared with traditional ferroelectric materials, Hf-FEs materials offer distinct advantages in artificial synapse applications, and Hf-FEs synaptic devices are continually being developed and implemented. Tong et al. reported a ferroelectric memristor with optoelectronic synaptic properties that generate excitatory postsynaptic currents (EPSC) upon stimulation by single UV light pulses (Fig. 8b) [144]. The paired-pulse facilitation/depression (PPF/PPD) phenomenon, a core mechanism of short-term plasticity in biological synapses, is achieved in ferroelectric neuromorphic devices through polarization relaxation dynamics (Fig. 8c) [145]. Repeated pulse trains can induce LTP/LTD in synaptic devices, resulting in persistent conductance increase/decrease that impacts neural network recognition accuracy (Fig. 8d) [5]. Mirroring biological synapses, where memories are classified as short-term (STM) or long-term (LTM) with interconversion potential, pulse amplitude, width, and frequency significantly influence postsynaptic current (PSC) values. These correspond to: spike-amplitude-dependent plasticity (SADP), spike-width-dependent plasticity (SWDP), and spike-rate-dependent plasticity (SRDP). As shown in Fig. 8e-g, pulses modulate device conductance to drive memory transitions between STM and LTM [146]. Spike-timing-dependent plasticity (STDP), a fundamental biological synaptic property, modifies synaptic strength based on the relative timing of pre- and postsynaptic action potentials. This bidirectional plasticity within temporal windows allows synapses to dynamically adjust connection strength according to neuronal activity correlations, a critical neurobiological basis for learning and memory (Fig. 8h) [147]. Furthermore, as biological brains adapt and learn from external stimuli, researchers actively replicate complex synaptic behaviors. Synaptic device experiments now focus on emulating advanced learning capabilities, with Fig. 8i demonstrating learning-forgetting-relearning behavior [144]. Achieving the aforementioned synaptic plasticity behaviors places specific demands on the key performance indicators of Hf-FEs devices. For instance, LTP/LTD requires devices to possess continuous, linear, and symmetric conductance modulation characteristics. This typically necessitates that Hf-FEs thin films exhibit high remnant polarization (Pr &gt; 20 μC cm−2) and moderate coercive field (Ec ≈ 1–2 MV cm−1) to achieve low-energy consumption (106 cycles) weight updates. For STDP, the switching speed of the device needs to reach the nanosecond level (τ &lt; 10 ns) to precisely match the time window of biological pulses. Recent studies have demonstrated that two-terminal AFE HZO capacitors based on work function engineering can achieve stable non-volatility resistive states (retention &gt; 10 years) under programming pulses of ± 2.1 V. They also exhibit excellent cycling endurance (&gt; 109 cycles) and low-energy consumption for synaptic function, down to 245fJ spike−1 [58]. These parameters lay the foundation for realizing low-power, highly reliable analog biological synapse functions. However, compared to ideal synaptic devices, Hf-FEs devices still have room for improvement in terms of linearity of conductance modulation, device-to-device uniformity, and low-voltage operation. These factors directly affect the accuracy and energy efficiency of network training.
随着数据生成和处理量的指数级增长，基于传统冯·诺依曼架构的现代计算系统已显不足。神经形态计算受人脑组织和功能启发，因其在计算并行性、能耗和系统可扩展性方面的显著仿生特性，被广泛认为是一种有前景的下一代AI技术 [137]。鉴于铁电器件类生物的行为，它们被用于模拟神经形态计算中的关键生物结构，如突触和神经元 [14, 138–142]。Hf-FEs突触器件凭借其无缝的CMOS工艺集成、多态行为模拟、低功耗和非易失性，完美满足了神经形态计算对硬件平台的核心要求。突触可塑性描述的是模拟生物突触功能的能力。铁电突触器件与生物突触具有固有的结构同源性（图8a）。结合类似的信息处理机制，它们能够精确模拟突触可塑性——学习与记忆的神经生物学基础。基于Hf-FEs突触器件实现生物突触模拟的核心在于，直接利用铁电材料的非易失性多级极化状态来模拟权重的连续变化。通过精确掺杂和界面调制，Hf-FEs可在超薄尺度下保持稳定的铁电性，其极化矢量在外加电场作用下可发生部分且连续的翻转。这一特性在器件层面表现为铁电层剩余极化的渐进式调节，进而线性调制沟道电导或隧道结电阻。施加模拟动作电位的短电压脉冲可诱导极化状态的微小变化，从而实现类似长时程增强/抑制（LTP/LTD）的电导渐变。同时，高矫顽场和优化的耐久性确保了权重的非易失性保持和可靠的循环耐受性，这是构建可学习、低功耗人工神经网络硬件的物理基础。Hf-FEs突触器件展现出极高的耐久性，意味着它们能够承受高频次的权重更新而不发生性能退化。此外，Hf-FEs材料的超薄特性允许这些突触器件以更低的工作电压运行，从而降低计算功耗。与传统铁电材料相比，Hf-FEs材料在人工突触应用中具有明显优势，Hf-FEs突触器件正不断被开发和实现。Tong等人报道了一种具有光电突触特性的铁电忆阻器，在单次紫外光脉冲刺激下产生兴奋性突触后电流（EPSC）（图8b）[144]。双脉冲易化/抑制（PPF/PPD）现象是生物突触短时程可塑性的核心机制，通过极化弛豫动力学在铁电神经形态器件中实现（图8c）[145]。重复脉冲序列可诱导突触器件的LTP/LTD，导致持续的电导增加/减少，影响神经网络识别精度（图8d）[5]。与生物突触类似，记忆被分为短时程（STM）和长时程（LTM），并具有相互转化的潜力，脉冲幅度、宽度和频率显著影响突触后电流（PSC）值。这些对应于：脉冲幅度依赖可塑性（SADP）、脉冲宽度依赖可塑性（SWDP）和脉冲频率依赖可塑性（SRDP）。如图8e-g所示，脉冲调制器件电导，驱动记忆在STM和LTM之间转换 [146]。脉冲时间依赖可塑性（STDP）是一种基本的生物突触特性，根据前突触和后突触动作电位的相对时序修改突触强度。这种时间窗口内的双向可塑性使突触能够根据神经元活动相关性动态调整连接强度，是学习和记忆的关键神经生物学基础（图8h）[147]。此外，由于生物大脑能适应外部刺激并学习，研究人员积极复制复杂的突触行为。突触器件实验目前专注于模拟高级学习能力，图8i展示了学习-遗忘-再学习行为 [144]。实现上述突触可塑性行为对Hf-FEs器件的关键性能指标提出了特定要求。例如，LTP/LTD要求器件具有连续、线性和对称的电导调制特性。这通常需要Hf-FEs薄膜展现出高剩余极化（Pr &gt; 20 μC cm−2）和适中的矫顽场（Ec ≈ 1–2 MV cm−1），以实现低功耗（106次循环）的权重更新。对于STDP，器件的开关速度需达到纳秒级（τ &lt; 10 ns），以精确匹配生物脉冲的时间窗口。近期研究表明，基于功函数工程的双端AFE HZO电容可在±2.1 V的编程脉冲下实现稳定的非易失性电阻态（保持&gt; 10年）。它们还表现出优异的循环耐久性（&gt; 109次循环）和低至245 fJ spike−1的突触功能能耗 [58]。这些参数为实现低功耗、高可靠性的模拟生物突触功能奠定了基础。然而，与理想突触器件相比，Hf-FEs器件在电导调制线性度、器件间均匀性和低电压工作方面仍有改进空间。这些因素直接影响网络训练的精度和能效。
<span class="hljs-strong">**LTP/LTD**</span>: Long-Term Potentiation/Depression，长时程增强/抑制，突触可塑性的一种形式，表现为突触传递效率的持续增强或减弱；<span class="hljs-strong">**STDP**</span>: Spike-Timing-Dependent Plasticity，脉冲时间依赖可塑性，根据前、后突触脉冲时序调整突触权重的规则；<span class="hljs-strong">**EPSC**</span>: Excitatory Postsynaptic Current，兴奋性突触后电流；<span class="hljs-strong">**PPF/PPD**</span>: Paired-Pulse Facilitation/Depression，双脉冲易化/抑制；<span class="hljs-strong">**SADP**</span>: Spike-Amplitude-Dependent Plasticity，脉冲幅度依赖可塑性；<span class="hljs-strong">**SWDP**</span>: Spike-Width-Dependent Plasticity，脉冲宽度依赖可塑性；<span class="hljs-strong">**SRDP**</span>: Spike-Rate-Dependent Plasticity，脉冲频率依赖可塑性；<span class="hljs-strong">**AFE**</span>: Antiferroelectric，反铁电性。

<span class="hljs-section">### 3.2 Neuromorphic Computing</span>
<span class="hljs-section">### 3.2 神经形态计算</span>
The essence of neuromorphic computing lies in emulating the structure and mechanisms of biological neural systems to enable highly energy-efficient and highly parallel information processing. Experimentally validated synaptic plasticity mechanisms, particularly the dynamic behavior of STDP and its capability for multimodal weight regulation, provide a physical basis for constructing large-scale neural networks. These networks, composed of layers of neurons interconnected via synapses, have attracted considerable attention due to their ability to significantly reduce computation time in various classification tasks [148, 149]. In contrast to software-based neural networks operating on conventional silicon chips, which incur high data transfer energy consumption caused by the separation of computing and memory, neuromorphic systems built on Hf-FEs synaptic devices realize in-memory computing. Each ferroelectric synaptic unit performs both storage and computing functions simultaneously, featuring a high degree of parallelism that significantly enhances energy efficiency and computational speed [150–152]. Neural networks built upon ideal synaptic units facilitate the development of neuromorphic systems that offer high parallel processing capability and excellent energy efficiency. For example, a single-layer perceptron (SLP) was used for MNIST handwritten digit recognition (Fig. 9a). Its input layer consisted of 784 neurons (corresponding to 28 × 28 pixel images), and the output layer contained 10 neurons to represent different digit categories. This configuration achieved a classification accuracy of 90% (Fig. 9b), comparable to theoretical performance [61]. The SLP’s strengths lie in its simple structure and efficient training, rendering it especially well-suited for fundamental pattern recognition tasks. Considering that images in real-world scenarios often contain noise, which can adversely affect classification performance, the noise robustness of the network is critically important. As shown in Fig. 9c, different types and intensities of noise progressively reduce recognition accuracy. Against this background, the spiking neural network (SNN) demonstrates superior adaptability. The SNN model proposed by Heng et al. (Fig. 9d) achieved nearly 98% accuracy in just 50 training epochs and maintained over 90% performance even under strong noise interference. This highlights its practical utility in environments with fluctuating image quality [153]. SNN, through its spike-timing encoding and processing mechanisms, exhibits significant robustness in noisy environments. Furthermore, for more complex image recognition tasks, the VGG-11 network was used to evaluate the applicability of FTJ synaptic units in a convolutional neural network (CNN). The VGG-11 network as shown in Fig. 9e comprises eight convolutional layers, five max-pooling layers, and two fully connected layers. This configuration allows for the effective extraction of multilevel features, making it well-suited for high-precision classification scenarios [154]. In neuromorphic systems based on hafnium ferroelectric devices, different network architectures place varying performance demands on the underlying hardware due to their characteristic differences. Hf-FEs devices must meet these demands through their specific physical properties to achieve efficient computing. The simple structure of the SLP primarily requires non-volatile storage of weights and stable one-write, multiple-read operations. The inherent ferroelectric non-volatility and programmable multilevel polarization states of Hf-FEs devices enable them to reliably store and maintain the weights of the SLP. This characteristic makes the SLP suitable for low-power linear classification and rapid prototyping, despite its limited processing capabilities. The core requirements of a CNN are high-parallelism multiply-accumulate (MAC) operations and efficient energy utilization to process structured data such as images. Hf-FEs devices achieve in-memory computing through crossbar array architecture, completing vector–matrix multiplication (VMM) directly in the analog domain by using Ohm’s law and Kirchhoff’s laws. This avoids the data transfer bottleneck in the traditional von Neumann architecture and achieves high-energy efficiency inference. The event-driven and asynchronous computing mechanisms of SNNs require hardware synapses to have the ability to respond to precise pulse timing and maintain robustness in noisy environments. The dynamic polarization relaxation characteristics of Hf-FEs devices naturally align with the pulse timing dynamics of SNNs. Experiments have demonstrated that Hf-FEs devices can simulate complex dynamic behaviors such as STDP, which gives them an advantage in time series signal processing and low-power edge computing. The ability of SNNs to maintain high recognition rates in noisy environments is also partly due to the pulse timing-based computing paradigm of Hf-FEs devices. However, realizing these advantages requires devices to have good switching speed consistency and controllable dynamic responses, which places higher demands on materials and interface engineering. Overall, the selection of the network architecture must be deeply matched with the core physical properties of Hf-FEs devices and optimized based on specific application scenarios through trade-offs [11, 157, 158]. At the hardware level, Hf-FEs exhibit substantial potential for implementing non-volatile storage and neuromorphic computing. In contrast to conventional silicon chips based on the von Neumann architecture, which rely on software emulation, incur high-power consumption from data transfer, and face limitations due to the "memory wall", Hf-FEs devices natively support in-memory computing at the physical level owing to their ferroelectric properties. A major strength of these devices is their ability to directly encode neural network weights into the conductance values of devices organized in crossbar arrays, enabling efficient analog VMM to be performed in a single operation. This approach completely bypasses the data movement bottleneck, resulting in orders of magnitude enhancements in both energy efficiency and processing speed. Moreover, such devices can replicate the continuous plasticity observed in biological synapses, facilitate in situ learning, and maintain high compatibility with established CMOS process technologies. These characteristics allow the computing paradigm to be seamlessly incorporated into conventional silicon-based circuits, thereby opening a pathway for heterogeneous integration and co-design of memory, logic, and neuromorphic computing units on a single chip. Memristor-based computing technology holds great potential in overcoming the so-called "von Neumann bottleneck" inherent in traditional computing architectures. It offers a promising avenue for implementing efficient on-chip learning in various edge intelligence applications [159–162]. Zhang et al. fabricated a full system integrated chip implementing the STELLAR architecture, which integrates multiple memristor arrays with all necessary CMOS peripheral circuits. Using this memristor chip, four continuous learning tasks were implemented. In the MNIST image classification, after three stages of chip learning, the classification accuracies of the training set and the test set on this chip have increased from 8.6% and 8.4% to 94.9% and 92.3%, respectively. At the same time, the energy consumption of the memristor chip is 35 times lower than that of the system based on a digital accelerator [163]. VMM is a core computational task in signal and image processing. VMM operations can inherently be performed in the analog domain using a memristor crossbar array. These memristor crossbar arrays possess advantages such as reconfigurability, reasonable accuracy and precision in physical computing, as well as speed and energy efficiency. Therefore, large-scale memristor crossbar arrays are highly suitable for analog signal and image processing. Figure 9f shows micrographs of two 1T-1R memristor crossbar chips with array sizes ranging from 4 × 4 to 128 × 64 cells. The system has an equivalent 6-bit or 64-level precision and a device yield rate of 99.8%. The energy efficiency is over 119.7 trillion operations per watt, and it uses a 10 ns readout [48]. Ferroelectric thin film transistors (FeTFTs) have attracted extensive attention in in-memory computing applications due to their low-power consumption and potential for monolithic 3D integration. Yang et al. fabricated FeTFT arrays with MFMIS structure units through BEOL processes. They experimentally demonstrated the full hardware implementation of MLP using this array (Fig. 9g), providing a potential hardware solution for efficient in-memory computing systems based on highly reliable FeTFT arrays. The system based on FeTFTs has improved power efficiency by more than two orders of magnitude compared to typical CMOS systems [155]. Figure 9h presents the hardware implementation of the MUSAN edge detector based on a 4 × 4 FeFET NAND array. The system utilizes an FPGA board with an embedded processor to manage data transmission between the FeFET NAND array and the host, as well as to control the array’s peripheral circuits, thereby demonstrating significant energy efficiency advantages [156].

[154]。在基于铪铁电器件的神经形态系统中，不同的网络架构因其特性差异而对底层硬件提出不同的性能要求。Hf-FEs器件必须通过其特定的物理性质满足这些要求，以实现高效计算。SLP的简单结构主要要求权重的非易失性存储和稳定的单次写入、多次读取操作。Hf-FEs器件固有的铁电非易失性和可编程多级极化状态，使其能够可靠地存储并保持SLP的权重。这一特性使SLP适用于低功耗线性分类和快速原型验证，尽管其处理能力有限。CNN的核心要求是高并行度的乘加运算（MAC）和高效的能量利用，以处理图像等结构化数据。Hf-FEs器件通过交叉阵列架构实现存内计算，利用欧姆定律和基尔霍夫定律直接在模拟域完成向量-矩阵乘法（VMM）。这避免了传统冯·诺依曼架构中的数据传输瓶颈，实现了高能效推理。SNN的事件驱动和异步计算机制要求硬件突触具备响应精确脉冲时序并在噪声环境中保持鲁棒的能力。Hf-FEs器件的动态极化弛豫特性天然与SNN的脉冲时序动力学相契合。实验证明，Hf-FEs器件可模拟STDP等复杂动态行为，这使其在时序信号处理和低功耗边缘计算中具有优势。SNN在噪声环境中保持高识别率的能力，也部分归因于Hf-FEs器件基于脉冲时序的计算范式。然而，实现这些优势要求器件具有良好的开关速度一致性和可控的动态响应，这对材料和界面工程提出了更高要求。总体而言，网络架构的选择必须与Hf-FEs器件的核心物理性质深度匹配，并根据具体应用场景通过权衡进行优化 [11, 157, <span class="hljs-section">### 3.3 Image Process</span>
<span class="hljs-section">### 3.3 图像处理</span>
In neuromorphic computing, the core of image processing lies in efficiently executing basic operations such as convolution and feature extraction. The effectiveness of its hardware implementation directly determines the performance of the system when processing tasks like handwritten digit recognition and image classification. Unlike the traditional silicon-based von Neumann architecture, where storage and computing are separated, synaptic devices based on Hf-FEs materials can achieve true "sensing-storage-computing" integration. The physical basis of this advantage is rooted in the unique properties of Hf-FEs. Their CMOS compatibility allows for the construction of high-density, large-scale parallel arrays in back-end processing. Their continuously modulated multilevel polarization states enable the non-volatile storage of neural network weights in the conductance values of the devices in analog form. In specific operations, the input image pixels, acting as voltage signals, are simultaneously applied to the word lines of the arrays. The conductance, representing the weight, of each Hf-FEs device in the arrays generates a corresponding current according to Ohm’s law. These currents are naturally summed on the bit lines according to Kirchhoff’s law, completing the MAC operation between the entire image patch and the convolution kernel in one hardware step. This analog computing method, based on physical laws, fundamentally avoids frequent data transfer between the processor and the memory, greatly reducing data traffic and energy consumption, and achieving extremely high processing speed and low latency due to high parallelism. Therefore, the Hf-FEs image processing system is not only an architectural innovation but also a direct embodiment of its material’s intrinsic properties in the computing paradigm. Neural networks are most commonly used for image recognition tasks. Figure 10a, b illustrates two common artificial neural network (ANN) and CNN architectures employed for this task. ANN is a basic fully connected network. For image processing, ANNs require images to be flattened into a one-dimensional vector. This flattening destroys the image’s spatial structure, leads to a large number of parameters, and results in low efficiency, thereby limiting their applicability to simple tasks. CNN is a network specifically designed for images. At its core, a CNN comprises convolutional and pooling layers. These layers automatically extract local image features like edges and textures, which are then combined into higher level representations through a hierarchical structure. Image processing tasks, such as those involving CNNs, demand that hardware possess a high degree of parallelism and low energy consumption for MAC operations. This translates to the following requirements at the Hf-FEs device level: high-density integration (array density &gt; 108 devices cm−2), multilevel conductance states (≥ 64 levels) to achieve high-precision weight mapping, low operating voltage (&lt; 2 V) to reduce system power consumption, and good device uniformity. Recently, Kim et al. reported on a digital recognition analog reservoir computing system based on multi-petal boundary transistors (MPB-TFTs) (Fig. 10c). In this system, FeTFT-based synaptic devices are utilized, with input pulses applied to a physical reservoir constructed from dualgate MPB-TFTs. The resulting reservoir states are then fed into a readout network, and its output current is transmitted to leaky integrate-and-fire neurons based on dual-gate MPB-TFTs. These neurons generate the final output of the analog reservoir computing system. This system accurately distinguishes 10 types of handwritten digits and achieves a high classification accuracy (~ 90.23%). The MPB-TFT-based physical reservoir consumes 22.5 pJ per input, while the FeTFT-based synaptic device consumes 0.2 pJ per input [165]. In addition to FeTFT-based synaptic devices, ferroelectric memristors also offer significant advantages in neuromorphic computing. Cheng et al. demonstrated the application of HYO-based memristors in image encryption (Fig. 10d). The inherent random distribution of high resistance state and low resistance state within a specific range is leveraged to convert variable resistance values into digital keys for encrypting digital images. As a result, in the encrypted image, the pixel data distribution on the RGB channel becomes uniform, rendering the contained information unrecognizable, which indicates that the image features are effectively hidden [166]. Bayesian neural networks (BNNs) have emerged to address the lack of robust uncertainty quantification in traditional neural networks. BNNs model weights as probability distributions, which enables quantitative uncertainty assessment. This capability makes BNNs particularly suitable for safety–critical applications such as medical diagnosis. Building on this foundation, Song et al. developed a 3D FeNAND-based BNN system for medical data analysis. When classifying chest X-ray images to identify various respiratory diseases, the system achieved a significantly higher diagnostic accuracy through probabilistic predictions compared to other neural networks (Fig. 10e). The classification accuracy of this model can reach approximately 96%, and it has the ability to distinguish different categories even with noisy input data [167]. Edge detection is one of the most critical research focuses in computing vision. Chen et al. reported a low-power edge detection hardware system based on Hf-FEs FeFET (Fig. 10f). In contrast to conventional edge detection methods based on emerging non-volatile memory, a matching unary segment assimilation nucleus method is proposed, bypassing convolution operations. This approach enables efficient image edge detection with low-power consumption (10 fJ per operation) and realizes a hardware system characterized by lossless precision, extremely low-power consumption, and the absence of an analog-to-digital converter, thus providing a viable solution for edge computing [156].
在神经形态计算中，图像处理的核心在于高效执行卷积和特征提取等基本操作。其硬件实现的有效性直接决定了系统在处理手写数字识别和图像分类等任务时的性能。与存储和计算分离的传统硅基冯·诺依曼架构不同，基于Hf-FEs材料的突触器件可实现真正的“传感-存储-计算”一体化。这一优势的物理基础植根于Hf-FEs的独特性质。其CMOS兼容性允许在后端工艺中构建高密度、大规模并行阵列。其连续调制的多级极化状态使得神经网络权重能够以模拟形式非易失性地存储在器件的电导值中。在具体操作中，输入图像像素作为电压信号，同时施加到阵列的字线上。阵列中每个Hf-FEs器件的电导（代表权重）根据欧姆定律产生相应电流。这些电流在位线上根据基尔霍夫定律自然求和，在单个硬件步骤中完成整个图像块与卷积核的MAC运算。这种基于物理定律的模拟计算方法，从根本上避免了处理器与存储器之间的频繁数据搬运，极大减少了数据流量和能耗，并因高度并行性而实现了极高的处理速度和低延迟。因此，Hf-FEs图像处理系统不仅是架构创新，更是其材料本征特性在计算范式中的直接体现。神经网络最常用于图像识别任务。图10a、b展示了两种常用的用于此任务的人工神经网络（ANN）和CNN架构。ANN是一种基本的全连接网络。对于图像处理，ANN要求将图像展平为一维向量。这种展平破坏了图像的空间结构，导致参数数量大、效率低，从而限制了其仅在简单任务中的适用性。CNN是专门为图像设计的网络。CNN的核心由卷积层和池化层组成。这些层自动提取边缘和纹理等局部图像特征，然后通过层次结构组合成更高级别的表示。图像处理任务，如涉及CNN的任务，要求硬件具有高度的并行性和低能耗的MAC运算。这转化为对Hf-FEs器件层面的以下要求：高密度集成（阵列密度&gt; 108 器件 cm−2）、多级电导态（≥ 64级）以实现高精度权重映射、低工作电压（&lt; 2 V）以降低系统功耗，以及良好的器件均匀性。近期，Kim等人报道了一种基于多瓣边界晶体管（MPB-TFTs）的数字识别模拟储备池计算系统（图10c）。在该系统中，使用基于FeTFT的突触器件，将输入脉冲施加到由双栅MPB-TFTs构成的物理储备池。产生的储备池状态随后馈入读出网络，其输出电流被传输至基于双栅MPB-TFTs的泄漏积分-发放神经元。这些神经元产生模拟储备池计算系统的最终输出。该系统准确区分了10种手写数字，并实现了高分类准确率（约90.23%）。基于MPB-TFT的物理储备池每次输入消耗22.5 pJ，而基于FeTFT的突触器件每次输入消耗0.2 pJ [165]。除基于FeTFT的突触器件外，铁电忆阻器在神经形态计算中也具有显著优势。Cheng等人展示了基于HYO的忆阻器在图像加密中的应用（图10d）。利用高阻态和低阻态在特定范围内的固有随机分布，将可变电阻值转换为数字密钥，用于加密数字图像。结果，在加密图像中，RGB通道上的像素数据分布变得均匀，包含的信息无法识别，表明图像特征被有效隐藏 [166]。贝叶斯神经网络（BNNs）的出现解决了传统神经网络缺乏鲁棒不确定性量化的问题。BNNs将权重建模为概率分布，从而能够进行定量不确定性评估。这一能力使BNNs特别适用于医疗诊断等安全关键型应用。在此基础上，Song等人开发了一种基于3D FeNAND的BNN系统，用于医学数据分析。在对胸部X光图像进行分类以识别各种呼吸系统疾病时，该系统通过概率预测实现了显著高于其他神经网络的诊断准确率（图10e）。该模型的分类准确率可达约96%，并且即使在输入数据含噪声时也能区分不同类别 [167]。边缘检测是计算视觉中最关键的研究焦点之一。Chen等人报道了一种基于Hf-FEs FeFET的低功耗边缘检测硬件系统（图10f）。与基于新兴非易失性存储器的传统边缘检测方法不同，他们提出了一种匹配一元片段同化核方法，绕过了卷积运算。这种方法实现了低功耗（每次操作10 fJ）的高效图像边缘检测，并实现了一个以无损精度、极低功耗和无模数转换器为特征的硬件系统，从而为边缘计算提供了一种可行的解决方案 [156]。

<span class="hljs-section">### 3.4 Logical Operation</span>
<span class="hljs-section">### 3.4 逻辑运算</span>
Reconfigurable polarization states in Hf-FEs have significantly advanced the development of a new logic-in-memory (LiM) paradigm, which is fundamentally based on realizing basic operations using logic gates. LiM requires devices to have both non-volatile memory and reconfigurable logic functions, with core indicators including nanosecond switching speeds and low operating voltages. Hf-FEs logic devices have achieved switching speeds of 500 ps and endurance of &gt; 107 cycles, supporting dynamic reconfiguration of multi-input logic gates. The energy consumption per logic function can be as low as 1 fJ, which is 1–2 orders of magnitude lower than traditional CMOS logic gates. Traditionally, logic gates, which serve as fundamental units for executing Boolean operations like AND, OR, and NOT, have been primarily realized through combinations of CMOS transistors. While this conventional approach is mature and reliable, it presents challenges such as fixed circuit functionality and constrained energy efficiency, largely due to the data movement bottleneck inherent in the von Neumann architecture. In contrast, Hf-FEs utilize the non-volatility and electrically reconfigurable properties of their ferroelectric domains polarization states to dynamically realize diverse logic functions within a single hardware unit. This capability facilitates in-memory computing operations without the need for data migration, thereby fundamentally circumventing the memory wall limitation and substantially boosting both computing energy efficiency and operational flexibility [168–170]. Precise writing and erasing of logic states hinges on the switching dynamics of ferroelectric polarization. In the context of FTJs, Kho et al. discovered that switching between intermediate resistance states follows a "separated nucleation spot dependent on the polarity of the applied pulse" mechanism. Specifically, positive and negative polarity voltage pulses induce domain switching near the top and bottom electrodes, respectively. This leads to potentially different microscopic domain distributions under the same macroscopic resistance, which is crucial for achieving multi-bit precise programming [172]. As for FeFETs, optimizing the HZO thin film through phase engineering can significantly enhance the accessibility of intermediate polarization states and cumulative polarization characteristics, thereby improving the linearity and number of conductance state modulations [153]. Based on the mechanisms mentioned above, two types of efficient operation paradigms have been developed. In FeFETs/Fe-TFTs, Boolean logic functions can be dynamically reconfigured within a single device through pulse combination strategies. Liu et al. demonstrated an artificial synapse Fe-TFT based on a HfLaOx ferroelectric layer and an ultra-thin ITO channel. By introducing a dual-pulse strategy, they achieved reconfigurable Boolean logic operations within a single Fe-TFT device. The array-level implementation of AND and OR logic gates is shown in Fig. 11a [171]. In FTJs, Kho et al. utilized their V-R timing logic characteristics to achieve parallel 2-bit logic operations on a single device by designing multi-terminal input pulse coding. This approach synchronously generates the output of all 16 Boolean logic functions in just two steps, and the working principle is shown in Fig. 11b [172]. Device level functionalities are driving array-level and system-level applications. Liu et al. have demonstrated image-level parallel AND/OR logic operations based on FeFET arrays, showcasing their potential in analog visual computing. More importantly, through multi-gate FeFET structures, the capacitive coupling effect of different gate regions can be utilized to generate logic outputs corresponding to multiple inputs within a single transistor, thereby enabling reconfigurable logic functions such as inverters, NOR gates, and NAND gates with just a single device, significantly enhancing area efficiency. Xiang et al. reported a multi-gate FeFET suitable for LiM. The operating principle of the multi-terminal FeFET logic gates, along with the corresponding NOR and NAND gate operations, is illustrated in Fig. 11c. This device can be executed within a single clock pulse, and all operation results are stored. The output retention, compared to traditional CMOS-based circuits (the latter requires a constant-voltage power supply to maintain an ideal conductive state), improves the energy efficiency of the device [153]. In addition, Fang et al. have developed an optoelectronic memristor based on MXene materials and Y-doped HfO2 thin film. By exploiting the optoelectronic response behavior and different set thresholds, they constructed logic gates driven by optical and electrical inputs. This approach offers a viable pathway for optoelectronic artificial neuromorphic devices. Fig. 11d illustrates the analogy between the optoelectronic memristor used for logic operation and the classical NAND gate and presents experimental demonstrations of AND and OR operations [143]. In conclusion, hafnium-based ferroelectric logic operations technology has progressed beyond fundamental functional verification. Its core lies in leveraging a profound understanding of polarization dynamics and innovative device structures to achieve in-memory computing with high parallelism, reconfigurability, and sensing integration. This offers a critical hardware foundation for developing next-generation energy-efficient intelligent systems.
Hf-FEs中可重构的极化态显著推动了新型存内逻辑（LiM）范式的发展，该范式根本上基于使用逻辑门实现基本操作。LiM要求器件同时具备非易失性存储和可重构逻辑功能，核心指标包括纳秒级开关速度和低工作电压。Hf-FEs逻辑器件已实现500 ps的开关速度，耐久性&gt;107次循环，支持多输入逻辑门的动态重构。每次逻辑功能的能耗可低至1 fJ，比传统CMOS逻辑门低1–2个数量级。传统上，作为执行AND、OR和NOT等布尔运算基本单元的逻辑门，主要通过CMOS晶体管的组合来实现。这种传统方法虽成熟可靠，但由于冯·诺依曼架构固有的数据搬运瓶颈，存在电路功能固定、能效受限等挑战。相比之下，Hf-FEs利用其铁电畴极化态的非易失性和电可重构特性，在单个硬件单元内动态实现多种逻辑功能。这一能力使得无需数据迁移即可执行存内计算操作，从而从根本上规避了存储墙限制，大幅提升了计算能效和操作灵活性 [168–170]。逻辑态的精确写入和擦除取决于铁电极化的开关动力学。在FTJs中，Kho等人发现，中间电阻态之间的切换遵循“取决于施加脉冲极性的分离成核点”机制。具体而言，正、负极性电压脉冲分别诱导靠近顶电极和底电极的畴开关。这导致在相同宏观电阻下可能存在不同的微观畴分布，这对于实现多位精确编程至关重要 [172]。对于FeFETs，通过相工程优化HZO薄膜可显著增强中间极化态的可及性和累积极化特性，从而改善电导态调制的线性度和数量 [153]。基于上述机制，已发展出两种高效操作范式。在FeFETs/Fe-TFTs中，通过脉冲组合策略可在单个器件内动态重构布尔逻辑功能。Liu等人展示了一种基于HfLaOx铁电层和超薄ITO沟道的人工突触Fe-TFT。通过引入双脉冲策略，他们在单个Fe-TFT器件内实现了可重构布尔逻辑运算。AND和OR逻辑门的阵列级实现如图11a所示 [171]。在FTJs中，Kho等人利用其V-R时序逻辑特性，通过设计多端输入脉冲编码，在单个器件上实现了并行2位逻辑运算。该方法仅需两步即可同步生成所有16种布尔逻辑函数的输出，其工作原理如图11b所示 [172]。器件级功能正推动阵列级和系统级应用。Liu等人已展示了基于FeFET阵列的图像级并行AND/OR逻辑运算，展现了其在模拟视觉计算中的潜力。更重要的是，通过多栅FeFET结构，可利用不同栅极区域的电容耦合效应，在单个晶体管中产生对应多个输入的逻辑输出，从而仅用单个器件即可实现反相器、NOR门和NAND门等可重构逻辑功能，显著提升面积效率。Xiang等人报道了一种适用于LiM的多栅FeFET。多端FeFET逻辑门的工作原理及相应的NOR和NAND门操作如图11c所示。该器件可在单个时钟脉冲内执行，所有操作结果均被存储。与传统的基于CMOS的电路（后者需要恒压电源维持理想导电状态）相比，其输出保持提高了器件的能效 [153]。此外，Fang等人基于MXene材料和Y掺杂HfO2薄膜开发了一种光电忆阻器。通过利用光电响应行为和不同的置位阈值，他们构建了由光和电输入驱动的逻辑门。该方法为光电人工神经形态器件提供了一条可行途径。图11d展示了用于逻辑运算的光电忆阻器与经典NAND门的类比，并给出了AND和OR操作的实验演示 [143]。总之，铪基铁电逻辑运算技术已超越基础功能验证阶段。其核心在于利用对极化动力学的深刻理解和创新器件结构，实现高并行性、可重构性和传感集成的存内计算。这为开发下一代高能效智能系统提供了关键的硬件基础。

<span class="hljs-section">## 4 Challenges and Prospects</span>
<span class="hljs-section">## 4 挑战与前景</span>
Hf-FEs devices play an important role in modern science and technology, and their rapid development demonstrates great application potential in the fields of next-generation non-volatile memory and neuromorphic computing. Meanwhile, there are still numerous challenges behind these extensive development prospects (Fig. 12).
Hf-FEs器件在现代科技中扮演着重要角色，其快速发展展现了在新一代非易失性存储器和神经形态计算领域巨大的应用潜力。同时，在这些广阔的发展前景背后，仍存在诸多挑战（图12）。

<span class="hljs-section">### 4.1 Challenges and Recent Strategies</span>
<span class="hljs-section">### 4.1 挑战与近期策略</span>
<span class="hljs-section">#### 4.1.1 Stabilization of the Metastable Orthorhombic Phase</span>
<span class="hljs-section">#### 4.1.1 亚稳态正交相的稳定化</span>
The ferroelectric orthorhombic phase in HfO2 is metastable, and its reliable stabilization under CMOS compatible conditions remains challenging. Recent research has primarily focused on external modulation through doping, strain engineering, and interface design. For instance, La-Al co-doping has been shown to synergistically regulate oxygen vacancy and lattice strain, enhancing polarization strength and endurance in 3D FeRAM arrays [71]. Furthermore, region-selective oxygen vacancy engineering via plasma treatment during ALD enables the stabilization of ferroelectric HZO thin films at annealing temperatures as low as 300 °C, significantly improving BEOL compatibility [173]. Another promising approach involves constructing multilayer or superlattice structures; for example, inserting HLO subnanolayers in HZO enhances interfacial polarization coupling and suppresses phase degradation [72]. While these methods improve thermal stability and endurance, they often increase process complexity and may introduce variability in ultra-thin films.
HfO2中的铁电正交相是亚稳态的，在CMOS兼容条件下可靠稳定化仍具挑战性。近期研究主要集中在通过掺杂、应变工程和界面设计进行外部调控。例如，La-Al共掺杂已被证明可协同调控氧空位和晶格应变，增强3D FeRAM阵列中的极化强度和耐久性 [71]。此外，通过在ALD过程中进行等离子体处理的区域选择性氧空位工程，可在低至300 °C的退火温度下稳定铁电HZO薄膜，显著改善BEOL兼容性 [173]。另一种有前景的方法涉及构建多层或超晶格结构；例如，在HZO中插入HLO亚纳米层可增强界面极化耦合并抑制相退化 [72]。虽然这些方法提高了热稳定性和耐久性，但它们常增加工艺复杂性，并可能在超薄膜中引入变异性。

<span class="hljs-section">#### 4.1.2 Oxygen Vacancy and Defect Management</span>
<span class="hljs-section">#### 4.1.2 氧空位与缺陷管理</span>
Oxygen vacancies play a dual role in Hf-FEs: they can stabilize the ferroelectric orthorhombic phase, but an excess of, or mobile, oxygen vacancies can lead to reliability issues such as fatigue, imprint effect, and retention degradation. Recent studies have shown that active control of oxygen vacancies can simultaneously improve device performance and stability. For example, in anti-ferroelectric HZO energy storage capacitors, controlling the oxygen vacancy concentration by tuning the O2 flow rate during the sputtering process can optimize the tetragonal/orthorhombic phase ratio, thereby significantly improving the energy storage density (86.3J cm−3) and efficiency while maintaining a cycle life of more than 109 cycles [174]. This demonstrates that controlled oxygen defects can enhance electrical performance while promoting the formation of the target phase without compromising cyclic reliability. Beyond oxygen vacancy modulation, nitrogen doping is also an effective interface passivation strategy. Nitridation of the ferroelectric layer/electrode interface in FeFETs can reduce interface trap density and suppress oxygen vacancy migration, thereby improving endurance and stabilizing the memory window [175, 176]. However, precise control of oxygen vacancy concentration and distribution at the nanometer scale remains challenging, especially in ultra-thin films and miniaturized devices, where the stochastic nature of defect behavior can affect device uniformity. Future efforts should combine in situ monitoring with real-time process control, as well as computing guidance, to achieve reproducible oxygen vacancy engineering and promote the development of high-density, high-reliability Hf-FEs devices.
氧空位在Hf-FEs中扮演双重角色：它们可以稳定铁电正交相，但过量或可移动的氧空位会导致疲劳、印记效应和保持退化等可靠性问题。近期研究表明，主动控制氧空位可同时提高器件性能和稳定性。例如，在反铁电HZO储能电容中，通过调节溅射过程中O2流量来控制氧空位浓度，可优化四方/正交相比例，从而显著提高储能密度（86.3 J cm−3）和效率，同时保持超过109次循环的寿命 [174]。这证明受控的氧缺陷可在不损害循环可靠性的前提下，增强电学性能并促进目标相的形成。除氧空位调控外，氮掺杂也是一种有效的界面钝化策略。对FeFETs中铁电层/电极界面进行氮化处理可降低界面陷阱密度并抑制氧空位迁移，从而提高耐久性并稳定存储窗口 [175, 176]。然而，在纳米尺度精确控制氧空位浓度和分布仍具挑战性，特别是在超薄膜和微型化器件中，缺陷行为的随机性可能影响器件均匀性。未来工作应将原位监测与实时工艺控制以及计算指导相结合，以实现可重复的氧空位工程，促进高密度、高可靠性Hf-FEs器件的发展。
<span class="hljs-strong">**imprint effect**</span>: 印记效应，铁电材料中一种导致极化状态偏向某一方向的退化现象。

<span class="hljs-section">#### 4.1.3 Memory Window and Endurance Trade‐offs</span>
<span class="hljs-section">#### 4.1.3 存储窗口与耐久性的权衡</span>
As device dimensions shrink, the memory window tends to narrow, which limits multilevel operation and sensing margin. Recent innovations, including stacked ferroelectric/dielectric heterostructures and negative capacitance effects, have been developed to amplify the effective memory window. For instance, a fully BEOL compatible HZO/IGZO FeFET with an engineered gate stack has achieved an ultrahigh memory window of approximately 10 V and an endurance of over 109 cycles through interface dipole engineering and optimized annealing at 300 °C [173]. Dynamic refresh circuits have also been integrated into FeRAM arrays to detect and mitigate fatigue, improving cycle endurance by orders of magnitude [87]. However, simultaneously achieving a high memory window, high endurance, and low operating voltage remains a critical challenge for ultra-dense memory arrays.
随着器件尺寸缩小，存储窗口趋于变窄，限制了多级操作和读出裕度。近期创新，包括堆叠铁电/介电异质结构和负电容效应，已被开发用于放大有效存储窗口。例如，一个完全BEOL兼容的HZO/IGZO FeFET，通过界面偶极子工程和300 °C优化退火，实现了约10 V的超高存储窗口和超过109次循环的耐久性 [173]。动态刷新电路也已集成到FeRAM阵列中，以检测和缓解疲劳，将循环耐久性提高数个数量级 [87]。然而，同时实现高存储窗口、高耐久性和低工作电压仍是超高密度存储阵列的关键挑战。
<span class="hljs-strong">**negative capacitance**</span>: 负电容，铁电材料在特定条件下表现出的一种状态，可用于增强晶体管的栅极效率。

<span class="hljs-section">#### 4.1.4 BEOL Thermal Budget and Scalability in Advanced CMOS Nodes</span>
<span class="hljs-section">#### 4.1.4 先进CMOS节点中的BEOL热预算与可微缩性</span>
While Hf-FEs exhibit promising CMOS compatibility, their integration into advanced technology nodes presents intertwined challenges of scalability and BEOL thermal budget incompatibility. The crystallization of ferroelectric HfO2 typically requires annealing temperatures above 450 °C, which directly conflicts with the thermal budget limit (&lt; 400 °C) of modern CMOS BEOL processes. To address this, low-thermal-budget crystallization strategies have emerged as critical enablers. Recent studies have achieved effective stabilization of the ferroelectric phase in processes below 400 °C through strategies such as plasma-enhanced atomic layer deposition (PE-ALD), ultrafast thermal processing, and material/interface engineering. PE-ALD technology enables direct deposition and crystallization of HZO thin films at 300 °C using highly reactive plasma, resulting in high remanent polarization (2Pr ≈ 20–40 μc cm−2) and excellent endurance (&gt; 1010 cycles) [177]. Material and interface modulation represents another effective approach. By optimizing the Zr/Hf ratio, ferroelectric ZrHfOx thin films can be obtained after deposition at 280 °C without annealing, exhibiting a high 2Pr of up to 30 μC cm−2 and an ultra-wide temperature operating range (4.55–473 K) [122]. Employing low thermal expansion coefficient electrodes, like Mo, can also induce interface stress, reducing the crystallization temperature of HZO to 300 °C and achieving a 10 V memory window [178]. Beyond thermal constraints, further scaling of Hf-FEs devices encounters fundamental material-level limits. As film thickness approaches 3 nm, the depolarization field intensifies and the stability of the orthorhombic phase becomes critically sensitive to interfacial strain and defect distributions. Moreover, achieving high-density 3D integration, essential for next-generation FeNAND and neuromorphic crossbars, demands conformal deposition of ferroelectric layers in high-aspect-ratio structures, a task that remains challenging for conventional ALD due to step-coverage and compositional-uniformity issues. Variability both within-device and between devices also escalates with scaling, imposing stringent requirements on process control and circuit-level compensation techniques. Moving forward, the co-design of novel device architectures with BEOL-compatible low-thermal-budget processes will be essential to fully exploit the scalability of Hf-FEs. Concurrently, efforts must focus on improving process stability, large-area uniformity, and systematic integration schemes to facilitate the adoption of ferroelectric HfO2 in next-generation memory and computing chips.
尽管Hf-FEs表现出良好的CMOS兼容性，但将其集成到先进技术节点面临着可微缩性和BEOL热预算不兼容性的交织挑战。铁电HfO2的结晶通常需要450 °C以上的退火温度，这与现代CMOS BEOL工艺的热预算限制（&lt; 400 °C）直接冲突。为解决这一问题，低热预算结晶策略已成为关键使能技术。近期研究通过等离子体增强原子层沉积（PE-ALD）、超快热处理和材料/界面工程等策略，已在低于400 °C的工艺中实现了铁电相的有效稳定。PE-ALD技术利用高活性等离子体在300 °C下直接沉积并结晶HZO薄膜，获得了高剩余极化（2Pr ≈ 20–40 μC cm−2）和优异的耐久性（&gt; 1010次循环）[177]。材料和界面调控是另一种有效方法。通过优化Zr/Hf比，在280 °C沉积后无需退火即可获得铁电ZrHfOx薄膜，展现出高达30 μC cm−2的2Pr和超宽温度工作范围（4.55–473 K）[122]。采用低热膨胀系数电极（如Mo）也可诱导界面应力，将HZO的结晶温度降至300 °C，并实现10 V的存储窗口 [178]。除热约束外，Hf-FEs器件的进一步微缩面临基本的材料级极限。当薄膜厚度接近3 nm时，退极化场增强，正交相的稳定性对界面应变和缺陷分布变得极为敏感。此外，实现下一代FeNAND和神经形态交叉阵列所需的高密度3D集成，要求在高深宽比结构中保形沉积铁电层，由于台阶覆盖和成分均匀性问题，这对传统ALD仍具挑战性。器件内和器件间的变异性也随微缩而加剧，对工艺控制和电路级补偿技术提出了严格要求。展望未来，新型器件架构与BEOL兼容的低热预算工艺的协同设计，对于充分发挥Hf-FEs的可微缩性至关重要。同时，必须致力于提高工艺稳定性、大面积均匀性和系统集成方案，以促进铁电HfO2在下一代存储和计算芯片中的应用。
<span class="hljs-strong">**PE-ALD**</span>: Plasma-Enhanced Atomic Layer Deposition，等离子体增强原子层沉积。

<span class="hljs-section">#### 4.1.5 System‐Level Integration</span>
<span class="hljs-section">#### 4.1.5 系统级集成</span>
Beyond individual device performance, Hf-FEs’ practical application in functional circuits faces critical system-level obstacles. Suppressing crosstalk in high-density crossbar arrays is essential to reduce sneak path currents, which degrade read/write accuracy in memory and synapse arrays. Heterogeneous integration compatibility between novel HfFEs devices and traditional CMOS logic requires collaborative optimization of process flows, thermal budgets, and interconnect schemes to ensure hybrid system performance and yield. For reliable neuromorphic computing, robust fault-tolerance mechanisms must be designed to mitigate inherent device-to-device and cycle-to-cycle variability of Hf-FEs synapses. Addressing these challenges demands a holistic, collaborative design approach spanning materials, devices, circuits, and systems.
除单个器件性能外，Hf-FEs在功能电路中的实际应用面临关键的系统级障碍。抑制高密度交叉阵列中的串扰对于减少潜行路径电流至关重要，这些电流会降低存储和突触阵列的读写精度。新型Hf-FEs器件与传统CMOS逻辑之间的异构集成兼容性，需要对工艺流程、热预算和互连方案进行协同优化，以确保混合系统的性能和良率。对于可靠的神经形态计算，必须设计鲁棒的容错机制，以减轻Hf-FEs突触固有的器件间和循环间变异性。应对这些挑战需要一种跨越材料、器件、电路和系统的整体协同设计方法。

<span class="hljs-section">### 4.2 Prospects</span>
<span class="hljs-section">### 4.2 展望</span>
<span class="hljs-bullet">1.</span> Fundamental mechanisms and material optimization: The atomic-scale origin of ferroelectricity in HfO2 remains unclear, particularly regarding the stabilization pathway of the non-centrosymmetric o-phase. Through advanced TEM studies combined with molecular dynamics, nucleation dynamics are expected to be resolved at sub-angstrom resolution, which will guide doping engineering to modulate the coercive field and switching uniformity.
<span class="hljs-bullet">1.</span> 基础机制与材料优化：HfO2中铁电性的原子尺度起源仍不清楚，特别是关于非中心对称o相的稳定化路径。通过先进TEM研究结合分子动力学，有望在亚埃分辨率下解析成核动力学，这将指导掺杂工程以调控矫顽场和开关均匀性。
<span class="hljs-bullet">2.</span> 3D Integration and ultra-dense storage: Leveraging ALD-based conformal three-dimensional stacking technology, Hf-FEs have pioneered the next-generation ultra-dense memory, such as 3D FeFET / FeRAM arrays with deep trench capacitors. It is anticipated to achieve more comprehensive performance breakthroughs in areas like storage capacity, read/write speed, and power consumption balance in the future.
<span class="hljs-bullet">2.</span> 3D集成与超高密度存储：利用基于ALD的保形三维堆叠技术，Hf-FEs已开创了下一代超高密度存储器，如具有深沟槽电容的3D FeFET / FeRAM阵列。预计未来将在存储容量、读写速度和功耗平衡等领域实现更全面的性能突破。
<span class="hljs-bullet">3.</span> Neuromorphic computing: In the field of neuromorphic computing, FET-based devices using fluorite-structured HZO materials have garnered significant attention. Antiferroelectric FETs, by virtue of their intrinsic properties, have emerged as promising candidates for artificial neurons. This breakthrough establishes a foundation for developing highly integrated artificial biomimetic nervous systems, offering the potential to achieve low-power and high-speed neuromorphic devices.
<span class="hljs-bullet">3.</span> 神经形态计算：在神经形态计算领域，使用萤石结构HZO材料的基于FET的器件已引起广泛关注。反铁电FET凭借其本征特性，已成为人工神经元的有前景候选者。这一突破为开发高度集成的人工仿生神经系统奠定了基础，提供了实现低功耗、高速神经形态器件的潜力。
<span class="hljs-bullet">4.</span> Overcoming reliability limitations: To address the wakeup, fatigue, and imprint issues in Hf-FEs, methods such as interface dipole engineering and work function tuning have been continuously proposed. In the future, these solutions are expected to enable devices to achieve ultra-high endurance cycling and longer data retention, thereby establishing a solid reliability foundation for the widespread application of hafnium-based ferroelectric devices.
<span class="hljs-bullet">4.</span> 克服可靠性限制：为解决Hf-FEs中的唤醒、疲劳和印记问题，界面偶极子工程和功函数调谐等方法不断被提出。未来，这些解决方案有望使器件实现超高耐久性循环和更长的数据保持，从而为铪基铁电器件的广泛应用奠定坚实的可靠性基础。
<span class="hljs-bullet">5.</span> Beyond memory: Multi-functional devices: The piezoelectric properties of hafnium-based materials enable their utilization in microelectromechanical systems resonators, which can function as 5G/6G radio frequency filters operating within the FR3 band; their pyroelectric effect allows applications in energy harvesting and storage. Within magnetoelectric systems, HfO2-based multiferroic materials hold promise for implementation in spin–orbit torque devices.
<span class="hljs-bullet">5.</span> 超越存储器：多功能器件：铪基材料的压电特性使其可用于微机电系统谐振器，可作为工作在FR3频段的5G/6G射频滤波器；其热释电效应允许在能量收集和存储中应用。在磁电系统中，基于HfO2的多铁性材料有望应用于自旋轨道转矩器件。
<span class="hljs-strong">**piezoelectric**</span>: 压电效应，材料在机械应力下产生电荷或在外加电场下产生形变的效应；<span class="hljs-strong">**pyroelectric**</span>: 热释电效应，材料随温度变化产生极化态变化的效应；<span class="hljs-strong">**multiferroic**</span>: 多铁性，同时具有两种或两种以上铁性序（如铁电性、铁磁性）的材料。

<span class="hljs-section">## 5 Conclusion</span>
<span class="hljs-section">## 5 结论</span>
Compared to other traditional ferroelectric materials, Hf-FEs materials exhibit good CMOS compatibility and excellent thickness scalability, addressing the demand for continuous device miniaturization. Furthermore, their higher endurance is crucial for memory and neuromorphic computing applications that require frequent writing and erasing, thereby significantly improving device lifetime and reliability. Due to these unique intrinsic advantages, Hf-FEs materials are driving innovation and development in various fields, from ultra-dense non-volatile memory to energy-efficient neuromorphic computing hardware. This paper comprehensively reviews the material system, device structure, and underlying mechanisms of Hf-FEs materials and summarizes their applications in memory, synaptic plasticity, image processing, and logic operations. The importance of HfFEs is highlighted through their application in cutting-edge fields, specifically in neural networks and hardware integration designed using these devices. Finally, the challenges and prospects of Hf-FEs devices for future applications in non-volatile memory and neuromorphic computing are thoroughly discussed.
与其他传统铁电材料相比，Hf-FEs材料具有良好的CMOS兼容性和优异的厚度可微缩性，满足了器件不断小型化的需求。此外，其更高的耐久性对于需要频繁写入和擦除的存储和神经形态计算应用至关重要，从而显著提高了器件寿命和可靠性。由于这些独特的内在优势，Hf-FEs材料正在推动从超高密度非易失性存储器到高能效神经形态计算硬件等各个领域的创新与发展。本文全面回顾了Hf-FEs材料的材料体系、器件结构和底层机制，并总结了其在存储器、突触可塑性、图像处理和逻辑运算中的应用。通过其在神经网络和利用这些器件设计的硬件集成等前沿领域的应用，凸显了Hf-FEs的重要性。最后，深入讨论了Hf-FEs器件在未来非易失性存储器和神经形态计算应用中的挑战与前景。

> **2. 主角登场——“铪基铁电材料”是啥？** 以前大家用的一种叫“钙钛矿”的铁电材料，虽然性能不错，但它跟现在做芯片的主流工艺（CMOS工艺）合不来，而且做不薄，一薄性能就完蛋了。这就好比一个手艺很好的裁缝，但只会用自家祖传的老式缝纫机，用不了现代化的流水线。

> 
> **那“铪基”又是啥？** 就是它的主要原料是金属“铪”（Hf）的氧化物，就像铁锈是铁的氧化物一样。这个材料牛就牛在，它跟现在芯片工厂里的生产线（CMOS工艺）是“亲兄弟”，能完美兼容，可以轻松地做成各种复杂的器件，而且能做到特别特别薄，只有几个纳米，性能还杠杠的。

> -   **铁电隧道结（FTJ）**：这像一个**电阻值能变的精密电阻**。通过改变电压，可以把它的电阻变得很大或很小，并且能记住这个电阻值。它结构更简单，只有两个端口，反应速度极快。
> -   **铁电存储器（FeRAM）**：这是一个**不怕断电的极速内存**。它像咱们电脑里的内存一样读写飞快，但又像U盘一样，断电了数据还在。它用的是一个晶体管加一个铁电电容（1T-1C）的结构。
> -   **铁电二极管（Fe-Diode）**：这是个**单向导通的智能门**。电流只能从一边流向另一边，而且这个“门”的开和关也能被记住，非常适合用来建造高楼大厦一样的三维立体存储阵列。
