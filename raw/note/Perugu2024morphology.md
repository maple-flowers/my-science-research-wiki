> # Synthesis, Structural, Morphology and Magnetic Properties: Effect of La on Multiferroic Nature of BiFeO3 Nanoparticles
> 
> 合成、结构、形貌与磁性能：La 对 $\text{BiFeO}_3$ 纳米粒子多铁性影响的研究 **Multiferroic**: 多铁性，指一种材料同时具有两种或两种以上原铁电/铁磁有序的性质。
> 
> Srinivas Perugu¹, G Bhanu Kiran², T Anil Babu³, B V Raghavaiah⁴* Srinivas Perugu¹，G Bhanu Kiran²，T Anil Babu³，B V Raghavaiah⁴*
> 
> ## Abstract
> 
> 摘要
> 
> **Objectives**: The present study focuses on developing La-doped $\text{BiFeO}_3$ multiferroic materials for dielectric absorber applications. **目标**：本研究重点开发用于电介质吸收体应用的 La 掺杂 $\text{BiFeO}_3$ 多铁材料。 **Dielectric absorber**: 电介质吸收体，能够吸收电磁波能量并将其转化为热能的材料。
> 
> **Methods**: The samples are characterized by X-ray Diffractometer (XRD). Further, the morphology is examined using field emission electron microscopy (FESEM) and Transmission Electron Microscopy (TEM). The dielectric parameters and ac-electrical parameters are carried out by impedance spectroscopy. The magnetic properties are studied using a vibrating sample magnetometer VSM, P-E loop. **方法**：采用 X 射线衍射仪 (XRD) 对样品进行表征。随后，利用场发射扫描电子显微镜 (FESEM) 和透射电子显微镜 (TEM) 检查其形貌。通过阻抗谱分析电介质参数和交流电参数。使用振动样品磁强计 (VSM) 和 P-E 滞后回线研究其磁性能。 **XRD**: X-ray Diffraction，X 射线衍射，用于分析晶体结构。 **FESEM**: Field Emission Scanning Electron Microscopy，场发射扫描电子显微镜，用于高分辨率表面形貌成像。 **TEM**: Transmission Electron Microscopy，透射电子显微镜，用于观察材料内部结构及晶格。 **Impedance spectroscopy**: 阻抗谱，一种通过测量复阻抗来研究材料电学性质的方法。 **VSM**: Vibrating Sample Magnetometer，振动样品磁强计，用于测量样品的磁化强度。
> 
> **Findings**: XRD confirms the rhombohedral (trigonal) structure of BLFO nanoparticles. The grain and particle size values are determined by SEM and TEM, respectively. The dielectric and ac-electrical parameters for all the samples are thoroughly discussed at room temperature. The Maxwell-Wagner or interfacial polarization is noticed at low input field frequencies for x=0.2-0.8. The impedance analysis shows the contribution of grain and its boundary in the electrical conduction mechanism. The multiferroic behavior is examined using M-H loops, P-E loops, and $\mu_i$-f plots. **结果**：XRD 证实了 BLFO 纳米粒子的菱方（三方）结构。晶粒尺寸和粒子尺寸分别由 SEM 和 TEM 确定。详细讨论了所有样品在室温下的电介质和交流电参数。对于 $x=0.2\text{-}0.8$ 的样品，在低输入场频率下观察到 Maxwell-Wagner 或界面极化现象。阻抗分析显示了晶粒及其晶界在电导机制中的贡献。通过 M-H 回线、P-E 回线和 $\mu_i\text{-}f$ 图研究了多铁行为。 **Interfacial polarization**: 界面极化，电荷在不同电导率的相界面处积聚而产生的极化。
> 
> **Novelty**: The samples are synthesized by the hydrothermal process to prepare La-doped $\text{BiFeO}_3$ nanomaterials. **创新点**：采用水热法合成 La 掺杂 $\text{BiFeO}_3$ 纳米材料。 **Hydrothermal process**: 水热法，在高温高压的水溶液环境下合成纳米材料的方法。
> 
> **Keywords**: Nanoparticles; Electron Microscopy; Multiferroic; Magnetization; Polarization; Permeability **关键词**：纳米粒子；电子显微镜；多铁性；磁化；极化；磁导率

> # 1 Introduction
> 
> 1 引言
> 
> Perovskite materials have been well-known to the scientific community for several years. Generally, among these perovskites, dielectric, ferroelectric, piezoelectric, pyroelectrics, ferromagnetic, and multiferroic materials achieved much more significance than other materials. 钙钛矿材料在科学界已为人所知多年。通常，在这些钙钛矿中，电介质、铁电、压电、热电、铁磁和多铁材料比其他材料具有更重要的意义。 **Perovskite**: 钙钛矿，具有 $\text{ABO}_3$ 结构的晶体材料。
> 
> As we know, the multiferroic perovskites show more than one ordering, such as magnetic, electric, elastic, etc. The magnetic ordering in multiferroics is established owing to the exchange interactions between magnetic dipoles within the $\text{ABO}_3$ perovskite structure. $\text{BiFeO}_3$ (BF) is a familiar multiferroic material that offers ferroelectricity, antiferromagnetism, and ferroelasticity. 众所周知，多铁钙钛矿表现出多种有序状态，如磁序、电序、弹性序等。多铁材料中的磁有序是由于 $\text{ABO}_3$ 钙钛矿结构内磁偶极子之间的交换相互作用而建立的。$\text{BiFeO}_3$ (BF) 是一种常见的多铁材料，具有铁电性、反铁磁性和铁弹性。 **Antiferromagnetism**: 反铁磁性，相邻磁矩方向相反且大小相等，导致宏观磁化强度为零的性质。
> 
> As a result, bismuth iron oxide attracted the scientific community to work further on it to study various optical, mechanical, photocatalytic, and electrical properties. Moreover, the BF exhibits a rhombohedral distorted structure and offers applications in memory, spintronic, photocatalytic, and photovoltaic devices(1). However, BF has difficulties providing low retentivity, weak magnetization, low polarization, and high leakage current(1). To avoid this problem, the rare earth elements must be incorporated into the BF perovskite system. Thus, it may enhance the magnetization and polarization behavior, decreasing leakage current. 因此，铁酸铋吸引了科学界进一步研究其各种光学、力学、光催化和电学性质。此外，BF 呈现出菱方畸变结构，可应用于存储器、自旋电子学、光催化和光伏器件(1)。然而，BF 存在剩余磁化强度低、磁化弱、极化低和漏电流高的问题(1)。为了解决这一问题，必须在 BF 钙钛矿体系中引入稀土元素。这样可以增强其磁化和极化行为，并降低漏电流。 **Spintronic**: 自旋电子学，利用电子的自旋特性而非仅仅是电荷来处理信息的学科。
> 
> ## 1.1 Related work
> 
> 1.1 相关工作
> 
> Based on previous reports, BF, and La doped BF materials were synthesized through different methods and prepared nanoparticles size ranges in between 50 -150 nm, and studied the effect of synthesis method on different properties like structure, electrical, dielectric and magnetic. Like the two-step anodization process (2), solid-state reaction technique, sol-gel method (3–6), ball-milled method (7,8), and spark plasma sintering (9). Using different synthesis methods, it is observed that the structural, electrical, optical, and magnetic properties have been optimized. 根据之前的报告，BF 和 La 掺杂的 BF 材料通过多种方法合成，制备的纳米粒子尺寸在 50-150 nm 之间，并研究了合成方法对结构、电学、电介质和磁性等不同性质的影响。这些方法包括两步阳极氧化工艺 (2)、固相反应技术、溶胶-凝胶法 (3-6)、球磨法 (7,8) 以及放电等离子体烧结 (9)。通过使用不同的合成方法，可以观察到结构、电学、光学和磁性能得到了优化。 **Sol-gel method**: 溶胶-凝胶法，一种通过化学溶液制备陶瓷或玻璃材料的方法。
> 
> ## 1.2 Research Gap

All the reports evidenced that the complete study on structure, morphology, dielectric, magnetic, ferroelectric, and impedance behavior is not provided in the literature. 文献调研表明，La 掺杂 $\text{BiFeO}_3$ 纳米粒子是通过溶胶-凝胶法、自动燃烧法、溶剂热法、水热法、Pechini 法等多种合成方法制备的 (1-17)。所有报告均证明，文献中尚未提供关于结构、形貌、电介质、磁性、铁电性和阻抗行为的完整研究。

> # 2 Methodology
> 
> 2 实验方法
> 
> The hydrothermal synthesis is employed to prepare $\text{Bi}_{1-X}\text{La}_X\text{FeO}_3$ (X = 0.2-0.8) (BLFO) nanoparticles (Figure 2). The starting materials are $\text{La}(\text{NO}_3)_3\cdot6\text{H}_2\text{O}$ (99.8 % purity, Sigma-Aldrich), $\text{Bi}(\text{NO}_3)_3\cdot5\text{H}_2\text{O}$ (99.8 % purity, Sigma-Aldrich), and $\text{Fe}(\text{NO}_3)_3\cdot9\text{H}_2\text{O}$ (99.9 % purity, Sigma-Aldrich). The details of the preparation method can be seen in (18). The characterization is carried out by XRD, TEM, SEM, Vibrating sample magnetometer VSM, P-E loop (Marain India Ltd.), and LCR controller (HIOKI 3532-50, 42 Hz–5 MHz) to study the structural, morphological, magnetic, ferroelectric, and dielectric properties, respectively. 采用水热合成法制备 $\text{Bi}_{1-X}\text{La}_X\text{FeO}_3$ (X = 0.2-0.8) (BLFO) 纳米粒子（图 2）。起始原材料为 $\text{La}(\text{NO}_3)_3\cdot6\text{H}_2\text{O}$（纯度 99.8%，Sigma-Aldrich）、$\text{Bi}(\text{NO}_3)_3\cdot5\text{H}_2\text{O}$（纯度 99.8%，Sigma-Aldrich）和 $\text{Fe}(\text{NO}_3)_3\cdot9\text{H}_2\text{O}$（纯度 99.9%，Sigma-Aldrich）。制备方法的详细步骤参见文献 (18)。分别使用 XRD、TEM、SEM、振动样品磁强计 VSM、P-E 回线（Marain India Ltd.）和 LCR 控制器（HIOKI 3532-50, 42 Hz-5 MHz）对结构、形貌、磁性、铁电性和电介质性质进行表征。
> 
> # 3 Results and Discussion
> 
> 3 结果与讨论
> 
> The X-ray diffraction spectra of BLFO nanoparticles are shown in Figure 1. It was found from the XRD patterns that the x=0.2-0.8 samples showed well crystalline peaks. The single-phase structure was attributed to all samples, including a few secondary phases. These secondary phases were incurred owing to the orthorhombic $\text{Bi}_2\text{Fe}_4\text{O}_9$ and cubic $\text{Bi}_{25}\text{FeO}_{40}$. The single phases were compared with standard JCPDS: 86-1518. BLFO 纳米粒子的 X 射线衍射谱如图 1 所示。从 XRD 图谱可见，$x=0.2\text{-}0.8$ 的样品显示出良好的晶体衍射峰。所有样品均归属于单相结构，但包含少量第二相。这些第二相是由正交相 $\text{Bi}_2\text{Fe}_4\text{O}_9$ 和立方相 $\text{Bi}_{25}\text{FeO}_{40}$ 引起的。单相结构与标准 JCPDS: 86-1518 进行了对比。
> 
> The maximum intensity was recorded at (110) plane. It was also observed that the secondary phases almost disappeared owing to the increase of La-content above 50 % in the BLFO system. This established that the high La-content induced the reduction of secondary phases. > The $\text{Bi}_{1-X}\text{La}_X\text{FeO}_3$ (X = 0.2-0.8) nanoparticles were successfully prepared using hydrothermal methods. The XRD patterns confirmed the rhombohedral phase structure, including some secondary peaks. Herein, the high La-content reduced the number of secondary phases. Further, the morphology (FESEM & HRTEM) suggested the formation of nanorods-like structures. The space charge polarization was well observed among the dielectric properties. La dopant optimized the conductivity properties of BFO samples. The saturation magnetization ($\text{M}_s$) values were increased from 13.2 to 21.5 emu/g as a function of ‘x’ from 0.2-0.8. Two relaxations were seen in the permeability versus frequency plots, suggesting the multiferroic nature of BLFO samples. The P-E loops of x=0.2-0.8 showed the ‘banana’ shape, indicating the presence of oxygen vacancies.
