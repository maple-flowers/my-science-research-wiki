> ## 一、引言
> 
> 本报告深度解读的文献是一篇2025年发表于《Frontiers of Physics》的综述文章，题为《二维多铁性材料的最新进展：生长、表征与应用》（_Recent advances in growth, characterization, and application of two-dimensional multiferroic materials_）。
> 
> **多铁性材料**（Multiferroic materials）是凝聚态物理和材料科学领域的研究热点，其核心特征是在单一相中同时存在两种或两种以上的铁性序（ferroic orders），例如**铁电性**（ferroelectricity, FE）、**铁磁性**（ferromagnetism, FM）和**铁弹性**（ferroelasticity, FA）。更关键的是，这些序之间可能存在交叉耦合，如**磁电耦合**（magnetoelectric coupling），即用电场控制磁性，或用磁场控制电极化，这为下一代多功能电子器件（如高密度存储器、低功耗自旋电子学器件）带来了革命性的潜力。
> 
> 传统块体多铁材料在尺寸、界面效应和集成度方面面临瓶颈。随着二维（2D）材料研究的兴起，特别是在单层NiI₂中成功实验观测到多铁性后，**二维多铁材料**迅速成为前沿领域。二维材料不仅易于达到原子级厚度，实现器件微型化，其层间弱的范德华力（van der Waals, vdW）还允许通过堆叠、转角等方式创造出新的性质和功能。
> 
> 本综述正是在这一背景下，系统地梳理了二维多铁材料（尤其是铁磁-铁电和铁磁-铁弹性材料）的**分类、生长方法、表征技术以及应用前景**，并指出了当前的研究挑战和未来方向。对于初学者、行业从业者及希望快速把握该领域全貌的研究者而言，本文是一份极其宝贵的知识地图。

> -   **第四部分：结论与展望 (Section 4)**

>     -   **提出挑战**：明确指出了当前面临的瓶颈，如室温多铁材料稀缺、材料稳定性不足、可控合成困难等。
>     -   **展望未来**：提出了未来可能的突破方向，如发现新型室温多铁材料、探索三铁性（triferroics）材料、降低操作电压、与量子物理结合等。

> ## 三、所有图表深度解析
> 
> 本综述包含大量关键图表，有助于直观理解多铁性的物理机制和材料特性。
> 
> ### 图1：铁性系统的时间反演与空间反演对称性
> 
> -   **内容**：此图解释了铁磁体、铁电体和多铁体在**时间反演**（t → -t）和**空间反演**（r → -r）对称性操作下的变化。
>     -   **(a) 铁磁体**：时间反演会使磁矩（m）方向反转，而空间反演不变。
>     -   **(b) 铁电体**：空间反演会使电极化（p）方向反转，而时间反演不变。
>     -   **(c) 多铁体**：同时打破时间反演和空间反演对称性，同时拥有磁矩和电极化。
> -   **核心意义**：_该图是理解多铁性的入门基石，阐明了为何铁磁和铁电这“两种序”在对称性上是“矛盾”的，天然共存于单一材料中非常罕见。_
> 
> ### 图2：不同铁性序及其交叉耦合
> 
> -   **内容**：
>     -   **(a) 初级铁性序与交叉耦合**：展示了铁磁性（M）、铁电性（P）、铁弹性（ε）三种初级序，以及它们对磁场（H）、电场（E）、应力（σ）的响应。箭头明确指出了**磁电耦合**、**压电效应**、**磁致伸缩**等交叉耦合关系。
>     -   **(b) 多铁性类型**：对比了**I型多铁**（铁电与磁性来源不同，耦合弱）和**II型多铁**（铁电由特殊磁结构产生，耦合强）在相变温度和序参量上的差异。
>     -   **(c) 铁性序间的交叉耦合**：更详细地展示了磁场、电场和应力场如何通过材料内部的耦合相互作用。
>     -   **(d) 磁-弹-电耦合示意图**：清晰说明了磁化（M）、应变（ε）和极化（P）三者的相互影响关系。
> -   **核心意义**：_此图是全文的“应用蓝图”。所有后续的器件应用，如电场控制磁性的存储器，都源于这些交叉耦合，尤其是磁电耦合。_
> 
> ### 图3：液相剥离机制示意图
> 
> -   **内容**：展示了三种主要的液相剥离方法：
>     -   **(a) 离子插层**：将离子插入层状材料的层间，增大层间距，削弱范德华力。
>     -   **(b) 离子交换**：通过溶液中的离子交换反应来剥离薄片。
>     -   **(c) 超声辅助剥离**：利用超声波产生的空化气泡破裂来剥离纳米片。
> -   **核心意义**：_这些是“自上而下”合成策略的代表，虽然讨论重点在气相沉积，但本文也提及了这些方法的局限，如产率低、尺寸不可控。_
> 
> ### 图4：气相沉积方法概览
> 
> -   **内容**：展示了四种主要的自下而上气相沉积技术，用于生长高质量的二维层状硫族化合物或卤化物：
>     -   **(a) CVD**：分为固体源和气体源CVD，使用管式炉加热前驱体，在基底上沉积。
>     -   **(b) PVD**：直接蒸发块体材料，在低温区基底上沉积。
>     -   **(c) MBE**：在超高真空（UHV）环境下，精确控制分子束在加热基底上外延生长。
>     -   **(d) ALD**：通过交替脉冲式引入气态前驱体，实现自限制的逐层生长。
> -   **核心意义**：_这是实现二维多铁材料从实验室走向应用的关键。不同的方法决定了材料的结晶质量、层数可控性、晶圆级尺寸等关键指标。_
> 
> ### 图5 & 图6 & 图7 & 图8 & 图9 & 图10：具体材料合成案例
> 
> -   **图5 (Cr₂S₃)**：展示了通过CVD实现晶圆级（1英寸）、取向可控的Cr₂S₃生长，其AFM图显示单层厚度仅为1.8 nm。这是从实验室小样品到工业级制备的重大突破。

> ## 四、正文核心内容剖析
> 
> ### 4.1 研究背景与问题
> 
> 多铁性研究始于对单一材料同时拥有多种铁性序的好奇，但这类材料极其罕见。根本原因在于，传统铁电性要求过渡金属离子具有空的d轨道（如Ti⁴⁺，d⁰），而磁性则要求具有未填满的d轨道（如Fe³⁺，d⁵），这在化学上相互排斥。二维材料的出现为解决这一“d⁰难题”提供了新思路。二维极限下，对称性破缺变得更加容易，层间滑移、吸附、应变等都能引入新的铁性序。特别是**II型多铁材料**，其铁电性直接源于特定的磁结构（如螺旋磁序），实现了强磁电耦合，但其极化通常较小，且转变温度（T）较低。因此，该领域的核心问题是：**如何实验制备高质量、室温下具有强磁电耦合的二维多铁材料，并将其应用于实际器件？**
> 
> ### 4.2 方法模型：生长与表征工具箱
> 
> 文章详细构建了一个从材料制备到性质验证的完整方法体系。
> 
> **生长方法**：重点在于**自下而上**的气相沉积技术，因为它们最有可能实现高质量、大面积、层数可控的工业级制备。
> 
> -   **CVD/CVT**：是目前成功制备NiI₂、Cr₂S₃、CuCrSe₂等多种II型二维多铁材料的主要方法。其优势在于设备相对简单，适合生长多种材料。
> -   **PVD**：为SnSe等多铁材料提供了直接合成的途径，适合工业薄膜制备。
> -   **MBE**：提供原子级精确控制，常用于生长超高质量单晶薄膜，并可在超高真空下原位监测。
> -   **ALD**：在解决空气敏感材料（如NiI₂）的器件封装和制备高质量异质结方面显示出巨大潜力。
> 
> **表征技术**：为了全面证实多铁性，必须同时探测铁电序和磁序，并证明其耦合。文中形成了一个强大的“表征矩阵”：
> 
> -   **探测铁电序**：SHG（对中心反演破缺敏感）、PFM（成像铁电畴）、XRD（探测结构相变）和双折射测量（观测各向异性）。
> -   **探测磁序**：STM（原子级磁结构成像）、中子散射（确定磁结构）、拉曼光谱（探测磁振子）、SQUID（宏观磁性测量）。
> -   **探测磁电耦合**：动态磁电耦合通过太赫兹光谱和拉曼光谱探测电磁振子；静态耦合则通过电场调控磁性或磁场调控电极化来直接验证。
> 
> ### 4.3 实验与数据：关键发现
> 
> 文章列举了多个里程碑式的实验发现，构成了该领域的核心进展：
> 
> -   **单层NiI₂**：首次在单层极限下证实了II型多铁序，其铁电性源于螺旋磁序和强自旋轨道耦合，转变温度约为21 K。
> -   **单层Cr₂S₃**：通过界面调制策略，在工业兼容的蓝宝石衬底上实现了晶圆级单层生长，并表现出室温铁电性和200 K的铁磁性，这是迄今报道最大、最薄的多铁材料之一。
> -   **单层CuCrSe₂**：展现了高温多铁性（室温铁电，120 K铁磁），并提出了一种新的铁电增强铁磁耦合的机制。
> -   **p型掺杂SnSe**：通过PVD合成，实现了室温下的铁磁性（337 K）和铁电性共存，为寻找室温多铁材料提供了新路径。
> 
> ### 4.4 讨论：应用驱动
> 
> 文章详细讨论了多铁耦合效应如何转化为器件功能。其核心逻辑是：**用电场（低功耗）代替磁场（高功耗）或电流（高焦耳热）来操控材料的磁性状态。**
> 
> -   **存储器**：在MERAM（磁电随机存取存储器）中，利用磁电耦合，通过电场控制铁电层极化方向，从而“写入”与其耦合的铁磁层的磁化方向，实现非易失、低功耗的数据存储。
> -   **自旋电子学**：在MFTJ（多铁隧道结）中，通过翻转铁电势垒的极化方向，可以切换自旋极化电流的极化方向，为自旋场效应晶体管等逻辑器件提供基础。
> -   **传感器/能量收集/微波器件**：均利用磁电耦合效应，将磁场信号高效转化为电信号，或反之。例如，磁电传感器可替代昂贵的SQUID进行微弱磁场探测，磁电能量收集器可从环境磁场中获取能量。

> ## 八、用户问题预测与解答
> 
> 1.  **问：什么是“铁性序”？“铁电”、“铁磁”、“铁弹”里的“铁”是什么意思？** **答**：“铁性”（ferroic）一词源于铁（Ferrum），但它们与铁的化学元素无关，而是指一种**集体性的、可被外场翻转的稳定状态**。就像许多小磁针在磁场下可以整齐排列，撤去磁场后仍能保持排列（磁滞回线）一样。
>     
>     -   **铁磁性 (FM)**：拥有自发磁矩（M），可被磁场（H）翻转。对应“磁滞回线”（M-H loop）。
>     -   **铁电性 (FE)**：拥有自发电极化（P），可被电场（E）翻转。对应“电滞回线”（P-E loop）。
>     -   **铁弹性 (FA)**：拥有自发应变（ε），可被应力（σ）翻转。对应“应力-应变回线”。
>     -   **多铁性**：就是同时拥有两种或以上这种“铁性”。
> 2.  **问：I型和II型多铁材料有什么区别？为什么II型更有吸引力？** **答**：核心区别在于**铁电性的起源**和**磁电耦合的强度**。
>     
>     -   **I型多铁**：铁电性和磁性的来源“各自为政”，通常由不同的原子或机制负责。例如，A位离子导致铁电性，B位离子导致磁性。因此，两者耦合很弱，像一个混住的室友，但互不干涉。优点是极化大、温度高，缺点是很难用电场控制磁性。
>     -   **II型多铁**：铁电性**完全由特殊的磁结构本身“衍生”出来**。例如，某些螺旋排列的磁序会打破空间反演对称性，从而产生电极化。这种“磁生电”的机制使得磁性和铁电性天生就是一对“孪生兄弟”，耦合极强。缺点是这种极化通常很小，且只在低温下存在。**II型更有吸引力，正是因为其强耦合特性是实现超低功耗“电场控制磁性”器件的物理基础。**
> 3.  **问：SHG（二次谐波产生）为什么能用来探测多铁性？** **答**：可以把它理解为一种“光学对称性探针”。SHG是一个非线性光学过程，简单说，就是两束频率为ω的光打到材料上，如果材料结构**不具备中心反演对称性**（就像人的左右手，无法通过空间平移重合），就会产生一束频率为2ω的新光。铁电性要求材料不具备中心反演对称性，所以SHG信号的出现，是证明材料存在铁电序的有力证据。类似地，某些磁序也会打破时间反演对称性，对SHG信号有贡献。因此，SHG可以同时对多种铁性序敏感。
>     
> 4.  **问：文章中提到的“电磁振子”（electromagnon）是什么？** **答**：这是一个动态磁电耦合的“活证据”。我们知道，磁有序材料中的自旋波量子称为**磁振子**（magnon）。电磁振子是一种特殊的自旋波激发，它同时带有电偶极矩的振荡。想象一下，一群磁矩在集体“舞蹈”（自旋进动），这种舞蹈模式不仅产生了磁性的波动，还因为磁电耦合，同时产生了电偶极矩的波动。这种“电-磁混合波”可以直接被太赫兹光和拉曼光谱探测到，从而成为证明材料存在强动态磁电耦合的“指纹”。
>     
> 5.  **问：既然二维多铁材料这么有前景，为什么现在还没有商用的二维多铁芯片？** **答**：主要障碍在于“三座大山”：
>     
>     1.  **温度关**：目前绝大多数实验证实的二维多铁材料，其工作温度（特别是磁电耦合温度）远低于室温（如NiI₂仅21 K），无法在常规电子设备中工作。
>     2.  **材料与制备关**：稳定、高产的二维多铁材料种类极少。许多材料在空气中不稳定，且难以实现晶圆级、层数绝对均匀的合成，这直接阻碍了大规模集成电路的制造。
>     3.  **性能与集成关**：虽然II型多铁耦合强，但其极化值太小，难以被外部电路有效读取。同时，将这些二维材料无缝集成到现有的硅基芯片工艺中，也是一个巨大的工程挑战。
> 6.  **问：什么是“三铁性”（triferroics）？它有什么特别之处？** **答**：**三铁性**是指在同一材料中，同时存在**铁电性、铁磁性和铁谷性**（ferrovalley）这三种铁性序。**铁谷性**是二维六角晶格材料（如过渡金属硫族化合物）中特有的一种新自由度，它指的是电子在能量上不等价的谷（K和K'能谷）上具有选择性占据，这种谷极化可被外场（如光场、电场）调控。三铁性材料是“梦想中的材料”，因为它将电荷、自旋和谷这三个自由度耦合在一起，有望实现一个材料单元存储和处理多个比特的信息，将器件的功能密度和信息处理能力推向极致。
>

> -   **内容描述**：这是全文最核心的技术示意图，对比了四种主要的“自下而上”气相沉积方法。
> -   **深度解析**：
>     -   **CVD (化学气相沉积)**：通过气态前驱体在高温下的化学反应生成目标材料。分为固体源和气体源CVD，是制备高质量、大面积薄膜的工业兼容方法。
>     -   **PVD (物理气相沉积)**：单纯通过物理过程（如蒸发、溅射）将源材料转移至衬底，不涉及化学反应。过程简单，适用于直接沉积。
>     -   **MBE (分子束外延)**：在超高真空下，精确控制分子束流量，实现原子层级别的逐层生长。能够制备最纯净、结晶质量最高的薄膜，并原位监控生长过程。
>     -   **ALD (原子层沉积)**：通过交替通入两种前驱体，利用其在衬底表面的自限制化学反应，实现**单原子层级别的精确厚度控制**，保形性极佳。

> ### 4.1 研究背景与动机
> 
> 传统三维多铁性材料（如**复合氧化物**，complex oxides）在尺寸缩小到纳米尺度时，其多铁性会减弱甚至消失，且难以与现代半导体工艺集成。二维范德华材料的出现打破了这一僵局：

> 1.  **层状结构**：弱范德华力允许其轻易剥离至单原子层，为在极限尺寸下研究多铁性提供了理想平台。
> 2.  **界面工程**：通过堆叠和扭转不同2D材料，可构建异质结，人工设计出新型多铁性或增强磁电耦合。
> 3.  **新机制**：二维材料中发现了由自旋驱动电极化的**II型多铁性**，为实现强磁电耦合开辟了新路径。

> #### 4.2.2 表征技术全景
> 
> 文章对表征技术的介绍从宏观到微观，从结构到性能，构成了一个完整的验证体系：
> 
> -   **磁序探测**：
>     
>     -   **SQUID (超导量子干涉仪)**：宏观磁测量，获得磁化率-温度曲线，确定居里温度（**Curie Temperature, Tc**）或奈尔温度（**Néel Temperature, TN**）等相变点。单位：emu/g (磁化强度)， Oe (磁场强度)。
>     -   **中子散射 (Neutron Scattering)**：唯一能直接测定磁结构（如螺旋磁序）的技术，是理解II型多铁性的关键。
> -   **电序与光学探测**：
>     
>     -   **SHG (光学二次谐波产生)**：对空间反演对称性破缺极其敏感，是探测铁电序和磁序的“指纹”技术。只需样品非中心对称，就会出现SHG信号。
>     -   **PFM (压电力显微镜)**：一种原子力显微镜技术，通过施加电压探测样品的局部压电响应，可在纳米尺度上成像铁电畴结构。
>     -   **双折射测量 (Birefringence)**：通过光学各向异性探测低对称性相，是验证NiI₂中多铁性序的重要光学证据。
> -   **结构与耦合动力学探测**：
>     
>     -   **STEM (扫描透射电子显微镜)**：实现原子级分辨率成像，可直接观察原子位移和晶格畸变，为铁电性起源提供直观证据。
>     -   **拉曼光谱 (Raman Spectroscopy)**：通过探测声子（晶格振动）和磁振子（自旋波）模式，可同时研究晶格结构和磁有序。特别是**圆偏振拉曼光谱**可以直接探测NiI₂中的磁手性基态。
>     -   **太赫兹时域光谱 (Terahertz spectroscopy)**：光子能量与自旋波（磁振子）和电活性激发的能量匹配，可直接探测到动态磁电耦合产生的**电磁振子**，是研究II型多铁性动力学的利器。
> 
> ### 4.3 实验结果与讨论：应用驱动
> 
> 文章并未停留在材料制备和表征，而是深入探讨了多种物理效应如何转化为器件功能：
> 
> -   **非易失性存储器 (Nonvolatile Memory)**：核心是利用**磁电耦合**实现电场写入、磁场读取。这结合了**铁电存储器 (FERAM)** 的快速写入、低功耗和**磁性存储器 (MRAM)** 的非易失性、高耐久性优点。一个多铁隧道结可实现由两个电极化方向(P↑， P↓)和两个磁化方向(M↑， M↓)组合而成的**四态存储**，大幅提升存储密度。
> -   **自旋电子学 (Spintronics)**：利用自旋而非电荷来传输和处理信息。二维多铁性材料为**电场控制自旋**提供了理想平台，可实现低功耗的自旋场效应晶体管。例如，通过电场翻转多铁性材料的电极化，可以切换其自旋极化输运特性。
> -   **传感器与执行器 (Sensors & Actuators)**：基于**磁电耦合**的磁场传感器，其灵敏度可媲美SQUID，但成本更低、体积更小，可在室温下工作，适用于生物磁场检测（如脑磁图MEG）。基于**压电效应**的执行器可在电场下产生精确的机械位移。
> -   **能量收集器 (Energy Harvesters)**：利用**磁电耦合**可以从环境中无处不在的微弱磁场（如电力线周围）中收集能量，为无线传感器网络等微型设备供电。
> -   **微波与射频器件 (Microwave & RF Devices)**：通过电场控制铁磁共振频率（**Ferromagnetic Resonance, FMR**），实现频率可调的滤波器、移相器和天线。其核心是**应变介导的磁电耦合**：电压施加于压电材料→产生应变→传递至磁性材料→改变其磁各向异性场→从而改变FMR频率。

> ## 五、结论与贡献
> 
> ### 5.1 核心发现与总结
> 
> 本文系统总结了二维多铁性材料领域的最新进展，主要结论如下：
> 
> 1.  **合成技术多样化**：CVD/CVT是制备高质量二维多铁性材料（如NiI₂、Cr₂S₃）的主流方法，基于衬底界面调控的策略可实现晶圆级单晶胞厚度材料的合成。
> 2.  **表征体系趋于完善**：从宏观物性测量到原子级结构分析，从静态序探测到动态耦合动力学研究，一套完整的表征范式已经建立。特别是圆偏振拉曼、太赫兹光谱和SHG等技术在验证II型多铁性中发挥了关键作用。
> 3.  **应用前景广阔但挑战犹存**：二维多铁性材料在低功耗存储器、自旋电子学、高灵敏度传感器等领域展现出巨大潜力。然而，**室温强磁电耦合**的实现、**晶圆级均匀制备**以及**低于100 mV的驱动电压**是走向实际应用必须跨越的障碍。
> 
> ### 5.2 理论贡献与实践价值
> 
> -   **理论贡献**：梳理了I型和II型多铁性的分类，并强调了II型多铁性（特别是由螺旋磁序通过DMI或自旋流机制诱导的铁电性）在二维体系中实现强耦合的重要性。提出了“三铁性”等新概念，为理论探索开辟了新方向。
> -   **实践价值**：为实验研究者提供了清晰的制备和表征技术路线图。为器件工程师提供了关于如何利用不同耦合效应（磁电、压电、磁弹）设计多功能器件的物理见解。

> ## 六、未来发展方向展望
> 
> 文章在总结部分提出了多个极具潜力的研究方向，可归纳如下：
> 
> 1.  **探索新型室温多铁性材料**：当前大多数二维多铁性材料的铁电或磁转变温度远低于室温，这是应用的致命短板。寻找具有**室温及以上**功能性的新材料是首要任务。
> 2.  **增强磁电耦合效应**：II型多铁性虽有强耦合，但通常极化很小，转变温度低。需要探索新的耦合机制（如界面电荷转移、轨道杂化）或设计异质结，在室温下实现巨大的、可被电场有效调控的磁电效应。
> 3.  **发展“三铁性”及更多序参量耦合**：除铁电-铁磁外，融合铁弹性、铁谷性（**Ferrovalley**）等更多序参量于一个二维材料中，实现“三铁”（Triferroics）乃至“多铁”，将为多功能器件提供前所未有的自由度。例如，文末提到的GdI₂、Nb₃X₈等材料已展现出此潜力。
> 4.  **晶圆级可控制备与无损集成**：从实验室的微米级样品走向晶圆级（英寸级）的均匀、高质量薄膜制备，并开发与现有硅基CMOS工艺兼容的洁净转移和集成技术，是实现工业化的关键。
> 5.  **动态多铁性与量子效应**：目前研究多集中于静态多铁性序。未来应更多关注超快时间尺度下的动态磁电耦合（如电磁振子），并探索多铁性与量子物态（如拓扑、超导）的联系，这可能会催生全新的量子器件原理。

> ## 七、学术思考与批判性分析
> 
> 尽管本文是一篇出色的综述，但为启迪深度思考，我们提出以下批判性问题：
> 
> 1.  **关于“单层多铁性”的普适性挑战**：文章重点介绍了NiI₂和Cr₂S₃的成功案例。然而，对于大多数被理论预测的二维多铁性材料，其合成仍面临巨大挑战。**一个关键问题是：在这些成功案例中，哪些是偶然的，哪些是普适的生长机理？** 例如，Cr₂S₃依靠衬底诱导的畸变，这种方法是否普遍适用于其他非范德华多铁性材料？如果不是，我们是否缺乏一种更通用的“自下而上”组装策略？
>     
> 2.  **表征技术的“伪影”与确证**：SHG和PFM是判定铁电性的“金标准”，但它们也可能产生误导。例如，表面电荷积累、离子迁移或非铁电性的压电效应也可能产生类似信号。**论文在整合这些表征结果时，并未充分讨论如何排除这些假象。** 一个严谨的验证逻辑链至关重要，例如，是否所有宣称有铁电性的材料都实现了电滞回线、PFM相位翻转和SHG开关等多重证据的相互印证？
>     
> 3.  **“强”磁电耦合的量化标准缺失**：文章多次提及“强磁电耦合”，但未给出一个清晰的量化标准。**如何定义“强”？** 是磁电耦合系数α（α = dP/dH 或 dM/dE）达到特定值？还是指电场可完全翻转磁化方向？缺乏统一的量化标准，使得不同材料体系间的比较变得困难，也模糊了从“有趣物理现象”到“实用器件性能”的界限。
>     
> 4.  **从“效应”到“器件”的鸿沟**：文章阐述了多种器件应用原理，但多为概念性展示。**一个关键的现实问题是：这些基于二维多铁性的器件性能，与现有成熟技术（如HfO₂基铁电存储器、STT-MRAM）相比，在写/读速度、耐久性、保持力、功耗和集成密度等关键指标上，是否有数量级的优势？** 综述未能提供这种对比视角，而这是判断其能否最终实现技术颠覆的关键。
>     
> 5.  **“三铁性”等复杂概念的物理内涵**：文章在展望中提及“三铁性”等概念，令人兴奋。但**这些多种序参量是真正独立且可被独立操控的，还是仅仅是一种复合效应？** 例如，一个“铁电-铁磁-铁谷”耦合材料，其谷极化能否在不改变铁电和铁磁态的情况下被单独翻转？如果不能，那么这种耦合带来的更多是功能叠加还是控制干扰？这需要更深入的物理分析。

> ## 八、用户问题预测与解答
> 
> 基于对文献的理解，我们预测了以下5个读者可能会提出的延伸问题并予以解答。
> 
> **问题1：像我这样的初学者，总是分不清I型和II型多铁性材料。它们最根本的区别是什么？为什么II型多铁性更受关注？**
> 
> **答：** 这是一个核心问题。最根本的区别在于**铁电性的起源机制**。
> 
> -   **I型多铁性**：铁电性和磁性**来源不同，彼此独立**。铁电性通常由结构相变（如孤对电子有序、电荷有序化）引起，磁性与局域电子自旋相关。它们就像同一间屋子里的两个租客，共处一室但关系不大，因此**耦合很弱**。优点是铁电或磁性转变温度可能较高。
> -   **II型多铁性**：铁电性**直接由特殊的磁结构所诱导**。例如，在NiI₂中，铁电性是由一种称为“螺旋磁序”的特殊自旋排列方式，通过**自旋-轨道耦合**效应产生的。这就好比自旋序本身带上了电荷序，二者是“共生”关系，因此**耦合非常强**。由于磁电耦合强，用磁场控制电极化或用电场控制磁化都更容易，这在器件应用上极为诱人，因此备受关注。但其缺点是这种特殊的磁结构通常只在低温下稳定，且诱导出的电极化强度较小。
> 
> **问题2：文章提到了很多表征技术，比如SHG和PFM。如果我想证明一个新合成的二维材料是多铁性的，最简洁有力的证据链是什么？**
> 
> **答：** 要证明一个材料是“多铁性”的，需要分两步，分别证明铁电性和磁性，并最后证明它们之间存在耦合。
> 
> 1.  **证明铁电性**：
>     -   **直接证据**：**PFM**（压电力显微镜）是首选。写一个畴（施加正/负电压），然后读畴（探测压电响应），如果出现相位翻转180°的滞回线，这是铁电畴翻转的强烈证据。
>     -   **结构证据**：**SHG**（光学二次谐波产生）可证明结构非中心对称，这是铁电体存在的先决条件。若SHG信号能随电场翻转，则证据更强。**STEM**直接观察到离子位移，是原子尺度的终极证据。
> 2.  **证明磁性**：**SQUID**测量磁化强度随温度（M-T曲线）和磁场（M-H磁滞回线）的变化，确定居里温度或奈尔温度，证明了磁矩的存在和有序排列。
> 3.  **证明耦合（多铁性）**：这是最关键的一步，证明两者不是独立共存。

> ## 1 Introduction
> 
> ## 1 引言
> 
> Multiferroic materials, which exhibit more than one ferroic ordering – ferroelectricity (FE), ferromagnetism (FM), ferroelasticity (FA), ferrotoroidicity (FT), ferrovalley – in a single phase, are well known for their fundamental and technological applications, such as nonvolatile memories, sensors, efficient renewable energy harvesting, spintronics, synaptic devices, and actuators [1–7]. 多铁性材料在单一相中表现出一种以上的铁性序——铁电性、铁磁性、铁弹性、铁旋性、铁谷性——因其在基础研究和技术应用（如非易失性存储器、传感器、高效可再生能源采集、自旋电子学、突触器件和执行器）中的重要价值而广为人知。 **Ferroelectricity (FE)**: 铁电性，指材料具有自发电极化，且极化方向可在外电场作用下反转的特性。 **Ferromagnetism (FM)**: 铁磁性，指材料具有自发磁化，且磁化方向可在外磁场作用下反转的特性。 **Ferroelasticity (FA)**: 铁弹性，指材料具有自发应变，且应变状态可在外应力作用下反转的特性。 **Ferrotoroidicity (FT)**: 铁旋性，一种由磁矩环形排列产生的铁性序，其序参量在空间反演和时间反演下均改变符号。 **Ferrovalley**: 铁谷性，指二维材料中能谷极化状态可被外部场调控和反转的特性。
> 
> Over the past decades, numerous studies on different bulk multiferroics have been reported, and most of them were complex oxides [1, 8–11]. However, traditional bulk multiferroic materials are difficult to meet the demands of practical applications because of their size limitation, polarization origin, interface effect, and reversal mechanism. In comparison to the traditional systems, 2D materials exhibit obviously different and fancifully physical properties with stable layered structures depending on weak interlayer and strong intralayer forces [4, 12–16]. 在过去的几十年里，已有大量关于不同块体多铁性材料的研究报道，其中大多数是复杂氧化物。然而，传统的块体多铁性材料由于尺寸限制、极化起源、界面效应和反转机制等问题，难以满足实际应用的需求。与传统体系相比，二维材料展现出明显不同的、奇妙的物理性质，其稳定的层状结构依赖于弱的层间力和强的层内力。
> 
> Recently, the first pure 2D monolayer NiI2 multiferroic material was experimentally prepared [13]. Therefore, new strategies for designing artificial 2D multiferroic materials have been naturally raised [17–18]. These compounds consisting of the weak van der Waals (vdW) forces between the layers can easily reach the 2D limit to achieve the building blocks with different and novel properties [19–26]. 最近，首个纯二维单层NiI2多铁性材料在实验上被制备出来。因此，设计人工二维多铁性材料的新策略自然被提出。这些由层间弱范德华力构成的化合物可以很容易地达到二维极限，从而获得具有不同新颖性质的基本构建单元。
> 
> 2D single layers can be easily exfoliated, stacked and twisted, resulting in a series of emergent phenomena including multiferroicity [27–30]. FE destructs the space-inversion symmetry, meanwhile FM breaks the time-reversal symmetry. However, FA does not break the space-inversion symmetry and the time-reversal symmetry. Moreover, FT destructs both the space-inversion and the time-reversal symmetry [4, 31], as shown in Fig. 1. 二维单层可以轻松地被剥离、堆叠和扭转，从而产生包括多铁性在内的一系列涌现现象。铁电性破坏空间反演对称性，同时铁磁性破坏时间反演对称性。然而，铁弹性既不破坏空间反演对称性，也不破坏时间反演对称性。此外，铁旋性同时破坏空间反演和时间反演对称性，如图1所示。 **Space-inversion symmetry**: 空间反演对称性，指物理系统在空间坐标反演变换下保持不变的对称性。 **Time-reversal symmetry**: 时间反演对称性，指物理系统在时间反演变换下保持不变的对称性。
> 
> Generally, multiferroics can often be classified as type I and type II, which are based on the weak or strong coupling between the orders [2, 31–33], as depicted in Fig. 2. Meanwhile, type I multiferroics can be also divided into three subgroups: (i) lone-pair multiferroics; (ii) charge ordered multiferroics; (iii) geometrically frustrated multiferroics. Charge-ordered multiferroics are often observed in transition metal compounds with ions that formally have a mixed valence. The advantages of type-I multiferroics are the large electric polarization (of nonmagnetic origin) and high magnetic transition temperatures, as magnetic frustration is not required. Bringing FE and magnetism together is a problem, since FE usually involves transition metal ions with empty d-shells that are non-magnetic [34]. 通常，多铁性材料可根据铁性序之间的弱耦合或强耦合分为第I类和第II类，如图2所示。同时，第I类多铁性材料也可分为三个亚类：(i)孤对电子多铁性材料；(ii)电荷有序多铁性材料；(iii)几何阻挫多铁性材料。电荷有序多铁性材料常见于具有混合价态离子的过渡金属化合物中。第I类多铁性材料的优点在于其大的电极化（非磁性起源）和高磁转变温度，因为不需要磁阻挫。将铁电性和磁性结合起来是一个难题，因为铁电性通常涉及具有空d壳层的非磁性过渡金属离子。
> 
> An additional challenge on the way to the electric control of magnetism is the coupling between the electric polarization and magnetization in bulk and at domain walls. Moreover, type II multiferroic is a FE as a derivative of magnetism in small polarizations and strong magnetoelectric coupling at low transition temperatures. Type-II multiferroics exhibiting strong magnetoelectric couplings [1, 32] offer enormous technological applications, such as spintronics, memory and efficient energy management in computation [38–40]. 实现电控磁的另一个挑战是块体和畴壁中电极化与磁化强度之间的耦合。此外，第II类多铁性材料是一种铁电性，作为磁性的衍生，具有小的极化强度和强磁电耦合，但转变温度较低。表现出强磁电耦合的第II类多铁性材料为自旋电子学、存储器和计算中的高效能量管理等提供了巨大的技术应用前景。 **Domain walls**: 畴壁，指材料中不同铁性畴（如铁电畴、铁磁畴）之间的界面区域。
> 
> Achieving multiferroicity and enhancing magnetoelectric coupling are accompanied by phase changes through magnetic phase transitions. Even plenty of theoretical studies on 2D multiferroic materials have been carried on, experimental explorations just have made a few breakthroughs in 2D multiferroic materials with single layers, such as NiI2 [13] and Cr2S3 [40]. 实现多铁性和增强磁电耦合伴随着通过磁相变发生的相变。尽管已经开展了大量关于二维多铁性材料的理论研究，但实验探索仅在单层二维多铁性材料（如NiI2和Cr2S3）中取得了少数突破。
> 
> In addition, most materials, particularly multiferroic materials, face significant technological challenges in terms of downsizing and integration, which prompt scientists to seek out low-dimensional multiferroic materials with atomic thickness. Unlike bulk materials, 2D systems intriguingly tend to host multiple ferroic orders in one single phase, e.g., FE and FA, which was predicted in monolayer group IV monochalcogenides [41], and FM and FE in monolayer α-SnO [42]. The type-II multiferroic order displayed by NiI2, arising from the combination of a magnetic spin spiral order and a strong spin-orbit coupling, allows probing the multiferroic order in the scanning tunnel microscopy (STM) experiments [43]. 此外，大多数材料，特别是多铁性材料，在小型化和集成方面面临重大的技术挑战，这促使科学家寻找具有原子厚度的低维多铁性材料。与块体材料不同，二维体系倾向于在单一相中包含多种铁性序，例如，单层IV族单硫族化物中预测的铁电性和铁弹性，以及单层α-SnO中的铁磁性和铁电性。NiI2展示的第II类多铁性序源于磁自旋螺旋序和强自旋轨道耦合的结合，使得在扫描隧道显微镜实验中探测多铁性序成为可能。 **Spin-orbit coupling**: 自旋轨道耦合，指电子自旋与其轨道运动之间的相互作用，在凝聚态物理中可导致多种新奇效应。
> 
> In this review, current research advances in 2D multiferroic materials have been discussed. A systematic review on fabrication methods, advances in characterization and experimental discoveries, as well as challenge and prospects for 2D multiferroic materials is introduced. 在本综述中，讨论了二维多铁性材料的最新研究进展。系统介绍了二维多铁性材料的制备方法、表征进展和实验发现，以及挑战和前景。

> ## 2 Growth methods of 2D multiferroic materials and advances in characterizations
> 
> ## 2 二维多铁性材料的生长方法与表征进展
> 
> Both top-down and bottom-up synthesis strategies of 2D materials are usually applied to research in the laboratory. Top-down schemes usually involve mechanical cleavage and chemical exfoliation by means of solvent or intercalated ion. The mechanical cleavage method depends on repeatedly exfoliating from bulk crystals with an adhesive tape, which was first used for the stripped graphene and then has been developed to boron nitride (BN), transition metal dichalcogenides (TMDs), etc. The acquired layered materials exhibit high crystalline nature and clean surface area, however, the thickness and lateral size of the obtained samples become uncontrollable. 二维材料的合成策略通常分为自上而下和自下而上两种，均常用于实验室研究。自上而下的方案通常涉及机械剥离和通过溶剂或插层离子进行的化学剥离。机械剥离法依赖于使用胶带从块体晶体上反复剥离，该方法最初用于剥离石墨烯，随后扩展到氮化硼、过渡金属二硫族化物等。获得的层状材料表现出高结晶性和清洁的表面，但所得样品的厚度和横向尺寸变得不可控。 **Top-down**: 自上而下法，指从块体材料出发，通过剥离等手段获得薄层或单层材料的方法。 **Bottom-up**: 自下而上法，指从原子、分子等前驱体出发，通过化学反应在基底上直接生长材料的方法。 **Transition metal dichalcogenides (TMDs)**: 过渡金属二硫族化物，一类具有MX2化学式的层状材料，其中M为过渡金属，X为硫族元素。
> 
> By contrast, chemical exfoliation represents a higher scalability, but there are increasing unavoidable yield comes for the flake size expense and extra solvent/ion contamination. Totally, the layered multiferroic materials can exfoliate from bulks despite mechanical, chemical, and electrical methods due to their weak interlayer forces. However, there exists challenges to remove from parent crystals attributing to the strong cross-binding for the non-vdW multiferroic materials such as Cr2S3 [40], BiFeO3 [44], and Cr5Te8 [45]. However, there still lack of an operatable and scalable top-down method for obtaining acquired sizes and thicknesses of 2D multiferroics. 相比之下，化学剥离具有更高的可扩展性，但以牺牲薄片尺寸和引入额外溶剂/离子污染为代价，其产率问题日益不可避免。总的来说，层状多铁性材料由于层间力弱，可以通过机械、化学和电学方法从块体上剥离。然而，对于非范德华多铁性材料（如Cr2S3、BiFeO3和Cr5Te8），由于存在强的交叉结合，将其从母体晶体上剥离存在挑战。然而，目前仍缺乏一种可操作且可扩展的自上而下方法来获得具有所需尺寸和厚度的二维多铁性材料。
> 
> Taking the bottom-up synthesis into considering, the techniques of chemical vapour deposition (CVD), physical vapor deposition (PVD) and molecular beam epitaxy (MBE) are comprehensively used to prepare atomic-thickness film via straightway depositing the premeditated compounds on a particular substrate. Then the obtained 2D materials reveal good crystallinity, high layer controllability, and large area uniformity, which provides a widespread prospect in fundamental research and device applications. Here, the synthesis and engineering of various types of ferroics (ferroelectrics, ferromagnetics and ferroelastics) based on the their characteristics are briefly described. 考虑自下而上的合成方法，化学气相沉积、物理气相沉积和分子束外延技术被广泛用于通过在特定基底上直接沉积预设化合物来制备原子厚度薄膜。获得的二维材料展现出良好的结晶性、高的层数可控性和大面积均匀性，为基础研究和器件应用提供了广阔的前景。在此，简要描述了基于其特性的各种铁性材料（铁电体、铁磁体和铁弹体）的合成与工程。
> 
> Generally, two current synthetic strategies, including top-down and bottom-up method, are used to prepare 2D materials [4]. The top-down method is that few-layer or single-layer 2D materials are exfoliated from bulk crystals by certain technical means, such as mechanical exfoliation and liquid phase exfoliation methods. The bottom-up method refers to nanoparticles (such as atoms, molecules, and clusters) through the interaction grow into 2D materials with vapour deposition method, typically including CVD, chemical vapor transport (CVT), PVD, MBE, etc. 通常，目前制备二维材料有两种合成策略，包括自上而下和自下而上的方法。自上而下法是通过机械剥离和液相剥离等技术手段从块体晶体上剥离少层或单层二维材料。自下而上法是指纳米粒子（如原子、分子和团簇）通过相互作用，利用气相沉积方法生长为二维材料，典型方法包括CVD、化学气相传输、PVD、MBE等。 **Chemical vapor transport (CVT)**: 化学气相传输，一种通过气相传输反应物并在目标区域沉积晶体的晶体生长方法。
> 
> The mechanical exfoliation method is firstly technically introduced to prepare 2D layered materials [46]. A process of the mechanical exfoliation method is shown in Fig. 3. In this exfoliation process, the few-layer materials are exfoliated with the tape on the multilayer bulk materials' surfaces and the normal force is to overcome the vdW attraction between adjacent flakes. Single-layer 2D materials can be achieved after repeating this process at numerous times. This mechanical exfoliation method exhibits fast and easily to obtain 2D layered materials. The qualities of preparing samples with this method are very high. It is a basically scientific research method to prepare 2D materials in the laboratory. And it is convenient to research their physical properties and electrical devices. However, this method faces many difficulties, such as poor repeatability, time-consuming, and low yield, to hinder extensive large-scale industrial applications. 机械剥离法是最早被技术性地引入用于制备二维层状材料的方法。机械剥离法的过程如图3所示。在此剥离过程中，使用胶带在多层块体材料表面上剥离少层材料，利用法向力克服相邻薄片之间的范德华吸引力。重复此过程多次后，可以获得单层二维材料。这种机械剥离法快速且容易获得二维层状材料。用此方法制备的样品质量非常高。这是一种在实验室中制备二维材料的基本科学研究方法。而且便于研究其物理性质和电学器件。然而，该方法面临许多困难，如重复性差、耗时长、产率低，阻碍了其广泛的规模化工业应用。
> 
> Liquid phase exfoliation method is classed as ultrasonic-assisted exfoliation, ion exchange and ion intercalation exfoliation method in Figs. 3(b, c). Due to the draw backs of the mechanical exfoliation method leading to low yield, the layered compounds are put into the liquid to produce numerous dispersed nanosheets. A range of 2D materials can be exfoliated to satisfy the demand of existing industrial technologies. Graphene was firstly reported to obtained by dispersing graphite powder into specific organic solvents. After sonication and centrifugation, the low-cost and large-scale yield of graphene was achieved. This method has an advantage on easily producing graphene. But the graphene concentration is extremely low for practical applications. At present, the exfoliation method originates from liquid cavitation. In the sonication-assisted exfoliation method, ultrasonic waves generate cavitation bubbles, which collapse into high-energy jets, destroy layered crystallites and produce exfoliated nanosheets [47], as shown in Fig. 3(c). This method for obtaining graphene is very successful, although several shortcomings of sonication exhibit basal and edges planes. Ions prefer intercalation into the vdW gap of 2D layered materials, which is a promising synthesis strategy at low temperature. 液相剥离法分为超声辅助剥离、离子交换和离子插层剥离法，如图3(b, c)所示。由于机械剥离法存在产率低的缺点，将层状化合物放入液体中可产生大量分散的纳米片。一系列二维材料可以通过剥离来满足现有工业技术的需求。石墨烯最早是通过将石墨粉末分散到特定有机溶剂中获得的。经过超声和离心处理后，实现了低成本、大规模的石墨烯生产。这种方法在易于生产石墨烯方面具有优势。但对于实际应用，石墨烯浓度极低。目前，剥离法源于液体空化效应。在超声辅助剥离法中，超声波产生空化气泡，这些气泡坍缩成高能射流，破坏层状微晶并产生剥离的纳米片，如图3(c)所示。这种获取石墨烯的方法非常成功，尽管超声处理存在一些缺点，如会影响基面和边缘面。离子倾向于插层到二维层状材料的范德华间隙中，这是一种在低温下有前景的合成策略。
> 
> Among a large number of research approaches, vapour-phase deposition (VPD) method is a high promising strategy for synthesizing scaled-up 2D layered chalcogenides or chalogenides for industrial manufacture [48, 49]. Vapour-phase deposition includes CVD shown in Fig. 4(a), CVT, PVD shown in Fig. 4(b), MBE in Fig. 4(c) and atomic-layer deposition (ALD) in Fig. 4(d). Compared to the "bottom-up" liquid-phase synthesis methods with hydrothermal reaction or colloidal synthesis [50] and "top-down" exfoliation approaches [51], VPD methods provide better control over the crystal qualities, the layer numbers, and the large crystal sizes, which is suitable for the characterization of materials properties and the fabrication of devices. Despite the recent numerous advances in the 2D materials, the desired layers, and sizes of the 2D materials with facile and controllable strategies still face various challenges and difficulties. 在众多研究方法中，气相沉积方法是合成用于工业制造的规模化二维层状硫族化物或卤族化物的极具前景的策略。气相沉积包括图4(a)所示的CVD、CVT、图4(b)所示的PVD、图4(c)所示的MBE和图4(d)所示的原子层沉积。与基于水热反应或胶体合成的"自下而上"液相合成方法以及"自上而下"剥离方法相比，气相沉积方法对晶体质量、层数和晶体尺寸提供了更好的控制，适用于材料性质表征和器件制备。尽管近年来二维材料取得了众多进展，但通过简便可控的策略获得所需层数和尺寸的二维材料仍然面临各种挑战和困难。 **Vapour-phase deposition (VPD)**: 气相沉积，一种在气相环境中将材料沉积到基底上的薄膜制备技术总称。 **Atomic-layer deposition (ALD)**: 原子层沉积，一种基于自限制表面反应的薄膜沉积技术，能够逐层精确控制膜厚。

> ### 2.1 Chemical vapour deposition (CVD)
> 
> ### 2.1 化学气相沉积
> 
> The CVD process involves the precursor species with vapour-phase reactions and the depositing materials. The metal and chalcogen or halogen precursors can be either solids that evaporate at elevated temperatures or volatile species introduced through a carrier gas [48, 49]. The distinct precursors lead to different growth setups and considerations. Accordingly, present CVD methods to synthesize 2D materials are often divided into two main categories, solid-source CVD and gas-source CVD as depicted in Fig. 4(a). CVD过程涉及前驱体物种的气相反应和材料沉积。金属和硫族或卤素前驱体可以是在高温下蒸发的固体，也可以是通过载气引入的挥发性物种。不同的前驱体导致不同的生长装置和考虑因素。因此，目前合成二维材料的CVD方法通常分为两大类：固源CVD和气源CVD，如图4(a)所示。 **Chemical vapour deposition (CVD)**: 化学气相沉积，一种通过气相化学反应在基底表面沉积固态薄膜的方法。
> 
> In a typical solid-source CVD, the solid metal or metal compound precursors and the chalcogen or chalogen precursors are located at different temperature zones in a same tube furnace in Fig. 4(a). After heating, the evaporated precursors either with the vapour phase or adsorbing on the substrate. To enhance the yield of the single-layer and increase the domain size of the monolayer, the growth promoters, such as alkali metal halides, are often used to increase the growth speed of precursor evaporation, lateral growth and 2D crystal nucleation [52]. 在典型的固源CVD中，固体金属或金属化合物前驱体与硫族或卤素前驱体位于同一管式炉中不同的温区，如图4(a)所示。加热后，蒸发的前驱体要么处于气相中，要么吸附在基底上。为了提高单层产率并增加单层畴尺寸，碱金属卤化物等生长促进剂常用于加快前驱体蒸发、横向生长和二维晶体成核的速度。
> 
> In a gas-source CVD process for 2D layered chalcogenide or chalogenides growth, the volatile metal and chalcogen precursors are either directly introduced through a mass flow controller (MFC) or bubbled into the system using a carrier gas in Fig. 4(a). Then the precursors produce reaction in the high-temperature zone and deposition of 2D layered chalcogenides or chalogenides. Apart from the hot-wall reactor, cold-wall reactors can also be used, in which only the substrate undergoes heating, leaving the chamber walls unheated [53]. 在用于二维层状硫族化物或卤族化物生长的气源CVD过程中，挥发性金属和硫族前驱体要么通过质量流量控制器直接引入，要么使用载气鼓泡进入系统，如图4(a)所示。然后前驱体在高温区发生反应，并沉积二维层状硫族化物或卤族化物。除热壁反应器外，还可以使用冷壁反应器，其中仅对基底进行加热，腔壁不被加热。
> 
> Recently, numerous 2D atomic-scale multiferroic materials have been synthensized by CVD method, for example, wafer-scale one-unit-cell of chromium sulfide multiferroic 2D materials has been successfully synthensized by CVD method in Fig. 5. Song et al. [40] have grown one-unit-cell non-layered Cr2S3 multiferroic material depending on an interface modulated strategy under the scheme of a facile CVD approach. 2D Cr2S3 contributes to tpye II multiferroicity, which exhibits room-temperature ferroelectricity, strong magnetoelectric coupling and long-range ferromagnetic order. The Curie temperature of ferromagneticity is 200 K, which is two times higher than bulk counterpart. Meanwhile, single crystal Cr2S3 is regarded as the largest and thinnest multiferroics, spontaneously making a great breakthrough toward the batch synthesis. These results can really provide a strategy for future industrial application of multiferroic materials in the fields of next-generation magnetoelectric devices. 最近，通过CVD方法合成了大量二维原子级多铁性材料，例如，晶圆级一单胞硫化铬多铁性二维材料已通过CVD方法成功合成，如图5所示。Song等人基于界面调制策略，在简便的CVD方法方案下，生长了一单胞非层状Cr2S3多铁性材料。二维Cr2S3具有第II类多铁性，表现出室温铁电性、强磁电耦合和长程铁磁序。铁磁性的居里温度为200 K，是块体对应物的两倍。同时，单晶Cr2S3被认为是最大和最薄的多铁性材料，在批量合成方面取得了重大突破。这些成果确实可以为未来多铁性材料在下一代磁电器件领域的工业应用提供一种策略。 **Curie temperature**: 居里温度，指铁磁材料从铁磁态转变为顺磁态的临界温度。
> 
> The CVT method is old yet powerful, which is recently adopted for synthesizing 2D layered materials, producing bulk crystals of layered materials or corresponding 2D films, such as single-layer NiI2 as shown in Fig. 6 [13, 43]. Notably, CVT reactions, which represent a common feature: a condensed phase, a typical solid, sublimes in a gaseous reactant, and the usual crystal form, are different from CVD. Specifically, the main difference between CVT and CVD depends on the source materials to synthesize the products, where solid reactants are usually applied in CVT and gaseous precursors are typically used in CVD. The difference induces subsequently consequent distinctions in designing reactor, controlling operational, and holding desired product features. CVT方法古老但强大，最近被用于合成二维层状材料，生产层状材料的块体晶体或相应的二维薄膜，如图6所示的单层NiI2。值得注意的是，CVT反应具有一个共同特征：凝聚相（典型的固体）在气态反应物中升华，并以通常的晶体形式生长，这与CVD不同。具体来说，CVT和CVD之间的主要区别在于合成产物的源材料不同，CVT中通常使用固体反应物，而CVD中通常使用气态前驱体。这种差异随后导致在反应器设计、操作控制和保持所需产品特性方面的不同。
> 
> 2D multiferroic materials, such as NiI2 and CuCrSe2, have been synthesized via a CVT method [13, 55]. And it is discovered that 2D NiI2 and CuCrSe2 present type-II multiferroic order in a single atomic layer. At the same time, single-layer CuCrSe2 has been synthesized by a CVT method with polycrystalline powders [55], as denoted in Fig. 7. Results show that single-layer CuCrSe2 represents multiferroicity at high-temperature, hosting 120 K ferromagnetism and room-temperature FE. Notably, the FE-induced orbital shift of Cr atoms in single-layer CuCrSe2 multiferroic material enhances the ferromagnetic coupling, which is different from both types-I and II multiferroicity, supported by a combination of scanning transmission electron microscopy (STEM), second-harmonic generation (SHG), piezo-response force microscopy (PFM), magnetic, and Hall measurements. These novel observations not only offer a unique avenue for studying intrinsic magnetoelectric couplings at the atomic thin limit but also have great breakthroughs in potential development of nanoelectronic and spintronic devices based on 2D multiferroics. 二维多铁性材料，如NiI2和CuCrSe2，已通过CVT方法合成。并且发现二维NiI2和CuCrSe2在单原子层中呈现出第II类多铁性序。同时，单层CuCrSe2已通过CVT方法使用多晶粉末合成，如图7所示。结果表明，单层CuCrSe2在高温下表现出多铁性，具有120 K的铁磁性和室温铁电性。值得注意的是，单层CuCrSe2多铁性材料中由铁电性诱导的Cr原子轨道位移增强了铁磁耦合，这与第I类和第II类多铁性都不同，并得到了扫描透射电子显微镜、二次谐波产生、压电响应力显微镜、磁性和霍尔测量组合的支持。这些新颖的观察不仅为研究原子薄极限下的本征磁电耦合提供了独特途径，而且在基于二维多铁性材料的纳米电子和自旋电子器件的潜在发展方面取得了重大突破。 **Scanning transmission electron microscopy (STEM)**: 扫描透射电子显微镜，一种结合了扫描电子显微镜与透射电子显微镜功能的成像技术，可提供原子级分辨率。 **Second-harmonic generation (SHG)**: 二次谐波产生，一种非线性光学过程，两个相同频率的光子与材料相互作用后产生一个频率加倍的光子，对反演对称性破缺敏感。 **Piezo-response force microscopy (PFM)**: 压电响应力显微镜，一种基于原子力显微镜的技术，用于在纳米尺度上表征铁电畴和局部压电特性。
> 
> ### 2.2 Physical vapour deposition (PVD)
> 
> ### 2.2 物理气相沉积
> 
> PVD is a very simple and representative vapour-phase technique, where only the precursors are the bulk form of target materials [48]. Even though the direct coupling between ferroelectric and ferromagnetic order in a single material still remains challenging, 2D multiferroic material p-doped SnSe has been successfully synthesized on mica substrates by a physical vapor deposition approach [56]. PVD是一种非常简单且具有代表性的气相技术，其中仅使用目标材料的块体形式作为前驱体。尽管在单一材料中实现铁电性和铁磁性序之间的直接耦合仍然具有挑战性，但二维多铁性材料p型掺杂SnSe已通过物理气相沉积方法在云母基底上成功合成。 **Physical vapour deposition (PVD)**: 物理气相沉积，一种通过物理过程（如蒸发、溅射）将材料沉积到基底上的薄膜制备技术。
> 
> To synthesize thin layers by PVD, offering a horizontal tube reactor is usually necessary. In a typical PVD process in Fig. 4(b), the bulk precursor SnSe powders are evaporated at an elevation-temperature and then deposited on the target mica substrates that lie in a lower temperature zone. A low reactor pressure (~101–102 Pa, pressure-achievement depending on a mechanical pump) often favorably results in improving the effectively evaporated precursor and gas-phase diffusion of the precursor [57]. It is indicated that PVD synthesized 2D metallic p-doped SnSe multiferroic material presents room-temperature ferrimagnetism of 337 K and ferroelectricity even under the depolarizing field in Fig. 8. The locally segregated-phase SnSe microdomains are emerged. And the interfacial charge transfer in fabricated sample is also accompanied at the same time. Consequently, 2D SnSe multiferroic material exhibits degenerate semiconductor and metallic feature. The 2D p-doped SnSe exists both ferrimagnetism and ferroelectricity, which represents its multiferroic feature in Fig. 8. > #### 2.5.1 Scanning tunneling microscopy (STM) and scanning probe microscopy (SPM)
> 
> #### 2.5.1 扫描隧道显微镜与扫描探针显微镜
> 
> Scanning tunneling microscopy (STM), as a powerful tool to detect the microscopic domain structures in 2D multiferroic monolayers, can provide detailed information on the atomic-structure surface, originating from the electron tunneling mechanism. Scanning probe microscopy (SPM) allows the spatial imaging, measurement, and manipulation of nano and atomic scale surfaces in real space. The tunneling current relies on the vacuum-barrier thickness between two metallic or semiconducting electrodes. For practical STM facilities, the one electrode is used as a metallic tip that consists of gold, silver, tungsten, or platinum-iridium, etc; the other electrode is an investigative sample. The feedback system in STM can adjust the height of the tip over the sample surface with a continuously variable gap so that any change in the tunneling current is compensated by the feedback loop during scanning. The STM image of the sample is thus obtained by continuously digitizing the feedback signal, which can derive the surface morphology of the sample. The constant current mode is very useful for the analysis of rough surfaces. The scan rate of another constant height mode can be very fast, which provides the opportunity of recording the real-time video of the rather flat sample. > #### 2.5.2 Second harmonic generation (SHG)
> 
> #### 2.5.2 二次谐波产生
> 
> Second harmonic generation (SHG) is a powerful tool to determine the ferroelectric and magnetic order in multiferroics, as an essential second-order nonlinear optical effect consisting of the synchronous transformation of two photons interplaying with other materials into a single photon. SHG effects often appear in the noncentrosymmetric mediums (materials lacking inversion symmetry), where the radiation frequency is twice of the incident with an extreme light field such as a pulsed laser [93, 94]. Among the various optical characterizing methods, optical SHG has generated most tremendous impact on characterization of multiferroic materials due to its sensitivity, simplicity, and versatility at the atomic scale. The SHG technique is sufficiently sensitive to study 2D multiferroic materials for both spatial-inversion symmetry and time-reversal symmetry. A variety of 2D multiferroics have exhibited excellent SHG response, such as NiI2 [43, 58, 99, 100], CuCrSe2 [61], Cr2S3 [40], CuCrP2S6 [12, 59], p-doped SnSe [56], Te-doped WSe2 [86], Ti3C2Tx [90], Bi2NiMnO6 [92], BaFe2O4 [93], and CrI3/In2Se3 [94], have exhibited strong SHG response due to their intrinsic noncentrosymmetric structures. 二次谐波产生是确定多铁性材料中铁电和磁序的有力工具，它是一种基本的二阶非线性光学效应，包括两个光子与物质相互作用后同步转化为单个光子。SHG效应常出现在非中心对称介质（缺乏反演对称性的材料）中，在极端光场（如脉冲激光）下，辐射频率是入射光的两倍。在众多光学表征方法中，光学SHG由于其灵敏度、简便性和在原子尺度上的多功能性，对多铁性材料的表征产生了最巨大的影响。SHG技术对于研究二维多铁性材料的空间反演对称性和时间反演对称性都足够灵敏。多种二维多铁性材料已展现出优异的SHG响应，如NiI2、CuCrSe2、Cr2S3、CuCrP2S6、p型掺杂SnSe、Te掺杂WSe2、Ti3C2Tx、Bi2NiMnO6、BaFe2O4和CrI3/In2Se3，由于其固有的非中心对称结构，表现出强烈的SHG响应。

> #### 2.5.4 Birefringence measurements
> 
> #### 2.5.4 双折射测量
> 
> Birefringence measurement is a powerful tool to emerge from the magnetoelectric coupling for multiferroic materials characterization. The gyrotropic birefringence (GB) represents light-propagation-direction-dependent nonreciprocal response, which originates from a multiferroic manganite of the dynamical magnetoelectric coupling effect with spin-cycloid order as far as time-domain terahertz polarimetry is concerned [101]. The GB exposes the existing electromagnon resonance for the enhanced nonreciprocal optical rotation. The GB magnitude scale is proportional to the bilinear coupling ferroic order parameters P·M (P: electric polarization, M: magnetization) via the magnetic-field induced spin-flop transition. The enhancing GB originates from the dynamical magnetoelectric intermode coupling of electromagnon and antiferromagnetic resonance in a multiferroic helimagnet [102]. Optical birefringence arises from the low lattice symmetry at both the TN,1 and TN,2 transitions, and breaking the c-axis three-fold rotational symmetry (C3z) in the parent point group and reducting a single in-plane two-fold symmetry operation (C2) [13]. The optical anisotropy of NiI2 directly extracted from cross-polarized microscopy on birefringent domains in the one- and two-layer regions at T = 5, 15, 25 K [see Fig. 6(b)]. Significantly, the reduction of rotational symmetry in single-layer NiI2 from three- to two-fold is verified by angular-dependent linear dichroism measurements [see Fig. 6(c)]. 双折射测量是源自磁电耦合的多铁性材料表征的有力工具。旋光双折射表示光传播方向依赖的非互易响应，它起源于多铁性锰氧化物中自旋回旋序的动态磁电耦合效应，就时域太赫兹偏振测量而言。旋光双折射揭示了增强的非互易旋光性的电磁子共振。旋光双折射的幅度标度与双线性耦合铁性序参量P·M（P：电极化，M：磁化强度）成正比，通过磁场诱导的自旋翻转转变实现。增强的旋光双折射源于多铁性螺旋磁体中电磁子和反铁磁共振的动态磁电模间耦合。光学双折射源于TN,1和TN,2转变时的低晶格对称性，打破了母体点群中的c轴三重旋转对称性（C3z），并简化为单一的平面内二重对称操作（C2）。在T = 5, 15, 25 K时，通过交叉偏振显微镜在单层和双层区域的双折射畴上直接提取了NiI2的光学各向异性[见图6(b)]。重要的是，单层NiI2中旋转对称性从三重降到二重通过角度依赖的线二色性测量得到验证[见图6(c)]。 **Gyrotropic birefringence (GB)**: 旋光双折射，一种光传播方向依赖的非互易光学效应，与材料的磁电耦合相关。 **Spin-flop transition**: 自旋翻转转变，反铁磁材料在外磁场作用下，磁矩从反平行排列转变为垂直于磁场方向的排列状态的相变。

> #### 2.5.5 Transmission electron microscopy (TEM)
> 
> #### 2.5.5 透射电子显微镜
> 
> STEM technologies have significant advancements in investigating material chemistry and structure from the micro to the atomic scale due to ultra-high energy resolution monochromators, aberration-correction technology and state-of-the-art detectors/cameras [103]. This technique is powerful to comprehend and characterize the origins of multiferroic material and engineering materials, such as ferroelectricity, ferromagnetism, ferroelasticity, and piezoelectricity. STEM is directly able to observe the structural characteristics to establish a link with macroscopic properties. In addition, the methods available for exploring structures of multiferroic materials on the nanoscale and atomic scale require high-resolution electron microscopy (HREM) [31, 104]. > #### 2.5.10 Atomic force microscope (AFM)
> 
> #### 2.5.10 原子力显微镜
> 
> The AFM is a multifunctional tool to image the topography of multiferroic systems at nanometre resolution [112]. An atomic force microscope crucially composes of a sharp tip apex less than nanometre, which attaches to the softcantilever end. The operating principle is mainly required to monitor the forces interacting between the tip and the surface of the preparation sample, which cause the cantilever deflected to record via a laser beam focusing on the cantilever end and reflecting onto a position-sensitive photodiode. A piezoelectric scanner that moves either the cantilever or the sample allows the tip to scan the sample surface in x and y directions whereas a feedback loop between the deflection of the cantilever and the z-piezo allows control of the precise position of the tip and the applied force. In dynamic mode AFM, the tip is oscillated near the sample surface, either in non-contact or intermittent contact, and the oscillation amplitude or frequency is kept constant through the feedback loop. Large forces acting between the tip and the sample during imaging may also dramatically reduce the image resolution and cause molecular damage or displacement. In theory, the spatial resolution achieved on flat and smooth surfaces can be ≤1 nm in the horizontal plane and as low as 0.1 nm in the vertical plane [113]. Numerous multifunctional AFM-based techniques have been developed to characterize the local mechanical properties, electrical properties, optical characteristics, and thermal properties of the sample. > ### 3.1 Nonvolatile memory
> 
> ### 3.1 非易失性存储器
> 
> The burgeoning nonvolatile memory technologies based on multiferroic materials show great promise in various applications of low-power, high-speed and high-density memory for integrated circuits, which arises from the observation of long-range ferroic orders in 2D materials [5]. Unlike traditional storage devices, 2D multiferroic memories use electricity to calculate and store data simultaneously with low energy [76, 120]. In multiferroics (MF), high-speed energy efficient memory devices based on electric-field control magnetism manipulate magnetic states by recommending weak electric field instead of magnetic field. Magnetoelectric random access memory device (MERAM) consists of a single-phase thin-layer multiferroic material, whose specific magnetization direction is produced by exchange bias between a free FM layer and the MF layer. In MF layers, magnetoelectric (ME) coupling between ferroelectric and magnetic orders permit multistate memory where two polarization (P↑, P↓) directions control two magnetization (M↑, M↓) directions. Multiferroics hold the future for the ultimate memory device. The demonstration of a four-state resistive memory element in a tunnel junction with multiferroic barriers represents a major step in this direction [121]. In multiferroics, the coexistence of several order parameters and the magnetoelectric coupling can both be exploited in novel types of memory elements. As ferroelectric polarization and magnetization are used to encode binary information in ferroelectric random access memories (FERAMs) and magnetic random access memories (MRAMs), respectively [122]. Beyond the combination of ferroic properties in a single device, the electrical control of magnetization via the magnetoelectric coupling offers the opportunity of combining the respective advantages of FERAMs and MRAMs in the form of nonvolatile magnetic storage bits that are switched by an electrical field. Indeed, although the characteristics of MRAMs equal or surpass those of alternative nonvolatile memory technologies in terms of access time and endurance, they have a large handicap in their high writing energy. A possible solution for reducing the writing energy uses a spin-polarized current to reverse the magnetization of the storage layer by spin-transfer [123] rather than magnetic fields. Spin-transfer MRAMs are currently being developed by several companies and a 2 Mb memory was recently demonstrated [124]. Rare-earth orthoferrite SmFeO3 (SFO) is a promising single-phase multiferroic material because of simultaneous coexistence of magnetic and ferroelectric orders at room temperature. This kind of material will produce wide application prospect in future electric-write magnetic-read high-density memories [76]. Multiferroic and magnetoelectric composites, which are at the breakthrough toward technological applications [125]. On the one hand there is an ever-growing multitude of efficient classic materials combinations. On the other hand, specific solutions still exist beyond this main-stream evolution. 基于多铁性材料的新兴非易失性存储技术在集成电路的低功耗、高速和高密度存储等各种应用中展现出巨大前景，这源于在二维材料中观察到长程铁性序。与传统存储设备不同，二维多铁性存储器使用电进行同时计算和存储数据，且能耗低。在多铁性材料中，基于电场控制磁性的高速节能存储器件通过引入弱电场而非磁场来操纵磁态。磁电随机存取存储器由单相薄层多铁性材料组成，其特定磁化方向由自由铁磁层与多铁性层之间的交换偏置产生。在多铁性层中，铁电序和磁序之间的磁电耦合允许多态存储，其中两个极化方向（P↑, P↓）控制两个磁化方向（M↑, M↓）。多铁性材料承载着终极存储器件的未来。在多铁性势垒隧道结中演示四态阻变存储元件代表了这一方向上的重要一步。在多铁性材料中，几个序参量的共存和磁电耦合都可以用于新型存储元件。铁电极化和磁化分别用于铁电随机存取存储器和磁性随机存取存储器中编码二进制信息。除了在单一器件中结合铁性性质之外，通过磁电耦合实现电控磁化，提供了以电场切换的非易失性磁存储位形式结合FERAM和MRAM各自优势的机会。确实，尽管MRAM在存取时间和耐久性方面的特性等于或超过其他非易失性存储技术，但它们在高写入能量方面存在很大障碍。降低写入能量的一种可能解决方案是使用自旋极化电流通过自旋转移而非磁场来反转存储层的磁化。自旋转移MRAM目前正由多家公司开发，最近演示了2 Mb存储器。稀土正铁氧体SmFeO3是一种有前景的单相多铁性材料，因为在室温下同时存在磁序和铁电序。这类材料将在未来电写磁读高密度存储器中产生广阔的应用前景。多铁性和磁电复合材料正处于技术应用的突破点。一方面，存在越来越多的高效经典材料组合。另一方面，在这一主流发展之外，仍然存在特定的解决方案。 **Ferroelectric random access memories (FERAMs)**: 铁电随机存取存储器，利用铁电材料的极化状态来存储信息的非易失性存储器。 **Magnetic random access memories (MRAMs)**: 磁性随机存取存储器，利用磁隧道结的磁化方向来存储信息的非易失性存储器。 **Spin-transfer**: 自旋转移矩，一种利用自旋极化电流来翻转磁矩的机制，可用于低功耗磁存储。

> ### 3.2 Spintronics
> 
> ### 3.2 自旋电子学
> 
> The magnetoelectric coupling in multiferroic materials opens up an avenue for energy efficient spintronic devices, which are prospectively relying on nanomaterials with a smaller size than the spin relaxation length and tunable interface characteristics [126]. In comparison to the charge degree of freedom, electrons also have a spin degree of freedom. When spin-up and spin-down electrons are intrinsically or extrinsically separated, the spin polarization can be generated for spin-based devices [127]. The high efficiency of spin injection (detection) and the easy-control spin polarization states are always desired [128]. In recent years, compared to magnetically controlled spin-based devices, the electrically controlled spin-based devices have become more attractive owing to the lower power consumption. However, the disordered system obtained by the traditional method usually has low magnetic properties and is not suitable for the application. Therefore, exploring 2D materials with inherent magnetic properties is the goal of spintronics. For next-generation technology, magnetic systems attract broad attention due to the natural ability to store information and propagate information for logic functions through spin transport. Currents manipulating the magnetization state is turned out to be energy inefficient. Multiferroic thin-film heterostructures, combining ferromagnetic and ferroelectric orders, witness a surge of research in energy efficient electronics. The electric-field controlling magnetic order reduce energy dissipation by 2–3 orders of magnitude with respect to the state-of-the-art current control. The interfacial coupling determine magnetoelectric coupling between magnetic and electrical orders in multiferroic thin-film heterostructures though mechanical strain or magnetic exchange and the correlation domains in the neighbourhood of functional ferroic layers [129]. 多铁性材料中的磁电耦合为高能效自旋电子器件开辟了道路，这些器件有望依赖于尺寸小于自旋弛豫长度的纳米材料和可调的界面特性。与电荷自由度相比，电子还具有自旋自由度。当自旋向上和自旋向下的电子被内禀或外禀地分离时，可以为基于自旋的器件产生自旋极化。高效的自旋注入（检测）和易于控制的自旋极化态始终是人们所追求的。近年来，与磁控自旋基器件相比，电控自旋基器件由于功耗更低而变得更具吸引力。然而，传统方法获得的无序体系通常具有较低的磁性能，不适合应用。因此，探索具有固有磁性的二维材料是自旋电子学的目标。对于下一代技术，磁性体系因其通过自旋传输存储信息和传播逻辑功能信息的天然能力而受到广泛关注。用电流操控磁化状态被证明是能量低效的。结合铁磁序和铁电序的多铁性薄膜异质结构见证了高能效电子学研究的激增。电场控制磁序相对于最先进的电流控制，可减少2–3个数量级的能量耗散。界面耦合决定了多铁性薄膜异质结构中磁序和电序之间的磁电耦合，通过机械应变或磁交换以及功能铁性层附近的相关畴实现。 **Spintronics**: 自旋电子学，一门利用电子自旋自由度来存储、处理和传输信息的学科。 **Spin relaxation length**: 自旋弛豫长度，指电子在失去自旋极化特性之前能够传播的平均距离。
> 
> The recent research focuses on the functional multiferroics for spintronics applications. Several types of integration architectures for antiferromagnetic and ferroelectric BiFeO3 are concerned to serve the ferroelectric and ferromagnetic (La,Bi)MnO3 as an active tunnel barrier under the magnetic tunnel junctions with (La,Sr)MnO3 and Au electrodes [130]. The antiferromagnetic and magnetoelectric features also have induced an exchange coupling with a ferromagnet, which paves the way for electrically controlling magnetization and possibly manipulating the logic state of spintronic devices. There have been four resistance states existing in device, where two states originate from a spin filtering effect because of the ferromagnetic character of the barrier and another two states arise from the ferroelectric behavior in the ultrathin (La,Bi)MnO3 film. The ferroelectric polarization provides additional degree of freedom to novel functional spintronics, either handling gate-controlled magnetic memories or extracting extra order parameter for multiple-state memory elements. The generation and manipulation of spin-polarized current are critical for spintronic devices [39, 131]. The mechanism to generate and switch spin-polarized current by an electric field in multiferroic tunnel junctions (MFTJs), with symmetric interface terminations in an antiparallel magnetic state are proposed. In such devices, different spin tunneling barriers are realized by the magnetoelectric coupling effect, resulting in a spin-polarized current. By reversing the electric polarization of the ferroelectric layer, the spin polarization current is efficiently switched for the exchange of spin tunneling barriers. Manipulating spin transport can enhance the functional electronic devices to surpass physical constraints in the speed and power. The multiferroics at the interface of heterostructures offer promising prospects for developing high-performance devices to control the electrical spin information. 2D multiferroic materials primarily focus on the stems from an interaction between antiferromagnetism and the breaking inversion symmetry in certain bilayers. An evidence of spin-electrical couplings, manipulating multiferroic edges via external voltages and subsequently controlling spin transport for fully multiferroic spin field-effect transistors, is exhibited [132]. 2D multiferroic materials garner widespread interest in field of facilitating the integration and miniaturization of nanodevices. However, one 2D material simultaneously possesses the magnetic, ferroelectric, and ferrovalleyic properties is rare to appear. Interlayer sliding manipulating magnetism, ferroelectric, and valley polarization in a 2D GdI2 bilayer material is amazed to discover at a ferromagnetic semiconductor with a valley polarization up to 155.5 meV [83]. More interestingly, the magnetism and valley polarization of bilayer GdI2 can be tunable and reversible. In addition, the magnetic phase transition by a spin Hamiltonian and electron hopping between layers is finally uncovered. These investigations of 2D multiferroics provide a novel direction with implications for next-generation valleytronic, electronic and spintronic devices. 最近的研究重点在于用于自旋电子学应用的功能性多铁性材料。关注了几种反铁磁和铁电BiFeO3的集成架构，用于在具有(La,Sr)MnO3和Au电极的磁隧道结下，作为有源隧道势垒的铁电和铁磁(La,Bi)MnO3。反铁磁和磁电特性也诱导了与铁磁体的交换耦合，这为电控磁化以及可能操控自旋电子器件的逻辑状态铺平了道路。器件中存在四种电阻状态，其中两种状态源于势垒铁磁特性的自旋过滤效应，另外两种状态源于超薄(La,Bi)MnO3薄膜中的铁电行为。铁电极化为新型功能性自旋电子学提供了额外的自由度，要么处理栅控磁存储器，要么为多态存储元件提取额外的序参量。自旋极化电流的产生和操控对于自旋电子器件至关重要。提出了在具有反平行磁态和对称界面终端的多铁性隧道结中，通过电场产生和切换自旋极化电流的机制。在此类器件中，通过磁电耦合效应实现不同的自旋隧穿势垒，从而产生自旋极化电流。通过反转铁电层的电极化，有效切换自旋极化电流，以交换自旋隧穿势垒。操控自旋输运可以增强功能性电子器件，以超越速度和功率方面的物理限制。异质结构界面处的多铁性材料为开发控制电学自旋信息的高性能器件提供了有前景的前景。二维多铁性材料主要关注源于反铁磁性与某些双层中打破反演对称性之间的相互作用。展示了自旋-电耦合的证据，即通过外部电压操纵多铁性边缘，并随后控制全多铁性自旋场效应晶体管的自旋输运。二维多铁性材料在促进纳米器件的集成和小型化方面引起了广泛兴趣。然而，一种二维材料同时具有磁性、铁电性和铁谷性是很罕见的。在二维GdI2双层材料中，层间滑动操控磁性、铁电性和谷极化令人惊讶地被发现，它是一种铁磁半导体，谷极化高达155.5 meV。更有趣的是，双层GdI2的磁性和谷极化是可调和可逆的。此外，最终揭示了通过自旋哈密顿量和层间电子跳跃实现的磁相变。这些对二维多铁性材料的研究为下一代谷电子学、电子学和自旋电子器件提供了一个具有意义的新方向。 **Multiferroic tunnel junctions (MFTJs)**: 多铁性隧道结，使用多铁性材料作为势垒层的磁隧道结，可实现电场对磁性的调控。 **Valleytronic**: 谷电子学，利用二维材料中能谷自由度来编码和处理信息的学科。

> ### 3.3 Sensors & actuators
> 
> ### 3.3 传感器与执行器
> 
> The multifunctional properties of multiferroics enable researchers to construct novel electronic devices for various sensors, transduction applications [37]. Multiferroic magnetoelectric (ME) composites are attractive for various electrically and magnetically cross-coupled devices. Focusing on fundamental understanding, fabrication processes, and applications of ME composite material systems has been always concerned in the last four decades, which stimulates the technology into realization for practical devices, for example, 2D multiferroic with piezoelectric effect as ideal material for actuators and sensors with wearable and low power consumption [133]. Magnetic-field ME material-based sensors are promisingly alternative for conventional Hall sensors and giant magnetoresistive (GMR) devices. Because of their passive nature and self-powered operation at room temperature, ME sensors enable the replacement of bulky and expensive superconducting quantum interference devices (SQUIDs). ME sensors generate tremendous impact on performing biomagnetic measurements parallel to all the performance of MEG and fMRI. The magnetic field sensors are put into practical applications including two key factors: (i) sensitivity of ~pT to fT per Hz at low frequencies (102 to 103 Hz) and (ii) ambient temperature and wide bandwidth (0.1 to 100 Hz) operation. Traditional current sensors, in which the induced electric current by magnetic fields are under detection, typically represent the Hall and reluctance devices [134]. The power of Hall devices needs highly stable constant current, and signal conditioners depend on the inherently weak Hall voltages (5–40 μV/Oe). The interface requirement of reluctance devices is highly precise for integrators, and real-time measurements at low frequencies (100 Hz) are generally inhibited. In contrast, current ME sensors are not subjected to these problems because of the extrinsic ME effect represented by the composites. According to Ampère's Law, a straight wire bringing an AC or a DC current generally stimulates an AC or DC vortex magnetic field around the wire. The strength of the magnetic field relies on the current I from the wire and the distance r in the wire [H = I/(2πr)]. Therefore, ring-type ME laminates catch the essential configure. 多铁性材料的多功能特性使研究人员能够为各种传感器、换能应用构建新型电子器件。多铁性磁电复合材料对各种电和磁交叉耦合器件具有吸引力。在过去的四十年里，对磁电复合材料体系的基本理解、制备工艺和应用的关注一直没有停止，这推动了该技术成为实用器件，例如，具有压电效应的二维多铁性材料作为可穿戴和低功耗执行器与传感器的理想材料。基于磁场磁电材料的传感器有望替代传统的霍尔传感器和巨磁阻器件。由于其无源特性和室温下的自供电操作，磁电传感器能够替代笨重且昂贵的超导量子干涉器件。磁电传感器在进行生物磁测量方面产生了巨大影响，可与脑磁图和功能磁共振成像的所有性能相媲美。磁场传感器进入实际应用包括两个关键因素：(i)在低频（102至103 Hz）下，灵敏度约为每赫兹pT至fT；(ii)环境温度和宽带宽（0.1至100 Hz）操作。传统的电流传感器（检测磁场感应的电流）通常代表霍尔和磁阻器件。霍尔器件的电源需要高度稳定的恒流源，信号调节器依赖于固有的微弱霍尔电压（5–40 μV/Oe）。磁阻器件对积分器的接口要求非常高，低频（100 Hz）下的实时测量通常受到抑制。相比之下，当前的磁电传感器不受这些问题的困扰，因为复合材料代表的外禀磁电效应。根据安培定律，一根承载交流或直流电流的直导线通常会在导线周围激发交流或直流涡旋磁场。磁场强度取决于来自导线的电流I和与导线的距离r [H = I/(2πr)]。因此，环形磁电层压板抓住了基本配置。 **Giant magnetoresistive (GMR)**: 巨磁阻效应，指在磁性多层膜中，电阻随外加磁场发生巨大变化的现象，广泛应用于磁传感器和读取头。

> ### 3.4 Energy harvesters
> 
> ### 3.4 能量采集器
> 
> The function for magnetoelectric energy harvester directly depends on magnetoelectric coupling where a magnetoelectric sensor extracts energy from external magnetic field [135]. First prototype magnetoelectric energy harvester consists of composite plates of PZT/Terfenol-D on an ultrasonic horn substrate [136]. Subsequently, many other similar different configuration devices are performed to improve the output power up to 2.11 mW [136, 137]. A dual-band ME energy harvester is of the particular attention for FeCoSiB/PVDF performing wireless energy harvesting and sensing at a limit detection in ultra-small magnetic field of 300–500 pT at 200 Hz, which provides suitable applications for biomedical fields [138]. Nowadays, energy harvesters are still subjected to narrow operational frequency bandwidth. A wideband energy harvester is turned out to compose of series of composite individual fibers for different lengths, which have their own resonance frequencies and the overlapping frequencies to broaden the operating band [139]. Recently, an effective wide band spiral shaped cantilever arrangement multimodal harvester can acquire energy from five frequency bands in the frequency range of 15–70 Hz [140]. What's more, nonlinear energy harvesters are extracted by interaction between movable magnets and fixed magnets in wide band energy [141]. 磁电能量采集器的功能直接依赖于磁电耦合，即磁电传感器从外部磁场中提取能量。第一个磁电能量采集器原型由PZT/Terfenol-D复合板在超声变幅杆基底上组成。随后，许多其他类似的不同配置器件被提出，以将输出功率提高到2.11 mW。双频带磁电能量采集器特别受到关注，FeCoSiB/PVDF在200 Hz下以300–500 pT的超小磁场极限检测进行无线能量采集和传感，为生物医学领域提供了合适的应用。如今，能量采集器仍然受到窄工作频率带宽的限制。宽带能量采集器被证明由一系列不同长度的复合单纤维组成，它们具有各自的共振频率，并通过重叠频率来拓宽工作频带。最近，一种有效的宽带螺旋形悬臂布置多模态采集器可以在15–70 Hz的频率范围内从五个频带获取能量。此外，通过可移动磁体和固定磁体之间的相互作用，在宽带能量中提取了非线性能量采集器。

> ### 3.5 Microwave and radio frequency (RF) devices
> 
> ### 3.5 微波与射频器件
> 
> Magnetoelectric (ME) coupling in multiferroics also opens up an avenue for constructing electrically controllable high frequency deices via switching ferromagnetic resonance frequency (FMR) to modulate ferroelectric polarization of multiferroic [142]. Giant voltage controlled FMR in strain-mediated magnetic/piezoelectric heterostructures provides a strategy for applications in microwave devices, voltage tunable RF, phase shifters, and mini antennas [143]. Electrically tunable strain states in a piezoelectric allow the strain-induced change in magnetic permeability of FM via ME coupling and electrically switchable FMR at very low voltage, which is easy to integrate on a chip for miniaturization and works at very low power consumption. The main drawback exists high noise due dielectric loss at the interface in magnetostrictive alloys. High-quality interface and low noise materials produce low magnetostriction and strain-mediated coupling, so the challenge remains to find low loss magnetic ferrites that can be grown epitaxially with high strain-mediated coupling with piezoelectric material. Among ferrite materials, yttrium iron garnet (YIG) possesses advantage of low absorption, narrow FMR line width and high Q factor at microwave frequencies. YIG/piezoelectric heterostructures have been frequency utilized for synthesizing microwave phase shifter, filter and antenna [114, 144]. ME coupling-induced large FMR shift is of particular attention at ambient temperature in YIG/BTO heterostructures as well as an efficient FMR tuning at low loss and small level voltage in YIG: BTO self-assembled heterostructure and YIG/PMN-Pt layered thin films [144, 145]. The multiferroic heterojunction with both ferroelectric and ferromagnetic properties can achieve voltage-controlled magnetic properties through the strain-induced magnetoelectric coupling effect. The microwave and RF device prepared by this mechanism can meet the requirements of miniaturization, ultra-fast response, and low power consumption, and can generate new functionality. > ### 3.6 移相器

> Multiferroic materials can be applied for phase shifters arising from the magnetoelectric coupling. Microwave phase shifters are important elements for telecommunications, oscillators, radar applications and phased array antenna systems. Ferroelectric materials conduct a second category of microwave phase shifters. The big different features are low power consumption and fast electric tunability. A ferrite-ferroelectric layered structure, consisting of a ME composite, provides a route for the application of dual-tunable microwave devices, which offers lower noise, higher efficiency, lightweight and compact size respect to conventional microwave deceives. Via exchange coupling or magnon excitation, the converse magnetoelectric coupling turns out to be a long spatial scalable effectiveness up to tens of nanometers near the interface. The other more promising solution is to take advantage of the high-density arrays of the layered magnetic-ferroelectric nanoislands. > ## 4 Conclusion and prospects
> 
> ## 4 结论与展望
> 
> 2D multiferroic materials have widespread application prospects in facilitating the integration and miniaturization of nanodevices, such as spintronics, nonvanatile memory, sensors, actuators, phase shifters, ratio frequency, energy harvesters. However, the magnetic, ferroelectric, and ferrovalley properties in one 2D material are rarely coupled. In this paper, the fundamental mechanism, category, growth method, advanced characterization method, application, prospects and challenges are overviewed. Multiferroic materials are usually divided into two categories, type I and type II multiferroics. Particularly, type-II multiferroics are rare, representing an inversion-symmetry-breaking magnetic order that immediately introduces ferroelectric polarization via a variety of originated mechanisms, such as the inverse Dzyaloshinskii–Moriya effects or the spin-current. 2D materials equipped with such intrinsic magnetoelectric coupling provide various routes to be long sought for applications in nanoelectronic devices. 二维多铁性材料在促进纳米器件（如自旋电子学、非易失性存储器、传感器、执行器、移相器、射频、能量采集器）的集成和小型化方面具有广泛的应用前景。然而，一种二维材料中的磁性、铁电性和铁谷性很少耦合。本文综述了二维多铁性材料的基本机制、分类、生长方法、先进表征方法、应用、前景和挑战。多铁性材料通常分为两类，第I类和第II类多铁性材料。特别是，第II类多铁性材料较为罕见，代表一种打破反演对称性的磁序，通过多种起源机制（如逆Dzyaloshinskii–Moriya效应或自旋流）立即引入铁电极化。配备这种本征磁电耦合的二维材料为纳米电子器件中长期寻求的应用提供了多种途径。 **Dzyaloshinskii–Moriya effects**: Dzyaloshinskii–Moriya效应，一种反对称交换相互作用，源于自旋轨道耦合，在非中心对称体系中可导致自旋倾斜或螺旋磁结构。
> 
> Bracingly, the discovery of type-II multiferroic order NiI2 with a single atomic layer, which couples to the charge degree freedoms to induce a chirality-controlled electrical polarization [13] promotes great development for experimentally synthesizing 2D multiferroic materials down to single layer limit. First, the growth methods of 2D multiferroic materials are viewed, such as mechanical exfoliation, liquid exfoliation, and vapour deposition. Vapour deposition is generally classified into chemical vapour deposition, chemical vapour transport, physical vapour deposition and molecular beam epitaxy. Among these methods, it is a typical technique of VPD to construct atomic-scale films or thin flakes through directly depositing suitable vapour-phase compounds on specific substrates, which can prepare well tunable, large-area uniform and high-quality 2D multiferroic materials. Normally, two main kinds of vapour phase deposition methods are CVD and PVD. CVD is a kind of technique that involves plasma excitation, heating and chemically reacting reactants under certain temperature and gaseous conditions, then the resulting substances are deposited on the substrate surface at an applicable position to fabricate a solid thin film. CVD method can prepare controllable-thickness, large-size, and high-quality 2D materials for further positive application in the electronic industry. PVD is a thin film preparation technique, where materials are deposited on substrates via evaporating, emitting and ion-beam evaporating in a high vacuum environment. It is an efficient method to prepare thin-film materials in industry and commerce, which is the reason that the PVD method can immediately construct 2D materials on the appropriate substrate without another additional chemical reactions. Moreover, MBE growth method and MS method in the PVD method are always applied to prepare 2D multilayer materials. Above all, the CVD and CVT methods are usually sued to prepare 2D multiferroic materials due to their strict requirement on large size, high quality, controllable thickness. 令人振奋的是，具有单原子层的第II类多铁性序NiI2的发现，该序与电荷自由度耦合以诱导手性控制的电极化，极大地推动了实验合成二维多铁性材料至单层极限的发展。首先，综述了二维多铁性材料的生长方法，如机械剥离、液相剥离和气相沉积。气相沉积通常分为化学气相沉积、化学气相传输、物理气相沉积和分子束外延。在这些方法中，典型的气相沉积技术通过直接在特定基底上沉积合适的气相化合物来构建原子级薄膜或薄片，可以制备出可调性好、大面积均匀且高质量的二维多铁性材料。通常，两种主要的气相沉积方法是CVD和PVD。CVD是一种在特定温度和气态条件下，涉及等离子体激发、加热和化学反应物，然后将所得物质沉积在基底表面适当位置以制备固体薄膜的技术。CVD方法可以制备厚度可控、尺寸大、质量高的二维材料，以便在电子工业中进一步应用。PVD是一种薄膜制备技术，材料在高真空环境中通过蒸发、发射和离子束蒸发沉积在基底上。它是工业界和商业中制备薄膜材料的一种高效方法，这就是PVD方法无需额外化学反应即可在适当基底上直接构建二维材料的原因。此外，PVD方法中的MBE生长方法和磁控溅射方法也常用于制备二维多层材料。综上所述，由于对尺寸大、质量高、厚度可控的严格要求，CVD和CVT方法通常用于制备二维多铁性材料。
> 
> Second, the characterization tools of 2D multiferroic materials have been outlined, such as Raman spectrum, optical second harmonic generator (SHG), XRD, TEM, STM, reflection birefringence (RB), and scanning probe microscope (SPM). Circular dichroic Raman measurements are used to directly probe the magneto-chiral ground state and its electromagnon modes originate from dynamic magnetoelectric coupling. Combining birefringence and second-harmonic-generation measurements to detect a highly anisotropic electronic state that simultaneously breaks three-fold rotational and inversion symmetry supports the results of polar order. The evolution of the optical signatures as a function of temperature and layer number surprisingly reveals an ordered magnetic polar state that persists down to the ultrathin limit of monolayer NiI2. These observations establish NiI2 and transition metal dihalides as a new platform for studying emergent multiferroic phenomena, chiral magnetic textures and ferroelectricity in the 2D limit. 其次，概述了二维多铁性材料的表征工具，如拉曼光谱、光学二次谐波产生器、XRD、TEM、STM、反射双折射和扫描探针显微镜。圆偏振拉曼测量用于直接探测磁手性基态，其电磁子模式源于动态磁电耦合。结合双折射和二次谐波产生测量来检测同时打破三重旋转对称性和反演对称性的高度各向异性电子态，支持了极性序的结果。光学特征随温度和层数的演变令人惊讶地揭示了一个有序的磁极性态，该态持续到单层NiI2的超薄极限。这些观察结果确立了NiI2和过渡金属二卤化物作为研究二维极限下涌现多铁性现象、手性磁织构和铁电性的新平台。
> 
> Third, the magnetoelectric counpling for 2D multiferroic materials provide extreme platforms for the application in nonvanatile low power memory, spintronics, sensors, actuators, phase shifters, microwave, ratio frequency, energy harvesters. Even the development of multifunctional 2D materials has been a long-standing research objective, it is still very challenging to observe stable multiferroicity at room temperature in the existing 2D materials. 第三，二维多铁性材料的磁电耦合为非易失性低功耗存储器、自旋电子学、传感器、执行器、移相器、微波、射频、能量采集器中的应用提供了极佳平台。尽管多功能二维材料的开发一直是一个长期的研究目标，但在现有二维材料中观察到室温下稳定的多铁性仍然非常具有挑战性。
> 
> Fourth, challenges and prospects of 2D multiferroic materials have been performed. Multiferroic materials provide a promising route to operate digital information through making use of the cross-coupling between ferromagnetic and ferroelectric orders. Even though the ferroelectricity has been appeared via interlayer-sliding or ion displacement, one-unit-cell design, and wafer-scale synthesis of multiferroic materials have yet to be achieved. Thus, the interface modulated approach to prepare one-unit-cell of one-inch non-layered multiferroic material Cr2S3 with single orientation on the substrate of industry-compatible c-plane sapphire is employed [40]. The interfacial interaction between Cr2S3 and substrate introduces the intralayer-sliding of self-intercalated Cr atoms and thus destroys the space reversal symmetry. Consequently, one-unit-cell Cr2S3 exhibits robust room-temperature ferroelectricity with ultrahigh remanent polarization. Besides, one-unit-cell Cr2S3 represents long-range ferromagnetic order with the Curie temperature of 200 K, which is nearly two times higher than bulk counterpart. Simultaneously, Cr2S3 has the magnetoelectric coupling to be certified for the largest and thinnest multiferroics [40]. 第四，阐述了二维多铁性材料的挑战和前景。多铁性材料通过利用铁磁序和铁电序之间的交叉耦合，为操作数字信息提供了一条有前景的途径。尽管通过层间滑动或离子位移已经出现了铁电性，但一单胞设计和晶圆级合成多铁性材料尚未实现。因此，采用了界面调制方法，在工业兼容的c面蓝宝石基底上，以单一取向制备一英寸的一单胞非层状多铁性材料Cr2S3。Cr2S3与基底之间的界面相互作用引入了自插层Cr原子的层内滑动，从而破坏了空间反演对称性。因此，一单胞Cr2S3表现出稳健的室温铁电性，具有超高剩余极化。此外，一单胞Cr2S3表现出长程铁磁序，居里温度为200 K，几乎是块体对应物的两倍。同时，Cr2S3具有磁电耦合，被认证为最大和最薄的多铁性材料。
> 
> In general, the researches on 2D multiferroics, including ferroelectric–ferromagnetic, ferromagnetic–ferroelastic, and ferroelectric–ferroelastic, triferroics are still in the infancy, and most of the reported studies are only based on theoretical simulations. In last few years, triferroics synchronously exhibiting ferromagnetic, ferroelectric, and ferrovalley features have been sequentially discovered, which opens greatly novel avenue to capturing the interaction of spin, electronic charge, and valley degrees of freedom. The curious appearance of new physical phenomena and multi-signal response provides great potential application in next-generation multi-information storage devices. The proposed valley polarization spontaneously existed in monolayer orthorhombic group-IV monochalcogenides, modulating valley states by means of electric field and the usual ferromagnetism [148]. Under non-degenerate light excitation, single-layer GeSe experiences an electrically switching polarization of selective x- or y-polarized light under electric field. Particularly, a rare 2D dual-metal trihalide WRuCl6 monolayer triferroic, ferromagnetism and ferroelasticity are strongly coupled to the ferroelectricity of 120° rotation symmetry from the perspectives of first-principle calculations [149], which enable the flexible and reversible switching of ferroic orders via electric field. In addition, WRuCl6 monolayer is an intrinsic bipolar magnetic semiconductor; thus, the switchable spin-polarized carrier can be obtained by applying an electric gate voltage to further improve the storage density. Later on, exfoliation from the layered bulk, single-layer FeO2H identified to harbor in-plane piezoelectric effect exists an intrinsically triferroic semiconductor, simultaneously presenting antiferromagnetism, ferroelasticity, and ferroelectricity, in which the directional-control ferroelectric polarization by 90° reversible ferroelastic switching [150]. Recently, the simultaneous ferroelectricity–ferromagnetism-ferrovalley coexistence in a Nb3X8 (X = Cl, Br, I) monolayer is discovered with the breathing kagome lattices [151]. The out-of-plane ferroelectricity polarization as well as the intralayer ferromagnetism is ascribed to the breathing process of Nb-trimer. Besides, ferrovalley polarization can also be switched by the robust ferroelectricity-valley coupling or spin–valley coupling. The breathing process of kagome lattice trimer patterns corresponds to the out-of-plane ferroelectric polarization directions. The magnetism of the Nb-trimer clusters can be generated and annihilated simultaneously at neighboring magnetic sites by reversing the direction of ferroelectric polarization due to magnetoelectric coupling. In addition, a single-phase triferroic in the hole doped GdCl2 monolayer where three ferroic orders-ferromagnetism, ferroelectricity, and ferroelasticity coexist, simultaneously represents a ferromagnetic semiconductor achieved by substituting 1/3rd of the Gd2+ ions with Eu2+ in the hexagonal structure of the GdCl2 monolayer [152]. The metallic state undergoes a bond-centered charge ordering driving a distortion in the hexagonal structure, making it semiconducting again and ferroelastic. Furthermore, the lattice distortion accompanied by breaking the lattice centrosymmetry renders a noncentrosymmetric charge distribution, which makes the monolayer ferroelectric, at the same time. The two ferroic orders, ferroelectricity and ferroelasticity, present in the Eu-substituted GdCl2 monolayer are found to be strongly coupled, making it a promising candidate for device applications. The Eu-substituted monolayer remains a ferromagnetic semiconductor with a large 4 magnetic moment just like the parent monolayer and possesses an even higher (out-of-plane) magnetic anisotropy energy than its pristine counterpart as desired for two-dimensional magnets to have high transition temperature. Moreover, a general mechanism for realizing intrinsic ferromagnetic triferroicity in two-dimensional van der Waals lattices through interlayer sliding in bilayer T′ VTe2, spontaneously exhibiting ferromagnetism, ferroelasticity, and ferroelectricity, shedding the long-sought light for intrinsic triferroicity [153]. Such a system could possess many distinctive physics, for example, the ferroelastic control of magnetization orientation and ferroelectric control of magnetic moment distribution. Additionally, ferroelectric–ferromagnetic-valley coupling has been achieved in monolayer 2H-VSe2/BiFeO3(111) heterostructure, where BiFeO3 is a multiferroic material with ferroelectric polarization and G-type antiferromagnetic orders [154]. 总的来说，二维多铁性材料（包括铁电-铁磁、铁磁-铁弹和铁电-铁弹）以及三铁性材料的研究仍处于起步阶段，大多数报道的研究仅基于理论模拟。在过去几年中，同时表现出铁磁、铁电和铁谷特征的三铁性材料已被陆续发现，这为捕捉自旋、电子电荷和谷自由度之间的相互作用开辟了非常新颖的途径。新物理现象和多信号响应的奇特出现为下一代多信息存储器件提供了巨大的潜在应用。提出的谷极化自发存在于单层正交晶系IV族单硫族化物中，通过电场和通常的铁磁性来调制谷态。在非简并光激发下，单层GeSe在电场作用下经历选择性x或y偏振光的电切换极化。特别地，一种罕见的二维双金属三卤化物WRuCl6单层三铁性材料，从第一性原理计算的角度来看，铁磁性和铁弹性与120°旋转对称的铁电性强耦合，这使得能够通过电场灵活和可逆地切换铁性序。此外，WRuCl6单层是一种本征双极磁性半导体；因此，可以通过施加电栅压获得可切换的自旋极化载流子，以进一步提高存储密度。随后，从层状块体剥离的单层FeO2H被鉴定为具有面内压电效应的本征三铁性半导体，同时表现出反铁磁性、铁弹性和铁电性，其中通过90°可逆铁弹切换实现方向可控的铁电极化。最近，在具有呼吸kagome晶格的Nb3X8 (X = Cl, Br, I)单层中发现了同时存在铁电性-铁磁性-铁谷性的共存。面外铁电极化以及层内铁磁性归因于Nb三聚体的呼吸过程。此外，铁谷极化也可以通过稳健的铁电性-谷耦合或自旋-谷耦合进行切换。kagome晶格三聚体图案的呼吸过程对应于面外铁电极化方向。由于磁电耦合，通过反转铁电极化方向，可以在相邻磁性位点同时产生和湮灭Nb三聚体团簇的磁性。此外，在空穴掺杂的GdCl2单层中，单相三铁性材料（其中铁磁、铁电和铁弹三种铁性序共存）同时代表了一种铁磁半导体，通过在GdCl2单层的六方结构中用Eu2+取代1/3的Gd2+离子实现。金属态经历键中心电荷有序，驱动六方结构中的畸变，使其再次成为半导体并具有铁弹性。此外，伴随打破晶格中心对称性的晶格畸变导致非中心对称的电荷分布，这同时使单层具有铁电性。在Eu取代的GdCl2单层中存在的两种铁性序——铁电性和铁弹性——被发现是强耦合的，使其成为器件应用的有前景的候选者。Eu取代的单层仍然是一种铁磁半导体，具有与母体单层一样大的4磁矩，并且具有比其原始对应物更高的（面外）磁各向异性，这是二维磁体具有高转变温度所期望的。此外，通过在双层T′ VTe2中的层间滑动，实现了二维范德华晶格中本征铁磁三铁性的一般机制，自发地表现出铁磁性、铁弹性和铁电性，为本征三铁性揭示了长期寻求的曙光。这样的系统可能拥有许多独特的物理性质，例如，铁弹控制磁化取向和铁电控制磁矩分布。另外，在单层2H-VSe2/BiFeO3(111)异质结构中实现了铁电-铁磁-谷耦合，其中BiFeO3是一种具有铁电极化和G型反铁磁序的多铁性材料。

> Although multiferroics are not new to us, there are still numerous issues that must be addressed. Till now, only a small amount of 2D multiferroic materials, such as NiI2, Cr2S3, CuCrP2S6, and CuCrSe2, have been successfully prepared in experiment with vapour phase deposition method. There plenty of efforts need make for preparing stable, high-quality, large-scale, uniform 2D multiferroic materials to provide various routes in the applications of nonvanatile memory, sensors, actuators, ratio frequency, energy harvesters, spintronics. A novel approach to fabricate 2D non-layered multiferroic materials need be promoted using vdW epitaxy through CVD. New room-temperature multiferroics with strong magnetoelectric coupling need to be discovered. The voltage required for ferroelectric/magnetoelectric switching should be less than 100 mV which can make good impact on those materials. A multiferroic device controlled by an electric field, especially at low voltages, at room temperature, and with rapid switching, remains a primary target. New more efficient mechanisms need to be developed, as the world is progressing at a faster rate. There has been no link to quantum physics as far as multiferroics are concerned, so if this link is established, it would be of great help to the entire community. Dynamical phenomena are still underappreciated issues in multiferroic studies. 尽管多铁性材料对我们来说并不陌生，但仍有许多问题必须解决。到目前为止，只有少量的二维多铁性材料，如NiI2、Cr2S3、CuCrP2S6和CuCrSe2，已通过气相沉积方法在实验中成功制备。需要付出大量努力来制备稳定、高质量、大规模、均匀的二维多铁性材料，以为非易失性存储器、传感器、执行器、射频、能量采集器、自旋电子学等应用提供多种途径。需要推广一种通过CVD进行范德华外延制备二维非层状多铁性材料的新方法。需要发现具有强磁电耦合的新型室温多铁性材料。铁电/磁电切换所需的电压应小于100 mV，这将对那些材料产生良好影响。一种由电场控制的、特别是在低电压下、室温下和快速切换的多铁性器件仍然是首要目标。随着世界以更快的速度发展，需要开发新的更有效的机制。就多铁性材料而言，目前与量子物理学还没有联系，因此如果建立了这种联系，将对整个领域大有帮助。动力学现象在多铁性研究中仍然是被低估的问题。

> -   **透射电子显微镜（TEM）**：这个更厉害，像个“超级透视眼”。用电子束打透材料，能直接看到材料内部的原子是怎么排队的，连原子级别的缺陷都能看到。
