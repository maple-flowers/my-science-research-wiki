> ## 一、引言
> 
> 在凝聚态物理领域，材料中不同有序态（如超导电性、磁性、电荷有序）之间的相互作用与竞争是产生新奇量子现象的核心驱动力。本篇论文聚焦于层状金属化合物——**过渡金属二硫族化物（Transition Metal Dichalcogenides, TMDs）**中，**超导电性（Superconductivity, SC）** 与**电荷密度波（Charge Density Wave, CDW）** 这两种宏观量子态的竞争关系。在TMDs中，超导态通常被电荷密度波态所抑制，这限制了其超导性能的提升。
> 
> 本文的核心价值在于，它针对一个看似悖论的实验现象提供了理论解释：**近期实验发现，在非磁性无序（如合金化）的影响下，超导电性会出人意料地重新出现并增强**。作者通过两种互补的理论方法——实空间的**Bogoliubov-de Gennes（BdG）自洽计算**和动量空间的**密度泛函理论结合动力学平均场理论（DFT+DMFT）计算**——揭示了无序如何削弱电荷密度波，从而为超导电性“让路”的微观机制。这篇文章为理解TMDs中复杂的相图提供了关键的理论支撑，并为通过无序工程调控超导性质开辟了新的思路。
> 
> ## 二、论文结构与逻辑概览
> 
> 本文遵循“提出问题 → 建立模型与方法 → 展示结果 → 总结讨论”的经典研究范式，其论证链条清晰，逻辑结构如下：
> 
> 1.  **问题提出**：TMDs中，SC通常被CDW抑制。但在TaSe₂₋ₓSₓ合金中，当CDW被无序压制时，SC不仅没有消失，反而以更高的超导转变温度（TSC）出现。
> 2.  **建立模型（两种方法论）**：
>     -   **实空间方法**：构建一个包含SC和CDW序参量的二维方格BdG模型，并引入两种类型的非磁性无序（随机无序和团簇无序），观察序参量随无序强度的演化。
>     -   **动量空间方法**：针对具体的TaSe₂₋ₓSₓ合金体系，采用DFT+DMFT方法，从强关联电子角度研究等电子替换（S取代Se）所引入的化学无序对能带结构、电阻率和超导序参量的影响。
> 3.  **结果论证**：
>     -   **BdG结果显示**：随着无序强度增加，CDW的序参量单调下降，而SC的序参量则从零开始上升，出现明显共存与增强。团簇无序比随机无序更能有效地促进SC。
>     -   **DFT+DMFT结果显示**：在x=0（纯TaSe₂）时，电阻率在120 K附近出现CDW转变的特征；在x=1（TaSeS）时，CDW特征完全消失，电阻率在高温下呈现线性行为，并在~5 K附近急剧下降，表明SC转变发生。直接计算超导序参量，其随温度变化符合BCS理论，证实了超导转变。
> 4.  **结论与机制**：无序引入了一个新的长度尺度，通过局域化电荷来破坏CDW的相干性，尤其是压制了“预成型激子”的凝聚。而s波超导对非磁性无序有很强的鲁棒性（安德森定理），因此当竞争者CDW被削弱后，SC便自然显现并占据主导地位。这解释了实验中观察到的SC在无序作用下“重入”的现象。
>

> ## 四、正文核心内容剖析
> 
> ### 1. 研究背景与核心问题
> 
> TMDs是典型的准二维材料，其相图中普遍存在CDW和SC。传统弱耦合理论认为两者都是费米面不稳定性（如费米面嵌套）的产物，竞争同一批电子，因此CDW的存在通常会压制SC。然而，实验上越来越多证据（如远超BCS理论预言的2∆/kBTSC比值、缺乏明显的声子信号、动量无关的电子自能）表明，TMDs属于强关联电子系统，其物理应由强耦合模型主导。核心问题是：**在强耦合图像下，近期实验发现的由非磁性无序/掺杂诱导的超导重入现象，其微观物理机制是什么？**
> 
> ### 2. 研究方法与模型
> 
> 本文采用两种互补的理论方法来研究SC与CDW的竞争：
> 
> -   **实空间BdG方法**：这是一个在平均场层面上处理非均匀体系的有效方法。它在二维方格子上的哈密顿量中同时包含了：
>     -   **s波超导**：由配对作用USC驱动。
>     -   **电荷密度波**：由相互作用UCDW驱动，通过一个周期性的化学势调制来模拟。
>     -   **非磁性无序**：通过一个随机的、位点依赖的化学势项Vi来模拟，其变化范围为[-Vd, Vd]。 通过自洽求解BdG方程，可以同时得到每个格点上的超导序参量∆iSC和CDW序参量∆iCDW。该方法物理图像清晰，能直观展示实空间上的序参量竞争。
> -   **动量空间DFT+DMFT方法**：这是一种处理强关联电子系统的第一性原理计算方法。它用DFT处理材料的能带结构，然后将复杂的多体相互作用问题映射到一个安德森杂质模型上，并用多轨道迭代微扰理论（MO-IPT）作为杂质求解器。该方法能够处理具体的材料（TaSe₂和TaSeS），并直接计算能带结构、电阻率和超导序参量等实的物理量，在强关联理论框架下解释了无序的作用。
> 
> ### 3. 实验结果与讨论
> 
> -   **BdG模拟的核心发现**：
>     1.  **无序是SC的“开关”**：在无无序时，CDW占主导，SC被完全压制。随着无序增强，CDW的长程有序被破坏，形成了若干SC“岛屿”，实现了宏观SC序参量的增长。
>     2.  **无序类型很重要**：与随机无序相比，**团簇状的无序**能更有效地促进SC。这是因为团簇无序能创造出更大的、空间上更连续的SC区域，有利于建立宏观的相位相干。
>     3.  **CDW波长与SC相干长度的竞争**：短波长的CDW（如棋盘状）对SC的抑制最强，因为它与SC的相干长度尺度不匹配。无序通过局域化电荷，有效地改变了CDW的有效波长，从而为SC的恢复创造了条件。
> -   **DFT+DMFT模拟的核心发现**：
>     1.  **实验现象复现**：成功复现了Li等人的实验结果。在x=0（纯TaSe₂）时，观察到CDW转变的电阻率特征；在x=1（TaSeS）时，CDW特征消失，并在~5 K出现超导转变。
>     2.  **S掺杂是随机无序**：DFT计算表明，S掺杂对能带结构和费米面的影响很小，因此其作用可被等效为一种**化学掺杂引起的无序势**。

> ## 一、引言
> 
> 在凝聚态物理中，不同有序态之间的竞争往往催生出新奇量子现象。**过渡金属二硫属化物**（Transition Metal Dichalcogenides, TMDs）为此提供了理想平台：这类层状金属化合物中，**电荷密度波**（Charge Density Wave, CDW）与**超导电性**（Superconductivity, SC）常常共存且相互拮抗。传统弱耦合理论认为两者均源于费米面不稳定性，它们会争夺同一套电子，因而CDW通常会压制超导。然而，近期实验发现，当通过化学掺杂、插层或施加压力等手段在体系中引入**非磁性无序**时，超导转变温度非但未受损害，反而显著回升，甚至超过纯相材料。这一反常现象揭示出传统费米面嵌套图像的局限，亟需从强耦合视角重新审视CDW与SC的竞争机制。
> 
> 本文献《Charge Density Wave and Superconductivity in Transition Metal Dichalcogenides》正是针对这一核心谜题展开。作者团队结合**实空间自洽Bogoliubov-de Gennes（BdG）方法**与**动量空间密度泛函理论+动力学平均场理论（DFT+DMFT）**，系统研究了非磁性无序涨落对CDW和SC的影响。研究明确指出，当无序以团簇形式存在时，能够有效破坏CDW序，从而使被压制的超导电性重新出现，这一发现与近期合金化实验（如TaSe₂₋ₓSₓ）高度吻合。该工作的核心价值在于：它不仅为理解TMDs中电荷序与超导序的竞争提供了统一的理论框架，更揭示了“无序诱导超导”这一反直觉现象背后的物理图像——即利用强耦合激子凝聚的观点，将CDW视为预形成激子的玻色凝聚，而无序通过破坏激子相干性来释放超导自由度。

> ## 四、正文核心内容剖析
> 
> ### 4.1 研究背景：弱耦合图像的困境与强耦合新范式
> 
> 传统上，CDW被理解为费米面嵌套引发的Peierls不稳定性，即电子-声子耦合导致晶格周期性畸变并伴随电荷密度调制。然而，在TMDs中，大量实验却与这一图像矛盾：
>

> # Charge Density Wave and Superconductivity in Transition Metal Dichalcogenides
> 
> 过渡金属二硫化物中的电荷密度波与超导电性 **Charge Density Wave (CDW)**: 电荷密度波，一种电子密度的周期性调制现象。 **Superconductivity (SC)**: 超导电性，材料在低温下电阻为零并排斥磁场的性质。 **Transition Metal Dichalcogenides (TMDs)**: 过渡金属二硫化物，一类层状化合物，化学式通常为MX₂，其中M为过渡金属，X为硫族元素。
>

>

> Competing orders in condensed matter give rise to the emergence of fascinating, new phenomena. Here, we investigate the competition between superconductivity and charge density wave in the context of layered-metallic compounds, transition metal dichalcogenides, in which the superconducting state is usually suppressed by the charge density wave. We show, using real-space self-consistent Bogoliubov-de Gennes calculations and momentum-space calculations involving density-functional theory and dynamical mean-field theory, that there is a surprising reappearance of superconductivity in the presence of non-magnetic disorder fluctuations, as observed in recent experiments. 凝聚态物质中相互竞争的序导致迷人新现象的出现。本文在层状金属化合物——过渡金属二硫化物的背景下，研究了超导电性与电荷密度波之间的竞争，其中超导态通常被电荷密度波所抑制。我们利用实空间自洽 Bogoliubov-de Gennes 计算和涉及密度泛函理论与动力学平均场理论的动量空间计算，表明在非磁性无序涨落存在下，超导电性会惊人地重新出现，正如近期实验所观察到的那样。 **Bogoliubov-de Gennes (BdG)**: 一种用于描述超导电性实空间自洽计算的微观理论方法。 **Density-Functional Theory (DFT)**: 密度泛函理论，一种研究多电子体系电子结构的量子力学方法。 **Dynamical Mean-Field Theory (DMFT)**: 动力学平均场理论，一种处理强关联电子系统的非微扰方法。

> # I. INTRODUCTION
> 
> 引言
> 
> The issue of disorder and Coulomb interaction, competing with each other, is an old one. In the absence of interaction, disorder is known to localize electronic states. The problem appears in systems where superconductivity (SC) competes with long-range orders like charge density wave (CDW)1–3 and spin density wave (SDW)4. There is a large body of literature on the competition between SC and SDW in presence of disorder5, while much less is discussed on SC and CDW competing with each other. 无序与库仑相互作用相互竞争的问题由来已久。在没有相互作用的情况下，无序被认为会使电子局域化。该问题出现在超导电性（SC）与电荷密度波（CDW）1–3及自旋密度波（SDW）4等长程有序相竞争的体系中。在存在无序时，SC与SDW竞争已有大量文献5，而关于SC与CDW相互竞争的讨论则少得多。 **Spin Density Wave (SDW)**: 自旋密度波，一种自旋密度的周期性调制，常伴有电荷密度调制。
> 
> In the weak coupling case, these orders compete for the same Fermi surface and when disorder suppresses one, the other grows at its expense. Note that for s-wave SC, non-magnetic disorder (unless very strong) has little effect, while it pushes the CDW instability down. From a traditional view point, superconductivity and CDW are considered as weak coupling Fermi surface instabilities mediated by phonons7. There have been reports of both cooperation6 and competition1,8 between the two, though on the face of it, they compete for the same electrons. The original idea of a CDW due to Fermi-surface nesting (Peierls instability) has rarely been borne out in real systems where nesting is scarce. Moreover, even if it exists, nesting appears at the wrong wave vectors and away from the Fermi surface quite often8,9. Therefore, such single-particle mean-field pictures, invoking large segments of the Fermi surface nested with each other, need to be abandoned in favor of an alternative, strong coupling view. The strong coupling approach is further supported by the finding of 2∆/kBTSC > 5 (where ∆ is the superconducting pairing gap at zero temperature and TSC is the superconducting transition temperature) in many transition metal dichalcogenides (TMDs)10 (about 7 for 1T-TiSe2 and 10 for 2H-TaSe2). 在弱耦合情况下，这些序竞争同一费米面，当无序抑制其中一种时，另一种会以牺牲前者为代价而增长。注意，对于s波超导，非磁性无序（除非非常强）影响很小，但它会压低CDW不稳定性。从传统观点看，超导电性和CDW被视为由声子介导的弱耦合费米面不稳定性7。已有报道称两者之间存在合作6和竞争1,8，尽管表面上它们竞争的是相同电子。基于费米面嵌套（Peierls不稳定性）的CDW原始想法在嵌套很少的实际系统中极少得到证实。而且，即使存在，嵌套也常常出现在错误的波矢处并远离费米面8,9。因此，这种调用大片费米面相互嵌套的单粒子平均场图像需要被抛弃，转而采用强耦合观点。强耦合方法进一步得到许多过渡金属二硫化物（TMDs）中2∆/kBTSC > 5这一发现的支持（其中∆是零温超导配对能隙，TSC是超导转变温度）10（1T-TiSe2约为7，2H-TaSe2约为10）。
> 
> The TMDs have typically shown bad metallic resistivity above TCDW, the CDW ordering temperature, becoming quadratic and more metallic below it. There is often little or no anomaly across the transition. The linear resistivity at high temperature is persistent till fairly high temperatures11,12. In spite of such overwhelming indications to the contrary, and the near-absence of required Fermi-surface nesting, much of the theoretical work on TMDs took recourse to mean-field considerations. Mottness has not been considered important for TMDs, except for 1T-TaS213 long back and for 2H-TaSe212,14 and 1T-TiSe216 lately. Large spectral weight transfer and absence of phonon signatures at typical energies in ARPES data, along with momentum-independent self-energy17 are strong pointers to physics beyond (mean-field) single-particle processes. Superconductivity in most of the TMDs also possesses interesting questions. It usually appears when the CDW is suppressed by disorder, intercalation or pressure18. One such approach has recently been taken by Li, et al., 19 where, in 2H-TaSe2, Se is replaced by sulphur (S) gradually, all the way to 2H-TaS2. They report an emergence of robust superconducting order in single crystal TaSe2−xSx (0 < x < 2) alloy. The SC transition temperature (TSC) of the alloy is more than that of the two end-members TaSe2 and TaS2. The conductivity near the middle of the alloy series is also found to be higher than that of either 2H-TaSe2 and 2H-TaS2. This observation clearly suggests that superconductivity in this system is in competition with CDW. In order to understand this competition, we take up the problem of co-existing CDW and SC and add nonmagnetic disorder to it. Two kinds of disorder are studied: random disorder (uniform probability in real space with fixed strength) and clusters of disordered regions of fixed strengths. Using a self-consistent Bogoliubov-de-Gennes (BdG) formulation, we observe the evolution of SC and charge order using a Monte Carlo method20. Such work has been used in the context of disordered superconductivity during the heyday of high Tc superconductivity21,22. In addition, we also approach the alloy problem from a strong-coupling point of view at specific dopings. A density functional theory, followed by multi-orbital dynamical mean field theory (DFT+MO-DMFT) calculations for TaSe2−xSx (at x=0 and 1) are performed to understand the nature of the competition between CDW and SC. The disorder-induced competition is introduced via doping of sulphur which affects the band structure slightly, while acting as a substitutional disorder. The strong coupling approach views the CDW as a condensate out of a pre-formed excitonic liquid14,16 of bad metals. The unconventional CDW found in the parent dichalcogenide is a Bose-condensate of pre-formed excitons from high temperature. As there is no magnetism found in TMD, the alloy is a bad metal without magnetic fluctuations. Therefore direct comparisons could be drawn with nonmagnetic disorder that degrades CDW and facilitates SC at low temperatures. The paper is arranged as follows: we discuss the methods employed in the following section. We then discuss the results and draw our conclusions at the end. TMDs通常在CDW有序温度TCDW以上表现出不良金属电阻率，在TCDW以下变为二次方并更接近金属行为。在相变处通常几乎没有或完全没有异常。高温下的线性电阻率持续到相当高的温度11,12。尽管有这些压倒性的反面证据，且所需费米面嵌套几乎不存在，大多数关于TMDs的理论工作仍诉诸平均场考虑。除很久以前的1T-TaS213和最近的2H-TaSe212,14及1T-TiSe216外，Mott性在TMDs中并未被认为重要。ARPES数据中大的谱权重转移、典型能量下声子特征的缺失以及动量无关的自能17，强烈表明存在超越（平均场）单粒子过程的物理。大多数TMDs中的超导电性也提出了有趣的问题。它通常出现在CDW被无序、插层或压力抑制时18。最近Li等人采取了这样一种方法19，在2H-TaSe2中，Se被硫（S）逐渐取代，直至2H-TaS2。他们报道了在单晶TaSe2−xSx（0 < x < 2）合金中出现稳健的超导序。该合金的SC转变温度（TSC）高于两个端元TaSe2和TaS2。还发现合金系列中间成分附近的电导率高于2H-TaSe2和2H-TaS2。这一观察清楚表明该体系中的超导电性与CDW竞争。为理解这一竞争，我们研究CDW与SC共存的问题，并加入非磁性无序。研究了两种无序：随机无序（实空间中强度固定的均匀概率分布）和固定强度无序区域的团簇。利用自洽Bogoliubov-de-Gennes（BdG）公式，我们采用蒙特卡罗方法20观察SC和电荷序的演化。此类工作曾在高温超导鼎盛时期用于无序超导电性的研究21,22。此外，我们还从特定掺杂下的强耦合视角处理合金问题。对TaSe2−xSx（x=0和1）进行了密度泛函理论结合多轨道动力学平均场理论（DFT+MO-DMFT）计算，以理解CDW与SC竞争的本质。无序诱导的竞争通过硫掺杂引入，它轻微影响能带结构，同时充当替代位无序。强耦合方法将CDW视为不良金属中预形成的激子液体14,16的凝聚。母体二硫化物中发现的非常规CDW是高温下预形成激子的玻色凝聚。由于TMD中未发现磁性，该合金是不具磁涨落的不良金属。因此可以直接与非磁性无序进行比较，后者破坏CDW并在低温下促进SC。本文安排如下：下一节讨论所采用的方法，然后讨论结果，最后得出结论。

> ## Combined DFT+DMFT approach:
> 
> DFT+DMFT联合方法：
> 
> 2H-TaSe2 has a layered hexagonal structure (space group P63/mmc). The crystal structure contains layers of transition metals and chalcogens, arranged along the z direction; these layers are separated by van der Waals gap. First principles calculations were performed using WIEN2k24 full-potential linearized augmented plane wave (FP-LAPW) ab initio package within the DFT formalism to get the band structure, density of states (DOS) and related quantities. A 10 × 10 × 10 k-mesh (with the cutoff parameter, Rkmax = 7.5) is used here and a generalized gradient approximation Perdew-Burke-Ernzerhof (GGA-PBE) exchange correlation potential is chosen. The muffin-tin radius, Rmt is chosen to be 2.5 a.u. for Ta, 2.39 for Se, and 2.21 for S (used for doping). The cell parameters are derived from an earlier experiment19. Finally the self-consistent field (scf) calculations are evaluated with an accuracy of 0.0001 eV and the energy-minimized crystal structure is obtained for the parent and doped dichalcogenide. From the converged structure we determined the band structure and DOS. To carry out transport calculations, a fully charge-self-consistent dynamical mean field theory (DMFT) is used. In a strongly-correlated system, like a TMD, DFT, combined with DMFT, has been successful in explaining a host of experimental results. The multi-orbital iterated perturbation theory (MO-IPT), a computationally fast and effective impurity solver, has been used here. > We present our results from the BdG calculation on an extended Hubbard model first. In our model, we have considered a 2D s-wave superconductor with CDW order and non-magnetic disorder. With the parameters (unit of energy t = 1) mentioned above we averaged all the observables for 200 independent realizations of two different types of disorder: (i) a random choice of disorder sites without any bias and (ii) a clustered disorder configuration. For better understanding we provided here spatial distribution of CDW order and superconducting gap with different disorder strengths in both random and clustered disorder configurations in Fig. 1. The spatial profiles of disorder ((a), (d), (g), (j)), ∆SC ((b), (e), (h), (k)) and ∆CDW ((c), (f), (i), (l)) are shown in Fig. 1. Though the disorder is completely uncorrelated from site to site, emergence of superconducting islands increases with increasing disorder. These superconducting islands are separated from one another by very small CDW regions and the size of the islands is the measure of coherence length and can be tuned with disorder strength and superconducting pair potential. For initial assumption we considered here incommensurate CDW and start from an initial guess of CDW and superconducting order. We find that: (i) random potential fluctuations which are not spatially coherent cannot lead to the reappearance of superconductivity. (ii) there is a connection between the coherence length of superconductivity and the wavelength of CDW vectors. (iii) we have considered disorder on a microscopic scale and the combination of the pairing interaction, disorder concentration and amplitude of the impurities. This combination leads to superconducting islands separated by insulating region. Our results on the disorder strength dependence of superconducting and CDW order for the two types of disorder are shown in Fig.2. For both randomly distributed and clustered disorder, CDW order parameter decreases slowly while the SC order parameter increases, indicating that the emergence of superconducting order takes place in a highly-disordered situation. For both kinds of disorder enhancement of superconductivity may be related to disorder induced changes in stoichiometry and lattice bond lengths. In our model, a disorder term essentially describes a random fluctuation in the chemical potential. In reality, however, such potential fluctuations could appear from any change in the stoichiometry and/or bond lengths. However a clustering of impurities is the most probable scenario for a macroscopic superconducting order to appear. For impurities distributed randomly over the two-dimensional space, the reappearance of robust superconducting order will require large concentration of impurities (more than 20 percent). (iv) The spectral gap (Fig.3) in one-particle density of states persists through all the ranges of disorder in spite of growing number of sites. Not only does the spectral gap persist in the disordered state, it increases with increasing disorder. In the present setting (shown in Fig. 1) the CDW wavelength is small to start with: in Fig. 1(b) for example, the CDW is like a checkerboard pattern. What we find is that if we take longer CDW wavelength, SC is not suppressed much (or by negligible amount) by CDW in the absence of disorder. This is also understandable as the density modulation due to CDW occurs over a large length scale which does not affect SC. On the other hand, if CDW has a small wave vector (i.e., rapidly changing carrier density) SC is strongly affected. The macroscopic superconducting coherence sets in over a coherence length which the short-wavelength CDW militates against. There is, therefore, a connection between the CDW wave vector and SC coherence length. Disorder sets in its own length scale in the problem by localizing charges, disturbing the existing charge-density profile, facilitating SC order to emerge. > As discussed above, the normal state of TMDs are dominated by incoherent scattering of electrons condensing in to a more coherent CDW state at T < TCDW. To study the competition between superconductivity and CDW order, we have used DFT+DMFT calculation on the transition metal dichalcogenide 2H-TaSe2, where Se is substituted gradually by S, as reported by Li, et al.19. A random concentration of substitution by this method entails huge computational efforts. We work therefore at only two composition (equal Se and S, x=1 and x=0). As we show, the chemical substitution does indeed lead to a competition between CDW and SC and our theory captures the essential results of Li, et al. The band structures for both the structures are calculated from WIEN2K. > The parent compound 2H-TaSe2 has two CDW transitions (120 K and 90 K) down to low temperatures and the reported superconductivity is below 1 K (∼ 0.14 K). It is well known that DMFT is an excellent approximation to compute transport coefficients (directly from DMFT Green’s function) since irreducible vertex corrections vanish for one-band model and are small for multiband situations. Examination of CDW state through resistivity in this dichalcogenide shows a change in slope of resistivity around 120 K in 2H-TaSe2 (Fig. 6a) which is the onset of incommensurate CDW. The resistivity decreases monotonically thereafter without any feature (Fig.6a inset). > In the excitonic CDW scenario14, the carrier scattering above TCDW is due predominantly to incoherent pre-formed excitonic fluctuations in TMD systems. An effective model for the incoherent metal is written down and a mean-field analysis for the CDW instability is performed thereon (vide reference [14] and [16]).
