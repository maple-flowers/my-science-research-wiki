> ## 四、正文核心内容剖析
> 
> ### 1. 研究背景与动机
> 
> -   **领域定位**：**IV-VI族过渡金属硫族化合物**是下一代光电材料的研究热点，SnTe是其中的明星材料。
> -   **核心问题**：SnTe薄膜的光学性质如何，特别是如何通过简单、可控的制备参数（如电压）来调节其性能，以适应不同的应用场景？这个问题在以往研究中尚不明确。
> -   **研究目标**：系统地研究电化学沉积过程中，**沉积电压**这一单一变量对SnTe薄膜一系列光学性质的影响，并筛选出最适合光伏应用的制备条件。
> 
> ### 2. 研究方法与模型
> 
> -   **材料制备**：采用**电化学沉积(ECD)** 技术，这是一种在溶液中进行，通过施加电压在导电基底上沉积薄膜的湿化学方法。其优点是设备简单、成本低、可在室温下操作、膜厚可控。
> -   **关键变量**：唯一变量是**沉积电压**，设置了10 V, 11 V, 12 V, 13 V四个水平，沉积时间固定为10秒。
> -   **表征手段**：核心工具是**UV-1800紫外-可见分光光度计**，测量薄膜的透射、吸收和反射光谱。
> -   **理论模型**：研究并不构建复杂模型，而是基于一系列经典的物理公式，从测量的光谱数据中提取关键光学参数。这些公式是连接原始数据（A, T, R）与物理内涵（Eg, a, σ）的桥梁。
>     -   **Tauc公式** `(αhν)ⁿ = A(hν - Eg)`：计算光学带隙的“金标准”。
>     -   **光学电导率公式** `σ = αhν / 4π`：量化材料在光照下的导电能力。
>     -   **消光系数公式** `k = αλ / 4π`：衡量光波在材料中传播时的衰减快慢。
>     -   **折射率公式** `n = (1 + √R) / (1 - √R)`：从反射率推导折射率，描述光在介质中传播速度的变化。
> 
> ### 3. 实验结果与讨论
> 
> -   **核心发现**：沉积电压对SnTe薄膜的光学性能有显著且非单调的影响。11V是最优条件。
> -   **性能调控机制**：作者将电压对性能的影响归因于对薄膜**微结构**的调控，包括：
>     -   **薄膜致密度与化学计量比**：11V下可能形成了更致密、Sn/Te比例更接近理想化学计量比的薄膜，缺陷少，因此带隙窄、吸收强。
>     -   **晶粒尺寸与结晶度**：合适的电压有利于晶粒生长和融合，形成更有序的晶格结构，从而提高载流子迁移率，表现为光学电导率升高。
> -   **结果对比与验证**：文中将获得的光学参数（如Eg=1.41-1.90 eV）与其他研究报道的SnTe薄膜数据进行对比，验证了其结果的合理性和竞争力。
> 
> ## 五、结论与贡献
> 
> 本研究成功通过电化学沉积法，在不同电压下制备了SnTe薄膜，并系统阐明了沉积电压对其光学性质的调控规律。
>

> ### ## 八、用户问题预测与解答
> 
> **问题1：什么是“光学带隙”？为什么它越小，太阳能电池效率可能越高？** **解答**：**光学带隙 (Optical Band Gap)** 是指电子从价带跃迁到导带所需要的最小光子能量，单位是电子伏特 (eV)。可以把它想象成一个门槛。太阳光里包含各种能量的光子。当光子能量小于带隙时，材料对它“视而不见”，光子直接穿过；当光子能量大于或等于带隙时，材料才能吸收它，激发出电子-空穴对，从而产生电流。根据**肖克利-奎伊瑟极限**理论，存在一个最佳带隙（约 1.34 eV），使得吸收太阳光能量与不可避免的能量损失（热弛豫）之间达到平衡。1.41 eV 非常接近这个值，因此理论上能实现较高的效率。
> 
> **问题2：论文中提到的“吸收系数”和“消光系数”有什么区别？** **解答**：两者都描述光在材料中如何衰减，但角度不同。
> 
> -   **吸收系数 (α)**：主要衡量材料“吸收”光的能力，即光能因转化为电子等其他形式的能量而导致的强度衰减。单位通常是 cm⁻¹。
> -   **消光系数 (k)**：是一个更广泛的物理量，它衡量的是电磁波在介质中传播时，由于“吸收”和“散射”共同造成的**总衰减**。它是个无量纲量，与吸收系数成正比（k = αλ / 4π）。在本文中，由于散射可能较弱，两者的变化趋势高度一致，都与吸收能力成正比。
> 
> **问题3：为什么11V的薄膜吸收最强，反射反而最弱？能量去哪里了？** **解答**：这完全符合能量守恒定律。当一束光照射到薄膜上时，会发生三件事：一部分被**透射 (T)**，一部分被**反射 (R)**，剩下的被**吸收 (A)**。可以写为 A + T + R = 1（或100%）。11V 的薄膜像一块“光学黑洞”，它把大部分光能都吸收了（A 很大），那么能用于透射和反射的光就自然很少了。所以我们看到它吸收最强，透射和反射都最弱。能量并没有消失，而是转化为了材料内部的电能或热能。
>

> ## Abstract
> 
> ## 摘要
> 
> Thin films of tin telluride (SnTe) were successfully deposited on fluorine doped tin oxide (FTO) conducting glass substrates by means of the electrochemical deposition technique. The films were prepared over a duration of 10 seconds at four distinct voltages (10 V, 11 V, 12 V, and 13 V). Optical characterization was carried out using a UV 1800 spectrophotometer across a wavelength range of 200 nm to 1000 nm. Although maximum optical absorption was recorded in the ultraviolet region for all films, the results indicate that the deposition voltage exerts a non uniform influence on the optical behaviour of the grown SnTe thin films. The film deposited at 11 V demonstrated the most favourable performance, yielding the lowest optical band gap of 1.41 eV and the highest optical conductivity of approximately 2.64 × 10−3 S/cm. Films grown at 10 V, 12 V, and 13 V exhibited high transparency in the visible and near infrared regions, whereas the 11 V sample showed markedly lower transmittance. Reflectivity across all samples was generally low, with the 11 V film recording the least reflectance over all measured wavelengths. Band gap energies ranged from 1.41 eV to 1.90 eV, signifying considerable tunability of the optical properties through voltage adjustment. Additional optical parameters, including absorption coefficient, extinction coefficient, and refractive index, were evaluated and discussed. > # 1. Introduction

> Transition metal chalcogenides belong to group IV–VI of the periodic table and have attracted sustained research interest as next generation optoelectronic and photovoltaic materials. These compounds are abundant in the earth’s crust, exhibit low toxicity, and possess remarkable chemical stability, qualities that make them attractive for large scale device fabrication (Rohitkumar et al., 2024; Subramanian et al., 2025). Among the members of this family, tin telluride (SnTe) stands out as one of the most extensively studied, owing to its narrow band gap, which has been reported in the range of 0.18–0.37 eV at room temperature (Tanwar et al., 2020). This narrow gap makes SnTe especially relevant for infrared detection, thermoelectric conversion, and photovoltaic energy harvesting. 过渡金属硫族化合物属于元素周期表 IV–VI 族，作为下一代光电和光伏材料，已吸引了持续的研究兴趣。这些化合物在地壳中储量丰富，毒性低，并具有显著的化学稳定性，这些特性使其对于大规模器件制造具有吸引力（Rohitkumar 等，2024；Subramanian 等，2025）。在该族成员中，碲化锡 (SnTe) 因其窄带隙而成为研究最广泛的材料之一，据报道其在室温下的带隙范围为 0.18–0.37 eV（Tanwar 等，2020）。这种窄带隙使 SnTe 特别适用于红外探测、热电转换和光伏能量收集。 **Transition metal chalcogenides**: 过渡金属硫族化合物，指过渡金属与硫族元素（如硫、硒、碲）形成的化合物。
> 
> From a crystallographic standpoint, SnTe adopts a face centred cubic (rock salt) structure and has been categorized as a topological crystalline insulator. The compound exhibits intrinsic p type conductivity as a consequence of tin vacancy defects, and its protected surface states offer prospects for novel spintronic and optoelectronic devices (Rohitkumar et al., 2024). A ferroelectric phase transition from the cubic to a rhombohedral structure occurs near 100 K, adding further interest to the low temperature physics of this material (Wang et al., 2011). These varied and tuneable electronic properties underscore the versatility of SnTe across multiple technological platforms. 从晶体学角度来看，SnTe 采用面心立方（岩盐）结构，并已被归类为拓扑晶体绝缘体。该化合物由于锡空位缺陷而表现出本征 p 型导电性，其受保护的表面态为新型自旋电子学和光电器件提供了前景（Rohitkumar 等，2024）。在约 100 K 时发生从立方结构到菱方结构的铁电相变，进一步增加了该材料低温物理学的趣味性（Wang 等，2011）。这些多样且可调的电子特性突显了 SnTe 在多个技术平台上的多功能性。 **Topological crystalline insulator**: 拓扑晶体绝缘体，一种体态绝缘但表面态受晶体对称性保护而导电的新型量子材料。
> 
> Semiconductor thin films are ultra thin layers of material whose thicknesses range from a few nanometres to several micrometres. By confining the active material to such reduced dimensions, high performance surfaces can be realized while consuming minimal raw material, thereby lowering production cost and environmental waste (Subramanian et al., 2025). The growth process is central to the manufacture of novel photovoltaic materials, and a thorough understanding of different deposition mechanisms remains essential for improving film quality (Cao and Qi, 2025). Synthesis and deposition routes govern the morphology, composition, and crystallinity of semiconductor thin films, enabling precise control over device architecture and performance (Toma et al., 2025). 半导体薄膜是厚度从几纳米到几微米的超薄材料层。通过将活性材料限制在如此小的尺寸内，可以在消耗最少原材料的同时实现高性能表面，从而降低生产成本和环境废物（Subramanian 等，2025）。生长过程是新型光伏材料制造的核心，彻底理解不同的沉积机制对于提高薄膜质量仍然至关重要（Cao 和 Qi，2025）。合成和沉积途径决定了半导体薄膜的形态、组成和结晶度，从而能够精确控制器件结构和性能（Toma 等，2025）。
> 
> Several techniques have been established for preparing SnTe thin films and SnTe based alloys, including thermal evaporation (Singh et al., 2023), chemical bath deposition (Toma et al., 2024), spin coating (Nimalan and Begam, 2024), molecular beam epitaxy, mechanical alloying, and pyrolysis from single source precursors. Among these, the electrochemical deposition (ECD) method is preferred for its simplicity, cost effectiveness, and the ability to exercise precise control over film thickness on glass substrates (Tabtimtea and Mahmood, 2023). 已建立了几种制备 SnTe 薄膜和 SnTe 基合金的技术，包括热蒸发（Singh 等，2023）、化学浴沉积（Toma 等，2024）、旋涂（Nimalan 和 Begam，2024）、分子束外延、机械合金化以及单源前驱体热解。其中，电化学沉积 (ECD) 方法因其简单、成本效益高以及在玻璃基底上能够精确控制薄膜厚度而受到青睐（Tabtimtea 和 Mahmood，2023）。 **ECD**: 电化学沉积 (Electrochemical Deposition)，一种利用电场驱动离子在电极表面沉积形成薄膜的技术。
> 
> In recent years, thin films have become increasingly vital in modern biomedicine, electronics, photovoltaic technology, and electrical component fabrication (Kotlarski et al., 2025). 近年来，薄膜在现代生物医学、电子学、光伏技术和电气元件制造中变得越来越重要（Kotlarski 等，2025）。
> 
> The potential of SnTe thin films in several areas of optoelectronics has been attributed to their unique and tuneable electronic properties (Wang et al., 2011). An in depth understanding of their optical behaviour is therefore crucial for identifying specific photovoltaic and optoelectronic applications. Nevertheless, the optical properties of SnTe thin films remain incompletely understood, particularly regarding how deposition parameters influence film performance. Chalcogenide thin films can be tailored at the nanoscale, allowing researchers to fine tune optical and electronic properties for targeted applications (Ikhioya et al., 2022). The present study investigates the optical properties of electrochemically deposited SnTe thin films as a function of deposition voltage, with the aim of elucidating how voltage variation affects absorbance, transmittance, reflectance, optical band gap, absorption coefficient, extinction coefficient, refractive index, and optical conductivity. SnTe 薄膜在光电学多个领域的潜力归因于其独特且可调的电子特性（Wang 等，2011）。因此，深入理解其光学行为对于确定特定的光伏和光电应用至关重要。然而，SnTe 薄膜的光学性质仍未完全被理解，特别是关于沉积参数如何影响薄膜性能。硫族化物薄膜可在纳米尺度上进行定制，使研究人员能够针对目标应用微调光学和电子特性（Ikhioya 等，2022）。本研究研究了电化学沉积 SnTe 薄膜的光学性质随沉积电压的变化，旨在阐明电压变化如何影响吸光度、透射率、反射率、光学带隙、吸收系数、消光系数、折射率和光导率。 **Absorbance**: 吸光度，衡量材料吸收光能力的物理量。 **Transmittance**: 透射率，透过材料的光通量与入射光通量之比。 **Reflectance**: 反射率，材料表面反射的光通量与入射光通量之比。

> # 3. 材料与方法
> 
> The materials employed in this study include methanol, concentrated hydrochloric acid (HCl), telluride oxide, tin sulphate, a digital multimeter, glass beakers, a syringe, distilled water, fluorine doped tin oxide (FTO) glass substrates, a digital weighing balance, a magnetic stirrer, a carbon electrode, a direct current (DC) power supply, a stopwatch, an electrothermal oven, and a UV Vis spectrophotometer (UV 1800 series). 本研究使用的材料包括甲醇、浓盐酸 (HCl)、氧化碲、硫酸锡、数字万用表、玻璃烧杯、注射器、蒸馏水、氟掺杂氧化锡 (FTO) 玻璃基底、数字天平、磁力搅拌器、碳电极、直流 (DC) 电源、秒表、电热烘箱和紫外-可见分光光度计（UV 1800 系列）。
> 
> ## 3.1 Substrate Preparation
> 
> ## 3.1 基底制备
> 
> The FTO glass substrates and beakers were washed in methanol for 10 minutes to remove dust particles and grease. They were subsequently rinsed in distilled water for another 10 minutes and then dried in the electrothermal oven for 30 minutes. FTO 玻璃基底和烧杯在甲醇中清洗 10 分钟以去除灰尘颗粒和油脂。随后在蒸馏水中漂洗 10 分钟，然后在电热烘箱中干燥 30 分钟。
> 
> ## 3.2 Precursor Preparation
> 
> ## 3.2 前驱体制备
> 
> The tin precursor was prepared by weighing 0.366 g of tin sulphate using a digital weighing balance, transferring it into a beaker, dissolving it in 100 ml of distilled water, and stirring the mixture carefully to form a homogeneous solution. For the tellurium precursor, 0.150 g of telluride oxide was weighed and dissolved in 5 ml of concentrated HCl measured by a syringe, followed by careful stirring and the addition of distilled water to bring the total volume to 100 ml. 锡前驱体的制备方法为：使用数字天平称取 0.366 g 硫酸锡，将其转移至烧杯中，溶解于 100 ml 蒸馏水中，并小心搅拌混合物以形成均匀溶液。对于碲前驱体，称取 0.150 g 氧化碲并溶解于通过注射器量取的 5 ml 浓盐酸中，随后小心搅拌并加入蒸馏水使总体积达到 100 ml。 **Precursor**: 前驱体，在化学反应中用于制备目标产物的起始化合物。
> 
> ## 3.3 Electrochemical Deposition
> 
> ## 3.3 电化学沉积
> 
> Using the ECD technique, thin films of SnTe were grown on four FTO conducting glass substrates at different voltages in the range of 10 V to 13 V, keeping the deposition time interval at 10 seconds in each cycle. The electrolyte consisted of 20 ml of the tin precursor and 20 ml of the tellurium precursor in a 50 ml beaker, which was gently stirred to obtain a homogeneous mixture. The electrodes were immersed into the electrolyte and connected to the DC power supply. After deposition, the SnTe thin films were dried in an electrothermal oven at 150 °C for 30 minutes and set aside for optical characterization. 使用 ECD 技术，在四个 FTO 导电玻璃基底上以 10 V 至 13 V 范围内的不同电压生长 SnTe 薄膜，每个循环中保持沉积时间为 10 秒。电解液由 20 ml 锡前驱体和 20 ml 碲前驱体在 50 ml 烧杯中组成，轻轻搅拌以获得均匀混合物。将电极浸入电解液中并连接到直流电源。沉积后，SnTe 薄膜在 150 °C 的电热烘箱中干燥 30 分钟，并放置一旁用于光学表征。 **Electrolyte**: 电解液，含有可移动离子并能传导电流的溶液。
>

> # 4. 结果与讨论

> ## 4.1 Optical Absorbance
> 
> ## 4.1 光吸收
>

All four films exhibit maximum absorbance in the ultraviolet (UV) region, with a progressive decline in absorbance as the wavelength extends into the visible and near infrared (NIR) regions of the electromagnetic spectrum. This pattern is typical of semiconductor thin films in which electronic transitions occur predominantly at energies corresponding to UV wavelengths (Ojegu and Ikhioya, 2024). The peak wavelengths and corresponding absorbance values for each sample are summarised in Table 1. 在 10 V、11 V、12 V 和 13 V 电压下沉积的 SnTe 薄膜的光吸收光谱如图 1 所示。所有四种薄膜在紫外 (UV) 区域表现出最大吸光度，随着波长延伸至电磁波谱的可见光和近红外 (NIR) 区域，吸光度逐渐下降。这种模式是半导体薄膜的典型特征，其中电子跃迁主要发生在对应于紫外波长的能量下（Ojegu 和 Ikhioya，2024）。每个样品的峰值波长和相应吸光度值汇总于表 1。
> 
> Among the four samples, the film deposited at 11 V recorded the highest absorbance value of 1.2077 arbitrary units (a.u.) at a peak wavelength of 321 nm. This observation indicates that the 11 V deposition condition favours a denser or more optically active film microstructure, possibly arising from improved nucleation and grain coalescence at that particular voltage (Rohitkumar et al., 2024). The 12 V sample registered the next highest absorbance of 1.1071 a.u. at 350 nm, while the 10 V and 13 V films showed comparable peak absorbance values of 0.9707 and 0.9651 a.u. at 340 nm and 330 nm, respectively. The non monotonic relationship between deposition voltage and peak absorbance suggests that the influence of voltage on the optical density of the film is mediated by competing factors such as film thickness, crystallinity, and defect concentration (Singh et al., 2023; Cao and Qi, 2025). 在四个样品中，11 V 下沉积的薄膜在峰值波长 321 nm 处记录了最高吸光度值 1.2077 任意单位 (a.u.)。这一观察结果表明，11 V 的沉积条件有利于更致密或光学活性更强的薄膜微结构，可能源于该特定电压下改善的成核和晶粒聚结（Rohitkumar 等，2024）。12 V 样品在 350 nm 处记录了次高吸光度 1.1071 a.u.，而 10 V 和 13 V 薄膜分别在 340 nm 和 330 nm 处显示出相当的峰值吸光度值 0.9707 和 0.9651 a.u.。沉积电压与峰值吸光度之间的非单调关系表明，电压对薄膜光密度的影响受到薄膜厚度、结晶度和缺陷浓度等竞争因素的中介作用（Singh 等，2023；Cao 和 Qi，2025）。

> The high absorbance observed in the UV region for all films is consistent with published reports on Group IV–VI chalcogenide thin films, where strong interband transitions dominate the short wavelength portion of the spectrum (Tanwar et al., 2020). > ## 4.2 Percentage Transmittance
> 
> ## 4.2 透射率百分比
> 
> The transmittance spectra of the SnTe thin films are shown in Figure 2. A consistent trend is observed across all four films: transmittance increases gradually from sharp dips at UV wavelengths towards higher values in the visible and near infrared regions. The film grown at 11 V exhibits the lowest percentage transmittance across all measured wavelengths, in direct agreement with its highest absorbance. This behaviour indicates that the 11 V film is the most optically dense of the four samples, absorbing a greater fraction of incident photons (Ikhioya et al., 2022). SnTe 薄膜的透射光谱如图 2 所示。在所有四种薄膜中观察到一致的趋势：透射率从紫外波长的急剧下降处逐渐增加，至可见光和近红外区域达到较高值。11 V 下生长的薄膜在所有测量波长上表现出最低的透射率百分比，与其最高的吸光度直接一致。这种行为表明，11 V 薄膜是四个样品中光学密度最高的，吸收了更大比例的入射光子（Ikhioya 等，2022）。
> 
> Figure 2: Percentage transmittance versus wavelength of SnTe thin films grown from 10 V to 13 V. 图 2：在 10 V 至 13 V 下生长的 SnTe 薄膜的透射率百分比与波长的关系。
> 
> The remaining three films (10 V, 12 V, and 13 V) display comparatively high transmittance in the visible and NIR regions, a quality that makes them suitable for window layer applications in photovoltaic devices and smart windows (Kotlarski et al., 2025). The transmittance values for the 12 V sample, in particular, are among the highest observed, which correlates with its relatively lower absorbance. Such a combination of high transparency in the visible range and controlled absorption in the UV region is desirable for protective coatings and anti reflection layers in solar cell architectures (Subramanian et al., 2025). 其余三种薄膜（10 V、12 V 和 13 V）在可见光和近红外区域显示出相对较高的透射率，这一特性使其适用于光伏器件和智能窗户中的窗口层应用（Kotlarski 等，2025）。特别是 12 V 样品的透射率值属于观察到的最高值之一，这与其相对较低的吸光度相关。在可见光范围内具有高透明度和在紫外区域具有可控吸收的这种组合对于太阳能电池结构中的保护涂层和抗反射层是理想的（Subramanian 等，2025）。 **Window layer**: 窗口层，太阳能电池中允许大部分入射光通过并到达吸收层的透明导电层。
> 
> ## 4.3 Reflectance
> 
> ## 4.3 反射率
> 
> The reflectance spectra of the deposited SnTe thin films are presented in Figure 3. All films show an increase in reflectance from a sharp dip in the UV region as the wavelength progresses into the visible and near infrared regions. The SnTe thin film deposited at 12 V displays the highest reflectivity across the measured wavelength range, whereas the 11 V film records the lowest reflectance. This inverse relationship between absorbance and reflectance is physically consistent: a film that absorbs a greater proportion of incident light inherently reflects less (Ojegu and Ikhioya, 2024). 沉积的 SnTe 薄膜的反射光谱如图 3 所示。随着波长进入可见光和近红外区域，所有薄膜均显示反射率从紫外区域的急剧下降处增加。在 12 V 下沉积的 SnTe 薄膜在整个测量波长范围内显示出最高的反射率，而 11 V 薄膜记录了最低的反射率。吸光度与反射率之间的这种反比关系在物理上是一致的：吸收更大比例入射光的薄膜固有地反射较少（Ojegu 和 Ikhioya，2024）。
> 
> Figure 3: Reflectance versus wavelength of SnTe thin films grown from 10 V to 13 V. 图 3：在 10 V 至 13 V 下生长的 SnTe 薄膜的反射率与波长的关系。
> 
> The generally low reflectance of all four films is a desirable characteristic for photovoltaic applications, as it implies that a larger proportion of incident solar radiation enters the active layer rather than being reflected away (Cao and Qi, 2025). The low reflectance of the 11 V film, combined with its high absorbance, positions it as a promising absorber material for thin film solar cells. Meanwhile, the relatively higher reflectance of the 12 V film, coupled with its high transmittance, suggests potential utility as a transparent conductive layer or as a back reflector in multi junction photovoltaic devices (Toma et al., 2024). 所有四种薄膜普遍较低的反射率是光伏应用的一个理想特性，因为它意味着更大比例的入射太阳辐射进入活性层而不是被反射掉（Cao 和 Qi，2025）。11 V 薄膜的低反射率，结合其高吸光度，使其成为薄膜太阳能电池有前景的吸收材料。同时，12 V 薄膜相对较高的反射率，加上其高透射率，表明其作为透明导电层或多结光伏器件中的背反射器具有潜在用途（Toma 等，2024）。 **Active layer**: 活性层，光伏器件中负责吸收光子并产生电荷载流子的功能层。
>

> The film deposited at 11 V yielded the lowest band gap of 1.41 eV, while the 13 V film exhibited the widest band gap of 1.90 eV. This range of tuneable band gap values is noteworthy from an applied physics perspective. A band gap of approximately 1.4 eV lies within the optimal range for single junction solar cell absorbers, as predicted by the Shockley Queisser limit (Rohitkumar et al., 2024). The band gap values of the 10 V and 12 V films (1.83 eV and 1.75 eV, respectively) are also relevant for tandem and multi junction solar cell configurations, where absorbers of different band gaps are stacked to harvest photons across a broader portion of the solar spectrum (Cao and Qi, 2025). 11 V 下沉积的薄膜产生了 1.41 eV 的最低带隙，而 13 V 薄膜表现出 1.90 eV 的最宽带隙。从应用物理学的角度来看，这种可调带隙值的范围值得注意。约 1.4 eV 的带隙处于单结太阳能电池吸收器的最佳范围内，正如 Shockley-Queisser 极限所预测的那样（Rohitkumar 等，2024）。10 V 和 12 V 薄膜的带隙值（分别为 1.83 eV 和 1.75 eV）也与叠层和多结太阳能电池配置相关，其中不同带隙的吸收器被堆叠以收集更宽范围太阳光谱的光子（Cao 和 Qi，2025）。 **Shockley Queisser limit**: Shockley-Queisser 极限，单结太阳能电池理论最大转换效率的物理极限，约为 33.7%。

> The non linear variation of band gap with voltage, illustrated in Figure 5, suggests that the deposition voltage influences the stoichiometry, defect density, and crystallite size of the deposited films in a complex manner. At 11 V, conditions appear to favour the formation of a more stoichiometric film with fewer defect states in the gap, resulting in a narrower band gap. At higher voltages (12 V and 13 V), increased overpotential may introduce lattice strain and point defects that widen the gap (Singh et al., 2023; Tanwar et al., 2020). 图 5 所示的带隙随电压的非线性变化表明，沉积电压以复杂的方式影响沉积薄膜的化学计量比、缺陷密度和晶粒尺寸。在 11 V 时，条件似乎有利于形成化学计量比更准确、带隙中缺陷态更少的薄膜，从而导致更窄的带隙。在较高电压（12 V 和 13 V）下，增加的过电位可能会引入晶格应变和点缺陷，从而扩大带隙（Singh 等，2023；Tanwar 等，2020）。 **Overpotential**: 过电位，电化学过程中实际电极电位与平衡电位之间的差值。

> ## 4.5 Absorption Coefficient
> 
> ## 4.5 吸收系数
> 
> The absorption coefficient (α) provides a quantitative measure of how strongly the thin film material absorbs photons at a given wavelength. Figure 6 presents the absorption coefficient as a function of wavelength for all four SnTe thin films. High absorption coefficients, of the order of 104 cm−1, were observed in the UV region for all samples, confirming the strong interband transitions observed in the absorbance spectra. The 11 V film recorded the highest absorption coefficient across most of the measured wavelength range, which is consistent with its superior absorbance performance. 吸收系数 (α) 提供了薄膜材料在给定波长下吸收光子强度的定量测量。图 6 展示了所有四种 SnTe 薄膜的吸收系数作为波长函数的曲线。所有样品在紫外区域均观察到高达 104 cm−1 数量级的高吸收系数，证实了在吸收光谱中观察到的强带间跃迁。11 V 薄膜在大部分测量波长范围内记录到最高的吸收系数，这与其优越的吸光度性能一致。 **Absorption coefficient**: 吸收系数，衡量材料在单位长度内吸收光强度的物理量。
> 
> Figure 6: Absorption coefficient versus wavelength for SnTe thin films grown from 10 V to 13 V. 图 6：在 10 V 至 13 V 下生长的 SnTe 薄膜的吸收系数与波长的关系。
> 
> The large absorption coefficient values indicate that these SnTe thin films are capable of efficiently absorbing incoming radiation within very thin layers, a critical requirement for thin film photovoltaic absorbers (Subramanian et al., 2025). The decrease in absorption coefficient with increasing wavelength reflects the transition from strong absorption in the UV band to weak absorption in the NIR region, which governs the spectral response of these films in device configurations. The magnitude of α obtained here compares favourably with values reported for other Group IV–VI thin films deposited by electrochemical methods (Ikhioya et al., 2022; Ojegu and Ikhioya, 2024). 较大的吸收系数值表明，这些 SnTe 薄膜能够在非常薄的层内有效吸收入射辐射，这是薄膜光伏吸收器的关键要求（Subramanian 等，2025）。吸收系数随波长增加而降低反映了从紫外波段的强吸收到近红外区域的弱吸收的转变，这决定了这些薄膜在器件配置中的光谱响应。此处获得的 α 值大小与通过电化学方法沉积的其他 IV–VI 族薄膜的报告值相当（Ikhioya 等，2022；Ojegu 和 Ikhioya，2024）。

> ## 4.6 Extinction Coefficient
> 
> ## 4.6 消光系数
> 
> The extinction coefficient (k) characterizes the attenuation of electromagnetic radiation within the thin film and is closely related to the absorption coefficient. Figure 7 displays the variation of k with wavelength for the four deposited films. All samples exhibit elevated extinction coefficient values in the UV region, which decrease progressively towards the visible and NIR portions of the spectrum. The 11 V sample again records the highest values of k, corroborating the trends observed in absorbance and absorption coefficient. 消光系数 (k) 表征了薄膜内电磁辐射的衰减，与吸收系数密切相关。图 7 显示了四种沉积薄膜的 k 随波长的变化。所有样品在紫外区域表现出较高的消光系数值，并向光谱的可见光和近红外部分逐渐降低。11 V 样品再次记录了最高的 k 值，证实了在吸光度和吸收系数中观察到的趋势。
> 
> Figure 7: Extinction coefficient versus wavelength for SnTe thin films grown from 10 V to 13 V. 图 7：在 10 V 至 13 V 下生长的 SnTe 薄膜的消光系数与波长的关系。
> 
> A low extinction coefficient in the visible and NIR regions, as exhibited by the 10 V, 12 V, and 13 V films, is characteristic of materials with high optical transparency in those spectral windows.
