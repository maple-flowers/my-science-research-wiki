> Recently a new, large family of two‐dimensional (2D) early transition metal carbides and carbonitrides, called MXenes, was discovered. MXenes are produced by selective etching of the A element from the MAX phases, which are metallically conductive, layered solids connected by strong metallic, ionic, and covalent bonds, such as Ti
>              2
>              AlC, Ti
>              3
>              AlC
>              2
>              , and Ta
>              4
>              AlC
>              3
>              . MXenes ­combine the metallic conductivity of transition metal carbides with the hydrophilic nature of their hydroxyl or oxygen terminated surfaces. In essence, they behave as “conductive clays”. This article reviews progress—both ­experimental and theoretical—on their synthesis, structure, properties, intercalation, delamination, and potential applications. MXenes are expected to be good candidates for a host of applications. They have already shown promising performance in electrochemical energy storage systems. A detailed outlook for future research on MXenes is also presented.
>
>【摘要翻译】最近发现了一个新的大家族的二维（2D）早期过渡金属碳化物和碳氮化物，称为MXenes。MXenes是通过选择腐蚀MAX相中的一种元素而产生的，MAX相是金属导电的、通过强金属、离子和共价键连接的层状固体，如Ti2AlC，Ti3AlC公司2，和Ta4AlC公司3.MXenes­将过渡金属碳化物的金属导电性与其端羟基或端氧表面的亲水性结合起来。本质上，它们表现为“导电粘土”。本文综述了它们的合成、结构、性能、插层、分层以及潜在应用等方面的实验和理论进展。MXenes有望成为一系列应用的良好候选产品。它们在电化学储能系统中已显示出良好的性能。并对MXenes的未来研究进行了展望。

> ## 一、引言
> 
> 在二维材料的研究领域，**石墨烯**的发现引领了一场革命。然而，科学家们持续探索具有更丰富多样特性的新型二维材料，以满足不同应用场景的需求。由德雷克塞尔大学的Yury Gogotsi、Michel W. Barsoum及其团队在2011年首次报道的**MXenes**，正是这一探索方向上里程碑式的突破。
> 
> 本解读报告基于该团队2014年在《Advanced Materials》上发表的题为“25th Anniversary Article: MXenes: A New Family of Two-Dimensional Materials”的综述文章。该文系统地总结了MXenes这一全新二维材料家族从发现到早期研究的关键进展。
> 
> **核心问题**：在众多层状材料中，如何突破传统范德华力作用的限制，通过化学手段从强化学键连接的三维层状材料中，创造性地剥离出具有独特性能的二维原子晶体？
>

> ## 四、正文核心内容剖析
> 
> ### 1. 研究背景与问题
> 
> 二维材料家族在石墨烯之后不断壮大，但大多数层状材料（如石墨、TMDs）是靠弱**范德华力**结合的，可通过机械剥离法直接分离。而**MAX相**是一类具有六方层状结构的三元碳化物/氮化物，其通式为Mn+1AXn，其中的M-X共价键与M-A金属键都很强，使得层间无法通过简单的机械力剪切。因此，本研究的核心问题是如何打破这种强化学键，从MAX相中“创造”出一种新的二维材料。
> 
> ### 2. 方法/模型
> 
> #### 合成策略：选择性刻蚀
> 
> 作者团队采取了一种巧妙的化学“剪刀”策略。由于M-A键与M-X键的化学性质与相对强度不同，他们选择使用**氢氟酸**水溶液作为选择性刻蚀剂。在室温下，HF优先攻击并溶解A（如Al）原子层，同时保留了Mn+1Xn层的二维结构完整性。刻蚀后，A原子层被O、OH和/或F等表面终端基团取代，这些基团使得层间结合力大大减弱，形成松散堆叠的“多层MXene”（ML-MXene）。
> 
> #### 理论模型：密度泛函理论
> 
> 在MXene的结构和性质研究中，**密度泛函理论**计算扮演了至关重要的角色。
> 
> -   **结构预测**：DFT计算首次提出了OH-终端Ti₃C₂的几何结构，并通过与实验XRD图谱的c晶格常数对比，验证了其合理性。
> -   **性质模拟**：DFT被广泛用于计算不同MXene的电子能带结构、态密度，预测其金属/半导体行为、磁性、弹性常数，以及锂离子在表面的吸附和扩散行为。这些计算为实验观察提供了深入的机理解释，并指导了新材料的筛选。
> 
> ### 3. 实验/数据
> 
> #### 合成与形貌
> 
> -   **关键参数**：如表1所示，不同MAX相（如Ti₂AlC, Ti₃AlC₂, Ta₄AlC₃）合成对应MXene所需的HF浓度（10%-50%）和时间（2-90小时）差异巨大，这反映了M-Al键能的不同。
> -   **形貌特征**：SEM图像（图3a）直观展示了MAX相颗粒在刻蚀后转变为具有“手风琴状”形貌的MXene。
> -   **结构证据**：XRD图谱（图3b）中(000l)峰的下移和宽化，以及TEM/SAED（图3c-g）中二维形貌和六方对称性，共同证实了3D到2D的成功转变。
> -   **分层技术**：通过将DMSO等有机分子插入层间进行插层（图6, 7），可进一步弱化层间作用力，随后通过简单的超声处理即可实现大规模分层，获得稳定的单层/少层MXene水溶胶。这是制备高质量MXene应用材料的关键技术突破。
> 
> #### 性能数据
> 
> -   **电子性质**：实验证实，冷压的MXene圆片具有类似多层石墨烯的良好导电性。首次接触角测量表明MXene是亲水的，这与表面的大量-OH和-O基团有关。
> -   **力学预测**：DFT计算（表2）预测，无终端基团的MXene单层其面内弹性常数c₁₁（如Ti₂C为636 GPa）甚至高于其母体MAX相，显示出优异的力学潜力。
> -   **储能性能**：
>     -   **锂离子电池**：原位XRD实验证实了Li⁺在MXene层间的可逆插层/脱插层是储锂的主要机制。分层后的Ti₃C₂Tx“纸”电极展现出410 mAhg⁻¹（1C）的高容量和优异的倍率性能（图9）。
>     -   **超级电容器**：Ti₃C₂Tx纸电极在KOH电解液中展现了超过330 F cm⁻³的体积比容量，这一数值优于当时最好的全碳双电层电容器。
> 
> ## 五、结论与贡献
> 
> ### 核心发现
> 
> 本文系统总结了一种全新的二维材料家族——**MXenes**。它们是通过选择性刻蚀MAX相中的A层元素而制备的过渡金属碳化物和/或碳氮化物。MXenes兼具过渡金属碳化物的**金属导电性**和其表面终端基团带来的**亲水性**，表现出“导电粘土”般的独特性质。
> 
> ### 理论及实践贡献
> 
> 1.  **开创了一个新的二维材料家族**：MXenes的发现极大地扩充了二维材料的世界，其组成和结构的多样性远超过石墨烯等单质材料。
> 2.  **建立了一种新的合成范式**：提出了“选择性刻蚀强化学键合层状材料”这一通用策略，为从其他三维层状前驱体制备二维材料提供了新的思路。
> 3.  **桥接了理论与实验**：本文展示了DFT计算在预测和解释MXene结构、电子、力学性质方面的强大能力，形成了一个“实验-理论-再实验”的紧密研究闭环。
> 4.  **验证了关键应用潜力**：初步展示了MXenes在锂离子电池和超级电容器等电化学储能领域的卓越性能，为其后续应用研究指明了方向，并提供了明确的优化策略（如插层和分层）。
> 
> ## 六、未来发展方向展望
> 
> ### 文章提及的未解决问题
> 
> 1.  **表面化学的精确控制**：如何精确控制合成后MXene表面的终端基团（F, OH, O）的种类、比例和分布，并理解其随时间的变化，是亟待解决的关键问题。
> 2.  **真实结构模型**：当前理论模型多为理想化的完全单端基团覆盖的单层模型，尚不能完全描述真实的多层堆叠体系中，混合终端基团、不完全覆盖、层间水分子等复杂情况。
> 3.  **合成方法的拓展**：
>     -   寻找更安全、无毒的刻蚀剂替代HF。
>     -   探索非水刻蚀体系，以避免O或OH的引入，从而获得“裸”MXene。
>     -   实现非Al元素的A层刻蚀，以合成基于Mo、Zr、Hf等金属的MXene。
>     -   实现氮化物MXene（如Ti₂N）的成功合成。
> 4.  **理论计算的挑战**：需要开发能够精确描述层间范德华力和氢键作用的DFT方法，以及适用于大规模插层、聚合物复合材料等复杂体系的经典力场。
> 
> ### 潜在研究路径
> 
> 1.  **MXene薄膜器件**：将MAX相薄膜直接刻蚀为MXene薄膜，是构建电子器件的关键一步。
> 2.  **性能表征的深化**：对单层MXene的电子、磁学、光学、热学和力学性能进行系统表征。
> 3.  **应用领域的拓展**：探索MXene在聚合物增强复合材料、催化、透明导电电极、传感器等领域的应用。
> 
> ## 七、学术思考与批判性分析
>

>     -   **证据链**：基于其独特的电子和电化学特性，实验证明了其在**锂离子电池（LIBs，Lithium-Ion Batteries）** 和**超级电容器（Supercapacitors）** 中作为电极材料的优异性能，并展示了其在聚合物增强、催化、传感等领域的巨大潜力。

> ## 四、正文核心内容剖析
> 
> **1. 研究背景与动机**
> 
> 文章开篇便点明了二维材料领域的机遇与挑战。以石墨烯为代表的2D材料研究如火如荼，但新材料的发现是推动该领域发展的核心动力。作者指出，MAX相拥有一百多种成员，是一个庞大的材料宝库。然而，由于其层间结合力强，传统剥离方法失效。因此，**开发一种全新的、能够打破层间强化学键的“化学剪刀”方法，成为了解锁这个宝库、开辟二维材料新疆域的关键**。
> 
> **2. 方法/模型：合成与计算的双轮驱动**
> 
> -   **合成方法**：本文的核心创新在于**选择性刻蚀**。作者巧妙利用了MAX相中M-A键和M-X键的化学活性差异，使用HF作为“化学剪刀”，精准地剪断了M-A键，而保留了M-X骨架。这一过程在室温下即可完成，条件温和，但HF的浓度、时间和温度必须因材料而异，进行精细调控（详见表1）。后续的**插层与分层**技术，特别是使用二甲基亚砜（**DMSO，Dimethylsulfoxide**）进行插层后超声，实现了大规模制备单层/少层Ti₃C₂Tx，并形成稳定的胶体溶液（如图7所示），这是MXene走向实际应用的关键一步。
> -   **理论计算模型**：**密度泛函理论（DFT）** 在本文中不仅是辅助工具，更是核心驱动力之一。它被用于：(1) 预测和验证晶体结构，计算XRD图谱的c参数；(2) 筛选表面官能团的最稳定构型（I, II, III）；(3) 揭示电子结构转变的底层机制，即通过分析**态密度（DOS）** 和**部分态密度（PDOS）**，阐明官能团如何通过与过渡金属原子形成新的p-d杂化键来消耗费米能级附近的电子，从而调控导电性和磁性；(4) 预测未合成MXene的性能，如高**塞贝克系数（Seebeck Coefficient）**，为实验提供方向。
> 
> **3. 实验结果与讨论**
> 
> -   **结构与化学组成**：通过XRD、TEM、**X射线光电子能谱（XPS，X-ray Photoelectron Spectroscopy）** 和**能量色散谱（EDS，Energy-Dispersive Spectroscopy）** 等一系列表征，确认了A元素被成功移除，得到了带有O、OH、F混合官能团的MXene。实验发现，c晶格参数的变化不仅取决于官能团，还与层间水分子有关，V₂CTx和Nb₂CTx的c值异常大（见表1，23.96 Å和22.34 Å），暗示了层间冰的存在。
> -   **电子与电化学性能**：实验证实了MXene的金属导电性，其冷压圆片的电阻率低至22 Ω·sq⁻¹（Ti₃C₂Tx），与多层石墨烯相当。同时，接触角测试（27°-41°）证实了其亲水性。在电化学储能应用中，MXene表现出色：(1) **锂离子电池**：V₂CTx在1C倍率下容量高达280 mAh/g，且所有MXene均表现出优异的高倍率性能，这归因于其快速的离子嵌入/脱出动力学。分层后的Ti₃C₂Tx“纸”电极容量更是提升至410 mAh/g（1C）。(2) **超级电容器**：Ti₃C₂Tx纸电极在KOH电解液中实现了超过330 F/cm³的体积电容，优于许多碳基电容器，且循环寿命超过10,000次。
> 
> **4. 讨论与关联**
> 
> 文章将实验结果与理论计算紧密关联。例如，DFT预测裸MXene理论上具有更高的容量和更快的锂离子扩散速率，但实验中的MXene均带有官能团，这解释了为何实验性能与理论预测存在差距，也指明了未来优化表面化学的方向。同时，理论计算成功解释了为何含氮的Ti₃CNTx能保持金属性（氮原子贡献了额外电子），而纯碳化物MXene在官能团化后可能变为半导体。这种理论与实验的相互印证，极大地增强了结论的可靠性，并为理性设计MXene材料提供了范式。

> ## 七、学术思考与批判性分析
> 
> **问题1：HF刻蚀的局限性及其对性能的“隐形”影响**
> 
> 文献坦诚地承认了HF的危险性，并提出寻找替代品的需求。但更深层次的问题是，**HF刻蚀过程引入的不可控缺陷和含氟官能团，是否会对MXene的长期稳定性、电化学性能和生物相容性产生尚未被充分认知的负面影响？** 例如，氟官能团在电化学循环中可能脱落，导致电解液副反应和性能衰减。这是否是造成首次循环不可逆容量损失过高的一个被低估的原因？
> 
> **问题2：理论预测与实验现实的鸿沟有多远？**
> 
> 文章高度依赖DFT计算来预测MXene的电子、磁性和电化学性能。然而，大部分计算都是基于**理想化的、完整终端的、单层MXene模型**。而真实的MXene是混合终端的、多层堆叠的、并含有大量缺陷和层间水的。**DFT计算中常用的GGA-PBE泛函是否会系统性地低估带隙，导致对金属性/半导体性的误判？** 作者虽然提到了HSE06杂化泛函更准确，但其计算成本更高。这种理论模型与真实材料之间的鸿沟，是否会导致对性能的预测过于乐观，或误导实验设计方向？
> 
> **问题3：“导电黏土”定位是机遇还是陷阱？**
> 
> “导电黏土”这一比喻非常形象，但**这种独特的亲水性是否在某些应用中成为一种劣势？** 例如，在聚合物复合材料中，亲水的MXene与疏水的聚合物基体（如PE、PP）不相容，会导致严重的团聚和界面问题，反而无法发挥其增强和增韧效果。这是否意味着MXene在聚合物领域的应用，将比预想的更依赖于复杂的表面功能化修饰，从而增加应用成本？
> 
> **问题4：电化学储能应用的“高倍率”优势是否可持续？**
> 
> MXene在LIBs和超级电容器中展示出卓越的高倍率性能，这主要归功于离子在其层间的快速迁移。然而，**这种层间空间在长期循环中是否稳定？** 持续的离子嵌入/脱出可能导致层状结构的逐渐坍塌或官能团的解离，从而造成性能的快速衰减。此外，其首次库仑效率低的问题，虽然是层状材料的通病，但对于全电池设计至关重要。如果无法通过预锂化等手段有效解决，其实际能量密度优势将大打折扣。
> 
> **问题5：在众多2D材料中，MXene的不可替代性体现在哪里？**
> 
> 文章总结了MXene作为2D材料家族新成员的地位。但一个尖锐的问题是：**在石墨烯、TMDs、金属氧化物等众多竞争者中，MXene的“杀手锏”应用究竟是什么？** 石墨烯的电子迁移率无与伦比，TMDs的带隙多样性无可比拟。MXene的核心优势是“金属导电性+亲水性+可调的层间距”。那么，除了文中提到的电化学储能，是否还存在一个只有MXene能做到，而其他材料难以企及的应用场景？寻找这个“杀手锏”应用，将是决定MXene研究能否持续繁荣的关键。

> ## 八、用户问题预测与解答
> 
> 1.  **问题：为什么MAX相不能用类似石墨烯的“胶带”方法进行机械剥离，而必须用化学刻蚀？**
>     
>     -   **解答**：MXene母体MAX相的结构与石墨截然不同。石墨的碳原子层之间由微弱的**范德华力**结合，因此用胶带就能轻易克服层间作用力，实现剥离。而MAX相中，Mn+1Xn层与A原子层之间是强度很高的**金属键**，层间结合力远强于范德华力。因此，剪切等机械力无法选择性地破坏层间键合，强行剥离会破坏整个晶体结构。必须使用化学刻蚀这种“化学剪刀”的方法，选择性地断裂M-A金属键，才能将A层移除，实现层间分离。
> 2.  **问题：MXene的通式是Mn+1XnTx，其中的‘Tx’代表什么？为什么它如此重要？**
>     
>     -   **解答**：‘Tx’代表在刻蚀和后续处理过程中，自发吸附在MXene片层表面的**表面官能团（Terminations）**，最常见的是O、OH和F。它之所以重要，是因为它决定了MXene的绝大部分关键性质：首先，Tx决定了**亲水性**，使其能在水中稳定分散；其次，Tx通过形成新的化学键，消耗了过渡金属的“悬挂键”，从而**调控电子结构**，可以将金属性MXene转变为半导体；最后，Tx直接影响**电化学性能**，因为它们占据了锂离子等活性物质的吸附位点，并影响离子扩散速率。毫不夸张地说，不理解Tx，就不理解MXene。
> 3.  **问题：文章提到用DMSO插层可以大规模分层Ti₃C₂Tx，其原理是什么？**
>     
>     -   **解答**：刚合成的MXene虽然层间作用力减弱，但仍是多层堆叠的。DMSO是一种大分子有机物。当它插入MXene层间后，会像楔子一样将层间距极大地撑开（c晶格参数从约20 Å增加到约35 Å甚至更高，如图7所示）。这极大地削弱了MXene层间本已很弱的相互作用力。此时，再施加轻微的超声振动，就能轻易地将这些被“撑开”的片层彻底分离，得到单层或少层的MXene悬浮液。这一过程温和且高效，是MXene走向实际应用的关键一步。
> 4.  **问题：MXene在电池和超级电容器中表现优异，但它的储锂/储电机制是什么？是像电容器一样吸附在表面，还是像电池一样嵌入层间？**
>     
>     -   **解答**：是两者兼有，但以**插层（Intercalation）机制**为主。对于锂离子电池，原位XRD实验已经证实，充电/放电过程中，MXene的层间距会随着锂离子的嵌入和脱出而发生可逆变化，这是一种典型的**法拉第反应**。同时，MXene的高比表面积也贡献了部分**双电层电容**。对于超级电容器，MXene同样表现出强烈的插层赝电容行为，多种阳离子（如K⁺、Na⁺、Mg²⁺等）可以自发或电化学地嵌入其层间，实现高效的电荷存储。因此，MXene被归类为**插层型赝电容材料**，这也解释了其为何能同时拥有高能量密度和高功率密度。
> 5.  **问题：对于我这样一个刚接触MXene的新手，阅读这篇2014年的综述后，应该重点关注近十年的哪些后续进展？**

> # 1. Introduction
> 
> # 1. 引言
> 
> Two-dimensional (2D) solids—defined as crystals with very high aspect ratios and thicknesses corresponding to a few atomic layers—have garnered tremendous interest recently. By far the most studied is graphene, which is comprised of atomically thin layers of sp2-bonded carbon atoms connected by aromatic in-plane bonds. Since graphene’s outstanding electronic properties were discovered by Novoselov, Geim et al.,[1] other 2D materials, such as hexagonal boron nitrides,[2] transition metal dichalcogenides (TMDs),[3] metal oxides, and hydroxides, have attracted much renewed attention.[4] Recently, the constellation of 2D materials has been augmented by a new, and potentially quite large, group of early transition metal carbides and/or carbonitrides labeled MXenes. These are produced by the etching out of the A layers from MAX phases.[5–7] The latter are so-called because of their composition: namely, Mn+1AXn, where M is an early transition metal, A is mainly a group IIIA or IVA (i.e., groups 13 or 14) element, X is C and/or N, and n = 1, 2, or 3. Currently more than 60 different pure MAX phases are known.[8] However, given that the MAX phases can also be synthesized with different combinations, or solid solutions, of M atoms, such as (Ti0.5,Nb0.5)2AlC,[9] A atoms, such as Ti3(Al0.5,Si0.5)C2,[10] and in the X sites such as Ti2Al(C0.5,N0.5),[11] their potential number is quite large indeed.
> 
> 二维（2D）固体——定义为具有极高纵横比且厚度仅为几个原子层的晶体——最近引起了极大的兴趣。迄今为止研究最多的是石墨烯，它由原子级厚度的sp2键合碳原子层组成，并通过芳香族面内键连接。自从Novoselov、Geim等人发现石墨烯出色的电子特性以来，[1] 其他二维材料，如六方氮化硼、[2] 过渡金属二硫属化物（TMDs）、[3] 金属氧化物和氢氧化物，也重新获得了广泛关注。[4] 最近，二维材料家族又增添了一个新的、且可能非常庞大的成员——早期过渡金属碳化物和/或碳氮化物，被标记为MXenes。它们是通过从MAX相中刻蚀掉A层而制备的。[5–7] 后者因其组成而得名：即Mn+1AXn，其中M是早期过渡金属，A主要是IIIA族或IVA族（即13或14族）元素，X是C和/或N，n = 1, 2, 或3。目前已知超过60种不同的纯MAX相。[8] 然而，考虑到MAX相还可以通过不同组合或固溶体形式合成，例如M原子如(Ti0.5,Nb0.5)2AlC，[9] A原子如Ti3(Al0.5,Si0.5)C2，[10] 以及X位点如Ti2Al(C0.5,N0.5)，[11] 它们的潜在数量确实非常庞大。
> 
> **Graphene**: 石墨烯，一种由单层碳原子以sp2杂化轨道组成六角型呈蜂巢晶格的二维碳纳米材料。 **Hexagonal boron nitrides**: 六方氮化硼，一种结构与石墨类似的二维材料，由交替的硼和氮原子组成。 **Transition metal dichalcogenides (TMDs)**: 过渡金属二硫属化物，一类具有MX2化学式的层状材料，其中M是过渡金属原子，X是硫族元素。 **Solid solutions**: 固溶体，指一种或多种溶质原子溶解在溶剂晶格中形成的均匀单相固体。
> 
> All known MAX phases are layered hexagonal with P63/mmc symmetry, where the M layers are nearly closed packed, and the X atoms fill the octahedral sites. The Mn+1Xn layers are, in turn, interleaved with layers of A atoms.[12] In other words, the MAX phase structure can be described as 2D layers of early transition metal carbides and/or nitrides “glued” together with an A element (Figure 1). The strong M–X bond has a mixed covalent/metallic/ ionic character, whereas the M–A bond is metallic.[13] So, in contrast to other layered materials, such as graphite and TMDs,[2] where weak van der Waals interactions hold the structure together, the bonds between the layers in the MAX phases are too strong to be broken by shear or any similar mechanical means. However, as discussed here, by taking advantage of the differences in character and relative strengths of the M–A compared with the M–X bonds, the A layers can be selectively etched by chemical means without disrupting the M–X bonds. Because the M–A bonds are weaker than the M–X bonds, heating of MAX phases under vacuum,[14] in molten salts,[15,16] or in certain molten metals[17] at high temperatures results in the selective loss of the A element. However, because of the elevated temperature needed, de-twinning of the Mn+1Xn layers takes place which results in formation of a 3D Mn+1Xn rock salt structure.[16,18] On the other hand, the use of strong etchants, such as Cl2 gas, at temperatures above 200 °C results in the etching of both the A and M atoms, to yield carbide derived carbons (CDC).[19,20] Similarly, reaction of Ti2AlC with anhydrous hydrofluoric acid (HF) at 55 °C resulted in the formation of a new, ternary metal fluoride phase, Ti2AlF9.[21] It follows that, in order to selectively etch the A element, while preserving the 2D nature of the Mn+1Xn layers, a delicate balance between temperature and the activity of the etchant needs to be maintained. In 2011 we reported, in Advanced Materials, on the selective etching of Al from Ti3AlC2 using aqueous HF at room temperature (RT).[5] In this process, the Al atoms are replaced by O, OH and/or F atoms. The removal of the Al layers dramatically weakens the interactions between the Mn+1Xn layers that, in turn, allows them to be readily separated. We labeled these new materials MXenes, to emphasize the loss of the A element from the MAX parent phase and to highlight their 2D nature, which is similar to graphene. Today, the MXene family includes Ti3C2, Ti2C, Nb2C, V2C, (Ti0.5,Nb0.5)2C, (V0.5,Cr0.5)3C2, Ti3CN, and Ta4C3.[6,7] Because the n values for the existing Mn+1AXn phases can vary from 1 to 3, the corresponding single MXene sheets consist of 3, 5 or 7 atomic layers for M2X, M3X2 and M4X3, respectively, (see Figure 1). In all cases, the individual MXene layer thicknesses are less than 1 nm, while their lateral dimensions can reach tens of microns.
> 
> 所有已知的MAX相都是层状六方结构，具有P63/mmc对称性，其中M层近似密堆积，X原子填充八面体间隙。Mn+1Xn层又与A原子层交错排列。[12] 换句话说，MAX相结构可以描述为早期过渡金属碳化物和/或氮化物的二维层被A元素“粘合”在一起（图1）。强的M–X键具有混合的共价/金属/离子特性，而M–A键是金属键。[13] 因此，与其他层状材料（如石墨和TMDs）不同，[2] 后者依靠弱的范德华相互作用保持结构完整，而MAX相层间的键合太强，无法通过剪切或任何类似的机械方式破坏。然而，如本文所述，利用M–A键与M–X键在特性和相对强度上的差异，可以通过化学方法选择性地刻蚀掉A层，同时不破坏M–X键。由于M–A键比M–X键弱，在真空中、[14] 熔盐中、[15,16] 或某些高温熔融金属中[17] 加热MAX相会导致A元素的选择性损失。然而，由于所需的高温，Mn+1Xn层会发生解孪晶，导致形成三维Mn+1Xn岩盐结构。[16,18] 另一方面，在200°C以上的温度下使用强刻蚀剂，如Cl2气体，会导致A和M原子同时被刻蚀，生成碳化物衍生碳（CDC）。[19,20] 类似地，Ti2AlC与无水氢氟酸（HF）在55°C下反应，会生成一种新的三元金属氟化物相Ti2AlF9。[21] 因此，为了选择性地刻蚀A元素，同时保持Mn+1Xn层的二维特性，需要在温度和刻蚀剂活性之间保持微妙的平衡。2011年，我们在《Advanced Materials》上报道了在室温（RT）下使用含水HF从Ti3AlC2中选择性刻蚀Al的方法。[5] 在这个过程里，Al原子被O、OH和/或F原子替代。Al层的去除极大地削弱了Mn+1Xn层之间的相互作用，从而使它们易于分离。我们把这些新材料标记为MXenes，以强调从MAX母相中损失了A元素，并突出其类似于石墨烯的二维特性。如今，MXene家族包括Ti3C2、Ti2C、Nb2C、V2C、(Ti0.5,Nb0.5)2C、(V0.5,Cr0.5)3C2、Ti3CN和Ta4C3。[6,7] 由于现有Mn+1AXn相的n值可以从1变化到3，对应的单层MXene片分别由3、5或7个原子层组成，分别对应M2X、M3X2和M4X3，见图1。在所有情况下，单个MXene层的厚度小于1纳米，而其横向尺寸可达数十微米。
> 
> **Van der Waals interactions**: 范德华相互作用，分子或原子间存在的较弱的、非共价的相互作用力。 **Carbide derived carbons (CDC)**: 碳化物衍生碳，一种通过选择性刻蚀金属碳化物中的金属原子而得到的多孔碳材料。 **Hydrofluoric acid (HF)**: 氢氟酸，一种强腐蚀性酸，在此用作刻蚀剂。 **Aqueous**: 含水的，指以水为溶剂的溶液。
> 
> With the increased attention to 2D materials beyond graphene, and with MXenes representing a new large family extending the world of 2D materials,[22–24] it is timely to have a progress report on the state of MXene research, covering experimental and theoretical studies related to their synthesis, structure, properties, and potential applications. We also provide an outlook of future directions for research of these new and exciting 2D materials.
> 
> 随着对石墨烯以外二维材料的关注度日益增加，以及MXenes作为一个扩展二维材料世界的新的大型家族出现，[22–24] 现在正是对MXene研究现状进行进展报告的好时机，内容涵盖其合成、结构、性质和潜在应用的实验和理论研究。本文总结了MXene研究的当前进展，并概述了实验和理论方面的突出挑战。我们还为这些新颖且令人兴奋的二维材料的未来研究方向提供了展望。

> # 2. Synthesis
> 
> # 2. 合成
> 
> As noted above, MXene synthesis is achieved by selective etching of the A element layers from the MAX phases at room temperature. In this process (Figure 2), the MAX phase powder is stirred in aqueous HF, of a specific concentration, for a given time followed by centrifugation and/or filtration of the mixture to separate the solid from the supernatant with subsequent washing of the solid with deionized water (DI) until the pH of the suspension reaches values of between 4 and 6. As a result of this treatment, solid dense MAX particles (not shown) are converted to a loosely packed accordion-like structure (Figure 3a) resembling exfoliated graphite.[25] Following the convention used in the graphene/nanotube literature, we refer to these loosely packed, stacked particles as multilayer, or ML–MXenes. When the number of stacked layers is less than 5, they will be referred to as few-layer MXenes (FL–MXene). Given that various surface terminations—the exact chemistries of which are still being explored—are possible (see below), a general labeling scheme is needed. Here we denote these surfaces with the general formula: Mn+1XnTx, where T stands for surface-terminating functional groups (OH, F, O, H, etc.). If a MAX phase is fully transformed to MXene, all but the (000l) peaks in the X-ray diffraction (XRD) patterns will weaken or vanish, especially in the case of the thinner M2X structures. Furthermore, the (000l) peaks should not only broaden, but downshift to lower angles, an indication of a larger c lattice parameter. If registry along the [0001] direction is lost (see below) then no XRD peaks are expected. Typical results for Nb2AlC are shown in Figure 3b, where indeed only (000l) peaks are present after etching. It is important to note that the diffractograms shown in Figure 3b are obtained on samples that were cold pressed to 450 MPa, a procedure that greatly enhances the intensity of the (000l) peaks. Also noteworthy is that in case of incomplete conversion, MAX phase peaks coexist with the MXene (000l) peaks. Along the same lines, and primarily because XRD peak intensities tend to fade with increasing degree of exfoliation (decreasing number of layers in the MXene lamellas), XRD alone cannot be used to quantify the fraction of unreacted MAX phase in a sample. Instead, energy-dispersive spectroscopy (EDS) is used to quantify the A:M atomic ratio. In a fully converted sample, this ratio would be negligible. However, this method tends to overestimate the MAX phase concentration because, in addition to its presence in the MAX phase, the A element could also be present in the MXene samples in the form of A-element-containing salts, if the etching products are not completely removed during washing. For example, the presence of aluminum fluoride after HF treatment of Ti3AlC2 was confirmed using X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS).[5,26]
> 
> 如上所述，MXene的合成是通过在室温下选择性刻蚀MAX相中的A元素层来实现的。在此过程中（图2），将MAX相粉末在特定浓度的含水HF中搅拌指定时间，随后通过离心和/或过滤混合物将固体与上清液分离，然后用去离子水（DI）洗涤固体，直至悬浮液的pH值达到4到6之间。作为这种处理的结果，致密的固体MAX颗粒（未显示）转变为松散堆积的手风琴状结构（图3a），类似于膨胀石墨。[25] 按照石墨烯/纳米管文献中使用的惯例，我们将这些松散堆积的堆叠颗粒称为多层MXenes，即ML–MXenes。当堆叠层数少于5时，它们将被称为少层MXenes（FL–MXene）。考虑到可能存在各种表面封端——其确切化学成分仍在探索中——（见下文），需要一个通用的标记方案。在此，我们用通式Mn+1XnTx表示这些表面，其中T代表表面封端官能团（OH、F、O、H等）。如果MAX相完全转化为MXene，X射线衍射（XRD）图谱中除(000l)峰外的所有峰都将减弱或消失，尤其是在较薄的M2X结构中。此外，(000l)峰不仅应该变宽，而且会向低角度方向移动，这表明c晶格参数变大。如果沿[0001]方向的堆垛有序性丧失（见下文），则预计不会出现XRD峰。Nb2AlC的典型结果如图3b所示，刻蚀后确实仅存在(000l)峰。需要注意的是，图3b所示的衍射图是在冷压至450 MPa的样品上获得的，该过程极大地增强了(000l)峰的强度。同样值得注意的是，在转化不完全的情况下，MAX相峰与MXene (000l)峰共存。同理，主要由于XRD峰强度倾向于随着剥离程度的增加（MXene薄片中层数减少）而减弱，XRD不能单独用于量化样品中未反应MAX相的分数。相反，使用能量色散谱（EDS）来量化A:M原子比。在完全转化的样品中，这个比率可以忽略不计。然而，这种方法倾向于高估MAX相浓度，因为除了存在于MAX相中之外，如果刻蚀产物在洗涤过程中未完全去除，A元素也可能以含A元素盐的形式存在于MXene样品中。例如，通过X射线光电子能谱（XPS）证实了Ti3AlC2经HF处理后存在氟化铝。[5,26]
> 
> **Supernatant**: 上清液，指沉淀或离心后位于固体上方的澄清液体。 **pH**: 氢离子浓度指数，衡量溶液酸碱性的指标。 **Exfoliated graphite**: 膨胀石墨，一种通过插层和加热等方法使石墨层间距显著增大的石墨衍生物。 **c lattice parameter**: c晶格参数，在六方晶系中，通常指垂直于基面的晶胞尺寸。 **X-ray diffraction (XRD)**: X射线衍射，一种通过X射线在晶体中的衍射现象来分析材料晶体结构的技术。 **Energy-dispersive spectroscopy (EDS)**: 能量色散谱，一种用于元素分析的技术，常与电子显微镜联用。 **X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS)**: X射线光电子能谱，一种表面敏感的定量光谱技术，用于测量材料中存在的元素组成、经验式、化学状态和电子状态。

> Table 1 summarizes the HF etching conditions needed to synthesize various MXenes, along with their c-lattice parameters and the c lattice parameters of their corresponding MAX phases. The MXene yield—defined here as the weight of powders after HF treatment divided by the weight of powders before HF treatment ×100—varied between 60–100%. Note that the atomic weight of Al is close to the combined weight of atoms in (OH)2. If the two are interchanged and if the resulting MXenes do not dissolve in the etchant, little weight loss is expected, as observed. The assumption of replacing each Al atom by two surface groups is reasonable, as one Al layer glues two Mn+1Xn layers in the MAX phases (each Al layer is shared by two Mn+1Xn layers) so after etching the Al, surface groups terminate the surface of each MXene layer. The etching times and HF concentrations needed to fully convert a given MAX powder depend primarily on its particle size, time, temperature, and HF concentration.[6,27] Tuning the etching conditions is important for achieving high yields and the complete conversion of MAX into MXene. Prolonged etching can result in the formation of defects, such as the holes observed in Figure 3c in Ta4C3Tx.[6] Reducing the V2AlC particle size by attrition milling reduces the etching time for complete conversion from 90 h to 8 h.[7] Although all of the MAX phases listed in Table 1 contain Al as an A element, the etching conditions varied widely, a fact that in part reflects the different M–Al bond energies in the different MAX phases. For example, the Ti–Al and Nb–Al bond energies in Ti2AlC and Nb2AlC have been estimated to be 0.98 eV and 1.21 eV, respectively.[29] This difference, in turn, can explain the experimental finding that the etching of Al from Nb2AlC requires longer times and higher HF concentrations than from Ti2AlC (see Table 1). Another important variable is the value of n for a given Mn+1AlCn phase. In general the higher the n, the more stable the MXene. For example, immersing Ti2AlC powders in 50% HF—the same conditions that yields Ti3C2—resulted in their complete dissolution. It is only by reducing the HF concentration from 50% to 10% that Ti2C was obtained from Ti2AlC.[6] To date, all attempts to produce nitride-based MXenes, such as Ti2N or Ti4N3, have failed. By contrast, it is possible to selectively etch the Al from Ti3AlCN to produce Ti3CN (see Table 1). Note that the calculated cohesive energies of Tin+1Nn are less than those of Tin+1Cn, whereas the formation energies of Tin+1Nn from Tin+1AlNn are higher than those of Tin+1Cn from Tin+1AlCn.[30] The lower cohesion energy implies lower stability of the structure, whereas the higher formation energy of the MXenes from their corresponding Al containing MAX phases implies that the Al atoms are bonded more strongly in Tin+1AlNn compared to Tin+1AlCn and thus require more energy for their extraction. These two factors may explain why nitride MXenes have to date not been produced. Another distinct possibility is that the Tin+1Nn layers dissolve in the HF solution due to their lower stability.
> 
> 表1总结了合成各种MXenes所需的HF刻蚀条件，以及它们的c晶格参数和对应MAX相的c晶格参数。MXene的产率——此处定义为HF处理后粉末重量除以HF处理前粉末重量×100——在60%至100%之间变化。请注意，Al的原子量接近于(OH)2中原子的总重量。如果两者互换，并且生成的MXenes不溶于刻蚀剂，则正如观察到的，预计重量损失很小。用两个表面基团替代每个Al原子的假设是合理的，因为在MAX相中，一层Al原子粘合两层Mn+1Xn层（每层Al由两层Mn+1Xn层共享），因此在刻蚀掉Al之后，表面基团终止于每个MXene层的表面。完全转化给定MAX粉末所需的刻蚀时间和HF浓度主要取决于其颗粒大小、时间、温度和HF浓度。[6,27] 调节刻蚀条件对于实现高产率和MAX向MXene的完全转化至关重要。长时间刻蚀可能导致缺陷的形成，例如在图3c中观察到的Ta4C3Tx中的孔洞。[6] 通过磨碎研磨减小V2AlC颗粒尺寸，将完全转化所需的刻蚀时间从90小时缩短到8小时。[7] 尽管表1中列出的所有MAX相都含有Al作为A元素，但刻蚀条件差异很大，这一事实部分反映了不同MAX相中不同的M–Al键能。例如，Ti2AlC和Nb2AlC中的Ti–Al和Nb–Al键能分别估计为0.98 eV和1.21 eV。[29] 这种差异反过来可以解释实验发现，即从Nb2AlC中刻蚀Al比从Ti2AlC中刻蚀Al需要更长的时间和更高的HF浓度（见表1）。另一个重要的变量是给定Mn+1AlCn相中的n值。一般来说，n越高，MXene越稳定。例如，将Ti2AlC粉末浸入50% HF中——与生成Ti3C2的条件相同——导致其完全溶解。只有将HF浓度从50%降低到10%时，才从Ti2AlC获得了Ti2C。[6] 迄今为止，所有制造氮化物基MXenes（如Ti2N或Ti4N3）的尝试都失败了。相比之下，可以成功地从Ti3AlCN中选择性刻蚀Al以生成Ti3CN（见表1）。注意，计算得到的Tin+1Nn的内聚能低于Tin+1Cn，而从Tin+1AlNn形成Tin+1Nn的生成能高于从Tin+1AlCn形成Tin+1Cn的生成能。[30] 较低的内聚能意味着结构稳定性较低，而从相应的含Al MAX相形成MXenes的生成能较高，意味着Al原子在Tin+1AlNn中的键合比在Tin+1AlCn中更强，因此需要更多的能量来提取它们。这两个因素可以解释为什么氮化物MXenes至今尚未被制备出来。另一个明显的可能性是，Tin+1Nn层由于其较低的稳定性而溶解在HF溶液中。
> 
> **Attrition milling**: 磨碎研磨，一种通过研磨介质冲击和摩擦来减小颗粒尺寸的机械过程。 **Cohesive energy**: 内聚能，将固体分解成其组成原子所需的能量，衡量材料稳定性。 **Formation energy**: 生成能，指由元素或化合物形成一种化合物时释放或吸收的能量。

> The replacement of the strong Al–M bonds by weaker hydrogen or van der Waals bonds allows for the facile delamination of MXene. This is best seen in Figure 3c–g, in which various delaminated MXene layers are imaged in a transmission electron microscope (TEM) and under an optical microscope (OM) (Figure 3h). To obtain the delaminated MXenes, the HF treated powders are ultrasonicated in isopropyl alcohol or methanol. We note in passing that this technique results in small yields of delaminated flakes.[5–7] An intercalation approach that dramatically increases the yield is discussed below.[26] The delaminated layers were found to be transparent not only to the electron beam in TEM (Figure 3c, d) but also to visible light (Figure 3h).[6] Selected area electron diffraction (SAED) of delaminated MXene (inset in Figure 3g) imaged along [0001] clearly show that the atomic arrangement in the basal planes is identical to that in the parent MAX phase. These results provide further compelling evidence for the 3D to 2D conversion of the material. Furthermore, no evidence for carbide amorphization was observed in the TEM. It is important to note here that the Ti3C2 sheets are significantly more stable than graphene sheets under a 200 kV electron beam in the TEM.[31,32] Figure 3e shows a cross-sectional TEM micrograph of two MXene layers, whereas Figure 3f shows their corresponding atomistic model.[5] During ultrasonication, some of the delaminated layers form scrolls with inner radii of <20 nm (Figure 4a–c).[5] Similar scrolls were also reported for graphene after ultrasonication.[33,34] In addition to scrolls, MXene nanotubes were also predicted to be stable and to become more stable as their radii increase owing to reduced strain.[35] In contrast to Tin+1Cn(OH)2 MXene sheets, which can be semiconducting (see below), the seamless MXene nanotubes, formed from either Tin+1Cn or Tin+1Cn(OH)2 are predicted to have metallic character. The synthesis of seamless MXene nanotubes remains to be demonstrated.
> 
> 用较弱的氢键或范德华键替代强的Al–M键，使得MXene的剥离变得容易。这在图3c–g中可以清楚地看到，图中展示了在透射电子显微镜（TEM）和光学显微镜（OM）下成像的各种剥离的MXene层（图3h）。为了获得剥离的MXenes，将HF处理过的粉末在异丙醇或甲醇中超声处理。我们顺便指出，这种技术只能得到少量剥离薄片。[5–7] 下文将讨论一种能够显著提高产率的插层方法。[26] 发现剥离的层不仅对TEM中的电子束透明（图3c, d），而且对可见光透明（图3h）。[6] 沿[0001]方向成像的剥离MXene的选区电子衍射（SAED）（图3g插图）清晰地显示，基面上的原子排列与母体MAX相中的相同。这些结果为材料的三维到二维的转化提供了进一步的令人信服的证据。此外，在TEM中没有观察到碳化物非晶化的证据。这里需要着重指出的是，在TEM中200 kV电子束照射下，Ti3C2片比石墨烯片显著更稳定。[31,32] 图3e展示了两层MXene层的横截面TEM显微照片，而图3f展示了它们对应的原子模型。[5] 在超声处理过程中，一些剥离的层形成内半径小于20纳米的卷轴（图4a–c）。[5] 石墨烯在超声处理后也有类似的卷轴报道。[33,34] 除了卷轴，MXene纳米管也被预测是稳定的，并且由于应变减小，随着其半径增大而变得更加稳定。[35] 与可以是半导体性质的Tin+1Cn(OH)2 MXene片（见下文）相比，由Tin+1Cn或Tin+1Cn(OH)2形成的无缝MXene纳米管被预测具有金属特性。无缝MXene纳米管的合成仍有待证明。

> **Transmission electron microscope (TEM)**: 透射电子显微镜，一种利用透射电子束观察材料超微结构的显微镜技术。 **Optical microscope (OM)**: 光学显微镜，利用可见光成像的显微镜。 **Selected area electron diffraction (SAED)**: 选区电子衍射，一种在TEM中用于分析晶体结构的技术。 **Basal planes**: 基面，在层状材料中，指垂直于c轴的原子平面。 **Amorphization**: 非晶化，指材料从有序的晶体结构转变为无序的非晶结构的过程。

> Very recently, Zhang et al.[36] reported on the exfoliation of Ti3Si0.75Al0.25C2 by the aid of ultrasonication in various solvents. They suggest that when the A layer is composed of different atoms instead of single elements, breaking the bonds between the carbide layers becomes easier. The resulting ultrathin sheets had thicknesses of ≈4 nm and lateral dimension of 100–200 nm. The resulting sheets had the same composition as the parent MAX phase, and the yields were quite low. It was found that the above approach worked only for doped MAX phase; pure Ti3SiC2 could not be exfoliated by this technique.
> 
> 最近，Zhang等人[36] 报道了通过在多种溶剂中辅助超声处理来剥离Ti3Si0.75Al0.25C2。他们认为，当A层由不同原子而非单一元素组成时，破坏碳化物层之间的键合变得更容易。得到的超薄片厚度约为4纳米，横向尺寸为100–200纳米。所得薄片具有与母体MAX相相同的组成，且产率相当低。研究发现，上述方法仅适用于掺杂的MAX相；纯Ti3SiC2无法通过此技术剥离。

> # 3. Structure
> 
> # 3. 结构
> 
> From the outset, modeling has played a crucial part in understanding the structure and properties of MXenes. In fact, the first structure of ML–MXenes—namely, stacked OH-terminated Ti3C2 layers—was proposed on the basis of a density functional theory (DFT) simulation.[5] The calculated c parameter from the XRD pattern of the geometry-optimized structure of the fully hydroxylated MXene was a close match with the experimental XRD results, despite the fact that a mixture of hydroxyl and fluorine terminations could not be ruled out.[5] The real situation may be even more complex with incomplete or mixed F, OH, and O terminations present (the presence of both OH and O was confirmed experimentally using XPS).[5,26] Further complicating the situation is the high probability that water molecules can be present in the interlayer space,[7] especially in the case of V2C and Nb2C, in which the c values after etching are quite large indeed (see Table 1).
> 
> 从一开始，建模在理解MXenes的结构和性质方面就发挥了关键作用。事实上，第一个ML–MXenes的结构——即堆叠的OH封端Ti3C2层——是基于密度泛函理论（DFT）模拟提出的。[5] 根据完全羟基化MXene的几何优化结构的XRD图谱计算出的c参数，与实验XRD结果非常匹配，尽管不能排除羟基和氟混合封端的存在。[5] 实际情况可能更为复杂，存在不完全或混合的F、OH和O封端（OH和O的存在均已通过XPS实验证实）。[5,26] 使情况更复杂的是，水分子极有可能存在于层间空间，[7] 特别是对于V2C和Nb2C，其刻蚀后的c值确实非常大（见表1）。
> 
> **Density functional theory (DFT)**: 密度泛函理论，一种用于研究多电子体系电子结构的量子力学计算方法。
> 
> Soon after their discovery, DFT studies[35,37] found two energetically favorable orientations for T in Ti3C2T2, resulting in two distinct configurations: I and II (Figure 5). In configuration I, the T groups are located above the hollow sites between three neighboring C atoms or, said differently, the T groups point directly toward the Ti(2) atoms on both sides of the Ti3C2 layers. In configuration II, the T groups are positioned above the C atoms on both sides of the Ti3C2 layers. A mixed structure, where one side of the sheet is in configuration I, and the opposing side is in configuration II, was also considered and referred to as configuration III. Different authors have used different notations to distinguish between these configurations. For example, a configuration labeled I in one study was labeled “model II” or “conformation A” in another, and so on. Throughout this progress report, we rename the configurations explored in the different studies to be consistent with those shown in Figure 5. The structural stabilities of different Ti3C2F2 and Ti3C2(OH)2 configurations—estimated by comparing their relative DFT total energies—were found to decrease in the order I > III > II. This suggests that both F and OH groups tend to adopt configuration I. The lowest structural stability of configuration II is ascribed to steric repulsion between T groups and the underlying C atoms.[37] Another possible configuration, where the T terminations are connected to, and positioned just above the Ti(1) atoms of Ti3C2, was shown to be unstable and often transformed into configurations I–III during geometry optimization and will therefore, not be discussed further.[37]
> 
> 在其被发现后不久，DFT研究[35,37] 发现了Ti3C2T2中T的两种能量有利的取向，形成了两种不同的构型：I和II（图5）。在构型I中，T基团位于三个相邻C原子之间的空心位点上方，或者换句话说，T基团直接指向Ti3C2层两侧的Ti(2)原子。在构型II中，T基团位于Ti3C2层两侧的C原子正上方。还考虑了一种混合结构，即层片一侧为构型I，另一侧为构型II，被称为构型III。不同作者使用了不同的符号来区分这些构型。例如，一项研究中标记为I的构型，在另一项研究中可能被标记为“模型II”或“构象A”等等。在本进展报告中，我们重新命名了不同研究中探讨的构型，以使其与图5中所示的保持一致。通过比较不同Ti3C2F2和Ti3C2(OH)2构型的相对DFT总能量，发现其结构稳定性按I > III > II的顺序降低。这表明F和OH基团都倾向于采取构型I。构型II的结构稳定性最低，归因于T基团与下方C原子之间的空间位阻排斥。[37] 另一种可能的构型，即T封端连接到Ti3C2的Ti(1)原子并位于其正上方，被证明是不稳定的，在几何优化过程中常常转变为构型I–III，因此将不再进一步讨论。[37]
> 
> **Steric repulsion**: 空间位阻排斥，指分子中原子或基团因体积效应而相互排斥，影响分子构型。 **Geometry optimization**: 几何优化，理论计算中寻找能量最低的分子或晶体结构的过程。
> 
> It is necessary to emphasize again that MXenes produced to date may have mixed functional groups (such as OH, O and F) on their surfaces that may affect their configurations. However, no MXenes models with mixed surface functional groups have been published to date. These configurations, and the order of their stability, for a broader range of T groups, including H, O, OH, and F, have been confirmed in other DFT studies for many MXenes with different n values and elemental compositions,[38,39] including solid solutions (carbonitrides).[40] The relative total energy differences between configuration I, and either II or III, were found to not be very sensitive to the layer thicknesses, with the largest differences observed in case of Mn+1XnO2 compared with Mn+1XnH2, Mn+1XnF2, and Mn+1Xn(OH)2 for X = C or N. Moreover, the energy differences for I–III Mn+1NnT2 are smaller than for I–III Mn+1CnT2.[39] The important question of the thermodynamic stability of fully terminated compared to partially terminated structures (that is, Mn+1XnTx, 0 < x < 2) or mixed structures with different Ts remains open. Thus far, the only study addressing this question[38] shows that in a wide range of oxygen gas chemical potentials between –4 and 0 eV, the fully oxygen terminated Ti2CO2 structure is thermodynamically the most favorable configuration compared with other configurations with lower oxygen contents. Phonon dispersion curves calculated in the same paper for Ti2CO2 have no imaginary frequencies, again signifying that this structure is stable.
> 
> 有必要再次强调，迄今为止生产的MXenes表面可能具有混合的官能团（如OH、O和F），这可能影响其构型。然而，迄今为止尚未发表具有混合表面官能团的MXene模型。对于更广泛的T基团，包括H、O、OH和F，这些构型及其稳定性顺序已在其他DFT研究中得到证实，研究对象包括具有不同n值和元素组成的多种MXenes，[38,39] 包括固溶体（碳氮化物）。[40] 发现构型I与构型II或III之间的相对总能量差对层厚度不太敏感，对于X = C或N，最大的差异出现在Mn+1XnO2与Mn+1XnH2、Mn+1XnF2和Mn+1Xn(OH)2的比较中。此外，I–III构型Mn+1NnT2的能量差小于I–III构型Mn+1CnT2。[39] 完全封端结构与部分封端结构（即Mn+1XnTx，0 < x < 2）或具有不同T的混合结构相比，其热力学稳定性这一重要问题仍未解决。迄今为止，唯一一项探讨此问题的研究[38] 表明，在–4至0 eV的宽范围氧气化学势内，完全氧封端的Ti2CO2结构在热力学上是最有利的构型，优于其他氧含量较低的构型。同一篇论文中计算出的Ti2CO2声子色散曲线没有虚频，再次表明该结构是稳定的。
> 
> **Phonon dispersion curves**: 声子色散曲线，描述晶格振动频率与波矢之间关系的曲线，是判断结构稳定性的重要工具。 **Chemical potential**: 化学势，在热力学中，指物质传递的驱动力，反映了系统组分变化的能量趋势。
> 
> Gan et al.[41] found, using both DFT and ab initio molecular dynamics (MD) calculations with the climbing image nudged elastic band method to calculate the energy barriers, that O2 adsorbs on Ti2C surface forming super- and per-oxo species, which then dissociate without any barrier, producing Ti2COx. Once saturation is achieved by forming Ti2CO2, additional O2 is repelled by the surface even at temperatures as high as 550 °C. This suggests that Ti2CO2 is stable and does not form TiO2 in oxidizing environment, in contrast to many transition metal carbide nanoparticles, such as tungsten carbide. The predicted Ti2CO2 stability in these conditions may be important for catalytic applications. It is worth noting that this study was based on perfect Ti2C surfaces (with no defects such as Ti vacancies) which could change the conclusions. In addition to OH, O, and F surface terminations, Enyashin et al.[42] found—using density-functional tight-binding (DFTB), DFT, and MD calculations—that methoxy-terminated MXenes may be stable. These findings suggest MXenes can be promising catalysts in, e.g., esterification processes.
> 
> Gan等人[41] 使用DFT和从头算分子动力学（MD）计算，结合爬坡图像微动弹性带方法计算能量势垒，发现O2吸附在Ti2C表面形成超氧和过氧物种，然后这些物种在没有任何势垒的情况下解离，生成Ti2COx。一旦形成Ti2CO2达到饱和，即使在高达550°C的温度下，额外的O2也会被表面排斥。这表明Ti2CO2是稳定的，并且在氧化环境中不会形成TiO2，这与许多过渡金属碳化物纳米颗粒（如碳化钨）形成对比。预测的Ti2CO2在这些条件下的稳定性可能对催化应用很重要。值得注意的是，这项研究基于完美的Ti2C表面（没有Ti空位等缺陷），这可能会改变结论。除了OH、O和F表面封端外，Enyashin等人[42] 使用密度泛函紧束缚（DFTB）、DFT和MD计算发现，甲氧基封端的MXenes可能稳定。这些发现表明MXenes在例如酯化过程中可能是有前景的催化剂。
> 
> **ab initio molecular dynamics (MD)**: 从头算分子动力学，一种基于第一性原理计算原子间相互作用力的分子动力学模拟方法。 **Nudged elastic band method**: 微动弹性带方法，一种用于寻找化学反应或扩散过程中最小能量路径和过渡态的计算方法。 **Density-functional tight-binding (DFTB)**: 密度泛函紧束缚方法，一种对DFT近似的、计算效率更高的量子力学方法。 **Esterification**: 酯化反应，酸与醇反应生成酯和水的化学反应。
> 
> Most, if not all, DFT calculations to date have been carried out on single, isolated MXene sheets. Experimentally, the latter are the exception rather than the rule. For the most part, the MXene flakes, similar to other 2D materials, are stacked as shown in Figure 3a. Indubitably, the stacking, and just as importantly what is in between the layers, will have a significant effect on the energetics of the system.
> 
> 迄今为止，大多数（如果不是全部）DFT计算都是在单个、孤立的MXene片上进行的。实验上，后者是例外而非规则。在大多数情况下，MXene薄片与其他二维材料类似，如图3a所示堆叠在一起。毫无疑问，堆叠方式，以及同样重要的层间物质，将对系统的能量学产生显著影响。
>

> # 4. Intercalation and Large-Scale Delamination of MXenes
> 
> # 4. MXenes的插层与大规模剥离
> 
> Intercalation is a well known phenomenon for many layered materials for which the bonds between the layers are not very strong, such as graphite[43] and clays.[44] The same is true of MXenes: the weak bonds between the Mn+1Xn layers allow for the intercalation of different species (organic, inorganic, and ionic) between the Ti3C2 layers.[26,45] In case of Ti3C2(OH)2 intercalated with hydrazine (N2H4), a comparison of the experimental and MD derived XRD patterns for different numbers of N2H4 molecules in the interlayer space of Ti3C2(OH)2 showed that the intercalated N2H4 molecules were most probably arranged in an orientation that is parallel relative to the MXene basal planes and formed a complete monolayer (see Figure 6).
> 
> 插层是许多层间键合不太强的层状材料（如石墨[43] 和粘土[44]）中众所周知的现象。MXenes也是如此：Mn+1Xn层之间的弱键允许不同物种（有机、无机和离子）插入到Ti3C2层之间。[26,45] 在用肼（N2H4）插层的Ti3C2(OH)2的情况下，比较实验和MD推导的XRD图谱，对于Ti3C2(OH)2层间空间中不同数量的N2H4分子，结果表明插层的N2H4分子很可能以平行于MXene基面的取向排列，并形成一个完整的单层（见图6）。
> 
> **Hydrazine**: 肼，一种无机化合物，化学式为N2H4，常被用作插层剂。
> 
> Here again the theoretical calculations have to be taken with a grain of salt. For example, owing to the unusual combination of elements in N2H4-intercalated Ti3C2(OH)2, including Ti, C, O, N, and H, a universal force field, which is broad in terms of included elements but not very precise, had to be used in the MD simulations.[26] Configuration III taken from Naguib et al.[5] was used for stacked Ti3C2(OH)2 in these simulations, which as we now know, is not the most stable in the case of Ti3C2(OH)2 monolayers. As noted above, however, nothing is known about the relative stabilities of configurations I to III in multilayer or stacked MXenes. In most cases, the intercalation results in an increase in the c lattice parameters values. For example, the intercalation induced changes in the c values (Δc) of Ti3C2Tx vary from 0.7 Å for sodium sulfate to 15.4 Å for dimethylsulfoxide (DMSO).[26,45] The large Δc after DMSO intercalation is due to the spontaneous co-intercalation of ambient moisture. Storing DMSO-intercalated Ti3C2Tx samples in air for 3 weeks, resulted in a doubling of the c lattice parameter over its value for Ti3C2Tx. This extraordinary increase of the interlayer spacing further weakens the bonds between the MXene layers to the extent that weak sonication of DMSO intercalated Ti3C2Tx powders in deionized (DI) water for 6 h resulted in further delamination of most of the layers, as schematically shown in Figure 7.[26] It is important to differentiate between as-produced multilayer Ti3C2Tx and delaminated single- or few-layer Ti3C2Tx. As MXenes are hydrophilic, once delaminated, they form stable, surfactant-free colloidal solutions in water (Figure 7 bottom left). To date, the only MXene that has been successfully delaminated in large quantities is Ti3C2Tx.
> 
> 这里再次需要谨慎对待理论计算。例如，由于N2H4插层的Ti3C2(OH)2中包含了Ti、C、O、N和H等不寻常的元素组合，在MD模拟中不得不使用一个涵盖元素范围广但精度不高的通用力场。[26] 在这些模拟中，使用了取自Naguib等人[5] 的构型III来表示堆叠的Ti3C2(OH)2，正如我们现在所知，对于Ti3C2(OH)2单层，这并不是最稳定的构型。然而，如上所述，对于多层或堆叠的MXenes中构型I至III的相对稳定性，目前一无所知。在大多数情况下，插层导致c晶格参数值增加。例如，插层引起的Ti3C2Tx c值变化（Δc）从硫酸钠的0.7 Å到二甲亚砜（DMSO）的15.4 Å不等。[26,45] DMSO插层后大的Δc是由于环境水分的自发共插层所致。将DMSO插层的Ti3C2Tx样品在空气中存放3周，导致c晶格参数比Ti3C2Tx的值增加了一倍。这种层间距的显著增加进一步削弱了MXene层之间的键合，以至于在去离子（DI）水中对DMSO插层的Ti3C2Tx粉末进行6小时的微弱超声处理，就导致了大部分层片的进一步剥离，如图7示意图所示。[26] 区分刚制备的多层Ti3C2Tx和剥离的单层或少层Ti3C2Tx非常重要。由于MXenes是亲水的，一旦剥离，它们就会在水中形成稳定的、无表面活性剂的胶体溶液（图7左下）。迄今为止，唯一成功大量剥离的MXene是Ti3C2Tx。
> 
> **Dimethylsulfoxide (DMSO)**: 二甲亚砜，一种含硫有机化合物，常用作溶剂和插层剂。 **Colloidal solutions**: 胶体溶液，一种分散质粒子直径在1-1000纳米之间的均匀混合物。
> 
> The possibility of intercalating MXenes with various organic molecules goes beyond delaminating MXenes on a large scale. This phenomenon will indubitably play a critical role for a range of MXene applications, from polymer reinforcements to energy storage systems (see below). Furthermore, it was found that the resistivities of cold pressed MXene discs increase by 1–2 orders of magnitude after intercalation with organic compounds.[26] Selectivity to intercalants, and changes in resistivity after intercalation, suggest that MXenes may also work as sensors for various chemicals.
> 
> 用各种有机分子插层MXenes的可能性，不仅仅局限于大规模剥离MXenes。毫无疑问，这一现象将对MXenes的广泛应用——从聚合物增强到能量存储系统（见下文）——发挥关键作用。此外，研究发现，冷压MXene圆片的电阻率在插层有机化合物后增加了1到2个数量级。[26] 对插层剂的选择性以及插层后电阻率的变化，表明MXenes也可能用作各种化学品的传感器。
> 
> **Resistivity**: 电阻率，衡量材料对电流阻碍作用的物理量。

> # 5. Properties
> 
> # 5. 性质
> 
> The electronic properties of MXenes are of special interest as they can, in principle, be tuned by changing the MXene elemental composition and/or their surface terminations. The MXenes’ band structure and electron density of states (DOSs) have been extensively studied by DFT. Bare MXene monolayers are predicted to be metallic, with a high electron density near the Fermi level.[5,30,37–39] Interestingly, the electron DOS near the Fermi level (N(Ef)) for bare individual MXene layers is higher than in their parent MAX phases. To understand these changes one needs to examine the partial electron density of states (Figure 8).[30,39,40,46] In the MAX phases, N(Ef) is dominated by M 3d orbitals. Referring to Ti2AlC (Figure 8a), it is clear that the valence states below Ef group into two sub-bands: sub-band A, which is near Ef and is made up of hybridized Ti 3d-Al 3p orbitals; and sub-band B, which is between –10 and –3 eV below Ef and is due to hybridized Ti 3d-C 2p and Ti 3d-Al 3s orbitals. In other words, sub-bands A and B give rise to the Ti-Al and Ti-C bonds, respectively. Removal of the A layers results in a redistribution of the Ti 3d states, or “dangling bonds”, from the missing Ti–Al bonds into delocalized Ti–Ti metallic-like bonding states that appear around Ef in Ti2C (Figure 8b). Thus, in MXenes, N(Ef) is 2.5–4.5 times higher than in the corresponding MAX phases for Tin+1Cn and Tin+1Nn according to Shein et al.[30]; or 1.9–3.2 times higher for Tin+1Cn and 2.8–4.8 times higher for Tin+1Nn according to Xie et al.[39], where the range of studied n was broader. The high N(Ef) values in Tin+1Xn, contributed by the Ti 3d states can lead to a magnetic instability, if the Stoner criterion I·N(Ef) > 1 (where I is Stoner exchange parameter, equal to 0.9 eV for 3d elements)[47] is satisfied, resulting in magnetic MXenes.[30,37–39,48] Magnetic MXenes can be both ferromagnetic (such as Cr2C, Cr2N,[38] or Ta3C2)[48] or antiferromagnetic (such as Ti3C2 or Ti3N2).[30] The acquired total magnetic moments per unit cell are in the range 2–3 μB for Tin+1Cn or fluctuate around 1.2 μB for Tin+1Nn as n increases from 1 to 9.[39] Although magnetism is an important property, for the most part it is only predicted for MXene with bare surfaces. When surface terminations (T, even when T = H) are present, the magnetism disappears due to the formation of p–d bonds between the M atoms and T groups, leading to a partial depopulation of the near Fermi states, which reduces N(Ef) (Ti2CO2, Ti2CF2, Ti2CH2, and Ti2C(OH)2 in Figure 8c, d and e, respectively). Prominent exceptions are Cr2C and Cr2N, which are predicted to retain significant magnetic moments in their terminated state (T = O, OH, or F) up to nearly room temperature.[38] Unfortunately, to date there have been no reports of experimentally produced Cr2XTx MXenes to test this important prediction.
> 
> MXenes的电子性质特别令人感兴趣，因为它们原则上可以通过改变MXene的元素组成和/或其表面封端来调节。MXenes的能带结构和电子态密度（DOS）已通过DFT进行了广泛研究。裸露的MXene单层被预测为金属性，在费米能级附近具有高电子密度。[5,30,37–39] 有趣的是，对于裸露的单个MXene层，其费米能级附近的电子DOS（N(Ef)）高于其母体MAX相。要理解这些变化，需要检查分波电子态密度（图8）。[30,39,40,46] 在MAX相中，N(Ef)主要由M 3d轨道贡献。以Ti2AlC为例（图8a），很明显，Ef以下的价态分为两个子带：子带A，靠近Ef，由杂化的Ti 3d-Al 3p轨道组成；子带B，位于Ef以下–10到–3 eV之间，由杂化的Ti 3d-C 2p和Ti 3d-Al 3s轨道形成。换句话说，子带A和B分别产生了Ti-Al键和Ti-C键。移除A层导致Ti 3d态，即来自缺失的Ti–Al键的“悬挂键”，重新分布为在Ti2C中Ef附近出现的离域Ti–Ti类金属键合态（图8b）。因此，根据Shein等人的研究，对于Tin+1Cn和Tin+1Nn，MXenes中的N(Ef)比相应的MAX相高2.5–4.5倍；[30] 或者根据Xie等人的研究，对于Tin+1Cn高1.9–3.2倍，对于Tin+1Nn高2.8–4.8倍，其中研究的n范围更广。[39] 如果满足Stoner判据I·N(Ef) > 1（其中I是Stoner交换参数，对于3d元素等于0.9 eV），[47] Tin+1Xn中由Ti 3d态贡献的高N(Ef)值可能导致磁不稳定性，从而产生磁性MXenes。[30,37–39,48] 磁性MXenes可以是铁磁性的（如Cr2C、Cr2N[38]或Ta3C2），[48] 也可以是反铁磁性的（如Ti3C2或Ti3N2）。[30] 每个晶胞获得的总磁矩，对于Tin+1Cn在2–3 μB范围内，或者随着n从1增加到9，对于Tin+1Nn在1.2 μB左右波动。[39] 尽管磁性是一种重要的性质，但在大多数情况下，它仅针对具有裸露表面的MXene被预测。当存在表面封端（T，即使T = H）时，由于M原子和T基团之间形成p–d键，导致近费米态部分去占据，从而降低了N(Ef)，磁性消失（分别见图8c、d和e中的Ti2CO2、Ti2CF2、Ti2CH2和Ti2C(OH)2）。突出的例外是Cr2C和Cr2N，它们被预测在其封端状态（T = O, OH, 或 F）下能保持显著的磁矩，直至接近室温。[38] 不幸的是，迄今为止还没有实验制备出Cr2XTx MXenes来验证这一重要预测。
> 
> **Fermi level**: 费米能级，在绝对零度下，电子占据的最高能级。 **Partial electron density of states**: 分波电子态密度，指将总电子态密度分解到不同原子或不同轨道角动量上的贡献。 **Dangling bonds**: 悬挂键，固体表面原子因晶格周期性中断而产生的未饱和键。 **Stoner criterion**: Stoner判据，一个用于判断金属中是否会出现铁磁性的理论标准。 **Ferromagnetic**: 铁磁性，材料中原子磁矩平行排列，表现出宏观磁性的现象。 **Antiferromagnetic**: 反铁磁性，材料中相邻原子磁矩反平行排列，宏观上不表现净磁矩的现象。
> 
> Surface terminations can influence other MXenes’ electronic properties, such as their bandgaps. In the first MXene paper,[5] it was theoretically shown that although Ti3C2 is a metallic conductor, small bandgaps of 0.05 eV and 0.1 eV open up for Ti3C2(OH)2 and Ti3C2F2, respectively. At the time, we suggested that it would be possible to tune the electronic structure of MXenes by varying T. It was further confirmed that the electronic structure of MXenes is sensitive not only to the type of surface terminations, but also to their orientation relative to the MXene sheets. In particular, Ti3C2F2 and Ti3C2(OH)2 in configurations I and III (see above) were shown to be semiconductors with narrow band gaps of 0.04 eV (I-Ti3C2F2), 0.03 eV (III-Ti3C2F2), 0.05 eV (I-Ti3C2(OH)2) and 0.07 eV (III-Ti3C2(OH)2). Interestingly, the same materials in configuration II are predicted to be metals.[37] Although most MXenes are metallic or have small bandgaps, DFT results predict that: Sc2CF2 should possess an indirect bandgap of 1.03 eV; Sc2C(OH)2 should have a direct bandgap of 0.45 eV; Sc2CO2 should have an indirect bandgap of 1.8 eV; Ti2CO2 should have an indirect band gap of 0.24 eV; Zr2CO2 should have an indirect bandgap of 0.88 eV; and Hf2CO2 should have an indirect bandgap of 1.0 eV.[38] Thus, many MXenes, especially O-terminated ones, are predicted to be semiconducting.[38]
> 
> 表面封端可以影响MXenes的其他电子性质，例如它们的带隙。在第一篇MXene论文中，[5] 理论上表明，虽然Ti3C2是金属导体，但Ti3C2(OH)2和Ti3C2F2分别打开了0.05 eV和0.1 eV的小带隙。当时，我们提出可以通过改变T来调节MXenes的电子结构。随后进一步证实，MXenes的电子结构不仅对表面封端的类型敏感，而且对它们相对于MXene片的取向也敏感。特别是，构型I和III中的Ti3C2F2和Ti3C2(OH)2（见上文）被证明是窄带隙半导体，带隙分别为0.04 eV（I-Ti3C2F2）、0.03 eV（III-Ti3C2F2）、0.05 eV（I-Ti3C2(OH)2）和0.07 eV（III-Ti3C2(OH)2）。有趣的是，构型II中的相同材料被预测为金属。[37] 尽管大多数MXenes是金属性或具有小带隙，但DFT结果预测：Sc2CF2应具有1.03 eV的间接带隙；Sc2C(OH)2应具有0.45 eV的直接带隙；Sc2CO2应具有1.8 eV的间接带隙；Ti2CO2应具有0.24 eV的间接带隙；Zr2CO2应具有0.88 eV的间接带隙；Hf2CO2应具有1.0 eV的间接带隙。[38] 因此，许多MXenes，特别是O封端的MXenes，被预测为半导体。[38]
> 
> **Bandgap**: 带隙，固体中电子占据的价带顶与未占据的导带底之间的能量范围。 **Indirect bandgap**: 间接带隙，指半导体中导带底和价带顶不在同一波矢处。 **Direct bandgap**: 直接带隙，指半导体中导带底和价带顶在同一波矢处。
> 
> To understand these changes, it is necessary again to examine the partial electron density of states (Figure 8).[30,39,40,46] In addition to sub-bands A and B, mentioned above, in surface terminated MXenes a new sub-band C, corresponding to Ti–T bonds, is formed below sub-band B, causing a shift of the gap between sub-bands A and B to lower energies and a depletion in the N(Ef). It is the latter effect that reduces the propensity for magnetism in Mn+1XnT2.[39] This is the basic mechanism through which chemical termination changes the electronic and magnetic properties of MXenes, although additional differences exist between different MXenes. For example, in Mn+1NnT2, the T contributes to both, the newly formed C subband and the existing B sub-band, in contrast to Mn+1CnT2, where T contributes only to sub-band C.[39] Note that in the carbonitrides, such as Ti3CNTx,[40] the increased electron count due to the presence of the N atoms, may outweight the withdrawal of electrons by surface groups, thus preserving their metallic character.
> 
> 要理解这些变化，有必要再次检查分波电子态密度（图8）。[30,39,40,46] 除了上述子带A和B之外，在表面封端的MXenes中，一个新的子带C，对应于Ti–T键，形成于子带B的下方，导致子带A和B之间的间隙向低能方向移动，并导致N(Ef)的耗尽。正是后者降低了Mn+1XnT2的磁性倾向。[39] 这是化学封端改变MXenes电子和磁性质的基本机制，尽管不同MXenes之间还存在其他差异。例如，在Mn+1NnT2中，T对新形成的C子带和已有的B子带都有贡献，这与Mn+1CnT2不同，后者的T仅对C子带有贡献。[39] 请注意，在碳氮化物中，如Ti3CNTx，[40] 由于N原子的存在而增加的电子计数，可能超过表面基团对电子的抽取，从而保持其金属特性。
> 
> Concluding the discussion of computational results, it should be noted that while producing stable and consistent geometries, DFT is known to have issues with predicting bandgaps. Thus, a thoughtful choice of the exchange-correlation functional must be made in order to correctly predict the differences between metals and narrow band semiconductors. A proper inclusion of interlayer van der Waals interactions is also important, as they may not only influence the geometric structures, but may also change the band structures (as has been demonstrated for other materials, for example, see Govaerts et al.[49]). In this context, a comparison of the band gaps of Ti3C2(OH)2 and Ti3C2F2 calculated using the GGA-PBE (Generalized Gradient Approximation–Perdew-Burke-Ernzerhof)[37] and HSE06 (Heyd-Scuseria-Ernzerhof) functionals is instructive.[39] According to GGA-PBE, Ti3C2(OH)2 and Ti3C2F2—in their most stable configurations I—are narrow band semiconductors with band gaps <0.1 eV.[37] However, the same MXenes, in the same configurations, are predicted to be metallic when HSE06 functional is used instead.[39] Another example is Ti3C2O2, in configuration I, for which PBE functional predicts a band gap of 0.24 eV, whereas HSE06 gives a value of 0.88 eV.[39] The HSE06 is a hybrid functional, which combines one-quarter of the exact Hartree–Fock exchange energy with three-quarters of an approximate exchange-correlation energy. This combination in general produces more reliable bandgaps than PBE.[39] Therefore, this functional should be preferred over PBE in calculations of electronic properties. In another study,[50] Wu–Cohen (WC) functional within GGA formalism was chosen, because it was deemed superior to PBE, in describing the electronic structure.
> 
> 在总结计算结果时，应该指出，虽然DFT能够产生稳定且一致的几何结构，但它在预测带隙方面存在已知的问题。因此，为了正确预测金属和窄带半导体之间的差异，必须对交换相关泛函进行深思熟虑的选择。适当地包含层间范德华相互作用也很重要，因为它们不仅可能影响几何结构，还可能改变能带结构（正如其他材料所证明的那样，例如，见Govaerts等人[49]）。在此背景下，比较使用GGA-PBE（广义梯度近似–Perdew-Burke-Ernzerhof）[37] 和HSE06（Heyd-Scuseria-Ernzerhof）泛函[39] 计算出的Ti3C2(OH)2和Ti3C2F2的带隙是很有启发性的。根据GGA-PBE，Ti3C2(OH)2和Ti3C2F2——在其最稳定的构型I中——是带隙小于0.1 eV的窄带隙半导体。[37] 然而，当使用HSE06泛函时，相同的MXenes在相同的构型下被预测为金属性。[39] 另一个例子是构型I中的Ti3C2O2，PBE泛函预测其带隙为0.24 eV，而HSE06给出的值为0.88 eV。[39] HSE06是一种杂化泛函，它将四分之一的精确Hartree–Fock交换能与四分之三的近似交换相关能相结合。这种组合通常产生比PBE更可靠的带隙。[39] 因此，在电子性质计算中，应优先选择此泛函而非PBE。在另一项研究中，[50] 选择了GGA框架内的Wu–Cohen（WC）泛函，因为它被认为在描述电子结构方面优于PBE。
> 
> **Exchange-correlation functional**: 交换相关泛函，在DFT中用于近似处理电子间交换和相关作用的数学函数。 **GGA-PBE**: 广义梯度近似的一种形式，是DFT中广泛使用的交换相关泛函。 **HSE06**: 一种杂化交换相关泛函，通过结合一部分精确交换能来提高计算精度，尤其对带隙的计算。
> 
> The importance of modeling in discovering novel and unexpected properties of MXenes is, and will remain, indispensible. Modeling can also be used for screening different MXenes, some of which have not been synthesized yet and determining which ones have attractive properties, thus guiding experimental efforts. Some of the predictions have already been confirmed experimentally. The conductivities of MXene free-standing thin discs (thickness around 300 μm)—prepared by cold pressing additive-free MXene powders at RT under a load corresponding to a stress of ≈1 GPa—were comparable to those of multilayer graphene.[51] For example, the resistivities of those discs were: 22 Ω –1 for Ti3C2Tx to 339 Ω –1 for Ti2CTx depending on their surface chemistry and n (M2X, M3X2, and M4X3). Contact angle measurements of DI water on these cold-pressed MXene discs revealed hydrophilic behavior, with contact angles in the 27 to 41 degrees range.[6] These values are comparable to oxygen terminated carbon surfaces.[52] MXenes’ 2D morphologies, combined with their metallic electrical conductivities may be beneficial for integration with other layered semiconducting materials, such as MoS2. In these hybrid systems, MXenes may be used as conductive 2D pads. They could also be used to modify the electronic properties of other 2D materials in contact with MXene in vertical hybrid heterostructures.
> 
> 建模在发现MXenes新颖和意想不到的性质方面的重要性，现在是、将来也仍然是不可或缺的。建模还可以用于筛选不同的MXenes，其中一些尚未合成，并确定哪些具有吸引人的性质，从而指导实验工作。一些预测已经得到实验证实。通过在不添加任何添加剂的条件下，于室温及约1 GPa的应力下冷压MXene粉末制备的MXene自支撑薄圆片（厚度约300微米），其电导率与多层石墨烯相当。[51] 例如，这些圆片的电阻率范围从Ti3C2Tx的22 Ω –1 到Ti2CTx的339 Ω –1，具体取决于它们的表面化学和n值（M2X、M3X2和M4X3）。在这些冷压MXene圆片上测量去离子水的接触角，显示出亲水行为，接触角在27到41度范围内。[6] 这些值与氧封端的碳表面相当。[52] MXenes的二维形态，结合其金属导电性，可能有利于与其他层状半导体材料（如MoS2）集成。在这些混合系统中，MXenes可用作导电二维垫片。它们也可用于修饰在垂直混合异质结构中与MXene接触的其他二维材料的电子性质。
> 
> **Contact angle**: 接触角，指在固、液、气三相交界处，自固-液界面经液体内部到气-液界面之间的夹角，是衡量固体表面润湿性的重要参数。 **Hydrophilic**: 亲水性，指表面与水分子有较强亲和力，容易被水润湿的特性。 **MoS2**: 二硫化钼，一种典型的过渡金属二硫属化物，具有层状结构和半导体性质。 **Heterostructures**: 异质结构，由两种或多种不同材料层组成的半导体结构。
> 
> To date, no experimental studies have been carried out in this promising direction, although recent computational results[53] predict that metallic behavior emerges in MoS2 when Ti2C is deposited on it as a consequence of strong chemical bonds formed at the MoS2/Ti2C interfaces. By contrast, the bonding on MoS2/Ti2CF2 and MoS2/Ti2C(OH)2 interfaces is non-covalent (physisorption), preserving the semiconducting nature of MoS2. The bond alignment induces weak and strong n-type doping of the MoS2 in MoS2/Ti2CF2 and MoS2/Ti2C(OH)2 with corresponding n-type Schottky barrier heights of 0.85 and 0.26 eV, respectively.[53] In another study,[38] high Seebeck coefficients were predicted for MXenes by DFT. The Seebeck coefficients ≈1000 μV K–1 predicted for semiconducting Ti2CO2 and Sc2C(OH)2 at ≈100 K are comparable to the reported giant Seebeck coefficients of SrTiO3 (850 μV K–1 at ≈90 K). This prediction opens a totally new area of potential applications for these surface terminated MXenes.
> 
> 迄今为止，在这个有前景的方向上尚未开展实验研究，尽管最近的计算结果[53] 预测，当Ti2C沉积在MoS2上时，由于在MoS2/Ti2C界面形成强化学键，MoS2会表现出金属行为。相比之下，MoS2/Ti2CF2和MoS2/Ti2C(OH)2界面上的键合是非共价的（物理吸附），保持了MoS2的半导体性质。键合对齐在MoS2/Ti2CF2和MoS2/Ti2C(OH)2中分别诱导了弱和强的n型掺杂，对应的n型肖特基势垒高度分别为0.85和0.26 eV。[53] 在另一项研究中，[38] DFT预测MXenes具有高塞贝克系数。对于半导体Ti2CO2和Sc2C(OH)2，在约100 K时预测的塞贝克系数≈1000 μV K–1，与已报道的SrTiO3的巨塞贝克系数（在约90 K时为850 μV K–1）相当。这一预测为这些表面封端的MXenes开辟了一个全新的潜在应用领域。
> 
> **Physisorption**: 物理吸附，指吸附质分子通过弱的范德华力附着在固体表面的现象。 **n-type doping**: n型掺杂，通过引入施主杂质，使半导体中电子浓度增加。 **Schottky barrier**: 肖特基势垒，金属与半导体接触时，在界面处形成的具有整流作用的能量势垒。 **Seebeck coefficient**: 塞贝克系数，描述材料在温差下产生电压的物理量，是热电材料性能的关键指标。
> 
> The mechanical properties of MXenes are also of great interest as the M–C and/or M–N bonds are some of the strongest known. At this juncture the only information available on how MXenes would respond to stress, are the elastic constants when they are stretched along the basal planes, i.e., c11. An early DFT study of Ti3C2(OH)2 predicted a c11 ≈300 GPa.[5] A follow up study[50] predicted that the c11 values of different, bare, or unfunctionalized, Mn+1Cn layers would be higher than in their parent MAX phases (Table 2). This could be due to the strengthening of the M–X bonds when the A atoms are removed and the electron density is more concentrated within the Mn+1Cn layer. However, the enhanced mechanical properties could also be due to the difficulty in accurately estimating the exact thickness of a MXene layer. And although the c11 values for MXenes are lower than those of graphene, the bending rigidity, which scales as ∼t3, where t is the layer thickness, should be significantly higher than for graphene, as many MXenes consists of at least 3 atomic layers. Moreover, t and the consequent elastic rigidity anisotropies can also be readily tuned by varying n in Mn+1Xn.[50]
> 
> MXenes的力学性质也备受关注，因为M–C和/或M–N键是已知最强的化学键之一。目前，关于MXenes如何响应应力的唯一信息是它们沿基面拉伸时的弹性常数，即c11。一项早期的DFT研究预测Ti3C2(OH)2的c11 ≈300 GPa。[5] 一项后续研究[50] 预测，不同的、裸露的或未官能化的Mn+1Cn层的c11值将高于其母体MAX相（表2）。这可能是由于A原子被移除后，M–X键增强，电子密度更集中在Mn+1Cn层内。然而，增强的力学性能也可能是因为难以准确估计MXene层的精确厚度。尽管MXenes的c11值低于石墨烯，但其弯曲刚度（与t3成正比，其中t是层厚度）应该显著高于石墨烯，因为许多MXenes至少由3个原子层组成。此外，通过改变Mn+1Xn中的n值，也可以轻松调节t以及随之而来的弹性刚度各向异性。[50]
> 
> **Elastic constants**: 弹性常数，描述材料在弹性变形范围内应力与应变关系的物理量。 **Bending rigidity**: 弯曲刚度，衡量材料抵抗弯曲变形的能力。 **Anisotropy**: 各向异性，指材料在不同方向上表现出不同物理性质的现象。
>

> 
> # 6. Applications
> 
> # 6. 应用
> 
> The rich chemistries and unique morphologies of MXenes, in addition to their good electronic conductivities, render them strong candidates for many applications that range from sensors and electronic device materials to catalysts in the chemical industry, conductive reinforcement additives to polymers, electrochemical energy storage materials, among many others. For the most part many of those applications are still hypothetical. The few experimental results that exist to date have explored the use of MXenes in energy storage applications such as lithium ion batteries, LIBs, electrochemical capacitors (supercapacitors), and fuel cells.[26,45,54–56]
> 
> MXenes丰富的化学性质、独特的形态，加上良好的电子导电性，使其成为众多应用领域的有力候选者，范围从传感器和电子器件材料到化学工业中的催化剂，再到聚合物的导电增强添加剂、电化学储能材料等等。在很大程度上，这些应用中的许多仍然是假设性的。迄今为止，现有的少数实验结果探索了MXenes在储能应用中的用途，例如锂离子电池（LIBs）、电化学电容器（超级电容器）和燃料电池。[26,45,54–56]
> 
> **Lithium ion batteries (LIBs)**: 锂离子电池，一种可充电电池，依靠锂离子在正负极之间移动工作。 **Electrochemical capacitors (supercapacitors)**: 电化学电容器（超级电容器），一种介于传统电容器和电池之间的储能装置，具有高功率密度和长循环寿命。 **Fuel cells**: 燃料电池，一种将燃料的化学能直接转化为电能的电化学装置。
> 
> Early DFT calculations on Li intercalation into multilayer Ti3C2 predicted the formation of Ti3C2Li2 in Li rich environments, with a slightly positive enthalpy explained by the larger size of Li versus Al atoms.[5] The theoretical Li capacity in that case was found to be 320 mAh g–1. Soon afterwards, DFT was again used to study the adsorption and diffusion of Li on monolayers of Ti3C2, Ti3C2F2 and Ti3C2(OH)2.[37] The most favorable Li adsorption sites for all three materials, in their most stable configuration I, were the positions above the C atoms of the carbide sheets. The adsorbed Li atoms become partially or fully ionized due to charge transfer between Li and Ti3C2, I-Ti3C2F2 or I-Ti3C2(OH)2. This charge transfer suggests that the adsorbed Li strongly interacts with MXenes via coulombic interactions. The calculated Li adsorption energies on Ti3C2, I-Ti3C2F2, and I-Ti3C2(OH)2 monolayers were found to be 0.50, −0.95, −0.20 eV per Li atom, respectively. The Li diffusion rates follow the order Ti3C2 > I-Ti3C2F2 > I-Ti3C2(OH)2, thus, bare Ti3C2 monolayers should possess the highest Li transport rates. The higher Li diffusion barriers on the I-Ti3C2F2 and I-Ti3C2(OH)2 surfaces were ascribed to steric hindrances induced by the surface F and OH groups. The open circuit voltages were predicted to be 0.62, 0.56, and 0.14 V for Ti3C2Li2, I-Ti3C2F2Li and I-Ti3C2(OH)2Li0.5, respectively. The corresponding theoretical specific capacities were found to be 320, 130, and 67 mAh g–1, respectively.[37]
> 
> 早期的DFT计算预测了在富Li环境下，多层Ti3C2中Li插层会形成Ti3C2Li2，其略正的生成焓归因于Li原子比Al原子大。[5] 在这种情况下，理论Li容量为320 mAh g–1。不久之后，DFT再次被用于研究Li在Ti3C2、Ti3C2F2和Ti3C2(OH)2单层上的吸附和扩散。[37] 对于这三种材料，在其最稳定的构型I中，最有利的Li吸附位点是碳化物片中C原子正上方的位置。由于Li与Ti3C2、I-Ti3C2F2或I-Ti3C2(OH)2之间的电荷转移，吸附的Li原子变得部分或完全离子化。这种电荷转移表明，吸附的Li通过库仑相互作用与MXenes强烈作用。计算得到的Ti3C2、I-Ti3C2F2和I-Ti3C2(OH)2单层上的Li吸附能分别为每个Li原子0.50、−0.95、−0.20 eV。Li扩散速率遵循Ti3C2 > I-Ti3C2F2 > I-Ti3C2(OH)2的顺序，因此，裸露的Ti3C2单层应具有最高的Li传输速率。I-Ti3C2F2和I-Ti3C2(OH)2表面上较高的Li扩散势垒归因于表面F和OH基团引起的空间位阻。预测的开路电压对于Ti3C2Li2、I-Ti3C2F2Li和I-Ti3C2(OH)2Li0.5分别为0.62、0.56和0.14 V。相应的理论比容量分别为320、130和67 mAh g–1。[37]
> 
> **Adsorption**: 吸附，指原子、离子或分子从气体、液体或溶解固体中附着到表面上的过程。 **Diffusion**: 扩散，指原子、离子或分子因热运动从高浓度区域向低浓度区域迁移的过程。 **Coulombic interactions**: 库仑相互作用，带电粒子之间的静电相互作用。 **Open circuit voltage**: 开路电压，在无电流流动时，电池正负极之间的电位差。 **Specific capacity**: 比容量，单位质量或体积的活性物质所能储存的电量。
> 
> All in all, the authors[37] conclude that bare 2D Ti3C2 monolayers would be better anode materials for LIBs than TiO2 due to their enhanced electronic conductivity (metallic character), smaller open circuit voltage, and improved Li storage capacity. Also, the predicted diffusion barrier (0.07 eV) for an isolated Li atom on a Ti3C2 surface was much lower than that in anatase TiO2 (0.35−0.65 eV) or graphite (≈0.3 eV), meaning that Ti3C2 should sustain higher charge/discharge rates than these materials, rendering it promising for high power batteries. However, as noted above, all MXenes produced to date are terminated with surface groups, which may affect their performance as LIBs anodes. It follows that modeling will probably continue to have a pivotal role in identifying optimal MXene compositions and surface terminations for ion intercalation, as well as in uncovering new important details of the mechanisms of these processes.
> 
> 总而言之，作者[37] 得出结论，裸露的二维Ti3C2单层将是比TiO2更好的LIBs负极材料，因为其具有增强的电子导电性（金属特性）、更小的开路电压和更高的Li存储容量。此外，预测的孤立Li原子在Ti3C2表面的扩散势垒（0.07 eV）远低于锐钛矿TiO2（0.35−0.65 eV）或石墨（≈0.3 eV）中的值，这意味着Ti3C2应能承受比这些材料更高的充放电倍率，使其在高功率电池方面具有前景。然而，如上所述，迄今为止生产的所有MXenes都带有表面基团封端，这可能会影响其作为LIBs负极的性能。因此，建模可能在确定用于离子插层的最佳MXene组成和表面封端，以及揭示这些过程机制的重要新细节方面，继续发挥关键作用。
> 
> **Anode**: 负极，在放电过程中发生氧化反应，向外电路输出电子的电极。 **Anatase TiO2**: 锐钛矿二氧化钛，二氧化钛的一种晶型，常用作锂离子电池负极材料。
> 
> Several MXenes (Ti2CTx,[54] Ti3C2Tx,[26] V2CTx, and Nb2CTx[7]) were experimentally investigated as electrode materials in LIBs. Among these compounds, in non-delaminated forms, V2CTx showed the highest capacity (280 mAhg−1 at a cycling rate of 1 C and 125 mAhg−1 at 10 C). Although Nb atoms are heavier than Ti, the gravimetric capacity of Nb2CTx is higher than that for Ti2CTx at the same cycling rates (180 mAhg−1 for Nb2CTx versus 110 mAhg−1 for Ti2CTx at 1C). An in situ XRD study on Ti2CTx showed that the mechanism governing lithiation and delithiation was Li intercalation and de-intercalation between the layers, respectively.[55] For a given chemistry, M2X electrodes will have higher gravimetric capacities than their M3X2 and M4X3 counterparts. For example, the gravimetric capacity of Ti2CTx was ≈1.5 times higher than that of Ti3C2Tx[26,54] for the simple reason that the former has the least number of atomic layers per MXene sheet. More recently, we showed that each MXene has its own active voltage window. For example, more than two-thirds of the reversible lithiation capacity of Nb2CTx is below 1 V; for V2CTx more than two-thirds of the reversible delithiation capacity is above 1.5 V.[7] Considering the rich chemistry of MXenes and solid solution compositions, it may in principle be possible to fine-tune and design the MXenes for specific battery applications. Thus, some MXenes could function as anodes and some could be used as cathodes for lithium ion and other batteries.
> 
> 几种MXenes（Ti2CTx、[54] Ti3C2Tx、[26] V2CTx和Nb2CTx[7]）作为LIBs中的电极材料进行了实验研究。在这些化合物中，在非剥离形式下，V2CTx显示出最高的容量（在1 C循环倍率下为280 mAhg−1，在10 C下为125 mAhg−1）。尽管Nb原子比Ti重，但在相同循环倍率下，Nb2CTx的重量比容量高于Ti2CTx（在1 C下，Nb2CTx为180 mAhg−1，而Ti2CTx为110 mAhg−1）。对Ti2CTx的原位XRD研究表明，控制锂化和脱锂的机制分别是Li在层间的插层和脱插层。[55] 对于给定的化学组成，M2X电极将具有比其M3X2和M4X3对应物更高的重量比容量。例如，Ti2CTx的重量比容量比Ti3C2Tx高约1.5倍，[26,54] 原因很简单，前者每个MXene片具有最少的原子层数。最近，我们展示了每种MXene都有其自身的活性电压窗口。例如，Nb2CTx超过三分之二的可逆锂化容量低于1 V；对于V2CTx，超过三分之二的可逆脱锂容量高于1.5 V。[7] 考虑到MXenes丰富的化学性质和固溶体组成，原则上可能可以针对特定电池应用来微调和设计MXenes。因此，一些MXenes可以用作负极，另一些可用作锂离子和其他电池的正极。
> 
> **Lithiation**: 锂化，指锂离子嵌入电极材料的过程。 **Delithiation**: 脱锂，指锂离子从电极材料中脱出的过程。 **Cathode**: 正极，在放电过程中发生还原反应，从外电路接收电子的电极。
> 
> As noted above, Ti3C2Tx can be readily delaminated, resulting in a colloidal solution of single- and few-layer Ti3C2Tx flakes, by sonicating a suspension of DMSO-intercalated Ti3C2 in DI water. Filtration of this solution yields additive-free, flexible paper that detaches readily from the anodic aluminum oxide filter membranes. This paper was in turn used to fabricate electrodes that were tested as LIB electrodes.[26] As shown in Figure 9, such an electrode yielded a reversible capacity of 410 mAhg−1 at 1 C (≈4 times higher than the capacity of the cast Ti3C2Tx film that has binder and carbon additives), and possessed excellent ability to handle extremely high cycling rates (110 mAhg−1 at 36 C after 700 cycles).[26] Although the MXenes’ gravimetric capacities are not as high as Si,[57] they have the great advantage of combining high cycling rates with good capacities. The cycling rates reported are as good as, and probably better than, lithium titanium oxide (LTO) based anodes.[58] These comments notwithstanding, first cycle irreversibility is a challenging problem in all tested MXenes so far. The exact reasons for the irreversibilities are not clear but could be due to solid-electrolyte interphase (SEI) formation or irreversible reactions between Li and the MXene surface groups. This problem, in principle, can be solved by prelithiation of the MXene electrodes, similar to what was reported for other nanostructured systems.[59]
> 
> 如上所述，通过将DMSO插层的Ti3C2悬浮液在DI水中超声处理，Ti3C2Tx可以轻易剥离，形成单层和少层Ti3C2Tx薄片的胶体溶液。过滤该溶液可得到无添加剂的柔性纸，该纸易于从阳极氧化铝过滤膜上分离。这种纸又被用来制备电极，并作为LIB电极进行了测试。[26] 如图9所示，这种电极在1 C下产生了410 mAhg−1的可逆容量（大约是含有粘合剂和碳添加剂的流延Ti3C2Tx薄膜容量的4倍），并且具有出色的处理极高循环倍率的能力（在36 C下，700次循环后为110 mAhg−1）。[26] 尽管MXenes的重量比容量不如Si，[57] 但它们具有将高循环倍率与良好容量相结合的显著优势。所报道的循环倍率与基于锂钛氧化物（LTO）的负极一样好，甚至可能更好。[58] 尽管有这些评论，但首圈不可逆性是迄今为止所有测试的MXenes中一个具有挑战性的问题。导致不可逆性的确切原因尚不清楚，但可能是由于固体电解质界面（SEI）的形成，或者Li与MXene表面基团之间发生了不可逆反应。原则上，这个问题可以通过对MXene电极进行预锂化来解决，类似于其他纳米结构系统所报道的那样。[59]
> 
> **Solid-electrolyte interphase (SEI)**: 固体电解质界面，在锂离子电池首次充放电过程中，电极材料与电解液在固液相界面上发生反应形成的一层钝化层。 **Prelithiation**: 预锂化，在电池组装前，向电极材料中预先嵌入一定量锂离子的过程，用以补偿首次循环的容量损失。
> 
> All the work to date on MXene electrodes in LIBs was carried out on as-synthesized materials, with no tailoring of their surface chemistries. Thus, many opportunities for enhancing the performance and reducing first cycle irreversibility remain unexplored. For example, and as noted above, bare MXene surfaces are predicted to perform better than terminated ones in LIBs.[37] Delaminating other MXenes may increase their Li uptake, similar to what was reported for Ti3C2.[26] Thin and lightweight M2X MXenes are of special interest for this purpose. Similar to other 2D materials, further optimization and enhancement can be achieved by engineering electrode architectures using different additives.[60–62] The capability of MXenes to handle high cycling rates renders them good candidates for use in asymmetric, nonaqueous energy storage devices (hybrid cells), that combine the high energy densities characteristic of LIBs and the high power densities of electrical double layer capacitors (EDLCs). Typically, in Li-ion capacitors activated carbon (AC) and a Li host material are used as the positive and negative electrodes, respectively.[63–65] When Ti2CTx was used as the negative electrode, an energy density of 30 Wh kg−1 at 930 W kg−1 for 1000 cycles was obtained.[55]
> 
> 迄今为止，所有关于LIBs中MXene电极的研究工作都是在合成后的材料上进行的，没有对其表面化学进行调控。因此，许多提高性能和减少首圈不可逆性的机会仍未得到探索。例如，如上所述，预测裸露的MXene表面在LIBs中的性能优于封端的表面。[37] 剥离其他MXenes可能会增加其Li吸收量，类似于对Ti3C2的报道。[26] 薄而轻的M2X MXenes对此目的特别有意义。与其他二维材料类似，可以通过使用不同的添加剂来设计电极结构，从而实现进一步的优化和增强。[60–62] MXenes处理高循环倍率的能力使其成为非对称、非水储能设备（混合电池）的良好候选者，这种设备结合了LIBs特有的高能量密度和双电层电容器（EDLCs）的高功率密度。通常，在锂离子电容器中，活性炭（AC）和锂主体材料分别用作正极和负极。[63–65] 当Ti2CTx用作负极时，在930 W kg−1的功率密度下，获得了30 Wh kg−1的能量密度，循环1000次。[55]
> 
> **Electrical double layer capacitors (EDLCs)**: 双电层电容器，一种通过电极与电解液界面形成的双电层来储存能量的超级电容器。 **Energy density**: 能量密度，单位体积或质量储存的能量。 **Power density**: 功率密度，单位体积或质量释放能量的速率。
> 
> Spontaneous chemical intercalation of different cations between Ti3C2Tx layers from aqueous salt solutions resulted in outstanding supercapacitors.[45] Reversible capacities—stable for more than 10 000 cycles—of more than 330 F cm–3 were achieved when Ti3C2Tx paper was tested in a KOH electrolyte, using a current of 1 A g–1.[45] This capacitance is higher than the volumetric capacitance of the best all-carbon EDLCs.[66] Ti3C2 paper electrodes showed better performance in supercapacitors than their multilayer counterparts made from MXene powders in a conventional way (i.e., rolling films of active material with binder and conductive additive).[45] The difference, however, was not as dramatic as observed when they were used as electrodes in LIBs. The better performance can in part be attributed to the better overall electronic conductivity of the Ti3C2-paper electrodes, because they are additive-free, while the powder electrodes contained a nonconductive polymer binder. Spontaneous intercalation of cations between MXenes’ layers makes each layer accessible for ion adsorption. Electrochemical intercalation of Na+ and multivalent ions, such as Mg2+ and Al3+, suggests MXenes can be promising host materials in multivalent ion batteries beyond Li.
> 
> 不同阳离子从盐水溶液中自发化学插层到Ti3C2Tx层间，获得了出色的超级电容器性能。[45] 当Ti3C2Tx纸在KOH电解液中以1 A g–1的电流进行测试时，实现了超过330 F cm–3的可逆容量（稳定循环超过10 000次）。[45] 该电容值高于最好的全碳EDLCs的体积电容。[66] Ti3C2纸电极在超级电容器中表现出比传统方式（即用粘合剂和导电添加剂滚压活性材料薄膜）由MXene粉末制成的多层对应物更好的性能。[45] 然而，这种差异并不像它们被用作LIBs电极时观察到的那么显著。更好的性能可以部分归因于Ti3C2-纸电极更好的整体电子导电性，因为它们不含添加剂，而粉末电极含有不导电的聚合物粘合剂。阳离子在MXenes层间的自发插层使得每一层都能进行离子吸附。Na+以及多价离子（如Mg2+和Al3+）的电化学插层，表明MXenes有望成为超越锂的多价离子电池中的主体材料。
> 
> Very recently, Xie et al.[56] used Ti3C2Tx as a supporting material for platinum nano particles (Pt NPs) for fuel cell applications. They found that the Pt/Ti3C2Tx combination was more durable and more electrochemically stable than the Pt/C catalysts that are conventionally used. For example, after 10 000 cycles, the Pt/Ti3C2Tx catalyst lost 15.7% of its initial Pt electrochemical surface area; the Pt/C electrode lost 40.8%.
> 
> 最近，Xie等人[56] 使用Ti3C2Tx作为铂纳米颗粒（Pt NPs）的载体材料，用于燃料电池应用。他们发现，Pt/Ti3C2Tx组合比传统使用的Pt/C催化剂更耐用，电化学稳定性更好。例如，在10 000次循环后，Pt/Ti3C2Tx催化剂损失了其初始Pt电化学表面积的15.7%；而Pt/C电极损失了40.8%。
> 
> To conclude this section, we note that although no MXene dispersion in polymers has been reported so far, Zhang et al.[36] used ultrathin nanolaminates of Ti3Si0.75Al0.25C2, as a conductive additive to poly(methyl methacrylate) (PMMA). The composites possessed excellent mechanical and thermal properties that were comparable to PMMA/graphene composites.[67] As the in-plane elastic properties of MXenes (Table 2) were found to be even higher than their parent MAX phases,[50] MXenes can potentially be used as additives to polymers to fabricate composites with outstanding mechanical properties and good electrical conductivities. As noted above, an important advantage of MXenes over the MAX phases is that the former have surface functional groups that should enhance the matrix-filler interface strengths and even allow for covalent bonding of the polymer to the MXenes layers, similar, for example, to nanodiamond–polymer composites.[68] These considerations certainly suggest that the use of MXenes in polymer matrices should pay large dividends.
> 
> 为了总结本节，我们注意到，尽管迄今为止尚未报道MXene在聚合物中的分散，但Zhang等人[36] 使用了Ti3Si0.75Al0.25C2的超薄纳米层压材料作为聚甲基丙烯酸甲酯（PMMA）的导电添加剂。所得复合材料具有优异的力学和热学性能，可与PMMA/石墨烯复合材料相媲美。[67] 由于发现MXenes的面内弹性性质（表2）甚至高于其母体MAX相，[50] MXenes有可能用作聚合物的添加剂，以制造具有出色力学性能和良好导电性的复合材料。如上所述，MXenes相比MAX相的一个重要优势是，前者具有表面官能团，这应能增强基体-填料界面强度，甚至允许聚合物与MXenes层形成共价键，类似于例如纳米金刚石-聚合物复合材料。[68] 这些考虑无疑表明，在聚合物基体中使用MXenes应会带来巨大回报。
> 
> **Poly(methyl methacrylate) (PMMA)**: 聚甲基丙烯酸甲酯，一种常见的透明热塑性聚合物，俗称有机玻璃。

> 
> # 7. Summary and Outlook
> 
> # 7. 总结与展望
> 
> In summary, a new family of 2D materials comprised of early transition metal carbides and carbonitrides has been produced by selectively etching the Al layers (A layers in MAX) with HF at room temperature. The replacement of the strong primary Al–M bonds in the Mn+1AXn phases with O, OH and F surface terminations allows for the conversion of a 3D—albeit a layered one—solid to a 2D solid. This new family of 2D materials has been labeled “MXenes” to denote the loss of the A element from the MAX phases and to emphasize their 2D structure. To date, the following MXenes have been produced: Ti3C2, Ti2C, Nb2C, V2C, (Ti0.5Nb0.5)2C, (V0.5Cr0.5)3C2, Ti3CN, Ta4C3, and Nb4C3. Many more have been predicted theoretically. XPS and EDS results indicate that MXenes are terminated with a mixture of O, OH, and/or F groups. The as-synthesized MXenes are simultaneously electronically conducting and hydrophilic, an uncommon combination. Sonication can be used to separate the 2D Mn+1Xn layers from each other and produce single-layer and few-layered flakes. Sonication also results in nanoscrolls with inner radii less than 20 nm. The yields are high and the process is simple and readily scalable. This comment notwithstanding, other synthesis techniques need to be explored. For example, it would be beneficial to identify etchants that are safer than HF. The use of nonaqueous etchants could in principle avoid O or OH termination of the MXene sheets. Furthermore, the etching of A elements other than Al needs to be explored to cover all possible transition metals carbides that form the MAX phases. For example, Al-containing Mo, Zr, and Hf-based MAX phases currently do not exist. If other A elements can be etched out, it would be possible to synthesize MXenes based on these metals.[12] As described above, some of the etching by-products can coexist with the MXene after etching the A element. The effect of these etching reaction by-products and surface contaminations on the performance and properties of various MXene layers need to be well investigated, understood and ultimately controlled.
> 
> 总之，通过在室温下用HF选择性刻蚀Al层（MAX中的A层），制备出了一个由早期过渡金属碳化物和碳氮化物组成的新二维材料家族。在Mn+1AXn相中，用O、OH和F表面封端取代强的原始Al–M键，使得一个三维固体——尽管是层状的——转化为一个二维固体。这个新的二维材料家族被标记为“MXenes”，以表示从MAX相中损失了A元素，并强调其二维结构。迄今为止，已经制备出以下MXenes：Ti3C2、Ti2C、Nb2C、V2C、(Ti0.5Nb0.5)2C、(V0.5Cr0.5)3C2、Ti3CN、Ta4C3和Nb4C3。理论上还预测了更多。XPS和EDS结果表明，MXenes被O、OH和/或F基团的混合物封端。合成后的MXenes同时具有电子导电性和亲水性，这是一种不常见的组合。超声处理可用于将二维Mn+1Xn层彼此分离，产生单层和少层薄片。超声处理还会产生内半径小于20纳米的纳米卷轴。产率高，过程简单，易于放大。尽管如此，还需要探索其他合成技术。例如，确定比HF更安全的刻蚀剂将是有益的。原则上，使用非水刻蚀剂可以避免MXene片的O或OH封端。此外，需要探索刻蚀除Al以外的A元素，以覆盖所有形成MAX相的可能过渡金属碳化物。例如，目前不存在含Al的Mo、Zr和Hf基MAX相。如果能刻蚀掉其他A元素，就有可能合成基于这些金属的MXenes。[12] 如上所述，在刻蚀掉A元素后，一些刻蚀副产物可能与MXene共存。这些刻蚀反应副产物和表面污染对各种MXene层性能和性质的影响，需要得到充分研究、理解和最终控制。
> 
> The space between the MXene sheets is extremely versatile and amenable to a host of compounds and cations. For example, Ti3C2Tx can be intercalated with various organic molecules as well as inorganic salts dissolved in water. In principle, ionic liquids and other intercalants can also be utilized. Sonication of DMSO intercalated Ti3C2Tx in DI water resulted in the large-scale delamination into single- and few Ti3C2Tx layers. Filtration of the latter resulted in thin, free-standing MXene paper that was flexible, hydrophilic, and conductive. MXenes’ 2D morphology and their good electronic conductivities render them promising electrode materials for LIBs, hybrid cells, and supercapacitors. They offer excellent capability to handle high cycling rates in LIBs, and outstanding volumetric capacitance in supercapacitors.
> 
> MXene片之间的空间用途极其广泛，可以容纳多种化合物和阳离子。例如，Ti3C2Tx可以被各种有机分子以及溶解在水中的无机盐插层。原则上，也可以使用离子液体和其他插层剂。在DI水中对DMSO插层的Ti3C2Tx进行超声处理，导致其大规模剥离成单层和少层Ti3C2Tx层。过滤后者得到薄而自支撑的MXene纸，该纸柔韧、亲水且导电。MXenes的二维形态和良好的电子导电性使其成为LIBs、混合电池和超级电容器的有前景的电极材料。它们在LIBs中提供了处理高循环倍率的优异能力，在超级电容器中则提供了出色的体积电容。
> 
> **Ionic liquids**: 离子液体，由离子组成的液态物质，通常具有低挥发性、高稳定性和良好的导电性。
> 
> The majority of current publications on MXenes are theoretical studies, mostly using DFT, as well as classical and ab initio molecular dynamics. However, many challenges remain for modeling as well, and the research in this area will likely continue at an increasing pace. First of all, we still need to fully understand the surface chemistry of the MXenes. Which functional groups are present on their surfaces after synthesis, and how the latter change over time when MXenes are stored or treated needs to be studied and understood. Upon drying of MXenes, a fraction of OH groups may be converted into O terminations by H2O elimination. Most current models assume complete termination by F, OH, or O groups. However, the coverage may well be incomplete or nonuniform or both, i.e., different functional groups may coexist with bare areas on the surface of a single MXene particle. In addition, water may be present on the surface and between the layers, forming hydrogen bonds and affecting MXene interlayer interactions. How these factors affect the predicted structural, electronic, magnetic, and other properties of MXenes remains an open question.
> 
> 当前关于MXenes的大多数出版物是理论研究，主要使用DFT，以及经典和从头算分子动力学。然而，建模也面临许多挑战，该领域的研究可能会以越来越快的速度继续。首先，我们仍然需要充分了解MXenes的表面化学。合成后，其表面存在哪些官能团，以及它们在MXenes储存或处理时如何随时间变化，这些都需要研究和理解。在MXenes干燥时，一部分OH基团可能通过消除H2O转化为O封端。目前大多数模型假设完全被F、OH或O基团封端。然而，覆盖率很可能是不完全或不均匀的，或者两者兼有，即不同的官能团可能与单个MXene颗粒表面的裸露区域共存。此外，水可能存在于表面和层间，形成氢键并影响MXene的层间相互作用。这些因素如何影响预测的MXenes结构、电子、磁性及其他性质，仍然是一个悬而未决的问题。
> 
> Although DFT showed that fully terminated Mn+1XnT2 monolayers may exist in three different configurations with one of them being energetically favored, it is still not clear which (if any) of these configurations would be favored in stacked multilayer MXenes. The properties of multilayer MXenes comprised of Mn+1XnTx monolayers stacked in different geometric configurations have never been probed by DFT. Despite its clear importance, this problem is likely to pose a significant challenge for DFT, which is inherently poor in describing weak interactions, such as the van der Waals and hydrogen bonds that are believed to hold the MXene monolayers in stacks. Recent developments in DFT for weakly interacting systems may prove useful in this situation. Many other properties of monolayer, few layers and multilayered MXenes need to be modeled. For example vibrational spectra, especially Raman, which can be easily compared with experiments, need to be calculated. Interpreting Raman spectra of MXenes is one of the challenges faced by experimentalists, and an area where modeling can provide considerable insights. Besides monolayers and stacks, other MXene structures, such as MXene nanotubes and nanoscrolls[5,35] need to be further studied. We currently know very little about their geometry, electronic structure, and properties. Finally, the development of a reliable classical force field for MXenes is urgently needed. Many practical phenomena such as intercalation, ion exchange, interactions and dynamics of polymers in MXene composites[36] can only be studied by classical techniques. Therefore, the development of reliable classical force fields for MXenes (similar to ClayFF or ReaxFF) will open new possibilities for studying these prohibitively large/complex for DFT systems/phenomena important for development of future applications.
> 
> 尽管DFT表明，完全封端的Mn+1XnT2单层可能以三种不同的构型存在，其中一种在能量上是有利的，但尚不清楚在堆叠的多层MXenes中，这些构型中的哪一种（如果有的话）会更有利。由以不同几何构型堆叠的Mn+1XnTx单层组成的多层MXenes的性质，从未被DFT探讨过。尽管其重要性显而易见，但这个问题可能对DFT构成重大挑战，因为DFT在描述弱相互作用（如被认为将MXene单层固定在堆叠中的范德华力和氢键）方面本质上是欠缺的。DFT在弱相互作用系统方面的最新进展可能在此情况下被证明是有用的。单层、少层和多层MXenes的许多其他性质也需要建模。例如，需要计算振动光谱，特别是拉曼光谱，这些可以很容易地与实验进行比较。解析MXenes的拉曼光谱是实验学家面临的挑战之一，也是建模可以提供相当深刻见解的领域。除了单层和堆叠，其他MXene结构，如MXene纳米管和纳米卷轴，[5,35] 也需要进一步研究。我们目前对它们的几何形状、电子结构和性质知之甚少。最后，迫切需要为MXenes开发一个可靠的经典力场。许多实际现象，如插层、离子交换、MXene复合材料中聚合物的相互作用和动力学，[36] 只能通过经典技术来研究。因此，为MXenes开发可靠的经典力场（类似于ClayFF或ReaxFF），将为研究这些对DFT来说过于庞大/复杂的系统/现象开辟新的可能性，这些现象对未来应用的发展至关重要。
> 
> **Raman spectra**: 拉曼光谱，一种基于非弹性散射的光谱技术，用于分析分子振动和晶格振动。 **Force field**: 力场，在计算化学中，用于计算原子或分子系统势能及其导数的函数和参数集。
> 
> Despite the fact that MAX thin films have been well studied,[69] as of yet no work has been published on the exfoliation of such thin films. Furthermore, some MAX phases, such as Ti4GeC3, have only been synthesized in thin film form.[70] Producing MXenes from thin films is key for the fabrication and characterization of MXene-based electronic devices. Experimentalists working with this emerging family of 2D materials should focus on addressing the following:
> 
> 尽管MAX薄膜已被充分研究，[69] 但迄今为止尚未有关于剥离此类薄膜的工作发表。此外，一些MAX相，如Ti4GeC3，仅以薄膜形式合成。[70] 从薄膜制备MXenes是制造和表征基于MXene的电子器件的关键。研究这一新兴二维材料家族的实验学家应重点关注解决以下问题：
> 
> -   Controlling and modifying MXenes’ surfaces.
> -   控制和修饰MXenes的表面。
> -   Establishing the exact structure of Mn+1XnTx as a function of T and x.
> -   确定Mn+1XnTx的精确结构随T和x的变化关系。
> -   Detailing and understanding the structure and properties of MXenes intercalated with various compounds/ions.
> -   详细说明并理解插层了各种化合物/离子的MXenes的结构和性质。
> -   Determining the chemical and thermal stabilities of MXenes in different environments.
> -   确定MXenes在不同环境中的化学和热稳定性。
> -   Large-scale delamination of MXenes other than Ti3C2Tx.
> -   除Ti3C2Tx之外的其他MXenes的大规模剥离。
> -   Finding alternative, robust, and safe routes of MAX phase exfoliation and MXene delamination.
> -   寻找替代的、稳健且安全的MAX相剥离和MXene剥离途径。
> -   Synthesizing MXenes without surface functional groups.
> -   合成无表面官能团的MXenes。
> -   Direct gas phase synthesis of single-layer MXene films.
> -   直接气相合成单层MXene薄膜。
> -   Characterizing single-layer MXenes, including electronic, magnetic, optical, thermal, and mechanical properties.
> -   表征单层MXenes，包括电子、磁性、光学、热学和力学性质。
> -   Exploring MXenes in various applications, such as composite reinforcement, catalysis, transparent electronic conductors, sensors, etc.
> -   探索MXenes在各种应用中的潜力，如复合材料增强、催化、透明电子导体、传感器等。
> -   Expanding the family through synthesis of new MXenes.
> -   通过合成新的MXenes拓宽该家族。
> 
> Success in addressing these points should lead to numerous applications. These comments notwithstanding, the discovery of MXenes is an exciting development that greatly expands the family of 2D materials.
> 
> 成功解决这些问题应能带来众多应用。尽管有这些评论，MXenes的发现是一项令人兴奋的进展，它大大扩展了二维材料的家族。
