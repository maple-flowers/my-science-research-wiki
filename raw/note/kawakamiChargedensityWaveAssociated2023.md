> ## 一、引言
> 
> 电荷密度波（**Charge-Density Wave, CDW**）是材料中电子密度发生周期性调制的一种量子现象，通常伴随着晶格的周期性畸变。理解其形成机制，尤其是费米面嵌套（**Fermi-surface Nesting, FS Nesting**）理论在其中的作用，是凝聚态物理中长期存在的核心问题。该理论源于对一维材料电子不稳定性的解释，但在二维材料中，其适用性一直备受争议。
> 
> 本研究聚焦于单层二硫化钒（**monolayer VS₂**），一种典型的二维过渡金属硫族化合物（**Transition-Metal Dichalcogenides, TMDs**）。该论文的核心价值在于，它通过实验和理论计算，揭示了一种全新的、由**高阶费米面嵌套**（Higher-order FS Nesting）驱动的电荷密度波机制。这一发现不仅解释了单层VS₂中独特的电荷密度波相，更为理解二维材料中复杂的电荷密度波行为开辟了新的视角，挑战并丰富了传统的费米面嵌套理论。
> 
> ## 二、论文结构与逻辑概览
> 
> 本论文的逻辑结构清晰，遵循了“发现问题 → 表征现象 → 提出理论解释 → 验证与拓展”的经典科学论证范式。
> 
> 1.  **背景与问题提出**：文章首先指出，费米面嵌套理论在解释二维TMD材料中CDW的多样性时遇到困难，而单层VX₂（X=S, Se, Te）家族是研究该问题的理想平台。其中，单层VS₂因制备困难，其CDW机制尚不明确。
> 2.  **材料制备与表征**：研究团队克服了技术难题，通过分子束外延（**MBE**）结合拓扑化学反应的创新方法，成功制备了高质量的单层1T-VS₂薄膜，并验证了其高纯度和晶体结构。
> 3.  **核心物理现象的表征**：利用角分辨光电子能谱（**ARPES**）和扫描隧道显微镜（**STM**）两种互补技术，实验上发现了两个关键现象：
>     -   **实空间**：观察到一个周期为√21 × √21 R10.9°的条纹状CDW超结构。
>     -   **动量空间**：在整个费米面上观察到了能隙的打开，且能隙大小呈各向异性。
> 4.  **理论模型的构建与验证**：研究发现，传统的单一费米面嵌套矢量（q）无法解释全费米面上的能隙。因此，作者提出了一个核心假设：**高阶费米面嵌套**，即一个长度为2q的嵌套矢量（q‘ = 2q）与q矢量协同作用，共同实现了对费米面的全覆盖，从而打开了完整的能隙。
> 5.  **结论与深远意义**：结合第一性原理计算（如声子谱和电子磁化率计算），验证了在2q矢量处存在不稳定性，支持了高阶嵌套的论点。该发现不仅解释了VS₂的特性，还为理解其他低维材料的CDW机制提供了一种通用框架。
>

> ## 四、正文核心内容剖析
> 
> #### 研究背景与问题意识
> 
> 文章的背景建立在TMD材料中CDW机制的争议之上。传统费米面嵌套理论预言，CDW波矢q应与费米面的平行段相匹配，从而在特定区域打开能隙并降低电子能量。然而，许多TMD材料的CDW波矢无法与明显的费米面平行段对应，促使学界探索如激子机制、范霍夫奇点等新理论。研究团队选择单层VS₂作为突破口，因为它属于VX₂家族，该家族材料普遍存在CDW，但VS₂因制备困难研究甚少，是检验和拓展嵌套理论的理想“空白地”。
> 
> #### 研究方法与模型
> 
> -   **材料制备**：创新性地将**分子束外延（MBE）**与**拓扑化学反应**相结合。先制备高质量的单层VTe₂，再在硫蒸气中将其转化为单层VS₂。这种方法克服了直接生长VS₂时硫蒸气压高、难以控制的难题。
> -   **实验表征**：
>     -   **微区角分辨光电子能谱（μ-ARPES）**：直接探测动量空间中的电子结构，能直观地观察到费米面形状和能隙的打开。
>     -   **扫描隧道显微镜（STM）**：在实空间直接观测原子尺度的晶格结构和CDW超结构，提供波矢的精确信息。
> -   **理论计算**：
>     -   **第一性原理计算（DFT）**：计算了理想1T相和CDW相的电子能带结构、声子谱和电子磁化率，为实验观测提供理论支持和微观机制解释。
> 
> #### 实验结果与讨论

> 本论文的逻辑链条清晰，遵循“实验现象 → 传统理论矛盾 → 新理论提出与验证”的经典科研范式。
> 
> -   **核心问题：** 单层VS₂的CDW机制是什么？传统的FS嵌套理论是否适用？
> -   **技术路线：** 通过创新的分子束外延+拓扑化学反应法（MBE+topotactic reaction）成功制备高质量单层VS₂，随后进行μ-ARPES和STM测量，并结合DFT计算。
> -   **核心论证链条：**
>     1.  **发现异常现象：** STM观测到一种新的、非公度的条纹状CDW图案（√21 × √3 R10.9°），与已知的VSe₂和VTe₂单层CDW周期均不同。
>     2.  **揭示电子结构：** ARPES测量显示，在费米能级（EF）附近，一个椭球形的电子口袋（electron pocket）上完全打开了一个**各向异性**的能隙，且能隙在高温下依然存在。
>     3.  **传统理论失效：** 仅用常规的嵌套矢量 q = g₁ 或 q = g₂ 无法解释整个费米面上能隙的完全打开，因为存在大量未嵌套的费米面区域。
>     4.  **提出新机制：** 提出“高阶费米面嵌套”概念，即嵌套矢量 q' = 2g₁。该矢量能有效连接常规q矢量无法覆盖的费米面弯曲区域。
>     5.  **理论验证：** 计算表明，q' = 2g₁ 处的电子极化率 χ(q) 有局域增强，且声子色散关系出现虚频（负的频率平方），这直接证实了该波矢处存在CDW不稳定性。
>     6.  **构建完整图像：** 常规q矢量（初级）和高阶2q矢量（次级）协同作用，q矢量负责连接平行区域，打开较大能隙；2q矢量负责连接弯曲区域，打开较小能隙，最终导致整个费米面被完全能隙化，形成各向异性的CDW态。
>

> ## 四、正文核心内容剖析
> 
> ### 1. 研究背景与问题
> 
> CDW是TMD材料中一个核心但充满争议的量子现象。其争议焦点在于，简单的**费米面嵌套（FS nesting）** 模型——即费米面的一个部分可以通过平移一个波矢q与另一部分重合——在很多二维TMDs中不足以解释CDW的形成。VX₂（X=S, Se, Te）家族是研究此问题的理想体系，因为其成员普遍存在CDW，且单层与块材表现迥异。单层VS₂由于硫的高蒸气压而导致制备困难，其CDW的物理本质一直未被清晰揭示。
> 
> ### 2. 方法、模型与核心发现
> 
> -   **创新制备方法：** 采用“先长VTe₂，再硫化置换成VS₂”的拓扑化学反应策略，成功制备了高质量单层VS₂薄膜，这是实验成功的基石。
> -   **核心发现一：新型CDW图案**。STM观测到周期为 **√21 × √3 R10.9°** 的条纹状CDW，它不同于VTe₂和VSe₂单层中已知的任何CDW模式，也不同于近期报道的7×√3或9×√3结构。这表明存在一种独特的CDW机制。
> -   **核心发现二：全费米面能隙**。ARPES首次在单层VS₂上揭示了CDW导致的电子结构改变：一个各向异性但完全的能隙在椭球电子口袋上打开，并且该能隙在室温下仍然存在，表现出类似赝能隙的行为。
> -   **核心发现三：高阶费米面嵌套**。这是本文最具创新性的理论贡献。作者发现，单一的常规嵌套矢量q（=g₁）无法解释全费米面能隙，因为椭圆口袋的弯曲部分无法被嵌套。因此，作者引入了**高阶嵌套矢量 2q（=2g₁）**。物理图像是：q矢量负责嵌套口袋的平行部分，起主导作用；而2q矢量负责嵌套弯曲部分，起辅助作用。两者协同，实现了对整个费米面的完全覆盖和能隙化。这一理论得到了DFT计算的强力支持：在2q矢量处发现了**电子极化率χ(q)的增强**和**声子谱的软模（虚频）**，这分别是电子和晶格不稳定性的直接证据，共同指向了该波矢处的CDW相变驱动力。
> 
> ### 3. 讨论与理论延伸
> 
> -   **与VTe₂的比较：** 文章还比较了VS₂和VTe₂的差异，解释了为何2q嵌套在VTe₂中不成立。原因在于二者的费米面拓扑结构不同：VS₂在M点有**椭球电子口袋**，其弯曲部分恰好能被2q矢量嵌套；而VTe₂在K点有**三角形空穴口袋**，其形状无法被高阶矢量有效嵌套，因此2q驱动的CDW在能量上不占优势，最终表现为4×4等常规CDW。
> -   **能隙各向异性的解释：** 文章提出了一个推测性解释：q矢量和2q矢量在CDW形成中扮演主次角色。q矢量散射概率高，导致的能隙大（沿M-K方向）；2q矢量散射概率低，导致的能隙小（靠近Γ点）。这种主次协同作用自然导致了实验观测到的能隙各向异性。
> 
> ## 五、结论与贡献
> 
> 1.  **发现了新型CDW态：** 在单层1T-VS₂中发现了具有 **√21 × √3 R10.9°** 周期的非公度条纹状CDW，并确认其在整个费米面上打开了一个各向异性的能隙。
> 2.  **提出了CDW新机制：** 首次通过谱学证据（ARPES, STM）和理论计算，提出了**“高阶费米面嵌套”** 的概念，并证明了常规嵌套矢量q与高阶嵌套矢量2q的协同作用是驱动该CDW并形成全能隙的关键。
> 3.  **澄清了材料体系差异：** 通过比较VS₂和VTe₂，揭示了费米面拓扑结构（椭圆口袋 vs. 三角口袋）对高阶嵌套机制是否适用的决定性作用，为理解整个VX₂家族的CDW多样性提供了统一视角。
> 4.  **概念价值：** 将1970年代理论提出的、在1D体系中讨论的“高阶嵌套”（4kF CDW）概念，推广并实验验证于一个二维材料体系，为解决其他低维材料中难以用简单嵌套解释的CDW问题提供了新的思路和范式。
> 
> ## 六、未来发展方向展望
> 
> 1.  **直接调制费米面嵌套条件：** 通过**静电栅压（electrical gating）**、**化学掺杂（carrier doping）** 或**外延应变（epitaxial strain）** 等手段，连续改变费米面的形状和大小，观察CDW波矢和能隙如何随之演化。如果CDW在特定应变或掺杂下消失或波矢突变，将为高阶嵌套理论提供强有力的因果性证据。
> 2.  **超快动力学研究：** 利用时间分辨ARPES（tr-ARPES），在飞秒尺度上观察CDW相的形成和熔化过程。可以研究q和2q声子模式在相变过程中的不同角色，以及它们之间是否存在能量转移的层级关系。
> 3.  **动量分辨的电子-声子耦合研究：** 精确测量**电子-声子耦合强度随动量的变化关系（k-dependent electron–phonon coupling, EPC）**。文章已指出，仅用嵌套条件无法完全解释能隙的精细结构，需要考虑EPC的k依赖性。这是未来理论工作的重要方向。
> 4.  **探索其他材料体系：** 扫描其他具有复杂费米面拓扑结构的二维材料（如其他TMDs、氧化物界面等），寻找高阶嵌套现象是否普遍存在。这可能会催生出一类新的“高阶嵌套CDW材料”。
> 
> ## 七、学术思考与批判性分析
> 
> 1.  **“高阶嵌套”的因果性强度问题：** 文章的核心论点是q和2q的协同作用导致了CDW。然而，在2q处计算的声子虚频和电子极化率增强，是CDW不稳定性的**自洽信号**，还是驱动CDW的**根本原因**？它是否仅仅是q矢量CDW形成后，折叠的能带引起的次级效应？文章通过q矢量无法解释全能隙这一现象，有力地论证了2q的必要性，但其因果主次关系仍需更直接的实验验证，例如直接观测到2q声子的软化。
>     
> 2.  **赝能隙行为的起源模糊：** 实验观测到能隙内存在残留的谱权重，类似于铜氧化物高温超导体中的赝能隙。文章将其归因于CDW涨落和电子-电子关联等**多体相互作用**。然而，这一解释较为笼统。这个赝能隙是CDW相变的前驱态涨落，还是与CDW共存的另一种有序态（如电子关联驱动的绝缘态）的表征？区分这两种可能性对于理解体系基态至关重要。
>     
> 3.  **对“完全能隙化”定义的探讨：** 虽然文章强调“全费米面能隙”，但EDCs显示在费米能级处始终存在残留谱权重。这更像是形成了一个极深的**赝能隙**，而非严格意义上的半导体型硬能隙。这种“全”和“不完全”之间的微妙界限需要在未来的研究中精确定义和讨论。
>     
> 4.  **理论模型中的假设问题：** 在DFT计算中，作者假设了三个120°旋转的CDW畴。虽然这符合晶体对称性，但畴壁的存在及其对电子结构的影响并未被考虑。此外，不同畴之间的相互作用是否会改变体系的能隙结构，也是一个值得探讨的问题。文章所使用的ISODISTORT工具虽然从对称性出发，但初始声子模式的选择对最终优化结构有影响，不同模式组合是否可能得到能量更低的亚稳态?
>     
> 5.  **与近期研究的矛盾：** 引言中提到，另一项研究在单层VS₂中观测到了7×√3和9×√3的CDW，且表现为无能隙行为。本文将其归因于样品差异，但这一矛盾恰恰凸显了单层VS₂中CDW基态的高度可调性和复杂性。是什么物理参数（如衬底效应、缺陷密度、应力）决定了体系选择哪种CDW态？解开这个谜团将为精确调控CDW提供关键线索。
>

> ## 八、用户问题预测与解答
> 
> 1.  **问题：什么是“费米面嵌套”？为什么它通常会导致CDW？**
>     
>     -   **解答：** 费米面是动量空间中能量等于费米能级（EF）的所有电子态的集合，它决定了金属的导电性质。**费米面嵌套（FS nesting）** 指的是费米面存在大面积的平行片段，可以通过一个特定的波矢**q（嵌套矢量）** 将一片费米面平移到另一片并与之重合。当存在良好嵌套时，电子系统会自发地发生晶格畸变，其周期正好由q决定。这个畸变会打开一个能隙，正好把嵌套的费米面片段“吃掉”，从而降低系统的总能量。这个物理过程就是**Peierls不稳定性**，是形成CDW的经典图像。可以理解为“电子系统为了降低能量，不惜让晶格发生形变，从而在费米能级处打开一个能隙，使自己变成绝缘体或降低导电能力”。
> 2.  **问题：“高阶”费米面嵌套新在哪里？和常规嵌套有什么本质区别？**
>     
>     -   **解答：** 常规嵌套通常只考虑一个基础波矢**q**，它对应着晶格畸变的基本周期。而“高阶”嵌套考虑的波矢是基本波矢的整数倍，如**2q**（或更高倍的3q等）。其本质区别在于它们“连接”的费米面区域不同：常规的q矢量擅长连接**平行**的费米面段，而2q矢量则能连接**弯曲**的费米面段。在单层VS₂中，椭球状费米面既有平行部分，也有弯曲部分。仅靠q无法覆盖弯曲部分，因此会留下未被能隙化的区域。而2q的引入，恰好弥补了这一不足，两者协同作用，实现了对整个费米面的“全包围”和完全能隙化。这就像用一把直尺（q）可以量平行的边，但要量一个曲边（弯曲的费米面），就需要一把曲尺（2q）。
> 3.  **问题：虚拟频率（虚频）在声子色散中代表什么物理意义？**
>     
>     -   **解答：** 声子频率的平方ω²通常为正，代表原子围绕平衡位置的简谐振动是稳定的。当计算出的ω²为负值时，频率ω就变成了虚数，即**虚频（imaginary frequency）**。虚频的出现是一个强烈的物理信号，表明当前计算所用的晶体结构（如未畸变的1T相）在能量上是不稳定的。原子会自发地沿着该虚频对应的声子模式的振动方向发生位移，进入一个能量更低、更稳定的新结构（如发生了CDW畸变的结构）。因此，在CDW研究中，在特定波矢q处发现虚频，是该波矢处存在晶格不稳定性、倾向于发生周期性畸变的最直接理论证据。

> The discovery of Dirac fermions in graphene has opened a new avenue to explore emergent quantum phenomena in two-dimensional (2D) materials1–5. Atomically thin transition-metal dichalcogenides (TMDs) offer a great opportunity to widen the functionality of 2D materials due to the tunability of band structure associated with the material variety, which has already manifested in the bulk counterparts as the emergence of rich electronic phases such as insulating, metallic, and superconducting phases. Besides the band tunability, two-dimensionalization of bulk TMDs further leads to unique quantum phenomena such as spin-valley Hall effect2, Ising superconductivity4, and robust Mott-insulator phase5, wherein the control of quantum confinement and interlayer interaction plays an essential role. The most intensively studied quantum phenomenon in bulk and monolayer TMDs is the charge-density wave (CDW). The CDW in TMDs has attracted tremendous attention because it provides an ideal platform to access a long-standing issue on the applicability of the Fermi-surface (FS)-nesting scenario, where the shift of FS with respect to the original one by the CDW wave vector causes the energy gain by the gap opening at the Fermi level (EF). There is growing evidence that many of the CDWs observed in TMDs cannot be explained in terms of the simple FS nesting, and more exotic mechanisms (e.g., exciton formation, van-Hove band singularity, and k-dependent electron–phonon coupling) may be at work in TMDs6–9. Among TMDs, vanadium dichalcogenides VXc2 (Xc = S, Se, and Te) are useful for investigating the interplay between FS nesting and CDW because this family of materials commonly undergoes CDW. Bulk 1T-VSe2 shows CDW with a 4 × 4 × ~3 periodicity triggered by the simple FS nesting10–12, whereas the monolayer counterpart exhibits CDW with various periodicities (e.g., 4 × 4, 4 × 1, √7×√3, 2×√3, 4×√3)12–20. The situation is similar in 1T-VTe2 where the bulk exhibits a 3 × 1 × 3 CDW21 while the monolayer shows various CDWs with, e.g., 4 × 4, 4 × 1, 5 × 1, or 2√3×2√3 periodicity22–27. This suggests that the simple FS-nesting scenario may be insufficient to account for a variety of CDW periodicities6. To access this question, we have deliberately chosen monolayer VS2 because the comprehensive understanding of CDW of the VXc2 family may be essential to obtain insights into this problem. However, the nature of CDW in monolayer VS2 has not been well investigated, partially because of the technical difficulty in employing the sample-fabrication method so far adopted for VSe2 and VTe213–19,22–27 due to the high vapor pressure of sulfur atom. In fact, previous works utilized catalytic reaction28 or MBE with FeS source29,30 instead of MBE with V and S sources. While the CDW with local 7×√3R30° or 9×√3R30° periodicity30 was observed in such monolayer films, the electronic band structure relevant to the occurrence of CDW and the mechanism of CDW are still unclear. We have overcome the difficulty in obtaining high-quality single-crystalline films by employing a unique sample-fabrication method that combines MBE and topotactic-reaction technique, as detailed later. We investigated the electronic structure of grown monolayer film by micro-focused angle-resolved photoemission spectroscopy (μ-ARPES) and scanning tunneling microscopy (STM), together with first-principles band-structure calculations.
> 石墨烯中狄拉克费米子的发现为探索二维材料中的涌现量子现象开辟了新途径¹⁻⁵。原子级薄的过渡金属二硫族化合物 (TMDs) 由于材料多样性带来的能带结构可调性，为拓宽二维材料的功能性提供了巨大机遇，这种可调性已在块体材料中表现为绝缘、金属和超导等丰富电子相。除能带可调性外，块体 TMDs 的二维化还带来了独特的量子现象，如自旋-谷霍尔效应²、伊辛超导⁴和鲁棒莫特绝缘相⁵，其中量子限域和层间相互作用的调控起着关键作用。在块体和单层 TMDs 中研究最深入的量子现象是电荷密度波 (CDW)。TMDs 中的 CDW 之所以引起极大关注，是因为它为探讨费米面嵌套机制的适用性这一长期悬而未决的问题提供了理想平台；在嵌套机制中，CDW 波矢使费米面相对于原始费米面平移，从而通过费米能级处的能隙打开获得能量增益。越来越多的证据表明，TMDs 中观察到的许多 CDW 无法用简单的费米面嵌套来解释，更奇特的机制（如激子形成、范霍夫奇点、k 依赖的电子-声子耦合）可能在 TMDs 中起作用⁶⁻⁹。在 TMDs 中，钒二硫族化合物 VX₂ (X = S, Se, Te) 对研究费米面嵌套与 CDW 之间的相互作用非常有用，因为该材料家族普遍发生 CDW。块体 1T-VSe₂ 表现出由简单费米面嵌套触发的 4×4×~3 周期 CDW¹⁰⁻¹²，而单层对应物则表现出多种周期（如 4×4、4×1、√7×√3、2×√3、4×√3）¹²⁻²⁰。1T-VTe₂ 的情况类似，块体呈现 3×1×3 CDW²¹，而单层则呈现多种 CDW，如 4×4、4×1、5×1 或 2√3×2√3 周期²²⁻²⁷。这表明简单的费米面嵌套机制可能不足以解释多样的 CDW 周期⁶。为了探究这一问题，我们特意选择了单层 VS₂，因为对 VX₂ 家族 CDW 的全面理解可能对获得该问题的深入认识至关重要。然而，单层 VS₂ 中 CDW 的性质尚未得到充分研究，部分原因是由于硫原子的高蒸气压，迄今用于 VSe₂ 和 VTe₂ 的样品制备方法在技术上存在困难¹³⁻¹⁹,²²⁻²⁷。事实上，以往的工作使用催化反应²⁸ 或使用 FeS 源的 MBE²⁹,³⁰，而非使用 V 和 S 源的 MBE。尽管在此类单层薄膜中观察到了具有局域 7×√3R30° 或 9×√3R30° 周期的 CDW³⁰，但与 CDW 发生相关的电子能带结构及其机制仍不清楚。我们通过采用独特的样品制备方法，即结合 MBE 和拓扑化学反应技术，克服了获得高质量单晶薄膜的困难，详见下文。我们利用微区角分辨光电子能谱 (μ-ARPES) 和扫描隧道显微镜 (STM) 以及第一性原理能带计算，研究了生长单层薄膜的电子结构。

> ## Fabrication and characterization of monolayer VS2
> 单层 VS₂ 的制备与表征
> 
> First, we explain the fabrication and characterization of monolayer VS2. We epitaxially grew monolayer VTe2 film on bilayer graphene/ SiC(0001) by the MBE method (Fig. 1a) and then replaced Te atoms with S atoms by topotactic-reaction technique in which sulfurization was carried out by exposing the VTe2 film to a S atmosphere (for details, see “Methods”)31. The reflection high-energy electron diffraction (RHEED) pattern for VTe2 film (Fig. 1b) shows a sharp 1 × 1 streak pattern to confirm the monolayer nature of grown VTe2 film22 since it is known that multilayer films have a 2 × 1 periodicity32. After sulfurization, the RHEED image still maintains a sharp 1 × 1 feature (Fig. 1c). The intensity profile of the RHEED pattern in Fig. 1d reveals a slight outward shift of peak position relative to the central peak after sulfurization, corresponding to the shrinkage of lattice constant. The photoemission spectrum of the sulfurized film (Fig. 1e) signifies sharp peaks originating from the S 2p1/2 and 2p3/2 spin-orbit satellites at binding energy (EB) of 162–163 eV. Prominent Te 4d3/2 and 4d5/2 spin-orbit satellites at EB of 40–42 eV observed in monolayer VTe2 completely vanish after sulfurization (inset to Fig. 1e), indicative of the complete replacement of Te with S. STM measurements for this sulfurized film (Fig. 1f) signify monolayer islands on bilayer graphene with a step height of 0.9 nm (Fig. 1g), supporting its monolayer nature33,34 (note that the STM image shows a double-layer-like island in some areas due to the artifact associated with the shape of the tip, as reported in other monolayer TMDs35,36). These results indicate that a monolayer VS2 film with a negligible mixture of VTe2 was successfully grown on bilayer graphene/ SiC(0001).
> 首先，我们说明单层 VS₂ 的制备与表征。我们采用 MBE 方法在双层石墨烯/SiC(0001) 上外延生长单层 VTe₂ 薄膜（图 1a），然后通过拓扑化学反应技术，将 VTe₂ 薄膜暴露于硫气氛中，使 Te 原子被 S 原子取代（详见“方法”部分）³¹。VTe₂ 薄膜的反射高能电子衍射 (RHEED) 图案（图 1b）显示了清晰的 1×1 条纹，证实了生长 VTe₂ 薄膜的单层特性²²，因为已知多层膜具有 2×1 周期³²。硫化后，RHEED 图像仍保持清晰的 1×1 特征（图 1c）。图 1d 中 RHEED 图案的强度分布显示，硫化后峰位相对于中心峰略微向外移动，对应于晶格常数的收缩。硫化薄膜的光电子能谱（图 1e）显示，在结合能 (EB) 162–163 eV 处存在源自 S 2p₁/₂ 和 2p₃/₂ 自旋轨道伴峰的尖锐峰。在单层 VTe₂ 中观察到的位于 EB 40–42 eV 的显著 Te 4d₃/₂ 和 4d₅/₂ 自旋轨道伴峰在硫化后完全消失（图 1e 插图），表明 Te 已被 S 完全取代。对硫化薄膜的 STM 测量（图 1f）显示双层石墨烯上存在单层岛，台阶高度为 0.9 nm（图 1g），支持其单层特性³³,³⁴（注意，由于针尖形状引起的伪影，STM 图像在某些区域显示类似双层岛的特征，正如其他单层 TMDs 所报道的那样³⁵,³⁶）。这些结果表明，在双层石墨烯/SiC(0001) 上成功生长了几乎不含 VTe₂ 混合的单层 VS₂ 薄膜。

> ## Electronic states of monolayer VS2
> 单层 VS₂ 的电子态
> 
> Figure 2a shows the valence-band ARPES intensity of monolayer VTe2 measured at T = 40 K along the KΓM cut of the hexagonal Brillouin zone (BZ). The band structure of monolayer VTe2 is characterized by highly dispersive holelike Te 5p bands and a weakly dispersive V 3d band near EF22. The topmost Te 5p band touches the V 3d band at the Γ point near EF. After sulfurization (Fig. 2b), the holelike bands shift downward, and a relatively flat feature appears at EB = 2.5–3.0 eV. This downward shift, which is attributed to the lower atomic energy level of the S 3p orbital relative to that of the Te 5p orbital, is better visualized in the ARPES-intensity plot near EF in Fig. 2c, d. The width of the V 3d band along the ΓM cut is enhanced after sulfurization, suggesting the increase in the transfer integral between V 3d orbitals due to the shrinkage of the lattice constant. Our first-principles band-structure calculations for free-standing monolayer 1T-VS2 capture the overall characteristics observed in the experiment. As seen from a comparison of Fig. 2b, e, the relatively flat feature at EB = 2.5–3.0 eV and the highly dispersive topmost S 3p band observed in the experiment are well reproduced in the calculation. This suggests that our monolayer VS2 film has the 1T structure. Also, the quantitative matching in the energy position of S 3p bands at the Γ point between experiments and calculations suggests a negligible charge transfer from the graphene substrate due to a weak van der Waals coupling at the interface. We also found small quantitative mismatches in the energy position and bandwidth between experiments and calculations. For example, the width of the V 3d band near EF is 0.5 eV in the experiment, whereas it is 0.75 eV in the calculation. This is likely due to the mass-renormalization effect associated with the correlation of V 3d electrons. We found no evidence for the energy splitting of V 3d bands at T = 40 K within our experimental uncertainty, consistent with the DFT calculation for the non-magnetic phase (Fig. 2e), suggesting the absence of ferromagnetism.
> 图 2a 显示了在 T = 40 K 时沿六角布里渊区 (BZ) KΓM 方向测量的单层 VTe₂ 价带 ARPES 强度。单层 VTe₂ 的能带结构特征为高度色散的空穴型 Te 5p 带和近 EF 的弱色散 V 3d 带²²。最顶端的 Te 5p 带在 Γ 点与 V 3d 带在 EF 附近相接。硫化后（图 2b），空穴型带向下移动，在 EB = 2.5–3.0 eV 处出现一个相对平坦的特征。这种下移归因于 S 3p 轨道相对于 Te 5p 轨道更低的原子能级，这在图 2c、d 中近 EF 的 ARPES 强度分布中得到了更好的可视化。硫化后，V 3d 带沿 ΓM 方向的宽度增加，表明由于晶格常数收缩，V 3d 轨道间的转移积分增加。我们对自由站立单层 1T-VS₂ 的第一性原理能带计算捕获了实验中观察到的整体特征。通过比较图 2b 和 e 可以看出，实验中观察到的 EB = 2.5–3.0 eV 处相对平坦的特征和高度色散的最顶端 S 3p 带在计算中得到了很好的重现。这表明我们的单层 VS₂ 薄膜具有 1T 结构。此外，实验与计算在 Γ 点处 S 3p 带能量位置上的定量吻合，表明由于界面处弱的范德华耦合，来自石墨烯衬底的电荷转移可忽略不计。我们还发现实验与计算在能量位置和带宽上存在小的定量差异。例如，近 EF 处 V 3d 带的宽度在实验中为 0.5 eV，而在计算中为 0.75 eV。这很可能源于与 V 3d 电子关联相关的质量重整化效应。在 40 K 时，我们在实验不确定度范围内未发现 V 3d 带分裂的证据，与非磁相的 DFT 计算结果一致（图 2e），表明不存在铁磁性。

> To clarify the electronic states near EF, we have performed ARPES measurements in 2D k-space for monolayer VS2. Figure 3a shows the contour plots at representative EB slices at T = 40 K, together with the calculated equi-energy plots (red curves) for monolayer 1T-VS2. At EB = EF, one can recognize a single bright intensity spot concentrated at the Γ point. This is in sharp contrast to the calculation that predicts a large ellipsoidal electron pocket centered at the M point. While such disagreement is seen up to EB ~ 0.2 eV, the ARPES intensity appears to follow the calculated contours at EB ≧ 0.3 eV. This can be recognized from the gradual movement of the intensity maxima from the Γ point toward the M point with increasing EB, which roughly follows the k location of the corner of ellipsoids. These results suggest that the spectral-weight suppression on the ellipsoidal pocket is associated with the energy-gap opening. To see more clearly the gap opening, we show in Fig. 3b the ARPES intensity at T = 40 K measured along representative k cuts (cuts 1–5 in Fig. 3a), which cross the ellipsoidal pocket. Along cut 5 (Fig. 3b5), which nearly passes the M points, one can see two U-shaped features corresponding to the electron bands bottomed at EB ~ 0.4 eV at each M point. These features are qualitatively reproduced by the calculation along the same k cut in Fig. 3c, although the bandwidth is narrower in the experiment due to the mass-renormalization effect. Intriguingly, although the calculation shows a clear EF-crossing of bands, the experimental dispersive feature loses its intensity around EF due to the gap opening. This suppression is also observed along the k cuts around Γ (e.g., cut 1), while the energy scale of the suppression is systematically reduced on approaching the Γ point (cut 5 to 1). Characteristic k dependence of the gap is better visualized by the energy distribution curves (EDCs) at the Fermi wave vectors (kF’s) in Fig. 3d. At point 5 (which is almost on the MK line), one can find a broad hump at EB ~ 0.35 eV, reflecting a large energy gap. This hump gradually moves toward EF on approaching the Γ point and reaches EB ~ 0.1 eV at the corner of the ellipsoidal pocket (point 1), accompanied by a positive leading-edge shift as seen in the inset to Fig. 3d. This suggests that the energy gap opens on the entire pocket at T = 40 K and is strongly anisotropic, as also highlighted by the plot of gap size Δ and leading-edge midpoint against k (Fig. 3e), which always takes a positive value irrespective of k (note that the leading-edge shift serves as an effective energy gap under a strong spectral broadening; for details, see Supplementary Note 1). We have confirmed that the gap is robust against temperature variation, as seen from the fact that it survives even at room temperature at point 1 (see Supplementary Fig. 1). It is noted that the EDCs in Fig. 3d always show a residual spectral weight around EF, reminiscent of the pseudogap in underdoped high-Tc cuprate superconductors37. Such a pseudogap was also recognized in the CDW phase of other monolayer TMDs such as VSe2 and VTe210,14–17,22,23. While the exact mechanism to produce the pseudogap is unclear at the moment, we speculate that the strong damping of quasiparticle lifetime due to many-body interactions, such as coupling of electrons to CDW fluctuations and electron–electron scattering associated with electron correlation may play a role.
> 为阐明近 EF 的电子态，我们对单层 VS₂ 进行了二维 k 空间 ARPES 测量。图 3a 显示了在 T = 40 K 时代表性 EB 切片的等强度图，以及单层 1T-VS₂ 的计算能量等高线（红色曲线）。在 EB = EF 处，可识别出一个集中在 Γ 点的单一亮斑。这与计算预测的以 M 点为中心的大椭球电子口袋形成鲜明对比。尽管这种差异持续到 EB ~ 0.2 eV，但在 EB ≧ 0.3 eV 时，ARPES 强度似乎开始遵循计算等高线。这可以通过强度极大值随 EB 增加从 Γ 点向 M 点逐渐移动来识别，大致遵循椭球拐角处的 k 位置。这些结果表明，椭球口袋上的谱权重抑制与能隙打开有关。为了更清晰地观察能隙打开，我们在图 3b 中展示了 T = 40 K 时沿代表性 k 路径（图 3a 中的截线 1–5）测量的 ARPES 强度，这些路径穿过椭球口袋。沿几乎穿过 M 点的截线 5（图 3b5），可以看到两个 U 形特征，对应于在每个 M 点处能量底端为 EB ~ 0.4 eV 的电子带。这些特征在沿相同 k 截线的计算中得到了定性重现（图 3c），尽管由于质量重整化效应，实验中的带宽较窄。有趣的是，尽管计算显示能带清晰地穿过 EF，但实验色散特征由于能隙打开而在 EF 附近失去强度。这种抑制也在 Γ 点附近的 k 截线（如截线 1）上观察到，而抑制的能量尺度在接近 Γ 点时系统性地减小（从截线 5 到 1）。图 3d 中费米波矢 (kF) 处的能量分布曲线 (EDCs) 更好地可视化了能隙的特征性 k 依赖关系。在点 5（几乎在 MK 线上），可发现一个位于 EB ~ 0.35 eV 处的宽峰，反映了较大的能隙。该峰在接近 Γ 点时逐渐向 EF 移动，并在椭球口袋的拐角处（点 1）达到 EB ~ 0.1 eV，同时伴随正的前沿位移，如图 3d 插图所示。这表明在 T = 40 K 时，整个口袋上均打开了能隙，且具有强烈的各向异性，正如能隙大小 Δ 和前沿中点相对于 k 的曲线图（图 3e）所强调的那样，该值始终为正，与 k 无关（注意，在强谱展宽下，前沿位移相当于有效能隙；详见补充注释 1）。我们已确认该能隙对温度变化具有鲁棒性，因为在点 1 处即使在室温下它仍然存在（见补充图 1）。值得注意的是，图 3d 中的 EDCs 在 EF 附近总是显示出残余谱权重，让人联想到欠掺杂高温铜氧化物超导体中的赝能隙³⁷。这种赝能隙也在其他单层 TMDs（如 VSe₂ 和 VTe₂）的 CDW 相中被观察到¹⁰,¹⁴⁻¹⁷,²²,²³。尽管目前产生赝能隙的精确机制尚不清楚，我们推测，多体相互作用（如电子与 CDW 涨落的耦合以及与电子关联相关的电子-电子散射）导致的准粒子寿命强烈衰减可能起了作用。

> ## Stripe charge-density wave of monolayer VS2
> 单层 VS₂ 的条纹状电荷密度波
> 
> To clarify the origin of the energy gap, we have performed STM measurements. One can recognize a stripe feature associated with the CDW formation in the STM image at T = 4.8 K (Fig. 4a). The Fast Fourier transformation (FFT) image in Fig. 4b signifies the superstructure spots with a √2×√1 R10.9° and √3×R30° periodicity. Intriguingly, this periodicity is apparently different from that of bulk crystal (3√3×3√3 R30°)38. Further, it is also different from those of VSe2 and VTe2 monolayers in the CDW phase (4 × 4, 4 × 1, √7×√3, 2×√3, and 4×√3 for VSe217,18; 4 × 4, 4 × 1, 5 × 1, and 2√3×2√3 for VTe223–27). It is noted here that a recent STM study on monolayer VS230 has reported the CDW composed of 7×√3 R30° and 9×√3 R30° superstructures and its gapless behavior at EF. This is in contrast to the present study showing a full gap at EF associated with √2×√1 R10.9° and √3×R30° CDW, which cannot be reproduced by the combination of 7×√3 R30° and 9×√3 R30° CDW (see Supplementary Fig. 2). Note that there exist intrinsic differences between our work and the work of ref. 30 regarding the experimental observation and interpretation of the CDW mechanism (for details, see Supplementary Note 3). Reciprocal lattice vectors of the observed superlattice, g1 and g2 (white arrows in Fig. 4b), are written as g1 = (2/9)b1 − (1/9)b2 and g2 = −(1/9)b1 + (5/9)b2 with the original 1 × 1 reciprocal lattice vectors, b1 and b2. The corresponding crystal structure drawn after the structural optimization in our first-principles band-structure calculations is shown in Fig. 4d, in which both V and S atoms are slightly displaced to form a superlattice with a large parallelogram unit cell. To clarify the influence of CDW on the band structure, we have performed band calculations for this superstructure, and the obtained band structure projected onto the original 1 × 1 BZ is shown in Fig. 4e (note that we assumed the existence of three domains rotated by 120° from each other, by taking into account the crystal symmetry). One can recognize that although the overall spectral weight in the CDW phase traces the band structure of the original 1 × 1 lattice (dashed red curves), there exist characteristic deviations. For example, while the original V 3d band crosses EF midway between the M and K points, the spectral weight in the CDW phase is suppressed around the crossing point, accompanied by a marked deviation of the band position from the original one. Such a CDW signature in the calculation is better visualized in an expanded view near EF of the calculated spectral weight along the MK cut in Fig. 4f. Corresponding ARPES intensity shown in Fig. 4g also signifies the spectral-weight suppression near EF. To clarify the influence of CDW on the ARPES intensity in more detail, we compare in Fig. 4h, i the calculated spectral weight in the CDW phase with the ARPES intensity at T = 40 K along the KΓM cut. Although the bandwidth of the topmost V 3d band is smaller in the experiment due to the mass-renormalization effect associated with the electron correlation, the overall ARPES intensity shows a better agreement with the calculated spectral weight for the CDW phase in comparison to the calculated band structure for the undistorted 1T phase (white dashed curves). Specifically, the V 3d band located slightly below EF at the Γ point is well reproduced by the calculation for the CDW phase, whereas the calculated band for the undistorted phase is located above EF. Such a critical difference can also be confirmed by the band dispersion along the ΓK cut (left half of Fig. 4i), which signifies the existence of a dispersive V 3d band below EF associated with the band folding due to the superstructure-induced periodic potential, consistent with the calculation for the CDW phase, but inconsistent with that for the 1 × 1 phase showing fully unoccupied bands along the ΓK cut (Fig. 4h). These results suggest that the anisotropic gap observed by ARPES originates from CDW, and some other mechanisms such as electron correlation are unlikely responsible for the gap opening (see Supplementary note 4). We also found from the quantitative analysis that the calculated energy gap along cut 1 (the ΓM cut) in Fig. 3a is 110 meV, whereas that along cut 5 (the MM cut) is 260 meV, qualitatively consistent with the anisotropic gap observed by ARPES (Fig. 3d). The CDW origin of the energy gap is also supported by the STM image which exhibits clear charge reversal between positive and negative bias voltages, which is a typical characteristic of the CDW (see Supplementary Fig. 5). While we cannot completely rule out the possibility of a non-nesting-originated CDW mechanism at the moment, a natural starting point to understand the CDW mechanism may be the FS nesting which connects parallel segments of the elongated pocket. The regular nesting vector is q = g1 or q = g2, as widely discussed in many 2D and quasi-2D materials. In both cases, the k space spanned by g1 or g2 reproduces the superlattice spots in the FFT image of STM data. We shifted the calculated FS of monolayer VS2 with respect to the original one by q = g2 (Fig. 5a) and found that the parallel segments of FSs are not well connected; it only produces several crossings of original and shifted FSs at discrete k points as shown by orange circles. This would be natural because the g2 vector is not parallel to the 1D array of superspots in the FFT image and, thereby, unsuited to represent the observed CDW periodicity. On the other hand, the parallel segments of the vertically elongated pockets centered at M1 appear to show a rather good nesting condition with q = g1, whereas a large area of side pockets at M2 and M3, as well as the curved region of pockets around the Γ point remains un-nested. Obviously, the normal FS nesting with q = g1 (as well as q = g2) is insufficient to explain the gap opening on the entire FS, unlike the case of some other TMDs where the simple FS nesting plays a dominant role10,11 (note that the consideration of 3q nature of CDW is still insufficient to explain the gap opening at the curved region of the pockets; for details, see Supplementary Fig. 6).

> ## Higher-order Fermi-surface nesting
> 高阶费米面嵌套
> 
> We propose that a key to pinning down the mechanism of CDW in VS2 lies in consideration of a longer q vector, q’ = 2g1, termed here higher-order FS nesting vector, which also reproduces the superlattice points in the FFT image. The nesting vector twice (integer times in the more general form) larger than that of the original nesting vector q (= g1) is not usually taken into account as a CDW nesting vector. As shown in Fig. 5c, the original and shifted FSs show an overlap in a larger k area, not only in the straight segment part of the pockets at M2 and M3 but also in the curved region around the corner of these pockets. Although the nesting vector q’ (= 2g1) does not cover the whole pocket by the nesting with leaving the poorly nested region, e.g., along the pocket at M1, the cooperation of two nesting vectors q = g1 and q’ = 2g1 enables the full-gap opening as observed by ARPES, since the nested area almost fully covers the pocket as shown in Fig. 5d. Such a visual argument on the FS nesting is more quantitatively supported by our calculations. Calculated electronic susceptibility χ(q) shows a local enhancement (see Supplementary Fig. 7), and the calculated acoustic phonon dispersion shows a negative frequency (see Supplementary Figs. 8–10) at q’ = 2g1, supporting the CDW instability at this wave vector. These results suggest that the cooperation of q and 2q nesting is likely responsible for the occurrence of CDW in monolayer VS2. It is noted that the CDW gap monotonically increases on moving away from the BZ center, as shown in Fig. 3d, e. Since this seems to be not fully consistent with the fine structure of the FS-overlap condition discussed in Fig. 5d, one may need to take into account the influence of strong spectral broadening associated with the many-body interactions that smears out fine structures in the FS-overlap condition. Also, one may need to consider that the nesting vector of g1 and 2g1 plays primary and secondary roles, respectively, to the total magnitude of the CDW gap, while consideration of q-dependent electron–phonon coupling may be necessary to fully account for the observed k-dependence of the CDW gap.
