> # Unconventional hysteretic transition in a charge density wave
> 
> 电荷密度波中非常规的滞后相变 **Hysteresis**: 滞后现象，指系统的响应取决于其历史状态的特性。
>

> (Dated: December 16, 2021) (日期：2021年12月16日)
> 
> Hysteresis underlies a large number of phase transitions in solids, giving rise to exotic metastable states that are otherwise inaccessible. Here, we report an unconventional hysteretic transition in a quasi-2D material, $\text{EuTe}_4$. By combining transport, photoemission, diffraction, and x-ray absorption measurements, we observed that the hysteresis loop has a temperature width of more than 400 K, setting a record among crystalline solids. The transition has an origin distinct from known mechanisms, lying entirely within the incommensurate charge-density-wave (CDW) phase of $\text{EuTe}_4$ with no change in the CDW modulation periodicity. We interpret the hysteresis as an unusual switching of the relative CDW phases in different layers, a phenomenon unique to quasi-2D compounds that is not present in either purely 2D or strongly-coupled 3D systems. Our findings challenge the established theories on metastable states in density wave systems, pushing the boundary of understanding hysteretic transitions in a broken-symmetry state. 滞后现象是固体中大量相变的基础，由此产生在其他情况下无法实现的奇异亚稳态。在此，我们报道了一种准二维材料 $\text{EuTe}_4$ 中非常规的滞后相变。通过结合输运、光电子能谱、衍射和 X 射线吸收测量，我们观察到其滞后回线具有超过 400 K 的温度宽度，在晶体固体中创下了纪录。该相变的起源与已知机制不同，完全处于 $\text{EuTe}_4$ 的非共度电荷密度波 (CDW) 相之内，且 CDW 调制周期没有变化。我们将这种滞后解释为不同层之间相对 CDW 相位的异常切换，这是一种准二维化合物特有的现象，在纯二维或强耦合三维系统中均不存在。我们的发现挑战了关于密度波系统中亚稳态的既有理论，拓展了对对称破缺态中滞后相变理解的边界。 **Metastable state**: 亚稳态，一种处于局部能量最低点但非全局最低点的状态。 **Incommensurate**: 非共度的，指 CDW 的调制周期与晶格周期之比不是简单的整数比。
> 
> Hysteresis is a history-dependent response of a system when subjected to an external perturbation. This phenomenon is ubiquitous in physics, biology, and even economics. In condensed matter, it forms the pillar of modern technologies ranging from memory device [1] to functional polymers [2]. In a hysteretic transition, phase change occurs at different values of a control parameter when it is swept in opposite directions. The complex interplay of states involved in these transitions provides a fertile ground for realizing novel metastable phases that do not normally exist in equilibrium [3–5]. 滞后是指系统在受到外部扰动时产生的一种依赖于历史的响应。这种现象在物理学、生物学甚至经济学中都普遍存在。在凝聚态物理中，它是从存储设备 [1] 到功能聚合物 [2] 等现代技术的基石。在滞后相变中，当控制参数沿相反方向扫描时，相变发生在不同的参数值处。这些相变中涉及的状态之间复杂的相互作用，为实现通常在平衡态下不存在的新型亚稳相提供了肥沃的土壤 [3–5]。
> 
> A model system for observing metastable states manifested through a hysteretic transition is offered by compounds that exhibit a charge density wave (CDW). It was realized early on that CDWs possess an array of nonequilibrium states accessible by application of an electric field or variation of temperature [6], and more recently it was shown that a “hidden”, long-lasting state could form following photoexcitation by a femtosecond laser pulse [3, 7]. These metastable phenomena are well understood in terms of the interaction among defects, electron condensate, and lattice superstructure, which leads to a slight deformation of the CDW periodicity or a phase slip between adjacent CDW domains [811]. The metastable states hold great potential in applications from nonvolatile memory to ultrafast switch [12, 13]. They also provide experimentally accessible platforms to tackle perennial questions on jamming transitions and glass dynamics [7, 14, 15]. Here, we report an unusual type of metastability in a layered CDW compound, $\text{EuTe}_4$. The existence of hysteresis in $\text{EuTe}_4$ was first evidenced by transport measurements [16], but the underlying mechanism and characteristics remain unexplored. The present multi-probe study reveals that the hysteresis possesses the following peculiarities: (i) Unlike other CDW systems, the hysteresis manifests solely in the order parameter amplitude without any effect on the modulation wavevector; (ii) The hysteresis has a giant temperature span of more than 400 K, the largest value known in crystalline solids. Our study raises new possibilities of hysteretic behavior in the solid state, offering an important dimension for how an order parameter evolves in a symmetry-broken phase. 表现为滞后相变的亚稳态观察模型系统是由展现出电荷密度波 (CDW) 的化合物提供的。很早人们就意识到 CDW 拥有一系列可以通过施加电场或改变温度来访问的非平衡态 [6]，最近的研究表明，在飞秒激光脉冲光激发后可以形成一种“隐藏”且持久的状态 [3, 7]。这些亚稳现象通过缺陷、电子凝聚体和晶格超结构之间的相互作用得到了很好的理解，这种相互作用会导致 CDW 周期性的轻微形变或相邻 CDW 畴之间的相位滑移 [8-11]。亚稳态在从非易失性存储器到超快开关的应用中具有巨大潜力 [12, 13]。它们还提供了实验可行的平台，用以解决关于阻塞相变和玻璃动力学的长久问题 [7, 14, 15]。在此，我们报道了一种层状 CDW 化合物 $\text{EuTe}_4$ 中不寻常的亚稳性。$\text{EuTe}_4$ 中滞后现象的存在最早由输运测量证明 [16]，但其潜在机制和特性尚未被探索。本项多探针研究揭示了该滞后现象具有以下特点：(i) 与其他 CDW 系统不同，滞后仅体现在序参量幅值上，而对调制波矢没有任何影响；(ii) 滞后具有超过 400 K 的巨大温度跨度，是已知晶体固体中的最大值。我们的研究为固体状态下的滞后行为提出了新的可能性，为序参量在对称破缺相中如何演化提供了一个重要维度。 **Order parameter**: 序参量，用于描述相变中系统对称性破缺程度的物理量。 **Wavevector**: 波矢，描述波的传播方向和周期的矢量。
> 
> $\text{EuTe}_4$ possesses a layered structure where the CDW modulation originates from nearly square-shaped Te sheets [Fig. 1(a)] [16]. We begin by characterizing the structural signatures of the CDW. The electron diffraction pattern of $\text{EuTe}_4$ in the (HK0) plane reveals a unidirectional CDW along the b-axis. As shown in the inset of Fig. 2(a), the CDW is marked by a pair of satellite peaks flanking the crystal Bragg peaks, where the CDW modulation is at $q_{\text{CDW}} \approx \frac{2}{3} b^_$, where $b^_ \equiv \frac{2\pi}{b}$. To determine whether the CDW is commensurate to the lattice, we performed high-momentum-resolution x-ray diffraction along the K cut [Fig. 1(b)(c)]. The single CDW peak in electron diffraction is split into two distinct satellites. They can be attributed to the first and second harmonics of the CDW peaks with a modulation wavevector $q_{\text{CDW}} = 0.643(3)b^_$. As no such splitting is allowed for a commensurate CDW at $\frac{2}{3} b^_$, we hence conclude that the density wave is not commensurate with a small integer denominator. Based on the width of the high-resolution diffraction peak [Figs. 1(c) and S1], we estimated the CDW correlation length to be at least 77 nm within the Te plane and 107 nm perpendicular to the planes, confirming the long-range nature of the superlattice modulation. $\text{EuTe}_4$ 具有层状结构，其 CDW 调制源自近正方形的 Te 片层 [图 1(a)] [16]。我们首先表征 CDW 的结构特征。$\text{EuTe}_4$ 在 (HK0) 平面的电子衍射图谱显示沿 b 轴存在单向 CDW。如图 2(a) 插图所示，CDW 的标志是位于晶体布拉格峰两侧的一对卫星峰，其中 CDW 调制位于 $q_{\text{CDW}} \approx \frac{2}{3} b^_$，此处 $b^_ \equiv \frac{2\pi}{b}$。为了确定 CDW 是否与晶格共度，我们沿 K 切方向进行了高动量分辨率的 X 射线衍射 [图 1(b)(c)]。电子衍射中的单个 CDW 峰分裂成两个截然不同的卫星峰。它们可归因于调制波矢为 $q_{\text{CDW}} = 0.643(3)b^_$ 的 CDW 峰的一阶和二阶谐波。由于在 $\frac{2}{3} b^_$ 处的共度 CDW 不允许出现此类分裂，因此我们得出结论：该密度波与小整数分母不共度。根据高分辨率衍射峰的宽度 [图 1(c) 和 S1]，我们估计 CDW 相关长度在 Te 平面内至少为 77 nm，垂直于平面的方向为 107 nm，确认了超晶格调制的长程特性。 **Bragg peak**: 布拉格峰，衍射实验中由周期性晶格产生的强相干散射峰。 **Correlation length**: 相关长度，描述系统中物理量在空间上保持相关性的特征距离。
> 
> The CDW formation also leads to a spectral gap of the entire Fermi surface, as revealed by angle-resolved photoemission spectroscopy (ARPES) [Fig. 1(d)]. The gap is not uniform along the Fermi surface contour, peaking near the $\bar{\Gamma}–\bar{X}$ cut. At 55 K, the maximum gap value is $E_g = 196(7)$ meV, measured by the distance from the leading edge of the valence band to the chemical potential [Fig. 1(f)]. This gap size yields 646 K as a lower bound for the mean-field transition temperature [17]; indeed, the actual CDW transition occurs well above 400 K, the highest temperature attainable in most experiments conducted. The gap opening is accompanied by the appearance of a folded CDW band, visible in the $\bar{\Gamma}–\bar{X}$ cut and in the constant energy map [Fig. 1(e)(f)] (see ref. [18] for a detailed description of band topology). The sharp appearance of the folded band and a complete absence of spectral weight within the energy gap suggest a well-defined CDW order with long-range phase coherence, corroborating the diffraction experiments. While diffraction and photoemission measurements reveal a prototypical CDW in $\text{EuTe}_4$, an unusual hysteresis is seen in the electrical resistivity between 80 K and 400 K, a temperature range within the CDW state [Fig. 2(a)]. CDW 的形成还导致整个费米面出现能隙，这一点由角分辨光电子能谱 (ARPES) 揭示 [图 1(d)]。该能隙沿费米面轮廓并不均匀，在 $\bar{\Gamma}–\bar{X}$ 切面附近达到峰值。在 55 K 时，最大能隙值为 $E_g = 196(7)$ meV，由价带前沿到化学势的距离测得 [图 1(f)]。该能隙大小给出了平均场相变温度的下限 646 K [17]；事实上，实际的 CDW 相变发生在 400 K 以上，这是大多数实验所能达到的最高温度。能隙的开启伴随着折叠 CDW 能带的出现，这在 $\bar{\Gamma}–\bar{X}$ 切面和恒能图 [图 1(e)(f)] 中可见（详细的能带拓扑描述见文献 [18]）。折叠能带的锐利出现以及能隙内谱权重完全缺失，表明存在具有长程相位相干性的明确 CDW 序，这与衍射实验相吻合。虽然衍射和光电子测量揭示了 $\text{EuTe}_4$ 中典型的 CDW，但在 80 K 到 400 K 之间（处于 CDW 状态内的温度范围）观察到了电电阻率中不寻常的滞后 [图 2(a)]。 **Fermi surface**: 费米面，动量空间中能量等于费米能级的等能面。 **ARPES**: 角分辨光电子能谱，一种通过测量光电子的能量和发射角度来研究固体电子结构的技术。
>

> 
> The hysteresis is reproduced across different sample batches and orientations [Fig. S9(a)]. It is also a minor loop [24], where the upper onset temperature of the major loop is higher than 500 K [Fig. S9(b)], which is the maximum temperature we could access experimentally. To investigate how the hysteretic transition is possibly linked to the density wave state, we examined the structural and electronic properties of the CDW at different temperatures along the loop, labeled as $T_1–T_5$ in Fig. 2(a). No additional satellite peaks emerge in the (HK0) diffraction plane during the $300 \text{ K} \rightarrow 30 \text{ K} \rightarrow 250\text{K}$ thermal cycle [Fig. 2(a), inset, and Fig. S4]. Furthermore, the CDW wavevector $q_{\text{CDW}}$ shows negligible variation within experimental uncertainty [Fig. 2(c)]. The lack of temperature-dependent feature is echoed in the electronic dispersion, which displays no renormalization of the folded CDW band except for thermal broadening and a rigid shift along the energy axis [Figs. 2(b) and S5]. 这种滞后在不同批次和取向的样品中均能重现 [图 S9(a)]。它也是一个小回线 [24]，其主回线的上限起始温度高于 500 K [图 S9(b)]，这是我们实验能达到的最高温度。为了研究滞后相变如何与密度波状态相关联，我们检查了回线上不同温度（图 2(a) 中标记为 $T_1–T_5$）下 CDW 的结构和电子特性。在 $300 \text{ K} \rightarrow 30 \text{ K} \rightarrow 250\text{K}$ 的热循环过程中，(HK0) 衍射平面中没有出现额外的卫星峰 [图 2(a) 插图和图 S4]。此外，CDW 波矢 $q_{\text{CDW}}$ 在实验不确定度范围内显示出可以忽略不计的变化 [图 2(c)]。这种缺乏温度依赖特性的现象在电子色散中也得到了呼应，除了热展宽和沿能量轴的刚性移位外，折叠 CDW 能带没有表现出任何重整化 [图 2(b) 和 S5]。 **Minor loop**: 小回线，指在未完成完整主滞后循环的情况下进行的局部循环。 **Renormalization**: 重整化，物理学中通过调整参数来处理发散或描述相互作用影响的过程。
> 
> Motivated by the energy shift of the band dispersion, we performed ARPES measurements between 50 K and 400 K to track the leading-edge gap $E_g$ in the high-symmetry $\bar{\Gamma}–\bar{X}$ direction. These experiments are difficult to perform because the sample surface can be easily contaminated by outgassing at elevated temperatures. Using a local heating technique [25], we observed no surface degradation, as evidenced by the excellent agreement between two thermal cycles ($50 \text{ K} \rightarrow 400 \text{ K} \rightarrow 50 \text{ K} \rightarrow 400 \text{ K} \rightarrow 50 \text{ K}$) performed in succession on the same sample [Fig. 3(a)]. Remarkably, the gap value $E_g$ exhibits hysteretic behavior with a similar temperature range as resistivity. An evolving leading-edge gap can be caused by changes in either the CDW gap size $\Delta$, or the chemical potential, or both. To distinguish these two contributions, we examine the temperature dependence of the first-order CDW peak, whose integrated intensity $I_{\text{CDW}}$ scales as $\Delta^2$ but does not depend on the chemical potential. As shown in Fig. 3(b), hysteresis is observed in $I_{\text{CDW}}$ but is negligible in the corresponding Bragg peak (Fig. S7). Based on the values of $I_{\text{CDW}}$ and $E_g$ in the heating and cooling branches, we further estimate that the hysteresis in the leading-edge gap has a dominant contribution from the CDW gap instead of the chemical potential [18]. 受能带色散能量移位的启发，我们在 50 K 到 400 K 之间进行了 ARPES 测量，以追踪高对称 $\bar{\Gamma}–\bar{X}$ 方向的前沿能隙 $E_g$。由于样品表面在高温下容易被出气污染，这些实验难以实施。通过使用局部加热技术 [25]，我们观察到没有表面退化，这由在同一样品上连续进行的两次热循环（$50 \text{ K} \rightarrow 400 \text{ K} \rightarrow 50 \text{ K} \rightarrow 400 \text{ K} \rightarrow 50 \text{ K}$）之间极佳的一致性所证明 [图 3(a)]。值得注意的是，能隙值 $E_g$ 表现出与电阻率相似温度范围的滞后行为。前沿能隙的演变可能是由 CDW 能隙大小 $\Delta$ 的变化、化学势的变化或两者共同引起的。为了区分这两种贡献，我们检查了一阶 CDW 峰的温度依赖性，其积分强度 $I_{\text{CDW}}$ 与 $\Delta^2$ 成正比，但不依赖于化学势。如图 3(b) 所示，在 $I_{\text{CDW}}$ 中观察到了滞后，但在相应的布拉格峰中可以忽略不计（图 S7）。根据加热和冷却分支中 $I_{\text{CDW}}$ 和 $E_g$ 的值，我们进一步估计，前沿能隙的滞后主要来自 CDW 能隙而非化学势 [18]。
> 
> Taken together, our transport, photoemission, and diffraction experiments depict a unified phenomenology for the hysteresis loop: Compared to the cooling branch, the average CDW amplitude is stronger in the heating branch, leading to a larger energy gap and a higher resistivity value. The presence of a hysteresis loop across more than 400 K is surprising. The hysteresis occurs entirely within the CDW phase, which is to be contrasted to a hysteresis due to the metal-to-CDW transition. This is because the electronic structure does not show any remnant state inside the energy gap for all temperatures probed [Fig. 2(b)]. 综合来看，我们的输运、光电子和衍射实验描绘了滞后回线的一个统一现象学：与冷却分支相比，加热分支中的平均 CDW 幅值更强，导致更大的能隙和更高的电阻率值。存在一个跨越 400 K 以上的滞后回线令人惊讶。滞后完全发生在 CDW 相之内，这与由于金属-CDW 相变引起的滞后形成了对比。这是因为在所有探测的温度下，电子结构在能隙内都没有显示出任何残留状态 [图 2(b)]。
> 
> Previous reports of thermal hysteresis in CDW compounds – including quasi-1D Peierlslike systems [26], quasi-2D transition metal dichalcogenides [27–31], and 3D spinels [32] – are ascribed to incommensurate-to-commensurate CDW transitions. In $\text{EuTe}_4$, however, the absence of detectable variation in its CDW wavevector renders this scenario improbable [Fig. 2(c)]. For certain Eu-based compounds, another plausible mechanism for thermal hysteresis is the valence transition between the $\text{Eu}^{2+}$ and $\text{Eu}^{3+}$ states [33–35]. To test this hypothesis, we performed x-ray absorption near edge spectroscopy on the Eu $L_3$ edge [Fig. 4(a)]. All spectra collected from 43 K to 325 K feature a single peak at the $\text{Eu}^{2+}$ energy with no peak resolved from the background at the $\text{Eu}^{3+}$ energy. This observation is thus incongruent with a temperature-induced valence transition from $\text{Eu}^{2+}$ to $\text{Eu}^{3+}$. 先前关于 CDW 化合物中热滞后的报道——包括准一维 Peierls 类系统 [26]、准二维过渡金属硫属化物 [27–31] 和三维尖晶石 [32]——均归因于非共度到共度的 CDW 相变。然而在 $\text{EuTe}_4$ 中，由于无法检测到其 CDW 波矢的变化，这种情景是不可能的 [图 2(c)]。对于某些铕基化合物，热滞后的另一个可能机制是 $\text{Eu}^{2+}$ 和 $\text{Eu}^{3+}$ 状态之间的价态转变 [33–35]。为了验证这一假设，我们对 $\text{Eu}$ $L_3$ 边进行了 X 射线吸收近边光谱测量 [图 4(a)]。所有在 43 K 到 325 K 之间采集的光谱在 $\text{Eu}^{2+}$ 能量处显示单个峰，而在 $\text{Eu}^{3+}$ 能量处没有从背景中分辨出峰。因此，这一观察结果与温度诱导的 $\text{Eu}^{2+}$ 到 $\text{Eu}^{3+}$ 的价态转变不一致。 **Peierlslike systems**: 类 Peierls 系统，指发生 Peierls 不稳定性导致金属-绝缘体相变的低维系统。 **XANES**: X 射线吸收近边结构光谱，用于分析元素价态和局部配位环境的技术。

> To gain more insights into the nature of the hysteresis, we turned to out-of-equilibrium response of the system. Specifically, we measured sample aging, a common characteristic of a hysteresis loop. Figure 4b shows the temporal evolution of the in-plane resistivity at 300 K, measured immediately after a $400 \text{ K} \rightarrow 20 \text{ K} \rightarrow 300 \text{ K}$ cycle. The resistivity follows a quasi-logarithmic decay over 3000 min, suggestive of multiple metastable configurations in the system [36, 37]. Within the measured time window, the resistivity drop is less than 10%, in stark contrast to the order-of-magnitude difference between the cooling and warming branches. Therefore, regardless of microscopic details, the energy barriers between different metastable configurations are well in excess of $k_BT \sim 26$ meV at room temperature, leading to long-lived states. A tighter bound on the energy barrier $w$ can be obtained from the resistivity relaxation time, $\tau \approx 3000$ min, which implies $w \sim k_BT \ln(\nu_0\tau) \approx 1$ eV, where $\nu_0 \sim 1$ THz is the attempt frequency estimated from the typical phonon energy in this family of compounds [38, 39]. 为了进一步深入了解滞后的本质，我们转向研究系统的非平衡响应。具体来说，我们测量了样品的时效性 (aging)，这是滞后回线的一个共同特征。图 4b 显示了在 $400 \text{ K} \rightarrow 20 \text{ K} \rightarrow 300 \text{ K}$ 循环后立即测得的 300 K 下面内电阻率的时间演化。电阻率在 3000 分钟内遵循准对数衰减，这表明系统中存在多种亚稳构型 [36, 37]。在测量的时窗内，电阻率的下降幅度小于 10%，与冷却分支和加热分支之间量级上的差异形成鲜明对比。因此，无论微观细节如何，不同亚稳构型之间的能垒在室温下远高于 $k_BT \sim 26$ meV，从而导致长寿命状态。可以通过电阻率弛豫时间 $\tau \approx 3000$ 分钟获得能垒 $w$ 的更紧约束，这意味着 $w \sim k_BT \ln(\nu_0\tau) \approx 1$ eV，其中 $\nu_0 \sim 1$ THz 是根据该类化合物典型声子能量估算的尝试频率 [38, 39]。
> 
> An implication of this long relaxation is that any experimentally accessible cooling and warming rates are too fast in comparison with the characteristic timescale of a complete equilibration within the system. Indeed, we observed negligible difference between resistivity loops when we increased the temperature sweep rate from 0.15 K/min to 10 K/min [Fig. S9(c)]. The lack of change between loops also contradicts to a scenario of a strong glass-like transition, which is expected to sensitively depend on the sweep rate and sample history [37, 40]. 这种长弛豫的含义是，与系统内完全平衡的特征时间尺度相比，任何实验可行的冷却和加热速率都太快了。事实上，当我们把温度扫描速率从 0.15 K/min 提高到 10 K/min 时，我们观察到电阻率回线之间的差异可以忽略不计 [图 S9(c)]。回线之间缺乏变化也反驳了强玻璃态相变的情景，因为后者预计会敏感地依赖于扫描速率和样品历史 [37, 40]。
> 
> Our combined equilibrium and nonequilibrium measurements point towards a rather unconventional hysteretic transition in $\text{EuTe}_4$, which is clearly distinguished from metal-to-CDW, incommensurate-to-commensurate, valence state, or strong glass-like transitions. An additional clue to understanding the giant thermal hysteresis in $\text{EuTe}_4$ is its conspicuous absence in the closely related $\text{RTe}_3$ family [41–46]. In both tetratellurides and tritellurides, CDW originates from the Te layers. The apparent difference between $\text{RTe}_3$ and $\text{EuTe}_4$ is that the former only has Te bilayers while the latter has additional Te monolayers [Fig. 4(c)]. In $\text{EuTe}_4$, the bilayer and monolayer CDWs share the same wavevector but exhibit distinct Te distortions [16]. The above comparison suggests that the hysteresis is related to the interaction between the bilayer and the monolayer CDWs. 我们的平衡态和非平衡态综合测量指向 $\text{EuTe}_4$ 中一种相当非常规的滞后相变，这与金属-CDW、非共度-共度、价态或强玻璃态相变有明显的区别。理解 $\text{EuTe}_4$ 中巨大热滞后的另一个线索是，在密切相关的 $\text{RTe}_3$ 系列中明显缺失此现象 [41–46]。在四碲化物和三碲化物中，CDW 均起源于 Te 层。$\text{RTe}_3$ 和 $\text{EuTe}_4$ 之间的明显区别在于，前者仅有 Te 双层，而后者具有额外的 Te 单层 [图 4(c)]。在 $\text{EuTe}_4$ 中，双层和单层 CDW 共享相同的波矢，但表现出不同的 Te 畸变 [16]。上述对比表明，滞后与双层和单层 CDW 之间的相互作用有关。
> 
> Specifically, their interaction is expected to most significantly modify the degree of freedom that remains degenerate for isolated bilayers or monolayers – the relative phase between the planar CDWs. In this regard, we propose that the giant thermal hysteresis is due to the switching of the relative phase between the CDW orders in the two types of Te layers. As illustrated in Fig. 4(d), density waves in adjacent monolayer and bilayer planes can either be in phase or out of phase. These two configurations result from the competition between the CDW coupling to the lattice and the Coulomb repulsion between the mono- and bilayer CDWs. On symmetry grounds [18], the relative CDW phase $\phi$ enters the free energy as $F = F_0 + a(T) \cos \phi + b \cos^2 \phi$, where $F_0, a$ and $b$ are phenomenological coefficients. For sufficiently small $a$, the free energy $F$ has two minima approximately shifted by $\pi$ (Fig. S10). We propose the switching between these two minima to be the mechanism underlying the hysteresis. 具体而言，它们的相互作用预计将最显著地修改在孤立双层或单层中保持简并的自由度——即平面 CDW 之间的相对相位。基于此，我们提出巨大的热滞后是由两种 Te 层中 CDW 序的相对相位切换引起的。如图 4(d) 所示，相邻单层和双层平面中的密度波可以是同相或反相的。这两种构型源于 CDW 与晶格的耦合以及单层和双层 CDW 之间库仑排斥的竞争。基于对称性 [18]，相对 CDW 相位 $\phi$ 进入自由能的形式为 $F = F_0 + a(T) \cos \phi + b \cos^2 \phi$，其中 $F_0, a$ 和 $b$ 是唯象系数。对于足够小的 $a$，自由能 $F$ 具有两个相差约 $\pi$ 的极小值（图 S10）。我们提出这两个极小值之间的切换是滞后现象的潜在机制。
> 
> This mechanism entails two theoretically distinct scenarios discussed in detail in ref. [18]. According to the first scenario, there could be a first-order phase transition due to a sign change of $a(T)$ at a particular temperature. The second scenario stipulates that the symmetry of the 3D CDW arrangement may impose $a = 0$ in the entire temperature range, which leads to the Ising-like degeneracy of the two free energy minima and hence the hysteretic formation of corresponding CDW domains. The atypically large thermal width of the hysteresis would be particularly natural for this scenario. The proposed scenario can, in principle, be tested directly through high-resolution cross-sectional transmission electron microscopy [47]. However, thermal instability across the wide temperature range from 40 K to 400 K makes such measurements challenging, and we defer to future experiments. 该机制包含文献 [18] 中详细讨论的两种在理论上截然不同的情景。根据第一种情景，由于 $a(T)$ 在特定温度下符号改变，可能会发生一级相变。第二种情景规定，三维 CDW 排布的对称性可能会使整个温度范围内的 $a = 0$，从而导致两个自由能极小值的类 Ising 简并，进而导致相应 CDW 畴的滞后形成。对于这种情景，滞后现象异常巨大的热宽度将显得尤为自然。所提出的方案原则上可以通过高分辨率截面透射电子显微镜 [47] 直接测试。然而，从 40 K 到 400 K 宽温度范围内的热不稳定性使得此类测量具有挑战性，我们将其留给未来的实验。
> 
> Adding to the preceding considerations, we highlight two microscopic factors that set apart the interplane CDW couplings in $\text{EuTe}_4$ from those in $\text{RTe}_3$. The divalence of the $\text{Eu}$ ions implies that the Te planes neither accept nor donate electrons to the $\text{Eu-Te}$ slab, so the Te mono- and bilayers remain nominally neutral. By contrast, Te planes in $\text{RTe}_3$ receive one electron per bilayer from the $\text{R-Te}$ layer. In addition, the Coulomb coupling between CDWs in different Te layers is expected to be better screened by quasiparticles in $\text{RTe}_3$ than in $\text{EuTe}_4$ [15]. This is because the Fermi surface in $\text{EuTe}_4$ is fully gapped by the CDW whereas there is a remnant Fermi surface in the CDW state of $\text{RTe}_3$. The charge neutrality of the Te layers and the reduced number of mobile carriers likely play an important role in setting the energy scale of the out-of-plane CDW coupling, leading to a delicate balance between different relative phases that underlies the giant hysteretic transition. 在上述考量之外，我们强调了使 $\text{EuTe}_4$ 中的层间 CDW 耦合与 $\text{RTe}_3$ 不同两个微观因素。$\text{Eu}$ 离子的二价意味着 Te 面既不接收也不捐赠电子给 $\text{Eu-Te}$ 层，因此 Te 单层和双层在名义上保持中性。相比之下，$\text{RTe}_3$ 中的 Te 面从 $\text{R-Te}$ 层接收每个双层一个电子。此外，预计不同 Te 层中 CDW 之间的库仑耦合在 $\text{RTe}_3$ 中比在 $\text{EuTe}_4$ 中更容易被准粒子屏蔽 [15]。这是因为 $\text{EuTe}_4$ 中的费米面被 CDW 完全打开能隙，而 $\text{RTe}_3$ 的 CDW 状态中存在残留费米面。Te 层的电中性和可移动载流子数量的减少可能在设定面外 CDW 耦合的能量尺度方面起到了重要作用，导致不同相对相位之间达成微妙平衡，从而产生了巨大的滞后相变。
> 
> Looking forward, we expect such hysteretic transitions to be found in other CDW systems that have similar properties as $\text{EuTe}_4$, namely, two nonequivalent charge-neutral layers sharing the same modulation wave vector while possessing a reduced number of free carriers. Our multi-probe investigation of the incommensurate CDW in $\text{EuTe}_4$ demonstrates several peculiar features of the thermal hysteresis: (i) it has a giant temperature span, (ii) it is accompanied by large differences in the average CDW amplitude, (iii) it does not involve any change in the valence state or CDW commensuration within our experimental resolution, (iv) no symmetry breaking is observed in the course of the transition, and (v) it is rather insensitive to cooling or heating rate. These observations demonstrate an unconventional origin of the hysteresis. 展望未来，我们预计在具有与 $\text{EuTe}_4$ 相似性质的其他 CDW 系统中也能发现此类滞后相变，即：具有相同调制波矢的两个非等价电中性层，且自由载流子数量减少。我们对 $\text{EuTe}_4$ 中非共度 CDW 的多探针研究证明了热滞后的几个 peculiar 特征：(i) 它具有巨大的温度跨度，(ii) 它伴随着平均 CDW 幅值的巨大差异，(iii) 在我们的实验分辨率内，它不涉及任何价态变化或 CDW 共度性的改变，(iv) 在相变过程中未观察到对称破缺，以及 (v) 它对冷却或加热速率相当不敏感。这些观察结果证明了滞后现象的非常规起源。
> 
> Our analysis suggests that the hysteresis may be a manifestation of the switching between different 3D configurations of the in-plane density waves. This switching, resulting from the delicate balance of interlayer couplings, points to a transition unique in quasi-2D systems, which is exclusively associated with out-of-plane orders as opposed to instabilities within each plane. Our findings not only expand the phenomenology of hysteretic transitions in broken-symmetry states, they also open the possibility of manipulating the rich internal structure of CDWs, raising numerous opportunities for device application that capitalizes on the wide temperature range of the metastable behavior. 我们的分析表明，滞后可能是面内密度波之间不同三维构型切换的体现。这种由层间耦合微妙平衡引起的切换，指向了一种准二维系统中特有的相变，它专门与面外序相关，而不是每层内部的不稳定性。我们的发现不仅扩展了对称破缺态中滞后相变的现象学，还开启了操纵 CDW 丰富内部结构的可能性，为利用亚稳行为宽温度范围的器件应用创造了众多机会。
