> Abstract
>            Two-dimensional (2D) transition metal dichalcogenides (TMDCs) and graphene compose a new family of crystalline materials with atomic thicknesses and exotic mechanical, electronic, and optical properties. Due to their inherent exceptional mechanical flexibility and strength, these 2D materials provide an ideal platform for strain engineering, enabling versatile modulation and significant enhancement of their optical properties. For instance, recent theoretical and experimental investigations have demonstrated flexible control over their electronic states via application of external strains, such as uniaxial strain and biaxial strain. Meanwhile, many nondestructive optical measurement methods, typically including absorption, reflectance, photoluminescence, and Raman spectroscopies, can be readily exploited to quantitatively determine strain-engineered optical properties. This review begins with an introduction to the macroscopic theory of crystal elasticity and microscopic effective low-energy Hamiltonians coupled with strain fields, and then summarizes recent advances in strain-induced optical responses of 2D TMDCs and graphene, followed by the strain engineering techniques. It concludes with exciting applications associated with strained 2D materials, discussions on existing open questions, and an outlook on this intriguing emerging field.
>
>【摘要翻译】摘要二维（2D）过渡金属二卤化物（TMDC）和石墨烯构成了一类具有原子厚度和奇特机械、电子和光学性质的新型晶体材料。由于其固有的特殊机械柔性和强度，这些2D材料为应变工程提供了一个理想的平台，实现了多功能调制和显著增强其光学性能。例如，最近的理论和实验研究表明，通过施加外部应变，如单轴应变和双轴应变，可以灵活地控制它们的电子状态。同时，许多非破坏性的光学测量方法，通常包括吸收光谱、反射光谱、光致发光光谱和拉曼光谱，可以很容易地用于定量测定应变工程光学性质。本文首先介绍了晶体弹性的宏观理论和与应变场耦合的微观有效低能哈密顿量，然后综述了二维TMDC和石墨烯的应变诱导光学响应的最新研究进展以及应变工程技术。文章最后介绍了与应变二维材料相关的令人兴奋的应用，讨论了现有的开放性问题，并展望了这一有趣的新兴领域。

> ## 一、引言
> 
> ### 领域背景
> 
> 自2004年石墨烯被发现以来，二维（2D）材料因其原子级厚度和独特的物理、化学性质，成为科学界和产业界的研究热点。然而，零带隙的石墨烯在发光应用上受限，而二维过渡金属硫族化物（TMDCs）则因其本征的、可通过层数调控的直接带隙，在光电子学领域显示出巨大潜力。
> 
> ### 研究问题
> 
> 为了进一步拓展这些材料的应用，研究者们一直在寻找有效调控其性质的方法。“应变工程”作为一种不引入杂质、可逆且连续的调控手段，在二维材料领域展现出非凡的潜力。本文所讨论的核心问题是：**如何系统地理解和利用机械应变来调控二维TMDCs和石墨烯的电子能带结构，并进而改变其光学性质，以实现新型光子学应用？**
> 
> ### 文献定位
> 
> 本文是一篇**综述文章**（Review Article），发表于高水平期刊《Light: Science & Applications》。它并非原创性的实验或理论发现，而是系统性地梳理和总结了该领域截至2020年的关键理论模型、实验现象、技术手段和前沿应用，为初学者和资深研究者提供了一个完整的知识框架。
> 
> ### 核心价值
> 
> 本文的核心价值在于：
> 
> 1.  **知识体系构建**：从宏观弹性理论到微观哈密顿量，构建了完整的“应变-能带-光学性质”关联图景。
> 2.  **实验与理论的桥梁**：将理论预测（如DFT计算）与实验观测（如PL、Raman光谱）紧密结合，清晰展示了应变调控的效果。
> 3.  **技术汇总与展望**：归纳了多种施加应变的技术及其优缺点，并明确了当前挑战与未来方向，特别是局部应变在单光子源等前沿应用中的巨大潜力。
> 
> ## 二、论文结构与逻辑概览
> 
> 本文遵循“理论 → 现象 → 技术 → 应用”的经典综述逻辑，其论证链条清晰，框架如下：
> 
> 1.  **理论基础（Section 2）**：首先建立理解应变效应的理论工具，包括**宏观连续弹性理论**（描述应变如何产生）和**微观有效哈密顿量**（描述应变如何影响电子）。这是全文的基石。
> 2.  **现象阐述（Section 3-5）**：分别阐述应变在**TMDCs**（Section 3&4）和**石墨烯**中的具体光学效应。
>     -   **TMDCs（Section 3&4）**：从无应变的优异光学性质（激子、谷效应）出发，然后重点讨论应变导致的**PL峰位移动、带隙转变、激子漏斗效应、二次谐波（SHG）调控及相变**。
>     -   **石墨烯（Section 5）**：从无应变的拉曼光谱和恒定吸收率出发，讨论应变导致的**拉曼峰位移与分裂、各向异性光吸收**。
> 3.  **技术实现（Section 6）**：介绍了从均匀应变（单轴、双轴）到非均匀局部应变的多种实现手段，并比较了其优缺点和可达应变量级。
> 4.  **应用与展望（Section 7-8）**：展示了基于上述应变效应的光子学应用，如**应变传感器、太阳能漏斗、单光子源**等，并总结了当前挑战和未来机遇。
>

> ### 图1：未应变的二维TMDCs基础
> 
> -   **(a) 晶体结构示意**：展示了`MX₂`（M=金属，X=硫族）的“三明治”结构。
> -   **(b) 激子示意**：在动量空间中，导带（CB）电子和价带（VB）空穴因库仑力束缚形成激子。
> -   **(c) 自旋-轨道耦合与激子**：在K/K'点，自旋-轨道耦合导致价带分裂，产生**A激子和B激子**（亮激子）和暗激子。
> -   **(d) DFT计算能带**：第一性原理计算展示了单层`MoS₂`的直接带隙位于K点。
> -   **(e) 吸收与PL光谱**：实验光谱证实了单层`MoS₂`（1L）在~1.85 eV和~2.0 eV处的A、B激子峰，而双层`MoS₂`（2L）因间接带隙，PL强度骤降，并出现间接带隙峰（I）。
> 
> ### 图2：应变对TMDCs能带和光谱的调控
> 
> -   **(a) 能带随应变演化**：DFT计算清晰显示，随着拉伸应变从0%增至3%，单层`MoS₂`在K点的直接带隙**单调减小**。同时，Γ点价带顶上升，预示了直接带隙到间接带隙的转变。
> -   **(b-c) PL与吸收谱的应变响应**：实验表明，在单轴拉伸应变下，单层`MoS₂`的A、B激子吸收峰和PL峰均发生**线性红移**。图(c)精确量化了位移速率，如A激子约为`-64 meV/%`。
> -   **(d) 双层MoS₂的PL红移**：应变同样使双层`MoS₂`的PL峰红移，且间接带隙峰（I）的位移速率远大于A激子，表明应变对间接带隙的调控更显著。
> -   **(f) 间接-直接带隙转变**：在双层`WSe₂`中，施加仅0.73%的单轴拉伸应变，PL强度急剧增强，这是**应变诱导间接带隙向直接带隙转变**的直接证据。这对于增强发光效率至关重要。
> 
> ### 图3：TMDCs的局部应变效应
> 
> -   **(a) 非均匀应变下的能带**：基于紧束缚模型的计算显示，在锯齿形`MoS₂`纳米带中，应变增加导致带隙减小，这为激子漏斗效应提供了能带基础。
> -   **(b-c) 激子漏斗效应**：(b)原理图展示了激子向高应变、低带隙区域漂移并复合发光的过程。(c)实验证实，在褶皱顶部，A激子发射波长红移，证明了漏斗效应的存在。
> -   **(d-f) 单光子源**：图(d)示意了在粗糙金属表面产生应变局域量子发射器。图(f)的光谱显示了极窄的发射线，这是单光子发射的特征，其线宽远小于图1(e)中的自由激子峰。
> -   **(g-i) 二次谐波（SHG）应变传感**：SHG对晶体对称性极其敏感。图(i)展示了应变不仅改变了SHG的强度，还改变了其偏振图案（从蓝线到红线），为应变成像提供了高分辨率手段。
> -   **(j-l) 应变诱导相变**：拉曼光谱是识别物相的“指纹”。图(l)中，140 cm⁻¹峰的出现（1T'相）和230 cm⁻¹峰的消失（2H相），证明了在室温下，局部应变即可驱动`MoTe₂`从半导体2H相转变为金属1T'相。
> 
> ### 图4：未应变石墨烯的基础
> 
> -   **(a-b) 晶格与能带**：展示了石墨烯的蜂窝状晶格和狄拉克锥状能带结构，其导带和价带在K点接触，呈现零带隙。
> -   **(c-d) 拉曼光谱**：图(c)是典型的石墨烯拉曼光谱，G峰（~1582 cm⁻¹）和2D峰（~2700 cm⁻¹）是其主要特征。图(d)清晰地解释了G峰、D峰（缺陷相关）和2D峰的声子散射物理过程。
> -   **(e) 透射率**：实验显示，单层石墨烯在可见光范围的透射率约为97.7%，其吸收率（~2.3%）由精细结构常数πα决定。
> 
> ### 图5：应变对石墨烯拉曼光谱的调控
> 
> -   **(a) 应变下的能带**：理论计算表明，结合剪切和单轴应变（15%），石墨烯的狄拉克点处可打开一个高达0.9 eV的带隙，预示着其从半金属到半导体的转变。
> -   **(b-c) 拉曼峰位移与分裂**：图(b)显示2D峰在单轴应变下发生红移和分裂（2D⁺和2D⁻）。图(c)显示G峰同样发生红移并分裂为G⁺和G⁻两个子峰，这源于应力破坏了晶格对称性，解除了声子简并。压缩应变则导致蓝移。
> -   **(d) G峰分裂机制**：直观展示了G⁺和G⁻两种振动模式的本征矢量方向，G⁻沿应变轴方向极化。
> -   **(f-g) 局部应变与SERS**：将石墨烯置于粗糙银膜上，不仅拉曼信号因表面增强拉曼散射（SERS）而增强，G峰和2D峰也因银纳米结构诱导的局部应变而发生红移和分裂。这完美结合了电磁增强和应变调控两种效应。

> ## 四、正文核心内容剖析
> 
> ### 1. 研究背景：为何需要应变工程？
> 
> -   **石墨烯的局限**：零带隙，限制了其在发光器件和数字逻辑电路中的应用。
> -   **TMDCs的优势与调控需求**：3原子层厚度，具有可调的本征带隙，但需要一种方法连续、可逆地改变其能带结构和光学性质，以满足不同光电器件（如LED、探测器、太阳能电池）对特定能带的要求。
> -   **应变作为理想手段**：二维材料具有超高的机械强度和柔韧性，能承受远超传统三维半导体的弹性应变（如`MoS₂`可达>10%），这为应变工程提供了巨大的操作空间。
> 
> ### 2. 核心理论：从应变到电子能带
> 
> 该模块是理解一切实验现象的基础。作者构建了一个双层次的理论框架：
> 
> -   **宏观层次-连续弹性理论**：将二维材料视为弹性薄膜，定义**应变张量(ε)**来描述其形变。给出了弹性能量密度公式（公式2），其中包含拉伸项（由杨氏模量`Y2d`和泊松比`ν`描述）和弯曲项（由弯曲刚度`κ`描述）。这部分回答了“**施加外力后，材料内部原子位移和应变场是如何分布的？**”
> -   **微观层次-有效低能哈密顿量**：
>     -   **紧束缚(TB)模型**：直接关联原子间距（由应变改变）和电子跃迁能（决定能带）。例如，式(5)的哈密顿量通过引入应变后的键矢量δ'ₙ，直观地描述了应变如何改变石墨烯的能带结构。
>     -   **k·p模型**：在布里渊区高对称点（如K点）附近，用简洁的哈密顿量（如式7）描述应变效应。该模型将应变张量（εᵢⱼ）与哈密顿量中的赝规范场（pseudo-gauge field）项直接耦合，能清晰解释带隙打开、狄拉克锥移动等物理图像。
> 
> ### 3. 实验现象：应变如何调控光学性质？
> 
> 这部分是理论的具体展现，作者通过对比未应变和应变下的光谱数据，系统阐述了两大类材料的光学响应。
> 
> #### 3.1 二维过渡金属硫族化物（TMDCs）
> 
> -   **能带与PL峰的线性调控**：拉伸应变导致能带隙减小，表现为PL和吸收峰的红移，且红移速率（meV/%）可被精确测定，如图2c所示。这是最基础、最直接的应变效应。
> -   **带隙类型的转变**：这是更深刻的调控。当应变足够大时，TMDCs的带隙可以从直接带隙变为间接带隙（如单层`WS₂`），反之亦然（如双层`WSe₂`），同时伴随PL强度的剧烈变化。这为设计高效发光器件提供了开关机制。
> -   **激子漏斗效应**：应变不必是均匀的。通过褶皱等结构引入非均匀应变，会在空间上产生一个连续变化的带隙分布。光生激子会像“漏斗”一样漂移并聚集在低带隙的高应变区，从而实现激子的**空间精准操控**和**高密度聚集**，这对于提高太阳能电池和发光器件效率至关重要。
> -   **二次谐波（SHG）的应变响应**：SHG对晶体对称性破缺极为敏感。应变会改变晶格对称性，从而调制SHG的强度和偏振，如式(8)和(9)所示。这使得SHG成为一种高分辨率、非破坏性的**应变成像工具**，能够以亚微米分辨率绘制应变张量图。
> -   **应变诱导的结构相变**：在`MoTe₂`等材料中，应变可以作为一种“清洁”的驱动力，在室温下触发从半导体2H相到金属1T'相的转变，这为构建**新型非易失性存储器**和**可重构器件**提供了可能。
> 
> #### 3.2 石墨烯
> 
> -   **拉曼光谱的应变指纹**：拉曼光谱是探测石墨烯性质最有力的工具。应变主要导致两个核心效应：
>     1.  **峰位移**：G峰和2D峰在拉伸（压缩）应变下发生红移（蓝移），位移量与应变成正比。
>     2.  **峰分裂**：单轴应变打破了石墨烯的六重旋转对称性，解除了G峰和2D峰对应的声子简并，使其分裂为G⁺/G⁻和2D⁺/2D⁻子峰。分裂的程度和方向直接反映了应变的**大小和方向**。
> -   **光学各向异性**：无应变石墨烯的光吸收是各向同性的。施加应变后，其光学电导率变为各向异性，导致光的透射率和吸收率开始依赖于入射光的偏振方向，这为设计**偏振敏感的光学器件**（如光学隔离器）打下基础。
> 
> ### 4. 技术实现：如何施加应变？
> 
> 作者系统地比较了不同应变技术的特点，这对于实验设计至关重要。
> 
> -   **均匀单轴应变**：通过**弯曲**柔性基底、**滚动**或直接**拉伸**实现。技术成熟，应变场可控，但最大应变量通常有限（<4%），且对于MEMS中的拉伸可达10%以上。
> -   **均匀双轴应变**：通过**热膨胀系数失配**或**压电基底**驱动实现。热膨胀法简单，但应变小且与温度耦合；压电法则可通过电信号精确控制，应变可正可负，但装置复杂，应变量也较小（~0.2%）。
> -   **非均匀局部应变**：通过**激光加热**、**预拉伸基底释放（褶皱）** 或**转移至纳米结构（纳米柱、粗糙表面）** 实现。这种方法能在纳米尺度上产生极高的局部应变，是实现单光子源等量子器件的关键，但其复杂性在于应变场的精确表征和控制难度大。
> 
> ## 五、结论与贡献
> 
> 本文的核心结论是：**应变工程是调控二维半导体（TMDCs）和半金属（石墨烯）光学性质的一种强大、通用且有效的策略。**
> 
> 其主要贡献可以归纳为：
> 
> 1.  **建立了一个统一的“理论-性质-技术-应用”知识框架**，将宏观力学与微观量子力学结合起来，为理解和预测应变效应提供了清晰的物理图像。
> 2.  **系统量化了应变对二维材料光学性质的影响**，汇总了大量实验测得的红移/蓝移速率、分裂宽度等关键参数，为后续研究提供了宝贵的参考数据。
> 3.  **详尽梳理并比较了各类应变加载技术**，明确了其优缺点、适用范围和可达应变水平，为实验选择提供了指南。
> 4.  **指明了应变工程在光子学领域的应用前景**，尤其突出了其在应变传感器、宽谱太阳能收集和单光子源等前沿方向上的巨大潜力。

> ## 六、未来发展方向展望
> 
> 基于本文的总结，未来发展方向可分为以下几个层面：
> 
> 1.  **理论模型的深化**：目前DFT难以处理非均匀、大尺度的应变问题。发展跨尺度的理论工具，如将TB模型与分子动力学相结合，或将机器学习引入势函数和电子结构计算，是未来的重要方向。
> 2.  **动态与可逆应变工程**：从静态应变走向动态应变。利用声表面波（SAW）或电磁波实现高频、动态的应变场，从而实时、超快调制二维材料的光学性质，可能催生新型光调制器和声光器件。
> 3.  **多场耦合调控**：将应变与电场、磁场、光场、温度等多物理场结合，探索更丰富的物性和功能。例如，应变调控的谷极化与光学谐振腔耦合，实现手性可控的谷电子学器件。
> 4.  **先进表征技术融合**：发展原位、高分辨的应变表征技术，如将SHG成像与透射电子显微学（TEM）、扫描隧道显微学（STM）结合，在原子尺度关联应变分布与局域电子态。
> 5.  **异质结与摩尔超晶格中的应变**：在二维材料范德华异质结中，层间转角会自发形成摩尔超晶格，其本身就伴随着周期性的面内应变场。探索这种内禀应变对异质结中激子、莫尔激子等新奇量子物态的调控，是当前极为活跃的前沿。
> 
> ## 七、学术思考与批判性分析
> 
> 虽然本文内容全面，但作为深度解读，我们仍需提出批判性思考：
> 
> 1.  **应变与缺陷的耦合效应问题**：论文中讨论的多为理想模型或高质量样品。在实际应用中，二维材料不可避免地存在空位、晶界等缺陷。**当应变场与缺陷相遇时，它们如何相互作用？是缺陷会钉扎应变场，还是应变场能驱动缺陷的运动？** 这种耦合如何影响最终的PL量子产率和拉曼光谱线宽？本文对此讨论较少，但这对于理解实际器件性能的衰减和失效机制至关重要。
>     
> 2.  **层间滑移与应变传递效率**：在多层TMDCs或异质结中，应变从基底或一层材料传递到另一层时，界面（尤其是范德华界面）的滑移会消耗大量能量。**论文中提及的应变值（如通过弯曲基底施加的应变）中，有多少是有效传递到了二维材料层，又有多少消耗在了界面滑移上？** 缺乏对界面力学行为的定量分析，可能导致对实验结果的过度解读。
>     
> 3.  **SHG应变传感的局限性与复杂性**：本文推崇SHG作为高分辨率应变成像工具。然而，**SHG信号不仅对应变敏感，还受到材料层数、堆叠角度、界面电场、激子共振增强等多种因素影响。** 在复杂环境中，如何从SHG信号中唯一、准确地解耦出完整应变张量，是一个极具挑战性的逆问题。论文中提出的两个参数模型（P1, P2）是简化处理，对于复杂的非均匀应变场可能失效。
>     
> 4.  **“单光子源”的确定性机理**：文章将应变诱导的量子发射归因于“漏斗效应”和“激子局域化”。但**其确切的微观起源是什么？是应变直接创造的深能级缺陷态，还是应变改变了局域带电状态，亦或是应变诱导了局部相变？** 这种机理的不清晰使得实现“确定性”的、位置和波长可控的单光子源阵列仍然困难重重。
>     
> 5.  **从实验室到应用的鸿沟**：文章展示了多种应变技术，但多数（如纳米压痕、AFM尖端拉伸）仅限于实验室研究。**如何开发出与CMOS工艺兼容、晶圆级、高可靠性的应变施加和封装技术？** 例如，如何保证在百万次弯曲后，应变传感器中的二维材料不脱落、不破裂，且性能无漂移？这是从“科学发现”走向“产品应用”必须跨越的鸿沟。
>

> ## 八、用户问题预测与解答
> 
> 基于对文献的理解，预测初学者可能提出的延伸问题并解答：
> 
> **Q1: 什么是“激子”？为什么它对TMDCs的光学性质如此重要，而对石墨烯不那么重要？** **A:** **激子**是半导体中因库仑力相互吸引而束缚在一起的电子-空穴对。在TMDCs中，由于量子限域效应增强和介电屏蔽减弱，激子束缚能非常大（数百meV），在室温下即可稳定存在，因此其吸收和发光光谱主要由激子决定，而非简单的电子-空穴对复合。而石墨烯是零带隙的半金属，其光谱响应主要由带内跃迁和狄拉克电子行为主导，无法形成传统意义上的稳定激子。
> 
> **Q2: 直接带隙和间接带隙有什么区别？为什么这个转变在光电器件中很重要？** **A:** 在**直接带隙**半导体中，电子从导带底跃迁到价带顶时，动量（k矢量）不变，可以直接与光子耦合，发光效率高。在**间接带隙**半导体中，跃迁需要额外的声子参与来改变动量，这是一个二级过程，概率远低于直接跃迁，因此发光效率极低。对于发光器件（如LED），我们需要直接带隙；而某些探测或光伏应用，可能可以利用间接带隙的长载流子寿命。应变能在这两种带隙间切换，相当于给了我们一个控制发光“开关”和效率的“旋钮”。
> 
> **Q3: 为什么石墨烯的拉曼G峰在应变下会分裂，而TMDCs的PL峰一般只发生位移？** **A:** 这源于它们不同的物理机制。石墨烯的G峰源于双重简并的面内光学声子。单轴**应变**破坏了晶格的对称性，解除了这两个声子模式的简并，使其振动频率变得不同，从而在光谱上表现为一个峰分裂成两个（G⁺和G⁻）。而TMDCs的PL峰源于**激子**的辐射复合，其能量主要由带隙决定。应变主要改变的是带隙的值，导致整个PL峰发生位移。虽然应变也会影响TMDCs的声子模，但其分裂程度远不如石墨烯显著，且通常不是PL研究的重点。
> 
> **Q4: 论文中提到的“赝磁场”是什么？它和真实磁场有什么异同？** **A:** **赝磁场**是一个精彩的物理类比。当石墨烯受到非均匀应变时，其晶格变形会改变电子跃迁的相位，这在数学上等效于在电子运动的哈密顿量中引入了一个矢量势，进而产生一个等效的磁场，即赝磁场。
> 
> -   **相同点**：都像真实磁场一样，能使电子作回旋运动，形成朗道能级，引发量子霍尔效应等现象。
> -   **不同点**：赝磁场只作用于电子，其方向在K和K'能谷是相反的，不破坏时间反演对称性；而真实磁场作用于所有带电粒子，破坏时间反演对称性。赝磁场为研究强磁场下的物理提供了一种无外加真实磁场的途径。
> 
> **Q5: 为什么要在金属表面制造单光子源？这不会导致发光被金属淬灭吗？** **A:** 这是一个很好的问题。直接将发光体放在平坦金属表面，确实会发生能量转移，导致发光的**淬灭**。但本文中的策略是巧妙的：
> 
> 1.  **引入隔离层**：通常会在金属和TMDCs之间生长一层几纳米厚的惰性介质层（如`Al₂O₃`），物理隔离以抑制非辐射能量转移，同时将TMDCs精确地置于金属纳米结构的局域强电磁场“热点”中。
> 2.  **利用Purcell效应**：金属纳米结构（如纳米锥、纳米天线）的局域表面等离激元共振（LSPR）能将光场高度局域在极小的模式体积内。根据**Purcell效应**，置于该模式体积内的发光体的自发辐射速率会得到极大增强，其增强倍数远大于淬灭速率，最终结果是发光强度增强且发光寿命缩短，成为了更亮、更快的单光子源。
> 
> ## 九、专区：便于 Obsidian Dataview 插件调用
> 
> 领域基础知识:: 二维材料（2D Materials），如石墨烯（Graphene）和过渡金属硫族化物（TMDCs），是原子级厚度的晶体，具有独特的力学、电子学和光学性质。其面内由强共价键连接，层间由弱范德华力（van der Waals interactions）结合。应变工程（Strain engineering）是利用其优异的机械柔韧性，通过施加形变来调控其物理性质的手段。
> 
> 研究背景:: 石墨烯尽管具有超高载流子迁移率，但零带隙（zero bandgap）限制了其在发光器件中的应用。TMDCs（如MoS2, WSe2）具有本征的、层数依赖的带隙，从间接带隙转变为直接带隙，展现出优异的光致发光（Photoluminescence, PL）特性。然而，需要一种连续、可逆的方法来调控这些材料的能带结构和光学性质，以满足光电器件的多样化需求。应变工程为此提供了理想的解决方案。
> 
> 作者的问题意识:: 如何将宏观的弹性应变理论、微观的低能有效哈密顿量（Low-energy effective Hamiltonians）与二维材料（TMDCs和石墨烯）的光学响应（如PL、拉曼散射、SHG）系统地关联起来，并梳理当前施加应变的技术手段及其在光子学前沿应用中的潜力与挑战？
> 
> 主要研究对象:: 二维过渡金属硫族化物（2D TMDCs，以MoS2, WSe2, MoTe2等为代表）和石墨烯（Graphene）。重点关注其**激子（Exciton）发光、拉曼（Raman）散射、二次谐波（Second-harmonic generation, SHG）**等光学性质。
> 
> 主要研究方法:: 综述。系统整合了**第一性原理计算（DFT）、紧束缚模型（Tight-binding model）、k·p理论**等理论方法，以及**光致发光（PL）、吸收（Absorption）、拉曼（Raman）、二次谐波（SHG）光谱**和**原子力显微镜（AFM）**等实验表征技术，并结合多种应变加载技术（如基底弯曲、压电驱动、纳米结构支撑）进行研究。
>

> #### 4.1 理论基石：从宏观弹性到微观电子
> 
> -   **宏观理论 (`Fel = Fst + Fb`)**：文章首先将二维材料视为一张**弹性薄膜**。其总弹性能（`Fel`）由两部分构成：**拉伸能**（`Fst`，源于面内原子间距变化）和**弯曲能**（`Fb`，源于偏离平面的形变）。这里的核心是**应变张量**（`εij`），它是一个二阶张量，完整描述了材料中某一点在三个方向的拉伸/压缩和剪切形变。杨氏模量（`Y2d`）和泊松比（`ν`）是描述材料刚度的关键参数。
> -   **微观理论 (`H = H0 + Hstrain`)**：宏观应变如何改变电子行为？这是通过**有效哈密顿量**（`H`）来实现的。可以把哈密顿量理解为描述电子在晶格中运动的总能量的“算符”。未应变时的哈密顿量（`H0`）决定了其本征的能带结构（如石墨烯的狄拉克锥）。应变引入了一个附加项（`Hstrain`），它就像一个“人造场”，其效果包括：
>     -   **标量势（形变势）**：等效于施加了一个电压，使得整个能带发生相对移动。
>     -   **赝规范场**：这是最奇妙的部分。在石墨烯中，非均匀的应变会使得电子在K和K'谷感受到方向相反的有效磁场，即**赝磁场**。这个磁场可以非常巨大（>300特斯拉），使得电子做回旋运动，形成朗道能级，从而打开带隙，这被称为“零场量子霍尔效应”。
> 
> #### 4.2 应变如何“调色”：TMDCs的光学响应
> 
> -   **PL光谱的线性移动**：这是最直观的效应。施加拉伸应变，原子间距增大，轨道交叠减小，通常导致带隙减小，PL峰位发生**红移**。反之，压缩应变导致**蓝移**。实验上测得的红移速率（如MoS₂的A激子约-45至-64 meV/%）是应变-光学性质转换的标尺。
> -   **直接-间接带隙转换**：TMDCs的价带顶在K点，导带底在K点和Γ点（位于K和K′点之间）的能量差很小。应变可以改变不同k点能量的相对位置。当拉伸应变足够大时，Γ点的导带底可能低于K点，导致材料从**直接带隙**变为**间接带隙**。这对光电器件至关重要，因为直接带隙是高效发光的前提。

>     -   **拉伸法**：直接拉伸承载二维材料的基底，或通过MEMS器件直接拉伸悬浮的二维材料。后者可实现高达10%的极端应变，但工艺复杂。

>     -   **压电法**：将二维材料置于压电基底（如PMN-PT）上，通过施加电压使基底形变，从而对二维材料施加可控的双轴拉伸或压缩应变。响应速度快，但应变幅度有限。

>     -   **纳米结构支撑**：将二维材料转移到刻有纳米柱、纳米孔或粗糙金属表面的基底上。由于二维材料优异的柔韧性，它会贴合在纳米结构表面，从而在尖端、边角或悬挂区域产生高度局域化的应变场。这是当前制备**单光子源**的主流方法。

> **问题1：什么是“激子”？为什么它在二维TMDCs中如此重要，而在石墨烯中却很少提及？**
> 
> **解答**：**激子**是一个由带负电的电子和带正电的空穴通过库仑引力结合形成的电中性准粒子，类似于一个微型的“氢原子”。当光照射半导体，会激发电子从价带跃迁到导带，留下空穴。这个电子-空穴对就是激子。 在**TMDCs**中，由于其能带结构与独特的介电屏蔽效应，激子结合能非常大（几百毫电子伏特），远高于室温下的热扰动能量，因此激子非常稳定，主导了材料的光吸收和发光过程。 而在**石墨烯**中，由于其零带隙的狄拉克锥结构，电子和空穴的有效质量为零，库仑相互作用极弱，难以形成稳定的激子。因此，石墨烯的光学性质主要讨论的是带间跃迁和等离激元，而非激子。

> **问题2：文章中反复提到“红移”和“蓝移”，它们分别代表什么物理含义？**
> 
> **解答**：这两个术语是光谱学中描述谱峰位置变化的常用词。
> 
> -   **红移**：指光谱峰向**长波长（低能量）** 方向移动。在本文中，拉伸应变导致TMDCs的带隙减小，电子跃迁需要更低的能量，因此PL或吸收峰就红移了。
> -   **蓝移**：指光谱峰向**短波长（高能量）** 方向移动。文中提到，压缩应变或某些情况下应变释放，会导致带隙增大，光谱峰就蓝移了。 可以简单记忆：拉伸→带隙小→能量低→红移；压缩→带隙大→能量高→蓝移。
> 
> **问题3：石墨烯的拉曼G峰为什么会分裂成G⁺和G⁻？这能告诉我们什么信息？**
> 
> **解答**：G峰源于石墨烯中碳原子的面内伸缩振动，在未应变时，沿x轴和y轴方向的振动能量相同（简并），只有一个峰。**单轴应变**破坏了这种对称性。垂直于应变方向的碳-碳键受形变影响较小，振动频率变化较小，形成**G⁺峰**；而平行于应变方向的碳-碳键被显著拉长，键能减弱，振动频率大幅降低，形成**G⁻峰**。因此，G峰的分裂是存在**单轴应变**的直接证据。通过分析G⁺和G⁻的位移量，我们可以定量地计算出应变的大小和方向，这对器件可靠性分析至关重要。
> 
> **问题4：什么是“赝磁场”？它和真实的磁场有什么不同？**
> 
> **解答**：**赝磁场**是一个绝妙的理论概念。它不是由磁荷运动产生的真实磁场，而是由**非均匀应变**在空间上调制电子跳跃能量而产生的一种等效场。在石墨烯中，应变会改变碳原子间距，进而改变电子在相邻原子间跳跃的“难易程度”。这种跳跃能量的空间变化，作用于狄拉克锥附近的电子，在数学上等价于一个巨大的垂直磁场。 关键区别在于：
> 
> -   **真实磁场**：作用在所有电荷上，时间反演对称性被打破。
> -   **赝磁场**：在石墨烯中，它作用于一个谷（如K点）的电子，就像一个磁场，而对另一个谷（K′点）的电子，则像是一个方向相反的磁场。因此，整个晶体的时间反演对称性得以保持，但每个谷的对称性被打破了。它能让电子在没有真实磁场的情况下，也表现出朗道能级和量子霍尔效应。
> 
> **问题5：用“应变”来调控性质，相比其他方法（如掺杂、电场），有什么独特优势？**
> 
> **解答**：与掺杂或外加电场相比，应变工程具有几个不可替代的优势：
> 
> 1.  **高连续性与可逆性**：应变可以连续、平滑地改变带隙大小，不像掺杂那样受限于杂质原子的固溶度，且多数情况是弹性形变，可逆。
> 2.  **不引入额外杂质**：掺杂会引入杂质散射中心，降低载流子迁移率。应变调控不改变材料化学成分，保持了材料的本征高迁移率。
> 3.  **可产生独特物理效应**：应变能打破晶格对称性，产生如**赝磁场**、**谷极化**等电场和掺杂无法实现的拓扑效应。
> 4.  **空间分辨率**：尤其是通过纳米结构支撑技术，应变可以局域在几十纳米甚至更小的尺度，实现对材料性质的高度局域化调控，这是全局电场调控难以实现的。
> 5.  **普适性**：几乎所有材料都有弹性响应，应变工程是一种极其普适的调控方法，不依赖于特定的电子结构或化学组成。

> # 7. Two-dimensional (2D) transition metal dichalcogenides (TMDCs) and graphene compose a new family of crystalline materials with atomic thicknesses and exotic mechanical, electronic, and optical properties. Due to their inherent exceptional mechanical flexibility and strength, these 2D materials provide an ideal platform for strain engineering, enabling versatile modulation and significant enhancement of their optical properties. For instance, recent theoretical and experimental investigations have demonstrated flexible control over their electronic states via application of external strains, such as uniaxial strain and biaxial strain. Meanwhile, many nondestructive optical measurement methods, typically including absorption, reflectance, photoluminescence, and Raman spectroscopies, can be readily exploited to quantitatively determine strain-engineered optical properties. This review begins with an introduction to the macroscopic theory of crystal elasticity and microscopic effective low-energy Hamiltonians coupled with strain fields, and then summarizes recent advances in strain-induced optical responses of 2D TMDCs and graphene, followed by the strain engineering techniques. It concludes with exciting applications associated with strained 2D materials, discussions on existing open questions, and an outlook on this intriguing emerging field.
> 
> 二维过渡金属二硫族化物（TMDCs）和石墨烯构成了一类具有原子级厚度和奇异力学、电子及光学性质的新型晶体材料家族。由于它们固有的卓越机械柔韧性和强度，这些二维材料为应变工程提供了一个理想平台，能够实现对其光学性质的多功能调制和显著增强。例如，近期的理论与实验研究已证明，通过施加外部应变（如单轴应变和双轴应变）可灵活调控其电子态。同时，许多非破坏性光学测量方法，通常包括吸收、反射、光致发光和拉曼光谱，可被便捷地用于定量测定应变工程调控的光学性质。本综述首先介绍晶体弹性宏观理论和与应变场耦合的微观有效低能哈密顿量，然后总结二维TMDCs和石墨烯在应变诱导光学响应方面的最新进展，接着讨论应变工程技术，最后介绍与应变二维材料相关的激动人心的应用、对现存开放问题的讨论，并对这一引人入胜的新兴领域进行展望。 **Transition metal dichalcogenides (TMDCs)**: 过渡金属二硫族化物，一类由过渡金属原子和硫族原子组成的层状二维材料。 **Graphene**: 石墨烯，由碳原子以sp²杂化形成的单原子层二维材料。 **Strain engineering**: 应变工程，通过施加机械应变来调控材料物理性质的技术。 **Uniaxial strain**: 单轴应变，沿单一方向施加的拉伸或压缩形变。 **Biaxial strain**: 双轴应变，在平面内两个互相垂直方向上同时施加的形变。 **Photoluminescence (PL)**: 光致发光，材料吸收光子后重新辐射光子的现象。 **Raman spectroscopy**: 拉曼光谱，基于光子与材料中声子非弹性散射的光谱分析技术。 **Hamiltonian**: 哈密顿量，描述系统总能量的算符，在量子力学中用于计算能带结构。

> # 8. Introduction
> 
> 引言
> 
> # 9. Graphene, the earliest discovered two-dimensional (2D) material (in 2004)1, which flakes from bulk graphite via mechanical exfoliation, opened the door to the investigation of a large variety of 2D materials within the scientific and technological community2–4. These layered materials have strong covalent in-plane bonding yet weak interlayer van der Waals interactions. The 2D nature of these monolayer materials endows them with unique electronic structures, and extraordinary physical and chemical properties. For example, charge carriers in graphene exhibit massless Dirac fermion behavior with relativistic particles traveling at near the “speed of light” (106 m/s) near the gapless K and K′ points in the Brillouin zone (BZ)5. However, the lack of an intrinsic optical bandgap makes pristine graphene far less appealing for light-emitting applications since zero-bandgap materials do not emit light under continuous-wave excitation (except under ultrafast laser pulses, ~30 fs)6. Hence, one of the most powerful optical characterization techniques for graphene is Raman spectroscopy, which involves inelastic scattering of photons that allows monitoring of doping, defect, disorder, and edge site effects7. In addition to the tremendous theoretical and experimental advances in graphene research over the past decade, many additional 2D materials with unprecedented properties have been discovered and studied8. Among them, the 2D group-VI transition metal dichalcogenides (TMDCs) represent a class of promising materials that are candidates for overcoming some of the disadvantages of graphene for future applications, such as ultrathin, flexible photonic, and optoelectronic devices that require optically transparent semiconductors9.
> 
> 石墨烯是最早发现的二维材料（2004年）1，通过机械剥离从块状石墨中分离出来，为科学和技术界研究种类繁多的二维材料打开了大门2–4。这些层状材料具有强的面内共价键，但层间范德华相互作用较弱。这些单层材料的二维本性赋予它们独特的电子结构以及非凡的物理和化学性质。例如，石墨烯中的载流子在布里渊区（BZ）无带隙的K和K′点附近表现出无质量狄拉克费米子行为，如同相对论粒子以接近“光速”（106 m/s）的速率运动5。然而，由于缺乏本征光学带隙，原始石墨烯在发光应用中远不如人意，因为零带隙材料在连续波激发下不发光（除非在超快激光脉冲下，约30 fs）6。因此，石墨烯最强大的光学表征技术之一是拉曼光谱，它涉及光子的非弹性散射，可用来监测掺杂、缺陷、无序和边缘位点效应7。过去十年中，除了石墨烯研究在理论和实验上的巨大进展外，许多具有前所未有性质的额外二维材料也被发现和研究8。其中，第VI族二维过渡金属二硫族化物（TMDCs）代表了一类有前途的材料，有望克服石墨烯在未来应用中的一些缺点，例如需要光学透明半导体的超薄柔性光子和光电器件9。 **Dirac fermion**: 狄拉克费米子，满足狄拉克方程的无质量或相对论性费米子，在石墨烯中描述低能载流子。 **Brillouin zone (BZ)**: 布里渊区，倒易空间中的原胞，用于描述晶体中电子能带结构。 **Optical bandgap**: 光学带隙，材料中电子从价带跃迁到导带所需的最小光子能量，对应于光吸收边的能量。 **Inelastic scattering**: 非弹性散射，散射过程中光子与物质交换能量，导致散射光子频率改变。
> 
> # 10. TMDC monolayers of the MX2 form have a hexagonal layer of metal atoms (M) sandwiched between atomic layers of chalcogen atoms (X). The X-M-X layers can be stacked to form van der Waals-bonded, multilayered TMDCs. While consideration of MX2 TMDCs with X = {S, Se, Te} and M = {Mo, W, Re, V} implies 12 different types of TMDCs10, the Janus structures of the X1-M-X2 type (i.e., different chalcogens in the nonmetal layers) yield 24 more TMDCs11, and alloying within the M- and X-layers produces an infinite set of possible TMDCs. When a TMDC monolayer is extracted from a bulk TMDC crystal, the bandgap may change from indirect-to-direct via the inversion symmetry breaking, resulting in an increase in the optical bandgap because of the significantly enhanced Coulomb interaction arising from the reduction in the dielectric screening effect (e.g., 1.9 eV for monolayer MoS2 compared to 1.29 eV for bulk MoS212,13). Because of these distinctive electronic properties (mostly absent in graphene), TMDC monolayers exhibit prominent photoluminescence (PL) with composition- and layer number-dependent emission energies, thereby enriching their optical properties and opening up new optoelectronic applications. Theoretical calculations and subsequent experimental demonstrations have also shown direct bandgaps in other TMDCs, such as MoSe2, WS2, and WSe2 (ref. 14). These direct bandgaps in TMDC monolayers, together with their spin-valley-coupling-induced valleytronic effects15–17, have triggered a plethora of potential optoelectronic applications, such as field-effect transistors, logic circuits, amplifiers, photodetectors, and excitonic light-emitting devices9,18–21.
> 
> MX2形式的TMDC单层具有六方排列的金属原子层（M），夹在两层硫族原子（X）之间。X-M-X层可以堆叠形成范德华键合的多层TMDCs。考虑X = {S, Se, Te}和M = {Mo, W, Re, V}的MX2 TMDCs，意味着有12种不同类型的TMDCs10，而X1-M-X2类型的Janus结构（即非金属层中使用不同的硫族元素）又产生24种TMDCs11，并且在M和X层内进行合金化可产生无限多种可能的TMDCs。当TMDC单层从块状TMDC晶体中提取出来时，由于反演对称性破缺，带隙可能从间接带隙转变为直接带隙，并因介电屏蔽效应减弱导致的库仑相互作用显著增强而使光学带隙增大（例如，单层MoS2为1.9 eV，而块状MoS2为1.29 eV）12,13。由于这些独特的电子性质（大多在石墨烯中不存在），TMDC单层表现出显著的光致发光（PL），其发射能量依赖于成分和层数，从而丰富了它们的光学性质，并开辟了新的光电应用。理论计算和随后的实验验证也表明，其他TMDCs（如MoSe2、WS2和WSe2）具有直接带隙14。TMDC单层中的这些直接带隙，以及它们自旋-谷耦合诱导的谷电子学效应15–17，引发了大量潜在的光电应用，如场效应晶体管、逻辑电路、放大器、光电探测器和激子发光器件9,18–21。 **Janus structure**: Janus结构，一种非对称的二维材料结构，上下表面的硫族原子不同，类似于罗马神话中的双面神Janus。 **Indirect bandgap**: 间接带隙，半导体中价带顶和导带底在动量空间中位于不同波矢处，电子跃迁需要声子参与。 **Direct bandgap**: 直接带隙，价带顶和导带底位于同一波矢处，电子跃迁可直接吸收或发射光子。 **Dielectric screening**: 介电屏蔽，材料中自由载流子对电场作用的减弱效应，影响激子结合能。 **Valleytronics**: 谷电子学，利用能带中不同能谷的自由度来编码和处理信息的电子学分支。

> # 11. The weak interactions between 2D monolayers provide an opportunity for easy heterostructure design via vertical stacking of different 2D materials (graphene, hexagonal boron nitride, TMDCs, etc.)22–26. 2D-material-based heterojunctions/stacks also provide an effective means of tuning optical properties and an atomic-precision, semiconducting building block device design/assembly approach, which endows these materials with the potential for integration with advanced optical platforms, such as microcavities27 and metasurfaces17, to realize versatile functionalities.
> 
> 二维单层之间的弱相互作用为通过不同二维材料（石墨烯、六方氮化硼、TMDCs等）的垂直堆叠进行异质结构设计提供了机会22–26。基于二维材料的异质结/堆叠也提供了一种有效调控光学性质的手段，以及一种原子级精度的半导体构建模块器件设计/组装方法，这赋予这些材料与先进光学平台（如微腔27和超表面17）集成的潜力，以实现多种功能。 **Heterostructure**: 异质结构，由两种或多种不同材料组成的层状结构，界面处可产生新的物理性质。 **Hexagonal boron nitride (h-BN)**: 六方氮化硼，一种类石墨结构的绝缘二维材料，常用作高质量二维材料的衬底或介电层。 **Microcavity**: 微腔，尺寸在微米量级的光学谐振腔，可增强光与物质相互作用。 **Metasurface**: 超表面，由亚波长人工结构组成的二维平面，可对光波的相位、振幅和偏振进行灵活调控。
> 
> # 12. Recent theoretical and experimental work has demonstrated that strain can also play an important role in manipulating the electronic and optical properties of TMDCs and graphene28–32. The remarkable strength of TMDCs33, compared with their conventional semiconducting counterparts34–36, allows the application of large strains for band structure manipulation. In 1921, Griffith argued that a perfect (defect-free), elastic crystal has a theoretical fracture strength approximately one-ninth its Young’s modulus37. Motivated by this insight, researchers sought this theoretical upper limit of the fracture strength in the fertile ground of defect-free, atomically thick, 2D materials. For example, density-functional theory (DFT) calculations and atomic force microscope (AFM) tip-based experiments showed that the fracture stress of a freely suspended MoS2 monolayer is approximately one-eighth of its Young’s modulus, which approaches the theoretical limit suggested by Griffith theory34,35,37,38. For example, MoS2 monolayers show a maximum strain at fracture of >10%, whereas bulk silicon usually breaks at a strain of ≤1.5% (ref. 39). While application of macroscopically homogeneous strains on a large scale are achievable, inhomogeneous strains provide a means of locally creating spatially varying band structures within 2D TMDCs on the nanoscale. For example, wrinkles in MoS2 produce such locally varying strains40,41. Similar to 2D TMDCs, graphene can also sustain very large elastic strains (up to 25%), comparable to Griffith’s theoretical limit (note that the Young’s modulus of graphene is much greater than that of monolayer TMDCs). As a consequence, graphene is also expected to be an excellent platform for the study of strain engineering in 2D materials42. The remarkable strain limit of 2D TMDCs and graphene provides a straightforward, effective means of continuously tuning electronic and optical properties; this has led to the bourgeoning research on optoelectronic devices based on strain-induced modification of optical and electronic properties39.
> 
> 近期的理论和实验工作表明，应变在调控TMDCs和石墨烯的电子与光学性质方面也能发挥重要作用28–32。与传统的半导体对应物相比34–36，TMDCs具有卓越的强度33，这允许施加较大的应变来实现能带结构操控。1921年，Griffith提出，完美的（无缺陷）弹性晶体的理论断裂强度约为其杨氏模量的九分之一37。受此启发，研究人员在无缺陷、原子级厚度的二维材料这一沃土中寻求这一理论断裂强度的上限。例如，密度泛函理论（DFT）计算和基于原子力显微镜（AFM）针尖的实验表明，自由悬浮的MoS2单层的断裂应力约为其杨氏模量的八分之一，接近Griffith理论所建议的理论极限34,35,37,38。例如，MoS2单层在断裂时表现出大于10%的最大应变，而块状硅通常在≤1.5%的应变下断裂39。虽然宏观均匀的大尺度应变是可以实现的，但非均匀应变提供了一种在纳米尺度上局域创建二维TMDCs中空间变化的能带结构的方法。例如，MoS2中的褶皱产生了这种局域变化的应变40,41。与二维TMDCs类似，石墨烯也能承受非常大的弹性应变（高达25%），可与Griffith理论极限相媲美（注意，石墨烯的杨氏模量远大于单层TMDCs）。因此，石墨烯也被认为是研究二维材料中应变工程的绝佳平台42。二维TMDCs和石墨烯卓越的应变极限为连续调控电子和光学性质提供了一种直接有效的手段；这导致了基于应变诱导光电性质改变的光电器件研究的蓬勃发展39。 **Young's modulus**: 杨氏模量，描述材料刚性的一种弹性常数，定义为应力与应变之比。 **Density-functional theory (DFT)**: 密度泛函理论，一种基于电子密度而非多体波函数计算材料的电子结构的量子力学方法。 **Atomic force microscope (AFM)**: 原子力显微镜，通过探测针尖与样品表面之间的相互作用力来进行表面形貌和力学性质测量的仪器。 **Inhomogeneous strain**: 非均匀应变，在空间上分布不均匀的应变场，常导致局部带隙变化。

> # 13. Many intriguing theoretical studies and experimental measurements of strained 2D materials have demonstrated how strains modify electronic and optical properties. For example, a direct-to-indirect bandgap transition and a semiconductor-to-metal phase transition are expected to occur in monolayer MoS2 under ~2% uniaxial tensile strain and 10–15% biaxial tensile strain, respectively43–45. Theoretically, strain-induced changes in the optical bandgap of 2D TMDCs (larger than 1 eV)43 enable nondestructive spectroscopic investigations of the effect of different components of strain on their mechanical, electronic, optical, and chemical properties. Similarly, the electronic and optical properties of strained graphene also strongly depend on the strain distribution. In fact, a large strain can drastically modify the electronic band structure of graphene, and even give rise to the appearance of an optical bandgap46 and a significant change in the optical conductivity47. Therefore, strain engineering of both 2D TMDCs and graphene can play a significant role in understanding the electronic and optical properties of 2D materials, and their device applications. Since many review articles have summarized the fundamental properties48,49, synthesis approaches3,48,49, and optoelectronics applications48–51 of unstrained TMDCs and graphene, in this review, we mainly concentrate on the effects of strain on intrinsic optical properties, strain-induced optical effects, and their applications. We first present an overview of the linear theory of elasticity and effective low-energy Hamiltonians to facilitate the understanding of the peculiar properties induced by strains in TMDCs and graphene. Next, we introduce both optical and electronic properties of pristine TMDCs and graphene, as well as strain-induced optical effects, such as the tuneable PL of TMDCs and Raman modes of graphene under different types of strains. Then, we describe some current strain engineering techniques, and consider their applications and achievable strain levels. Finally, we discuss a series of important photonic applications of strain-induced optical effects in 2D TMDCs and graphene, and provide an outlook for future work in this fast-growing area.

> # 31. TMDCs exhibit brilliant PL and absorption spectra because of their excellent luminescent properties (stemming from their large intrinsic bandgaps), which can be used to investigate and characterize their exotic optical properties. The unit cell of the crystal structure of monolayer TMDCs consists of transition metal atoms sandwiched between two layers of chalcogen atoms-typically forming a trigonal prismatic structure, as depicted in Fig. 1a. In general, the optical properties of TMDC monolayers are predominantly determined by the direct bandgap at the K and K′ points in the BZ, where the excitonic effect resulting from the strong Coulomb interaction between electron–hole pairs exists and plays a significant role in the optical spectrum resonance response (the sharp linewidth is determined by the lifetime of radiative excitons)61,62. We begin this section by briefly introducing some fundamental aspects of excitons in TMDCs. An exciton, a hydrogen-like quasiparticle, is a bound state of an electron and a hole that are attracted to each other by the electrostatic Coulomb force, as depicted in Fig. 1b (refs. 63–65). This creates an optical bandgap significantly smaller than the electronic bandgap, e.g., 1.93 and 2.15 eV for the optical bandgap and electronic bandgap of 2D MoS2 (ref. 66). The discovery of A and B excitons in MoS2 in 2010 motivated researchers to focus on the fine structure of TMDC monolayers associated with electron spin12. The electronic spin–orbit interaction, originating from the d orbitals of heavy metal atoms, leads to splitting of both the conduction and valence bands. As a consequence, two direct optical transitions are allowed when the electron and hole states have identical spin, while the other two are forbidden due to the misalignment of their spin momenta at the K (K′) valley. The two optically allowed transitions are “bright excitons”, as shown in Fig. 1c (black arrows), and the other two are “dark excitons”. The electronic band landscape depicted in Fig. 1d unambiguously demonstrates the existence of A and B excitons associated with the spin splitting of the valence band. The two degenerate K and K′ valleys in TMDC monolayers exhibit opposite spin momenta at the band edges. This and the spin splitting of the conduction and valence bands (originating from inversion symmetry breaking) give rise to the unique valley-dependent optical locking effect in these materials. Thus, the valley-dependent selection rules allow the binary information stored in each valley to be addressed by circularly polarized light, enabling potential applications in communications that exploit the valley pseudospin degree of freedom17. Evidence of A and B excitons in spectroscopy experiments has been seen in the absorption spectra of single and bilayer MoS2 (black lines in Fig. 1e).

> ## 34. Electronic band structures of strained 2D TMDCs
> 
> 应变二维TMDCs的电子能带结构
> 
> # 35. The intriguing optical properties of 2D materials are related to their complex electronic band structures73. As noted in the previous section, the dependence of the Hamiltonians of 2D materials on external strains can be used to identify strain-dependent effects in the electronic band structure. For example, both DFT and GW calculations predict a decrease in the bandgap in monolayer MoS2 with increasing tensile strain32, and suggest that a semiconductor-to-metal phase transition can occur under very large strains43. As illustrated in Fig. 2a, the impact of mechanical deformation on the electronic band structure is especially noticeable at the K point in the BZ, where the intrinsic direct bandgap decreases with increasing strain. Meanwhile, the top of the valence band shifts slightly upwards at the Γ point, stemming from the decrease in the orbital overlap between the metal and sulfur atoms associated with strain-induced changes in the bond distance. At sufficiently large strain, the valence band maximum moves from the K to Γ point, leading to a direct-to-indirect bandgap transition32. In addition to these uniform strain effects, wrinkling in MoS2 creates a nonuniform strain distribution that can reduce the direct bandgap (consistent with TB calculations)41.
> 
> 二维材料的奇异光学性质与其复杂的电子能带结构有关73。如前一节所述，二维材料哈密顿量对外部应变的依赖性可用于识别电子能带结构中的应变依赖效应。例如，DFT和GW计算均预测随着拉伸应变增加，单层MoS2的带隙减小32，并表明在非常大的应变下可能发生半导体-金属相变43。如图2a所示，机械变形对电子能带结构的影响在BZ的K点处尤为明显，那里的本征直接带隙随应变增加而减小。同时，价带顶在Γ点轻微上移，这是由于与应变引起的键长变化相关的金属和硫原子轨道重叠减少所致。在足够大的应变下，价带最大值从K点移动到Γ点，导致直接-间接带隙转变32。除了这些均匀应变效应外，MoS2中的褶皱产生了非均匀应变分布，可减小直接带隙（与TB计算结果一致）41。 **GW calculation**: GW近似，一种基于格林函数和屏蔽库仑相互作用的多体微扰理论方法，用于更准确地计算准粒子能带结构。

> ## 36. Strain tuning of the photoluminescence, absorption, and bandgap transition
> 
> 光致发光、吸收和带隙转变的应变调控

> # 37. As noted above, low-symmetry 2D TMDCs provide an excellent platform to continuously tune the electronic band structures and optical properties via external strains, as seen in many spectroscopy experiments28,74,75. For example, the peak positions in both the PL and absorption spectra of 2D TMDCs show a linear redshift for both the A and B excitons, when homogeneous tensile strain is applied (Fig. 2b). The experimental results shown in Fig. 2c reveal that the redshift rates in the absorption spectrum of monolayer MoS2 are −64 ± 5 meV/% (/% means per percent of tensile strain) for the A exciton and −68 ± 5 meV/% for the B exciton29. In addition, redshift rates of −54 ± 2 and −50 ± 3 meV/% are obtained for the A and B excitons in monolayer WSe2, respectively74. In addition to shifts in absorption peaks, PL spectral peaks also exhibit an approximately linear dependence on tensile strain75; for example, Fig. 2d shows redshift rates of −45 ± 7 meV/% for the A exciton in MoS2 monolayers, and −53 ± 10 meV/% for the A exciton and 129 ± 20 meV/% for the indirect bandgap (marked as “I” in the insert) in MoS2 bilayers28. Figure 2e shows a linear blueshift of the peak in trilayer MoS2 on a piezoelectric substrate with the application of a compressive biaxial strain76. In addition to the redshift, when the strain is sufficiently large, a direct-to-indirect bandgap transition or an indirect-to-direct bandgap transition can be induced in TMDCs. For example, an indirect peak emerges when the strain is larger than 2.5% in strained WS2 monolayers77, while an indirect-to-direct transition occurs in strained WSe2 bilayers with an obvious enhancement of the PL intensity at 0.73% uniaxial tensile strain (Fig. 2f)78. Motivated by the aforementioned controllable optical properties, the variation in the measurable strain-related parameters with temperature79, input laser intensity79, and voltage76 can also be used to quantify the strains on TMDCs; this further broadens the potential optoelectronic, as well as strain sensor applications.

> ## 38. Exciton funnel effect and spontaneous emission enhancement
> 
> 激子漏斗效应与自发辐射增强
> 
> # 39. Apart from homogenous in-plane strains, local inhomogeneous strains can also be induced by local strain engineering, providing other exciting avenues for tailoring distinctive optical properties of 2D materials on the nanoscale. As a concrete example, the electronic band structures of a zigzag MoS2 ribbon calculated using the TB model with periodic boundary conditions are plotted in Fig. 3a. The corresponding bandgap decreases as the strain increases, modifying the electronic band structure on the nanoscale. As a result, the excitons drift hundreds of nanometers to the lower bandgap regions on the top of the wrinkles before recombination, as depicted in Fig. 3b; this is referred to as the “funnel effect”. The PL spectrum of the A exciton on top of four different wrinkles (Fig. 3c) demonstrates that the A exciton can be confined by local strains41. The funnelling of photogenerated excitons toward regions of higher strain has potential for many applications in diverse fields, such as single-photon sources80 for quantum networks and communications and solar cells44 for photovoltaic devices. When a homogeneous strain is applied to two- or three-layer TMDCs, a rapid increase in the PL intensity can be observed (Fig. 2f, e), which can be attributed to the transition from an indirect to direct band structure. In addition, TMDCs can be designed as ultracompact quantum light emitters under local strain (due to tight exciton localization) to produce large spontaneous emission enhancements and very sharp spectral lines at several wavelengths. For example, depositing monolayer WSe2 on a rough metal (Ag) surface creates strain-induced quantum emitters at Ag islands and nanoparticles (as shown in Fig. 3d), and their emission is largely enhanced by the localized surface plasmon resonances (LSPRs) of the Ag nanostructures through the Purcell effect80. A finite-difference time-domain simulation showed an ~30-fold field enhancement at the tip of a Ag nanocone excited by a dipole source, which induces large decay rate enhancements. The emission lines (Fig. 3f) corresponding to selected positions on the WSe2 on the rough Ag surface (Fig. 3e) show very sharp PL peaks (linewidths much narrower than those of the unstrained material). This phenomenon motivated the rapid growth of research on TMDC-based single-photon emission on metallic substrates.

> ## 46. Phase transition of TMDCs engineered by strain
> 
> 应变调控的TMDCs相变
> 
> # 47. Many TMDCs exhibit different crystal structures (polymorphs) for various deposition or postprocessing conditions. These polymorphs retain the general MX2 (X-M-X trilayer) structure, but exhibit different chalcogen coordination structures around the transition metal atoms. The most common structures are the 2H (trigonal prismatic coordination) phase, 1T (octahedral coordination) phase, and 1T′ (distorted octahedral coordination) phase84. For group-VI TMDC monolayers, theoretical predictions showed that the most stable phase under ambient conditions is 2H (except for in WTe285)—these materials are semiconductors with bandgaps in the 1–2 eV range. On the other hand, 1T and 1T′ tend to be metallic. The coexistence of metallic and semiconducting TMDC monolayer polymorphs has spurred studies of phase transitions in these systems. The existence of metal–insulator transitions under or close to ambient conditions suggests possible nonvolatile information storage applications85. A strain-driven semiconductor-to-metal (2H-1T′) transition of MoTe2 under room temperature was reported in 2016 (ref. 86; such a reversible phase transition is shown in Fig. 3j). Given that the 2H and 1T′ phases of monolayer MoTe2 present different Raman signatures and peaks (140 cm−1 for the 1T′ phase and 230 cm−1 for the 2H phase), the Raman spectra could be used to identify the occurrence of phase transitions (Fig. 3k). In this experiment, a thin film of MoTe2 was transferred onto a substrate patterned with cavities of different diameters. The MoTe2 monolayers suspended over the cavities were then subjected to external tensile strains using AFM tips. Evidence of a phase transition from 2H to 1T′ was only observed in the suspended regions. These suspended areas showed 1T′ Raman signals (140 cm−1 in the top panel of Fig. 3l), while only 2H MoTe2 signals were found in the supported (unstrained) areas (230 cm−1 in the bottom panel of Fig. 3l). The application of strain reduced the phase transition temperature from 855 °C to room temperature in this experiment. Thus, phase-change-related TMDC electrical and optical property manipulation at room temperature may find applications in extremely sensitive optical and electrical sensors86.

> # 48. Optical behavior of unstrained graphene
> 
> 未应变石墨烯的光学行为
> 
> # 49. Many intriguing physical properties of graphene can be understood by applying fundamental knowledge of topology87, differential geometry88, and quantum electrodynamics89. Graphene is composed of carbon atoms arranged in a hexagonal structure, as shown in Fig. 4a. This structure can be described as a triangular lattice with a two-atom basis (labeled black points and white points), which can be called A atoms and B atoms. In this structure, there is contact between the bonding (π) and antibonding (π*) orbitals at each K and K′ energy valley (only considering hopping between the nearest-neighbor atomic sites). The A (B) atoms generate a triangular A (B) sublattice that can be considered as an additional degree of freedom, namely, “pseudospin”90. Calculations on pristine graphene showed that the electronic band structure exhibits a linearly tapered dispersion relationship in the vicinity of the K and K′ points (Fig. 4b)90. The zero bandgap at these Dirac (K, K′) points endows graphene with peculiar electrical properties, e.g., an outstanding carrier mobility. Compared to the high absorptivity of TMDC monolayers (~15%)91, intrinsic graphene exhibits zero bandgap at the Dirac points, and a poor out-of-plane absorptivity of ~2.3% (ref. 89). These properties can be modified by doping to tune the Fermi level92, or by stacking graphene and dielectric layers (via self-assembly) to form a metamaterial grating structure that transforms incident spatial light into waveguided modes93 (where the in-plane absorption of graphene can be up to 10 dB due to the large area covered by graphene with a loss of 0.23 dB/μm (ref. 94)). In this section, we review some peculiar optical properties of unstrained graphene.
> 
> 石墨烯的许多奇异物理性质可通过应用拓扑学87、微分几何88和量子电动力学89的基础知识来理解。石墨烯由排列成六方结构的碳原子组成，如图4a所示。该结构可描述为具有两原子基（标记为黑点和白点）的三角晶格，可称为A原子和B原子。在此结构中，每个K和K′能谷处的成键（π）和反键（π*）轨道之间存在接触（仅考虑最近邻原子位点之间的跃迁）。A（B）原子产生一个三角A（B）子晶格，可视为一个额外的自由度，即“赝自旋”90。对原始石墨烯的计算表明，电子能带结构在K和K′点附近表现出线性锥形色散关系（图4b）90。这些狄拉克点（K, K′）处的零带隙赋予石墨烯奇特的电学性质，例如出色的载流子迁移率。与TMDC单层的高吸收率（~15%）91相比，本征石墨烯在狄拉克点处表现出零带隙，且面外吸收率很低，约为2.3%（参考文献89）。这些性质可通过掺杂来调节费米能级92，或通过堆叠石墨烯和介电层（通过自组装）形成超材料光栅结构，将入射空间光转化为波导模式93（其中，由于石墨烯覆盖面积大，损耗为0.23 dB/μm，其面内吸收可达10 dB（参考文献94））。在本节中，我们回顾未应变石墨烯的一些奇特光学性质。 **Carrier mobility**: 载流子迁移率，载流子在单位电场作用下的平均漂移速度，是衡量材料导电性能的重要参数。 **Fermi level**: 费米能级，绝对零度下电子占据的最高能态的能量，决定材料的电学和光学性质。 **Metamaterial**: 超材料，具有天然材料所不具备的超常物理性质的人工复合材料，通常由周期性的亚波长结构组成。

> ## 50. Raman spectroscopy of unstrained graphene
> 
> 未应变石墨烯的拉曼光谱
> 
> # 51. Because of its distinctive electronic properties, Raman spectroscopy is the most effective technique for illustrating the optical properties of graphene. We begin this section by considering the phonon dispersion of graphene, which is essential to understand the Raman spectra. The six phonon modes existing in graphene can be classified into three types: two in-plane optical phonons, two in-plane acoustic phonons, and two out-of-plane phonons (one optical and the other acoustic). Several peaks in the Raman spectrum of graphene originate from these different phonon modes, such as G, D, and G′ (also called 2D in some articles).
> 
> 由于其独特的电子性质，拉曼光谱是阐明石墨烯光学性质的最有效技术。我们从石墨烯的声子色散开始本节，这对理解拉曼光谱至关重要。石墨烯中存在的六种声子模式可分为三类：两个面内光学声子、两个面内声学声子和两个面外声子（一个光学声子和一个声学声子）。石墨烯拉曼光谱中的几个峰源自这些不同的声子模式，例如G峰、D峰和G′峰（在某些文章中也称为2D峰）。 **Acoustic phonon**: 声学声子，低频长波长的晶格振动模式，相邻原子振动相位相同，类似于声波传播。 **Optical phonon**: 光学声子，高频的晶格振动模式，相邻原子振动相位相反，在极性晶体中可与光场耦合。
> 
> # 52. The G peak is caused by the in-plane vibration of sp2-bonded carbon; it is associated with the doubly degenerate phonon mode, in which the in-plane optical phonons vibrate parallel and perpendicular to the A–B carbon atom bond (Fig. 4a) at the center of the BZ. The D peak is usually considered to be a disordered vibration, which depends on the incident wavelength7 and requires a defect to activate it95. Thus, the D peak can be used to characterize structural defects (including edges) in graphene. The G′ peak is the second-order Raman peak of the double phonon resonance, in which the two in-plane optical phonons vibrate perpendicular to the A–B carbon atom bond and reflects the number of layers in multilayer graphene96, the degree of susceptibility (elastic constant) to strain30,97, and the stacking order of graphene layers7. In addition, the weak D′ peak is related to disorder-induced intravalley inelastic scattering processes. For example, Fig. 4c shows a typical Raman spectrum of graphene measured with a laser excitation energy of 2.41 eV (ref. 7); the G and G′ peaks are near 1582 and 2700 cm−1, respectively. If multiple edges or defects exist in graphene, then the D and D′ peaks would be at ~1350 and 1620 cm−1, respectively. Figure 4d shows a schematic illustration of the underlying Raman peak formation physics.
> 
> G峰由sp2键合碳的面内振动引起；它与双重简并的声子模式相关，其中面内光学声子在BZ中心沿平行和垂直于A–B碳原子键的方向振动（图4a）。D峰通常被认为是无序振动，依赖于入射波长7，并需要缺陷来激活它95。因此，D峰可用于表征石墨烯中的结构缺陷（包括边缘）。G′峰是双声子共振的二阶拉曼峰，其中两个面内光学声子垂直于A–B碳原子键振动，并反映了多层石墨烯的层数96、对应变的敏感度（弹性常数）30,97以及石墨烯层的堆叠顺序7。此外，弱的D′峰与无序诱导的谷内非弹性散射过程相关。例如，图4c显示了用2.41 eV激光激发能测量的石墨烯的典型拉曼光谱（参考文献7）；G和G′峰分别位于约1582和2700 cm-1处。如果石墨烯中存在多个边缘或缺陷，则D和D′峰将分别位于约1350和1620 cm-1处。图4d显示了基础拉曼峰形成物理的示意图。

> ## 53. Optical transmission and absorption of unstrained graphene
> 
> 未应变石墨烯的光学透射与吸收
> 
> # 54. The light transmission and absorption of graphene related to electron and photon interactions have attracted considerable attention. Because the high-frequency optical conductivity under the Dirac fermion approximation in graphene is universal (equal to e2/4ħ for all hω > 2E_F, where E_F is the Fermi energy)53,98–100, the optical response of graphene is limited by the fine-structure constant α = e2/ħc ≈ 1/137, which describes the coupling between light and relativistic electrons in quantum electrodynamics89. For example, the optical transmittance and reflectance of graphene are given by T = (1 + 1/2πα)^–2 and R = 1/4π^2α^2T, respectively, under normal incidence. As a result, the graphene opacity ((1 − T) ≈ πα) is light frequency-independent. The experimental light transmittance spectrum in the visible range is shown in Fig. 4e. Although atomically thick graphene exhibits an absorptivity of only ~2.3% for white light89, its transmittance should be tunable through external strains.
> 
> 石墨烯与电子和光子相互作用相关的光透射和吸收引起了相当大的关注。由于在狄拉克费米子近似下，石墨烯的高频光学电导率是普适的（对于所有hω > 2EF，等于e2/4ħ，其中EF是费米能）53,98–100，石墨烯的光学响应受限于精细结构常数α = e2/ħc ≈ 1/137，该常数描述了量子电动力学中光与相对论性电子的耦合89。例如，在正入射下，石墨烯的光学透射率和反射率分别由T = (1 + 1/2πα)–2和R = 1/4π2α2T给出。因此，石墨烯的不透明度（(1 − T) ≈ πα）与光频率无关。可见光范围内的实验光透射谱如图4e所示。尽管原子级厚度的石墨烯对白光的吸收率仅为约2.3%89，但其透射率应可通过外部应变进行调控。 **Fine-structure constant**: 精细结构常数，描述电磁相互作用强度的基本物理常数，约等于1/137，在量子电动力学中起核心作用。

> ## 56. Electronic band structure of strained graphene
> 
> 应变石墨烯的电子能带结构
> 
> # 57. Although pristine graphene exhibits distinctive electronic and optical properties, its gapless electronic band structure greatly limits its practical optoelectronic applications. However, strain engineering can be applied to produce a range of appealing properties, such as strain-induced opening of the bandgap and formation of a giant pseudomagnetic field. For uniaxial strain exceeding 20%, the bandgap of graphene opens (theoretically predicted by TB model calculations57). A combination of shear and uniaxial strain can open the gap from 0 to 0.9 eV under a reversible and more accessible deformation range of 12–17% (ref. 58). The gap opens at various points in the BZ of graphene under combined shear and uniaxial strain of ε = 15% except for strain application along the armchair direction101. Strain also modifies the magnetic behavior of graphene. Designed distortion of the graphene lattice induces a very large, (nearly) uniform pseudomagnetic field and pseudo quantum Hall effect46. For example, the application of a modest strain field with triangular symmetry creates a uniform pseudomagnetic field, which can be as large as 10–10^2 T in graphene102. Similar strain-induced pseudomagnetic fields are also predicted in TMDCs; e.g., in MoS2, the gaps between the Landau levels should scale as hω_c/K_B ≈ 2.7 B_0^(1/2) T K (ref. 103).
> 
> 虽然原始石墨烯表现出独特的电子和光学性质，但其无带隙的电子能带结构极大地限制了其实际的光电应用。然而，应变工程可用于产生一系列吸引人的性质，例如应变诱导的带隙打开和巨赝磁场的形成。对于超过20%的单轴应变，石墨烯的带隙会打开（由TB模型计算理论预测57）。在12–17%的可逆且更易实现的形变范围内，剪切应变和单轴应变的组合可使带隙从0打开到0.9 eV（参考文献58）。在15%的组合剪切和单轴应变下，石墨烯的BZ中多个点会打开带隙，但沿扶手椅方向施加应变时除外101。应变还改变了石墨烯的磁行为。设计扭曲的石墨烯晶格可诱导出非常大的、（近乎）均匀的赝磁场和赝量子霍尔效应46。例如，施加具有三角形对称性的适度应变场可产生均匀的赝磁场，在石墨烯中可高达10–102 T（参考文献102）。在TMDCs中也预言了类似的应变诱导赝磁场；例如，在MoS2中，朗道能级之间的间隙应遵循hωc/KB ≈ 2.7 B01/2 T K（参考文献103）。 **Pseudomagnetic field**: 赝磁场，由非均匀应变引起的有效磁场，仅作用于谷电子，不违反时间反演对称性，类似于真实磁场对电子的作用。 **Quantum Hall effect**: 量子霍尔效应，在强磁场下二维电子气中霍尔电导呈现量子化平台的效应。 **Landau level**: 朗道能级，在磁场中运动的电子所具有的量子化能量本征值，形成分立能级。

> ## 58. Strain tuning and splitting of Raman resonances
> 
> 拉曼共振的应变调控与分裂
> 
> # 59. Strain engineering in graphene has a longer history than that in TMDCs, which has enabled various applications. Depending on the straining techniques, graphene optical signals (e.g., the Raman resonance) can be indirectly modified by electrical signals104, gas pressure105, and even sound waves106. As a result of the modified phonon dispersion and electronic band structure, both theoretical analysis and experiments have demonstrated two common characteristics in the Raman spectra: strain-induced shifting and splitting of Raman spectral peaks (both of which depend on the direction and magnitude of the applied strain). Experimental results showed that the G and G′ bands in graphene undergo linear redshifts (blueshifts), as a function of applied tensile (compressive) strain. More specifically, when the C–C bond length or angle in graphene is changed under strain, the hexagonal symmetry of graphene is destroyed, removing the degeneracy of the two optical phonons at the Γ point in the BZ, and the G band splits into two subbands (called G+ and G−). Therefore, for sufficiently large, asymmetric, or nonuniform local strain, G band splitting can be observed107–109. Similar splitting of the G′ (2D) band has also been reported30,31,97,110. We now present some representative experimental results on Raman spectral peak shifting and band splitting. The results of a typical experiment are illustrated in Fig. 5b, c, which show both shifting and splitting of the G and G′ (2D) bands. Figure 5d shows a schematic of the orthogonal eigenvectors of the G+ and G− modes, as determined from density-functional perturbation theory109. The redshift coefficients for the G′ (2D), G+, and G− bands under tensile strain are −64 cm−1/%, −10.8 cm−1/%, and −31.7 cm−1/%, respectively109. Interestingly, splitting of the G band and a blueshift also occur when graphene is subjected to compressive strain (Fig. 5c)111. The magnitude of the shift and G band splitting is more severe under tension than compression (Fig. 5c), as indicated by the subband shift coefficients (−31.4 cm−1/% for the G+ band and −9.6 cm−1/% for the G− band under tension; 22.3 cm−1/% for the G+ band and 5.5 cm−1/% for the G− band under compression). In some specific experiments (without band splitting), the shift coefficient for the G′ band is much larger than that for the G band (−27.8 cm−1/% for the G′ band and −14.2 cm−1/% for the G band under uniaxial tension112; −160.3 cm−1/% for the G′ band and −57.3 cm−1/% for the G band under biaxial tension97). With increased strain magnitude, the 2D (G′) peak also splits into two peaks (labeled 2D+ and 2D−) under homogeneous uniaxial strains (Fig. 5b); this is associated with anisotropic modifications of the phonon dispersion and distorted Dirac cones. A significant enhancement of Raman signals, i.e., surface-enhanced Raman scattering (SERS), can be observed when graphene is placed on nanoscale rough metal surfaces, or vice versa. Two main physical mechanisms are responsible for this phenomenon. On the one hand, the LSPRs of subwavelength metal nanoparticles can confine and enhance the incident electromagnetic fields at the dielectric/metal interface. On the other hand, nanoparticles can induce local inhomogeneous strains in graphene that further modify the phonon dispersion. Here, two examples are introduced to elucidate these phenomena. First, graphene is placed on corrugated silver films with different surface roughnesses (Fig. 5f, g; ref. 113). From these figures, it can be concluded that the peaks of the G and 2D bands are enhanced and redshifted (Fig. 5f), and that the G band splits (Fig. 5g) with increasing roughness amplitude (in the 0.83–7.14 nm range). The second example108 concerns the Raman spectra of pristine graphene, and four different morphologies of Au nanostructures deposited on graphene (Fig. 5h), where morphology-dependent Raman intensity enhancement and G band splitting are also observed. These two examples indicate that the Raman response of graphene can be controlled by both local electromagnetic environment and strain engineering.

> ## 60. Anisotropic optical response of strained graphene
> 
> 应变石墨烯的各向异性光学响应
> 
> # 61. As discussed above, the optical transmission and absorption of unstrained graphene only depend on the fine-structure constant irrespective of the frequency or polarization of incident light in the visible spectrum. From a theoretical point of view, the striking optical properties of graphene, such as graphene plasmons and magneto-optical effects (Faraday rotation), are related to the optical conductivity. In 2010, Pellegrino et al. studied strain-induced variation in the optical conductivity of graphene. In this work, calculations were performed within the TB approximation to predict the frequency dependence of the longitudinal optical conductivity as a function of strain47. They reported the longitudinal optical conductivity (determining the optical absorption) as a function of the frequency (polar axis) and polarization (polar angle) of incident linearly polarized light for different applied uniaxial strains (Fig. 6a–c)47. They observed that the optical conductivity is isotropic and exhibits a maximum at the Van Hove singularity under vanishing strain (Fig. 6a). However, the application of strain induces anisotropy in the optical conductivity (Fig. 6b, c), implying that the optical transmission and absorption of strained graphene depend on the polarization of incident light. Later, in 2014, an experiment114 verified the tuneable strain-induced anisotropic optical conductivity of graphene. This optical absorption measurement setup is depicted in Fig. 6d, where the sample is strained by bending. This work directly demonstrated controllable optical anisotropy in graphene, i.e., polarization-dependent transparency, as depicted in Fig. 6e, where the tuneable optical conductivity is evidenced by the different transmittances as a function of strain. This is a new approach for manipulating the optical properties of graphene through strain engineering, possibly paving the way for new practical applications, e.g., optical isolators.

> ### 76. Thermal expansion
> 
> 热膨胀法
> 
> # 77. A biaxial strain can be applied using the idea of differential thermal expansion (Fig. 8a (ref. 121)). This requires a large difference in the thermal expansion coefficients of the 2D materials and the substrate. In the example, in Fig. 8a, the thermal expansion coefficient of the 2D material exceeds that of the substrate. When the 2D material is strongly adhered to the substrate and the system if heated or cooled, the expansion difference between these materials generates a homogeneous tensile biaxial strain79,121. While the thermal expansion coefficient is a symmetric second-rank tensor, the thermal strain will be (balanced) equal in two orthogonal directions if the substrate is amorphous, cubic, or has a (0001) surface in a hexagonal crystal or a (001) surface in a tetragonal crystal. There are two obvious deficiencies of this method: (1) the modest magnitude of the induced strain (a few tenths of a percent) and (2) the difficulty in studying the temperature dependence of a property that depends on strain.

> ### 78. Piezoelectric straining
>

> 
> # 79. Piezoelectric materials are strained under the application of an applied external electric field. > ### 88. Nanostructure support
> 
> 纳米结构支撑法
> 
> # 89. In this class of techniques, the morphology of the substrate must be patterned prior to transferring the 2D material. This can be accomplished by positioning nanopillars126–129, nanorods130, etched nanoholes131, or other nanostructures on a flat surface, by producing rough substrates80,113 or by substrate patterning via nanolithography methods before transferring the 2D materials. In all of these cases, the non-flatness of the substrate and the conformality of the 2D materials induce strains in the 2D material via bending/stretching. These types of approaches have been applied in recent years due to the rising interest in single-photon emission, which is a key element in optical quantum computation. For the sake of clarity, two typical examples are introduced here, in which nanopillars and rough metallic surfaces are used. In the first example126, exfoliated monolayer WSe2 flakes are deposited on a substrate with a square lattice of Si nanopillars. Figure 9d shows a schematic of the formation of the inhomogeneous local strain, while Fig. 9c, e show an SEM micrograph of the Si nanopillar array and an AFM image of monolayer WSe2 on a Si nanopillar, respectively. In the second example80, a sapphire substrate coated by a thin silver film (200 ± 10 nm) followed by deposition of a 3 nm Al2O3 layer to protect the silver film from oxidation is used as a rough metallic surface to apply local strains to the later transferred WSe2 (ref. 80).

> ## 90. Summary and comparison of strain-induced optical effects and strain engineering techniques
> 
> 应变诱导光学效应与应变工程技术的总结与比较
> 
> # 91. We first provide a brief summary of the optical effects induced by various types of strain (homogeneous uniaxial strain, homogeneous biaxial strain, and local inhomogeneous strain). Table 1 summarizes the spectral shift coefficients and optical properties of TMDCs and graphene under various strains. Among them, homogeneous uniaxial strain is viewed as the easiest-to-access strain technique with the highest achievable reliability, associated with the simplicity of the equipment and the ease of manipulation of the environment. Such an approach is simple to implement through bending28,74,75,115 or elongation77. Moreover, the induced uniaxial strains can be easily quantified and thus controlled. Alternatively, homogeneous biaxial strains in 2D materials can be applied in situations where harsher conditions can be sustained (e.g., high temperature79,121, high electrical voltage76,104, and high laser intensity79). However, such methods typically require more complex experimental instruments and more control. We note that in experiments that produce macroscopic strains, viscoelastic stamps can act as a mechanical clamp to improve the stability, and prevent interfacial sliding between the 2D material and the substrate during straining, when the interfacial adhesion is low (Figs. 7a, c and 8b). However, such an approach requires special attention to avoid the build-up of strain when the carried 2D material is transferred from the viscoelastic stamp to the stretchable substrate. Apart from the techniques for generating homogeneous strain, new approaches for inducing spatially inhomogeneous strains are gaining popularity for application on scales down to tens of nanometers, e.g., wrinkling 2D materials or depositing 2D materials on nanostructures. These approaches include the use of gels as an elastomeric substrate to realize buckling-induced delamination41, where a viscoelastic stamp is used as a sample carrier (to transfer the 2D material to the nanostructure) that can later be washed away (or peeled off)80,108,113,122,127. The local strains induced by these methods are usually much stronger, leading to a significant enhancement of the local photon emission and long lifetimes of excited electrons. Because of these characteristics, such methods are finding increasing uses in the field of 2D-material-based quantum emitters80,127. On the other hand, these methods suffer several disadvantages, including the costly and challenging nanofabrication process, and the lack of an effective model to quantify the local strains.

> ## 97. Artificial atom-based broad-spectrum solar energy funnel
> 
> 基于人工原子的宽光谱太阳能漏斗
> 
> # 98. In 2012, Feng, et al.44 showed (theoretically and numerically) that inducing local elastic strains provides an accessible method to continuously decrease the bandgaps of MoS2 monolayers. This suggests the idea of “artificial atoms” composed of a pressure-sensitive MoS2 monolayer to absorb a wide range of the solar spectrum from 2 to 1.1 eV when the applied biaxial strain increases from 0 to 9%. Photovoltaic devices with such strained MoS2 monolayers with a continuously varying bandgap profile can capture photons over a wide range of the solar spectrum. In 2015, a proof-of-concept implementation of such a photovoltaic “artificial atoms” device demonstrated the tunability of the optical bandgap of MoS2 via local strains. An SEM image of this device and an STM image of an “artificial atom” are shown in Fig. 10d, e, respectively134. Figure 10f shows the corresponding simulation of the local strain distribution, and Fig. 10g exhibits the scanning PL map with peak intensities of the strained MoS2. The subsequent measurements in this work demonstrated achievement of broadband optical absorption from 677 nm (pristine MoS2) to 905 nm (the most highly strained MoS2), covering the whole visible spectrum and the most intensive wavelengths of the solar spectrum.
> 
> 2012年，Feng等人44（从理论和数值上）表明，诱导局域弹性应变提供了一种连续减小MoS2单层带隙的可行方法。这暗示了由压力敏感MoS2单层构成的“人工原子”概念，当施加的双轴应变从0增加到9%时，可吸收从2 eV到1.1 eV的宽范围太阳光谱。具有这种具有连续变化带隙轮廓的应变MoS2单层的光伏器件可以捕获宽范围太阳光谱中的光子。2015年，对这种光伏“人工原子”器件的概念验证实现证明了通过局域应变可调谐MoS2的光学带隙。该器件的SEM图像和“人工原子”的STM图像分别如图10d, e所示134。图10f显示了局域应变分布的相应模拟，图10g展示了应变MoS2的峰值强度扫描PL图。这项工作中的后续测量证明了从677 nm（原始MoS2）到905 nm（最高应变MoS2）的宽带光学吸收，覆盖了整个可见光谱和太阳光谱中强度最大的波长。 **STM**: 扫描隧道显微镜，通过探测隧道电流来获得原子级表面形貌的仪器。

> ## 99. Single-photon emitters
> 
> 单光子发射体
> 
> # 100. Single-photon emitters with long excited state lifetimes and spin coherence times (nanosecond scale) enable promising applications in quantum computation135. In semiconductor single-photon emitters, the allowed quantum interface between stationary spin qubits and propagating single photons makes 2D materials promising candidates for quantum information processing. Many experiments have demonstrated the formation of 2D TMDC-based single-photon sources136–138. In these experiments, the emission linewidth of the quantum dot-like defects in TMDC monolayers is much narrower (typically 0.1 meV) than that of free excitons (10 meV). Weak electrical136 and finite magnetic field137,138-controlled single-photon emissions have also been demonstrated for these artificially engineered TMDC defects. 2D material-based single quantum emitters possess more degrees of freedom for external tuning than their bulk material counterparts. Strained monolayer and bilayer WSe2-based quantum light sources on an array of dielectric nanopillars were recently reported (Fig. 11a–c)127. The enhancement of the spontaneous emission from the strained area (compared to the pristine area) is seen in Fig. 11a, and the second-order photon correlation shown in Fig. 11c unambiguously demonstrates the single-photon emitting behavior of the locally strained WSe2. The authors attributed this single-photon quantum emission to the funnel-like effect in the strained region (Fig. 11b). More interestingly, the PL measurements show a stable linewidth with a much longer decay time (compared to the pristine region) of 2.8 ns in monolayer WSe2 and 4.8 ns in bilayer WSe2. This work demonstrates a new paradigm for the design of single-photon emitters that exploits strain engineering; this is expected to motivate further research on TMDC-based single-photon source integration. More recently, metal surfaces have also been employed to couple surface plasmon polaritons with 2D TMDCs to enhance the single-photon emission80,139. The emission enhancement is mainly attributed to the very large Purcell factor (e.g., up to 551 in the Au mirror case)139, and results in a much shorter decay time (down to ~98 ps) compared with results for a Si substrate127. Finally, 2D material-based single-photon emitters are expected to show more highly tuneable optical properties (via external applied electrical field, magnetic field, local strains, and substrate effects) than their conventional counterparts (e.g., nitrogen-vacancy centers in diamond).
> 
> 具有长激发态寿命和自旋相干时间（纳秒级）的单光子发射体在量子计算中具有广阔的应用前景135。在半导体单光子发射体中，静止自旋量子比特与传播单光子之间的允许量子接口使二维材料成为量子信息处理的有希望的候选者。许多实验已证明基于二维TMDC的单光子源的形成136–138。在这些实验中，TMDC单层中类量子点缺陷的发射线宽远窄于自由激子（10 meV），通常为0.1 meV。弱电学136和有限磁场137,138控制的单光子发射也已在这些人造TMDC缺陷中得到了证明。基于二维材料的单量子发射体与其体材料对应物相比，拥有更多外部调谐的自由度。最近报道了在介电纳米柱阵列上基于应变单层和双层WSe2的量子光源（图11a–c）127。图11a显示了应变区域（与原始区域相比）自发辐射的增强，图11c所示的二阶光子关联明确证明了局域应变WSe2的单光子发射行为。作者将这种单光子量子发射归因于应变区域的漏斗状效应（图11b）。更有趣的是，PL测量显示单层WSe2中具有稳定的线宽和长达2.8 ns的衰减时间（与原始区域相比），双层WSe2中为4.8 ns。这项工作展示了利用应变工程设计单光子发射体的新范式；预计将推动基于TMDC的单光子源集成的进一步研究。更近一些，金属表面也被用于耦合表面等离激元极化激元与二维TMDCs以增强单光子发射80,139。发射增强主要归因于非常大的Purcell因子（例如，在金镜情况下高达551）139，并导致与Si衬底结果相比更短的衰减时间（低至约98 ps）127。最后，基于二维材料的单光子发射体有望表现出比其传统对应物（例如金刚石中的氮-空位中心）更高可调谐性的光学性质（通过外部施加的电场、磁场、局域应变和衬底效应）。 **Single-photon emitter**: 单光子发射体，能够逐个发射单个光子的量子光源，是量子通信和计算的关键组件。 **Surface plasmon polariton (SPP)**: 表面等离激元极化激元，金属表面自由电子与光子耦合形成的混合激发态，具有强近场增强特性。 **Second-order photon correlation**: 二阶光子关联，测量光子发射时间关联性的函数，g²(0)<1表示单光子发射。

> ## 100. High-resolution nanoimaging
> 
> 高分辨率纳米成像
> 
> # 101. A novel hybrid nano-optomechanical tip-enhanced spectroscopy and nanoimaging approach combining the tip-enhanced Raman scattering and tip-enhanced photoluminescence (TEPL) techniques was recently reported140. A WSe2 monolayer grown by physical vapor deposition possesses a large built-in tensile strain, and its PL curve is shown in the lower panel of Fig. 11d (red line) in comparison with that of a strain-relaxed monolayer (blue line). A Au tip was used to apply different forces to release the initial strain of the sample. When the force is sufficiently large, the PL suggests that the strain relaxation is irreversible (see the PL data in the upper panel of Fig. 11d, which correspond to the PL map in the bright region in the upper panel of Fig. 11f). In contrast, a small force applied by the Au tip enables reversible strain release (see the PL curves in Fig. 11e, which correspond to the dark region in the upper panel of Fig. 11f). By combining the PL curves in Fig. 11d, e, this work showed that strain can be manipulated reversibly (elastic) or irreversibly (plastic) on the nanoscale, and that TEPL (Fig. 11g) can be used to systematically investigate strained 2D material morphologies (along with their corresponding electronic and optical properties) at very high resolution (<15 nm)140.
> 
> 最近，一种结合了尖端增强拉曼散射和尖端增强光致发光（TEPL）技术的新型混合纳米光机械尖端增强光谱和纳米成像方法被报道140。通过物理气相沉积生长的WSe2单层具有较大的本征拉伸应变，其PL曲线如图11d下方面板所示（红线），与应变松弛单层（蓝线）进行了比较。使用金尖端施加不同的力来释放样品的初始应变。当力足够大时，PL表明应变松弛是不可逆的（参见图11d上方面板中的PL数据，对应于图11f上方面板中亮区域的PL图）。相比之下，金尖端施加的小力可实现可逆应变释放（参见图11e中的PL曲线，对应于图11f上方面板中的暗区域）。通过结合图11d, e中的PL曲线，这项工作表明，在纳米尺度上应变可以可逆（弹性）或不可逆（塑性）地操控，并且TEPL（图11g）可用于以超高分辨率（<15 nm）系统地研究应变二维材料形貌（及其相应的电子和光学性质）140。 **Tip-enhanced photoluminescence (TEPL)**: 尖端增强光致发光，利用金属纳米尖端产生的局域增强光场来激发并增强样品的光致发光信号，实现超高分辨率成像。

> ## 102. Other potential applications
> 
> 其他潜在应用
> 
> # 103. 2D materials are also widely used in photodetectors, excitonic LEDs, photovoltaic devices, composite materials, sensors, microelectromechanical systems, and even in applications requiring biocompatibility132. For example, in 2007, Bunch et al.141 developed an optical resonator based on a suspended graphene sheet that can be driven either electrically or optically. This structure has potential for a wide range of applications, including pressure sensors, mass sensors, and other optoelectronic devices. In addition, strained graphene also exhibits giant Faraday rotations as a result of the pseudomagnetic field, which opens up opportunities for the design of optical isolators with two strained graphene sheets142. One of the main challenges in using pristine 2D materials in photonic and optoelectronic applications is determining and understanding the excitonic dissociation and related optical responses. However, there is no doubt that defects and/or strain-induced structural changes will play an important role in the performance of related devices. Thus, a deep understanding of this issue is of great importance for enabling the design of high-efficiency optoelectronic devices. To this end, the strain information easily reflected in the optical properties of 2D materials provides a good source for understanding the excitonic fine structures of 2D materials, and their optical properties in different configurations.
> 
> 二维材料还广泛用于光电探测器、激子LED、光伏器件、复合材料、传感器、微机电系统，甚至需要生物相容性的应用中132。例如，2007年，Bunch等人141开发了一种基于悬浮石墨烯片的光学谐振器，该谐振器可通过电学或光学方式驱动。这种结构具有广泛的应用潜力，包括压力传感器、质量传感器和其他光电器件。此外，应变石墨烯还表现出巨大的法拉第旋转，这是由于赝磁场引起的，这为使用两个应变石墨烯片设计光隔离器提供了机会142。在光子学和光电应用中使用原始二维材料的主要挑战之一是确定和理解激子解离以及相关的光学响应。然而，毫无疑问，缺陷和/或应变诱导的结构变化将在相关器件的性能中发挥重要作用。因此，深入理解这一问题对于实现高效光电器件的设计至关重要。为此，二维材料光学性质中容易反映的应变信息为理解二维材料的激子精细结构以及它们在不同构型下的光学性质提供了良好的来源。

> # 104. Summary and outlook
> 
> 总结与展望
> 
> # 105. This review article systematically summarizes many of the recent advances in the application of strain in 2D materials (especially TMDCs and graphene) to modify and control their unique optical properties. We first reviewed the theory of elasticity and effective low-energy Hamiltonians as powerful theoretical tools to better understand the physical properties of 2D materials under macroscopic and microscopic strains, and then examined the effect of strain on their optical properties. This review of the theoretical and experimental literature indicates that strains can be effectively manipulated to modify the electronic band structure of 2D materials to create unexpected (novel and/or exotic) optical properties (e.g., gigantic pseudomagnetic fields). We also summarized techniques for generating different types of strains along with their corresponding photonic applications.
> 
> 本综述文章系统地总结了应变在二维材料（特别是TMDCs和石墨烯）中应用以改变和调控其独特光学性质的许多最新进展。我们首先回顾了弹性理论和有效低能哈密顿量作为强大的理论工具，以更好地理解二维材料在宏观和微观应变下的物理性质，然后考察了应变对其光学性质的影响。对理论和实验文献的综述表明，应变可以被有效操控以改变二维材料的电子能带结构，从而产生意想不到的（新颖和/或奇异的）光学性质（例如巨赝磁场）。我们还总结了产生不同类型应变的技术及其相应的光子学应用。
> 
> ## 106. Challenges
> 
> 挑战
> 
> # 107. Strain engineering, especially local strain engineering, remains a rapidly evolving technology and may provide some of the most promising candidates for next-generation, semiconductor optoelectronic components (in large part because of the small dimensions inherent to 2D materials). Nonetheless, further study (e.g., of exciton transport properties) is still required. To date, local strain engineering has found its greatest application in the field of quantum emitters (see the previous section), but other relevant applications are expected to be developed. Theoretical tools (DFT) widely applied to illustrate and predict 2D material properties encounter difficulties in applications, where the strain is not uniform (due to the large number of atoms involved). This indicates the need for the development of new theoretical tools that can be applied on the appropriate scale for application to strain patterning of 2D materials.
> 
> 应变工程，特别是局域应变工程，仍然是一项快速发展的技术，并可能为下一代半导体光电器件提供一些最有希望的候选者（很大程度上是由于二维材料固有的小尺寸）。尽管如此，仍然需要进一步的研究（例如，激子输运性质）。迄今为止，局域应变工程在量子发射体领域得到了最大的应用（见前一节），但预计将开发出其他相关应用。广泛用于阐明和预测二维材料性质的理论工具（DFT）在应变非均匀的情况下（由于涉及的原子数量巨大）遇到困难。这表明需要开发新的理论工具，能够在适当的尺度上应用于二维材料的应变图案化。
> 
> ## 108. Future perspectives
> 
> 未来展望
> 
> # 109. Apart from the techniques and applications presented above, other interesting work on strain engineering of 2D materials is being performed, e.g., on catalytic143, magnetic144, and electrical145,146 effects. Other related work on strain engineering includes localized strain associated with twin boundaries in bilayer TMDC materials147, and tuneable enhanced light emission and SHG from strained WS2-optical-fiber-nanowire structures148 (for environmentally robust fiber-based sensors), application of 2D van der Waals heterostructures, and superlattices of 2D materials with different (strained) optoelectronic characteristics, etc. Special attention should be paid to understanding the effects of interlayer interactions in van der Waals heterostructures149 when designing coherent, atomically thin superlattices for tuning the PL150. Undoubtedly, atomic-level 2D heterostructures are among the most promising candidates for reviving Moore’s law. The past decades have seen the application of strain as a “key” to opening the bandgap in graphene102,112. However, few recent experimental results suggest that this potential is practically achievable. Recently, Motla et al.151 has taken a step forward by applying a modulated inhomogeneous local asymmetric elastic–plastic strain to graphene using GPa-level laser shocking to induce tunable bandgaps of up to 2.1 eV (ref. 151). This high-energy laser shocking provides another approach for the application of large inhomogeneous strain to 2D materials. However, the photodamage caused by high-energy laser pulses requires additional attention. In conclusion, the flexibility and optical properties of 2D materials (compared to their bulky counterparts) opens the door for the development of potentially important new optical applications. 2D materials are key components in strain-engineered optoelectronic applications.
> 
> 除了上面介绍的技术和应用外，其他有关二维材料应变工程的有趣工作也在进行中，例如，关于催化143、磁性144和电学145,146效应。其他应变工程相关工作包括双层TMDC材料中与孪晶界相关的局域应变147，以及应变WS2-光纤-纳米线结构中的可调谐增强光发射和SHG148（用于环境鲁棒的光纤基传感器），二维范德华异质结构以及具有不同（应变）光电特性的二维材料超晶格的应用等。在设计用于调控PL的相干原子级薄超晶格时，应特别注意理解范德华异质结构中的层间相互作用效应149,150。毫无疑问，原子级二维异质结构是复兴摩尔定律最有希望的候选者之一。过去几十年，应变被视为打开石墨烯带隙的“钥匙”102,112。然而，近期很少有实验结果表明这一潜力是实际可实现的。最近，Motla等人151通过使用GPa级激光冲击对石墨烯施加调制的非均匀局部不对称弹塑性应变，诱导了高达2.1 eV的可调谐带隙，向前迈出了一步（参考文献151）。这种高能激光冲击为对二维材料施加大的非均匀应变提供了另一种方法。然而，由高能激光脉冲引起的光损伤需要额外关注。总之，二维材料的柔韧性和光学性质（与其体块对应物相比）为开发潜在重要的新光学应用打开了大门。二维材料是应变工程光电器件中的关键组件。 **Moore's law**: 摩尔定律，集成电路上可容纳的晶体管数量大约每两年翻一番的经验规律，反映了半导体技术的快速发展。

> ### 啥叫“应变工程”？太奶，这就是给材料“松松骨、抻抻筋”
> 
> 这个“应变”啊，说白了，就是**给材料施加一个外力，把它拉一拉、压一压、或者弄出些褶皱来**。就像您揉面、抻面条一样，面团的形状和内部结构就变了。
> 
> 科学家们发现，这些跟保鲜膜一样薄的二维材料，虽然薄，但特别柔韧，经得起拉伸。这么一拉一压，它们内部的电子结构就会发生改变，从而影响到它们的光学性质，比如：
> 
> -   **发光的颜色可以变**：拉一拉，发的光可能就从红色变成了更深的红色（这叫**红移**）；压一压，可能就变蓝了（这叫**蓝移**）。
> -   **发光的亮暗可以变**：原本不咋发光的材料，给它弄出些褶皱，在特定的地方发光就突然变得特别亮。
> -   **能打开“开关”**：比如石墨烯本来没“开关”，给它使点劲儿拉一拉，说不定就能弄出个“开关”来，让它也能发光。
> 
> 所以，“应变工程”就是科学家的巧手，通过“抻拉揉捏”这些超薄材料，变出各种神奇的光学新花样。

> ### 专业术语大扫盲，太奶咱们一个一个来认

> 
> -   **能带 (Band structure)**：您可以把一种材料想象成一栋大楼，电子就是住在楼里的居民。楼里有一些楼层是允许电子居住的，叫“允带”；有些楼层是禁止居住的，叫“禁带”。这栋楼的高度和楼层布局，就是“能带结构”。**带隙**就是那个“禁带”的宽度，电子想从低楼层跳到高楼层，就得跨过这个宽度。有带隙的是半导体，没带隙的是导体（石墨烯就类似没带隙的）。
> -   **激子 (Exciton)**：光照到材料上，会把一个电子从低楼层（价带）打到高楼层（导带），留下一个空位，这个空位咱们叫“空穴”。这电子和空穴，一个带负电，一个带正电，就像一对小情侣，互相吸引，手拉手一块儿走。这对“情侣”就叫“激子”。这对小情侣最终会复合，电子跳回空穴，多余的能量就以光的形式放出来，这就是我们看到的**光致发光**。
> -   **光致发光 (Photoluminescence, PL)**：这就是前面说的，材料被光照了之后，自己再发出来的光。科学家通过分析这个光的颜色和亮度，就能知道材料内部“激子”的情况，反推出材料被拉得有多紧、压得有多实。
> -   **拉曼光谱 (Raman spectroscopy)**：这就好比是材料的“指纹”。用一种特定颜色的光照材料，材料会把光反弹回来，但反弹回来的光，颜色会有些微小的变化。这个变化就对应着材料内部原子振动的频率，就像人的指纹一样，独一无二。材料被拉伸后，原子振动会变，这个“指纹”也会跟着变，科学家用这个方法来精确测量材料究竟受了多大的力。
> -   **吸收光谱 (Absorption spectroscopy)**：这个是看材料“吃”了哪种颜色的光。把一束白光（包含所有颜色）照过去，看哪些颜色的光被材料吸收了，变暗了。这也能告诉我们材料的带隙和激子等信息。
> -   **二次谐波 (Second-Harmonic Generation, SHG)**：这是一种特殊的光学现象。好比我们唱一首歌，材料听进去后，却唱出了一个高八度的和声。这个和声的强弱和方向，对材料的结构是否对称、是否被扭曲（即受应变）特别敏感，所以也是一种探测微小应变的“听诊器”。
