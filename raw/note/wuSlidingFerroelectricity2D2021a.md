> Near the 100th anniversary of the discovery of ferroelectricity, so-called sliding ferroelectricity has been proposed and confirmed recently in a series of experiments that have stimulated remarkable interest. Such ferroelectricity exists widely and exists only in two-dimensional (2D) van der Waals stacked layers, where the vertical electric polarization is switched by in-plane interlayer sliding. Reciprocally, interlayer sliding and the “ripplocation” domain wall can be driven by an external vertical electric field. The unique combination of intralayer stiffness and interlayer slipperiness of 2D van der Waals layers greatly facilitates such switching while still maintaining environmental and mechanical robustness at ambient conditions. In this perspective, we discuss the progress and future opportunities in this behavior. The origin of such ferroelectricity as well as a general rule for judging its existence are summarized, where the vertical stacking sequence is crucial for its formation. This discovery broadens 2D ferroelectrics from very few material candidates to most of the known 2D materials. Their low switching barriers enable high-speed data writing with low energy cost. Related physics like Moiré ferroelectricity, the ferroelectric nonlinear anomalous Hall effect, and multiferroic coupling are discussed. For 2D valleytronics, nontrivial band topology and superconductivity, their possible couplings with sliding ferroelectricity via certain stacking or Moiré ferroelectricity also deserve interest. We provide critical reviews on the current challenges in this emerging area.
>
>【摘要翻译】在铁电性发现100周年之际，最近在一系列实验中提出并证实了所谓的滑动铁电性，引起了人们极大的兴趣。这种铁电性广泛存在，仅存在于二维范德华叠层中，垂直极化通过层间滑动实现。反过来，层间滑动和“涟漪”畴壁可由外部垂直电场驱动。二维范德华层的层内刚度和层间滑移度的独特组合极大地促进了这种切换，同时在环境条件下仍保持环境和机械鲁棒性。在此视角下，我们讨论了这一行为的进展和未来机遇。总结了这种铁电性的起源和判断其存在的一般规律，其中垂直叠加序列是其形成的关键。这一发现将二维铁电体从极少数的候选材料扩展到大多数已知的二维材料。它们的低开关势垒实现了低能耗的高速数据写入。讨论了云纹铁电性、铁电非线性反常霍尔效应、多铁性耦合等相关物理问题。对于二维valleytronics、非平凡能带拓扑和超导电性，它们可能通过某种堆垛或Moiré铁电性与滑动铁电性耦合也值得关注。我们对这一新兴领域当前面临的挑战进行了批判性审查。

> ## 一、引言
> 
> 在铁电性被发现近百年之际，一种全新的铁电机制——**滑动铁电性 (Sliding Ferroelectricity)**——被提出并迅速得到实验验证，引发了学界的高度关注。该文章由华中科技大学吴梦昊教授与麻省理工学院李巨教授联合撰写，发表于《美国国家科学院院刊》(PNAS)，是一篇对新兴领域的权威性展望。
> 
> **研究的核心问题**在于：传统铁电体在与现代半导体工艺集成时面临严重的界面兼容性问题，而二维范德华材料的垂直极化翻转又受限于原子厚度。滑动铁电性完美地解决了这一矛盾，它通过**层间范德华滑移(In-plane interlayer sliding)** 而非传统的离子垂直位移，实现了垂直电偶极矩的翻转。
> 
> **本文的定位与价值**在于，它并非一篇原始研究论文，而是一篇“前瞻性综述(Perspective)”，系统性地构建了滑动铁电性的理论框架，总结了其普适性判据，梳理了实验验证的里程碑，并极具远见地指出了该领域未来在基础物理（如莫尔铁电性、多铁耦合、非线性反常霍尔效应）和技术应用（如非易失性高速低功耗存储器）上的巨大机遇。对于希望快速进入这一前沿领域的初学者，本文是理解其核心逻辑的绝佳入口。
>

> ## 四、正文核心内容剖析
> 
> ### 1. 研究背景与问题
> 
> 传统铁电存储器面临“**外延生长兼容性**”瓶颈，即铁电薄膜与半导体衬底之间的晶格失配和界面缺陷会严重影响器件性能。二维范德华铁电体因其原子级厚度和无需悬挂键的惰性界面，被视为解决此问题的理想方案。然而，早期发现的二维铁电体（如CuInP₂S₆, In₂Se₃）种类稀少，极化方向多为面内，而更利于器件集成的面外极化材料则更为稀少。根本原因在于，在原子级厚度的二维层内，实现垂直方向的离子位移来翻转极化极为困难。
> 
> ### 2. 核心理论模型：滑动铁电性
> 
> 作者通过构建一个精巧的理论模型，彻底解决了上述困境：
> 
> -   **微观起源**：以AB堆垛的双层BN为例，两层不等价，导致层间电荷转移，形成由B原子指向相邻层N原子的面外电偶极矩。
> -   **翻转机制**：极化翻转并非通过离子垂直移动，而是通过层间一个B-N键长（**t∥**）的横向滑移完成。滑移后，上层N原子移至下层B原子上方，极化方向反转。这种机制是颠覆性的。
> -   **普适性判据**：一个体系若具备滑动铁电性，需满足：该体系是非中心对称的，且其通过水平镜像操作得到的极化反转状态，可以通过层间平移操作来实现。这一判据将滑动铁电性推广至绝大多数二维材料。
> -   **动力学与畴壁**：翻转过程涉及一种特殊的畴壁——**ripplocation**。由于范德华层间耦合弱，层内共价键刚性强，这种畴壁结合了面内位错和面外褶皱，其运动能垒（又称Peierls势垒）极低，且无长程弹性应力场，因此移动极其迅速，能垒仅约单位晶胞毫电子伏特量级。
> 
> ### 3. 实验验证的里程碑
> 
> 文章系统梳理了实验证据链，从特殊到一般，验证了理论的正确性。
> 
> -   **WTe₂ (2018年)**: 首次在金属性少层WTe₂中探测到滑动铁电性。利用石墨烯作为电荷传感器，观测到其电导率随栅压的滞回行为，估算出极化值约0.16 pC/m，居里温度约350 K。
> -   **BN (2020年)**: 两个独立课题组分别在绝缘体BN薄层中观察到滑动铁电性。通过双栅石墨烯探测器或开尔文探针力显微镜，清晰地探测并操控了极化翻转和铁电畴，实验结果与第一性原理计算高度吻合。
> -   **InSe, TMDs (2019-2021年)**: 研究进一步拓展到半导体材料，证明了该现象的普适性。例如，在β-InSe纳米片中观察到典型的PFM振幅“蝴蝶曲线”和相位滞回线，在MoS₂、WS₂等双层中探测到极化翻转。
> 
> ### 4. 拓展的物理现象与讨论
> 
> 基于滑动铁电性的核心思想，文章进一步发散，探讨了其与多个前沿物理领域的交叉：
> 
> -   **莫尔铁电性 (Moiré Ferroelectricity)**: 当两层材料以一个小角度扭转时，形成周期性的莫尔超晶格。不同堆垛区域（AA, AB, BA）具有不同的极化状态，形成了周期排列的纳米级铁电畴网络。这为调控激子、电子关联等提供了全新的平台。
> -   **金属铁电性 (Metallic Ferroelectricity)**: 传统理论认为铁电性与金属性不兼容，因为自由电子会屏蔽外电场。但WTe₂的实验证明，在超薄极限下，电场可以穿透二维金属，实现极化翻转。这为探寻铁电性与超导、等离激元等性质的耦合打开了大门。
> -   **多铁与自旋-轨道耦合 (Multiferroicity & Spin-Orbitronics)**: 将铁磁层与铁电层结合，或利用非磁性但具有强自旋轨道耦合的铁电层，可以实现电场对磁矩、自旋纹理的调控，即“电写磁读”或自旋场效应管。
> -   **铁电非线性反常霍尔效应 (Ferroelectric Nonlinear Anomalous Hall Effect)**: 在WTe₂中，极化翻转会伴随贝里曲率偶极子的反转，导致非线性霍尔电压的符号改变。这为基于贝里曲率的存储器提供了非破坏性读取的新机制。
> 
> ## 五、结论与贡献
> 
> 本文的核心结论是，滑动铁电性是一种广泛存在于二维范德华层状材料中的全新铁电机制，其本质是**通过面内层间滑移实现面外极化翻转**。
> 
> **文章的主要贡献在于**：
> 
> 1.  **理论概念的构建与推广**：系统阐述了滑动铁电性的物理起源，并提出了一个简洁的普适性判据，将二维铁电体的候选材料从寥寥几种极大地拓展到大多数已知的二维材料。
> 2.  **物理图像的澄清**：深刻揭示了其“低集体能垒（易翻转）与高孤立能垒（抗热扰动）”并存的独特动力学特性，并通过“ripplocation”这一概念统一了其微观机制。
> 3.  **研究蓝图的设计**：指出了滑动铁电性与莫尔物理、拓扑物理、多铁性、自旋电子学等前沿领域的交叉融合点，为该领域的未来发展描绘了清晰的路线图。
> 
> ## 六、未来发展方向展望
> 
> 文章在结尾部分提出了该领域存在的挑战和值得探索的方向：
> 
> 1.  **材料筛选与优化**：现有材料的极化强度仍偏低（如BN的2.08 pC/m）。未来需要通过高通量计算，在材料数据库中寻找极化强度更高（如AlN双层，10.29 pC/m）、同时具备高迁移率、合适带隙等优良半导体特性的材料体系。
> 2.  **实际界面问题**：理论模型多基于理想的自支撑状态。在实际器件中，二维材料将与衬底、封装层和电极接触，界面处的离子杂质、电荷掺杂等可能会带来有害的退极化场，影响器件性能。如何通过惰性封装（如BN）等手段维持洁净界面是关键。
> 3.  **可控合成与相工程**：滑动铁电性对堆垛序极其敏感（如AB堆垛有极性，AA'堆垛无极性）。实现大面积、高均一性的特定堆垛相（如3R相）的精准合成，是推动其走向应用的前提。
> 4.  **超快动力学与多场调控**：文章预测了利用光机械效应实现皮秒级超快堆垛转变和极化翻转的可能性。探索光、电、应力等多物理场协同调控堆垛序和铁电畴的新方法，将是一个极具吸引力的方向。
> 5.  **新物理的深入探索**：对金属铁电性、莫尔铁电性与强关联物理（如超导、磁性）的耦合、基于拓扑性质的铁电存储等新兴方向，目前的实验工作还非常初步，亟待深入和系统性的研究。
> 
> ## 七、学术思考与批判性分析
> 
> 作为一篇前瞻性文章，作者清晰勾勒了滑动铁电性的宏伟蓝图，但立论背后仍有一些值得深思的问题：
> 
> 1.  **极化强度的量化定义问题**：文章指出，极化强度高度依赖于层间差分电荷密度，而差分电荷密度如何定义和计算（例如参考态的选取）本身就存在任意性。对于体相材料，极化强度是一个量子化的多值物理量，其绝对值（而非相对变化量）的测量存在根本性困难。那么，文中计算得到的极化值绝对大小（如表1，2所示）在不同测量方案下是否具有鲁棒性？这对其作为存储单元的“读”操作可靠性有何影响？
> 2.  **“金属铁电性”的普适性边界**：虽然WTe₂证实了金属中铁电翻转的可能性，但其有效翻转的厚度极限是多少？自由载流子的屏蔽效应究竟在多大程度上被抑制？对于载流子浓度更高的典型金属，电场穿透深度可能仅有几个原子层，那么“滑动铁电性”是否将仅限于超薄膜，从而限制了其体材料应用？
> 3.  **“铁电”与“铁弹”的耦合与混淆**：文章明确指出，滑动铁电性本质上是面内滑移引起的面外极化翻转，因此它天然地也是“铁弹性 (Ferroelasticity)”。问题在于，实验中观测到的电滞回线、畴壁移动等现象，在多大程度上可以清晰地归因于纯粹的“铁电”翻转，而非电-力耦合效应下的“铁弹”翻转？电场驱动下的层间滑移，其驱动力究竟是纯粹的静电力，还是压电/电致伸缩效应导致的应力梯度的作用？
> 4.  **莫尔铁电性中的极化与电子关联的博弈**：图4讨论了莫尔铁电性为激子和电子关联提供了平台。然而，一个关键问题是，在魔角石墨烯等强关联体系中，由电子相互作用（如Wigner晶化）本身就可以诱导出铁电性（即“电子铁电性”）。那么，在莫尔超晶格中，如何区分观测到的铁电性是源于文章所述的“结构型”滑动铁电性，还是源于强关联电子体系的“电子型”铁电性？两者对电场的响应和动力学行为可能截然不同。
> 5.  **与传统铁电体的竞争分析**：文章宣称滑动铁电性的低能垒和高速度是其巨大优势，但传统铁电体，例如基于HfO₂的掺杂薄膜，其工艺与CMOS平台高度兼容，且已经在产业界投入应用。滑动铁电体在具体性能指标（如保持力、疲劳特性、存储窗口、多值化潜力）上，与传统铁电体及新兴的HfO₂基铁电体相比，究竟有没有不可替代的“杀手锏”？文章对此缺乏定量化的对比分析。

> ## 八、用户问题预测与解答
> 
> 1.  **问题**：滑动铁电性中的“滑动”是宏观的机械滑动，还是原子尺度的集体位移？
>     
>     -   **解答**：是**原子尺度的集体位移**。它指的是多层结构中的一层晶体，相对于另一层，沿着一个固定的晶格矢量（如BN中的一个B-N键长）发生整体平移，而不是肉眼可见的宏观滑动。这种平移是晶格级别的，是极化的翻转路径。
> 2.  **问题**：为什么说滑动铁电性打破了“速度-稳定性”权衡？这不矛盾吗？
>     
>     -   **解答**：不矛盾，这就是它的精妙之处，源于“集体”与“孤立”行为之别。**写入速度**由“集体翻转势垒”决定，即整层原子协同滑动的势垒，因层间范德华力弱而极低，所以写入快。**保持稳定性**则由“孤立翻转势垒”决定，即某小块区域在没有协同的情况下自发滑动的势垒，因层内共价键强而极高，所以抗热扰动，能长久保存数据。这就像一本书，整体翻页（集体）需要克服的摩擦力很小，但要撕下其中一页的一部分（孤立）却需要克服很强的纤维间结合力。
> 3.  **问题**：摩尔铁电性与滑动铁电性是什么关系？
>     
>     -   **解答**：摩尔铁电性是滑动铁电性在**扭转双层体系中的一种空间调制表现**。当两层相同的二维材料以一个小角度扭转堆叠时，会形成周期性的摩尔超晶格。在超晶格的不同位置，局域的堆叠构型（如AA, AB, BA）不同，导致局域极化大小和方向也周期性变化，从而形成规则的铁电畴网络。它的微观翻转机制仍然是层间滑动，但宏观上表现为这些微畴的扩张或收缩。
> 4.  **问题**：既然金属中自由电子会屏蔽电场，为什么WTe₂这种金属还能有铁电性？
>     
>     -   **解答**：关键在于**极薄的厚度**。虽然金属中的自由电子会屏蔽外电场，但WTe₂实验所用的样品仅为原子层级的厚度（双层或三层）。在如此薄的尺度下，电场能够充分穿透整个材料，其屏蔽效果是不完全的。因此，外电场仍然能对层间原子施加足够的力，驱动其发生层间滑移，从而翻转极化。这为在金属中探索铁电性开辟了先例。
> 5.  **问题**：除了制备存储器，滑动铁电性还有什么更深刻的物理意义？
>     
>     -   **解答**：它提供了一个**全新的、电场可调的量子物态调控平台**。由于极化状态与堆叠方式直接绑定，而堆叠方式又深刻影响着电子能带结构、自旋-轨道耦合、贝里曲率、拓扑性质等。因此，通过栅压翻转极化，就可能实现对**自旋流、谷电流、非线性霍尔效应、甚至拓扑相变**的直接电学调控。这相当于给二维量子材料安上了一个电控“旋钮”，为探索新奇物性和发展自旋电子学、谷电子学等后摩尔时代器件提供了强大工具。

> ## Abstract
> 
> 摘要
> 
> Near the 100th anniversary of the discovery of ferroelectricity, so-called sliding ferroelectricity has been proposed and confirmed recently in a series of experiments that have stimulated remarkable interest. Such ferroelectricity exists widely and exists only in two-dimensional (2D) van der Waals stacked layers, where the vertical electric polarization is switched by in-plane interlayer sliding. Reciprocally, interlayer sliding and the "ripplocation" domain wall can be driven by an external vertical electric field. The unique combination of intralayer stiffness and interlayer slipperiness of 2D van der Waals layers greatly facilitates such switching while still maintaining environmental and mechanical robustness at ambient conditions. In this perspective, we discuss the progress and future opportunities in this behavior. The origin of such ferroelectricity as well as a general rule for judging its existence are summarized, where the vertical stacking sequence is crucial for its formation. This discovery broadens 2D ferroelectrics from very few material candidates to most of the known 2D materials. Their low switching barriers enable high-speed data writing with low energy cost. Related physics like Moire ferroelectricity, the ferroelectric nonlinear anomalous Hall effect, and multiferroic coupling are discussed. For 2D valleytronics, nontrivial band topology and superconductivity, their possible couplings with sliding ferroelectricity via certain stacking or Moire ferroelectricity also deserve interest. We provide critical reviews on the current challenges in this emerging area. 在铁电性发现近100周年之际，所谓的滑动铁电性在最近的一系列实验中被提出并得到证实，引发了显著的研究兴趣。这种铁电性广泛存在，且仅存在于二维范德华堆叠层中，其中垂直电极化通过面内层间滑动实现翻转。反之，层间滑动和"ripplocation"畴壁可由外部垂直电场驱动。二维范德华层的层内刚度和层间滑移性的独特组合，极大地促进了这种翻转，同时在环境条件下保持环境和机械稳健性。在本视角中，我们讨论这一行为的进展和未来机遇。总结了这种铁电性的起源以及判断其存在的一般规则，其中垂直堆叠顺序对其形成至关重要。这一发现将二维铁电体从极少数的候选材料扩展到大多数已知的二维材料。它们的低翻转势垒使高速数据写入和低能耗成为可能。讨论了相关物理，如莫尔铁电性、铁电非线性反常霍尔效应和多铁耦合。对于二维谷电子学、非平庸能带拓扑和超导电性，它们通过特定堆叠或莫尔铁电性与滑动铁电性的可能耦合也值得关注。我们对这一新兴领域当前的挑战提供了批判性评述。

> ## Introduction
> 
> 引言
> 
> Ferroelectrics with spontaneous switchable electric polarizations are important candidates for realizing nonvolatile random-access memory. Their bistable states with moderate switching barriers may enable memories with room-temperature robustness at nanoscale. A successful combination of ferroelectricity and semiconductors will be highly desirable in the coming era of ferroelectronics (1), which enables ferroelectric field-effect transistor, merging logic, and memory functionalities in a single device, overcoming the interconnect bottleneck in von Neumann architecture. The field of ferroelectricity continues to grow a century after its discovery by Valasek, with emerging new topics (2). 具有可翻转自发电极化的铁电体是实现非易失性随机存取存储器的重要候选材料。它们具有适中翻转势垒的双稳态可在纳米尺度实现室温鲁棒性。铁电性与半导体的成功结合将在即将到来的铁电子学时代（1）中极具吸引力，这使得铁电场效应晶体管能够将逻辑和存储功能合并在单一器件中，克服冯·诺依曼架构中的互连瓶颈。在Valasek发现铁电性一个世纪后，该领域仍在持续发展，涌现出新的主题（2）。
> 
> **nonvolatile random-access memory**: 非易失性随机存取存储器——断电后数据仍能保持的存储器。 **ferroelectronics**: 铁电子学——利用铁电材料的极化状态进行信息存储和处理的电子学领域。 **ferroelectric field-effect transistor**: 铁电场效应晶体管——利用铁电材料作为栅极介质的场效应晶体管，可实现非易失性存储。
> 
> Studies on two-dimensional (2D) van der Waals ferroelectrics since 2013 (3–5) offer a possible solution to the well-known epitaxial compatibility problem with the substrate: If the media of ferroelectricity are 2D materials with atomic thickness, also with a clean van der Waals interface that does not require lattice match with substrates, they should be exempt from the interfacial issues for device integration. Additionally, many of them are high-mobility semiconductors (>1,000 cm2 V-1 s-1) like phosphorene (6), MoS2 (7), and InSe (8). Similar studies have even been extended to 1D/0D ferroelectricity (9, 10). 自2013年以来对二维范德华铁电体的研究（3-5）为众所周知的外延兼容性问题提供了可能的解决方案：如果铁电介质是具有原子厚度的二维材料，且具有不需要与衬底晶格匹配的清洁范德华界面，它们应能免除器件集成中的界面问题。此外，其中许多是高迁移率半导体（>1,000 cm2 V-1 s-1），如磷烯（6）、MoS2（7）和InSe（8）。类似研究甚至已扩展到一维/零维铁电性（9, 10）。
> 
> **epitaxial compatibility**: 外延兼容性——薄膜材料与衬底之间晶格常数和结构匹配的能力。 **phosphorene**: 磷烯——由黑磷剥离得到的单层或少数层二维材料。
> 
> Nevertheless, successful trials in searching for 2D van der Waals ferroelectrics were once limited to very few cases: The electric polarizations of IV–VI monolayers are strictly in-plane (11–14), and the more favorable vertical ferroelectricity was experimentally confirmed in only CuInP2S6 (15, 16), In2Se3 (17–19), and MoTe2 (20) down to the monolayer limit. After all, the vertical ion displacements for ferroelectric switching are limited in a confined 2D layer. 然而，在寻找二维范德华铁电体的成功尝试曾仅限于极少数案例：IV-VI族单层的电极化严格限于面内（11-14），而更有利的垂直铁电性仅在CuInP2S6（15, 16）、In2Se3（17-19）和MoTe2（20）中被实验证实，可达到单层极限。毕竟，用于铁电翻转的垂直离子位移在受限的二维层中是有限的。
> 
> However, the theory of sliding ferroelectricity proposed in 2017 (21) reveals the universal existence of vertical ferroelectricity in 2D layers, which has been confirmed in a series of van der Waals systems within several years. Strikingly, herein the vertical ferroelectric switching is induced by in-plane interlayer sliding without any requisite vertical ion displacements, which was unreported in previous ferroelectrics. Such a unique mechanism gives rise to ultralow switching barriers (approximate millielectron volts per formula unit [f.u.]) that enable high-speed data writing with low energy cost, while the robustness against thermal fluctuations is still ensured. In this perspective, we discuss the research trajectory of sliding ferroelectricity, clarify its origin, and examine its potential and challenges. We suggest promising directions for future research and broader emerging topics like Moire ferroelectricity, metallic ferroelectricity, and so on. 然而，2017年提出的滑动铁电性理论（21）揭示了垂直铁电性在二维层中的普遍存在，并在数年内在一系列范德华体系中被证实。引人注目的是，此处的垂直铁电翻转是由面内层间滑动引起的，无需任何垂直离子位移，这在以往的铁电体中未曾报道。这种独特机制产生了超低翻转势垒（约每公式单位毫电子伏特），使高速数据写入和低能耗成为可能，同时仍确保对抗热涨落的稳健性。在本视角中，我们讨论滑动铁电性的研究轨迹，阐明其起源，并考察其潜力和挑战。我们提出未来研究的有前景方向以及更广泛的新兴主题，如莫尔铁电性、金属铁电性等。
> 
> **sliding ferroelectricity**: 滑动铁电性——通过二维材料层间面内滑动实现的垂直电极化翻转机制。 **switching barrier**: 翻转势垒——铁电体从一个极化状态翻转到另一个所需克服的能量势垒。
> 
> ## Theoretical Model
> 
> 理论模型
> 
> According to the model of sliding ferroelectricity (21, 22), for the van der Waals bilayer of many 2D materials the two layers are actually not identical, and such inequality leads to a net interlayer charge transfer. Taking the AB stacking boron nitride (BN) bilayer displayed in Fig. 1A as an example, the two layers are related by Mz mirror reflection symmetry and the in-plane translation t∥ along the B–N bond by a bond length, which places the N atom in the top BN layer atop the hexagon center of the bottom BN layer, while the B atom in the top layer is right over the N atom in the down layer (staggered with B over N marked by the black arrow). Such noncentrosymmetric coordination supports 2D ferroelectricity with an out-of-plane electric polarization, which is estimated to be 2.08 pC/m by first-principles calculations (0.68 μC cm-2 in 3D). 根据滑动铁电性模型（21, 22），对于许多二维材料的范德华双层，两个层实际上并不相同，这种不等价导致净层间电荷转移。以图1A所示的AB堆叠氮化硼（BN）双层为例，两个层通过Mz镜面反射对称性和沿B-N键方向平移一个键长的面内平移t∥相关联，这使得顶层BN中的N原子位于底层BN的六边形中心上方，而顶层中的B原子正好位于下层N原子的正上方（交错排列，B在N上，如黑色箭头所示）。这种非中心对称配位支持具有面外电极化的二维铁电性，通过第一性原理计算估计为2.08 pC/m（在三维中为0.68 μC cm-2）。
> 
> **first-principles calculations**: 第一性原理计算——基于量子力学基本原理，不依赖经验参数的材料性质计算方法。 **noncentrosymmetric**: 非中心对称的——结构缺乏反演对称中心，是铁电性存在的必要条件。
> 
> An important consequence of this symmetry is a possibility to control the lateral alignment between the two monolayers by an applied electric field. This follows from the fact that two opposite polarization orientations in the AB (BA)-stacked BN are distinguished by a 2t∥ translation along the weakly coupled interface. As a result, the polarization reversal can be regarded as lateral sliding of one BN monolayer with respect to the other, which can be achieved via interlayer translation by one B–N bond length along either one of the three directions marked by the red arrows (three equivalent reversed states), moving the N atoms in the upper layer right over the B atoms of the down layer, as shown in Fig. 1A. 这种对称性的一个重要结果是通过外加电场控制两个单层之间横向对齐的可能性。这是因为AB（BA）堆叠BN中两个相反的极化方向通过沿弱耦合界面的2t∥平移来区分。因此，极化翻转可被视为一个BN单层相对于另一个的横向滑动，这可通过沿红色箭头标记的三个方向中任一方向平移一个B-N键长的层间平移来实现（三个等效翻转态），将上层中的N原子移动到下层B原子的正上方，如图1A所示。
> 
> The minimum energy path at the unit-cell level, i.e., the ferroelectric switching pathway, entails an areal energy barrier (q) of only around 9 meV per unit cell, or a few millielectron volts per square angstrom, which indicates a potentially very small energy cost for data writing. 在单胞层面的最小能量路径，即铁电翻转路径，每单胞仅需约9 meV的面能量势垒（q），或每平方埃数毫电子伏特，这表明数据写入的能耗可能非常小。
> 
> **areal energy barrier**: 面能量势垒——单位面积上铁电翻转所需克服的能量势垒。
> 
> Periodic ferroelectric domains with spatially varying polarization signs can also be formed upon a small-angle twist within the bilayer, as shown in Fig. 1B. In such a Moire superlattice with large-scale modulation of local stacking, the polarization vanished in AA stacking domains (see the red circle), while for AB or BA stacking domains the polarization can be either downward (see the yellow circle) or upward (see the green circle). Similar types of Moire patterns for graphene/BN hetero-bilayers can be constructed, where periodic n or p doped graphene domains can be generated and the electronic properties of graphene can be tuned. 在双层内小角度扭转时，还可形成具有空间变化极化符号的周期性铁电畴，如图1B所示。在这种具有局部堆叠大规模调制的莫尔超晶格中，极化在AA堆叠区域消失（见红色圆圈），而对于AB或BA堆叠区域，极化可向下（见黄色圆圈）或向上（见绿色圆圈）。石墨烯/BN异质双层可构建类似类型的莫尔图案，其中可产生周期性n型或p型掺杂石墨烯区域，并可调控石墨烯的电子性质。
> 
> **Moire superlattice**: 莫尔超晶格——由两层或多层晶体材料以小角度扭转或晶格常数差异产生的大周期超晶格结构。
> 
> Similar principles can be applied to a series of 2D materials, including most binary compounds with honeycomb lattices, e.g. the already-synthesized graphitic few-layer GaN (23), SiC (24), and ZnO (25) with much higher computed polarizations, as summarized in Table 1. Van der Waals bilayers and multilayers like MoS2, InSe, and GaSe, as well as their ground state of 3D bulk phase with R3m symmetry, can also exhibit such sliding ferroelectricity, as shown in Fig. 1C. Meanwhile, they are also semiconductors with suitable bandgaps and high mobility for nanoelectronics, enabling novel in-memory computing device constructs. 类似原理可应用于一系列二维材料，包括大多数具有蜂窝晶格的二元化合物，例如已合成的石墨型少层GaN（23）、SiC（24）和ZnO（25），其计算极化值高得多，如表1所示。范德华双层和多层如MoS2、InSe和GaSe，以及它们具有R3m对称性的三维体相基态，也可表现出这种滑动铁电性，如图1C所示。同时，它们也是具有合适带隙和高迁移率的半导体，可用于纳米电子学，实现新型存内计算器件架构。
> 
> **honeycomb lattice**: 蜂窝晶格——具有六角形蜂窝状结构的二维晶格。 **in-memory computing**: 存内计算——在存储器内部直接进行计算操作，减少数据搬运的新型计算范式。

> Aside from honeycomb lattices with three equivalent sliding variants, some other bilayer structures are ferroelectric, but with bistable states, e.g. WTe2 bilayer with a much smaller computed polarization of ∼0.38 pC/m (22). Actually, most van der Waals bilayers or multilayers can be endowed with sliding ferroelectricity with certain stacking-sequence control, and its existence can be judged by using a general principle, as displayed in Fig. 1D: The van der Waals bilayer or multilayer should be noncentrosymmetric, and the equivalent state obtained via mirror operation with respect to the horizontal plane in the center can also be obtained via interlayer translation, revealing a feasible switching pathway of sliding ferroelectricity. 除了具有三个等效滑动变体的蜂窝晶格外，一些其他双层结构也是铁电的，但具有双稳态，例如WTe2双层，其计算极化值小得多，约为0.38 pC/m（22）。实际上，大多数范德华双层或多层可通过特定堆叠顺序控制赋予滑动铁电性，其存在可通过一般原理判断，如图1D所示：范德华双层或多层应是非中心对称的，且通过关于中心水平面的镜面操作获得的等效状态也可通过层间平移获得，揭示了滑动铁电性的可行翻转路径。
> 
> Not all stacking configurations can give rise to ferroelectricity. The aforementioned ferroelectric systems may turn nonpolar when the stacking sequence is altered, e.g. to the nonpolar AA' stacking for BN bilayer (eclipsed with B over N) that is almost degenerate in energy compared with polar AB stacking. For bulk phase BN, MoS2 or InSe stacked by honeycomb monolayers, AA' stacking is nonpolar, and the vertical polarization is also missing for AB stacking: The vertical mirror symmetry is preserved in the AB stacking bulk phase although the AB layers are not identical, either positively or negatively charged, as revealed by the interlayer differential charge density in Fig. 2A. All the planar layers in the ABC stacking bulk phase are identical and neutral, while their nonsymmetric charge density distributions due to the interlayer configuration give rise to vertical polarity. Meanwhile, the AB stacking phase may be transformed to a polar 3R phase (ABC stacking; see 3R MoS2 in Figs. 1C and 2B) via interlayer translation upon the application of an external electric field. 并非所有堆叠构型都能产生铁电性。上述铁电体系在堆叠顺序改变时可能变为非极性，例如对BN双层变为非极性AA'堆叠（重叠，B在N上），其能量与极性AB堆叠几乎简并。对于由蜂窝状单层堆叠的体相BN、MoS2或InSe，AA'堆叠是非极性的，AB堆叠也缺失垂直极化：如图2A中层间差分电荷密度所示，AB堆叠体相中保持了垂直镜面对称性，尽管AB层不完全相同，无论是带正电还是负电。ABC堆叠体相中的所有平面层是相同且中性的，而它们由于层间构型产生的不对称电荷密度分布导致垂直极性。同时，在外加电场作用下，AB堆叠相可通过层间平移转变为极性3R相（ABC堆叠；见图1C和图2B中的3R MoS2）。
> 
> **differential charge density**: 差分电荷密度——材料形成后电荷密度与孤立原子电荷密度之差，用于分析层间电荷转移。 **degenerate**: 简并的——不同构型具有相同或非常接近的能量状态。
> 
> Without limitation by the depolarization field as in 3D ferroelectric material with complex interfaces with the substrate, their bulk polarizations can be greatly enhanced; e.g., the 3R BN possesses a large bulk polarization of 7.6 μC/cm2 as listed in Table 2. For 1T/1T' transition metal dichalcogenides or trihalide monolayers that are centrosymmetric, similarly their corresponding bulk phase can be either polar or nonpolar depending on the stacking phases, e.g. the well-known two phases for SnS2 and SnSe2 (trigonal and hexagonal phases in Fig. 2C) (26), WTe2 and ZrI2 (orthorhombic and monoclinic phases in Fig. 2D) (27), CrI3, and CrBr3 (R-type and H-type in Fig. 2E) (28). The nonpolar monoclinic WTe2 can be transformed to polar orthorhombic phase via lateral translation, which cannot be applied to transform the nonpolar trigonal SnS2 to ferroelectric hexagonal phase, or the nonpolar R-type CrI3 to ferroelectric H-type. Those phases of different stacking are almost degenerate in energy, so phase control during the synthesis is crucial for the realization of sliding ferroelectricity. 不像三维铁电材料那样受限于与衬底复杂界面的退极化场，它们的体极化可大幅增强；例如，如表2所列，3R BN具有7.6 μC/cm2的大体极化。对于中心对称的1T/1T'过渡金属二硫化物或三卤化物单层，类似地，它们对应的体相可以是极性或非极性的，取决于堆叠相，例如众所周知的SnS2和SnSe2的两个相（图2C中的三角相和六角相）（26），WTe2和ZrI2（图2D中的正交相和单斜相）（27），CrI3和CrBr3（图2E中的R型和H型）（28）。非极性单斜WTe2可通过横向平移转变为极性正交相，但这一方法不能用于将非极性三角SnS2转变为铁电六角相，或将非极性R型CrI3转变为铁电H型。这些不同堆叠的相在能量上几乎简并，因此合成过程中的相控制对于实现滑动铁电性至关重要。
>

> The kinetics of 2D displacive phase transformations (29) deserve careful discussion. When an externally applied forcing F, e.g. applied vertical electric field or shear stress, is below the athermal threshold (30) FC for domain switching, the transformation will happen heterogeneously and asynchronously, assisted by thermal activation. That is to say, there will be a nucleation event and a subsequent growth process of a particular polarization/sliding domain variant, with a finite characteristic nucleation and/or migration activation energy Q(F < FC) > 0, that are related to the domain walls ["ripplocations" (30, 31) in the case of 2D sliding ferroelectricity]. On the other hand, if the applied field is strong enough, F ≥ FC, the transformation will be barrierless and does not need thermal activation (30), Q(FC) = 0, in which case a nucleation-and-growth sequence is not necessary, and the transformation can take place in the entire region almost instantaneously like in spinodal decomposition. 二维位移型相变的动力学（29）值得仔细讨论。当外加驱动力F（例如外加垂直电场或剪切应力）低于畴翻转的无热阈值（30）FC时，相变将在热激活辅助下以异质和异步方式发生。也就是说，将存在特定极化/滑动畴变体的形核事件和随后的生长过程，具有有限的形核和/或迁移特征激活能Q(F < FC) > 0，这与畴壁有关[在二维滑动铁电性情况下为"ripplocations"（30, 31）]。另一方面，如果外加场足够强，F ≥ FC，相变将是无势垒的，不需要热激活（30），Q(FC) = 0，在这种情况下不需要形核-生长序列，相变可在整个区域几乎瞬时发生，如同调幅分解。
> 
> **displacive phase transformation**: 位移型相变——原子通过微小位移而非长程扩散实现的相变。 **athermal threshold**: 无热阈值——不需热激活即可发生相变的临界驱动力。 **spinodal decomposition**: 调幅分解——不需要形核过程、通过浓度涨落自发进行的相分离过程。
> 
> Generally speaking, FC is proportional to q, so a smaller areal energy barrier (in units of millielectron volts per square angstrom) means a smaller athermal coercive field. On the other hand, when F < FC and there is a barrier, the relationship between the absolute activation energy Q (in units of electron volts) and q can be somewhat complex and depends on the detailed geometric boundary condition. Here we are talking about one layer sliding over another layer, forming a ripplocation (31) loop as the domain wall, which is both a crystallographic dislocation and potentially a surface ripple in its core region due to out-of-plane buckling. In a free-standing bilayer, trilayer, and so on with some excess area "slack" due to precompression (32), the formation energy of the ripplocation (31) should be much smaller than standard dislocation or martensitic domain walls in a 3D solid, due to the lack of long-range elastic interactions. In fact, it has been shown that the standard Frank's law for dislocations, dislocation core energy ∝ |b|2 (b is the Burgers vector or the sliding displacement in the case of sliding ferroelectricity), no longer works in 2D van der Waals stacked layers, due to the near-complete relaxation of the long-range in-plane elastic energy, by buckling "ripple." The lattice friction (Peierls barrier) to a gliding 2D ripplocation (31) is also expected to be small when q is small. So, one can expect facile 2D domain nucleation and growth for certain subthreshold F < FC. In the case of high-field, F > FC, this issue is largely moot, and the transformation is expected to happen rapidly with appropriate boundary conditions. 一般来说，FC与q成正比，因此较小的面能量势垒（以毫电子伏特每平方埃为单位）意味着较小的无热矫顽场。另一方面，当F < FC且存在势垒时，绝对激活能Q（以电子伏特为单位）与q之间的关系可能相当复杂，取决于具体的几何边界条件。这里我们讨论的是一层在另一层上滑动，形成ripplocation（31）环作为畴壁，这既是晶体学位错，在其核心区域也可能由于面外屈曲产生表面波纹。在具有因预压缩产生某些多余面积"松弛"的自支撑双层、三层等中（32），ripplocation（31）的形成能应远小于三维固体中的标准位错或马氏体畴壁，因为缺乏长程弹性相互作用。事实上，已证明位错的标准Frank定律，位错核心能量∝ |b|2（b是Burgers矢量，在滑动铁电性情况下为滑动位移），在二维范德华堆叠层中不再适用，因为通过屈曲"波纹"几乎完全弛豫了长程面内弹性能。当q较小时，滑动二维ripplocation（31）的晶格摩擦（Peierls势垒）预计也很小。因此，对于某些亚阈值F < FC，可以预期容易的二维畴形核和生长。在高场情况下，F > FC，这个问题基本上无关紧要，在适当的边界条件下相变有望快速发生。
> 
> **Burgers vector**: 伯格斯矢量——描述晶体中位错强度和方向的矢量。 **Peierls barrier**: 派尔斯势垒——位错在晶格中运动所需克服的周期性势垒。
> 
> The nonvolatile memory function of sliding ferroelectricity would work well if and only if 1) Q(F = 0) ≫ 50 kBT, so when there is zero field a ferroelectric domain variant state can stay "locked in" despite the thermal fluctuations, and 2) for certain achievable F (such as < 10 MV/cm vertical electric field), Q < 5 kBT, so the transformation can be accomplished within a nanosecond or even picosecond timescale (32). Due to the fundamental difference between 2D and 3D dislocations, we expect sliding ferroelectricity in 2D van der Waals materials to be both facile under field and nonvolatile without field, when appropriate size and geometric boundary condition are implemented to relieve the elastic energy constraints (29) that have long confined 3D martensitic transformations. 滑动铁电性的非易失性存储功能良好运行当且仅当：1) Q(F = 0) ≫ 50 kBT，因此在零场时铁电畴变体态可保持"锁定"而不受热涨落影响，2) 对于某些可实现的F（如< 10 MV/cm的垂直电场），Q < 5 kBT，因此相变可在纳秒甚至皮秒时间尺度内完成（32）。由于二维和三维位错之间的根本差异，我们预期二维范德华材料中的滑动铁电性在适当尺寸和几何边界条件下，既能在外场下容易翻转，又能在无外场时保持非易失性，从而解除长期限制三维马氏体相变的弹性能约束（29）。
> 
> **nonvolatile**: 非易失性——在无外加电源条件下保持存储状态的能力。

> ## Experimental Investigations
> 
> 实验研究
> 
> The first system where sliding ferroelectricity was experimentally detected was few-layer WTe2 despite its metallicity and relatively small polarization. In 2018, Fei et al. designed a device using a graphene monolayer as an electrode that can quantify the polarization of WTe2 bilayer or trilayer below (33). The graphene conductance is measured when a voltage bias is applied to the bottom gate with the intervening WTe2 grounded. The ferroelectric switching between two vertical polarization states is then revealed by the hysteresis loop in the graphene conductance sensitive to the polarization. The conductance measurements at a series of temperatures indicate its Curie temperature around 350 K, and no bistability is detected in monolayer WTe2. 实验上检测到滑动铁电性的第一个体系是少层WTe2，尽管其具有金属性和相对较小的极化。2018年，Fei等人设计了一种器件，使用石墨烯单层作为电极，可量化下方WTe2双层或三层的极化（33）。当对底栅施加电压偏置且中间的WTe2接地时，测量石墨烯电导。对极化敏感的石墨烯电导中的回滞环揭示了两个垂直极化态之间的铁电翻转。一系列温度下的电导测量表明其居里温度约为350 K，在单层WTe2中未检测到双稳态。
> 
> **Curie temperature**: 居里温度——铁电体或铁磁体失去自发极化或磁化的临界温度。
> 
> Such a device is able to estimate the areal polarization density of WTe2 bilayer (∼1 × 104 e/cm = 0.16 pC/m measured at 20 K), which can be approximately fitted by density functional theory calculations (∼0.38 pC/m) considering the influence of defects, substrate, and thermal fluctuations (22). In a later report, the vertical ferroelectricity of bulk WTe2 was further verified by piezoresponse force microscopy (PFM), where the antiparallel ferroelectric domain can be visualized directly as shown in Fig. 3A, implying the existence of bistable polarization states (34). The domains usually exhibit a distorted circular profile with an average domain size in the range of ∼20 to 50 nm, with domain walls that appear as dark lines in the amplitude image. Spectroscopic PFM measurements were performed through the top electrode in a capacitor geometry with WTe2 multilayer with a thickness around 15 nm. The acquired piezoresponse as a function of applied bias shows switchable hysteretic behavior, and the bias-driven, oppositely oriented remnant polarization can be clearly seen in the PFM images of different color contrasts, indicating that the polarization of WTe2 is switchable under an external bias. 这种器件能够估算WTe2双层的面极化密度（在20 K测量约1 × 104 e/cm = 0.16 pC/m），考虑到缺陷、衬底和热涨落的影响，可通过密度泛函理论计算（约0.38 pC/m）近似拟合（22）。在随后的一份报告中，通过压电响应力显微镜（PFM）进一步验证了体相WTe2的垂直铁电性，其中反平行铁电畴可直接可视化，如图3A所示，表明存在双稳态极化态（34）。畴通常表现出扭曲的圆形轮廓，平均畴尺寸在约20至50 nm范围内，畴壁在振幅图像中呈现为暗线。通过顶部电极在电容器几何结构中对约15 nm厚的WTe2多层进行光谱PFM测量。获得的压电响应作为外加偏置的函数显示可切换的回滞行为，在对比色不同的PFM图像中可清晰看到偏置驱动的、相反方向的剩余极化，表明WTe2的极化在外加偏置下是可切换的。
> 
> **piezoresponse force microscopy**: 压电响应力显微镜——通过检测铁电材料在电场下的压电形变来表征铁电畴的扫描探针显微技术。 **density functional theory**: 密度泛函理论——以电子密度为基本变量求解多电子体系薛定谔方程的计算方法。
> 
> The stacking transition during ferroelectric switching in few-layer WTe2 was confirmed via in situ nonlinear Hall transport measurement utilizing its large Berry curvature near the Fermi level, where the transition via interlayer sliding enables synchronized annihilation and switching of ferroelectric polarization and the Berry curvature (35). 少层WTe2中铁电翻转过程中的堆叠转变通过利用其费米能级附近大贝里曲率的原位非线性霍尔输运测量得到证实，其中通过层间滑动的转变使铁电极化和贝里曲率的同步湮灭和切换成为可能（35）。
> 
> **Berry curvature**: 贝里曲率——在动量空间中描述电子波函数几何性质的物理量，与拓扑效应和反常输运相关。
> 
> In 2020, the existence of sliding ferroelectricity in BN thin layers was observed by several research groups. Yasuda et al. designed a dual-gated van der Waals heterostructure device shown in Fig. 3B, using a graphene monolayer as a detector that can quantify the polarization of BN bilayer (36). The resistance of the graphene sensor as a function of top gate voltage (for both forward and backward gate sweep directions) exhibits a typical maximum without hysteresis. In contrast, the forward and backward scans of the resistance versus the bottom gate voltage show hysteresis, revealing the polarization switching of bilayer BN by the applied electric field. Another case in point is the measured carrier density nH of graphene extracted from Hall resistance measurements that exhibit hysteretic behavior with an abrupt jump of nH sweeping VB, which is almost independent of temperature, up to room temperature. They also investigated the Moire pattern of twisted bilayer BN with different stacking domains, which can expand or shrink through domain wall motion upon a vertical electric field. 2020年，多个研究组观察到BN薄层中滑动铁电性的存在。Yasuda等人设计了图3B所示的双栅范德华异质结构器件，使用石墨烯单层作为检测器，可量化BN双层的极化（36）。石墨烯传感器电阻作为顶栅电压的函数（正反向栅压扫描方向）展示典型的无回滞最大值。相比之下，电阻对底栅电压的正反向扫描显示回滞，揭示了外加电场对双层BN极化的翻转。另一例证是从霍尔电阻测量中提取的石墨烯载流子密度nH，在扫描VB时表现出具有突跳的回滞行为，该行为几乎不依赖于温度，直至室温。他们还研究了具有不同堆叠畴的扭转双层BN的莫尔图案，这些畴可在垂直电场下通过畴壁运动扩展或收缩。
> 
> In another report, Vizner Stern et al. (37) observed alternating domains of inverted normal polarization around several square micrometers in 3-nm-thick BN layers by atomic force microscope (Fig. 3C) operated in a Kelvin probe mode, which can measure the variations in the electrostatic potential at the surface regions of different stacking configurations. The polarization coupled to lateral sliding can be switched by scanning a biased tip above the surface, where the redistribution of domain walls to orient the local polarization can be visualized by Kelvin-probe maps. The measured polarizations in both reports on BN layers can fit well with the results predicted by first-principles calculations. 在另一份报告中，Vizner Stern等人（37）通过以开尔文探针模式运行的原子力显微镜（图3C）观察到3 nm厚BN层中约数平方微米范围内交替的反向正常极化畴，该模式可测量不同堆叠构型表面区域的静电势变化。与横向滑动耦合的极化可通过在表面上扫描偏置探针来切换，其中畴壁重新分布以定向局部极化可通过开尔文探针图像可视化。两份关于BN层的报告中测量的极化与第一性原理计算预测的结果吻合良好。
> 
> **Kelvin probe mode**: 开尔文探针模式——原子力显微镜的一种模式，用于测量样品表面电势分布。
> 
> Aside from metallic WTe2 and insulating BN, the possible existence of sliding ferroelectricity in semiconducting van der Waals layers has also been explored. For example, the amplitude-off and bias-on PFM amplitude and phase hysteresis loops were measured on a 7-nm-thick β-InSe nanoflake at room temperature (38). A clear amplitude butterfly loop and phase hysteresis loops were observed, revealing the switching of vertical polarization. In another recent report (39), the authors of ref. 36 successfully detected sliding ferroelectricity in a series of transition-metal dichalcogenides (e.g., MoS2, WS2, MoSe2, and WSe2) bilayers, all with polarizations almost half as large compared with bilayer BN. 除金属性WTe2和绝缘性BN外，半导体范德华层中滑动铁电性的可能存在也已被探索。例如，在室温下对7 nm厚的β-InSe纳米片测量了振幅关断和偏置开的PFM振幅和相位回滞环（38）。观察到清晰的振幅蝴蝶曲线和相位回滞环，揭示了垂直极化的翻转。在另一份近期报告中（39），参考文献36的作者成功在一系列过渡金属二硫化物（如MoS2、WS2、MoSe2和WSe2）双层中检测到滑动铁电性，其极化均约为双层BN的一半。
>

> ### Moire Ferroelectricity
> 
> 莫尔铁电性
> 
> The proposed Moire ferroelectric domain with spatially varying potential upon a twisted angle in Fig. 1B has been observed in both aforementioned reports on ferroelectric BN thin layers (36, 37). In addition, Woods et al. used electrostatic force microscopy for the visualization of local electric charges on twisted BN bilayer by detecting the variations in the surface potential (40). Owing to the large potential variation, the regular ferroelectric domains arranged in triangular superlattice can be clearly visualized, as shown in Fig. 4A. 图1B中提出的具有空间变化电势的莫尔铁电畴在扭转角下的情况已在上述两份关于铁电BN薄层的报告中被观察到（36, 37）。此外，Woods等人使用静电力显微镜通过检测表面电势变化来可视化扭转BN双层上的局域电荷（40）。由于大的电势变化，排列成三角形超晶格的规则铁电畴可以清晰可视化，如图4A所示。
> 
> Moire ferroelectricity not only generates periodic n/p doped domains and shapes the periodicity of the potential energy landscape but also gives rise to a new research focus concerning the coupling between ferroelectricity and twistronic properties, like Moire flat bands that induce correlation (43) and superconductivity (44) and the spatially varying bandgaps of twisted bilayer semiconductors driving the exciton flow (45). For a semiconducting bilayer, a small twist angle creates an internal stacking translation u(r) varying gently with position r that controls the local bandgap Eg(u(r)). Aside from the spatially varying interlayer hybridizations, the Moire potential and local band variation can also be induced by the varying potential of Moire ferroelectricity, which determines the relative shift between the bands of the upper and down layer in different stacking regions. With the band alignment shown in Fig. 4B (41), the twisted semiconducting bilayer combined with Moire ferroelectricity can mimic a quantum dot array with type-II junctions (both in-plane and out-of-plane), which facilitates exciton trapping. 莫尔铁电性不仅产生周期性n/p掺杂畴并塑造势能景观的周期性，还引发了一个新的研究焦点，即铁电性与扭转电子学性质之间的耦合，如诱导关联效应（43）和超导电性（44）的莫尔平带，以及驱动激子流动的扭转双层半导体空间变化带隙（45）。对于半导体双层，小扭转角产生随位置r缓慢变化的内部堆叠平移u(r)，控制局部带隙Eg(u(r))。除了空间变化的层间杂化外，莫尔电势和局域能带变化也可由莫尔铁电性的变化电势诱导，这决定了不同堆叠区域中上层和下层能带之间的相对位移。通过图4B所示的能带对齐（41），与莫尔铁电性结合的扭转半导体双层可模拟具有II型结（面内和面外）的量子点阵列，这有利于激子捕获。
> 
> **twistronic**: 扭转电子学——通过控制二维材料层间扭转角来调控电子性质的研究领域。 **flat band**: 平带——在动量空间中色散很小的能带，通常导致强关联效应。 **exciton**: 激子——半导体中电子和空穴通过库仑相互作用形成的束缚态。
> 
> A theoretical study (42) revealed the trapping of electrons, holes, and excitons in twisted WX2/MoX2 bilayers (X = S, Se), which is attributed to the domain wall networks of Moire ferroelectricity, as shown in Fig. 4C. Tong et al. predicted bichromatic Moire potentials for valley electrons, holes, and interlayer trions in MX2/M'X'2/MX2 trilayers, with two independently configurable triangular superlattices of neutral excitons with opposite electric dipoles (46). In a recent experimental study, unconventional ferroelectricity was observed in bilayer graphene sandwiched between two hexagonal BN layers with a Moire superlattice potential formed at the graphene/BN interface, which is attributed to the electronic correlation effect of Moire flat bands (47). 一项理论研究（42）揭示了扭转WX2/MoX2双层（X = S, Se）中电子、空穴和激子的捕获，这归因于莫尔铁电性的畴壁网络，如图4C所示。Tong等人预测了MX2/M'X'2/MX2三层中谷电子、空穴和层间三离子的双色莫尔电势，具有两个可独立配置的、具有相反电偶极的中性激子三角超晶格（46）。在最近的一项实验研究中，在夹于两个六方BN层之间的双层石墨烯中观察到非常规铁电性，石墨烯/BN界面处形成莫尔超晶格势，这归因于莫尔平带的电子关联效应（47）。
> 
> **trion**: 三离子——由两个电子和一个空穴或两个空穴和一个电子组成的束缚态激子复合体。
> 
> ### Multiferroicity and Spin-Orbitronics
> 
> 多铁性与自旋轨道电子学
> 
> Multiferroic materials with robust magnetism and ferroelectricity that are strongly coupled have been long sought but remain elusive to date (48). For two ferromagnetic monolayers that are antiferromagnetically coupled, inequivalent stacking may give rise to both sliding ferroelectricity and uncompensated magnetic moments. The total magnetization can be switched with the reversal of polarization and interlayer voltage, rendering a desirable mode of "electrical writing + magnetic reading." The switchable magnetization for MXene Cr2NO2, VS2, and MoN2 bilayer are, respectively, 0.008, 0.016, and 0.09 μB per unit cell (21), where μB is a Bohr magneton, which is already much larger compared with most type-II multiferroics. 具有强耦合的稳健磁性和铁电性的多铁材料长期以来一直在被寻求，但至今仍难以捉摸（48）。对于反铁磁耦合的两个铁磁单层，不等价堆叠可能同时产生滑动铁电性和未补偿的磁矩。总磁化强度可随极化和层间电压的翻转而切换，呈现出理想的"电写入+磁读出"模式。MXene Cr2NO2、VS2和MoN2双层的可切换磁化强度分别为每单胞0.008、0.016和0.09 μB（21），其中μB是玻尔磁子，这已比大多数II型多铁体大得多。
> 
> **multiferroic**: 多铁性——在同一相中同时存在铁电性和（反）铁磁性的材料。 **Bohr magneton**: 玻尔磁子——电子磁矩的基本单位，μB = 9.274 × 10-24 J/T。
> 
> In a recent study on VS2 bilayer (49), the coupling of valley between magnetism and ferroelectricity was noted. Owing to the interaction of interlayer ferrovalleys the splitting values at the bottom of the conduction band and the top of the valence band are not equal at valley K+ and K-, which will be exchanged via ferroelectric switching or magnetic reversal for both layers. The predicted manipulations of the couplings between electron charge, spin, and valley degrees of freedom are yet to be experimentally validated. 在最近一项关于VS2双层的研究中（49），注意到磁性与铁电性之间谷的耦合。由于层间铁谷的相互作用，导带底和价带顶的劈裂值在谷K+和K-处不相等，这将通过铁电翻转或两个层的磁性反转进行交换。对电子电荷、自旋和谷自由度之间耦合的预测调控尚未得到实验验证。
> 
> Actually, even for ferroelectric bilayer that is nonmagnetic, its noncentrosymmetricity allows linear in momentum spin-orbit coupling (SOC) which locks the electron's spin direction to its momentum, resulting in nontrivial spin textures in the reciprocal space. The spin textures are coupled to the ferroelectric polarization and thus can be controlled by its orientation and magnitude for spintronic and valleytronic applications (50), especially for those ferroelectrics with strong SOC-like WSe2 bilayer. Nontrivial topology in bilayer ferroelectrics with strong SOC also deserves further exploration (51). 实际上，即使对于非磁性的铁电双层，其非中心对称性允许动量线性的自旋-轨道耦合（SOC），将电子自旋方向锁定到其动量上，在倒空间产生非平庸的自旋纹理。自旋纹理与铁电极化耦合，因此可通过其方向和大小进行控制，用于自旋电子学和谷电子学应用（50），特别是对于具有强SOC的WSe2双层等铁电体。具有强SOC的双层铁电体中的非平庸拓扑也值得进一步探索（51）。
> 
> **spin-orbit coupling**: 自旋-轨道耦合——电子自旋与其轨道运动之间的相对论性相互作用。 **spin texture**: 自旋纹理——在动量空间中电子自旋方向的分布模式。
> 
> ### Metallic Ferroelectricity
> 
> 金属铁电性
> 
> The multiferroic bilayers mentioned above are mostly metallic (and strong ferromagnets are usually metallic). The coexistence of ferroelectricity and metallicity is forbidden in conventional ferroelectrics as polarization switching upon an external electric field should be hindered by free-carrier screening. This principle also applies to the "ferroelectric metals" proposed by Anderson and Blount (52), referred to as "polar metals" with nonswitchable polarization [e.g., LiOsO3 (53)]. However, the experimentally confirmed sliding ferroelectricity in metallic thin-layer WTe2 indicates that a thin enough polar metal can be sufficiently penetrated by an electric field for its polarization switching, where the ultralow sliding barrier may also play an important role. It is likely that most 2D metallic layers can be endowed with a switchable polarization via stacking sequence control (which is yet to be confirmed), and for those properties that do not or scarcely coexist in pervious insulator ferroelectrics (plasmon, superconductivity, ferromagnetism, etc.), now their possible couplings or interactions with ferroelectricity can be explored. 上述多铁双层大多是金属性的（强铁磁体通常是金属性的）。铁电性和金属性的共存在传统铁电体中是被禁止的，因为外加电场下的极化翻转应被自由载流子屏蔽所阻碍。这一原理也适用于Anderson和Blount提出的"铁电金属"（52），被称为具有不可切换极化的"极性金属" [如LiOsO3（53）]。然而，实验证实的金属性薄层WTe2中的滑动铁电性表明，足够薄的极性金属可被电场充分穿透以实现极化翻转，其中超低滑动势垒也可能起重要作用。很可能大多数二维金属层可通过堆叠顺序控制赋予可切换极化（这尚待证实），对于那些在以往绝缘体铁电体中不存在或几乎不共存的性质（等离激元、超导电性、铁磁性等），现在可以探索它们与铁电性的可能耦合或相互作用。
> 
> **polar metal**: 极性金属——具有极性结构但极化不可翻转的金属材料。 **plasmon**: 等离激元——金属中自由电子气的集体振荡量子。
> 
> ### Ferroelectric Nonlinear Anomalous Hall Effect
> 
> 铁电非线性反常霍尔效应
> 
> For few-layer WTe2, Wang and Qian predicted a remarkable ferroelectric nonlinear anomalous Hall effect where nonlinear anomalous Hall current switches in odd-layer WTe2 (except for 1T' monolayer) while remaining invariant in even-layer WTe2 upon ferroelectric transition, which is attributed to the absence and presence of Berry curvature dipole reversal and shift dipole reversal due to distinct ferroelectric transformation in even- and odd-layer WTe2 (54). It was later confirmed (35) by nonlinear Hall effect measurement, where an applied current flow along the a-axis results in the generation of nonlinear Hall voltage along the b-axis, proportional to the Berry curvature dipole strength at the Fermi level. Although with similar butterfly-shaped hysteresis in longitudinal conductance, the sign of the nonlinear Hall signal was observed to be reversed in the trilayer while remaining unchanged in the four-layer crystal. Such Berry curvature memory based on sliding ferroelectric systems with nondestructive reading is worthy of further investigation for applications. 对于少层WTe2，Wang和Qian预测了一种显著的铁电非线性反常霍尔效应，其中非线性反常霍尔电流在奇数层WTe2中切换（1T'单层除外），而在偶数层WTe2中铁电转变时保持不变，这归因于偶数和奇数层WTe2中不同的铁电转变导致贝里曲率偶极子翻转和位移偶极子翻转的存在与否（54）。后来通过非线性霍尔效应测量得到证实（35），其中沿a轴施加的电流导致沿b轴产生非线性霍尔电压，正比于费米能级处的贝里曲率偶极子强度。尽管纵向电导具有类似的蝴蝶形回滞，观察到非线性霍尔信号的符号在三层中反转而在四层晶体中保持不变。这种基于滑动铁电系统且具有非破坏性读出的贝里曲率存储器值得进一步研究应用。
> 
> **Berry curvature dipole**: 贝里曲率偶极子——贝里曲率在动量空间中的一阶矩，可产生非线性霍尔效应。
>

> ## Potential Applications
> 
> 潜在应用
> 
> Despite the promise of 2D van der Waals materials in nanoelectronics, it is still unclear which of them will prevail like silicon today. The wide existence of sliding FE ensures that no matter which one it can be endowed with nonvolatile ferroelectric memory property via certain stacking. The research on sliding ferroelectricity has greatly broadened the candidates of 2D ferroelectrics from very few cases to most 2D materials, ranging from metals to insulators. Boron nitride is widely used as a substrate and gate insulator with high dielectric constant, metallic NbSe2 or WTe2 can be used as 2D electrodes, and InSe and MoS2 can be high-performance semiconductors with suitable bandgaps and high mobility, so they may all possess the function of nonvolatile memory while playing a vital part in devices like field-effect transistors. 尽管二维范德华材料在纳米电子学中前景广阔，但目前尚不清楚哪种材料会像今天的硅一样占据主导地位。滑动铁电性的广泛存在确保无论哪种材料，都可通过特定堆叠赋予非易失性铁电存储特性。滑动铁电性的研究将二维铁电体的候选材料从极少案例大幅扩展到大多数二维材料，涵盖从金属到绝缘体的范围。氮化硼被广泛用作高介电常数的衬底和栅绝缘体，金属性NbSe2或WTe2可用作二维电极，InSe和MoS2可作为具有合适带隙和高迁移率的高性能半导体，因此它们都可能具有非易失性存储功能，同时在诸如场效应晶体管等器件中发挥重要作用。
> 
> The functional fatigue of current perovskite ferroelectrics has been attributed to their mobile oxygen defects at the interface between ferroelectric oxides and metallic electrodes (55). For sliding ferroelectric layers, however, the clean van der Waals surface are usually free of such defects, while all the ion displacements are strictly in-plane despite the vertical polarizations are tuned by the perpendicular electrodes. Hence, the sliding ferroelectrics should be exempt from this kind of fatigue mechanism. 当前钙钛矿铁电体的功能疲劳归因于铁电氧化物与金属电极界面处的可移动氧缺陷（55）。然而，对于滑动铁电层，清洁的范德华表面通常没有此类缺陷，而所有离子位移严格限于面内，尽管垂直极化由垂直电极调控。因此，滑动铁电体应免受这种疲劳机制的影响。
> 
> **perovskite**: 钙钛矿——具有ABX3结构类型的晶体材料，是重要的铁电材料体系。 **functional fatigue**: 功能疲劳——铁电材料在反复极化翻转后性能退化的现象。
> 
> There is usually a trade-off in optimizing the performance for data storage when considering the intensive switching barrier q (in units of millielectron volts per cubic or square angstrom) between ferroic variants: A low q is favorable to reduce the energy cost and enhance the speed for data writing while also reducing the robustness against thermal fluctuations. For example, the low switching barrier of magnetic memories gives rise to their high-speed writing and thermal instability at nanoscale (like "superparamagnetism"). However, the key quantity for estimating thermal stability is somewhat different from the switching barrier. For the anisotropic Heisenberg model of a ferromagnet with exchange coupling J (usually <∼10 meV) and single-ion anisotropy K (usually <~1 meV), 在优化数据存储性能时，当考虑铁性变体之间的密集翻转势垒q（以每立方或平方埃的毫电子伏特为单位）时，通常存在权衡：低q有利于降低能耗和提高数据写入速度，但同时也会降低对抗热涨落的稳健性。例如，磁存储器的低翻转势垒导致其高速写入和纳米尺度的热不稳定性（如"超顺磁性"）。然而，估算热稳定性的关键量与翻转势垒有所不同。对于具有交换耦合J（通常<∼10 meV）和单离子各向异性K（通常<~1 meV）的铁磁体各向异性海森堡模型：
> 
> **superparamagnetism**: 超顺磁性——小尺寸磁性颗粒在热涨落下磁矩随机翻转的现象。
> 
> H = -J ∑_{<i,j>} S_i · S_j - K ∑_j (S_j^z)^2
> 
> For each spin with N nearest neighbors, the thermal stability for nonvolatility is determined by the "isolated" barrier (= NJ + K) for one spin to flip while all other spins around are fixed, while the switching barrier controlling the speed and energy cost is the "collective" barrier (= K) where all spins must "simultaneously" switch to the opposite direction. 对于具有N个最近邻的每个自旋，非易失性的热稳定性由单个自旋在周围所有其他自旋固定时翻转的"孤立"势垒（= NJ + K）决定，而控制速度和能耗的翻转势垒是"集体"势垒（= K），其中所有自旋必须"同时"翻转到相反方向。
> 
> Similar model can be applied for ferroelectricity. The "collective" switching barriers of all room-temperature ferroelectrics, to our knowledge, are higher than 27 meV/f.u. (∼kBT/f.u. at 300 K), e.g. 0.43 eV/f.u. for BiFeO3 (56). In comparison, the barriers for interlayer sliding are much lower [4.5 meV/f.u. for BN (21) and 0.15 meV/f.u. for WTe2 bilayer (22)]. This has to do with the weak van der Waals interaction between adjacent layers. The intralayer rigidity, on the other hand, leads to high "isolated" barrier and high stability against zero-field volatility (see the case of WTe2 bilayer in Fig. 5A, where the artificial antiferroelectric state formed by in-plane inhomogeneous strain is highly unstable) (22). 类似模型可应用于铁电性。据我们所知，所有室温铁电体的"集体"翻转势垒都高于27 meV/f.u.（∼300 K时的kBT/f.u.），例如BiFeO3为0.43 eV/f.u.（56）。相比之下，层间滑动的势垒要低得多[BN为4.5 meV/f.u.（21），WTe2双层为0.15 meV/f.u.（22）]。这与相邻层间弱的范德华相互作用有关。另一方面，层内刚性导致高"孤立"势垒和高抗零场挥发性稳定性（参见图5A中WTe2双层的情况，其中由面内非均匀应变形成的人工反铁电态高度不稳定）（22）。
> 
> This is because as the in-plane stretching modulus is much larger than the interlayer shearing modulus, sliding ferroelectricity needs to be quite collective and necessarily involves a relatively large "activation area" and collective defects, namely the ripplocation (31) (Fig. 5B). However, because of the atomic thickness, the total transformed volume and heat dissipation is still small. Such a unique combination enables picosecond-timescale high-speed data writing with experimentally acceptable field strength, as predicted (32, 57) and recently shown with optomechanical light pulses (58). 这是因为面内拉伸模量远大于层间剪切模量，滑动铁电性需要相当集体化，必然涉及相对较大的"激活面积"和集体缺陷，即ripplocation（31）（图5B）。然而，由于原子厚度，总转变体积和热耗散仍然很小。这种独特组合使皮秒时间尺度高速数据写入成为可能，且场强在实验可接受范围内，正如预测（32, 57）和最近用光机械光脉冲所展示的（58）。
> 
> Aside from ferroelectric switching between bistable states from AB to BA, the optical-driven transformation between inequivalent stacking orders from AA' to AB' has also been predicted (57) (see Fig. 5C, where the data can be written by laser beam and read by nonlinear Edelstein effect), where low switching barrier and high robustness are similarly ensured. 除了从AB到BA的双稳态之间的铁电翻转外，不等价堆叠顺序之间从AA'到AB'的光驱动转变也已被预测（57）（见图5C，其中数据可通过激光束写入并通过非线性Edelstein效应读出），同样确保了低翻转势垒和高稳健性。
> 
> **Edelstein effect**: 埃德尔斯坦效应——在自旋-轨道耦合系统中，外加电场产生自旋极化的效应。
> 
> Finally, we want to comment that interlayer sliding generates large xz or yz plastic strains, so in the "xz/yz" sense sliding ferroelectricity can also be called sliding "ferroelasticity." However, z is not a Bravais replication direction in 2D van der Waals materials, as only x and y are extensible. So, such a sliding displacement is often considered to be internal shuffling in a 2D Bravais lattice framework. Nonetheless, there will also be in-plane relaxation strains in xx, yy, and zz, and sometimes even xy, slaved to such "internal shuffling" (59) sliding displacement. The coupling between the electric polarization vector, xz/yz plastic strain, xx/yy/xy/zz relaxation (elastic) strain tensor, and the bending curvature inside ripplocation [flexoelectric effect (29)] makes this problem an extremely rich one. 最后，我们想评论的是，层间滑动产生大的xz或yz塑性应变，因此在"xz/yz"意义上，滑动铁电性也可称为滑动"铁弹性"。然而，z在二维范德华材料中不是布拉维平移方向，因为只有x和y是可延伸的。因此，这种滑动位移通常被视为二维布拉维晶格框架中的内部重排。尽管如此，在xx、yy和zz方向，有时甚至在xy方向，也会存在受制于这种"内部重排"（59）滑动位移的面内弛豫应变。电极化矢量、xz/yz塑性应变、xx/yy/xy/zz弛豫（弹性）应变张量以及ripplocation内部弯曲曲率[挠曲电效应（29）]之间的耦合使这一问题极为丰富。
> 
> **ferroelasticity**: 铁弹性——材料具有自发应变且该应变可被外加应力翻转的特性。 **flexoelectric effect**: 挠曲电效应——应变梯度与电极化之间的耦合效应。
>

> ## Outlook
> 
> 展望
> 
> In the booming field of 2D ferroelectricity, sliding ferroelectricity is unique in that it exists widely and exists only in 2D van der Waals stacked layers. Without significant vertical ion displacements (except in the ripplocation domain wall core), the vertical polarization can be switched by in-plane interlayer sliding with an ultralow barrier, which can be utilized for high-speed data writing with low heat dissipation. In addition to its unique switching mode, other related new physics like Moire ferroelectricity, metallic ferroelectricity, ferroelectric nonlinear anomalous Hall effect, sliding multiferroic couplings, and negative piezoelectricity [see the increase in polarizations for BN (21) and ZrI2 (60) bilayer upon vertical pressure] are emerging. 在蓬勃发展的二维铁电性领域中，滑动铁电性的独特之处在于它广泛存在且仅存在于二维范德华堆叠层中。无需显著的垂直离子位移（ripplocation畴壁核心除外），垂直极化可通过面内层间滑动以超低势垒翻转，可用于低热耗散的高速数据写入。除其独特的翻转模式外，其他相关的新物理如莫尔铁电性、金属铁电性、铁电非线性反常霍尔效应、滑动多铁耦合以及负压电性[参见BN（21）和ZrI2（60）双层在垂直压力下极化的增加]正在涌现。
> 
> **negative piezoelectricity**: 负压电性——材料在施加压力时极化增加而非减少的异常压电效应。
> 
> Actually, for many intrinsic physical properties of 2D monolayers (magnetism, excitonics, nontrivial topology, valleytronics, superconductivity, etc.) their potential couplings with sliding ferroelectricity via certain stacking or Moire ferroelectricity upon a twisted angle can be worthy of further exploration, as summarized in Fig. 6. 实际上，对于二维单层的许多本征物理性质（磁性、激子学、非平庸拓扑、谷电子学、超导电性等），它们通过特定堆叠的滑动铁电性或扭转角下的莫尔铁电性的潜在耦合值得进一步探索，如图6所总结。
> 
> There are still challenges to overcome at this early stage. The van der Waals charge transfer due to interlayer inequality is usually weak, leading to a low magnitude in polarizations. Even for the polarization obtained in BN bilayer that is an order of magnitude higher compared with WTe2 bilayer it is still less than one-half of the computed vertical polarization for thin-layer CuInP2S6 and In2Se3, almost two orders of magnitude lower compared with the bulk polarization of BiFeO3. However, it has been shown that even the WTe2 bilayer with a weak polarization below 0.2 pC/m can give rise to two distinct conductance states when using graphene as a detector (33). Moreover, many systems with much higher polarizations are yet to be explored via high-throughput screening of the material database, noting that the computed polarization of AlN bilayer in Table 1, almost five times higher than that of BN bilayer, is also the highest vertical polarization of current 2D ferroelectrics known to date. 在这一早期阶段仍有挑战需要克服。由于层间不等价导致的范德华电荷转移通常很弱，导致极化幅度较低。即使BN双层获得的极化比WTe2双层高一个数量级，仍不到薄层CuInP2S6和In2Se3计算垂直极化的一半，比BiFeO3的体极化低近两个数量级。然而，已证明即使极化低于0.2 pC/m的WTe2双层在使用石墨烯作为检测器时也能产生两个不同的电导态（33）。此外，许多具有更高极化的体系尚待通过材料数据库的高通量筛选进行探索，注意到表1中AlN双层的计算极化几乎是BN双层的五倍，也是迄今为止已知的当前二维铁电体中最高的垂直极化。
> 
> **high-throughput screening**: 高通量筛选——利用计算方法和数据库快速筛选大量候选材料的策略。
> 
> While a free-standing, slightly slacked geometry in a vacuum is theoretically the cleanest to discuss sliding ferroelectricity, in practical applications it might be advantageous to put the 2D van der Waals materials on some solid substrate, and exposed to some gas environment or solid electrode on the top. The issue of mobile ionic impurities and electronic charge puddles that might segregate on top/bottom interfaces and depolarize the vertical electric dipole is something one has to contend with. Covering relatively inert 2D encapsulations like BN on top and bottom to maintain clean interfaces could be a solution, but this will alter the bending modulus and thus the ripplocation domain wall width, and thus the domain kinetics, from the idealized situation. 虽然真空中稍微松弛的自由支撑几何结构在理论上讨论滑动铁电性最为干净，但在实际应用中，将二维范德华材料放置在某种固体衬底上，并在顶部暴露于某种气体环境或固体电极可能更有优势。可能在顶/底界面偏析并去极化垂直电偶极的可移动离子杂质和电子电荷聚集问题是必须应对的。在顶部和底部覆盖相对惰性的二维封装层如BN以维持清洁界面可能是一种解决方案，但这将改变弯曲模量，从而改变ripplocation畴壁宽度，进而改变畴动力学，偏离理想情况。
>
