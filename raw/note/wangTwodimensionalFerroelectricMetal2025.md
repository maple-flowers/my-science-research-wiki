> ## 一、引言
> 
> 电催化是解决能源与环境问题的关键技术，尤其在**氧还原反应（Oxygen Reduction Reaction, ORR）** 和**氧析出反应（Oxygen Evolution Reaction, OER）** 中扮演核心角色。传统高效催化剂多依赖贵金属（如Pt, IrO₂），其高昂的成本和稀缺性限制了大规模应用。因此，寻找低成本、高活性的非贵金属替代催化剂成为该领域的研究热点。
> 
> 本文献《二维铁电金属CuCrX₂ (X = S, Se) 用于高效电催化》发表于 _Computational Materials Today_ 期刊，为这一挑战提供了一个极具创新性的解决思路。研究的核心价值在于，它打破了“铁电体必须是绝缘体”的传统认知，巧妙地利用了二维材料**CuCrX₂**同时具备的**铁电性**与**金属性**。通过第一性原理计算，作者团队证明了这种独特的“铁电金属”能够作为高效的ORR/OER双功能催化剂，其性能甚至超越了部分贵金属催化剂。这项工作不仅为电催化剂设计开辟了新范式，也为二维铁电材料的应用拓展了新疆域。
> 
> ## 二、论文结构与逻辑概览
> 
> 本文遵循“提出概念 → 验证性质 → 评估性能 → 揭示机理”的清晰逻辑链条。
> 
> 1.  **提出概念**：首先，作者点明研究背景与矛盾——电催化剂需导电（金属性），而传统铁电体是绝缘体。接着，他们提出解决方案：二维铁电金属**CuCrX₂**，这种材料在实验上已被证实存在。
> 2.  **验证性质**：通过展示**CuCrX₂**的几何结构、能带图和态密度，作者直观地证明了该材料确实同时具备金属性（无带隙）和铁电性（不对称结构导致的垂直极化）。
> 3.  **评估性能**：这是论文的核心部分。作者系统计算了不同层数（2-4层）的**CuCrS₂**和**CuCrSe₂**在两个不同极化表面（P+和P-）上的ORR和OER反应路径、吉布斯自由能变化和过电位。
> 4.  **揭示机理**：通过对比不同表面、不同材料的计算数据，作者揭示了铁电极化如何调控电荷转移，从而影响中间体的吸附能，并最终决定了催化活性的差异。
> 
> 全文的逻辑论证是通过第一性原理计算提供直接证据，并基于电化学理论框架（如萨巴蒂尔原理）进行严密推论，最终得出结论。
>

> ## 四、正文核心内容剖析
> 
> #### 1. 研究背景与问题
> 
> 文章开篇即明确了电催化在能源转换中的重要性，并指出ORR/OER反应受限于高过电位。现有非贵金属催化剂（如单原子/二维材料复合体系）虽取得进展，但在合成控制和活性位点密度上仍面临挑战。关键在于，作者提出了一个根本性的科学矛盾：**铁电体**因极化电场可调控表面化学，但传统上必须是**绝缘体**；而**电催化剂**必须是**导体**以快速传输电子。这一矛盾因“二维铁电金属”这一新物态的出现而得以化解。
> 
> #### 2. 方法与模型
> 
> 本研究采用基于**密度泛函理论（Density Functional Theory, DFT）** 的**第一性原理计算**作为核心研究方法。具体设置包括：
> 
> -   **软件包**：VASP
> -   **交换关联泛函**：使用自旋极化的PBE形式GGA。
> -   **离子-电子相互作用**：使用PAW赝势。
> -   **范德华修正**：采用DFT-D3方法，这对于二维层状材料至关重要。
> -   **电化学模型**：采用了**计算氢电极（Computational Hydrogen Electrode, CHE）** 模型，该模型由Nørskov等人提出，是电催化理论计算的标准框架。它将质子-电子对（H⁺ + e⁻）的化学势与气相H₂分子的一半能量联系起来，巧妙地避开了对溶剂化质子的直接计算。反应路径遵循经典的**联合路径**，涉及OOH*、O*、OH*等中间体。
> 
> #### 3. 实验结果与讨论
> 
> -   **材料特性**：首先确认了**CuCrX₂**的金属性（能带无间隙）和垂直铁电性（结构不对称），且费米能级附近的态密度主要由表面S/Se原子贡献，预示了高密度的催化活性位点。
> -   **电荷转移机制**：通过功函数和差分电荷密度分析，揭示了铁电极化对催化作用的直接调控机制：极化导致P+表面积累更多电子，在吸附氧分子时能提供更大的电荷转移，从而增强了对含氧中间体的吸附。
> -   **催化活性评估**：
>     -   **ORR性能**：核心发现是，极化方向对催化性能影响巨大。P+表面的性能远优于P-表面。其中，三层CuCrS₂的P+表面取得了极低的ORR过电位（**0.28 V**），不仅优于Pt基催化剂（~0.45 V），也是作者声称的已知最低值之一。相反，CuCrSe₂因对O₂吸附过强，导致第一步反应能垒过高，表现不佳。
>     -   **OER性能**：同样，CuCrS₂的P+表面表现出色，过电位（**0.43 V**）低于IrO₂（0.56 V），展现了优异的双功能催化潜力。

> ABSTRACT 摘要 Electrocatalysts play an important role in the oxygen reduction and evolution reactions (ORR/OER) in fuel cells. Although ferroelectric polarizations may favor catalysis in some cases, ferroelectrics are supposed to be insulators while electrocatalysts should be metallic to facilitate rapid electron transfer. We note that robust vertical ferroelectricity in 2D metallic CuCrX2 (X = S, Se) has been experimentally confirmed in recent reports. In this paper we show first-principles evidence that they can be ideal candidates as noble-metal-free OER/ORR bifunctional catalysts with low overpotentials, high conductivity and active site density, which are long-sought but remain elusive. The catalysis is remarkably facilitated by their ferroelectric polarizations, which can be revealed by charge analysis and the large difference in overpotential between two surfaces, and the estimated minimum ORR overpotential (0.28 V) and OER overpotential (0.43 V) of few-layer CuCrX2 are all much lower compared with prevalent electrocatalysts based on noble metal. 电催化剂在燃料电池的氧还原和氧析出反应（ORR/OER）中扮演着重要角色。尽管铁电极化在某些情况下可能有利于催化，但铁电体理应是绝缘体，而电催化剂则应为金属性以促进快速电子转移。我们注意到，二维金属性CuCrX2（X = S, Se）中稳健的垂直铁电性已在近期的实验报道中得到证实。在本文中，我们展示了第一性原理证据，表明它们可以作为不含贵金属的OER/ORR双功能催化剂的理想候选者，具有低过电位、高导电性和高活性位点密度，这些特性长期以来备受追求但难以实现。其铁电极化显著促进了催化作用，这可以通过电荷分析以及两个表面之间过电位的巨大差异来揭示，并且估算的少层CuCrX2的最低ORR过电位（0.28 V）和OER过电位（0.43 V）均远低于基于贵金属的主流电催化剂。 **Electrocatalyst**: 电催化剂，加速电极表面电荷转移反应的物质。 **Oxygen Reduction Reaction (ORR)**: 氧还原反应，氧气获得电子被还原的过程。 **Oxygen Evolution Reaction (OER)**: 氧析出反应，水被氧化产生氧气的过程。 **Overpotential**: 过电位，驱动电化学反应所需超出热力学平衡电势的额外电压。 **First-principles**: 第一性原理，基于量子力学基本原理、不依赖经验参数的计算方法。

> Introduction 引言 Electrocatalysis using electrical energy to initiate chemical reactions have garnered significant attention in past decades [1–3], and in particular, electrochemical water cycle has been explored as a sustainable approach for resolving environmental issues like the greenhouse effect and air pollution. For example, oxygen evolution and reduction reactions (OER and ORR, respectively) are involved in fuel cells and metal–air batteries with high energy conversion efficiency and zero pollution. However, the reaction efficiency in both processes is limited by high overpotentials. Traditional commercial electrocatalysts for ORR/OER include noble metal atoms and their compounds like Pt-based catalysts where the ORR overpotential can be reduced to 0.45 V [4]. Their high cost and limited availability have stimulated efforts on developing alternative catalysts, including electrocatalysts of single metal atom supported on two-dimensional (2D) materials [5–8], where low ORR potentials around 0.3–0.4 eV have been predicted [5,9–13]. However, the control of metal dispersion on those 2D materials is still challenging in synthesis. It is noteworthy that ferroelectrics have also been explored for controllable chemical applications to overcome the Sabatier limitation, where the polarizations lead to different electrostatic potentials and electron distributions on different surfaces for controllable surface chemistry of catalysts [14,15]. Ferroelectrics are supposed to be insulators (or the polarizations will be screened by mobile charges upon an external field) while electrocatalysts should be metallic to facilitate rapid electron transfer between reactants and electrodes. However, recent studies have shown that the polarizations are not fully screened in a few 2D ferroelectric metals since the electrons are vertically confined [16,17], which may serve as electrocatalysts [18]. In 2020 we predicted the existence of robust vertical ferroelectricity in 2D metallic CuCrX2 (X = S, Se) [19], which have been experimentally confirmed in later reports [20–23]. In this paper we predict by ab initio calculations that those 2D ferroelectric metals may serve as low-cost electrocatalysts with low ORR overpotential (down to 0.28 V) and OER overpotential (down to 0.43 V), also meeting the requirements of high conductivity, carrier mobility and active site density for efficient electrocatalysis. 利用电能引发化学反应的電催化在过去几十年中引起了广泛关注[1–3]，特别是电化学水循环已被探索作为解决温室效应和空气污染等环境问题的可持续方法。例如，氧析出和氧还原反应（分别为OER和ORR）涉及具有高能量转换效率和零污染的燃料电池和金属-空气电池。然而，这两个过程的反应效率都受到高过电位的限制。传统的ORR/OER商业电催化剂包括贵金属原子及其化合物，如Pt基催化剂，其ORR过电位可降至0.45 V [4]。它们的高成本和有限供应激发了开发替代催化剂的努力，包括负载在二维（2D）材料上的单金属原子电催化剂[5–8]，其中预测了约0.3–0.4 eV的低ORR电位[5,9–13]。然而，在这些2D材料上控制金属分散在合成中仍然具有挑战性。值得注意的是，铁电体也已被探索用于可控化学应用以克服Sabatier限制，其中极化导致不同表面具有不同的静电势和电子分布，从而实现可控的催化剂表面化学[14,15]。铁电体理应是绝缘体（否则极化会被外加电场下的可移动电荷屏蔽），而电催化剂则应为金属性以促进反应物和电极之间的快速电子转移。然而，近期研究表明，在少数二维铁电金属中，由于电子在垂直方向上受限，极化并未被完全屏蔽[16,17]，这可能使其用作电催化剂[18]。我们在2020年预测了二维金属性CuCrX2（X = S, Se）中存在稳健的垂直铁电性[19]，这在后来的报道中得到了实验证实[20–23]。在本文中，我们通过从头算计算预测，这些二维铁电金属可作为低成本电催化剂，具有低ORR过电位（低至0.28 V）和OER过电位（低至0.43 V），同时满足高效电催化对高导电性、载流子迁移率和活性位点密度的要求。 **Sabatier principle**: 萨巴蒂尔原理，指出催化剂与反应中间体之间的结合能应适中，过强或过弱都不利于催化活性的原理。

> Results and discussion 结果与讨论 According to crystal field theory, the sp3 tetrahedral configuration is usually energetically favorable for univalent Cu+ with a full d electron shell configuration of d [10]. In CuCrX2 (X = S, Se) systems, each Cu atom is tetrahedrally coordinated with one S/Se in one adjacent layer and three S/Se atoms in another adjacent layer, so the vertical distance between the Cu ions and the two adjacent layers are different, resulting in a vertical electrical polarization, which are all around 2 pC/m for 2–4 layers [19]. The geometric structure, band structure and partial density of states (PDOS) for bilayer CuCrS2 are shown in Fig. 1(b), revealing its metallicity combined with ferroelectricity. The PDOS near the Fermi level seem to be mainly contributed by S atoms instead of metal ions, where the surface S atoms serving as active sites should render efficient catalysis with high active site density. The work functions (Φ) of two surfaces are 3.98 eV and 4.34 eV, respectively (see Fig. 1(c)), where electrons migrate from the low work function side (denoted as P - surface) to the high work function side (denoted as P + surface), generally favoring the catalytic process in the latter surface. The differential charge densities along -z direction of bilayer CuCrS2 with oxygen atom adsorbed at two surfaces are compared in Fig. 1(d) and (e), indicating a larger charge transfer for P + surface compared with P - surface. As an important factor that determines the ORR activity of a given catalyst, the adsorption behaviors of O2 molecules on CuCrX2 are investigated, as shown in Fig. 2. According to our simulations, the adsorption energies of O2 molecules on 2 L, 3 L, and 4 L CuCrX2 ranging from 0.78 to 1.31 eV, are all within desirable range based on the well-known Sabatier principle, where the high density surface X atoms serve as active sites. It is also shown that the adsorption of oxygen molecules is generally stronger in CuCrSe2 compared with CuCrS2 of the same layer, which may be attributed to the difference in electronegativity between S and Se. Four reactions are generally involved in the ORR process: (1) O2 molecules adsorbed on the active sites on the catalyst surface are hydrogenated to form OOH* intermediates by capturing proton– electron pairs. (2) The OOH* intermediate is further hydrogenated to form the O* intermediate with an H2O molecule desorbed from the surface. (3) OH* species are further formed after O* upon the capturing of the third proton – electron pair. (4) A second H2O molecule is formed upon the hydrogenation of OH* species, which is weakly bound to the catalyst surface and eventually spontaneous desorption. The adsorption of the intermediate on the catalyst surface is a prerequisite for the reaction [24], and our calculated Gibbs adsorption free energy (ΔG) of three oxygen-containing intermediates involved in the ORR/OER reactions on the surface of CuCrX2 (see Fig. S1, Tables S1 and S2) are within the desirable range. Fig. 3 displays the ORR Gibbs free energy curve of CuCrX2 along the four-electron transfer path at different electrode voltages,where the four-step elementary reactions can be spontaneous in thermodynamics when U= 0.00 V. The potentiometric steps (PDS) of most catalysts are located at different electron transfer steps at the equilibrium potential of 1.23 V. The ground state of the 2 L CuCrX2 is ferromagnetic, and the PDOS step occurs respectively at the final step OH*→H2O and the third step O*→OH* on the P - and P + surfaces of 2 L CuCrS2. In comparison, PDS occurs at the first step O2→OOH* for both surfaces of 2 L CuCrSe2. It turns out that the ORR overpotential on the P - and P + surfaces of 2 L CuCrS2 and CuCrSe2 are 0.68 V, 0.38 V, 0.60 V and 1.06 V, respectively (Fig. 3b-e), where the value of 0.38 V for P + surface of bilayer CuCrS2 is much lower compared with Pt-based catalyst (0.45 V), indicating its potential high efficiency as ORR catalyst. For three-layer and four-layer CuCrX2 (X = S, Se) respectively denoted as Cu2(CrX2)3 and Cu3(CrX2)4, their in-plane spin-couplings are all ferromagnetic in the ground state, with mixing configuration ferromagnetic coupling and anti-ferromagnetic interlayer couplings shown in Figs. 4(a) and 5(a), and they are all metallic with remarkable PDOS near the Fermi level, which accord with previous calculations [19]. Akin to the bilayer, the PDS step occurs respectively at the final step OH*→H2O and the third step O*→OH* on the P - and P + surfaces of 3 L & 4 L CuCrS2, while for both surfaces of 3 L & 4 L CuCrSe2, PDS occurs at the first step O2→OOH* . The ORR overpotentials on the P - and P + surfaces of trilayer CuCrS2 and CuCrSe2 are estimated to be 0.70 V, 0.28 V, 0.54 V and 1.02 V, respectively (Fig. 4(b)), which are 0.71 V, 0.33 V, 0.51 V and 0.99 V, respectively for corresponding quadrilayers (Fig. 5(b)). In particular, the ORR overpotential of 3 L P +-CuCrS2 (0.28 V) is not only lower than 4 L (0.33 V) and 2 L (0.38 V), but also the lowest value in current reported ORR catalysts to our knowledge. > It seems that the catalytic performances of P +surfaces are all superior to P – surfaces in those few-layer CuCrS2, suggesting the important role of ferroelectric polarizations also revealed in Fig. 1(d) and (e). In the abovementioned Gibbs free energy change diagrams, the final step OH*→H2O is more favorable in P +surfaces compared with P – surfaces, which is contrary for the third step O*→OH* , leading to the change in PDS. The inferior ORR catalytic performance of CuCrSe2 systems can be attributed to the electronegativity of Se atoms making the first step O2→OOH* uphill, despite the larger adsorption energy of oxygen molecule revealed in Fig. 2(b), which also accords with the well-known Sabatier principle. Herein the first step O2→OOH* is favored in P -surfaces compared with P + surfaces, leading to lower overpotentials. We also investigate their OER reactions that can be deemed as the inverse process of ORR reactions. Fig. 6 displays the OER Gibbs free energy curves of few-layer CuCrX2 along the four-electron transfer path at different electrode voltages. These catalysts show a gradual upward trend in the ladder diagram of free energy change, indicating that the OER reaction is an energy dissipation process, with the black arrow indicating its potential determining step (PDS). Most of the PDS occur in the third step (O* -OOH*), in which the ηOER values of bilayer and trilayer CuCrS2 on P + surface (respectively 0.43 V and 0.50 V) are smaller compared with prevalent material IrO2 (0.56 V) [25], revealing their excellent OER catalytic activity, also suggesting that they are ideal candidates as ORR/OER bifunctional catalysts considering their combination of low ηOER and ηORR values. 在这些少层CuCrS2中，P+表面的催化性能似乎均优于P-表面，表明铁电极化的重要作用，这一点也在图1(d)和(e)中得以揭示。在上述吉布斯自由能变化图中，与P-表面相比，最后一步OH*→H2O在P+表面更有利，而第三步O*→OH_则相反，导致了PDS的改变。CuCrSe2体系较差的ORR催化性能可归因于Se原子的电负性使得第一步O2→OOH_上坡，尽管图2(b)显示其氧分子吸附能更大，这也符合著名的Sabatier原理。在此，第一步O2→OOH_在P-表面比在P+表面更有利，导致更低的过电位。我们还研究了它们的OER反应，这可以视为ORR反应的逆过程。图6展示了少层CuCrX2在不同电极电压下沿四电子转移路径的OER吉布斯自由能曲线。这些催化剂在自由能变化的阶梯图中呈现逐渐上升的趋势，表明OER反应是一个能量耗散过程，黑色箭头指示其电位决定步骤（PDS）。大多数PDS发生在第三步（O_-OOH*），其中双层和三层CuCrS2在P+表面的ηOER值（分别为0.43 V和0.50 V）小于主流材料IrO2（0.56 V）[25]，揭示了其优异的OER催化活性，同时考虑到它们兼具低ηOER和ηORR值，表明它们是ORR/OER双功能催化剂的理想候选者。

> Conclusion 结论 In summary, we predict few-layer ferroelectric metal CuCrX2 can be ideal candidates as noble-metal-free OER/ORR bifunctional catalysts with low overpotentials, high conductivity and active site density. Facilitated by their ferroelectric polarizations, their minimum ORR overpotential (0.28 V) and OER overpotential (0.43 V) of few-layer CuCrX2 are lower compared with prevalent electrocatalysts based on noble metal, indicating that they can be ideal OER/ORR bifunctional catalysts. 总之，我们预测少层铁电金属CuCrX2可以作为不含贵金属的OER/ORR双功能催化剂的理想候选者，具有低过电位、高导电性和高活性位点密度。在其铁电极化的促进下，少层CuCrX2的最低ORR过电位（0.28 V）和OER过电位（0.43 V）低于基于贵金属的主流电催化剂，表明它们可以成为理想的OER/ORR双功能催化剂。
