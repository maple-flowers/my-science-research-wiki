> ## 一、引言
> 
> **过渡金属硫族化合物 (Transition Metal Dichalcogenides, TMDs)** 是一类层状材料，其层间由微弱的**范德华 (van der Waals, vdW) 力**连接，因此可以像撕胶带一样被剥离至单原子层厚度。这种特性为研究量子现象（如**电荷密度波 (Charge Density Wave, CDW)** ）如何随材料厚度演变提供了绝佳平台。长期以来，科学界对TMDs中CDW的形成机制，以及其与超导、磁性等强关联电子态的关系争论不休。
> 
> 本研究聚焦于典型的TMD材料 **2H-NbSe₂**。过去，对2H-NbSe₂表面CDW的研究几乎都基于单层模型，而忽略了层间堆叠的影响。然而，近期在1T型TMDs（如1T-TaS₂）中的突破性发现表明，层间CDW的堆叠方式足以解释其块体材料的绝缘性，这颠覆了传统认知。这引出了一个核心问题：**在金属性的2H-NbSe₂中，层间CDW堆叠是否也扮演着重要角色？如果有，其规律是什么？能否被实验检测到？**
> 
> 这篇论文正是为了回答这些问题而生。它首次系统性地揭示了2H-NbSe₂双层中CDW的垂直堆叠规律，发现层间耦合会导致一种**自发对称性破缺**，产生比单层更丰富的CDW模式。该研究不仅为重新审视过去大量的表面实验数据提供了理论基础，也为未来通过设计层间堆叠来调控TMDs的量子特性指明了方向。
> 
> ## 二、论文结构与逻辑概览
> 
> 本文遵循“**提出问题 → 构建模型与方法 → 呈现结果 → 讨论与结论**”的经典科学论证结构，逻辑链条清晰严密。
> 
> 1.  **背景与问题提出 (引言)**：阐明TMDs中CDW研究的重要性，指出当前研究忽略层间CDW堆叠的局限性，并以1T-TaS₂的发现为引子，明确提出了金属性2H-NbSe₂中是否存在类似效应的核心问题。
> 2.  **研究方法 (方法)**：详细介绍了基于**密度泛函理论 (Density-Functional Theory, DFT)** 的第一性原理计算方法，并特别强调了为准确描述层间vdW相互作用而采用的不同修正方案。
> 3.  **核心发现 (结果)**：
>     -   **A. 正常相结构**：首先确定了不同计算精度下双层2H-NbSe₂在没有CDW时的基础结构参数，为后续研究奠定基准。
>     -   **B. CDW的垂直堆叠**：系统构建并计算了由单层中三种最稳定CDW模式（HC, CC, HX）组合而成的所有可能双层堆叠构型（称为“混合物”和“位移”），建立了能量层级图，找到了基态和激发态。
>     -   **C. 电子性质**：通过分析**态密度 (Density of States, DOS)**，揭示了不同堆叠方式对电子结构，特别是费米能级附近载流子浓度的影响。
>     -   **D. Se图案与STM模拟**：模拟了不同堆叠构型的**扫描隧道显微镜 (Scanning Tunneling Microscope, STM)** 图像，发现它们在实空间展现出独特的指纹特征，为实验鉴别提供了直接证据。
>     -   **E. 电荷密度与结构因子**：计算了倒空间的**几何结构因子 (Geometric Structure Factor)**，从另一个维度展示了不同堆叠结构的对称性差异，为X射线衍射等技术提供了理论对照。
> 4.  **总结与展望 (讨论与结论)**：总结了层间堆叠在2H型TMDs中的重要性，并提出了未来可能的研究方向（如磁性、调控集体激发、扭转电子学等）。
>

> ## 四、正文核心内容剖析
> 
> ### 1. 研究背景与动机
> 
> 本文的出发点基于一个深刻的矛盾：**实验上观测到2H-NbSe₂的CDW性质具有明显的厚度依赖性，但理论解释却大量依赖单层模型，忽视了层间相互作用。** 1T-TaS₂中“层间堆叠驱动金属-绝缘体转变”的重大发现，为作者提供了灵感。他们敏锐地意识到，即使是在没有如此剧烈相变的金属体系2H-NbSe₂中，层间CDW的堆叠也可能产生丰富的、可观测的物理效应。研究对象锁定在双层，是因为它是保持块体中心对称性的最小单元，也是理解从单层到块体演变的关键环节。
> 
> ### 2. 研究方法与模型
> 
> 研究采用基于DFT的**第一性原理计算方法**。核心挑战在于准确描述层间微弱的vdW作用力。作者采用了多管齐下的策略，对比了多种vdW修正泛函（GGA+D2, GGA+TS, GGA+DF, GGA+MBD@FI）的计算结果，确保了结论的可靠性。为模拟CDW，他们构建了 **3 × 3超胞**，这足以容纳已知的周期性晶格畸变。通过对离子初始位置进行微调，他们获得了不同的亚稳态，即不同的CDW堆叠结构。这种构建“混合物”和“位移”的方法论，是系统探索复杂堆叠构型的关键。
> 
> ### 3. 实验结果与讨论
> 
> （注：本文为纯理论计算，其“实验”指计算实验）
> 
> **A. 能量景观与基态** 计算揭示了双层2H-NbSe₂中CDW堆叠的能量景观远比单层复杂。单层只需考虑HC、CC、HX三种模式，而双层由于存在两种单层的组合（混合物）和它们之间的相对位移，产生了多种能量相近的构型。**核心发现是基态为HC-HC(_{S3})**，即两层都是HC结构，并具有特定位移。更有趣的是，**HC-CC混合物（如HC-CC(_{S4})）的能量仅略高于基态**，这意味着在实验中（如缺陷、热激发）极有可能观察到这些混合堆叠结构。
> 
> **B. 电子结构指纹** 总DOS分析（图3）显示，不同堆叠构型的整体电子结构相似，但在费米能级附近有细微差别。能量最低的构型，其费米能级处的态密度被消耗得最明显。这为理解稳定性的微观来源提供了线索：尽管2H-NbSe₂的CDW形成机制复杂，但其能量增益仍与费米面附近的电子结构重构密切相关。此外，层间Se原子的p_z轨道杂化形成的尖锐峰，是层间耦合的直接电子结构证据。
> 
> **C. 实验可检测的指纹** 这是本文最突出的贡献。作者通过模拟STM图像和计算几何结构因子，为实验学家提供了两种直接观测层间堆叠的方法。
> 
> -   **实空间 (STM)**：不同的堆叠构型，如HC-HC(_{S3})、HC-HC(_{S1})和HC-CC(_{S4})，在STM图像上表现出截然不同的对称性破缺模式。例如，模拟图像中绿色高亮斑点的位置和形状都有明显区别（图5）。作者量化了这些图像的起伏度（29-39 pm），认为当今高分辨率STM技术（优于10 pm垂直分辨率）完全有能力分辨。
> -   **倒空间 (结构因子)**：通过分析来自费米能级附近电荷的结构因子，滤除了原子核的强贡献后，CDW信号变得突出。不同堆叠构型的差异模式（图6(d) vs 6(f)）同样清晰可辨，为X射线衍射、TEM等技术提供了理论依据。
> 
> ## 五、结论与贡献
> 
> 本文通过系统的第一性原理计算，得出以下核心结论：
> 
> 1.  **垂直堆叠至关重要**：在2H-NbSe₂双层中，CDW的层间堆叠不可忽略，它会导致**自发对称性破缺**，产生比单层更丰富的CDW模式。
> 2.  **能量景观复杂**：双层CDW的基态是HC-HC(_{S3})，但存在多种能量接近的亚稳态，特别是HC-HC和HC-CC混合物，它们可能在实验中共存。
> 3.  **堆叠效应可检测**：不同的CDW堆叠构型在模拟STM图像和几何结构因子中展现出独特的“指纹”，为当前实验技术提供了明确的识别目标。
> 
> **本文的主要贡献在于：**
> 
> -   **理论范式转变**：将TMDs中CDW的研究从“单层主导”的范式，系统性地推向“考虑层间堆叠”的新范式，这适用于所有金属性层状TMDs。
> -   **提供实验指南**：为高分辨率STM、X射线衍射、TEM等实验提供了具体的、可量化的理论预测和对比基准，直接指导未来的实验探索。
> -   **连接微观与宏观**：在微观的层间耦合与宏观可测量的电子结构、STM图像之间建立了清晰的因果联系。
> 
> ## 六、未来发展方向展望
> 
> 基于本文的研究，未来可在以下方向深入拓展：
> 
> -   **纳入更多自由度**：研究层间**扭转角度 (twist angle)** 和**外部压力**对CDW堆叠的影响，这将是“扭转电子学”和高压物理在该领域的前沿交叉。
> -   **从静态到动态**：计算不同CDW堆叠构型的**声子谱**，特别是拉曼活性模式，可以帮助解释拉曼光谱实验，并评估振动熵对能量稳定性的贡献，从而理解温度效应。
> -   **从双层到块体/异质结**：将双层模型推广到块体或多层薄膜，研究三维长程堆叠序的形成。同时，探索2H-NbSe₂与其他二维材料（如石墨烯、hBN）构成的范德华异质结中的近邻效应。
> -   **探索奇异量子态**：深入挖掘文中提到的对称性破缺与潜在磁性的关联，以及不同堆叠对超导配对机制的影响，寻找可能存在的拓扑超导或非常规超导态。
> 
> ## 七、学术思考与批判性分析
> 
> 作为深度解读，我们需要对本文进行批判性思考，提出以下问题：
> 
> 1.  **静态总能 vs. 热力学稳定性**：论文主要基于0 K下的总能计算来讨论能量层级。虽然作者通过类比1T-TaS₂和估算振动熵来论证其热力学稳定性，但缺乏对有限温度下**自由能**的直接计算或分子动力学模拟。实验临界温度（~33 K）下的热激发是否会完全抹平这些微小的能量差异（~0.18 meV/f.u.），导致实验中观察到的是不同构型的动态平均，而非某种纯态？
> 2.  **衬底效应的忽略**：论文的模型是悬浮的双层，但几乎所有实验都需要衬底支撑。衬底的晶格失配、电荷转移和界面vdW作用，可能会显著改变双层CDW的能量景观，甚至稳定某些在悬浮状态下不存在的构型。论文的结论能否直接应用于在SiC或石墨烯衬底上生长的2H-NbSe₂，是一个待检验的问题。
> 3.  **超胞约束与缺陷自由度**：研究使用3×3超胞来施加周期性边界条件，这本质上排除了非公度CDW或更大尺度的畴结构。实验中，CDW总是以畴的形式存在，并由缺陷钉扎。本文的“混合物”和“位移”模型能否准确描述真实畴壁的结构和能量，值得商榷。将不同堆叠构型理解为畴内的基态，可能是一个更合理的尝试。
> 4.  **STM模拟的局限性**：文中使用的STM模拟是基于Tersoff-Hamann模型，它假设针尖是完美的球形s波轨道，并忽略了针尖的实际电子态和样品的形变。真实的STM图像，尤其是高分辨率的，可能会受到针尖效应（如p波、d波成分）的显著影响，导致理论与实验图像存在系统偏差。
> 5.  **CDW堆叠与超导的关联**：论文在引言和展望中多次提及超导，但正文并未计算不同CDW堆叠对超导性质（如库珀对密度、能隙对称性）的影响。作为一种与CDW共存的量子态，超导性对费米面的细节变化极为敏感。不同堆叠构型所导致的费米面结构差异，是否会显著影响超导转变温度或配对机制，是一个极具价值但未被触及的深层问题。
> 
> ## 八、用户问题预测与解答
> 
> 为了帮助读者更好地理解，我预测以下5个可能的问题并给出解答：
> 
> **Q1: 什么是“混合物”和“位移”？它们为什么如此重要？** **A1:** 可以这样类比：我们把上下两层单层的CDW模式想象成两块各有特定花纹（HC、CC或HX）的透明玻璃板。**“混合物”（Blend）** 指的是你选择哪两块花纹的玻璃板叠在一起，比如两块都是HC花纹（HC-HC），或者一块HC、一块CC（HC-CC）。**“位移”（Displacement）** 则是在选定两块玻璃板后，你如何将它们相互对齐，比如是让花纹完全对齐，还是错开一点。它们之所以重要，是因为不同的混合物和位移组合，就像不同的叠放方式，会导致整个双层系统的最终图案（对称性）和能量发生根本变化，这正是本文的核心发现。
> 
> **Q2: 为什么研究2H-NbSe₂要从双层开始，而不是直接研究单层或块体？** **A2:** 这里有一个“金凤花姑娘原理”（Goldilocks principle）。**单层**过于简单，丢失了层间耦合这一关键信息，而它正是本文要研究的主题。**块体**又过于复杂，有无限多层的堆叠，自由度太多，难以进行精确的解析和建模。**双层**恰好是“刚刚好”的模型：它是最简单的能体现层间相互作用的系统，同时也是保持块体材料中心对称性的最小单元。因此，它是连接单层物理和块体物理的完美桥梁。
> 
> **Q3: 既然STM是表面灵敏的，为什么能探测到下层的信息？** **A3:** 这是一个很好的问题。虽然STM探测的隧穿电流主要来自最表层的原子，但它的强弱并不仅仅取决于表面原子的“地形”。最终成像的**表观高度**，实际上是表面在特定偏压下的**局域电子态密度**的等能面。下层CDW的电荷分布会通过层间杂化作用，调制上层原子的电子态，从而改变表面的局域态密度。这种“电子结构”的调制，会像水印一样“穿透”到上层，被STM捕捉到，虽然信号可能比直接的表面形貌调制要弱，但正如文中所证明的，其强度差异仍在现代仪器的探测范围之内。
> 
> **Q4: 在1T-TaS₂中观察到的堆叠效应，与本文在2H-NbSe₂中发现的有什么本质区别？** **A4:** 这是理解本文意义的关键。区别在于效应的“剧烈”程度。在1T-TaS₂中，层间CDW（大卫之星）的特定堆叠直接导致了电子结构的根本性改变——从金属变成了绝缘体，这是一个巨大的、易于观测的信号。而在2H-NbSe₂中，体系始终是金属性的，没有发生如此剧烈的相变。因此，堆叠效应更加“微妙”，表现为电子结构的精细变化、对称性的破缺和STM图像上图案的差异，而不是一个“开-关”式的转变。所以，本文的发现更具挑战性，也更具普遍意义，它证明了即使在没有巨大效应的金属体系中，层间堆叠依然扮演着重要角色。
> 
> **Q5: 这项研究对我们理解或应用2H-NbSe₂的超导性有什么帮助？** **A5:** 这触及了凝聚态物理的一个核心问题：CDW和超导之间的共存与竞争。2H-NbSe₂同时拥有CDW和超导两种量子基态。本文揭示，CDW本身的堆叠方式就存在多种可能性，且能量相近。这意味着，在真实的样品中，CDW的微观状态可能非常不均匀，由不同堆叠构型的“畴”组成。这些畴界和畴内的不同电子结构，可能会对超导电子的配对和输运产生复杂影响。例如，它可能导致超导能隙在空间上的不均匀分布，或者为非常规的配对机制（如FFLO态）提供额外的自由度。因此，这项研究为理解超导的微观环境提供了更精确的蓝图，也为未来通过调控CDW堆叠来调控超导性开辟了思路。
>

> ### Q1：什么是“电荷密度波（CDW）”？为什么它很重要？
> 
> **A:** 想象一杯水结成冰，水分子从无序变为有序。**电荷密度波（CDW）** 是电子世界里的类似现象。在特定温度下，材料中的电子浓度不再均匀分布，而是像士兵列队一样，自发形成周期性的疏密波。伴随这种电子密度的重新排布，原子核也会发生微小位移，形成**周期性晶格畸变（PLD, Periodic Lattice Distortion）**。 它之所以重要，是因为：
> 
> 1.  **它是量子多体效应的宏观体现**，研究它有助于理解电子之间、电子与晶格之间复杂的相互作用。
> 2.  **它与超导密切相关**，在许多材料中，CDW与超导相竞争或共存，调控CDW是理解超导机理的关键。
> 3.  **它具有应用潜力**，CDW的相变伴随着电阻率等物理性质的巨大改变，可用于制造新型传感器、开关和存储器。
> 
> ### Q2：为什么研究“双层”而不是直接研究“块体”？
> 
> **A:** 这是一个“化繁为简”的科学策略。
> 
> 1.  **最小模型**：2H-NbSe₂的块体结构可以看作是由许多“双层”单元堆叠而成。双层是**保持中心对称性的最小重复单元**，包含了层间耦合的关键信息，同时又比块体简单得多。
> 2.  **计算可行性**：块体包含无数层，直接进行高精度的第一性原理计算（尤其是考虑多种CDW堆叠组合）在计算量上是巨大的，甚至是不可能的。双层模型在计算开销和物理准确性之间取得了平衡。
> 3.  **与实验对接**：许多实验（如STM）探测的是表面几层的性质，使用双层模型比单层模型更贴近实际，也比块体模型更易于解析实验结果。
> 
> ### Q3：什么是“范德华力（vdW）修正”？为什么它在这里如此重要？
> 
> **A:** **范德华力（vdW）** 是原子或分子间普遍存在的微弱电吸引力。对于TMDs这类层状材料，层与层之间正是靠范德华力“粘合”在一起的。 标准的DFT计算（如GGA）能很好地描述强化学键，但无法准确描述这种远程的、非局域的范德华相互作用。如果不加修正，计算出的层间距会偏大，导致层间耦合过弱，从而得出错误的结论。 **“范德华力修正”** 就是在DFT框架中添加额外项，来补全对范德华力的描述。作者系统地测试了多种修正方法，就是为了确保计算出的层间距和层间相互作用强度尽可能接近真实物理情况，这是所有后续分析和结论的基础。
> 
> ### Q4：STM图像是如何模拟出来的？它和真实实验有什么关系？
> 
> **A:** STM的工作原理是，一个非常尖锐的金属探针在样品表面上方几个原子直径的高度扫描，通过测量探针与样品间的**隧穿电流**来成像。电流大小与样品表面的**局域电子态密度（LDOS, Local Density of States）** 紧密相关。 理论模拟就是基于这个原理。作者先用DFT计算出整个系统的电子波函数，然后利用**Tersoff-Hamann模型**，模拟一个理想化的球形探针，计算在恒定电流模式下，探针在每一点需要移动的垂直距离（即**表观高度**），从而绘制出像图5那样的图像。 因此，**模拟的STM图像就是理论预测的、在理想实验条件下应该看到的“图像”**。它为实验学家提供了一个直接对比的“地图”，帮助他们识别实验中观察到的复杂图案源于哪种微观结构。
> 
> ### Q5：这项工作对理解“莫尔超晶格”中的物理有什么启示？
> 
> **A:** 这是一个非常前瞻性的问题。 **莫尔超晶格**是将两层二维材料以微小角度扭转堆叠时，形成的新的大周期图案。本文研究的核心是**层间堆叠（平移）对CDW的影响**。 这两者深刻关联：
> 
> 1.  **连续性视角**：在莫尔超晶格中，不同区域会局部地近似于本文所研究的不同堆叠构型（如HC-HCS1, HC-HCS3等）。也就是说，莫尔图案可以看作是一个连续变化的堆叠构型场。
> 2.  **空间调制**：本文的结论意味着，在莫尔超晶格的不同区域，由于局域堆叠方式不同，CDW的稳定性、对称性和电子结构也会发生空间调制。这将导致一个极其复杂的“CDW在莫尔条纹上的超结构”。
> 3.  **新的自由度**：扭转角为调控CDW提供了一个全新的实验维度。通过改变扭转角，可以系统地改变层间堆叠的局域配比，从而实现前所未有的CDW物态，甚至可能为理解高温超导提供新的平台。

> ## I. INTRODUCTION
> 
> ## I. 引言
> 
> Transition metal dichalcogenides (TMD) are a class of layered materials that have been under intense scrutiny for several decades [1]. The weak interlayer coupling, originating from the van der Waals (vdW) interaction, makes them relatively easy to exfoliate [2], allowing for the systematic study of thickness-dependent quantum phenomena. This is very important in the study of charge-density waves (CDWs) [3,4], as understanding their evolution from monolayer to bulk may clarify the precise mechanisms driving their formation, which are still heavily debated [5–7]. In turn, this may help to understand the connection between CDWs, their dimensionality, and other phenomena driven by strong electronic correlations, as, e.g., superconductivity and magnetism [7,8]. 过渡金属硫族化合物(TMD)是一类层状材料,几十年来一直备受关注[1]。由于范德华(vdW)相互作用产生的弱层间耦合,它们相对容易剥离[2],从而可以对厚度依赖的量子现象进行系统研究。这对于电荷密度波(CDW)[3,4]的研究非常重要,因为理解它们从单层到块体的演变可能阐明其形成的确切机制,而这些机制至今仍存在激烈争论[5–7]。反过来,这有助于理解CDW、它们的维度以及由强电子关联驱动的其他现象(例如超导性和磁性)[7,8]之间的联系。
> 
> Besides a few exceptions like 1T-TiSe2, the CDWs in bulk TMDs have been traditionally assumed to retain a two-dimensional periodicity, which seems to find a plausible justification in the weak nature of the interlayer coupling [3]. Nevertheless, the interlayer stacking was recently found to be sufficient for explaining the insulating character of bulk 1T-TaS2 even without introducing an explicit Hubbard-type Coulomb correction [9–15]. These works motivated similar research for other materials where effects related to a metal-to-insulator transition are expected to emerge in thin films, as, e.g., 1T-TaSe2 [16,17], 1T-NbS2 [18], and 1T-NbSe2 [19]. As one may notice, all the aforementioned studies are focused on the role of the interlayer stacking in structures of 1T-type, where a change in the conductive character is expected to leave fingerprints that are (relatively) easy to detect. One may naturally ask if an interlayer stacking of CDWs emerges also in metallic TMDs and if this order can be detected through current experimental techniques. These questions not only have a fundamental, speculative motivation, but they are also intimately connected to our ability to model the CDW state in solids, thin films, and monolayers. The aim of the present manuscript is precisely to shed light on these issues by focusing on a bilayer of 2H-NbSe2. 除了少数例外,如1T-TiSe2,块体TMDs中的电荷密度波(CDWs)传统上被认为保留了二维周期性,这似乎在层间耦合的弱性质[3]中找到了合理的解释。然而,最近发现层间堆叠足以解释块体1T-TaS2的绝缘特性,甚至无需引入显式的Hubbard型库仑修正[9–15]。这些研究工作促使人们针对其他材料进行类似的研究,这些材料在薄膜中预期会出现金属-绝缘体转变效应,例如1T-TaSe2 [16,17], 1T-NbS2 [18],和1T-NbSe2 [19]。众所周知,上述所有研究都集中在1T型结构的层间堆叠上,其中导电特性的变化预计会留下(相对)容易检测的痕迹。人们自然会问,电荷密度波的层间堆叠是否也会出现在金属TMDs中,以及这种有序结构是否可以通过当前的实验技术检测到。这些问题不仅具有基础性、推测性的动机,而且与我们模拟固体、薄膜和单层中的CDW状态的能力密切相关。本文的目的正是通过聚焦于2H-NbSe2的双层结构,阐明这些问题。
> 
> **Exfoliate**: 剥离，指通过机械或化学方法将层状材料分离成单层或少层的过程。 **Hubbard-type Coulomb correction**: Hubbard型库仑修正，一种在密度泛函理论中用于处理强关联电子体系的修正方法，通过引入一个参数U来描述电子间的在位库仑排斥作用。

> ## II. METHODS
> 
> ## II. 方法
> 
> Electronic structure calculations are performed in DFT by means of the projected augmented wave (PAW) method, as implemented in the Vienna Ab-initio Simulation Package (VASP). Pseudopotentials are used to treat 4s24p65s15d4 for Nb and 4s24p4 for Se as valence electrons. The exchange-correlation functional is treated in the generalized gradient approximation (GGA) as parametrized by Perdew-Burke-Ernzerhof (PBE) [35,36]. The plane-wave basis is constructed with a cutoff energy of 500 eV. Structural relaxation is performed using a quasi-Newton algorithm to minimize forces down to 10−3 eV/Å on each atom, while an energy tolerance of 10−7 eV is imposed for the convergence of the electronic loop. For tasks requiring a more accurate determination of the electronic properties, such as plotting charge density distributions and density of states (DOS), the electronic loop is converged until the energy variation became lower than 10−9 eV for the primitive unit cells and 10−7 eV for the supercells. 电子结构计算采用投影增强波(PAW)方法在密度泛函理论(DFT)框架下进行,该方法由维也纳从头算模拟软件包(VASP)实现。采用赝势处理Nb的4s24p65s15d4电子和Se的4s24p4电子作为价电子。交换关联泛函采用广义梯度近似(GGA)形式,具体参数化为Perdew-Burke-Ernzerhof(PBE)[35,36]。平面波基组采用500 eV的能量截断构建。结构弛豫通过准牛顿算法进行,以使每个原子的力最小化至10−3eV/Å,同时电子循环收敛时施加10−7eV的能量容差。对于需要更精确确定电子性质的任务,如绘制电荷密度分布和态密度(DOS),电子循环会收敛至原始单元胞的能量变化低于10−9 eV,超胞的能量变化低于 10−7eV。
> 
> **PAW**: Projected Augmented Wave，投影增强波，一种用于密度泛函理论计算的赝势方法，能够高效且准确地描述电子-离子间的相互作用。 **VASP**: Vienna Ab-initio Simulation Package，维也纳从头算模拟软件包，一款广泛使用的第一性原理电子结构计算和量子力学-分子动力学模拟软件。 **Pseudopotentials**: 赝势，一种在计算中用于近似描述原子核和内层电子对价电子作用的有效势，可以简化计算并提高效率。 **PBE**: Perdew-Burke-Ernzerhof，一种广泛使用的广义梯度近似交换关联泛函形式。
> 
> Calculations for the 2H-NbSe2 bilayer are performed with a vacuum region of about 20 Å, added along the zˆ direction. The normal phase is modeled with the primitive unit cell, where the two composing monolayers form two sublattices whose in-plane coordinates are in correspondence with each other by an inversion symmetry; see Fig. 1(a). In the 2H-NbSe2 bulk, this becomes equivalent to a rotation by 2nπ /3 + π /3 around the Nb lattice site plus a translation along zˆ. The in-plane lattice constant is set to 3.45 Å, which is usually used to model monolayers [26,37–42], corresponding to the substrates often adopted for growing monolayers and thin films [33,43,44]. The analysis of the CDW state is performed using 3 × 3 supercells, which can accommodate the distortions associated with the phonon instabilities [26]. Each CDW corresponds to a metastable state that can be obtained via DFT by changing the initial positions of the ions before structural relaxation. A k-mesh of 45 × 45 × 1 (20 × 20 × 1) is used for the primitive unit cell (supercell), in combination with a Gaussian smearing of 0.01 eV. The employed computational parameters have been determined to ensure that the energy differences reported in Table II are well converged. 计算2H-NbSe2双层结构采用约20 Å的真空区域,沿ˆz方向添加。正常相使用原始单元胞建模,其中两个组成单层形成两个子晶格,其平面坐标通过倒易对称性对应;参见图1(a)。在2H-NbSe2块体中,这等效于绕Nb晶格位点旋转2nπ /3 +π /3 加上沿ˆz方向的平移。平面晶格常数为3.45 Å,通常用于建模单层[26,37–42],这些单层结构对应于生长单层和薄膜常用的衬底[33,43,44]。CDW态的分析使用3 × 3 超胞进行,超胞可以容纳与声子不稳定性相关的畸变[26]。每个CDW对应一个亚稳态,可以通过DFT通过改变离子初始位置在结构弛豫之前获得。Ak‐网格的 45 × 45 × 1 (20 × 20 × 1) 用于原始单元胞(超胞),结合0.01 eV的高斯展宽。所使用的计算参数已确定,以确保表II中报告的能量差收敛良好。
> 
> **Phonon instabilities**: 声子不稳定性，指晶格振动模式中出现虚频，预示着结构将发生自发畸变以形成能量更低的新相。 **Supercells**: 超胞，比原始晶胞更大的周期性结构单元，常用于模拟缺陷、掺杂或晶格畸变。 **Metastable state**: 亚稳态，一种能量处于局部极小值而非全局最小值的稳定状态，系统需要越过能量势垒才能转变为更稳定的状态。
> 
> The vdW interaction is taken into account within DFT, adding corrections to the PBE functional. As explained below, the normal phase is studied via several approaches, namely Grimme-D2 (GGA + D2) [45], Tkatchenko-Scheffler (GGA + TS) [46], nonlocal correction by Dion et al. [47] in the formulation of Klimeš (GGA + DF) [48], and many-body dispersion with fractional ion (GGA + MBD@FI) [49,50]. The supercell calculations for the CDW are performed by means of GGA + DF and GGA + TS. For the latter, we obtain results consistent with GGA + DF regarding the HC-HC and CC-CC blends, which supports our results. However, we did not manage to converge the mixed blends in CDW states with the correct symmetry. This instability is a consequence of the large reduction of the inter-layer distance, which will be discussed in the analysis of Table I, where dSe-Se is shown to be reduced by more than 20% with respect to the pure GGA value. These aspects are consistent with the results reported for the bulk [51] and can be traced back to the general tendency of GGA + TS to overestimate the vdW bonding in some systems [52]. 范德华相互作用在密度泛函理论中加以考虑,对PBE泛函进行修正。如下文所述,通过多种方法研究正常相,即 Grimme-D2(GGA +D2) [45], Tkatchenko-Scheffler(GGA +TS) [46], Dion等人[47]在Klimeš公式中提出的非局域修正(GGA +DF) [48],以及多体含分数离子的色散(广义梯度近似 +MBD@FI) [49,50]。电荷密度波的超级胞计算采用广义梯度近似 + DF和广义梯度近似 + TS。对于后者,我们得到的混合物结果与广义梯度近似 +DF关于HC-HC和CC-CC混合物一致,这支持了我们的结果。然而,我们没有成功地将具有正确对称性的电荷密度波混合物收敛。这种不稳定性是层间距离大幅减小的一个结果,这将在表 I的分析中进行讨论,其中 dSe-Se 显示比纯广义梯度近似值减小了20%以上。这些方面与块体[51]的结果一致,并可追溯到广义梯度近似 +TS在某些体系中高估范德华键合的普遍趋势[52]。
> 
> Finally, the STM simulations are performed by means of the BSKAN code [53,54] using the electronic structure obtained from the VASP calculations. The revised Chen method [55] with an electronically flat and spatially spherical tip orbital is used, which is equivalent to the Tersoff-Hamann model of electron tunneling [56]. The reported STM images are in constant current mode obtained at −0.2 V bias voltage applied to the STM tip (with the maxima of the current contours at 5.8 Å for all structures for a better comparability). The visualization of the ionic positions and the charge density as well as the calculation of the structure factors are produced with VESTA JP-Minerals [57], supplemented by our in-house developed postprocessing code. For completeness, the data before postprocessing are reported in Appendix B. The analysis of the electronic properties is performed with the aid of VASPKIT [58]. 最后,通过BSKAN代码[53,54]进行STM模拟,使用VASP计算得到的电子结构。采用改进的陈方法[55],该方法具有电子平坦且空间上球形的探针轨道,这等效于电子隧穿Tersoff‐Hamann模型[56]。报道的STM图像是在恒定电流模式下获得的,施加在STM探针上的偏置电压为 −0.2V(对于所有结构,电流等高线的最大值在5.8 Å处,以便更好地比较)。离子位置、电荷密度以及结构因子可视化是通过VESTA JP-Minerals [57],结合我们开发的内部后处理代码生成的。为了完整性,后处理前的数据报告在附录B中。电子性质的分析借助VASPKIT [58]进行。
> 
> **Tersoff-Hamann model**: Tersoff-Hamann模型，一种描述扫描隧道显微镜中电子隧穿过程的简化理论模型，将针尖波函数近似为s波，使得隧穿电流与样品表面的局域态密度成正比。

> ## III. 结果

> ### A. Structural properties in the normal phase
> 
> ### A. 正常相中的结构性质
> 
> The treatment of the vdW interaction affects significantly the interatomic distances in TMDs inside a monolayer as well as across different monolayers [59,60]. For bulk 2H-NbSe2, this is well illustrated in the work by Buˇcko et al. [51], where the nearest-neighbor distance dNb-Nb between two Nb atoms belonging to different layers is found to vary from 6.89 Å, if vdW corrections are ignored, to 6.38 Å and even 6.03 Å, if vdW corrections are applied, via different approaches. The experimentally reported value, which corresponds to half the lattice parameter c, is around 6.27 Å [61,62]. The lack of an ideal method to account for the vdW interaction in NbSe2 is further accentuated in systems composed of a few layers, since one needs to account for large regions of vacuum accurately [60]. In fact, previous works on monolayer 1H-NbSe2 have all used different DFT functionals for including vdW corrections [33,34,63]. Due to this uncertainty, before delving into the details of the CDWs, it is instructive to analyze how the basic structural properties depend on the choice of the DFT functional. Structural optimization of bilayer 2H-NbSe2 was performed by DFT in the generalized gradient approximation (GGA), without and with vdW corrections, using the functionals GGA + D2 [45], GGA + TS [46], GGA + DF [47,48], and GGA + MBD@FI [49,50]. 范德华相互作用的处理显著影响过渡金属二硫族化合物(TMDs)单层内的原子间距离以及不同单层之间的距离[59,60]。对于块体2H-NbSe2,这一点在Buˇcko等人[51]的工作中得到了很好的说明,其中发现属于不同层的两个Nb原子之间的最近邻距离dNb-Nb,如果忽略范德华修正,则为6.89 Å,如果应用范德华修正,则通过不同方法变化为6.38 Å甚至6.03 Å。实验报道的值,对应于晶格参数c的一半,约为6.27 Å [61,62]。由于在NbSe2中缺乏理想的方法来考虑范德华相互作用,在由几层组成的系统中,这一点更加突出,因为需要精确地考虑大面积的真空[60]。事实上,之前关于单层1H-NbSe2的所有工作都使用了不同的DFT泛函来包含范德华修正[33,34,63]。由于这种不确定性,在深入探讨电荷密度波(CDWs)的细节之前,分析基本结构性质如何依赖于DFT泛函的选择是有益的。通过广义梯度近似(GGA)对双层2H-NbSe2进行了结构优化,不使用和使用范德华修正,使用泛函GGA + D2 [45], GGA + TS [46], GGA +DF [47,48],和GGA +MBD@FI [49,50]。

For both distances, the largest values are found for plain GGA, as expected. Corrections resulting from the treatment of the vdW interaction are smallest for GGA + DF and largest for GGA + TS, which is in agreement with the trend of the bulk data discussed above [51]. Since there are no experimental data to use as a reference for determining the best functional to model bilayer 2H-NbSe2, we perform two sets of calculations for the CDWs, using GGA + DF and GGA + TS, respectively. These two methods not only correspond to the largest variation of the interlayer distances in Table I, but they also originate from unrelated derivations. As explained in the preceding section, not all possible CDWs could be stabilized in the correct symmetry with GGA + TS, while the most important structures could be found to be in good agreement with GGA + DF. Therefore, for the sake of simplicity, only the results for GGA + DF will be presented and discussed in the rest of the article. 正常相的结构优化结果,包括上述dNb-Nb和类似的dSe-Se,报告在表I中。对于这两个距离,最大的值出现在普通GGA中,正如预期的那样。范德华相互作用处理的修正结果,GGA +DF最小,GGA +TS最大,这与上面讨论的块体数据趋势一致[51]。由于没有实验数据可以用来作为参考来确定模拟双层2H-NbSe2的最佳泛函,我们对CDWs进行了两组计算,分别使用GGA + DF和GGA +TS。这两种方法不仅对应于表I中层间距离的最大变化,而且它们也来自无关的推导。正如前一节所述,并非所有可能的CDWs都能在正确的对称性下被GGA + TS稳定,而最重要的结构被发现与GGA +DF非常一致。因此,为了简单起见,本文其余部分将仅展示和讨论GGA +DF的结果。

> ### B. Vertical stacking of CDWs
> 
> ### B. 电荷密度波的垂直堆叠
> 
> A wealth of literature produced during the past decade has contributed to identifying several competing CDW structures in monolayer NbSe2 [26,31,33,38–40,63–65]. Among those, three structures have a significantly lower energy, namely the hexagonal (HX), chalcogen-centered triangular (CC), and hollow centered triangular (HC) structures shown in Figs. 1(c)–1(e). As illustrated on the right side of Table II, these structures are all within an energy range of about 1 meV (GGA) or 2 meV (GGA + DF) per formula unit (f.u.), in agreement with the most recent literature [26,31,40]. When the CDW state is formed in a bilayer, there are more degrees of freedom to take into account. Since the HX, CC, and HC structures dominate the physical properties of the bulk as well [64], we expect that the stacking of two or more layers is not going to affect the in-plane modulation so much as to involve higher-energy solutions, such as, e.g., the star phase reported by Guster et al. [65]. Therefore, we proceed to investigate all possible stacking configurations allowed by the symmetry of the previous modulations, including the mixing of different structures in the two monolayers forming the bilayer. For clarity, we are going to refer to the combinations of possible structures, e.g., HC-HC or HX-CC, as CDW blends for the bilayer. Like for the stacking of Stars of David CDWs in 1T -TaS2 [11], only a limited number of nonequivalent configurations are possible for any given blend, constructed by combining a rotation of the layers with a translation vector of the direct lattice. For simplicity, we are going to refer to these different configurations as displacements and label them with a subscript Sn, where n is an integer up to the number of possibilities allowed by symmetry. Hence, the CDWs arising from different blends and displacements may be uniquely identified by labels such as, e.g., HC-HCS2 or HC-CCS4 . It is important to stress that both blend and displacement affect that full symmetry of the crystal in the CDW state, reducing it with respect to the normal phase. In fact, the space group goes from P3m1 (normal phase) to C2/m (HC-HC blend, all displacements), P3m1 (HC-CC blend, with the exception of HC-CCS2 , which maintains only the inversion symmetry, C1), P1 (CC-CCS2 ), and Cm (CC-CC blend, all other displacements). An important difference between bilayers of the widely studied 1T-type and the present 2H-type is that the two composing monolayers are rotated by π with respect to each other, and therefore even in the case of nonmixed blends, such as, e.g., HC-HC, there is no in-plane displacement for which the two sublattices (structural and/or charge modulations) are mapped into each other by a translation along zˆ. 过去十年间,大量文献研究有助于识别出单层NbSe2中的几种竞争性电荷密度波(CDW)结构[26,31,33,38-40,63–65]。其中,三种结构的能量显著更低,分别是六方(HX)、硫族元素中心三角(CC)和空心中心三角(HC)结构,如图1(c)–1(e)所示。如表II右侧所示,这些结构在约1 meV(广义梯度近似GGA)或2 meV(广义梯度近似GGA+DF)的能范围内,与最新文献[26,31,40]报道的结果一致。当CDW状态形成于双层结构中时,需要考虑更多的自由度。由于六方(HX)、硫族元素中心三角(CC)和空心中心三角(HC)结构主导了块体的物理性质[64],我们预计,多层堆叠不会显著影响平面调制,从而不会涉及更高能量的解,例如Guster等人报道的星相[65]。因此,我们继续研究由先前调制对称性允许的所有可能堆叠构型,包括形成双层结构的两层不同结构的混合。为清晰起见,我们将可能结构的组合,例如HC‐HC或HX‐CC,称为双层CDW混合物。与大卫之星CDW堆叠在1T‐TaS2[11]中类似,对于任何给定的混合物,只有有限数量的非等价构型是可能的,这些构型由层旋转和直接晶格的平移矢量组合而成。为简化起见,我们将这些不同构型称为位移,并用下标Sn标记它们,其中n是允许的对称性可能性的整数。因此,由不同混合物和位移产生的CDW可以通过标签,例如HC‐HCS2或HC‐CCS4唯一识别。必须强调的是,混合物和位移都会影响晶体在CDW状态下的完全对称性,将其相对于正常相降低。事实上,空间群从P3m1(正常相)变为C2/m (HC‐HC混合物,所有位移)、P3m1(HC‐CC混合物,HC‐CCS2除外,它仅保持反演对称性,C1)、P1 (CC‐CCS2)和Cm (CC‐CC混合物,所有其他位移)。广泛研究的1T型与当前2H型之间一个重要区别是,两个组成单层相对于彼此旋转了π,因此在非混合混合物,例如HC‐HC的情况下,没有平面位移可以使两个子晶格(结构和/或电荷调制)通过沿ˆz方向的平移相互映射。
> 
> **Space group**: 空间群，描述晶体内部原子排列对称性的数学组合，包含了所有可能的平移、旋转、反映和滑移等对称操作。
> 
> The total energies of the optimized CDW structures in a bilayer of 2H-NbSe2 are reported in Table II. Blends not listed in the table possess a much higher energy and therefore are not discussed. Independently from the inclusion of the vdW interaction, the most favorable energies are obtained for the HC-HC and HC-CC blends, whose geometries for all nonequivalent displacements are sketched in Figs. 1(f)–1(l). > While the order of the lowest-lying states does not change between GGA and GGA + DF, their relative energies depend on them. Table II demonstrates that in both the monolayer 1H-NbSe2 and bilayer 2H-NbSe2, the stability of the CDWs increases when the vdW corrections are considered, as a consequence of the reduced interlayer distances. The larger orbital overlap also increases the energy differences between the various blends and displacements, as expected. > Concerning the differences between monolayer and bilayer, Table II shows that the stability of the CDW with respect to the normal phase is stronger in the former. This is consistent with existing observations on the trend of the critical temperature with respect to layer thickness [7,21], albeit there is still an ongoing controversy regarding the role played by substrate and sample preparation [7,22–25]. Intuitively, the higher critical temperature observed in the bilayer can be rationalized in terms of interlayer coupling, as the larger coherence of the Se-Se interlayer bonds in the normal phase increases its stability with respect to the CDW state, which has a larger periodicity [7]. Regarding the landscape of excited states, the energy difference between the HC and CC structures for the 1H-NbSe2 monolayer amounts to 0.51 (1.43) meV/f.u. in GGA (GGA + DF). This value can be compared with the energy difference between the HC-HCS3 and CC-CCS2 structures for the 2H-NbSe2 bilayer, which amounts to 0.48 (1.03) meV/f.u. This reduction is less trivial to explain without implying a direct decrease of the instability associated with the electron-phonon coupling, in accordance with the analysis by Xi et al. [21]. As a result of the weaker strength of the CDWs as well as the increased number of degrees of freedom, the energy landscape reported in Table II shows that the excited states are much closer to the ground state in the 2H-NbSe2 bilayer than in the 1H-NbSe2 monolayer. An interesting question arising from these data is how they are going to evolve in a corresponding model for the bulk, where we expect a convergence behavior analogous to that exhibited by stacking fault energies for close-packed solids. 关于单层和双层之间的差异,表II显示,电荷密度波(CDW)相对于正常相的稳定性在前者中更强。这与其它观察到的临界温度随层厚度变化的趋势一致[7,21],尽管关于衬底和样品制备所起作用的问题仍然存在争议[7,22–25]。直观地讲,双层中观察到的较高临界温度可以用层间耦合来解释,因为正常相中Se‐Se层间键的更大相干性增加了其相对于CDW状态的稳定性,而CDW状态具有更大的周期性[7]。关于激发态的图景,1H‐NbSe2单层中HC和CC结构之间的能量差为0.51 (1.43) meV/f.u.(广义梯度近似GGA(GGA+DF))。此值可与2H‐NbSe2双层中HC‐HCS3和CC‐CCS2结构之间的能量差进行比较,该能量差为0.48 (1.03) meV/f.u.这种减少在没有暗示电子‐声子耦合不稳定性直接降低的情况下很难解释,这与Xi等人[21]的分析一致。因此,由于电荷密度波(CDW)的强度较弱以及自由度数量的增加,表II中报告的能量图景显示,在2H‐NbSe2双层中的激发态比在1H‐NbSe2单层中更接近基态。从这些数据中产生的一个有趣问题是,在相应的块体模型中,它们将如何演变,我们预计其行为将与密堆积固体的层错能所表现出的行为类似。

> **Electron-phonon coupling**: 电子-声子耦合，描述电子与晶格振动（声子）之间相互作用的物理机制，是理解电荷密度波、超导等现象的关键。

> Focusing on the first three excited states identified above for the bilayer, we can see that their energies all lie within a range of 0.18 (0.39) meV/f.u. in GGA (GGA + DF). This energy range is smaller than the difference between HC and CC structures in the monolayer, which was shown to be overcome by defects, leading to domain formation observable by STM [33]. Furthermore, the excitation energies are also much smaller than the experimental critical temperature, which suggests that thermal effects may also lead to a nontrivial mixing in the vertical stacking [12]. While a proper analysis of the stability with respect to thermal fluctuations would require the evaluation of the potential energy barriers between the various metastable states, an estimate can be obtained by comparing our results to existing modeling for 1T-TaS2 bulk. For this system, thermally induced transitions between the two most favorable stacking configurations were demonstrated to be locked at about 60 K [12]. The energy difference between these states amounts to 0.08 meV/f.u. in GGA + TS, which is less than half what we obtain for the 2H-NbSe2 bilayer in GGA + DF. Therefore, we may reasonably expect the stacking predicted here to be stable at temperatures below the experimental critical temperature. Another thermal effect worth considering is the correction due to the vibrational entropy, which has the potential to overcome the small energy differences discussed so far. Previous results for the symmetric 2H-NbSe2 bilayer from GGA + D3 calculations showed that large translations (up to 3.5 Å) of one monolayer with respect to the other one may induce a correction as large as 0.4 meV/f.u. at 10 K [68]. This correction is a consequence of the different degree of overlap between the orbitals of Se atoms belonging to different monolayers [see the right side of Fig. 1(a)], which may change substantially the energy of the shear mode. For the CDWs investigated here, this strong variation of overlap is never possible, since the atomic displacements are small (up to 0.2 Å) and not in phase. Thus, we expect a correction that is at least an order of magnitude smaller, which would not be sufficient to change the energy hierarchy of Table II. Note that this scenario may be very different for 1T-type bilayer structures, where the composing monolayers may potentially generate very different shear modes depending on the vertical stacking of the CDWs. A more precise answer beyond this speculation would require the calculation of the phonon spectra of the bilayer with the CDWs with a sufficient accuracy to resolve these tiny contributions. > ### C. Electronic properties
> 
> ### C. 电子性质
> 
> The total DOS of the 2H-NbSe2 bilayer is illustrated in Fig. 3 for the symmetric state as well as for the most favorable CDW configurations. The general shapes of the four curves are very similar, and the differences induced by the stacking across the whole valence band are minimal. An interesting feature is the formation of a few sharp peaks in certain configurations, such as, e.g., the peak at −1.8 eV for HC-CCS4 . The analysis of the projected DOS for individual Se atoms (data not shown) makes it possible to identify that these sharp peaks originate from the pz orbitals of the Se atoms comprised between the two monolayers. This suggests that the periodic lattice distortion in each monolayer may lead to pairs of Se atoms in the out-of-plane direction that are characterized by an increased coherence, which in turn affects the energetic stability of the CDW configurations. This argument can be seen as a generalization of the mechanism proposed in Ref. [25] to explain the thickness dependence observed in Raman spectroscopy. It is also instructive to analyze the distributions of states in the vicinity of the Fermi level, which is shown in the bottom panel of Fig. 3. A clear correlation is visible between the energy gained by the CDW structures and the depletion of states at the Fermi level. The same effect may also be observed among the different CDWs in the 1H-NbSe2 monolayer, with HC showing a larger depletion than CC and HX [40]. The gain in (electronic band) energy associated with the depletion of states at the Fermi level has been largely investigated in the literature since it is a fundamental aspect of the one-dimensional Peierls model and its generalization to two- and three-dimensional systems, where it induces a full gap or a pseudogap, respectively [3]. 2H-NbSe2 is more complex in this regard, since the CDW originates from the momentum dependence of the electron-phonon coupling [6] and only a tiny fraction of electrons are involved in its formation [27,28]. Therefore, it is very interesting that our data show such a clear correlation between the energetic order and the depletion of states at the Fermi level. 2H‐NbSe的态密度2双层结构在图3中展示了对称状态以及最有利于电荷密度波构型的总态密度。这四条曲线的形状非常相似,并且由堆叠引起的整个价带范围内的差异非常小。一个有趣的特征是,在某些构型中形成了一些尖锐的峰值,例如HC‐CC构型中−1.8 eV处的峰值。对单个Se原子投影态密度的分析(未显示的数据)表明,这些尖锐的峰值起源于两层之间Se原子的pz轨道。这表明,每个单层的周期性晶格畸变可能导致平面外方向上成对Se原子,这些原子具有更高的相干性,进而影响电荷密度波构型的能量稳定性。这一观点可以看作是参考文献[25]中提出的机制的推广,该机制用于解释拉曼光谱中观察到的厚度依赖性。分析费米能级附近的状态分布也很有启发性,该分布显示在图3的底部面板中。电荷密度波结构获得的能量与费米能级处的态耗尽之间存在明显的相关性。在不同的1H‐NbSe2单层中也可能观察到相同的效果,其中HC的耗尽程度大于CC和HX[40]。与费米能级处态耗尽相关的(电子带)能量在文献中已被广泛研究,因为它是一维Peierls模型及其推广到二维和三维系统的基本方面,分别诱导出完全的能隙或赝能隙[3]。2H‐NbSe2在这方面更为复杂,因为电荷密度波起源于电子‐声子耦合的动量依赖性,只有极少数电子参与其形成[6]。因此,我们的数据显示出能量顺序与费米能级处态耗尽之间如此明显的相关性,这一点非常有趣。

Fundamentally, we are interested in identifying signatures of the CDW stacking in a metallic system that may be detected in possible experiments, whether it be for a homogeneous, uniform structure or for a coexistence of domains, in analogy to the work by Oh et al. [33]. In the following, we will explore two options, i.e., via STM measurements or structure factors analysis. 从图3中报告的总态密度,我们可以推断出CDW不同堆叠构型的输运性质将相当相似。缺乏像1T‐TaS2中表现出的金属‐绝缘体转变(具有滞后循环)这样的大效应,人们可能会想知道2H‐NbSe2双层以及块体中是否还有其他更微妙的结果。从根本上说,我们感兴趣的是在金属系统中识别CDW堆叠的迹象,这些迹象可能在可能的实验中检测到,无论是对于均匀结构还是对于畴共存,类似于Oh等[33]的工作。在下文中,我们将探讨两种方案,即通过STM测量或结构因子分析。

> ### D. Se patterns and STM simulations
> 
> ### D. Se 图案和扫描隧道显微镜模拟
> 
> In the past years, the comparison between theoretical and experimental STM images has made an important contribution to the research on monolayer, bulk, and thin films of 2H-NbSe2 [4]. Although STM experiments are by construction surface-sensitive, one may think about the possibility of discriminating between stacking configurations by analyzing their STM characteristics. The idea behind it is that STM is not only sensitive to the surface topography but also to the electronic structure, which is likely modulated in 2H-NbSe2 depending on the variation of the coupled monolayer structures and the stabilized CDWs in each layer. In STM, one usually has access to the Se-Se bond pattern of the top sublattice, which thus becomes of crucial relevance. The Se atoms display long/short bond modulations along the vertical direction as well as in the plane parallel to the Nb plane. These modulations accompany the Nb-Nb bond patterns [38,40], as illustrated in Ref. [40] for the 1H-NbSe2 monolayer. In the latter, in-plane Se-Se bonds form triangular (hexagonal) patterns around the HC layer when coupled to HC and CC (HX); they form hexagonal patterns around the CC layer when coupled to the HC; and finally they form triangular patterns around the HX layer. In the 2H-NbSe2 bilayer, the Se-Se patterns depend on the CDW blend and displacement and are therefore a secondary effect of the interlayer coupling that stabilizes these states. The patterns of the Se-Se bond distances in the most relevant CDW states are illustrated in Fig. 4, as calculated in GGA + DF. We notice that, for each blend, the lowest energy corresponds to Se-Se patches that do not superimpose; see HC-HCS3 and HC-CCS4 . > ### E. Charge density and structure factors
> 
> ### E. 电荷密度和结构因子
> 
> The charge distributions of the modeled systems are computed integrating the electronic states over the occupied Nb band, down to −0.4 eV below the Fermi level. From the three-dimensional object thus produced, we obtain geometric structure factors f (h, k, l ) for the 2H-NbSe2 bilayer, which have the advantage of factoring out the in-plane displacement, thus highlighting the symmetry of the lattice and its charge distribution. By treating separately lattice and charge modulations, we can assess their roles in the transition from one symmetry to another [57]. In general, this analysis can also provide a tool to detect changes of symmetry not occurring at the surface but in lower layers. 模型化系统的电荷分布是通过将电子态积分到费米能级以下eV处计算的。从这样产生的三维对象中,我们获得了2H‐NbSe2双层的结构因子f (h, k, l),这些结构因子具有消除平面位移的优点,从而突出晶格及其电荷分布的对称性。通过分别处理晶格和电荷调制,我们可以评估它们在从一种对称性向另一种对称性转变[57]中的作用。通常,这种分析还可以提供一种工具,用于检测表面以下层中发生的对称性变化。
> 
> We select vectors in the (h, k, l ) plane with l = 0 to retain the in-plane electron momentum. In principle, any constant l would do that, but for l = 0 an additional high peak appears at (0, 0, l ). We first calculate geometric structure factors for x-ray diffraction from structural data only, in the approximation of noninteracting atoms [57,74]. These plots are reported on the left-hand side of Fig. 6: panel (a) shows data for the ground-state system, panel (c) shows its difference with respect to the fully symmetric (no CDW) structure, and panel (e) shows the difference between the excited-state HC-CCS4 and the fully symmetric structure. Due to the large intensity of the Bragg peaks from the structural model, the CDW peaks appear very dim in the modulus of the structure factors, even when we filter the symmetric structure out, as in Fig. 6(c). Nevertheless, the difference patterns shown in panels (c) and (e) have a very different shape, which suggests that experimental techniques suitable for determining the periodic lattice distortions directly should be able to distinguish among different stacking sequences, if sufficiently accurate. 我们选择在(h, k, l)平面内的矢量l = 0以保留平面内的电子动量。原则上,任何常数l都可以做到这一点,但对于l = 0在(0, 0, l)处会出现一个额外的峰值。我们首先仅根据结构数据计算X射线衍射的几何结构因子,在非相互作用原子的近似下[57,74]。这些图显示在图6的左侧:面板(a)显示了基态系统的数据,面板(c)显示了其与完全对称(无电荷密度波)结构的差异,面板(e)显示了激发态HC‐CCS4与完全对称结构的差异。正如预期的那样,结构因子的模量具有高布拉格峰;在每个常数l的平面内,这些峰出现在(±√3, ±1),(0, ±2),以2π /a为单位,其中a是原始单元胞的平面晶格常数。由于结构模型中的布拉格峰强度很大,即使在滤除对称结构后,如图6(c)所示,电荷密度波的峰在结构因子的模量中也非常暗淡。尽管面板(c)和(e)中显示的差异模式形状非常不同,这表明适合直接确定周期性晶格畸变的实验技术应该能够区分不同的堆叠序列,如果足够精确的话。

> **Bragg peaks**: 布拉格峰，在X射线衍射或电子衍射图谱中，由于晶体对入射波的相干散射，在特定角度出现的强度极大值，反映了晶体的周期性结构。

> More detailed information can be obtained by inspecting the geometric structure factors calculated directly from the charge density. > ## IV. DISCUSSION AND CONCLUSIONS
> 
> ## IV. 讨论与结论
> 
> The results outlined in the previous sections demonstrate that the interlayer stacking may play an important role in the physics of the 2H-NbSe2 bilayer, changing the symmetry of the ground state with respect to the 1H-NbSe2 monolayer. Due to the higher number of degrees of freedom, a plethora of complex excited states form, each of them characterized by subtle differences in the electronic excitations. The most visible ones are peaks derived from hybridized pz states of Se atoms belonging to the layers connecting the two monolayers. Around the Fermi level, the most favorable CDW structures exhibit a similar density of carriers, but more evident effects are expected to appear in the transport properties, especially in light of the expected anisotropy. The latter becomes particularly evident in the results obtained from the STM simulation, where the combination of the different modulations leaves interesting fingerprints that do seem in reach for experimental detection. Similar conclusions can be reached from the analysis of the geometric structure factors, which can be connected to direct experiments of x-ray diffraction or to the Fourier transforms of data from STM or TEM. Further insight into this problem can also be obtained from high-resolution data from noncontact atomic-force microscopy (AFM) [77,78] and scanning transmission electron microscopy (STEM) [80,81]. Therefore, we encourage experimental groups to consider looking for fingerprints of the predicted stacking, which is likely to appear in mixed grain boundaries, as reported in previous literature. Since many studies were focused on the 2H-NbSe2 surfaces and discussed in terms of theoretical data for the 1H-NbSe2 monolayer, we should also think of reevaluating former interpretations by assuming a non-negligible role of the stacking on the measured quantities. 前几节概述的结果表明,层间堆叠可能在2H‐NbSe2双层物理学中发挥重要作用,改变其相对于1H‐NbSe2单层的基态对称性。由于自由度数量更多,形成了一系列复杂的激发态,每个激发态在电子激发方面都有微妙的不同。最明显的是,来自连接两层单层的Se原子杂化pz态的峰值。在费米能级附近,最有利的电荷密度波(CDW)结构表现出相似的载流子密度,但在输运性质中预期会有更明显的影响,尤其是在考虑到预期的各向异性时。后者在STM模拟结果中尤为明显,其中不同调制的组合留下了有趣的指纹,这些指纹似乎在实验检测范围内。从几何结构因子的分析中也可以得出类似的结论,这些因子可以与X射线衍射的直接实验或STM或TEM数据的傅里叶变换联系起来。还可以从非接触原子力显微镜(AFM)[77,78]和扫描透射电子显微镜(STEM)[80,81]的高分辨率数据中获得对该问题的进一步见解。因此,我们鼓励实验小组考虑寻找预测堆叠的指纹,这些指纹可能出现在混合晶界中,正如先前文献中报道的那样。由于许多研究都集中在2H‐NbSe2表面,并以1H‐NbSe2单层的理论数据为依据进行讨论,我们应该重新评估以前的解释,假设堆叠对测量量有不可忽略的作用。
> 
> **AFM**: Atomic-Force Microscopy，原子力显微镜，一种通过探测针尖与样品表面之间微弱的原子间作用力来获取表面形貌和性质的高分辨率显微镜。 **STEM**: Scanning Transmission Electron Microscopy，扫描透射电子显微镜，一种结合了扫描和透射模式的电子显微镜，能提供原子级分辨率的成像和谱学分析。
> 
> Although our work has been focused on the 2H-NbSe2 bilayer, we do not expect that the qualitative conclusions summarized above will change for the bulk, which is composed of stacked bilayers. While an increased number of degrees of freedom may allow for the construction of more complex blends and displacements, their effect on the physical properties of the system will not be as marked as the one associated with the bilayer formation. After many investigations on 1T-type TMDs, our results demonstrate that the interlayer stacking plays a role in the 2H-type as well. Albeit no dramatic effects such as metal-to-insulator transitions are observed, we do expect fingerprints of the stacking to emerge with the constantly improving opportunities offered by modern facilities for microscopy and spectroscopy. 尽管我们的工作一直聚焦于2H‐NbSe2双层膜,但我们不预期上述总结的定性结论会因块体而改变,块体是由堆叠的双层膜组成的。虽然自由度的增加可能允许构建更复杂的混合物和位移,但它们对系统物理性质的影响不会像双层膜形成所关联的影响那么显著。在对1T型TMDs进行了许多研究后,我们的结果表明,层间堆叠在2H型中也发挥作用。尽管没有观察到金属‐绝缘体转变等剧烈效应,但我们确实预期随着现代显微镜和光谱学设施不断提供更好的机会,堆叠的指纹会逐渐显现。
> 
> Concerning possible applications or interesting points of analysis for future work, the lower symmetry observed in the most stable CDW structures can be interesting for magnetism, as shown in many reports so far [26,40,82–84]. The sensitivity of bilayers suggests that vibrational modes could be used in combination with other external stimuli to tune collective electronic states. In this regard, it has been pointed out that external pressure can tune the types and functionalities of CDWs. Furthermore, we also note that instead of in-plane displacements, addressed in this work, bilayers can feature other geometrical configurations, such as layers twisting with suitable angles [85–87]. Naturally, the interaction between layers will play a similar role in the combination of lattice distortions and charge modulations to promote or hinder collective excitations such as superconductivity. 关于未来工作的可能应用或分析有趣点,最稳定电荷密度波(CDW)结构中观察到的较低对称性可能对磁性有趣,正如许多报告所显示的[26,40,82–84]。双层结构的敏感性表明振动模式可以与其它外部刺激结合来调节集体电子态。在这方面,已经指出外部压力可以调节电荷密度波(CDW)的类型和功能。此外,我们还注意到,除了本工作中提到的面内位移之外,双层结构可以具有其他几何构型,例如层以合适的角度扭曲[85–87]。当然,层间相互作用在晶格畸变和电荷调制组合中会起到类似的作用,以促进或阻碍集体激发,如超导性。
> 
> Regarding electronic-structure calculations, future theoretical work may be focused on understanding the connection between Raman modes and interlayer stacking in relation to both blend and displacement. Previous studies have suggested that the vertical alignment of CDWs does not cause a discontinuity in the frequency of the shear mode; see, e.g., Fig. 4(b) in Ref. [88]. Although the authors interpreted this feature as a hint of a vertical alignment of the CDWs, likely induced by defects, this does not seem possible for a 2H type, due to the particular layer stacking illustrated in Fig. 1(a). Interpreting Raman data will require understanding the variations of phonon spectra across the various CDW structures. Although this task involves several methodological difficulties, it could help to identify the key signatures of CDW stacking, possibly even separating blend and displacements. In addition, such calculations would make it possible to evaluate the corrections associated with vibrational entropy and to understand their role in the energy hierarchy of the interlayer stacking. 关于电子结构计算,未来的理论研究工作可以集中于理解拉曼模态与层间堆叠之间的联系,这与混合和位移均有关。先前研究表明,电荷密度波(CDW)的垂直排列不会导致剪切模态频率的间断;例如,参见图4(b)在参考文献[88]。尽管作者将这一特征解释为CDW垂直排列的迹象,这可能是缺陷引起的,但由于图1(a)中所示的特定层堆叠,这似乎对2H型不可能。解释拉曼数据需要理解声子谱在各种CDW结构中的变化。尽管这项任务涉及多种方法论上的困难,但它可能有助于识别CDW堆叠的关键特征,甚至可能区分混合和位移。此外,此类计算将使有可能评估振动熵相关的修正,并理解它们在层间堆叠的能量层级中的作用。
> 
> **Raman modes**: 拉曼模式，指通过拉曼光谱学探测到的分子或晶格的振动模式，可以提供关于材料结构、对称性和电子性质的信息。
