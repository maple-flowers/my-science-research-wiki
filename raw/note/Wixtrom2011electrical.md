> Low dimensional donor-acceptor compounds based on the tetrathiafulvalene (TTF) molecule and its derivatives have many applications such as molecular electronics, supramolecular systems, C60 complexes, non-linear optical materials, Langmuir-Blodgett films, molecular shuttles, semiconducting, conducting and superconducting solids, sensors, conducting polymers, etc [1]. 基于四硫富瓦烯（TTF）分子及其衍生物的低维给体-受体化合物具有许多应用，如分子电子学、超分子系统、C60配合物、非线性光学材料、Langmuir-Blodgett薄膜、分子梭、半导体、导体和超导体固体、传感器、导电聚合物等[1]。

> Charge transfer salts exhibit many interesting physical properties, such as low dimensionality, strong electron–electron and electron–phonon interactions and the proximity of antiferromagnetism, insulator states and superconductivity [2]. The green polymorph of tetrathiafulvalene–chloranil (TTF–CA) has been extensively studied due to its unique electrical properties and as a prototype material used to understand the neutral to ionic transition in one-dimensional TTF-based charge transfer salts [2]. 电荷转移盐展现出许多有趣的物理性质，如低维性、强电子-电子和电子-声子相互作用，以及反铁磁性、绝缘体态和超导电性的邻近性[2]。四硫富瓦烯-氯冉酸（TTF-CA）的绿色多晶型物因其独特的电学性质而被广泛研究，并作为理解一维基于TTF的电荷转移盐中中性-离子转变的原型材料[2]。
> 
> Mechanochemical synthesis have the economical and environmental advantages of avoiding the use of reaction solvents, thus providing "green chemistry" synthetic alternatives.
