> [!quote]- <center>Abstract</center>
> 
> The recently discovered ferroelectricity of van der Waals bilayers offers an unconventional route to improve the performance of devices. Key parameters such as switching field and speed depend on the static and dynamic properties of domain walls (DWs). Here we theoretically explore the properties of textures in stacking-engineered ferroelectrics from first principles. Employing a machine-learning potential model, we present results of large-scale atomistic simulations of stacking DWs and Moir\'e structure of boron nitride bilayers. We predict that the competition between the switching barrier of stable ferroelectric states and the in-plane lattice distortion leads to a DW width of the order of ten nanometers. DWs motion reduces the critical ferroelectric switching field of a monodomain by two orders of magnitude, while high domain-wall velocities allow domain switching on a picosecond-timescale. The superior performance compared to conventional ferroelectrics (or ferromagnets) may enable ultrafast and power-saving non-volatile memories. By twisting the bilayer into a stacking Moir\'e structure, the ferroelectric transforms into a super-paraelectric since DWs move under ultralow electric fields.
>
>【摘要翻译】最近发现的范德华双层膜的铁电性为提高器件性能提供了一条非常规的途径。开关场和速度等关键参数取决于畴壁的静态和动态特性。本文从第一性原理出发，从理论上探讨了堆垛工程铁电体中织构的性质。利用机器学习势模型，我们给出了氮化硼双层膜的DWs堆积和莫尔结构的大规模原子模拟结果。我们预测，稳定铁电态的开关势垒和面内晶格畸变之间的竞争将导致10纳米量级的DW宽度。DWs运动使单畴的临界铁电开关场减小了两个数量级，而高畴壁速度使畴开关在皮秒时间尺度上实现。与传统铁电体（或铁磁体）相比，其优越的性能可以实现超快且节能的非易失性存储器。通过将双层膜扭曲成堆叠的莫尔结构，由于DWs在超低电场下运动，铁电体转变为超顺电体。

> ## 一、引言
> 
> 铁电材料因其可翻转的自发极化特性，在非易失性存储器等电子器件中具有巨大应用潜力。传统铁电体的极化源于离子位移，而近期发现的一类新型铁电体——**堆叠工程铁电体**，其极化源于两层范德华材料层间的电荷转移，并可通过层间滑移实现翻转。这种机制带来了超低的翻转势垒和极高的热稳定性，为开发低功耗、高性能器件开辟了新路径。
> 
> 然而，该领域仍存在两个核心问题亟待解决：(1) 实验中观测到的临界翻转电场，比理论预测值低一个数量级；(2) 由扭转双层引起的莫尔条纹结构，会显著降低矫顽场并导致极窄的电滞回线。本文献（He et al., _Acta Materialia_, 2024）正是针对这两大难题，通过构建**机器学习势函数模型**，首次系统性地从原子尺度揭示了**铁电畴壁**的静态结构与动态行为，阐明了畴壁运动是实现超快、低能耗翻转的关键，为下一代电子器件的设计提供了重要的理论指导。
>

> ## 一、引言

> ### 1.1 领域背景：从传统铁电到二维滑移铁电
> 
> **铁电材料**是一类能够在外电场作用下翻转其自发极化方向的材料，是制造非易失性存储器、传感器和致动器的关键。传统铁电体的极化源于晶胞内正负离子的相对位移，这种位移需要克服较大的能量势垒，导致翻转速度较慢且能耗较高。
> 
> 近年来，一种全新的铁电机制——**堆叠工程铁电性**在二维范德华材料中被发现。其极化不再源于离子位移，而是通过两层原子层之间的层间滑移和电荷转移产生。这一机制为实现超低功耗、超快响应的电子器件开辟了新路径。
> 
> ### 1.2 研究问题与文献定位
> 
> 尽管前景广阔，该领域仍存在两大核心谜题：
> 
> 1.  **临界翻转场谜题**：实验上观测到的极化翻转所需的电场，比理论预测值低一个数量级，动因不明。
> 2.  **摩尔纹效应谜题**：当双层材料以微小角度扭转形成**摩尔纹**时，其铁电回滞线会变得极窄，矫顽场显著降低，其物理本质尚不清晰。

> ## 四、正文核心内容剖析
> 
> ### 4.1 研究背景与动机
> 
> 作者开篇即点明，**堆叠工程铁电体**虽潜力巨大，但其基础物理问题尚未解决。核心矛盾在于实验观测到的**临界翻转电场**远小于理论计算值，且**摩尔纹结构**会显著改变其铁电行为。作者将答案指向**铁电畴壁**，认为这些纳米尺度的拓扑缺陷是理解上述问题的关键。传统第一性原理方法受限于计算规模，无法研究大体系中的畴壁，因此，开发一种能保持DFT精度又能处理大尺度问题的新方法成为必需。
> 
> ### 4.2 方法与模型
> 
> 本研究的方法论核心是构建**深度势**模型。
> 
> 1.  **训练数据生成**：采用“**并行学习**”方案，通过DP-Gen流程自动迭代。从初始的DFT结构出发，通过DPMD在不同温度、压力下探索构型空间，并自动筛选出当前模型预测不准的构型，送回DFT重新计算，作为新的训练数据。这个过程循环了23次，最终生成了11,580个代表性构型。
> 2.  **模型构建**：使用DeePMD-kit代码，将体系总能量分解为每个原子的能量之和（原子能量由描述其局域化学环境的**描述符**通过神经网络映射得到）。模型的关键参数包括**截断半径**（6 Å）和描述符/拟合网络的尺寸。
> 3.  **模型验证**：通过与DFT结果对比能量、原子受力、声子谱和不同堆叠构型的能量及结构参数，证明了DP模型达到了与DFT相当的精度，为后续的大规模模拟提供了可靠基础。
> 
> ### 4.3 实验结果与讨论
> 
> #### 4.3.1 静态畴壁结构
> 
> 作者系统研究了四种角度（0°、30°、60°、90°）的畴壁。发现：
> 
> -   **极化构型**：0°畴壁呈现**布洛赫型**纹理，90°畴壁呈现**奈尔型**纹理，并伴有面外屈曲，这是由垂直于畴壁的层间滑移引起的应力释放所致。
> -   **畴壁宽度**：宽度从9.7 nm到40.7 nm不等，远大于传统铁电体。作者通过一个**一维弹性模型**给出了定量解释：宽度与单层材料的**面内刚度**的平方根成正比，与**翻转能垒**的平方根成反比。极低的翻转能垒和较大的面内刚度共同导致了巨大的畴壁宽度。
> 
> #### 4.3.2 动态畴壁运动
> 
> 这是本文的核心发现。
> 
> -   **临界翻转场**：单畴直接翻转所需的临界场约为1.3-1.4 V/nm。而通过畴壁运动实现翻转，其临界场骤降至约0.026 V/nm（对0°畴壁），**降低两个数量级**。这完美解释了引言中提出的第一个谜题。
> -   **翻转速度**：畴壁运动速度可达每秒数千米，使得一个100 nm器件的极化翻转时间缩短至约**15皮秒**，这预示了超快非易失性存储器的可能性。
> 
> #### 4.3.3 摩尔纹中的超顺电性
> 
> 作者模拟了0.385°扭转角的摩尔纹结构，发现弛豫后，高能AA畴收缩为点，AB/BA畴扩张为三角形。
> 
> -   **超顺电态**：在垂直电场下，BA和AB畴的体积可逆地变化，产生宏观极化，但撤去电场后，极化归零，整个P-E曲线无磁滞，表现出典型的**超顺电**行为，而非铁电性。这澄清了第二个谜题：摩尔纹的本征状态是超顺电的。
> -   **缺陷钉扎效应**：为了解释实验上可能观测到的铁电回滞，作者进一步研究了**氮空位**缺陷对畴壁的钉扎作用。计算表明，单个空位就能形成约50 meV的势阱，足以钉扎畴壁，阻止其自由运动，从而可能导致体系表现出类似铁电的磁滞行为。
> 
> ## 五、结论与贡献
> 
> 本文通过构建高精度的机器学习势，对h-BN双层中的铁电畴壁和摩尔纹结构进行了前所未有的系统研究，得出以下核心结论：
> 
> 1.  **畴壁结构**：揭示了四种畴壁的原子结构和极化纹理，其宽度可达10-40 nm，由一个一维弹性模型定量描述。
> 2.  **畴壁动力学**：直接证明了畴壁运动是极化翻转的主要机制，它能使**临界翻转电场降低两个数量级**，并实现**皮秒级超快翻转**。

> 1.  Introduction
>     
> 2.  引言 Ferroelectric materials display a switchable spontaneous electric polarization, that usually emerges from ion displacements. By contrast, the spontaneous polarization in recently discovered stacking-engineered ferroelectrics of two van der Waals monolayers originates from the charge transfer. It can be controlled by interlayer sliding without vertical ion displacements [1,2] and leads to ultralow ferroelectric switching barriers and high thermal stability [3–6]. The research of stacking-engineered ferroelectricity is still in its infancy since many fundamental and practical issues associated with the emerging ferroelectricity remain to be settled [7,8]. Here, we focus on two fundamental observations: (i) the experimentally observed critical switching field is an order magnitude smaller than the theoretical value [1,6,7], and (ii) the Moiré structure caused by twisting the bilayers significantly reduces the coercive field and resulting a very narrow hysteresis loop [3,9]. An in-depth understanding of the atomistic characteristics of ferroelectric domain walls (DWs) in stacked van der Waals bilayers [10,11] is crucial in solving the above two issues. A ferroelectric DW is a topological defect that affects various properties such as electric conductivity [12,13], dielectric permittivity [14], multiferroic phenomena [15,16], and the photovoltaic effect [17,18]. Moreover, DW motion driven by an external field facilitates ferroelectric switching [19–21]. However, the rotation of the polarization in Bloch and Néel DWs of conventional ferroelectrics costs significant elastic energy [10,11,20,21]. On the other hand, DWs in stacking ferroelectrics with weak van der Waals interactions remain yet unexplored, experimentally and theoretically [8]. Indeed, the complexity of DWs in stacking-engineered ferroelectric far exceeds the computing capacity of traditional theoretical first-principles methods such as density functional theory (DFT) with a computation time that scales polynomially with the unit cell size. In this article, we report a machine-learning potential model using training data from DFT calculations that allows us to systematically and directly study large-scale DWs and Moiré structures in hexagonal boron nitride (h-BN) bilayers with DFT accuracy. The DWs in stacking ferroelectrics can be classified into four types (i.e. 0°, 30°, 60°, and 90°) based on the angle between one-dimensional DW and sliding vector. We find that the DWs exhibit in-plane polarization component, resulting in Bloch- and Néel-type textures for the 0° and 90° DWs, respectively. Their widths turn out to be 10~40 nm, while the critical switching field facilitated by DW motion is two orders of magnitude smaller than that for monodomain reversal. The high DW velocities allow domain switching on a picosecond-timescale. Finally, we demonstrate that a twisted Moiré structure is a super-paraelectric because the ferroelectric order is reversibly broken by DW motion already at ultralow electric fields. To the best of our knowledge, this research represents the first comprehensive investigation of the atomic structure of stacking-engineered ferroelectric DWs and Moiré structure, as well as their dynamic response under an electric field, with the exceptional precision of DFT. Our findings reveal that the stacking DWs in bilayers exhibit an intricate and diverse topological polarization structure, exceeding previous understanding of this phenomenon. The construction of a deep potential (DP) model 2.1. 深度势函数（DP）模型的构建 Deep neural network-based potential model offers a promising approach to describe atomic interactions, bridging the gap between accurate first-principles DFT calculations and large-scale atomistic simulations. We employ the recently proposed deep potential (DP) method to train a deep neural network-based potential for h-BN bilayer using data from first-principles DFT calculations [22,23]. Selecting the appropriate the configurations for the training dataset is crucial for an accurate DP model. Our focus is on the studying the dynamical response of the DW and Moiré structure at finite temperature, which requires inclusion of all characteristic structures of various stacking modes, including intermediate states of interlayer sliding and rotation, and in-plane distortion. Manual construction of these configurations is a tedious task, particularly for the states far from the equilibrium configuration. Therefore, we adopt a concurrent learning procedure that automatically generates training data, covering all relevant configuration space [24]. The workflow of each iteration in concurrent learning procedure includes three main steps: (1) training the DP model, (2) exploring configurations by running DP molecular dynamics (DPMD) simulations at different temperatures, (3) labeling configurations based on specific criteria and adding them to the training dataset, and then repeating the step (1) again. We start with AA, AA', AB, BA, AB' and BA' bilayer configurations (Fig. S1) and their intermediate transition states that have been optimized by density functional theory (DFT) calculations. In the first iteration of the DP-Gen workflow, the initial training dataset contains 1000 randomly perturbed 3 × 3 supercells containing 36 atoms by displacement of the atomic coordinates from the ground states by <0.01 Å that cause in-plane strains of -0.003 to 0.003. Next, we train four different DP models by the DPMD method with different values of deep neural network parameters [22]. In the exploration step, one of the DPs is used for DPMD simulations for different pressures (1 to 50,000 bar) and temperatures (10 to 1200 K) to explore the configuration space. The other three DPs predict the atomic forces for all configurations visited by the MD trajectories. > 4.  结果与讨论 3.1. Stacking mode and polarization 3.1. 堆叠模式与极化 The h-BN stacking configurations can be divided into two categories, i.e. with parallel and antiparallel twists [3,7,40–42]. Bulk h-BN crystals typically exhibit the centrosymmetric antiparallel AA' structure as the preferred stacking configuration. However, theoretical and experimental research has demonstrated that when two BN monolayer sheets are stacked without any rotational offset, the parallel AA configuration is unstable and tends to decay into more energetically favorable polar -AB or -BA configurations, as illustrated in Fig. S1. Here we focus on h-BN bilayers and start by comparing domains with parallel AA, AB, and BA stackings. In the AB (BA) domain, the N anion (B cation) in the upper layer is over the hexagon center in the bottom layer, while the B (N) in the upper layer is right over the N (B) atom in the lower one. This configuration breaks inversion symmetry and gives rise to a vertical upward (downward) electric polarization of 1.46 × 10⁻¹² C/m by charge transfer as illustrated in Fig. 1. Interlayer sliding along the a, b, and c axes allows for the morphing of AB and BA domains, with the polarization rotating during this process as illustrated in Fig. 1a. The intermediate saddle-point (SP) in the sliding pathway exhibits a purely in-plane polarization of 1.38 × 10⁻¹² C/m as shown in Fig. 1b. > 3.2. Accuracy of the machine-learning potential 3.2. 机器学习势函数的精度 A DP model trained from DFT dataset allows us to directly compare DP results with DFT results. To evaluate the performance of the model, we calculated the energies and atomic forces for all configurations in the final training dataset using both DFT and the DP model. The results, as illustrated in Fig. 2, show excellent agreement between the two methods, with a mean absolute error of energy (|ΔE DP-DFT|) and atomic force (|ΔF DP-DFT|) of 0.60 meV/atoms, 0.051 eV/Å for in-plane and 0.017 eV/Å for out-of-plane. Additionally, we employ the nudged elastic band method to compute the DFT kinetic barriers of polarization reversal with interlayer sliding pathways, and the DP model was able to reproduce these barriers accurately, as shown in Fig. 1c. The DP model also yields structure properties and energies for various stacking bilayers that were in excellent agreement with those obtained from DFT, as listed in Table 1. Moreover, the calculated phonon dispersion of the ferroelectric BN bilayer by the DP model is found to be almost overlapping with DFT curves, as shown in Fig. 3. The 4-atom unit cell produces three acoustic and nine optical branches, the three acoustic branches exist only in the low-frequency regime, and approach zero frequency towards the high-symmetry Gamma point. The phonon dispersion demonstrated the dynamical stability of AB stacking bilayer at room temperature and ambient pressure, with no negative phonon frequencies. > 3.3. Atomic structures and polarization properties of DWs 3.3. DW的原子结构和极化特性 The one-dimensional DW separating AB and BA domains in h-BN bilayers is a continuous variation of the sliding vector b along one of the three-equivalent a, b, and c orientations (Fig. 1a), resulting in a phase slip of π between domains of opposite polarization. We choose coordinates such that the DWs always lie in the y direction. They can be categorized into four types by the angle φ between DW and sliding vector b, viz. 0°, 30°, 60°, and 90° as sketched by red arrows in Figs. 5 and S4. In consideration of the large width of DW in stacking ferroelectrics and to prevent their coupling, we construct the periodic h-BN freestanding bilayer supercells (~30,000 atoms for 0° and 60°, ~40,000 atoms for 30° and 90°) containing two DWs separated by 120 nm to obtain the converged DW energy. The DP model for the structural relaxation leads to the optimized supercells with various DWs as shown in Fig. S5, and zoom-in's on the DW region and the local atomic structure are shown in Figs. 5 and S4. In 0° DWs the sliding vector b varies smoothly across the DW and the out-of-plane geometry remains flat (Fig. 5a). In contrast, we observe a significant out-of-plane buckling for other three DWs (Figs. 5b and S4). To further validate the generalizability of learned force field, we constructed the buckling structures consisting of 36 atoms with different curvature as illustrated in Fig. S6a. We regard these buckling structures as separate testing dataset to validate the generalizability of the trained model, and the results are reported in the Fig. S6b. We observed a strong agreement between DP and DFT energies and forces, with a mean absolute error of 1.2 meV/atom and 0.054 eV/Å, respectively, for the buckling structures. This demonstrated that the DP model accurately describes the buckling of DW. The buckling in DWs originates from the interlayer sliding perpendicular to the DW, inducing an extra column of atoms in top (or bottom) layer that generates a gradient stress to bend the bilayers. It may be associated with the flexoelectric effects in van der Waals free-standing films. Table SI shows that the flat 0° DW is the most stable, and the DW energies increase linearly with the angle φ. When the bilayers are attached to a substrate that suppresses buckling, the 30°, 60°, and 90° DW energies would shift to much higher energies because the gradient strain cannot be released by bending. A rotation of the polarization in conventional bulk ferroelectrics is energetically expensive due to the associated substantial elastic strains. This leads to DWs that are usually of the "Ising type", i.e. collinear with a vanishing polarization at the DW center [11]. The DWs plotted in Fig. 5a, b exhibit textures closer to those of DWs in ferromagnets that are characterized by a rotation with fixed modulus. Here, the polarization rotates with nearly constant P, with pure in-plane polarization for DW center at 1.38 × 10⁻¹² C/m, within 5% of the pure out-of-plane polarization value of 1.46 × 10⁻¹² C/m for monodomain region. The polarization exhibits a Bloch configuration in the 0° DW, while it shows a Néel-like texture in the 90° DW. To estimate the characteristic DW width, we plot the distribution of local out-of-plane polarization (PZ) in four kinds of DWs (Fig. 5c). It can be seen that the characteristic DW width increases monotonously as the angle φ increases: 9.7 nm for φ=0°, 17.6 nm for φ=30°, 32.1 nm for φ=60°, and 40.7 nm for φ=90°, respectively, which are an order of magnitude larger than those in perovskite ferroelectrics (~0.7 nm). A quantitative analysis of the origin of such a large DW width is desired on the lines of a one-dimensional (1D) elastic model. The 1D elastic model is simplified from the two-dimensional one which proposed by recent literature [6]. The proportionality of the DW width (w) with angle (φ) follows from a one-dimensional elastic model for the DW energy: E = ∫ [ (λ1D/2) (∂us/∂x)² + V(us) ] dx, (3) where us is the displacements of the top and the bottom layer along x, λ1D the corresponding 1D Lamé force coefficient, and V(us) the interlayer potential energy density as a function of us. The width w = u0 / 2 √(λ1D / Δ), (4) where Δ is the barrier height per unit length, minimizes the energy of a hyperbolic tangent shape (for detail formula derivation, see Supplemental Material S1). It can be seen from the Eq. (4): the DW widths in stacked ferroelectrics is so large because of both the ultralow ferroelectric switching barrier (Δ) and the large in-plane monolayer stiffness (λ1D). Since Δ does not depend on φ, the elastic constant λ1D governs w(φ). The λ1D is related to in-plane distortion energies, when fitting the 1D model to the DFT calculated distortion energies (Supplementary S2 and Table SII), w = 10 nm (φ=0°), 14 nm (φ=30°), 30 nm (φ=60°), and 43 nm (φ=90°), which are consistent with the predictions of the full DP model. While Eq. (4) predicts as well the width of ferromagnetic DWs when λ1D parameterizes the exchange interactions and Δ the magnetic anisotropy, but λ1D as a new degree of freedom in ferroelectric, can be conveniently controlled by strain and electric field, further manipulating the phenomenology of DWs and polar states. It should be noted that in the 1D elastic model, only one component sliding motion is considered, and the interlayer displacement was not employed as it does not directly affect the polar states. > 3.4. Dynamics of the DW motion 3.4. DW运动的动力学 In multidomain bilayer h-BN, vertical electric fields (Ev) or lateral shear stresses (Fs) can reverse the ferroelectric order. Ev and Fs are related by Fs = Ev Z_13, where Z_13 is a non-diagonal element of the Born effective charge tensor. For convenience, the two neighboring B and N ions in the layer may however be treated as a single ion. A Berry phase calculation gives Z_13_top = 0.027 and Z_13_bottom = -0.027, which is two orders of magnitude smaller than in BaTiO₃. In the DPMD simulations, we can study the effects of additional strains Fs on top and bottom layer. The critical Fs (Ev) at which the ferroelectric order of a single domain switches is 3.8 × 10⁻³ eV/Å (~1.41 V/nm) at 100 K, and 3.5 × 10⁻³ eV/Å (~1.32 V/nm) at 300 K. The critical Fs is not sensitive to thermal fluctuation, in agreement with the predicted large thermal stability [6]. However, it is an order of magnitude larger than has been observed [3,4]. Many experiments report that the polarization switching by an electric field is mediated by DW motion [4]. When applying an Fs of 5 × 10⁻⁴ eV/Å (Ev of 0.18 V/nm) at 300 K, we observe soliton-like motion of the DWs. Two 0° DWs with opposite polarity move closer to each other since the field decreases the energy density of the BA domain. After 9 ps they annihilate into single domain as shown in Fig. 6a (the 90° DW case is shown in Fig. S7). Fig. 6b shows time-dependent results for the BA domain size as a function of Fs. When Fs less than 7 × 10⁻⁵ eV/Å, the DWs are immobile and the BA domain fraction remains constant, indicating an intrinsic pinning potential in the absence of disorder. For larger Fs, the BA domain growths linearly with time indicating a constant DW velocity of ~6000 m/s at 0.18 V/nm (Fig. S8), corresponding to switching times of ~15 ps for 100-nm-diameter electronic devices, thereby enabling the ultrafast non-volatile memory devices with low operation voltage. The critical Fs for 0° and 90° DWs motion are 7 × 10⁻⁵ eV/Å and 3 × 10⁻⁴ eV/Å (corresponding to Ev=0.026 V/nm and 0.11 V/nm), respectively, recalling that the monodomain switching field (force) of 3.8 × 10⁻³ eV/Å (1.41 V/nm) is larger by two orders of magnitude. Although there is currently no proper physical model to determine the critical field for DW switching for specific materials, we can provide a phenomenological analysis. It can be understood through Landau-Ginzburg-Devonshire (LDG) free energy expression. The contribution of DWs to the LDG free energy is introduced through gradient energy term. It reveals that as the gradient energy coefficient (Gij) increases, the DW velocity also increases. Moreover, a higher Gij value leads to a wider DW. Consequently, the critical field shows an inverse relationship with the width of DWs. 在多畴双层h-BN中，垂直电场（Ev）或横向剪切应力（Fs）可以反转铁电序。Ev和Fs的关系为Fs = Ev Z_13，其中Z_13是波恩有效电荷张量的非对角元。为方便起见，层中相邻的B和N离子可以被视为单个离子。Berry相位计算给出Z_13_top = 0.027和Z_13_bottom = -0.027，这比BaTiO₃中的值小两个数量级。在DPMD模拟中，我们可以研究额外应变Fs对顶层和底层的影响。使单畴铁电序翻转的临界Fs（Ev）在100 K时为3.8 × 10⁻³ eV/Å (~1.41 V/nm)，在300 K时为3.5 × 10⁻³ eV/Å (~1.32 V/nm)。临界Fs对热涨落不敏感，这与预测的高热稳定性一致[6]。然而，它比实验观察到的值大一个数量级[3,4]。许多实验报告称，电场驱动的极化翻转是由DW运动介导的[4]。当在300 K下施加5 × 10⁻⁴ eV/Å的Fs（0.18 V/nm的Ev）时，我们观察到DW的类孤子运动。两个极性相反的0° DW相互靠近，因为电场降低了BA畴的能量密度。9 ps后，它们湮灭成单畴，如图6a所示（90° DW情况见图S7）。图6b显示了BA畴尺寸随时间随Fs变化的结果。当Fs小于7 × 10⁻⁵ eV/Å时，DW固定不动，BA畴比例保持不变，表明在没有无序的情况下存在内禀的钉扎势。对于较大的Fs，BA畴随时间线性增长，表明在0.18 V/nm下DW速度恒定为~6000 m/s（图S8），对应于100 nm直径电子器件约15 ps的翻转时间，从而实现了具有低工作电压的超快非易失性存储器件。0°和90° DW运动的临界Fs分别为7 × 10⁻⁵ eV/Å和3 × 10⁻⁴ eV/Å（对应Ev=0.026 V/nm和0.11 V/nm），而单畴开关场（力）为3.8 × 10⁻³ eV/Å (1.41 V/nm)，大了两个数量级。尽管目前没有合适的物理模型来确定特定材料DW开关的临界场，但我们可以提供一个唯象分析。这可以通过朗道-金兹堡-德文希尔（LDG）自由能表达式来理解。DW对LDG自由能的贡献通过梯度能项引入。它揭示，随着梯度能量系数（Gij）的增加，DW速度也增加。此外，更高的Gij值导致更宽的DW。因此，临界场与DW的宽度呈反比关系。 **Born effective charge**: 波恩有效电荷，一个描述晶体中原子位移如何导致极化改变的张量，其非对角元描述了面外电场与面内力的耦合。 **Landau-Ginzburg-Devonshire (LDG) free energy**: 朗道-金兹堡-德文希尔自由能，一种唯象理论框架，通过极化作为序参量构建自由能，用于描述铁电相变及相关性质。 **Soliton**: 孤子，一种能保持其形状和速度在介质中传播的孤立波，此处比喻畴壁的稳定运动。

> 3.5. Super-paraelectricity in Moiré structure 3.5. 莫尔结构中的超顺电性 Twisting two van der Waals layers by a small angle gives rise to Moiré patterns that according to several experiments cause a ferroelectric response [3,4,9,43]. On the other hand, recent analytical models do not find a macroscopic polarization of a commensurate Moiré structure at zero field [44–46]. We clarify this contradiction by carrying out a large-scale atomistic simulation of a Moiré structures of h-BN bilayers with a small 0.385° twist angle, which corresponds to a lateral supercell containing 355,012 atoms as displayed in Fig. S3a. Initially, the areas of AB, BA, and AA domains are the same. Allowing atoms to fully relax by the DP model, the AB, BA domain expand into triangles, while the high-energy AA domain shrinks to a point as shown in Fig. S3b. The 0° DWs accommodate the conserved global twist when the Moiré structure disintegrates into commensurate AB and BA domains. In the corners of the triangular domains, fully eclipsed AA domains persist without twist (Fig. S3c). A periodic triangular configuration agrees well with experimentally observed patterns [3,4,9,43]. The fully relaxed Moiré structure is very stable against thermal fluctuations as shown in Fig. S9. To study the response of the Moiré structure and polarization to a vertical electric field, we utilize a machine learning method to train three models using DFT self-consistent calculations with out-of-plane electric fields of 0.1, 0.5, and 1 V/nm. The configurations of training datasets remain the same as before. As a result, the well-trained DP models allow us to perform dynamics simulations directly under vertical electric field. For Ev = 1 V/nm, the BA domain changes from a triangular to a hexagonal-like pattern, while the AB domain shrinks (see the right upper insets of Fig. 7), leading to a net macroscopic out-of-plane polarization Pz = 1.32 × 10⁻¹² C/m. The main plot in Fig. 7 shows the spatial average of Pz as a function of Ev (i.e. P-E hysteresis). We attribute the initial increase and rapid saturation at an ultra-low critical Ev = 0.026 V/nm for 0° DWs motion to pinning by the AA domain, which prevents an increase of Pz for larger Ev. The polarization recovers its original texture when the field is removed, as shown in left bottom inset of Fig. 7. At zero field, the AB and BA domains are of the same size, and the macroscopic polarization almost vanishes. The absence of a hysteresis in the P-E diagram of this twisted configuration indicates super-paraelectric rather than ferroelectric order [46]. Thus, we suspect that the observed ferroelectric response of Moiré structures maybe caused by defects that pin the DW motion that would equalize the AB and BA domain sizes such that a net polarization persists. Previous studies have identified various types of defects in h-BN, such as nitrogen vacancy, carbon substituting for boron or nitrogen, and oxygen substituting for nitrogen [47,48]. Here, we take nitrogen vacancy (VN) as an example to explore the pinning effect. To ensure the accuracy of machine-learning potential in describing the structures of VN in large-scale systems, we incorporated configurations containing VN into the training dataset. Subsequently, we examined the interaction between DW and VN, and the result is shown in Fig. 8. The energy profile of the DW approaching and moving away from the VN was plotted. We found that the VN tends to trap the DW, requiring approximately 50 meV of energy for the DW to traverse a single VN. This indicates that a small number of VN defects are sufficient to pin the DW. 将两个范德华层扭转一个小角度会产生莫尔图案，根据几项实验，这会引起铁电响应[3,4,9,43]。另一方面，最近的分析模型在零场下并未发现共格莫尔结构的宏观极化[44–46]。我们通过对具有0.385°小扭转角的h-BN双层莫尔结构进行大规模原子级模拟来澄清这一矛盾，该结构对应一个包含355,012个原子的横向超胞，如图S3a所示。最初，AB、BA和AA畴的面积相同。通过DP模型使原子完全弛豫后，AB、BA畴扩展成三角形，而高能量的AA畴收缩成一个点，如图S3b所示。当莫尔结构分解为共格的AB和BA畴时，0° DW容纳了守恒的全局扭转。在三角形畴的角落，完全重叠的AA畴持续存在，无扭转（图S3c）。周期性的三角形构型与实验观察到的图案非常吻合[3,4,9,43]。完全弛豫的莫尔结构对热涨落非常稳定，如图S9所示。为了研究莫尔结构和极化对垂直电场的响应，我们利用机器学习方法，使用面外电场为0.1、0.5和1 V/nm的DFT自洽计算训练了三个模型。训练数据集的构型与之前保持一致。结果，训练好的DP模型使我们能够直接进行垂直电场下的动力学模拟。对于Ev = 1 V/nm，BA畴从三角形变为类六边形图案，而AB畴收缩（见图7右上角插图），导致净宏观面外极化Pz = 1.32 × 10⁻¹² C/m。图7的主图显示了Pz的空间平均值与Ev的函数关系（即P-E磁滞回线）。我们将0° DW运动在超低临界Ev = 0.026 V/nm处的初始增加和快速饱和归因于AA畴的钉扎作用，这阻止了Pz在更大Ev下的增加。当电场移除时，极化恢复其原始织构，如图7左下角插图所示。在零场下，AB和BA畴尺寸相同，宏观极化几乎消失。这种扭转构型的P-E图中没有磁滞回线，表明其为超顺电序而非铁电序[46]。因此，我们怀疑观察到的莫尔结构的铁电响应可能是由缺陷引起的，这些缺陷钉扎了DW运动，而DW运动会平衡AB和BA畴的尺寸，从而导致净极化持续存在。先前的研究已经确定了h-BN中的各种缺陷类型，如氮空位、碳取代硼或氮、氧取代氮[47,48]。在此，我们以氮空位（VN）为例来探索钉扎效应。为了确保机器学习势函数在大规模系统中描述VN结构的准确性，我们将包含VN的构型纳入训练数据集。随后，我们检查了DW与VN之间的相互作用，结果如图8所示。绘制了DW接近和远离VN的能量轮廓。我们发现VN倾向于捕获DW，DW穿过单个VN大约需要50 meV的能量。这表明少量VN缺陷就足以钉扎DW。 **P-E hysteresis**: 极化-电场（P-E）磁滞回线，铁电材料中用于表征极化随外加电场变化的特征曲线，其面积代表能量损耗。

> 4.  Conclusions
> 5.  结论 In summary, using a machine-learning-based DP model, we predict diverse polarization profiles in h-BN bilayers, the archetypal van der Waals stacking ferroelectric. Our findings reveal that DWs lower the critical electric switching field by two orders of magnitude and reduce switching times to picoseconds. The Moiré structures in twisted bilayers can be tuned reversibly by a vertical electric field and exhibit characteristics more similar to super-paraelectric rather than ferroelectric. Our simulations emphasize the importance of DWs in understanding the dynamic properties of ferroelectricity and provide necessary theoretical guidance for future exploration and application of van der Waals ferroelectrics. 总之，利用基于机器学习的DP模型，我们预测了h-BN双层（典型的范德华堆叠铁电体）中多样化的极化分布。我们的发现揭示了DW将临界电开关场降低了两个数量级，并将开关时间缩短至皮秒级。扭转双层中的莫尔结构可以通过垂直电场可逆地调控，并表现出更类似于超顺电而非铁电的特性。我们的模拟强调了DW在理解铁电性动态特性中的重要性，并为未来范德华铁电体的探索和应用提供了必要的理论指导。

> ### 给太奶“唠”论文
> 
> 这篇文章的题目叫 **《堆叠工程铁电体中铁电序的超快翻转动力学》** 。咱先别管题目多长，您就想像成：科学家发现了一种新型“电子开关”，它开关的速度快得惊人，而且特别省电。这篇文章就是研究这个开关到底怎么动，有多快，怎么才能更好用。
> 
> **1. 这研究的到底是什么东西？** 文章说的是“铁电体”。咱们老祖宗讲“铁”是金属，但这儿说的“铁”跟磁性没关系，是指一种材料：它自己内部正负电荷天生就分开了，一头带正电，一头带负电，正像一块小小“永久电池”。而且这个正负方向还可以用外加电场给翻过来，就像翻烙饼一样。这种翻转就是“铁电翻转”。
> 
> 传统的铁电体是靠原子上下挪动产生正负分开的，但本文研究的是一种新式的铁电体，叫“堆叠工程铁电体”。它用的是像石墨一样一层一层的薄片——六方氮化硼（h-BN），两层摞在一起。这两层原子之间不是靠上下挪动，而是靠一层相对于另一层水平滑动，一滑动，电荷就转移了，正负电荷中心就分开了，也就产生了极化（就是正负电分开的状态）。这种滑动就像你推一下桌面上的两张纸，纸没提起来，但位置变了，性质就变了。这种材料的优点是：滑动需要的能量特别低，翻转起来特别容易，还不怕热，很稳定。
> 
> **2. 他们遇到了什么难题？** 科学家发现，实验里让这种材料翻转的电压，比理论算出来的要小一个数量级（也就是十分之一），而且如果把两层原子扭一下，形成一个叫“摩尔纹”的图案，翻转电压会更低，电滞回线（一种反映翻转难易的曲线）会变得很窄。这到底是怎么回事呢？根本原因就在“畴壁”上。
> 
> **什么又是“畴壁”？** 太奶，您就把材料想象成一块稻田，有的地方稻子朝左倒，有的地方朝右倒，那中间过渡的埂子就是“畴壁”。在这个材料里，有的区域极化向上，有的向下，它们之间的过渡带就是畴壁。畴壁怎么动，直接决定了翻转需要多大力气、有多快。所以，要想弄明白上面那两个问题，就得把畴壁的原子结构、运动规律研究透。但靠传统精确计算（第一性原理）算不动，因为畴壁太大，原子太多，电脑带不动。于是他们就用了一种聪明的办法——机器学习势。
> 
> **3. 他们是怎么做的？（方法）** 他们用了一种叫“机器学习势”的方法。简单说，就是先用精确的量子力学方法（密度泛函理论，DFT）算一些小体系的能量和受力，然后把这些数据喂给一个深度神经网络（像教小孩认图一样），让这个网络学会怎么预测原子之间的作用力。学会以后，这个网络就算得非常快，可以模拟几十万个原子的大体系，而且精度跟量子力学差不多。他们就用这个办法，在电脑里建了各种畴壁——根据畴壁和滑动方向的角度，分了0°、30°、60°、90°四种，还建了一个扭转了0.385°的摩尔纹结构，里面有35万多个原子。然后在外加电场下，看畴壁怎么跑，极化怎么翻转。
> 
> **4. 他们发现了什么？（结论）**
> 
> -   **畴壁很宽，而且分类型：** 这些畴壁宽度有10到40纳米，比传统铁电体宽了几十倍。0°畴壁是“布洛赫型”，极化在畴壁里像拧麻花一样在平面内转；90°畴壁是“奈尔型”，极化在垂直于畴壁的平面里转，像波浪一样。
> -   **畴壁动起来，开关就特别省力、特别快：** 如果没有畴壁，直接让整个区域一起翻转，需要的电场很大（约1.4 V/nm）。但如果有畴壁存在，电场只要原来的几十分之一（0.026 V/nm）就能推动畴壁，让极化翻转。畴壁跑起来的速度能达到每秒6000米，一个100纳米大小的器件，开关时间只要15皮秒（1皮秒=一万亿分之一秒）。这比眨眼快多了，意味着能做超快、超省电的存储器。
> -   **扭转的摩尔纹结构是“超顺电态”：** 他们把两层扭个微小角度，会出现像席子压出来的那种摩尔纹图案。不加电场时，向上和向下的区域一样大，整体不显电性；一加电场，畴壁一下子就移动了，整体极化马上出现；但电场一撤，畴壁又回去了，整体又没电了。这个过程没有普通铁电体那种“滞后”现象（就是加电和撤电走的路径不一样），所以他们管它叫“超顺电态”，不是真正的铁电体。那为什么实验上看到有点像铁电呢？他们猜测，可能是材料里的缺陷（比如缺了氮原子）把畴壁钉住了，让它回不去，才显出铁电性。
>

> -   发现了畴壁复杂的拓扑极化结构，比以前以为的丰富得多。
> -   揭示了畴壁运动能让翻转电压降低两个数量级，翻转时间达到皮秒级，为设计超快低功耗存储器指出了新路。
> -   明确提出了扭转摩尔纹结构是“超顺电态”，并指出缺陷可能起着关键作用，为解释实验矛盾提供了思路。

> -   对扭转结构的超顺电态，靠缺陷钉扎来解释实验现象，但没详细研究各种缺陷的影响，也没模拟衬底等实际环境，结论还比较理想化。
