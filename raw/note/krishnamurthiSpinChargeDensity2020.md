> ## 一、引言
> 
> **领域背景**：二维过渡金属硫族化物（TMDCs），如二硒化钼（MoSe₂）和二硫化钼（MoS₂），是继石墨烯之后备受关注的新型半导体材料，其厚度仅为数个原子层，在电子学、光电子学领域具有巨大潜力。然而，在实际材料中，不可避免地会存在晶界（Grain Boundary, GB）这类一维（1D）线缺陷。其中，**镜像孪晶界**（Mirror Twin Boundary, MTB）是最常见的一种。
> 
> **核心问题**：大量实验表明，这些本应是半导体的TMDCs的晶界，在室温下却表现出奇异的金属性。这种金属性背后的物理根源是什么？它是否稳定？一直是学术界争论的焦点。
> 
> **文献定位**：本文发表于2020年，是一篇理论计算研究。它直面一个冲突：一些实验认为晶界表现出**朝永-拉亭格液体**（Tomonaga-Luttinger Liquid, TLL，一种因电子间强相互作用而形成的特殊量子液体）特征，而另一些实验则在低温下观测到了**电荷密度波**（Charge Density Wave, CDW，电子密度的周期性调制）并打开了能隙。本文通过第一性原理计算，为这一矛盾提供了统一的理论解释。
> 
> **核心价值**：本文首次提出，TMDC晶界的金属性源于其体相材料一个深层拓扑性质——**极化**（Polarization）的突变，并预测了一种无需原子位移的、纯粹由电子效应驱动的、自旋与电荷密度波共存的基态，完美解释了实验现象，并预言了携带分数电荷的奇特准粒子——**孤子**（Soliton）的存在。
>

> ## 二、论文结构与逻辑概览
> 
> 本文遵循“**提出问题 → 分析根源 → 提出解决方案 → 验证与拓展**”的经典论证逻辑。
> 
> 1.  **提出问题：实验矛盾**
>     
>     -   **现象A**：TMDC的镜像孪晶界（MTB）在室温下是金属性的。
>     -   **现象B**：对于这种金属性的本质，实验存在TLL和CDW两种对立的解释。
>     -   **理论困境**：先前的DFT计算未能自发地找到导致CDW的结构畸变。
> 2.  **分析根源：拓扑极化**
>     
>     -   **核心洞察**：TMDC单层具有D₃ₕ对称性，其面内电极化P是一个**拓扑不变量**（Z₃分类）。
>     -   **逻辑推演**：MTB两侧的极化方向相反，导致极化线电荷积累，这必然在能隙中产生1D金属态来补偿。**电荷补偿机制**决定了这些边界态具有1/3的分数占据。
> 3.  **提出解决方案：纯电子效应**
>     
>     -   **关键假设**：1D金属态对电子-电子相互作用敏感。作者引入**DFT+U**方法（一种处理强关联电子体系的计算方法）来考察钼（Mo）原子d电子的现场库仑相互作用。
>     -   **核心发现**：在不发生任何原子位移的前提下，电子体系自发地形成周期三倍的**自旋密度波/电荷密度波**（SDW/CDW），并打开一个约0.1 eV的能隙，降低了系统总能量。
> 4.  **验证与拓展**
>     
>     -   **模型验证**：模拟的STM图像与实验观测到的3倍周期调制完全吻合。
>     -   **理论拓展**：在此SDW/CDW基态上，预言了可携带分数电荷（±1/3 e）的孤子激发。
>

> ## 四、正文核心内容剖析
> 
> ### 1. 研究背景：1D MTB的金属性之谜
> 
> -   **普遍现象**：TMDC（如MoS₂, MoSe₂）的许多边缘和晶界在室温下呈金属性，这与作为二维半导体的体相材料形成鲜明对比。这种金属性似乎与原子终止结构无关，具有很强的普适性。
> -   **实验矛盾**：
>     -   **TLL派**：角分辨光电子能谱（ARPES）和STM/STS研究认为，MoSe₂的MTB是**Tomonaga-Luttinger液体**，这是1D强关联金属的典型行为。
>     -   **CDW派**：低温STM/STS研究则明确观测到MTB上存在波长为3倍晶格常数的**电荷密度波**（CDW），并伴随约0.1 eV的能隙打开，这是绝缘体行为。
> -   **理论困境**：此前的第一性原理计算（DFT）虽然能预测MTB的金属性，但无法自发地找到导致3倍周期CDW的结构畸变（即**佩尔斯相变**，Peierls distortion），使得理论无法解释实验上的CDW现象。
> 
> ### 2. 方法/模型：拓扑极化与DFT+U
> 
> -   **模型构建**：采用周期性超胞方法模拟一条孤立的MTB。超胞包含12个MX₂单元宽度的纳米带，并用真空层隔离。
> -   **核心理论：现代极化理论**
>     -   **拓扑不变量**：作者指出，具有D₃ₕ对称性的TMDC晶格，其面内极化**P**是一个**Z₃拓扑不变量**。计算表明，所有研究的MX₂都处于(2/3, 1/3)这一类。
>     -   **极化突变**：在MTB处，极化方向发生了从P到-P的翻转。
>     -   **必然结果**：极化的突变导致在MTB处积累极化线电荷，λ = 2P·n = 2e/(3a)。
>     -   **电荷补偿**：为避免“极化灾难”，系统必须在MTB处产生等量的电子电荷来补偿。这个补偿电荷只能由位于带隙中的1D边界态提供。这直接决定了这些边界态的总占据率必须是**1/3**（即每条能带填充1/3的电子，考虑自旋简并）。
> -   **计算方法：DFT+U**
>     -   **动机**：边界态主要由Mo原子的d轨道构成，具有强局域性。1D体系本身对电子关联效应敏感。尤其是部分占据（1/3填充）的能带，极易发生电子-电子相互作用驱动的对称性破缺。
>     -   **实现**：引入**哈伯德U**（Hubbard U）参数，以修正标准DFT对现场库仑相互作用描述不足的问题。作者选用了U-J = 3 eV的典型值，并研究了其影响。
> 
> ### 3. 实验结果/讨论：纯电子SDW/CDW的涌现
> 
> -   **核心发现**：在使用DFT+U并放开电子自由度后，在没有发生任何原子结构位移的情况下，MTB体系自发地形成了一种三重周期性的**自旋密度波与电荷密度波的共存态**（SDW/CDW）。
>     -   **SDW**：沿MTB的三个Mo原子获得不等价的自旋磁矩，形成“大-小-小”的排列。
>     -   **CDW**：伴随自旋调制，电荷密度也发生微弱的3倍周期调制。
>     -   **能隙打开**：这一SDW/CDW态成功打开了一个约0.1 eV的能隙，与实验观测值（Barja等人）吻合。
>     -   **能量降低**：该态的总能量低于无调制的金属态，表明它是热力学更稳定的基态。

> 本文遵循“现象观察 → 理论解释 → 计算验证 → 预言展望”的经典理论物理研究范式，其核心论证链条如下：
> 
> 1.  **确立拓扑起源**：运用现代极化理论，作者指出TMDC的晶格极化 **P** 是一个**Z₃拓扑不变量**。在MTB处，极化方向发生反转，导致拓扑不变量突变，从而必然在半导体能隙中产生金属性边界态。
> 2.  **推导电子占位**：由极化突变产生的极化电荷（λ），必须由边界态电子来补偿，以实现电中性。这决定了两条关键边界能带的总电子占据数必须是2/3，导致下能带恰好被1/3填充。
> 3.  **提出不稳定性**：一个被1/3填充的一维金属系统，极易发生一种使周期变为三倍的电子不稳定性。但与传统的佩尔斯（Peierls）相变不同，作者发现该体系不会发生结构畸变。
> 4.  **揭示新机制**：通过引入描述电子间库仑排斥作用的**DFT+U**计算，作者发现体系会自发产生一种**自旋密度波（SDW）**，即电子自旋的周期性排列，并伴随微弱的**电荷密度波（CDW）**。这种纯电子效应使周期变为三倍，并打开能隙，使得系统能量降低，进入绝缘基态。
> 5.  **预言可观测效应**：该基态允许产生携带±1/3 e分数电荷的孤子激发，并提出了通过库仑阻塞实验进行观测的方案。
> 
> **逻辑流程图示意：** _晶格D3h对称性 → 电极化P为Z₃拓扑不变量 → MTB处P反转 → 产生拓扑保护的金属边界态 → 极化电荷λ = 2P·n → 边界态电子必须补偿λ → 边界态能带填充1/3 → 1/3填充的一维系统不稳定 → 电子关联引发SDW/CDW（三倍周期） → 打开能隙，降低能量 → 产生分数电荷±1/3 e的孤子激发_
>

> ## 四、正文核心内容剖析
> 
> ### 4.1 研究背景与现状
> 
> -   **MTBs的普遍性与金属性**：实验发现，无论衬底或终端原子的具体构型，TMDCs的MTBs在室温下总是金属性的[1-4]。这暗示其金属性源于更基本的物理原理，而非材料的偶然细节。
> -   **两种对立的理论解释**：
>     -   **朝永-拉亭格液体（TLL）**：一种强关联一维电子体系，其集体激发表现为自旋和电荷的分离。部分实验支持这一观点[7, 8]。
>     -   **电荷密度波（CDW）**：一种电子密度周期性调制的基态，伴随晶格周期畸变（佩尔斯相变）和能隙打开。另有实验观察到3倍周期的CDW和约0.1 eV的能隙[3, 7]。
> -   **理论计算的困境**：先前的DFT计算虽然预测了金属性，但未能自发发现结构畸变以形成CDW，这使得理论解释与部分实验脱节。
> 
> ### 4.2 方法模型：从拓扑极化的束缚到电子关联的引入
> 
> -   **拓扑极化理论**：作者首先运用现代极化理论，将MTB的金属性归结为**拓扑保护的边界态**。TMDC的晶格极化 **P** 是一个Z₃拓扑不变量，在MTB处，P翻转为-P，导致拓扑不变量发生突变，从而必然在界面处产生闭合体能隙的金属态。
> -   **电子数的精确锁定**：极化翻转导致了极化线电荷λ = 2P·n = (2e)/(3a)。为了维持宏观电中性，MTB必须通过其边界态捕获等量的电子(-λ)。这直接决定了边界态能带的总填充数为2/3电子，即能量较低的一条能带恰好被1/3填充（考虑自旋简并）。
> -   **DFT+U方法**：为了准确描述Mo原子d电子的库仑排斥作用，作者采用了DFT+U方法。U-J参数（有效库仑排斥能）被设置为3 eV，并研究了其对结果的敏感性。这是揭示纯电子SDW/CDW的关键。
> 
> ### 4.3 实验结果与讨论
> 
> -   **纯电子的SDW/CDW**：DFT+U计算揭示了一个关键发现：系统无需任何原子位移，即可通过形成**自旋密度波（SDW）**来降低能量。在MoSe₂中，沿MTB方向的三个Mo原子自旋排列为“0.40, -0.20, -0.21 μB”，导致三倍周期。同时，伴随有微弱的电荷密度调制（CDW）。这解释了为何先前的DFT计算无法找到结构畸变。
> -   **能隙的打开与能量得益**：SDW/CDW态在费米能级处打开了一个约0.26 eV的间接能隙，使系统从金属变为绝缘体。过程中，每个三倍晶胞的能量降低了67 meV。这证实了SDW/CDW是能量更优的基态。
> -   **与实验的对比**：
>     -   **STM观测**：模拟的STM图像（图3c）完美复现了实验中观察到的3×周期调制[3]。
>     -   **能隙大小**：实验观测到的能隙约为0.1 eV。图4显示，这与U-J值约为1-1.5 eV时的计算结果相符，该值对于Mo的4d电子而言是合理的。
>     -   **对TLL的兼容性**：作者并未全盘否定TLL，而是指出SDW/CDW是基态，而TLL可能在高温或不同MTB结构中作为激发态存在。
> 
> ## 五、结论与贡献
> 
> ### 5.1 核心发现
> 
> 本研究通过第一性原理计算，证明了在TMDCs的镜面孪晶界（MTBs）上，电子关联效应会自发诱导出一种独特的**自旋密度波/电荷密度波（SDW/CDW）共存态**，其周期为晶格常数的三倍，并打开一个~0.1 eV的能隙。该过程是纯电子效应，不涉及原子结构畸变。
> 
> ### 5.2 理论贡献
> 
> 1.  **统一了实验争议**：成功调和了TLL与CDW两种看似矛盾的理论。揭示了SDW/CDW是基态，其能隙解释了CDW实验观测，而TLL可能是高温激发态。
> 2.  **建立了拓扑-电子关联的联系**：首次将TMDC的Z₃拓扑极化不变量与边界上的电子关联效应（SDW/CDW）和电子占据数（1/3填充）联系起来，形成了一个完整的物理图像。
> 3.  **提出了新的物态**：预测了一种无需原子位移的纯电子SDW/CDW态，丰富了低维量子系统的物态种类。
> 
> ### 5.3 实践贡献
> 
> 1.  **解释了微观机制**：为通过STM等实验手段观测到的3×周期调制和能隙提供了清晰、自洽的微观理论解释。
> 2.  **预言了可观测的新现象**：预言了边界上可存在携带**±1/3 e分数电荷**的孤子，并为实验验证（如库仑阻塞实验）指明了方向，为未来实现拓扑量子计算等应用提供了潜在的材料平台。
> 
> ## 六、未来发展方向展望
> 
> 1.  **自旋的直接观测**：本文最关键的预言之一是SDW的存在。未来需要通过**自旋极化扫描隧道显微镜（SP-STM）**等实验手段，直接观测MTB上自旋的周期性排列，以最终证实该理论。
> 2.  **分数电荷孤子的验证**：论文预言的携带±1/3 e分数电荷的孤子是其最引人入胜的推论。未来可尝试在弱耦合衬底上制备孤立、有限长度的MTB，并通过库仑阻塞谱等手段探测其分数电荷激发。
> 3.  **动力学与温度效应**：需要建立理论模型，研究SDW/CDW态随温度升高如何融解，以及它是否会转变为一个TLL相。这将为理解不同温度下的实验现象提供动态图像。
> 4.  **材料与缺陷工程**：探索其他具有不同拓扑极化类型的TMDC材料（如WTe₂），或在边界处通过化学掺杂、应力调控等手段，能否实现对SDW/CDW态乃至孤子激发的调控。
> 5.  **器件应用探索**：分数电荷是拓扑量子计算等方案的核心元素。研究如何在器件中利用MTB上的孤子，实现对其电荷和自旋的操控，是一个长远但极具潜力的方向。
> 
> ## 七、学术思考与批判性分析
> 
> 1.  **DFT+U方法的局限性**：论文的核心结论依赖于DFT+U方法。该方法本质上是一种静态平均场近似，对于描述强关联的一维体系固有的量子涨落可能存在不足。U−J值的选取也带有一定经验性。虽然作者论证了结果的鲁棒性，但更精确的量子多体方法（如动力学平均场理论DMFT）的验证将是强有力的支持。
> 2.  **核心论点的逻辑强度**：论文的论证链“极化 → 拓扑边界态 → 1/3填充 → SDW/CDW”极具洞察力。然而，1/3填充导致三倍周期SDW的结论，是否完全排除了其他可能的序（如自旋-电荷分离的TLL）作为基态？论文在论证SDW/CDW是基态时，主要基于能量更低，但对TLL态的能量计算或比较并未直接给出。
> 3.  **与实验的直接对比与潜在偏差**：模拟STM图像与实验吻合是一个强有力的证据。但需注意，LDoS模拟与真实STM图像之间仍存在差异（如针尖效应）。此外，实验中的MTB可能存在缺陷、长度有限等问题，这些因素对SDW/CDW态的影响尚需进一步评估。
> 4.  **孤子激发的理论深度**：关于分数电荷孤子的讨论虽然诱人，但在论文中更多是定性的推测。孤子的形成能、稳定性、有效质量以及相互作用的微观机制，都需要未来更深入的理论工作来阐明。
> 5.  **“三”的征兆---标题的深意**：标题“The Sign of Three”巧妙地借用了福尔摩斯探案集中的一个故事标题，暗示了“三”这个数字在本文中的核心地位：Z₃拓扑不变量、1/3电子填充、三倍周期的SDW/CDW、以及分数电荷±1/3 e。这个标题不仅文学性强，更精准地概括了全文由“三”贯穿始终的物理主线。
> 
> ## 八、用户问题预测与解答
> 
> 1.  **问题：什么是“拓扑不变量”，为什么它会导致金属边界态？** **解答**：您可以想象一个莫比乌斯带。它的“扭”是一个全局性质，无法通过局部拉伸变得和普通纸带一样，这就是一个拓扑不变量。在本文中，TMDC晶格的电极化模式就像一个“Z₃拓扑不变量”，它只能是三种状态之一（比如0、1/3、2/3）。在MTB的左右两侧，材料像是镜像对称的，因此它们的极化状态必须相反（例如从左到右从1/3变为-1/3）。这两种状态就像两片无法平滑连接的、具有不同“扭”的纸带，连接处必然产生一个“奇点”来弥合差异。在材料中，这个“奇点”就是金属性的边界态，它必须存在以闭合体能隙，确保电子在不同拓扑态之间过渡。
>     
> 2.  **问题：既然是被1/3填充，为什么不是形成三倍周期的电荷密度波（CDW），而是自旋密度波（SDW）？** **解答**：这涉及能量最低原则。1/3填充确实会导致三倍周期的不稳定性，但如何实现这种三倍周期，取决于哪种方式能量更低。如果只是电荷密度波（CDW），电子会以“空-空-满”或“满-满-空”的模式排列，但这会迫使电子在空间上配对，产生较大的库仑排斥能。而自旋密度波（SDW）允许电子自旋在三个原子上以“上-下-下”的方式排列。这样，电子电荷分布可以更均匀（CDW很微弱），同时自旋间的交换作用能为系统带来能量增益。DFT+U计算表明，对于Mo原子，这种SDW主导的序结构在能量上更有利，因此是基态。
>

> > 然后第一段： The MX2 monolayer owing to its lack of inversion symmetry, has an in-plane electric polarization. Using the modern theory of polarization [21, 22], it has been shown that the polarization of lattices with D3h symmetry, such as the TMDCs discussed here, is a topological invariant [23]. D3h symmetry only allows for polarizations P = p1a1 + p2a2 with (p1, p2) = (α + n1, β + n2); n1,2 = 0, ±, 1, ±2..., where a1,2 are the lattice vectors of the primitive 2D unit cell, and (α, β) is one out of three possible values: (2/3, 1/3), (1/3, 2/3), or (0, 0) (hence, the topological invariant is Z3). Straightforward DFT calculations show that all our MX2 TMDCs belong to the same class and take on the specific value (α, β) = (2/3, 1/3), see Fig. 1, which is in agreement with previous calculations [22]. Crossing the MTB, the polarization is inverted (P ↔ −P), see Fig. 1. This abrupt jump in the topological invariant causes the semiconducting band gap to close, and gives rise to metallic states [24]. These localised interface states are responsible for compensating the line charge (λ = 2P · nˆ, with nˆ the normal to the MTB) that originates from the polarization [25]. We stress that these are additional electronic gap states created as a result of the abrupt change of the topological invariant at the MTB, and not bulk bands pulled towards the Fermi level, as is sometimes argued to explain the metallic edges of a nanoribbon [22, 25], see Fig. 1. 中文：MX2单层由于缺乏反演对称性，具有面内电极化。利用现代极化理论[21,22]，已证明具有D3h对称性的晶格（如此处讨论的TMDCs）的极化是一个拓扑不变量[23]。D3h对称性仅允许极化P = p1a1 + p2a2，其中(p1, p2) = (α + n1, β + n2); n1,2 = 0, ±1, ±2...，a1,2是原胞的二维晶格矢量，而(α, β)为三个可能值之一：(2/3, 1/3), (1/3, 2/3), 或 (0, 0)（因此，拓扑不变量为Z3）。直接的DFT计算表明，我们所有的MX2 TMDCs都属于同一类，并取特定值(α, β) = (2/3, 1/3)，见图1，与先前计算一致[22]。穿过MTB时，极化发生反转（P ↔ −P），见图1。拓扑不变量的这种突变导致半导体带隙闭合，并产生金属态[24]。这些局域界面态负责补偿源于极化的线电荷（λ = 2P · nˆ，nˆ为MTB的法线）[25]。我们强调，这些是由于MTB处拓扑不变量突变而产生的额外电子隙态，而不是有时用来解释纳米带金属边缘的体带向费米能级拉近的态[22,25]，见图1。 术语解释：**Topological invariant**: 拓扑不变量，描述系统拓扑性质的量，这里指极化取值的离散分类。**Fermi level**: 费米能级，电子占据的最高能级。

> > 第二段： Indeed, similar states are found at edges modelled with a simple tight-binding model based on bulk parameters [24, 26]. A DFT calculation of the band structure of a MoSe2 MTB with the smallest possible periodicity, shows two bands, one partially occupied and the other completely empty, that lie within the band gap, see Fig. 1, in agreement with previous calculations [8]. MTBs of other MX2 give similar band structures. On projecting on the atoms at the MTB, we find that one of these bands has mostly M dxz character (shown in red in Fig. 1), whereas the other band has mostly M dxy/dz2 character (highlighted in green). The occupancy of these MTB states can be deduced from a simple general reasoning. As the polarization jumps from P to −P going across the MTB, the result is a polarization line charge λ = 2P · nˆ = (2e)/(3a) at the MTB (where a is the lattice constant along the MTB). In a system that consists of macroscopic domains separated by MTBs, all of these boundaries have to be neutral, such as to avoid a polarization catastrophe [27]. This means that each MTB must also carry an electronic charge −λ, which compensates for the polarization charge. Such an electronic charge can only be carried by the 1D MTB interface states, located inside the band gap, as discussed above. This means that these particular bands must have a total occupancy of 2/3 electrons. Referring to Fig. 1, this results in the lower of the two bands being 1/3 filled (accounting for spin degeneracy), whereas the upper one is completely empty [28]. 中文：确实，在基于体参数构建的简单紧束缚模型所模拟的边缘处也发现了类似的态[24,26]。对具有最小可能周期性的MoSe2 MTB的能带结构的DFT计算显示，在带隙内存在两条能带，一条部分占据，另一条完全空着，见图1，与先前计算一致[8]。其他MX2的MTBs给出类似的能带结构。通过投影到MTB处的原子上，我们发现其中一条能带主要具有M dxz特征（图1中红色显示），而另一条主要具有M dxy/dz2特征（绿色高亮）。这些MTB态的占据数可以通过简单的普遍推理得出。当极化穿过MTB从P跳变到−P时，在MTB处产生极化线电荷λ = 2P · nˆ = (2e)/(3a)（a为沿MTB的晶格常数）。在由MTBs分隔的宏观畴组成的系统中，所有这些边界都必须是中性的，以避免极化灾难[27]。这意味着每个MTB还必须携带电子电荷−λ来补偿极化电荷。这种电子电荷只能由位于带隙内的上述一维MTB界面态来承载。这意味着这些特定能带的总占据数必须为2/3个电子。参照图1，这导致两条能带中下面那条被填充1/3（考虑自旋简并），而上面那条完全空着[28]。 术语解释：**Tight-binding model**: 紧束缚模型，一种近似计算电子能带的方法。**Spin degeneracy**: 自旋简并，每个能态可容纳两个自旋相反的电子。

> > 中文：自旋密度波/电荷密度波 术语解释：**Spin Density Wave (SDW)**: 自旋密度波，电子自旋密度的周期性调制。**Charge Density Wave (CDW)**: 电荷密度波，电荷密度的周期性调制。

> > 然后第一段： Nevertheless, it is highly unlikely that such 1D metallic states can escape electronic perturbations unscathed. We study the possibility of charge ordering and concomitant spin ordering using DFT+U calculations. The on-site electron-electron Coulomb interaction in 4d transition metal atoms (TMs), such as Mo, is supposed to be weaker than that in 3d TMs, and is thus often assumed to be negligible. Explicit calculations of the screened Hubbard U in TMs [29], and in TM oxides [30], however show that the latter assumption is often not justified, and that a moderate value of U ∼ 3 eV for Mo 4d states is not unreasonable. The states at the 1D grain boundaries have predominant Mo d character, which, because of the 2D surroundings, one may expect to be relatively weakly screened. It is therefore appropriate to include the on-site Coulomb interaction. 中文：然而，这样的一维金属态几乎不可能不受电子微扰的影响。我们使用DFT+U计算研究了电荷有序及伴随的自旋有序的可能性。4d过渡金属原子（如Mo）中的在位电子-电子库仑相互作用被认为弱于3d过渡金属，因此常被假定为可忽略。然而，对过渡金属[29]和过渡金属氧化物[30]中屏蔽Hubbard U的显式计算表明，后一假设常常不成立，对于Mo 4d态，适中的U值约3 eV并非不合理。一维晶界处的态主要具有Mo d特征，由于二维环境，可能预期其屏蔽相对较弱。因此，包含在位库仑相互作用是合适的。 术语解释：**DFT+U**: 密度泛函理论加上Hubbard U修正，用于处理强关联电子体系。**Hubbard U**: 哈伯德U参数，描述同一轨道上两个电子之间的库仑排斥能。

> > 第二段： As a starting point, Fig. 2(a) shows the same MoSe2 band structure as in Fig. 1, but now in a 3× cell, i.e., a cell that is tripled along the direction of the MTB. The Mo dxz band (red) that was 1/3 occupied in the simple unit cell, is now of course folded such, that the lowest branch is completely filled, and the upper two branches are completely empty. The Mo dxy/dz2 band (green) is also folded twice, but its three branches lie above the Fermi level. Using a Hubbard U − J = 3 eV [31], and reoptimizing the electronic structure [32], opens up a gap of ∼ 0.47 eV between the filled and the empty states of the Mo dxz band, as can be observed clearly in Fig. 2(b). The (empty) Mo dxy/dz2 bands change very little. The result is a band structure showing an indirect gap of ∼ 0.26 eV between the occupied Mo dxz band at X, and the unoccupied Mo dxy/dz2 band at Γ. The corresponding DoS, shown in Fig. 2(c), shows this band gap, clearly marked by van Hove singularities characteristic of 1D structures. The emergence of this SDW decreases the total energy of the MTB by 67 meV/3× cell. 中文：作为起点，图2(a)展示了与图1相同的MoSe2能带结构，但现处于3×超胞中，即沿MTB方向三倍化的晶胞。原本在简单原胞中占据1/3的Mo dxz能带（红色）现在折叠，使得最低分支完全填满，上面两个分支完全空着。Mo dxy/dz2能带（绿色）也折叠了两次，但其三个分支均位于费米能级以上。采用Hubbard U − J = 3 eV [31]并重新优化电子结构[32]后，在Mo dxz能带的占据态和空态之间打开了一个约0.47 eV的带隙，这可在图2(b)中清晰观察到。（空的）Mo dxy/dz2能带变化很小。结果能带结构显示，在X点占据的Mo dxz能带与在Γ点未占据的Mo dxy/dz2能带之间有一个约0.26 eV的间接带隙。相应的态密度（DoS）如图2(c)所示，显示了这一带隙，并清晰地标记出一维结构特有的范霍夫奇点。这种SDW的出现使MTB的总能量降低了67 meV/3×胞。 术语解释：**Van Hove singularities**: 范霍夫奇点，态密度中由于能带结构临界点产生的尖峰，在一维体系中尤为显著。

> > 第三段： The origin of this band gap opening lies at the emergence of a SDW localized on the atoms closest to the MTB, which leads to magnetic moments on the three Mo atoms on one side of the MTB of 0.40, −0.20, and −0.21 μB, respectively (the three Mo atoms on the other, the mirrored, side of the MTB have exactly the same magnetic moments). The inequivalence of the three Mo atoms is clearly visible in the spin density shown in Fig. 3(a). This SDW is accompanied by a quite subtle CDW, as shown in the corresponding local density of states (LDoS) in Fig. 3(b), which leads to a tripling of the period as observed in STM [3], compare Fig. 3(c). Although we let the geometry of the MTB free to relax with the SDW/CDW, we observe no visible distortion in the structure. For instance, the bond lengths and bond angles between all atoms along the MTB remain the same. This means that the SDW/CDW is a purely electronic effect, and not a Peierls distortion. 中文：这一带隙打开的根源在于局域在MTB最近邻原子上的SDW的出现，这导致MTB一侧的三个Mo原子分别具有0.40、−0.20和−0.21 μB的磁矩（MTB另一侧镜像位置的三个Mo原子具有完全相同的磁矩）。三个Mo原子的不等效性在图3(a)所示的自旋密度中清晰可见。这一SDW伴随着一个相当微妙的CDW，如图3(b)中相应的局域态密度（LDoS）所示，这导致了在STM中观察到的周期三倍化[3]，对比图3(c)。尽管我们允许MTB的几何结构随着SDW/CDW自由弛豫，但结构中未观察到可见的畸变。例如，沿MTB的所有原子之间的键长和键角保持不变。这意味着SDW/CDW是纯电子效应，而非Peierls畸变。 术语解释：**Local Density of States (LDoS)**: 局域态密度，空间分辨的电子态密度。

> > 第四段： MoS2 behaves similarly to MoSe2; in the 4|4P MTB structure of MoS2 a gap is opened by a SDW/CDW with 3× periodicity. Using U − J = 3 eV, the resulting magnetic moments on the Mo atoms closest to the MTB are 0.25, −0.21, and −0.05 μB. The moments are somewhat smaller than for MoSe2, as is the induced gap at X. The resulting band structures in the gap region of MoS2 and MoSe2 are however quite similar, with MoS2 showing an overall indirect band gap of 0.10 eV between the occupied Mo dxz band at X, and the unoccupied Mo dxy/dz2 band at Γ. The total energy of the MoS2 MTB is decreased by 27 meV per 3× cell., see Supporting Information. 中文：MoS2的行为与MoSe2相似；在MoS2的4|4P MTB结构中，具有3×周期性的SDW/CDW打开了一个带隙。使用U − J = 3 eV，MTB最近邻Mo原子上的磁矩分别为0.25、−0.21和−0.05 μB。这些磁矩比MoSe2的略小，X点处的诱导带隙也较小。然而，MoS2和MoSe2在带隙区域的能带结构相当相似，MoS2在X点占据的Mo dxz能带与Γ点未占据的Mo dxy/dz2能带之间显示出0.10 eV的整体间接带隙。MoS2 MTB的总能量每3×胞降低了27 meV，见支持信息。

> > 第五段： The onsite electron-electron Coulomb interaction is essential for the development of a SDW/CDW, i.e., in a calculation with U − J = 0 it does not happen. Figure 4 shows the size of the band gap, the total energy decrease, as well as the size of the maximal magnetic moment on the Mo atoms at the MTB, as a function of the Hubbard U − J value used in the calculation. It can be observed that both the band gap and the magnetic moments, increase monotonically with increasing U − J, whereas the total energy decreases monotonically. All, however, remain sizeable even for relatively small values of U − J, which indicates the robust presence of a SDW/CDW. Only if U − J becomes smaller than ∼ 0.5 eV, a SDW/CDW fails to develop. 中文：在位电子-电子库仑相互作用对于SDW/CDW的形成至关重要，即在U − J = 0的计算中不会发生。图4展示了带隙大小、总能量降低以及MTB处Mo原子上最大磁矩的大小，作为计算中所用Hubbard U − J值的函数。可以观察到，带隙和磁矩均随U − J的增加而单调增加，而总能量单调降低。然而，即使对于相对较小的U − J值，所有这些量仍保持相当大，这表明SDW/CDW的稳定存在。只有当U − J小于约0.5 eV时，SDW/CDW才不会形成。

> > 第六段： Experimentally, the electronic structure of 4|4P MoSe2 MTBs has been interpreted using STM and STS in terms of CDWs by Barja et al. [3], where they observed the characteristic 3× periodicity. The observed band gap of ∼ 0.1 eV suggest that the effective value of U − J in their case is rather moderate, i.e., in the range 1-1.5 eV. Similarly, these CDWs have been seen in STM by Ma et al., and characterized by means of temperature-dependent conductivity measurements. We suggest that the CDW is accompanied by a SDW, which, although the magnetic moments are moderate, may be observed using spin-polarized STM. Whereas the SDW/CDW should represent the ground state of the MTB, we cannot exclude that at a higher temperature, or for a markedly different MTB structure, electron correlations take over that are typical of 1D TLLs, as argued in Refs. [7, 8]. In the mean time, SDW/CDWs of 3× periodicity allow for interesting soliton excitations, i.e., localized quasiparticles with fractional charges ±1/3 e or ±2/3 e, and spin 1/2, 0, or even an irrational number [35, 36]. Such solitons will occur naturally on MTBs with an overall length that is not a mutiple of 3a, because the boundary conditions at both ends of the MTB introduce frustration in the lattice [37]. In MTBs with lengths that are a multiple of 3a, solitons do not exist in the ground state, but may be introduced by excitation. In particular, depositing TMDCs on substrates with which the electronic coupling is very weak, it may be possible to observe their fractional charges in a Coulomb blockade experiment, using STM, for instance. 中文：实验上，Barja等人[3]利用STM和STS将4|4P MoSe2 MTBs的电子结构解释为CDW，他们观察到了特征性的3×周期性。观测到的约0.1 eV带隙表明，他们情况下的有效U − J值适中，即在1-1.5 eV范围内。类似地，Ma等人通过STM也观察到了这些CDW，并利用温度依赖电导测量进行了表征。我们认为CDW伴随有SDW，尽管磁矩不大，但可能通过自旋极化STM观测到。虽然SDW/CDW应代表MTB的基态，但我们不能排除在更高温度下，或对于显著不同的MTB结构，电子关联会占据主导，呈现典型的一维TLL行为，正如文献[7,8]所述。同时，3×周期性的SDW/CDW允许有趣的孤子激发，即携带分数电荷±1/3 e或±2/3 e以及自旋1/2、0甚至无理数的局域准粒子[35,36]。这些孤子将自然出现在总长度不是3a整数倍的MTBs上，因为MTB两端的边界条件会在晶格中引入阻挫[37]。在长度为3a整数倍的MTBs中，基态下不存在孤子，但可通过激发引入。特别是，将TMDCs沉积在电子耦合非常弱的衬底上，或许可以通过库仑阻塞实验（例如使用STM）观测到它们的分数电荷。 术语解释：**Soliton**: 孤子，这里指携带分数电荷的拓扑激子。**Coulomb blockade**: 库仑阻塞，单电子隧穿效应，可用于测量分数电荷。

> > 然后总结段落： In summary, using DFT+U calculations we have shown that a combined SDW and CDW of triple period arises in MTBs of TMDCs, which open up a band gap of ∼ 0.1 eV in the 1D metallic band structure of a MTB. We argue that the triple period is necessarily the result of the topological invariant of these systems, i.e., the lattice polarization, which leads to metallic states in the 2D band gap, localized at the MTB, with a total occupancy of 1/3. The emergence of a SDW/CDW lifts the metallicity, but it also allows for topological soliton excitations, with charges that are multiples of 1/3 e. 中文：总之，利用DFT+U计算，我们证明在TMDCs的MTBs中会出现三重周期的组合SDW和CDW，它们在MTB的一维金属能带结构中打开了一个约0.1 eV的带隙。我们认为，三重周期必然是这些系统拓扑不变量的结果，即晶格极化导致在二维带隙中产生局域于MTB的金属态，总占据数为1/3。SDW/CDW的出现消除了金属性，但也允许拓扑孤子激发，其电荷为1/3 e的倍数。

> # The Sign of Three: Spin/Charge Density Waves at the Boundaries of Transition Metal Dichalcogenides
> 
> 三之征兆：过渡金属二硫族化物边界处的自旋/电荷密度波
> 
> Sridevi Krishnamurthi and Geert Brocks∗ Computational Materials Science, Faculty of Science and Technology and MESA+ Institute for Nanotechnology, University of Twente, the Netherlands. (Dated: May 7, 2020) 斯里德维·克里希纳穆尔蒂 和 吉尔特·布罗克斯∗，计算材料科学，科学与技术学院及MESA+纳米技术研究所，特温特大学，荷兰。（日期：2020年5月7日）
> 
> arXiv:2005.02519v1 [cond-mat.mtrl-sci] 5 May 2020 arXiv:2005.02519v1 [cond-mat.mtrl-sci] 2020年5月5日
> 
> One-dimensional grain boundaries of two-dimensional semiconducting MX2 (M= Mo,W; X=S,Se) transition metal di-chalcogenides are typically metallic at room temperature. The metallicity has its origin in the lattice polarization, which for these lattices with D3h symmetry is a topological invariant, and leads to one-dimenional boundary states inside the band gap. For boundaries perpendicular to the polarization direction, these states are necessarily 1/3 occupied by electrons or holes, making them susceptible to a metal-insulator transition that triples the translation period. Using density-functional-theory calculations we demonstrate the emergence of combined one-dimensional spin density/charge density waves of that period at the boundary, opening up a small band gap of ∼ 0.1 eV. This unique electronic structure allows for soliton excitations at the boundary that carry a fractional charge of ±1/3 e. 二维半导体MX2（M=Mo,W；X=S,Se）过渡金属二硫族化物的一维晶界通常在室温下呈金属性。这种金属性源于晶格极化，对于具有D3h对称性的晶格，这种极化是一个拓扑不变量，并导致带隙内出现一维边界态。对于垂直于极化方向的边界，这些态必然被电子或空穴占据1/3，使其易于发生使平移周期三倍化的金属-绝缘体转变。通过密度泛函理论计算，我们证明了在边界处出现了该周期的一维自旋密度/电荷密度波组合，打开了一个约0.1 eV的小带隙。这种独特的电子结构允许在边界处产生携带±1/3 e分数电荷的孤子激发。 **Soliton**: 孤子，一种携带分数电荷的局域化激发。

> ## Introduction
> 
> 引言
> 
> The two-dimensional transition metal dichalcogenides (TMDCs) MX2 (M= Mo,W; X=S,Se,Te), in their common H-structure, are semiconductors with band gaps of 1-2 eV. Surprisingly, many edges and grain boundaries of these TMDCs are metallic at room temperature [1]. This seems to be true irrespective of the substrate on which the TMDC is deposited, or whether UHV conditions are used or not [2, 3]. Indeed, the exact atomic termination of edges and boundaries seems to be irrelevant for their metallicity [4]. 二维过渡金属二硫族化物（TMDCs）MX2（M=Mo,W；X=S,Se,Te），在其常见的H相结构中，是带隙为1-2 eV的半导体。令人惊讶的是，这些TMDCs的许多边缘和晶界在室温下呈金属性[1]。这似乎与TMDC沉积的衬底无关，也与是否使用超高真空条件无关[2,3]。实际上，边缘和边界的确切原子终止似乎对其金属性无关紧要[4]。 **Transition Metal Dichalcogenides (TMDCs)**: 过渡金属二硫族化物，通式为MX2的二维层状半导体材料。
> 
> Experimentally, of the different possible TMDC edge configurations possible, mirror twin boundaries (MTBs) have been studied most extensively [2, 3, 5, 6]. MTBs occur spontaneously when TMDC monolayers are grown on isotropic substrates, or on substrates with a high inplane symmetry, such as graphite. In essence, a MTB is formed between two TMDC crystallites that have their crystal growth directions rotated by 60 degrees, thereby forming mirror images of one another along the line of coalescence, see Fig. 1. MTBs are among the most predominant one-dimensional (1D) defects occurring during TMDC growth. 实验上，在可能的不同TMDC边缘构型中，镜像孪晶界（MTBs）被研究得最为广泛[2,3,5,6]。当TMDC单层在各向同性衬底或具有高面内对称性的衬底（如石墨）上生长时，MTBs会自发形成。本质上，MTB形成于两个晶体生长方向旋转60度的TMDC微晶之间，从而沿合并线形成彼此的镜像，见图1。MTBs是TMDC生长过程中最主要的准一维缺陷之一。 **Mirror Twin Boundaries (MTBs)**: 镜像孪晶界，两个镜像对称的晶粒之间的边界，旋转60度形成。
> 
> Whereas the presence of MTBs can be desirable or undesirable from the point of view of applications, the 1D metallic nature of the MTBs makes them interesting from a fundamental perspective. Two distinct views exist on the basic electronic structure of such MTBs. From angle-resolved photoemission (ARPES) results on monolayer MoSe2, the existence of a Tomonaga-L ̈uttinger liquid (TLL) has been put forward [7], which has also been claimed from scanning tunnelling microscopy and spectroscopy (STM, STS) on finite-length MTBs [8]. In contrast, low-temperature STM and STS on the same material demonstrate the presence of a charge density wave (CDW) at the MTB with a wavelength of three lattice constants, opening up a band gap of ∼ 0.1 eV [3, 7]. 从应用的角度来看，MTBs的存在可能是有益的也可能是有害的，但MTBs的一维金属性使其在基础研究方面具有吸引力。关于这类MTBs的基本电子结构，存在两种不同的观点。基于单层MoSe2的角分辨光电子能谱（ARPES）结果，提出了Tomonaga-Luttinger液体（TLL）的存在[7]，有限长度MTBs的扫描隧道显微镜和光谱学（STM, STS）研究也声称如此[8]。相比之下，对同一材料的低温STM和STS研究则表明，在MTB处存在波长为三个晶格常数的电荷密度波（CDW），打开了一个约0.1 eV的带隙[3,7]。 **Tomonaga-Luttinger Liquid (TLL)**: 朝永-拉廷格液体，一维相互作用电子系统的模型，表现出电荷-自旋分离。**Charge Density Wave (CDW)**: 电荷密度波，电荷密度的周期性调制，常伴随晶格畸变。**Angle-Resolved Photoemission Spectroscopy (ARPES)**: 角分辨光电子能谱，一种测量电子能带结构的技术。
> 
> Density-functional-theory (DFT) first-principles studies of MTBs have focused foremost on their atomic structure, and their stability and formation energies [9]. There are several possible MTB structures, but overall the stoichiometric 4|4P structure, shown in Fig. 1, seems to occur most often experimentally [3, 10]. DFT calculations predict this structure to be metallic if a periodicity of one lattice constant along the MTB is assumed. Although it may appear likely that such 1D metallic structures are susceptible to CDW Peierls distortions [3], so far, first-principles calculations have not been able to identify the presence of such structural distortions at MTBs, without resorting to artificial displacements of atoms. 对MTBs的密度泛函理论（DFT）第一性原理研究主要关注其原子结构、稳定性和形成能[9]。有几种可能的MTB结构，但总体上，化学计量比的4|4P结构（如图1所示）似乎在实验中最常见[3,10]。DFT计算预测，如果假设沿MTB的晶格常数周期为1，则该结构为金属性。尽管这类一维金属结构可能容易发生CDW Peierls畸变[3]，但迄今为止，第一性原理计算尚未能在不借助人工原子位移的情况下识别出MTBs处存在这种结构畸变。 **Density Functional Theory (DFT)**: 密度泛函理论，一种求解多电子体系电子结构的量子力学方法。**Peierls distortion**: 派尔斯畸变，一维金属中由于电子-声子耦合导致的结构畸变，使晶格周期加倍并打开能隙。

> ## MTB states
> 
> MTB态
> 
> The MX2 monolayer owing to its lack of inversion symmetry, has an in-plane electric polarization. Using the modern theory of polarization [21, 22], it has been shown that the polarization of lattices with D3h symmetry, such as the TMDCs discussed here, is a topological invariant [23]. D3h symmetry only allows for polarizations P = p1a1 + p2a2 with (p1, p2) = (α + n1, β + n2); n1,2 = 0, ±, 1, ±2..., where a1,2 are the lattice vectors of the primitive 2D unit cell, and (α, β) is one out of three possible values: (2/3, 1/3), (1/3, 2/3), or (0, 0) (hence, the topological invariant is Z3). Straightforward DFT calculations show that all our MX2 TMDCs belong to the same class and take on the specific value (α, β) = (2/3, 1/3), see Fig. 1, which is in agreement with previous calculations [22]. Crossing the MTB, the polarization is inverted (P ↔ −P), see Fig. 1. This abrupt jump in the topological invariant causes the semiconducting band gap to close, and gives rise to metallic states [24]. These localised interface states are responsible for compensating the line charge (λ = 2P · nˆ, with nˆ the normal to the MTB) that originates from the polarization [25]. We stress that these are additional electronic gap states created as a result of the abrupt change of the topological invariant at the MTB, and not bulk bands pulled towards the Fermi level, as is sometimes argued to explain the metallic edges of a nanoribbon [22, 25], see Fig. 1. MX2单层由于缺乏反演对称性，具有面内电极化。利用现代极化理论[21,22]，已证明具有D3h对称性的晶格（如此处讨论的TMDCs）的极化是一个拓扑不变量[23]。D3h对称性仅允许极化P = p1a1 + p2a2，其中(p1, p2) = (α + n1, β + n2); n1,2 = 0, ±1, ±2...，a1,2是原胞的二维晶格矢量，而(α, β)为三个可能值之一：(2/3, 1/3), (1/3, 2/3), 或 (0, 0)（因此，拓扑不变量为Z3）。直接的DFT计算表明，我们所有的MX2 TMDCs都属于同一类，并取特定值(α, β) = (2/3, 1/3)，见图1，与先前计算一致[22]。穿过MTB时，极化发生反转（P ↔ −P），见图1。拓扑不变量的这种突变导致半导体带隙闭合，并产生金属态[24]。这些局域界面态负责补偿源于极化的线电荷（λ = 2P · nˆ，nˆ为MTB的法线）[25]。我们强调，这些是由于MTB处拓扑不变量突变而产生的额外电子隙态，而不是有时用来解释纳米带金属边缘的体带向费米能级拉近的态[22,25]，见图1。 **Topological invariant**: 拓扑不变量，描述系统拓扑性质的量，这里指极化取值的离散分类。**Fermi level**: 费米能级，电子占据的最高能级。
> 
> Indeed, similar states are found at edges modelled with a simple tight-binding model based on bulk parameters [24, 26]. A DFT calculation of the band structure of a MoSe2 MTB with the smallest possible periodicity, shows two bands, one partially occupied and the other completely empty, that lie within the band gap, see Fig. 1, in agreement with previous calculations [8]. MTBs of other MX2 give similar band structures. On projecting on the atoms at the MTB, we find that one of these bands has mostly M dxz character (shown in red in Fig. 1), whereas the other band has mostly M dxy/dz2 character (highlighted in green). The occupancy of these MTB states can be deduced from a simple general reasoning. As the polarization jumps from P to −P going across the MTB, the result is a polarization line charge λ = 2P · nˆ = (2e)/(3a) at the MTB (where a is the lattice constant along the MTB). In a system that consists of macroscopic domains separated by MTBs, all of these boundaries have to be neutral, such as to avoid a polarization catastrophe [27]. This means that each MTB must also carry an electronic charge −λ, which compensates for the polarization charge. Such an electronic charge can only be carried by the 1D MTB interface states, located inside the band gap, as discussed above. This means that these particular bands must have a total occupancy of 2/3 electrons. Referring to Fig. 1, this results in the lower of the two bands being 1/3 filled (accounting for spin degeneracy), whereas the upper one is completely empty [28]. 确实，在基于体参数构建的简单紧束缚模型所模拟的边缘处也发现了类似的态[24,26]。对具有最小可能周期性的MoSe2 MTB的能带结构的DFT计算显示，在带隙内存在两条能带，一条部分占据，另一条完全空着，见图1，与先前计算一致[8]。其他MX2的MTBs给出类似的能带结构。通过投影到MTB处的原子上，我们发现其中一条能带主要具有M dxz特征（图1中红色显示），而另一条主要具有M dxy/dz2特征（绿色高亮）。这些MTB态的占据数可以通过简单的普遍推理得出。当极化穿过MTB从P跳变到−P时，在MTB处产生极化线电荷λ = 2P · nˆ = (2e)/(3a)（a为沿MTB的晶格常数）。在由MTBs分隔的宏观畴组成的系统中，所有这些边界都必须是中性的，以避免极化灾难[27]。这意味着每个MTB还必须携带电子电荷−λ来补偿极化电荷。这种电子电荷只能由位于带隙内的上述一维MTB界面态来承载。这意味着这些特定能带的总占据数必须为2/3个电子。参照图1，这导致两条能带中下面那条被填充1/3（考虑自旋简并），而上面那条完全空着[28]。 **Tight-binding model**: 紧束缚模型，一种近似计算电子能带的方法。**Spin degeneracy**: 自旋简并，每个能态可容纳两个自旋相反的电子。
> 
> Given their 1D character and the partial occupancy of 1/3, these metallic states might then be susceptible to a Peierls-type structural distortion that leads to a tripling of the period. However, like previous calculations, our DFT calculations do not give such a spontaneous structural distortion of the MTB [3, 8]. 鉴于它们的一维特性和1/3的部分占据，这些金属态可能容易发生导致周期三倍化的Peierls型结构畸变。然而，与先前的计算一样，我们的DFT计算并未给出MTB的这种自发结构畸变[3,8]。
> 
> ## SDW/CDWs
> 
> 自旋密度波/电荷密度波 **Spin Density Wave (SDW)**: 自旋密度波，电子自旋密度的周期性调制。**Charge Density Wave (CDW)**: 电荷密度波，电荷密度的周期性调制。
> 
> Nevertheless, it is highly unlikely that such 1D metallic states can escape electronic perturbations unscathed. We study the possibility of charge ordering and concomitant spin ordering using DFT+U calculations. The on-site electron-electron Coulomb interaction in 4d transition metal atoms (TMs), such as Mo, is supposed to be weaker than that in 3d TMs, and is thus often assumed to be negligible. Explicit calculations of the screened Hubbard U in TMs [29], and in TM oxides [30], however show that the latter assumption is often not justified, and that a moderate value of U ∼ 3 eV for Mo 4d states is not unreasonable. The states at the 1D grain boundaries have predominant Mo d character, which, because of the 2D surroundings, one may expect to be relatively weakly screened. It is therefore appropriate to include the on-site Coulomb interaction. 然而，这样的一维金属态几乎不可能不受电子微扰的影响。我们使用DFT+U计算研究了电荷有序及伴随的自旋有序的可能性。4d过渡金属原子（如Mo）中的在位电子-电子库仑相互作用被认为弱于3d过渡金属，因此常被假定为可忽略。然而，对过渡金属[29]和过渡金属氧化物[30]中屏蔽Hubbard U的显式计算表明，后一假设常常不成立，对于Mo 4d态，适中的U值约3 eV并非不合理。一维晶界处的态主要具有Mo d特征，由于二维环境，可能预期其屏蔽相对较弱。因此，包含在位库仑相互作用是合适的。 **DFT+U**: 密度泛函理论加上Hubbard U修正，用于处理强关联电子体系。**Hubbard U**: 哈伯德U参数，描述同一轨道上两个电子之间的库仑排斥能。
> 
> As a starting point, Fig. 2(a) shows the same MoSe2 band structure as in Fig. 1, but now in a 3× cell, i.e., a cell that is tripled along the direction of the MTB. The Mo dxz band (red) that was 1/3 occupied in the simple unit cell, is now of course folded such, that the lowest branch is completely filled, and the upper two branches are completely empty. The Mo dxy/dz2 band (green) is also folded twice, but its three branches lie above the Fermi level. Using a Hubbard U − J = 3 eV [31], and reoptimizing the electronic structure [32], opens up a gap of ∼ 0.47 eV between the filled and the empty states of the Mo dxz band, as can be observed clearly in Fig. 2(b). The (empty) Mo dxy/dz2 bands change very little. The result is a band structure showing an indirect gap of ∼ 0.26 eV between the occupied Mo dxz band at X, and the unoccupied Mo dxy/dz2 band at Γ. The corresponding DoS, shown in Fig. 2(c), shows this band gap, clearly marked by van Hove singularities characteristic of 1D structures. The emergence of this SDW decreases the total energy of the MTB by 67 meV/3× cell. 作为起点，图2(a)展示了与图1相同的MoSe2能带结构，但现处于3×超胞中，即沿MTB方向三倍化的晶胞。原本在简单原胞中占据1/3的Mo dxz能带（红色）现在折叠，使得最低分支完全填满，上面两个分支完全空着。Mo dxy/dz2能带（绿色）也折叠了两次，但其三个分支均位于费米能级以上。采用Hubbard U − J = 3 eV [31]并重新优化电子结构[32]后，在Mo dxz能带的占据态和空态之间打开了一个约0.47 eV的带隙，这可在图2(b)中清晰观察到。（空的）Mo dxy/dz2能带变化很小。结果能带结构显示，在X点占据的Mo dxz能带与在Γ点未占据的Mo dxy/dz2能带之间有一个约0.26 eV的间接带隙。相应的态密度（DoS）如图2(c)所示，显示了这一带隙，并清晰地标记出一维结构特有的范霍夫奇点。这种SDW的出现使MTB的总能量降低了67 meV/3×胞。 **Van Hove singularities**: 范霍夫奇点，态密度中由于能带结构临界点产生的尖峰，在一维体系中尤为显著。
> 
> The origin of this band gap opening lies at the emergence of a SDW localized on the atoms closest to the MTB, which leads to magnetic moments on the three Mo atoms on one side of the MTB of 0.40, −0.20, and −0.21 μB, respectively (the three Mo atoms on the other, the mirrored, side of the MTB have exactly the same magnetic moments). The inequivalence of the three Mo atoms is clearly visible in the spin density shown in Fig. 3(a). This SDW is accompanied by a quite subtle CDW, as shown in the corresponding local density of states (LDoS) in Fig. 3(b), which leads to a tripling of the period as observed in STM [3], compare Fig. 3(c). Although we let the geometry of the MTB free to relax with the SDW/CDW, we observe no visible distortion in the structure. For instance, the bond lengths and bond angles between all atoms along the MTB remain the same. This means that the SDW/CDW is a purely electronic effect, and not a Peierls distortion. 这一带隙打开的根源在于局域在MTB最近邻原子上的SDW的出现，这导致MTB一侧的三个Mo原子分别具有0.40、−0.20和−0.21 μB的磁矩（MTB另一侧镜像位置的三个Mo原子具有完全相同的磁矩）。三个Mo原子的不等效性在图3(a)所示的自旋密度中清晰可见。这一SDW伴随着一个相当微妙的CDW，如图3(b)中相应的局域态密度（LDoS）所示，这导致了在STM中观察到的周期三倍化[3]，对比图3(c)。尽管我们允许MTB的几何结构随着SDW/CDW自由弛豫，但结构中未观察到可见的畸变。例如，沿MTB的所有原子之间的键长和键角保持不变。这意味着SDW/CDW是纯电子效应，而非Peierls畸变。 **Local Density of States (LDoS)**: 局域态密度，空间分辨的电子态密度。
> 
> MoS2 behaves similarly to MoSe2; in the 4|4P MTB structure of MoS2 a gap is opened by a SDW/CDW with 3× periodicity. Using U − J = 3 eV, the resulting magnetic moments on the Mo atoms closest to the MTB are 0.25, −0.21, and −0.05 μB. The moments are somewhat smaller than for MoSe2, as is the induced gap at X. The resulting band structures in the gap region of MoS2 and MoSe2 are however quite similar, with MoS2 showing an overall indirect band gap of 0.10 eV between the occupied Mo dxz band at X, and the unoccupied Mo dxy/dz2 band at Γ. The total energy of the MoS2 MTB is decreased by 27 meV per 3× cell., see Supporting Information. MoS2的行为与MoSe2相似；在MoS2的4|4P MTB结构中，具有3×周期性的SDW/CDW打开了一个带隙。使用U − J = 3 eV，MTB最近邻Mo原子上的磁矩分别为0.25、−0.21和−0.05 μB。这些磁矩比MoSe2的略小，X点处的诱导带隙也较小。然而，MoS2和MoSe2在带隙区域的能带结构相当相似，MoS2在X点占据的Mo dxz能带与Γ点未占据的Mo dxy/dz2能带之间显示出0.10 eV的整体间接带隙。MoS2 MTB的总能量每3×胞降低了27 meV，见支持信息。
> 
> The onsite electron-electron Coulomb interaction is essential for the development of a SDW/CDW, i.e., in a calculation with U − J = 0 it does not happen. Figure 4 shows the size of the band gap, the total energy decrease, as well as the size of the maximal magnetic moment on the Mo atoms at the MTB, as a function of the Hubbard U − J value used in the calculation. It can be observed that both the band gap and the magnetic moments, increase monotonically with increasing U − J, whereas the total energy decreases monotonically. All, however, remain sizeable even for relatively small values of U − J, which indicates the robust presence of a SDW/CDW. Only if U − J becomes smaller than ∼ 0.5 eV, a SDW/CDW fails to develop. 在位电子-电子库仑相互作用对于SDW/CDW的形成至关重要，即在U − J = 0的计算中不会发生。图4展示了带隙大小、总能量降低以及MTB处Mo原子上最大磁矩的大小，作为计算中所用Hubbard U − J值的函数。可以观察到，带隙和磁矩均随U − J的增加而单调增加，而总能量单调降低。然而，即使对于相对较小的U − J值，所有这些量仍保持相当大，这表明SDW/CDW的稳定存在。只有当U − J小于约0.5 eV时，SDW/CDW才不会形成。
> 
> Experimentally, the electronic structure of 4|4P MoSe2 MTBs has been interpreted using STM and STS in terms of CDWs by Barja et al. [3], where they observed the characteristic 3× periodicity. The observed band gap of ∼ 0.1 eV suggest that the effective value of U − J in their case is rather moderate, i.e., in the range 1-1.5 eV. Similarly, these CDWs have been seen in STM by Ma et al., and characterized by means of temperature-dependent conductivity measurements. We suggest that the CDW is accompanied by a SDW, which, although the magnetic moments are moderate, may be observed using spin-polarized STM. Whereas the SDW/CDW should represent the ground state of the MTB, we cannot exclude that at a higher temperature, or for a markedly different MTB structure, electron correlations take over that are typical of 1D TLLs, as argued in Refs. [7, 8]. In the mean time, SDW/CDWs of 3× periodicity allow for interesting soliton excitations, i.e., localized quasiparticles with fractional charges ±1/3 e or ±2/3 e, and spin 1/2, 0, or even an irrational number [35, 36]. Such solitons will occur naturally on MTBs with an overall length that is not a mutiple of 3a, because the boundary conditions at both ends of the MTB introduce frustration in the lattice [37]. In MTBs with lengths that are a multiple of 3a, solitons do not exist in the ground state, but may be introduced by excitation. In particular, depositing TMDCs on substrates with which the electronic coupling is very weak, it may be possible to observe their fractional charges in a Coulomb blockade experiment, using STM, for instance. 实验上，Barja等人[3]利用STM和STS将4|4P MoSe2 MTBs的电子结构解释为CDW，他们观察到了特征性的3×周期性。观测到的约0.1 eV带隙表明，他们情况下的有效U − J值适中，即在1-1.5 eV范围内。类似地，Ma等人通过STM也观察到了这些CDW，并利用温度依赖电导测量进行了表征。我们认为CDW伴随有SDW，尽管磁矩不大，但可能通过自旋极化STM观测到。虽然SDW/CDW应代表MTB的基态，但我们不能排除在更高温度下，或对于显著不同的MTB结构，电子关联会占据主导，呈现典型的一维TLL行为，正如文献[7,8]所述。同时，3×周期性的SDW/CDW允许有趣的孤子激发，即携带分数电荷±1/3 e或±2/3 e以及自旋1/2、0甚至无理数的局域准粒子[35,36]。这些孤子将自然出现在总长度不是3a整数倍的MTBs上，因为MTB两端的边界条件会在晶格中引入阻挫[37]。在长度为3a整数倍的MTBs中，基态下不存在孤子，但可通过激发引入。特别是，将TMDCs沉积在电子耦合非常弱的衬底上，或许可以通过库仑阻塞实验（例如使用STM）观测到它们的分数电荷。 **Coulomb blockade**: 库仑阻塞，单电子隧穿效应，可用于测量分数电荷。
> 
> In summary, using DFT+U calculations we have shown that a combined SDW and CDW of triple period arises in MTBs of TMDCs, which open up a band gap of ∼ 0.1 eV in the 1D metallic band structure of a MTB. We argue that the triple period is necessarily the result of the topological invariant of these systems, i.e., the lattice polarization, which leads to metallic states in the 2D band gap, localized at the MTB, with a total occupancy of 1/3. The emergence of a SDW/CDW lifts the metallicity, but it also allows for topological soliton excitations, with charges that are multiples of 1/3 e. 总之，利用DFT+U计算，我们证明在TMDCs的MTBs中会出现三重周期的组合SDW和CDW，它们在MTB的一维金属能带结构中打开了一个约0.1 eV的带隙。我们认为，三重周期必然是这些系统拓扑不变量的结果，即晶格极化导致在二维带隙中产生局域于MTB的金属态，总占据数为1/3。SDW/CDW的出现消除了金属性，但也允许拓扑孤子激发，其电荷为1/3 e的倍数。

> 
> **1. 研究的是个啥东西？** 这群科学家研究的是这张“原子网”上的一种常见缺陷，叫做“镜像孪晶界”。这名字听着玄乎，其实道理很简单。
> 
> 您看，这张网不是一整块完美无缺的，它是由很多个小块儿（叫“晶畴”）像拼图一样拼起来的。有时候，两个小块儿拼接的时候，一个没对准，转了个60度的角，就像您照镜子，镜子里的您和镜子外的您，动作一样，但方向是反的。这两个小块儿拼接的那条缝，就是“镜像孪晶界”，咱们就管它叫“拼接缝”或“晶界”吧。
> 
> **2. 这条缝有什么特别？** 科学家们发现，这张“原子网”本身是不导电的（像橡胶），但这条“拼接缝”在室温下竟然是导电的（像铜丝）！这就好比一张橡胶薄膜上，天然长出了一根根比头发丝还细无数倍的铜线，非常神奇。
> 
> **3. 为什么会导电？——“极化”的秘密** 这就引出了第一个关键概念：**极化**。
> 
> 您可以把这张“原子网”想象成一块特殊的地板砖，它有个“上头”和“下头”，或者说，它内部的电荷分布天生就是一头正一头负，这个方向性就是“极化”。这个极化方向是固定的，是个天生的性质，所以叫“拓扑不变量”，您就理解成“胎里带的、改不了的天然属性”。
>
