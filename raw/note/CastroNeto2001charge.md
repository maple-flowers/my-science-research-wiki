> # arXiv:cond-mat/0012147v1 [cond-mat.str-el] 8 Dec 2000

> # CDW, Superconductivity and Anomalous Metallic Behavior in 2D Transition Metal Dichalcogenides
> 
> # 二维过渡金属二硫化物中的CDW、超导和反常金属行为
> 
> **CDW**: 电荷密度波（Charge Density Wave），一种电子密度在空间上周期性调制的有序态。 **Transition Metal Dichalcogenides (TMD)**: 过渡金属二硫化物，层状化合物，通式为MX₂，其中M为过渡金属，X为硫族元素。
> 
> A. H. Castro Neto∗ Department of Physics, Boston University, Boston, MA 02215 (July 24, 2013) A. H. Castro Neto∗ 波士顿大学物理系，波士顿，MA 02215（2013年7月24日） （∗注：休假中，原单位：加州大学河滨分校物理系，CA 92521）
> 
> We propose a theory for quasi-two-dimensional transition metal dichalcogenides that provides a unified microscopic picture of the charge density wave (CDW) and superconducting phases. We show, based on the electron-phonon coupling and Fermi surface topology, that a CDW order parameter with six-fold symmetry and nodes (f-wave) gives a consistent description of the available experimental data. The elementary excitations in the CDW phase are Dirac electrons. The superconducting state has its origin on the attractive interaction mediated by phonons. The theory predicts strong deviations from Fermi liquid theory in the CDW phase. 我们提出了一个针对准二维过渡金属二硫化物的理论，为电荷密度波（CDW）和超导相提供了统一的微观图像。我们基于电子-声子耦合和费米面拓扑表明，具有六重对称性和节点的CDW序参量（f波）能够一致地描述现有实验数据。CDW相中的元激发为狄拉克电子。超导态源于声子介导的吸引相互作用。该理论预言CDW相中存在对费米液体理论的强烈偏离。 **Dirac electrons**: 狄拉克电子，其低能激发满足狄拉克方程，具有线性色散关系。 **Fermi liquid theory**: 费米液体理论，描述相互作用费米子体系的唯象理论，元激发为准粒子。
> 
> PACS numbers: 74.70.A, 71.45.Lr, 71.10.-w PACS编号：74.70.A, 71.45.Lr, 71.10.-w
> 
> The quasi-two-dimensional (2D) transition metal dichalcogenides (TMD), 2H-TaSe2, 2H-TaS2, 2H-NbSe2 and 2H-NbS2 have been extensively studied since their discovery [1]. These are layered systems with a phase diagram where CDW order co-exists with superconductivity at low temperatures (see Fig.1). Many physical properties such as resistivity, thermal expansion, sound velocity and so on, are very anisotropic and differ by orders of magnitude if measured parallel or perpendicular to the metallic planes [2,3]. Neutron scattering studies have shown that these systems undergo a triple incommensurate CDW transition at a temperature Tcdw with wave-vectors Qi = (1 − δ)bi/3 where bi are the three reciprocal lattice vectors for a triangular lattice with lattice spacing a (|bi| = 4π/(√3a)) and δ ≪ 1 is the incommensurability (TaSe2 also has a transition into a commensurate state, δ = 0) [4]. 准二维（2D）过渡金属二硫化物（TMD），包括2H-TaSe2、2H-TaS2、2H-NbSe2和2H-NbS2，自发现以来已被广泛研究[1]。这些层状体系的相图中，CDW有序与超导在低温下共存（见图1）。许多物理性质，如电阻率、热膨胀、声速等，表现出强烈的各向异性，平行或垂直于金属平面测量时数值相差数个量级[2,3]。中子散射研究表明，这些体系在温度Tcdw处经历一个三重非公度CDW转变，波矢为Qi = (1 − δ)bi/3，其中bi是三角晶格的三个倒格矢，晶格常数为a（|bi| = 4π/(√3a)），δ ≪ 1为非公度度（TaSe2还有一个进入公度态δ = 0的转变）[4]。 **Incommensurate CDW**: 非公度电荷密度波，其调制周期与晶格周期之比为无理数。
> 
> Although the phenomenological description of the CDW transition is well understood due to the work of McMillan [5] the current understanding of the microscopic origin of the CDW state is not complete. Fermi surface nesting, which is the main mechanism for CDW formation in 1D systems (such as 1T-TaSe2 [3]) leads to a lattice deformation with momentum 2kF (where kF is the Fermi momentum), to the gapping of the Fermi surface and therefore to insulating behavior. Band structure calculations (BSC) [1,6] do not show, however, strong nesting at the Fermi surface of TMD. Moreover, TMD become better metals (with a resistivity drop [3]) inside of the CDW phase and the specific heat behaves like a power of the temperature, T , (instead of exponentially as in the case of an 1D CDW transition). What is more striking is that TMD become superconductors at low temperatures. An alternative scenario was proposed by Rice and Scott (RS) [7] based on earlier BSC [8] in which electron scattering with momentum QSP between different saddle-points (which produce a logarithmically divergent density of states) would drive the system into the CDW phase. The advantages of this picture are clear: it does not require nesting, leads to a gap at the saddle points and to the reduction of the resistivity. More refined BSC have shown that the saddle points are not as close to the Fermi surface as initially believed [6] (see Fig.2). 尽管由于McMillan的工作[5]，CDW转变的唯象描述已很清晰，但当前对CDW态微观起源的理解仍不完整。费米面嵌套是一维体系（如1T-TaSe2 [3]）中CDW形成的主要机制，它导致动量为2kF（kF为费米动量）的晶格畸变，使费米面打开能隙，从而产生绝缘行为。然而，能带结构计算（BSC）[1,6]并未显示TMD的费米面存在强嵌套。此外，TMD在CDW相内反而成为更好的金属（电阻率下降[3]），且比热表现为温度的幂律行为（而非一维CDW转变中的指数行为）。更令人惊讶的是，TMD在低温下成为超导体。Rice和Scott（RS）[7]基于早期BSC [8]提出了另一种图像：具有动量QSP的电子在不同鞍点（产生对数发散的态密度）之间的散射会驱动体系进入CDW相。该图像的优点很明显：无需嵌套，在鞍点处打开能隙，并导致电阻率降低。更精细的BSC表明，鞍点并不像最初认为的那样接近费米面[6]（见图2）。 **Fermi surface nesting**: 费米面嵌套，费米面不同部分可通过某波矢平移重合，导致电子-空穴对不稳定性。 **Saddle points**: 鞍点，能带中能量梯度为零但非极值点，态密度在此处发散。
> 
> With the advent of high temperature superconductors (HTC) many experimental techniques have been perfected. Among them, angle resolved photoemission (ARPES) has nowadays momentum and energy resolution that was unavailable when TMD were discovered. ARPES measures the electronic spectral function, the loci of the Fermi surface, and the momentum, k, and frequency, ω, dependent electron self-energy, Σ(k, ω). The imaginary part of Σ(k, ω) provides a direct measurement of the electron lifetime and for a Fermi liquid behaves like I[Σ(kF , ω → 0)] ∝ 1/τ0 + b ω2/EF , where τ0 is the impurity scattering lifetime, EF is the Fermi energy and b is a constant (we use units such that ħ = kB = 1). Recent measurements of Σ(k, ω) for HTC [9] have shown that I[Σ(kF , ω)] ∝ |ω| in clear disagreement with Fermi liquid theory but in agreement with the marginal Fermi liquid description [10]. Stimulated by the recent developments in ARPES, TMD have been extensively studied in the last two years. As a result of these studies a contradictory picture of the origin of the CDW state has emerged. Although there are indications for the opening of a CDW gap along the saddle point direction (but in no other place along the Fermi surface) [11], the measured |QSP | is larger than 2kF and |Qi| (see Fig.2) [12]. Furthermore, ARPES measurements of the electronic selfenergy have shown that, analogously to the case of HTC, I[Σ(kF , ω → 0)] ∝ 1/τ + b |ω| [13]. These measurements clearly show that the current theories for CDW formation cannot describe the current experimental situation. 随着高温超导体（HTC）的出现，许多实验技术得到了完善。其中，角分辨光电子能谱（ARPES）如今具有的动量和能量分辨率在TMD被发现时是无法获得的。ARPES测量电子谱函数、费米面的位置以及依赖于动量k和频率ω的电子自能Σ(k, ω)。Σ(k, ω)的虚部直接反映电子寿命，对于费米液体，其行为为I[Σ(kF , ω → 0)] ∝ 1/τ0 + b ω²/EF，其中τ0为杂质散射寿命，EF为费米能量，b为常数（我们采用ħ = kB = 1的单位制）。最近对HTC的Σ(k, ω)测量[9]显示I[Σ(kF , ω)] ∝ |ω|，与费米液体理论明显不符，但与边缘费米液体描述一致[10]。受ARPES最新进展的激励，TMD在过去两年中得到了广泛研究。这些研究的结果是，关于CDW态起源出现了矛盾的图像。尽管有迹象表明沿鞍点方向（而非费米面的其他位置）打开了CDW能隙[11]，但测得的|QSP|大于2kF和|Qi|（见图2）[12]。此外，电子自能的ARPES测量显示，与HTC类似，I[Σ(kF , ω → 0)] ∝ 1/τ + b |ω| [13]。这些测量清楚地表明，当前关于CDW形成的理论无法描述现有的实验情况。 **High temperature superconductors (HTC)**: 高温超导体，通常指临界温度高于液氮温度（77 K）的超导材料。 **ARPES**: 角分辨光电子能谱（Angle Resolved Photoemission Spectroscopy），通过测量光电子发射角度和能量来探测电子结构。 **Marginal Fermi liquid**: 边缘费米液体，一种非费米液体理论，其自能虚部与频率的绝对值成正比，而非费米液体中的平方关系。
> 
> We propose that, due to strong variations in the electron-phonon coupling and to imperfect nesting of the Fermi surface, the CDW gap is six-fold symmetric and has nodes (f-wave). This f-wave state has lobes (the largest value of the CDW gap, M ax[|∆cdw(k)|]) along the saddle point direction. This effect leads to the reduction of the electron-phonon scattering in the same way as the RS mechanism. The low-lying excitations in the CDW state are Dirac electrons associate with the nesting between different branches (around the Γ and K points) of the Fermi surface which are connected by Qi. Thus, the system remains a good metal in the CDW phase. Due to the loss of lattice inversion symmetry in the CDW phase, the Dirac electrons are coupled via a piezo-electric coupling to acoustic phonons. This coupling produces a damping of the Dirac electrons and leads to a Σ(k, ω) of the form (T ≪ Tcdw):
> 
> I[Σ(kF , ω → 0)] = τ −1 0 + α T + γ |ω| (1)
> 
> for ω < M ax[|∆cdw|] and I[Σ(kF , ω → 0)] = 1/τ1, constant, for M ax[|∆cdw|] < ω. We show that the data is in good agreement with these results. Finally, we argue that phonons drive the system to a superconducting state via a Kosterlitz-Thouless (KT) phase transition. We propose that critical fluctuations of the superconducting order parameter, ∆s, lead to a pseudo-gap behavior above the transition temperature, Tc, to a drop of the magnetic susceptibility, and to diamagnetic response above Tc (as seen experimentally [14]). 我们提出，由于电子-声子耦合的强烈变化和费米面的不完全嵌套，CDW能隙具有六重对称性并存在节点（f波）。该f波态的瓣（CDW能隙最大值Max[|Δcdw(k)|]）沿鞍点方向。这一效应以与RS机制相同的方式导致电子-声子散射的减弱。CDW态中的低能激发是与费米面不同分支（Γ点和K点附近）之间由Qi连接的嵌套相关的狄拉克电子。因此，体系在CDW相中仍为良好金属。由于CDW相中晶格反演对称性的破缺，狄拉克电子通过压电耦合与声学声子相互作用。该耦合导致狄拉克电子的阻尼，并给出如下形式的Σ(k, ω)（T ≪ Tcdw）：
> 
> I[Σ(kF , ω → 0)] = τ₀⁻¹ + α T + γ |ω| (1)
>

> Unlike ordinary metals, the electron-phonon coupling, gλ(k, q), (where q = k′ − k is the momentum transfer for an electron being scattered by a phonon from k to k′ and λ is the phonon polarization) in transition metals is highly dependent on the electron momentum. This dependence is responsible for many anomalies observed in the phonon spectrum in these systems [15]. Numerical studies have shown that gλ(k, q) can vanish at certain points in the Brillouin zone [16]. A simple tight-binding calculation gives,
> 
> gλ(k, q) = √2/(N M ωλ,q) ∑ (c|n| eλ,q · n sin(q · n/2) × cos[(k + q/2) · n]) , (2)
> 
> where N is the number of atoms, M is the atom mass, ωλ,q is the phonon frequency, eλ,q the polarization vector (e*λ,-q = eλ,q), n the nearest neighbor vector and c|n| depends only on |n|. It is easy to see that gλ(k, Q1) vanishes along the nodal lines in Fig.2. BSC (and ARPES measurements) show that the Fermi surface is hole-like, centered around the Γ and K points, and intercepts the nodal lines at the Dirac points (see Fig.2). At these points the CDW order parameter vanishes since ∆cdw(k) ∝ gλ(k, Qi). Observe that Q1 connects nodal points located at different branches of the Fermi surface. Moreover, as shown by numerical calculations [6] these points are well nested and a CDW state can emerge. In a triple CDW phase, however, the situation is more complicated because Q2 connects the nodal points in the same branch of the Fermi surface. This would imply a finite coupling at these points and the opening of a gap there as well. We observe, however, that in this direction the Fermi surface is not nested [6] in contrast with the case discussed above (these details are not shown in Fig.2). Thus, a gap cannot open at the Dirac points. The conclusion is that the CDW gap will change sign in six points along the Fermi surface leading to the situation depicted on Fig.2. > Besides leading to damping, the phonons generate a retarded interaction. This retarded interaction, like in an ordinary superconductor, leads to pairing in the singlet channel. After tracing out the phonons the pairing Hamiltonian becomes:
>

> 
> where g ≈ κ²/ωD is the coupling constant (ωD ≈ csΛ, with Λ ≈ 1/a, is a Debye frequency). At the mean-field level the pairing Hamiltonian is written as
> 
> HP = ∑ k,a,b (σy a,b Δs ψ†a,↑(k) ψ†b,↓(−k) + h.c.) (8)
> 
> where
> 
> Δs = −g ∑ k,a,b σy a,b 〈ψa,↑(k) ψb,↓(−k)〉 (9)
> 
> is the superconducting order parameter. The problem described by (4) and (8) reduces to the diagonalization of a 4 × 4 matrix via a Bogoliubov transformation. >

>

>

> 
> which shows that superconductivity is only possible for g > gc at T = 0. Thus, g = gc is a quantum critical point (QCP) and gc is the critical coupling constant. Notice that the dependence of the order parameter with the coupling constant is very different from the BCS expression (which does not require a critical coupling constant). This transition, as was noted earlier [20], can be associated with the spontaneous chiral symmetry breaking in the Yukawa-Higgs model. In our case, however, the order parameter is complex implying that there is an extra U(1) symmetry associated with the phase of the order parameter. > As we have shown there are many similarities between the problem discussed here and HTC. Perhaps one of the most interesting is the anti-correlation between the Tcdw and Tc. This is the same type of anti-correlation observed between the pseudo-gap energy scale and Tc in the case of HTC [22]. The main difference, of course, is that at Tcdw there is a true second order phase transition while the pseudo-gap temperature seems to be a crossover energy scale. In fact, the metallic state described here is similar in many respects to the nodal liquid description of HTC [23]. However, the MTD Dirac fermions are not related to superconductivity but to a CDW state. In TMD the anomalous metallic behavior is due to the coupling to acoustic phonons (which are critical modes associated with the breaking of translation symmetry) while in the case of HTC this is not so clear. As shown in ref. [18], phase fluctuations alone are not capable of producing marginal Fermi liquid behavior. In order to obtain this kind of behavior the system needs be close to a different type of QCP. There are many attempts to generate hidden QCP for HTC. Among them, the idea of stripes (a state closely related to CDW) have been discussed in the literature [24]. It is worth noticing that TMD also show stripe phases associated with the breaking of the hexagonal symmetry in the triple CDW phase [25]. Another possibility is related with the breaking of the time reversal symmetry and generation of gapped superconducting phases [20]. More recently it has been proposed that due to strong electron-electron correlations an exotic CDW state might be the hidden QCP of HTC [26] and that the pseudo-gap temperature marks a real second order transition that is smeared by disorder. If this is indeed correct then the anomalies in these 2D systems can have the same origin. 正如我们所示，本文讨论的问题与HTC之间存在许多相似之处。也许最有趣的一点是Tcdw与Tc之间的反关联。这与HTC中赝能隙能量尺度与Tc之间的反关联类型相同[22]。当然，主要区别在于，在Tcdw处存在真正的二级相变，而赝能隙温度似乎是一个渡越能量尺度。事实上，这里描述的金属态在很多方面与HTC的节点液体描述相似[23]。然而，MTD的狄拉克费米子与超导无关，而是与CDW态相关。在TMD中，反常金属行为源于与声学声子（与平移对称性破缺相关的临界模）的耦合，而在HTC中这一点尚不明确。如文献[18]所示，仅相位涨落不足以产生边缘费米液体行为。为了获得这类行为，体系需要接近不同类型的QCP。已有许多尝试为HTC构造隐藏的QCP。其中，条纹（与CDW密切相关的态）的概念已在文献中讨论[24]。值得注意的是，TMD中也存在与三重CDW相中六角对称性破缺相关的条纹相[25]。另一种可能性与时间反演对称性破缺及有能隙超导相的产生有关[20]。最近有人提出，由于强电子-电子关联，一种奇异的CDW态可能是HTC的隐藏QCP [26]，且赝能隙温度标志着一个被无序抹平的真正二级相变。如果这确实正确，那么这些二维体系中的反常现象可能具有相同的起源。

> In summary, we have proposed a microscopic theory for the CDW and superconducting phases of TMD that involve the formation of a gapless CDW state. The elementary excitations are Dirac fermions that pair up due to the coupling to phonons and generate a superconducting state at low temperatures. We show that in the CDW state the coupling to phonons leads to marginal Fermi liquid behavior and to many anomalies in the physical properties. We show that our theory can explain the available experimental data including ARPES. We predict that high-resolution ARPES should be able to measure the modulation of the CDW gap around the Fermi surface and the opening of a superconducting gap at the Dirac points. The similarities between the phenomena described here and the one observed in other layered systems (such as HTC) is striking and the study of such exotic CDW might illuminate the way for the understanding of the complex physics in transition metal systems. 总之，我们提出了一个关于TMD中CDW和超导相的微观理论，涉及无能隙CDW态的形成。元激发为狄拉克费米子，它们通过声子耦合配对，在低温下产生超导态。我们表明，在CDW态中，与声子的耦合导致边缘费米液体行为以及物理性质中的许多反常。我们表明，我们的理论可以解释现有的实验数据，包括ARPES。我们预测，高分辨率ARPES应该能够测量CDW能隙围绕费米面的调制以及狄拉克点处超导能隙的打开。本文描述的现象与其他层状体系（如HTC）中观察到的现象之间的相似性令人震惊，对此类奇异CDW的研究可能为理解过渡金属体系中复杂的物理指明方向。
