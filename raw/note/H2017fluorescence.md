> # Dicyanostilbene-Based Two-Photon Thermo-Solvatochromic Fluorescence Probes with Two-Photon Triple Fluorescence
> 
> 基于二氰基芪的双光子热致溶剂变色荧光探针及其双光子三重荧光
> 
> **Dicyanostilbene**: 二氰基芪，一种含有两个氰基的芪类化合物，是本文研究的核心荧光团结构。 **Two-Photon**: 双光子，一种非线性光学过程，分子同时吸收两个光子达到激发态。 **Solvatochromic**: 溶剂化显色，指物质的吸收或发射光谱随溶剂极性变化而改变的现象。 **Fluorescence Probe**: 荧光探针，一种通过荧光信号变化来检测特定分析物或环境条件的分子工具。
> 
> * * *
> 
> ## Abstract
> 
> 摘要
> 
> The unusually sensitive solvatochromism of the two-photon fluorescence fluorophore of 2,5-dicyano-4-methyl-4’-dimethylaminostilbene (P1) whose emission maximum varies from 445 nm in cyclohexane to 641 nm in DMSO are described. P1 with remarkably large two-photon cross sections exhibits very strong polarity-, viscosity-, and temperature-dependence of fluorescence, and can be used to detect polarities, viscosities, and temperature. P1 presents a two-photon triple fluorescence. 本文描述了2,5-二氰基-4-甲基-4'-二甲氨基芪（P1）双光子荧光团异常灵敏的溶剂化显色现象，其最大发射波长从环己烷中的445 nm变化到二甲基亚砜（DMSO）中的641 nm。P1具有非常大的双光子截面，其荧光表现出极强的极性、粘度和温度依赖性，可用于检测极性、粘度和温度。P1呈现出双光子三重荧光现象。
> 
> **Solvatochromism**: 溶剂化显色现象，指化学物质在不同极性溶剂中展现出不同颜色的现象，具体表现为吸收或发射光谱的位移。 **Fluorophore**: 荧光团，分子中能够吸收光能并重新发射荧光的特定原子团。 **Two-photon cross section**: 双光子吸收截面，衡量分子双光子吸收能力的物理量，单位通常为GM。 **DMSO**: 二甲基亚砜，一种强极性非质子溶剂。
> 
> **Keywords**: Dicyanostilbene; Solvatochromism; Triple fluorescence; Fluorescence probe **关键词**: 二氰基芪；溶剂化显色；三重荧光；荧光探针
>

> 
> ## Introduction
> 
> 引言
> 
> For the development of Two-Photon (TP) and Two-Photon Fluorescence (TPF)-optimized molecular probes, it is important to have a significant modulation of the photophysical properties of the chromophore in response to external stimuli, such as polarity, pH, ion concentration, and temperature. At the same time, the chromophore should retain a significant Two-Photon Absorption (TPA) cross section (δ) in a suitable excitation window for biological imaging (700–1000 nm, corresponding to an optimum combination of reduced scattering and absorption in biological samples). In contrast to PET-based fluorescence probes, which only show increasing or decreasing emission intensity at fixed wavelength, solvatochromic probes may indicate the presence of an analyte by a significant change of emission wavelength, which is unequivocally detectable. Although a small number of TPF probes for polarity were reported, and two among them were used for lipid rafts imaging, their applications can’t be extended from polarity detection to viscosities and temperature. Furthermore, in them, small molecule-size always is accompanied by narrow solvatochromic range and small δ [1-5]. So they are not ideal candidates for TP solvatochromic probes. An ideal TP solvatochromic probe should have wide solvatochromic range, small molecule-size, and large δ and excellent photostability. Herein, we report two TP solvatochromic probes (P1 and P2) (Scheme 1) derived from 4-methyl-2,5-dicyano-4'-amino stilbene (DCS) with remarkably large δ, which has been successfully employed in the design of TPF probes for metal ions [6-9]. DCS is a push-pull chromophore with a donor-π-acceptor (D-π-A) architecture. It is studied that these two probes are applied to the detection of solvent polarities, viscosities and temperature, especially two-photon triple fluorescence is found for the first time. 对于开发优化的双光子（TP）和双光子荧光（TPF）分子探针，其发色团的光物理性质能够响应外部刺激（如极性、pH、离子浓度和温度）发生显著调控至关重要。同时，该发色团应在适合生物成像的激发窗口（700–1000 nm，对应于生物样品中散射和吸收降低的最佳组合）内保留显著的双光子吸收（TPA）截面（δ）。与仅显示固定波长处发射强度增减的PET基荧光探针不同，溶剂化显色探针可以通过发射波长的显著变化来指示分析物的存在，这种变化是明确可检测的。尽管已报道了少数用于极性检测的TPF探针，其中两个被用于脂筏成像，但它们的应用无法从极性检测扩展到粘度和温度检测。此外，这些小分子通常伴随着窄的溶剂化显色范围和小的δ [1-5]。因此，它们并非理想的TP溶剂化显色探针。理想的TP溶剂化显色探针应具有宽的溶剂化显色范围、小的分子尺寸、大的δ和优异的光稳定性。在此，我们报道了两种衍生自4-甲基-2,5-二氰基-4'-氨基芪（DCS）、具有非常大δ的TP溶剂化显色探针（P1和P2）（方案1），DCS已成功应用于金属离子TPF探针的设计中 [6-9]。DCS是一种具有给体-π-受体（D-π-A）结构的推拉型发色团。研究表明，这两种探针可用于检测溶剂极性、粘度和温度，特别是首次发现了双光子三重荧光。
> 
> **Two-Photon Absorption (TPA)**: 双光子吸收，一种双光子激发过程。 **Chromophore**: 发色团，分子中负责吸收特定波长光并产生颜色的原子团。 **PET**: 光诱导电子转移，一种常见的荧光传感机制。 **Lipid rafts**: 脂筏，细胞膜上富含胆固醇和鞘脂的微结构域。 **Push-pull chromophore**: 推拉型发色团，分子一端为给电子基团，另一端为吸电子基团，通过π桥连接，有利于分子内电荷转移。 **D-π-A**: 给体-π桥-受体，一种经典的分子设计构型。
> 
> * * *
> 
> The stilbene derivative P1 (P2) was obtained in 65 % (62%) yield by the reaction of dimethylaminobenzaldehyde (diphenylaminobenzaldehyde) with (2,5-dicyano-4-methylbenzyl) phosphonic acid diethyl ester in the presence of sodium hydride (Supporting Information (SI) and Scheme 2). The absorption properties of P1 are almost independent on the solvent (Table 1). However, with respect to the emission properties, P1 exhibits an unexpected strong solvatochromism (Figures 1, Supplementary Figures S1 and S2). Only a few previous examples of solvatochromic behavior for stilbene derivatives (10-14) which just possess one substituent in the single aromatic ring and are not ideal candidates for solvatochromic probes had been reported. Thus the Emission Maximum (EM) for P1 (P2) varies from 445 (452) nm in cyclohexane to 641 (604) nm in DMSO. The reason why the solvatochromic shifts of P2 are somewhat smaller (Tables S1) is that both decreasing transition energy and increasing sensitivity to solvent polarity for the weak electron donor diphenylamino group are inferior to ones for the strong electron donor dimethylamino group. 芪类衍生物P1（P2）通过二甲氨基苯甲醛（二苯氨基苯甲醛）与（2,5-二氰基-4-甲基苄基）膦酸二乙酯在氢化钠存在下反应获得，产率为65% (62%)（支持信息（SI）和方案2）。P1的吸收性质几乎不受溶剂影响（表1）。然而，在发射性质方面，P1表现出意想不到的强溶剂化显色现象（图1，补充图S1和S2）。此前仅报道过少数芪类衍生物（10-14）的溶剂化显色行为，这些衍生物仅在单个芳环上含有一个取代基，并非理想的溶剂化显色探针候选物。因此，P1（P2）的最大发射波长（EM）从环己烷中的445 (452) nm变化到DMSO中的641 (604) nm。P2的溶剂化显色位移较小（表S1）的原因在于，弱电子给体二苯氨基在降低跃迁能和增加对溶剂极性的敏感度方面均不如强电子给体二甲氨基。
> 
> **Emission Maximum (EM)**: 最大发射波长，荧光光谱中强度最高点对应的波长。 **Electron donor**: 电子给体，能够提供电子的原子或基团。
> 
> * * *
> 
> Protic solvents (Hydrogen-Bonding-Donor, HBD) can interact with the donor (dimethylamino) for the ground state of P1 to stabilize the ground state more than the excited state, and thus, lower energy for the ground state results in a relative blue shift as compared with strong polar aprotic solvents (Non-Hydrogen-Bonding-Donor, NHD) (λex (EtOH) (606 nm) <λex (DMF) (634 nm)). The fitting between EM in wavenumbers and the solvent parameters such as the ET (30) data, the Kosower parameter, Z, the Gibbs energy of activation of the solvolysis (δΔG≠) , the index of solvent dipolarity/polarizability (π*) and the Lippert-Mataga scale (Δf) gave an almost linear relationship (r2=0.90, 0.90, 0.90, 0.85, 0.82, respectively) (Figure 2) [10-18]. 质子溶剂（氢键给体，HBD）可与P1基态的给体（二甲氨基）相互作用，使基态比激发态更稳定，因此，与强极性非质子溶剂（非氢键给体，NHD）相比，基态能量降低导致相对蓝移（λex (EtOH) (606 nm) < λex (DMF) (634 nm)）。以波数表示的EM与溶剂参数（如ET (30) 数据、Kosower参数Z、溶剂解活化吉布斯自由能(δΔG≠)、溶剂偶极性/极化率指数(π*)以及Lippert-Mataga标度(Δf)）之间的拟合呈现近似线性关系（r²分别为0.90, 0.90, 0.90, 0.85, 0.82）（图2）[10-18]。
> 
> **Protic solvents**: 质子溶剂，能够提供质子（氢离子）的溶剂，常含有羟基或氨基。 **Blue shift**: 蓝移，光谱峰向短波长方向的移动。 **ET (30)**: 一种基于甜菜碱染料的溶剂极性经验参数。 **Wavenumber**: 波数，波长的倒数，在光谱学中常用于表示能量，单位常为cm⁻¹。
> 
> * * *
> 
> In one- and two-photon emissions (OPE and TPE), band A (1La) of P1 in glycerol all shift to the red with increasing temperature, and the relative intensities of band B (1Lb) gradually decreases (Figure 3a and 3b). The viscosity dependence of the fluorescence for P1 exhibits differences between A band and B band, and between OPE and TPE (Figure 3c and 3d). The intensities of B band in OPE and TPE steadily increase upon the augment of viscosities, whereas the intensities of A band in TPE at first rapidly decline, and then enhance, and a third band at 542 nm, namely Exciplex (E) fluorescence from strong intermolecular donor-acceptor systems [19-21], finally disappears in pure glycerol (Figure 3d). Never has this property in two-photon event been reported. 在单光子和双光子发射（OPE和TPE）中，P1在甘油中的A带（1La）均随温度升高而红移，而B带（1Lb）的相对强度逐渐降低（图3a和3b）。P1荧光的粘度依赖性在A带和B带之间，以及OPE和TPE之间均表现出差异（图3c和3d）。在OPE和TPE中，B带的强度随粘度增加而稳步上升，而TPE中A带的强度则先快速下降，随后增强，位于542 nm处的第三带，即来自强分子间给体-受体体系的激基复合物（E）荧光 [19-21]，最终在纯甘油中消失（图3d）。这种在双光子事件中的性质此前从未被报道。
> 
> **Exciplex**: 激基复合物，由激发态分子与基态分子形成的短暂复合物，常发出红移的荧光。 **Red shift**: 红移，光谱峰向长波长方向的移动。
> 
> * * *
> 
> High viscosity preventing intermolecular charges from transfer should be responsible for the disappearance of the third band whose no emergence in the OPE possibly consists in its low intensity and short lifetime. In addition, at the low concentration (10-6 M) of P1, the intensity ratio (IR) Ia/Ib of the emission bands A and B decreases with increasing viscosity, but at the high concentration (3 × 10-6 M) the IR Ia/Ib increases, irrespective of OPE and TPE (Supplementary Figures S3-S5). Furthermore, the concentration dependence [22] is found for the IR Ia/Ib of the emission bands A and B of P1 in alcohol, and the intensities of band E progressively increase with increasing concentrations of P1 (Figure S6). The above environmental influences upon triple fluorescence bands B, A and E usually indicate three emissive species, i.e., the localized excited state (LE), a twisted intramolecular charge transfer state (TICT) and an intermolecular exciplex [23]. 高粘度阻止分子间电荷转移应是第三带消失的原因，而该带在OPE中未出现可能是由于其低强度和短寿命。此外，在低浓度（10⁻⁶ M）的P1下，发射带A和B的强度比（IR）Ia/Ib随粘度增加而降低，但在高浓度（3 × 10⁻⁶ M）下，无论OPE还是TPE，IR Ia/Ib均增加（补充图S3-S5）。此外，在醇中发现了P1发射带A和B的IR Ia/Ib具有浓度依赖性 [22]，且E带强度随P1浓度增加而逐渐增强（图S6）。上述环境对三重荧光带B、A和E的影响通常表明存在三种发光物种，即局域激发态（LE）、扭曲分子内电荷转移态（TICT）和分子间激基复合物 [23]。
> 
> **TICT**: 扭曲分子内电荷转移，指分子在激发态发生几何构型扭曲，导致电荷分离的一种状态。 **Localized Excited State (LE)**: 局域激发态，激发态电子云主要定域在分子某一部分。 **Intensity Ratio (IR)**: 强度比，指两个不同发射带荧光强度的比值。
> 
> * * *
> 
> As expected, δ of P1 climbs its maximum of 5560 GM in nonpolar cyclohexane, while in strongly polar aprotic DMF it falls to its minimum of 130 GM. In general, δ values of P1 are much larger in low polarity aprotic solvents (PAS) than in high PAS, which should be attributed to the excited state configuration transformation (Table 1). The reduction in emission intensity for P1 in acetone and other more polar solvents together with a near lack of photoreactivity is certainly consistent with decay of the emissive state to one or more of the possible noncoplanar and nonemissive TICT which subsequently decay rapidly to the ground state [12]. 正如预期，P1的δ值在非极性环己烷中达到最大值5560 GM，而在强极性非质子DMF中降至最小值130 GM。总的来说，P1在低极性非质子溶剂（PAS）中的δ值远大于在高PAS中的值，这应归因于激发态构型的转变（表1）。P1在丙酮和其他极性更强的溶剂中发射强度降低，且几乎缺乏光反应性，这无疑与发光态衰变到一个或多个可能的非共面和非发光TICT态，随后迅速衰变回基态的现象一致 [12]。
> 
> **GM**: Goeppert-Mayer单位，双光子吸收截面的常用单位，1 GM = 10⁻⁵⁰ cm⁴ s photon⁻¹。
> 
> * * *
> 
> As in other solvatochromic systems [24], an Internal Charge Transfer (ICT) due to a strong donor-acceptor interplay between the dimethylamino and the 2,5-dicyano-4-methyl stilbyl moieties can take place in the excited state of P1. On one hand, ortho- and meta-cyano groups of the double bond (C=C) are fairly strong electron acceptor in the ground state, which can remarkably decrease transition energy, stabilize resonance structures and increase both the excited state dipole moment and molecular sensitivity to solvent polarity. On the other hand, two cyano groups can notably extend conjugated system, which consumedly improves δ and the fluorescence quantum yield. Additionally, in the excited state, the adiabatic ICT leads to an intermediate that most likely develops a negative charge for the nitrogen atom of the ortho-cyano group, that is, after solvent relaxation [15,24,25], stabilized not only by polar solvents, especially those with high π*, but also by the meta-cyano group with strong electron-withdrawing capacity for greater diffusion of the concentrated negative charge. This ICT-solvent relaxation sequence explains the significant red shift of the emission spectra in polar solvents as well as the temperature and viscosity dependence of the emission properties [15,25]. 与其他溶剂化显色体系一样 [24]，P1激发态中二甲氨基与2,5-二氰基-4-甲基芪基部分之间强烈的给体-受体相互作用可引发分子内电荷转移（ICT）。一方面，双键（C=C）邻位和间位的氰基在基态是相当强的电子受体，能显著降低跃迁能，稳定共振结构，并增加激发态偶极矩和分子对溶剂极性的敏感度。另一方面，两个氰基能显著扩展共轭体系，从而极大地提高δ值和荧光量子产率。此外，在激发态，绝热ICT导致一个中间体：邻位氰基的氮原子很可能带有负电荷，即在溶剂弛豫后 [15,24,25]，该电荷不仅被极性溶剂，特别是高π*值溶剂稳定，还被具有强吸电子能力的间位氰基稳定，以便集中负电荷的更大扩散。这一ICT-溶剂弛豫序列解释了在极性溶剂中发射光谱的显著红移，以及发射性质的温度和粘度依赖性 [15,25]。
> 
> **Internal Charge Transfer (ICT)**: 分子内电荷转移，分子内部电子从给体部分转移到受体部分的过程。 **Fluorescence quantum yield**: 荧光量子产率，发射光子数与吸收光子数之比，衡量荧光效率。 **Electron-withdrawing capacity**: 吸电子能力，原子或基团吸引电子的能力。

> 
> In conclusion, we have shown that the covalent attachment of two cyano groups in the single aromatic ring and dimethylamino group to stilbene is a considerably ingenious strategy, which makes the molecule a highly sensitive two-photon thermo-solvatochromic probe with a remarkably large δ and a wide solvatochromic range capable of being used to detect the analytes with high π*, polarities, viscosities, and temperature. It was the first time that TP triple fluorescence was sighted. 总之，我们证明了在单芳环上共价连接两个氰基和二甲氨基到芪骨架是一个相当巧妙的策略，这使得该分子成为一种高灵敏度的双光子热致溶剂变色探针，具有非常大的δ和宽的溶剂化显色范围，能够用于检测高π*分析物、极性、粘度和温度。这是首次观察到双光子三重荧光。
