> ## 一、引言
> 
> ### 1. 研究背景与核心问题
> 
> -   **领域背景**：**多铁性材料**（Multiferroics）是凝聚态物理和材料科学的前沿领域，它指在同一材料中同时存在两种或两种以上**铁序**（如铁磁性、铁电性）。其中，**第二类多铁性材料**（Type-II Multiferroics）因其磁性序与铁电序之间存在强耦合（即**磁电耦合**（Magnetoelectric Coupling））而备受关注，这为自旋电子学、高密度数据存储等下一代技术提供了巨大潜力。
> -   **核心问题**：长期以来，寻找在足够高温度下仍表现出强磁电耦合的多铁材料，并将其推向二维极限，是领域内的一个重大挑战。最近，单层**碘化镍**（NiI₂）被报道为首个纯粹的二维多铁性材料，但其多铁序的微观起源一直缺乏直接的实验证据，因为传统的宏观光学或散射技术在原子级厚度的二维材料上应用受限。
> 
> ### 2. 文献定位与核心价值
> 
> -   **文献定位**：本文是一项开创性的实验与理论相结合的研究，旨在从原子尺度上直接观测和验证单层NiI₂的多铁性。它不满足于间接证据，而是利用**扫描隧道显微镜**（STM）这一“原子级显微镜”，首次实现了对二维多铁性材料中磁电耦合效应的实空间可视化。
> -   **核心价值**：本文的核心价值在于**方法论和认知层面的突破**。
>     1.  **方法论**：证明了一种全新的、通用的探测手段——利用STM直接“看”到由多铁序引起的静电势调制，而无需复杂的自旋极化STM。
>     2.  **认知突破**：首次在原子尺度上揭示了单层NiI₂多铁性的微观机制，即自旋螺旋磁序与强自旋-轨道耦合共同作用，产生了可被STM观测的周期性电调制。
>     3.  **实践演示**：通过STM针尖施加电场，成功实现了对多铁畴壁的操纵，为未来基于电场控制磁性的器件设计提供了原子级原理验证。
> 
> ## 二、论文结构与逻辑概览
> 
> 本文遵循“**理论预测 → 实验观测 → 理论验证 → 调控应用**”的严密逻辑链条。
> 
> _画布提示：可设计为流程图，展示从理论模型到实验验证再到应用演示的完整路径。_
> 
> 1.  **理论模型**：首先，作者基于理论模型，阐明了单层NiI₂中多铁性的起源。其核心逻辑是：特定的**自旋螺旋磁序**（由相邻镍原子的铁磁和反铁磁交换作用竞争产生）与碘原子的强**自旋-轨道耦合**（SOC）结合，按公式 **P = Λ M × (∇ × M) / M²** 产生一个空间调制的**铁电极化（P）**。这个极化周期是磁螺旋周期的一半，并会调制材料的静电势，这正是STM可以探测的物理量。
>     
> 2.  **实验观测与验证**：随后，作者利用STM对在高定向热解石墨（HOPG）上生长的单层NiI₂进行了表征。
>     
>     -   **形貌与电子结构**：确认了单层高质量晶体，并测量出其带隙约为2.3 eV。
>     -   **核心发现**：在导带能量处，STM图像上出现了清晰的条纹状图案，其周期性（约17.8 Å）恰好是理论预测的电极化周期。通过傅里叶变换分析，精确提取了自旋螺旋的波矢 `q`。
>     -   **理论验证**：第一性原理计算（DFT）模拟的STM图像与实验高度吻合，确认了条纹的起源。通过分析“能带弯曲”效应，定量估算了局域电极化强度。
> 3.  **调控与应用演示**：最后，作者通过STM针尖施加局域电场脉冲，成功移动了多铁畴壁，直接证明了该体系存在可被电场操控的磁电耦合，这是其走向应用的关键一步。
>

> ## 四、正文核心内容剖析
> 
> ### 1. 研究背景与动机
> 
> 作者首先点明了多铁性材料，特别是第二类多铁性材料的重要性。然后，将视角聚焦到二维范德华材料领域，指出虽然单层NiI₂已被报道为二维多铁性材料，但其微观机制尚是“黑箱”。传统的宏观探测技术（如圆二色拉曼散射、二次谐波产生）在二维极限下要么失效，要么无法提供决定性的原子级证据。因此，**本文的核心动机是：利用具有原子级分辨能力的STM，直接“看见”并理解单层NiI₂的多铁序。**
> 
> ### 2. 方法/模型
> 
> -   **理论模型**：作者清晰地构建了一个物理模型：
>     -   **磁性起源**：竞争交换作用（J₁-J₃模型）→ 自旋螺旋磁序（波矢q）。
>     -   **电极化起源**：自旋螺旋磁序 + 强SOC → 非均匀铁电极化（P ∝ M × (∇ × M)）。
>     -   **实验可观测量**：非均匀铁电极化 → 调制静电势 → 调制电子能带 → STM图像中的条纹衬度。
> -   **实验方法**：分子束外延（MBE）在HOPG上生长单层NiI₂，在4.7 K下进行低温STM测量，结合dI/dV谱和电压脉冲操控。
> -   **计算方法**：采用考虑非共线磁性和自旋-轨道耦合的第一性原理计算（DFT，LDA近似），模拟了9a × √3a超晶胞中的自旋螺旋态，并计算了STM图像和谱学性质。
> 
> ### 3. 实验/数据
> 
> -   **核心发现**：在NiI₂导带能量下，STM图像出现与理论预测完全一致的、周期为磁螺旋一半的条纹。这是最直接的证据。
> -   **定量分析**：通过FFT精确测定q矢量，通过能带弯曲测量估算电极化强度。其估算的平均极化值（~5 × 10⁻¹³ C m⁻¹）与体材料输运测量的结果（~10⁻¹³ C m⁻¹）在数量级上一致，增强了结论的可靠性。
> -   **关键差异解释**：实验q矢量略微偏离ΓK高对称线，作者给出了合理的解释，如局域缺陷、高阶交换作用（如Kitaev作用）等，体现了严谨的科学态度。
> -   **衬底效应讨论**：对比了不同研究组在hBN、SiO₂和HOPG上的实验结果，提出了衬底可能对多铁性及其参数有重要影响，这是一个重要的开放性问题。
> 
> ### 4. 讨论
> 
> -   **方法适用性**：作者强调，这种STM探测方法并不仅限于NiI₂，它适用于任何由自旋螺旋驱动的第二类多铁性材料，为研究范德华材料及其异质结中的多铁性开辟了新途径。
> -   **畴壁操控**：成功用电场操纵多铁畴壁，不仅是磁电耦合的直接证据，也展示了NiI₂在电场调控磁性器件中的潜力。
> 
> ## 五、结论与贡献
> 
> 1.  **核心发现**：首次在原子尺度上，利用STM实现了对单层NiI₂多铁序的直接可视化和表征。
> 2.  **理论贡献**：实验验证了“自旋螺旋 + SOC → 可探测电调制”的理论模型，精确测定了自旋螺旋的波矢 `q = (0.069, 0.041, 0)` 和交换参数比值 `J₃/J₁ = -0.263`。
> 3.  **方法学贡献**：确立了一种新的、通用的探测二维多铁性的微观方法，证明了无需复杂自旋极化技术，普通的STM即可通过探测静电势调制来研究此类多铁性。
> 4.  **应用潜力**：通过直接演示电场对多铁畴壁的操纵，为未来开发基于二维多铁材料的低功耗、电场控制的磁存储和逻辑器件提供了原理验证。

> ## 七、学术思考与批判性分析
> 
> **思考1：条纹衬度的确切来源是否完全排除了其他可能性？** 论文核心论点是，导带条纹是铁电极化调制的直接结果。作者通过DFT模拟验证了这一观点。然而，是否存在其他理论也能解释这种衬度？例如，纯电荷密度波（CDW）或结构相变也能产生类似的周期性条纹。文中通过对比带隙内无条纹、导带有条纹，以及与理论预测的极化周期（半磁螺旋周期）吻合，有力排除了简单的CDW，但一个更严谨的证明可以是展示在某个特定偏压下，条纹衬度发生反转，因为这直接对应着极化方向的改变。
> 
> **思考2：电极化强度估算的准确性如何？** 估算基于能带弯曲量（16.8 meV）并将其与已知铁电材料（如SnSe）进行类比。这一过程有多处近似：1) 能带弯曲量与极化强度并非简单线性关系，还涉及材料的介电屏蔽特性；2) 类比本身的合理性，SnSe是位移型铁电体，其极化机制与NiI₂的电子型机制完全不同。因此，**P ≈ 10⁻¹² C m⁻¹** 应被视为一个可靠的数量级估算，而非精确测量值。更精确的测量需要结合宏观的电荷积分法（如PFM的升级版在二维材料中的应用）或更精细的理论计算。
> 
> **思考3：J₁-J₃模型是否足够？** 作者承认了实验得到的q矢量略微偏离高对称线，并讨论了Kitaev相互作用、Dzyaloshinskii-Moriya相互作用（DMI）等可能的影响。这表明，简单的J₁-J₃海森堡模型可能只是对真实物理的一个低阶近似。尤其在单层极限下，对称性破缺可能导致DMI等非平凡相互作用变得重要，它们会与磁交换作用竞争，共同决定基态磁结构。未来的理论工作必须包含这些更复杂的相互作用，才能实现对体系的定量描述。
> 
> **思考4：畴壁操控的物理机制是什么？** 外电场脉冲如何移动畴壁？论文将其归因于磁电耦合，但未深入探讨微观机制。一种可能是，电场直接作用于局域极化，改变了畴壁两侧的自由能密度，从而驱动畴壁向能量较低的方向移动。另一种可能是，电场脉冲通过焦耳热效应导致了局部升温，降低了畴壁运动的势垒。区分这两种机制，需要更精细的对照实验，比如改变脉冲宽度、幅度或在不同温度下进行实验。
> 
> ## 八、用户问题预测与解答
> 
> **问题1：什么是“第二类多铁性材料（Type-II Multiferroics）”？它和“第一类”有什么区别？** **解答**：这是多铁性材料的重要分类。关键区别在于磁性和铁电性之间的耦合强度与起源。
> 
> -   **第一类多铁性材料**：磁性和铁电性的起源不同，两者相对独立，因此耦合通常很弱。例如，铁电性来自结构相变，磁性来自部分填充的d/f轨道。
> -   **第二类多铁性材料**：**铁电性完全由特定的磁性序所诱导**。在本文中，NiI₂的铁电性就是由自旋螺旋磁序直接产生的。这种同源性导致两者之间存在很强的**磁电耦合**，即磁场可以显著改变电极化，电场也能显著影响磁化，这正是其应用价值所在。
> 
> **问题2：为什么STM能看到多铁性？它直接看到的是磁结构还是电结构？** **解答**：这是一个非常关键的问题。STM**直接探测的是局域电子态密度**，而不是磁矩本身。在NiI₂中，STM并不是直接“看到”了自旋螺旋，而是“看到”了由自旋螺旋和SOC共同诱导产生的**非均匀铁电极化**。这个极化会产生一个空间调制的静电势，进而调制了NiI₂的电子能带（即产生了能带弯曲）。当STM针尖在恒定电流模式下扫描时，为维持恒定电流，针尖高度会随这种能带调制而变化，从而在图像上形成明暗相间的条纹。所以，STM是通过“电”的窗口，间接但清晰地“看见”了“磁”的序。
> 
> **问题3：为什么观测到的条纹周期是磁螺旋周期的一半？** **解答**：这是由公式 **P ∝ M × (∇ × M)** 决定的。考虑一个简单的螺旋磁序，其磁化矢量M在空间中旋转，一个完整的旋转周期为L。当我们计算叉乘 M × (∇ × M) 时，它的变化频率是M的两倍。这意味着，虽然自旋的方向在转过一个完整的圆周（360°）后回到原点，但由它诱导出的极化P的方向，在自旋转过180°时就已经反向一次，转过360°时则反向两次。因此，P的空间周期是磁螺旋周期的一半。这个“半周期”关系是验证该理论模型正确性的一个关键预测。
> 
> **问题4：为什么要操纵多铁畴壁？这有什么意义？** **解答**：操纵多铁畴壁是本文从“发现/表征”走向“调控/应用”的关键一步。其意义在于：
> 
> 1.  **证明强磁电耦合**：用电场移动畴壁，是磁电耦合存在的直接功能体现。
> 2.  **器件原理验证**：在自旋电子学中，信息可以被编码在畴壁的位置或磁畴的取向中。如果能够用低功耗的电场（而非高功耗的电流或磁场）来移动畴壁，就意味着可以设计出更节能的“**磁电存储器**”或“**逻辑器件**”。本文的实验正是这样一个原子尺度的原理验证。
> 
> **问题5：文中提到的FFT在这里起到了什么作用？** **解答**：**快速傅里叶变换**（FFT）是分析周期性结构的强大数学工具。在本文中，STM图像在实空间中看起来是复杂的原子排列和条纹的叠加，人眼难以定量。而FFT将图像转换到**倒空间**（或频率空间）：
> 
> -   **原子晶格**：周期性小，在FFT中表现为相距较远的明亮斑点（图2f中的红圈）。
> -   **多铁性条纹**：周期性大，在FFT中表现为非常靠近中心斑点的成对斑点（图2f中的绿圈）。 通过精确测量这些斑点在倒空间中的位置，作者就能**精确、客观地**计算出条纹的周期（L_S）和方向，并进而推导出磁螺旋的波矢q，避免了人工测量带来的误差和主观性。
> 
> ## 九、专区：便于 Obsidian Dataview 插件调用
> 
> 领域基础知识:: 多铁性材料（Multiferroics）是同时存在两种及以上铁序（如铁磁性、铁电性）的材料。第二类多铁性材料（Type-II Multiferroics）的电极化由特定磁序（如自旋螺旋）诱导，具有强磁电耦合（Magnetoelectric Coupling）特性，是开发低功耗自旋电子器件的理想候选。二维范德华材料（2D van der Waals Materials）为在原子极限下探索多铁性提供了新平台。
> 
> 研究背景:: 单层碘化镍（NiI₂）近期被报道为首个纯粹的二维多铁性材料，但其多铁性微观起源缺乏直接实验证据。传统宏观探测技术（如光学、散射）在原子级厚度的二维材料上应用受限，无法提供原子尺度的决定性信息，迫切需要微观尺度的表征手段来揭示其多铁序的物理本质。
> 
> 作者的问题意识:: 如何从原子尺度上直接观测和验证单层NiI₂的多铁性？其磁电耦合的微观机制是什么？能否利用该机制实现对多铁序的电场调控？作者旨在通过扫描隧道显微镜（STM）的实空间成像能力，超越间接证据，直接“看见”并操纵由多铁性导致的物理效应。
> 
> 主要研究对象:: 通过分子束外延（MBE）在高定向热解石墨（HOPG）衬底上生长的单层碘化镍（NiI₂）。
> 
> 主要研究方法:: 采用低温扫描隧道显微镜（STM）和扫描隧道谱（dI/dV）进行原子级形貌与电子结构表征，并结合第一性原理密度泛函理论（DFT）计算，构建非共线磁结构模型，模拟STM图像以验证实验结果。同时，利用STM针尖施加电压脉冲以操纵多铁畴。
>

> **问题1：什么是“第二类（type-II）多铁性”？它与第一类有何不同？** **答**：简单来说，第一类多铁性的磁序和铁电序来源不同，耦合较弱。例如，铁电性由结构相变引起，而磁性由另一套独立的原子产生。第二类多铁性的铁电序则完全由特殊的磁序（如螺旋、涡旋）驱动，两者同源，因此具有极强的**磁电耦合效应**。本文的NiI₂就是典型，其电极化直接源于自旋螺旋序，磁场能翻转电极化，电场也能改变磁序。

> **问题2：为什么STM能看到磁序产生的电信号？** **答**：STM本身对电子态密度敏感。其原理是，非共线的自旋螺旋序通过**自旋轨道耦合**，就像扭曲的弹簧一样，会“拉扯”电荷，产生一个在空间上周期性变化的电极化。这个变化的电极化又会像一块起伏的地毯，导致材料的电子能带发生周期性的弯曲（**能带弯曲**）。当STM的探针在不同位置扫描时，就会有电流差异，从而形成明暗相间的条纹图案。所以，STM看到的不是磁矩本身，而是磁序引起的“电”的地形图。

> Multiferroics are materials that exhibit simultaneously more than one ferroic order. Over the past years, numerous studies have reported different bulk multiferroics, most of them complex oxides. Multiferroics can be classified based on the presence of weak or strong coupling between the orders as type I or type II, respectively. In the particular case of magnetic and ferroelectric orders, type-II multiferroics showing a strong magnetoelectric coupling have a huge potential for technological applications including spintronics, data storage, and efficient energy management in computation. Nevertheless, multiferroics displaying a strong magnetoelectric coupling at sufficiently high temperatures to build functional devices remain elusive. The dawn of 2D materials has enabled new strategies for the design of artificial multiferroics. The weak van der Waals bonding between the layers allows to easily reach the 2D limit with these compounds, thus obtaining families of building blocks with different properties. These can be stacked and twisted in heterostructures, leading to a wide range of emergent phenomena including multiferroicity. Recently, it was suggested that the multiferroicity of the bulk van der Waals compound NiI₂ remains in the mechanically-exfoliated few-layer and monolayer limits. Theoretical analyses have addressed the origin of this type-II multiferroic order to the combination of the magnetic spin-spiral order of NiI₂ and the strong spin-orbit (SOC) coupling of the I atoms. To provide experimental evidence of 2D multiferroicity, circular dichroic Raman, birefringence, and second-harmonic-generation measurements on both mono- and multi-layer NiI₂ were performed, establishing NiI₂ as the first purely 2D multiferroic with a transition temperature TC = 21 K. However, optical measurements might not suffice to demonstrate the emergent ferroelectricity in the 2D limit. In addition, typical bulk techniques used to identify the magnetic spin spiral and to measure the electric polarization that gives rise to the multiferroic order, cannot be applied in the monolayer limit. These factors suggest that the origin of the multiferroic order in monolayer NiI₂ has not been experimentally established and its full characterization requires measurements at the microscopic level. In this work, we present real-space visualization of the multiferroicity in monolayer NiI₂. We show that the magnetoelectric coupling present in this kind of type-II multiferroics allows us to directly probe and characterize the multiferroic order by scanning tunneling microscopy (STM). These results are supported by non-collinear ab initio calculations based on density functional theory (DFT) in monolayer NiI₂. Moreover, we provide evidence of the magnetoelectric coupling of this 2D system by electrical manipulation of the multiferroic domains. > ### 2.1. Multiferroic Order in NiI₂
> 
> ### 2.1. NiI₂中的多铁序
> 
> In bulk, NiI₂ displays a magnetic spin spiral at low temperatures. DFT calculations have determined that the q vector characterizing the spin spiral order in bulk is a consequence of the competition between magnetic exchange interactions between Ni atoms. In particular, ferromagnetic intralayer first-neighbor J₁, antiferromagnetic intralayer third-neighbor J₃, and antiferromagnetic interlayer second-neighbor J₂ magnetic exchange interactions are the most relevant. These interactions lead to a q vector in bulk whose in-plane component lies on the ΓM segment in reciprocal space, and whose value can be modified by external parameters, such as pressure, that change the ratio between the different magnetic exchanges. In the monolayer limit, there are no interlayer interactions, hence the q vector is determined by the competition between J₁ and J₃ (Figure 1b). DFT calculations have predicted that in this scenario the q vector lies on the ΓK segment in reciprocal space, corresponding to the third-neighbor bond direction. The classical Heisenberg model solution predicts that q = (q, q, 0), with q in units of the reciprocal lattice of the structural single-unit cell with lattice vector a, is determined by the J₁/J₃ ratio. 在体相中，NiI₂在低温下显示磁自旋螺旋序。DFT计算已确定，表征体相中自旋螺旋序的q矢量是Ni原子间磁交换相互作用竞争的结果。特别是，铁磁层内第一近邻J₁、反铁磁层内第三近邻J₃和反铁磁层间第二近邻J₂磁交换相互作用最为相关。这些相互作用导致体相中的q矢量，其面内分量位于倒空间中的ΓM段，并且其值可以通过改变不同磁交换比率的外部参数（如压力）来修改。在单层极限下，不存在层间相互作用，因此q矢量由J₁和J₃之间的竞争决定（图1b）。DFT计算预测，在这种情况下，q矢量位于倒空间中的ΓK段，对应于第三近邻键方向。经典海森堡模型解预测，q = (q, q, 0)，其中q以结构单胞晶格矢量a的倒格矢为单位，由J₁/J₃比率决定。

> ### 2.2. STM Characterization of Monolayer NiI₂
> 
> ### 2.2. 单层NiI₂的STM表征
> 
> Figure 2a shows a large area STM scan of our sample (see Experimental Section for details on sample preparation). It reveals the presence of a well-formed monolayer NiI₂ on HOPG. The dI/dV spectrum of monolayer NiI₂ is shown in Figure 2b. The valence and conduction bands can be distinguished at −1.9 V and 0.4 V, respectively, indicating that NiI₂ is an insulator with a gap of 2.3 eV. The negative conductance occurring at high bias can be associated with the resonant tunneling through confined energy levels within the 2D layer. Figure 2c,d shows small-area STM images at 0.3 V (within the NiI₂ bandgap) and at 0.9 V (within the NiI₂ conduction band). A systematic analysis with scans at different energies can be found in the Supporting Information. Inside the gap, the contrast is not affected by the polarization of the NiI₂, and we observe a hexagonal moiré modulation arising from the lattice mismatch between HOPG and NiI₂ (Figure 2c). However, as the conduction band is modulated by the ferroelectric polarization, the stripe-like pattern expected for this kind of type-II multiferroic appears at biases corresponding to the NiI₂ conduction band (Figure 2d). Two distinct regions with different stripe orientations can be observed. These correspond to two different multiferroic domains. Finally, there are different types of atomic scale defects that are analyzed in detail in the Supporting Information. Figure 2e shows an atomic-resolution scan in a single domain region, the FFT of this scan is shown in Figure 2f. The spin-spiral q vector can be determined from the relationship between the single unit cell a × a and the stripe modulation. From our experimental results, we obtain a = 3.85 Å for monolayer NiI₂. The periodicity of the stripes is LS = 17.8 Å corresponding to about 4.6a. According to Equation (1) the modulation of the stripes caused by the emergent electric polarization displays at half the periodicity of the spin spiral (Figure 1c,d), leading to a spin spiral periodicity of 9.2a in the experiment. The spin-spiral q vector can be directly extracted from the peaks associated with the stripes in the FFT (green circles in Figure 2f) as half of the peak value. We have obtained q = (0.069, 0.041, 0) in units of the reciprocal lattice vectors, showing a small deviation from ΓK segment expected for the J₁ − J₃ spin model. The projection in the ΓK segment is 0.173ΓK, corresponding to q = (0.057, 0.057, 0). It can be observed that this small variation in the experimental q vector from the ΓK segment leads to a small tilt (≈4.6°) of the stripe modulation (Figure 2f). This effect could be a consequence of local defects in the sample, the proximity of other multiferroic domains, or the influence of other parameters such as intralayer second-neighbor magnetic exchange, or biquadratic interactions not considered in the J₁ − J₃ spin model. In particular, it has been highlighted that the Kitaev interaction plays a major role in stabilizing the q vector observed in bulk. However, based on the phase diagrams obtained in that work, the different q vector that we have measured in the monolayer should lie in the regime dominated by the J₁ and J₃ parameters, in agreement with recent theoretical works. Other spin-orbit coupling interactions such as Dzyaloshinskii-Moriya interaction may compete with Kitaev interactions, due to the explicit breaking of mirror symmetry in our system. Finally, the diffuse peaks around the M points are associated with the defect seen in the scan. The analysis of the filtered FFT images is shown in the Supporting Information. Extracting the information on the spin spiral from the STM measurements allows us to model monolayer NiI₂ using DFT. Neglecting the small tilt in the ferroelectric modulation, the unit cell describing the multiferroic order in monolayer NiI₂ can be well approximated by the commensurate 9a × √3a supercell shown in Figure 1a, corresponding to q = (0.064, 0.064, 0) and √3/9ΓK ∼ 0.192ΓK. From the experimental q = (0.057, 0.057, 0) we can estimate J₃/J₁ = −0.263. This result shows that monolayer NiI₂ is on the verge of a spin-spiral to ferromagnetic transition (J₃/J₁ = −0.25), which might explain why in some studies the presence of ferroelectric polarization was only reported down to the bilayer limit but not in the monolayer. An important factor to consider could be the effect of the substrate. Song et al. grew monolayer NiI₂ on hBN and found evidence of multiferroic behavior, while Ju et al. grew NiI₂ on SiO₂ and did not report multiferroicity in the single layer limit. Our results on monolayer NiI₂ on HOPG show multiferroic order. Therefore, the substrate could have a critical influence not only on the presence of multiferroicity but also on its order parameters. A comparison of the experimental q vector based on our experiments with previous theoretical predictions for free-standing monolayer NiI₂, could point out that DFT tends to overestimate the |J₃/J₁| ratio. This could be rationalized by the typical limitations of the different DFT functionals to deal with electronic correlations and electronic localization, thus introducing an overestimation of the long-range magnetic exchanges. This could explain why "ab initio" calculations predict a spin spiral for NiCl₂ while the observed ground state is ferromagnetic. Nevertheless, the effect of the substrate on the multiferroic order of NiI₂ should be further analyzed to reconcile the different experimental and theoretical works. In order to shed light on the microscopic origin of the STM modulation observed in the experiments, we have performed non-collinear DFT calculations in the commensurate 9a × √3a supercell and the q = (0.064, 0.064, 0) spin spiral (see Experimental Section for computational details). When SOC is included in the atomic relaxations, we observe the emergence of ferroelectric forces that drive the atoms to new positions following the same periodicity as the electric polarization (Figure 1d). Due to this symmetry breaking in real space, the computed STM image (Figure 2g) and its FFT (Figure 2h) display a modulation with the periodicity of the electric polarization, i.e., half of the magnetic spiral, in a good agreement with the experimental observations. Having characterized the spiral order of monolayer NiI₂, we now proceed to determine the strength of the ferroelectric polarization. The image contrast over the stripes depends on the bias voltage, i.e., at a certain bias voltage, the maximum (minimum) in the local electric polarization corresponds to a bright (dark) area of the stripe pattern, while at a different bias, the opposite will occur. A more convenient way to analyze where the minima and maxima of the local electric polarization are located is by considering the band bending of the conduction band. Local variations of the electric polarization, such as those encountered on the edges of ferroelectric islands, induce a local energy band bending that can be used to estimate the strength of the electric polarization. The emergence of an inhomogeneous electric polarization in NiI₂ is expected to produce similar band bendings, but in this case within the same multiferroic domain and with the periodicity of the ferroelectric modulation (Figure 1d). This behavior is experimentally demonstrated and theoretically reproduced in Figure 3. Figure 3b shows dI/dV spectra recorded along a line in a single domain region (green arrow shown in Figure 3a) parallel to the q vector. Figure 3e shows the DFT simulated line spectra for the commensurate 9a × √3a supercell and the q = (0.064, 0.064, 0) spin spiral. Both plots show the conduction bands. A clear underestimation of the bandgap is observed for the DFT calculations, which is typical for the LDA approximation. However, both experiment and theory show a band bending modulation of the conduction band that becomes more apparent when analyzing the dI/dV spectra at a maximum and a minimum of the stripe modulation in Figure 3c (spectra taken at the positions indicated by the blue and red dots in Figure 3a, respectively). A clear shift in energies between these two positions occurs as a consequence of the ferroelectric modulation introducing a band bending. The shift in energies is also seen in the DFT calculations (Figure 3f). At a bias of 1 V (Figure 3a), this shift produces a higher intensity in the areas where the local polarization is maximal (blue line in Figure 3c) compared to where the polarization is zero (red line in Figure 3c). This allows us to resolve the local polarization for the stripe pattern (black arrows in Figure 3a). However, note that there is a 180° ambiguity in the determination of the direction of this local polarization. The band bending can be analyzed by tracking the shift in the differential conductance maxima as a function of the position across the modulation. This is shown in Figure 3d,g for the experimental and theoretical data, respectively. First, we should stress that DFT in the LDA approximation underestimates the energy gap, which is a well-known issue in DFT. In addition, it is difficult to capture the band alignment with the substrate quantitatively. Therefore, the absolute energy position of the conduction bands should be treated as a free parameter when comparing theory and experiment. However, this does not affect the prediction of electric polarization. The important point is to compare the cosine modulation produced by the emergent electric polarization. The observed oscillatory behavior can be fitted to E = E₀ + EPsin (2πx/LS + φ), where E₀ corresponds to an average of the differential conductance maximum at the bottom of the conduction band, EP is the band bending modulation caused by the inhomogeneous emergent polarization, LS is the stripe periodicity that has already been analyzed and, φ is a trivial phase related to the starting point considered for the fitting. We obtain EP = 16.8 mV from the experiment and EP = 5.0 meV from the DFT calculation. This underestimation of the ab initio calculations could be a consequence of the limitations of the LDA approximation for the atomic relaxations. However, apart from this small difference, the qualitative agreement between theory and experiment is remarkable. For phonon-driven ferroelectrics such as SnSe and SnTe, STM experiments have shown the band bending is on the order of several hundreds of meV and an associated electric polarization P ≈ 10⁻¹⁰ C m⁻¹ has been estimated. Therefore, considering that in monolayer NiI₂ we observe a band bending of 1 or 2 orders of magnitude smaller, a P ≈ 10⁻¹² C m⁻¹ can be estimated for monolayer NiI₂. However, this is a local value that corresponds to the maximum amplitude of the electric polarization. An across-the-stripes spatial average needs to be performed to compare with transport measurements. This yields an estimate of the polarization of P̄ ≃ 5 × 10⁻¹³ C m⁻¹. As a reference, it is interesting to compare this value to the one reported for bulk. There, transport measurements report a value for the in-plane volumetric electric polarization in the bulk of 150 × 10⁻⁶ C m⁻². This corresponds to an in-plane electric polarization per layer of P̄_bulk = 10⁻¹³ C m⁻¹, consistent with our estimations. Note that the different dimensionality as well as substrate effects in our case can explain the difference between our estimate and the bulk measurement. m⁻¹。然而，这是一个对应于电极化最大振幅的局部值。需要进行跨条纹的空间平均，以与输运测量进行比较。这得出极化估计值为P̄ ≃ 5 × 10⁻¹³ C m⁻¹。作为参考，将此值与体相报道的值进行比较是有趣的。那里，输运测量报道体相中面内体积电极化的值为150 × 10⁻⁶ C m⁻²。这对应于每层的面内电极化P̄_bulk = 10⁻¹³ C > ### 2.3. Manipulation of Multiferroic Domain Boundaries
> 
> ### 2.3. 多铁畴界的操控
> 
> We have also directly probed the magnetoelectric coupling in monolayer NiI₂ by external electric field manipulation of the multiferroic domains. Figure 4a–d shows the movement of the multiferroic domain wall induced by voltage pulses from the STM tip (the bias is swept from 1 to 4 V with a closed feedback loop and a current set point of 100 pA). Starting in Figure 4a, the multiferroic domain wall (black-dashed line) and different kinds of defects (highlighted with circles, detailed analysis in the Supporting Information) can be identified. The neutral defects (pink circle) can be used as a spatial reference for the domain manipulation. A first voltage pulse is performed in the position indicated by the sky blue dot (in the left domain) and it leads to the configuration shown in Figure 4b. We can observe that the domain wall has moved to the right increasing the size of the left domain. Moreover, a clear difference between the different types of defects can be observed. While the charged defects are mobile, the neutral ones do not move and can be used as a reference to analyze the domain manipulation. After the scan shown in Figure 4b, we applied a second voltage pulse in the right domain leading to Figure 4c, and a third one leading to Figure 4d. As before, the location of the domain wall moves away from the position of the voltage pulse and charged defects are mobile, while neutral ones are not. Ferroelectric domain wall manipulation through voltage pulses had been achieved before in ferroelectric SnTe. Now, we show here that multiferroic domains in this kind of magnetic spin-spiral multiferroics are also tunable by external electric fields, thus showing direct evidence of the magnetoelectric coupling in monolayer NiI₂. 我们还通过外部电场操控多铁畴，直接探测了单层NiI₂中的磁电耦合。图4a–d显示了由STM针尖施加的电压脉冲（偏压从1 V扫描到4 V，闭环反馈，电流设定点100 pA）引起的多铁畴壁的移动。从图4a开始，可以识别多铁畴壁（黑色虚线）和不同类型的缺陷（用圆圈突出显示，详细分析见支持信息）。中性缺陷（粉色圆圈）可用作畴操控的空间参考。在左侧畴中天蓝色点指示的位置施加第一个电压脉冲，导致图4b所示的构型。我们可以观察到畴壁向右移动，增加了左侧畴的尺寸。此外，可以观察到不同类型缺陷之间的明显差异。带电缺陷是可移动的，而中性缺陷不移动，可用作分析畴操控的参考。在如图4b所示的扫描之后，我们在右侧畴中施加了第二个电压脉冲，导致图4c，然后是第三个电压脉冲，导致图4d。与之前一样，畴壁的位置远离电压脉冲的位置移动，带电缺陷是可移动的，而中性缺陷则不是。通过电压脉冲进行铁电畴壁操控此前已在铁电SnTe中实现。现在，我们在此表明，这类磁自旋螺旋多铁性材料中的多铁畴也可以通过外部电场进行调节，从而显示了单层NiI₂中磁电耦合的直接证据。

> ## 3. Conclusion
> 
> ## 3. 结论
> 
> In conclusion, we have probed and characterized the multiferroic order in monolayer NiI₂ down to the atomic scale. Using a combination of STM experiments and non-collinear ab initio calculations, we show that in this spin-spiral SOC-driven multiferroic, the emergent ferroelectric polarization induces a modulation of the electrostatic potential. This is directly reflected in the local density of states allowing its direct visualization by STM. This modulation has half of the spin-spiral periodicity, allowing us to characterize the spin-spiral vector as q = (0.069, 0.041, 0) and the parameter relation J₃/J₁ = −0.263 of the associated spin model. The observed band bending allows us to estimate the polarization P ≈ 10⁻¹² C m⁻¹ for monolayer NiI₂. Finally, we have probed the magnetoelectric coupling of NiI₂ by manipulating the multiferroic domains by local electric fields induced by the STM tip. Our results firmly establish the atomic scale origin of multiferroicity in NiI₂ and pave the way for future studies on multiferroics and 2D van der Waals materials at the microscopic level. 总之，我们已在原子尺度上探测并表征了单层NiI₂中的多铁序。通过结合STM实验和非共线从头计算，我们表明，在这种自旋螺旋SOC驱动的多铁性材料中，涌现的铁电极化诱导了静电势的调制。这直接反映在局域态密度中，允许其通过STM直接可视化。这种调制具有自旋螺旋周期性的一半，使我们能够将自旋螺旋矢量表征为q = (0.069, 0.041, 0)，以及相关自旋模型的参数关系J₃/J₁ = −0.263。观察到的能带弯曲使我们能够估计单层NiI₂的极化P ≈ 10⁻¹² C m⁻¹。最后，我们通过STM针尖诱导的局部电场操控多铁畴，探测了NiI₂的磁电耦合。我们的结果牢固地确立了NiI₂中多铁性的原子尺度起源，并为未来在微观层面研究多铁性材料和二维范德华材料铺平了道路。
> 
> **Local density of states (LDOS)**: 局域态密度，在实空间中特定位置的能量态密度，STM的dI/dV谱正比于LDOS。

> ## 4. Experimental Section
> 
> ## 4. 实验部分
> 
> **Sample Preparation**: **样品制备**:
> 
> NiI₂ was grown by molecular beam epitaxy (MBE) on highly oriented pyrolytic graphite (HOPG) under ultra-high vacuum conditions (UHV, base pressure ≈1 × 10⁻¹⁰ mbar). Initial preparation of the HOPG substrate involved multiple cleaving steps to ensure cleanliness of the surface, followed by outgassing at T ≈ 300 °C for one hour to remove any remaining surface contamination. After that, the substrate temperature was stabilized at ≈100 °C for the growth of monolayer NiI₂. For the growth, two sources of material were used: Ni (rod with 99.999% purity) was evaporated using an electron-beam evaporator while iodine was deposited from a Knudsen cell using NiI₂ (anhydrous powder, 99.95% purity) as an iodine source (NiI₂ decomposes at a temperature of around ≈400 °C). The sample was grown in an iodine background pressure of ≈9 × 10⁻⁸ mbar and the growth duration was 30 min. After the growth, the sample was annealed for 5 min in an iodine background. Two factors were critical to the successful growth of NiI₂: the precise control of substrate temperature and maintaining an iodine pressure consistently above ≈7 × 10⁻⁸ mbar. NiI₂是通过分子束外延（MBE）在超高真空条件（UHV，基础压力≈1 × 10⁻¹⁰ mbar）下在高定向热解石墨（HOPG）上生长的。HOPG衬底的初始准备包括多次解理步骤以确保表面清洁，随后在T ≈ 300 °C下脱气一小时，以去除任何残留的表面污染。之后，将衬底温度稳定在≈100 °C，用于单层NiI₂的生长。生长使用了两种材料源：Ni（纯度99.999%的棒材）使用电子束蒸发器蒸发，而碘则从使用NiI₂（无水粉末，纯度99.95%）作为碘源的Knudsen池中沉积（NiI₂在约≈400 °C的温度下分解）。样品在约9 × 10⁻⁸ mbar的碘背景压力下生长，生长持续时间为30分钟。生长后，样品在碘气氛中退火5分钟。成功生长NiI₂的两个关键因素是：精确控制衬底温度，以及将碘压力始终保持在≈7 × 10⁻⁸ mbar以上。
> 
> **Molecular beam epitaxy (MBE)**: 分子束外延，一种在超高真空下沉积高质量单晶薄膜的技术。 **Ultra-high vacuum (UHV)**: 超高真空，指压力低于10⁻⁹ mbar的真空环境。 **Knudsen cell**: Knudsen池，一种用于产生可控分子束的蒸发源装置。
> 
> **STM Measurements**: **STM测量**:
> 
> After the preparation, the sample was inserted into the low-temperature STM (CreaTec LT-STM) connected to the same UHV system, and subsequent experiments were performed at T = 4.7 K. STM images were taken in the constant-current mode. dI/dV spectra were recorded by standard lock-in detection while sweeping the sample bias in an open feedback loop configuration, with a peak-to-peak bias modulation specified for each measurement and at a frequency of 515 Hz. 制备后，样品被插入连接到同一UHV系统的低温STM（CreaTec LT-STM）中，随后的实验在T = 4.7 K下进行。STM图像在恒流模式下获取。dI/dV谱通过标准锁相检测记录，同时以开环反馈配置扫描样品偏压，每次测量指定峰峰值偏压调制，频率为515 Hz。
> 
> **Constant-current mode**: 恒流模式，STM的一种工作模式，通过调节针尖高度保持隧道电流恒定。 **Lock-in detection**: 锁相检测，一种用于从噪声中提取特定频率信号的技术。
> 
> **Computational Details**: **计算细节**:
> 
> Ab initio electronic structure calculations based on density functional theory (DFT) were performed in monolayer NiI₂. Calculations were carried out with the all-electron full-potential linearized augmented-plane-wave method, using a fully non-collinear formalism with spin-orbit coupling (SOC) as implemented in Elk. The local density approximation (LDA) was used for the exchange-correlation functional. The results presented were converged with respect to all the parameters, considering the 9a × √3a supercell and the q = (0.064, 0.064, 0) spin spiral, a 2 × 6 × 1 k-mesh, and a vacuum spacing of 20 Å. Relaxations of the atomic positions in the spin spiral state including SOC require careful convergence of the forces (10⁻⁴ a.u) and the Kohn-Sham potential (10⁻⁷ a.u). The DFT STM images were computed in a 312 × 60 real space mesh and interpolated to a finer orthogonal mesh (800 × 800) to obtain a higher resolution STM image and to compute the FFT. 基于密度泛函理论（DFT）的从头计算电子结构计算在单层NiI₂中进行。计算使用全电子全势线性化缀加平面波方法进行，采用完全非共线形式，并包含自旋轨道耦合（SOC），如Elk代码中所实现。交换关联泛函使用局域密度近似（LDA）。所呈现的结果在所有参数方面均已收敛，考虑了9a × √3a超胞和q = (0.064, 0.064, 0)自旋螺旋，一个2 × 6 × 1 k点网格，以及20 Å的真空层间距。在包含SOC的自旋螺旋态中弛豫原子位置需要仔细收敛力（10⁻⁴ a.u）和Kohn-Sham势（10⁻⁷ a.u）。DFT STM图像在312 × 60实空间网格中计算，并内插到更精细的正交网格（800 × 800）以获得更高分辨率的STM图像并计算FFT。
> 
> **Linearized augmented-plane-wave method (LAPW)**: 线性化缀加平面波方法，一种高精度的电子结构计算方法。 **Kohn-Sham potential**: Kohn-Sham势，密度泛函理论中定义的有效单电子势。 **k-mesh**: k点网格，在倒空间中对第一布里渊区进行离散采样的网格。

> ### 第一部分：咱们先弄懂那些“洋词儿”和“专业话”
> 
> 这些词儿看着吓人，其实啊，就是给一些东西起了个学名。
> 
> -   **多铁性 (Multiferroicity)**：您可以把一个材料想象成一个“多功能小能手”。比如，一个材料它既有磁性（像吸铁石），又有铁电性（自带正负极，能产生电场），那它就同时拥有两种“铁”的本领，这就是“多铁性”。
> -   **单层 (Monolayer)**：就是只有一层原子那么薄的“纸片”材料。您看咱们平时用的铅笔芯，它的材料叫石墨，一层一层的。如果能撕下来只有一层，那个就叫单层石墨烯。这篇论文研究的东西，就是撕到只有一层原子的厚度。
> -   **磁自旋螺旋 (Magnetic Spin Spiral)**：咱们把材料里的原子想象成一个个微小的指南针。通常，指南针都指向一个方向，但在这个材料里，这些指南针的指向是转着圈排列的，像螺旋的楼梯一样，这就是“磁自旋螺旋”。
> -   **自旋-轨道耦合 (Spin-Orbit Coupling, SOC)**：这个有点复杂，您就理解为原子内部的“自转”和“公转”之间产生的一种微妙的相互作用力。因为有这个力，那种螺旋排列的磁性，才能“变”出电性来。
> -   **磁电耦合 (Magnetoelectric Coupling)**：这就是“磁”和“电”能互相影响的效应。你用磁铁能改变它的电性，用电场也能改变它的磁性，就跟变戏法一样，这就是这篇论文研究的关键。
> -   **扫描隧道显微镜 (Scanning Tunneling Microscopy, STM)**：这是一个超级厉害的“眼睛”，它能看到一个一个的原子。它不是用光来看，而是用一根极细的探针，像盲人摸象一样，在材料表面“摸”过去，就能把原子级别的凹凸不平给“摸”出来，画成图像。
> -   **密度泛函理论 (Density Functional Theory, DFT)**：这是一个用计算机模拟材料的“超级大脑”。科学家把材料的原子种类和排列方式告诉电脑，它就能算出这个材料大概会有什么样的性质，比如磁性强不强，带不带电。咱们可以把它理解成在电脑里做实验。

> **标题：原子尺度下观察单层碘化镍的多铁性**

> **摘要 (Abstract):** 咱们越研究那些像书本一样可以一层层撕开的“范德华材料”，就越能找到好东西。现在，我们发现了这些材料即使在只有一层原子那么薄的时候，也能有磁性（铁磁性）和电性（铁电性）。最近，有证据表明，在单层的碘化镍（NiI2）里，第一次发现了纯粹的二维多铁性材料。但是，以前用来研究块状材料的多铁性的那些老方法，用在二维材料上就不灵了，很难得到结果。所以，我们需要在原子那么小的尺度上做实验，才能把单层材料的多铁性弄清楚。 在这项研究里，我们用了一种叫“扫描隧道显微镜”的尖端技术，再配合上“密度泛函理论”的计算机模拟，来探测和描述单层碘化镍里的多铁性。我们证明了，碘化镍展现出的这种特殊的多铁性（称为II型多铁性），是由它的磁自旋螺旋结构和强自旋-轨道耦合一起产生的，这使得我们能用扫描隧道显微镜直接“看”到它。而且，我们还通过用外加电场来移动多铁性领域的边界，直接证明了碘化镍的磁电耦合效应。我们的发现，是从微观角度看磁电效应的一种全新视角，为以后在范德华材料和它们的组合结构里，设计出新的多铁性材料铺平了道路。
> 
> **1. 引言 (Introduction)** 多铁性材料，就是那种同时拥有不止一种“铁”的本领的材料。过去这些年，科学家们研究了很多不同的块状多铁性材料，其中大部分是复杂的氧化物。根据这两种本领之间耦合的强弱，可以把多铁性材料分成I型和II型。对于那种既有磁性又有铁电性，并且还表现出强磁电耦合的II型多铁性材料，它们在自旋电子学、数据存储和计算节能这些高科技领域，潜力巨大。但是，想找到一种在足够高的温度下还能表现出强磁电耦合，能拿来做实用器件的材料，一直是个难题。 二维材料的兴起，为我们设计人造多铁性材料提供了新思路。这些材料层与层之间是很弱的范德华力，所以很容易把它们撕到只剩一层，得到一堆不同性质的基本“积木”。我们可以把这些“积木”堆叠、扭转，组成各种组合，这样就能产生很多新奇的现象，其中就包括多铁性。最近，有研究说，块状的碘化镍（NiI2）这种范德华材料，即使被撕到几层甚至只有一层，它的多铁性依然存在。理论分析认为，这种II型多铁性的源头，是碘化镍的磁自旋螺旋结构和碘原子很强的自旋-轨道耦合共同作用的结果。 为了证明二维多铁性确实存在，科学家们对单层和多层碘化镍做了各种光学测量，最终确定它是第一个纯粹的二维多铁性材料，转变温度是-252.15℃（21K）。然而，这些光学测量可能还不够充分，没法证明这种铁电性确实是在二维极限下产生的。更重要的是，以前用来研究块状材料的那种测量磁螺旋和电极化的传统方法，在单层材料上根本用不了。这些因素都说明，单层碘化镍多铁性的起源，还没有在实验上被真正证实，我们必须要在微观尺度上进行测量，才能全面了解它。 在这项工作中，我们展示了在真实空间中，对单层碘化镍多铁性的直接“观察”。我们证明了，这种II型多铁性材料里的磁电耦合，让我们能直接用扫描隧道显微镜来探测和描述它的多铁性。这些实验结果，也得到了基于密度泛函理论的计算机模拟的支持。而且，我们通过用电场控制多铁性领域，为这个二维系统的磁电耦合提供了证据。我们的发现，证明了我们可以用原子级的分辨率来观察范德华材料中的多铁性，这为以后研究人造范德华多铁性组合结构，提供了一套好方法。

> **2. 结果与讨论 (Results and Discussion)** **2.1. 碘化镍中的多铁性** 在块状时，碘化镍在低温下会表现出磁自旋螺旋。计算机模拟表明，这种螺旋结构，是镍原子之间不同的磁交换作用相互竞争的结果。简单说，就是最近邻的镍原子喜欢同向排列（铁磁），而隔一位的邻居喜欢反向排列（反铁磁），这两种力量一较劲，就形成了螺旋。在单层时，没有层间作用，这个螺旋的特性就只由层内的这两种力来决定。 理论分析还表明，单层碘化镍之所以会产生铁电极化，正是因为这种特殊的螺旋磁结构和碘原子强大的自旋-轨道耦合碰到了一起。这个理论预测，当螺旋磁结构存在时，会产生一个净的铁电极化，方向垂直于螺旋传播的方向。这个电极化在空间上不是均匀的，而是有强有弱，其变化周期正好是磁螺旋周期的一半。而这种电极化会引起材料表面电荷分布发生变化，产生一个变化的静电场。这个静电场会调制材料的电子能带，最终在扫描隧道显微镜的图像上形成明暗相间的条纹，让我们能直接“看”到它，从而研究它的多铁性。
