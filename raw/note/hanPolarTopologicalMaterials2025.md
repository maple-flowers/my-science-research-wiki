> [!quote]- <center>Abstract</center>
> 
> Polar topologies possess immense potential to revolutionize ferroelectric technology by offering nontrivial polarization configurations and a range of emergent functionalities, including unique resistive properties, negative capacitance, chirality, and ferroelectricity. Recent advancements in synthesis and characterization techniques have significantly accelerated the exploration of novel polar topological textures. This review highlights key milestones in expanding the topological family, manipulating polar topological textures through multi-field strategies, and uncovering their extraordinary functionalities, while also emphasizing future challenges and research directions. We examine the theoretical foundations and development of polar topological structures in ferroelectric materials, addressing the challenges of experimental realization and the current limitations in understanding multi-field-driven topological phase transitions, which have hindered practical implementation. Finally, this review outlines the scientific and technological prospects of polar topological structures, emphasizing their critical role in advancing materials science and device technology. We hope this review will inspire intensified research efforts that align closely with the practical applications of polar topological structures.
>
>【摘要翻译】极性拓扑具有巨大的潜力，通过提供非平凡的极化配置和一系列新兴功能，包括独特的电阻特性、负电容、手性和铁电性，彻底改变铁电技术。近年来合成和表征技术的发展极大地促进了新型极性拓扑结构的探索。本文重点介绍了拓扑家族的扩展、多领域策略对极性拓扑纹理的操控以及揭示其特殊功能的关键里程碑，同时也强调了未来的挑战和研究方向。我们研究了铁电材料中极性拓扑结构的理论基础和发展，解决了实验实现的挑战和目前理解多场驱动拓扑相变的局限性，这些限制了实际实现。最后，概述了极性拓扑结构的科学和技术前景，强调了其在推动材料科学和器件技术发展中的重要作用。我们希望这篇综述能激励人们加强研究，使之与极性拓扑结构的实际应用紧密结合。

> ## 一、引言
> 
> 在凝聚态物理和材料科学的前沿，**拓扑材料**（Topological materials）因其非平庸的电子能带结构和量子现象备受关注。近年来，这一概念已从电子系统拓展至铁性材料中的序参量（如自旋、极化）空间，催生了**极性拓扑结构**（Polar topological structures）这一新兴领域。与磁性体系中已广泛研究的**斯格明子**（Skyrmions）等自旋拓扑结构相对应，铁电材料中的极化拓扑结构展现出连续旋转的极化矢量，形成如**涡旋**（Vortex）、**斯格明子**、**麦纫**（Meron）等新奇构型，并涌现出局域导电、**负电容**（Negative capacitance，一种可以使材料表现出反常电容响应，有望大幅降低晶体管功耗的物理现象）、手性（Chirality，一个物体与其镜像不重合的几何性质）等非凡功能。
> 
> 本篇发表于《Progress in Materials Science》的综述《Polar topological materials and devices: Prospects and challenges》系统性地梳理了极性拓扑结构从理论预言到实验实现的发展历程。其核心价值在于：1）从能量角度（体自由能、静电学能、弹性能、畴壁能）提出了普适性的设计原理；2）全面总结了多物理场（电、力、光、热）调控这些拓扑结构的最新进展；3）深入剖析了电阻开关、负电容、手性光学、太赫兹动力学等新兴功能；4）明确指出了该领域面临的关键挑战与未来发展方向，为从基础研究走向器件应用架设了桥梁。
> 
> ## 二、论文结构与逻辑概览
> 
> 本综述遵循“从基础到应用，再到展望”的经典逻辑链条，其核心论证结构如下：
> 
> -   **总览与基本原理（引言、第2章）**：首先，作者定义了极性拓扑结构，并回顾其发展历程。接着，文章的核心立论在于，极性拓扑结构的形成是系统总自由能（体自由能、静电学能、弹性能、梯度能）相互竞争与平衡的结果。通过调控这些能量项（如利用尺寸效应、应变工程、去极化场等），可以打破铁电体中极化矢量的晶格对称性约束，实现可控的连续旋转，从而构建出各种拓扑构型。
> -   **材料平台与实现（第2章）**：基于上述设计原理，文章系统归纳了实现极性拓扑结构的三大材料体系：1） **铁电氧化物异质结**（如(PbTiO₃)n/(SrTiO₃)n超晶格），这是研究最深入、成果最丰富的体系；2）**铁电氧化物纳米结构**（如BiFeO₃纳米岛），利用几何约束和自组装技术；3）**其他材料**，包括有机聚合物、液晶、范德华层状材料等，展现了该领域的广阔前景。
> -   **调控与功能特性（第3、4章）**：这两章是全文的重心，承上启下。第三章详细阐述了如何利用**多物理场**（电场、机械力、光场、热场）动态地操控极性拓扑结构，实现不同拓扑态之间的相变。第四章则聚焦于这些拓扑结构所带来的**新兴功能**，如源于畴壁和核心的导电性、负电容效应、手性光学响应和太赫兹波段的集体动力学，并探讨了其在存储器、逻辑器件、光电集成等领域的应用潜力。
> -   **挑战与展望（第5章）**：最后，作者总结了当前研究的五大瓶颈，包括材料体系局限、表征与调控难题、器件集成挑战、多功能耦合探索以及理论框架的完善，为未来研究指明了方向。
> 
> 整个论述逻辑清晰，从宏观的理论框架，到具体的材料体系、调控手段和功能应用，最后落脚于未来的挑战与机遇，构成了一个完整的知识闭环。

> ## 四、正文核心内容剖析
> 
> ### 4.1 研究背景
> 
> 铁电材料因其自发且可翻转的极化，广泛应用于存储器、传感器等。传统铁电畴的极化方向受限于晶格对称性，形成“头对头”或“尾对尾”的带电畴壁，能量较高。而极性拓扑结构，如极化涡旋、斯格明子等，其特征是极化矢量在纳米尺度内连续旋转，避免了高能带电畴壁的形成，是系统能量最低化的结果。这一概念最早受磁性拓扑结构（如Dzyaloshinskii-Moriya相互作用驱动的磁性斯格明子）的启发，但由于铁电体中缺少类似磁性的反对称交换作用，实现极化连续旋转极具挑战。
> 
> ### 4.2 方法模型
> 
> 文章的核心方法论是**时间依赖的Ginzburg-Landau方程**及其相场模拟，它将系统的总自由能（Fp）分解为四项的竞争：
> 
> -   **体自由能 (fbulk)**：由晶格对称性决定，倾向于极化沿特定晶向排列，形成各向异性。
> -   **静电学能 (felec)**：源于去极化场和束缚电荷，倾向于形成闭合的极化构型以消除净电荷。
> -   **弹性能 (felas)**：源于晶格失配、应变等，通过应变-极化耦合影响极化方向。
> -   **梯度能/畴壁能 (fgrad)**：源于极化的空间变化，倾向于扩大极化渐变区域，使畴壁弥散化。
> 
> 实验上，**扫描探针显微镜**（SPM）和**透射电子显微镜**（TEM）是观察和调控拓扑结构的关键工具。SPM可通过针尖施加局域电场/机械力写入拓扑结构，而像差校正的TEM能实现原子尺度的极化矢量成像。
> 
> ### 4.3 实验结果与讨论
> 
> 文章的核心内容围绕设计、调控和功能展开：
> 
> **1. 设计原理与材料体系**
> 
> -   **异质结**：在(PbTiO₃)n/(SrTiO₃)n超晶格中，PbTiO₃层提供铁电性，SrTiO₃层作为绝缘“隔离层”，引入巨大的去极化场。通过精确控制各层厚度（n）和衬底应变，这一平台成功实现了涡旋、斯格明子、麦纫、波阵列等多种拓扑结构。例如，在DyScO₃衬底上，拉伸应变导致PbTiO₃层内形成交替的顺时针/逆时针涡旋对。
> -   **纳米结构**：通过模板法或自组装生长制备的BiFeO₃纳米岛，利用其三维几何约束和界面条件，稳定了中心型畴、涡旋畴，乃至复杂的“所罗门环”（两个嵌套的涡旋）。这些结构中的带电畴壁展现出极高的导电性。
> 
> **2. 多场调控**
> 
> -   **电场调控**：最直接的方法。利用SPM针尖或原位TEM施加电场，可驱动涡旋→波→单畴的转变，或斯格明子尺寸的缩小。在BiFeO₃纳米岛中，电场可在中心畴和涡旋畴之间实现非易失性切换。这种调控通常通过改变束缚电荷的分布，进而改变静电学能来实现。
> -   **机械力场调控**：通过探针压力或柔性衬底，引入局域应变梯度，利用**挠曲电效应**（Flexoelectric effect，应变梯度产生电场的效应）来翻转极化或驱动拓扑相变。例如，在弯曲的SrTiO₃薄膜中，不同的褶皱模式可以产生从非极性态到麦纫状、迷宫状等多种极性结构。
> -   **光场调控**：超快光脉冲可以激发光生载流子，屏蔽束缚电荷，瞬间改变静电学能环境，从而诱导出新的亚稳态拓扑相，如“超晶体”相。涡旋光携带的轨道角动量也能驱动斯格明子的动力学。
> -   **热场调控**：通过改变温度，可以调节朗道自由能的各向异性，从而驱动拓扑相变，如Kosterlitz-Thouless型相变，以及涡旋-波-顺电态的转变。
> 
> **3. 新兴功能特性**
> 
> -   **电阻开关**：带电畴壁（如BiFeO₃纳米岛中的十字形畴壁）和极性涡旋/斯格明子核心，因束缚电荷的积累或载流子浓度的改变，可形成导电通道。其导电态可通过电场进行“开-关”切换，展现出巨大的开关比（>10³）和优异的耐久性，被视为下一代高密度、低功耗存储器和逻辑器件的候选。
> -   **负电容**：在极性涡旋的核心和斯格明子的外围，由于极化处于亚稳态的能量状态，其局部极化-电场响应出现负的斜率（dP/dE < 0），即瞬态负电容效应。当与介电层串联形成异质结时，可观察到稳态负电容，这为突破晶体管“玻尔兹曼暴政”提供了可能。
> -   **手性**：具有轴向极化分量的连续旋转拓扑结构（如涡旋、Bloch型斯格明子）天然具有手性。通过**圆二色性**（CD）和**二次谐波产生-圆二色性**（SHG-CD）等技术，可以探测到这些结构的手性光学信号。更重要的是，通过电场翻转涡旋的手性（如从左旋变为右旋），可以实现手性信号的非易失性电控，这为“电写光读”的新型光电集成芯片铺平了道路。
> -   **太赫兹动力学**：极化涡旋阵列具有独特的集体激发模式，称为“涡旋子”（Vortexon）。通过太赫兹泵浦-X射线衍射探测，研究人员观察到涡旋核心在太赫兹频段（亚皮秒时间尺度）的动态反转和振荡，其频率远高于磁学体系的GHz级动力学，在超高速数据处理上潜力巨大。
>

> 
> 1.  **建立了普适性的设计原理**：明确指出，通过巧妙地调控铁电材料中的体自由能、静电学能、弹性能和梯度能之间的竞争，可以克服晶格对称性的限制，设计并实现各种极性拓扑结构。

> ## 七、学术思考与批判性分析
> 
> 本文作为一篇权威综述，构建了清晰的逻辑框架，但依然存在一些值得深度思考的问题：
> 
> 1.  **“演化”与“本源”的模糊性**：文章通过能量竞争完美解释了拓扑结构的“存在”和“演化”，但这是否真正触及了其“拓扑性”的本源？拓扑保护是一个全局性质，而文中描述的多是局域的能量最小化过程。这种由能量涨落和边界条件驱动的结构，其拓扑保护性的鲁棒性究竟如何？在多大程度上可以类比于量子霍尔效应中的拓扑保护？
>     
> 2.  **多场调控的协同性与冲突**：文章分别讨论了电、力、光、热场的作用，但在实际器件操作中，这些场常常是耦合的（例如，电场会通过逆压电效应产生应力，光场会带来热效应）。如何解耦这些效应，并利用它们的协同作用实现更高效、更精确的调控，是一个研究空白。是否存在一个广义的能量景观，可以统一描述多场耦合下的拓扑相变？
>     
> 3.  **功能性的“层展”与“设计”**：文章强调了拓扑结构带来的新奇功能，如导电性、负电容等。但这些功能是特定拓扑结构“层展”的必然结果，还是可以通过材料工程（如掺杂、界面设计）进行独立“设计”和优化的？例如，导电性是否必须通过带电畴壁实现，能否通过构建特定的能带结构来实现拓扑态的金属性？功能与拓扑结构之间的联系是强耦合还是弱耦合，这决定了器件设计的自由度。
>     
> 4.  **从“现象”到“器件”的鸿沟**：文章列举了众多潜在应用，但离实际器件仍有巨大鸿沟。以高密度存储为例，除了集成度，还需考虑室温下的读写速度（纳秒级）、能耗（飞焦级）、数据保持能力（>10年）和抗疲劳特性（>10¹²次）。这些实用化指标在当前的拓扑体系中尚未得到系统验证。此外，如何实现阵列中单个拓扑态的无串扰、高精度读写，是工程上的巨大挑战。
>     
> 5.  **理论框架的完备性**：文章主要基于经典的Ginzburg-Landau理论进行相场模拟。然而，当拓扑结构尺寸小至几个纳米时，量子效应（如表面效应、量子隧穿）是否会变得显著？现有的连续介质模型是否仍然适用？是否需要发展第一性原理与多尺度相场模拟相结合的新方法，来更准确地捕捉纳米尺度下的拓扑物理？
>

> ## 八、用户问题预测与解答
> 
> **Q1：什么是拓扑电荷？它在极性拓扑结构中是如何计算的？** **A:** 拓扑电荷是一个描述拓扑结构全局性质的整数，在极化体系中，它量化了极化矢量在二维平面内旋转的“圈数”。其计算公式为 ( N = \frac{1}{4\pi} \iint \hat{P} \cdot (\frac{\partial \hat{P}}{\partial x} \times \frac{\partial \hat{P}}{\partial y}) dxdy )，其中(\hat{P})是局域极化方向的单位矢量。这个积分的结果就像一个绕数（winding number）。例如，一个标准的涡旋或反涡旋，其极化矢量绕核心旋转一圈，得到的拓扑电荷为±0.5。而斯格明子，其核心的极化向下，外围的极化向上，中间区域极化连续翻转，绕一圈得到的拓扑电荷为±1。它是区分不同拓扑构型的“量子数”。
> 
> **Q2：为什么极性拓扑结构主要在PbTiO₃/SrTiO₃异质结和BiFeO₃纳米岛中实现？** **A:** 这并非巧合，而是因为这些体系恰好能满足设计原理中苛刻的能量竞争条件。**PbTiO₃/SrTiO₃异质结**：PbTiO₃是强铁电体，提供大的极化；SrTiO₃是顺电体，作为隔离层引入巨大的去极化场，大幅提升了静电学能的作用。通过精确控制原子层厚度的“层厚工程”，可以精细调控弹性能和静电学能的平衡，从而稳定各种拓扑结构。**BiFeO₃纳米岛**：通过自组装生长，可以形成三维受限的纳米岛，其几何边界条件创造了独特的应力状态和去极化场分布。同时，BiFeO₃本身存在大量缺陷电荷，这些电荷的分布会显著影响静电学能，为调控提供了额外的自由度。这两个体系都提供了强大的能量调控杠杆，因此成为研究热点。
> 
> **Q3：文中提到电场调控拓扑相变有“挥发性”和“非挥发性”两种，区别是什么？为什么会有这种区别？** **A:** “挥发性”是指当外加电场撤除后，被电场驱动而形成的拓扑结构会自发回到初始状态，就像一按就弹起的弹簧。“非挥发性”则是指电场撤除后，新形成的拓扑状态能够稳定保持，像被按下去后能锁住的圆珠笔。 产生这种区别的核心原因在于**能量势垒和局域能量最低**。在TEM中的原位实验，样品通常被制备成薄片，其边界条件（如应力）发生了变化，并且施加的电场极强。这个过程可能只改变了系统的能量景观，但初始状态仍是最深的能量最低点，一旦外场消失，系统就会弛豫回去，表现为挥发性。而在SPM实验中，局域的针尖电场可以精确地在一个纳米岛内注入或抽出电荷，或是通过压电力改变局部应力，从而彻底改变了该微小区域的热力学能量景观，使得新的拓扑态成为该条件下的全局能量最低态，因此能够稳定保持，实现非挥发性。这本质上取决于外界刺激是否足以让系统从一个能量最低点跨越势垒，进入另一个能量最低点。
> 
> **Q4：如何真正实现“电写光读”技术？手性信号能作为“0”和“1”来存储信息吗？** **A:** “电写光读”的核心思想是利用电场的高精度和易集成性来写入信息，再利用光学读取的非接触、高速率优点来读取信息。文章中的La掺杂BiFeO₃纳米岛提供了一个绝佳模型：一个电场脉冲可以将一个纳米岛内的涡旋畴从左旋变为右旋（“写”操作），而这两种手性态在SHG-CD光学表征下，呈现出符号相反、强度不同的信号（“读”操作）。因此，我们可以将“左旋态”定义为“0”，“右旋态”定义为“1”，这样每个纳米岛就成为一个可电学写入、光学读出的二进制存储单元。通过自组装技术将这些纳米岛排列成高密度阵列，就构成了一个“电写光读”的存储芯片原型。这种技术有望结合电学存储的非易失性和光学通信的高带宽，在未来的光电融合计算中扮演重要角色。
> 
> **Q5：什么是“涡旋子”（Vortexon）？它与声子有什么关系？** **A:** “涡旋子”是极性涡旋阵列的一种集体激发模式，可以理解为一种特殊的、高度协同的声子模式。在标准的晶体中，声子是原子在其平衡位置附近的集体振动。而在极性涡旋阵列中，每个涡旋是一个由数十个原子组成的、具有特定极化构型的“准粒子”单元。涡旋子描述的是这些涡旋单元作为一个整体的集体振荡，特别是其涡旋性（手性）在亚皮秒时间尺度上的动态翻转。这种运动模式不是单个原子的简单振动，而是整个极化涡旋从顺时针到逆时针的协同扭转，是原子位移和极化旋转的复杂耦合。因此，涡旋子是拓扑结构在有序阵列中产生的一种新的、低频的集体动力学模式，它的发现为在太赫兹频段进行“声子工程”提供了全新的可能性。
> 
> ## 九、专区：便于 Obsidian Dataview 插件调用
>

> ABSTRACT Polar topologies possess immense potential to revolutionize ferroelectric technology by offering nontrivial polarization configurations and a range of emergent functionalities, including unique resistive properties, negative capacitance, chirality, and ferroelectricity. Recent advancements in synthesis and characterization techniques have significantly accelerated the exploration of novel polar topological textures. This review highlights key milestones in expanding the topological family, manipulating polar topological textures through multi-field strategies, and uncovering their extraordinary functionalities, while also emphasizing future challenges and research directions. We examine the theoretical foundations and development of polar topological structures in ferroelectric materials, addressing the challenges of experimental realization and the current limitations in understanding multi-field-driven topological phase transitions, which have hindered practical implementation. Finally, this review outlines the scientific and technological prospects of polar topological structures, emphasizing their critical role in advancing materials science and device technology. We hope this review will inspire intensified research efforts that align closely with the practical applications of polar topological structures.

By leveraging topological protection and unique quasi-particle characteristics, these topological structures provide a fertile ground for uncovering novel physical phenomena, intriguing functionalities, and advancing device designs, holding substantial promise for the development of next-generation information technologies [8–11]. Given that polarization can be readily manipulated by electrical and mechanical fields, substantial efforts have been devoted to tuning polar topological structures and their associated properties, such as electrical [27,33,40,54–56], optical [41,57], and other functionalities [58]. The emergent functionalities of polar topological structures are fundamentally determined by their specific polarization configurations. Enhanced electrical conductivity predominantly occurs in Néel-type skyrmion domains [33] and vertex structures [40], characterized by tail-to-tail or head-to-head polarization arrangements. These configurations facilitate the formation of conductive pathways due to uncompensated bound charges. Additionally, domain cores and walls typically display unconventional dielectric responses, such as localized negative capacitance effects that arise from metastable polarization transitions, which have been observed in polar vortices [59] and skyrmions [51]. Notably, chiral optical phenomena are observed in vortex-type topologies, including vortices [60] and Bloch-type skyrmions [32], where continuous head-to-tail polarization rotations generate quantized circular dichroism signatures directly linked to winding number parity. However, due to current limitations in characterization techniques, the mechanisms underlying multi-field-driven topological phase transition remain unresolved, and the practical application of polar topological structures is significantly lagged behind. Recently, several reviews have focused on elucidating the concept of polar topology, providing summaries of identified polar topological structures [8,10,11]. Despite rapid advancements in this field, especially as the topological family expands across various material systems, including BiFeO3-based materials, ferroelectric polymers, and ferroelectric liquid crystals, there remains a critical need to establish broadly applicable design principles. Additionally, a more comprehensive understanding of the intricate multi-field driven evolution and emergent functionalities of these structures is essential. This review provides a comprehensive overview of polar topological structures, beginning with a historical perspective on their development and analyzing the design principles of polar topological structures in a variety of polar materials. It then delves into an in-depth exploration of multi-field manipulation techniques that enable precise control of these polar topological structures, highlighting their unique functionalities and phenomena, including resistive behaviors [27,33,40], negative capacitance [51,59], chirality [32,57,60,61], terahertz response [41,62,63], and others [64–66]. By systematically analyzing these advancements, this review underscores the transformative potential of polar topological structures in driving both scientific research and technological innovation. Furthermore, it addresses the challenges and opportunities in this field, particularly concerning the integration of these structures into next-generation devices. > Bulk free energy in ferroelectric materials refers to the energy associated with the bulk phases under stress-free boundary conditions. This energy term is fundamentally determined by the crystal symmetry and the thermodynamic behaviors of the material [72]. Polar topological structures in ferroelectrics are typically characterized by the continuous rotation of local dipoles. The inherent coupling poses a significant challenge in engineering the structures that deviate from symmetry constraints, due to the strong polar anisotropy-induced high energy consumption associated with the polarization rotation, making the realization of polar topological structures more challengeable than their magnetic counterparts. In the case of ABO3-type perovskite epitaxial thin films (Fig. 6a), strain engineering, stemming from the controlled lattice mismatch between the film and substrate, is a powerful tool for modulating lattice parameters, oxygen octahedra rotations, and overall crystal symmetry. For example, in (001)-oriented tetragonal phase ferroelectric films, in-plane tensile strain typically induces a transition from c-domain to a-domain (Fig. 6b). Conversely, compressive strain prompts the reverse transition from a-domain to c-domain. When isotropic biaxial strain is applied, these films initially with a-domain, often exhibit complex multi-domain configurations. While under anisotropic biaxial strain, 180° ferroelectric domains are more likely to form, attributed to the coexistence of energetically degenerate ferroelastic variants (Fig. 6c). The elastic energy derived from strain engineering thus facilitates the discovery and manipulation of diverse nontrivial polarization configurations in ferroelectric materials. In recent years, elastic energy has been extensively leveraged to design polar topological structures. Fig. 6d highlights a variety of typical polar topological structures stabilized in PTO-based films, including polar skyrmions [32], vortices [28], vertices [89], dipole waves [43,90], flux-closure domains [23], merons [35], and Bloch points [42], through changing substrates to induce different strain states. In a specific case, Yadav et al. constructed symmetric (SrTiO3)n/(PbTiO3)n superlattice (where n refers to the thickness of SrTiO3 and PbTiO3 layers in unit cells) on DyScO3 (DSO) substrate with a ~ 1.2 % tensile strain and a specific superlattice period [28]. Optimizing the thickness of PTO and STO layer further enabled the observation of polar vortex structures in (SrTiO3)n/(PbTiO3)n/(SrTiO3)n trilayer heterostructures [91]. Additionally, by applying a slight tensile strain with an STO substrate, Das et al. reported the first observation of polar-skyrmion bubbles in PTO layers of (SrTiO3)n/(PbTiO3)n superlattices [32]. Using 4D-STEM, phase-field modelling and second-principles calculations, they comprehensively revealed the three-dimensional polarization configuration of these skyrmion structures, which exhibited a hedgehog-like (Néel) structure overall, while a 'combed hedgehog' (Bloch) skyrmion appeared in the center, imparting chirality to the structure. Building on these breakthrough, electric dipole wave arrays were proposed in PTO/STO superlattice on different rare-earth scandate substrates, through thermodynamic quenching treatments in addition to tensile strain [43,90]. Moreover, applying a large tensile strain with SmScO3 (SSO) substrate has led to the emergence of periodic polar meron structures with topological charge of 0.5 in an ultrathin PTO film [35]. More recently, a PTO thin film with symmetric mechanical boundary conditions, achieved by sandwiching it between two 5 nm-thick SrRuO3 (SRO) layers, was fabricated on an orthorhombic (110)-oriented SSO substrate [42]. This configuration facilitated the first observation of polar Bloch points, which originate from the convergence of two merons (up-convergent and down-divergent). Such intriguing results indicate the promising potential for stabilizing novel polar topological structures in PTO-based films by modulating the mechanical boundary conditions. As displayed in Fig. 6f, the electric dipoles derived from CF2 and –CH2 groups rotate within the lamellar plane, accompanied by the ordered alignment of molecular chains. > 2.2.2. Ferroelectric oxide nanostructures 2.2.2. 铁电氧化物纳米结构
> 
> In the past decade, ferroelectric nanostructures have emerged pivotal elements, not only in the miniaturization and integration of ferroelectric devices but also in the investigation of unique physical phenomena [100–102]. These nanostructures are particularly attractive for exploring polar topological structures due to their size-constrained mechanical, electrical and geometric boundary conditions (Fig. 8a). Despite significant theoretical predictions about polar topological domains in ferroelectric nanostructures, fabricating well-defined oxide nanostructures remains challenging. Using an ultrathin anodic aluminum oxide (AAO) mask, Rodriguez et al. successfully fabricated PZT (40/60) nanodot arrays on SRO-buffered STO substrate [26]. In these nanodots, vortex-like polarization states were observed, demonstrating the potential for stabilizing polar topological structures in ferroelectric nanostructures. Subsequently, a top-down strategy using focused ion beam (FIB) lithography was utilized to fabricate isolated PZT (42/58) nanodots, which successfully stabilized flux-closure domains composed of parallel stripe domains [103]. These pioneering works laid the foundation for further exploration into polar topological structures in nanostructured ferroelectrics. A major breakthrough came in 2017, when Li et al. developed an AAO template-assisted pulsed laser deposition (PLD) method to synthesize high-density arrayed BFO nanodots with dimensions of approximately 60 × 30 nm (diameter × height) [39]. These BFO nanodots exhibited novel topological domains, including center-convergent/divergent domain, double-center domain, and reverse double-center domain, where the topological domains deviate from the intrinsic rhombohedral phase symmetry of BFO. This phenomenon was attributed to the competition among depolarizing effects, polarization-symmetry coupling, and surface strain, which greatly alter the local anisotropy and polarization distribution. Similarly, high crystalline quality PZT (52/48) nanodot arrays, fabricated by the same method, revealed a rich variety of polar topological structures, including antivortices, flux-closure and center divergent/convergent domains [104]. The appearance of fruitful domain patterns can be attributed to the well-designed composition close to MPB and moderate screening, together with the peculiar size and geometry of the PZT nanodots. Further progress was made by Tian et al., who employed a well-ordered polystyrene sphere (PS) array-assisted PLD strategy to create ferroelectric nanodot arrays of varying sizes [55,102,105,106]. BFO nanodots with a diameter of ~ 400 nm and a height of ~ 40 nm exhibit stripe, vortex, and center-type domains, which could be switched among these states by an applied electrical field. As shown in Fig. 8b, the in-plane polarization configurations of center and vortex domains were experimentally observed and simulated. In the center domain, distorted domain walls were driven by the release of large electrostatic energy, thus resulting in a vortex-like core. In contrast, the vortex domain presents a twisted domain wall with a conductive center-convergent core, induced by a brief application of a large bias voltage. This switching behavior resulted from the redistribution of bound charge during polarizing. The complex interplay among intrinsic geometric energy, defects conditions, and external electrostatic energy disturbance employed by tip field, synergistically facilitate the formation of polar topological structures. Moreover, the use of PS template enabled the growth of highly ordered BFO nanoisland arrays with quad-domain structures by promoting nucleation at specific sites, offering a controlled method for stabilizing complex polar topologies [107]. Apart from the template-assisted strategies, self-assembly growth has garnered considerable attention due to its high stability, ultrafine size control, simple procedure and low cost. Self-assembly provides a versatile and cost-effective method to fabricate ferroelectric nanostructures, making it suitable for exploring novel polar topological structures. In 2018, Kim et al. designed a mixed-phase target comprising 65 % BFO and 35 % spinel CoFe2O4 (CFO) and successfully fabricated a BFO-CFO composite film on LaAlO3 (LAO) substrates [108]. Under large compressive strain, BFO nanoplates with lateral sizes of ~ 300 nm were observed in the composite film. Using the angle-resolved PFM technique, the authors reconstructed the complex domain structures, where a buffer ferroelectric domain with a 180°-reversed polarization was built between each pair of ferroelastic quad-domain to reduce the depolarizing energy. Concurrently, Ma et al. reported a self-assembly growth strategy for fabricating ferroelectric nanoislands, such as BFO nanoislands with lateral sizes of 200–600 nm and heights of 10–40 nm, by controlling the growth thermodynamics and kinetics [40,109,110]. > 3.  Multi-fields manipulation of polar topological structures
> 4.  极性拓扑结构的多场操控
> 
> Benefitting from the reversible and nonvolatile switching of ferroelectric polarization driven by external fields, such as electrical, mechanical, optical, and thermal stimuli, the creation, manipulation, and dynamic motion of polar topological structures have garnered significant attention. These progresses not only provide deep physical insights into the behavior of these structures, but also unlock exotic properties that are invaluable for reconfiguring topological states and designing innovative nanoscale ferroelectric devices. Understanding the mechanisms and controlling ferroelectric polarization switching in polar topological structures is critical for integrating these polar structures into next-generation electronic devices, bringing the gap between fundamental science and practical applications.
> 
> 得益于电场、机械、光学和热刺激等外场驱动的铁电极化的可逆和非易失翻转，极性拓扑结构的创建、操控和动态运动引起了极大关注。这些进展不仅为这些结构的行为提供了深刻的物理见解，还揭示了对于重构拓扑态和设计创新纳米级铁电器件非常有价值的奇异特性。理解极性拓扑结构中铁电极化翻转的机制并加以控制，对于将这些极性结构集成到下一代电子器件中至关重要，弥合了基础科学与实际应用之间的差距。
> 
> 3.1. Electrical-field modulation 3.1. 电场调控
> 
> The most common approach to manipulating polar topological structures is through the application of electric fields, which allows for writing, detection, and erasure of these structures by modifying charge carriers, electrostatic energy and domain wall properties. Phase-field simulations have predicted the evolution of polar topological structures and phase transitions under electrical fields in ferroelectric materials. In PTO/STO superlattices, external electric fields can adjust elastic, electrostatic, and gradient energies to reduce skyrmion size, weaken inter-skyrmion interactions, and lower motion barriers [124]. Additionally, by controlling the applied electrical fields, topological transitions from polar skyrmions to different topological states have also been predicted [125,126]. Experimentally, in PTO/STO multilayers with topological polar structures, the real-time atomic resolution observation reveals the topological transformations that occur under the application of large electric fields in STEM. These include transitions from vortex to wave state, and eventually to a polarization-down state as a result of the giant electrostatic energy modulation by the electric field [127] (Fig. 10a). Similarly, in-situ STEM has also shown the lateral movement of chiral vortex cores to form close-pair structures that transform into a/c domain stripes, and eventually into a nonchiral c-domain in PTO/STO superlattices [128]. It is important to note that under strong electric fields, vortex domains in ferroelectric materials typically transition into topologically trivial domain states, with polarization aligning parallel to the applied electric field. These transformations are generally volatile, meaning that once the electric field stimulus is removed, the structure reverts to its original state, whether it is a polar vortex or a polar skyrmion [128–130]. Although the observed behavior can be ascribed to the topological protection characteristic of these structures under specific boundary conditions, the volatile nature of the switching process contradicts the stable polarization expected in ferroelectric materials. Recent studies have confirmed that nonvolatile topological transitions can be observed in larger polar topological structures under different boundary conditions imposed by an SPM probe, in contrast to those transformations driven by a TEM probe. For example, in BFO nanoislands, applying a suitable bias voltage with an SPM tip enables programmable writing of distinct topological states (vortex, center, and wedge) along with control over their conduction states [55] (Fig. 10b). In BFO thin films, radial electric field pulses have been shown to stabilize ferroelectric center states, which exhibit flux closures of antiferromagnetic spin cycloids with distinct entities at their cores depending on the field polarity [64]. Moreover, spiral and flower-like scanning-probe trajectories have been used to achieve the on-demand formation and hierarchical super-switching of intricate polar structures in strained Pb0.6Sr0.4TiO3 films [131]. These results highlight the potential for dynamic control over the complex polarization switching process by considering various electrical boundaries within the system. However, it is essential to recognize that traditional electric switching methods often come with undesirable side effects, such as leakage currents, dielectric breakdown, and charge injection, which could limit the experimental realization of topological phase transitions and hinder the practical application of polarization switching in devices. These challenges underscore the necessity for developing voltage-free strategies, such as mechanical, optical, or thermal modulation, to achieve polarization switching without compromising the integrity of the material.
> 
> 操控极性拓扑结构最常用的方法是通过施加电场，这允许通过修改电荷载流子、静电能和畴壁特性来写入、检测和擦除这些结构。相场模拟预测了铁电材料中极性拓扑结构在电场下的演化和相变。在PTO/STO超晶格中，外部电场可以调整弹性、静电和梯度能量来减小斯格明子尺寸、减弱斯格明子间相互作用并降低运动势垒[124]。此外，通过控制施加的电场，也预测了从极性斯格明子到不同拓扑态的拓扑转变[125,126]。在实验上，在具有拓扑极性结构的PTO/STO多层中，实时的原子分辨率观察揭示了在STEM中施加大的电场下发生的拓扑转变。这些包括从涡旋到波态，最终由于电场产生的巨大静电能调控而转变为极化向下态[127]（图10a）。类似地，原位STEM也显示了手性涡旋核心的横向运动，形成近对结构，转变为a/c畴条纹，最终在PTO/STO超晶格中转变为非手性c畴[128]。需要注意的是，在强电场下，铁电材料中的涡旋畴通常转变为拓扑平庸的畴态，极化平行于施加的电场排列。这些转变通常是易失的，意味着一旦电场刺激被移除，结构就恢复到其原始状态，无论是极性涡旋还是极性斯格明子[128–130]。尽管观察到的行为可归因于这些结构在特定边界条件下的拓扑保护特性，但翻转过程的易失性与铁电材料中预期的稳定极化相矛盾。最近的研究已证实，与TEM探针驱动的转变相比，在SPM探针施加的不同边界条件下，较大的极性拓扑结构中可以观察到非易失拓扑转变。例如，在BFO纳米岛中，使用SPM探针施加合适的偏压使得可编程写入不同的拓扑态（涡旋、中心和楔形）以及控制其导电态成为可能[55]（图10b）。在BFO薄膜中，径向电场脉冲已被证明可以稳定铁电中心态，这些态表现出反铁磁自旋摆线的通量闭合，其核心处根据场极性具有不同的实体[64]。此外，螺旋和花状扫描探针轨迹已被用于在应变Pb0.6Sr0.4TiO3薄膜中实现复杂极性结构的按需形成和分层超级翻转[131]。这些结果突显了通过考虑系统内各种电学边界条件来动态控制复杂极化翻转过程的潜力。然而，必须认识到，传统的电学翻转方法通常伴随着不良副作用，如漏电流、介电击穿和电荷注入，这可能限制拓扑相变的实验实现并阻碍极化翻转在器件中的实际应用。这些挑战强调需要开发无电压策略，如机械、光学或热调控，以在不损害材料完整性的情况下实现极化翻转。

> 3.3. Optical-field modulation 3.3. 光场调控
> 
> Light, owing to its robust interaction with ferroic order parameters through electromagnetic radiation (including elasto-, electro-, and magneto-optical effects), has gained prominence as both a versatile non-contact probe and a powerful tool for manipulating ferroic order parameters [136]. Specifically, the effects of light on ferroelectric polarization, which hold potential for controlling the polar topological structures, can be broadly categorized into two main types: electronic effect and thermal effect. By using sub-picosecond optical pulse excitation with photons above the bandgap energy, the electrostatic landscape can be altered to facilitate the emergence of a stable structure with long-range nanoscale polar order, consisting of polar vortices that are three-dimensionally intertwined in PTO/STO superlattices [50]. Fig. 10d depicts the formation and transition of a supercrystal phase in these superlattices, which contain ferroelectric, ferroelastic, and polar vortex sub-regions, induced by sub-picosecond optical pulses and thermal annealing. Similarly, single-shot optical pump X-ray probe measurements have captured the emergence of a persistent polar vortex supercrystal in PTO/STO heterostructures [137]. Such ultrafast light-induced phase transitions are ascribed to the perturbance of the delicate equilibrium between intrinsic built-in electrostatic and elastic frustrations by photoinduced charges. Additionally, above-bandgap light-excited charge carriers provide charge compensation for polarization-bound charges and have also been proven to contribute to lattice thermal energy, both of which are essential for stabilizing the three-dimensionally periodic nanoscale polar structures in the PTO/STO ferroelectric superlattice [138]. Moreover, twisted light, which carries a nonzero orbital angular momentum, has been found to excite and drive dynamical polar skyrmions at interfacial layers in ferroelectric PZT ultrathin films [139]. The nonzero winding number of the twisted light is transferred to the films, enabling the tuning of the intrinsic characteristics of polar skyrmions, such as their size and switching speed, by controlling the external beam parameters. Besides, mid-infrared laser light has also been demonstrated the capability to drive resonant switching of vortex tube orientation in vortex crystals in ultrathin PZT(40/60) films [140]. Therefore, optical field with varied wavelengths, powers, and other properties can trigger multiple coupling effects among electrons, phonons, and polarization, which play a significant role in manipulating polar topological structures and ferroelectrics.

> 3.4. Thermal-field modulation 3.4. 热场调控
> 
> Thermal modulation, typical achieved by varying temperature, is another effective method to manipulate the degree of long-range order in polar structures, which can be exploited in optical memories [141], sensors [142], and so on. By varying the temperature, thermal phase transition and thermal expansion usually drive the change of Landu energy, symmetry anisotropy, and elastic energy. In P(VDF-TrFE), thermal modulation has been employed to control toroidal polar topologies through the pyrotoroidic transition [52]. And in PTO/STO superlattice, the real-time dynamic evolution of the polar topologies induced by thermal field has been observed based on in-situ STEM [143]. During heating, the initial polar vortices unwound the helix and transformed into polar wave and eventually into c-domain. Additionally, by designing the cooling pathways from different temperature, different polar topologies can be obtained, including polar vortex and wave. Kosterlitz-Thouless-like phase transitions have also been demonstrated in PTO/STO superlattices through temperature changes [31]. Similarly, a reversible transition from a skyrmion state to a two-dimensional tetratic lattice of merons, accompanied by a change in chirality, has been observed in (PTO16/STO16)8 membranes under varying temperature and elastic boundary conditions [144]. Besides the thermal phase transitions, external heating has also been predicted to induce the expansion of crystal lattice, thereby reducing both in-plane and out-of-plane polarization and lowering the skyrmion migration barrier without altering the skyrmion shape [124]. Although the thermal-field modulation has sparked extensive attention in multiple fields including phonon physics and infrared optics, researches are still lagged behind due to the limited characterization probe techniques.
> 
> 热调控通常通过改变温度来实现，是操控极性结构长程有序程度的另一种有效方法，可用于光学存储器[141]、传感器[142]等。通过改变温度，热相变和热膨胀通常驱动朗道能量、对称各向异性和弹性能的变化。在P（VDF-TrFE）中，热调控已被用于通过热环形转变来控制环形极性拓扑[52]。在PTO/STO超晶格中，基于原位STEM观察到了热场诱导的极性拓扑的实时动态演化[143]。在加热过程中，初始的极性涡旋解开螺旋并转变为极性波，最终转变为c畴。此外，通过设计从不同温度的冷却路径，可以获得不同的极性拓扑，包括极性涡旋和波。在PTO/STO超晶格中也通过温度变化证明了类Kosterlitz-Thouless相变[31]。类似地，在变温和弹性边界条件下，在（PTO16/STO16）8膜中观察到从斯格明子态到二维四方半子晶格的可逆转变，伴随着手性的变化[144]。除了热相变外，外部加热也被预测能够诱导晶格膨胀，从而减少面内和面外极化，并降低斯格明子迁移势垒而不改变斯格明子形状[124]。尽管热场调控在包括声子物理和红外光学的多个领域引发了广泛关注，但由于表征探针技术的限制，研究仍然滞后。 **Kosterlitz-Thouless phase transition**: Kosterlitz-Thouless相变，二维系统中拓扑缺陷（涡旋-反涡旋对）解束缚导致的相变。

> 4.  Functionalities of polar topological structures
> 5.  极性拓扑结构的功能性
> 
> Polar topological structures, with their inherent topological protection and locally broken symmetry, hold promising potential for the development of multifunctional materials and devices. The switchable polarization characteristics of these structures endow them with a wide range of exceptional properties, including electric [33,40,55,65,145], optic [57,84,129], acoustic [62,146], mechanic [58,66], and magnetic functionalities [15,95], that make them highly attractive compared to their magnetic counterparts. Furthermore, the size spectrum of polar topological structures, spanning from several nanometers to micrometers, further expands their applicability across various applications.
> 
> 极性拓扑结构以其固有的拓扑保护和局部破缺的对称性，为多功能材料和器件的发展带来了可观的潜力。这些结构的可切换极化特性赋予它们广泛的卓越性能，包括电学[33,40,55,65,145]、光学[57,84,129]、声学[62,146]、力学[58,66]和磁性功能[15,95]，使其与磁性对应物相比极具吸引力。此外，极性拓扑结构的尺寸范围从几纳米到微米，进一步扩展了其在各种应用中的适用性。

Since the discovery of polar topological structures with unexpected electronic and dielectric properties, these nanoscale topological units have attracted considerable interests in the development of topological electronics. Moreover, the topologically protected property and ultrafine size of polar topological structures enable them to serve as independent memory units for high density resistive devices. > 4.2. Negative capacitance effects 4.2. 负电容效应
> 
> Among the most attractive functionalities of polar topological structures, negative capacitance has garnered extensive attention due to its rich underlying physics as well as promising applications in low-power, steep-switching and voltage-scalable electronics [31,51,59,150]. Although the nature of negative capacitance in ferroelectrics has been a subject of intense debate, intrinsic negative capacitance effects have been observed under specific conditions [151,152]. This phenomenon typically originates from the double-well profile of the ferroelectric polarization-energy landscape, which contains an unstable region characterized by negative dP/dE in isolated ferroelectric layer, often referred to as the transient negative capacitance effect. Recently, imperfect screening, driven by ferroelectric heterostructures composed of dielectric/semiconductor and ferroelectric layers, has been engineered to manipulate the free energy of the entire system, wherein the double-well energy curve of ferroelectric layer combined with the parabolic potential of the screen layer result in quasi-static (steady) negative capacitance effect. Theoretically, the negative capacitance effect is ubiquitous in multidomain ferroelectric heterostructures, where the large susceptibility of domain walls can be amplified in the vicinity of interfaces with screen layers [151]. Since the discovery of polar topological structures, the unique energy states found in local crystal structures, such as domain cores and walls, have provided favorable platforms for investigating ferroelectric negative capacitance effects. Remarkably, the steady negative capacitance state was first observed microscopically in the vortex cores of PTO/STO superlattices, as well as in the domain walls of PTO layers with suppressed polarization [59]. The authors attributed the emergence of negative permittivity at the vortex core and the higher susceptibility at the domain walls/vortex cores to their distinct polarization transition compared to the domains. Therefore, by engineering the energies of the system to adjust the size of the domain walls, the negative capacitance can be modulated. Apart from the vortex cores, local negative permittivity has also been observed at the periphery of polar skyrmions [51]. Consequently, polar topological structures offer a new opportunity for inducing steady negative capacitance in nanoscale region, owing to the exotic energetic states at the topological cores or domain walls. These breakthroughs have generated significant interest in the potential for negative capacitance to emerge in relaxor ferroelectrics and other ferroelectrics with a large density of domain walls, extending beyond polar topological structures.

> 4.4. Terahertz dynamics 4.4. 太赫兹动力学
> 
> Terahertz (THz) technology, which operates in the frequency range between infrared and microwaves (0.1–30 THz), has made significant strides in the past two decades [160–162]. With its unique spectral properties, THz radiation promises a wide array of cutting-edge applications, particularly in information and communication technology, biology and medical sciences, high-resolution radar and imaging [162]. Notably, THz spectroscopy has proven invaluable for elucidating intermolecular interactions, which are pivotal in determining the properties of numerous condensed phase materials [163]. As an integral component of condensed matter systems, ferroelectrics have garnered particular attention in the field of THz dynamics. This is largely due to the fact that the ionic and molecular motions inherent in ferroelectricity typically occur on the picosecond timescale, making them accessible to THz spectroscopic investigations [164]. Motivated by the great potential in ferroelectric THz dynamics, extensive research efforts have been directed toward understanding the interaction between ferroelectrics and THz radiation [62,146,165–171], which have steadily propelling THz research into the spotlight. Following the predictions of polar topological structures in ferroelectrics, it has been hypothesized that the coupling of ferroelectric domains with phonons could lead to ultrafast dynamics in the THz regime, potentially surpassing the capabilities of their magnetic counterparts, which primarily function in the gigahertz (GHz) regime [62,140,146,172–177]. In particular, the investigation of whether these newly identified topological structures can accommodate novel soft modes and how their dynamics behavior on ultrafast timescales has become a critical area of research, which has important implications for both the advancement of fundamental physics and the development of innovative device applications. Polar vortex, as a prototypical topological structure with continuously rotation of polarization vectors around a core, has been investigated both theoretically [140,146,177] and experimentally [62]. Fig. 13a displays typical vortex arrays including neighboring clockwise and counter-clockwise vortices, which originate from the +c/-c domain or c/a domains. Each polar vortex in ordered arrays, with clockwise or counter-clockwise vorticity, can be identified as a periodic unit that resembles a linear chain of balls connected by elastic springs [178]. By utilizing THz pump and hard X-ray diffraction probe techniques based on synchrotron radiation, Li et al. firstly observed the collective motion of polarization vortices directly on picosecond timescales [62] (Fig. 13b). They also clearly demonstrated the dynamic reversal of the vortex vorticity sign from clockwise to counter-clockwise (Fig. 13c) and observed the diversity of the vortex dynamics at different modes (Fig. 13d), indicating the transient vertical motion of the vortex unit (vortexon). Therefore, due to the unique connectivity of electric polarization and its coupling with the lattice, novel collective vibration modes were revealed. Further work suggested that the elastic modulation of the energy function and spring constants could lead to a condensation of the vortexon mode [177]. These fascinating breakthroughs lay the groundwork for innovative phonon engineering within complex oxide materials, holding promise to enable direct electric-field manipulation of phonon dynamics, which could revolutionize high-speed, high-density data processing and storage technologies. Despite the relatively slow progress in experimental ferroelectric THz dynamics, emerging technologies including THz time-domain spectroscopy (THz-TDS) and THz field-induced second harmonic effect are anticipated to bridge the gap between ferroelectrics and THz optoelectronics, paving the way for exciting future applications.

> 4.5. Ferroelectricity and energy storage 4.5. 铁电性与能量存储
> 
> Polar topological structures exhibit intrinsic ferroelectricity and stimulus-responsive dynamics through their switchable polarization states, positioning them as transformative candidates for next-generation nonvolatile memory and high-density energy storage systems. Unlike traditional ferroelectrics governed by discrete dipole inversion, these topologically confined systems enable anomalous polarization evolution via continuous vector rotation and coherent domain restructuring while preserving topological protection. Recently, Behera and colleagues reported characteristic pinched polarization–electric field (P-E) hysteresis loops in STO20/PTO20/STO20 trilayers containing polar vortex arrays [54]. By employing interdigitated electrodes to generate in-plane electric fields orthogonal to vortex tube axes, they identified two distinct switching regimes: (1) reversible out-of-plane polarization modulation under subcritical fields, and (2) irreversible domain wall propagation beyond threshold fields. Notably, the observed collective domain reconfiguration under out-of-plane biasing fundamentally differs from the discrete dipole switching seen in conventional ferroelectrics. This dual-mode switching mechanism enables controlled reduction of remnant polarization, offering new design principles for enhanced dielectric energy storage capacity. However, the integration of non-polar STO layers in PTO/STO heterostructures restricts device functionality due to limited electrical responsiveness, leading to a substantial reduction in saturation polarization and compromising practical performance. To address this issue, Kavle et al. engineered (PbTiO3)n/(PbxSr1-xTiO3)n heterostructures, where the PbxSr1-xTiO3 (x < 0.5) layer stabilizes polar vortices by providing in-plane polarization boundary conditions [179]. Increasing the Sr content in PbxSr1-xTiO3 layer enhances both out-of-plane saturation polarization and electric field-induced strain, coinciding with structural transitions: vortices convert to c-domains in the PTO layer, while a-domains transform into c-domains in the PbxSr1-xTiO3 layer. Analogous pinched P-E loops in these systems were attributed to vortex unit migration and subsequent transitions from polar vortices to a-domains. Collectively, these findings reveal that topologically protected polar structures exhibit cooperative dynamical behavior under moderate fields but degrade under extreme fields, highlighting their distinct ferroelectric properties compared to traditional paradigms. > 5.  Outlook
> 6.  展望
> 
> Polar topological materials, distinguished by their diverse polarization structures, have emerged as a pivotal research focus over the past decade, offering both fundamental scientific insights and promising technological applications (Fig. 14). The intricate interplay among bulk free energy, domain wall energy, elastic energy, and electrostatic energy in ferroelectrics plays a crucial role in designing these polar topological structures. This review systematically highlights significant advancements in the field and establishes key principles for developing polar topological materials. As this material family continues to expand, understanding and controlling the evolution of these structures under external stimuli remain fundamental to their applications. Furthermore, the novel functionalities arising from multi-field interactions offer exciting opportunities for next-generation applications in electronics, photonics, and magnetics. Despite rapid progress in theoretical conception and experimental realization, several challenges and unresolved questions persist, requiring further investigation to propel the field forward.
> 
> 极性拓扑材料以其多样化的极化结构为特征，在过去十年中已成为一个关键研究焦点，提供了基础科学见解和有前景的技术应用（图14）。铁电体中体自由能、畴壁能、弹性能和静电能之间复杂的相互作用在设计这些极性拓扑结构中起着关键作用。本综述系统性地突出了该领域的重大进展，并建立了发展极性拓扑材料的关键原则。随着这个材料家族的持续扩展，理解和控制这些结构在外部刺激下的演化仍然是其应用的基础。此外，多场相互作用产生的新颖功能为电子学、光子学和磁学中的下一代应用提供了激动人心的机会。尽管理论构想和实验实现取得了快速进展，但仍存在几个挑战和未解决的问题，需要进一步研究以推动该领域向前发展。
> 
> (1) While substantial progress has been made in exploring new polar topological structures, PTO-based systems remain at the forefront of research. Considering the demand for lead-free materials and the development of next-generation information devices, it is highly desirable to engineer polar topological materials compatible with existing CMOS process technologies, including fluorite-based ferroelectrics (e.g., HfO2), wurtzite-structured aluminum nitride (AlN), freestanding oxide membranes, and other emerging materials. Moreover, the development and understanding of polarization switching behaviors driven by external stimuli remain in their early stages. In PTO-based heterostructures, domain evolution induced by electric fields can be either volatile or nonvolatile. Given the intrinsic topological protection characteristic of topological materials, further investigation is required to elucidate the relationship between topological invariance and volatile or nonvolatile behavior. Additionally, a deeper understanding is also needed to determine whether these novel polar topological structures should be classified as "polar materials" or "ferroelectric materials".
> 
> (1) 虽然在探索新的极性拓扑结构方面取得了实质性进展，但PTO基系统仍处于研究前沿。考虑到对无铅材料的需求和下一代信息器件的发展，迫切需要设计与现有CMOS工艺技术兼容的极性拓扑材料，包括萤石基铁电体（如HfO2）、纤锌矿结构氮化铝（AlN）、自支撑氧化物膜和其他新兴材料。此外，对外部刺激驱动的极化翻转行为的发展和理解仍处于早期阶段。在PTO基异质结构中，电场诱导的畴演化可以是易失的或非易失的。鉴于拓扑材料固有的拓扑保护特性，需要进一步研究以阐明拓扑不变性与易失或非易失行为之间的关系。此外，还需要更深入的理解来确定这些新型极性拓扑结构应被归类为"极性材料"还是"铁电材料"。

> (3) For the practical implementation of polar topological structures in next-generation information storage and logic processing units, several critical challenges remain beyond CMOS compatibility. First, since reported polar topological structures are typically interconnected, it is essential to develop methods for manipulating individual domains without disrupting the surrounding polar structure, enabling precise control of single storage or logic units. Additionally, achieving polar topological structures with a regular arrangement, uniform size, and sufficiently small dimensions is crucial to meet high-density requirements. Finally, a deeper understanding of the dynamic evolution of these structure under electric stimuli is highly desirable to assess the read/write speed and power consumption of polar topologies in information devices, as well as to enhance the retention and endurance performance.

> (3) 对于极性拓扑结构在下一代信息存储和逻辑处理单元中的实际应用，除了CMOS兼容性之外，还存在几个关键挑战。首先，由于报道的极性拓扑结构通常是相互连接的，因此开发在不破坏周围极性结构的情况下操控单个畴的方法至关重要，以实现对单个存储或逻辑单元的精确控制。此外，实现具有规则排列、均匀尺寸和足够小尺寸的极性拓扑结构对于满足高密度要求至关重要。最后，更深入地理解这些结构在电刺激下的动态演化对于评估信息器件中极性拓扑的读写速度和功耗以及增强保持和耐久性能是非常可取的。

> (4) Polar topological structures have demonstrated numerous unexpected functionalities, presenting significant potential for the development of novel devices. Given the multi-field coupling effect observed in various functional materials, similar effects or multifunctionality can be reasonably anticipated in polar topological materials. A recent breakthrough has revealed the spatial coexistence of polar and antiferromagnetic topological structures in BFO films, referred to as multiferroic topological solitons [64]. Investigating the underlying physics and functionalities of these novel multiferroic topological structures is expected to unlock new possibilities for advanced magnetoelectric devices. Future research should prioritize the design and development of devices based on these polar topological structures, including transistors, memory devices, optoelectronic components and terahertz technology, to leverage their unique properties for next-generation applications.
> 
> (4) 极性拓扑结构已经展示了许多意想不到的功能，为新型器件的发展带来了重大潜力。鉴于在各种功能材料中观察到的多场耦合效应，可以合理地预期极性拓扑材料中会出现类似的效应或多功能性。最近的一项突破揭示了BFO薄膜中极性和反铁磁拓扑结构的空间共存，被称为多铁性拓扑孤子[64]。研究这些新型多铁性拓扑结构的基础物理和功能有望为先进磁电器件解锁新的可能性。未来的研究应优先考虑基于这些极性拓扑结构的器件的设计和开发，包括晶体管、存储器件、光电子元件和太赫兹技术，以利用其独特性质进行下一代应用。 **Multiferroic**: 多铁性，同时具有两种或以上铁性序参量（如铁电性和铁磁性）的材料。

> (5) Drawing parallels with magnetic topological systems, polar topological structures have rapidly emerged as a frontier in condensed matter physics over the past decade. These systems exhibit fundamental similarities to their magnetic counterparts, as both feature topologically protected textures governed by ordered parameter configurations. This cross-disciplinary synergy provides fertile ground for translating well-established magnetic phenomena into polar systems. A notable example is the recent theoretical prediction [120,181] and subsequent experimental verification of electric DMI stabilization in ultrathin BFO films, which enables room-temperature polar skyrmion lattices [182]. While electric DMI was historically considered physical implausible, unlike its magnetic counterpart which arises from spin-orbit coupling, emerging evidence suggests that oxygen octahedral tilts in ABO3 perovskites and oxygen sublattice distortions in hafnia provide a mechanistic pathway for electric DMI. These lattice symmetry-breaking mechanisms stabilize non-collinear dipole arrangements, facilitating the formation of unconventional polar textures such as skyrmions. These discoveries hint at an expansive unexplored landscape, including exotic topological configurations and dynamic phase transitions.
> 
> (5) 与磁性拓扑系统相比，极性拓扑结构在过去十年中迅速成为凝聚态物理学的前沿。这些系统与其磁性对应物表现出基本的相似性，因为两者都具有由有序参量构型统治的拓扑保护纹理。这种跨学科协同作用为将成熟的磁学现象转化为极性系统提供了沃土。一个显著的例子是最近的理论预测[120,181]和随后的实验验证了超薄BFO薄膜中电DMI的稳定化，这使得室温极性斯格明子晶格成为可能[182]。虽然电DMI在历史上被认为在物理上不可行，与其源自自旋-轨道耦合的磁性对应物不同，但新出现的证据表明，ABO3钙钛矿中的氧八面体倾斜和氧化铪中的氧亚晶格畸变为电DMI提供了机制途径。这些晶格对称性破缺机制稳定了非共线偶极子排列，促进了如斯格明子等非常规极性纹理的形成。这些发现暗示了一个广阔未开发的领域，包括奇异的拓扑构型和动态相变。
