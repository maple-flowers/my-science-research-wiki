> <span class="highlight" style="background-color: #5fb236">MXenes, composed of transition metal layers with various surface passivation groups, exhibit diverse magnetic and electron transport properties essential for miniaturized spintronic devices. Through density functional theory calculations, we reveal that the electronic properties of Mn2NOT (T = O, F, OH, Cl) MXenes, i.e., half-metallicity and semiconductivity, are determined by the energy-level separation between two side Mn atoms arising from the distinctive two-surface passivation, in contrast to other typical 2D magnetic materials like transition metal dichalcogenides that are determined by the local metallic atom coordination symmetry. The separation degree of energy levels decides the electron configuration of Mn 3d orbitals and the Fermi-level position, thereby determining the half-metallicity or semiconductivity. This mechanism enables dynamic control methods for tuning electronic and magnetic properties. For instance, charge (electron or hole) doping and applying uniaxial strain can drive transitions between semiconductor and half-metal states by modulating the Mn energy levels, Mn-N electrostatic potential, and Mn 3d orbitals filling at two surface sides. Our findings reveal the physical origins of MXenes’ electromagnetic properties and offer strategies for designing tunable and highly integrated spintronic devices.</span>
> 
> MXene 由具有各种表面钝化基团的过渡金属层组成，具有微型自旋电子器件所必需的多种磁性和电子传输特性。通过密度泛函理论计算，我们揭示了Mn2NOT (T = O, F, OH, Cl) MXenes的电子特性，即半金属性和半导电性，是由独特的双表面钝化引起的两侧Mn原子之间的能级分离决定的，而其他典型的二维磁性材料如过渡金属二硫属化物则由局部金属原子配位对称性决定。能级的分离程度决定了Mn 3d轨道的电子排布和费米能级位置，从而决定了半金属性或半导体性。这种机制使得动态控制方法能够用于调整电子和磁性特性。例如，电荷（电子或空穴）掺杂和施加单轴应变可以通过调制 Mn 能级、Mn-N 静电势和填充在两个表面侧的 Mn 3d 轨道来驱动半导体和半金属态之间的转变。我们的研究结果揭示了 MXene 电磁特性的物理起源，并为设计可调谐和高度集成的自旋电子器件提供了策略。【👈 译】 ([p1](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/5FVMXPEI?page=1&annotation=H5TKG2WS))

> ## 一、引言
> 
> 随着信息技术的飞速发展，**自旋电子学**（Spintronics）作为一种利用电子自旋属性进行信息处理和存储的前沿技术，正受到广泛关注。然而，传统基于多层膜异质结构的自旋电子器件面临界面缺陷和制造复杂性等挑战。近年来，以**过渡金属二硫化物**（TMDs）和**MXenes**为代表的二维材料，因其丰富的电子和磁学性质，为构建微型化、高性能自旋电子器件提供了新的平台。
> 
> 在这篇文献中，作者聚焦于一类典型的MXene材料——**Mn₂NT**（T = O, F, OH, Cl）。MXenes的电子特性（如金属性、半导体性或半金属性）对其在自旋电子学中的应用至关重要。此前的研究表明，表面钝化可以调控MXenes的性质，但其内在的物理机制，尤其是在**双面不对称钝化**（Janus结构）的情况下，仍不清晰，甚至存在理论预测与实验/计算结果相矛盾的情况（例如对Mn₂NOF是金属还是半导体的预测分歧）。
> 
> 本文的核心价值在于，通过第一性原理计算，揭示了决定Mn₂N基MXenes电子性质（半金属性或半导体性）的全新物理机制——**两侧金属层Mn原子的3d能级分离程度**，而非传统认知中的局域配位环境。这一发现不仅澄清了长期存在的争议，还据此提出了通过**电荷掺杂**和**单轴应变**动态调控其电子性质的有效策略，为设计可调谐、高集成度的自旋电子器件提供了理论指导和新思路。
>

> ## 四、正文核心内容剖析
> 
> ### 1. 研究背景与问题提出
> 
> 作者指出，虽然TMDs和MXenes都是热门的二维自旋电子材料，但TMDs的物性由中心金属原子在局域配位环境（如八面体或三棱柱）下的d轨道分裂和电子占据决定，其相变（如半导体到金属）需要苛刻的条件（如高应变、高电荷掺杂）。相比之下，MXenes具有更高的居里温度和更易通过表面修饰调控的电子性质。然而，现有的基于“局域对称性”的晶体场理论在解释Janus MXenes的电子性质时遇到了困难，例如错误预测了Mn₂NOF为金属。因此，揭示其内在的、普适的调控机制是亟待解决的科学问题。
> 
> ### 2. 方法模型与实验设计
> 
> -   **计算方法**：采用基于**密度泛函理论**（DFT）的维也纳从头算模拟软件包（VASP）。核心计算设置包括：
>     -   **泛函**：使用GGA-PBE交换关联泛函，并采用**DFT+U**方法（U=4 eV，并对U=5 eV进行了验证）处理Mn 3d电子的强关联效应。
>     -   **结构模型**：构建了单层Mn₂N，并使其一侧被O钝化，另一侧被O、F、OH、Cl钝化，形成对称和不对称（Janus）结构。所有计算均采用矩形晶胞，并施加30 Å的真空层以消除周期性相互作用。
> -   **验证性实验**：
>     -   **磁基态确定**：通过计算不同磁构型（铁磁、反铁磁）的能量，确认所有结构的铁磁态为最稳定基态。
>     -   **稳定性验证**：通过300K下的从头算分子动力学模拟，确认了结构的热力学稳定性。
>     -   **电子结构计算**：计算了态密度、投影能带、静电势和电子局域函数，以系统性分析电子性质及其起源。
> 
> ### 3. 实验结果与讨论
> 
> -   **核心发现：超越局域对称性的新机制**
>     1.  **现象**：计算确认Mn₂NO₂和Mn₂NOCl为半金属，而Mn₂NOF和Mn₂NOOH为半导体。
>     2.  **证伪**：通过分析所有结构中Mn的3d轨道PDOS，发现它们都遵循C₃ᵥ对称性下的分裂模式（`a1`， `eI`， `eII`），其简并度并未发生根本性改变。这表明，传统的“局域配位对称性决定d轨道分裂”理论无法解释观察到的电子性质差异。
>     3.  **立论**：提出新机制——**“3d能级分离度”**。核心逻辑是：不同表面钝化原子（O、F、OH、Cl）具有不同的电负性和化学硬度，导致两侧Mn原子的3d轨道能级发生不同程度的相对位移。
>         -   **能级分离小（如Mn₂NO₂， Mn₂NOCl）**：两侧Mn原子向N原子转移的电荷量相当，导致Mn氧化态相似（均为+3.5），`eI`轨道均为部分填充，费米能级穿过，呈现半金属性。
>         -   **能级分离大（如Mn₂NOF， Mn₂NOOH）**：F/OH侧电负性更强，导致该侧Mn（MnF）的3d能级被拉低，而另一侧Mn（MnO）的3d能级被推高。这导致电荷重新分配：MnO向N转移更多电子，自身变为+4价（3d³），`eI`轨道全空；MnF则转移较少电子，变为+2价（3d⁵），所有轨道全满。费米能级恰好位于全满和全空的`eI`轨道之间，从而打开带隙，呈现半导体性。
> -   **调控策略的实现**： 基于上述机制，提出了两种动态调控电子性质的方法：
>     1.  **电荷掺杂**：直接改变3d轨道填充数。向半导体Mn₂NOF注入电子，可填充空的MnO-`eI`轨道；注入空穴，可清空满的MnF-`eI`轨道。两者都能使费米能级重新切割`eI`轨道，使其恢复半金属性。
>     2.  **单轴应变**：间接调控能级分离和电荷转移。施加拉伸应变，可减弱F侧对电子的强吸引，使得电荷从MnF回流至MnO，减小了两侧能级分离，最终使`eI`轨道再次部分填充，实现了从半导体到半金属的转变。压缩应变则效果相反，加剧了电荷分离，增大了带隙。
> 
> ## 五、结论与贡献
> 
> **核心结论**： 本文揭示了Mn₂N MXenes（Mn₂NOT）中半金属性与半导体性的物理起源并非传统的局域金属原子配位对称性，而是由**两侧Mn层3d能级的分离程度**决定。这种能级分离主导了Mn原子间的电荷转移和最终的3d电子排布，进而决定了费米能级的位置和材料的电输运特性。
> 
> **理论贡献**：
> 
> 1.  **范式修正**：修正并拓展了用于解释MXenes电子性质的晶体场理论框架，为理解其复杂性质提供了更准确的物理图像。
> 2.  **机制澄清**：阐明了长期存在争议的Janus Mn₂N MXenes性质差异的根源，统一了看似矛盾的现象。
> 3.  **普适性推广**：指出该机制可推广至其他MXenes体系（如V₂NOF），具有重要的理论指导意义。
> 
> **实践贡献**：
> 
> 1.  **提供了可调谐设计策略**：基于该机制，明确提出了通过电荷掺杂和单轴应变动态切换半导体/半金属态的有效方法，为设计可实时编程、擦除的自旋电子器件铺平了道路。
> 2.  **材料筛选指南**：该理论框架为未来通过筛选不同的表面钝化基团组合来精准设计具有特定电子性质的MXenes提供了指导。

> ## 七、学术思考与批判性分析
> 
> 1.  **关于U值选择的敏感性**：计算中使用了DFT+U方法，这是处理过渡金属d电子强关联效应的标准做法。作者测试了U=4 eV和5 eV并声称结果无本质变化。这是否足够？不同的U值是否会定量地改变带隙大小和临界应变值，从而影响与未来实验结果的精确比较？对于不同钝化基团，最优U值是否应有所不同？
> 2.  **“能级分离”的量化定义**：文章提出了“能级分离程度”这一核心概念，但并未给出一个严格、可量化的定义（如能量差的数值）。这使得该机制在跨体系比较时，可能缺乏一个精确的评判标准。未来是否可以定义一个基于电子结构的“分离指数”？
> 3.  **从静态到动态的跨越**：文章提出的应变调控是静态的，而电荷掺杂通常是动态的。在真实的器件操作中，动态施加高达7%的单轴应变而不破坏材料结构，在技术上极具挑战性。这是否意味着电荷掺杂是实现动态调控的更现实路径？应变调控更适用于一次性的器件“编程”？
> 4.  **缺陷与无序的影响**：本文的计算基于完美的周期性结构。在实际制备中，表面钝化基团的分布可能不均匀，存在空位或混合相。这些缺陷会对文中提出的精妙机制（尤其是依赖两侧电荷平衡的机制）产生多大程度的破坏？
> 5.  **理论与TMDs的对比深度**：作者强调新机制与TMDs不同，但未深入讨论其物理根源。这种差异本质上源于MXenes中金属原子层与配位原子层分离的结构特征，使得“层间”相互作用比TMDs中“层内”的局域配位场更为重要。能否将这一思想提炼为更普适的“二维多原子层材料的电子结构设计原则”？

> ## 八、用户问题预测与解答
> 
> 1.  **问题**：什么是半金属（Half-metal）？它与普通金属和半导体有何不同？ **解答**：半金属是一种特殊的电子状态。它不是指导电性只有金属的一半，而是指材料对一种自旋取向的电子表现为金属（在费米能级处有电子态，可导电），而对另一种自旋取向的电子表现为绝缘体或半导体（在费米能级处有带隙，不导电）。这导致其自旋极化率为100%，是理想的自旋电流源。
>

> **Q1：MXene和过渡金属硫族化合物在结构上有什么本质区别？为什么它们的电子性质调控机制不同？** **A**：可以将它们比作两种“三明治”。**MXene是“面包-肉-面包”结构**，即两层金属原子（面包）夹着一层非金属原子（肉）。而**TMD是“面包-肉-肉-面包”结构**，是两层非金属原子夹着一层金属原子。在TMD中，中心的金属原子是决定性质的关键，其周围的非金属原子形成的“牢笼”（配位场）是什么形状，直接决定了它的d轨道如何分裂。而在MXene中，金属原子在最外层，它不仅要与内部的“肉”（N）作用，还要与外部的“酱料”（表面钝化原子）作用。因此，外部“酱料”对金属原子能级的影响巨大，甚至能通过改变内部电荷分布来影响整个体系的电子结构，这就是本文揭示的新机制。

> **Q2：什么是“半金属”？它和半导体、金属有什么区别？** **A**：这三种材料代表了不同的导电状态。
> 
> -   **金属**：费米能级处有大量电子可自由移动，如铜、铁。就像一条任何时候都畅通无阻的高速公路。
> -   **半导体**：费米能级处存在一个“禁带”（带隙），电子需要获得能量（如加热、光照）才能跃迁过去导电，如硅。就像一条中间有断崖的公路，需要加油门（能量）才能飞过去。
> -   **半金属**：是一种“人格分裂”的材料。对于一种自旋方向的电子（如自旋向上），它像金属一样导电；对于另一种自旋方向的电子（自旋向下），它像半导体一样有带隙。这就像一个只允许单一方向车流通过的高速公路收费站。这种特性使其能产生100%自旋极化的电流，是自旋电子学的理想材料。

> **Q3：什么是Janus结构？为什么以Janus命名？** **A**：Janus是罗马神话中的双面神，拥有前后两张朝向相反的面孔。在材料科学中，**Janus结构**专指那些两侧表面具有不对称化学组成的二维材料。本文中的Mn₂NOF就是一个典型例子，它的一面是O原子，另一面是F原子。这种不对称性打破了空间反演对称性，常常会诱发一些独特的物理性质，如本征的面外压电性、Rashba自旋劈裂等。

> **Q4：DFT计算中的“+U”和“SOC”是做什么的？** **A**：它们是用来“修正”标准DFT计算缺陷的补丁。
> 
> -   **DFT+U**：标准DFT倾向于低估像Mn这类含有d电子的原子的电子局域性，导致错误地预测某些材料为金属。**+U修正**相当于在计算中人为地增加一个库仑排斥力，惩罚电子在同一个原子轨道上“挤在一起”，从而更准确地描述它们的强关联效应，得到正确的带隙和磁性。
> -   **SOC**：**自旋-轨道耦合**是一个相对论效应，可以理解为电子的自旋和它绕原子核的轨道运动之间产生了相互作用。这个效应虽然微小，但在含重元素的材料中至关重要，它能引起能带劈裂，改变带隙的大小，甚至影响拓扑性质。

> **Q5：这篇论文提出的“能级分离”机制，和传统的“能带弯曲”是一个概念吗？** **A**：不是，它们是两个不同层面的概念。

> MXenes, composed of transition metal layers with various surface passivation groups, exhibit diverse magnetic and electron transport properties essential for miniaturized spintronic devices. Through density functional theory calculations, we reveal that the electronic properties of Mn2NOT (T = O, F, OH, Cl) MXenes, i.e., half-metallicity and semiconductivity, are determined by the energy-level separation between two side Mn atoms arising from the distinctive two-surface passivation, in contrast to other typical 2D magnetic materials like transition metal dichalcogenides that are determined by the local metallic atom coordination symmetry. The separation degree of energy levels decides the electron configuration of Mn 3d orbitals and the Fermi-level position, thereby determining the half-metallicity or semiconductivity. This mechanism enables dynamic control methods for tuning electronic and magnetic properties. For instance, charge (electron or hole) doping and applying uniaxial strain can drive transitions between semiconductor and half-metal states by modulating the Mn energy levels, Mn-N electrostatic potential, and Mn 3d orbitals filling at two surface sides. Our findings reveal the physical origins of MXenes' electromagnetic properties and offer strategies for designing tunable and highly integrated spintronic devices. MXenes由过渡金属层与多种表面钝化基团组成，展现出对于微型化自旋电子器件至关重要的多样磁性与电子输运特性。通过密度泛函理论计算，我们揭示了Mn₂NOT (T = O, F, OH, Cl) MXenes的电子性质（即半金属性和半导体性）是由两侧Mn原子之间的能级分离决定的，这种分离源于独特的双表面钝化效应。这与其他典型二维磁性材料（如过渡金属二硫属化物）由局域金属原子配位对称性决定的性质形成对比。能级分离的程度决定了Mn 3d轨道的电子构型和费米能级位置，从而决定了半金属性或半导体性。这一机制实现了调控电子和磁性质的动态控制方法。例如，电荷（电子或空穴）掺杂和施加单轴应变可以通过调制Mn能级、Mn-N静电势以及两侧Mn 3d轨道的填充，驱动半导体态与半金属态之间的转变。我们的发现揭示了MXenes电磁性质的物理起源，并为设计可调谐且高度集成的自旋电子器件提供了策略。 **MXenes**: 一类由过渡金属碳化物/氮化物构成的二维材料家族。 **density functional theory**: 密度泛函理论，一种基于电子密度研究多电子体系电子结构的量子力学计算方法。 **half-metallicity**: 半金属性，一种材料中一个自旋通道呈现金属性而另一个自旋通道呈现绝缘体或半导体性的电子性质。 **semiconductivity**: 半导体性，电导率介于导体和绝缘体之间的材料特性。 **transition metal dichalcogenides (TMDs)**: 过渡金属二硫属化物，化学式为MX₂的二维层状材料，其中M为过渡金属，X为硫族元素。 **Fermi-level**: 费米能级，绝对零度下电子占据的最高能级。 **doping**: 掺杂，向材料中引入杂质以改变其电学性质的过程。 **uniaxial strain**: 单轴应变，沿单一方向施加的机械形变。

> ## I. INTRODUCTION
> 
> ## I. 引言
> 
> Spintronics has the potential to revolutionize information technology in the coming decades, with applications already emerging in areas like hard disk read heads and magnetoresistive random access memory [1–4]. Significant progress has been made by developing high-efficiency spintronic devices using multilayers of ferroelectric or magnetostrictive dissimilar materials [5,6]. However, these multilayer structures often suffer from interfacial defects between chemically different layers, which can reduce their efficiency. In addition, the fabrication complexity poses challenges for creating miniaturized, high-performance devices. Recently, advanced chemical vapor deposition techniques have shown promise in overcoming these limitations by enabling the fabrication of complex two-dimensional (2D) lateral heterostructures with atomically sharp interfaces, such as graphene/h-BN and MoS2/WS2 [7,8]. Transition metal dichalcogenides (TMDs) are widely studied for 2D lateral heterostructures due to their phase-dependent diverse electronic properties, such as semiconductivity and metallicity, governed by d-orbital splitting and electron occupation of the metal atoms in various coordination symmetries. For example, in MoS2, the Mo 4d orbitals split into t2g and eg states under an octahedral crystal field in the 1T phase, while in the 2H phase, they split into singlet dz2 , doublet eI (dxy and dx2−y2), and doublet eII (dyz and dxz) states due to a trigonal prismatic crystal field. As a result, the two electrons on Mo4+ partially occupy the t2g orbitals in the 1T phase, leading to metallicity, whereas they fully occupy the dz2 orbital in the 2H phase, resulting in semiconductivity. Doping transition elements and even point defects can introduce magnetism (spin injection) into both metallic and semiconducting TMD phases [9–11]. However, achieving controlled phase transitions for integrating these phases into the lateral heterostructures is challenging due to the need for critical conditions such as high in-plane strain (> 10%) and charge doping levels (～1013 e−/cm2) [12–14], limiting their applications for the real-time construction, reorganization, and erasure of spintronic devices. 自旋电子学有潜力在未来几十年革新信息技术，其应用已出现在硬盘读头和磁阻随机存取存储器等领域[1–4]。通过利用铁电或磁致伸缩异质材料的多层膜开发高效自旋电子器件，已取得显著进展[5,6]。然而，这些多层结构常常因化学性质不同的层间界面缺陷而降低效率。此外，制造复杂性对创建微型化、高性能器件构成了挑战。最近，先进的化学气相沉积技术通过能够制造具有原子级锐利界面的复杂二维(2D)横向异质结构（如石墨烯/h-BN和MoS₂/WS₂）[7,8]，显示出克服这些局限性的前景。过渡金属二硫属化物(TMDs)因其依赖于物相的不同电子性质（如半导体性和金属性）而被广泛研究用于二维横向异质结构，这些性质由不同配位对称性下金属原子的d轨道分裂和电子占据决定。例如，在MoS₂中，1T相下Mo 4d轨道在八面体晶体场中分裂为t₂g和eg态，而在2H相中，由于三角棱柱晶体场，它们分裂为单重态dz₂、双重态eI (dxy和dx₂₋y₂)以及双重态eII (dyz和dxz)态。因此，Mo⁴⁺上的两个电子在1T相中部分占据t₂g轨道，导致金属性，而在2H相中它们完全占据dz₂轨道，导致半导体性。掺杂过渡元素甚至点缺陷可以向金属性和半导体性TMD相中引入磁性（自旋注入）[9–11]。然而，实现将这些相集成到横向异质结构中的受控相变具有挑战性，因为需要临界条件，如高面内应变(>10%)和电荷掺杂水平(～10¹³ e⁻/cm²)[12–14]，这限制了它们在自旋电子器件的实时构建、重构和擦除中的应用。 **spintronics**: 自旋电子学，利用电子自旋而非仅电荷进行信息处理和存储的技术。 **magnetoresistive random access memory**: 磁阻随机存取存储器，一种利用磁阻效应存储数据的非易失性存储器。 **heterostructures**: 异质结构，由两种或多种不同半导体材料组成的结构。 **chemical vapor deposition**: 化学气相沉积，一种通过气相化学反应在基底上沉积固态薄膜的方法。 **graphene**: 石墨烯，由单层碳原子以六角晶格构成的二维材料。 **h-BN**: 六方氮化硼，一种与石墨烯结构相似的二维绝缘材料。 **metallicity**: 金属性，材料表现出类似金属的高电导率特性。 **d-orbital splitting**: d轨道分裂，在晶体场中简并d轨道能量分裂为不同能级组的现象。 **crystal field**: 晶体场，由周围原子或离子产生的静电场。 **1T phase**: 1T相，TMDs的一种八面体配位结构相。 **2H phase**: 2H相，TMDs的一种三角棱柱配位结构相。 **spin injection**: 自旋注入，将自旋极化电子引入材料的过程。
> 
> Recently, MXenes M2X T T′, where M is a transition element, X = C and N, T /T′ = O and F, etc., has emerged as another family of promising 2D magnetic materials for spintronics. They exhibit high Curie temperature TC (～566 to ～1877 K) and the required electronic and magnetic properties (e.g., metallicity, half-metallicity, and semiconductor) [15–17]. Regulating surface passivation and doping with foreign metal atoms can tune electronic properties and magnetism [18–20], e.g., the F passivation-induced transition from ferromagnetic half-metal Cr2C to an antiferromagnetic semiconductor [21]. However, these methods are challenging in dynamically constructing chemical interfaces free half-metal/semiconductor heterostructures for real-time operating or erasing, which is highly desirable for miniaturized spintronic devices. This capability is currently lacking due to a limited understanding of how intrinsic factors, such as chemical composition and the presence of surface atoms or groups, influence electronic properties, specifically half-metallicity and semiconducting behavior. A theoretical prediction framework utilizing crystal field theory has been developed to explain electron configurations across different chemical compositions, successfully identifying robust ferromagnetic MXenes [22,23]. However, establishing a theoretical framework for electronic properties remains challenging, particularly in understanding the relationship between the passivated atoms in Janus MXenes and their electronic characteristics. For instance, while Mn2NOF was predicted to be a metal, density functional theory (DFT) calculations indicate that it actually exhibits semiconducting behavior [24,25]. 最近，MXenes M₂X T T'（其中M为过渡元素，X = C和N，T/T' = O和F等）已成为自旋电子学中另一类有前景的二维磁性材料。它们展现出高居里温度TC（～566至～1877 K）以及所需的电子和磁性质（例如，金属性、半金属性和半导体性）[15–17]。调控表面钝化和外来金属原子掺杂可以调节电子性质和磁性[18–20]，例如，F钝化诱导的铁磁半金属Cr₂C转变为反铁磁半导体[21]。然而，这些方法在动态构建无化学界面的半金属/半导体异质结构以实现实时操作或擦除方面面临挑战，而这对于微型化自旋电子器件是极为理想的。目前缺乏这种能力，是由于对内在因素（如化学成分和表面原子或基团的存在）如何影响电子性质（特别是半金属性和半导体行为）的理解有限。已发展出一种利用晶体场理论的理论预测框架来解释不同化学成分下的电子构型，成功识别了稳健的铁磁MXenes[22,23]。然而，建立电子性质的理论框架仍然具有挑战性，特别是在理解Janus MXenes中钝化原子与其电子特性之间的关系方面。例如，虽然Mn₂NOF被预测为金属，但密度泛函理论(DFT)计算表明它实际上表现出半导体行为[24,25]。 **Curie temperature**: 居里温度，铁磁材料失去其铁磁性的临界温度。 **ferromagnetic**: 铁磁性，材料具有自发磁化且磁矩可在外磁场下定向排列的性质。 **antiferromagnetic**: 反铁磁性，相邻自旋反平行排列的磁性有序态。 **Janus MXenes**: Janus MXenes，上下表面具有不同钝化基团的不对称MXene结构。
> 
> In this study, we employed DFT calculations to investigate the physical origin of the half-metal and semiconductor electronics structure of Mn2NOT (T = O, F, Cl, and OH). We find that the diverse electronic properties (half-metal and semiconductor) and degeneration of 3d orbitals arising from the different surface-passivated atoms cannot be explained by the local coordination environment/symmetry. Instead, the 3d energy-level separation degree of the Mn atoms at two surface sides determines the magnitude of the electron transfer from Mn to other atoms (especially to N) and then controls the Mn 3d-orbital configuration, leading to half-metal and semiconductor properties. Based on this understanding, both the charge doping (directly changing the electron occupation) and strain engineering (inducing charge redistribution between the two side Mn layers) can be adopted to dynamically modulate the half-metal and semiconductor transition. The theoretical framework and strategies of the tunable electronic properties provide opportunities for the magnetic MXenes in minimized, low-energy, and high-density spintronics devices. 在本研究中，我们采用DFT计算来研究Mn₂NOT (T = O, F, Cl, 和 OH)的半金属和半导体电子结构的物理起源。我们发现，由不同表面钝化原子引起的多样电子性质（半金属和半导体）以及3d轨道简并不能用局域配位环境/对称性来解释。相反，两侧表面Mn原子的3d能级分离程度决定了从Mn到其他原子（特别是N）的电子转移量，进而控制了Mn 3d轨道构型，导致半金属和半导体性质。基于这一理解，电荷掺杂（直接改变电子占据）和应变工程（诱导两侧Mn层之间的电荷重新分布）均可用于动态调制半金属和半导体转变。可调谐电子性质的理论框架和策略为磁性MXenes在微型化、低能耗和高密度自旋电子器件中的应用提供了机遇。 **strain engineering**: 应变工程，通过施加机械应变来调控材料性质的策略。

> ## II. COMPUTATIONAL METHODS
> 
> ## II. 计算方法
> 
> Our DFT calculations were performed with the VIENNA AB-INITIO SIMULATION PACKAGE [26,27]. Projector-augmented wave [28] pseudopotentials were used with an energy cutoff of 500 eV for plane-wave expansions. The Perdew-Burke-Ernzerhof generalized gradient approximation (GGA) to the exchange-correlation functional was used [29]. To account for the strongly correlated Mn transition metals, all electronic structure calculations and geometry relaxations were done with the spin-polarized DFT+U correction [30,31]. A U value of 4 eV was used for Mn, as is typical in the literature on on-site Coulombic repulsion [22,32,33]. The impact of U values on magnetism was studied by changing U values from 0 eV to 5 eV for Mn2NO2, while the band structure of Mn2NOT (T = O, F, OH, and Cl) was calculated with U = 5 eV. There are no fundamental changes for U = 4 eV and 5 eV (see Supplemental Material Figs. S1 and S2). A Monkhorst-pack k-point mesh [34] of 15 × 11 × 1 in the first Brillouin zone was used for structural relaxations of the unit cell. Atomic positions within unit cells and supercells were optimized to within 0.005 eV/Å force on each atom, and total energy changes were converged to within 10−5 eV. To ensure the ground state of these MXenes is obtained, further optimization was performed after a small atomic displacement and slight lattice constants variation were applied. For all static electronic structure calculations with and without spin-orbit coupling (SOC), the Monkhorst-pack k-point mesh was increased to 21 × 19 × 1, and total energy changes were converged to within 10−6 eV to ensure accurate calculations of electronic structures. Periodic boundary conditions were applied in all three directions. In addition, a 30 Å vacuum space was applied along z directions to avoid the interaction between two periodic images. The dipole correction along vacuum space was applied for the asymmetry Mn2NOF, Mn2NOOH, and Mn2NOCl. 我们的DFT计算使用VIENNA AB-INITIO SIMULATION PACKAGE进行[26,27]。采用投影缀加波[28]赝势，平面波展开的能量截断值为500 eV。交换关联泛函采用Perdew-Burke-Ernzerhof广义梯度近似(GGA)[29]。为考虑Mn过渡金属的强关联效应，所有电子结构计算和几何弛豫均采用自旋极化DFT+U修正[30,31]。Mn的U值采用4 eV，这是文献中关于在位库仑排斥的典型值[22,32,33]。通过对Mn₂NO₂改变U值从0 eV到5 eV，研究了U值对磁性的影响，而Mn₂NOT (T = O, F, OH, 和 Cl)的能带结构采用U = 5 eV计算。U = 4 eV和5 eV之间没有根本性变化（见补充材料图S1和S2）。对于晶胞的结构弛豫，采用了第一布里渊区中15 × 11 × 1的Monkhorst-pack k点网格[34]。晶胞和超胞内的原子位置被优化至每个原子上的力小于0.005 eV/Å，总能量变化收敛至10⁻⁵ eV以内。为确保获得这些MXenes的基态，在施加小的原子位移和轻微的晶格常数变化后进行了进一步优化。对于所有含和不含自旋轨道耦合(SOC)的静态电子结构计算，Monkhorst-pack k点网格增加到21 × 19 × 1，总能量变化收敛至10⁻⁶ eV以内，以确保电子结构的精确计算。在所有三个方向上施加周期性边界条件。此外，沿z方向施加30 Å的真空层，以避免两个周期性镜像之间的相互作用。对于不对称的Mn₂NOF、Mn₂NOOH和Mn₂NOCl，沿真空方向施加了偶极修正。 **pseudopotentials**: 赝势，一种用于简化电子结构计算的近似势函数。 **generalized gradient approximation**: 广义梯度近似，一种考虑电子密度及其梯度的交换关联能近似方法。 **spin-orbit coupling**: 自旋轨道耦合，电子自旋与其轨道运动之间的相对论性相互作用。 **Brillouin zone**: 布里渊区，倒空间中的原胞。 **periodic boundary conditions**: 周期性边界条件，假设系统在空间中周期性重复的边界条件。

> ## III. RESULTS AND DISCUSSION
> 
> ## III. 结果与讨论
> 
> ### A. Surface-passivation-induced semiconductor/half-metal transition
> 
> ### A. 表面钝化诱导的半导体/半金属转变
> 
> Figure 1(a) illustrates the crystal structure of monolayer Mn2N MXene, which consists of three layers: a nitrogen atom layer sandwiched between two manganese layers. This structure is exactly inverse to the monolayer 1T -TMDs, where the metal and nonmetal atoms exchange their positions. Mn2N forms a 120° rhombic primitive unit cell, indicated by the black rhombus in Fig. 1(a). In our DFT calculations, the rectangular unit cell (highlighted in the orange box) was adopted to facilitate easier comparisons of magnetic ordering and strain modulations. The exposed manganese atoms in monolayer MXenes are typically passivated by various functional groups such as O, F, OH, and Cl. In our study, we passivated one side of Mn2N with oxygen, while the other side was passivated by other functional groups, generating both symmetric (Mn2NO2) and asymmetric structures (Mn2NOF, Mn2NOOH, and Mn2NOCl). These asymmetric structures are referred to as Janus structures, as summarized in Fig. 1(b). The termination atoms are located at the top of Mn at the bottom layer, forming the N-Mn-T octahedron (T = O, F, OH, and Cl). Such configuration is confirmed as illustrated in Supplemental Material Note 1 (Fig. S3 and Table S1) [35] (see also Refs. [22,24,25,36,37] therein). The lattice constants of these MXenes are listed in Supplemental Material Table S2 [35]. The magnetic ground state of these four structures was carefully examined through the different magnetic configurations schematically depicted in Fig. S4 [35]. The corresponding relative energies associated with these magnetic orders of Mn2NOT (T = O, F, OH, and Cl) are summarized in Supplemental Material Table S3 [35] (see also Refs. [22,35] therein). > ### B. Mn 3d-orbital degeneracy analysis for Mn2N MXenes
> 
> ### B. Mn₂N MXenes的Mn 3d轨道简并分析

> The local coordination environment influences the Mn electron configuration of the 3d orbitals and its degeneracy. Conventionally, the Mn 3d orbitals can be categorized into the higher doublet eg (dx2−y2 and dz2 ) and lower triplet t2g (dxy, dyz, and dxz) [the left of Fig. 2(a)] [23,38], provided ideal octahedral coordination formed by the bonded O and N atoms. Note that the Mn-N/O bonds do not perfectly align with the axis of the ideal octahedral in our 2D MXenes (with different surface passivation), as shown in Fig. 1(a). Therefore, the Mn 3d-orbital splitting is discussed in a rotated coordinate [consistent with the coordinate axis in Fig. 1(a)] that is governed by the triangular crystal fields (C3v symmetry) as shown in the right of Fig. 2(a). In this scenario, the 3d orbitals of Mn are split into a singlet dz2 and doublet eI (dyz + dxz) and eII (dxy and dx2−y2 ) orbitals under C3v symmetry, similar to previous studies of Cr-based MXenes and 1T -MoS2 [39,40]. 局域配位环境影响Mn 3d轨道的电子构型及其简并度。传统上，在由键合的O和N原子形成的理想八面体配位下，Mn 3d轨道可以分类为较高的双重态eg (dx₂₋y₂和dz₂)和较低的三重态t₂g (dxy, dyz, 和 dxz)[图2(a)左侧][23,38]。注意，在我们的二维MXenes中（具有不同的表面钝化），Mn-N/O键并不完全与理想八面体的轴对齐，如图1(a)所示。因此，Mn 3d轨道分裂在一个旋转坐标系中讨论[与图1(a)中坐标轴一致]，该坐标系由三角晶体场（C₃v对称性）决定，如图2(a)右侧所示。在这种情况下，Mn的3d轨道在C₃v对称性下分裂为一个单重态dz₂和双重态eI (dyz + dxz)以及eII (dxy和dx₂₋y₂)轨道，类似于先前对Cr基MXenes和1T-MoS₂的研究[39,40]。 **degeneracy**: 简并，不同量子态具有相同能量的现象。

> ### C. Mechanism for half-metal and semiconductor of Mn2NOT MXenes
> 
> ### C. Mn₂NOT MXenes的半金属和半导体机制

> Despite the resembling Mn 3d-orbital degeneracy, the relevant energy levels of Mn at the O and T (F, OH, and Cl) sides are different from those of Mn2NO2. As shown in Figs. 2(c) and 2(d), the PDOS intensity of MnF and MnCl is more significant than that of the MnO atoms below the Fermi level, whereas it is lower than that of MnO above the Fermi level. Such results are also observed in Mn2NOOH in Fig. S9 [35]. To further reveal the electronic structure of Mn 3d orbital and the electron occupation, the spin majority projected bands (PBANDs) of Mn 3d orbitals in Mn2NOT (T = O, F, and Cl) are shown in Figs. 3(a)–3(c). For the symmetric Mn2NO2, the eI (dyz + dxz) shows the electron pocket character at both Γ and S points around the Fermi level, while the band from eII (dxy + dx2−y2 ) crosses the Fermi level. Such character leads to higher electron density on eII than eI, consistent with Fig. 2(b). Once one side of O is replaced by F, the valence band maximum (VBM) and conduction band minimum (CBM) should be contributed from eI of MnF and MnO, respectively. Thus, the MnO 3d orbital shifts to a higher level than that of MnT , consistent with Fig. 2(c), i.e., MnO showing higher PDOS density above the Fermi level. Furthermore, the electron pocket from eI around S at the Fermi level in Mn2NO2 disappears in the Mn2NOT (T = F and OH). In other words, the eI of MnO turns empty while the eI of MnT (T = F and OH) should be fully filled. Similar results are observed in Mn2NOOH (Fig. S9(b) [35]). It means that the energy level of MnO and MnCl are not fully separated and the eI of MnO and MnCl is still partially occupied by electrons. > After the structural symmetry is broken by other surface-passivation atoms (F and OH), the energy levels of MnO and MnT turn different despite the similar 3d-orbital degeneracy (Fig. 2 and Supplemental Material Fig. S9 [35]). Figure 3(h) schematically depicts the energy relationship of the 3d orbital between MnO and MnF in Mn2NOF. The MnO 3d orbital is promoted to a higher level than that of MnF. Based on the energy-level separation, MnO should donate more electrons (two electrons) to N compared to MnF (one electron) evidenced by the calculated charge transfer (Table S5 [35]), asymmetric potential, and ELF results [Figs. 3(d)–3(f)]. The oxidation states of MnF and MnO become Mn2+ and Mn4+, respectively. Then, the five electrons of a high spin state (d5) on MnF fully fill the singlet dz2 , doublet eII, and eI. Meanwhile, the three electrons of low spin state (d3) on MnO fill the singlet dz2 and doublet eII (dxy + dx2−y2 ) but leave the highest eI empty. Such configuration is confirmed by a higher 4.480 μB of MnF and lower 3.624 μB of MnO from our DFT calculations. The Fermi level is, thus, promoted to the eI of MnF but leaves the eII of MnO empty. Then, Mn2NOF shows semiconductivity. Considering the electron configuration of the OH group is close to that of F, a similar Mn 3d configuration could be observed in Mn2NOOH, also leading to semiconductivity. However, the degree of two-side 3d-level separation of Mn2NOOH is smaller than that of Mn2NOF, thus leading to a smaller gap. This might be attributed to the weaker electron-accepting ability of hydroxides compared to fluorine atoms. 在结构对称性被其他表面钝化原子（F和OH）破坏后，尽管3d轨道简并相似（图2和补充材料图S9[35]），MnO和MnT的能级变得不同。图3(h)示意性地描绘了Mn₂NOF中MnO和MnF之间3d轨道的能量关系。MnO 3d轨道被提升到比MnF更高的能级。基于能级分离，与MnF（一个电子）相比，MnO应向N提供更多电子（两个电子），这由计算得到的电荷转移（表S5[35]）、不对称电势和ELF结果[图3(d)–3(f)]所证实。MnF和MnO的氧化态分别变为Mn²⁺和Mn⁴⁺。然后，MnF上高自旋态(d⁵)的五个电子完全填满单重态dz²、双重态eII和eI。同时，MnO上低自旋态(d³)的三个电子填满单重态dz²和双重态eII (dxy + dx₂₋y₂)，但使最高的eI为空。这种构型由我们DFT计算得到的MnF较高的4.480 μB和MnO较低的3.624 μB所确认。因此，费米能级被提升到MnF的eI，但使MnO的eII为空。于是，Mn₂NOF显示半导体性。考虑到OH基团的电子构型与F相近，在Mn₂NOOH中可观察到类似的Mn 3d构型，也导致半导体性。然而，Mn₂NOOH的两侧3d能级分离程度小于Mn₂NOF，因此导致较小的带隙。这可能归因于氢氧化物与氟原子相比较弱的电子接受能力。 **high spin state**: 高自旋态，电子尽可能占据不同轨道且自旋平行的状态。 **low spin state**: 低自旋态，电子优先配对在低能轨道上的状态。
> 
> Different from F and OH, the Cl atoms seem not to cause a sufficiently large difference between the energy level of the two sides Mn. The electron transfer from two sides, Mn to N, should be close to each other after Mn-N bonding, which is indeed confirmed by the Bader analysis in Table S5 [35] and the nearly symmetrical potential in Fig. 3(f). The partially occupied MnO 3d orbital is, therefore, similar to that in Mn2NO2, and thus, Mn2NOCl still shows half-metallic. Overall, the intrinsic mechanism of the half-metal and semiconductor of Mn2N with different surface passivated atoms should be electron transfer and redistribution decided by the relationship of energy level between two sides of Mn. 与F和OH不同，Cl原子似乎不会在两侧Mn的能级之间造成足够大的差异。Mn-N键合后，从两侧Mn到N的电子转移应彼此接近，这确实由表S5[35]中的Bader分析和图3(f)中近乎对称的电势所确认。部分占据的MnO 3d轨道因此与Mn₂NO₂中的相似，因此Mn₂NOCl仍显示半金属性。总体而言，具有不同表面钝化原子的Mn₂N的半金属和半导体的内在机制应是由两侧Mn之间的能级关系决定的电子转移和重新分布。
> 
> In previous studies, DFT calculations were performed for magnetic MXenes (Ti-, V-, Cr-, and Mn-based MXenes) and a crystal field framework was proposed to explain the magnetic order of MXene, which was then successful employed to seek robust ferromagnetic materials with high Curie temperature [23,23]. However, the transport properties of some MXenes contradict the models, especially in the Janus MXenes. For example, the Mn 3d at both sides of Mn2NOF is regarded to be partially filled, suggesting the half-metal, but it shows the semiconductor in the DFT calculation. The main deviation arises from the inaccurate consideration of the charge transfer and electron configuration. In previous analysis, the same magnitude of electrons is simply regarded to be donated from two sides, Mn to N, leading to the Mn3+ for all Mn [25]. Thus, any remaining electrons are forced into the eg, leading to half-metal in their model. Similar contradictions could be observed in V2NOF. If the same electrons are regarded to be donated from two sides, V to C (2 electrons), only one V3+ oxidation state should be obtained at both F and O sides, respectively. Therefore, the t2g should be partially filled due to the 3d2 spin state of V3+ and then predicted to be half-metal. However, the V2NOF is a semiconductor from DFT calculation [41]. According to our mechanism, the V at F and O sides should donate one and two electrons to N, leading to the 3d3 and 3d1 spin states of V2+ (F side) and V4+ (O side). Only the V eII and eI at O are empty, while the V dz2 + eII at the F side is fully filled by spin majority electrons but leaves eI empty, leading to the semiconductor V2NOF in DFT calculations. 在先前的研究中，对磁性MXenes（Ti基、V基、Cr基和Mn基MXenes）进行了DFT计算，并提出了一个晶体场框架来解释MXene的磁序，该框架随后成功用于寻找具有高居里温度的稳健铁磁材料[22,23]。然而，一些MXenes的输运性质与模型相矛盾，特别是在Janus MXenes中。例如，Mn₂NOF两侧的Mn 3d被认为部分填充，暗示半金属性，但在DFT计算中它显示半导体性。主要偏差源于对电荷转移和电子构型的不准确考虑。在先前的分析中，简单地认为两侧Mn向N提供相同数量的电子，导致所有Mn为Mn³⁺[25]。因此，任何剩余电子被迫进入eg，导致其模型中的半金属性。在V₂NOF中也可观察到类似的矛盾。如果认为两侧V向C（2个电子）提供相同数量的电子，则在F和O侧应分别仅获得一种V³⁺氧化态。因此，由于V³⁺的3d²自旋态，t₂g应部分填充，然后预测为半金属。然而，根据DFT计算，V₂NOF是半导体[41]。根据我们的机制，F侧和O侧的V应向N提供一个和两个电子，导致V²⁺（F侧）和V⁴⁺（O侧）的3d³和3d¹自旋态。只有O侧V的eII和eI为空，而F侧V的dz² + eII被自旋多数电子完全填满但eI为空，导致DFT计算中V₂NOF为半导体。
> 
> ### D. Modulating half-metal and semiconductor properties
> 
> ### D. 调制半金属和半导体性质
> 
> We note that the eI band of MnO is empty, and the lower eI band of the MnF side is fully filled in the semiconductive Mn2NOF [Fig. 3(h)]. It is thus possible to modulate the transition from semiconductor to half-metal by partly emptying the MnO eI band or partly filling the MnF eI band using positive (hole) or negative (electron) doping. The DFT calculation results of the total DOS of Mn2NOF under both hole (0.02 h+/atom) and electron (0.02 e−/atom) doping are shown in Fig. S11 [35]. Our results show that such charge doping only involves adding or removing electrons on the highest eI level instead of transferring electrons between two side Mn atoms. The spin majority PBAND of MnF and MnO in Mn2NOF with electron (0.02 e−/atom) and hole (0.02 h+/atom) doping are shown in Figs. 4(a) and 4(b), respectively. Compared with the neutral state [Fig. 3(b)], the doped electrons fill the eI of the MnO 3d band (the electron pocket between Γ and X ). This process promotes the gap downward shift and causes the CBM (eI of MnO) to cross the Fermi level. The empty doublet eI (dxy + dyz) of MnO is thus partially filled. Mn2NOF transitions to half-metal. In contrast, the hole doping into Mn2NOF promotes the gap to a higher level and causes the VBM (eI of MnF) to cross the Fermi level. After that, the eI of MnF is partially filled, leading to the half-metallicity. These processes are schematically shown in the insert of Fig. 4, where blue and orange arrows represent the electron and hole doping into the 3d orbital, respectively. 我们注意到，在半导体性Mn₂NOF中，MnO的eI带为空，而MnF侧较低的eI带完全填满[图3(h)]。因此，通过使用正（空穴）或负（电子）掺杂部分清空MnO eI带或部分填充MnF eI带，有可能调制从半导体到半金属的转变。Mn₂NOF在空穴(0.02 h⁺/atom)和电子(0.02 e⁻/atom)掺杂下的总DOS的DFT计算结果如图S11[35]所示。我们的结果表明，这种电荷掺杂仅涉及在最高eI能级上添加或移除电子，而不是在两侧Mn原子之间转移电子。Mn₂NOF中MnF和MnO在电子(0.02 e⁻/atom)和空穴(0.02 h⁺/atom)掺杂下的自旋多数PBAND分别显示在图4(a)和4(b)中。与中性态[图3(b)]相比，掺杂电子填充了MnO 3d带的eI（Γ和X之间的电子口袋）。这一过程促使带隙向下移动，并导致CBM（MnO的eI）穿过费米能级。MnO的空双重态eI (dxy + dyz)因此被部分填充。Mn₂NOF转变为半金属。相反，向Mn₂NOF中掺杂空穴将带隙提升到更高能级，并导致VBM（MnF的eI）穿过费米能级。此后，MnF的eI被部分填充，导致半金属性。这些过程示意性地显示在图4的插图中，其中蓝色和橙色箭头分别代表向3d轨道的电子和空穴掺杂。
> 
> According to our discovered mechanism [Figs. 3(g) and 3(h)], balancing the charge transfer between two sides of Mn could enable the semiconductor to half-metal transition in Mn2NOT . To examine this hypothesis, we applied uniaxial strain in the y direction to Mn2NOF and Mn2NOOH. The atomic charge variation of Mn as a function of strain is summarized in Fig. 5(a) to identify the strain-induced electron transfer in Mn2NOF. As the tensile strain increases, the negative charge of MnF/MnO decreases/increases. Some electrons are transferred from MnF to MnO, leading to a reduced atomic charge difference on both sides. Figure 5(b) shows the electrostatic potential of Mn2NOF with various z-direction strains. Indeed, the tensile strain reduces the Mn-N potential difference at both sides. The fully filled 3d orbital of MnF and empty eI of MnO become partially filled under strain. Similar potential results are also observed in Mn2NOOH under the y-direction uniaxial strain (Supplemental Material Fig. S13 [35]). On the contrary, the compressive strain causes the opposite charge transfer from MnO to MnF [Fig. 5(a)], consistent with the increased Mn-N potential difference between the two sides [Fig. 5(b)]. The band structure of Mn2NOF under the y-direction uniaxial strain from −5% and +10% is shown in Fig. S14 [35]. The gap variation is summarized in Fig. 5(c). The compressive and tensile strain lead to the spin majority gap increasing and decreasing, respectively. The SOC effect was also considered and the results are shown in Fig. 5(c), where the trend of band gap variation is similar to the GGA + U results. However, the critical strain values (for zero band gap) are different, i.e., ～7% for SOC vs 3% for GGA + U . The band structure of Mn2NOOH under the compressive and tensile strain is shown in Fig. S15 [35]. The corresponding band gap as the function of tensile strain from −5% to +10% is summarized in Supplemental Material Fig. S13 [35]. The critical strain for semiconductor to half-metal transition is about +2% and +5%. 根据我们发现的机制[图3(g)和3(h)]，平衡两侧Mn之间的电荷转移可以实现Mn₂NOT中半导体到半金属的转变。为检验这一假设，我们对Mn₂NOF和Mn₂NOOH施加了y方向的单轴应变。Mn原子电荷随应变的变化总结在图5(a)中，以识别Mn₂NOF中应变诱导的电子转移。随着拉伸应变增加，MnF/MnO的负电荷减少/增加。一些电子从MnF转移到MnO，导致两侧原子电荷差异减小。图5(b)显示了Mn₂NOF在不同z方向应变下的静电势。确实，拉伸应变减小了两侧的Mn-N电势差。MnF完全填满的3d轨道和MnO空的eI在应变下变为部分填充。在y方向单轴应变下的Mn₂NOOH中也观察到类似的电势结果（补充材料图S13[35]）。相反，压缩应变导致从MnO到MnF的相反电荷转移[图5(a)]，与两侧Mn-N电势差增加一致[图5(b)]。Mn₂NOF在y方向单轴应变从−5%到+10%下的能带结构如图S14[35]所示。带隙变化总结在图5(c)中。压缩和拉伸应变分别导致自旋多数带隙增加和减小。还考虑了SOC效应，结果显示在图5(c)中，其中带隙变化的趋势与GGA + U结果相似。然而，临界应变值（零带隙）不同，即SOC约为7%，而GGA + U为3%。Mn₂NOOH在压缩和拉伸应变下的能带结构如图S15[35]所示。作为从−5%到+10%拉伸应变函数的相应带隙总结在补充材料图S13[35]中。半导体到半金属转变的临界应变约为+2%和+5%。 **tensile strain**: 拉伸应变，材料在拉伸力作用下产生的形变。 **compressive strain**: 压缩应变，材料在压缩力作用下产生的形变。
> 
> The 3d PBAND of MnF and MnO in Mn2NOF under εy = +10% are presented in Fig. 5(d). Both the VBM and CBM cross over the Fermi level at Γ-X under tensile strain. Considering that the VBM (hole pocket) and CBM (electron pocket) are mainly contributed from the eI (dyz + dxz) orbital of MnF and MnO, there is a reverse charge transfer from MnF-eI to MnO-eI beyond the critical strain corresponding to the weakened asymmetric Mn-N bond potential (close to the symmetrical Mn2NO2) under a tensile strain. Therefore, the CBM and VBM gradually approach until the crossover (i.e., a zero or negative gap) under tensile strain. This process makes both the eI (dyz + dxz) orbital of MnF and MnO partially filled and then leads the Mn2NOF transition to half-metal. These results agree with the electric static potential and electron transfer results in Figs. 5(a) and 5(b) and further confirm the schematic mechanism in Figs. 3(g) and 3(h). 图5(d)展示了Mn₂NOF中MnF和MnO在εy = +10%下的3d PBAND。在拉伸应变下，VBM和CBM都在Γ-X处穿过费米能级。考虑到VBM（空穴口袋）和CBM（电子口袋）主要来自MnF和MnO的eI (dyz + dxz)轨道，在超过临界应变时，存在从MnF-eI到MnO-eI的反向电荷转移，这对应于拉伸应变下不对称Mn-N键电势的减弱（接近对称的Mn₂NO₂）。因此，在拉伸应变下，CBM和VBM逐渐接近直至交叉（即零或负带隙）。这一过程使MnF和MnO的eI (dyz + dxz)轨道都部分填充，然后导致Mn₂NOF转变为半金属。这些结果与图5(a)和5(b)中的静电势和电子转移结果一致，并进一步证实了图3(g)和3(h)中的示意性机制。

> ## IV. CONCLUSIONS
> 
> ## IV. 结论
> 
> This work unravels the underlying physical mechanisms of half-metallicity and semiconductivity in Mn2N MXenes (Mn2NOT , T = O, F, OH, and Cl). We find that the half-metallicity and semiconductivity are independent of the structural symmetry and the 3d-orbital degeneracy of metal atoms, in sharp contrast to the well-studied TMDs. The Mn-N bond length is always larger than that of Mn-X bond (X = O, OH, F). Despite the Mn-N bond length being shorter than the Mn-Cl bond in Mn2NOCl, distorted symmetry of the Mn-octahedron is still consistent with O, OH, and F passivated Mn2N. The half-metallicity and semiconductivity are determined by the 3d energy level separation and electron configuration within the two sides of Mn, influenced by different surface passivated atoms. The distinct electron configuration after the 3d energy level separation at the two Mn sides (fully filled 3d orbitals of MnF and empty eI of MnO) corresponds to an asymmetrical Mn-N potential, leading to a gap opening in Mn2NOF and Mn2NOOH. In contrast, the closed 3d-orbital configuration, where both eI orbitals of two side Mn are partially filled, corresponds to a symmetrical Mn-N potential and results in half-metallicity in Mn2NO2 and Mn2NOCl. Our mechanisms inspire us to use charge doping and uniaxial strain to modulate the semiconductor to half-metal transition. Charge doping will make the Janus Mn2NOT (T = F and OH) transition to the semiconductor due to eI of the MnO and MnF bands being partially filled after electron and hole doping, respectively. The uniaxial tensile strain will also promote the Janus Mn2NOT transit to half-metal because the electrons are transferred from the high-spin MnF (d5) to the low-spin state MnO (d3) accompanied by the two-side Mn-N bonds turn to symmetry. Our results provide an in-depth understanding of the electronic properties strategy for exploiting asymmetric surface functionalization to achieve the transition between half-metal and semiconductor in MXenes. 本工作揭示了Mn₂N MXenes (Mn₂NOT, T = O, F, OH, 和 Cl)中半金属性和半导体性的内在物理机制。我们发现半金属性和半导体性与结构对称性和金属原子的3d轨道简并无关，这与已被广泛研究的TMDs形成鲜明对比。Mn-N键长始终大于Mn-X键（X = O, OH, F）的键长。尽管Mn₂NOCl中Mn-N键长短于Mn-Cl键，但Mn八面体的畸变对称性仍与O、OH和F钝化的Mn₂N一致。半金属性和半导体性由两侧Mn的3d能级分离和电子构型决定，并受不同表面钝化原子的影响。两侧Mn的3d能级分离后不同的电子构型（MnF完全填满的3d轨道和MnO空的eI）对应于不对称的Mn-N电势，导致Mn₂NOF和Mn₂NOOH中的带隙打开。相反，两侧Mn的eI轨道都部分填充的闭合3d轨道构型对应于对称的Mn-N电势，导致Mn₂NO₂和Mn₂NOCl中的半金属性。我们的机制启发了我们使用电荷掺杂和单轴应变来调制半导体到半金属的转变。电荷掺杂将使Janus Mn₂NOT (T = F和OH)转变为半导体，因为MnO和MnF带的eI分别在电子和空穴掺杂后部分填充。单轴拉伸应变也将促进Janus Mn₂NOT转变为半金属，因为电子从高自旋态MnF (d⁵)转移到低自旋态MnO (d³)，伴随两侧Mn-N键趋于对称。我们的结果提供了对利用不对称表面功能化实现MXenes中半金属与半导体之间转变的电子性质策略的深入理解。
