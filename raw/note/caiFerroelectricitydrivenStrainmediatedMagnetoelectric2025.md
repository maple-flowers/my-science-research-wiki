> [!quote]- <center>Abstract</center>
> 
> In the post-Moore era, CMOS technology faces challenges in storage and power consumption. Two-dimensional van der Waals ferromagnets, with their atomically sharp interfaces, enable heterostructure with ferroelectric materials. Through strong magnetoelectric coupling effects, they provide an ideal platform for developing highly efficient magnetoelectric interfaces. Leveraging this ideal platform, this study proposes a strain-modulation strategy based on vertically integrated two-dimensional van der Waals multiferroic heterojunctions Fe3GaTe2/P(VDF-TrFE) to address these challenges. This structure utilizes the inverse piezoelectric effect of ferroelectric polymers to induce strain. Through magnetoelectric coupling, the heterojunction achieves non-volatile reconfiguration of the magnetic anisotropy constant of Fe3GaTe2 at room temperature. This enables fully reversible electrical control of the anomalous Hall resistance and inverter functionality. Device integration validated reconfigurable logic gates and half-adder circuits, demonstrating ultra-low energy consumption (0.5 aJ), nanosecond-scale write speeds (5 ns), and high operational stability. Two-dimensional van der Waals ferromagnetic materials can achieve efficient integration with ferroelectric materials. The authors propose a strain-modulation strategy based on multiferroic heterostructure Fe3GaTe2/P(VDF-TrFE) to achieve low power operation.
>
>【摘要翻译】在后摩尔时代，CMOS技术在存储和功耗方面面临挑战。二维范德瓦尔斯铁磁体具有原子尖锐的界面，可以与铁电材料形成异质结构。通过强磁电耦合效应，为开发高效的磁电界面提供了理想的平台。利用这一理想平台，本研究提出了一种基于垂直集成二维范德华多铁性异质结Fe3GaTe2/P（VDF-TrFE）的应变调制策略。这种结构利用铁电聚合物的逆压电效应来诱导应变。该异质结通过磁电耦合实现了室温下Fe3GaTe2磁各向异性常数的非易失性重构。这使得完全可逆的电气控制异常霍尔电阻和逆变器的功能。器件集成验证了可重构逻辑门和半加器电路，展示了超低能耗（0.5 aJ）、纳秒级写入速度（5 ns）和高操作稳定性。二维范德华铁磁材料可以实现与铁电材料的有效集成。提出了一种基于多铁性异质结构Fe3GaTe2/P（VDF-TrFE）的应变调制策略，以实现低功耗工作。

> ## 一、引言
> 
> 在“后摩尔时代”的今天，我们熟知的传统硅基芯片技术（CMOS）正面临两大发展瓶颈：一是**存储墙**，即数据在处理器和存储器之间频繁搬运造成的速度与能耗瓶颈；二是**功耗墙**，即随着晶体管尺寸微缩，芯片的发热和功耗急剧增加。这促使科学家们寻找全新的技术路径，其中，利用电子的“自旋”属性而非仅靠“电荷”来工作的**自旋电子学**成为一个极具潜力的方向。
> 
> 本文正是在这一背景下，聚焦于如何利用二维材料构建超低功耗、可重构的逻辑器件。研究的核心是**应变介导的磁电耦合效应**。简单来说，就是通过施加电压，让一种材料发生形变，这种形变再传递到另一种磁性材料上，从而改变其磁学性质，最终实现用电场（而非电流）来控制磁性信息。这好比不是直接用手去转动一个开关，而是通过一个精密的杠杆系统，用更省力、更精准的方式去控制它。
> 
> 本文提出了一种基于**二维多铁异质结**的应变调控策略，该结构由室温铁磁体**Fe₃GaTe₂**和铁电聚合物**P(VDF-TrFE)**垂直堆叠而成。这项工作不仅首次在室温下实现了对二维磁性材料磁各向异性常数的非易失性、全电学可逆调控，还成功演示了基于此原理的、具有超低功耗（0.5 aJ）和纳秒级写入速度（5 ns）的可重构逻辑门（AND/NAND/NOT）和半加器电路，为下一代非易失性存储和存算一体技术提供了重要的理论和实验基础。
> 
> ## 二、论文结构与逻辑概览
> 
> 本文遵循一条清晰的“问题提出→策略设计→机理验证→功能演示”的逻辑主线。
> 
> _流程图示意：_
> 
> 1.  **背景与挑战**：后摩尔时代CMOS技术的功耗与存储瓶颈，传统磁电耦合器件界面缺陷多、效率低。
> 2.  **研究策略**：提出基于二维范德华**多铁异质结 Fe₃GaTe₂/P(VDF-TrFE)** 的应变调控策略。
> 3.  **核心机理**：
>     -   **顶层调控**：顶层P(VDF-TrFE)通过**铁电极化**的电荷调控，主要改变Fe₃GaTe₂的载流子浓度，从而影响其矫顽场和霍尔电阻。
>     -   **底层调控**：底层P(VDF-TrFE)通过**逆压电效应**产生应变，并传递至Fe₃GaTe₂，通过改变其晶格常数来调制**磁各向异性常数(K₁)**，实现易磁化轴方向的偏转。这是本文的核心创新点。
> 4.  **多维验证**：
>     -   **输运测量**：通过反常霍尔效应（AHE）曲线变化，直接观测到磁化方向的变化。
>     -   **结构表征**：通过**拉曼光谱**和**X射线衍射（XRD）**，定量表征了应变的大小和晶格变化。
>     -   **理论计算**：通过**第一性原理计算**，从原子尺度揭示了应变如何影响交换耦合能和磁各向异性能（MAE），从而改变磁化方向。
> 5.  **功能演示**：利用这一调控机制，构建了可重构逻辑门阵列和半加器电路，并演示了其在二分类模式识别中的应用潜力。
> 6.  **结论与展望**：总结了该策略在低功耗、高速度、可重构性方面的优势，并展望了其在新型计算架构中的应用前景。
>

> ## 四、正文核心内容剖析
> 
> ### 1. 研究背景与动机
> 
> 文章开篇立意于后摩尔时代的“存储墙”和“功耗墙”挑战，明确指出了基于电压调控的磁性技术是破局的关键。传统的多铁异质结因晶格失配导致界面缺陷，严重阻碍了磁电耦合效率。二维范德华铁磁体的出现，以其原子级平整的界面，为解决这一难题提供了理想平台。作者指出现有研究的两个核心切入点：一是利用应变精确调控磁性，避免了传统方法的非线性与迟滞问题；二是利用二维材料的弱层间耦合，极大地放大了界面效应。
> 
> ### 2. 核心方法与模型
> 
> 本文的核心方法是**应变介导的磁电耦合**，其巧妙之处在于“非对称双栅极结构”的设计：
> 
> -   **材料选择**：选择室温铁磁体Fe₃GaTe₂（具有强垂直磁各向异性PMA）和铁电聚合物P(VDF-TrFE)（具有强压电效应和良好的柔性）。
> -   **调控机制**：
>     -   **顶层栅极**：主要利用P(VDF-TrFE)的**铁电场效应**，通过界面电荷转移/轨道重组来调控Fe₃GaTe₂的载流子浓度，从而影响其矫顽场(Hc)和反常霍尔电阻(Rxy)。这是一种“电流”调控。
>     -   **底层栅极**：这是本文的灵魂。当电压施加到底栅极时，P(VDF-TrFE)的**逆压电效应**使其发生形变。由于底部硅基底的刚性约束，这种形变被高效地转化为面内应力，并通过范德华界面传递到Fe₃GaTe₂层，使其晶格发生拉伸或压缩。这种“机械”应变直接调制了Fe₃GaTe₂的磁各向异性常数K₁，改变了其易磁化轴的方向，实现了一种“磁场”调控。
> -   **理论模型**：通过第一性原理计算，构建了不同应变下的Fe₃GaTe₂模型，定量分析了应变对交换耦合能和磁各向异性能的影响，为实验观察提供了坚实的微观物理图像。
> 
> ### 3. 实验与数据
> 
> 实验部分环环相扣，证据链完整：
> 
> -   **电学输运测量**：通过测量不同栅压下的AHE曲线，直接观测到磁化翻转和易磁化轴偏转，计算了K₁的变化。
> -   **微观结构表征**：TEM证实了原子级陡峭的界面，为高效应变传递提供了结构前提。
> -   **光谱学验证**：拉曼光谱的峰位移动和XRD的晶格常数变化，共同、定量地证实了应变的存在及其对Fe₃GaTe₂晶格的影响。这是连接“电压”和“磁性变化”的关键证据链。
> -   **功能器件演示**：成功演示了可重构逻辑门和半加器，并将器件性能（0.5 aJ, 5 ns）与同类技术进行了对比，证明了其优越性。此外，二分类的演示证明该器件在模拟计算和神经网络硬件化方面也具有潜力。
> 
> ## 五、结论与贡献
> 
> 本研究的核心发现是：利用二维多铁异质结（Fe₃GaTe₂/P(VDF-TrFE)）中的应变介导磁电耦合效应，在室温下实现了对磁性的非易失、全电学可逆调控。
> 
> 其主要贡献在于：
> 
> 1.  **提出了一种新的调控机制**：首次阐明了在双栅极结构中，底栅极通过逆压电效应引入的应变，可以高效、非对称地调控二维铁磁体的磁各向异性常数，而顶栅极则主要通过铁电场效应进行调控。这种“双机制”解耦为多功能器件设计提供了新范式。
> 2.  **构建了完整的证据链**：通过电学测量、微观结构表征、光谱学分析和第一性原理计算，从宏观到微观，从现象到本质，系统性地揭示了应变-晶格-磁性的耦合机制。
> 3.  **展示了优越的性能指标**：基于该机制的器件实现了0.5 aJ的超低写入能耗和5 ns的写入速度，在同类器件中名列前茅，极具应用潜力。
> 4.  **验证了功能可扩展性**：成功演示了可重构逻辑门、半加器乃至二分类任务，展示了该技术在存储、逻辑计算和新兴计算（如神经形态计算）领域的广阔前景。

> ## 七、学术思考与批判性分析
> 
> 1.  **应变传递的定量问题**：文章通过拉曼和XRD的变化证实了应变，但其定量分析依赖于模型和近似。应变在Fe₃GaTe₂层内部是否均匀？在垂直方向（厚度方向）上是否存在应变梯度？这些细节可能影响对磁化翻转过程更精确的理解。
> 2.  **器件疲劳与寿命**：P(VDF-TrFE)作为有机聚合物，其铁电和压电性能在反复极化后是否存在疲劳效应？文章的稳定性测试（如120小时）相对较短，对于实际应用，需要进行更长时间、更多循环次数的可靠性测试（如10^12次以上的读写循环）。
> 3.  **双栅极调控的串扰问题**：文章强调顶栅和底栅调控机制的独立性，但在实际器件中，当两个栅极同时工作时，它们产生的电场和应变场是否会产生复杂的串扰？文章在补充材料中讨论了这一点，但更深入、系统的串扰模型和控制策略仍需建立。
> 4.  **温度依赖性**：文章主要在室温下进行实验。Fe₃GaTe₂的居里温度，以及P(VDF-TrFE)的铁电相变温度，对器件的工作温度范围构成限制。全面研究其性能随温度的变化，对于评估其在不同环境下的应用至关重要。
> 5.  **理论模型的简化**：第一性原理计算基于块体或单层模型，且可能未完全考虑界面处的复杂相互作用（如电荷转移、轨道杂化、范德华力修正等）。一个包含界面效应的全尺度、多物理场模型将更有助于精确指导器件设计。
> 
> ## 八、用户问题预测与解答
> 
> 1.  **问：什么是“反常霍尔效应”，为什么它能用来表征磁性？** **答**：当电流通过磁性导体时，即使没有外加磁场，也会在垂直于电流的方向上产生一个电压，这就是AHE。其物理根源在于材料内部的自旋-轨道耦合作用，使得不同自旋方向的电子在运动时发生偏转，并产生电荷积累。AHE的强度（即反常霍尔电阻 Rxy）与材料的磁化强度成正比，且其符号取决于磁化方向（向上或向下）。因此，通过测量Rxy，我们就能灵敏地“读出”材料的磁化状态。本研究中，Rxy随电场变化，就直观地反映了磁性被电场调控的过程。
>     
> 2.  **问：为什么选择二维材料Fe₃GaTe₂，而不是传统的金属铁磁体？** **答**：主要有三个优势：**a）原子级平整的界面**：二维材料层间依靠弱的范德华力结合，可以像搭积木一样堆叠，形成没有悬空键和晶格失配缺陷的超高质量界面，这对高效传递应变至关重要。**b）强的界面效应**：二维材料很薄，其性质极易受到界面影响。一点微小的应变或电荷转移，就能在整个材料中引发巨大的性能变化，调控效率极高。**c）突破传统限制**：传统铁磁薄膜受刚性基底束缚，易产生横向晶格弛豫，导致应变梯度耗散，调控效果不佳。二维材料的量子限域效应和层间滑移特性克服了这一问题。
>     
> 3.  **问：文中提到的“非易失性”是什么意思？为什么很重要？** **答**：“非易失性”是指断电后，数据或状态不会丢失。在本文中，指的是通过电压脉冲改变P(VDF-TrFE)的极化状态后，即使撤去电压，这种极化状态以及它引起的Fe₃GaTe₂的磁性变化都能长期保持（如文中展示的120小时）。这非常重要，因为它意味着用这种器件做成的存储器，数据在不通电时也能保存，不像传统电脑内存（DRAM）那样需要不断刷新，从而极大降低静态功耗，是实现超低功耗计算的关键。
>     
> 4.  **问：什么是“存算一体”？本文的工作如何体现这一概念？** **答**：“存算一体”是打破传统冯·诺依曼架构中“存储”与“计算”物理分离瓶颈的一种新范式。它旨在让存储器本身具备计算能力，从而避免数据在CPU和内存之间频繁搬运带来的能耗和延迟。本文完美体现了这一概念：**a）逻辑运算**：同一个Fe₃GaTe₂/P(VDF-TrFE)器件，既是存储单元（通过铁电极化状态存储信息），又是计算单元（通过调控磁性直接实现AND、NAND等逻辑功能）。**b）物理实现**：在图5的二分类演示中，器件阵列直接根据输入信号进行加权求和与非线性激活，这本身就是一种模拟计算，将“存”与“算”在物理层面融为一体。
>     
> 5.  **问：这个研究距离实际应用还有多远？面临的主要挑战是什么？** **答**：这项工作仍处于实验室原理性验证阶段，距离商业化还有相当距离。主要挑战包括：**a）规模化生产**：如何在大尺寸晶圆上均匀、高质量地制备这种复杂的多层异质结，并确保器件性能的高度一致性，是巨大的工程挑战。**b）与CMOS工艺的兼容性**：P(VDF-TrFE)是一种有机聚合物，其耐温性、图案化工艺等需要与现有的硅基CMOS后道工艺兼容，这需要大量的工艺开发。**c）长期可靠性**：如前文所述，器件的疲劳、保持力、耐久性等还需要经过工业级标准的严格测试。**d）寄生效应**：在超大规模集成时，互连线的寄生电阻、电容和电感可能会严重影响器件的高速低功耗优势。
>

> ## 九、专区：便于 Obsidian Dataview 插件调用
> 
> 领域基础知识:: 后摩尔时代，传统CMOS技术面临存储墙和功耗墙，自旋电子学利用电子自旋属性进行信息处理，是重要突破方向。二维范德华(vdW)铁磁体因其原子级平整界面，为构建高效磁电耦合异质结提供了理想平台。多铁异质结通过铁电/铁磁界面的耦合，可实现电场调控磁性，其中应变介导的磁电耦合是实现低功耗控制的关键机制。
> 
> 研究背景:: 传统磁电耦合器件存在晶格失配导致的界面缺陷、耦合效率低和热稳定性差等问题。二维vdW铁磁体（如Fe₃GaTe₂）的出现克服了界面问题，其弱层间耦合和强的界面效应使得通过应变精确调控磁性成为可能。如何利用这一优势，在室温下实现非易失、超低功耗的纯电学磁性调控，并构建可重构自旋逻辑器件，是该领域的前沿挑战。
> 
> 作者的问题意识:: 如何利用二维范德华异质结的独特优势，设计一种新型的应变调控策略，以克服传统磁电耦合器件的局限性，实现在室温下对二维铁磁体磁性的非易失、全电学、超低功耗的精准调控，并将其应用于构建可重构逻辑和存算一体器件。
> 
> 主要研究对象:: 基于二维室温铁磁体Fe₃GaTe₂和铁电聚合物P(VDF-TrFE)垂直堆叠的双栅极范德华多铁异质结。
>

> 
> 1.  **问题：为什么选择P(VDF-TrFE)这种有机聚合物作为铁电/压电层，而不是无机压电材料（如PMN-PT）？** **解答**：有几个关键优势：1）**界面质量**：聚合物与二维材料同为范德华材料，可形成更紧密、少缺陷的界面，有利于应力传递。2）**制备工艺**：P(VDF-TrFE)可通过简单的旋涂法制备，工艺温度低，与CMOS后端工艺兼容性更好，而生长高质量无机压电薄膜通常需要高温外延。3）**固有保护**：P(VDF-TrFE)的C-F链疏水，可作为天然的封装层，保护易氧化的Fe₃GaTe₂。

> 2.  **问题：文中的“非易失性”是如何实现的？“写入”后撤掉电压，磁性状态会恢复吗？** **解答**：这正是应变调控的巧妙之处。当对底栅P(VDF-TrFE)施加电压脉冲极化后，即使撤去电压，P(VDF-TrFE)的剩余极化强度（Pr）依然存在，其维持的逆压电应变状态也得以保留。这个残留的应变持续作用于Fe₃GaTe₂，使其晶格畸变和磁各向异性状态的改变被“冻结”下来，从而实现了非易失性。这与铁电存储器的原理类似，只是存储的中间媒介是“应变”。

> 3.  **问题：什么是“反常霍尔效应”（AHE），为什么用它来读取磁性状态？** **解答**：**反常霍尔效应**（AHE，Anomalous Hall Effect）是铁磁材料特有的一种物理现象。当电流通过一个铁磁导体时，即使没有外加磁场，电流也会受到材料内部自发磁化强度的影响，在垂直于电流的方向上产生一个霍尔电压。这个电压的大小与材料的磁化强度（M）成正比，方向则取决于磁化方向。因此，通过测量AHE电阻（Rxy = V_Hall / I），就可以直接、简洁地“读出”Fe₃GaTe₂的磁化方向和大小，是一种电学读出磁状态的标准方法。
>     
> 4.  **问题：文中提到的“0.5 aJ”和“5 ns”是什么概念？** **解答**：**aJ**（阿托焦耳）是10⁻¹⁸焦耳的能量单位，这是一个极其微小的能量。作为对比，一个传统CMOS晶体管一次开关大约消耗几十**fJ**（飞焦耳，10⁻¹⁵焦耳），是其数千倍。**ns**（纳秒）是10⁻⁹秒。这意味着该器件每进行一次状态的写入，仅消耗0.5 aJ的能量，且仅需5 ns就能完成。这组数据表明该技术路径在超低功耗、高速计算领域具有压倒性的优势。

> 5.  **问题：既然Fe₃GaTe₂本身就有室温铁磁性，为什么还要费力气去调控它的磁各向异性？** **解答**：调控磁各向异性是实现信息存储和处理的关键。一个有室温铁磁性的材料，只意味着它有磁性，但它的磁矩可能指向任意方向，无法稳定地代表“0”和“1”。**垂直磁各向异性（PMA）** 意味着磁矩天然倾向于稳定指向“向上”或“向下”这两个垂直于薄膜平面的方向。这两个方向正好可以定义二进制中的“0”和“1”。通过电场调控PMA，就是通过“拨动”磁矩，让它在“向上”和“向下”之间翻转，从而写入信息。本研究的核心价值，就是找到了一个巧妙的方法（电控应变）来高效地“拨动”这个磁矩。

> In the post-Moore era, CMOS technology faces challenges in storage and power consumption. Two-dimensional van der Waals ferromagnets, with their atomically sharp interfaces, enable heterostructure with ferroelectric materials. Through strong magnetoelectric coupling effects, they provide an ideal platform for developing highly efficient magnetoelectric interfaces. Leveraging this ideal platform, this study proposes a strain-modulation strategy based on vertically integrated two-dimensional van der Waals multiferroic heterojunctions Fe3GaTe2/P(VDF-TrFE) to address these challenges. This structure utilizes the inverse piezoelectric effect of ferroelectric polymers to induce strain. Through magnetoelectric coupling, the heterojunction achieves non-volatile reconfiguration of the magnetic anisotropy constant of Fe3GaTe2 at room temperature. This enables fully reversible electrical control of the anomalous Hall resistance and inverter functionality. Device integration validated reconfigurable logic gates and half-adder circuits, demonstrating ultralow energy consumption (0.5 aJ), nanosecond-scale write speeds (5 ns), and high operational stability. 在后摩尔时代，CMOS技术在存储和功耗方面面临挑战。二维范德华铁磁体凭借其原子级锐利界面，能够与铁电材料形成异质结构。通过强磁电耦合效应，它们为开发高效磁电界面提供了理想平台。基于此理想平台，本研究提出了一种基于垂直集成二维范德华多铁异质结Fe3GaTe2/P(VDF-TrFE)的应变调控策略，以应对这些挑战。该结构利用铁电聚合物的逆压电效应引入应变。通过磁电耦合，异质结在室温下实现了Fe3GaTe2磁各向异性常数的非易失性重构，从而实现了对反常霍尔电阻的全可逆电控和反相器功能。器件集成验证了可重构逻辑门和半加器电路，展现出超低功耗（0.5 aJ）、纳秒级写入速度（5 ns）和高操作稳定性。 **CMOS**: 互补金属氧化物半导体，一种主要的集成电路制造技术，基于n型和p型MOSFET的互补配对。 **van der Waals ferromagnets**: 范德华铁磁体，层间通过弱范德华力结合的二维铁磁材料，具有原子级平整界面。 **Inverse piezoelectric effect**: 逆压电效应，施加电场导致压电材料产生机械形变的现象。 **Magnetic anisotropy constant**: 磁各向异性常数，描述材料磁化方向对特定晶轴偏好程度的物理量。 **Anomalous Hall resistance**: 反常霍尔电阻，在铁磁材料中，由于自旋-轨道耦合产生的额外霍尔效应所对应的电阻。

> In the development process of integrated circuits in the post-Moore era, the traditional silicon-based CMOS technology is facing the dual challenges of memory wall and power consumption limitation, which prompts researchers to turn their attention to the voltage-controlled magnetic technology with the characteristics of nonvolatility, reconfigurability, and high energy efficiency. As an important carrier in this field, spintronic devices realize information encoding through magnetic moment quantum states, and their natural non-volatile characteristics and operation energy consumption on the order of pico-joules provide a physical basis for breaking through the bottleneck of von Neumann architectures1,2. Conventional heterostructure devices suffer from lattice mismatch-induced interfacial defects, leading to inefficient magnetoelectric coupling and inadequate thermal stability, which severely hinder large-scale integration3,4. The emergence of 2D van der Waals (vdW) ferromagnets has addressed this challenge by offering atomically smooth interfaces, thus enabling the integration of diverse crystals with atomically sharp interfaces for intriguing functionalities and advanced strain regulation5,6. Precise regulation of magnetic exchange by strain can overcome the lattice continuity limitation and Poisson effect of conventional ferromagnetic materials, and its high linearity and low hysteresis are superior to the nonlinear response and strain dissipation defects of conventional ferromagnetic materials2. The latest research demonstrates that the strong coupling effect between interface strain and magnetic order in these materials not only overcomes the energy efficiency bottleneck of traditional charge-based regulation but also elucidates the synergistic mechanism of magnetoelectric coupling of multi-physical fields7. This finding provides a theoretical foundation for constructing ultra-lowpower spin-logic units. 2D vdW ferromagnetic materials provide a compelling platform for both logic device design and the investigation of magnetoelectric coupling, as their magnetoelectric properties can be effectively modulated via strain engineering and electrical gating8,9. The inherently weak interlayer magnetic interactions in these materials amplify interface effects, particularly in ferromagnetic/ferroelectric heterostructures. For example, ferroelectric polarization can significantly modulate the magnetic order and magnetic anisotropy of the ferromagnet through mechanisms such as charge transfer and spin-orbit coupling10,11. Additionally, a piezoelectric layer can couple to the magnetosphere via mechanical deformation, enabling dynamic modulation of magnetic properties12. As an illustrative case, ferroelectric gating of Fe3GeTe2 can not only reorient its magnetization axis but also modulate the carrier type and spin polarization of Schottky barriers13. Furthermore, the engineered design of the vdW gap enhances the tunability of the magnetoelectric response14. Beyond Fe3GeTe2, novel materials like Fe3GaTe2, which exhibit room-temperature perpendicular magnetic anisotropy, a high spin-Hall angle, efficient charge-spin conversion, and excellent air stability, offer promising pathways for enabling low-power, nonvolatile operation in magnetooptical nanodevices and spin logic circuits15. These magnetoelectric couplingbased regulation strategies, which directly manipulate magnetic parameters such as Curie temperature and spin polarization through voltage or strain, represent a departure from traditional logic devices reliant on complex complementary MOSFET structures. Consequently, they are driving the advancement of highly integrated magnetoelectric heterostructure devices towards practical application. In the ultra-low power consumption strategy based on strain control proposed here, the ferroelectric P(VDF-TrFE) bottom gate has been demonstrated to effectively regulate the magnetic anisotropy constant via strain-mediated magnetoelectric coupling. Specifically, the inverse piezoelectric effect in the P(VDF-TrFE) layer induces a nonuniform strain field, which, through mechanical conduction, acts upon the Fe3GaTe2 lattice. This interaction leads to a reorganization of the magnetic crystal’s anisotropic properties, resulting in a dynamic deflection of the magnetization axis. Consequently, the magnetic anisotropy constant, K1, can be modulated by applying a polarization voltage. Reversing the polarization direction of the P(VDF-TrFE) layer induces contraction via the inverse piezoelectric effect, thereby altering the direction of the easy magnetization axis and enabling fully electrically reversible modulation of the anomalous Hall resistance. This strain-mediated coupling mechanism has been experimentally corroborated using Raman spectroscopy and X-ray diffraction (XRD). Furthermore, Fe3GaTe2 models incorporating various strain configurations were developed to elucidate the effects of strain on the exchange coupling energy and magnetic anisotropy of Fe3GaTe2. Finally, device integration was used to demonstrate reconfigurable logic gate arrays (AND/NAND/NOT) and half-adder circuits, providing a crucial theoretical foundation and a feasible technical pathway for the design of innovative magnetoelectric coupling devices. 在后摩尔时代集成电路的发展进程中，传统硅基CMOS技术正面临存储墙与功耗限制的双重挑战，这促使研究者将目光转向具有非易失性、可重构性和高能效特征的电压控制磁性技术。作为该领域的重要载体，自旋电子器件通过磁矩量子态实现信息编码，其固有的非易失特性和皮焦量级的操作功耗为突破冯·诺依曼架构瓶颈提供了物理基础1,2。传统异质结器件因晶格失配导致的界面缺陷，造成磁电耦合效率低下和热稳定性不足，严重阻碍了大规模集成3,4。二维范德华(vdW)铁磁体的出现解决了这一挑战，其原子级光滑界面使得具有原子级陡峭界面的多种晶体能够集成，从而实现新奇功能与先进的应变调控5,6。通过应变精确调控磁交换作用，可克服传统铁磁材料的晶格连续性限制和泊松效应，其高线性度和低迟滞性优于传统铁磁材料的非线性响应和应变耗散缺陷2。最新研究表明，这些材料中界面应变与磁序之间的强耦合效应不仅克服了传统电荷调控的能效瓶颈，还阐明了多物理场磁电耦合的协同机制7。这一发现为构建超低功耗自旋逻辑单元提供了理论基础。二维vdW铁磁材料为逻辑器件设计和磁电耦合研究提供了极具吸引力的平台，因为其磁电特性可通过应变工程和电栅极进行有效调控8,9。这些材料固有的弱层间磁相互作用放大了界面效应，特别是在铁磁/铁电异质结构中。例如，铁电极化可通过电荷转移和自旋-轨道耦合等机制显著调控铁磁体的磁序和磁各向异性10,11。此外，压电层可通过机械形变与磁层耦合，实现磁性的动态调控12。一个典型的例子是，Fe3GeTe2的铁电栅控不仅能重新定向其磁化轴，还能调控肖特基势垒的载流子类型和自旋极化13。此外，vdW间隙的工程化设计增强了磁电响应的可调性14。除Fe3GeTe2外，新型材料如Fe3GaTe2表现出室温垂直磁各向异性、高自旋霍尔角、高效的电荷-自旋转换以及优异的空气稳定性，为磁光纳米器件和自旋逻辑电路中的低功耗、非易失性操作提供了极具前景的途径15。这些基于磁电耦合的调控策略，通过电压或应变直接操控居里温度和自旋极化等磁性参数，摆脱了对复杂互补MOSFET结构的依赖，从而推动高度集成的磁电异质结器件向实际应用迈进。在此提出的基于应变控制的超低功耗策略中，铁电P(VDF-TrFE)底栅已被证实能通过应变介导的磁电耦合有效调控磁各向异性常数。具体而言，P(VDF-TrFE)层中的逆压电效应诱导非均匀应变场，通过机械传导作用于Fe3GaTe2晶格，导致磁性晶体各向异性重新组织，引起磁化轴的动态偏转。因此，施加极化电压可调控磁各向异性常数K1。反转P(VDF-TrFE)层的极化方向通过逆压电效应引起收缩，从而改变易磁化轴方向，实现对反常霍尔电阻的全电可逆调制。该应变介导的耦合机制已通过拉曼光谱和X射线衍射(XRD)实验验证。此外，构建了包含不同应变构型的Fe3GaTe2模型，以阐明应变对交换耦合能和磁各向异性的影响。最后，通过器件集成展示了可重构逻辑门阵列(AND/NAND/NOT)和半加器电路，为创新磁电耦合器件的设计提供了关键理论基础和可行技术路径。 **Spintronic devices**: 自旋电子器件，利用电子自旋而非仅电荷来进行信息处理和存储的器件。 **von Neumann architecture**: 冯·诺依曼架构，一种将程序指令存储和数据存储合在一起的计算机设计概念，面临处理器与内存之间速度不匹配的"存储墙"问题。 **Perpendicular magnetic anisotropy (PMA)**: 垂直磁各向异性，磁化易轴垂直于材料平面的特性，有利于高密度数据存储。 **Spin-Hall angle**: 自旋霍尔角，表征自旋霍尔效应中横向自旋流与纵向电荷流转换效率的物理量。 **Curie temperature**: 居里温度，铁磁材料失去其铁磁性的临界温度。

> ### Electric field-dependent modulation of magnetic properties
> 
> 磁场对磁性性能的电场依赖性调控

> To modulate the magnetic properties of two-dimensional ferromagnetic materials, we propose a method that introduces strain by stretching or compressing the crystal lattice through the inverse piezoelectric effect. To achieve this, we fabricated a vertically stacked, multi-layer magnetic device, schematically illustrated in Fig. 1a (optical images are shown in Supplementary Fig. 1a), with the fabrication process detailed in the Methods section. The ferroelectric layer comprises P(VDF-TrFE), which possesses a spiral ferroelectric β-phase crystalline structure, as depicted in the inset of Fig. 1a. X-ray diffraction (XRD) and Fourier transform infrared spectroscopy (FTIR) characterization confirmed substantial ferroelectric β-phase crystallization within the P(VDF-TrFE) thin film. Moreover, piezoelectric force microscopy (PFM) and P-E loop measurements demonstrated its excellent ferroelectric properties (Supplementary Fig. 2). The twodimensional ferromagnetic layer consists of Fe3GaTe2, wherein each layer features two non-equivalent Fe atomic sites among the five adjacent atoms (Fig. 1a inset)16. The Fe atoms, specifically their 3 d electrons and magnetic moment arrangement, are the primary contributors to the ferromagnetism of Fe3GaTe2. The advantage of this organic-inorganic heterostructure lies in the strong polarization characteristics of the P(VDF-TrFE) spiral β phase at the interface. Coupling between the C-F dipoles and the lone pair electrons of Te atoms on the Fe3GaTe2 surface induces a local electric field, triggering the recombination of Fe atomic d-orbital electrons in spin states within Fe3GaTe2 and thereby significantly enhancing the magnetoelectric coupling efficiency of the device17. Furthermore, the synergistic effect of the Te atom passivation layer and the hydrophobic C-F chains of P(VDF-TrFE) effectively prevents the penetration of environmental oxygen or water molecules, inhibiting the oxidation of Fe3+ and preserving the intrinsic ferromagnetism of Fe3GaTe218,19. Concurrently, the lateral ferroelectric polarization of P(VDF-TrFE) can couple with the magnetic moments of Fe3GaTe2 through the interfacial polarization field, forming a magnetoelectric structure where the vertical magnetic anisotropy is orthogonal to the lateral electric polarization. The magnetoelectric coupling efficiency is further enhanced via the inverse Rashba-Edelstein effect (IREE)20–22. The high-quality, atomic-level steep P(VDF-TrFE)/ Fe3GaTe2 interface effectively minimizes interface scattering effects during carrier migration23, leading to a more concentrated electric field distribution within the P(VDF-TrFE) layer. This, in turn, enhances the uniformity and stability of ferroelectric domain polarization reversal24. In addition, strong interface bonding, facilitated by hydrogen bonds or van der Waals interactions, ensures high consistency between the dipole alignment direction and the electric field direction, enabling a dynamic modulation range exceeding 104 in amplitude. Finally, the high-quality P(VDF-TrFE)/Fe3GaTe2 interface helps to mitigate lattice mismatch issues during organic film preparation, facilitating efficient strain transfer via the van der Waals interface through the inverse piezoelectric effect of P(VDF-TrFE), thereby enabling strain-modulated magnetism. The anomalous Hall effect (AHE) curves for the device were measured at 300 K within a magnetic field range of −5 T to 5 T, as a function of magnetic field angle (θH) (Supplementary Fig. 3a), without the application of a polarization voltage. These magnetic fielddependent anomalous Hall resistance (Rxy-B) curves indicate strong perpendicular magnetization (PMA) in the device, primarily stemming from the unique crystal and electronic structure of Fe3GaTe2, which results in strong spin-orbit coupling (literature). Fitting the angular dependence of θH and θM (the tilt angle between the magnetization direction and the sample plane, as shown in Supplementary Fig. 3b) yields an estimated anisotropy constant Ku of approximately 4.82 × 105 J/m3. Applying a polarization voltage to the dual gate induces asymmetric modulation effects in the top and bottom layers of P(VDFTrFE). Modulating Fe3GaTe2 via a polarization voltage applied to the bottom P(VDF-TrFE) layer involves both ferroelectric polarization modulation and strain regulation. Ferroelectric polarization is achieved through charge regulation mechanisms at the ferroelectricferromagnetic interface. Specifically, polarization reversal in the ferroelectric P(VDF-TrFE) generates a dynamic dipole field through interfacial charge transfer, indirectly and linearly regulating the carrier concentration of Fe3GaTe2. This change in carrier concentration within Fe3GaTe2, a self-doped semiconductor, alters the occupation state of the Fe atom 3 d orbitals, thereby affecting the strength of electronic correlations. Consequently, this influences magnetic exchange interactions, leading to alterations in the magnetic moment and density of states of Fe3GaTe2, ultimately achieving magnetic modulation25. As shown in Supplementary Fig. 4, applying different polarization voltages to the bottom P(VDF-TrFE) layer, with a fixed magnetic field angle θH = 90°, results in variations in the device’s coercive field and anomalous Hall resistance. The coercive field (HC) and anomalous Hall resistance (Rxy) decrease under positive polarization voltages and increase under negative polarization voltages, consistent with prior reports26. Calculations indicate that the magnetoelectric coupling coefficient αCME is 8.6 × 10−6 S/m at a polarization voltage of −10 V and 6.4 × 10-6 S/m at a polarization voltage of 10 V27 (Supplementary Note 1). P(VDF-TrFE)‘s inherent ferroelectric and piezoelectric properties are crucial to the observed behavior. Application of a polarization voltage not only reorients the dipole distribution within the material but also induces deformation via the inverse piezoelectric effect. This phenomenon arises from the highly polarized nature of the C-F bonds in P(VDF-TrFE), causing the molecular dipole moment to respond to the electric field28, which in turn modifies intermolecular forces and results in macroscopic deformation. The P(VDF-TrFE) layer, forming a robust interfacial bond with the silicon substrate (Fig. 1b and Supplementary Fig. 5a), experiences significantly restricted out-of-plane displacement, effectively anchoring it to the substrate surface. Consequently, when the P(VDF-TrFE) layer undergoes inverse piezoelectric deformation, the rigid substrate prevents the release of strain in the downward direction. This constraint results in a substantial conversion of the deformation into in-plane stress, either tensile or compressive. This stress is then efficiently transferred to the adjacent Fe3GaTe2 layer via interfacial shear, facilitating effective utilization of the strain energy. Fundamentally, these constraint conditions promote the preferential transfer of piezoelectric strain energy as shear stress along the interface, rather than allowing for its dissipation through unconstrained deformation, thereby enhancing the overall efficiency of strain transfer. The resulting mechanical stress is transmitted to the ferromagnetic Fe3GaTe2 layer, inducing tensile strain and lattice expansion. Critically, the two-dimensional nature of the ferromagnetic material overcomes the limitations of traditional ferromagnetic systems imposed by rigid substrates, which often lead to lateral lattice relaxation and strain gradient dissipation. This is achieved through the synergistic effects of quantum confinement and interlayer slip barriers4,6. This unique structure enables efficient control of magnetic anisotropy under stress coupling, achieving lattice stress transmission efficiency far exceeding that of conventional ferromagnetic materials. Fe3GaTe2 exhibits strong perpendicular magnetic anisotropy (PMA), with its easy magnetization axis oriented perpendicular to the material plane. The applied strain distorts the lattice structure, leading to a redistribution of magnetic anisotropy by altering the orbital occupation state of iron atoms, thereby influencing the orientation of the easy magnetization axis29. Furthermore, to better characterize the magnetic anisotropy of the dual-gate device, the magnetic anisotropy constant K1 was calculated under various polarization voltages16,30 (calculation details are provided in Supplementary Note 2). As shown in Fig. 1c, d, applying a polarization voltage to the bottom P(VDF-TrFE) ferroelectric layer, regardless of its polarity, induces a significant reduction in the magnetic crystalline anisotropy constant K1 of Fe3GaTe2, exhibiting a distinct hysteresis loop during the ±10 V voltage sweep. This demonstrates that the polarization state of the underlying P(VDF-TrFE) layer effectively regulates the perpendicular magnetic anisotropy (PMA) of Fe3GaTe2. The underlying mechanism primarily stems from the electric field effect generated by the polarization charges of the ferroelectric layer at the interface. This electric field modulates the electronic structure of Fe3GaTe2 that determines the perpendicular anisotropy (particularly the orbital occupation states related to spinorbit coupling) through interfacial charge transfer or orbital restructuring, thereby weakening its PMA strength, which is observed as a decrease in K1. In contrast, the K1 value for the top P(VDF-TrFE) layer exhibits no consistent changes upon application of a polarization voltage, remaining relatively stable. This indicates that the ferroelectric polarization of the top layer has a negligible effect on the vertical magnetic anisotropy of Fe3GaTe2. In summary, the charge transfer induced by the polarization of the top ferroelectric layer exhibits a negligible effect on the magnetic anisotropy of Fe3GaTe2. Conversely, the strain-mediated control exerted by the polarization of the bottom ferroelectric layer significantly modulates the vertical magnetic anisotropy of the Fe3GaTe2 film, as evidenced by a decrease in the K1 value. Consequently, as shown in Fig. 2a, b, when the magnetic field angle θH = 0°, applying different polarization voltages causes the easy magnetization axis direction of the anomalous Hall curve in the device to deflect, reflecting changes in the in-plane magnetization direction. Specifically, applying a negative polarization voltage induces tensile strain in the P(VDF-TrFE), and subsequent strain transmission causes the Fe3GaTe2 lattice to stretch, thereby enhancing the out-of-plane component of the magnetization axis. This enhancement increases with the magnitude of the applied polarization voltage; at −10 V, the easy magnetization axis is deflected from its original 90° orientation to 95°. Conversely, reversing the polarization of the P(VDF-TrFE) layer induces contraction via the inverse piezoelectric effect, enhancing the in-plane component of the easy axis and causing the magnetization axis to deflect in the in-plane direction. The distinct modulation mechanisms arising from the device’s asymmetry stem from the differential effects of voltage polarization on each P(VDF-TrFE) layer. Specifically, polarization of the bottom P(VDF-TrFE) layer induces both ferroelectric polarization modulation and strain modulation, whereas the top layer’s polarization primarily elicits ferroelectric polarization modulation. The former leverages charge control to effectively regulate HC, Rxy, and magnetic moment of the Fe3GaTe2 layer. Concurrently, strain modulation alters the Fe3GaTe2 lattice via strain transmission, thereby affecting the direction of the easy magnetization axis. The observed asymmetry between the top and bottom P(VDF-TrFE) layers is attributed to the presence of the rigid silicon substrate. The rigid substrate acts as a significant constraint, exerting considerable mechanical influence on both the top and bottom P(VDF-TrFE) layers. Analogous to a piezoelectric actuator configuration, the polymer layer functions as the driving element, generating strain through the inverse piezoelectric effect upon the application of an electric field. The robust adhesive interface between the polymer and the substrate mirrors the rigid substrate found in piezoelectric actuators31, effectively suppressing unproductive strain and facilitating the directional transfer of strain energy. However, disparities in interfacial constraints dictate that the stress transmission efficiency of the P(VDF-TrFE) layer is substantially bottom downward (towards the substrate) compared to its upward transmission (towards the functional material), thereby influencing the overall device performance. Voltage polarization of the top P(VDF-TrFE) layer also induces carrier changes and deformation. Conversely, the mechanical environment of the top P(VDF-TrFE) layer differs significantly. Its bottom surface is bonded to the Fe3GaTe2/bottom P(VDF-TrFE)/substrate system, imposing constraints, while its top surface remains a free interface. Under voltage excitation, this disparity renders the top layer more susceptible to out-of-plane relaxation, such as warping or bending. Consequently, a greater proportion of mechanical energy dissipates through volumetric deformation within the layer itself, rather than being converted into in-plane strain directed toward the Fe3GaTe2 layer. This stems from insufficient constraints to effectively suppress unproductive deformation, leading to a shift in the strained transfer mechanism from efficient shear coupling to less efficient bending deformation. This transition ultimately contributes to a substantial decline in overall driving performance. Resulting in mechanical strain transmitted to the Fe3GaTe2 layer that remains below the critical threshold (Fig. 1b and Supplementary Fig. 5b). As shown in Supplementary Fig. 6, application of varying polarization voltages to the top P(VDF-TrFE) layer at a magnetic field angle (θH) of 90° leads to a reduction in HC and Rxy under positive polarization and an enhancement under negative polarization26. However, at θH = 90°, the easy magnetization axis of Fe3GaTe2 remains predominantly perpendicular, irrespective of the direction of the applied polarization voltage, and the anomalous Hall curve’s shape remains invariant, with only the magnitudes of HC and Rxy changing. This observation further suggests a negligible impact of strain on the magnetic properties of Fe3GaTe2 under these conditions. A systematic investigation was performed to control the polarization voltages of the top and bottom P(VDF-TrFE) layers in devices by sweeping the external voltage from −10 V to 10 V. The voltagedependent coercive field and residual Hall resistance, as presented in Supplementary Fig. 7, both exhibit hysteresis loops. Supplementary Fig. 7 illustrates the regulatory effects of the top and bottom P(VDFTrFE) ferroelectric gates on the residual Hall resistance and coercive field of the Fe3GaTe2 device, along with their corresponding hysteresis loops. The residual polarization intensity (Pr) of the device was measured under different polarization pulse voltages (Supplementary Fig. 8). The magnetically modulated hysteresis loop and the V-shaped hysteresis curve of the residual polarization intensity exhibited highly consistent trends, strongly confirming the effective modulation of ferromagnetic properties by ferroelectric polarization (Supplementary Note 3). Notably, the hysteresis loop associated with the bottom P(VDF-TrFE) structure is significantly wider than that of the top layer. This disparity arises from the distinct regulatory mechanisms in the device. The top layer structure is primarily governed by the direct influence of the ferroelectric polarization field (P), whereas the bottom layer structure is subject to the combined effects of the ferroelectric polarization field and the in-plane strain induced by the inverse piezoelectric effect of P(VDF-TrFE). The ferroelectric polarization field primarily regulates Fe3GaTe2 carriers through the ferroelectricferromagnetic interface. Conversely, the in-plane piezoelectric strain generated in the bottom layer structure can effectively modify the lattice parameters of Fe3GaTe2 or induce magnetoelastic coupling effects, directly influencing the easy axis orientation or altering the magnitude of its magnetic anisotropy constant. This dual orthogonal regulation mechanism, involving ferroelectric polarization and piezoelectric strain, synergistically enhances the energy barrier for magnetic domain switching in the bottom layer structure. Consequently, the magnetization reversal process requires overcoming a greater resistance, macroscopically manifesting as a larger coercive field and a broader hysteresis loop. > ### Voltage-induced strain in dual-gate devices
> 
> 双栅器件中的电压诱导应变
> 
> To further elucidate the strain regulation mechanism, transmission electron microscopy (TEM) samples were prepared using focused ion beam (FIB) milling for detailed interface microstructural characterization of the device (Fig. 2c, and Supplementary Fig. 9). These analyses revealed that Fe3GaTe2 forms molecular-scale, intimate heterointerfaces with both the top and bottom layers of the P(VDFTrFE) ferroelectric polymer. Furthermore, TEM observations confirmed the characteristic spiral crystalline morphology of P(VDF-TrFE), indicative of molecular chains adopting a fully trans conformation and spontaneously twisting along the chain axis, a key structural signature of the dominant ferroelectric β phase. This close molecular-level interfacial contact is critical for efficient carrier control, as it minimizes interface defect state density, reduces carrier scattering barriers, and enhances the direct modulation of carrier concentration/type in the Fe3GaTe2 channel by the ferroelectric polarization field. Moreover, this interface provides an ideal platform for effective strain transmission. The robust physical contact between the two phases, coupled with low interfacial shear resistance, ensures that the strain generated within the polymer layer under applied gate voltage is efficiently transferred to the Fe3GaTe2 lattice via strong interfacial coupling. In summary, TEM characterization revealed the intimately coupled nature of the Fe3GaTe2/P(VDF-TrFE) interface at the molecular scale, providing key insights into the microscopic mechanisms underlying the simultaneous achievement of efficient carrier control and strain transmission. This analysis offers direct structural evidence crucial for understanding the multifunctional control mechanism operating within these dual-gate devices. > 第一性原理计算：应变诱导的磁各向异性变化
> 
> To elucidate the physical mechanism underlying the strain-modulated magnetic anisotropy observed in the devices, we developed a multiscale theoretical model for bulk Fe3GaTe2, detailed in Supplementary Fig. 15. Utilizing Green's function perturbation theory, we calculated the exchange coupling constants for two strain-induced configurations of bulk Fe3GaTe2, considering magnetization aligned along the in-plane (mx) and out-of-plane (mz) directions, respectively. These analyses suggest that the Fe3GaTe2 layers within the structure exhibit distinct atomic arrangements under strain conditions of (a −0.5% c + 0.45%) and (a + 0.8% c-0.48%). As illustrated in Fig. 2f, our calculations demonstrate that under the a −0.5% c + 0.45% strain, interlayer coupling is dominated by the in-plane direction, and the total coupling also favors in-plane alignment. Conversely, under the a + 0.8% c-0.48% strain, while the interlayer coupling remains predominantly in-plane, the total coupling favors the out-of-plane direction. Furthermore, we systematically investigated the influence of the strain field on the magnetic anisotropy energy (MAE) of Fe3GaTe2, employing density functional theory (DFT) first-principles calculations. Here, MAE is defined as the energy difference: MAE = E[100] E[001], where a positive MAE indicates a perpendicular, or out-ofplane, easy axis of magnetization, and a negative MAE signifies an inplane easy axis. We subjected the Fe3GaTe2 layer to in-plane biaxial tensile strain ranging from 0.6% to 0.8%, mirroring the 0.8% strain implemented in our theoretical model. As shown in Supplementary Fig. 16, our calculations reveal that, at zero strain, the MAE of bulk Fe3GaTe2 is +2.158 meV/atom, and that of single-layer Fe3GaTe2 is +0.354 meV/atom42, both indicating a strong inherent preference for perpendicular magnetization. However, upon application of a + 0.8% tensile strain, the MAE of the bulk and monolayer systems decreases to −0.233 meV/atom and −0.58 meV/atom, respectively, signifying a transition in magnetic anisotropy from perpendicular to in-plane. Strain modulation of magnetic anisotropy in Fe3GaTe2 is a complex interaction between electronic states and magnetic order, involving atomic-scale orbital reconfiguration as well as synergistic responses to exchange interactions. The layered crystal structure of Fe3GaTe2 provides a unique ligand-field environment, where Fe atoms occupy the centers of Te atom octahedra. Consequently, the Fe dorbitals split into energy levels due to the crystal field, with t2g orbitals (dxy, dxz, dyz) at bottom energies and eg orbitals (dx2-y2, dz2) at higher energies due to increased electron repulsion. As shown in Supplementary Fig. 17, the magnetic moment orientation of the Fe sites in Fe3GaTe2 exhibits anisotropy, with Fe atoms occupying specific lattice sites whose localized coordination environment leads to crystal field splitting, which causes the d-orbital energy level splitting to show anisotropy and affects the magnetic moment orientation. In the absence of strain, out-of-plane lattice contraction (c axis) results in a bottom energy level for dz2 orbitals compared to dx2-y2 orbitals, thermodynamically favoring out-of-plane magnetic anisotropy (MAE > 0). However, applying in-plane biaxial strain alters the energy competition that governs the orientation of the magnetic moment by modulating the d-orbit energy level difference (ΔE). When ΔE falls below 0.1 eV, the energy barrier for in-plane magnetization is significantly reduced, triggering a magnetic anisotropy reorientation (MAE < 0). Strain significantly affects the magnetic anisotropy energy (MAE) of Fe3GaTe2 by modulating its magnetic exchange interaction. It is shown that under biaxial strain, the magnitude of MAE modulation in monolayer Fe3GaTe2 (−263.8%) is much higher than that of the bulk model (−110.7%), and this difference stems from the competing mechanisms of intra-layer exchange and inter-layer super-exchange. For the monolayer system, strain weakens the orbital hybridization between Fe atoms in the layer by changing the lattice constant (a axis elongation), leading to a decrease in d-orbital occupation, which reduces the perpendicular magnetic anisotropy and contributes to the MAE shift from out-of-plane to in-plane. In contrast, in the block model, the tensile strain induces c axis contraction while elongating the a axis, which significantly enhances the interlayer super-exchange effect, improves the hybridization strength of adjacent Fe atomic orbitals through the compression of the vdW gap, strengthens the perpendicular magnetic coupling and stabilizes the spin orientation. The competitive mechanism within the bulk Fe3GaTe2 results in the interlayer coupling partially counteracting the weakening effect of the intralayer exchange, causing the strain response of the bulk MAE to show a smaller amplitude modulation. Synchrotron X-ray magnetic circular dichroism measurements (XMCD) show that the contribution to the orbital magnetic moment of the Fe1 site increases under strain and is significantly higher than in the unstrained condition43, this enhanced orbital-spin coupling directly modulates the magnetic anisotropy axis orientation. Meanwhile, large biaxial strains (especially compressive strains close to −4% or tensile strains exceeding 1%) trigger significant changes in the MAE of Fe atoms, bringing the system close to the critical point of in-plane magnetization44. And interfacial strains along different in-plane directions trigger the switching of the susceptible magnetization axis. Atom- and orbital-resolved analyses attribute these anisotropic effects to the contributions of Fe-II and Te orbitals, especially the dyz-dz2 and dxz-dz2 orbitals of Fe atoms and the px-pz and py-pz orbitals of Te atoms45. First-principles calculations suggest that stress, including compressive strain, can modify magnetic exchange interactions by altering the spacing between Fe and Ga/Te atoms. In bulk Fe3GaTe2, Ga/Te atoms are antiferromagnetically coupled to Fe atoms, and its weak roving ferromagnetism suggests a significant spin upturn. Compressive strains may further inhibit the strength of the magnetic exchange couplings, leading to a reduction in the localization of the arrangement of the magnetic moments, thus weakening the dominant magnetic anisotropy in the perpendicular direction and promoting the deflection of the susceptibility axis of magnetization40. In the layered ferromagnetic material Fe3GaTe2, which exhibits perpendicular magnetic anisotropy (PMA), the phenomenon of magnetic polarity reversal is influenced by the interplay between the external magnetic field and the magnetocrystalline anisotropy energy (MAE). The out-of-plane PMA is primarily governed by electron orbital rearrangement and crystal field cleavage effects, which are induced by strong spin-orbit coupling (SOC) at the Fe-Te interface46. When the magnetic field is tilted away from the out-of-plane direction (e.g., θ < 0°), this rotational symmetry breaking leads to a biostability characterized by axial (D) and equatorial (E) dipole configurations. In this scenario, the in-plane component of the external field facilitates a dynamic reorientation of the magnetic moments from the out-of-plane to the in-plane orientation, effectively bottoming the domain-wall pinning potentials47. This reorientation is accompanied by a symmetry reconfiguration of the effective magnetic anisotropy tensor, resulting in a notable enhancement of the in-plane monoclinic anisotropy term48. Concurrently, the SOC-mediated inversion of the Berry curvature in momentum space induces a significant alteration in the topological nature of the energy bands, as evidenced by the sign reversal of the anomalous Hall conductance40. The anomalous Hall conductance is directly dependent on the weight integral of the Berry curvature (σxAyHE = e2/h ∫BZ Ω(k)f(k)dk, f(k) is the Fermi distribution function), and the reversal of the sign of the anomalous Hall resistivity indicates a change in the direction of the magnetization, corresponding to the inverse sign of the Berry curvature integral49,50. The underlying microscopic mechanism for this phenomenon can be elucidated using the orbital-selective hybridization model. Furthermore, studies on magnetic domain dynamics reveal that the collective motion of domain walls in response to the tilted magnetic field, along with the gradient distribution of local demagnetization field energy, synergistically drives the transition from multi-domain to single-domain states51. Magnetic moment reorientation builds a theoretical framework system for the development of spintronic devices based on critical angle modulation52. Through systematic experimental studies and theoretical analysis, this study reveals for the first time the dual regulation mechanism of Fe3GaTe2 magnetic materials by P(VDF-TrFE) ferroelectric polymers in heterostructures. The results show that the P(VDF-TrFE) located in the top layer regulates the carrier concentration distribution of Fe3GaTe2 through the ferroelectric polarization effect, while the bottom P(VDFTrFE) achieves the dual regulation of carrier concentration and magnetic anisotropy axis orientation through the ferroelectric polarization effect and the inverse piezoelectric effect. In order to investigate the dual-gate synergistic modulation mechanism, a systematic voltage application scheme is designed: when −10 V polarization voltage is applied to both the top and bottom P(VDF-TrFE) layers, the synergistic enhancement of the isotropic ferroelectric polarization and the inverse piezoelectric stretching effect occurs (Supplementary Note 6), and the two effects show a significant voltage dependence (Supplementary Note 7); when both 10 V polarization voltages are applied, a synergistic enhancement of the ferroelectric polarization and inverse piezoelectric compression effect occurs (Supplementary Note 8). Notably, when the polarization voltages are applied to the top and bottom P(VDF-TrFE) layers with opposite polarity (V1/V2 = −10 V/10 V and 10 V/−10 V), the system exhibits a special coupling of ferroelectric competition with piezoelectric deformation competition (Supplementary Note 9). In addition, the properties of the ferromagnet Fe3GaTe2 and the organic P(VDF-TrFE) are closely related to the temperature, and the changes in the performance of the dual-gate device by applying different temperatures are shown in Supplementary Note 10. By optimizing the process parameters, a complete preparation scheme was ultimately obtained, controlling the differences in coercive field, Hall resistance, and switching voltage of the device within a fluctuation range of 5–10% (Supplementary Fig. 25).The dualgate synergistic regulation system constructed in this work breaks through the functional limitation of the traditional single-gate device, and realizes a multidimensional parameter regulation space through the coupling of multi-physical fields. This breakthrough design not only provides a paradigm for the development of polymorphic nonvolatile memory devices and reconfigurable spintronic devices. > ### Programmable logic operation of spin logic devices
> 
> 自旋逻辑器件的可编程逻辑运算
> 
> Based on the above regulation principle, the device system can realize a multi-dimensional logic operation system through two modes: inplane magnetization and out-plane magnetization. Compared to conventional single-axis magnetization devices, the dual-mode magnetization architecture greatly extends the coverage of logic operations with both in-plane and out-of-plane magnetization modes. It is noteworthy that the in-plane magnetization system using Fe3GaTe2/P(VDF-TrFE) heterostructure shows unique spin logic complementary characteristics, through the orthogonal coupling of spin orientation in the ferromagnetic layer and the synergistic regulation of the strain field in the piezoelectric layer, the complementary conversion of logic states can be realized in a single device, and this symmetric structure not only enhances the stability of the operation, but also significantly reduces the error code in the signal transmission by the spin polarization compensation effect. This symmetric structure not only enhances the arithmetic stability, but also significantly reduces the BER in signal transmission through the spin-polarization compensation effect. For the out-of-plane magnetization mode, the device exhibits excellent reconfigurable computing capability by constructing a dual-gate P(VDF-TrFE)/Fe3GaTe2/P(VDF-TrFE) heterostructure. The ferroelectric polarization generated by the dual-gate structure can independently modulate the strength of the out-of-plane magnetization vector, which, combined with the dynamic modulation of magnetic anisotropy by the anisotropic strain of the piezoelectric material, enables a single device to reconfigure complex and different logic functions, such as NAND, NOR, XOR, etc., in accordance with the combination of different gate pressures in real time. This hardwarelevel dynamic programmability breaks through the limitations of traditional logic devices in terms of function curing and provides a physical basis for adaptive computing architectures. In particular, it should be pointed out that both magnetization modes adopt an all-electric manipulation mechanism, which utilizes the piezoelectric and inverse piezoelectric effects of P(VDF-TrFE) to generate a non-volatile regulation of the magnetization state, resulting in a reduced energy consumption as compared to current-driven methods based on spin transfer moments or orbital moments. Specifically, as illustrated in the Supplementary Fig 28, the device maintains its properties for 120 hours following voltage polarization and subsequent removal of the electric field. Based on the concept of ultra-low power consumption, the single-operation power consumption of the bottom-gate device Fe3GaTe2/P(VDF-TrFE) is half that of the device. Therefore, the bottom-gate device was used in the demonstration, while the demonstration of the device was included in the supplementary information. This low power consumption feature combined with a scalable heterostructure preparation process provides an innovative solution for the development of energy-efficient storage-computing integrated systems. The in-plane magnetization system based on Fe3GaTe2/P(VDFTrFE) heterostructure is demonstrated first. Figure 3a shows a schematic of the logic devices in a Fe3GaTe2/P(VDF-TrFE)-based memory with two separate input terminals. Two input signals (denoted by V1 and V2) are fed into the P(VDF-TrFE) layer, and one control signal (denoted by IIN) is fed into the Fe3GaTe2 layer (spin logic devices A and B) for deterministic inversion of perpendicular magnetization in Fe3GaTe2 via strain-induced deflection control of the susceptibility axis of the magnetization in the P(VDF-TrFE). Therefore, it can be used to realize different logic functions (i.e., AND, NAND, NOT). The operation of the spin logic device is given in Fig. 3b, where we define the positive values of the inputs (V1 = 10 V, V2 = 10 V) as digital inputs “1” and their negative values (V1 = −10 V, V2 = −10 V) as digital inputs “0”. These inputs determine the outputs of the spin logic device, which are monitored by measuring the sum of the anomalous Hall resistor Hall resistors (Rout = Rxy, A + Rxy, B). To describe the logic operation, we define the reference value of the magnetization of the two devices Rref = (Rxy, A + Rxy, B)/2. The NOT gate can be implemented by flowing two voltages (V1 = V2) in the same direction (the implementation of the NOT gate is independent of the initial memory state), where UP magnetization (Rout > 0) corresponds to the digital output “1”, and DOWN magnetization (Rout < 0) is equivalent to the digital output “0”, and the truth table is shown in Fig. 3c. Next demonstrated is an XOR gate fabricated based on two Fe3GaTe2/P(VDF-TrFE) heterostructure devices. The devices were fed with a current of IIN and magnetized using an in-plane magnetic field (θH = 0°). The connection diagrams of devices A and B are shown in Supplementary Fig. 29a. Before the demonstration, the device was processed using the V1/V2 input (00), at which time the overall magnetization state of the device was mz = 0, corresponding to the value of the anomalous Hall resistor: Rout=Rref, which corresponds to the digital output “0” (Supplementary Fig. 29b). Only when V1 ≠ V2, the overall magnetization state of the device is mz = 1, Rout ≠ Rref, corresponding to the digital output “1”; while V1 = V2, the overall magnetization state of the device is mz = 0, Rout = Rref, corresponding to the digital output “0”. > ## Discussion
> 
> 讨论
> 
> This study introduces a novel ultra-low-power approach leveraging vertically integrated two-dimensional van der Waals multiferroic heterostructures composed of Fe3GaTe2 and P(VDF-TrFE). Exploiting strainmediated magnetoelectric coupling, we achieve effective modulation of the magnetic anisotropy constant. Specifically, the inverse piezoelectric effect within the ferroelectric polymer P(VDF-TrFE) induces strain, which, through lattice symmetry breaking, facilitates non-volatile reconfiguration of the magnetic order in Fe3GaTe2 at room temperature. This strainmediated mechanism modulates the magnetic anisotropy constant K1, enabling fully electrically reversible modulation of the anomalous Hall resistance and demonstrating inverter functionality upon application of a polarization voltage. Quantitative validation employing Raman spectroscopy, X-ray diffraction, and first-principles calculations establishes a clear correlation between strain, exchange coupling energy, and magnetic anisotropy. Device integration further demonstrates the feasibility of constructing reconfigurable logic gate arrays (AND/NOR/NOT gates) and half-adder circuits. Significantly, these spin logic devices exhibit exceptional performance characteristics, including ultra-low energy consumption (0.5 aJ), high-speed writing (5 ns), and robust operational stability. This research not only advances the fundamental understanding of van der Waals magnetoelectric coupling mechanisms but also provides a scalable platform for next-generation non-volatile memory and reconfigurable logic technologies. 本研究提出了一种新颖的超低功耗方法，利用由Fe3GaTe2和P(VDF-TrFE)组成的垂直集成二维范德华多铁异质结构。通过应变介导的磁电耦合，我们实现了对磁各向异性常数的有效调控。具体而言，铁电聚合物P(VDF-TrFE)中的逆压电效应诱导应变，通过晶格对称性破缺，在室温下实现Fe3GaTe2磁序的非易失性重构。这种应变介导机制调控磁各向异性常数K1，在施加极化电压时实现对反常霍尔电阻的全电可逆调制，并展示反相器功能。利用拉曼光谱、X射线衍射和第一性原理计算进行的定量验证，建立了应变、交换耦合能和磁各向异性之间的明确关联。器件集成进一步展示了构建可重构逻辑门阵列（AND/NOR/NOT门）和半加器电路的可行性。重要的是，这些自旋逻辑器件展现出卓越的性能特性，包括超低功耗（0.5 aJ）、高速写入（5 ns）和稳健的操作稳定性。本研究不仅推进了对范德华磁电耦合机制的基础理解，也为下一代非易失性存储器和可重构逻辑技术提供了一个可扩展的平台。

> ## Methods

> ### Preparation of samples
> 
> 样品制备
> 
> P(VDF-TrFE) powder for the ferroelectric layer in multiferroic heterostructures was purchased at Arkema with a molar ratio of 70/30. 0.5 g of P(VDF-TrFE) powder and 10 ml of N, N-dimethylformamide (DNF) or dimethylsulfoxide (DMSO) were mixed under anhydrous conditions to obtain a homogeneous P(VDF-TrFE) solution and stirred using a magnetic stirrer at 300 rpm for 12 h. Excess air bubbles were removed using an evacuation machine. The sample of ferromagnet Fe3GaTe2 in multiferroic heterostructure was obtained by mechanical exfoliation of bulk Fe3GaTe2 crystals on SiO2/Si substrate, the thickness was initially determined by observing the optical comparison of the exfoliated sample with the substrate under an optical microscope, and the thickness was confirmed to be 12 nm by AFM (Dimension, ICON).The samples and the solutions were prepared and stored in an environment with oxygen and moisture content below 0.1 ppm in a glove box. 多铁异质结构中铁电层所用的P(VDF-TrFE)粉末购自Arkema，摩尔比为70/30。在无水条件下，将0.5 g P(VDF-TrFE)粉末与10 ml N,N-二甲基甲酰胺(DMF)或二甲基亚砜(DMSO)混合，得到均匀的P(VDF-TrFE)溶液，使用磁力搅拌器以300 rpm转速搅拌12小时。使用抽真空机去除多余气泡。多铁异质结构中的铁磁体Fe3GaTe2样品通过机械剥离块体Fe3GaTe2晶体于SiO2/Si衬底上获得，厚度最初通过光学显微镜观察剥离样品与衬底的光学对比确定，并通过AFM(Dimension, ICON)确认厚度为12 nm。样品和溶液均在手套箱中氧和水分含量低于0.1 ppm的环境下制备和储存。

> ### Dual-gate device construction
> 
> 双栅器件构建
> 
> Electrodes were drawn on a 300 nm silicon oxide-coated silicon substrate using a photolithography machine (ATS-07-UV, Litho), and Cr (5 nm)/Au (50 nm) electrodes were evaporated using electron beam evaporation. The ferroelectric layer P(VDF-TrFE) was spin-coated using a spin coater (EZ4-S), and the coating was rotated at 5000 rpm for 30 s to obtain a 10 nm thick ferroelectric layer. After drying at 70 °C for 1 h, it was then annealed at 130 °C in a vacuum environment for 10 h for crystallinity. Selected Fe3GaTe2 samples were transferred onto spincoated P(VDF-TrFE) gold-plated substrates by the PPMS thermal transfer method, and specific Hall device mask plates were mounted on the Fe3GaTe2 samples under a microscope, and the Cr (5 nm)/Au (50 nm) electrodes were prepared into Hall devices by evaporation using electron beam evaporation, and the electrodes were leaded out using a silver paste and a gold wire.
