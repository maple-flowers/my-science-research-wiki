> ## 一、引言：迈向第一性原理计算的“黄金标准”
> 
> 在计算材料科学和计算化学领域，基于**密度泛函理论**和**平面波基组**的第一性原理计算是模拟材料微观性质的最有力工具之一。然而，计算的精度与效率始终是一对核心矛盾。本论文诞生于一个关键的技术演进节点，它系统性地揭示了两种里程碑式方法——**Vanderbilt超软赝势** 和 **Blöchl投影增强波方法**——之间的深刻内在联系，并通过严谨的数学推导和全面的数值测试，论证了PAW方法在兼顾US-PP的高效率与全电子方法的高精度方面的卓越能力。
> 
> 该文献的核心价值在于：
> 
> 1.  **理论统一**：首次从数学上严格推导出US-PP只是PAW方法在特定条件下的线性化近似，为理解两种方法的精度与局限提供了坚实的理论基础。
> 2.  **工程简化**：提出了一种在现有US-PP代码框架中轻松实现PAW方法的简便途径，极大地推动了PAW方法在学术界的普及。
> 3.  **性能基准**：通过对小分子、块体材料及磁性体系（Fe, Co, Ni）的详尽测试，为PAW方法设立了“全电子精度”的标杆，并明确指出US-PP在处理强磁性材料时的潜在误差。
> 
> * * *

> ## 二、论文结构与逻辑概览
> 
> 本文遵循“**理论推导 → 算法实现 → 数值验证 → 结论展望**”的经典逻辑链条，具体结构如下：
> 
> 1.  **引言**：回顾了**模守恒赝势**的局限性，引出Vanderbilt US-PP和Blöchl PAW这两种突破性方法，并指出二者的关联尚未被正式建立。
> 2.  **PAW总能量泛函重构**：
>     -   **基本形式**：从Kohn-Sham泛函出发，引入线性变换，将全电子波函数与赝波函数关联。
>     -   **能量项分解**：对电子的**Hartree能**和**交换关联能**进行巧妙的“补丁式”分解，分离出在平面波网格上平滑的部分和围绕原子的局域修正部分。
>     -   **关键引入**：引入**补偿电荷**，用于修正赝波函数在原子核附近丢失的多极矩，确保长程静电相互作用的精确描述。
> 3.  **形式关系的建立与US-PP的导出**：这是本文的理论核心。作者证明，将PAW总能量泛函中的两个局域项（E1和E1）在其原子参考态附近进行一阶**线性化**，即可严格得到US-PP的总能量泛函。
> 4.  **哈密顿量与力的推导**：基于重构的PAW泛函，系统推导了在实际计算中求解电子基态所需的**重叠算符**、**哈密顿算符**、**力**和**应力张量**的表达式。
> 5.  **PAW数据集构建**：详细讨论了生成PAW计算所需的核心参数（如部分波、投影函数、补偿电荷等）的具体方案和数值技巧（如**双网格技术**）。
> 6.  **数值测试与讨论**：
>     -   **小分子测试**：对比PAW、US-PP与全电子方法计算H₂O、N₂、F₂等分子的键长，PAW误差<0.1%。
>     -   **块体材料测试**：计算金刚石、Si、CaF₂等材料的晶格常数和体模量，与FLAPW结果高度一致。
>     -   **磁性材料测试**：重点检验Fe、Co、Ni。发现PAW与全电子方法结果完美吻合，而US-PP高估了Fe的磁化能，尤其在GGA下误差显著。
> 7.  **结论**：PAW方法以相近的计算成本，实现了与全电子方法同等的精度，尤其适用于磁性、强电负性差等“疑难”体系，是比US-PP更可靠、更普适的下一代方法。
>

> ## 四、正文核心内容剖析
> 
> ### 1. 研究背景与问题
> 
> 在1990年代初，以Vanderbilt US-PP为代表的方法虽然大幅降低了对过渡金属和第一行元素的计算成本，但其赝势构建过程复杂，且对特定化学环境（如强磁性）的精度存疑。Blöchl提出的PAW方法概念优雅，但因其形式与现有赝势代码差异较大，实现困难，未得到广泛应用。本文的核心驱动力是：**如何从理论上弥合这两种方法的鸿沟，并找到一种既能继承US-PP的效率，又能实现PAW全电子精度的实用方案？**
> 
> ### 2. 核心方法与模型：PAW总能量泛函的“精装修”
> 
> 本文并未发明全新的理论，而是对Blochl的PAW框架进行了“重装修”，使其更贴近赝势代码的工程习惯。其核心思想可概括为“**平滑网格 + 局域修正**”的“补丁”策略：
> 
> -   **基本变换**：`|AE波函数⟩ = |赝波函数⟩ + Σ(|AE部分波⟩ - |赝部分波⟩)⟨投影函数|赝波函数⟩`。这个公式是PAW的基石，它告诉我们如何从一个平滑的赝波函数精确地恢复出在原子核附近剧烈振荡的全电子波函数。
> -   **电荷密度的分解**：`n = ñ + n¹ - ñ¹`。总电荷密度被分解为在平面波网格上平滑的赝密度`ñ`，加上仅在原子球内计算的高精度局域密度`n¹`，再减去两者在球内重叠的部分`ñ¹`。
> -   **能量项的“补丁”分解**：

> ## 一、引言
> 
> 在计算材料科学和化学领域，我们常常需要借助计算机来模拟原子和电子的行为，从而预测材料的性质。这项任务的核心是求解量子力学中的**薛定谔方程**，但严格求解极其困难。**密度泛函理论**和**赝势**方法的出现，极大地简化了计算，使得我们可以用有限的资源研究复杂的体系。
> 
> 本文将为您解读一篇连接了两种主流赝势方法的关键文献：**Vanderbilt的超软赝势**和**Blöchl的投影缀加波方法**。
> 
> -   **领域背景**：简单来说，赝势方法的核心思想是，材料的许多性质主要由外层电子（价电子）决定，而内层电子和原子核可以被视为一个整体，用一个较弱的“假”势（即赝势）来代替。这样就避免了处理内层电子复杂的振荡行为，大大降低了计算成本。
> -   **研究问题**：US-PP方法虽然高效，但其构建过程复杂，且在某些情况下精度受限。PAW方法则以其高精度和优雅的理论框架著称，但实现起来似乎更复杂。这两种方法之间是否存在内在联系？能否在保留US-PP易用性的同时，实现PAW的精度？
> -   **文献定位与核心价值**：这篇发表于1999年的论文，是计算物理领域的里程碑式工作。它的核心贡献在于：
>     1.  **架起桥梁**：首次从数学上严格推导了US-PP和PAW方法之间的形式化关系，证明US-PP可以通过对PAW总能量泛函进行线性化得到。
>     2.  **简化实现**：为在现有支持US-PP的平面波程序中轻松实现PAW方法指明了道路。
>     3.  **系统对比**：通过大量测试，系统比较了PAW、US-PP和全电子方法的精度与效率，特别是针对磁性材料等挑战性体系，确立了PAW方法的优越性。

> ## 四、正文核心内容剖析
> 
> ### 1. 研究背景：从一步到位的“赝化”到分步走的“变换”
> 
> -   **赝势方法的困境**：传统的**模守恒赝势**要求赝波函数在原子核附近与全电子波函数拥有相同的电荷量（即“模守恒”）。这个严格要求导致对于第一行元素、3d过渡金属等，赝势非常“硬”，需要巨大的计算量。
> -   **超软赝势的突破**：Vanderbilt的US-PP方法放弃了“模守恒”这一强约束，通过引入“增强电荷”来弥补电荷缺失，从而允许构建极为“软”的赝波函数，大幅降低了计算门槛。但代价是，这些增强电荷被“赝化”在了一个规则网格上，这引入了一定的近似，且赝势构建过程复杂。
> -   **PAW方法的优雅**：Blöchl的PAW方法另辟蹊径。它不直接在波函数层面做近似，而是定义了一个从平滑的赝波函数到精确的全电子波函数的**线性变换**。这个变换是一个“分步走”的过程：在原子间区域，波函数用平滑的赝波函数描述；而在每个原子周围的球形增强区域，赝波函数会被“恢复”成精确的全电子波函数。这个“恢复”过程通过预计算好的原子数据（**分波**和**投影函数**）来完成。
> 
> ### 2. 方法模型：PAW泛函的重构与US-PP的导出
> 
> 这是本文最核心的理论部分。作者并没有直接使用Blöchl的原始公式，而是巧妙地对PAW总能量泛函进行了重构。
> 
> -   **重构PAW总能量**：核心思想是将总能量分解为三个部分：
>     
>     -   `E = Ẽ + E¹ - Ẽ¹`
>     -   **Ẽ （网格部分）**：这是在一个规则的平面波网格上计算的主要部分，它类似于US-PP的总能量表达式，包含了动能、赝电荷密度的静电作用能和交换-关联能。
>     -   **E¹ （原子中心全电子部分）**：在每个原子周围的球形区域，使用精确的全电子分波和电荷密度计算出的能量。
>     -   **Ẽ¹ （原子中心赝部分）**：同样在球形区域，但使用平滑的赝分波和赝电荷密度计算出的能量。
>     -   **精妙之处**：`E¹ - Ẽ¹` 这一项，精确地修正了网格部分 `Ẽ` 的误差，在每个原子周围将平滑的赝值“校正”回了精确的全电子值。这使得计算既保留了平面波的简单性，又获得了全电子的精度。
> -   **推导US-PP：揭示线性近似的本质**
>     
>     -   作者证明，US-PP的总能量泛函可以通过对PAW总能量中的 `E¹` 和 `Ẽ¹` 这两项在原子参考态附近进行**一阶线性化**得到。
>     -   这意味着，US-PP本质上是PAW方法的一种特例或近似。它假设电子在形成化学键时，其原子核附近的电荷密度分布变化不大，因此只需要一个线性修正就足够了。
>     -   `E¹` 线性化后，其复杂的能量项被简化为一个固定的势能项 `ve1ff`（全电子有效势）。
>     -   `Ẽ¹` 线性化后，产生了一个类似的固定赝势项 `ṽe1ff`。
>     -   最终，US-PP的非局域势强度 `G_ij^US` 就被表达为PAW中这些固定势的差值，清晰地展示了US-PP的物理来源。
> 
> ### 3. 实验与数据：精度与效率的权衡
> 
> -   **小分子测试**：结果（表I）清晰地表明，PAW方法在键长等基本性质上可以达到与全电子方法几乎同等的精度（误差 < 0.1%）。这验证了PAW理论框架的正确性以及其“全电子方法”的称号。
> -   **体材料测试**：对Si、C、CaF₂等典型共价、离子晶体的计算（表III）再次证实了PAW和US-PP的高精度，与全电子方法差异很小。
> -   **磁性材料的关键测试**：对Fe、Co、Ni的计算（表V, VI）是本文的亮点，揭示了US-PP方法的局限性。
>     -   **现象**：US-PP显著高估了bcc Fe的磁矩和稳定性。
>     -   **原因**：作者通过对比分析指出，其根本原因在于US-PP的“增强电荷”被赝化了，无法精确捕捉到磁性原子（如Fe）在接近原子核区域的3d电子波函数的精确形状。而**广义梯度近似**（GGA）的交换-关联泛函对电荷密度形状的微妙变化比**局域密度近似**（LDA）更为敏感，因此在GGA下US-PP的误差更大。
>     -   **解决方案与代价**：当作者使用一个增强电荷截断半径极小（0.5 a.u.）的“超硬”US-PP时，它可以完美重现PAW的结果。这证明了问题的根源，但同时也表明，要消除这个误差，US-PP会变得和传统模守恒赝势一样“硬”，从而丧失其计算效率优势。
> 
> ## 五、结论与贡献
> 
> 本文的核心结论清晰而有力：
>

<span class="hljs-section">## I. INTRODUCTION</span>
一、引言

First-principles Kohn-Sham density-functional methods ~see, e.g., Refs. 1 and 2! employing a plane-wave basis set and the pseudopotential ~PP! approximation are currently among the most successful techniques in computational chemistry and computational material science.3–5 The biggest merit of these methods is their formal simplicity, but unfortunately this simplicity has a price: first-row elements, transition metals, and rare-earth elements are computationally demanding to treat with standard norm-conserving pseudopotentials.6 Therefore, various attempts have been made to generate soft pseudopotentials, and the most successful approach to date is the concept of ultrasoft PP introduced by Vanderbilt.7 Blöchl8 has further developed the US-PP concept by combining ideas from pseudopotential and LAPW ~linearized augmented-plane-wave! methods in a conceptually elegant framework, called the projector augmented-wave method ~PAW!. Although Blöchl has indicated in his work that similarities between ultrasoft PP’s and his method exist, no formal relationship was established. In the present work, we will derive this relationship, which shows that the only difference between Vanderbilt’s and Blöchl’s approach are simple one-center terms. We will also indicate a simple way to implement the PAW method in existing plane-wave codes that use norm-conserving or ultrasoft pseudopotentials.

Norm-conserving pseudopotentials were first introduced and used by Hamann, Schlüter, and Chiang.6 In their scheme, inside some core radius, the all-electron ~AE! wave function is replaced by a soft nodeless pseudo- ~PS! wave function, with the crucial restriction that the PS wave function must have the same norm as the all-electron wave function within the chosen core radius; outside the core radius the PS and AE wave function are identical. It is now well established that good transferability requires a core radius around the outermost maximum of the AE wave function, because only then the charge distribution and moments of the AE wave functions are well reproduced by the PS wave functions ~see, e.g., Ref. 9!. Therefore, for elements with strongly localized orbitals ~like first-row, 3d, and rare-earth elements! the resulting pseudopotentials require a large plane-wave basis set. To work around this, compromises are often made by increasing the core radius significantly beyond the outermost maximum in the AE wave function. But this is usually not a satisfactory solution because the transferability is always adversely affected when the core radius is increased, and for any new chemical environment, additional tests are required to establish the reliability of such soft PP’s.

An elegant solution to this problem was proposed by Vanderbilt.7 In his method, the norm-conservation constraint is relaxed and to make up for the resulting charge deficit, localized atom-centered augmentation charges are introduced. These augmentation charges are defined as the charge density difference between the AE and the PS wave function, but for convenience they are pseudized to allow an efficient treatment of the augmentation charges on a regular grid. The core radius of the pseudopotential can now be chosen around half the nearest-neighbor distance—independent of the position of the maximum of the AE wave function ~see Ref. 10!. Only for the augmentation charges a small cutoff radius must be used to restore the moments and the charge distribution of the AE wave function accurately ~for details see Ref. 11!. The pseudized augmentation charges are usually treated on a regular grid in real space, which is not necessarily the same as the one used for the representation of the wave functions. The relation between the ultrasoft PP method and other plane-wave-based methods was discussed in detail by Singh.5

一种优雅的解决方案由Vanderbilt提出。7在他的方法中,规范守恒约束被放宽,为弥补由此产生的电荷亏损,引入了局域的原子中心增强电荷。这些增强电荷被定义为AE波函数与PS波函数之间的电荷密度差异,但为了便于在正规网格上高效处理增强电荷,它们被伪化。赝势的核心半径现在可以选为最近邻距离的一半——与AE波函数最大值的位置无关 ~见参考文献 10!。只有在增强电荷的处理中,必须使用一个小的截断半径以准确恢复AE波函数的矩和电荷分布 ~详见参考文献 11!。伪化的增强电荷通常在实空间的正规网格上进行处理,这不一定与波函数的表示所使用的网格相同。Singh详细讨论了超软赝势方法与其他基于平面波的方法之间的关系。5

Vanderbilt’s approach is now adopted quite widely,11–19 and especially for the 3d transition-metals savings in the computer and improvements in the accuracy can be significant.20 But the success of the method is partly hampered by the rather difficult construction of the pseudopotentials, i.e., too many parameters ~several cutoff radii! must be chosen and therefore extensive tests are required in order to obtain an accurate and highly transferable PP.

Vanderbilt的方法现在被广泛采用,11–19并且对于3d过渡金属的计算中的节省以及精度的提高可能具有显著意义。20 但该方法的成功部分受到阻碍,因为赝势的构建相当困难,即过多的参数11–19多个截断半径20 必须被选择,因此需要进行大量测试以获得准确且高度可迁移的赝势。

Some of these disadvantages are avoided in Blöchl’s PAW method. Blöchl introduces a linear transformation from the PS to the AE wave function and derives the PAW total energy functional in a consistent manner applying this transformation to the KS functional. The construction of PAW datasets is easier because the pseudization of the augmentation charges is avoided, i.e., the PAW method works directly with the full AE wave functions and AE potentials. This is achieved using radial support grids around each atom instead of regular grids. The decomposition into radial grid and regular grid is complete, insofar that no cross term between the grids must be evaluated. Despite these advantages, the method is not yet used very often, and in addition to Blöchl’s own implementation of the method we are aware of only one second program supporting the PAW method.21,22 This is partly due to the fact that the PAW approach was introduced a few years after Vanderbilt’s method, but another reason is that—apart from its formal elegance—it was not obvious at the time that the PAW method has significant advantages over other frozen core approaches like the US-PP approach. There are also some aspects in Blöchl’s work that deviate so significantly from conventional pseudopotential methods, that the implementation and testing of the method seems to be fairly difficult. In this work, we will rewrite the PAW total energy functional so that it resembles more closely the usual expressions used in pseudopotential programs and we will establish the exact formal relationship between both the US-PP and the PAW method. Our results show that only very few additional terms must be evaluated in order to implement the PAW method in programs supporting US-PP’s.

这些缺点中的部分在Blöchl的PAW方法中得以避免。Blöchl引入了一种从赝势到全电子波函数的线性变换,并通过将该变换应用于KS泛函,以一种一致的方式导出了PAW总能量泛函。由于避免了增广电荷的伪化,PAW数据集的构建更为简便,即PAW方法直接使用全电子波函数和全电子势能。这是通过在每个原子周围使用径向支持网格而非常规网格实现的。径向网格与常规网格的分解是完全的,以至于无需计算网格间的交叉项。尽管有这些优势,该方法尚未被广泛使用,除了Blöchl自己实现的该方法外,我们仅知有一个第二程序支持PAW方法。21,22 这在一定程度上是由于PAW方法是在Vanderbilt方法之后几年才被引入的,但另一个原因是——除了其形式上的优雅性之外——当时并不明显PAW方法相较于其他冻结核心方法(如US‐PP方法)具有显著优势。此外,Blöchl的工作中有一些方面与传统赝势方法存在显著差异,使得该方法的实现和测试似乎相当困难。在本工作中,我们将重写PAW总能量泛函,使其更接近赝势程序中通常使用的表达式,并建立US‐PP方法与PAW方法之间的确切形式关系。我们的结果表明,仅需计算非常少的额外项,即可在支持US‐PP的程序中实现PAW方法。

### E. Ghost states
E. 鬼态

We want to discuss briefly the problem of ghost states because authors have reported difficulties in the construction of PAW datasets for some elements.22 In contrast to their findings, we never encountered serious problems when employing the scheme outlined in the previous subsections, but it is true that the construction must be done carefully. Fortunately, we had a well-tested database of US-PP for all s, p, and d elements on which our construction of PAW datasets could be based. Our findings are that ghost-states tend to exist if an unreasonable attractive local atomic pseudopotential ˜v_eff^a is chosen, which is in accordance with the observation of Gonze et al. ~Ref. 31!. Anyway for all s, p, and d elements we were able to obtain a reasonable local atomic pseudopotential that avoids ghost states and describes the scattering properties for high angular components (l &gt; 2) very accurately. Another problem is the construction of accurate pseudopotentials for elements with a large covalent radius and a small ionic radius ~e.g., K-Mn, Rb-Ru, Cs-Os!. In fact, it seems to be impossible to construct PAW data sets and USPP’s with a small r_c ~approaching the ionic radius! for these elements if the 3p, 4p, or 5p semicore states ~and sometimes 3s, 4s, or 5s states! are kept in the core. But this is in no way unexpected and similar problems are well known in the LAPW method:5 Let us consider, for example, Ca, for which the covalent radius is 3.7 a.u. For many calculations, a convenient core radius for this elements is around the ionic radius that equals 2.0 a.u. In the valence-band region at this radius, the logarithmic derivatives of the p states exhibit a ~albeit very weak! curvature towards the lower-lying 3p core states. A PAW data set or an US-PP constructed for the valence-band region only will thus ‘‘see’’ the lower-lying 3p semicore state and the resulting potential posses a p ghost state. Once the 3p semicore state is treated as a valence state this problem is entirely removed, and very accurate ghost-state-free pseudopotentials can be obtained even with small core radii.

我们想简要讨论鬼态问题,因为作者报告了某些元素的PAW数据集构建过程中遇到的困难。22 与他们的发现相反,我们在使用前一子节中概述的方案时从未遇到严重问题,但确实必须谨慎进行构建。Fortu‐ nately, 我们有一个经过良好测试的US‐PP数据库,适用于所有 s、p和d元素,我们的PAW数据集构建可以基于此。我们的发现是,如果选择了一个不合理的吸引性局部原子赝势 ˜v_eff^a ,鬼态就会存在,这与Gonze等人 ~的观察结果一致。无论如何,对于所有s、p和d元素,我们都能获得一个合理的局部原子赝势,避免鬼态,并且非常准确地描述了高角分量 (l &gt; 2) 的散射性质。另一个问题是为具有较大共价半径和较小离子半径的元素构建准确的赝势 ~例如,K‐Mn, Rb‐Ru, Cs‐Os!。实际上,如果将3p, 4p或5p半核心态 ~以及有时3s, 4s或5s态! 保留在核心中,似乎无法为这些元素构建具有小r_c ~接近离子半径! 的PAW数据集和US‐PP。但这种情况并不令人意外,在LAPW方法中也存在类似问题:5 例如,考虑Ca元素,其共价半径为3.7原子单位。对于许多计算,这种元素的方便核心半径通常位于等于2.0原子单位的离子半径附近。在该半径下的价带区域,p态的对数导数表现出~尽管非常微弱!的曲率,朝向更低的3p核心态。因此,仅针对价带区域构建的PAW数据集或US‐PP将‘‘看到’’更低的3p半核心态,导致生成的势具有p鬼态。一旦将3p半核心态视为价态,此问题将完全消除,即使使用较小的核心半径也能获得非常精确的无鬼态赝势。

Therefore, a delicate optimization of the cutoff radius of the augmentation functions is required to balance accuracy versus computational efficiency. We now turn to the comparison between the ~relaxed core! AE calculations and the frozen core PAW and US-PP method. The agreement is generally excellent, the errors in the dimer bond length being smaller than 0.1% ~with the exception of F2 for which the error is around 0.2%!. Similar agreement between plane-wave pseudopotential calculations and AE calculations can rarely be found in the literature ~plane-wave-based calculations that come close in terms of accuracy have been reported by Goedecker et al.33 and Andrews et al.37!. In order to obtain this excellent accuracy, we had to decrease the core radius for the first-row elements to 1.1 a.u. and increase the cutoff energy at the same time to 700 eV. But even with the ‘‘standard’’ 400-eV first-row pseudopotentials, in which the overlap between the core regions is appreciable, the bond length errors are generally smaller than 0.5%. In addition, these first-row pseudopotentials yield reliable results even at 300 eV, the bond lengths errors are than 1% for N2, CO, and F2, which is probably acceptable for many calculations. We want to stress that the large overlap between the core regions is only allowed for our specific construction scheme using spherical Bessel functions and one should not expect a similar behavior for other schemes ~see also Ref. 10!.

### B. Bulk
B. 体

To test our implementation for bulk systems we have chosen the test systems of Ref. 21. These systems are representative of typical covalent, ionic, and metallic materials and can be considered as a rather stringent test of the current implementation. In our calculations, we have used 11×11×11 k points in the full wedge of the Brillouin zone for the metallic systems and 7×7×7 k points for insulating systems ~cubic-diamond structure and CaF2). The parameters used for the pseudopotential generation and the cutoff energies are again summarized in Table II. Our results—together with those of Ref. 21—are shown in Table III. The energies in Table III have been calculated as the energy difference between the ~non-spin-polarized! atom for which the PAW dataset and the pseudopotential have been constructed, and should not be compared directly with experimental values. It is evident that the agreement between the results of the current PAW implementation and the results of Ref. 21 @PAW, and full potential linearized augmented plane wave ~FLAPW!# is excellent. Differences in the lattice constant, bulk modulus, and cohesive energy are smaller than 0.5%, 5%, and 1%, respectively. The slightly larger discrepancies of the bulk moduli are probably related to the fact that elastic properties are very sensitive to the choice of data points and the equation of state used in the fit of the energy-volume curve. Because of the huge difference in electronegativity the most difficult test case is CaF2. Very reliable results for this material can be obtained only if the 3p semicore states are unfrozen. As can be seen, it does not matter whether the 3s states are treated as valence or core states, but unless the 3s states are also treated as valence states a rather large cutoff of 3.0 a.u. must be used for the s partial wave to avoid ghost states. Therefore, the second dataset with an unfrozen 3s state is more elegant and symmetric. The general conclusion of the bulk calculations is that our current implementation of the PAW method is able to describe bulk properties very accurately, the level of accuracy is comparable to FLAPW calculations.

We now come to the comparison between US-PP’s and all-electron calculations ~PAW and FLAPW!. Table III clearly shows that the US-PP’s give results that are almost indistinguishable from AE calculations. Even for the rather difficult CaF2 no differences can be observed. But it should be stressed that the construction of a suitable US-PP for Ca was again not as straightforward as the construction of the PAW dataset. As we have already pointed out before, this is a general obstacle we observed when constructing US-PP for alkali, alkali earth, and transition metals in which semicore states are treated as valence states. Another point that we want to discuss here is the introduction of nonlinear core corrections. We have claimed in Sec. II C that these corrections improve the robustness of the PAW method with respect to the completeness of the partial waves. That this is in fact true is shown for bcc Li. If the 1s electron is treated as a valence electron, our results agree perfectly with the AE results of Perdew et al.38 If the 1s electrons are kept frozen and if two s and two p partial waves are included ~‘‘1s frozen’’! very similar results are obtained with nonlinear core corrections. Without nonlinear core corrections the error is slightly larger ~‘‘1s frozen, no pc’’!. If p partial waves are, however, not used the results without nonlinear core corrections ~‘‘s only, no pc’’! become unreliable, whereas those with nonlinear core correction remain correct ~‘‘s only’’!. This behavior is easy to understand: Without p partial waves, the 2p contribution of Li is handled by the local potential. This is no problem per se, because the local potential can be chose so that the p-scattering properties are described very precisely. But without partial core corrections, the large overlap between the 2p and 1s electrons is not treated correctly in the exchange correction part of the total energy resulting in rather large errors.

我们现在比较超软赝势与全电子计算 ~PAW和 FLAPW!。表III清楚地表明,超软赝势的结果几乎与全电子计算无法区分。即使对于相对困难的CaF2 ,也无法观察到任何差异。但必须强调的是,为Ca构建合适的超软赝势并不像构建PAW数据集那样直接。正如我们之前已经指出的,这是我们在构建超软赝势时遇到的普遍障碍,特别是在碱金属、碱土金属和过渡金属中,半核心态被当作价态处理。我们想在这里讨论的另一个问题是非线性核心修正的引入。我们在Sec. IIC中声称,这些修正提高了PAW方法在部分波完整性方面的表现。对于bcc锂,这一说法确实成立。如果将1s电子视为价电子,我们的结果与Perdew等的AE结果完全一致38 如果保持1s电子冻结,并且包含两个s和两个p部分波 ~‘‘1s冻结’’! 则通过非线性核心修正可以得到非常相似的结果。如果没有非线性核心修正,误差会稍大一些 ~‘‘1s冻结, 无pc’’!。然而,如果未使用p部分波,则没有非线性核心修正的结果 ~‘‘s仅,无pc’’! 变得不可靠,而具有非线性核心修正的结果 ~‘‘s仅’’!仍然正确。这种行为很容易理解:在没有p部分波的情况下,Li的2p贡献由局部势处理。这本身并不成问题,因为局部势可以选择使得p散射性质被非常精确地描述。但在不进行部分核心修正的情况下,2p和1s电子之间的大重叠在总能量的交换修正部分中未被正确处理,导致相当大的误差。

Finally, we want to mention that we have undertaken systematic calculations with US-PP’s and the PAW method for simple structures ~fcc, bcc, sc, hcp, cubic diamond, dimers! for all s, p, and d elements and found that the differences in the equilibrium volumes usually do not exceed 1%. The largest differences are found for materials with a large magnetic moment and we will come back to this point in the next section. In all other cases, results for US-PP’s and the PAW method are practically indistinguishable. This is a clear indication for the reliability of the US-PP approach, and it also points to the close link between both approaches.

最后,我们想提到,我们已经对简单结构 ~fcc, bcc, sc, hcp, 立方金刚石结构, 二聚体!的所有s、p和d元素进行了系统性计算,并发现平衡体积的差异通常不超过1%。larg‐est差异出现在具有较大磁矩的材料中,我们将在下一节回到这一点。在所有其他情况下, US‐PP和PAW方法的结果实际上无法区分。这清楚地表明了US‐PP方法的可靠性,并指出了两种方法之间的紧密联系。

### C. Fe, Co, and Ni
C. 铁、钴和镍

Recently, the accuracy of US-PP’s was investigated by Moroni et al.20 for spin polarized calculations in atoms, dimers, and solids. Although a satisfactory agreement with AE methods was found for most properties, a few discrepancies remained. In view of the increased accuracy of the PAW method in comparison to US-PP’s, we have investigated whether the PAW improves upon these discrepancies.

最近,Moroni等人20 对原子、二聚体和固体中的自旋极化计算中US‐PP的精度进行了研究。尽管大多数性质与全电子方法AE的符合程度令人满意,但仍存在一些差异。鉴于PAW方法相比US‐PP具有更高的精度,我们研究了PAW是否能改善这些差异。

#### 1. Pseudopotential generation
1. 赝势生成

The pseudopotentials are constructed in essentially the same way as in Ref. 20, but here we have chosen the atomic reference configurations 4s^1.5 3d^{n-1.5} for the construction of the pseudopotentials for Fe, Co, and Ni. This configuration minimizes interconfigurational errors in atoms ~see below!. The core radius is set to 2.2 a.u. for the US-PP and the PAW method. For the augmentation charges in the US-PP method a cutoff of 1.9 a.u. is chosen ~see also Table II!, only for the d-augmentation charges of Fe a smaller radius was used. Partial core corrections and local pseudopotential are equivalent to Ref. 20. For the LDA calculation we use the Ceperley and Alder34 exchange correlation as parametrized by Perdew and Zunger ~CA-PZ!.35 For the calculations based on the generalized gradient approximation ~GGA!, we also used the CA parametrization for the LDA part and applied the gradient corrections according to Perdew-Wang 1991 ~PW91!.38,39 The spin interpolation of the correlation energy was done either with the standard interpolation formula ~atoms! or the Vosko-Wilk-Nusair ~VWN! interpolation ~bulk!.40
