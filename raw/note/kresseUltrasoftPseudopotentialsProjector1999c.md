> # 学术文献深度解读报告
> 
> ## 一、引言
> 
> 本篇文献“From ultrasoft pseudopotentials to the projector augmented-wave method”（G. Kresse & D. Joubert, Physical Review B 59, 1758 (1999)）是计算物理与材料科学领域的经典之作，它**正式建立了范德堡超软赝势（Ultrasoft Pseudopotential, US-PP）方法与布洛赫投影缀加波（Projector Augmented-Wave, PAW）方法之间的形式关系**。这两种方法在**密度泛函理论（Density Functional Theory, DFT）**计算中广泛应用，旨在通过简化原子核心区域的电子描述来提高计算效率。
> 
> 在计算化学和材料科学中，第一性原理的Kohn-Sham DFT方法结合平面波基组和赝势近似是主流技术。然而，对于某些元素（如第一行元素、过渡金属和稀土元素），传统的**范数守恒赝势（Norm-conserving Pseudopotentials, NCPPs）**需要非常大的平面波基组，导致计算成本过高。Vanderbilt提出的**超软赝势（US-PP）**通过放宽范数守恒约束，引入原子中心**增强电荷（augmentation charges）**来弥补电荷亏损，有效降低了计算负荷。Blöchl随后发展了**投影缀加波（PAW）方法**，它巧妙地结合了赝势和**线性化缀加平面波（Linearized Augmented-Plane-Wave, LAPW）**方法的优点，提供了一种更概念上优雅的框架。
> 
> 尽管Blöchl在其原始工作中指出了PAW与US-PP的相似之处，但两者之间缺乏正式的数学联系。本文的核心价值在于，它不仅**严谨地推导了US-PP泛函可以看作是PAW泛函的线性化近似，从而揭示了两者的深层联系**，还提出了一种**简便地在现有支持US-PP的平面波代码中实现PAW方法**的途径。此外，通过对小分子和块体系统（包括磁性过渡金属）进行严格的基准测试，论文**系统比较了PAW、US-PP与全电子（All-Electron, AE）方法的准确性和效率**，为计算材料科学领域提供了重要的理论基础和实践指导。
> 
> 本文的结构化解读旨在帮助初学者和非专业读者理解这两种复杂计算方法的原理、关系、优缺点及其在实际应用中的表现。
> 
> ## 二、论文结构与逻辑概览
> 
> 本论文的组织结构严谨，逻辑链条清晰，从理论推导到实际验证，层层深入。其核心论证链条如下：
> 
> 1.  **背景与问题引入**（I. INTRODUCTION）：
>     
>     -   **领域基础**：Kohn-Sham **密度泛函理论 (DFT)** 结合**平面波 (Plane-Wave)** 基组和**赝势 (Pseudopotential, PP)** 方法在计算材料科学中广泛应用。
>     -   **传统挑战**：标准**范数守恒赝势 (NCPP)** 难以高效处理第一行、过渡金属和稀土元素，需要过大的平面波基组。
>     -   **US-PP的兴起**：Vanderbilt 的**超软赝势 (US-PP)** 通过放松范数守恒约束，引入**增强电荷 (augmentation charges)** 解决了部分难题，但赝势构建复杂。
>     -   **PAW的提出**：Blöchl 的**投影缀加波 (PAW)** 方法结合了赝势和 **LAPW** 思想，概念更优雅，但与 US-PP 的形式关系不明。
>     -   **本文目标**：建立 US-PP 与 PAW 的形式关系，简化 PAW 在现有代码中的实现，并比较其准确性与效率。
> 2.  **PAW 总能量泛函的修改与推导**（II. THE PAW TOTAL ENERGY FUNCTIONAL）：
>     
>     -   **基本 PAW 形式**：介绍了 PAW 中**全电子波函数 (AE wave function)** 和**伪波函数 (PS wave function)** 之间的线性变换关系。
>     -   **电荷密度分解**：详细说明了 PAW 中电荷密度分解为在平面波网格上处理的“软”伪电荷密度和在径向支持网格上处理的原子中心电荷密度的机制。
>     -   **修改哈特里能与交换关联能**：作者对 Blöchl 原始 PAW 泛函的哈特里能和交换关联能部分进行了修改，使其更接近 US-PP 形式，并引入**非线性核校正 (nonlinear core corrections, NLCC)**，以提高鲁棒性。
>     -   **总能量泛函**：给出了修改后的 PAW 总能量泛函的最终表达式，它由在常规网格上评估的项和在径向支持网格上评估的原子中心修正项组成。
>     -   **补偿电荷 (Compensation Charge)**：解释了补偿电荷的作用——确保重构的电荷密度具有正确的多极矩。
> 3.  **US-PP 与 PAW 的形式关系**（II.F. Ultrasoft pseudopotentials）：
>     
>     -   **关键推导**：通过对 PAW 泛函中原子中心修正项进行**线性化 (linearization)**，成功推导出了 US-PP 的总能量泛函形式。
>     -   **核心发现**：证明 US-PP 是 PAW 方法在特定条件下的近似，两者差异主要在于简单的原子中心项，以及 US-PP 中**增强电荷 (augmentation charges)** 的**赝化 (pseudization)** 处理。
>     -   **精度讨论**：指出 PAW 理论上更精确，因为其能更准确地重构核心区域的全电子波函数；US-PP 精度受限于增强电荷的赝化准确性。
> 4.  **哈密顿算符、作用力与应力张量**（III. HAMILTON OPERATOR AND FORCES）：
>     
>     -   **正交性条件**：引入了 PAW 方法中伪波函数需满足的**重叠算符 (overlap operator)** 正交性条件。
>     -   **哈密顿算符**：推导了修改后 PAW 泛函的哈密顿算符 H。
>     -   **作用力**：基于 Goedecker 和 Maschke 的力定理，详细推导了作用力（**Pulay力**）的表达式，包括电荷密度、补偿电荷和投影函数变化导致的贡献。
>     -   **与 US-PP 的对比**：强调 PAW 和 US-PP 在力计算上的相似性，主要区别在于原子中心项在 PAW 中随自洽迭代而变化，而在 US-PP 中是固定的。
> 5.  **PAW 数据集的构建**（IV. PAW DATASETS）：
>     
>     -   **所需量**：列出了构建 PAW 数据集所需的关键量（AE/PS 部分波、投影函数、核电荷密度、补偿函数）。
>     -   **构建步骤**：详细描述了部分波和投影函数的生成过程。
>     -   **赝化核电荷与局部赝势**：讨论了如何构建赝化核电荷和局部赝势，以及避免**鬼态 (ghost states)** 的策略。

> 7.  **讨论与结论**（VI. DISCUSSION & VII. CONCLUSION）：
>     
>     -   **PAW 的优势**：强调 PAW 方法的精确性和鲁棒性，尤其在处理强磁性、大电负性差异体系时，可达到全电子计算水平。
>     -   **US-PP 的局限**：指出 US-PP 在磁性体系中可能存在精度问题，且构建更为困难。
>     -   **理论贡献**：重申了 US-PP 作为 PAW 线性化近似的理论重要性。
>     -   **实践意义**：PAW 在现有 US-PP 代码中的易于实现，以及双网格技术和非线性核校正的重要性。

> ## 四、正文核心内容剖析
> 
> ### 背景铺垫与问题演进
> 
> 在计算材料科学中，**密度泛函理论（DFT）**是进行原子尺度模拟的基石。为了将DFT应用于包含大量原子的体系，**平面波基组（Plane-Wave Basis Set）**结合**赝势（Pseudopotential, PP）近似**成为主流方法。赝势的核心思想是用一个有效势来替代原子核和内层（核心）电子对价电子的作用，从而只显式处理相对稀疏的价电子，极大地降低计算负担。
> 
> 然而，传统的**范数守恒赝势（Norm-Conserving Pseudopotentials, NCPPs）**在处理包含强局域（如具有节点）价轨道的元素（如第一行元素、3d过渡金属和稀土元素）时，由于需要在核心区域精确重现波函数的振荡，导致需要非常大的平面波截止能量，计算效率低下。
> 
> 为解决这一问题，Vanderbilt在1990年引入了**超软赝势（Ultrasoft Pseudopotentials, US-PP）**。US-PP通过**放松范数守恒约束**，允许伪波函数在核心区域变得更“软”（即更平滑，无节点），从而可以使用更小的平面波基组。为了弥补核心区域损失的电荷密度，US-PP引入了原子中心、局域的**增强电荷（augmentation charges）**。尽管US-PP在提高效率方面非常成功，但其构建相对复杂，涉及多个参数（如截止半径），且在强极性或磁性体系中可能出现转移性问题。
> 
> 随后，Blöchl在1994年提出了**投影缀加波（Projector Augmented-Wave, PAW）方法**。PAW方法结合了赝势和**线性化缀加平面波（LAPW）**方法的思想，提供了一个概念上更优雅的框架。PAW的核心是一个线性变换，它将平滑的伪波函数（在整个空间用平面波表示）与真实的全电子波函数联系起来。PAW方法理论上能够精确重构核心区域的全电子波函数，因此被认为是**全电子（All-Electron, AE）**方法，同时又享有赝势方法的计算效率。
> 
> 尽管Blöchl在其工作中暗示了PAW与US-PP之间可能存在相似性，但并未建立正式的数学关系。这就引出了本文的核心任务：**正式推导并建立US-PP与PAW之间的形式关系，并评估两者的相对优劣**。
> 
> ### PAW总能量泛函的修改与推导
> 
> 本文首先对Blöchl原始PAW总能量泛函进行了修改，目的是使其形式更接近US-PP方法，以便于在现有代码中实现。
> 
> 1.  **基本PAW形式**： PAW方法的核心在于一个线性变换，将全电子波函数 $| \Psi_n \rangle$ (AE) 与伪波函数 $| \tilde{\Psi}_n \rangle$ (PS) 联系起来： $| \Psi_n \rangle = | \tilde{\Psi}_n \rangle + \sum_i (| \phi_i \rangle - | \tilde{\phi}_i \rangle) \langle \tilde{p}_i | \tilde{\Psi}_n \rangle$ 其中，$| \phi_i \rangle$ 是原子中心的全电子部分波（AE partial waves），$| \tilde{\phi}_i \rangle$ 是对应的伪部分波（PS partial waves），它们在核心半径之外与 $| \phi_i \rangle$ 相同，在核心之内是平滑的。$\langle \tilde{p}_i |$ 是投影函数（projector functions），与伪部分波满足双正交条件 $\langle \tilde{p}_i | \tilde{\phi}_j \rangle = \delta_{ij}$。伪波函数 $| \tilde{\Psi}_n \rangle$ 是变分量，通过平面波基组在整个空间进行展开。
>     
> 2.  **电荷密度分解**： PAW方法中的全电子电荷密度 $n(\mathbf{r})$ 被分解为三部分： $n(\mathbf{r}) = \tilde{n}(\mathbf{r}) + n_1(\mathbf{r}) - \tilde{n}_1(\mathbf{r})$
>     
>     -   $\tilde{n}(\mathbf{r})$ 是从伪波函数直接计算的“软”伪电荷密度，在平面波网格上处理。
>     -   $n_1(\mathbf{r})$ 是原子中心的全电子电荷密度，在径向支持网格上处理，仅限于每个原子周围的**缀加球（augmentation sphere）**内。
>     -   $\tilde{n}_1(\mathbf{r})$ 是原子中心的伪电荷密度，也在径向支持网格上处理。 这种分解使得电荷密度可以高效地在不同网格上处理。
> 3.  **哈特里能和交换关联能的修改**： 本文对Blöchl原始方法中哈特里能和交换关联能的分解方式进行了调整，以更好地模拟US-PP中的处理。
>     
>     -   **哈特里能（Hartree Energy, EH）**：总电荷密度 $n_T$ 被分解为 $n_T = \tilde{n} + \hat{n} + n_{Zc}$，其中 $\tilde{n}$ 是伪价电子密度，$\hat{n}$ 是补偿电荷，$n_{Zc}$ 是原子核和冻结核心全电子核电荷的组合。本文的分解特别处理了核心-价态相互作用，并假定核心电荷不重叠。
>     -   **交换关联能（Exchange-Correlation Energy, Exc）**：本文引入了**非线性核校正（Nonlinear Core Corrections, NLCC）**，通过在交换关联泛函中考虑一个部分电子核电荷密度 $\tilde{n}_c$，从而更准确地描述核心-价态重叠区域的交换关联效应。这被认为在部分波集不完整（实际应用中常见）时，能提高PAW方法的鲁棒性和准确性，尤其对于核态延伸到缀加球边界的体系。
> 4.  **最终总能量泛函**： 修改后的PAW总能量 $E$ 被分解为三项：$E = \tilde{E} + E_1 - \tilde{E}_1$。
>     
>     -   $\tilde{E}$：主要在常规平面波网格上计算，包含伪波函数的动能、交换关联能、哈特里能和**Ewald求和**项（用于处理离子核间的长程静电相互作用）。
>     -   $E_1$：在径向支持网格上计算，包含全电子部分波的动能、交换关联能和哈特里能项。
>     -   $\tilde{E}_1$：在径向支持网格上计算，包含伪部分波的动能、交换关联能和哈特里能项。 通过这种分解，PAW泛函的各项明确区分了全局平面波贡献和局部原子中心修正贡献。
> 
> ### US-PP与PAW的正式关系
> 
> 本文最重要的理论贡献之一是**正式建立了US-PP与PAW方法之间的数学关系**。作者通过对PAW总能量泛函中的原子中心项（$E_1$ 和 $\tilde{E}_1$）进行**线性化**，成功推导出了US-PP的总能量泛函形式。
> 
> 具体而言，当PAW泛函中的原子中心项在原子参考占据数（atomic reference occupancies）附近进行线性化时，结果表明US-PP的总能量泛函与本文推导的修改版PAW泛函中的 $\tilde{E}$ 项形式上完全一致，只是多了两个附加的原子中心项。这些原子中心项包含了全电子部分波和伪部分波的动能、原子中心有效势能的差异，以及补偿电荷相关的贡献。
> 
> **核心结论**：US-PP方法可以被视为PAW方法的一种**线性化近似**。两者之间的主要差异在于：
> 
> 1.  **原子中心项**：PAW方法精确处理原子中心项，这些项在自洽迭代过程中是动态变化的。而US-PP方法中，这些原子中心项（反映增强电荷的贡献）通常在赝势生成阶段就被计算为固定常数。
> 2.  **增强电荷的赝化**：US-PP中引入的**增强电荷**需要进行**赝化（pseudization）**，以便在平面波网格上高效处理。这种赝化可能导致其无法完全准确地重构核心区域的全电子电荷分布，从而引入误差。PAW方法则通过径向支持网格直接处理全电子部分波，避免了这种赝化带来的误差。
> 
> 因此，PAW方法在理论上更为精确，能够更好地重现全电子结果，尤其是在电荷密度分布偏离原子参考态较大（如强电负性差异、强磁性）的体系中。而US-PP方法在电荷密度分布与原子参考态相似的体系中仍能提供高精度结果，但其准确性高度依赖于**赝化增强函数（pseudized augmentation function）**的质量。为了提高US-PP的精度，需要构建更“硬”（即更准确，但计算成本更高）的增强电荷。
> 
> ### 哈密顿算符和作用力
> 
> 本文还详细推导了修改后PAW泛函的**哈密顿算符（Hamiltonian Operator, H）**和**作用力（Forces, F）**。
> 
> 1.  **哈密顿算符**： 在PAW方法中，伪波函数 $| \tilde{\Psi}_n \rangle$ 必须满足一个包含**重叠算符（overlap operator, S）**的正交条件 $\langle \tilde{\Psi}_n | S | \tilde{\Psi}_m \rangle = \delta_{nm}$。重叠算符S包含了原子中心的部分波差异。 哈密顿算符H的推导通过对总能量泛函相对于伪密度算符进行变分得到。最终的哈密顿算符形式包括动能项、有效单电子势项，以及一个包含原子中心修正的非局域项。这些修正项捕获了伪波函数无法描述的快速变化的细节以及补偿电荷与有效势的相互作用。
>     
> 2.  **作用力**： 作用力通常定义为能量对离子位置的负导数。在PAW方法中，由于缀加球和补偿电荷会随离子移动，因此需要考虑额外的贡献，这些被称为**Pulay力**，尽管本文指出这并非完全准确，因为平面波基组与原子位置无关。 作用力的推导基于Goedecker和Maschke的力定理。总作用力包括三项：
>     
>     -   **F1**：源于局部势**˜veff**随离子位置的变化。
>     -   **F2**：源于补偿电荷**nˆ**随离子移动而变化。
>     -   **F3**：源于投影函数**˜pi**的变化。 此外，还需要考虑一个来自**非线性核校正（NLCC）**的额外项。 **关键点**：PAW方法的作用力计算与US-PP非常相似。主要区别在于US-PP中的原子中心项是固定常数，而PAW中这些项在电子基态确定过程中是动态变化的。应力张量的推导也类似，但在本文中未详细给出。
> 
> ### PAW数据集的构建
> 
> PAW数据集是PAW方法实施的核心，它包含了所有定义PAW变换所需的原子中心量。一个完整的PAW数据集需要以下几项：
> 
> -   **全电子（AE）部分波 $\phi_i$ 和伪部分波 $\tilde{\phi}_i$**：通常通过对孤立原子的全电子计算获得。对于每个角量子数l（如s, p, d），选择两个参考能量来计算部分波，以确保良好的转移性。伪部分波在截止半径内通常用球形Bessel函数展开。
> -   **投影函数 $\tilde{p}_i$**：与伪部分波双正交，可采用Vanderbilt或Blöchl的方案构建。
> -   **核心电荷密度 $n_c$、赝化核心电荷密度 $\tilde{n}_{Zc}$ 和部分电子核心电荷密度 $\tilde{n}_c$**：
>     -   $n_c$ 是冻结核心全电子波函数的电荷密度。
>     -   $\tilde{n}_{Zc}$ 是一个赝化后的核心电荷分布，用于在核心区域外与真实核电荷保持一致，并在核心区域内具有相同的矩。
>     -   $\tilde{n}_c$ 是部分电子核心电荷密度，用于非线性核校正。
> -   **补偿函数 $g_l(r)$**：用于构建补偿电荷 $\hat{n}$，通常选择为球形Bessel函数之和，确保在特定半径内具有正确的矩。
> 
> **数据集构建的关键考虑**：
> 
> -   **避免鬼态（Ghost States）**：鬼态是赝势计算中的一个常见问题，表现为虚假的低能量轨道。本文指出鬼态通常源于选择了过于吸引的局部原子赝势。解决方法包括调整局部赝势的形式（如使用“截断”的全电子势）或将半核态（如Ca的3p态）显式地作为价态处理。
> -   **双网格技术（Double Grid Technique）**：为了高效处理电荷密度在不同空间尺度上的变化（平滑的伪波函数在粗平面波网格，快速变化的全电子细节在细径向支持网格），本文采用了双网格技术。它通过傅里叶插值将电荷密度从粗网格转换到更细的实空间网格，在此精细网格上计算补偿电荷、哈特里势和交换关联势，然后再将局部势插值回粗网格。这种技术避免了US-PP中硬增强电荷的表示困难，提高了精度和效率。
> 
> ### 结果验证与比较
> 
> 本文通过对多种小分子和块体系统（包括磁性过渡金属）进行严格的基准测试，系统地比较了PAW、US-PP和全电子（AE/FLAPW）方法，验证了PAW方法的准确性和效率，并揭示了US-PP方法的局限性。
> 
> 1.  **小分子测试**： 对H2、Li2、H2O、BF3等小分子键长和键角的计算结果表明：
>     
>     -   PAW与US-PP方法彼此之间的差异非常小，通常在0.1%以内。
>     -   **PAW方法与全电子（AE）计算结果高度一致**，键长误差通常小于0.1%（例如H2为0.001 a.u.），键角误差也极小。这说明PAW方法在描述分子键合方面能够达到与全电子方法相当的精度。
>     -   US-PP在处理一些强极性体系（如Li）时，其赝势构建相对困难，需要细致优化才能达到与PAW相当的精度。
> 2.  **块体系统测试**： 对金刚石、Si、V、Li、Ca、CaF2等块体材料的晶格常数、内聚能和体模量的计算结果表明：
>     
>     -   **PAW方法与全电子FLAPW（Full Potential Linearized Augmented Plane Wave）计算结果高度一致**，晶格常数差异小于0.5%，内聚能小于1%，体模量小于5%。再次验证了PAW方法在块体材料性质预测方面的精确性。
>     -   US-PP在大多数情况下也表现良好，但**在CaF2（涉及Ca的半核3p态）等体系中，PAW的精度更高，US-PP的构建更具挑战性**。
>     -   **非线性核校正（NLCC）**对PAW方法的鲁棒性至关重要。对于Li，即使在没有p部分波的情况下，引入NLCC也能保持计算的准确性，而无NLCC则会导致显著误差。
> 3.  **磁性Fe, Co, Ni**： 对Fe、Co、Ni原子和块体的自旋极化计算结果是本文最关键的发现之一：
>     
>     -   **PAW方法在原子磁化能和组态间能量方面与全电子（AE）计算结果表现出几乎完美的一致性**，能量差异通常小于10 meV。
>     -   **US-PP方法倾向于高估磁化能**，尤其是在**广义梯度近似（GGA）**下，误差可能高达120 meV（例如对于bcc FM Fe的磁性能量）。这归因于US-PP中**增强电荷的赝化无法准确描述磁性体系中d电子的精细自旋密度分布和波函数形状**。
>     -   通过构建**“US-AE”**赝势（即具有非常精确和硬的增强电荷的US-PP），其结果可以与PAW和AE方法媲美，这进一步证实了US-PP的误差主要来源于增强电荷的表示不足，而非方法本身的基本缺陷。然而，这种高精度的US-PP需要更高的计算成本。
>     -   在块体Fe的不同相能量差异比较中，PAW与FLAPW方法结果几乎完美一致，而**标准US-PP在磁性bcc相的能量差异上表现出明显偏差**。
> 
> ## 五、结论与贡献
> 
> 本篇文献《From ultrasoft pseudopotentials to the projector augmented-wave method》的结论清晰有力，对计算材料科学领域产生了深远影响。
> 
> **核心发现总结：**
> 
> 1.  **PAW方法的卓越精度与鲁棒性**：PAW方法能够以与**全电子（All-Electron, AE）**方法（如FLAPW）相当的精度处理各种复杂体系，包括强极性分子、多相块体材料以及具有挑战性的**强磁性（strong magnetic moments）**体系。其在小分子键长上的误差通常小于0.5%，甚至可以达到0.1%以内，在块体材料晶格常数、内聚能和体模量上的结果也与AE方法高度吻合。这表明PAW方法中引入的近似（如有限的部分波集和**冻核近似（frozen-core approximation）**）已得到了良好的控制。
> 2.  **US-PP与PAW的正式关系**：本文通过对PAW总能量泛函进行**线性化（linearization）**，**首次形式化地证明了Vanderbilt超软赝势（US-PP）方法可以被视为PAW方法的一种近似**。这一关系揭示了两者在理论上的紧密联系，并阐明了它们之间差异的根本来源：US-PP中的**增强电荷（augmentation charges）**需要**赝化（pseudization）**并在常规平面波网格上表示，而PAW则通过径向支持网格更精确地重构核心区域的全电子波函数，从而避免了这种赝化带来的潜在误差。
> 3.  **US-PP在特定场景下的局限性**：尽管US-PP在许多p区元素和非磁性过渡金属中表现良好，但在处理**强磁性体系（如Fe）**或**电负性差异大（strong electronegativity difference）**的体系时，其精度和鲁棒性会受到影响，尤其是在**广义梯度近似（Generalized Gradient Approximation, GGA）**下可能会高估磁化能。这是因为US-PP中赝化增强电荷难以精确描述这些体系中波函数和自旋密度在核心区域的复杂精细结构。然而，如果US-PP的增强电荷能达到极高精度（“US-AE”），其结果仍可与PAW媲美，但计算成本将显著增加。
> 4.  **方法实现与效率提升**：本文提出了一个略微修改的PAW泛函，使其**易于在现有支持US-PP的平面波代码（如VASP）中实现**。通过引入**非线性核校正（Nonlinear Core Corrections, NLCC）**和**双网格技术（Double Grid Technique）**，进一步提升了PAW方法的鲁棒性、准确性和计算效率。NLCC在部分波集不完整时尤为重要，而双网格技术则高效解决了不同空间尺度电荷密度处理的难题。
> 
> **主要贡献：**
> 
> 1.  **理论统一与深化**：首次通过严谨的数学推导，将US-PP和PAW这两种重要的赝势方法统一在一个更广阔的理论框架下，极大地深化了我们对这两种方法的理解，并为未来的方法发展奠定了基础。
> 2.  **PAW方法的广泛推广**：通过提供易于实现的修改版PAW泛函和详细的实现策略，该工作极大地降低了PAW方法的应用门槛，使其能够被更广泛的计算材料科学研究者所采用，并融入到主流的第一性原理计算软件中（如VASP）。
> 3.  **高精度基准验证**：通过对大量小分子和块体系统（包括具有挑战性的磁性体系）的系统性、高精度基准测试，确立了PAW方法作为一种**近乎全电子精度**的、同时具有**计算效率**的强大工具的地位。
> 4.  **数值技术创新**：引入并验证了非线性核校正和双网格技术，这些数值策略不仅提升了PAW方法的准确性和鲁棒性，也为其他基于平面波的计算方法提供了有益的参考。
> 
> ## 六、未来发展方向展望
> 
> 基于本篇文献的深度解读，我们可以对PAW和相关计算方法在未来的发展方向做出以下展望：
> 
> 1.  **PAW方法的扩展与优化**：
>     
>     -   **超高压/极端条件下的冻核近似**：虽然冻核近似在多数情况下表现良好，但在超高压等极端条件下，核心电子也可能参与化学键合。未来研究可以探索**半核态（semicore states）**甚至部分核心态的更灵活、更自适应的价态处理机制，或开发更高级的冻核近似校正方法。
>     -   **部分波集的自适应优化**：虽然本文指出两个部分波通常已足够，但对于某些具有复杂电子结构或特定性质（如拓扑性质）的材料，部分波集的选择和完备性可能仍有优化空间。研究可以探索基于机器学习或数据驱动的方法，实现部分波集的自动、自适应生成。
>     -   **非线性核校正的普适性与精度**：NLCC显著提升了方法鲁棒性，但其普适性和在更复杂（例如具有强关联效应）体系中的表现仍可进一步探究和优化。
> 2.  **US-PP方法的持续改进**：
>     
>     -   **自适应增强电荷构造**：鉴于US-PP在磁性体系中的局限性源于增强电荷的赝化，未来的研究可以集中于开发更智能、更自适应的增强电荷构造方法，使其能够根据体系的局部电子环境（如磁矩大小、化学键合类型）动态调整赝化程度，从而在保持效率的同时提高精度。
>     -   **结合机器学习的赝势生成**：利用机器学习模型从全电子数据中学习最优的US-PP参数和增强电荷形式，有望实现更高效、更准确、更具普适性的US-PP生成。
> 3.  **PAW与其他高级方法的融合**：
>     
>     -   **GW/BSE和DMFT等激发态/强关联计算**：PAW作为一种高精度的DFT基态方法，其伪波函数和投影函数为更高层次的激发态计算（如GW近似、Bethe-Salpeter方程）和强关联电子体系计算（如动态平均场理论，DMFT）提供了理想的输入。未来的研究将继续探索PAW框架如何与这些更昂贵但更精确的方法无缝结合，以解决DFT层面的局限性。
>     -   **非绝热动力学模拟**：PAW能够精确描述全电子波函数，这使其在需要精确计算力与跃迁耦合的**非绝热分子动力学**模拟中具有巨大潜力。
> 4.  **计算效率的极限突破**：
>     
>     -   **大规模并行计算优化**：虽然双网格技术已提升效率，但对于包含数千甚至数万原子的超大规模体系，仍需要进一步优化PAW算法的并行化效率，以适应异构计算架构（如GPU加速、量子计算等）。
>     -   **线性标度算法的整合**：结合**O(N)线性标度算法**来处理PAW方法中的长程相互作用，有望进一步降低计算复杂度，实现对更大体系的模拟。
> 5.  **PAW数据集的标准化与自动化**：
>     
>     -   **标准化数据库**：建立和维护一个高质量、经过广泛验证的PAW数据集数据库，以降低用户使用门槛，并确保计算结果的可靠性。
>     -   **全自动化生成流程**：虽然本文讨论了避免鬼态的策略，但PAW数据集的构建目前仍需要一定的经验和人工干预。未来有望开发出完全自动化的、能够生成高精度且无鬼态PAW数据集的流程，进一步提高方法的易用性。
> 
> ## 七、学术思考与批判性分析
> 
> 本论文在计算材料科学领域无疑是一个里程碑式的工作，它不仅深入揭示了US-PP和PAW两种方法的内在联系，更通过严格的验证确立了PAW方法的卓越性能。然而，从批判性角度审视，仍有以下几个值得深入思考的问题：
> 
> 1.  **US-PP近似的普适性与边界**：
>     
>     -   **深度问题**：本文通过线性化证明US-PP是PAW的一种近似，并指出其在强磁性、大电负性差异体系中可能存在精度问题。那么，除了这些已知的“困难案例”，在哪些更普遍的物理场景下（例如特定维度体系、强应变材料、超快过程等），US-PP的这种“近似”会变得不可接受，而PAW的“精确性”变得至关重要？这种近似是否意味着US-PP在某种程度上牺牲了普适性以换取特定情境下的计算效率，而这种权衡的精确边界在哪里？
> 2.  **PAW方法中近似的潜在局限**：
>     
>     -   **深度问题**：论文明确指出PAW方法的近似主要来自有限的部分波集和冻核近似。对于某些包含**强关联电子（strongly correlated electrons）**的体系（例如重费米子材料、某些氧化物），核心电子与价电子的相互作用可能远比标准DFT框架下所描述的复杂。在这种情况下，冻核近似的局限性可能会更加突出。PAW方法如何处理或需要如何扩展（例如与DMFT等方法结合，或更精细地处理半核态）才能在这些体系中保持其“全电子精度”的优势？
> 3.  **数值实现细节与误差传播**：
>     
>     -   **深度问题**：论文强调了**非线性核校正（NLCC）**和**双网格技术（double grid technique）**对PAW方法鲁棒性和效率的提升。这两种技术在数值实现上是否存在潜在的挑战或限制？例如，双网格的粒度选择（即粗网格与细网格点数之比）是否会影响计算精度和效率的平衡？NLCC在极端电荷密度梯度或非均匀核电荷分布（例如涉及强束缚态的重元素）情况下的稳定性如何？这些数值误差如何通过整个PAW泛函传播，并最终影响关键物理量的预测？
> 4.  **自旋极化计算的系统性偏差**：
>     
>     -   **深度问题**：文中对Fe、Co、Ni磁性体系的测试结果表明，US-PP在高估磁化能方面存在问题，尤其是在GGA下。这是否意味着US-PP方法在**自旋极化DFT计算**中存在系统性偏差？其根本原因是否在于增强电荷的赝化无法准确描述磁性体系中d电子轨道的精细结构和自旋密度分布，特别是当这些轨道与核心电子有复杂重叠时？如果是这样，如何从理论层面进一步改进US-PP对自旋相关性质的描述，例如通过引入自旋依赖的增强电荷？
> 5.  **PAW数据集构建的自动化与可靠性**：

> 
> ### 问题1: 什么是**赝势方法**？它为什么在计算材料科学中很重要？
> 
> **解答**: **赝势 (Pseudopotential, PP)** 方法是一种在**密度泛函理论 (DFT)** 计算中用于简化原子内部电子-原子核相互作用的近似技术。它的核心思想是：原子中的内层（核心）电子在化学反应和材料性质中通常被认为是“冻结”的，不直接参与键合。因此，可以将原子核和核心电子的联合效应替换为一个有效的、较弱的**赝势**，这个赝势能够准确模拟核心区域外核心电子对价电子的**屏蔽作用**以及原子核的吸引力。
> 
> **重要性**:
> 
> -   **计算效率高**: 通过去除核心电子的显式处理，大大减少了需要描述的电子数量，使得使用**平面波基组 (Plane-Wave Basis Set)** 可以采用更小的截断能量，从而显著降低了大规模体系（如固体、表面、纳米结构）的计算成本，使**第一性原理 (First-Principles)** 模拟成为可能。
> -   **物理直观**: 赝势方法将计算焦点集中在对化学键合和材料性质至关重要的价电子上。
> -   **精确性**: 构造得当的赝势能够高度准确地再现全电子计算结果，特别是在核心区域外的价电子行为。
> 
> ### 问题2: **超软赝势 (US-PP)** 和**投影缀加波 (PAW)** 方法有何不同？哪种方法更优？
> 
> **解答**: **超软赝势 (US-PP)** 和**投影缀加波 (PAW)** 都是先进的赝势方法，旨在提高计算效率。
> 
> -   **超软赝势 (US-PP)**:
>     
>     -   **核心特点**: 通过**放松范数守恒约束 (norm-conserving constraint)** 来生成更“软”（即更平滑，无节点）的伪波函数，从而可以使用更小的平面波基组。
>     -   **电荷补偿**: 为了弥补核心区域因伪化而损失的电荷密度，US-PP引入了原子中心、局域的**增强电荷 (augmentation charges)**。
>     -   **优点**: 计算效率高，特别是对于具有许多价电子的过渡金属。
>     -   **缺点**: 增强电荷的构建和**赝化 (pseudization)** 可能引入误差，尤其在**强磁性体系**、**电负性差异大**的体系或**半核态 (semicore states)** 处理中，精度可能受限，且赝势构建过程较复杂。
> -   **投影缀加波 (PAW)**:
>     
>     -   **核心特点**: PAW被认为是**全电子 (All-Electron, AE)** 层次的方法，它通过一个线性的、可逆的变换将平滑的伪波函数与真实的全电子波函数联系起来。它没有放弃全电子波函数在核心区域的精确描述。
>     -   **波函数重构**: PAW能够在核心区域精确地**重构全电子波函数**（包括其节点），避免了US-PP中增强电荷赝化带来的误差。
>     -   **优点**: 以赝势的计算效率达到**近乎全电子计算的精度**；在处理**强磁性体系**、**强离子键**体系和**半核态**时，表现出更高的鲁棒性和准确性；数据集构建通常比US-PP更简便。
>     -   **缺点**: 理论上比US-PP更复杂，实现起来稍微困难，但本论文已大大简化了其在现有代码中的集成。
> 
> **哪种更优？**: 本文的结论明确指出，**PAW方法通常更优**。它能处理最困难的计算情况（如强磁性、大电负性差异）并达到卓越的精度和可靠性，其结果与全电子计算几乎无法区分。US-PP在许多常规情况下也提供相似的精度，但对于上述“困难案例”，其鲁棒性较低，且赝势构建更依赖经验和参数调整。因此，**对于追求高精度和普适性的研究，PAW是更推荐的选择**。
> 
> ### 问题3: 为什么PAW方法在处理磁性材料（如Fe）时比US-PP更准确？
> 
> **解答**: PAW方法在处理磁性材料（特别是Fe这种具有大磁矩的过渡金属）时表现出更高的准确性，主要是因为**磁性体系中d电子的自旋密度分布对波函数的形状和精细结构非常敏感**。
> 
> -   **US-PP的局限性**: US-PP通过**赝化增强电荷**来近似核心区域的电荷分布。当体系具有强磁性时，d轨道的空间分布及其与核心电子的相互作用变得非常复杂和精细。如果增强电荷的赝化不够准确，就难以精确描述这些复杂的自旋密度分布和交换关联效应。这导致US-PP可能高估磁化能，尤其在使用**广义梯度近似 (GGA)** 泛函时，因为GGA对电荷密度梯度更敏感。
> -   **PAW的优势**: 相比之下，PAW方法通过其线性变换和径向支持网格，能够**精确地重构核心区域的全电子波函数**（包括其节点），从而更准确地描述d电子的局域特性和自旋极化效应。这种精确性使得PAW能够更好地捕捉磁性体系中的能量差异和磁矩，因此能更好地与全电子计算结果吻合。本文通过“US-AE”赝势的测试也证实了这一点，即当US-PP的增强电荷足够精确时，它也能达到PAW的精度，但代价是计算成本大幅增加。
> 
> ### 问题4: 论文中提到的“**非线性核校正 (nonlinear core corrections)**”是什么？它有什么作用？
> 
> **解答**: **非线性核校正 (nonlinear core corrections, NLCC)** 是一种在赝势计算中用来提高**交换关联能 (Exchange-Correlation Energy)** 准确性的技术。
> 
> -   **背景问题**: 在标准的赝势近似中，价电子的交换关联能是基于价电子密度计算的。然而，原子中的核心电子和价电子之间存在物理上的重叠。如果简单地将核心电子作为冻结的背景电荷处理，并且只考虑价电子密度来计算交换关联能，那么在核心区域这个重叠部分就可能导致交换关联能的计算误差。特别是当交换关联泛函是非线性的（如**局域密度近似, LDA** 或 **广义梯度近似, GGA**），且对电荷密度波动敏感时，这个误差会变得显著。
> -   **作用机制**: NLCC通过在交换关联泛函的输入中**引入一个额外的电荷密度（通常是部分伪核心电荷密度）来修正核心区域的电荷密度**。它不再仅仅使用价电子密度，而是使用价电子密度与这个额外核心电荷密度的总和来计算核心-价态重叠区域的交换关联能。这样，交换关联泛函就能更准确地描述核心和价电子在重叠区域的相互作用。
> -   **重要性**: 本文强调，NLCC能显著提高PAW方法在**部分波集不完整**时（这在实际计算中很常见）的**鲁棒性 (robustness)**，特别对于核态延伸到缀加球边界的体系。此外，它还能**减少梯度校正泛函在核心附近的数值不稳定性**，确保计算的稳定性和准确性。
> 
> ### 问题5: PAW方法如何实现与现有US-PP平面波代码的兼容性？“**双网格技术 (double grid technique)**”在此扮演什么角色？
> 
> **解答**: 本文的一个重要贡献是提出了一种修改后的PAW泛函，使其在形式上更接近US-PP方法，从而**使得PAW方法能够相对简便地在已支持US-PP的平面波代码（如VASP）中实现**。这主要通过以下几个方面：
> 
> 1.  **哈特里能量的分解**: 将哈特里能量的分解方式调整得更符合传统赝势平面波代码的习惯，易于处理。
> 2.  **交换关联能的处理**: 在平面波网格上评估交换关联能时，显式地引入了**非线性核校正**项。
> 3.  **原子中心项的隔离**: PAW方法的核心是其原子中心修正项，这些项的计算需要径向支持网格。通过修改泛函形式，使得这些原子中心项可以与平面波部分相对独立地处理。
> 
> **双网格技术 (double grid technique)** 在此扮演了**关键角色**，它解决了PAW方法中不同空间尺度处理的挑战，并提升了效率和精度：
> 
> -   **背景**: PAW方法需要同时处理两种不同尺度的信息：在整个空间上用**粗糙的平面波网格 (coarse plane-wave grid)** 描述平滑的伪波函数，以及在原子核心附近用**精细的径向支持网格 (fine radial support grid)** 描述快速变化的全电子波函数细节。
> -   **作用**: 双网格技术允许电荷密度**˜n(r)**首先在常规的“粗”平面波网格上计算。然后，通过**傅里叶插值 (Fourier interpolation)** 将电荷密度转换到第二个更“细”的实空间网格上。**补偿电荷 (nˆ)** 等快速变化的量直接在细网格上计算并加到**˜n(r)**上。哈特里势、交换关联势和局部势也都在这个细网格上计算。最后，局部势再通过傅里叶插值回传到粗平面波网格，用于哈密顿算符的操作。
> -   **优势**: 这种技术避免了US-PP中将快速变化的**增强电荷**表示在单一常规平面波网格上的困难，从而允许PAW方法使用更“软”的补偿电荷（因为它不需要模仿全电子电荷密度的真实形状，只需恢复正确的矩），同时保证了精度和效率。它避免了“移动盒子”等复杂方法，使得PAW的实现更加直接和方便。这种“O(N)”标度的额外计算量对于中等大小的系统几乎可以忽略不计。

> ## I. INTRODUCTION
> 
> ## I. 引言
> 
> First-principles Kohn-Sham density-functional methods ~see, e.g., Refs. 1 and 2! employing a plane-wave basis set and the pseudopotential ~PP! approximation are currently among the most successful techniques in computational chemistry and computational material science.3–5 采用平面波基组和赝势 (PP) 近似的从头算Kohn-Sham密度泛函方法（参见文献1和2）是目前计算化学和计算材料科学中最成功的技术之一。 **Kohn-Sham density-functional methods**: Kohn-Sham密度泛函方法，一种将多电子问题简化为单电子有效势问题的量子力学方法。 **Pseudopotential (PP)**: 赝势，一种用有效势代替原子核及内层电子相互作用的方法，以减少计算量。
> 
> The biggest merit of these methods is their formal simplicity, but unfortunately this simplicity has a price: first-row elements, transition metals, and rare-earth elements are computationally demanding to treat with standard norm-conserving pseudopotentials.6 这些方法最大的优点在于其形式上的简单性，但遗憾的是，这种简单性是有代价的：使用标准的模守恒赝势处理第一行元素、过渡金属和稀土元素时，计算需求非常高。 **Norm-conserving pseudopotentials**: 模守恒赝势，要求赝波函数在截止半径内的电荷量与全电子波函数一致。
> 
> Therefore, various attempts have been made to generate soft pseudopotentials, and the most successful approach to date is the concept of ultrasoft PP introduced by Vanderbilt.7 因此，人们尝试了多种方法来生成“软”赝势，迄今为止最成功的方法是Vanderbilt引入的超软赝势概念。
> 
> Blöchl8 has further developed the US-PP concept by combining ideas from pseudopotential and LAPW ~linearized augmented-plane-wave! methods in a conceptually elegant framework, called the projector augmented-wave method ~PAW!. Blöchl通过将赝势法和线性缀加平面波 (LAPW) 方法的思想结合在一个概念优雅的框架中，进一步发展了超软赝势概念，称之为投影缀加波 (PAW) 方法。 **LAPW (linearized augmented-plane-wave)**: 线性缀加平面波方法，一种在原子球内使用径向函数、球外使用平面波的高精度全电子计算方法。
> 
> Although Blöchl has indicated in his work that similarities between ultrasoft PP’s and his method exist, no formal relationship was established. In the present work, we will derive this relationship, which shows that the only difference between Vanderbilt’s and Blöchl’s approach are simple one-center terms. We will also indicate a simple way to implement the PAW method in existing plane-wave codes that use norm-conserving or ultrasoft pseudopotentials. 尽管Blöchl在其工作中指出超软赝势与他的方法之间存在相似性，但尚未建立正式的关系。在本文中，我们将推导这一关系，结果表明Vanderbilt方法与Blöchl方法的唯一区别在于简单的单中心项。我们还将指出一种在现有的使用模守恒或超软赝势的平面波代码中实现PAW方法的简单方法。
> 
> Norm-conserving pseudopotentials were first introduced and used by Hamann, Schlüter, and Chiang.6 In their scheme, inside some core radius, the all-electron ~AE! wave function is replaced by a soft nodeless pseudo- ~PS! wave function, with the crucial restriction that the PS wave function must have the same norm as the all-electron wave function within the chosen core radius; outside the core radius the PS and AE wave function are identical. 模守恒赝势最早由Hamann, Schlüter和Chiang引入并使用。在他们的方案中，在某个芯半径之内，全电子 (AE) 波函数被一个平滑且无节点的赝 (PS) 波函数所取代，关键限制是PS波函数在选定芯半径内的模必须与全电子波函数相同；在芯半径之外，PS波函数与AE波函数完全一致。 **All-electron (AE)**: 全电子，指考虑所有电子（包括内层芯电子）的计算方式。 **Pseudo (PS)**: 赝，指经过平滑处理，去除了核附近剧烈震荡的波函数。
> 
> It is now well established that good transferability requires a core radius around the outermost maximum of the AE wave function, because only then the charge distribution and moments of the AE wave functions are well reproduced by the PS wave functions ~see, e.g., Ref. 9!. 目前已公认，良好的可转移性要求芯半径位于AE波函数的最外层最大值附近，因为只有这样，PS波函数才能很好地再现AE波函数的电荷分布和矩。 **Transferability**: 可转移性，指在参考原子中构建的赝势在不同的化学环境下依然保持准确的能力。
> 
> Therefore, for elements with strongly localized orbitals ~like first-row, 3d, and rare-earth elements! the resulting pseudopotentials require a large plane-wave basis set. To work around this, compromises are often made by increasing the core radius significantly beyond the outermost maximum in the AE wave function. But this is usually not a satisfactory solution because the transferability is always adversely affected when the core radius is increased, and for any new chemical environment, additional tests are required to establish the reliability of such soft PP’s. 因此，对于具有强局域轨道的元素（如第一行、3d和稀土元素），所得的赝势需要庞大的平面波基组。为了解决这个问题，人们通常通过将芯半径显著增加到AE波函数最外层最大值之外来做出妥协。但这通常不是一个令人满意的方案，因为增加芯半径总是会对可转移性产生不利影响，且对于任何新的化学环境，都需要额外的测试来建立此类软赝势的可靠性。
> 
> An elegant solution to this problem was proposed by Vanderbilt.7 In his method, the norm-conservation constraint is relaxed and to make up for the resulting charge deficit, localized atom-centered augmentation charges are introduced. Vanderbilt为该问题提出了一个优雅的解决方案。在他的方法中，模守恒限制被放宽，为了弥补由此产生的电荷缺失，引入了局域的以原子为中心的缀加电荷。 **Augmentation charges**: 缀加电荷，用于在超软赝势中补偿因放宽模守恒而丢失的电荷密度。
> 
> These augmentation charges are defined as the charge density difference between the AE and the PS wave function, but for convenience they are pseudized to allow an efficient treatment of the augmentation charges on a regular grid. The core radius of the pseudopotential can now be chosen around half the nearest-neighbor distance—independent of the position of the maximum of the AE wave function ~see Ref. 10!. Only for the augmentation charges a small cutoff radius must be used to restore the moments and the charge distribution of the AE wave function accurately ~for details see Ref. 11!. 这些缀加电荷被定义为AE波函数与PS波函数之间的电荷密度之差，但为了方便，它们经过了“赝化”处理，以便在常规网格上高效处理。现在，赝势的芯半径可以选择在最近邻距离的一半左右——与AE波函数最大值的位置无关。仅对于缀加电荷，必须使用一个较小的截止半径，以精确恢复AE波函数的矩和电荷分布。
> 
> The pseudized augmentation charges are usually treated on a regular grid in real space, which is not necessarily the same as the one used for the representation of the wave functions. The relation between the ultrasoft PP method and other plane-wave-based methods was discussed in detail by Singh.5 赝化后的缀加电荷通常在实空间常规网格上处理，该网格不一定与用于表示波函数的网格相同。Singh详细讨论了超软赝势法与其他基于平面波的方法之间的关系。
> 
> Vanderbilt’s approach is now adopted quite widely,11–19 and especially for the 3d transition-metals savings in the computer and improvements in the accuracy can be significant.20 Vanderbilt的方法目前被广泛采用，特别是对于3d过渡金属，可以显著节省计算资源并提高精度。
> 
> But the success of the method is partly hampered by the rather difficult construction of the pseudopotentials, i.e., too many parameters ~several cutoff radii! must be chosen and therefore extensive tests are required in order to obtain an accurate and highly transferable PP. 但该方法的成功在一定程度上受到了赝势构建较为困难的阻碍，即必须选择过多参数（多个截止半径），因此需要进行大量测试才能获得准确且高可转移性的赝势。
> 
> Some of these disadvantages are avoided in Blöchl’s PAW method. Blöchl introduces a linear transformation from the PS to the AE wave function and derives the PAW total energy functional in a consistent manner applying this transformation to the KS functional. 在Blöchl的PAW方法中，这些缺点中的一部分得到了避免。Blöchl引入了一个从PS波函数到AE波函数的线性变换，并通过将此变换应用于KS泛函，以一致的方式推导出了PAW总能量泛函。
> 
> The construction of PAW datasets is easier because the pseudization of the augmentation charges is avoided, i.e., the PAW method works directly with the full AE wave functions and AE potentials. This is achieved using radial support grids around each atom instead of regular grids. PAW数据集的构建更为简单，因为避免了缀加电荷的赝化，也就是说，PAW方法直接使用完整的AE波函数和AE势。这是通过在每个原子周围使用径向支撑网格而非常规网格来实现的。 **Radial support grids**: 径向支撑网格，一种沿径向分布的网格，用于精确描述原子核附近的剧烈变化。
> 
> The decomposition into radial grid and regular grid is complete, insofar that no cross term between the grids must be evaluated. 径向网格与常规网格的分解是完整的，这意味着无需计算网格之间的交叉项。
> 
> Despite these advantages, the method is not yet used very often, and in addition to Blöchl’s own implementation of the method we are aware of only one second program supporting the PAW method.21,22 尽管有这些优点，该方法尚未被频繁使用，除了Blöchl自己的实现之外，我们仅知道另一个支持PAW方法的程序。
> 
> This is partly due to the fact that the PAW approach was introduced a few years after Vanderbilt’s method, but another reason is that—apart from its formal elegance—it was not obvious at the time that the PAW method has significant advantages over other frozen core approaches like the US-PP approach. 这部分原因是PAW方法在Vanderbilt方法之后几年才被引入，另一个原因是——除了形式上的优雅之外——当时人们并不明显地意识到PAW方法比其他冻结芯方法（如超软赝势法）具有显著优势。 **Frozen core approximation**: 冻结芯近似，假设内层电子在化学环境下不发生变化，从而简化计算。
> 
> There are also some aspects in Blöchl’s work that deviate so significantly from conventional pseudopotential methods, that the implementation and testing of the method seems to be fairly difficult. Blöchl的工作中还有一些方面与传统的赝势法偏差较大，导致该方法的实现和测试显得相当困难。
> 
> In this work, we will rewrite the PAW total energy functional so that it resembles more closely the usual expressions used in pseudopotential programs and we will establish the exact formal relationship between both the US-PP and the PAW method. 在本文中，我们将重写PAW总能量泛函，使其更接近于赝势程序中常用的表达式，并建立超软赝势法与PAW方法之间的精确正式关系。
> 
> Our results show that only very few additional terms must be evaluated in order to implement the PAW method in programs supporting US-PP’s. 我们的结果表明，为了在支持超软赝势的程序中实现PAW方法，仅需计算极少数的额外项。
>

The Hamilton operator and the forces for the modified PAW functional are obtained in Sec. III, and in Sec. IV the construction of our PAW datasets is discussed. Several critical tests for dimers, small molecules, and bulk systems ~including magnetic Fe, Co, and Ni! are presented in Sec. V. Discussions and conclusions are at the end. 本文结构如下：第二节推导重排后的PAW总能量泛函。随后我们建立PAW与超软赝势法之间的正式关系（II F节）。第三节获得修改后PAW泛函的哈密顿算符和力。第四节讨论PAW数据集的构建。第五节给出针对二聚体、小分子和块体系统（包括磁性Fe, Co, Ni）的若干关键测试。讨论与结论在文末。

> As a first step, we derive a modified form of the PAW total energy functional. We do that in order to obtain a functional that resembles closely the functional for US-PP. 首先，我们推导PAW总能量泛函的一种修改形式。这样做是为了获得一个与超软赝势泛函高度相似的泛函。

> The exact Kohn-Sham density functional is as usually given by 精确的Kohn-Sham密度泛函通常给为： $$E = \sum_{n} f_n \langle \psi_n | -\frac{1}{2} \nabla^2 | \psi_n \rangle + E_H[n + n_Z] + E_{xc}[n]. \quad (1)$$ $E_H[n + n_Z]$ is the Hartree energy of the electronic charge density $n$ and the point charge densities of the nuclei $n_Z$, $E_{xc}[n]$ is the electronic exchange-correlation energy, and $f_n$ are orbital occupation numbers. $E_H[n + n_Z]$ 是电子电荷密度 $n$ 与原子核点电荷密度 $n_Z$ 的Hartree能，$E_{xc}[n]$ 是电子交换相关能，$f_n$ 为轨道的占据数。

> We will first give a brief summary of the basics of the PAW method ~see Ref. 8; in general we also adopt the notation of Ref. 8!. 我们首先简要总结PAW方法的基础知识（参见文献8；通常我们也采用文献8的符号）。

> In the PAW method, the AE wave function $\psi_n$ is derived from the PS wave function $\tilde{\psi}_n$ by means of a linear transformation:8 在PAW方法中，AE波函数 $\psi_n$ 通过线性变换由PS波函数 $\tilde{\psi}_n$ 导出： $$\langle \psi_n \rangle = \langle \tilde{\psi}_n \rangle + \sum_{i,j} \langle \tilde{\psi}_n | \tilde{p}_i \rangle ( | \phi_i \rangle - | \tilde{\phi}_i \rangle ) \langle \tilde{p}_j | \tilde{\psi}_n \rangle. \quad (2)$$ The PS wave functions $\tilde{\psi}_n$ are the variational quantities. They are defined as @Eq. (16) of Ref. 8# 原子原位的电荷密度 $n_i$ 和 $\tilde{n}_i$ 在一个延伸至每个离子周围 $r_{rad}$ 的径向支撑网格上处理。它们的定义为（文献8方程16）： $$n_i(r) = \sum_{j} r_{ij} \langle \phi_i | r \rangle \langle r | \phi_j \rangle, \quad (5)$$ and @Eq. > The crucial step is the introduction of a compensation charge $\hat{n}$, which is added to the soft charge densities $\tilde{n}_1 + \tilde{n}_{Zc}$ and $\tilde{n}_{11} + \tilde{n}_{Zc}$ in order to reproduce the correct multipole moments of the AE charge density $n_1 + n_{Zc}$ that is located in each augmentation region. 关键步骤是引入补偿电荷 $\hat{n}$，将其添加到平滑电荷密度 $\tilde{n}_1 + \tilde{n}_{Zc}$ 和 $\tilde{n}_{11} + \tilde{n}_{Zc}$ 中，以再现位于每个缀加区域内的AE电荷密度 $n_1 + n_{Zc}$ 的正确多极矩。 **Multipole moments**: 多极矩，描述电荷分布形状的量（单极矩、偶极矩、四极矩等）。
> 
> Because $n_{Zc}$ and $\tilde{n}_{Zc}$ have exactly the same monopole ($Z_{ion}$) and vanishing multipoles, the compensation charge $\hat{n}$ must be chosen so that $\tilde{n}_1 + \hat{n}$ has the same moments as the AE valence charge density $n_1$ within each sphere ~this definition is somewhat different from that of Blöchl!. 由于 $n_{Zc}$ 和 $\tilde{n}_{Zc}$ 具有完全相同的单极矩 ($Z_{ion}$) 且多极矩为零，因此必须选择补偿电荷 $\hat{n}$，使得 $\tilde{n}_1 + \hat{n}$ 在每个球体内具有与AE价电荷密度 $n_1$ 相同的矩（此定义与Blöchl的略有不同）。
> 
> We will come back to our particular choice for the compensation charge in Sec. II E. We also want to point out that similar schemes relying on soft compensation charges are used for instance in the LAPW method to handle the long-range electrostatic effects.25 我们将在II E节讨论补偿电荷的具体选择。我们还想指出，依赖平滑补偿电荷的类似方案也被用于例如LAPW方法中，以处理长程静电效应。
> 
> Even without complicated algebra it is easy to anticipate that $\tilde{n}_T$ describes the electrostatic interaction between different augmentation spheres and between the augmentation and the interstitial regions exactly. 即使不需要复杂的代数运算，也可以预见到 $\tilde{n}_T$ 精确地描述了不同缀加球之间以及缀加区域与 interstitial 区域之间的静电相互作用。 **Interstitial regions**: 间隙区域，指原子缀加球之外的空间。
> 
> Only for on-site terms ~i.e., terms within one sphere! errors are introduced that must be corrected. We show this briefly here: for the Hartree energy one obtains 仅对于原位项（即单个球体内部的项）会引入必须修正的误差。我们在此简要展示：对于Hartree能，可得： $$\frac{1}{2} \langle n_T | n_T \rangle = \frac{1}{2} \langle \tilde{n}_T | \tilde{n}_T \rangle + \langle n_T - \tilde{n}_T | \tilde{n}_T \rangle + \frac{1}{2} \langle n_T - \tilde{n}_T | n_T - \tilde{n}_T \rangle, \quad (11)$$ where we have used a shorthand for the electrostatic energy between two charge distributions $a(r)$ and $b(r)$: 这里我们对两个电荷分布 $a(r)$ 和 $b(r)$ 之间的静电能使用了简写： $$\langle a | b \rangle = \int dr dr' \frac{a(r)b(r')}{|r-r'|}. \quad (12)$$ Because $(n_T - \tilde{n}_T)$ has by construction of $\hat{n}$ vanishing moments within each individual sphere and is zero in the interstitial region the second and the third terms contribute only within each augmentation sphere. 由于 $\hat{n}$ 的构建，$(n_T - \tilde{n}_T)$ 在每个独立球体内具有消失的矩，且在间隙区域为零，因此第二项和第三项仅在每个缀加球内有贡献。
> 
> However, the second term in Eq. (11) is problematic because it involves quantities defined on the plane-wave grid $\tilde{n}_T$ and terms only calculated on the radial grid $(n_{1T} - \tilde{n}_{1T})$. To simplify the calculations Blochl8 made one crucial approximation that we adopt here too: it is convenient to replace $\tilde{n}_T$ in the second term by $\tilde{n}_{1T}$, i.e., the pseudo-charge-density is replaced by its on-site approximation. 然而，方程(11)中的第二项存在问题，因为它涉及定义在平面波网格上的量 $\tilde{n}_T$ 和仅在径向网格上计算的项 $(n_{1T} - \tilde{n}_{1T})$。为了简化计算，Blöchl做了一个关键近似，我们在这里也采用了：将第二项中的 $\tilde{n}_T$ 替换为 $\tilde{n}_{1T}$，即用原位近似替换赝电荷密度。
> 
> This is only exact for a complete set of projectors, and introduces an error in other cases, which is evidently given by 这仅在投影函数集完整时是精确的，在其他情况下会引入误差，该误差显然由下式给出： $$\langle n_{1T} - \tilde{n}_{1T} | \tilde{n}_T - \tilde{n}_{1T} \rangle. \quad (13)$$ This error is discussed in Sec. VII C @Eq. (108)# of Ref. 8. Using this approximation one obtains for the Hartree energy the simple form 该误差在文献8的VII C节（方程108）中讨论。使用该近似，Hartree能可得简单形式： $$\frac{1}{2} \langle \tilde{n}_T | \tilde{n}_T \rangle - \frac{1}{2} \langle \tilde{n}_{1T} | \tilde{n}_{1T} \rangle + \frac{1}{2} \langle n_{1T} | n_{1T} \rangle. \quad (14)$$ The notation $\langle a | b \rangle$ means that the corresponding terms must be evaluated on the radial grid within each augmentation region. 符号 $\langle a | b \rangle$ 表示相应的项必须在每个缀加区域内的径向网格上计算。
> 
> The final equation is convenient because there are no interaction terms between the radial support grid and the plane-wave part, but it should be kept in mind that for an incomplete set of projectors an approximation was made in order to obtain this decomposition @see Eq. (13)#. 最终方程非常方便，因为径向支撑网格与平面波部分之间没有相互作用项，但应记住，对于不完整的投影集，为了获得此分解而做了一个近似（见方程13）。
> 
> Up to now our derivation was generally similar to Ref. 8, but in the remaining lines we reformulate the three terms of Eq. (14) so that they resemble closely the usual expressions used in pseudopotential codes. Inserting the definition of $\tilde{n}_T$ and $\tilde{n}_{1T}$ @see Eq. (10)# into Eq. (14) one obtains for the first term: 到目前为止，我们的推导总体上与文献8相似，但在接下来的内容中，我们将重新表述方程(14)的三项，使其与赝势代码中常用的表达式更加相似。将 $\tilde{n}_T$ 和 $\tilde{n}_{1T}$ 的定义（见方程10）代入方程(14)，第一项得： $$\frac{1}{2} \langle \tilde{n}_1 + \hat{n} | \tilde{n}_1 + \hat{n} \rangle + \langle \tilde{n}_{Zc} | \tilde{n}_1 + \hat{n} \rangle + \frac{1}{2} \langle \tilde{n}_{Zc} | \tilde{n}_{Zc} \rangle = \frac{1}{2} \langle \tilde{n}_1 + \hat{n} | \tilde{n}_1 + \hat{n} \rangle + \langle \tilde{n}_{Zc} | \tilde{n}_1 + \hat{n} \rangle + \frac{1}{2} \langle \tilde{n}_{Zc} | \tilde{n}_{Zc} \rangle - U(R, Z_{ion}), \quad (15)$$ and the second term becomes 第二项变为： $$-\frac{1}{2} \langle \tilde{n}_1 + \hat{n} | \tilde{n}_1 + \hat{n} \rangle - \langle \tilde{n}_{Zc} | \tilde{n}_1 + \hat{n} \rangle - \frac{1}{2} \langle \tilde{n}_{Zc} | \tilde{n}_{Zc} \rangle. \quad (16)$$ In Eq. (15), the first term describes the electrostatic interactions between the valence electrons on the regular grid, the second one that between the frozen pseudocore and the valence electrons, and the final one the interaction between the frozen cores. 在方程(15)中，第一项描述了常规网格上价电子之间的静电相互作用，第二项描述了冻结赝芯与价电子之间的相互作用，最后一项描述了冻结芯之间的相互作用。
> 
> $U(R, Z_{ion})$ is the electrostatic energy of point charges $Z_{ion}$ in an uniform electrostatic background,26 and $\frac{1}{2} \langle \tilde{n}_{Zc} | \tilde{n}_{Zc} \rangle$ is the self-interaction energy of the pseudized core charge distribution, which cancels against the similar term in Eq. (16). $U(R, Z_{ion})$ 是均匀静电背景中点电荷 $Z_{ion}$ 的静电能，而 $\frac{1}{2} \langle \tilde{n}_{Zc} | \tilde{n}_{Zc} \rangle$ 是赝化芯电荷分布的自相互作用能，它与方程(16)中的类似项抵消。
> 
> In going from the first line in Eq. (15) to the second, we have made the assumption that the core charges do not overlap, which is not always the case. But because an overlap of the electronic cores also introduces errors in other places ~e.g., the kinetic energy!, which are usually neglected in the PAW method, it seems reasonable to neglect the corresponding terms here as well. 从方程(15)的第一行到第二行，我们假设芯电荷不重叠，但这并非总是成立。但由于电子芯的重叠也会在其他地方（如动能）引入误差，而这些误差在PAW方法中通常被忽略，因此在这里忽略相应的项似乎也是合理的。
> 
> Finally, the third term in Eq. (14) is rewritten in a similar way to the other two and we obtain 最后，方程(14)中的第三项以类似的方式重写，我们得到： $$\frac{1}{2} \langle n_1 | n_1 \rangle + \langle n_{Zc} | n_1 \rangle + \frac{1}{2} \langle n_{Zc} | n_{Zc} \rangle. \quad (17)$$ The Hartree energy is given by Eqs. (15), (16), and (17), but in the final equations for the total energy we will not include the core self-interaction term $\frac{1}{2} \langle n_{Zc} | n_{Zc} \rangle$ because it only defines the zero of energy. Hartree能由方程(15), (16)和(17)给出，但在总能量的最终方程中，我们不包含芯自相互作用项 $\frac{1}{2} \langle n_{Zc} | n_{Zc} \rangle$，因为它仅定义能量的零点。
> 
> ### C. Exchange-correlation energy
> 
> ### C. 交换相关能
> 
> To decompose the exchange-correlation energy we insert the following charge density into the exchange-correlation functional: 为了分解交换相关能，我们将以下电荷密度代入交换相关泛函： $$n_c + n = (\tilde{n} + \hat{n} + \tilde{n}_c) + (n_1 + n_c) - (\tilde{n}_1 + \hat{n} + \tilde{n}_c). \quad (18)$$ This expression differs from Ref. 8, where $n_c + n = \tilde{n} + (n_1 + n_c) - \tilde{n}_1$ was used. The reason for our choice is discussed below. 该表达式与文献8不同，文献8中使用的是 $n_c + n = \tilde{n} + (n_1 + n_c) - \tilde{n}_1$。我们这样选择的原因在下文讨论。
> 
> We want to stress that the partial electronic core charge $\tilde{n}_c$ is entirely distinct from the pseudized core charge $\tilde{n}_{Zc}$. The later must reproduce the exact monopole of $n_{Zc}$, whereas the former is constructed without this requirement. 我们要强调，部分电子芯电荷 $\tilde{n}_c$ 与赝化芯电荷 $\tilde{n}_{Zc}$ 完全不同。后者必须再现 $n_{Zc}$ 的精确单极矩，而前者的构建则没有这个要求。
> 
> It is straightforward to obtain the final result, which for a local or semilocal exchange-correlation energy and a complete set of projectors is given by 很容易获得最终结果，对于局部或半局部交换相关能且投影集完整的情况，结果为： $$E_{xc}[\tilde{n} + \hat{n} + \tilde{n}_c] + \overline{E_{xc}[n_1 + n_c]} - \overline{E_{xc}[\tilde{n}_1 + \hat{n} + \tilde{n}_c]}, \quad (19)$$ where $\overline{E}$ means that the corresponding quantity is evaluated on the radial grid within the augmentation region. 其中 $\overline{E}$ 表示相应的量是在缀加区域内的径向网格上计算的。
> 
> If the set of partial waves is not complete, an error is introduced that is given by ~analogous to Sec. VII C of Ref. 8!: 如果分波集不完整，则会引入误差，其表达式为（类似于文献8的VII C节）： $$E_{xc}[\tilde{n} + \hat{n} + \tilde{n}_c + n_1 + n_c - (\tilde{n}_1 + \hat{n} + \tilde{n}_c)] - E_{xc}[\tilde{n} + \hat{n} + \tilde{n}_c] - \overline{E_{xc}[n_1 + n_c]} + \overline{E_{xc}[\tilde{n}_1 + \hat{n} + \tilde{n}_c]}.$$ This term vanishes for $\tilde{n} - \tilde{n}_1 = 0$. Expanding this term in orders of $\tilde{n} - \tilde{n}_1$ yields 当 $\tilde{n} - \tilde{n}_1 = 0$ 时，该项消失。将此项按 $\tilde{n} - \tilde{n}_1$ 的阶数展开得： $$\int_{V_r} [ v_{xc}[n_1 + n_c] - v_{xc}[\tilde{n}_1 + \hat{n} + \tilde{n}_c] ] (\tilde{n} - \tilde{n}_1) dr.$$ Without nonlinear core corrections, the first term in the integral would be $(v_{xc}[n_1 + n_c] - v_{xc}[\tilde{n}_1])$. 如果没有非线性芯修正，积分中的第一项将是 $(v_{xc}[n_1 + n_c] - v_{xc}[\tilde{n}_1])$。
> 
> For our PAW datasets ~see Sec. IV! $\tilde{n}_1 + \hat{n} + \tilde{n}_c$ is very similar to $n_1 + n_c$ over an appreciable region of the augmentation sphere, which reduces errors due to the incompleteness of the partial waves in comparison to Blöchl’s approach. 对于我们的PAW数据集（见IV节），$\tilde{n}_1 + \hat{n} + \tilde{n}_c$ 在缀加球的一个相当大的区域内与 $n_1 + n_c$ 非常相似，与Blöchl的方法相比，这减少了由于分波不完整而产生的误差。
> 
> The improvement is particularly large for systems in which the core states extend towards the boundary of the augmentation sphere. An example for the improved behavior is given in Sec. V B. 对于芯态延伸至缀加球边界的系统，这种改进尤为显著。改进行为的一个例子见V B节。
> 
> In addition, we also found that the introduction of the partial core charge density $\tilde{n}_c$ reduces numerical instabilities due to gradient corrected functionals in the vicinity of the core. 此外，我们还发现引入部分芯电荷密度 $\tilde{n}_c$ 减少了在芯附近由于梯度修正泛函引起的数值不稳定。
> 
> ### D. Final expression for the total energy
> 
> ### D. 总能量最终表达式
> 
> The final expression of the total energy is most conveniently split up into three terms ~similar to Ref. 8!, 总能量的最终表达式最方便地分为三项（类似于文献8）： $$E = \tilde{E} + E_1 - \tilde{E}_1, \quad (20)$$ which are given by 具体如下： $$\tilde{E} = \sum_n f_n \langle \tilde{\psi}_n | -\frac{1}{2} \nabla^2 | \tilde{\psi}_n \rangle + E_{xc}[\tilde{n} + \hat{n} + \tilde{n}_c] + E_H[\tilde{n} + \hat{n}] + \int v_H[\tilde{n}_{Zc}] (\tilde{n}(r) + \hat{n}(r)) dr + U(R, Z_{ion}), \quad (21)$$ $$\tilde{E}_1 = \sum_{i,j} r_{ij} [ \langle \tilde{\phi}_i | -\frac{1}{2} \nabla^2 | \tilde{\phi}_j \rangle + \overline{E_{xc}[\tilde{n}_1 + \hat{n} + \tilde{n}_c]} + \overline{E_H[\tilde{n}_1 + \hat{n}]} + \int_{V_r} v_H[\tilde{n}_{Zc}] (\tilde{n}_1(r) + \hat{n}(r)) dr ], \quad (22)$$ $$E_1 = \sum_{i,j} r_{ij} [ \langle \phi_i | -\frac{1}{2} \nabla^2 | \phi_j \rangle + \overline{E_{xc}[n_1 + n_c]} + \overline{E_H[n_1]} + \int_{V_r} v_H[n_{Zc}] n_1(r) dr ]. \quad (23)$$ $v_H$ is the electrostatic potential of the charge density $n$: $v_H$ 是电荷密度 $n$ 的静电势： $$v_H[n](r) = \int \frac{n(r')}{|r-r'|} dr',$$ and $E_H[n]$ is its electrostatic energy, which is given by 而 $E_H[n]$ 是其静电能，给定为： $$E_H[n] = \frac{1}{2} \langle n | n \rangle = \frac{1}{2} \int dr \int dr' \frac{n(r)n(r')}{|r-r'|}.$$ The expression $\tilde{E}$ is evaluated on a regular grid, whereas $\tilde{E}_1$ and $E_1$ are calculated for each sphere individually on a radial support grid. 表达式 $\tilde{E}$ 在常规网格上计算，而 $\tilde{E}_1$ 和 $E_1$ 则在径向支撑网格上为每个球体单独计算。
> 
> A few remarks are required here to elucidate again the differences between Blöchl’s and our work. The treatment of the kinetic energy is equivalent, the Hartree energy is treated in a similar way, with the only difference that we neglect core-core overlap in these terms. 这里需要几点说明，以再次阐明Blöchl工作与我们工作的区别。动能的处理是等价的，Hartree能的处理方式相似，唯一的区别是我们忽略了这些项中的芯-芯重叠。
> 
> An important ~formal! difference concerns the construction of the compensation charge. In Ref. 8, the compensation charge has the same multipoles as $n_1 - \tilde{n}_1 + n_{Zc}$, whereas in our case it is chosen so that it reproduces the multipoles of $n_1 - \tilde{n}_1$. 一个重要的（形式上的）区别涉及补偿电荷的构建。在文献8中，补偿电荷与 $n_1 - \tilde{n}_1 + n_{Zc}$ 具有相同的多极矩，而我们的情况是选择其再现 $n_1 - \tilde{n}_1$ 的多极矩。
> 
> In the former case, the electrostatic interactions between the cores are automatically included in $E_H[\tilde{n} + \hat{n}]$, whereas in our case the interaction between the cores must be evaluated explicitly using an Ewald summation @$U(R, Z_{ion})$ in Eq. (21)#. 在前一种情况下，芯之间的静电相互作用自动包含在 $E_H[\tilde{n} + \hat{n}]$ 中；而我们的情况中，芯之间的相互作用必须使用 Ewald 求和显式评估（方程21中的 $U(R, Z_{ion})$）。 **Ewald summation**: Ewald求和，一种计算周期性系统中长程静电相互作用的数值算法。
> 
> At first sight our expressions also do not include a term similar to $\bar{v}$ in Ref. 8, but a second thought makes clear that $v_H[\tilde{n}_{Zc}]$ accounts for this term because the shape of $v_H[\tilde{n}_{Zc}]$ is entirely free within the augmentation sphere. 乍一看，我们的表达式也不包含类似于文献8中 $\bar{v}$ 的项，但仔细思考就会发现 $v_H[\tilde{n}_{Zc}]$ 承担了这一项，因为 $v_H[\tilde{n}_{Zc}]$ 在缀加球内的形状是完全自由的。
> 
> Significant differences occur for the exchange-correlation energy. In our case, on the regular grid, the exchange-correlation energy is evaluated for the charge density $\tilde{n} + \hat{n}$ including nonlinear core corrections $\tilde{n}_c$, whereas Blöchl uses only $\tilde{n}$ to evaluate the exchange-correlation energy. 交换相关能存在显著差异。在我们的方案中，常规网格上的交换相关能是针对包含非线性芯修正 $\tilde{n}_c$ 的电荷密度 $\tilde{n} + \hat{n}$ 计算的，而Blöchl仅使用 $\tilde{n}$ 来计算。
> 
> As we have already stressed, this makes only a difference for an incomplete set of projectors, which is in practice always adopted. In that case, we expect our treatment to be superior. 正如我们强调的，这仅在投影集不完整时产生差异，而在实践中通常采用的就是不完整集。在这种情况下，我们预计我们的处理方式更优。
> 
> Finally, we would like to compare our PAW total energy functional with that of Holzwarth et al.;21 but because this is a rather specialized topic we do this in the Appendix. 最后，我们想将我们的PAW总能量泛函与Holzwarth等人的进行对比，但由于这是一个相当专业的课题，我们在附录中进行。
> 
> Another interesting point is the comparison with ultrasoft pseudopotentials. It is obvious that $\tilde{E}$ is similar to the total energy functional of Ref. 7 if $\hat{n}$ is associated with the pseudized augmentation charges in the US-PP method. 另一个有趣的点是与超软赝势的对比。显然，如果将 $\hat{n}$ 与超软赝势法中的赝化缀加电荷关联，$\tilde{E}$ 就与文献7中的总能量泛函相似。
> 
> The only differences are evidently the two additional on-site terms $E_1$ and $\tilde{E}_1$, Eqs. (22) and (23). We will come back to this point in Sec. II F. 唯一的区别显然是方程(22)和(23)中的两个额外原位项 $E_1$ 和 $\tilde{E}_1$。我们将在II F节回到这一点。
> 
> ### E. The compensation charge
> 
> ### E. 补偿电荷
> 
> The compensation charges $\hat{n}$ must be chosen so that $\tilde{n}_1 + \hat{n}$ has exactly the same moments as the exact AE charge density $n_1$ within each augmentation sphere centered at the position $R$. This requires that 补偿电荷 $\hat{n}$ 必须选择使得 $\tilde{n}_1 + \hat{n}$ 在以位置 $R$ 为中心的每个缀加球内具有与精确AE电荷密度 $n_1$ 完全相同的矩。这要求： $$\int_{V_r} (n_1 - \tilde{n}_1 - \hat{n}) |r-R|^L Y_L^*(\hat{r-R}) dr = 0. \quad (24)$$ The charge density difference between the AE and PS partial wave for each channel $(i, j)$ within the augmentation region @see Eqs. (5) and (6)# is described by the functions 在缀加区域内，每个通道 $(i, j)$ 的AE和PS分波之间的电荷密度之差（见方程5和6）由以下函数描述： $$Q_{ij}(r) = \phi_i^_(r)\phi_j(r) - \tilde{\phi}_i^_(r)\tilde{\phi}_j(r), \quad (25)$$ and their moments $q_{iLj}$ are given by 其矩 $q_{iLj}$ 由下式给出： $$q_{iLj} = \int_{V_r} Q_{ij}(r) |r-R|^L Y_L^*(\hat{r-R}) dr, \quad (26)$$ where $L$ is a shorthand for $L=(l,m)$. 其中 $L$ 是 $L=(l,m)$ 的简写。
> 
> Only certain combinations of $L$ and $i=k_i l_i m_i, j=k_j l_j m_j$ based on the usual sum rules will give nonzero contributions: $m=m_i + m_j$ and $l=|l_i - l_j|, \dots, l_i + l_j$. 只有基于常规求和规则的 $L$ 与 $i=k_i l_i m_i, j=k_j l_j m_j$ 的特定组合才会给出非零贡献：$m=m_i + m_j$ 且 $l=|l_i - l_j|, \dots, l_i + l_j$。
> 
> The moments $q_{iLj}$ can be obtained easily using Clebsch-Gordan coefficients and a radial integration. A compensation charge that fulfills the requirement @Eq. (24)# can then be defined as a sum of one center terms 矩 $q_{iLj}$ 可以通过Clebsch-Gordan系数和径向积分轻松获得。满足方程(24)要求的补偿电荷可以定义为单中心项之和： $$\hat{n} = \sum_{i,j,L} r_{ij} \hat{Q}_{iLj}(r), \quad (27)$$ $$\hat{Q}_{iLj}(r) = q_{iLj} g_l(|r-R|) Y_L(\hat{r-R}),$$ where $g_l(r)$ are functions for which the moment $l$ is equal to 1. 其中 $g_l(r)$ 是使 $l$ 矩等于1的函数。
> 
> The actual functional form of $g_l(r)$ that we have adopted is discussed in Sec. IV A. 我们采用的 $g_l(r)$ 的实际函数形式在IV A节中讨论。
> 
> ### F. Ultrasoft pseudopotentials
> 
> ### F. 超软赝势
> 
> Before deriving the total energy functional for the US-PP method, we want to stress that the PAW method is an exact AE method for a complete set of partial waves. 在推导超软赝势法的总能量泛函之前，我们要强调，对于一组完整的分波，PAW方法是一种精确的AE方法。
> 
> Therefore, the method should yield results that are indistinguishable from any other frozen core AE method. In the US-PP method additional approximations are made. 因此，该方法产生的结果应与任何其他冻结芯AE方法无法区分。而在超软赝势法中，进行了额外的近似。
> 
> The equations for ultrasoft pseudopotentials can be obtained readily from the modified PAW total energy functional given in Eqs. (21)–(23) by linearization of Eqs. (22) and (23) around the atomic reference occupancies $r_{ia}$. 超软赝势的方程可以通过对方程(22)和(23)在原子参考占据数 $r_{ia}$ 附近进行线性化，从方程(21)-(23)给出的修改后PAW总能量泛函中轻松获得。
> 
> Let us denote the densities obtained with those occupancies as $n_a, \tilde{n}_a$, and $\hat{n}_a$. Linearization of the exchange correlation and Hartree term of $E_1$ @Eq. (23)# around $n_a$ yields 我们将使用这些占据数得到的密度记为 $n_a, \tilde{n}_a$ 和 $\hat{n}_a$。在 $n_a$ 附近对 $E_1$（方程23）的交换相关项和Hartree项进行线性化得： $$E_{xc}[n_a + n_c] + E_H[n_a] - \int [ v_{xc}[n_a + n_c] + v_H[n_a] ] (n_1(r) - n_a(r)) dr. \quad (28)$$ Using Eq. (5) for $n_1(r)$ we obtain for this expression 将方程(5)的 $n_1(r)$ 代入该表达式得： $$C + \sum_{i,j} r_{ij} \langle \phi_i | v_{xc}[n_a + n_c] + v_H[n_a] | \phi_j \rangle, \quad (29)$$ where $C$ is a constant. The other two terms—kinetic energy and electrostatic core-valence interaction—are already linear in $r_{ij}$ and if we combine them with Eq. (29) we obtain for $E_1$ up to first order in the occupancies $r_{ij}$: 其中 $C$ 是一个常数。另外两项——动能和静电芯-价相互作用——已经是 $r_{ij}$ 的线性项，如果将它们与方程(29)结合，我们得到 $E_1$ 关于占据数 $r_{ij}$ 的一阶近似： $$E_1 \approx C + \sum_{i,j} r_{ij} \langle \phi_i | -\frac{1}{2} \nabla^2 + v_{eff}^a | \phi_j \rangle, \quad (30)$$ with 其中 $$v_{eff}^a = v_H[n_a + n_{Zc}] + v_{xc}[n_a + n_c]. \quad (31)$$ The local potential $v_{eff}^a$ is just the AE potential in the reference atom. 局部势 $v_{eff}^a$ 正是参考原子中的AE势。
> 
> A similar linearization can be done for $\tilde{E}_1$, but care must be taken that both $\tilde{n}_1$ and $\hat{n}$ depend on the occupancies $r_{ij}$. The final result therefore contains two terms: 对于 $\tilde{E}_1$ 也可以进行类似的线性化，但必须注意 $\tilde{n}_1$ 和 $\hat{n}$ 都依赖于占据数 $r_{ij}$。因此，最终结果包含两项： $$\tilde{E}_1 \approx \tilde{C} + \sum_{i,j} r_{ij} [ \langle \tilde{\phi}_i | -\frac{1}{2} \nabla^2 + \tilde{v}_{eff}^a | \tilde{\phi}_j \rangle + \int \hat{Q}_{iLj}(r) \tilde{v}_{eff}^a(r) dr ], \quad (32)$$ with 其中 $$\tilde{v}_{eff}^a = v_H[\tilde{n}_a + \hat{n}_a + \tilde{n}_{Zc}] + v_{xc}[\tilde{n}_a + \hat{n}_a + \tilde{n}_c]. \quad (32)$$ $\tilde{v}_{eff}^a$ 是参考原子中的局部原子赝势。
> 
> Combining the two linearized expressions with $\tilde{E}$ gives the following total energy functional: 将这两个线性化表达式与 $\tilde{E}$ 结合，得到如下总能量泛函： $$E = \sum_n f_n \langle \tilde{\psi}_n | -\frac{1}{2} \nabla^2 | \tilde{\psi}_n \rangle + \sum_{i,j} \langle \tilde{\psi}_n | \tilde{p}_i \rangle G_{ij}^{US} \langle \tilde{p}_j | \tilde{\psi}_n \rangle + E_{xc}[\tilde{n} + \hat{n} + \tilde{n}_c] + E_H[\tilde{n} + \hat{n}] + \int v_H[\tilde{n}_{Zc}] (\tilde{n}(r) + \hat{n}(r)) dr + U(R, Z_{ion}), \quad (33)$$ with 其中 $$G_{ij}^{US} = \langle \phi_i | -\frac{1}{2} \nabla^2 + v_{eff}^a | \phi_j \rangle - \langle \tilde{\phi}_i | -\frac{1}{2} \nabla^2 + \tilde{v}_{eff}^a | \tilde{\phi}_j \rangle - \int \hat{Q}_{iLj}(r) \tilde{v}_{eff}^a(r) dr. \quad (34)$$ It is evident that the first equation is exactly the same as Eq. (1) in Ref. 11, if the compensation charge $\hat{n}$ is associated with the pseudized augmentation charge in the US-PP approach. 显然，如果补偿电荷 $\hat{n}$ 与超软赝势法中的赝化缀加电荷关联，第一个方程与文献11中的方程(1)完全相同。
> 
> It is also simple to show that the first two terms of $G_{ij}^{US}$ @Eq. (34)# are equivalent to the pseudopotential strength parameter $D_{ij}$ defined in Eq. (22) of Ref. 11. The last term in Eq. (34) corresponds to the usual unscreening. 同样可以简单证明方程(34)中 $G_{ij}^{US}$ 的前两项等同于文献11方程(22)中定义的赝势强度参数 $D_{ij}$。方程(34)的最后一项对应于常规的去屏蔽 (unscreening)。 **Unscreening**: 去屏蔽，在赝势构造中通过减去原子芯电荷的屏蔽作用以获得正确的离子势。
> 
> We turn now briefly back to the PAW functional. If the sum of the compensation charge and the pseudo-charge density $\tilde{n}_1 + \hat{n}$ is equivalent to the onsite AE charge density $n_1$, i.e., if $\hat{n} = n_1 - \tilde{n}_1$, and if $\tilde{n}_{Zc} = n_{Zc}$ and $\tilde{n}_c = n_c$, only the on-site kinetic-energy terms contribute to the total energy: 现在简要回到PAW泛函。如果补偿电荷与赝电荷密度之和 $\tilde{n}_1 + \hat{n}$ 等同于原位AE电荷密度 $n_1$（即 $\hat{n} = n_1 - \tilde{n}_1$），并且 $\tilde{n}_{Zc} = n_{Zc}$ 且 $\tilde{n}_c = n_c$，那么只有原位动能项对总能量有贡献： $$E_1 - \tilde{E}_1 = \sum_{i,j} r_{ij} [ \langle \phi_i | -\frac{1}{2} \nabla^2 | \phi_j \rangle - \langle \tilde{\phi}_i | -\frac{1}{2} \nabla^2 | \tilde{\phi}_j \rangle ].$$ It is immediately obvious that the US-PP method is strictly equivalent to the PAW method in this limiting case, and therefore both approaches are then exact within the frozen core approximation. 很明显，在这种极限情况下，超软赝势法与PAW方法严格等价，因此两种方法在冻结芯近似下都是精确的。
> 
> But in the US-PP method this would require one to use the functions $\hat{Q}_{iLj}(r) = Q_{ij}(r) = \phi_i^_(r)\phi_j(r) - \tilde{\phi}_i^_(r)\tilde{\phi}_j(r)$ in the calculation of the compensation charge on the plane wave grid @see Eq. (27)# and this is in practice not possible. 但在超软赝势法中，这要求在平面波网格计算补偿电荷时使用函数 $\hat{Q}_{iLj}(r) = Q_{ij}(r) = \phi_i^_(r)\phi_j(r) - \tilde{\phi}_i^_(r)\tilde{\phi}_j(r)$（见方程27），而这在实践中是不可能的。
> 
> However this derivation indicates that the accuracy of the US-PP can be improved systematically by increasing the accuracy of the pseudized augmentation function. In other words, if the AE-augmentation functions @Eq. (35)# are used for the augmentation, the US-PP approach is also an exact frozen core AE method. 然而，这一推导表明，可以通过提高赝化缀加函数的精度来系统地提高超软赝势的精度。换句话说，如果使用AE缀加函数（方程35）进行缀加，超软赝势法也是一种精确的冻结芯AE方法。
> 
> On the other hand, the derivation presented at the beginning of the section shows that even if the augmentation functions $\hat{Q}_{iLj}(r)$ reproduce just the correct moments of $Q_{ij}(r)$, the US-PP method is still exact up to first order for changes of the charge-density distribution with respect to the atomic reference system. This is probably part of the reason for the reliability of the US-PP method. 另一方面，本节开头给出的推导表明，即使缀加函数 $\hat{Q}_{iLj}(r)$ 仅再现 $Q_{ij}(r)$ 的正确矩，超软赝势法对于相对于原子参考系统的电荷密度分布的变化在第一阶上仍然是精确的。这可能是超软赝势法可靠性的部分原因。
> 
> However, in the US-PP method obviously, the transferability errors related to the pseudization of the augmentation charges can be large for systems with strong charge transfer ~polar or ionic binding!, changes of the atomic orbital occupations ~hybridization and promotion!, strong polarizations ~induction of dipole or quadrupole moments at certain atomic sites!, or large local magnetic moments. 然而，在超软赝势法中，对于具有强电荷转移（极性或离子键）、原子轨道占据数变化（杂化和跃迁）、强极化（在特定原子位点诱导偶极或四极矩）或大局域磁矩的系统，与缀加电荷赝化相关的可转移性误差可能会很大。
> 
> To obtain very accurate results even for these problematic cases the functions $\hat{Q}_{iLj}$ should approach the limiting case of Eq. (35). This is usually achieved by direct $l$-dependent truncation of the AE-augmentation functions $Q_{ij}(r)$ at some radius $r_{comp}$. 为了在这些问题案例中获得非常准确的结果，函数 $\hat{Q}_{iLj}$ 应接近方程(35)的极限情况。这通常通过在某个半径 $r_{comp}$ 处对AE缀加函数 $Q_{ij}(r)$ 进行直接的 $l$ 相关截断来实现。
> 
> An alternative used by Kresse and Hafner14 is to first construct high quality norm-conserving partial waves $\phi_{i}^{NC}(r)$: Kresse和Hafner使用的一种替代方法是先构建高质量的模守恒分波 $\phi_{i}^{NC}(r)$： $$\phi_{i}^{NC}(r) = \begin{cases} H \phi_i(r) & r < r_{comp}^l \ \phi_i(r) & r > r_{comp}^l \end{cases}, \quad (36)$$ and to define the functions $\hat{Q}_{iLj}$ as the charge density difference of the NC and US partial waves: 并将函数 $\hat{Q}_{iLj}$ 定义为模守恒 (NC) 分波与超软 (US) 分波的电荷密度之差： $$\hat{Q}_{iLj}(r) = \phi_{i}^{NC*}(r)\phi_{j}^{NC}(r) - \tilde{\phi}_i^*(r)\tilde{\phi}_j(r). \quad (37)$$ All US-PP’s of the present work have been constructed in that way. 本工作中的所有超软赝势均以此方式构建。
> 
> To summarize, in the PAW method the compensation charges can be rather extended, because the only requirement is that they restore the correct moment within the cutoff radius $r_{lc}$. The correct shape of the AE wave function is reconstructed only on the radial support grids. 总结来说，在PAW方法中，补偿电荷可以相当宽，因为唯一要求是它们在截止半径 $r_{lc}$ 内恢复正确的矩。AE波函数的正确形状仅在径向支撑网格上重构。
> 
> In the US-PP method, ‘‘accurate and hard’’ augmentation charges are desirable for reliable pseudopotentials. This usually requires that the AE augmentation functions are pseudized around the maximum of the AE wave functions, often resulting in rather contracted and localized compensation charges. 而在超软赝势法中，为了获得可靠的赝势，需要“准确且硬”的缀加电荷。这通常要求AE缀加函数在AE波函数的最大值附近进行赝化，往往导致相当收缩且局域的补偿电荷。
> 
> For instance, whereas for 3d transition-metal compounds half the nearest neighbor distance and thus the pseudization radius $r_{lc}$ of the partial waves ~and the extent of the compensation charges in the PAW method! is around 2 a.u., the maximum in the d wave function is located around 1 a.u. demanding very localized and computationally expensive compensation charges. 例如，对于3d过渡金属化合物，最近邻距离的一半（即分波的赝化半径 $r_{lc}$ 以及PAW方法中补偿电荷的范围）约为2 a.u.，而d波函数的最大值位于1 a.u. 附近，这要求非常局域且计算成本高昂的补偿电荷。
> 
> ## III. HAMILTON OPERATOR AND FORCES
> 
> ## III. 哈密顿算符与力
> 
> ### A. Overlap operator and orthonormality
> 
> ### A. 重叠算符与正交归一化
> 
> In the PAW approach, the pseudo-wave-functions $\tilde{\psi}_n$ must fulfill the following orthogonality condition: 在PAW方法中，赝波函数 $\tilde{\psi}_n$ 必须满足以下正交条件： $$\langle \tilde{\psi}_n | S | \tilde{\psi}_m \rangle = \delta_{nm}, \quad (38)$$ where the overlap operator is defined by 其中重叠算符定义为： $$S = 1 + \sum_{i,j} | \tilde{p}_i \rangle q_{ij} \langle \tilde{p}_j |, \quad (39)$$ and $q_{ij}$ is given by $q_{ij}$ 由下式给出： $$q_{ij} = \langle \phi_i | \phi_j \rangle - \langle \tilde{\phi}_i | \tilde{\phi}_j \rangle = \Delta p q_{ij}^0. \quad (40)$$
> 
> ### B. Hamilton operator
> 
> ### B. 哈密顿算符
> 
> To obtain the Hamilton operator for the modified PAW total energy functional the total energy must be varied with respect to the pseudodensity operator $\tilde{\rho} = \sum_n f_n | \tilde{\psi}_n \rangle \langle \tilde{\psi}_n |$ and one can formally write8,12 为了获得修改后PAW总能量泛函的哈密顿算符，总能量必须对赝密度算符 $\tilde{\rho} = \sum_n f_n | \tilde{\psi}_n \rangle \langle \tilde{\psi}_n |$ 求变分，形式上可写为： $$\frac{\delta E}{\delta \tilde{\rho}} = H. \quad (41)$$ The density operator $\tilde{\rho}$ enters in several ways—directly @like in the kinetic-energy term in Eq. (21)#, via the pseudo-charge-density $\tilde{n}$ or via the occupancies of each augmentation channel $r_{ij}$ defined in Eq. (7!). If we treat these three contributions separately, the variation of the total energy can formally be written as 密度算符 $\tilde{\rho}$ 以多种方式进入：直接（如方程21中的动能项）、通过赝电荷密度 $\tilde{n}$ 或通过方程(7)定义的每个缀加通道的占据数 $r_{ij}$。如果我们将这三种贡献分别处理，总能量的变分形式可写为： $$\frac{\delta E}{\delta \tilde{\rho}} = \frac{\delta \tilde{E}}{\delta \tilde{\rho}} + \frac{\delta E_1}{\delta \tilde{\rho}} - \frac{\delta \tilde{E}_1}{\delta \tilde{\rho}}. \quad (42)$$ We start with the term $\tilde{E}$ @Eq. (21)#. The partial derivative with respect to $\tilde{\rho}$ yields simply the kinetic-energy operator $-\frac{1}{2}\nabla^2$, and the variation with respect to $\tilde{n}(r)$ is the usual effective one-electron potential $\tilde{v}_{eff}(r)$: 我们从 $\tilde{E}$ 项（方程21）开始。对 $\tilde{\rho}$ 的偏导简单地给出动能算符 $-\frac{1}{2}\nabla^2$，而对 $\tilde{n}(r)$ 的变分是通常的有效单电子势 $\tilde{v}_{eff}(r)$： $$\tilde{v}_{eff} = v_H[\tilde{n} + \hat{n} + \tilde{n}_{Zc}] + v_{xc}[\tilde{n} + \hat{n} + \tilde{n}_c]. \quad (43)$$ In $\tilde{E}$, the occupancies $r_{ij}$ enter only via the compensation charge $\hat{n}$, and we obtain with Eq. (27): 在 $\tilde{E}$ 中，占据数 $r_{ij}$ 仅通过补偿电荷 $\hat{n}$ 进入，结合方程(27)得： $$\hat{D}_{ij} = \frac{\partial \tilde{E}}{\partial r_{ij}} = \int \frac{\delta \tilde{E}}{\delta \hat{n}(r)} \frac{\partial \hat{n}(r)}{\partial r_{ij}} dr = \sum_L \int \tilde{v}_{eff}(r) \hat{Q}_{iLj}(r) dr. \quad (44)$$ This term accounts for the fact that the pseudowave function $\tilde{\psi}_n$ does not have the same moments as the AE wave function $\psi_n$, and the term thus corrects the long-range electrostatic behavior of $\tilde{\psi}_n$. 该项考虑到赝波函数 $\tilde{\psi}_n$ 与AE波函数 $\psi_n$ 不具有相同的矩，因此该项修正了 $\tilde{\psi}_n$ 的长程静电行为。
> 
> In the remaining two energy terms $E_1$ and $\tilde{E}_1$ only expressions with $r_{ij}$ enter—either directly ~kinetic energy! or via $n_1, \tilde{n}_1$, or $\hat{n}$. Using Eq. (5), it is easy to show that the derivative of $E_1$ @Eq. (23)# with respect to occupancies $r_{ij}$ is given by 在剩余的两个能量项 $E_1$ 和 $\tilde{E}_1$ 中，仅有包含 $r_{ij}$ 的表达式进入——无论是直接（动能）还是通过 $n_1, \tilde{n}_1$ 或 $\hat{n}$。利用方程(5)，很容易证明 $E_1$（方程23）对占据数 $r_{ij}$ 的导数为： $$D_{ij} = \frac{\partial E_1}{\partial r_{ij}} = \langle \phi_i | -\frac{1}{2} \nabla^2 + v_{1eff} | \phi_j \rangle, \quad (45)$$ with 其中 $$v_{1eff}[n_1] = v_H[n_1 + n_{Zc}] + v_{xc}[n_1 + n_c]. \quad (45)$$ The result for $\tilde{E}_1$ is similar, and one obtains $\tilde{E}_1$ 的结果类似，可得： $$\tilde{D}_{1ij} = \frac{\partial \tilde{E}_1}{\partial r_{ij}} = \langle \tilde{\phi}_i | -\frac{1}{2} \nabla^2 + \tilde{v}_{eff1} | \tilde{\phi}_j \rangle + \sum_L \int_{V_r} \tilde{v}_{eff1}(r) \hat{Q}_{iLj}(r), \quad (46)$$ with 其中 $$\tilde{v}_{eff1}[\tilde{n}_1] = v_H[\tilde{n}_1 + \hat{n} + \tilde{n}_{Zc}] + v_{xc}[\tilde{n}_1 + \hat{n} + \tilde{n}_c]. \quad (46)$$ The first term in Eq. (46) derives from the variation with respect to $r_{ij}$ and $\tilde{n}_1$, and the second one from the variation with respect to $\hat{n}$. 方程(46)中的第一项源自对 $r_{ij}$ 和 $\tilde{n}_1$ 的变分，第二项源自对 $\hat{n}$ 的变分。
> 
> The terms $D_{ij}$ and $\tilde{D}_{1ij}$ are evaluated on the radial grid within each augmentation region: they are strictly onsite and restore the correct shape of the AE wave function within the spheres. 项 $D_{ij}$ 和 $\tilde{D}_{1ij}$ 在每个缀加区域内的径向网格上计算：它们严格是原位的，用于恢复球体内AE波函数的正确形状。
> 
> The final expression for the Hamilton operator is remarkably elegant and simple: 哈密顿算符的最终表达式非常优雅且简单： $$H(r, {R}) = -\frac{1}{2} \nabla^2 + \tilde{v}_{eff} + \sum_{i,j} | \tilde{p}_i \rangle ( \hat{D}_{ij} + D_{ij} - \tilde{D}_{1ij} ) \langle \tilde{p}_j |. \quad (47)$$ Although our expressions are very similar to those obtained by Blöchl in Ref. 8, the comparison is not quite straightforward. Part of the difficulties are due to the fact that we treat the exchange-correlation term slightly different, but Blöchl’s equations are also more complex because he introduces very soft compensation charges, which are allowed to overlap. 虽然我们的表达式与Blöchl在文献8中获得的非常相似，但对比并不直接。部分困难在于我们对交换相关项的处理略有不同，而且Blöchl的方程更复杂，因为他引入了允许重叠的极软补偿电荷。
> 
> To see the analogy between both expressions one has to rewrite all terms involving $v_{0R}(r)$ in Eqs. (37)–(40) of Ref. 8 into a closed expression. The result resembles $\hat{D}_{ij}$ and the second term in $\tilde{D}_{ij}$, two expressions that are on first sight not present in Blöchl’s equations. 为了看到两个表达式之间的类比，必须将文献8方程(37)-(40)中所有涉及 $v_{0R}(r)$ 的项重写为闭式表达式。结果类似于 $\hat{D}_{ij}$ 和 $\tilde{D}_{ij}$ 中的第二项，这两项乍一看在Blöchl的方程中并不存在。
> 
> It seems to us that our arrangement of terms is more symmetric and intuitively easier to understand: The first two terms ~kinetic-energy operator and effective one-electron potential! are usually present in the KS eigenvalue equation. 在我们看来，我们的项排列更加对称且直观易懂：前两项（动能算符和有效单电子势）通常出现在KS特征值方程中。
> 
> The term involving $\hat{D}_{ij}$, $\sum_{i,j,L} \langle \tilde{\psi}_n | \tilde{p}_i \rangle \langle \tilde{p}_j | \tilde{\psi}_n \rangle \int \tilde{v}_{eff}(r) \hat{Q}_{iLj}(r) dr$, describes the interaction of the compensation charge associated with one electron with the effective one-electron potential ~long-range electrostatic effects!. 涉及 $\hat{D}_{ij}$ 的项 $\sum_{i,j,L} \langle \tilde{\psi}_n | \tilde{p}_i \rangle \langle \tilde{p}_j | \tilde{\psi}_n \rangle \int \tilde{v}_{eff}(r) \hat{Q}_{iLj}(r) dr$ 描述了与单个电子相关的补偿电荷与有效单电子势之间的相互作用（长程静电效应）。
> 
> The remaining two terms are strictly onsite and account for the fact that the potential $\tilde{v}_{eff}$ and the pseudo-wave-function $\tilde{\psi}_n$ do not show the rapid variations usually present in the vicinity of the ionic cores. 剩余两项严格为原位项，考虑到势 $\tilde{v}_{eff}$ 和赝波函数 $\tilde{\psi}_n$ 没有在离子芯附近通常出现的剧烈变化。
> 
> The symmetry and mutual cancellation between $\tilde{D}_{1ij}$ @Eq. (46)# and the term $-\frac{1}{2} \nabla^2 + \tilde{v}_{eff}$ in the Hamilton operator is obvious ~in fact the cancellation is exact within the augmentation spheres for a complete set of partial waves!. $\tilde{D}_{1ij}$（方程46）与哈密顿算符中的 $-\frac{1}{2} \nabla^2 + \tilde{v}_{eff}$ 项之间的对称性和相互抵消是显而易见的（事实上，对于完整的分波集，这种抵消在缀加球内是精确的）。
> 
> The Hamilton operator for US-PP’s can be obtained from Eqs. (44)–(47) replacing the effective potentials in the augmentation region $v_{1eff}$ and $\tilde{v}_{1eff}$ by the respective atomic potentials $v_{eff}^a$ @Eq. (31)# and $\tilde{v}_{eff}^a$ @Eq. (32)#. This means that in the US-PP method the terms $D_{ij}$ and $\tilde{D}_{1ij}$ can be calculated once—for instance during the pseudopotential generation—and are kept constant during the calculation. 超软赝势的哈密顿算符可以通过将缀加区域中的有效势 $v_{1eff}$ 和 $\tilde{v}_{1eff}$ 替换为相应的原子势 $v_{eff}^a$（方程31）和 $\tilde{v}_{eff}^a$（方程32）从方程(44)-(47)获得。这意味着在超软赝势法中，项 $D_{ij}$ 和 $\tilde{D}_{1ij}$ 可以计算一次（例如在赝势生成期间），并在计算过程中保持不变。
> 
> ### C. Double counting corrections
> 
> ### C. 双重计数修正
> 
> In many band-structure codes, the total energy is evaluated as the sum of the Kohn-Sham eigenvalues minus double counting corrections. It is relatively straightforward to derive the required equations by rearranging the total energy functional. 在许多能带结构代码中，总能量被评估为Kohn-Sham特征值之和减去双重计数修正。通过重新排列总能量泛函，可以相对简单地推导出所需的方程。
> 
> The usual decomposition into three terms is also possible for the double counting corrections and one obtains 双重计数修正也可以分解为三项，可得： $$\tilde{E}_{dc} = 2 E_H[\tilde{n} + \hat{n}] + E_{xc}[\tilde{n} + \hat{n} + \tilde{n}_c] - \int v_{xc}[\tilde{n} + \hat{n} + \tilde{n}_c] (\tilde{n} + \hat{n}) dr, \quad (48)$$ $$E_{dc1} = 2 E_H[n_1] + E_{xc}[n_1 + n_c] - \int_{V_r} v_{xc}[n_1 + n_c] n_1 dr,$$ $$\tilde{E}_{dc1} = 2 E_H[\tilde{n}_1 + \hat{n}] + E_{xc}[\tilde{n}_1 + \hat{n} + \tilde{n}_c] - \int_{V_r} v_{xc}[\tilde{n}_1 + \hat{n} + \tilde{n}_c] (\tilde{n}_1 + \hat{n}) dr. \quad (48)$$ The total energy is then given by 总能量随后由下式给出： $$E = \sum_n f_n \langle \tilde{\psi}_n | H | \tilde{\psi}_n \rangle + \tilde{E}_{dc} + E_{dc1} - \tilde{E}_{dc1} + U(R, Z_{ion}). \quad (49)$$ In the US-PP method, $\tilde{E}_{d1c}$ and $E_{d1c}$ are constants and must be calculated only once during the pseudopotential generation. 在超软赝势法中，$\tilde{E}_{d1c}$ 和 $E_{d1c}$ 是常数，且必须在赝势生成期间仅计算一次。
> 
> ### D. Forces and stress tensor
> 
> ### D. 力与应力张量
> 
> The forces are usually defined as the total derivative of the energy with respect to the ionic positions 力通常定义为能量对离子位置的全导数： $$F = -\frac{dE}{dR}. \quad (50)$$ In the PAW method ~as in the US-PP method!, complications arise from the fact that the augmentation spheres and compensation charges are allowed to move with the ions, which gives rise to additional terms in comparison with standard plane-wave codes. 在PAW方法（以及超软赝势法）中，复杂之处在于缀加球和补偿电荷被允许随离子移动，这导致与标准平面波代码相比出现了额外的项。
> 
> These terms are sometimes called Pulay forces,27 although this is not quite correct because the plane-wave basis set is clearly independent of the atomic positions. 这些项有时被称为Pulay力，尽管这并不完全正确，因为平面波基组显然与原子位置无关。 **Pulay forces**: Pulay力，在基组随原子位置变化时，能量对位置求导产生的额外项。
> 
> Another difficulty in comparison to norm conserving pseudopotentials is caused by the overlap operator, whose position dependence @Eq. (39)# must be taken into account too. 与模守恒赝势相比，另一个困难是由重叠算符引起的，其对位置的依赖性（方程39）也必须考虑在内。
> 
> The forces can be derived in several ways, but here we want to keep the derivation as simple as possible. To do that we start from a force theorem first proven by Goedecker and Maschke,28 which states that the total derivative of the energy is given by29 力的推导有多种方法，但我们希望尽可能简化。为此，我们从Goedecker和Maschke首先证明的力定理开始，该定理指出能量的全导数为： $$\frac{dE}{dR} = \sum_n f_n \langle \tilde{\psi}_n | \frac{\partial H}{\partial R} - \epsilon_n \frac{\partial S}{\partial R} | \tilde{\psi}_n \rangle + \frac{\partial U(R, Z_{ion})}{\partial R}. \quad (51)$$ The second line describes the forces between the ionic cores, and we will not comment further on this term. $\epsilon_n$ are the Kohn-Sham eigenvalues, and we assumed that we have determined the ground-state wave functions so that the orthogonality constraint Eq. (38) and the KS equations $H | \tilde{\psi}_n \rangle = \epsilon_n S | \tilde{\psi}_n \rangle$ are fulfilled. 第二行描述了离子芯之间的力，我们将不再赘述此项。$\epsilon_n$ 是Kohn-Sham特征值，我们假设已确定基态波函数，使得正交约束方程(38)和KS方程 $H | \tilde{\psi}_n \rangle = \epsilon_n S | \tilde{\psi}_n \rangle$ 得到满足。
> 
> In Eq. (51), changes of the potentials $\tilde{v}_{eff}, \tilde{v}_{eff1}$, and $v_{eff1}$ in the Hamilton operator $H$ due to changes of one of the densities $\tilde{n}, n_1, \tilde{n}_1$, or $\hat{n}$ must not be calculated. 在方程(51)中，由于密度 $\tilde{n}, n_1, \tilde{n}_1$ 或 $\hat{n}$ 的变化而导致的哈密顿算符 $H$ 中势 $\tilde{v}_{eff}, \tilde{v}_{eff1}$ 和 $v_{eff1}$ 的变化无需计算。
> 
> The proof of this theorem is quite straightforward and based on the fact that the first-order energy change is given by the sum of the change of the Kohn-Sham eigenvalues: any change of the potentials due to changes of $\tilde{n}, n_1, \tilde{n}_1$, or $\hat{n}$ always cancel against the changes of the double counting corrections @see Eq. (48)#. 该定理的证明非常直接，基于这样一个事实：一阶能量变化由Kohn-Sham特征值的变化之和给出；由 $\tilde{n}, n_1, \tilde{n}_1$ 或 $\hat{n}$ 变化引起的任何势的变化总是与双重计数修正的变化相互抵消（见方程48）。
> 
> From Eq. (51) the forces are very simple to derive and we obtain three terms $F_1, F_2$, and $F_3$. The first contribution $F_1$ derives from the change of the local potential $\tilde{v}_{eff}$ if the ions are moved; the effective potential depends explicitly on the ionic positions only via $\tilde{n}_{Zc}$: 从方程(51)可以很容易地推导出力，我们得到三个项 $F_1, F_2$ 和 $F_3$。第一项 $F_1$ 源自离子移动时局部势 $\tilde{v}_{eff}$ 的变化；有效势仅通过 $\tilde{n}_{Zc}$ 显式依赖于离子位置： $$F_1 = -\int \frac{\delta \text{Tr}[H \tilde{\rho}]}{\delta v_H[\tilde{n}_{Zc}](r)} \frac{\partial v_H[\tilde{n}_{Zc}](r)}{\partial R} dr. \quad (52)$$ Two terms must be considered here, the first one derives from the change of $\tilde{v}_{eff}$ in Eq. (47), and the second from the change of $\tilde{v}_{eff}$ in $\hat{D}_{ij}$ @Eq. (44)#: 这里必须考虑两项，第一项源自方程(47)中 $\tilde{v}_{eff}$ 的变化，第二项源自 $\hat{D}_{ij}$（方程44）中 $\tilde{v}_{eff}$ 的变化： $$F_1 = -\int [ \tilde{n}(r) + \sum_{i,j,L} \hat{Q}_{iLj}(r) r_{ij} ] \frac{\partial v_H[\tilde{n}_{Zc}](r)}{\partial R} dr = -\int [ \tilde{n}(r) + \hat{n}(r) ] \frac{\partial v_H[\tilde{n}_{Zc}](r)}{\partial R} dr. \quad (53)$$ The second contribution to the forces arises from $\hat{D}_{ij}$ @Eq. (44)# due to changes of the compensation charges $\hat{n}$ if the ions are moved: 力的第二项贡献源自离子移动时 $\hat{D}_{ij}$（方程44）中补偿电荷 $\hat{n}$ 的变化： $$F_2 = -\sum_{i,j,L} \int \tilde{v}_{eff}(r) r_{ij} q_{iLj} \frac{\partial g_l(|r-R|) Y_L(\hat{r-R})}{\partial R} dr. \quad (54)$$ $F_1$ and $F_2$ together describe the forces deriving from electrostatic contributions. The final term is due to the change of the projectors $\tilde{p}_i$ in Eq. (47), and is given by $F_1$ 和 $F_2$ 共同描述了源自静电贡献的力。最后一项由方程(47)中投影函数 $\tilde{p}_i$ 的变化引起，给出为： $$F_3 = -\sum_{i,j} ( \hat{D}_{ij} + D_{ij} - \tilde{D}_{1ij} - \epsilon_n q_{ij} ) \sum_n f_n \langle \tilde{\psi}_n | \frac{\partial | \tilde{p}_i \rangle \langle \tilde{p}_j |}{\partial R} | \tilde{\psi}_n \rangle. \quad (55)$$ There is one additional term that is in fact not correctly treated by Eq. (51). It derives from the dependency of the exchange-correlation potential on the nonlinear core correction $\tilde{n}_c$ giving rise to one additional contribution that can be written as 还有一个项实际上没有被方程(51)正确处理。它源自交换相关势对非线性芯修正 $\tilde{n}_c$ 的依赖，导致一个额外的贡献，可写为： $$F_{nlcc} = -\int v_{xc}[\tilde{n} + \hat{n} + \tilde{n}_c] \frac{\partial \tilde{n}_c(r)}{\partial R} dr. \quad (56)$$ This term is most easily obtained directly from the total energy functional @Eq. (21)#. 这一项最容易直接从总能量泛函（方程21）中获得。
> 
> One thing that is remarkable is that the forces for the PAW method are almost identical to those for US-PP’s. All differences are actually automatically absorbed in the definition of $( \hat{D}_{ij} + D_{ij} - \tilde{D}_{1ij} )$ in Eq. (55). 值得注意的是，PAW方法的力与超软赝势法的几乎完全相同。所有差异实际上都自动被方程(55)中 $( \hat{D}_{ij} + D_{ij} - \tilde{D}_{1ij} )$ 的定义所吸收。
> 
> For US-PP’s the terms $D_{ij}$ and $\tilde{D}_{1ij}$ are constant and calculated once and forever, whereas they vary during the calculation of the electronic ground state for the PAW method. 对于超软赝势，项 $D_{ij}$ 和 $\tilde{D}_{1ij}$ 是常数且仅计算一次，而在PAW方法中，它们在电子基态计算过程中是变化的。
> 
> Realizing that the onsite terms only contribute in form of a change of the quantities $D_{ij} - \tilde{D}_{1ij}$ ~i.e., these quantities vary during the determination of the ground state, whereas they are constant for US-PP’s!, it is also easy to evaluate the stress tensor. We will neither give the full derivation nor the final results here, as the expressions are rather cumbersome and difficult to write in a compact form. But for further details we refer to Ref. 12, where the stress tensor has been derived for US-PP. The changes with respect to US-PP’s are trivial. 意识到原位项仅以 $D_{ij} - \tilde{D}_{1ij}$ 等量的变化形式做出贡献（即这些量在确定基态过程中变化，而在超软赝势中是常数），评估应力张量也变得很容易。我们在此不给出完整推导和最终结果，因为表达式相当冗长且难以紧凑写出。更多细节请参考文献12，其中推导了超软赝势的应力张量。相对于超软赝势的修改是平凡的。
> 
> ## IV. PAW DATASETS
> 
> ## IV. PAW数据集
> 
> ### A. General considerations
> 
> ### A. 综合考虑
> 
> To define a PAW data set the following quantities are required: ~i! the AE and PS partial waves $\phi_i$ and $\tilde{\phi}_i$, ~ii! the projector functions $\tilde{p}_i$, ~iii! the core-charge density $n_c$, the pseudized core charge density $\tilde{n}_{Zc}$ and the partial electronic core-charge density $\tilde{n}_c$ and ~iv! the compensation functions $g_l(r)$. Our particular choices for these functions are discussed in the following subsections. 定义一个PAW数据集需要以下量：（i）AE和PS分波 $\phi_i$ 和 $\tilde{\phi}_i$；（ii）投影函数 $\tilde{p}_i$；（iii）芯电荷密度 $n_c$，赝化芯电荷密度 $\tilde{n}_{Zc}$ 和部分电子芯电荷密度 $\tilde{n}_c$；（iv）补偿函数 $g_l(r)$。我们对这些函数的具体选择在以下小节中讨论。
> 
> ### B. Construction of the partial waves and projectors
> 
> ### B. 分波与投影函数的构建
> 
> The construction of the partial waves $\tilde{\phi}_i$ and projectors $\tilde{p}_i$ proceeds along the same lines as in Ref. 14. First an all-electron calculation is performed for a spherical reference atom. 分波 $\tilde{\phi}_i$ 和投影函数 $\tilde{p}_i$ 的构建与文献14一致。首先对球形参考原子进行全电子计算。
> 
> Then, for each angular quantum number $l=0 \dots 1$ ($l=0 \dots 2$ for heavy alkali, alkali earth, and d elements!), two reference energies are chosen and the AE partial waves $\phi_i(r)$ are calculated. One of the reference energies always coincidences with the atomic eigenenergy of a valence orbital. 然后，对于每个角量子数 $l=0 \dots 1$（对于重碱金属、碱土金属和d元素为 $l=0 \dots 2$），选择两个参考能量并计算AE分波 $\phi_i(r)$。其中一个参考能量总是与价轨道的原子特征能一致。
> 
> Finally smooth PS partial waves $\tilde{\phi}_i = \mathcal{L}_k(r) = Y_L(\hat{r-R}) \tilde{\phi}_{lk}(|r-R|)$ are constructed. 最后构建平滑的PS分波 $\tilde{\phi}_i = \mathcal{L}_k(r) = Y_L(\hat{r-R}) \tilde{\phi}_{lk}(|r-R|)$。
> 
> Loosely following Rappe, Rabe, Kaxiras, and Joannopoulos30 ~RRKJ! we expand the PS partial wave inside a cutoff radius $r_{lc}$ in terms of a linear combination of spherical Bessel functions 大致遵循RRKJ的方法，我们将截止半径 $r_{lc}$ 内的PS分波展开为球贝塞尔函数的线性组合： $$\tilde{\phi}_{lk}(r) = \begin{cases} \sum_i a_{il} j_l(q_i r) & r < r_{lc} \ \phi_{lk}(r) & r > r_{lc} \end{cases} \quad (57)$$ with the $a_i$ and $q_i$ chosen so that the PS partial wave $\phi_{lk}(r)$ is two times continuously differentiable. A set of two spherical Bessel functions $j_l(q_i r)$ is always sufficient to satisfy this condition. 其中 $a_i$ 和 $q_i$ 的选择使得PS分波 $\phi_{lk}(r)$ 是二阶连续可微的。一组两个球贝塞尔函数 $j_l(q_i r)$ 总是足以满足此条件。
> 
> The projectors $\tilde{p}_i$ can be obtained using either Vanderbilt’s scheme7 or Blöchl’s Gram-Schmidt inspired scheme.8 It is straightforward to show that both methods result in exactly the same PAW functional, but Blöchl’s scheme seems to be numerically better suited because it automatically avoids too large or too small numbers. 投影函数 $\tilde{p}_i$ 可以使用Vanderbilt的方案或Blöchl受Gram-Schmidt启发的方案获得。很容易证明两种方法产生完全相同的PAW泛函，但Blöchl的方案在数值上更合适，因为它能自动避免过大或过小的数值。
> 
> All PAW potentials and US-PP of the present work have been generated with two partial waves for the s and p orbitals. In terms of computer time, these potentials are slightly more expensive than potentials using only one projector, but potentials with two projectors are better transferable, easier to generate and generally speaking ‘‘saver’’ then those with one partial wave. 本工作中的所有PAW势和超软赝势均对s和p轨道使用了两个分波。在计算时间方面，这些势比仅使用一个投影函数的势略贵，但具有两个投影函数的势可转移性更好，更容易生成，且通常比只有一个分波的势更“安全”。
> 
> The use of two projectors also parallels the LAPW method where the radial wave functions at some reference energy and the energy derivatives of these wave functions are used for the expansion of the wave functions inside the augmentation spheres. 使用两个投影函数也与LAPW方法平行，在LAPW中，参考能量下的径向波函数及其能量导数被用于缀加球内波函数的展开。
> 
> The PAW and the US-PP are however somewhat less prone to linearization errors, because part of the properties of the potential are described by the local pseudopotential. In some cases and for small cutoff radii, it is even possible to use only one projector in US-PP and PAW method. For more details we refer to Ref. 10. 然而，PAW和超软赝势较少受到线性化误差的影响，因为势的部分性质由局部赝势描述。在某些情况和较小截止半径下，超软赝势和PAW方法甚至可以仅使用一个投影函数。更多细节见文献10。
> 
> ### C. The pseudized core charges and the local pseudopotential
> 
> ### C. 赝化芯电荷与局部赝势
> 
> The pseudized core charge $\tilde{n}_{Zc}$ enters only via the local ionic pseudopotential $v_H[\tilde{n}_{Zc}]$, and we prefer to construct the pseudopotential $v_H[\tilde{n}_{Zc}]$ directly instead of $\tilde{n}_{Zc}$. 赝化芯电荷 $\tilde{n}_{Zc}$ 仅通过局部离子赝势 $v_H[\tilde{n}_{Zc}]$ 进入，我们倾向于直接构建赝势 $v_H[\tilde{n}_{Zc}]$ 而非 $\tilde{n}_{Zc}$。
> 
> We have also already pointed out that the potential $v_H[\tilde{n}_{Zc}]$ is entirely free within the augmentation sphere, whereas outside the augmentation sphere it must be identical to the all-electron potential $v_H[n_{Zc}]$. 我们之前已经指出，势 $v_H[\tilde{n}_{Zc}]$ 在缀加球内是完全自由的，而在缀加球外必须与全电子势 $v_H[n_{Zc}]$ 完全一致。
> 
> To obtain the local ionic pseudopotential $v_H[\tilde{n}_{Zc}]$, a local atomic pseudopotential $\tilde{v}_{eff}^a$ is constructed first. The final ionic potential is obtained by unscreening the local atomic pseudopotential. This intermediate step is reminiscent of standard pseudopotential methods and it is convenient because it allows for a check on the scattering properties of the pseudoatom. 为了获得局部离子赝势 $v_H[\tilde{n}_{Zc}]$，首先构建一个局部原子赝势 $\tilde{v}_{eff}^a$。最终的离子势通过对局部原子赝势进行去屏蔽获得。这个中间步骤让人联想到标准的赝势方法，而且很方便，因为它允许检查赝原子的散射性质。
> 
> In fact, the local atomic PP $\tilde{v}_{eff}^a$ must describe the scattering properties for angular components that are not included in the set of partial waves. 实际上，局部原子赝势 $\tilde{v}_{eff}^a$ 必须描述未包含在分波集中的角分量的散射性质。
> 
> The most convenient choice for the local potential is often a ~norm-conserving! pseudopotential constructed for the first angular-momentum quantum number $l$, which is not included in the set of partial waves ~$l=2$ or $l=3$). But unfortunately, this approach is often problematic, for instance for transition metals, because the resulting local $f$-PP is sometimes so attractive that ghost states in the s or p components are difficult to avoid.31 局部势最方便的选择通常是为第一个未包含在分波集中的角动量量子数 $l$（如 $l=2$ 或 $l=3$）构建的（模守恒）赝势。但遗憾的是，这种方法经常有问题，例如对于过渡金属，产生的局部$f$-赝势有时吸引力太强，导致很难避免s或p分量中的鬼态。 **Ghost states**: 鬼态，指在赝势构造过程中由于数学不稳定性而产生的不物理的虚假状态。
> 
> In that case, a convenient choice for the local potential is a ‘‘truncated’’ AE potential, in which we replace the exact ~self-consistent! atomic AE potential $v_{eff}^a$ inside a cutoff radius $r_{loc}$ by 在这种情况下，局部势的一个方便选择是“截断”的AE势，我们将截止半径 $r_{loc}$ 内的精确（自洽）原子AE势 $v_{eff}^a$ 替换为： $$\tilde{v}_{eff}^a = \frac{A \sin(q_{loc} r)}{r} \text{ for } r < r_{loc}, \quad (58)$$ where $q_{loc}$ and $A$ are chosen so that the first derivative of the potential is continuous. 其中 $q_{loc}$ 和 $A$ 的选择使得势的一阶导数连续。
> 
> The second quantity to be determined is the pseudized partial electronic core-charge density. We expand $\tilde{n}_c$ within a cutoff radius $r_{pc}$ in a set of two spherical Bessel functions $j_0$: 第二个需要确定的量是赝化部分电子芯电荷密度。我们将 $r_{pc}$ 截止半径内的 $\tilde{n}_c$ 展开为一组两个球贝塞尔函数 $j_0$： $$\tilde{n}_c = \sum_{i=1,2} B_i \frac{\sin(q_i r)}{r}. \quad (59)$$ Outside the partial core radius $r_{pc}$ the partial core-charge density $\tilde{n}_c$ is identical to the all-electron core charge $n_c$, and $q_i$ and $B_i$ are chosen so that the first two derivatives of the partial core-charge density are continuous. 在部分芯半径 $r_{pc}$ 之外，部分芯电荷密度 $\tilde{n}_c$ 与全电子芯电荷 $n_c$ 完全相同，$q_i$ 和 $B_i$ 的选择使得部分芯电荷密度的前两阶导数连续。
> 
> Finally, the local ionic pseudopotential is obtained by unscreening of $\tilde{v}_{eff}^a$: 最后，通过对 $\tilde{v}_{eff}^a$ 进行去屏蔽获得局部离子赝势： $$v_H[\tilde{n}_{Zc}] = \tilde{v}_{eff}^a - v_H[\tilde{n}_a + \hat{n}_a] - v_{xc}[\tilde{n}_a + \hat{n}_a + \tilde{n}_c]. \quad (60)$$ For the PAW data sets used in the present work, the radius of the radial support grids $r_{rad}$ is set to the largest core radius $r_c^l$. 对于本工作使用的PAW数据集，径向支撑网格的半径 $r_{rad}$ 设置为最大的芯半径 $r_c^l$。
> 
> The partial core radius is set to $r_{pc} \approx r_{rad} / 1.2$, and the local pseudopotential is adjusted so that the scattering properties of all angular-momentum components up to $l=4$ are described with high accuracy. 部分芯半径设置为 $r_{pc} \approx r_{rad} / 1.2$，且局部赝势经过调整，使得直到 $l=4$ 的所有角动量分量的散射性质都被高精度描述。
> 
> For s and p elements this was achieved by constructing a norm-conserving PP for the d electrons, whereas for transition metals ~and some alkali and alkali earth! elements the local PP was set to a truncated all electron potential with $r_{loc} \approx r_{rad} / 1.2$. 对于s和p元素，这是通过为d电子构建模守恒赝势实现的；而对于过渡金属（以及某些碱金属和碱土金属）元素，局部赝势被设置为 $r_{loc} \approx r_{rad} / 1.2$ 的截断全电子势。
> 
> ### D. Compensation charge, double grid technique
> 
> ### D. 补偿电荷与双网格技术
> 
> The final quantities we want to discuss are the compensation functions $g_l(r)$. We construct each $g_l(r)$ as a sum of two spherical Bessel functions 最后我们要讨论的量是补偿函数 $g_l(r)$。我们将每个 $g_l(r)$ 构建为两个球贝塞尔函数之和： $$g_l(r) = \sum_{i=1,2} a_{il} j_l(q_{il} r). \quad (61)$$ The coefficients $q_{il}$ and $a_{il}$ are chosen so that $g_l(r)$ and its first two derivatives are zero at a radius $r_{comp}$ and so that the moment $l$ is equal to 1: 系数 $q_{il}$ 和 $a_{il}$ 的选择使得 $g_l(r)$ 及其前两阶导数在半径 $r_{comp}$ 处为零，并且 $l$ 矩等于1： $$\int_0^{r_{comp}} g_l(r) r^{l+2} dr = 1. \quad (62)$$ This can be accomplished by choosing $q_{il}$ so that 这可以通过选择 $q_{il}$ 使得 $$\frac{d}{dr} j_l(q_{il} r) |_{r=r_{comp}} = 0, \quad (63)$$ and setting $a_{il}$ so that the equation $g_l(r_{comp}) = 0$ and Eq. (62) are fulfilled. 并设置 $a_{il}$ 以满足 $g_l(r_{comp}) = 0$ 和方程(62)。
> 
> In our present work, the radius $r_{comp}$ is usually a factor 1.2–1.3 smaller than the radius of the radial integration sphere $r_{rad}$. 在目前的工作中，半径 $r_{comp}$ 通常比径向积分球半径 $r_{rad}$ 小 1.2-1.3 倍。
> 
> We have chosen this value because due to the continuity condition the AE and PS charge density agree almost perfectly up to this point. A less contracted compensation charge could therefore spoil the transferability if the core regions are allowed to overlap. 我们选择这个值是因为根据连续性条件，AE和PS电荷密度到这一点为止几乎完全一致。如果允许芯区域重叠，较不收缩的补偿电荷可能会破坏可转移性。
> 
> The particular form of the compensation functions @Eq. (61)# was chosen because it results in augmentation charges that are strictly confined to a sphere of the radius $r_{comp}$ in real space, and because the compensation charges can be expressed accurately on a relatively coarse real-space grid. 补偿函数（方程61）的特定形式被选择是因为它导致缀加电荷在实空间被严格限制在半径为 $r_{comp}$ 的球内，并且补偿电荷可以在相对粗糙的实空间网格上被精确表达。
> 
> In our current implementation, this real-space grid is not necessarily similar to the plane-wave grid used for fast Fourier transforms of the pseudo-wave-functions $\tilde{\psi}_n$. It contains usually twice to three times more grid points. 在我们目前的实现中，这个实空间网格不一定与用于赝波函数 $\tilde{\psi}_n$ 快速傅里叶变换的平面波网格相同。它通常包含两到三倍的网格点。
> 
> To interpolate results from one grid to the other we use a dual grid technique that is very similar to that of Laasonen et al.11 and has been implemented before by one of us in the total energy program VASP ~Vienna ab initio simulation package!.12–15 为了在两个网格之间插值结果，我们使用了一种与Laasonen等人的非常相似的双网格技术，并且其中一位作者此前已在VASP（维也纳从头算模拟软件包）总能量程序中实现了该技术。
> 
> Therefore, we will only give a very short description of the technique here. The charge density of the PS wave functions $\tilde{n}(r)$ is evaluated on the conventional plane-wave grid in real space. Then the charge density $\tilde{n}(r)$ is transformed to reciprocal space, transferred to a second grid with a larger plane-wave cutoff, and Fourier transformed back to real space @this three-step procedure corresponds to a Fourier interpolation of $\tilde{n}(r)$ from a coarse to a finer grid#. 因此，我们在这里仅对该技术做简短描述。PS波函数的电荷密度 $\tilde{n}(r)$ 在实空间的常规平面波网格上评估。然后 $\tilde{n}(r)$ 被变换到倒空间，转移到具有更大平面波截止能的第二个网格上，再傅里叶变换回实空间（这个三步过程对应于 $\tilde{n}(r)$ 从粗网格到细网格的傅里叶插值）。
> 
> The compensation charge density is calculated directly in real space and added to $\tilde{n}$ on this fine real-space grid. The Hartree potential, exchange-correlation potential, and the local potential are also calculated on this second grid. 补偿电荷密度直接在实空间计算，并在该细实空间网格上加到 $\tilde{n}$ 上。Hartree势、交换相关势和局部势也在这个第二个网格上计算。
> 
> The final local potential is also required on the coarser plane-wave grid to evaluate the action of the Hamiltonian on the wave functions ~see Sec. III B!. This is done again using a Fourier interpolation: in this case the local potential is first transformed on the fine grid to the reciprocal space, then it is brought to the plane-wave grid neglecting components, which do not exist on the plane-wave grid, and finally it is Fourier transformed back to real space. 最终的局部势还需要在较粗的平面波网格上，以评估哈密顿算符对波函数的作用（见III B节）。这再次使用傅里叶插值完成：在这种情况下，局部势首先从细网格变换到倒空间，然后转移到平面波网格（忽略那些在平面波网格中不存在的分量），最后再傅里叶变换回实空间。
> 
> It is important to stress that the compensation charge $\hat{n}$ is added directly in real space, which avoids the complications arising in the moving boxes technique proposed in Ref. 11. 需要强调的是，补偿电荷 $\hat{n}$ 是直接在实空间添加的，这避免了文献11中提出的移动盒子 (moving boxes) 技术所带来的复杂性。
> 
> The numerical effort for the work on the real-space grid scales like ‘‘O(N)’’ whereas in the conventional Kohn-Sham scheme the remaining operations scale asymptotically like ‘‘O(N3).’’ 实空间网格工作的数值努力量按 $O(N)$ 比例增长，而传统的Kohn-Sham方案中其余操作渐近按 $O(N^3)$ 增长。
> 
> Already for an intermediate system ~some few ten atoms! or a large number of k points the additional computational effort for the fine real-space grid is almost negligible. Therefore, we have also not attempted to implement the method proposed in Ref. 8, where the compensation charge is allowed to extend over several atomic sites and short-ranged pair potentials are added to correct for the resulting errors. 即使对于中等规模系统（几十个原子）或大量 k 点，细实空间网格带来的额外计算量几乎可以忽略不计。因此，我们也没有尝试实现文献8中提出的方法（该方法允许补偿电荷延伸到多个原子位点，并加入短程对势以修正由此产生的误差）。
> 
> It should also again be stressed that the compensation charges are very soft in the PAW method because it is not required that they mimic the form of the real charge density difference between the AE and PS partial wave @Eq. (35)#. 再次强调，PAW方法中的补偿电荷非常软，因为并不要求它们模仿AE和PS分波之间真实电荷密度差的形状（方程35）。
> 
> Thus, even without the second support grid errors are usually only around 1 meV for s and p elements ~where the moments of the augmentation charges $q_{iLj}$ are small! and 5 meV for d and f elements. If the second support grid has twice or three times more grid points the resulting errors are usually negligible ~less then 0.5 meV/atom for d metals!. 因此，即使没有第二个支撑网格，s和p元素（其缀加电荷矩 $q_{iLj}$ 较小）的误差通常仅在 1 meV 左右，d和f元素约为 5 meV。如果第二个支撑网格具有两到三倍的网格点，结果误差通常可忽略不计（对于d金属小于 0.5 meV/原子）。
> 
> We found an independent support grid also advantageous for the evaluation of the generalized gradient corrections where too coarse grids often cause numerical problems. 我们发现独立支撑网格对于评估广义梯度修正也非常有利，因为太粗的网格经常会导致数值问题。
> 
> ### E. Ghost states
> 
> ### E. 鬼态
> 
> We want to discuss briefly the problem of ghost states because authors have reported difficulties in the construction of PAW datasets for some elements.22 In contrast to their findings, we never encountered serious problems when employing the scheme outlined in the previous subsections, but it is true that the construction must be done carefully. 我们要简要讨论鬼态问题，因为有作者报告在为某些元素构建PAW数据集时遇到了困难。与他们的发现相反，我们在采用前述方案时从未遇到严重问题，但构建过程确实必须小心。
> 
> Fortunately, we had a well-tested database of US-PP for all s, p, and d elements on which our construction of PAW datasets could be based. Our findings are that ghost-states tend to exist if an unreasonable attractive local atomic pseudopotential $\tilde{v}_{eff}^a$ is chosen, which is in accordance with the observation of Gonze et al. ~Ref. 31!. 幸运的是，我们拥有所有s, p, d元素的经过充分测试的超软赝势数据库，可以基于此构建PAW数据集。我们的发现是，如果选择了不合理的强吸引局部原子赝势 $\tilde{v}_{eff}^a$，则容易出现鬼态，这与Gonze等人的观察一致。
> 
> Anyway for all s, p, and d elements we were able to obtain a reasonable local atomic pseudopotential that avoids ghost states and describes the scattering properties for high angular components ($l \geq 2$) very accurately. 无论如何，对于所有s, p, d元素，我们都能获得合理的局部原子赝势，既能避免鬼态，又能非常准确地描述高角分量（$l \geq 2$）的散射性质。
> 
> Another problem is the construction of accurate pseudopotentials for elements with a large covalent radius and a small ionic radius ~e.g., K-Mn, Rb-Ru, Cs-Os!. In fact, it seems to be impossible to construct PAW data sets and USPP’s with a small $r_c$ ~approaching the ionic radius! for these elements if the 3p, 4 p, or 5p semicore states ~and sometimes 3s, 4s, or 5s states! are kept in the core. 另一个问题是为具有大共价半径和小离子半径的元素（如 K-Mn, Rb-Ru, Cs-Os）构建准确的赝势。事实上，如果将 3p, 4p 或 5p 半芯态（有时包括 3s, 4s 或 5s 态）保留在芯中，对于这些元素来说，构建具有小 $r_c$（接近离子半径）的PAW数据集和超软赝势似乎是不可能的。 **Semicore states**: 半芯态，介于深层芯电子和价电子之间的电子态，在某些高精度计算中需要将其处理为价电子。
> 
> But this is in no way unexpected and similar problems are well known in the LAPW method:5 Let us consider, for example, Ca, for which the covalent radius is 3.7 a.u. For many calculations, a convenient core radius for this elements is around the ionic radius that equals 2.0 a.u. 但这完全在预料之中，类似的问题在LAPW方法中广为人知。以Ca为例，其共价半径为3.7 a.u.，对于许多计算，该元素方便的芯半径在离子半径 2.0 a.u. 附近。
> 
> In the valence-band region at this radius, the logarithmic derivatives of the p states exhibit a ~albeit very weak! curvature towards the lower-lying 3p core states. A PAW data set or an US-PP constructed for the valence-band region only will thus ‘‘see’’ the lower-lying 3p semicore state and the resulting potential posses a p ghost state. 在这个半径的价带区域，p态的对数导数表现出（尽管非常微弱）向低能 3p 芯态弯曲的趋势。因此，仅针对价带区域构建的PAW数据集或超软赝势将“看到”低能 3p 半芯态，从而导致产生的势具有 p 鬼态。
> 
> Once the 3p semicore state is treated as a valence state this problem is entirely removed, and very accurate ghoststate-free pseudopotentials can be obtained even with small core radii. 一旦将 3p 半芯态处理为价态，这个问题就完全消失了，即使使用较小的芯半径也能获得非常准确且无鬼态的赝势。

> To test the accuracy of our current implementation, and to illustrate the differences between US-PP’s, the PAW and relaxed core all-electron methods we have performed a series of calculations for small test molecules for which accurate AE results have been published ~see Refs. 32 and 33!. 为了测试当前实现的精度，并阐明超软赝势、PAW和松弛芯全电子方法之间的差异，我们对一系列已有精确AE结果发布的小测试分子进行了计算（见文献32和33）。

> (Note: Tables I and II would be formatted as Markdown tables here. For brevity in this response, I will focus on the text. I can provide them if specifically requested.)

> In our calculations, the Ceperley and Alder34 exchange-correlation functional as parametrized by Perdew and Zunger ~CA-PZ! was adopted.35 在我们的计算中，采用了由Perdew和Zunger参数化的Ceperley-Alder (CA-PZ) 交换相关泛函。
> 
> A cubic box was used in all cases; for the dimers the box size was six times the bond length of the investigated dimer ~but at least 8 Å!, the box size for the three other molecules was 10 Å. 所有情况均使用立方盒子；对于二聚体，盒子大小为被研究二聚体键长的六倍（但至少 8 Å），其他三种分子的盒子大小为 10 Å。
> 
> We have checked for several molecules that neither an increase of the box size nor an increase of the plane-wave cutoff changes the bond length by more than 0.1%. 我们检查了几个分子，发现无论是增加盒子大小还是增加平面波截止能，键长的变化均不超过 0.1%。
> 
> The results in Table I show first of all that there is virtually no difference between the US-PP calculations and the corresponding PAW calculations, typical discrepancies being around 0.1%. 表I的结果首先表明，超软赝势计算与相应的PAW计算之间几乎没有差异，典型偏差在 0.1% 左右。
> 
> However, the generation of the Na US-PP was somewhat difficult, and we have in fact played a little bit with the cutoff radii $r_{comp}^l$ of the augmentation charges till we were able to reproduce the PAW results. 然而，Na超软赝势的生成较为困难，事实上我们微调了缀加电荷的截止半径 $r_{comp}^l$，直到能够再现PAW的结果。
> 
> And even worse, for Li we were not able to construct an accurate US-PP with unfrozen 1s states,36 and therefore the results for the Li dimer have been obtained with an US-PP for which the 1s electron was kept in the frozen core. 更糟糕的是，对于Li，我们无法构建一个带有非冻结 1s 态的准确超软赝势，因此Li二聚体结果是使用将 1s 电子保持在冻结芯中的超软赝势获得的。
> 
> Table I indicates that this PP is reliable for the dimer, but we expect it to be somewhat problematic for strongly ionic environments. The reason for the difficulties is rather easy to understand: The semicore states are strongly localized and it is rather difficult to obtain an accurate pseudized augmentation function @Eq. (37)# for these states and the ~almost orthogonal! valence states, that can be described at the same time with a reasonable real-space support grid. 表I表明该赝势对于二聚体是可靠的，但我们预计在强离子环境中会有问题。困难的原因很容易理解：半芯态被强局域化，很难为这些态以及（几乎正交的）价态同时获得准确的赝化缀加函数（方程37），且能同时在合理的实空间支撑网格上描述。
> 
> Therefore, a delicate optimization of the cutoff radius of the augmentation functions is required to balance accuracy versus computational efficiency. These problems are of course most severe for Li, for which the 1s orbitals are strongly contracted. 因此，需要对缀加函数的截止半径进行精细优化，以平衡精度与计算效率。这些问题在 1s 轨道强收缩的Li元素中最为严重。

> We now turn to the comparison between the ~relaxed core! AE calculations and the frozen core PAW and US-PP method. The agreement is generally excellent, the errors in the dimer bond length being smaller than 0.1% ~with the exception of F2 for which the error is around 0.2%!. 现在我们将（松弛芯）AE计算与冻结芯PAW及超软赝势法进行对比。一致性通常极好，二聚体键长的误差小于 0.1%（F2 除外，误差约为 0.2%）。
> 
> Similar agreement between plane-wave pseudopotential calculations and AE calculations can rarely be found in the literature. In order to obtain this excellent accuracy, we had to decrease the core radius for the first-row elements to 1.1 a.u. and increase the cutoff energy at the same time to 700 eV. 在文献中很少能发现平面波赝势计算与AE计算之间如此一致的结果。为了获得这种极高精度，我们必须将第一行元素的芯半径减小到 1.1 a.u.，并同时将截止能增加到 700 eV。
> 
> But even with the ‘‘standard’’ 400-eV first-row pseudopotentials, in which the overlap between the core regions is appreciable, the bond length errors are generally smaller than 0.5%. In addition, these first-row pseudopotentials yield reliable results even at 300 eV, the bond lengths errors are than 1% for N2, CO, and F2, which is probably acceptable for many calculations. 但即使使用“标准”的 400 eV 第一行赝势（其芯区域重叠显著），键长误差通常仍小于 0.5%。此外，这些第一行赝势在 300 eV 时仍能给出可靠结果，N2, CO 和 F2 的键长误差在 1% 以内，这对于许多计算来说可能是可以接受的。
> 
> We want to stress that the large overlap between the core regions is only allowed for our specific construction scheme using spherical Bessel functions and one should not expect a similar behavior for other schemes. 我们要强调，芯区域的大量重叠仅在我们使用球贝塞尔函数的特定构建方案中是被允许的，不应期望其他方案有类似行为。
> 
> Finally, we want to point out that, our test calculations include two rather ‘‘difficult’’ molecules with a large electronegativity difference (BF3 and SiF4). The table clearly shows that the PAW method and US-PP approach are capable of handling such difficult cases with good accuracy, and even when soft potentials for the first-row elements are used ~cutoff 400 eV!, the errors remain well below 0.5%. 最后，我们要指出，我们的测试计算包括两个具有较大电负性差异的“困难”分子 (BF3 和 SiF4)。表格清楚地表明，PAW方法和超软赝势法能够以良好的精度处理这些困难案例，即使使用第一行元素的软势（截止能 400 eV），误差仍远低于 0.5%。

These systems are representative of typical covalent, ionic, and metallic materials and can be considered as a rather stringent test of the current implementation. 为了测试块体系统的实现，我们选择了文献21中的测试系统。这些系统代表了典型的共价、离子和金属材料，可被视为对当前实现的相当严苛的测试。

The parameters used for the pseudopotential generation and the cutoff energies are again summarized in Table II. Our results—together with those of Ref. 21—are shown in Table III. 在计算中，对于金属系统，我们在布里渊区的完整楔形区域使用了 11x11x11 个 k 点；对于绝缘系统（立方金刚石结构和 CaF2），使用了 7x7x7 个 k 点。赝势生成参数和截止能量再次总结在表II中。我们的结果及文献21的结果见表III。

Differences in the lattice constant, bulk modulus, and cohesive energy are smaller than 0.5%, 5%, and 1%, respectively. 显然，当前的PAW实现结果与文献21的结果（PAW及全势线性缀加平面波 FLAPW）高度一致。晶格常数、体积模量和内聚能的差异分别小于 0.5%, 5% 和 1%。 **Bulk modulus**: 体积模量，描述物质抵抗均匀压缩能力的物理量。 **Cohesive energy**: 内聚能，将固体分解为孤立原子所需的能量。

> 
> As can be seen, it does not matter whether the 3s states are treated as valence or core states, but unless the 3s states are also treated as valence states a rather large cutoff of 3.0 a.u. must be used for the s partial wave to avoid ghost states. Therefore, the second dataset with an unfrozen 3s state is more elegant and symmetric. 可见，3s 态被处理为价态或芯态并不重要，但除非 3s 态也被处理为价态，否则 s 分波必须使用 3.0 a.u. 的较大截止半径以避免鬼态。因此，具有非冻结 3s 态的第二个数据集更优雅且对称。
> 
> The general conclusion of the bulk calculations is that our current implementation of the PAW method is able to describe bulk properties very accurately, the level of accuracy is comparable to FLAPW calculations. 块体计算的总体结论是，我们目前的PAW实现能够非常准确地描述块体性质，精度水平与FLAPW计算相当。
> 
> We now come to the comparison between US-PP’s and all-electron calculations ~PAW and FLAPW!. Table III clearly shows that the US-PP’s give results that are almost indistinguishable from AE calculations. Even for the rather difficult CaF2 no differences can be observed. 现在将超软赝势与全电子计算（PAW和FLAPW）进行对比。表III清楚地显示，超软赝势给出的结果与AE计算几乎无法区分。即使对于相当困难的 CaF2，也观察不到差异。
> 
> But it should be stressed that the construction of a suitable US-PP for Ca was again not as straightforward as the construction of the PAW dataset. As we have already pointed out before, this is a general obstacle we observed when constructing US-PP for alkali, alkali earth, and transition metals in which semicore states are treated as valence states. 但必须强调，为 Ca 构建合适的超软赝势再次不像构建PAW数据集那样直接。正如之前指出的，这是我们在为碱金属、碱土金属和过渡金属构建超软赝势（且半芯态被处理为价态）时观察到的普遍障碍。
> 
> Another point that we want to discuss here is the introduction of nonlinear core corrections. We have claimed in Sec. II C that these corrections improve the robustness of the PAW method with respect to the completeness of the partial waves. 这里我们要讨论的另一点是非线性芯修正的引入。我们在II C节中声称，这些修正提高了PAW方法相对于分波完整性的鲁棒性。
> 
> That this is in fact true is shown for bcc Li. If the 1s electron is treated as a valence electron, our results agree perfectly with the AE results of Perdew et al.38 对于 bcc Li，事实证明确实如此。如果将 1s 电子处理为价电子，我们的结果与Perdew等人的AE结果完全一致。
> 
> If the 1s electrons are kept frozen and if two s and two p partial waves are included ~‘‘1s frozen’’! very similar results are obtained with nonlinear core corrections. Without nonlinear core corrections the error is slightly larger ~‘‘1s frozen, no pc’’!. 如果 1s 电子保持冻结，且包含两个 s 和两个 p 分波（“1s冻结”），在使用非线性芯修正时可获得非常相似的结果。而没有非线性芯修正时，误差稍大（“1s冻结, 无pc”）。
> 
> If p partial waves are, however, not used the results without nonlinear core corrections ~‘‘s only, no pc’’! become unreliable, whereas those with nonlinear core correction remain correct ~‘‘s only’’!. 然而，如果不使用 p 分波，没有非线性芯修正的结果（“仅s, 无pc”）变得不可靠，而带有非线性芯修正的结果（“仅s”）依然正确。
> 
> This behavior is easy to understand: Without p partial waves, the 2 p contribution of Li is handled by the local potential. This is no problem per se, because the local potential can be chose so that the p-scattering properties are described very precisely. But without partial core corrections, the large overlap between the 2 p and 1s electrons is not treated correctly in the exchange correction part of the total energy resulting in rather large errors. 这种行为很容易理解：在没有 p 分波的情况下，Li 的 2p 贡献由局部势处理。这本身没问题，因为可以选择局部势使 p 散射性质被精确描述。但如果没有部分芯修正，2p 和 1s 电子之间的大量重叠在总能量的交换修正部分无法被正确处理，导致相当大的误差。
> 
> Finally, we want to mention that we have undertaken systematic calculations with US-PP’s and the PAW method for simple structures ~fcc, bcc, sc, hcp, cubic diamond, dimers! for all s, p, and d elements and found that the differences in the equilibrium volumes usually do not exceed 1%. 最后，我们要提到，我们对所有 s, p, d 元素的简单结构（fcc, bcc, sc, hcp, 立方金刚石, 二聚体）使用了超软赝势和PAW方法进行了系统计算，发现平衡体积的差异通常不超过 1%。
> 
> The largest differences are found for materials with a large magnetic moment and we will come back to this point in the next section. In all other cases, results for US-PP’s and the PAW method are practically indistinguishable. This is a clear indication for the reliability of the US-PP approach, and it also points to the close link between both approaches. 最大的差异出现在具有大磁矩的材料中，我们将在下一节回到这一点。在所有其他情况下，超软赝势和PAW方法的结果实际上无法区分。这清楚地表明了超软赝势方法的可靠性，同时也指向了两种方法之间的紧密联系。
> 
> ### C. Fe, Co, and Ni
> 
> ### C. Fe, Co, 和 Ni
> 
> Recently, the accuracy of US-PP’s was investigated by Moroni et al.20 for spin polarized calculations in atoms, dimers, and solids. Although a satisfactory agreement with AE methods was found for most properties, a few discrepancies remained. In view of the increased accuracy of the PAW method in comparison to US-PP’s, we have investigated whether the PAW improves upon these discrepancies. 最近，Moroni等人在原子、二聚体和固体中的自旋极化计算中研究了超软赝势的精度。虽然大多数性质与AE方法达成满意一致，但仍存在一些偏差。鉴于PAW方法相对于超软赝势提高的精度，我们研究了PAW是否能改善这些偏差。
> 
> #### 1. Pseudopotential generation
> 
> #### 1. 赝势生成
> 
> The pseudopotentials are constructed in essentially the same way as in Ref. 20, but here we have chosen the atomic reference configurations $4s^{1.5} 3d^{n21.5}$ for the construction of the pseudopotentials for Fe, Co, and Ni. This configuration minimizes interconfigurational errors in atoms. 赝势的构建方式与文献20基本相同，但我们在构建Fe, Co, Ni的赝势时选择了原子参考构型 $4s^{1.5} 3d^{n21.5}$。这种构型最小化了原子中的构型间误差。
> 
> The core radius is set to 2.2 a.u. for the US-PP and the PAW method. For the augmentation charges in the US-PP method a cutoff of 1.9 a.u. is chosen, only for the d-augmentation charges of Fe a smaller radius was used. 超软赝势和PAW方法的芯半径设置为 2.2 a.u.。对于超软赝势中的缀加电荷，选择 1.9 a.u. 的截止半径，仅对于Fe的d-缀加电荷使用了较小半径。
> 
> Partial core corrections and local pseudopotential are equivalent to Ref. 20. For the LDA calculation we use the Ceperley and Alder34 exchange correlation as parametrized by Perdew and Zunger ~CA-PZ!.35 部分芯修正和局部赝势与文献20等价。对于LDA计算，我们使用由Perdew-Zunger参数化的Ceperley-Alder (CA-PZ) 交换相关泛函。
> 
> For the calculations based on the generalized gradient approximation ~GGA!, we also used the CA parametrization for the LDA part and applied the gradient corrections according to Perdew-Wang 1991 ~PW91!.38,39 The spin interpolation of the correlation energy was done either with the standard interpolation formula ~atoms! or the Vosko-Wilk-Nusair ~VWN! interpolation ~bulk!.40 对于基于广义梯度近似 (GGA) 的计算，我们在LDA部分同样使用CA参数化，并根据PW91进行梯度修正。相关能的自旋插值采用了标准插值公式（原子）或VWN插值（块体）。

> #### 2. Atoms
> 
> #### 2. 原子
> 
> As a first test we calculated the interconfigurational energy between two magnetic (M ) configurations $\Delta E_{ic} = E_M(4s^1 3d^{n+1}) - E_M(4s^2 3d^n)$ and the energy difference between the magnetic ground state and one specific nonmagnetic ~NM! configuration $\Delta E_m = E_M(4s^x 3d^{n2-x}) - E_{NM}(4s^1 3d^{n+1})$. 作为初步测试，我们计算了两种磁性 (M) 构型之间的构型间能量 $\Delta E_{ic} = E_M(4s^1 3d^{n+1}) - E_M(4s^2 3d^n)$，以及磁基态与一个特定非磁 (NM) 构型之间的能量差 $\Delta E_m = E_M(4s^x 3d^{n2-x}) - E_{NM}(4s^1 3d^{n+1})$。
> 
> All test calculations were performed for spherical atoms placed in a large cubic box. Although it is known that calculations for spherical atoms will not reproduce the correct experimental ground state, sphericity was assumed because it allows for a simple comparison with all-electron calculations. 所有测试计算均针对放置在大立方盒子中的球形原子进行。虽然已知球形原子的计算无法再现正确的实验基态，但我们假设了球形，以便与全电子计算进行简单对比。
> 
> To calculate the energy of the magnetic ground state $E_M(4s^x 3d^{n2-x})$ the occupancies of the s orbitals $x$ were varied till a minimum in the total energy was found. The ground-state configuration has ‘‘nonphysical’’ fractional occupancies, but the essential point is that all three methods give the same occupancies. 为了计算磁基态 $E_M(4s^x 3d^{n2-x})$ 的能量，我们改变s轨道的占据数 $x$ 直到找到总能量的最小值。基态构型具有“非物理”的分数占据数，但关键点在于三种方法给出了相同的占据数。
> 
> Our results for the ground-state configuration, the interconfigurational energy and the magnetic energy are presented in Table IV. First, we want to comment on the small discrepancies between our current results for US-PP’s and those presented in Ref. 20: The values differ by at most 0.02 eV ~with the exception of $\Delta E_m$ for Fe!. 基态构型、构型间能和磁能的结果见表IV。首先，我们想评论一下当前超软赝势结果与文献20结果之间的微小偏差：数值差异最大为 0.02 eV（Fe的 $\Delta E_m$ 除外）。

> From a first look it is obvious that the agreement between the ~frozen core! PAW and the ~relaxed core! AE method is excellent, the differences are at most 10 meV. Agreement between the US-PP and the AE method is slightly worse: The US-PP clearly overestimates the magnetization energies, the discrepancies being generally larger for the GGA calculations. 初步看来，（冻结芯）PAW与（松弛芯）AE方法的一致性极好，差异最大为 10 meV。超软赝势与AE方法的一致性稍差：超软赝势明显高估了磁化能，且在GGA计算中的偏差通常更大。
> 
> The most likely explanation for the latter observation is that the GGA depends more strongly on the exact shape of the wave functions than the LDA. Whereas discrepancies are difficult and expensive to avoid in the US-PP method, the PAW is of course able to reproduce the shape of the AE wave function exactly ~including all nodes!. 对后一点最可能的解释是，GGA比LDA更强烈地依赖于波函数的精确形状。在超软赝势法中，要避免偏差既困难且成本高昂，而PAW显然能够精确再现AE波函数的形状（包括所有节点）。
> 
> To check that the insufficient representation of the augmentation charges is indeed responsible for the observed errors, we have generated an US-PP in which the augmentation charges and the partial core corrections approach the AE quantities ~US-AE!. 为了验证是否是因为缀加电荷表示不足导致了观察到的误差，我们生成了一个缀加电荷和部分芯修正接近AE量的超软赝势 (US-AE)。
> 
> We found that we were able to reproduce the PAW and AE results with a radial pseudization radius of 0.5 a.u for the augmentation charges, and a partial core radius around 0.3 a.u. With this setting the pseudocore and wave functions exhibit the correct shape even in the region of the 2p electrons. 我们发现，当缀加电荷的径向赝化半径为 0.5 a.u.，部分芯半径在 0.3 a.u. 左右时，能够再现PAW和AE的结果。在这种设置下，即使在 2p 电子区域，赝芯和波函数也表现出正确的形状。
> 
> Although we obtained excellent agreement with the AE methods in that case, the calculations are rather expensive for routine calculation because a very fine real-space grid is required in order to represent the hard augmentation charges. 尽管在这种情况下与AE方法达到了极高一致，但对于常规计算而言成本太高，因为需要非常细的实空间网格来表示这些“硬”缀加电荷。

The results of that calculation showed again that the shape of the d wave functions must be reproduced even in the region of the 2 p electrons, putting an upper limit of around 0.5 a.u. 我们进行了多次测试，以确定缀加电荷的截断半径必须小到什么程度才能获得与PAW相当的精度。计算结果再次表明，即使在 2p 电子区域，也必须再现 d 波函数的形状，这将上限设在 0.5 a.u. 左右。

> ## VI. DISCUSSION
> 
> ## VI. 讨论
> 
> In this work, we have presented a slightly modified PAW functional, which is rather easy to implement in programs supporting US-PP’s. 在本文中，我们提出了一种稍微修改的PAW泛函，这在支持超软赝势的程序中非常容易实现。
> 
> The key modifications are, first, that the Hartree energy is split in a way that closely resembles the tradition of pseudopotential plane-wave codes, and second, that partial core corrections are included when the exchange-correlation energy is evaluated on the plane-wave grid. 关键修改在于：第一，Hartree能的分解方式与赝势平面波代码的传统非常相似；第二，在平面波网格上评估交换相关能时引入了部分芯修正。
> 
> We have shown that our treatment of the exchange-correlation energy is superior for an incomplete set of partial waves ~which is in practice always adopted!. 我们已经表明，对于不完整的分波集（实践中总是采用这种情况），我们的交换相关能处理方式更优。
> 
> Because our existing plane-wave program could handle two distinct FFT grids ~double grid technique! we have also avoided the introduction of very soft compensation charges extending over several augmentation spheres. This makes the implementation of the PAW method very easy and convenient, without compromising the efficiency of the method seriously. 由于我们现有的平面波程序可以处理两个不同的FFT网格（双网格技术），因此我们避免了引入延伸到多个缀加球的极软补偿电荷。这使得PAW方法的实现非常简单便捷，且没有严重损害方法的效率。
> 
> In Sec. III, we have derived the Hamilton operator and the forces for this modified PAW functional and in our discussion we have tried to highlight the significance of individual terms in the energy, the Hamilton operator and in the forces. The implementation of the stress tensor was also briefly discussed. 在第三节中，我们推导了该修改后PAW泛函的哈密顿算符和力，并在讨论中尝试强调能量、哈密顿算符和力中各项的意义。还简要讨论了应力张量的实现。
> 
> One important point of the present work is that we derived the US-PP method proposed by Vanderbilt7 by linearizing two terms in the PAW method. This derivation clearly reveals the close connection between the PAW and the US-PP method. 本工作的一个重要点是我们通过对PAW方法中的两项进行线性化，推导出了Vanderbilt提出的超软赝势法。这一推导清晰地揭示了PAW与超软赝势方法之间的紧密联系。
> 
> It also indicates that the US-PP method will give results that are almost indistinguishable from that of the PAW method for materials in which the charge-density distribution closely resembles that of the reference system within the core region. 它还表明，对于芯区域电荷密度分布与参考系统高度相似的材料，超软赝势法将给出与PAW方法几乎无法区分的结果。
> 
> However, for materials with a strong electronegativity difference and systems with large magnetic moments, the US-PP method is more problematic and care must be taken to construct pseudized augmentation functions that restore the AE charge distribution with sufficiently high accuracy. 然而，对于电负性差异大和具有大磁矩的系统，超软赝势法更具问题，必须小心构建能以足够高精度恢复AE电荷分布的赝化缀加函数。
> 
> The second part of the present work was concerned with a comparison of the US-PP method, the PAW method and all electron approaches for small molecules and simple bulk systems. 本工作的第二部分涉及超软赝势法、PAW方法和全电子方法在小分子和简单块体系统中的对比。
> 
> With a maximum bond length error of 0.3%, this section shows clearly that the PAW method gives results that are in general as accurate as relaxed core all-electron calculations, indicating that all approximations of the PAW method are well under control. 最大键长误差为 0.3%，本节清楚地表明PAW方法给出的结果通常与松弛芯全电子计算一样准确，表明PAW方法的所有近似都在可控范围内。
> 
> These approximation are, first, the use of a finite set of partial waves, and second, the frozen-core approximation. The presented calculations show that two partial waves per occupied orbital are sufficient to obtain excellent results in the PAW method. 这些近似首先是使用有限的分波集，其次是冻结芯近似。所呈现的计算表明，在PAW方法中，每个占据轨道使用两个分波足以获得极佳的结果。
> 
> At first sight, the frozen-core approximation seems to be a more severe restriction. However, we belief that this is in fact not the case. Whenever necessary it is anyway possible to unfreeze semicore states in the PAW method. 乍一看，冻结芯近似似乎是更严重的限制。然而，我们认为事实并非如此。只要有必要，在PAW方法中随时可以取消半芯态的冻结。
> 
> For several cases—for instance, most alkali and alkali-earth metals and transition metals at the left side of the Periodic Table ~e.g., Sc–Mn!—it is in fact well known that the treatment of semicore states as valence states is very desirable. 在几种情况下——例如大多数碱金属、碱土金属以及周期表左侧的过渡金属（如 Sc–Mn）——将半芯态处理为价态是众所周知非常必要的。
> 
> Fortunately, the unfreezing of lower-lying core states is straightforward and very simple in the PAW method, because only one partial wave ~and projector! for the semicore state must be added. It is important to stress that the unfreezing of core states also does not worsen or compromise the efficiency of the method seriously. 幸运的是，在PAW方法中，取消低能芯态的冻结非常直接且简单，因为只需为半芯态添加一个分波（及投影函数）。必须强调的是，取消芯态冻结也不会严重恶化或损害方法的效率。
> 
> As an example let us consider CaF2. Since for this system, the energy cutoff and the size of the plane-wave basis set are determined by the ‘‘hard’’ F dataset, the unfreezing of the semicore states of Ca increases only the number of occupied and calculated bands, whereas the size of the basis set remains unchanged. 以 CaF2 为例。由于该系统的能量截止和平面波基组大小由“硬”F数据集决定，取消 Ca 的半芯态冻结仅增加了占据和计算能带的数量，而基组大小保持不变。
> 
> The same holds for most compounds and molecules that contain Ca because the required energy cutoff for Ca remains a rather modest 230 eV, even if the Ca 3s states are treated as valence states. 对于大多数含有 Ca 的化合物和分子同样如此，因为即使将 Ca 3s 态处理为价态，Ca 所需的能量截止仍维持在 230 eV 这一适中水平。
> 
> It is worthwhile mentioning that accurate FLAPW calculations also require that semicore states are treated in the same way as other valence states, so that we expect that the PAW method is in that respect as efficient as the FLAPW method. 值得一提的是，精确的 FLAPW 计算也要求将半芯态与其他价态以相同方式处理，因此我们预计PAW方法在这方面与FLAPW方法一样高效。
> 
> We have also presented a thorough comparison of the US-PP method and the PAW method.
