> ## 一、引言
> 
> **多铁性材料（multiferroics）** 是指在同一相中同时具备两种或三种“铁性”性质（**铁电性（ferroelectricity）**、**铁磁性（ferromagnetism）** 或**铁弹性（ferroelasticity）**）的材料。其中，**磁电多铁体（magnetoelectric multiferroics）** 同时具有可被外磁场切换的自发磁化强度、可被外电场切换的自发极化，并且两者往往存在耦合。这类材料在数据存储、传感器、自旋电子学等领域具有巨大应用潜力，但在自然界和实验室中却极为稀少。本文作者 **Nicola A. Hill** 发表于 2000 年的经典论文《Why Are There so Few Magnetic Ferroelectrics?》系统探讨了这一稀缺性背后的基本物理机制。文章通过回顾铁磁与铁电的基本理论，结合第一性原理计算，深入分析了**过渡金属 d 电子**在磁性铁电共存中的核心矛盾，并提出著名的“**d0 规则**”。该文是多铁性研究领域公认的奠基性文献之一，为此后数十年多铁材料的设计与发现提供了关键理论框架。
> 
> ## 二、论文结构与逻辑概览
> 
> 本文采用“问题—背景—分析—案例—结论”的总体结构：
> 
> 1.  **引言**（第 1 节）：从铁电和磁性材料的发展史出发，引出磁电多铁体的概念与应用前景，提出核心问题“为什么磁性铁电体如此稀少？”。
> 2.  **基础理论**（第 2、3 节）：分别简述铁磁性（局域矩理论、Stoner 能带理论）与铁电性（软模理论、有序-无序模型）的基本机制，重点指出铁电钙钛矿氧化物中 B 位阳离子的 **d0 电子构型** 是常见特征。
> 3.  **多铁性分析**（第 4 节）：讨论多铁体的对称性、电学性质、化学因素（尤其是 d 电子占据）对铁磁与铁电共存的限制，排除尺寸和导电性等简单解释，提出 d 电子占据是抑制铁电畸变的关键变量。
> 4.  **案例研究**（第 5 节）：以 **BiMnO3** 和 **YMnO3** 为对象，利用密度泛函理论（DFT）计算其电子结构、能带、声子不稳定性，并与非铁电的 **LaMnO3** 对比，揭示 Bi-O 杂化和六方结构五配位如何打破 d0 规则。
> 5.  **总结与展望**（第 6 节）：归纳 d0 规则及其例外条件，提出未来研究方向。
> 
> 整体论证链条可概括为：  
> **铁电需要 d0 → d 电子是磁性必需 → 两者冲突 → 但可通过 A 位杂化或特殊配位制造不对称势垒来克服 → BiMnO3 与 YMnO3 是典型例证。**
>

> ## 四、正文核心内容剖析
> 
> ### 4.1 铁磁性与铁电性的基本矛盾
> 
> 文章首先回顾铁磁理论。**局域矩理论（Curie-Weiss）** 认为存在内部分子场（源于量子力学交换能）使磁矩平行排列；**Stoner 能带理论** 则解释过渡金属 Fe、Co、Ni 的非整数磁矩：Fermi 能级位于 3d 带高态密度区，交换劈裂导致自旋不平衡。铁电体方面，钙钛矿氧化物（如 BaTiO3、PbTiO3）的 B 位 Ti⁴⁺ 是 **d0 构型**，Ti 3d 与 O 2p 的杂化稳定了偏心位移。这里出现根本冲突：**磁性需要未占据的 d 态产生局域磁矩；铁电畸变却偏好 d0 离子**。
> 
> ### 4.2 多铁性稀缺的原因分析
> 
> 作者逐项排除简单解释：
> 
> -   **对称性**：同时允许铁电极化和磁极化的点群有 13 个（如 1, 2, m, 3, 4, 6 等），数量并不少，不是稀缺主因。
> -   **导电性**：铁电体必须是绝缘体，但许多铁磁/亚铁磁体也是绝缘体；反铁磁铁电体同样稀少，因此不能归咎于磁性绝缘体缺乏。
> -   **化学 d0 性**：B 位阳离子 d 电子占据会破坏偏离中心的铁电畸变趋势。通过对离子半径的比较，排除尺寸效应；Jahn-Teller 畸变会占据主导，抑制铁电位移。关键问题：是 d 电子占据本身还是磁交换作用抑制铁电？后文通过计算对比自旋极化与非自旋极化情况来回答。
> 
> ### 4.3 BiMnO3：A 位 Bi 杂化驱动铁电
> 
> -   **实验背景**：BiMnO3 是铁磁体（居里温度约 105 K），可能铁电；LaMnO3 是反铁磁 Jahn-Teller 畸变绝缘体，非铁电。
> -   **DFT 计算**：在立方顺磁相中，两者 DOS 类似，但 BiMnO3 有明显的 Bi 6s 深带和 Bi 6p 导带成分。紧束缚拟合显示，仅用 Mn/O 轨道无法很好描述 BiMnO3，加入 Bi 6s/6p 后拟合大幅改善，关键转移积分很大。
> -   **铁磁相计算**：自旋极化降低两者能量约 1 eV；BiMnO3 的少数自旋导带含 Bi 6p 成分，这是与 LaMnO3 的关键区别。
> -   **声子计算**：顺磁立方相中，BiMnO3 的虚频铁电模（98.20i cm⁻¹）远强于 LaMnO3（44.69i cm⁻¹）；铁磁相中 BiMnO3 仍为 82.30i，而 LaMnO3 降至 21.1i，说明 LaMnO3 的磁序进一步削弱铁电不稳定。这表明 d 电子占据本身（而非磁性自旋极化）是抑制铁电的主要因素。
> -   **机制**：Bi 6p–O 2p 杂化产生的共价键驱动 Bi 离子与氧八面体相对位移，形成铁电极化。这打破了传统“B 位驱动”的模式，是 A 位驱动的新型铁电机制。
> -   **电子铁电性**：作者提出 BiMnO3 可能存在**电子铁电性（electronic ferroelectricity）**，即极化由电子关联驱动而非晶格软模。具体表现为 Bi 6p 与 Mn 3d 能带交叉、电荷转移导致 d-p 库仑相互作用，可能形成 d-f 激子凝聚（类比 Falicov-Kimball 模型），但需进一步验证。
> 
> ### 4.4 YMnO3：五配位结构驱动铁电
> 
> -   **实验事实**：六方 YMnO3 在 914 K 以下铁电，80 K 以下反铁磁；介电异常揭示磁电耦合。正交相 YMnO3 则非铁电。
> -   **DFT 计算**：立方顺磁 YMnO3 与 LaMnO3 的能带和紧束缚拟合高度相似，Y 4d 不参与显著共价键。铁磁态下，YMnO3 的自旋极化能量降低 0.7 eV，Mn-O 杂化弱于 LaMnO3。
> -   **结论**：YMnO3 的六方结构稳定性并非来自 Y 的共价效应，而是 Y³⁺ 离子尺寸较小，导致 Mn 形成五配位三角双锥，这种结构本身缺乏中心对称，从而产生铁电极化。因此，铁电性源于**结构几何效应**，而非电子杂化。
> 
> ### 4.5 d0 规则与两种例外
> 
> 最终归纳为：**在钙钛矿氧化物中，B 位阳离子的 d 电子占据会抑制铁电位移（d0 规则）**。但两种途径可打破：
> 
> 1.  **A 位共价**：如 BiMnO3 中 Bi-O 杂化提供额外驱动力，形成不对称双阱。
> 2.  **特殊配位**：如六方 YMnO3 中 Mn 的五配位三角双锥天然具有不对称结构。
> 
> ## 五、结论与贡献
> 
> ### 核心发现
> 
> 1.  铁电性与磁性共存的主要障碍是**过渡金属 d 电子**对铁电畸变的抑制作用，而非对称性或导电性限制。
> 2.  提出 **d0 规则**：铁电钙钛矿的 B 位阳离子通常为 d0 构型；d 电子占据会破坏偏离中心的位移。
> 3.  通过 BiMnO3 和 YMnO3 两个反例证明，**A 位 Bi 共价**和**五配位几何**可以提供额外驱动力，使 d 电子存在时仍能实现铁电。
> 4.  计算表明，**自旋极化本身对铁电不稳定性的影响远小于 d 轨道占据**。
> 
> ### 理论贡献
> 
> -   为多铁性材料的稀缺性提供了统一物理解释，将铁电化学机制（d0 性）与磁性要求（d 电子）联系起来。
> -   首次系统提出 A 位驱动的铁电机制（Bi-O 杂化）和电子铁电可能性，拓展了铁电物理的视野。
> -   为后续寻找新型多铁材料提供了明确设计原则：**选择非 Jahn-Teller 磁性离子，利用 Bi 或其他孤对电子阳离子或特殊配位结构**。
> 
> ### 实践贡献
> 
> -   指导了 BiFeO3 等 B 位磁性铁电体的研究。
> -   激发了实验界对 BiMnO3、YMnO3 以及非氧化物铁电体的关注。
> 
> ## 六、未来发展方向展望
> 
> 作者在文中明确提出的未来方向包括：
> 
> 1.  **回答 d0 偏好为何如此之强的根本理论问题**：需要更严格的量子力学分析。
> 2.  **合成已知和候选多铁材料的单晶**：现有实验工作较少，单晶对测量本征性质至关重要。
> 3.  **研究非氧化物铁电体**：如 NaCaF3 等由库仑相互作用驱动的铁电体，可能绕过 d0 规则。
> 4.  **探索更多 Bi 基钙钛矿**（如 BiCrO3、BiCoO3、BiNiO3 等），以及 BiFeO3 的详细多铁性质。
> 5.  **深入研究电子铁电性**：在强关联体系中寻找 d-f 激子凝聚的证据。
> 
> 结合今天视角，这些方向已成为多铁研究的主流：BiFeO3 薄膜器件、六方锰酸盐（YMnO3、HoMnO3）的磁电耦合、以及“铁性序参量耦合”设计等均受益于此。
> 
> ## 七、学术思考与批判性分析
> 
> 1.  **计算方法的局限**：文章采用 LSDA 泛函，已知会低估带隙并高估金属性，对强关联 Mn 3d 电子描述不够精确。当时尚无 DFT+U 或杂化泛函，因此定量结果可能偏差，定性结论是否稳健？例如，BiMnO3 的铁电模虚频可能受自旋序和关联效应影响。
> 2.  **立方相假设的合理性**：BiMnO3 实际基态是三斜畸变，LaMnO3 是正交 Jahn-Teller 畸变。文中仅分析立方相声子不稳定性的相对强弱，能否充分预测真实结构的铁电性？如果立方相以外还有更强的非铁电不稳定性（如 Jahn-Teller 模），可能会淹没铁电模。
> 3.  **“d0 规则”的适用范围**：文章主要针对钙钛矿氧化物。非钙钛矿铁电体（如有机铁电体、氟化物）是否存在类似规则？A 位孤对电子驱动的铁电在更多材料（如 BiFeO3）中是否普适？作者没有系统性检验。
> 4.  **电子铁电性的证据链**：BiMnO3 中 Bi 6p 与 Mn 3d 带交叉和电荷转移似乎暗示电子不稳定性，但缺乏多体计算验证；实验也未观察到相应的激子凝聚特征。这一想法在当时是思辨性的。
> 5.  **磁电耦合的微观机制**：文章主要解决“为什么能共存”，未深入分析“共存后如何强耦合”。BiMnO3 和 YMnO3 的磁电耦合系数有多大？如何增强？后来的研究表明几何阻挫和自旋序可诱导铁电，但本文学者未预测到这一重要方向。
> 
> ## 八、用户问题预测与解答
> 
> 1.  **什么是“d0 规则”？所有铁电体都遵循吗？**  
>     d0 规则指的是钙钛矿氧化物中，B 位阳离子为 d0 电子构型（如 Ti⁴⁺、Nb⁵⁺）时有利于铁电偏离中心位移。因为空的 d 轨道易于与 O 2p 杂化，稳定畸变。但并非所有铁电体严格遵循：例如 BiFeO3 的 Fe³⁺ 为 d5，仍具有铁电性，这是靠 Bi 6s 孤对电子共价驱动；六方 YMnO3 的 Mn³⁺ 为 d4，靠五配位几何产生极化。因此 d0 规则是“经验倾向”而非绝对定律。
>     
> 2.  **为什么磁性需要 d 电子，而铁电却不喜欢 d 电子？**  
>     磁性起源于未配对 d 电子的自旋和交换相互作用，所以需要部分占据的 d 轨道。但在钙钛矿铁电体中，铁电畸变依赖于 B 位阳离子与氧的共价杂化，特别是 d0 构型下空的 d 轨道接受氧的电子云，造成电子云形变和短程排斥平衡的调整，从而允许阳离子偏心移动。而 d 电子占据会增强 Jahn-Teller 畸变或改变电子相关性，使对称结构更稳定，抑制偏心位移。
>     
> 3.  **BiMnO3 的铁电极化机制与传统钙钛矿铁电体有何不同？**  
>     传统钙钛矿（如 BaTiO3）铁电由 **B 位 Ti–O 共价杂化** 驱动，Ti 离子偏离氧八面体中心。BiMnO3 中 Mn d 电子抑制了 Mn 的位移，但 **Bi 6p–O 2p 杂化** 很强，造成 Bi 离子相对氧笼移动，形成极化。因此是 **A 位驱动** 的铁电，这种机制也被称为“孤对电子立体化学活性”或“共价铁电”。
>     
> 4.  **YMnO3 的六方结构为何天然具有铁电性？**  
>     六方 YMnO3 中 Mn³⁺ 处于三角双锥五配位（O₅），该配位几何本身没有反对称中心。Mn 沿锥体轴向偏移形成自发极化。这种铁电不是传统软模机制，而是 **几何铁电（geometric ferroelectricity）**——由离子的大小和配位几何决定，而非电子杂化。Y³⁺ 较小的半径有利于稳定六方相而不是正交相。
>     
> 5.  **本文对后来多铁材料研究有什么实际影响？**  
>     它指明了寻找新材料的方向：从“d0 规则”反推，既然磁性离子有 d 电子，就需要额外驱动。后来研究者：
>     
>     -   深入研究 Bi 基多铁体（如 BiFeO3）；
>     -   利用六方锰酸盐几何铁电与磁性耦合；
>     -   发现自旋序导致铁电（如 TbMnO3 等），这虽非本文直接预测，但打破了需要 A 位或几何驱动才能共存的限制；
>     -   发展了应变、异质结和薄膜手段调控耦合。  
>         本文为多铁性领域的爆发奠定了概念基础。
> 
> ## 九、专区：便于 Obsidian Dataview 插件调用
> 
> 领域基础知识:: 多铁性材料是同时具有铁电性、铁磁性（或反铁磁/亚铁磁等磁有序）和可能的铁弹性的一类材料。铁电性要求材料具有可切换的自发极化，通常发生在非中心对称晶体中；铁磁性要求存在未配对 d 电子的局域磁矩和交换耦合。在钙钛矿氧化物中，B 位阳离子的 d0 电子构型有利于铁电畸变，而磁性需要部分占据的 d 轨道，这是两者难以共存的根源。
> 
> 研究背景:: 20 世纪 90 年代，磁性材料研究复兴（巨磁阻等），铁电物理理论也取得进展。但磁电多铁体极为稀少，已发现的材料如硼碳镍石（Ni3B7O13I）结构复杂、难以揭示机制，因此需要从基础物理层面解释稀缺性并指导新材料的探索。
> 
> 作者的问题意识:: 为什么磁性和铁电性在同一材料中很难共存？是哪些基本物理/化学因素限制了二者的同时出现？是否存在可以突破这一限制的材料设计原则？
> 
> 主要研究对象:: 磁电多铁材料，特别是钙钛矿结构锰酸盐中的铁磁性铁电候选材料 BiMnO3 和反铁磁性铁电材料 YMnO3，并与非铁电的 LaMnO3 对比。
> 
> 主要研究方法:: 基于局域自旋密度近似（LSDA）的平面波赝势密度泛函理论（DFT）计算，包括能带结构、态密度、紧束缚拟合和声子（软模）不稳定性分析；辅以文献中已有的实验数据。
> 
> 研究意义:: 从理论上阐明了磁性铁电体稀缺的根本原因，提出了 d0 规则及其两种破缺机制（A 位 Bi-O 共价杂化、五配位几何铁电），为后续多铁材料的设计、预测和实验合成提供了重要的指导思想。
>

> Multiferroic magnetoelectrics are materials that are both ferromagnetic and ferroelectric in the same phase. As a result, they have a spontaneous magnetization that can be switched by an applied magnetic field, a spontaneous polarization that can be switched by an applied electric field, and often some coupling between the two. Very few exist in nature or have been synthesized in the laboratory. In this paper, we explore the fundamental physics behind the scarcity of ferromagnetic ferroelectric coexistence. In addition, we examine the properties of some known magnetically ordered ferroelectric materials. We find that, in general, the transition metal d electrons, which are essential for magnetism, reduce the tendency for off-center ferroelectric distortion. > ## 2. Ferromagnets (and Other Magnetic Materials)

> ## 2. 铁磁体（及其他磁性材料）

> A ferromagnetic material is one that undergoes a phase transition from a high-temperature phase that does not have a macroscopic magnetic moment to a low-temperature phase that has a spontaneous magnetization even in the absence of an applied magnetic field. The macroscopic magnetization is caused by the magnetic dipole moments of the atoms (which are aligned randomly in the high-temperature paramagnetic phase, Figure 1a) tending to line up in the same direction, as shown in Figure 1b. The spontaneous magnetization means that ferromagnetic materials tend to concentrate magnetic flux density (they have a large positive permeability), which leads to their widespread use in applications such as transformer cores, permanent magnets, and electromagnets, for which large magnetic fields are required. As-prepared samples of ferromagnets often lack a macroscopic magnetization because of the presence of domains of magnetization oriented in different directions. The subsequent alignment and reorientation of the domains upon the application of a magnetic field, H, results in a hysteresis in the magnetization and flux density, B, as shown in Figure 2. The ferromagnetic material starts in an unmagnetized state, and as the field is increased in the positive direction, the magnetic induction increases from zero to the saturation induction, Bs. When the field is reduced to zero after saturation, the induction decreases from Bs to Br, the residual induction, or retentivity. > ### 2.1. Theories of Ferromagnetism: What Makes Ferromagnets Ferromagnetic?
> 
> ### 2.1. 铁磁性理论：是什么让铁磁体具有铁磁性？
> 
> There are two phenomenological theories of ferromagnetism that have been successful in explaining many of the properties of ferromagnets: the Curie-Weiss localized-moment theory and the Stoner band theory of ferromagnetism. In a classic 1907 paper, Weiss postulated that an internal “molecular field” acts in ferromagnetic materials to align the magnetic moments parallel to each other. We now understand the origin of this molecular field to be the quantum mechanical exchange energy, which causes electrons with parallel spins (and therefore parallel magnetic moments) to have a lower energy than electrons with antiparallel spins, all other factors being equal. Below the Curie temperature, Tc, the molecular field is so strong that it magnetizes the substance even in the absence of an external applied field. At high enough temperatures, the thermal energy, kT, is larger than the alignment energy of the molecular field, resulting in the random orientation of the magnetic moments and paramagnetic behavior. > There is a divergence in the magnetic susceptibility at the Curie temperature when the magnetic moments align spontaneously even in the absence of an applied magnetic field. The susceptibility of many antiferromagnets and ferrimagnets is also well described within the localized-moment picture. The Weiss localized-moment theory breaks down in one important aspect however: it is unable to account for the measured values of the magnetic moment per atom in some ferromagnetic materials, particularly in ferromagnetic metals. There are two significant discrepancies. First, according to the Weiss theory, the magnetic dipole moment on each atom or ion should be the same in both the ferromagnetic and paramagnetic phases. Experimentally this is not the case. Second, in the localized-moment theory, the magnetic dipole moment on each atom or ion should correspond to an integer number of electrons. Again, this is not observed experimentally. To explain these data we need to use the Stoner band theory of ferromagnetism. 当磁矩即使在没有外加磁场的情况下也自发排列时，磁化率在居里温度处会出现发散。许多反铁磁体和亚铁磁体的磁化率在局域矩图景中也得到了很好的描述。然而，外斯局域矩理论在一个重要方面失效了：它无法解释某些铁磁材料（特别是铁磁金属）中每个原子的实测磁矩值。存在两个显著的差异。首先，根据外斯理论，每个原子或离子在铁磁相和顺磁相中的磁偶极矩应该是相同的。实验证明并非如此。其次，在局域矩理论中，每个原子或离子的磁偶极矩应对应于整数个电子。这在实验中同样没有被观察到。为了解释这些数据，我们需要使用斯通纳能带理论。 **Stoner band theory**: 斯通纳能带理论，基于能带结构和交换相互作用解释金属铁磁性的理论。
> 
> In the Stoner theory, the fundamental driving force for ferromagnetism is again the exchange energy, which is minimized if all of the electrons have the same spin. Opposing the alignment of spins is the increased band energy involved in transferring electrons from the lowest band states (occupied equally with up- and down-spin electrons) to band states of higher energy. This band energy prevents simple metals from being ferromagnetic. In the elemental ferromagnetic transition metals Fe, Ni, and Co, the Fermi energy lies in a region of overlapping 3d and 4s bands, as shown schematically in Figure 3. As a result of the overlap between the 4s and 3d bands, the valence electrons partially occupy both the 3d and 4s bands. For example, Ni, with 10 valence electrons per atom, has 9.46 electrons in the 3d bands and 0.54 electrons in the 4s bands. The 4s bands are broad, with a low density of states at the Fermi level. Consequently, the energy that would be required to promote a 4s electron into a vacant state so that it could reverse its spin is more than the energy that would be gained by the resulting decrease in exchange energy. In contrast, the 3d band is narrow and has a much higher density of states at the Fermi level. The large number of electrons near the Fermi level reduces the band energy required to reverse a spin, and the exchange effect dominates. It is useful to picture the exchange interaction as shifting the energy of the 3d band for electrons with one spin direction relative to the band for electrons with the opposite spin direction. 在斯通纳理论中，铁磁性的基本驱动力同样是交换能，如果所有电子具有相同自旋，交换能将最小。与自旋排列相反的是，将电子从最低能带状态（由上自旋和下自旋电子等量占据）转移到较高能带状态所增加的能带能量。这种能带能量阻止了简单金属成为铁磁体。在元素铁磁过渡金属 Fe、Ni 和 Co 中，费米能级位于 3d 和 4s 能带重叠的区域，如图 3 示意图所示。由于 4s 和 3d 能带的重叠，价电子部分占据 3d 和 4s 能带。例如，Ni 每个原子有 10 个价电子，其中 9.46 个电子在 3d 能带，0.54 个电子在 4s 能带。4s 能带较宽，费米能级处的态密度较低。因此，将 4s 电子提升到空状态以反转其自旋所需的能量，高于由此产生的交换能降低所带来的能量增益。相比之下，3d 能带较窄，且在费米能级处具有极高的态密度。费米能级附近的大量电子降低了反转自旋所需的能带能量，从而使交换效应占主导地位。将交换相互作用想象成将一种自旋方向电子的 3d 能带能量相对于相反自旋方向电子的能带进行移动是有用的。 **Fermi energy**: 费米能级，绝对零度时电子能占据的最高能量状态。 **Density of states (DOS)**: 态密度，单位能量范围内允许电子占据的状态数。
> 
> Figure 4 shows the 4s and 3d densities of states within this picture. The magnitude of the shift is independent of the wavevector, giving a rigid displacement of the states in a band with one spin direction relative to the states with the opposite spin direction. If the Fermi energy lies within the 3d band, then the displacement will lead to more electrons of the lower-energy spin direction and, hence, a spontaneous magnetic moment in the ground state. In Ni, for example, the exchange interaction displacement is so strong that one 3d subband is filled with five electrons, and the other contains all 0.54 holes. Thus, the saturation magnetization of Ni is $M \approx 0.54N\mu_B$, where N is the total number of Ni atoms in the sample. We now see why the magnetic moments of the transition metals do not correspond to integer numbers of electrons! This model also explains why the later transition metals, Cu and Zn, are not ferromagnetic. In Cu, the Fermi level lies aboVe the 3d bands. Because both of the 3d bands are filled and the 4s band has no exchange splitting, the numbers of up- and down- spin electrons are equal. In Zn, both the 3d and 4s bands are filled and so do not contribute a magnetic moment. 图 4 显示了该图景下的 4s 和 3d 态密度。移位的幅度与波矢无关，使得一个自旋方向的能带状态相对于相反自旋方向的状态发生了刚性位移。如果费米能级位于 3d 能带内，那么这种位移将导致低能自旋方向的电子更多，从而在基态产生自发磁矩。例如在 Ni 中，交换相互作用的位移非常强，以至于一个 3d 子带被 5 个电子填满，而另一个包含所有 0.54 个空穴。因此，Ni 的饱和磁化强度为 $M \approx 0.54N\mu_B$，其中 $N$ 是样本中 Ni 原子的总数。现在我们明白为什么过渡金属的磁矩不对应于整数个电子了！该模型还解释了为什么较后的过渡金属 Cu 和 Zn 不是铁磁体。在 Cu 中，费米能级位于 3d 能带之上。由于两个 3d 能带都已填满，且 4s 能带没有交换分裂，因此上自旋和下自旋电子的数量相等。在 Zn 中，3d 和 4s 能带都已填满，因此不产生磁矩。

> ## 3. Ferroelectrics
> 
> ## 3. 铁电体
> 
> The formal definition of a ferroelectric material is one that undergoes a phase transition from a high-temperature phase that behaves as an ordinary dielectric (so that an applied electric field induces an electric polarization, which goes to zero when the field is removed) to a low-temperature phase that has a spontaneous polarization whose direction can be switched by an applied field. Many properties of ferroelectric materials are analogous to those of ferromagnets, but with the electric polarization, P, corresponding to the magnetization, M; the electric field, E, corresponding to the magnetic field, H; and the electric displacement, D, corresponding to the magnetic flux density, B. For example, ferroelectric materials also have domains and show a hysteretic response of both polarization and electric displacement to an applied electric field. As a result, they also find applications in data storage. The onset of spontaneous electric polarization coincides with a divergence in the static dielectric permeability, $\epsilon$, because at Tc, an infinitesimally small external electric field will cause a large polarization. They find application as capacitors because their concentration of electric flux density results in high dielectric permeabilities. They are also used in electromechanical transducers and actuators (because the change in electric polarization is accompanied by a change in shape). Early work on ferroelectric materials focused primarily on Rochelle salt, KNa(C4H4O6)‚4H2O. Although studies of Rochelle salt were pivotal in establishing many of the basic properties of ferroelectric materials, the complex structure and large number of ions per unit cell made it difficult to elucidate a coherent theory of ferroelectricity from the results of experiments on this material. The most widely studied and widely used ferroelectrics today are perovskite-structure oxides, ABO3, which have the prototypical cubic structure shown in Figure 5. The cubic perovskite structure is characterized by a small cation, B, at the center of an octahedron of oxygen anions, with large cations, A, at the unit cell corners. Below the Curie temperature, there is a structural distortion to a lower-symmetry phase accompanied by the shift off-center of the small cation. The spontaneous polarization derives largely from the electric dipole moment created by this shift. The comparatively simple perovskite structure and the small number of atoms per unit cell have made detailed theoretical studies of perovskite ferroelectrics possible and resulted in a good understanding of the fundamentals of ferroelectricity. 铁电材料的正式定义是：一种经历相变的材料，从一个表现为普通介电体的高温相（外加电场诱导电极化，撤去场后极化消失），相变为一个具有自发极化且其方向可通过外加电场切换的低温相。铁电材料的许多性质与铁磁体类似，但电极化 $P$ 对应于磁化强度 $M$，电场 $E$ 对应于磁场 $H$，电位移 $D$ 对应于磁通密度 $B$。例如，铁电材料同样具有畴，且极化和电位移对外加电场表现出滞后响应。因此，它们也被应用于数据存储。自发电极化的出现伴随着静态介电常数 $\epsilon$ 的发散，因为在 $T_c$ 时，极小的外电场就会引起巨大的极化。它们被用作电容器，因为其电通量的集中导致了高介电常数。它们还被用于机电换能器和执行器（因为电极化的变化伴随着形状的变化）。早期的铁电材料工作主要集中在罗谢盐 KNa(C4H4O6)‚4H2O 上。虽然对罗谢盐的研究在确立铁电材料许多基本性质方面起到了关键作用，但其复杂的结构和每个晶胞中大量的离子使得很难从该材料的实验结果中阐明一个统一的铁电理论。如今被研究最广泛、使用最广泛的铁电体是钙钛矿结构氧化物 $ABO_3$，它们具有如图 5 所示的典型立方结构。立方钙钛矿结构的特点是：一个小阳离子 $B$ 位于氧阴离子八面体的中心，大阳离子 $A$ 位于晶胞顶点。在居里温度以下，结构会畸变为较低对称性的相，并伴随着小阳离子的离心位移。自发极化主要源于这种位移产生的电偶极矩。相对简单的钙钛矿结构和每个晶胞中较少的原子数使得对钙钛矿铁电体进行详细的理论研究成为可能，并使得人们对铁电性的基础有了深入的理解。

> ### 3.1. Theories of Ferroelectricity: What Makes Ferroelectrics Ferroelectric?
> 
> ### 3.1. 铁电性理论：是什么让铁电体具有铁电性？
> 
> Any lattice of oppositely signed point charges is inherently unstable. Ionic materials are stable because of short-range repulsions between adjacent electron clouds. The existence or absence of ferroelectricity is determined by a balance between these short-range repulsions, which favor the nonferroelectric symmetric structure, and additional bonding considerations, which might stabilize the ferroelectric phase. Even in ferroelectric materials, the short-range repulsions dominate at high temperature, resulting in the symmetric, unpolarized state. As the temperature is decreased, the stabilizing forces associated with the polarization of the ions as they are displaced become stronger than the short-range repulsive ion-ion interactions, and the polarized state becomes stable, even in the absence of an applied field. First-principles density functional theory calculations have contributed significantly to our current understanding of the origin of ferroelectric behavior, particularly in perovskite-structure oxides. The developments over the last 10 years are described in detail in ref 7; here, we summarize the important results. In 1992, Cohen and Krakauer used density functional theory (DFT) within the local density approximation (LDA) to investigate the ferroelectricity in two related perovskite-structure compounds: PbTiO3 and BaTiO3. Both materials have the cubic structure at high temperatures. PbTiO3 undergoes a phase transition to a tetragonal phase below 766 K, with the polarization along the [100] direction. BaTiO3 undergoes a series of phase transitions from cubic to tetragonal to orthorhombic to rhombohedral, with the low-temperature polarization along the [111] direction. Cohen and Krakauer found that, in both cases, the Ti 3d-O 2p hybridization is essential for stabilizing the ferroelectric distortion. In BaTiO3, the Ba-O interaction is largely ionic in nature. In PbTiO3, there is hybridization between the Pb 6s and O 2p electrons. This results in a large Pb polarizability and a strain that stabilizes the tetragonal phase over the rhombohedral phase in PbTiO3. 任何由相反电荷的点电荷组成的点阵本质上都是不稳定的。离子材料之所以稳定，是因为相邻电子云之间存在短程斥力。铁电性的存在与否取决于这些倾向于非铁电对称结构的短程斥力与可能稳定铁电相的其他键合因素之间的平衡。即使在铁电材料中，高温下短程斥力占主导，导致对称的无极化状态。随着温度降低，与离子位移极化相关的稳定力变得强于短程斥力离子-离子相互作用，极化状态变得稳定，即使在没有外场的情况下也是如此。第一原理密度泛函理论计算极大地促进了我们目前对铁电行为起源的理解，特别是在钙钛矿结构氧化物中。过去10年的发展在文献 7 中有详细描述；此处我们总结重要结果。1992年，Cohen 和 Krakauer 在局部密度近似 (LDA) 下使用密度泛函理论 (DFT) 研究了两种相关钙钛矿化合物：PbTiO3 和 BaTiO3。两种材料在高温下均具有立方结构。PbTiO3 在 766 K 以下发生相变为四方相，极化沿 [100] 方向。BaTiO3 经历了一系列相变：立方 $\to$ 四方 $\to$ 正交 $\to$ 三方，低温极化沿 [111] 方向。Cohen 和 Krakauer 发现，在这两种情况下，Ti 3d-O 2p 杂化对于稳定铁电畸变至关重要。在 BaTiO3 中，Ba-O 相互作用在性质上主要是离子性的。在 PbTiO3 中，Pb 6s 和 O 2p 电子之间存在杂化。这导致了较大的 Pb 极化率以及一种应变，使得 PbTiO3 中的四方相比三方相更稳定。 **DFT (Density Functional Theory)**: 密度泛函理论，一种研究多电子体系电子结构的量子力学计算方法。 **LDA (Local Density Approximation)**: 局部密度近似，DFT 中计算交换相关能的一种近似方法。 **Hybridization**: 杂化，指原子轨道组合形成新轨道的现象。
> 
> Two complementary phenomenological models exist for describing the phase transition to the ferroelectric state. The small B cation in ferroelectric perovskite oxides should always be able to lower its energy by shifting along one of the $\langle 111 \rangle$ directions (unless strain in the lattice stabilizes a different displacement). This leads to the characteristic “double-well” potential energy for the position of the small cation as a function of position between the oxygen anions, as shown in Figure 6. In the order-disorder model, the B cations are always displaced along one of the cube diagonals. At high temperature, all possible $\langle 111 \rangle$ directions are allowed, and at low temperature, the displacements all adopt either the same orientation (giving rhombohedral symmetry) or two or three preferred $\langle 111 \rangle$ directions (giving tetragonal or orthorhombic symmetry). This model predicts a large change in configurational entropy at the phase transition, which is not in fact observed, but the model may be applicable in higher-temperature regimes, away from the phase transition temperature. In the soft-mode model, the B cation displacement is stable only at low temperatures. Above the Curie temperature, there is a restoring force that tends to push the B cation back to the center if it is displaced. As the temperature is reduced, the phonon associated with this restoring force (the so-called “soft-mode” phonon) becomes weaker, until at the Curie temperature, the frequency is zero and the displacement occurs spontaneously. The large effect from changing the identity of the A cation is significant for our later discussion of multiferroic materials. The strain introduced by the A cation hybridization with O 2p electrons is long-range in nature and helps lead to the longrange ordering required for a phase transition. Therefore, the identity of the A cation can significantly affect the ferroelectricity of the material, even though the ferroelectric polarization results primarily from the displacement of the B cation relative to the oxygen cage. Also significant is the observation that the $\text{Ti}^{4+}$ ion is formally in a $d^0$ state so that the lowest unoccupied energy levels are d states that tend to hybridize with O 2p ions. Most other perovskite ferroelectrics also contain $d^0$ B cations, such as $\text{Zr}^{4+}$ and $\text{Nb}^{5+}$. In contrast, d-orbital occupancy is a requirement for the existence of magnetic moments and the consequent existence of magnetic ordering. 有两种互补的现象学模型用于描述向铁电状态的相变。铁电钙钛矿氧化物中的小 $B$ 阳离子总是能够通过沿 $\langle 111 \rangle$ 方向之一的位移来降低其能量（除非晶格中的应变稳定了不同的位移）。这导致了小阳离子在氧阴离子之间位置的特征性“双阱”势能，如图 6 所示。在有序-无序模型（order-disorder model）中，$B$ 阳离子总是沿立方体对角线之一位移。在高温下，所有可能的 $\langle 111 \rangle$ 方向都是允许的；在低温下，位移全部采用同一方向（产生三方对称性）或两种/三种首选 $\langle 111 \rangle$ 方向（产生四方或正交对称性）。该模型预测相变时构型熵会发生剧烈变化，但实际上并未观察到，不过该模型可能适用于远离相变温度的高温区域。在软模模型（soft-mode model）中，$B$ 阳离子的位移仅在低温下稳定。在居里温度之上，存在一个恢复力，如果 $B$ 阳离子发生位移，恢复力倾向于将其推回中心。随着温度降低，与该恢复力相关的声子（即所谓的“软模”声子）变得越来越弱，直到在居里温度时，频率变为零，位移自发发生。改变 $A$ 阳离子的种类所产生的巨大影响对于我们后续讨论多铁材料至关重要。$A$ 阳离子与 O 2p 电子杂化引入的应变在性质上是长程的，有助于实现相变所需的长程有序。因此，$A$ 阳离子的种类可以显著影响材料的铁电性，尽管铁电极化主要源于 $B$ 阳离子相对于氧笼的位移。同样重要的是观察到 $\text{Ti}^{4+}$ 离子形式上处于 $d^0$ 状态，因此最低未占据能级是倾向于与 O 2p 离子杂化的 d 状态。大多数其他钙钛矿铁电体也包含 $d^0$ $B$ 阳离子，如 $\text{Zr}^{4+}$ 和 $\text{Nb}^{5+}$。相比之下，d 轨道的占据是磁矩存在以及随之产生磁有序的必要条件。 **Double-well potential**: 双阱势，一种势能分布，具有两个局部极小值，常用于描述铁电体中离子的跳跃。 **Soft-mode**: 软模，指某种晶格振动模式的频率随温度降低而趋于零，从而导致结构不稳定性并引发相变的现象。 **$d^0$ state**: $d^0$ 状态，指过渡金属离子的 d 轨道完全没有电子，这通常有利于铁电畸变的产生。

> ## 4. Multiferroism
> 
> ## 4. 多铁性
> 
> The term multiferroism has been coined to describe materials in which two or all three of the properties ferroelectricity, ferromagnetism, and ferroelasticity occur in the same phase. This means that they have a spontaneous magnetization that can be reoriented by an applied magnetic field, a spontaneous polarization that can be reoriented by an applied electric field, and a spontaneous deformation that can be reoriented by an applied stress. Specific device applications that have been suggested for such “multiferroic” materials include multiplestate memory elements, electric-field-controlled ferromagnetic resonance devices, and transducers with magnetically modulated piezoelectricity. In addition, the ability to couple with either the magnetic or the electric polarization offers an extra degree of freedom in the design of conventional actuators, transducers, and storage devices. In this work, we focus on magnetoelectric multiferroics, which are simultaneously ferromagnetic and ferroelectric, with or without ferroelasticity. “多铁性”（multiferroism）一词被创造出来描述在同一相中同时出现铁电性、铁磁性和铁弹性（ferroelasticity）这两种或三种性质的材料。这意味着它们具有可通过外加磁场重新定向的自发磁化强度，可通过外加电场重新定向的自发极化，以及可通过外加应力重新定向的自发形变。为这类“多铁”材料建议的特定器件应用包括多态存储元件、电场控制的铁磁共振器件以及具有磁调制压电性的换能器。此外，能够与磁极化或电极化耦合为传统执行器、换能器和存储器件的设计提供了额外的自由度。在这项工作中，我们专注于磁电多铁体，它们同时具有铁磁性和铁电性，无论是否具有铁弹性。 **Ferroelasticity**: 铁弹性，指材料在应力作用下具有自发应变且可通过外应力切换的性质。
> 
> ### 4.1. History of Magnetoelectric Multiferroics
> 
> ### 4.1. 磁电多铁体的历史
> 
> #### 4.1.1. Boracites
> 
> #### 4.1.1. 硼酸盐（Boracites）
> 
> The first ferromagnetic ferroelectric material to be discovered was nickel iodine boracite, $\text{Ni}_3\text{B}_7\text{O}_{13}\text{I}$. This was followed by the synthesis of many more multiferroic boracite compounds, all of which have complex structures with many atoms per formula unit and more than one formula unit per unit cell. The large number of inter-ionic interactions in these materials prevented the isolation both of the essential factors causing multiferroicity and of the nature of the coupling between the magnetic, electric polarization, and structural order parameters. Nickel iodine boracite can be thought of as the “Rochelle salt” of magnetic ferroelectrics—invaluable for demonstrating proof of concept, but unlikely to find wide applicability or to contribute to our increased understanding in the field. 第一个被发现的铁磁铁电材料是碘镍硼酸盐 $\text{Ni}_3\text{B}_7\text{O}_{13}\text{I}$。随后又合成了很多多铁硼酸盐化合物，所有这些化合物都具有复杂结构，每个化学式单位包含许多原子，且每个晶胞包含多个化学式单位。这些材料中大量的离子间相互作用使得人们无法分离出导致多铁性的关键因素，也无法确定磁、电极化和结构序参数之间耦合的性质。碘镍硼酸盐可以被认为是磁性铁电体中的“罗谢盐”——对于概念验证来说极其宝贵，但不太可能获得广泛应用，也不太可能对该领域理解的深化做出重大贡献。
> 
> #### 4.1.2. Mixed Perovskites
> 
> #### 4.1.2. 混合钙钛矿
> 
> The search for other ferromagnetic ferroelectrics began in Russia in the 1950s, with the replacement of some of the $d^0$ B cations in ferroelectric perovskite oxides by magnetic $d^n$ cations. The first synthetic ferromagnetic ferroelectric material, $(1 - x)\text{Pb}(\text{Fe}_{2/3}\text{W}_{1/3})\text{O}_3 - x\text{Pb}(\text{Mg}_{1/2}\text{W}_{1/2})\text{O}_3$, was produced in the early 1960s using this approach. Here, the Mg and W ions are diamagnetic and cause the ferroelectricity, and the formally $d^5$ $\text{Fe}^{3+}$ ion is responsible for the magnetic ordering. Other examples include B-site-ordered $\text{Pb}_2(\text{CoW})\text{O}_6$, which is ferroelectric and ferromagnetic; B-site disordered $\text{Pb}_2(\text{FeTa})\text{O}_6$, which is ferroelectric and antiferromagnetic; and $\text{Pb}_2(\text{FeTa})\text{O}_6$ and $\text{Pb}_2(\text{FeTa})\text{O}_6$, which are both ferroelectric and antiferromagnetic, with weak ferromagnetism below around 10K. As a result of dilution of the magnetic ions, these materials all have rather low Curie or N'{e}el temperatures. 寻找其他铁磁铁电体的尝试始于20世纪50年代的俄罗斯，方法是用磁性 $d^n$ 阳离子取代铁电钙钛矿氧化物中的部分 $d^0$ $B$ 阳离子。20世纪60年代初，利用这种方法制备了第一种合成铁磁铁电材料 $(1 - x)\text{Pb}(\text{Fe}_{2/3}\text{W}_{1/3})\text{O}_3 - x\text{Pb}(\text{Mg}_{1/2}\text{W}_{1/2})\text{O}_3$。在这里，Mg 和 W 离子是抗磁性的并引起铁电性，而形式上为 $d^5$ 的 $\text{Fe}^{3+}$ 离子负责磁有序。其他例子包括 $B$ 位有序的 $\text{Pb}_2(\text{CoW})\text{O}_6$（铁电且铁磁）；$B$ 位无序的 $\text{Pb}_2(\text{FeTa})\text{O}_6$（铁电且反铁磁）；以及 $\text{Pb}_2(\text{FeTa})\text{O}_6$ 和 $\text{Pb}_2(\text{FeTa})\text{O}_6$（两者均铁电且反铁磁，并在 10K 以下表现出弱铁磁性）。由于磁性离子的稀释，这些材料的居里温度或尼尔温度（N'{e}el temperature）都相当低。 **N'{e}el temperature**: 尼尔温度，反铁磁体从顺磁相转变为反铁磁相的临界温度。

> #### 4.1.3. 其他钙钛矿

These include the manganites of the small rare earth elements and yttrium and a few compounds in which Bi is the large cation. > ### 4.2. Requirements for Magnetoelectric Multiferroicity
> 
> ### 4.2. 磁电多铁性的要求
> 
> By definition, for a material to be a magnetoelectric multiferroic, it must be simultaneously ferromagnetic and ferroelectric. Therefore, its allowed physical, structural, and electronic properties are restricted to those that occur both in ferromagnetic and in ferroelectric materials. In this section, we analyze a range of properties and discuss how these properties limit our choice of potential materials. We discuss those that are the limiting factors in preventing the simultaneous existence of ferromagnetism and ferroelectricity. 根据定义，一个材料要成为磁电多铁体，必须同时具有铁磁性和铁电性。因此，其允许的物理、结构和电子性质被限制在铁磁材料和铁电材料共同具备的性质范围内。在本节中，我们分析一系列性质，并讨论这些性质如何限制我们对潜在材料的选择。我们将讨论那些阻碍铁磁性和铁电性同时存在的限制因素。
> 
> #### 4.2.1. Symmetry
> 
> #### 4.2.1. 对称性
> 
> A primary requirement for the existence of ferroelectricity is a structural distortion from the prototypical high-symmetry phase that removes the center of symmetry and allows an electric polarization. There are 31 point groups that allow a spontaneous electric polarization, P, and 31 that allow a spontaneous magnetic polarization, M. Thirteen point groups (1, 2, 2′, m, m ′, 3, 3m ′, 4, 4m′m′, m′m2′, m′m′2′, 6, and 6m′m′) are found in both sets, allowing both properties to exist in the same phase. Although this represents a considerable reduction from the total number of possible crystal structures (the total number of Shubnikov point groups is 122), it is not an insignificant number, and many candidate materials that are not in fact ferromagnetic and ferroelectric exist in one of the allowed symmetries. Therefore, it is unlikely that symmetry considerations are responsible for the scarcity of ferromagnetic ferroelectric materials. 铁电性存在的一个首要要求是：从原型的具有高对称性的相发生结构畸变，从而消除对称中心并允许电极化。共有 31 个点群允许自发电极化 $P$，以及 31 个点群允许自发磁极化 $M$。在两组中共同发现了 13 个点群（1, 2, 2′, m, m ′, 3, 3m ′, 4, 4m′m′, m′m2′, m′m′2′, 6, 和 6m′m′），允许这两种性质在同一相中存在。虽然这相对于所有可能的晶体结构总数（Shubnikov 点群总数为 122 个）有了大幅减少，但这仍然是一个不小的数字，而且许多实际上并非铁磁铁电的候选材料就存在于这些允许的对称性中。因此，对称性考量不太可能是导致铁磁铁电材料稀缺的原因。
> 
> #### 4.2.2. Electrical Properties
> 
> #### 4.2.2. 电学性质
> 
> By definition, a ferroelectric material must be an insulator (otherwise, an applied electric field would inducing an electric current to flow, rather than causing an electrical polarization.) Ferromagnets, although not required to have specific electrical properties, are often metals. For example, the driving force for ferromagnetism in the elemental ferromagnets Fe, Co, and Ni and their alloys is the high density of states at the Fermi level, which also, of course, results in metallicity. Therefore, one could assume that the lack of the simultaneous occurrence of magnetic and ferroelectric ordering is simply the result of a dearth of magnetic insulators. However, if we extend our search to include ferrimagnets or weak ferromagnets (which have canted antiferromagnetic ordering, resulting in a weak magnetic moment in the direction of the canting), this argument no longer holds, because most ferrimagnets or weak ferromagnets are, in fact, insulators. In addition, there are also very few antiferromagnetic ferroelectrics, even though antiferromagnets are usually insulating materials. Therefore, it appears that we cannot blame the lack of magnetically ordered ferroelectrics simply on a shortage of magnetically ordered insulators. 根据定义，铁电材料必须是绝缘体（否则，外加电场会引起电流流动，而不是产生电极化）。铁磁体虽然不要求具有特定的电学性质，但通常是金属。例如，元素铁磁体 Fe, Co, Ni 及其合金产生铁磁性的驱动力是费米能级处的高态密度，这当然也导致了金属性。因此，人们可能会假设，磁有序和铁电有序不能同时出现仅仅是因为缺乏磁性绝缘体。然而，如果我们扩大搜索范围，将亚铁磁体或弱铁磁体（具有倾斜的反铁磁有序，从而在倾斜方向产生弱磁矩）纳入其中，这个论点就不再成立，因为大多数亚铁磁体或弱铁磁体实际上是绝缘体。此外，反铁磁铁电体也非常少，尽管反铁磁体通常是绝缘材料。因此，看来我们不能简单地将磁有序铁电体的缺失归咎于磁有序绝缘体的短缺。
> 
> #### 4.2.3. Chemistry: “$d^0$-ness”
> 
> #### 4.2.3. 化学： “$d^0$ 性质”
> 
> We noted in section 3.1 that the common perovskite oxide ferroelectric materials have a formal charge corresponding to the $d^0$ electron configuration on the B cation. Clearly, if there are no d electrons creating localized magnetic moments, then there can be no magnetic ordering of any type, either ferro-, ferri-, or antiferromagnetic. It appears however that, in most cases, as soon as the d shell on the small cation is partially occupied, the tendency for it to make a distortion that removes the center of symmetry is eliminated. This could be the result of a number of effects, including size, the tendency to undergo a different, more dominant distortion, electronic properties, magnetic properties, or some combination of the above. We now consider the likely influence of each of these factors. 我们在 3.1 节中注意到，常见的钙钛矿氧化物铁电材料在 $B$ 阳离子上具有对应于 $d^0$ 电子构型的形式电荷。显然，如果没有 d 电子产生局域磁矩，那么任何类型的磁有序（铁磁、亚铁磁或反铁磁）都不可能存在。然而，在大多数情况下，只要小阳离子的 d 壳层被部分占据，其产生消除对称中心之畸变的趋势就会消失。这可能是多种效应的结果，包括尺寸、产生另一种更主导畸变的倾向、电子性质、磁性质，或上述因素的某种组合。我们现在考虑这些因素可能产生的影响。
> 
> **Size of the Small Cation.** Are transition metal ions with occupied d shells simply too large to move away from the large space at the center of the oxygen octahedron? In this section, we compare the ionic radii of typical $d^0$ cations in perovskite ferroelectrics with those of typical $d^n$ cations in nonferroelectric perovskite oxides to see if this argument is correct. The Shannon ionic radii of some common $d^0$ small cations found in ferroelectric perovskite oxides are: $\text{Ti}^{4+}$, 74.5 pm; $\text{Nb}^{5+}$, 78 pm; and $\text{Zr}^{4+}$, 86 pm. Some representative $d^n$ cations that are found as the small cations in nonferroelectric perovskite oxides include $\text{Mn}^{3+}$ ($d^4$), $\text{Ti}^{3+}$ ($d^1$), and $\text{V}^{4+}$ ($d^1$) with radii of 78.5, 81, and 72 pm, respectively. Therefore, typical B-site cations with d electron occupation do not have systematically larger radii than typical $d^0$ B-site cations. We conclude that the size of the B cation is not the deciding factor in the existence or otherwise of ferroelectricity. **小阳离子的尺寸**。具有占据 d 壳层的过渡金属离子是否仅仅是因为太大，而无法离开氧八面体中心的宽敞空间？在本节中，我们将钙钛矿铁电体中典型的 $d^0$ 阳离子与非铁电钙钛矿氧化物中典型的 $d^n$ 阳离子的离子半径进行比较，以验证这一论点是否正确。在铁电钙钛矿氧化物中发现的一些常见 $d^0$ 小阳离子的 Shannon 离子半径为：$\text{Ti}^{4+}$ 74.5 pm；$\text{Nb}^{5+}$ 78 pm；$\text{Zr}^{4+}$ 86 pm。在非铁电钙钛矿氧化物中作为小阳离子的代表性 $d^n$ 阳离子包括 $\text{Mn}^{3+}$ ($d^4$)、$\text{Ti}^{3+}$ ($d^1$) 和 $\text{V}^{4+}$ ($d^1$)，半径分别为 78.5, 81 和 72 pm。因此，具有 d 电子占据的典型 $B$ 位阳离子并不比典型的 $d^0$ $B$ 位阳离子具有系统性更大的半径。我们得出结论：$B$ 阳离子的尺寸并非决定铁电性存在与否的决定性因素。
> 
> **Structural Distortions.** Ferroelectric materials must undergo a phase transition to a low-temperature phase that does not have a center of symmetry. This is most often achieved in conventional perovskite ferroelectrics by an off-center displacement of the small (B) cation from the center of the oxygen octahedron. However, for cations with certain d-orbital occupancies, the tendency to undergo a Jahn-Teller distortion is strong and will likely be the dominant structural effect. The Jahn-Teller distorted structure might have a lower driving force for off-center displacement than the otherwise undistorted structure. Examples of this effect are seen in lanthanum manganite, $\text{LaMnO}_3$, in which the $\text{Mn}^{3+}$ ion has a $d^4$ configuration, and yttrium titanate, $\text{YTiO}_3$, in which the $\text{Ti}^{3+}$ ion is $d^1$. Both materials have a d-type Jahn-Teller distortion, in which the elongated axes of the oxygen octahedra are oriented parallel to each other along the crystallographic c axis. Orbital ordering is observed to occur simultaneously with the Jahn-Teller ordering. $\text{LaMnO}_3$ is insulating and an A-type antiferromagnet, in which planes of ferromagnetically aligned $\text{Mn}^{3+}$ ions are aligned antiparallel to each other. $\text{YTiO}_3$ is a strongly correlated ferromagnetic Mott-Hubbard insulator. Neither material is ferroelectric. Note the relevance of the formal charge on the B cation here. In $\text{BaTiO}_3$, the actual occupation of the 3d electrons is closer to $d^1$ than to $d^0$ because of donation of charge density from the oxygen ligands. A $d^1$ cation should undergo a Jahn-Teller distortion. No Jahn-Teller distortion is observed in $\text{BaTiO}_3$ however, which is consistent with the $d^0$ formal charge. **结构畸变**。铁电材料必须经历相变，进入一个没有对称中心的低温相。在传统的钙钛矿铁电体中，这通常是通过小 ($B$) 阳离子从氧八面体中心发生离心位移来实现的。然而，对于具有某些 d 轨道占据的阳离子，产生 Jahn-Teller 畸变的趋势非常强烈，并且很可能是主导的结构效应。Jahn-Teller 畸变结构产生离心位移的驱动力可能低于未畸变结构。这种效应的例子可见于锰酸镧 $\text{LaMnO}_3$（$\text{Mn}^{3+}$ 离子为 $d^4$ 构型）和钛酸钇 $\text{YTiO}_3$（$\text{Ti}^{3+}$ 离子为 $d^1$）。这两种材料都具有 d 型 Jahn-Teller 畸变，其中氧八面体的伸长轴沿晶体 c 轴彼此平行。观察到轨道有序与 Jahn-Teller 有序同时发生。$\text{LaMnO}_3$ 是绝缘体且为 A 型反铁磁体，其中铁磁排列的 $\text{Mn}^{3+}$ 离子平面彼此反平行排列。$\text{YTiO}_3$ 是一种强关联铁磁 Mott-Hubbard 绝缘体。这两种材料都不是铁电体。注意这里 $B$ 阳离子形式电荷的相关性。在 $\text{BaTiO}_3$ 中，由于氧配体的电荷密度捐赠，3d 电子的实际占据情况更接近 $d^1$ 而非 $d^0$。$d^1$ 阳离子理应经历 Jahn-Teller 畸变。然而，在 $\text{BaTiO}_3$ 中未观察到 Jahn-Teller 畸变，这与 $d^0$ 形式电荷一致。 **Jahn-Teller distortion**: 姜-泰勒畸变，指非线性分子或晶体中，具有简并电子态的配合物通过结构畸变来降低能量并解除简并的现象。
> 
> **Magnetism Versus d-Orbital Occupancy.** It is clear that the existence of d electrons on the B-site cation reduces the tendency of perovskite structure oxides to display ferroelectricity. However, it is not clear whether it is merely the presence of d electron density or the influence of the magnetic spin polarization that is the dominant factor in creating this effect. Using first-principles density functional theory calculations, it is possible to answer this question by artificially removing the spin polarization in materials that are experimentally found to be magnetic and determining whether they then become ferroelectric. In sections 5.1 and 5.2, we perform such comparisons for some representative systems and show that removal of the magnetic polarization does not significantly affect the calculated phonon frequencies, suggesting that d-orbital occupancy, rather than d-electron magnetism, is dominant in overriding the tendency to undergo ferroelectric distortion. **磁性对比 d 轨道占据**。显然，$B$ 位阳离子上 d 电子的存在降低了钙钛矿结构氧化物表现出铁电性的倾向。然而，目前尚不清楚主导这一效应的因素仅仅是 d 电子密度的存在，还是磁自旋极化的影响。利用第一原理密度泛函理论计算，可以通过在实验发现为磁性的材料中人为地移除自旋极化，并确定它们是否随之变为铁电体来回答这个问题。在 5.1 和 5.2 节中，我们对一些代表性系统进行了此类比较，结果表明移除磁极化并不会显著影响计算出的声子频率，这表明 d 轨道的占据（而非 d 电子磁性）在抑制铁电畸变倾向方面起主导作用。
