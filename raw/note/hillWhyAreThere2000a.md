> ## 一、引言
> 
> 多铁性材料，特别是磁电多铁性材料，因其在同一相中同时具备**铁磁性**和**铁电性**，在基础物理和器件应用（如多态存储、新型传感器）方面展现出巨大潜力。然而，自然界和实验室中发现的此类材料极为稀少。本文献《Why Are There so Few Magnetic Ferroelectrics?》正是针对这一核心矛盾展开的经典论述。作者Nicola A. Hill通过第一性原理计算，从电子结构和化学键合的角度，系统性地探究了磁性（特别是铁磁性）与铁电性难以共存的根本物理原因，并提出了著名的“**d0规则**”，同时分析了能够打破该规则、实现多铁性的特殊机制。本文是理解多铁性材料物理限制和设计新型多铁材料的基石，其核心价值在于将复杂的多铁性共存问题，解构为电子排布、化学键合和结构畸变等基本物理化学要素的竞争。
> 
> ## 二、论文结构与逻辑概览
> 
> 本文遵循“**提出问题 → 分析矛盾 → 寻找例外 → 总结规律**”的逻辑链条。
> 
> 1.  **引言（第1节）**：分别概述铁电材料和磁性材料的发展历程与应用，指出磁电多铁性材料兼具两者优势，但极为稀少，由此引出核心问题。
> 2.  **基础理论（第2、3节）**：分别回顾铁磁性和铁电性的微观理论，为后续分析矛盾奠定基础。关键点在于指出铁磁性需要部分填充的d轨道，而典型铁电性需要d⁰构型。
> 3.  **矛盾分析（第4节）**：系统性地从对称性、电学性质、化学键合（“d0-ness”）等角度分析铁磁性与铁电性难以共存的原因，初步得出d轨道占据是决定性因素的推论。
> 4.  **案例研究（第5节）**：通过第一性原理，深入计算并对比了两种已知的多铁性材料：
>     -   **BiMnO₃**：铁磁+（可能）铁电。探究其铁电性源于**Bi 6p-O 2p**的共价键合作用。
>     -   **YMnO₃**：反铁磁+铁电。探究其铁电性源于其特殊的六方结构。
> 5.  **总结与展望（第6节）**：总结了“d0规则”及其例外情况，并提出了未来寻找多铁性材料的方向，如非Jahn-Teller离子和非氧化物体系。
>

>     -   **深度解析**：这是全文讨论的晶体结构基础。绝大部分经典铁电体和本文研究的材料都属于此结构。B位离子位于氧八面体的中心，其微小位移是产生铁电性的直接来源。
> -   **图6：铁电体中B位阳离子势能的特征双阱**
>     
>     -   **内容描述**：展示B位阳离子沿氧八面体中心位移时的能量变化，呈现“W”形。
>     -   **深度解析**：这是**铁电性位移型相变**的直观模型。在高温顺电相，热动能足以使离子在中心位置附近振动；在低温铁电相，离子会“冻结”在其中一个能量最低的偏离中心的位置，从而产生自发极化。这个双阱势的存在与否，是判断铁电性的关键。
> -   **图7-11：立方顺磁LaMnO₃和BiMnO₃的电子结构对比**
>     
>     -   **内容描述**：包括**态密度(DOS)图**、**能带结构图**、沿Γ-X轴的能带图以及**紧束缚拟合**结果。
>     -   **深度解析**：这是计算部分的核心。对比揭示了BiMnO₃与典型非铁电材料LaMnO₃的关键区别：BiMnO₃在-10到-12 eV处存在一个**Bi 6s**轨道产生的孤立能带，且其**Bi 6p**轨道与**O 2p**轨道存在强共价杂化，这改变了Mn-O之间的相互作用，削弱了反铁磁超交换，并驱动了铁电畸变。紧束缚分析量化了这种差异，证明Bi-O杂化是BiMnO₃多铁性的起源。
> -   **图12-13：立方铁磁LaMnO₃和BiMnO₃的电子结构对比**
>     
>     -   **内容描述**：自旋极化的态密度和能带结构。
>     -   **深度解析**：这些图表明，引入铁磁性后，两者在顺磁态下观察到的本质差异依然存在。BiMnO₃的少数自旋导带具有明显的Bi 6p特征，这为理解其可能存在的“电子铁电性”提供了线索。
> -   **图14：不稳定声子模式的本征矢**
>     
>     -   **内容描述**：(a) 导致铁电位移的模式，A位离子（Bi/La）与氧笼反向运动。(b) 非铁电模式，仅涉及赤道面氧原子的运动。
>     -   **深度解析**：此图直观地展示了通过计算预测的晶格不稳定性。BiMnO₃的铁电不稳定性远强于LaMnO₃，且其模式中A位离子（Bi）的参与度极高，再次证实了Bi-O共价键对铁电性的驱动作用，这与传统BaTiO₃中Ti-O杂化驱动机制完全不同。
> -   **图15：六方钙钛矿结构**
>     
>     -   **内容描述**：展示了YMnO₃的六方结构，Mn离子是5配位，形成三角双锥。
>     -   **深度解析**：该图解释了YMnO₃成为多铁材料的原因。由于其小的A位离子（Y³⁺），无法稳定立方钙钛矿结构，转而形成六方结构。这种结构本身就缺乏中心对称性，为铁电性提供了结构上的“双阱势”，从而绕过了d0规则的限制。
> -   **图16-18：立方顺磁和铁磁YMnO₃的电子结构对比**
>     
>     -   **内容描述**：展示了YMnO₃在假设的立方结构下的能带和态密度，并与LaMnO₃对比。
>     -   **深度解析**：计算表明，YMnO₃和LaMnO₃的电子结构高度相似，Y-O键合主要是离子性的，没有Bi-O那样的强共价杂化。这有力地证明了YMnO₃的铁电性并非源于电子机制，而是源于其六方晶格的结构效应，即尺寸效应是其形成不同结构并最终导致多铁性的根本原因。

> ## 四、正文核心内容剖析
> 
> ### 4.1 研究背景与问题提出
> 
> 文章开篇梳理了铁电体和磁性材料这两条独立发展的历史脉络。铁电体的研究从罗息盐开始，到BaTiO₃等钙钛矿氧化物达到高峰，其应用依赖于高介电常数、压电性和极化翻转。磁性材料则经历了复兴，特别是巨磁阻效应的发现，使其在信息存储领域大放异彩，其核心在于利用了电子的自旋属性。**多铁性磁电材料**正是这两种“智慧”的结晶，有望实现电场控制磁性或磁场控制电极化，带来器件设计的革命。
> 
> 然而，现实是此类材料屈指可数。这引出了本文的核心问题：**为什么铁磁性和铁电性在单一材料中共存如此困难？**
> 
> ### 4.2 矛盾根源：方法论与模型分析
> 
> 作者从铁磁性和铁电性的微观理论出发，揭示了其内在矛盾。
> 
> -   **铁磁性的要求**：无论是基于局域磁矩的Weiss理论，还是基于能带的Stoner理论，其核心都是**电子间的交换作用**。这要求材料中存在未配对的电子，即过渡金属离子必须具有**部分填充的d轨道**（如Fe³⁺的d⁵，Mn³⁺的d⁴）。
> -   **铁电性的要求**：以BaTiO₃和PbTiO₃为例，第一性原理计算表明，B位离子（Ti⁴⁺）与O²⁻之间的**共价杂化**是稳定铁电畸变的关键。在这些经典铁电体中，B位离子的价电子构型均为**d⁰**（即d轨道全空）。全空的d轨道是接受O 2p电子形成共价键的必要条件。
> 
> 由此，一个尖锐的矛盾出现了：**磁性需要d轨道有电子，而典型的铁电性机制需要d轨道为空（d⁰）**。作者将此称为“d0-ness”要求。
> 
> ### 4.3 实验与数据验证
> 
> 为验证这一猜想，作者使用第一性原理**密度泛函理论(DFT)** 计算，对比研究了两种已知的多铁性钙钛矿锰氧化物。

>

>     -   **结论**：Bi³⁺离子具有活性的6s²孤对电子，其与O 2p的共价作用提供了额外的结构驱动力，克服了B位Mn³⁺(d⁴)对铁电位移的抑制，从而打破了“d0规则”。
> 2.  **YMnO₃的案例（对比LaMnO₃）**：
>     
>     -   **实验现象**：YMnO₃在低温下是六方结构，具有反铁磁性(AFM)和铁电性(FE)；而LaMnO₃是正交结构，仅有反铁磁性。

>     -   **结论**：YMnO₃的多铁性并非源于电子机制，而是源于**结构效应**。由于Y³⁺半径很小，钙钛矿结构的容忍因子极小，导致其无法稳定在立方相，转而形成一种具有5配位Mn离子的六方结构。这个六方结构本身是非中心对称的，为铁电性提供了“结构双阱势”，从而绕开了d0规则。

> 1.  **问题：什么是“多铁性”？它和“磁电耦合”是一回事吗？**

> 2.  **问题：为什么绝缘体是铁电性存在的必要条件？**
>     
>     -   **解答**：铁电体被定义为具有可被外电场翻转的自发极化。如果材料是金属，其内部有大量自由电子，这些自由电子会迅速移动，完美屏蔽掉任何外加电场或内部电偶极子产生的电场，从而无法在材料内部建立和维持宏观的电极化。因此，所有的铁电体本质上都必须是绝缘体。
> 3.  **问题：文中提到的“Jahn-Teller畸变”是什么？为什么它不利于铁电性？**
>     
>     -   **解答**：**Jahn-Teller(JT)效应**是指，对于具有简并电子基态的非线性分子或离子，其几何结构会发生畸变以消除简并性，降低体系能量。例如，Mn³⁺(d⁴)在八面体晶体场中，其e_g轨道上有一个电子，导致了轨道简并。为消除简并，MnO₆八面体会发生拉长或压扁的畸变。对于铁电性而言，这是一种“竞争性”畸变。体系通过JT畸变降低能量后，再进行铁电性所需的偏离中心位移的驱动力就可能减弱甚至消失，因为一种不稳定性已经通过另一种方式被“冻结”了。

> ## 四、正文核心内容剖析
> 
> ### 4.1 铁磁性与铁电性：两种不相容的“天赋”
> 
> Hill教授首先带我们回顾了两种性质的基础。
> 
> -   **铁磁性**：其微观根源是**量子力学交换作用**，它使电子自旋平行排列以降低能量。这需要原子具有未成对的d电子，即过渡金属离子处于**dⁿ（n>0）** 状态。Stoner理论进一步指出，要实现宏观铁磁性，费米能级必须落在高态密度的窄d带上，这使得许多铁磁体同时也是金属，如Fe、Co、Ni。
>     
> -   **铁电性**：在钙钛矿氧化物中，其微观根源是B位离子的d轨道与O的2p轨道**杂化**。这种杂化会削弱短程排斥力，使B位离子偏离中心，形成一个**双势阱**，从而产生可翻转的自发极化。Cohen和Krakauer对BaTiO₃和PbTiO₃的经典计算表明，这种杂化稳定铁电畸变的核心前提是B位离子的d轨道为空，即**d⁰态**。例如，Ti⁴⁺、Zr⁴⁺、Nb⁵⁺都是d⁰离子。
>     
> 
> **核心矛盾**在此刻尖锐地浮现：**磁性要求dⁿ，而标准铁电性要求d⁰。这是同一个离子无法同时满足的条件。**
> 
> ### 4.2 多铁性探查：为何“d⁰规则”如此坚固？
> 
> 面对“为何磁性铁电体如此稀少”的标题问题，Hill教授带领我们进行了一场排除法探案：
> 
> 1.  **对称性要求**：虽然同时允许自发极化和磁化的点群有限（13个），但并非微不足道，所以不是主要瓶颈。
> 2.  **电学性质要求**：铁电体必须是绝缘体，否则极化会被自由电荷屏蔽。虽然许多铁磁体是金属，但存在大量反铁磁绝缘体。然而，反铁磁铁电体也极少，说明绝缘性也不是主因。
> 3.  **化学成键的“d⁰-ness”**：这是真正的“罪魁祸首”。一旦B位离子有d电子，几种效应会共同抑制铁电畸变：
>     -   **Jahn-Teller效应**：对于d¹、d⁴等电子构型的离子，其电子云不对称分布，会驱动一个更强烈的、使氧八面体发生拉长或压扁的畸变（Jahn-Teller畸变），从而“抢占”了铁电畸变的发生空间。
>     -   **d电子填充本身**：即使在非Jahn-Teller离子中，d电子的存在似乎也削弱了使离子偏离中心的驱动力。Hill教授通过计算技巧，人为地去除了磁性（使上下自旋密度相等），发现铁电不稳定性并未恢复。这表明**抑制铁电性的是d电子的占据本身，而非由其引发的磁序**。
> 
> ### 4.3 打破规则的两种策略：两个案例研究
> 
> “d⁰规则”是坚不可摧的吗？不，论文通过两个案例展示了两种巧妙的“破局”之道。
> 
> #### 案例一：BiMnO₃——化学驱动的“声东击西”
> 
> BiMnO₃是罕见的铁磁钙钛矿，Mn³⁺为d⁴态，本应受Jahn-Teller效应主导而成为反铁磁绝缘体。然而，它却表现出铁磁性和可能的铁电性。
> 
> -   **破局关键**：**A位Bi³⁺离子的6s²孤对电子**。
> -   **机制**：Hill教授的紧束缚分析（表1-2）和声子计算（表3，图14a）揭示，Bi的6s和6p轨道与O的2p轨道存在显著的**共价杂化**。这种杂化形成了一个由Bi离子主导的巨大铁电不稳定模式，其能量尺度远大于由Mn离子可能驱动的任何畸变。形象地说，当“主角”（Mn离子）无法表演时，原本是“配角”的A位离子（Bi离子）站了出来，主导了铁电畸变。BiMnO₃的铁电性是由A位驱动的，绕开了对B位d⁰的要求。
> 
> #### 案例二：YMnO₃——结构拓扑的“另辟蹊径”
> 
> 与LaMnO₃不同，YMnO₃在常压下稳定于六方结构，而非立方钙钛矿。
> 
> -   **破局关键**：**由小尺寸Y³⁺离子稳定化的六方结构**。
> -   **机制**：计算表明，Y和La一样，与O的成键是离子性的，没有Bi那样的共价杂化。区别在于离子半径：Y³⁺远小于La³⁺。这个小尺寸的A位离子无法支撑立方钙钛矿结构，转而形成了一种Mn离子被5个氧离子包围的层状六方结构（图15）。这种**5配位三角双锥**环境本身就缺乏中心对称性，为铁电畸变提供了天然的“双势阱”。因此，YMnO₃的铁电性是由其独特的结构拓扑决定的，同样绕开了对B位d⁰的依赖。

> The formal study of ferroelectric materials began over a century ago when anomalously large piezoelectric constants were observed in the material known as Rochelle salt.1 对铁电材料的正式研究始于一个多世纪前，当时在一种被称为罗谢尔盐的材料中观察到了异常大的压电常数。1 **Piezoelectric**: 压电性，指某些材料在受到机械应力时产生电荷，或在施加电场时产生机械变形的现象。
> 
> Over the following few decades, hysteresis was reported in the polarization, dielectric function, and strain of this material,2 and theories emerged to account for its anomalous dielectric, piezoelectric, elastic, and phase transition behaviors.3 在随后的几十年里，人们报告了这种材料在极化、介电函数和应变方面的滞后现象，2并出现了理论来解释其异常的介电、压电、弹性和相变行为。3 **Hysteresis**: 滞后现象，指物理系统的状态不仅依赖于当前输入，还依赖于其历史状态的现象，常表现为闭合回线。 **Dielectric**: 介电体，指在外加电场作用下能被极化的绝缘材料。
> 
> In the 1930s, another group of materials, based on KH2PO4, was discovered to be ferroelectric,4 and a theory of the ferroelectric phase transition was developed.5 在20世纪30年代，另一组基于KH2PO4的材料被发现是铁电体，4并且发展出了铁电相变的理论。5 **Phase transition**: 相变，指物质从一种相（如固、液、气）或结构状态转变为另一种相的过程。
> 
> Ferroelectricity was observed in BaTiO3 and related perovskite-structure oxides in the 1940s,6 stimulating research in both discovery and characterization of ferroelectric materials. 20世纪40年代，在BaTiO3和相关的钙钛矿结构氧化物中观察到了铁电性，6这激发了对铁电材料的发现和表征研究。 **Perovskite**: 钙钛矿，指一类具有与CaTiO3相似晶体结构的材料，通式为ABX3。
> 
> There has been a great increase in the understanding of the fundamental physics of ferroelectrics over the last 10 years,7 which, in turn, has contributed to the optimization of materials for specific device applications. 在过去的10年里，对铁电体基础物理的理解有了很大的提高，7这反过来又有助于针对特定器件应用进行材料优化。
> 
> Today, ferroelectrics are used in a range of applications, including transducers and actuators (because of their piezoelectricity), capacitors (because of their high dielectric permittivity), and memory applications (because their hysteresis properties result in two stable states of opposite polarization). 如今，铁电体被用于一系列应用中，包括换能器和执行器（因其压电性）、电容器（因其高介电常数）以及存储器应用（因为其滞后特性导致存在两个相反的极化稳定状态）。 **Transducer**: 换能器，指将一种形式的能量转换成另一种形式的能量的器件。 **Actuator**: 执行器，指将输入信号（通常是电信号）转换成机械运动的器件。 **Capacitor**: 电容器，指储存电荷和电能的被动电子元件。 **Dielectric permittivity**: 介电常数，衡量材料在电场中储存电能的能力的物理量。
> 
> The past few years have also seen a flurry of activity in the study of magnetic materials, which previously (according to a recent article in Physics Today) had an image of “musty physics laboratories peopled by old codgers with iron filings under their nails”.8 过去几年，磁性材料的研究也掀起了一股热潮，而此前（据《今日物理》最近的一篇文章称）它们给人的印象是“布满灰尘的物理实验室，里面挤满了指甲缝里嵌着铁屑的老家伙”。8
> 
> However advances in atomic- and nanoscale growth and characterization techniques over the past few years have led to the production of modern magnetic materials that reveal a range of fascinating phenomena. 然而，过去几年在原子和纳米尺度的生长与表征技术方面的进步，促成了现代磁性材料的生产，这些材料展示了一系列引人入胜的现象。
> 
> These phenomena derive from the fact that electrons have spin as well as charge, giving an extra level of complexity to the physics and an extra degree of freedom in device design. 这些现象源于电子除了电荷外还有自旋这一事实，这为物理学增加了额外的复杂性，也为器件设计增加了额外的自由度。 **Spin**: 自旋，指电子的内禀角动量，是量子力学中的一个基本性质，与磁矩相关。
> 
> Today, the science and technology of magnetism have undergone a renaissance, driven both by the urge to understand the new physics and by demand from industry for better materials.9 如今，磁学的科学与技术经历了一场复兴，这既源于理解新物理学的强烈愿望，也源于工业界对更好材料的需求。9
> 
> The potential impact on society of the recent advances in magnetic materials is extensive. 磁性材料的最新进展对社会的潜在影响是广泛的。
> 
> Some examples, such as dramatic increases in data storage density, are already evident. 一些例子，例如数据存储密度的急剧增加，已经显而易见。
> 
> The discoveries of giant10 and colossal11 magnetoresistive materials, in which magnetic fields cause order of magnitude changes in conductivity, have been particularly significant, and sensors, read heads, and memories based on giant magnetoresistive magnetic multilayers are already commercially available. 巨磁阻10和庞磁阻11材料的发现尤其重要，在这些材料中，磁场会引起电导率数量级的变化。基于巨磁阻磁性多层膜的传感器、读取磁头和存储器已经商业化。 **Giant magnetoresistive (GMR)**: 巨磁阻，指某些材料在磁场作用下电阻发生巨大变化的现象。 **Colossal magnetoresistive (CMR)**: 庞磁阻，指某些材料（通常是锰氧化物）在磁场作用下电阻发生比巨磁阻效应更显著的变化的现象。
> 
> Other proposed technologies are still under development. 其他提出的技术仍在开发中。
> 
> These include magnetic valves, which allow current to pass only when adjacent ferromagnetic layers are oriented by an applied magnetic field, and spin transistors,12 in which spin-polarized currents are biased and controlled by magnetic rather than electric fields. 这些技术包括磁阀和自旋晶体管，磁阀仅在相邻的铁磁层被外加磁场定向时才允许电流通过，而自旋晶体管12中的自旋极化电流则通过磁场而非电场进行偏置和控制。 **Spin transistor**: 自旋晶体管，一种基于电子自旋而非电荷来操作和传输信息的晶体管器件。
> 
> In addition, the long relaxation times and persistent coherence of electron spins are being investigated for use in quantum computing.13 此外，电子自旋的长弛豫时间和持久的相干性正在被研究用于量子计算。13 **Quantum computing**: 量子计算，一种利用量子力学现象（如叠加和纠缠）来执行计算的技术。
> 
> This paper explores a somewhat obscure class of materials known as magnetoelectric multiferroics, which are simultaneously ferroelectric and ferromagnetic (or at least show some kind of magnetic ordering). 本文探讨了一类较为鲜为人知的材料，即磁电多铁性材料，它们同时具有铁电性和铁磁性（或者至少表现出某种磁有序）。 **Magnetoelectric**: 磁电效应，指材料中磁场可以诱导电极化，或电场可以诱导磁化的现象。
> 
> Such materials have all the potential applications of both their parent ferroelectric and ferromagnetic materials. 这类材料兼具其母体铁电材料和铁磁材料的所有潜在应用。
> 
> In addition, a whole range of new applications can be envisaged.14 此外，还可以设想一系列全新的应用。14
> 
> First, the ability to couple to either the electric or the magnetic polarization allows an additional degree of freedom in device design. 首先，能够与电极化或磁极化耦合的能力为器件设计提供了额外的自由度。
> 
> Other applications include multiple-state memory elements, in which data is stored both in the electric and the magnetic polarizations, or novel memory media, which might allow the writing of a ferroelectric data bit and the reading of the magnetic field generated by association. 其他应用包括多态存储元件，其中数据同时存储在电极化和磁极化中，或新型存储介质，可能允许写入铁电数据位，并通过关联读取由此产生的磁场。
> 
> Aside from the potential applications, the fundamental physics of multiferroic materials is rich and fascinating. 除了潜在的应用，多铁性材料的基础物理也是丰富而迷人的。
> 
> In this work, we focus on one aspect of the physics of multiferroic magnetoelectrics, which is the puzzling question of why so few exist. 在这项工作中，我们专注于多铁性磁电体物理学的一个方面，即为什么这类材料如此稀少的令人费解的问题。

> # 2. Ferromagnets (and Other Magnetic Materials)
> 
> # 2. 铁磁体（及其他磁性材料）
> 
> A ferromagnetic material is one that undergoes a phase transition from a high-temperature phase that does not have a macroscopic magnetic moment to a low-temperature phase that has a spontaneous magnetization even in the absence of an applied magnetic field. 铁磁材料是一种经历相变的材料，从没有宏观磁矩的高温相转变为即使在没有外加磁场时也具有自发磁化强度的低温相。
> 
> The macroscopic magnetization is caused by the magnetic dipole moments of the atoms (which are aligned randomly in the high-temperature paramagnetic phase, Figure 1a) tending to line up in the same direction, as shown in Figure 1b. 宏观磁化是由原子的磁偶极矩（在高温顺磁相中随机排列，图1a）趋向于沿同一方向排列引起的，如图1b所示。 **Paramagnetic**: 顺磁性，指材料在无外加磁场时不表现宏观磁性，在外加磁场下被弱吸引，磁矩方向与外加磁场方向一致的现象。
> 
> The spontaneous magnetization means that ferromagnetic materials tend to concentrate magnetic flux density (they have a large positive permeability), which leads to their widespread use in applications such as transformer cores, permanent magnets, and electromagnets, for which large magnetic fields are required. 自发磁化意味着铁磁材料倾向于集中磁通密度（它们具有很大的正磁导率），这导致它们广泛用于需要大磁场的应用中，如变压器铁芯、永磁体和电磁铁。 **Magnetic flux density**: 磁通密度，也叫磁感应强度，描述磁场中某点的强弱和方向的物理量。 **Permeability**: 磁导率，衡量材料响应外加磁场产生磁化能力的物理量。
> 
> As-prepared samples of ferromagnets often lack a macroscopic magnetization because of the presence of domains of magnetization oriented in different directions. 刚制备好的铁磁体样品通常缺乏宏观磁化，因为存在不同取向的磁化畴。 **Domain**: 磁畴，指铁磁材料内部自发磁化方向一致的小区域。
> 
> The subsequent alignment and reorientation of the domains upon the application of a magnetic field, H, results in a hysteresis in the magnetization and flux density, B, as shown in Figure 2. 随后，在施加磁场H时，磁畴的排列和重新取向导致了磁化强度和磁通密度B的滞后，如图2所示。
> 
> The ferromagnetic material starts in an unmagnetized state, and as the field is increased in the positive direction, the magnetic induction increases from zero to the saturation induction, Bs. 铁磁材料起始于未磁化状态，随着磁场在正方向增加，磁感应强度从零增加到饱和磁感应强度Bs。
> 
> When the field is reduced to zero after saturation, the induction decreases from Bs to Br, the residual induction, or retentivity. 饱和后，当磁场减小到零时，磁感应强度从Bs减小到Br，即剩余磁感应强度，或称剩磁。 **Retentivity**: 剩磁，指铁磁体在磁化场撤去后仍能保留的磁感应强度。
> 
> The reversed field required to reduce the induction to zero is called the coercivity, Hc. 将磁感应强度减小到零所需的反向磁场称为矫顽力Hc。 **Coercivity**: 矫顽力，指将铁磁体的磁感应强度降为零所需施加的反向磁场强度。
> 
> The suitability of ferromagnetic materials for particular applications is determined largely from characteristics shown by their hysteresis loops. 铁磁材料对特定应用的适用性主要由其磁滞回线显示的特性决定。 **Hysteresis loop**: 磁滞回线，表示铁磁体在交变磁场中磁化强度（或磁感应强度）随磁场强度变化而变化的闭合曲线。
> 
> For example, a square-shaped hysteresis loop, with two stable magnetization states, is suitable for magnetic data storage, whereas a small hysteresis loop that is easily cycled between states is suitable for a transformer core with a rapidly switching field direction. 例如，具有两个稳定磁化状态的矩形磁滞回线适用于磁数据存储，而易于在状态之间循环的小磁滞回线则适用于具有快速切换场方向的变压器铁芯。
> 
> Although ferromagnetic ferroelectric materials are the focus of this work, we will also consider ferroelectric materials displaying other types of magnetic ordering such as antiferromagnetism, ferrimagnetism, or weak ferromagnetism. 尽管铁磁铁电材料是这项工作的重点，我们也将考虑显示其他类型磁有序的铁电材料，如反铁磁性、亚铁磁性或弱铁磁性。 **Antiferromagnetism**: 反铁磁性，指相邻原子或离子的磁矩反向平行排列，导致宏观净磁矩为零的现象。 **Ferrimagnetism**: 亚铁磁性，指相邻原子或离子的磁矩反向平行排列，但因大小不等而导致存在宏观净磁矩的现象。
> 
> In antiferromagnetic materials, the constituent atoms or ions have magnetic dipole moments that are ordered antiparallel to each other (Figure 1c). 在反铁磁材料中，组成原子或离子的磁偶极矩彼此反向平行有序排列（图1c）。
> 
> Antiferromagnets do not find wide application in magnetic technologies because they do not have a net overall magnetization. 反铁磁体在磁技术中没有广泛的应用，因为它们没有净宏观磁化强度。
> 
> Ferrimagnets are somewhat like antiferromagnets, in that the dipoles align antiparallel; however, some of the dipole moments are larger than others, so the material has a net overall magnetic moment. 亚铁磁体有些类似于反铁磁体，其偶极子也是反向平行排列；然而，其中一些偶极矩大于其他偶极矩，因此材料具有净宏观磁矩。
> 
> As a result, ferrimagnets, like ferromagnets, tend to concentrate magnetic flux in their interiors and, therefore, to have wide applicability. 因此，亚铁磁体像铁磁体一样，倾向于在其内部集中磁通量，从而具有广泛的应用性。
> 
> The term “weak ferromagnetism” is used to describe antiferromagnets with a small canting of the spins away from antiparallel alignment. “弱铁磁性”一词用于描述自旋略微偏离反向平行排列的反铁磁体。 **Weak ferromagnetism**: 弱铁磁性，指由于反铁磁材料中自旋的微小倾斜而产生的微弱净磁矩。
> 
> This results in a small net magnetization, usually at low temperature. 这导致了一种小的净磁化强度，通常在低温下出现。
> 
> ## 2.1. Theories of Ferromagnetism: What Makes Ferromagnets Ferromagnetic?
> 
> ## 2.1. 铁磁性理论：什么使铁磁体具有铁磁性？
> 
> There are two phenomenological theories of ferromagnetism that have been successful in explaining many of the properties of ferromagnets: the Curie-Weiss localized-moment theory and the Stoner band theory of ferromagnetism. 有两种成功的铁磁性唯象理论解释了铁磁体的许多性质：居里-外斯局域矩理论和斯托纳能带铁磁性理论。 **Curie-Weiss law**: 居里-外斯定律，描述顺磁体磁化率随温度变化关系的定律，在居里温度以上适用。 **Stoner band theory**: 斯托纳能带理论，一种基于能带结构解释金属中铁磁性的理论。
> 
> In a classic 1907 paper,15 Weiss postulated that an internal “molecular field” acts in ferromagnetic materials to align the magnetic moments parallel to each other. 在1907年的一篇经典论文中，15外斯假设铁磁材料中存在一个内部的“分子场”，使磁矩彼此平行排列。
> 
> We now understand the origin of this molecular field to be the quantum mechanical exchange energy, which causes electrons with parallel spins (and therefore parallel magnetic moments) to have a lower energy than electrons with antiparallel spins, all other factors being equal. 我们现在理解这个分子场的起源是量子力学交换能，在所有其他因素相同的情况下，它使具有平行自旋（因此具有平行磁矩）的电子比具有反平行自旋的电子具有更低的能量。 **Exchange energy**: 交换能，指由于电子波函数的反对称性而产生的量子力学效应，导致自旋平行和反平行排列的电子系统具有能量差异。
> 
> Below the Curie temperature, Tc, the molecular field is so strong that it magnetizes the substance even in the absence of an external applied field. 在居里温度Tc以下，分子场非常强，以至于即使没有外加磁场，它也能使物质磁化。 **Curie temperature**: 居里温度，指铁磁体从铁磁相转变为顺磁相的临界温度。
> 
> At high enough temperatures, the thermal energy, kT, is larger than the alignment energy of the molecular field, resulting in the random orientation of the magnetic moments and paramagnetic behavior. 在足够高的温度下，热能kT大于分子场的排列能，导致磁矩随机取向和顺磁行为。
>

> 
> There is a divergence in the magnetic susceptibility at the Curie temperature when the magnetic moments align spontaneously even in the absence of an applied magnetic field. 在居里温度处，磁化率出现发散，此时磁矩即使在没有外加磁场时也会自发排列。 **Magnetic susceptibility**: 磁化率，衡量材料对外加磁场磁化响应程度的无量纲物理量。

> The Weiss localized-moment theory breaks down in one important aspect however: it is unable to account for the measured values of the magnetic moment per atom in some ferromagnetic materials, particularly in ferromagnetic metals. 然而，外斯局域矩理论在一个重要方面存在缺陷：它无法解释某些铁磁材料，特别是铁磁金属中测得的每个原子的磁矩值。

> There are two significant discrepancies. 存在两个显著的差异。
> 
> First, according to the Weiss theory, the magnetic dipole moment on each atom or ion should be the same in both the ferromagnetic and paramagnetic phases. 首先，根据外斯理论，在铁磁相和顺磁相中，每个原子或离子上的磁偶极矩应该是相同的。
> 
> Experimentally this is not the case. 实验上情况并非如此。
> 
> Second, in the localized-moment theory, the magnetic dipole moment on each atom or ion should correspond to an integer number of electrons. 其次，在局域矩理论中，每个原子或离子上的磁偶极矩应对应于整数个电子。
> 
> Again, this is not observed experimentally. 再次，这在实验上也没有观察到。
> 
> To explain these data we need to use the Stoner band theory of ferromagnetism. 为了解释这些数据，我们需要使用斯托纳能带铁磁性理论。
> 
> In the Stoner theory,16 the fundamental driving force for ferromagnetism is again the exchange energy, which is minimized if all of the electrons have the same spin. 在斯托纳理论中，16铁磁性的基本驱动力再次是交换能，如果所有电子具有相同的自旋，交换能最小。
> 
> Opposing the alignment of spins is the increased band energy involved in transferring electrons from the lowest band states (occupied equally with up- and down-spin electrons) to band states of higher energy. 与自旋排列相对抗的是将电子从最低能带态（被自旋向上和向下电子均等占据）转移到更高能带态所涉及的能带能量增加。 **Band energy**: 能带能量，指固体中电子占据的能带的总能量。
> 
> This band energy prevents simple metals from being ferromagnetic. 这种能带能量阻止了简单金属成为铁磁性的。
> 
> In the elemental ferromagnetic transition metals Fe, Ni, and Co, the Fermi energy lies in a region of overlapping 3d and 4s bands, as shown schematically in Figure 3. 在元素铁磁过渡金属Fe、Ni和Co中，费米能级位于3d和4s能带重叠的区域，如图3所示。 **Fermi energy**: 费米能级，指在绝对零度下，电子占据的最高能级的能量。
> 
> As a result of the overlap between the 4s and 3d bands, the valence electrons partially occupy both the 3d and 4s bands. 由于4s和3d能带之间的重叠，价电子部分占据3d和4s能带。 **Valence electron**: 价电子，指原子最外层或参与化学键合的电子。
> 
> For example, Ni, with 10 valence electrons per atom, has 9.46 electrons in the 3d bands and 0.54 electrons in the 4s bands. 例如，Ni每个原子有10个价电子，其中3d能带中有9.46个电子，4s能带中有0.54个电子。
> 
> The 4s bands are broad, with a low density of states at the Fermi level. 4s能带很宽，在费米能级处的态密度低。 **Density of states (DOS)**: 态密度，指在特定能量范围内，每单位能量间隔内的电子态数量。
> 
> Consequently, the energy that would be required to promote a 4s electron into a vacant state so that it could reverse its spin is more than the energy that would be gained by the resulting decrease in exchange energy. 因此，将一个4s电子激发到一个空态以使其自旋反转所需的能量，多于由此产生的交换能降低所获得的能量。
> 
> In contrast, the 3d band is narrow and has a much higher density of states at the Fermi level. 相比之下，3d能带很窄，在费米能级处的态密度高得多。
> 
> The large number of electrons near the Fermi level reduces the band energy required to reverse a spin, and the exchange effect dominates. 费米能级附近的大量电子减少了反转自旋所需的能带能量，交换效应占主导地位。
> 
> It is useful to picture the exchange interaction as shifting the energy of the 3d band for electrons with one spin direction relative to the band for electrons with the opposite spin direction. 将交换相互作用想象为，使具有一种自旋方向的电子的3d能带能量相对于具有相反自旋方向的电子的能带发生移动，是很有用的。
> 
> Figure 4 shows the 4s and 3d densities of states within this picture. 图4显示了在此图像中的4s和3d态密度。
> 
> The magnitude of the shift is independent of the wavevector, giving a rigid displacement of the states in a band with one spin direction relative to the states with the opposite spin direction. 移动的大小与波矢无关，使得具有一种自旋方向的能带中的态相对于具有相反自旋方向的态发生刚性位移。 **Wavevector**: 波矢，描述波传播方向和波长的矢量。
> 
> If the Fermi energy lies within the 3d band, then the displacement will lead to more electrons of the lower-energy spin direction and, hence, a spontaneous magnetic moment in the ground state. 如果费米能级位于3d能带内，那么这种位移将导致更多电子处于较低能量的自旋方向，从而在基态产生自发磁矩。 **Ground state**: 基态，指量子力学系统处于最低能量的状态。
> 
> In Ni, for example, the exchange interaction displacement is so strong that one 3d subband is filled with five electrons, and the other contains all 0.54 holes. 例如，在Ni中，交换相互作用的位移如此之强，以至于一个3d子带被5个电子填满，而另一个子带包含了所有0.54个空穴。 **Hole**: 空穴，指半导体或绝缘体价带中未被电子占据的量子态，表现为带正电的载流子。
> 
> Thus, the saturation magnetization of Ni is M = 0.54NμB, where N is the total number of Ni atoms in the sample. 因此，Ni的饱和磁化强度为M = 0.54NμB，其中N是样品中Ni原子的总数。 **Bohr magneton (μB)**: 玻尔磁子，是磁矩的物理常数，通常用于表示原子和电子的磁矩。
> 
> We now see why the magnetic moments of the transition metals do not correspond to integer numbers of electrons! 我们现在明白了为什么过渡金属的磁矩不对应于整数个电子！
> 
> This model also explains why the later transition metals, Cu and Zn, are not ferromagnetic. 这个模型也解释了为什么晚过渡金属Cu和Zn不是铁磁性的。
> 
> In Cu, the Fermi level lies above the 3d bands. 在Cu中，费米能级位于3d能带之上。
> 
> Because both of the 3d bands are filled and the 4s band has no exchange splitting, the numbers of up- and down- spin electrons are equal. 由于两个3d能带都已填满，且4s能带没有交换分裂，因此自旋向上和向下的电子数量相等。 **Exchange splitting**: 交换分裂，指由于交换相互作用，导致不同自旋方向的电子能带在能量上发生分离的现象。
> 
> In Zn, both the 3d and 4s bands are filled and so do not contribute a magnetic moment. 在Zn中，3d和4s能带都已填满，因此不贡献磁矩。
> 
> # 3. Ferroelectrics
> 
> # 3. 铁电体
> 
> The formal definition of a ferroelectric material is one that undergoes a phase transition from a high-temperature phase that behaves as an ordinary dielectric (so that an applied electric field induces an electric polarization, which goes to zero when the field is removed) to a low-temperature phase that has a spontaneous polarization whose direction can be switched by an applied field. 铁电材料的正式定义是，它经历从一种表现为普通介电体的高温相（因此外加电场会感应出电极化，当电场移除时极化消失）转变为具有自发极化的低温相，且该自发极化的方向可以被外加电场反转。
> 
> Many properties of ferroelectric materials are analogous to those of ferromagnets, but with the electric polarization, P, corresponding to the magnetization, M; the electric field, E, corresponding to the magnetic field, H; and the electric displacement, D, corresponding to the magnetic flux density, B. 铁电材料的许多性质与铁磁体类似，但电极化P对应于磁化强度M；电场E对应于磁场H；电位移D对应于磁通密度B。 **Electric displacement**: 电位移，指电介质中电场与极化强度的矢量和，描述电介质中电场分布的物理量。
> 
> For example, ferroelectric materials also have domains and show a hysteretic response of both polarization and electric displacement to an applied electric field. 例如，铁电材料也具有畴，并且在极化强度和电位移上对外加电场显示滞后响应。
> 
> As a result, they also find applications in data storage. 因此，它们也在数据存储中找到了应用。
> 
> The onset of spontaneous electric polarization coincides with a divergence in the static dielectric permeability, ε, because at Tc, an infinitesimally small external electric field will cause a large polarization. 自发电极化的开始与静态介电常数ε的发散同时发生，因为在Tc处，一个无限小的外加电场将导致大的极化。
> 
> They find application as capacitors because their concentration of electric flux density results in high dielectric permeabilities. 它们被用作电容器，因为它们对电通密度的集中作用导致了高介电常数。
> 
> They are also used in electromechanical transducers and actuators (because the change in electric polarization is accompanied by a change in shape.17) 它们也用于机电换能器和执行器（因为电极化的变化伴随着形状的变化。17）
> 
> Early work on ferroelectric materials1,2 focused primarily on Rochelle salt, KNa(C4H4O6)·4H2O. 早期对铁电材料的研究1,2主要集中在罗谢尔盐，即KNa(C4H4O6)·4H2O。
> 
> Although studies of Rochelle salt were pivotal in establishing many of the basic properties of ferroelectric materials, the complex structure and large number of ions per unit cell made it difficult to elucidate a coherent theory of ferroelectricity from the results of experiments on this material. 尽管对罗谢尔盐的研究对于确立铁电材料的许多基本性质至关重要，但其复杂的结构和每个晶胞中大量的离子，使得从该材料的实验结果中阐明一个连贯的铁电性理论变得困难。 **Unit cell**: 晶胞，指晶体中空间点阵的最小重复单元。
> 
> The most widely studied and widely used ferroelectrics today are perovskite-structure oxides, ABO3, which have the prototypical cubic structure shown in Figure 5. 如今，研究最广泛、使用最多的铁电体是钙钛矿结构氧化物ABO3，它们具有图5所示的典型立方结构。
> 
> The cubic perovskite structure is characterized by a small cation, B, at the center of an octahedron of oxygen anions, with large cations, A, at the unit cell corners. 立方钙钛矿结构的特点是，一个小阳离子B位于氧阴离子八面体的中心，大阳离子A位于晶胞的角上。 **Cation**: 阳离子，带正电荷的离子。 **Anion**: 阴离子，带负电荷的离子。 **Octahedron**: 八面体，一种具有八个面的多面体，通常指由六个等距点构成的形状。
> 
> Below the Curie temperature, there is a structural distortion to a lower-symmetry phase accompanied by the shift off-center of the small cation. 在居里温度以下，会发生向低对称相的结构畸变，同时伴着小阳离子偏离中心的位移。
> 
> The spontaneous polarization derives largely from the electric dipole moment created by this shift. 自发极化主要源于这种位移产生的电偶极矩。 **Electric dipole moment**: 电偶极矩，衡量一对等量异号电荷或正负电荷中心分离程度的物理量，大小为电荷量与距离的乘积。
> 
> The comparatively simple perovskite structure and the small number of atoms per unit cell have made detailed theoretical studies of perovskite ferroelectrics possible and resulted in a good understanding of the fundamentals of ferroelectricity. 相对简单的钙钛矿结构和每个晶胞中较少的原子数，使得对钙钛矿铁电体的详细理论研究成为可能，并导致了对铁电性基本原理的良好理解。
> 
> ## 3.1. Theories of Ferroelectricity: What Makes Ferroelectrics Ferroelectric?
> 
> ## 3.1. 铁电性理论：什么使铁电体具有铁电性？
> 
> Any lattice of oppositely signed point charges is inherently unstable. 任何由相反符号点电荷组成的晶格本质上都是不稳定的。
> 
> Ionic materials are stable because of short-range repulsions between adjacent electron clouds. 离子材料之所以稳定，是因为相邻电子云之间存在短程排斥力。 **Short-range repulsion**: 短程排斥力，指原子或离子在极近距离内由于电子云重叠而产生的排斥相互作用。
> 
> The existence or absence of ferroelectricity is determined by a balance between these short-range repulsions, which favor the nonferroelectric symmetric structure, and additional bonding considerations, which might stabilize the ferroelectric phase. 铁电性的存在或缺失取决于这些短程排斥力（有利于非铁电对称结构）与可能稳定铁电相的额外键合因素之间的平衡。
> 
> Even in ferroelectric materials, the short-range repulsions dominate at high temperature, resulting in the symmetric, unpolarized state. 即使在铁电材料中，短程排斥力在高温下也占主导地位，导致对称的非极化状态。
> 
> As the temperature is decreased, the stabilizing forces associated with the polarization of the ions as they are displaced become stronger than the short-range repulsive ion-ion interactions, and the polarized state becomes stable, even in the absence of an applied field. 随着温度降低，与离子位移时极化相关的稳定力变得强于短程排斥的离子-离子相互作用，极化状态变得稳定，即使在没有外加电场时也是如此。
> 
> First-principles density functional theory calculations have contributed significantly to our current understanding of the origin of ferroelectric behavior, particularly in perovskite-structure oxides. 第一性原理密度泛函理论计算对我们目前理解铁电行为（特别是在钙钛矿结构氧化物中）的起源做出了重大贡献。 **Density functional theory (DFT)**: 密度泛函理论，一种基于电子密度而非波函数来研究多电子体系电子结构的量子力学方法。
> 
> The developments over the last 10 years are described in detail in ref 7; here, we summarize the important results. 过去10年的发展在参考文献7中有详细描述；在这里，我们总结重要的结果。
> 
> In 1992, Cohen and Krakauer18 used density functional theory (DFT) within the local density approximation (LDA) to investigate the ferroelectricity in two related perovskite-structure compounds: PbTiO3 and BaTiO3. 1992年，Cohen和Krakauer18使用局域密度近似下的密度泛函理论，研究了两种相关的钙钛矿结构化合物PbTiO3和BaTiO3的铁电性。 **Local density approximation (LDA)**: 局域密度近似，是密度泛函理论中处理交换关联能的一种常用近似方法。
> 
> Both materials have the cubic structure at high temperatures. 这两种材料在高温下都具有立方结构。
> 
> PbTiO3 undergoes a phase transition to a tetragonal phase below 766 K, with the polarization along the [100] direction. PbTiO3在766 K以下经历向四方相的相变，极化沿[100]方向。
> 
> BaTiO3 undergoes a series of phase transitions from cubic to tetragonal to orthorhombic to rhombohedral, with the low-temperature polarization along the [111] direction. BaTiO3经历一系列相变，从立方到四方，再到正交，最后到菱方，其低温极化沿[111]方向。
> 
> Cohen and Krakauer found that, in both cases, the Ti 3d-O 2p hybridization is essential for stabilizing the ferroelectric distortion. Cohen和Krakauer发现，在这两种情况下，Ti 3d-O 2p的杂化对于稳定铁电畸变至关重要。 **Hybridization**: 杂化，指原子轨道线性组合形成新的等价轨道的过程，用于解释分子的几何构型和键合性质。
> 
> In BaTiO3, the Ba-O interaction is largely ionic in nature. 在BaTiO3中，Ba-O相互作用本质上是离子性的。
> 
> In PbTiO3, there is hybridization between the Pb 6s and O 2p electrons. 在PbTiO3中，Pb 6s和O 2p电子之间存在杂化。
> 
> This results in a large Pb polarizability and a strain that stabilizes the tetragonal phase over the rhombohedral phase in PbTiO3. 这导致了PbTiO3中大的Pb极化率和一个应变，该应变使四方相相对于菱方相更稳定。 **Polarizability**: 极化率，衡量原子或离子在电场作用下电子云变形能力的物理量。
> 
> Two complementary phenomenological models exist for describing the phase transition to the ferroelectric state. 存在两种互补的唯象模型来描述向铁电态的相变。
> 
> The small B cation in ferroelectric perovskite oxides should always be able to lower its energy by shifting along one of the ⟨111⟩ directions (unless strain in the lattice stabilizes a different displacement). 铁电钙钛矿氧化物中的小B阳离子，应该总能通过沿⟨111⟩方向之一移动来降低其能量（除非晶格中的应变稳定了不同的位移）。
> 
> This leads to the characteristic “double-well” potential energy for the position of the small cation as a function of position between the oxygen anions, as shown in Figure 6. 这导致了小阳离子位置作为氧阴离子之间位置函数的特征“双阱”势能，如图6所示。
> 
> In the order-disorder model, the B cations are always displaced along one of the cube diagonals. 在有序-无序模型中，B阳离子总是沿立方体对角线之一位移。
> 
> At high temperature, all possible ⟨111⟩ directions are allowed, and at low temperature, the displacements all adopt either the same orientation (giving rhombohedral symmetry) or two or three preferred ⟨111⟩ directions (giving tetragonal or orthorhombic symmetry). 在高温下，所有可能的⟨111⟩方向都是允许的，而在低温下，位移要么全部采取相同的取向（产生菱方对称性），要么采取两个或三个择优的⟨111⟩方向（产生四方或正交对称性）。
> 
> This model predicts a large change in configurational entropy at the phase transition, which is not in fact observed, but the model may be applicable in higher-temperature regimes, away from the phase transition temperature. 该模型预测在相变处有大的构型熵变化，但实际上并未观察到，但该模型可能适用于远离相变温度的高温区域。 **Configurational entropy**: 构型熵，指系统中由于粒子排列方式的不同而产生的熵。
> 
> In the soft-mode model, the B cation displacement is stable only at low temperatures. 在软模模型中，B阳离子位移仅在低温下稳定。
> 
> Above the Curie temperature, there is a restoring force that tends to push the B cation back to the center if it is displaced. 在居里温度以上，存在一个恢复力，如果B阳离子发生位移，该力倾向于将其推回中心。
> 
> As the temperature is reduced, the phonon associated with this restoring force (the so-called “soft-mode” phonon) becomes weaker, until at the Curie temperature, the frequency is zero and the displacement occurs spontaneously. 随着温度降低，与这种恢复力相关的声子（所谓的“软模”声子）变弱，直到在居里温度处，频率变为零，位移自发发生。 **Phonon**: 声子，指晶格振动的集体激发的量子化能量单位，是固体物理中的一种准粒子。 **Soft mode**: 软模，指在相变温度附近，其频率趋于零的晶格振动模式。
> 
> The large effect from changing the identity of the A cation is significant for our later discussion of multiferroic materials. 改变A阳离子的种类所产生的巨大效应，对我们稍后讨论多铁性材料非常重要。
> 
> The strain introduced by the A cation hybridization with O 2p electrons is long-range in nature and helps lead to the long-range ordering required for a phase transition. 由A阳离子与O 2p电子杂化引入的应变是长程性质的，有助于导致相变所需的长程有序。
> 
> Therefore, the identity of the A cation can significantly affect the ferroelectricity of the material, even though the ferroelectric polarization results primarily from the displacement of the B cation relative to the oxygen cage. 因此，A阳离子的种类可以显著影响材料的铁电性，即使铁电极化主要源于B阳离子相对于氧笼的位移。
> 
> Also significant is the observation that the Ti4+ ion is formally in a d0 state so that the lowest unoccupied energy levels are d states that tend to hybridize with O 2p ions. 同样重要的是观察到Ti4+离子形式上处于d0态，因此最低未占据能级是倾向于与O 2p离子杂化的d态。
> 
> Most other perovskite ferroelectrics also contain d0 B cations, such as Zr4+ and Nb5+. 大多数其他钙钛矿铁电体也包含d0 B阳离子，如Zr4+和Nb5+。
> 
> In contrast, d-orbital occupancy is a requirement for the existence of magnetic moments and the consequent existence of magnetic ordering. 相比之下，d轨道占据是存在磁矩以及随之而来的磁有序的必要条件。
>

> Figure 6. Characteristic double-well potential energy as a function of the position of the B cation between the oxygen anions in perovskite ferroelectrics. 图6. 钙钛矿铁电体中，B阳离子在氧阴离子之间位置函数的特征双阱势能。

> # 4. Multiferroism
> 
> # 4. 多铁性
> 
> The term multiferroism has been coined19 to describe materials in which two or all three of the properties ferroelectricity, ferromagnetism, and ferroelasticity occur in the same phase. 多铁性这个术语被创造出来19用以描述在同一相中同时具有铁电性、铁磁性和铁弹性这三种性质中的两种或全部三种的材料。 **Ferroelasticity**: 铁弹性，指材料在应力作用下产生自发应变，且该应变可被外加应力反转的现象。
> 
> This means that they have a spontaneous magnetization that can be reoriented by an applied magnetic field, a spontaneous polarization that can be reoriented by an applied electric field, and a spontaneous deformation that can be reoriented by an applied stress. 这意味着它们具有可被外加磁场重新定向的自发磁化强度、可被外加电场重新定向的自发极化强度，以及可被外加应力重新定向的自发形变。
> 
> Specific device applications that have been suggested for such “multiferroic” materials14 include multiple-state memory elements, electric-field-controlled ferromagnetic resonance devices, and transducers with magnetically modulated piezoelectricity. 已经为这类“多铁性”材料提出的特定器件应用14包括多态存储元件、电场控制的铁磁共振器件，以及具有磁调制压电性的换能器。 **Ferromagnetic resonance**: 铁磁共振，指铁磁体中磁矩在稳恒磁场和交变磁场共同作用下产生的共振吸收现象。
> 
> In addition, the ability to couple with either the magnetic or the electric polarization offers an extra degree of freedom in the design of conventional actuators, transducers, and storage devices. 此外，能够与磁极化或电极化耦合的能力，为传统执行器、换能器和存储设备的设计提供了额外的自由度。
> 
> In this work, we focus on magnetoelectric multiferroics, which are simultaneously ferromagnetic and ferroelectric, with or without ferroelasticity. 在这项工作中，我们专注于磁电多铁性材料，它们同时具有铁磁性和铁电性，无论是否具有铁弹性。
> 
> ## 4.1. History of Magnetoelectric Multiferroics.
> 
> ## 4.1. 磁电多铁性材料的历史。
> 
> ### 4.1.1. Boracites.
> 
> ### 4.1.1. 方硼石。
> 
> The first ferromagnetic ferroelectric material to be discovered20 was nickel iodine boracite, Ni3B7O13I. 第一个被发现的铁磁铁电材料20是碘镍方硼石，Ni3B7O13I。
> 
> This was followed by the synthesis of many more multiferroic boracite compounds, all of which have complex structures with many atoms per formula unit and more than one formula unit per unit cell. 随后又合成了许多多铁性方硼石化合物，所有这些化合物都具有复杂的结构，每个化学式单元包含许多原子，且每个晶胞中包含多个化学式单元。
> 
> The large number of inter-ionic interactions in these materials prevented the isolation both of the essential factors causing multiferroicity and of the nature of the coupling between the magnetic, electric polarization, and structural order parameters. 这些材料中大量的离子间相互作用，阻碍了分离出导致多铁性的基本因素，以及磁、电极化和结构序参量之间耦合的本质。 **Order parameter**: 序参量，在相变理论中，描述系统有序程度的物理量，在有序相中不为零，在无序相中为零。
> 
> Nickel iodine boracite can be thought of as the “Rochelle salt” of magnetic ferroelectrics—invaluable for demonstrating proof of concept, but unlikely to find wide applicability or to contribute to our increased understanding in the field. 碘镍方硼石可以被认为是磁性铁电体中的“罗谢尔盐”——对于证明概念至关重要，但不太可能找到广泛的应用，或有助于提高我们在该领域的理解。
> 
> ### 4.1.2. Mixed Perovskites.
> 
> ### 4.1.2. 混合钙钛矿。
> 
> The search for other ferromagnetic ferroelectrics began in Russia in the 1950s, with the replacement of some of the d0 B cations in ferroelectric perovskite oxides by magnetic dn cations.21 对其他铁磁铁电体的探索始于20世纪50年代的俄罗斯，方法是用磁性dn阳离子取代铁电钙钛矿氧化物中的一些d0 B阳离子。21
> 
> The first synthetic ferromagnetic ferroelectric material, (1 - x)Pb(Fe2/3W1/3)O3 - xPb(Mg1/2W1/2)O3, was produced in the early 1960s using this approach.22 第一种合成的铁磁铁电材料(1 - x)Pb(Fe2/3W1/3)O3 - xPb(Mg1/2W1/2)O3是在20世纪60年代早期使用这种方法制造的。22
> 
> Here, the Mg and W ions are diamagnetic and cause the ferroelectricity, and the formally d5 Fe3+ ion is responsible for the magnetic ordering. 在这里，Mg和W离子是抗磁性的，并导致铁电性，而形式上为d5的Fe3+离子负责磁有序。 **Diamagnetic**: 抗磁性，指材料在外磁场作用下产生一个与外加磁场方向相反的微弱磁矩的现象。
> 
> Other examples include B-site-ordered Pb2(CoW)O6,23 which is ferroelectric and ferromagnetic; B-site disordered Pb2(FeTa)O6,23 which is ferroelectric and antiferromagnetic; and Pb2(FeTa)O624 and Pb2(FeTa)O6,25 which are both ferroelectric and antiferromagnetic, with weak ferromagnetism below around 10K. 其他例子包括B位有序的Pb2(CoW)O6，23它是铁电和铁磁性的；B位无序的Pb2(FeTa)O6，23它是铁电和反铁磁性的；以及Pb2(FeTa)O6，24和Pb2(FeTa)O6，25它们都是铁电和反铁磁性的，并在大约10K以下表现出弱铁磁性。
> 
> As a result of dilution of the magnetic ions, these materials all have rather low Curie or Néel temperatures. 由于磁性离子的稀释，这些材料都具有相当低的居里温度或奈尔温度。 **Néel temperature**: 奈尔温度，指反铁磁体从反铁磁相转变为顺磁相的临界温度。
> 
> ### 4.1.3. Other Perovskites.
> 
> ### 4.1.3. 其他钙钛矿。
> 
> A number of other perovskite materials are known to have ferroelectric and magnetic (mostly of the antiferromagnetic type) ordering. 已知许多其他钙钛矿材料具有铁电性和磁性（主要是反铁磁类型）有序。
> 
> These include the manganites of the small rare earth elements and yttrium and a few compounds in which Bi is the large cation. 这些包括小稀土元素和钇的锰酸盐，以及一些以Bi为大阳离子的化合物。 **Rare earth element**: 稀土元素，指钪、钇和镧系共17种元素，常用于高科技材料。
> 
> These materials will be discussed in detail in section 5. 这些材料将在第5节中详细讨论。
> 
> ## 4.2. Requirements for Magnetoelectric Multiferroicity.
> 
> ## 4.2. 磁电多铁性的必要条件。
> 
> By definition, for a material to be a magnetoelectric multiferroic, it must be simultaneously ferromagnetic and ferroelectric. 根据定义，一种材料要成为磁电多铁性体，它必须同时具有铁磁性和铁电性。
> 
> Therefore, its allowed physical, structural, and electronic properties are restricted to those that occur both in ferromagnetic and in ferroelectric materials. 因此，其允许的物理、结构和电子性质，被限制在那些同时出现在铁磁和铁电材料中的性质。
> 
> In this section, we analyze a range of properties and discuss how these properties limit our choice of potential materials. 在本节中，我们分析一系列性质，并讨论这些性质如何限制我们对潜在材料的选择。
> 
> We discuss those that are the limiting factors in preventing the simultaneous existence of ferromagnetism and ferroelectricity. 我们讨论那些阻碍铁磁性和铁电性同时存在的限制因素。
> 
> ### 4.2.1. Symmetry.
> 
> ### 4.2.1. 对称性。
> 
> A primary requirement for the existence of ferroelectricity is a structural distortion from the prototypical high-symmetry phase that removes the center of symmetry and allows an electric polarization. 铁电性存在的主要要求是，从原型高对称相发生结构畸变，消除对称中心并允许电极化。
> 
> There are 31 point groups that allow a spontaneous electric polarization, P, and 31 that allow a spontaneous magnetic polarization, M.19 有31种点群允许自发电极化P，31种点群允许自发磁极化M。19 **Point group**: 点群，指晶体宏观对称操作的集合，描述晶体外形的对称性。
> 
> Thirteen point groups (1, 2, 2′, m, m′, 3, 3m′, 4, 4m′m′, m′m2′, m′m′2′, 6, and 6m′m′) are found in both sets, allowing both properties to exist in the same phase. 13种点群（1, 2, 2′, m, m′, 3, 3m′, 4, 4m′m′, m′m2′, m′m′2′, 6, 和 6m′m′）在两组中都存在，允许两种性质在同一相中共存。
> 
> Although this represents a considerable reduction from the total number of possible crystal structures (the total number of Shubnikov point groups is 122), it is not an insignificant number, and many candidate materials that are not in fact ferromagnetic and ferroelectric exist in one of the allowed symmetries. 尽管这代表了从可能的晶体结构总数（舒布尼科夫点群总数为122）中的大幅减少，但这并非一个微不足道的数字，许多实际上并非铁磁和铁电性的候选材料，也存在于允许的对称性之一中。
> 
> Therefore, it is unlikely that symmetry considerations are responsible for the scarcity of ferromagnetic ferroelectric materials. 因此，对称性考虑不太可能是导致铁磁铁电材料稀缺的原因。
> 
> ### 4.2.2. Electrical Properties.
> 
> ### 4.2.2. 电学性质。
> 
> By definition, a ferroelectric material must be an insulator (otherwise, an applied electric field would inducing an electric current to flow, rather than causing an electrical polarization.) 根据定义，铁电材料必须是绝缘体（否则，外加电场将导致电流流动，而不是引起电极化）。 **Insulator**: 绝缘体，指导电性极差的材料，其价带和导带之间有较大的带隙。
> 
> Ferromagnets, although not required to have specific electrical properties, are often metals. 铁磁体，虽然不需要具有特定的电学性质，但通常是金属。
> 
> For example, the driving force for ferromagnetism in the elemental ferromagnets Fe, Co, and Ni and their alloys is the high density of states at the Fermi level, which also, of course, results in metallicity. 例如，元素铁磁体Fe、Co和Ni及其合金中铁磁性的驱动力，是费米能级处的高态密度，这当然也导致了金属性。
> 
> Therefore, one could assume that the lack of the simultaneous occurrence of magnetic and ferroelectric ordering is simply the result of a dearth of magnetic insulators. 因此，人们可能会假设，磁有序和铁电有序不能同时发生，仅仅是缺乏磁性绝缘体的结果。
> 
> However, if we extend our search to include ferrimagnets or weak ferromagnets (which have canted antiferromagnetic ordering, resulting in a weak magnetic moment in the direction of the canting), this argument no longer holds, because most ferrimagnets or weak ferromagnets are, in fact, insulators. 然而，如果我们扩展搜索范围，将亚铁磁体或弱铁磁体（它们具有倾斜的反铁磁有序，导致在倾斜方向上产生弱磁矩）包括在内，这个论点就不再成立，因为大多数亚铁磁体或弱铁磁体实际上是绝缘体。
> 
> In addition, there are also very few antiferromagnetic ferroelectrics, even though antiferromagnets are usually insulating materials. 此外，反铁磁铁电体也非常少，尽管反铁磁体通常是绝缘材料。
> 
> Therefore, it appears that we cannot blame the lack of magnetically ordered ferroelectrics simply on a shortage of magnetically ordered insulators. 因此，似乎我们不能将磁有序铁电体的缺乏，简单地归咎于磁有序绝缘体的短缺。
> 
> ### 4.2.3. Chemistry:“d0-ness”.
> 
> ### 4.2.3. 化学：“d0态”。
> 
> We noted in section 3.1 that the common perovskite oxide ferroelectric materials have a formal charge corresponding to the d0 electron configuration on the B cation. 我们在3.1节中注意到，常见的钙钛矿氧化物铁电材料具有对应于B阳离子上d0电子构型的形式电荷。
> 
> Clearly, if there are no d electrons creating localized magnetic moments, then there can be no magnetic ordering of any type, either ferro-, ferri-, or antiferromagnetic. 显然，如果没有d电子产生局域磁矩，那么就不可能有任何类型的磁有序，无论是铁磁、亚铁磁还是反铁磁。
> 
> It appears however that, in most cases, as soon as the d shell on the small cation is partially occupied, the tendency for it to make a distortion that removes the center of symmetry is eliminated. 然而，似乎大多数情况下，一旦小阳离子上的d壳层被部分占据，它发生消除对称中心的畸变的倾向就会消失。
> 
> This could be the result of a number of effects, including size, the tendency to undergo a different, more dominant distortion, electronic properties, magnetic properties, or some combination of the above. 这可能是多种因素的结果，包括尺寸、发生不同且更占优势的畸变的倾向、电子性质、磁性质，或上述因素的某种组合。
> 
> We now consider the likely influence of each of these factors. 我们现在考虑每个因素可能的影响。
> 
> Size of the Small Cation. 小阳离子的尺寸。
> 
> Are transition metal ions with occupied d shells simply too large to move away from the large space at the center of the oxygen octahedron? 具有占据d壳层的过渡金属离子，是否仅仅因为太大，而无法从氧八面体中心的大空间移开？
> 
> In this section, we compare the ionic radii of typical d0 cations in perovskite ferroelectrics with those of typical dn cations in nonferroelectric perovskite oxides to see if this argument is correct. 在本节中，我们比较了钙钛矿铁电体中典型d0阳离子与非铁电钙钛矿氧化物中典型dn阳离子的离子半径，以检验这个论点是否正确。 **Ionic radius**: 离子半径，描述离子大小的物理量。
> 
> The Shannon ionic radii26 of some common d0 small cations found in ferroelectric perovskite oxides are: Ti4+, 74.5 pm; Nb5+, 78 pm; and Zr4+, 86 pm. 在铁电钙钛矿氧化物中发现的一些常见d0小阳离子的香农离子半径为：Ti4+，74.5 pm；Nb5+，78 pm；和Zr4+，86 pm。
> 
> Some representative dn cations that are found as the small cations in nonferroelectric perovskite oxides include Mn3+ (d4), Ti3+ (d1), and V4+ (d1) with radii of 78.5, 81, and 72 pm, respectively. 在非铁电钙钛矿氧化物中作为小阳离子被发现的一些代表性dn阳离子包括Mn3+ (d4)、Ti3+ (d1)和V4+ (d1)，半径分别为78.5、81和72 pm。
> 
> Therefore, typical B-site cations with d electron occupation do not have systematically larger radii than typical d0 B-site cations. 因此，具有d电子占据的典型B位阳离子，其半径并没有系统地大于典型的d0 B位阳离子。
> 
> We conclude that the size of the B cation is not the deciding factor in the existence or otherwise of ferroelectricity. 我们得出结论，B阳离子的大小不是铁电性存在与否的决定性因素。
> 
> Structural Distortions. 结构畸变。
> 
> Ferroelectric materials must undergo a phase transition to a low-temperature phase that does not have a center of symmetry. 铁电材料必须经历向没有对称中心的低温相的相变。
> 
> This is most often achieved in conventional perovskite ferroelectrics by an off-center displacement of the small (B) cation from the center of the oxygen octahedron. 在传统钙钛矿铁电体中，这通常是通过小(B)阳离子从氧八面体中心发生偏心位移来实现的。
> 
> However, for cations with certain d-orbital occupancies, the tendency to undergo a Jahn-Teller distortion is strong and will likely be the dominant structural effect. 然而，对于具有某些d轨道占据的阳离子，发生姜-泰勒畸变的倾向很强，并且很可能是主导的结构效应。 **Jahn-Teller distortion**: 姜-泰勒畸变，指非线性分子在简并电子态下，为了降低能量而发生几何构型畸变的现象。
> 
> The Jahn-Teller distorted structure might have a lower driving force for off-center displacement than the otherwise undistorted structure. 与未畸变的结构相比，姜-泰勒畸变结构可能具有更低的偏心位移驱动力。
> 
> Examples of this effect are seen in lanthanum manganite, LaMnO3, in which the Mn3+ ion has a d4 configuration, and yttrium titanate, YTiO3, in which the Ti3+ ion is d1. 在锰酸镧LaMnO3（其中Mn3+离子具有d4构型）和钛酸钇YTiO3（其中Ti3+离子是d1）中可以看到这种效应的例子。
> 
> Both materials have a d-type Jahn-Teller distortion, in which the elongated axes of the oxygen octahedra are oriented parallel to each other along the crystallographic c axis.27 这两种材料都具有d型姜-泰勒畸变，其中氧八面体的长轴沿晶体学c轴相互平行取向。27
> 
> Orbital ordering is observed to occur simultaneously with the Jahn-Teller ordering.27 观察到轨道有序与姜-泰勒有序同时发生。27 **Orbital ordering**: 轨道有序，指在固体中，电子轨道的占据不是随机的，而是呈现出长程有序的排列模式。
> 
> LaMnO3 is insulating and an A-type antiferromagnet, in which planes of ferromagnetically aligned Mn3+ ions are aligned antiparallel to each other. LaMnO3是绝缘体，并且是A型反铁磁体，其中铁磁排列的Mn3+离子平面彼此反向平行排列。
> 
> YTiO3 is a strongly correlated ferromagnetic Mott-Hubbard insulator. YTiO3是一种强关联的铁磁莫特-哈伯德绝缘体。 **Mott-Hubbard insulator**: 莫特-哈伯德绝缘体，指由于强电子-电子关联效应而成为绝缘体的材料，即使能带理论预测其为导体。
> 
> Neither material is ferroelectric. 这两种材料都不是铁电体。
> 
> Note the relevance of the formal charge on the B cation here. 注意这里B阳离子上形式电荷的相关性。
> 
> In BaTiO3, the actual occupation of the 3d electrons is closer to d1 than to d0 because of donation of charge density from the oxygen ligands.18 在BaTiO3中，由于氧配体提供电荷密度，3d电子的实际占据情况更接近d1而非d0。18
> 
> A d1 cation should undergo a Jahn-Teller distortion. d1阳离子应发生姜-泰勒畸变。
> 
> No Jahn-Teller distortion is observed in BaTiO3 however, which is consistent with the d0 formal charge. 然而，在BaTiO3中未观察到姜-泰勒畸变，这与d0形式电荷一致。
> 
> Magnetism Versus d-Orbital Occupancy. 磁性与d轨道占据。
> 
> It is clear that the existence of d electrons on the B-site cation reduces the tendency of perovskite structure oxides to display ferroelectricity. 很明显，B位阳离子上d电子的存在降低了钙钛矿结构氧化物显示铁电性的倾向。
> 
> However, it is not clear whether it is merely the presence of d electron density or the influence of the magnetic spin polarization that is the dominant factor in creating this effect. 然而，尚不清楚仅仅是d电子密度的存在，还是磁自旋极化的影响，是造成这种效应的主导因素。 **Spin polarization**: 自旋极化，指系统中不同自旋方向的电子数不相等，导致净磁矩产生的现象。
> 
> Using first-principles density functional theory calculations, it is possible to answer this question by artificially removing the spin polarization in materials that are experimentally found to be magnetic and determining whether they then become ferroelectric. 使用第一性原理密度泛函理论计算，可以通过人为地去除实验上发现为磁性的材料中的自旋极化，并确定它们随后是否变成铁电体，来回答这个问题。
> 
> In sections 5.1 and 5.2, we perform such comparisons for some representative systems and show that removal of the magnetic polarization does not significantly affect the calculated phonon frequencies, suggesting that d-orbital occupancy, rather than d-electron magnetism, is dominant in overriding the tendency to undergo ferroelectric distortion. 在第5.1和5.2节中，我们对一些代表性体系进行了此类比较，并表明去除磁极化并没有显著影响计算出的声子频率，这表明d轨道占据，而不是d电子磁性，在压倒发生铁电畸变的倾向中起主导作用。
> 
> ### 4.2.4. Conclusion.
> 
> ### 4.2.4. 结论。
> 
> The short answer to the title of the paper is that we do not know for sure why there are so few magnetic ferroelectrics. 对本文标题的简短回答是，我们尚不确定为什么磁性铁电体如此之少。
> 
> We do, however, have some clues: multiferroism involves a number of subtle competing factors, with d-electron occupancy on the transition metal being a critical variable. 然而，我们确实有一些线索：多铁性涉及许多微妙的竞争因素，其中过渡金属上的d电子占据是一个关键变量。
> 
> In the next section, we present detailed theoretical studies of two known magnetoelectric multiferroic materials with the goal of understanding what makes these particular materials multiferroic. 在下一节中，我们展示了对两种已知的磁电多铁性材料的详细理论研究，目的是理解是什么使这些特定材料成为多铁性的。
> 
> First, we consider bismuth manganite, BiMnO3, which is ferromagnetic and possibly ferroelectric. 首先，我们考虑锰酸铋BiMnO3，它是铁磁性的，并且可能是铁电性的。
> 
> Second, we study yttrium manganite, YMnO3, which is known to be antiferromagnetic and ferroelectric. 其次，我们研究锰酸钇YMnO3，已知它是反铁磁和铁电性的。
> 
> # 5. Perovskite-Structure Manganites
> 
> # 5. 钙钛矿结构锰酸盐
> 
> As we discussed in section 3.1, first-principles studies of perovskite ferroelectrics contributed greatly to our understanding of the fundamentals of ferroelectricity. 正如我们在3.1节中讨论的，钙钛矿铁电体的第一性原理研究极大地促进了我们对铁电性基本原理的理解。
> 
> Similar investigation of multiferroic perovskites should allow us to isolate the essentials of multiferroism. 对多铁性钙钛矿的类似研究，应该能让我们分离出多铁性的本质。
> 
> In this section, we focus on one specific class of magnetic perovskites—the rare earth (and related) manganites. 在本节中，我们专注于一类特定的磁性钙钛矿——稀土（及相关的）锰酸盐。
> 
> A large amount of recent research activity28 has been focused on the rare earth manganites, following the observation of colossal magnetoresistance (CMR) in Ca-doped LaMnO3.11 在观察到Ca掺杂LaMnO3中的庞磁阻效应后，大量近期研究活动28都集中在稀土锰酸盐上。11
> 
> During these recent studies, many rare earth perovskite manganites have been found to show strong coupling between their magnetic and structural order parameters. 在这些近期研究中，发现许多稀土钙钛矿锰酸盐在其磁性和结构序参量之间显示出强耦合。
> 
> For example, a magnetically induced structural phase transition has been observed in La0.83Sr0.17MnO3,29 indicating strong coupling between the local magnetic spin moments and the lattice structure. 例如，在La0.83Sr0.17MnO3中观察到了磁致结构相变，29表明局域磁自旋矩与晶格结构之间存在强耦合。
> 
> In Nd0.5Sr0.5MnO3, strong coupling between the magnetic spin moments and the electronic charge carriers was demonstrated when an electronic metal-insulator transition was induced by an external magnetic field.30 在Nd0.5Sr0.5MnO3中，当外部磁场诱导电子金属-绝缘体转变时，证明了磁自旋矩与电子电荷载流子之间的强耦合。30 **Metal-insulator transition**: 金属-绝缘体转变，指材料在外部条件（如温度、压力、磁场）变化下，其导电性在金属态和绝缘态之间发生转变的现象。
> 
> The large change in conductivity with applied magnetic field, which gives these materials potential technological importance as the read element in magnetic recording heads,31 is believed to originate from a similar type of phase transition.32 电导率随外加磁场发生的巨大变化，赋予了这些材料作为磁记录头读取元件的潜在技术重要性，31这种变化被认为源于类似类型的相变。32
> 
> It is clear that, although the combination of ferromagnetism and ferroelectricity is rare, coupling between electric and magnetic order parameters is not. 很明显，尽管铁磁性和铁电性的组合很少见，但电序参量和磁序参量之间的耦合并不罕见。
> 
> All of the rare earth manganites crystallize in the perovskite phase with antiferromagnetic ordering between adjacent Mn3+ ions, caused by the superexchange mechanism.33 所有稀土锰酸盐都以钙钛矿相结晶，相邻Mn3+离子之间由于超交换机制而呈反铁磁有序。33 **Superexchange**: 超交换，指通过非磁性阴离子（如氧离子）介导的磁性离子之间的间接交换相互作用。
> 
> Manganites of the lighter rare earths (LaMnO3 through DyMnO3) have a low-temperature orthorhombic distortion and do not show ferroelectricity. 轻稀土（从LaMnO3到DyMnO3）的锰酸盐具有低温正交畸变，并且不显示铁电性。
> 
> In YMnO3, and in HoMnO3 through LuMnO3, the orthorhombic phase is not the most stable structure, and instead, a hexagonal perovskite structure is formed. 在YMnO3中，以及从HoMnO3到LuMnO3中，正交相不是最稳定的结构，而是形成六方钙钛矿结构。
> 
> The hexagonal manganites are ferroelectric.34 六方锰酸盐是铁电性的。34
> 
> Bismuth manganite differs markedly from both the orthorhombic and hexagonal rare earth manganites. 锰酸铋与正交和六方稀土锰酸盐都显著不同。
> 
> Although information about BiMnO3 is sparse,35 it is known to be ferromagnetic, with a triclinic structural distortion in its ground state. 尽管关于BiMnO3的信息很少，35但已知它是铁磁性的，在其基态具有三斜结构畸变。
> 
> The ferromagnetism is unexpected, because, like the rare earth manganites, BiMnO3 has trivalent Mn cations with four 3d electrons. 铁磁性是出乎意料的，因为像稀土锰酸盐一样，BiMnO3具有三价Mn阳离子和四个3d电子。
> 
> The four 3d electrons should, in all cases, drive a Jahn-Teller distortion of the surrounding oxygen octahedra, which, combined with the superexchange interaction, should cause antiferromagnetic magnetic symmetry,33 as is indeed observed in the rare earth manganites. 这四个3d电子在所有情况下都应驱动周围氧八面体的姜-泰勒畸变，这与超交换相互作用结合，应导致反铁磁对称性，33正如在稀土锰酸盐中确实观察到的那样。
> 
> In addition, preliminary theoretical work has confirmed an early suggestion35 that BiMnO3 might be ferroelectric.36 此外，初步的理论工作已经证实了早期的一个建议，35即BiMnO3可能是铁电性的。36
> 
> In this section, we study the chemical origins of the differences between BiMnO3 and the rare earth manganites and between the hexagonal and orthorhombic manganites. 在本节中，我们研究了BiMnO3与稀土锰酸盐之间，以及六方和正交锰酸盐之间差异的化学起源。
> 
> We choose LaMnO3 as our prototypical orthorhombic rare earth manganite and YMnO3 as our prototypical hexagonal manganite. 我们选择LaMnO3作为我们的原型正交稀土锰酸盐，并选择YMnO3作为我们的原型六方锰酸盐。
> 
> The motivations for our choices are two-fold. 我们选择的动机有两个。
> 
> First, the majority of the recent experimental data on orthorhombic and hexagonal manganites has been obtained for LaMnO3 and YMnO3, respectively. 首先，关于正交和六方锰酸盐的大部分近期实验数据，分别是从LaMnO3和YMnO3获得的。
> 
> Second, because La3+ and Y3+ do not contain f electrons, we are able to use a plane-wave pseudopotential implementation of density functional theory, without the complication of developing pseudopotentials for tightly bound f electrons. 其次，由于La3+和Y3+不包含f电子，我们能够使用密度泛函理论的平面波赝势实现，而无需为紧密结合的f电子开发赝势的复杂性。 **Pseudopotential**: 赝势，指在计算中用于替代原子核和内层电子对价电子真实势能的有效势，以简化计算。 **Plane-wave**: 平面波，一种波前为平面的波，常用于扩展固体中的电子波函数。
> 
> The goal of our computational work in this section is to identify the factors that promote ferroelectricity in BiMnO3 and YMnO3, but override it in the conventional cubic rare earth manganites. 我们在本节中的计算工作目标是，确定在BiMnO3和YMnO3中促进铁电性，但在传统立方稀土锰酸盐中压倒铁电性的因素。
> 
> In particular, we study the effect of artificially removing the magnetic interactions on the d electrons (by restricting the up- and down-spin charge densities to be identical). 特别是，我们研究了人为去除d电子上的磁相互作用（通过限制自旋向上和向下的电荷密度相同）的影响。
> 
> This allows us to determine whether it is the magnetism or the d-orbital occupation that prevents the ferroelectric instabilities from being unstable in the perovskite manganites. 这使我们能够确定，阻止钙钛矿锰酸盐中铁电不稳定性的，是磁性还是d轨道占据。
> 
> The theoretical methods and computational details used in this work are described in the Appendix. 这项工作中使用的理论方法和计算细节在附录中描述。
> 
> ## 5.1. Bismuth Manganite.
> 
> ## 5.1. 锰酸铋。
> 
> In this section, we investigate the origin of the differences between ferroelectric BiMnO3 and the nonferroelectric rare earth manganites by comparing the calculated electronic properties of BiMnO3 with those of LaMnO3. 在本节中，我们通过比较计算出的BiMnO3和LaMnO3的电子性质，来研究铁电性BiMnO3与非铁电性稀土锰酸盐之间差异的起源。
> 
> We begin by calculating the electronic structure for the high-symmetry cubic phases, without including magnetic effects [the so-called paramagnetic (PM) phase], and then lower the magnetic symmetry to the ferromagnetic (FM) phase. 我们首先计算高对称立方相的电子结构，不包括磁效应[所谓的顺磁相]，然后将磁对称性降低到铁磁相。
> 
> Finally, we introduce structural distortions in both paramagnetic and ferromagnetic calculations. 最后，在顺磁和铁磁计算中我们都引入结构畸变。
> 
> This ability to isolate structural and magnetic distortions is unique to computational studies and allows for an identification of the essential microscopic interactions that cause the observed macroscopic behavior. 这种分离结构和磁畸变的能力是计算研究独有的，它允许识别导致观察到的宏观行为的基本微观相互作用。
> 
> There are two important questions to answer here. 这里有两个重要的问题需要回答。
> 
> First, why is BiMnO3 ferromagnetic, when the superexchange mechanism causes the other rare earth manganites to be antiferromagnetic? 首先，为什么当超交换机制导致其他稀土锰酸盐为反铁磁时，BiMnO3却是铁磁性的？
> 
> Second, what causes BiMnO3 to be ferroelectric, when the other cubic rare earth manganites do not show a ferroelectric distortion? 其次，当其他立方稀土锰酸盐不显示铁电畸变时，是什么导致BiMnO3成为铁电性的？
> 
> ### 5.1.1. Cubic Paramagnetic Structures.
> 
> ### 5.1.1. 立方顺磁结构。
> 
> Figure 7 shows the calculated densities of states for cubic paramagnetic LaMnO3 and BiMnO3. 图7显示了立方顺磁LaMnO3和BiMnO3的计算态密度。
> 
> The plotted energy range is from -12 eV to 4 eV, and the lower-lying semi-core states have been omitted for clarity. 绘制的能量范围是从-12 eV到4 eV，为清晰起见，省略了较低能量的半芯态。
> 
> The Fermi level is set to zero in both cases. 在两种情况下，费米能级都设为零。
> 
> The broad series of bands between -2 and -7 eV in both materials arises from the oxygen 2p orbitals. 两种材料中-2 eV到-7 eV之间的宽系列能带，源于氧的2p轨道。
> 
> Above the oxygen 2p bands, and separated from them by an energy gap, are the Mn 3d bands. 在氧2p能带之上，并被能隙隔开的是Mn 3d能带。 **Energy gap**: 能隙，指固体中电子不允许存在的能量范围，位于价带顶和导带底之间。
> 
> The Mn 3d bands are divided into two subbands—the lower-energy t2g bands and the higher energy eg bands—as a result of crystal-field splitting by the octahedral oxygen anions. 由于八面体氧阴离子的晶体场分裂，Mn 3d能带被分成两个子带——低能的t2g带和高能的eg带。 **Crystal-field splitting**: 晶体场分裂，指在配位化合物中，中心离子的简并d轨道在配体电场作用下发生能级分裂的现象。
> 
> In both cases, the Fermi level lies near the top of the Mn 3d t2g bands and is in a region of high density of states (DOS). 在两种情况下，费米能级都位于Mn 3d t2g带顶部附近，并且处于高态密度区域。
> 
> The large DOS at the Fermi level suggests that the cubic PM structure is unstable and that a lower-energy structure could be achieved by allowing spin-polarization and/or structural distortion. 费米能级处的大态密度表明，立方顺磁结构是不稳定的，通过允许自旋极化和/或结构畸变可以实现更低的能量结构。
> 
> One striking difference between the two DOS plots is the presence of a band between -10 and -12 eV in the BiMnO3 band structure that does not exist in the LaMnO3 case. 两个态密度图之间的一个显著差异是，在BiMnO3能带结构中存在一个位于-10 eV到-12 eV之间的能带，这在LaMnO3中不存在。
> 
> This band corresponds to the high-lying occupied Bi 6s electrons. 这个能带对应于高占据的Bi 6s电子。
> 
> In addition, the high-energy La 3d electrons have a very different form than the Bi 6p electrons, which occupy a similar energy range. 此外，高能的La 3d电子与占据相似能量范围的Bi 6p电子具有非常不同的形态。
> 
> Figure 8 shows the corresponding band structures along the high-symmetry axes of the simple cubic Brillouin zone. 图8显示了沿简单立方布里渊区高对称轴的相应能带结构。 **Brillouin zone**: 布里渊区，在倒易空间中的原胞，是描述固体中电子、声子等波动性质的基本单元。
> 
> Again, the broad O 2p bands between -2 and -7 eV can be seen clearly in both materials, with the Mn 3d bands above them, separated by an energy gap. 再次，两种材料中都可以清楚地看到-2 eV到-7 eV之间的宽O 2p能带，Mn 3d能带在其上方，被能隙隔开。
> 
> Again, we observe the Bi 6s band, which can be seen to have considerable dispersion, suggesting that it is involved in covalent bonding. 再次，我们观察到Bi 6s能带，可以看到它具有相当大的色散，表明它参与了共价键合。 **Covalent bond**: 共价键，指原子间通过共享电子对而形成的化学键。
> 
> Note that the Mn 3d bands in BiMnO3 overlap with the partially occupied Bi 6p orbitals, whereas in LaMnO3, the next highest bands are the unoccupied La 5d bands. 注意，BiMnO3中的Mn 3d能带与部分占据的Bi 6p轨道重叠，而在LaMnO3中，次高能带是未占据的La 5d能带。
> 
> The differences between the two band structures show up clearly in the bands along the Γ → X line. 两种能带结构之间的差异，清晰地显示在沿Γ → X线的能带中。
> 
> This region of the band structure is shown in Figure 9 with the symmetry labels added and the energy scale extended to include the lower energy O 2s and La 5p bands. 该能带结构区域显示在图9中，添加了对称性标记，并扩展了能量范围以包括较低能量的O 2s和La 5p能带。

> In the following discussion, we focus on the bands with Δ1 symmetry as capturing the essential features. 在下面的讨论中，我们专注于具有Δ1对称性的能带，因为它们抓住了基本特征。
> 
> The Δ1 bands are plotted with solid lines, and all other bands with dashed lines in Figure 9. 在图9中，Δ1能带用实线绘制，所有其他能带用虚线绘制。
> 
> In LaMnO3, the two oxygen 2p Δ1 bands decrease monotonically in energy from Γ to X, and the Mn 3d Δ1 band increases monotonically from Γ to X. 在LaMnO3中，两个氧2p Δ1能带从Γ到X能量单调递减，而Mn 3d Δ1能带从Γ到X能量单调递增。
> 
> Analysis of the charge density shows that the Fermi surface at the Γ point consists largely of Mn 3d electrons, with contributions from other atoms being very small. 电荷密度分析表明，Γ点处的费米面主要由Mn 3d电子组成，来自其他原子的贡献非常小。 **Fermi surface**: 费米面，在k空间中，将占据态和非占据态分开的等能面，能量等于费米能级。
> 
> The charge density at the X point is very similar, except that there is a small oxygen contribution, indicating that the amount of Mn 3d-O 2p hybridization increases along the Γ to X line. X点处的电荷密度非常相似，只是有少量的氧贡献，表明Mn 3d-O 2p杂化的量沿Γ到X线增加。
> 
> In BiMnO3 the behavior is quite different. 在BiMnO3中，行为完全不同。
> 
> First, the X1 symmetry of the Bi 6s band at the X point causes the X1 O 2p band to be “pushed up” in energy, resulting in a different ordering of the O 2p bands at the X point. 首先，Bi 6s能带在X点处的X1对称性，导致X1 O 2p能带在能量上被“推高”，从而导致X点处O 2p能带的顺序不同。
> 
> The very dispersive Bi 6p Δ1 band crosses the Mn 3d Δ1 band and moves below the Fermi level near the X point. 强色散的Bi 6p Δ1能带穿过Mn 3d Δ1能带，并在X点附近移动到费米能级以下。
> 
> As a result, the charge distribution at the Γ point is similar to that of LaMnO3; however, at X, there is a significant amount of charge density around the Bi atoms. 因此，Γ点处的电荷分布与LaMnO3相似；然而，在X点，Bi原子周围有大量的电荷密度。
> 
> Further analysis reveals that the Bi component is in the X′4 band, which crossed the highest Mn 3d band. 进一步分析表明，Bi分量位于X′4能带中，该能带穿过了最高的Mn 3d能带。
>

>

> ### 5.1.2. Preliminary Experimental Results for BiMnO3.
> 
> ### 5.1.2. BiMnO3的初步实验结果。
> 
> The conclusion from our tight-binding analysis, that there is strong hybridization between the O 2p and Bi 6p orbitals, is consistent with recent measurements showing an enhancement of charge ordering in Bi-doped CaMnO3.38 我们从紧束缚分析中得出的结论，即O 2p和Bi 6p轨道之间存在强杂化，与最近显示Bi掺杂CaMnO3中电荷有序增强的测量结果一致。38 **Charge ordering**: 电荷有序，指在混合价态化合物中，不同价态的离子在晶格中呈现规则排列的现象。
> 
> Cheetham and co-workers observed that the charge-ordered state in Bi0.3Ca0.7MnO3 persists to a higher temperature than does the charge-ordered state in La0.3Ca0.7MnO3. Cheetham及其同事观察到，Bi0.3Ca0.7MnO3中的电荷有序态，比La0.3Ca0.7MnO3中的电荷有序态持续到更高的温度。
> 
> They explained their observations by noting that the electronegativity of Bi enhances Bi-O hybridization and, in turn, reduces the amount of Mn-O hybridization. 他们通过指出Bi的电负性增强了Bi-O杂化，进而减少了Mn-O杂化的量，来解释他们的观察结果。 **Electronegativity**: 电负性，衡量原子吸引电子能力的相对标度。
> 
> This also provides a plausible explanation for the existence of ferromagnetism in BiMnO3. 这也为BiMnO3中铁磁性的存在提供了一个合理的解释。
> 
> The combination of structural distortion and reduced Mn 3d-O 2p overlap (both driven by bismuth-oxygen covalency) reduces the strength of the antiferromagnetic superexchange interaction, making ferromagnetic coupling more favorable. 结构畸变和Mn 3d-O 2p重叠的减少（两者都由铋-氧共价性驱动）降低了反铁磁超交换相互作用的强度，使铁磁耦合更有利。
> 
> Additional evidence in support of this phenomenon can be extracted from ref 39, in which a temperature-composition phase diagram for Bi1-xCaxMnO3, showing the charge-ordered transition and the Néel temperature, is plotted. 支持这一现象的其他证据，可以从参考文献39中提取，其中绘制了Bi1-xCaxMnO3的温度-组成相图，显示了电荷有序转变和奈尔温度。
> 
> Comparison with similar data for La1-xCaxMnO3 (for example, in ref 40) confirms that the charge-ordered phase persists to higher temperature in Bi-doped CaMnO3 than in La-doped CaMnO3. 与La1-xCaxMnO3的类似数据（例如，参考文献40）进行比较，证实了在Bi掺杂的CaMnO3中，电荷有序相持续到比La掺杂CaMnO3更高的温度。
> 
> Therefore, in addition to their potential utility as multiferroic materials, Bi compounds provide the ability to tune the positions of phase boundaries by substitution of Bi for the rare earth ions, which might prove valuable in optimizing materials properties for specific device applications. 因此，除了它们作为多铁性材料的潜在用途外，Bi化合物还提供了通过用Bi替代稀土离子来调节相边界位置的能力，这在优化特定器件应用的材料性能方面可能很有价值。 **Phase boundary**: 相界，指相图中不同相之间的分界线，在该线上两个相可以共存。
> 
> ### 5.1.3. Cubic Ferromagnetic Structures.
> 
> ### 5.1.3. 立方铁磁结构。
> 
> Next, we present the results of calculations in which the high-symmetry cubic structure is retained, but the electrons are allowed to spin polarize. 接下来，我们展示在高对称立方结构被保留，但允许电子自旋极化的情况下的计算结果。
> 
> We find that the introduction of spin polarization reduces the energy of both BiMnO3 and LaMnO3 by around 1 eV per unit cell compared with the paramagnetic case. 我们发现，与顺磁情况相比，引入自旋极化使BiMnO3和LaMnO3的能量每个晶胞降低约1 eV。
> 
> The most important observation of this section is that the differences between BiMnO3 and LaMnO3 that we observed in the paramagnetic calculations persist into the ferromagnetic phase, with the PM to FM transition introducing the same kinds of changes in both materials. 本节最重要的观察结果是，我们在顺磁计算中观察到的BiMnO3和LaMnO3之间的差异，持续到铁磁相，顺磁到铁磁的转变在两种材料中引入了相同类型的变化。
> 
> Figure 12 shows the calculated densities of states for cubic ferromagnetic LaMnO3 and BiMnO3. 图12显示了立方铁磁LaMnO3和BiMnO3的计算态密度。
> 
> The majority spins are represented by the solid line on the positive y axis, and the minority spins by the dotted line on the negative y axis. 多数自旋用正y轴上的实线表示，少数自旋用负y轴上的虚线表示。
> 
> In both LaMnO3 and BiMnO3, the down-spin Mn 3d band is split off from the O 2p band and has a similar form to the corresponding paramagnetic band. 在LaMnO3和BiMnO3中，自旋向下的Mn 3d能带都与O 2p能带分开，并且具有与相应顺磁能带相似的形式。
> 
> The up-spin Mn 3d hybridizes strongly with the O 2p, and there is no band gap for the majority carriers. 自旋向上的Mn 3d与O 2p强烈杂化，并且对于多数载流子没有带隙。
> 
> The up-spin DOS at the Fermi level in LaMnO3 is still high, indicating that the cubic FM state has a high energy. LaMnO3中费米能级处的自旋向上态密度仍然很高，表明立方铁磁态具有高能量。
> 
> This is consistent with the fact that the lowest-energy spin polarization in structurally relaxed LaMnO3 is antiferromagnetic.41 这与结构弛豫的LaMnO3中最低能量的自旋极化是反铁磁的事实一致。41
> 
> The DOS at the Fermi level in BiMnO3 is somewhat lower, suggesting that the FM phase should be more stable in BiMnO3 than in LaMnO3. BiMnO3中费米能级处的态密度稍低，表明铁磁相在BiMnO3中应比在LaMnO3中更稳定。
> 
> For both compounds, the Fermi level cuts through the very bottom of the down-spin Mn 3d bands, and the conduction band is occupied almost entirely by up-spin electrons. 对于这两种化合物，费米能级都穿过自旋向下Mn 3d能带的最底部，导带几乎完全由自旋向上的电子占据。
> 
> Again, the most obvious differences between the two electronic structures are the presence of the Bi 6s band between -10 and -12 eV and the contrasting forms of the Bi 6p and La 5d bands. 再次，两种电子结构之间最明显的差异，是-10 eV到-12 eV之间Bi 6s能带的存在，以及Bi 6p和La 5d能带的对比形式。
> 
> Figure 13 shows the up- and down-spin band structures for BiMnO3 and LaMnO3 along the high-symmetry axes of the simple cubic Brillouin zone. 图13显示了BiMnO3和LaMnO3沿简单立方布里渊区高对称轴的自旋向上和向下能带结构。
> 
> There are many similarities between the FM and PM energy bands, and our earlier conclusions regarding the origins of the differences between BiMnO3 and LaMnO3 continue to be valid. 铁磁和顺磁能带之间有许多相似之处，我们之前关于BiMnO3和LaMnO3之间差异起源的结论仍然有效。
> 
> A detailed analysis of the band structures is presented in ref 36. 对能带结构的详细分析见参考文献36。
> 
> Here, we summarize the important conclusions. 在这里，我们总结重要的结论。
> 
> First, the states that correspond to nonmagnetic orbitals (that is, the lower O 2p bands and the Bi 6s and La 5d bands) are unchanged from the paramagnetic state and are identical for up- and down-spin electrons. 首先，对应于非磁性轨道的态（即较低的O 2p能带以及Bi 6s和La 5d能带）与顺磁态相比没有变化，并且对于自旋向上和向下的电子是相同的。
> 
> Second, for both materials, the up-spin Mn 3d and O 2p bands are strongly hybridized, and there is no gap between them. 其次，对于两种材料，自旋向上的Mn 3d和O 2p能带是强烈杂化的，它们之间没有间隙。
> 
> However, the down-spin Mn 3d are split off from the O 2p bands by a larger gap than in the PM case. 然而，自旋向下的Mn 3d能带与O 2p能带之间的间隙，比顺磁情况下的间隙更大。
> 
> Finally, the BiMnO3 minority conduction electrons have a large directional Bi 6p component, in striking contrast to those in LaMnO3, in which the electrons are localized entirely in the Mn 3d orbitals. 最后，BiMnO3的少数载流子传导电子具有很大的方向性Bi 6p分量，这与LaMnO3形成鲜明对比，在LaMnO3中，电子完全局域在Mn 3d轨道中。
> 
> The fact that the conduction electrons partly occupy p-type atomic orbitals should produce quite different transport characteristics than those observed in conventional rare earth manganites, in which the conduction bands are entirely Mn 3d-type. 传导电子部分占据p型原子轨道这一事实，应该会产生与传统稀土锰酸盐中观察到的完全不同的输运特性，在传统稀土锰酸盐中，导带完全是Mn 3d型的。
> 
> ### 5.1.4. Soft-Mode Ferroelectric Distortions.
> 
> ### 5.1.4. 软模铁电畸变。
> 
> Finally, we calculate the phonon modes in BiMnO3 and LaMnO3 to determine the existence and nature of ferroelectric instabilities. 最后，我们计算BiMnO3和LaMnO3中的声子模式，以确定铁电不稳定性的存在和性质。
> 
> In keeping with the philosophy of this paper, we study the lattice distortions of the high-symmetry cubic phases and compare our results for BiMnO3 with those for LaMnO3. 为了与本文的理念保持一致，我们研究高对称立方相的晶格畸变，并比较BiMnO3与LaMnO3的结果。
> 
> We restrict our discussion to zone-center phonons. 我们的讨论仅限于区中心声子。
> 
> The perovskite manganites have 5 atoms per unit cell, which results in 15 phonon branches, 3 acoustical and 12 optical. 钙钛矿锰酸盐每个晶胞有5个原子，这导致15条声子分支，3条声学支和12条光学支。 **Acoustical phonon**: 声学声子，指晶格中原子整体协同振动，频率在长波极限下趋于零的声子模式。 **Optical phonon**: 光学声子，指晶格中原胞内原子相对振动，在长波极限下频率不为零的声子模式。
> 
> At the Γ point, all phonons are 3-fold degenerate, so there is one acoustical phonon frequency (which is zero) and four optical frequencies. 在Γ点，所有声子都是三重简并的，因此有一个声学声子频率（为零）和四个光学频率。
> 
> We are interested in the optical phonons that have negative eigenvalues, indicating lattice instabilities. 我们感兴趣的是具有负本征值的光学声子，这表明晶格不稳定。
> 
> The force constant matrices for LaMnO3 and BiMnO3 were determined by calculating the Hellmann-Feynman forces resulting from the displacement of each atom in turn 0.1 Å along the z direction of the unit cell. LaMnO3和BiMnO3的力常数矩阵，是通过计算每个原子依次沿晶胞z方向位移0.1 Å所产生的赫尔曼-费曼力来确定的。 **Hellmann-Feynman force**: 赫尔曼-费曼力，在量子力学中，作用在原子核上的力可以通过电子波函数和静电学原理计算得出。
> 
> The forces exerted on the Mn ions by the other ions were determined using the acoustic sum rule. 通过声学求和规则确定其他离子对Mn离子施加的力。
> 
> We calculated the Mn-Mn force by applying the acoustic sum rule to both the columns and the rows of the resulting matrix. 我们通过对结果矩阵的行和列都应用声学求和规则来计算Mn-Mn力。
> 
> The two values differed by less than ∼0.001 eV/Å. 两个值相差小于约0.001 eV/Å。
> 
> The paramagnetic cubic phases of both LaMnO3 and BiMnO3 have two unstable zone-center phonon modes. LaMnO3和BiMnO3的顺磁立方相，都有两个不稳定的区中心声子模式。
> 
> The frequencies and eigenvectors of the unstable modes are given in Table 3. 不稳定模式的频率和本征矢量在表3中给出。
> 
> The mode involving motion of the equatorial oxygens, shown in Figure 14b, does not correspond to a ferroelectric distortion and will not be discussed further. 涉及赤道氧运动的模式，如图14b所示，不对应于铁电畸变，将不再进一步讨论。
> 
> The displacement pattern for the second unstable mode is shown in Figure 14a. 第二个不稳定模式的位移模式如图14a所示。
> 
> Here, the large (Bi or La) cations are moving in opposition to the oxygen cage, resulting in a ferroelectric displacement. 在这里，大的（Bi或La）阳离子相对于氧笼反向运动，导致铁电位移。
> 
> The imaginary frequency of this mode is twice as large in BiMnO3 than in LaMnO3, indicating a stronger instability in the Bi compound. 该模式的虚频在BiMnO3中比在LaMnO3中大一倍，表明Bi化合物中的不稳定性更强。
> 
> It is interesting to note that the Mn is moving in the same direction as the oxygen ions. 有趣的是，Mn与氧离子沿相同方向运动。
> 
> This is opposite to the behavior of the Ti ion in BaTiO3 and PbTiO3, but similar to the behavior of Zr in PbZrO3.42 这与BaTiO3和PbTiO3中Ti离子的行为相反，但与PbZrO3中Zr的行为相似。42
> 
> In the ferromagnetic phase of BiMnO3, the ferroelectric mode remains strongly unstable, at 82.30i cm-1. 在BiMnO3的铁磁相中，铁电模式仍然强烈不稳定，频率为82.30i cm-1。
> 
> However, he imaginary frequency phonons in the ferromagnetic phase of LaMnO3 are considerably less unstable than the corresponding imaginary frequency paramagnetic phonons. 然而，LaMnO3铁磁相中的虚频声子，其不稳定性远低于相应的顺磁虚频声子。
> 
> In fact, the ferroelectric mode in LaMnO3 is now only slightly unstable, at 21.1i cm-1, and the other formerly unstable mode now has a positive frequency. 实际上，LaMnO3中的铁电模式现在仅略微不稳定，频率为21.1i cm-1，而另一个先前不稳定的模式现在具有正频率。
> 
> Note, however, that the internal stresses are the same for the paramagnetic and ferromagnetic cubic phases in both materials. 然而，请注意，两种材料中顺磁和铁磁立方相的内应力是相同的。
> 
> This weakening of the zone-center ferroelectric instability in LaMnO3 is likely indicative of the tendency of the spin-polarized d4 ion to Jahn-Teller distort in the absence of other bonding considerations. LaMnO3中区中心铁电不稳定性的减弱，可能表明自旋极化的d4离子在没有其他键合因素时，倾向于发生姜-泰勒畸变。
> 
> It is clear from our analysis that the presence of ferroelectricity in BiMnO3, and the absence of ferroelectricity in LaMnO3, can be explained by the different zone-center lattice instabilities. 从我们的分析中可以清楚地看出，BiMnO3中铁电性的存在，以及LaMnO3中铁电性的缺失，可以用不同的区中心晶格不稳定性来解释。
> 
> Although a definitive prediction requires calculation of the phonon dispersion throughout the entire Brillouin zone, it is likely that the weakly unstable zone-center phonon in LaMnO3 will be overshadowed by a stronger instability elsewhere in the Brillouin zone, reproducing theoretically the experimentally observed Jahn-Teller distortion. 虽然确定的预测需要计算整个布里渊区的声子色散，但LaMnO3中弱不稳定的区中心声子，很可能会被布里渊区其他地方更强的的不稳定性所掩盖，从而在理论上重现了实验观察到的姜-泰勒畸变。
> 
> Similarly, the very unstable ferroelectric mode at the zone center in BiMnO3 is likely to dominate over possible unstable modes at other frequencies, confirming theoretically the suggested existence of ferroelectricity in BiMnO3. 类似地，BiMnO3区中心非常不稳定的铁电模式，很可能主导其他频率上可能的不稳定模式，从而在理论上证实了BiMnO3中铁电性存在的建议。
> 
> This mode is driven by Bi-O covalency and creates a Bi-O electric dipole moment, in contrast to the conventional perovskite oxide ferroelectrics, in which B cation-oxygen covalency is the principal driving force for the ferroelectric distortion. 该模式由Bi-O共价性驱动，并产生Bi-O电偶极矩，这与传统的钙钛矿氧化物铁电体形成对比，在后者中，B阳离子-氧共价性是铁电畸变的主要驱动力。
> 
> Thus, we conclude that the d electron occupation on the manganese ion in BiMnO3 continues to inhibit displacement of the B cation. 因此，我们得出结论，BiMnO3中锰离子上的d电子占据，继续抑制B阳离子的位移。
> 
> It is only because of the unusual behavior of the A cation that ferroelectricity can occur at all. 仅仅是因为A阳离子的异常行为，铁电性才可能发生。
> 
> ### 5.1.5. Electronic Ferroelectricity.
> 
> ### 5.1.5. 电子铁电性。
> 
> Ferroelectricity can also occur if the magnetic electronic state in the cubic phase (before the ferroelectric lattice distortion takes place) has a propensity to dipolar instability.43 如果在立方相（铁电晶格畸变发生之前）中的磁电子态具有偶极不稳定性的倾向，铁电性也可能发生。43
> 
> In this case, the driving mechanism for ferroelectricity is electronic instability,43 rather than the usual softening of the phonon mode.44,18 在这种情况下，铁电性的驱动机制是电子不稳定性，43而不是通常的声子模式软化。44,18
> 
> In these so-called “electronic ferroelectrics”, the ferroelectric phase transition is driven by a change in the electronic structure rather than the crystal structure. 在这些所谓的“电子铁电体”中，铁电相变是由电子结构的变化，而不是晶体结构的变化驱动的。 **Electronic ferroelectricity**: 电子铁电性，一种铁电性起源于电子不稳定性而非晶格离子位移的理论模型。
> 
> (Of course electron-phonon coupling can cause a lattice distortion as a secondary effect of the transition, but a change in crystal structure is not required). （当然，电子-声子耦合可以引起晶格畸变作为相变的次级效应，但并不需要晶体结构的变化）。
> 
> Electronic ferroelectricity has been demonstrated theoretically in the strongly correlated insulating phase of the Falicov-Kimball model, in which the Coulomb attraction between the localized f holes and itinerant d electrons leads to a built-in coherence between d and f states.43 电子铁电性已经在Falicov-Kimball模型的强关联绝缘相中得到了理论证明，在该模型中，局域f空穴和巡游d电子之间的库仑吸引，导致d态和f态之间产生内禀相干性。43 **Coulomb attraction**: 库仑吸引力，指带异号电荷的粒子之间的静电吸引力。
> 
> Subsequent Bose-Einstein condensation of d-f excitons, in which d and f states of opposite parity are paired, breaks the inversion symmetry of the underlying crystal. 随后的d-f激子的玻色-爱因斯坦凝聚（其中相反宇称的d态和f态配对）打破了底层晶体的反演对称性。 **Exciton**: 激子，指半导体中由库仑相互作用束缚在一起的电子-空穴对，是一种中性激发。 **Bose-Einstein condensation**: 玻色-爱因斯坦凝聚，指玻色子在极低温度下，大量占据系统最低能态的现象。
> 
> A requirement for the condensation is that the d-f Coulomb interaction dominates over the hybridization. 凝聚的一个要求是，d-f库仑相互作用主导于杂化作用。
> 
> The phase transition to the electronic ferroelectric state occurs when the d-f exciton energy goes to zero (instead of when a TO phonon frequency vanishes). 向电子铁电态的相变发生在d-f激子能量变为零时（而不是在横光学声子频率消失时）。
> 
> Predicted values of the built-in polarization in an electronic ferroelectric are around 10 μC/cm2, similar to the values found in perovskite ferroelectrics. 电子铁电体中内建极化强度的预测值约为10 μC/cm2，与钙钛矿铁电体中发现的值相似。
> 
> The results that we have obtained for BiMnO336 suggest that electronic ferroelectricity might occur in this and other multiferroic materials. 我们为BiMnO3获得的结果36表明，电子铁电性可能在这种材料和其他多铁性材料中发生。
> 
> In the case of BiMnO3, the localized state will be Mn 3d in origin, and the itinerant state will derive from Bi 6p. 在BiMnO3的情况下，局域态将源于Mn 3d，而巡游态将源于Bi 6p。
> 
> We observe a band crossing between the Bi 6p and Mn 3d bands of the same symmetry in the cubic phase, indicating zero hybridization between these orbitals. 我们观察到立方相中，具有相同对称性的Bi 6p和Mn 3d能带之间发生能带交叉，表明这些轨道之间杂化为零。
> 
> In addition, charge transfer occurs from a nominally occupied Mn 3d state into a Bi 6p band, resulting in an on-site d-p Coulomb interaction. 此外，电荷从名义上占据的Mn 3d态转移到Bi 6p能带，导致现场d-p库仑相互作用。
> 
> Confirmation of the existence of electronic ferroelectricity will require further theoretical and experimental work. 确认电子铁电性的存在将需要进一步的理论和实验工作。
> 
> ### 5.1.6. Related BiBO3 Materials.
> 
> ### 5.1.6. 相关的BiBO3材料。
> 
> Our calculations have shown that a Bi A-site cation is favorable for ferroelectricity in perovskite oxides, regardless of whether the mechanism for ferroelectricity is electronic or soft-mode. 我们的计算表明，无论铁电性的机制是电子的还是软模的，Bi A位阳离子都有利于钙钛矿氧化物中的铁电性。
> 
> Therefore, we anticipate that other Bi compounds will be good candidates for magnetoelectric multiferroism. 因此，我们预计其他Bi化合物将是磁电多铁性的良好候选者。
> 
> BiFeO3 is the only BiXO3 compound (X = trivalent transition metal) that has been studied in any detail. BiFeO3是唯一被详细研究过的BiXO3化合物（X = 三价过渡金属）。
> 
> It is known to be ferroelectric below 1110 K and antiferromagnetic with a Néel temperature of 670 K.45 已知它在1110 K以下为铁电性，并具有670 K的奈尔温度的反铁磁性。45
> 
> It shows the linear magnetoelectric effect, with applied magnetic fields inducing weak ferromagnetism and large increases in polarization.46 它显示出线性磁电效应，外加磁场可诱导弱铁磁性并使极化强度大幅增加。46 **Linear magnetoelectric effect**: 线性磁电效应，指材料中磁场诱导的极化强度（或电场诱导的磁化强度）与外加场大小成正比的现象。
> 
> Note that the Fe3+ ion in BiFeO3 has the d5 electron configuration and, therefore, is not a Jahn-Teller ion. 注意，BiFeO3中的Fe3+离子具有d5电子构型，因此不是姜-泰勒离子。
> 
> BiCrO3 (in which the Cr3+ ion has three d electrons) is also reported to be antiferromagnetic with a weak ferromagnetic moment,35 but there have been no investigations (to our knowledge) of ferroelectricity. BiCrO3（其中Cr3+离子有三个d电子）也被报道为具有弱铁磁矩的反铁磁性，35但（据我们所知）还没有关于铁电性的研究。
> 
> Little is known about BiCoO3 and BiNiO3 (d6 and d7 on the Co3+ and Ni3+ ions, respectively) beyond the fact that they have the cubic perovskite structure, but BiCo0.5Fe0.5O3 is known to be ferromagnetic,47 probably because of a double-exchange-type mechanism between neighboring d6 and d5 ions. 关于BiCoO3和BiNiO3（分别在Co3+和Ni3+离子上为d6和d7），除了知道它们具有立方钙钛矿结构外，知之甚少。但BiCo0.5Fe0.5O3已知是铁磁性的，47可能是因为相邻的d6和d5离子之间的双交换型机制。 **Double-exchange**: 双交换，指一种磁性耦合机制，通常发生在混合价态的过渡金属氧化物中，通过巡游电子（或空穴）实现铁磁耦合。
> 
> We are beginning theoretical work on other Bi perovskite oxides with the hope that our results will stimulate experimental interest in these less-well-studied materials. 我们正在开始对其他Bi钙钛矿氧化物进行理论研究，希望我们的结果能激发对这些研究较少材料的实验兴趣。
> 
> ## 5.2. Yttrium Manganite.
> 
> ## 5.2. 锰酸钇。
> 
> The literature on the hexagonal manganites is much more limited than that on the cubic perovskite manganites, and modern work focuses largely on yttrium manganite, YMnO3. 关于六方锰酸盐的文献，比立方钙钛矿锰酸盐的文献少得多，现代工作主要集中在锰酸钇YMnO3上。
> 
> Although yttrium is not a rare earth element, it forms a stable trivalent cation with an ionic radius similar to those of the smaller rare earth ions. 尽管钇不是稀土元素，但它形成稳定的三价阳离子，其离子半径与较小的稀土离子相似。
> 
> It is favored over the smaller rare earth ions in research studies for two reasons. 在研究中，它比更小的稀土离子更受青睐，原因有两个。
> 
> First, a study of magnetic ordering on the Mn3+ ions is more straightforward in YMnO3 than in the rare earth manganites as there is no perturbation from the rare earth f-electron magnetic moment. 首先，在YMnO3中研究Mn3+离子上的磁有序，比在稀土锰酸盐中更直接，因为没有来自稀土f电子磁矩的扰动。
> 
> In addition, YMnO3 forms in both the hexagonal and orthorhombic phases on solution synthesis at ambient pressure,48 making it particularly appealing for use in a comparative study. 此外，YMnO3在常压下的溶液合成中，形成六方和正交两种相，48这使得它在比较研究中特别有吸引力。
> 
> In this section, we compare the electronic properties of YMnO3 in its high-pressure cubic perovskite structure with those of the prototypical cubic manganite, LaMnO3. 在本节中，我们比较了YMnO3在其高压立方钙钛矿结构中的电子性质，与原型立方锰酸盐LaMnO3的电子性质。
> 
> The purpose of our calculations is to determine the factors that make the cubic structure unstable, leading to stabilization of the hexagonal ferroelectric structure. 我们计算的目的是确定使立方结构不稳定，导致六方铁电结构稳定的因素。
> 
> In particular, by analogy with our results for BiMnO3, we investigate whether involvement of the large cation (Y3+ in this case) in covalent bonding contributes to the stabilization of the ferroelectric phase. 特别是，通过类比我们对BiMnO3的结果，我们研究大阳离子（本例中为Y3+）是否参与共价键合，从而有助于稳定铁电相。
> 
> We begin by calculating the electronic structure without including magnetic effects, and then lower the magnetic symmetry to the ferromagnetic phase. 我们首先计算不包括磁效应的电子结构，然后将磁对称性降低到铁磁相。
> 
> Our calculations are performed at the experimental lattice constants of 3.95 Å for LaMnO3 and 3.84 for YMnO3. 我们的计算是在实验晶格常数下进行的，LaMnO3为3.95 Å，YMnO3为3.84 Å。 **Lattice constant**: 晶格常数，指晶体中晶胞的物理尺寸。
> 
> ### 5.2.1. Summary of Experimental Results.
> 
> ### 5.2.1. 实验结果总结。
> 
> Early (1960s) work on YMnO348 established the hexagonal phase to be ferroelectric, with the hexagonal perovskite structure and the P63cm space group. 早期（20世纪60年代）对YMnO3的研究48确定六方相是铁电性的，具有六方钙钛矿结构和P63cm空间群。 **Space group**: 空间群，指晶体内部微观结构的对称操作群，共有230种。
> 
> The hexagonal perovskite structure consists of ABC ACB... stacking of close packed O layers, with the Mn ions occupying 5-fold-coordinated sites and the rare earth atoms in 7-fold-coordinated interstices. 六方钙钛矿结构由ABC ACB...堆垛的密排O层组成，Mn离子占据五配位位点，稀土原子位于七配位的空隙中。
> 
> The ideal high-temperature paraelectric structure consists of two formula units per unit cell and is shown in Figure 15. 理想的高温顺电结构由每个晶胞两个化学式单元组成，如图15所示。
> 
> Because this structure does not occur in the perovskite ferrites, it has been suggested that the ability of Mn3+ to form 5-fold trigonal bipyramids through dsp3 hybrid bonding is a requirement.48 由于这种结构不出现在钙钛矿铁氧体中，有人提出，Mn3+通过dsp3杂化键形成五重三角双锥的能力是一个必要条件。48
> 
> Early reports of a weak parasitic ferromagnetism49 were soon shown to be the result of Mn3O4 impurity contaminating the powdered phase,50 and weak ferromagnetism was shown to be absent in single-crystal samples.50 早期关于弱寄生铁磁性的报告，49很快被证明是Mn3O4杂质污染粉末相的结果，50并且在单晶样品中证明不存在弱铁磁性。50
> 
> In the 1970s, work began on the orthorhombic phase. 在20世纪70年代，开始了对正交相的研究。
> 
> Susceptibility measurements established that orthorhombic YMnO3 is an antiferromagnet with a Néel temperature of 42 K, although significant deviations from Curie-Weiss behavior were seen well above the manganese ordering temperature.51 磁化率测量确定，正交YMnO3是一种反铁磁体，奈尔温度为42 K，尽管在远高于锰有序温度时，也观察到与居里-外斯行为的显著偏差。51
> 
> A more detailed study52 concluded that the Mn3+ ordering is helical with a propagation wavevector k = [0, 0.0786, 0] and a helical angle from plane to plane of 14°. 一项更详细的研究52得出结论，Mn3+有序是螺旋形的，传播波矢为k = [0, 0.0786, 0]，面间的螺旋角为14°。
> 
> Within each plane the ordering is A-type antiferromagnetic. 在每个平面内，有序是A型反铁磁性的。
> 
> Modern studies of hexagonal YMnO3 have revealed a coupling between the ferroelectric and magnetic ordering.34 对六方YMnO3的现代研究揭示了铁电有序和磁有序之间的耦合。34
> 
> Although the ferroelectric Curie temperature (914 K) is quite different from the Néel temperature (80 K), anomalies in the dielectric constant and loss tangent near the Néel temperature are indicative of coupling between the ferroelectric and antiferromagnetic orders. 尽管铁电居里温度（914 K）与奈尔温度（80 K）相差很大，但在奈尔温度附近的介电常数和损耗角正切异常，表明铁电和反铁磁有序之间存在耦合。 **Loss tangent**: 损耗角正切，衡量电介质在交变电场中能量损耗的物理量。
> 
> The coupling has been attributed to changes in the phonon spectrum associated with the antiferromagnetic transition.34 这种耦合被归因于与反铁磁转变相关的声子谱的变化。34
> 
> Raman and infrared spectroscopy of the high-temperature paraelectric and low-temperature ferroelectric phases show only weak bands in the ferroelectric phase because of the noncentrosymmetricity. 高温顺电相和低温铁电相的拉曼和红外光谱显示，由于非中心对称性，铁电相中只有弱带。
> 
> This indicates that the structural differences between the ferroelectric and paraelectric phases are very small. 这表明铁电相和顺电相之间的结构差异非常小。
> 
> Nonlinear optical spectroscopy53 shows two types of optical second-harmonic spectra of the Mn3+ ions in hexagonal YMnO3, one from the noncentrosymmetric ferroelectric ordering of charges and the other from the centrosymmetric antiferromagnetic ordering of spins. 非线性光学光谱53显示，六方YMnO3中Mn3+离子的两种光学二次谐波光谱，一种来自非中心对称的电荷铁电有序，另一种来自中心对称的自旋反铁磁有序。 **Second-harmonic generation**: 二次谐波产生，一种非线性光学效应，指两个相同频率的光子相互作用产生一个频率加倍的新光子的过程。
> 
> Partial overlapping between the electronic transitions results in a nonlinear optical polarization that depends on two order parameters. 电子跃迁之间的部分重叠，产生依赖于两个序参量的非线性光学极化。
> 
> A generalized Ginzburg-Landau formalism has been developed, which shows that the second-harmonic generation susceptibility is directly proportional to the bilinear combination of both order parameters.54 已经发展了一种广义金兹堡-朗道形式理论，它表明二次谐波产生磁化率与两个序参量的双线性组合成正比。54 **Ginzburg-Landau formalism**: 金兹堡-朗道理论，一种用于描述相变的唯象理论，通过引入序参量并用自由能展开来研究系统在相变点附近的行为。
> 
> Finally, epitaxial thin films of hexagonal YMnO3 have been grown on MgO(111) and ZnO/Al(0001)55 and explored for use as nonvolatile memory devices. 最后，六方YMnO3的外延薄膜已在MgO(111)和ZnO/Al(0001)上生长，55并被探索用于非易失性存储器件。 **Epitaxial**: 外延，指一种晶体生长技术，在单晶衬底上生长出与衬底有特定晶体取向关系的薄膜。 **Nonvolatile memory**: 非易失性存储器，指在断电后仍能保存数据的存储器件。
> 
> There has also been some modern work on the orthorhombic phase of YMnO3. 也有一些关于正交相YMnO3的现代工作。
> 
> Attempts have been made to find a synthesis that will produce a larger proportion of the metastable orthorhombic phase.56 已经尝试寻找一种合成方法，以产生更大比例的亚稳正交相。56
> 
> In addition, the Raman spectrum of orthorhombic YMnO3 has been measured, and the Raman active phonon modes determined and compared with those of LaMnO3.57 此外，测量了正交YMnO3的拉曼光谱，确定了拉曼活性声子模式，并与LaMnO3的模式进行了比较。57
> 
> ### 5.2.2. Cubic Paramagnetic Structures.
> 
> ### 5.2.2. 立方顺磁结构。
> 
> First, we present results for YMnO3 in the cubic paramagnetic phase and compare them with our earlier results for LaMnO3. 首先，我们展示立方顺磁相YMnO3的结果，并将其与我们之前对LaMnO3的结果进行比较。
> 
> Figure 16 shows the calculated band structure of cubic paramagnetic YMnO3, plotted along the high-symmetry axes of the cubic Brillouin zone. 图16显示了立方顺磁YMnO3沿立方布里渊区高对称轴的计算能带结构。
> 
> The plotted energy range is from -10 to 5 eV, and the lower-lying semi-core states have been omitted for clarity. 绘制的能量范围是从-10 eV到5 eV，为清晰起见，省略了较低能量的半芯态。
> 
> The Fermi level is set to zero. 费米能级设为零。
> 
> As in LaMnO3, the broad series of bands between approximately -2 and -7 eV arises from the oxygen 2p orbitals. 与LaMnO3中一样，大约-2 eV到-7 eV之间的宽系列能带源于氧的2p轨道。
> 
> Above the oxygen 2p bands, and separated from them by an energy gap, are the Mn 3d bands. 在氧2p能带之上，并被能隙隔开的是Mn 3d能带。
> 
> The Mn 3d bands are slightly broader than those in LaMnO3 because the smaller lattice constant results in greater Mn 3d-O 2p overlap. Mn 3d能带比LaMnO3中的略宽，因为较小的晶格常数导致更大的Mn 3d-O 2p重叠。
> 
> As in LaMnO3, the Mn 3d bands are divided into two subbands—the lower energy t2g bands and the higher energy eg bands—as a result of crystal-field splitting by the octahedral oxygen anions. 与LaMnO3中一样，由于八面体氧阴离子的晶体场分裂，Mn 3d能带被分成两个子带——低能的t2g带和高能的eg带。
> 
> The Fermi level lies near the top of the Mn 3d t2g bands and is in a region of high density of states, suggesting that a lower-energy structure could be achieved by allowing spin polarization and/or structural distortion. 费米能级位于Mn 3d t2g带顶部附近，并且处于高态密度区域，这表明通过允许自旋极化和/或结构畸变可以实现更低的能量结构。
> 
> In LaMnO3, the Mn 3d bands were largely separated in energy at each k point from the next-highest-energy La 5d bands. 在LaMnO3中，在每个k点处，Mn 3d能带在能量上与次高能量的La 5d能带基本分离。
> 
> The Y 4d bands are slightly lower in energy than the La 5d bands, resulting in a small amount of overlap with the top of the Mn 3d bands. Y 4d能带的能量略低于La 5d能带，导致与Mn 3d能带顶部有少量重叠。
> 
> This occurs at around 3 eV above the Fermi energy, however, and is unlikely to have a strong effect on structural stability or transport properties. 然而，这发生在费米能级以上约3 eV处，不太可能对结构稳定性或输运性质产生强烈影响。
> 
> Again, to quantify the interactions in YMnO3, we perform a tight-binding analysis of the band structure and compare our results with those obtained in section 5.1 for LaMnO3. 再次，为了量化YMnO3中的相互作用，我们对能带结构进行了紧束缚分析，并将结果与第5.1节中为LaMnO3获得的结果进行比较。
> 
> The similarities between LaMnO3 and YMnO3 are particularly striking along the Γ → X line. LaMnO3和YMnO3之间的相似性，沿Γ → X线尤为显著。
> 
> This region of the band structure is shown in Figure 17, with the symmetry labels added and the energy scale extended to include the lower-energy O 2s and La 5p bands. 该能带结构区域显示在图17中，添加了对称性标记，并扩展了能量范围以包括较低能量的O 2s和La 5p能带。
> 
> For clarity, the Δ1 bands are plotted with solid lines, all other bands are plotted with dashed lines, and the symmetry labels are written next to the line. 为清晰起见，Δ1能带用实线绘制，所有其他能带用虚线绘制，对称性标记写在线的旁边。
> 
> The behavior of the bands in YMnO3 is similar to that of the corresponding bands in LaMnO3. YMnO3中能带的行为与LaMnO3中相应能带的行为相似。
> 
> In both cases, the oxygen 2p Δ1 bands decrease monotonically in energy from Γ to X, and the Mn 3d Δ1 band increases monotonically from Γ to X. 在两种情况下，氧2p Δ1能带从Γ到X能量单调递减，Mn 3d Δ1能带从Γ到X能量单调递增。
> 
> Also, in both cases, the lowest-energy O 2p Δ5 band is approximately constant in energy, whereas the higher-energy O 2p Δ5 bands decrease monotonically and the Mn 3d Δ5 bands increase monotonically. 同样，在两种情况下，最低能量的O 2p Δ5能带在能量上近似恒定，而较高能量的O 2p Δ5能带单调递减，Mn 3d Δ5能带单调递增。
> 
> Both the O 2s and 2p Δ2 bands decrease monotonically along Γ to X, and one of the Mn 3d Δ2 bands is constant, while the other increases in energy. O 2s和2p Δ2能带都沿Γ到X单调递减，而Mn 3d Δ2能带之一恒定，另一个能量增加。
> 
> All Δ′2 bands are approximately constant in energy. 所有Δ′2能带在能量上近似恒定。
> 
> As in the case of LaMnO3, a good fit (RMS deviation = 0.2) is obtained using only the Mn 3d, O 2s, and O 2p orbitals in the basis. 与LaMnO3的情况一样，仅使用基组中的Mn 3d、O 2s和O 2p轨道就获得了良好的拟合（均方根偏差 = 0.2）。
> 
> The tight-binding parameters that we obtained by nonlinear least-squares fitting37 to the calculated ab initio energies at 21 points along the Γ to X axis are given in Table 1. 我们通过对沿Γ到X轴的21个点上的计算从头算能量进行非线性最小二乘拟合获得的紧束缚参数，37在表1中给出。
> 
> The parameters for YMnO3 are similar to those for LaMnO3, except for an increase of around 10% in the Mn 3d-O nearest-neighbor overlaps. YMnO3的参数与LaMnO3的参数相似，只是Mn 3d-O最近邻重叠增加了约10%。
> 
> This is a consequence of the smaller lattice constant in YMnO3 and results in the slightly broader Mn 3d bands noted above. 这是YMnO3中较小晶格常数的结果，并导致了上面提到的略宽的Mn 3d能带。
> 
> The results of our tight-binding analysis indicate that there is no significant covalent bonding between the Y 4d orbitals and either the oxygen 2p or the Mn 3d orbitals. 我们紧束缚分析的结果表明，Y 4d轨道与氧2p或Mn 3d轨道之间没有显著的共价键合。
> 
> We anticipate a similar situation in the smaller rare earth manganites, although the situation might be complicated slightly by the lanthanide contraction, which should cause the 5d orbital energies to be lowered into the energy range of the Mn 3d orbitals, with a resulting decrease in ionic character.58 我们预期在更小的稀土锰酸盐中，也有类似的情况，尽管情况可能因镧系收缩而略微复杂化，镧系收缩应导致5d轨道能量降低到Mn 3d轨道的能量范围内，从而降低离子性。58 **Lanthanide contraction**: 镧系收缩，指镧系元素中，随着原子序数增加，原子半径和离子半径反而减小的现象。
> 
> Therefore, we conjecture that the observed differences between the properties of hexagonal and cubic manganites are merely the result of the different sizes of the large cations in the two materials and are not due to any additional covalent bonding effects. 因此，我们推测，观察到的六方和立方锰酸盐性质之间的差异，仅仅是两种材料中大阳离子尺寸不同的结果，而不是由于任何额外的共价键合效应。
> 
> ### 5.2.3. Cubic Ferromagnetic Structures.
> 
> ### 5.2.3. 立方铁磁结构。
> 
> As in the case of BiMnO3 and LaMnO3, the differences and similarities between YMnO3 and LaMnO3 that we observed in the paramagnetic calculations persist into the ferromagnetic phase, with the PM to FM transition introducing the same kinds of changes in both materials. 与BiMnO3和LaMnO3的情况一样，我们在顺磁计算中观察到的YMnO3和LaMnO3之间的差异和相似性，持续到铁磁相，顺磁到铁磁的转变在两种材料中引入了相同类型的变化。
> 
> We find that the introduction of spin polarization reduces the energy of YMnO3 by ∼0.7 eV compared with the PM phase, slightly less than both BiMnO3 and LaMnO3, which were stabilized by ∼1 eV. 我们发现，与顺磁相相比，引入自旋极化使YMnO3的能量降低了约0.7 eV，略低于BiMnO3和LaMnO3两者，它们稳定化了约1 eV。
> 
> Figure 18 shows the calculated up- and down-spin band structures for YMnO3 along the high-symmetry axes of the simple cubic Brillouin zone. 图18显示了YMnO3沿简单立方布里渊区高对称轴的计算自旋向上和向下能带结构。
> 
> There are many similarities between the FM and PM energy bands, and our earlier conclusions regarding the differences and similarities between YMnO3 and LaMnO3 continue to be valid. 铁磁和顺磁能带之间有许多相似之处，我们之前关于YMnO3和LaMnO3之间差异和相似性的结论仍然有效。
> 
> The down-spin Mn 3d band in FM YMnO3 is split off from the O 2p band and has a form similar to that of the corresponding bands in PM YMnO3 and in PM and FM LaMnO3. 铁磁YMnO3中自旋向下的Mn 3d能带与O 2p能带分开，并且具有与顺磁YMnO3以及顺磁和铁磁LaMnO3中相应能带相似的形式。
> 
> The hybridization between up-spin Mn 3d electrons and oxygen remains weak in YMnO3, and the Mn 3d band remains split off from the O 2p band. 在YMnO3中，自旋向上的Mn 3d电子与氧之间的杂化仍然很弱，Mn 3d能带仍然与O 2p能带分开。
> 
> This is in contrast to LaMnO3, for which the majority Mn 3d band mixes strongly with the O 2p band. 这与LaMnO3形成对比，在LaMnO3中，多数Mn 3d能带与O 2p能带强烈混合。
> 
> As a result, the O 2p bands in YMnO3 are identical for up and down spin. 因此，YMnO3中的O 2p能带对于自旋向上和向下是相同的。
> 
> In contrast, in LaMnO3 the higher O 2p bands are strongly hybridized with the Mn 3d bands and, therefore, show a polarization dependence. 相比之下，在LaMnO3中，较高的O 2p能带与Mn 3d能带强烈杂化，因此显示出极化依赖性。
> 
> The Y 4d bands are largely spin-independent, although the minority Y 4d bands are at a slightly higher energy than the majority bands. Y 4d能带在很大程度上与自旋无关，尽管少数Y 4d能带的能量，略高于多数能带。
> 
> Finally, the Fermi level in YMnO3 is in a region of high density of states for both up- and down-spin bands, indicating that cubic YMnO3 is not a half-metallic material. 最后，YMnO3中的费米能级，对于自旋向上和向下能带都处于高态密度区域，表明立方YMnO3不是半金属材料。 **Half-metallic**: 半金属，指一种材料，其电子结构中，一种自旋方向的电子表现为金属性，而另一种自旋方向的电子表现为绝缘性或半导体性。
> 
> ### 5.2.4. Conclusions for YMnO3.
> 
> ### 5.2.4. YMnO3的结论。
> 
> In conclusion, our results indicate that the interaction between the Y 4d and O 2p atomic orbitals in YMnO3 is largely ionic in character. 总之，我们的结果表明，YMnO3中Y 4d和O 2p原子轨道之间的相互作用，在很大程度上是离子性的。
> 
> This is in contrast to BiMnO3, which has strong Bi-O covalency, but is analogous to LaMnO3, in which the La 5d-O 2p is largely ionic in character. 这与具有强Bi-O共价性的BiMnO3形成对比，但与LaMnO3类似，在LaMnO3中，La 5d-O 2p在很大程度上是离子性的。
> 
> Therefore, we suggest that the stabilization of different structural phases in LaMnO3 and YMnO3 is driven purely by the different sizes of the La and Y cations. 因此，我们认为，LaMnO3和YMnO3中不同结构相的稳定，纯粹是由La和Y阳离子的不同尺寸驱动的。
> 
> This is consistent with the previously held view that the formation of a cubic or hexagonal manganite is determined by the size of the large cation. 这与先前持有的观点一致，即形成立方或六方锰酸盐是由大阳离子的尺寸决定的。
> 
> The small size of the yttrium ion is then indirectly responsible for the ferroelectricity in YMnO3, which is the result of the unusual 5-fold coordination around the Mn ion in the hexagonal structure removing the center of symmetry and creating an electric dipole moment. 因此，钇离子的小尺寸间接导致了YMnO3中的铁电性，这是由于六方结构中Mn离子周围不寻常的五重配位，消除了对称中心并产生了电偶极矩。
> 
> # 6. Summary and Future Work
> 
> # 6. 总结与未来工作
> 
> In summary, we have presented an overview of the field of multiferroic magnetoelectric materials and a detailed study of two magnetically ordered ferroelectrics. 总之，我们概述了多铁性磁电材料领域，并详细研究了两种磁有序铁电体。
> 
> In the course of our discussion, a ferroelectric “d0” rule has emerged, that is, a ferroelectric displacement of the B cation in perovskite oxides is inhibited if the formal charge does not correspond to a d0 electron configuration. 在我们讨论的过程中，出现了一条铁电“d0”规则，即，如果形式电荷不对应于d0电子构型，钙钛矿氧化物中B阳离子的铁电位移就会被抑制。
> 
> We have seen that this d0 rule can be broken, providing the potential for simultaneous magnetic and ferroelectric ordering, if details of chemistry or structure create an asymmetric potential with a double potential well in spite of the d occupation of the magnetic cation. 我们已经看到，如果化学或结构的细节，在磁性阳离子d占据的情况下，仍然创造了一个具有双势阱的不对称势，那么这条d0规则可以被打破，从而为同时磁和铁电有序提供了可能性。
> 
> In the antiferromagnetic ferroelectric manganites of Y and the small rare earth elements, the double potential well is created by the 5-fold oxygen coordination. 在Y和小稀土元素的反铁磁铁电锰酸盐中，双势阱是由五重氧配位造成的。
> 
> In BiMnO3 (and also possibly in BiFeO3), the asymmetry is created by the Bi-O hybridization. 在BiMnO3（也可能在BiFeO3）中，不对称性是由Bi-O杂化造成的。
> 
> Also, in the classic example of nickel iodine boracite, Ni3B7O13I, the anisotropic iodine-oxygen octahedron is believed to cause an electrostatic double potential well along the I-Ni-I axis. 同样，在经典的碘镍方硼石Ni3B7O13I例子中，各向异性的碘-氧八面体被认为沿I-Ni-I轴产生了一个静电双势阱。
> 
> [Note, however, that experiments on materials including BaCr(O2F), BaFe(O2F), PbCr(O2F) and PbFe(O2F), in which asymmetry was sought by replacing part of the oxygen octahedron with fluorine, have not been successful in producing ferromagnetic ferroelectrics.59] [然而，请注意，对包括BaCr(O2F)、BaFe(O2F)、PbCr(O2F)和PbFe(O2F)在内的材料的实验，这些实验试图通过用氟替代部分氧八面体来寻求不对称性，但尚未成功产生铁磁铁电体。59]
> 
> The fundamental theoretical question of if (and why) d0-ness is so strongly favored remains to be answered rigorously and will undoubtedly contribute significantly to the future understanding of both ordinary ferroelectrics and of multiferroic materials. 关于d0态是否（以及为什么）如此强烈地被偏爱的基本理论问题，仍有待严格回答，并且无疑将极大地有助于未来对普通铁电体和多铁性材料的理解。
> 
> However, the existence of a Jahn-Teller distortion seems to be particularly unfavorable; therefore, we propose that non-Jahn-Teller ions are more likely candidates for magnetic ferroelectric ions. 然而，姜-泰勒畸变的存在似乎特别不利；因此，我们提出，非姜-泰勒离子更可能是磁性铁电离子的候选者。
> 
> There has been little modern experimental work on magnetic ferroelectrics, and the synthesis of single crystals of known or candidate multiferroic materials will be essential in elucidating the nature of the interactions involved. 关于磁性铁电体的现代实验工作很少，合成已知或候选多铁性材料的单晶，对于阐明所涉及相互作用的性质至关重要。
> 
> We hope that this paper will stimulate experimental work on some of the less well studied, but potentially interesting, candidate multiferroic materials. 我们希望本文能激发对一些研究较少但可能有趣的候选多铁性材料的实验工作。
> 
> In particular, this paper has focused exclusively on oxide ferroelectrics; however, non-oxide-based ferroelectrics should also be considered as candidates for multiferroism. 特别是，本文仅仅专注于氧化物铁电体；然而，非氧化物基铁电体也应被视为多铁性的候选者。
> 
> In ferroelectrics such as NaCaF3, the driving force for ferroelectricity is Coulomb interactions, rather than covalency. 在如NaCaF3等铁电体中，铁电性的驱动力是库仑相互作用，而不是共价性。
> 
> This might allow circumvention of the d0 requirement, provided that suitable stable structures can be found. 如果能够找到合适的稳定结构，这可能允许规避d0要求。

> # Appendix: Computational Techniques Used in this Work
> 
> # 附录：本工作中使用的计算技术
> 
> The calculations described in this work were performed using a plane-wave pseudopotential (PWPP) implementation60 of density functional theory (DFT)61 within the local spin density approximation (LSDA). 本工作中描述的计算，是在局域自旋密度近似下，使用密度泛函理论的平面波赝势实现进行的。60,61 **Local spin density approximation (LSDA)**: 局域自旋密度近似，是密度泛函理论中处理自旋极化系统交换关联能的一种近似方法，是局域密度近似的推广。
> 
> The accuracy and efficiency of ab initio pseudopotential calculations (compared with all-electron calculations) has long been established for nonmagnetic systems62 and has recently been verified for spin-polarized magnetic systems. 从头算赝势计算的精确性和效率（与全电子计算相比），早已在非磁系统中得到证实，62并且最近在自旋极化的磁系统中得到了验证。
> 
> [Traditionally, magnetic materials containing transition metals have been studied using all-electron methods with mixed basis sets, such as the linear-augmented plane wave (LAPW),63 linear muffin-tin orbital,64 or Korringa-Kohn-Rostoker65 approaches.] [传统上，包含过渡金属的磁性材料，使用混合基组的全电子方法进行研究，例如线性缀加平面波、63线性丸盒轨道、64或Korringa-Kohn-Rostoker方法。65]
> 
> ## A.1. Application of the Plane-Wave Pseudopotential Method to Magnetic Materials.
> 
> ## A.1. 平面波赝势方法在磁性材料中的应用。
> 
> Resistance to the use of the PWPP method to study magnetic systems was based on two perceived difficulties. 对使用PWPP方法研究磁系统的阻力，基于两个感知到的困难。
> 
> First, the magnetic properties of the transition metals and their compounds originate from tightly bound d-electrons, which were believed to require a prohibitively large number of plane waves to expand their pseudopotentials. 首先，过渡金属及其化合物的磁性质，源于紧密结合的d电子，据信这需要极其大量的平面波来展开其赝势。
> 
> In this work, we use the optimized pseudopotentials developed by Rappe et al.66 to reduce the energy cutoff of the plane-wave expansion to around 60 Ry. 在这项工作中，我们使用Rappe等人开发的优化赝势，66将平面波展开的能量截止降至约60 Ry。
> 
> Second, implicit within the pseudopotential approximation is the assumption that the exchange-correlation potential, Vxc, is separable into a valence part and a core part. 其次，赝势近似中隐含的假设是，交换关联势Vxc可以分离为价态部分和芯态部分。
> 
> Here, F is the electron density, σ is the spin polarization, and the superscript v refers to the contribution from the valence electrons. 这里，F是电子密度，σ是自旋极化，上标v指价电子的贡献。
> 
> 

<pre class="hljs"><code>Vxc(F,σ) = [Vxc(F,σ) - Vxc(Fv,σv)] + Vxc(Fv,σv)  (2)
</code></pre>


> 
> This separation is only rigorously correct if the core electrons are spatially separated from the valence electrons, which is not true for transition metal ions. 这种分离只有在芯电子与价电子在空间上分离时，才是严格正确的，这对于过渡金属离子是不成立的。
> 
> Here, we circumvent this problem using the partial nonlinear core correction scheme of Louie et al.,67 in which the core charge density is explicitly retained both in the construction of the ionic pseudopotential and in the DFT calculation. 在这里，我们使用Louie等人的部分非线性芯态修正方案来规避这个问题，67在该方案中，芯电荷密度在离子赝势的构建和DFT计算中都被明确保留。
> 
> The PWPP method with optimized pseudopotentials and partial nonlinear core corrections was first tested by Sasaki et al. for ferromagnetic nickel and iron.68 具有优化赝势和部分非线性芯态修正的PWPP方法，首先由Sasaki等人针对铁磁性的镍和铁进行了测试。68
> 
> The structural and magnetic properties calculated using the PWPP method were found to be in excellent agreement with results from all-electron calculations. 使用PWPP方法计算的结构和磁性质，发现与全电子计算的结果非常一致。
> 
> A similar study using different soft pseudopotentials obtained comparable results for the ground-state properties of nickel.69 一项使用不同软赝势的类似研究，对镍的基态性质获得了可比较的结果。69
> 
> More recently, Lewis and co-workers presented the first PWPP calculation for a magnetic compound (CrO2)70 and showed that the results agreed well with those of all-electron LSDA calculations. 最近，Lewis及其同事展示了第一个磁性化合物（CrO2）的PWPP计算，70并表明结果与全电子LSDA计算的结果非常吻合。
> 
> It has also been shown to give results in agreement with those of published all-electron calculations71 for magnetic perovskites related to those studied in this work.36 它也被证明，对于本工作中研究的相关的磁性钙钛矿，给出了与已发表的全电子计算结果一致的结果。71,36
> 
> ## A.2. Pseudopotential Construction.
> 
> ## A.2. 赝势构建。
> 
> For La, Bi, and Y, we constructed optimized scalar-relativistic pseudopotentials, using (for La) a 5s25p65d1 reference configuration with core radii rc = 2.6 au for all orbitals, and (for Y) a 4s24p64d1 reference configuration with rc(s) = rc(d) = 1.5 au and rc(p) = 1.6 au. 对于La、Bi和Y，我们构建了优化的标量相对论赝势，使用（对于La）5s25p65d1参考组态，所有轨道的芯半径rc = 2.6 au，以及（对于Y）4s24p64d1参考组态，rc(s) = rc(d) = 1.5 au，rc(p) = 1.6 au。
> 
> Neither La nor Y have 4f orbitals in their reference configuration, which facilitates the application of the pseudopotential method. La和Y在其参考组态中都没有4f轨道，这便于赝势方法的应用。
> 
> The pseudopotentials were optimized using four basis functions per orbital, with cutoff wavevectors, qc, of 7.5 au. 赝势使用每个轨道四个基函数进行优化，截止波矢qc为7.5 au。
> 
> qc determines the convergence of the kinetic energy with respect to the plane-wave cutoff energy in reciprocal-space calculations. qc决定了在倒空间计算中，动能相对于平面波截止能量的收敛性。
> 
> For Mn and O, we constructed nonrelativistic optimized pseudopotentials. 对于Mn和O，我们构建了非相对论性的优化赝势。
> 
> The oxygen pseudopotentials were generated from a 2s22p4 reference configuration with core radii of 1.5 au for both the s and the p orbitals. 氧赝势是从2s22p4参考组态产生的，s和p轨道的芯半径均为1.5 au。
> 
> They were then optimized using four and three basis functions with qc of 7.0 and 6.5 au for s and p orbitals, respectively. 然后，它们分别使用四个和三个基函数进行优化，s和p轨道的qc分别为7.0和6.5 au。
> 
> The oxygen pseudopotentials were used successfully in earlier calculations for nonmagnetic perovskite oxides.44 氧赝势在早期非磁钙钛矿氧化物的计算中得到了成功应用。44
> 
> For Mn, a 4s0.754p0.253d5 reference configuration was used, with rc equal to 2.0, 2.15, and 2.0 au for s, p, and d orbitals, respectively. 对于Mn，使用了4s0.754p0.253d5参考组态，s、p和d轨道的rc分别等于2.0、2.15和2.0 au。
> 
> The optimized pseudopotentials for Mn were constructed using four basis functions and qc = 7.5 au, and partial nonlinear core corrections67 were included. Mn的优化赝势使用四个基函数和qc = 7.5 au构建，并包含了部分非线性芯态修正。67
> 
> The core charge was approximated by a zeroth-order spherical Bessel function within a radius of 0.737 au, with the full core charge used outside this radius. 芯电荷在半径0.737 au内，用零阶球贝塞尔函数近似，在该半径外使用完整的芯电荷。
> 
> The pseudopotentials were tested for transferability by comparing with all-electron calculations for a range of typical atomic and ionic configurations. 通过比较一系列典型原子和离子组态的全电子计算，测试了赝势的可移植性。
> 
> The pseudo-eigenvalues and total energies were found to be equivalent to the all-electron values to within a few millielectronvolts. 发现赝本征值和总能量与全电子值，在几毫电子伏特范围内等价。
> 
> Plots of all pseudopotentials have been shown in earlier publications.36,72 所有赝势的图已在早期出版物中显示。36,72
> 
> All pseudopotentials were put into separable form73 using two projectors for each angular momentum.74 所有赝势都使用每个角动量两个投影子，转化为可分离形式。73,74
> 
> The first projector was taken as the atomic pseudo wave function, and the second projector as r2 times the first projector, suitably orthogonalized to the first.75 第一个投影子取为原子赝波函数，第二个投影子为r2乘以第一个投影子，并适当地正交化到第一个投影子。75
> 
> For La, Y, and Mn, the l = 0 component was chosen as the local potential. 对于La、Y和Mn，选择l = 0分量作为局域势。
> 
> For oxygen, the local potential was the l = 1 component. 对于氧，局域势是l = 1分量。
> 
> The absence of ghost states was confirmed using the ghost theorem of Gonze, Käckell, and Scheffler.76 使用Gonze、Käckell和Scheffler的幽灵定理，确认了不存在幽灵态。76
> 
> ## A.3. Technical Details.
> 
> ## A.3. 技术细节。
> 
> The total energy and band structure calculations were performed on Silicon Graphics Power Challenge L and O2 workstations using the conjugate gradient program CASTEP 2.1,77,78 which we have extended to study spin-polarized systems. 总能量和能带结构计算，是在Silicon Graphics Power Challenge L和O2工作站上，使用共轭梯度程序CASTEP 2.1进行的，77,78我们已将其扩展以研究自旋极化系统。
> 
> We used a plane-wave cut off of 60 ry, which corresponds to about 3500 plane waves in a cubic unit cell with a lattice constant of ∼4 Å. 我们使用了60 ry的平面波截止，这对应于晶格常数约为4 Å的立方晶胞中约3500个平面波。
> 
> A variable Gaussian broadening between 1 and 0.002 eV was applied to the k-point sampling to speed convergence for metallic systems. 对k点采样应用了1 eV到0.002 eV之间的可变高斯展宽，以加速金属体系的收敛。
> 
> A 6 × 6 × 6 Monkhorst-Pack79 grid was used for all calculations for cubic systems. 对所有立方体系的计算，使用了6 × 6 × 6的Monkhorst-Pack79网格。
> 
> This led to 10 k points in the irreducible Brillouin zone for the high-symmetry cubic structures and a correspondingly higher number for the distorted structures with lower symmetry. 这导致高对称立方结构在不可约布里渊区中有10个k点，而对称性较低的畸变结构则有相应更多的k点。
> 
> The exchange correlation was calculated using the Perdew-Zunger parametrization80 of the Ceperley-Alder potential81 with the von Barth-Hedin interpolation formula.82 交换关联的计算，使用了Ceperley-Alder势的Perdew-Zunger参数化形式，80,81并使用von Barth-Hedin插值公式。82
> 
> For density of states calculations, we interpolated the calculated eigenvalues to a grid of ∼350,000 k points in the irreducible simple cubic Brillouin zone using the interpolation scheme of Monkhorst and Pack.79 对于态密度计算，我们使用Monkhorst和Pack的插值方案，79将计算出的本征值插值到不可约简单立方布里渊区中约350,000个k点的网格上。
> 
> We then applied the Gilat-Raubenheimer method83 to integrate over this fine mesh. 然后，我们应用Gilat-Raubenheimer方法，83在这个精细网格上进行积分。
