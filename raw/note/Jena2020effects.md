> [!quote]- <center>Abstract</center>
> 
> We report the effects of the magnetic field on resistive switching behavior in the Ag/BiFeO3/FTO RRAM device through conventional I–V characteristics. The switching of the device from a high resistance state (HRS) to a low resistance state (LRS) at a certain threshold voltage (Vt) evidences a shift under the magnetic influence. The shifting of Vt toward higher voltage is due to the appearance of the Lorentz force and magnetoelectric effect when the magnetic field is applied perpendicular to the flow of current. The resistance of the HRS and LRS increases simultaneously under the application of the magnetic field due to the induced Hall resistance effect. The OFF/ON ratio∼12, which is repeatable for 100 multiple cycles. The discrete RESET state of the device is achieved by pulse-width and pulse-height modulation. Substantially, the resistance of these states increases systematically under the action of the magnetic field. The control of resistance states with the pulse-height and magnetic field in Ag/BiFeO3/FTO shows promise for future multilevel non-volatile memory technology.
>
>【摘要翻译】通过常规I–V特性，研究了磁场对Ag/BiFeO3/FTO RRAM器件电阻开关行为的影响。在一定的阈值电压（Vt）下，器件从高阻态（HRS）切换到低阻态（LRS），证明了器件在磁场影响下发生了位移。垂直于电流方向施加磁场时，Vt向高电压方向移动是由于洛伦兹力和磁电效应的出现。在磁场作用下，由于感应霍尔电阻效应，HRS和LRS的电阻同时增大。关闭/打开 比率∨12，可重复100次循环。通过脉宽和脉宽调制实现器件的离散复位状态。实质上，这些态的电阻在磁场的作用下系统地增加。Ag/BiFeO3/FTO中电阻态随脉冲高度和磁场的变化规律显示了其在多电平非易失性存储技术中的应用前景。
