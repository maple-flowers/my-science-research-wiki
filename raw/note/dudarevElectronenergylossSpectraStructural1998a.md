> ## 一、引言
> 
> 在材料科学和凝聚态物理领域，准确预测材料的电子结构与基态性质是计算材料学的核心目标。**密度泛函理论**（**D**ensity **F**unctional **T**heory, DFT）的**局域自旋密度近似**（**L**ocal **S**pin **D**ensity **A**pproximation, LSDA）在描述许多金属、半导体和绝缘体时取得了巨大成功。然而，对于含有部分填充的d或f壳层电子的体系，如**过渡金属氧化物**（Transition Metal Oxides），传统的DFT-LSDA方法常常失效。例如，它错误地预测氧化镍（NiO）等材料为金属态，或严重低估其绝缘能隙和晶格常数。
> 
> 本研究立足于这一长期存在的理论难题，聚焦于典型的**莫特绝缘体**（Mott Insulator）——氧化镍（NiO）。作者通过引入一种名为 **LSDA+U** 的改进计算方法，旨在更精确地处理镍离子（Ni²⁺）3d电子间的强库仑排斥作用。该研究的核心价值在于，它不仅通过计算复现了实验观测的**电子能量损失谱**（**E**lectron **E**nergy-**L**oss **S**pectroscopy, EELS），还同时成功预测了包括晶格常数、结合能和弹性模量在内的材料结构稳定性参数，从而首次在同一理论框架下，使用同一组参数（有效Hubbard U）实现了对NiO电子激发谱与基态性质的双重良好描述，证明了先进计算方法在解决复杂材料科学问题中的强大能力。
>

> 本文采用“问题提出 → 方法建立 → 实验/计算验证 → 综合分析”的经典科研论文结构，论证链条清晰。

> 1.  **问题明确**：引言部分明确指出DFT-LSDA在处理NiO等过渡金属氧化物时，因对3d电子强关联作用描述不足，导致在电子结构（金属/绝缘体、带隙）和结构性质（晶格常数、结合能）预测上均存在系统性偏差。
> 2.  **方法构建**：第二部分详细推导了LSDA+U方法的总能量泛函和单电子势。其核心思想是在LSDA泛函基础上，加入一个类似**无限制哈特里-福克**（**U**nrestricted **H**artree-**F**ock, UHF）方法的项，以轨道依赖的方式引入Hubbard U和Hund交换J参数，精确惩罚电子在同一离子上的非整数占据，从而修正自相互作用误差。

>     -   **结构验证**：采用LSDA和LSDA+U方法计算总能量随晶格常数的变化，从而得到平衡晶格常数、结合能和弹性模量，并与实验值和哈特里-福克计算值进行对比。
> 4.  **结论整合**：通过谱学（EELS）和结构（能量与弹性）两方面的证据，论证了LSDA+U方法能够用一个物理上合理的U值（6.2 eV），统一地描述NiO的激发态和基态性质，为后续研究该材料的表面和缺陷问题铺平了道路。

> ## 四、正文核心内容剖析
> 
> #### 1. 研究背景与问题
>

> > # III. EXPERIMENTAL EELS SPECTRA AND COMPARISON WITH LSDA1U RESULTS
> > 
> > III. 实验EELS谱及与LSDA+U结果的比较

</code></pre>


> > 
> > The specimens for the electron energy loss spectroscopy ~EELS! experiments were prepared by crushing the NiO crystals in pure ethanol and subsequently drying the dispersion onto a lacey carbon film deposited on a transmission electron microscope ~TEM! Cu grid. 用于电子能量损失谱（EELS）实验的样品是通过在纯乙醇中研磨NiO晶体，随后将分散液滴在沉积于透射电子显微镜（TEM）铜网上的多孔碳膜上干燥而制备的。 **电子能量损失谱 (EELS)**: 通过测量电子穿过样品时的能量损失来分析材料电子结构的技术。
> > 
> > 

<pre class="hljs"><code>
</code></pre>


> > 
> > The EELS measurements were carried out on a dedicated scanning transmission electron microscope equipped with a Gatan Imaging Filter ~UHV GIF model 678!. EELS测量在一台配备Gatan成像过滤器（UHV GIF 678型）的专用扫描透射电子显微镜上进行。
> > 
> > 

<pre class="hljs"><code>
</code></pre>


> > 
> > The system uses a cold field emission gun enabling an energy resolution of about 0.4–0.5 eV to be obtained ~as measured at the full width at half maximum of the zero loss peak!. 该系统使用冷场发射电子枪，可获得约0.4–0.5 eV的能量分辨率（按零损失峰的半高全宽测量）。
> > 
> > 

<pre class="hljs"><code>
</code></pre>


> > 
> > Spectra obtained from single grains of the powder were background subtracted and corrected for gain variation of the detector as described in Ref. 22. 所得粉末单颗粒谱图按文献22所述方法进行了背底扣除和探测器增益变化校正。
> > 
> > 

<pre class="hljs"><code>
</code></pre>


> > 
> > The near-edge structure of the O K spectrum obtained in such a way ~solid line in Fig. 1! represents the transitions from the occupied O 1s core levels to the conduction band states. 这样得到的O K边谱的近边结构（图1中实线）代表了从占据的O 1s芯能级到导带态的跃迁。
> > 
> > 

<pre class="hljs"><code>
</code></pre>


> > 
> > Due to dipole selection rules and the necessary overlap of the initial and final states for the transition to be observed ~i.e., nonzero transition matrix elements!, the final states probed are of p symmetry and at the oxygen sites. 由于偶极选择定则和观察跃迁所需的初态与末态交叠（即非零跃迁矩阵元），所探测的末态具有p对称性并位于氧格点上。
> > 
> > 

<pre class="hljs"><code>
</code></pre>


> > 
> > Numerical calculations were performed using the method described above and implemented on the basis of the full-potential LMTO program described in Ref. 16 and using the Moruzzi-Janak-Williams exchange-correlation potential. 数值计算使用上述方法，在文献16所述的全势LMTO程序基础上实现，并采用Moruzzi-Janak-Williams交换关联势。
> > 
> > 

<pre class="hljs"><code>
</code></pre>


> > 
> > The densities of one-particle electron states were calculated using three energy panels and 343 k points in the Brillouin zone. 单电子态密度使用三个能量面板和布里渊区中343个k点计算。 **布里渊区**: 晶体倒格子中的原胞，用于描述能带结构。
> > 
> > 

<pre class="hljs"><code>
</code></pre>


> > 
> > Since the structure of the experimental EELS spectrum shown in Fig. 1 is dominated by the dipole-allowed $filled 1s%→$empty 2p% transitions ~note that the presence of nonsphericity of the crystal field around oxygen sites in NiO does not alter this conclusion since23 the expansion of the nonspherical part of the electron density starts from l54), to simulate the experimental spectrum the oxygen 2p density of states ~DOS! was broadened with a Gaussian distribution to account for the lifetime of the excited state and the instrumental response of the system ~that is, the resolution and the energy spread of the primary electrons!. 由于图1所示实验EELS谱主要由偶极允许的{满1s}→{空2p}跃迁主导（注意，NiO中氧格点周围晶体场的非球对称性并不改变这一结论，因为23电子密度非球对称部分的展开始于l=4），为模拟实验谱，氧2p态密度（DOS）用高斯分布展宽，以考虑激发态寿命和系统的仪器响应（即分辨率和初级电子的能量展宽）。 **态密度 (DOS)**: 单位能量间隔内的电子态数量。
> > 
> > 

<pre class="hljs"><code>
</code></pre>


> > 
> > This method has already been successfully applied to simulations of L and K edges in semiconductors24 and oxygen K edges in early transition metal oxides.25 该方法已成功应用于半导体中L和K边24以及早期过渡金属氧化物中氧K边25的模拟。
> > 
> > 

<pre class="hljs"><code>
</code></pre>


> > 
> > Figure 1 also shows the DOS of empty oxygen 2p states calculated using a conventional LSDA ~i.e., assuming U ̄ 50). 图1还显示了使用传统LSDA（即假设Ū=0）计算的空氧2p态密度。
> > 
> > 

<pre class="hljs"><code>
</code></pre>


> > 
> > In agreement with the results of previous LSDA calculations,4 the ground state of nickel oxide in the LSDA is antiferromagnetic and insulating, where the 0.6 eV band gap separates two bands of nickel d states. 与先前LSDA计算的结果一致，4氧化镍的LSDA基态是反铁磁且绝缘的，其中0.6 eV的带隙分隔了两个镍d态带。
> > 
> > 

<pre class="hljs"><code>
</code></pre>


> > 
> > This LSDA result is known to be at odds with experimental data which exhibit a much larger band gap and different ordering of bands.5,13 已知这一LSDA结果与实验数据不符，后者显示出大得多的带隙和不同的带序。5,13
> > 
> > 

<pre class="hljs"><code>
</code></pre>


> > 
> > Figure 1 shows that the O 2p DOS spectrum calculated using the LSDA does not agree well with the experimental EELS spectrum. 图1表明，使用LSDA计算的O 2p DOS谱与实验EELS谱吻合不佳。
> > 
> > 

<pre class="hljs"><code>
</code></pre>


> > 
> > The separation between the two main peaks seen in the LSDA DOS curve is approximately 2 eV larger than the separation between the same peaks in the experimental spectrum, and the spectral weight of the low-energy peak in the DOS calculated using the LSDA is far too high. LSDA DOS曲线中两个主峰之间的间距比实验谱中相同峰间距大约2 eV，且LSDA计算DOS中低能峰的谱权重过高。
> > 
> > 

<pre class="hljs"><code>
</code></pre>

<pre class="hljs"><code>
</code></pre>

> > Figure 1 also shows oxygen 2p DOS calculated using the LSDA1U functional ~4! for U ̄ 56.2 eV and U ̄ 58.0 eV, and for J ̄ 50.95 eV. 图1还显示了使用LSDA+U泛函(4)在Ū=6.2 eV和Ū=8.0 eV，以及J̄=0.95 eV下计算的氧2p DOS。
> > 
> > 

<pre class="hljs"><code>
</code></pre>

> > Values of U ̄ 58.0 eV and ̄J 50.95 eV were suggested by Anisimov and Gunnarsson26 on the basis of a constrained LSDA calculation. Ū=8.0 eV和J̄=0.95 eV的值由Anisimov和Gunnarsson26根据约束LSDA计算提出。
> > 
> > 

<pre class="hljs"><code>
</code></pre>


> > 
> > Since the technique used in Ref. 26 did not take into account the self-screening of d electrons, it is likely that the actual value of U ̄ is slightly smaller than 8 eV. 由于文献26所用技术未考虑d电子的自屏蔽，实际Ū值很可能略小于8 eV。
> > 
> > 

<pre class="hljs"><code>
</code></pre>


> > 
> > By analyzing how the separation between the two main peaks in the spectrum of DOS shown in Fig. 1 depends on the value of ̄U, we concluded that the lower value of U ̄ 56.2 eV agrees better with experimental data. 通过分析图1所示DOS谱中两个主峰间距如何随Ū值变化，我们得出结论：较低的Ū=6.2 eV与实验数据更吻合。

> > 

<pre class="hljs"><code>
</code></pre>

<pre class="hljs"><code>

</code></pre>

> > The LSDA1U band gaps separating filled oxygen 2p and empty nickel 3d bands are, respectively, 3.0 eV and 3.2 eV. LSDA+U带隙（分隔满氧2p带和空镍3d带）分别为3.0 eV和3.2 eV。

<pre class="hljs"><code>

> > 
> > 

<pre class="hljs"><code>
</code></pre>


> > 
> > The distribution plotted in Fig. 2 shows that on-site Coulomb repulsion between Ni 3d electrons leads to a decrease of charge density in the interstitial regions, decreasing the strength of covalent bonding in the material. 图2所示分布表明，Ni 3d电子间的在位库仑排斥导致间隙区域电荷密度降低，减弱了材料中的共价键合强度。 **共价键合**: 原子间通过共享电子对形成的化学键。

> > 

<pre class="hljs"><code>
</code></pre>

> > 
> > 

<pre class="hljs"><code>

</code></pre>

> > 

<pre class="hljs"><code>
</code></pre>

> > 

<pre class="hljs"><code>
</code></pre>

> > # IV. CONCLUSIONS
> > 
> > IV. 结论
> > 
> > 

<pre class="hljs"><code>
</code></pre>


> > 
> > We have reported results of an application of the LSDA1U method to the calculation of the one-particle densities of states and the total energy of crystalline nickel oxide. 我们报道了应用LSDA+U方法计算晶态氧化镍的单粒子态密度和总能量的结果。
> > 
> > 

<pre class="hljs"><code>
</code></pre>


> > 
> > We have found that by using a combined DFT-LSDA and UHF description of one-electron states it is possible to achieve reasonable agreement between the calculated and the experimentally measured electron energy loss spectra, and at the same time to evaluate parameters characterizing the structural stability of the material. 我们发现，通过使用DFT-LSDA和UHF相结合的单电子态描述，可以在计算和实验测量的电子能量损失谱之间达到合理一致，同时评估表征材料结构稳定性的参数。
> > 
> > 

<pre class="hljs"><code>
</code></pre>


> > 
> > We believe that the reported findings open the way towards carrying out more accurate ab initio studies of surfaces and defects in transition metal oxides. 我们相信，所报道的结果为开展更精确的过渡金属氧化物表面和缺陷从头算研究开辟了道路。

# I. INTRODUCTION
I. 引言

In recent years substantial progress has been achieved in the development of accurate ab initio approaches to calculating the physical properties of various compounds, and in some cases it is now easier to predict the structure of a new material theoretically than to make it in a laboratory.1
近年来，在开发精确的从头算方法以计算各种化合物物理性质方面取得了实质性进展，并且在某些情况下，现在从理论上预测新材料的结构比在实验室中制备它更容易。1
**ab initio (从头算)**: 基于量子力学基本原理而不依赖经验参数的计算方法。

For many metals, semiconductors and insulators the local spin density approximation ~LSDA! to the density functional theory ~DFT! is known to provide a reliable variational description of the ground state of the electronic structure of the solid.2,3
对于许多金属、半导体和绝缘体，密度泛函理论（DFT）的局域自旋密度近似（LSDA）已知能提供固体电子结构基态的可靠变分描述。2,3
**密度泛函理论 (DFT)**: 一种研究多电子体系电子结构的量子力学方法，以电子密度作为基本变量。

At the same time there are cases where applications of the DFT-LSDA have been far less successful. In particular, difficulties arise when a conventional DFT-LSDA approach is applied to the treatment of the electronic structure of the material where some of the ions contain partly filled valence d or f shells.
同时，也存在DFT-LSDA应用远不那么成功的情况。特别是，当传统的DFT-LSDA方法应用于处理某些离子含有部分填充的价层d或f壳层的材料电子结构时，就会出现困难。

It was shown by Terakura et al.4 that for many of the transition metal oxides the DFT-LSDA predicts metallic ground states instead of experimentally observed insulating ones.
Terakura等人4指出，对于许多过渡金属氧化物，DFT-LSDA预测的是金属基态，而不是实验观察到的绝缘基态。
**过渡金属氧化物**: 由过渡金属元素与氧形成的化合物，如NiO、MnO等。

If antiferromagnetic order is taken into account, the DFT-LSDA treatment may lead to an insulating state but the forbidden gap still turns out to be an order of magnitude smaller than that observed using electron spectroscopy.5
如果考虑反铁磁有序，DFT-LSDA处理可能导致绝缘态，但禁带宽度仍然比电子能谱观测到的值小一个数量级。5
**反铁磁有序**: 相邻磁矩反向平行排列的磁有序结构。
**禁带 (forbidden gap)**: 固体中电子不能占据的能量区间，即带隙。

The origin of the failure of the DFT-LSDA in transition metal oxides is known to be associated with an inadequate description of the strong Coulomb repulsion between 3d electrons localized on metal ions.6
DFT-LSDA在过渡金属氧化物中失败的原因，已知与对局域在金属离子上的3d电子之间强库仑排斥的不充分描述有关。6

Uranium dioxide represents a similar example of a compound where uranium ions contain partly filled f shells and where all of the known LSDA solutions favor metallic conductivity.7
二氧化铀是一个类似的例子，其中铀离子含有部分填充的f壳层，所有已知的LSDA解都倾向于金属导电性。7

Experimentally UO2 is known to be a good insulator,8 and to explain the origin of the insulating ground state, it is necessary to go beyond the LSDA.9
实验上已知UO2是一种良好的绝缘体，8要解释绝缘基态的起源，有必要超越LSDA。9

In addition to making incorrect predictions regarding the nature of the ground state of transition metal and actinide oxides quoted above, the DFT-LSDA systematically underestimates their equilibrium lattice constants10 and overestimates binding energies, raising questions about the applicability of this approach to making predictions about equilibrium configurations of surface and defect structures.
除了对上述过渡金属和锕系氧化物基态性质做出错误预测外，DFT-LSDA还系统地低估了它们的平衡晶格常数10并高估了结合能，这就提出了该方法在预测表面和缺陷结构平衡构型方面的适用性问题。
**锕系氧化物**: 锕系元素（如U, Pu）与氧形成的化合物。
**结合能**: 将固体分解为组成原子所需能量，反映键合强度。

The rapid progress of experimental scanning tunneling microscope ~STM! techniques has already made possible the observation of atoms and individual defects on insulating transition metal oxide surfaces.11,12
实验扫描隧道显微镜（STM）技术的快速发展已经使得在绝缘过渡金属氧化物表面观察原子和单个缺陷成为可能。11,12
**扫描隧道显微镜 (STM)**: 利用量子隧道效应探测表面原子结构的仪器。

These new experimental data require the development of an approach that does not suffer from the inadequacies of the DFT-LSDA.
这些新的实验数据要求开发一种不因DFT-LSDA的不足而受影响的方法。

In order to describe surface structures and corresponding scanning tunneling images, it is necessary to be able to calculate total energies as well as certain spectroscopical quantities, for example, the spectrum of one-electron excitations of the material.
为了描述表面结构和相应的扫描隧道图像，需要能够计算总能量以及某些光谱学量，例如材料的一电子激发谱。

Below we address this question and, by using nickel oxide as an example, show how it can be answered within the framework of a recently proposed computational approach bridging the DFT-LSDA with the unrestricted Hartree-Fock ~UHF! approximation ~the LSDA1U method13–15!.
下面我们以氧化镍为例，讨论这一问题，并展示如何在一个最近提出的、将DFT-LSDA与非限制哈特里-福克（UHF）近似相结合的计算方法框架（LSDA+U方法13-15）内回答该问题。
**非限制哈特里-福克 (UHF)**: 一种允许自旋向上和向下电子占据不同空间轨道的哈特里-福克自洽场方法。

All the numerical results presented in this paper have been obtained using a full-potential linear muffin tin orbital ~LMTO!16 implementation of the LSDA1U approach.
本文所有数值结果均采用LSDA+U方法的全势线性缀加平面波轨道（LMTO）16实现获得。
**线性缀加平面波轨道 (LMTO)**: 一种基于密度泛函理论的能带计算方法，使用缀加平面波基组。

We have calculated the density of oxygen 2p states and compared the results with the near-edge structure of the oxygen K-loss spectrum of nickel oxide observed in a transmission electron microscope.
我们计算了氧2p态的密度，并将结果与透射电子显微镜中观察到的氧化镍氧K边损失谱的近边结构进行了比较。
**透射电子显微镜 (TEM)**: 使用电子束穿透样品成像的显微镜。
**K边**: 电子能谱中对应K壳层电子激发的吸收边。

We have also applied the LSDA1U method to evaluate the total energy of crystalline nickel oxide.
我们还应用LSDA+U方法评估了晶态氧化镍的总能量。

We have found that by using the same value of the effective Hubbard U characterizing on-site Coulomb repulsion between 3d electrons, it is possible to give a reasonbly good description of the measured electron energy loss spectra and values of the lattice constant, cohesive energy and elastic moduli.
我们发现，通过使用表征3d电子在格点间库仑排斥的同一有效哈伯德U值，可以合理地描述测量的电子能量损失谱以及晶格常数、内聚能和弹性模量。
**哈伯德U (Hubbard U)**: 描述同一格点上电子间库仑排斥能的有效参数。
**内聚能**: 见结合能，将固体分解为孤立原子所需能量。
**弹性模量**: 描述材料抵抗弹性形变能力的物理量。

# II. LSDA1U FUNCTIONAL AND THE TOTAL ENERGY
II. LSDA+U泛函与总能量
**泛函**: 函数的函数，此处指能量作为电子密度的泛函。

The main feature distinguishing transition metal oxides from more conventional oxides like MgO or TiO2 is that in the former case ions of metal contain partly filled d shells.
过渡金属氧化物区别于更传统氧化物（如MgO或TiO2）的主要特征是，在前者中金属离子含有部分填充的d壳层。

Since the strength of the effective on-site Coulomb interaction between d electrons ~characterized by the Hubbard U) is comparable with the valence bandwidth,6 the processes associated with electron transfer between two metal ions or resulting from addition or removal of d electrons give rise to large fluctuations of the energy of the system, leading to the localization of carriers and to the formation of band gaps.17
由于d电子之间的有效在位库仑相互作用强度（由哈伯德U表征）与价带宽度相当，6涉及两个金属离子之间电子转移或由添加或移除d电子引起的过程会导致系统能量的大幅涨落，从而引起载流子局域化和带隙形成。17
**载流子**: 电流的携带者，如电子或空穴。

> > The EELS measurements were carried out on a dedicated scanning transmission electron microscope equipped with a Gatan Imaging Filter ~UHV GIF model 678!. EELS测量在一台配备Gatan成像过滤器（UHV GIF 678型）的专用扫描透射电子显微镜上进行。
> > 
> > The system uses a cold field emission gun enabling an energy resolution of about 0.4–0.5 eV to be obtained ~as measured at the full width at half maximum of the zero loss peak!. 该系统使用冷场发射电子枪，可获得约0.4–0.5 eV的能量分辨率（按零损失峰的半高全宽测量）。
> > 
> > Spectra obtained from single grains of the powder were background subtracted and corrected for gain variation of the detector as described in Ref. 22. 所得粉末单颗粒谱图按文献22所述方法进行了背底扣除和探测器增益变化校正。
> > 
> > The near-edge structure of the O K spectrum obtained in such a way ~solid line in Fig. 1! represents the transitions from the occupied O 1s core levels to the conduction band states. 这样得到的O K边谱的近边结构（图1中实线）代表了从占据的O 1s芯能级到导带态的跃迁。
> > 
> > Due to dipole selection rules and the necessary overlap of the initial and final states for the transition to be observed ~i.e., nonzero transition matrix elements!, the final states probed are of p symmetry and at the oxygen sites. 由于偶极选择定则和观察跃迁所需的初态与末态交叠（即非零跃迁矩阵元），所探测的末态具有p对称性并位于氧格点上。
> > 
> > Numerical calculations were performed using the method described above and implemented on the basis of the full-potential LMTO program described in Ref. 16 and using the Moruzzi-Janak-Williams exchange-correlation potential. 数值计算使用上述方法，在文献16所述的全势LMTO程序基础上实现，并采用Moruzzi-Janak-Williams交换关联势。
> > 
> > The densities of one-particle electron states were calculated using three energy panels and 343 k points in the Brillouin zone. 单电子态密度使用三个能量面板和布里渊区中343个k点计算。 **布里渊区**: 晶体倒格子中的原胞，用于描述能带结构。
> > 
> > Since the structure of the experimental EELS spectrum shown in Fig. 1 is dominated by the dipole-allowed $filled 1s%→$empty 2p% transitions ~note that the presence of nonsphericity of the crystal field around oxygen sites in NiO does not alter this conclusion since23 the expansion of the nonspherical part of the electron density starts from l54), to simulate the experimental spectrum the oxygen 2p density of states ~DOS! was broadened with a Gaussian distribution to account for the lifetime of the excited state and the instrumental response of the system ~that is, the resolution and the energy spread of the primary electrons!. 由于图1所示实验EELS谱主要由偶极允许的{满1s}→{空2p}跃迁主导（注意，NiO中氧格点周围晶体场的非球对称性并不改变这一结论，因为23电子密度非球对称部分的展开始于l=4），为模拟实验谱，氧2p态密度（DOS）用高斯分布展宽，以考虑激发态寿命和系统的仪器响应（即分辨率和初级电子的能量展宽）。 **态密度 (DOS)**: 单位能量间隔内的电子态数量。
> > 
> > This method has already been successfully applied to simulations of L and K edges in semiconductors24 and oxygen K edges in early transition metal oxides.25 该方法已成功应用于半导体中L和K边24以及早期过渡金属氧化物中氧K边25的模拟。
> > 
> > Figure 1 also shows the DOS of empty oxygen 2p states calculated using a conventional LSDA ~i.e., assuming U ̄ 50). 图1还显示了使用传统LSDA（即假设Ū=0）计算的空氧2p态密度。
> > 
> > In agreement with the results of previous LSDA calculations,4 the ground state of nickel oxide in the LSDA is antiferromagnetic and insulating, where the 0.6 eV band gap separates two bands of nickel d states. 与先前LSDA计算的结果一致，4氧化镍的LSDA基态是反铁磁且绝缘的，其中0.6 eV的带隙分隔了两个镍d态带。
> > 
> > This LSDA result is known to be at odds with experimental data which exhibit a much larger band gap and different ordering of bands.5,13 已知这一LSDA结果与实验数据不符，后者显示出大得多的带隙和不同的带序。5,13
> > 
> > Figure 1 shows that the O 2p DOS spectrum calculated using the LSDA does not agree well with the experimental EELS spectrum. 图1表明，使用LSDA计算的O 2p DOS谱与实验EELS谱吻合不佳。
> > 
> > The separation between the two main peaks seen in the LSDA DOS curve is approximately 2 eV larger than the separation between the same peaks in the experimental spectrum, and the spectral weight of the low-energy peak in the DOS calculated using the LSDA is far too high. LSDA DOS曲线中两个主峰之间的间距比实验谱中相同峰间距大约2 eV，且LSDA计算DOS中低能峰的谱权重过高。
> > 
> > The equilibrium lattice constant aLSDA54.08 Å, although in better agreement with experiment than the LMTO atomic sphere approximation ~ASA! value of 3.98 Å,10 is still substantially lower than the experimentally observed one of 4.17 Å. 平衡晶格常数a_LSDA = 4.08 Å，虽然比LMTO原子球近似（ASA）值3.98 Å更符合实验，10但仍显著低于实验观测值4.17 Å。 **原子球近似 (ASA)**: 一种能带计算方法，将晶体空间划分为重叠的原子球区域。
> > 
> > Figure 1 also shows oxygen 2p DOS calculated using the LSDA1U functional ~4! for U ̄ 56.2 eV and U ̄ 58.0 eV, and for J ̄ 50.95 eV. 图1还显示了使用LSDA+U泛函(4)在Ū=6.2 eV和Ū=8.0 eV，以及J̄=0.95 eV下计算的氧2p DOS。
> > 
> > Values of U ̄ 58.0 eV and ̄J 50.95 eV were suggested by Anisimov and Gunnarsson26 on the basis of a constrained LSDA calculation. Ū=8.0 eV和J̄=0.95 eV的值由Anisimov和Gunnarsson26根据约束LSDA计算提出。
> > 
> > Since the technique used in Ref. 26 did not take into account the self-screening of d electrons, it is likely that the actual value of U ̄ is slightly smaller than 8 eV. 由于文献26所用技术未考虑d电子的自屏蔽，实际Ū值很可能略小于8 eV。
> > 
> > By analyzing how the separation between the two main peaks in the spectrum of DOS shown in Fig. 1 depends on the value of ̄U, we concluded that the lower value of U ̄ 56.2 eV agrees better with experimental data. 通过分析图1所示DOS谱中两个主峰间距如何随Ū值变化，我们得出结论：较低的Ū=6.2 eV与实验数据更吻合。
> > 
> > For comparison, we mention the empirical value of U ̄ 56.7 eV quoted in Ref. 13. 为作比较，我们提及文献13中引用的经验值Ū=6.7 eV。
> > 
> > The equilibrium lattice constants found using the above values of U ̄ are aLSDA1U (U56.2 eV) 54.19 Å and aLSDA1U (U58.0 eV)54.22 Å, and both of these values agree with experimental data better than the LSDA result. 使用上述Ū值得到的平衡晶格常数为a_LSDA+U (U=6.2 eV) = 4.19 Å和a_LSDA+U (U=8.0 eV) = 4.22 Å，这两个值都比LSDA结果更符合实验数据。
> > 
> > The LSDA1U band gaps separating filled oxygen 2p and empty nickel 3d bands are, respectively, 3.0 eV and 3.2 eV. LSDA+U带隙（分隔满氧2p带和空镍3d带）分别为3.0 eV和3.2 eV。
> > 
> > To explain the origin of the effect responsible for the dependence of the lattice constant on Hubbard ̄U, in Fig. 2 we plotted the difference between charge densities calculated using the LSDA1U and LSDA. 为解释晶格常数对哈伯德Ū依赖性的效应起源，我们在图2中绘制了LSDA+U与LSDA计算的电荷密度之差。
> > 
> > The distribution plotted in Fig. 2 shows that on-site Coulomb repulsion between Ni 3d electrons leads to a decrease of charge density in the interstitial regions, decreasing the strength of covalent bonding in the material. 图2所示分布表明，Ni 3d电子间的在位库仑排斥导致间隙区域电荷密度降低，减弱了材料中的共价键合强度。 **共价键合**: 原子间通过共享电子对形成的化学键。
> > 
> > This agrees with the findings of Ref. 9. 这与文献9的结果一致。
> > 
> > A similar reduction in the covalent contribution to bonding can be deduced from the calculated spectra of the oxygen K edge. 从计算得到的氧K边谱也可以推断出共价键合贡献的类似减小。
> > 
> > The major difference between the LSDA and LSDA1U oxygen 2p DOS is in the low-energy part of the spectrum where the peak is associated with the oxygen 2p –Ni 3d band. LSDA与LSDA+U氧2p DOS的主要区别在于谱的低能部分，那里峰与氧2p–Ni 3d带相关联。
> > 
> > In the LSDA1U solution this peak has a much lower intensity and it is shifted towards higher energies, leading to a weaker degree of hybridization between oxygen 2p and Ni 3d orbitals. 在LSDA+U解中，该峰的强度低得多，并向高能方向移动，导致氧2p与Ni 3d轨道之间的杂化程度减弱。 **杂化**: 原子轨道组合形成分子轨道的过程。
> > 
> > Other peaks on the EELS spectrum are associated with hybridization between oxygen 2p and nickel 4s and 4p states, the latter being known to be practically unaffected by Hubbard correlations in the 3d shell. EELS谱上的其他峰与氧2p和镍4s及4p态之间的杂化有关，后者已知几乎不受3d壳层中哈伯德关联的影响。
> >

> > # IV. CONCLUSIONS
> > 
> > IV. 结论
> > 
> > We have reported results of an application of the LSDA1U method to the calculation of the one-particle densities of states and the total energy of crystalline nickel oxide. 我们报道了应用LSDA+U方法计算晶态氧化镍的单粒子态密度和总能量的结果。
> > 
> > We have found that by using a combined DFT-LSDA and UHF description of one-electron states it is possible to achieve reasonable agreement between the calculated and the experimentally measured electron energy loss spectra, and at the same time to evaluate parameters characterizing the structural stability of the material. 我们发现，通过使用DFT-LSDA和UHF相结合的单电子态描述，可以在计算和实验测量的电子能量损失谱之间达到合理一致，同时评估表征材料结构稳定性的参数。
> > 
> > We believe that the reported findings open the way towards carrying out more accurate ab initio studies of surfaces and defects in transition metal oxides. 我们相信，所报道的结果为开展更精确的过渡金属氧化物表面和缺陷从头算研究开辟了道路。
