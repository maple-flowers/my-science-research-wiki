> ## 一、引言
> 
> 在光学领域，**无衍射光束**（Nondiffracting Beams）因其长距离传播不变形、障碍物后自修复等奇异特性，一直是研究热点。作为无衍射光束家族的重要成员，**马蒂厄光束**（Mathieu Beams）因其独特的椭圆晶格状横向光强分布，在光学捕获、光子晶格、激光加工等领域展现出巨大潜力。
> 
> 然而，如何高效、高质量地生成马蒂厄光束，尤其是实现器件的微型化和集成化，是该领域面临的关键挑战。传统方法，如全息图法或空间光调制器（SLM），要么受限于制造精度，要么因像素尺寸和器件体积过大而难以应用于集成光学系统。新兴的超表面方法虽有所改进，但本质上仍是二维像素化工艺，存在局限性。
> 
> 在此背景下，本篇解读的论文《Ultracompact phase plate fabricated by femtosecond laser two-photon polymerization for generation of Mathieu–Gauss beams》提出了一种创新的解决方案。该研究利用**飞秒激光双光子聚合**（TPP）技术，成功制造了具有不同参数的超紧凑型**马蒂厄相位板**（MPP），并实验验证了其生成高质量**马蒂厄-高斯光束**（Mathieu–Gauss beams）的能力。这项工作不仅证明了TPP技术在三维纳米光学器件制造上的巨大优势，也为无衍射光束在微纳光学系统中的集成化、小型化应用铺平了道路。
> 
> ## 二、论文结构与逻辑概览
> 
> 本论文遵循“**设计-制造-表征-结论**”的经典工程研究逻辑，结构清晰，论证链条完整。
> 
> 1.  **问题提出**：首先，论文指出生成高质量马蒂厄光束的现有方法（SLM，全息图）在体积、精度和光束质量上存在不足，确立了需要更优解决方案的动机。
> 2.  **方案设计与理论**：接着，论文阐述了利用4-f系统将高斯光束转化为马蒂厄-高斯光束的理论模型和设计原理，并给出了目标相位板的设计。
> 3.  **制造与误差分析**：论文核心部分介绍了利用飞秒激光TPP平台制造相位板的工艺流程，并对制造过程中产生的两种主要误差（整体高度误差和层间离散误差）进行了量化分析，证明了其工艺对误差的容忍度。
> 4.  **实验验证**：通过搭建光学表征系统，对制造出的相位板进行性能测试，生成了不同阶数和参数的偶数和螺旋马蒂厄-高斯光束，并测量了其长距离传播不变性（>800 mm），与仿真结果吻合。
> 5.  **结论与展望**：最后，总结了TPP技术在制造超紧凑、高精度微小光学器件上的优势，并展望了其在光通信、粒子操控等领域的应用前景。

> ## 四、正文核心内容剖析
> 
> ### 4.1 研究背景与问题
> 
> 论文开篇即点明，理想的马蒂厄光束因携带无限能量而无法在实验中实现，实际可生成的是能量有限的**马蒂厄-高斯光束**。现有生成方法如**空间光调制器**（SLM，一种通过电压控制液晶分子排列来改变光波特性的设备）虽然灵活，但其像素尺寸（微米级）和像素间隔限制了光束质量，且器件体积大，不利于集成。
> 
> ### 4.2 方法模型：从相位到光束
> 
> 论文提出的方法核心是**相位调制**，其原理如下：
> 
> 1.  **设计**：根据公式（2）和（4），计算出生成特定马蒂厄-高斯光束所需的相位分布，并将其转化为三维高度分布。这就像一个为光束量身定制的“地形图”。
> 2.  **转换**：利用**飞秒激光双光子聚合**（TPP，一种利用超快激光脉冲在光敏材料中引发非线性的双光子吸收，从而实现纳米级精度三维加工的技术）将设计的高度图转化为实体的聚合物相位板。
> 3.  **重构**：将制造的相位板置于图5所示的4-f系统。一束高斯光束照射相位板，其波前被调制，经过傅里叶透镜和空间滤波后，在系统的输出端重构出目标马蒂厄-高斯光束。
> 
> ### 4.3 实验结果与讨论
> 
> -   **制造结果**：成功制造了尺寸仅为300 μm × 300 μm的超紧凑相位板，表面高度误差被控制在~0.2 μm。
> -   **误差分析**：论文对误差的讨论是其亮点之一。它指出TPP逐层扫描会带来**相位离散化**（即连续的曲面变成阶梯状），而材料折射率与设计值的偏差会导致**整体相位调制深度**不准。但通过图4的模拟分析，证明即使在31级离散化和一定调制深度误差下，生成光束的**重叠积分**（一种衡量生成光束与理想光束相似度的指标，越接近100%越好）仍高于99.5%，表明该工艺具有很高的鲁棒性。
> -   **光束质量**：实验成功生成了多种参数的偶数和螺旋马蒂厄-高斯光束（图7、图9），其光强分布与理论模拟高度一致。
> -   **无衍射性能**：实验测量了光束在超过800 mm（约等于从您的办公桌到对面墙壁的距离）的传播距离内，光强分布几乎不变（图8、图10），强有力地证明了其无衍射特性。实测偶光束（m=2, q=12）的能量效率约为40%，略低于仿真值54.5%，主要归因于部分入射光未能照射到相位板上。
> 
> ## 五、结论与贡献
> 
> 论文成功论证了利用飞秒激光TPP技术制造超紧凑马蒂厄相位板，并以此生成高质量马蒂厄-高斯光束的可行性。
> 
> **核心贡献**：

> ## 四、正文核心内容剖析
> 
> ### 1. 研究背景与问题（引言）
> 
> -   **核心概念**：**无衍射光束（Nondiffracting Beam）** 是一种在传播过程中克服了自然衍射的光束，其横向光强分布保持不变。**马蒂厄光束（Mathieu Beam）** 是亥姆霍兹方程在椭圆坐标系下的解，其光场呈椭圆形网格分布。
> -   **研究痛点**：现有的马蒂厄光束生成方法各有利弊。全息法质量差，基于**空间光调制器（SLM）** 的方法受限于其像素尺寸（微米级）和间距，无法产生高质量光束，且体积庞大，不利于集成。
> -   **本文切入点**：利用**飞秒激光双光子聚合（TPP）** 技术，其加工精度可达 **100 nm**，是制造高精度、超紧凑（300 μm × 300 μm）相位板的理想工具。
> 
> ### 2. 方法与模型（设计与制造）
> 
> -   **理论模型**：理想马蒂厄光束能量无限，无法物理实现。因此，本文研究对象是**马蒂厄-高斯（MG）光束**，它携带有限能量，且在有限区域内保持无衍射特性。其生成方法是为高斯光束加载一个马蒂厄相位，再通过**4-f系统**进行空间滤波。
> -   **制造核心**：利用TPP技术，将相位分布( \phi(x, y) )转化为光刻胶的高度分布( h(x, y) )，公式为 ( h(x, y) = \frac{\lambda \phi(x, y)}{2\pi (n_p - 1)} )。对于1550 nm的设计波长和折射率1.5的光刻胶，π相位差对应的高度为 **1.55 μm**。
> -   **误差分析是亮点**：文章没有回避制造误差，而是定量分析了两种主要误差——**整体高度误差**和**相位离散化误差**——对光束**重叠积分**（质量）和**效率**的影响。结论是，即使在31级离散化水平下，TFabricated器件对误差仍表现出很强的鲁棒性。
> 
> ### 3. 实验与结果（表征与讨论）
> 
> -   **实验验证**：成功制造了不同阶数（m）和椭圆率参数（q）的偶数和螺旋型MPP，并利用4-f系统生成了对应的MG光束。CCD捕捉到的光强分布与理论仿真高度一致。
> -   **核心发现**：
>     -   **高光束质量**：产生的光束图案清晰，与理论吻合度高。
>     -   **优异的无衍射特性**：光束在**100 mm至800 mm**的传播距离内保持了横向光强分布的不变性，充分证明了器件性能。
>     -   **紧凑性**：相位板尺寸为300 μm，远小于传统SLM（厘米级），展现了巨大的小型化潜力。
> 
> ## 五、结论与贡献
> 
> -   **核心结论**：本文成功利用飞秒激光双光子聚合技术，制造出尺寸仅为300 μm × 300 μm的超紧凑、高精度马蒂厄相位板，并实验产生了高质量、长距离无衍射的偶数和螺旋型马蒂厄-高斯光束。
> -   **理论贡献**：建立了一套完整的从“设计-制造-误差分析-实验验证”的链路，定量分析了TPP制造误差对非衍射光束质量的影响，为工艺优化提供了理论指导。
> -   **实际贡献**：
>     1.  **提供了一种新方法**：证明了TPP技术是制造高性能、可集成的微纳光学器件的有效工具，能够替代传统SLM等方案。
>     2.  **实现了器件微型化**：将马蒂厄光束的产生器件尺寸从厘米级缩小到亚毫米级，极大推动了其在集成光学系统中的应用。
>     3.  **验证了高性能**：制造出的器件产生了高质量、高效率（约40%）且无衍射特性出色的光束，为光学通信、粒子操控、材料加工等领域的微型化应用铺平了道路。

> ## 八、用户问题预测与解答
> 
> **1. 什么是“无衍射光束”？它真的完全无衍射吗？** **答**：无衍射光束是指其横向光强分布在传播过程中保持不变的波场。理想的无衍射光束（如理想贝塞尔光束、理想马蒂厄光束）需要无限大的能量，在物理上无法实现。实验中产生的是**有限能量**的近似无衍射光束，如本文的**马蒂厄-高斯（MG）光束**。它们并非完全无衍射，而是在一个有限的传播距离内（本文中超过800毫米），其中心光斑的尺寸和形状基本保持不变，呈现出“无衍射”特性。这个距离远大于同等尺寸高斯光束的瑞利长度，因此具有重要的应用价值。
> 
> **2. 飞秒激光双光子聚合（TPP）的基本原理是什么？它为什么能制造3D结构？** **答**：双光子聚合是一种非线性光学过程。普通光聚合（如UV固化）需要材料吸收一个高能光子（紫外光）来引发反应。而TPP利用材料同时吸收两个低能光子（近红外光，如飞秒激光），其总能量等同于一个高能光子。这个双光子吸收的概率与光强的平方成正比，因此只有在飞秒激光的焦点处，光强极高，才能发生显著反应。通过3D位移台精确控制激光焦点在光刻胶内的移动路径，就能实现任意三维结构的“直写”制造，精度可达100纳米级别。这就像用一支极细的画笔，在三维空间里“画”出设计的结构。
>

> > 段落：The concept of nondiffracting beams was first brought up by Durnin in 1987.1 He presented exact solutions to the homogeneous Helmholtz equation that were not subject to diffraction. Since then, light with propagation invariance has been a focus of attention for researchers. The Mathieu beam is the solution of the Helmholtz equation based on its separability in elliptical cylindrical coordinates.2 Like other nondiffracting beams, the Mathieu beam is featured by its nondiffraction,3 self-reconstruction,4 and self-acceleration.5 Different from its relatives, for example, the Bessel beam, cosine beam, and parabolic beam,6 the transverse intensity of Mathieu beams are lattice-like, which is proper for scientific use. Because of the properties above, scientists have used the Mathieu beam in many fields, including photonic lattices,7 optical trapping,8 speckle phenomena,9 and laser processing.10,11 For further applications of Mathieu beams, the generation of Mathieu beams with high quality is necessary. Several efforts have been made to produce Mathieu beams in the experiment. The first experimental demonstration of the Mathieu beam was reported by Gutiérrez-Vega et al.12 After that, many methods emerged to generate such beams. The hologram method can produce high-order Mathieu beams, but the generated beams are of poor quality due to manufacturing errors.13 In addition, spatial light modulators (SLMs) are widely used to generate Mathieu beams.3,4,10,14 However, the inherent nature of the relatively large pixel size and spacing makes it hard to produce high-quality Mathieu beams. In addition, the relatively large size of SLM makes it hard to apply the Mathieu beam in an integrated optical system. Recently, more compact devices based on metasurfaces15,16 have emerged. Ring-shaped plasmonic metasurfaces have been proposed to yield Mathieu beams with high quality.17 Generally speaking, the metasurface is a method of pixelization. The size of the pixel is about 1 μm. Also, it is based on processes, such as the focused ion beam and electron beam lithography, which are suitable for 2D patterning. Femtosecond laser two-photon polymerization (TPP) has emerged as a new micronanoprocessing technology, which is the way to produce sophisticated three-dimensional (3D) structures with an accuracy of up to 100 nm.18–23 Additionally, it is an efficient tool for transforming the design into the final optical element. In this work, using femtosecond laser TPP, we propose and demonstrate the fabrication of even and helical Mathieu phase plates (MPPs) with different orders m and ellipticity parameters q in a miniature size (300 μm × 300 μm). Compared with traditional methods, the MPPs fabricated here can realize an ultracompact size while maintaining high accuracy that brings about high beam quality. Our study may provide new possibilities for miniature applications of nondiffracting beams in optical communications, particle manipulation, and material processing.

> > In summary, we have proposed, designed, and fabricated even and helical MPPs. The femtosecond laser TPP process has been utilized to fabricate MPPs with high accuracy, proving its ability in nanofabrication with rapidity and flexibility. The MG beams with high quality have been experimentally demonstrated using the MPPs. Also, their diffraction-free characteristics have been verified. Our method can achieve even more compact MPPs with micrometer size, which may facilitate future miniature applications of nondiffracting beams in optical communications, optical trapping, laser processing, and more.

> # 1 Introduction
> 
> # 1 引言
> 
> The concept of nondiffracting beams was first brought up by Durnin in 1987.1 He presented exact solutions to the homogeneous Helmholtz equation that were not subject to diffraction. Since then, light with propagation invariance has been a focus of attention for researchers. The Mathieu beam is the solution of the Helmholtz equation based on its separability in elliptical cylindrical coordinates.2 Like other nondiffracting beams, the Mathieu beam is featured by its nondiffraction,3 self-reconstruction,4 and self-acceleration.5 Different from its relatives, for example, the Bessel beam, cosine beam, and parabolic beam,6 the transverse intensity of Mathieu beams are lattice-like, which is proper for scientific use. Because of the properties above, scientists have used the Mathieu beam in many fields, including photonic lattices,7 optical trapping,8 speckle phenomena,9 and laser processing.10,11 For further applications of Mathieu beams, the generation of Mathieu beams with high quality is necessary. Several efforts have been made to produce Mathieu beams in the experiment. The first experimental demonstration of the Mathieu beam was reported by Gutiérrez-Vega et al.12 After that, many methods emerged to generate such beams. The hologram method can produce high-order Mathieu beams, but the generated beams are of poor quality due to manufacturing errors.13 In addition, spatial light modulators (SLMs) are widely used to generate Mathieu beams.3,4,10,14 However, the inherent nature of the relatively large pixel size and spacing makes it hard to produce high-quality Mathieu beams. In addition, the relatively large size of SLM makes it hard to apply the Mathieu beam in an integrated optical system. Recently, more compact devices based on metasurfaces15,16 have emerged. Ring-shaped plasmonic metasurfaces have been proposed to yield Mathieu beams with high quality.17 Generally speaking, the metasurface is a method of pixelization. The size of the pixel is about 1 μm. Also, it is based on processes, such as the focused ion beam and electron beam lithography, which are suitable for 2D patterning. Femtosecond laser two-photon polymerization (TPP) has emerged as a new micronanoprocessing technology, which is the way to produce sophisticated three-dimensional (3D) structures with an accuracy of up to 100 nm.18–23 Additionally, it is an efficient tool for transforming the design into the final optical element. In this work, using femtosecond laser TPP, we propose and demonstrate the fabrication of even and helical Mathieu phase plates (MPPs) with different orders m and ellipticity parameters q in a miniature size (300 μm × 300 μm). Compared with traditional methods, the MPPs fabricated here can realize an ultracompact size while maintaining high accuracy that brings about high beam quality. Our study may provide new possibilities for miniature applications of nondiffracting beams in optical communications, particle manipulation, and material processing. 无衍射光束的概念最早由Durnin于1987年提出。1他给出了不受衍射影响的齐次亥姆霍兹方程的精确解。自此，具有传播不变性的光一直是研究者的关注焦点。马蒂厄光束是基于亥姆霍兹方程在椭圆柱坐标系中可分离性的解。2与其他无衍射光束一样，马蒂厄光束具有无衍射3、自重建4和自加速5等特性。与贝塞尔光束、余弦光束和抛物线光束等同类光束不同，6马蒂厄光束的横向强度呈晶格状，适合科学应用。由于上述特性，科学家已将马蒂厄光束应用于光子晶格7、光捕获8、散斑现象9和激光加工10,11等领域。为了进一步应用马蒂厄光束，高质量的产生是必要的。实验上已有多项努力来产生马蒂厄光束。Gutiérrez-Vega等人首次实验演示了马蒂厄光束。12此后，涌现出多种产生此类光束的方法。全息法可以产生高阶马蒂厄光束，但由于制造误差，产生的光束质量较差。13此外，空间光调制器（SLM）被广泛用于产生马蒂厄光束。3,4,10,14然而，相对较大的像素尺寸和间距的本质特性使得难以产生高质量的马蒂厄光束。此外，SLM相对较大的尺寸也使其难以应用于集成光学系统。最近，基于超表面15,16的更紧凑器件已经出现。环形等离子体超表面已被提出用于产生高质量的马蒂厄光束。17总体来说，超表面是一种像素化方法，像素尺寸约为1 μm，并且基于聚焦离子束和电子束光刻等工艺，适用于二维图案化。飞秒激光双光子聚合（TPP）作为一种新型微纳加工技术，能够以高达100 nm的精度制造复杂的三维（3D）结构。18–23此外，它是一种将设计高效转化为最终光学元件的工具。在本工作中，我们利用飞秒激光TPP，提出并展示了在微型尺寸（300 μm × 300 μm）下制备不同阶数m和椭圆参数q的偶数和螺旋马蒂厄相位板（MPP）。与传统方法相比，这里制备的MPP可实现超紧凑尺寸，同时保持高精度，从而带来高光束质量。我们的研究可能为无衍射光束在光通信、粒子操控和材料加工中的微型化应用提供新的可能性。 **Bessel beam**: 贝塞尔光束，一种无衍射光束，具有贝塞尔函数形式的横向分布。 **spatial light modulators (SLMs)**: 空间光调制器，一种可对光波进行空间调制的器件。 **metasurfaces**: 超表面，由亚波长结构组成的二维平面，可实现对光波的灵活调控。 **focused ion beam**: 聚焦离子束，一种微纳加工技术。 **electron beam lithography**: 电子束光刻。 **two-photon polymerization (TPP)**: 双光子聚合，利用双光子吸收引发光聚合反应，实现三维加工。 **Mathieu phase plates (MPPs)**: 马蒂厄相位板，用于产生马蒂厄光束的相位调制元件。

> The ideal Mathieu beams are described in cylindrical elliptical coordinates $(\xi, \eta, z)$. The cylindrical elliptical coordinates can be transformed into Cartesian coordinates as 理想马蒂厄光束在椭圆柱坐标系 $(\xi, \eta, z)$ 中描述。椭圆柱坐标可以转换为直角坐标如下：
> 
> $$ \begin{cases} x = c \cosh(\xi) \cos(\eta) \ y = c \sinh(\xi) \sin(\eta) \ c^2 = a^2 - b^2; \end{cases} \qquad \xi \ge 0,\ 0 \le \eta < 2\pi \tag{1} $$ 其中 $a$ 和 $b$ 分别是椭圆的长半轴和短半轴，$c$ 是焦距。
> 
> where $a$ and $b$ are the semimajor and semiminor axes of the ellipse, and $c$ is the focus distance.24 Thus, the ideal scalar Mathieu beam of even, odd, and helical parity can be expressed, respectively, as 其中 $a$ 和 $b$ 分别是椭圆的长半轴和短半轴，$c$ 是焦距。24 因此，偶数、奇数和螺旋宇称的理想标量马蒂厄光束可分别表示为：
> 
> $$ \begin{cases} M_e(\xi, \eta, z; q) = Ce_m(\xi; q) ce_m(\eta; q) \exp(ik_z z) \ M_o(\xi, \eta, z; q) = Se_m(\xi; q) se_m(\eta; q) \exp(ik_z z) \ M_h(\xi, \eta, z; q) = [A_m(q) Ce_m(\xi; q) ce_m(\eta; q) + i B_m(q) Se_m(\xi; q) se_m(\eta; q)] \exp(ik_z z); \end{cases} \tag{2} $$ 其中 $Ce_m$ 和 $ce_m$ 分别是 $m$ 阶偶数宇称的径向（修正）和角向马蒂厄函数，$Se_m$ 和 $se_m$ 分别是 $m$ 阶奇数宇称的径向和角向马蒂厄函数。

> where $Ce_m$ and $ce_m$ are the radial (modified) and angular Mathieu functions of even parity for the $m$th order, and $Se_m$ and $se_m$ are the radial (modified) and angular Mathieu functions of odd parity for the $m$th order, respectively. The parameter $q = c^2 k_t^2 / 4$ is related to the focus distance $c$ and transverse wave vector $k_t$. $k_z$ is the longitudinal wave vector, which has a relation to the wave vector $k_0 = \omega/\nu = \sqrt{k_t^2 + k_z^2}$, where $\omega$ is the frequency of the wave and $\nu$ is its phase velocity. $A_m(q)$ and $B_m(q)$ are normalized coefficients related to $q$, which can be omitted for $m \ge 2$ and $q \le m^2/2 - 1$.25 Unlike ideal Mathieu beams, which cannot be realized experimentally due to their infinite energy, the Mathieu–Gauss (MG) beams can be generated in the lab, carrying finite energy and being diffraction-free in the finite region. > # 4 Conclusion
> 
> # 4 结论
> 
> In summary, we have proposed, designed, and fabricated even and helical MPPs. The femtosecond laser TPP process has been utilized to fabricate MPPs with high accuracy, proving its ability in nanofabrication with rapidity and flexibility. The MG beams with high quality have been experimentally demonstrated using the MPPs. Also, their diffraction-free characteristics have been verified. Our method can achieve even more compact MPPs with micrometer size, which may facilitate future miniature applications of nondiffracting beams in optical communications, optical trapping, laser processing, and more. 总之，我们提出、设计并制备了偶数和螺旋 MPP。利用飞秒激光 TPP 工艺高精度地制备了 MPP，证明了其快速、灵活性的纳米制造能力。使用 MPP 实验验证了高质量 MG 光束，并验证了其无衍射特性。我们的方法可以实现微米尺寸的更紧凑 MPP，这可能促进未来无衍射光束在光通信、光捕获、激光加工等领域的微型化应用。
