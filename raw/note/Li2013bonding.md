> > # Bonding Charge Density and Ultimate Strength of Monolayer Transition Metal Dichalcogenides
> > 单层过渡金属二硫族化合物的键合电荷密度与极限强度
> > **Transition Metal Dichalcogenides (TMDs)**: 过渡金属二硫族化合物，由过渡金属原子与硫族原子组成的层状二维材料。
> > 
> > Junwen Li,1 Nikhil V. Medhekar,2 and Vivek B. Shenoy1, ∗
> > 1Department of Materials Science and Engineering, University of Pennsylvania, Philadelphia, PA 19104, USA 2Department of Materials Engineering, Monash University, Clayton, Victoria 3800, Australia (Dated: November 27, 2024)
> > 李俊文1，尼基尔·V·梅德赫卡2，维韦克·B·谢诺伊1,*
> > 1宾夕法尼亚大学材料科学与工程系，美国宾夕法尼亚州费城19104 2莫纳什大学材料工程系，澳大利亚维多利亚州克莱顿3800 (日期：2024年11月27日)
> > 
> > Two-dimensional (2D) semiconducting transition metal dichalcogenides (TMDs) can withstand a large deformation without fracture or inelastic relaxation, making them attractive for application in novel strain-engineered and flexible electronic and optoelectronic devices. In this study, we characterize the mechanical response of monolayer group VI TMDs to large elastic deformation using first-principles density functional theory calculations. We find that the ultimate strength and the overall stress response of these 2D materials is strongly influenced by their chemical composition and loading direction. We demonstrate that differences in the observed mechanical behavior can be attributed to the spatial redistribution of the occupied hybridized electronic states in the region between the transition metal atom and the chalcogens. In spite of the strong covalent bonding between the transition metal and the chalcogens, we find that a simple linear relationship can be established to describe the dependence of the mechanical strength on the charge transfer from the transition metal atom to the chalcogens.
> > 二维半导体过渡金属二硫族化合物（TMDs）能够承受大变形而不发生断裂或非弹性弛豫，这使其在新型应变工程和柔性电子与光电器件应用中极具吸引力。在本研究中，我们通过第一性原理密度泛函理论计算，表征了单层VI族TMDs在大弹性变形下的力学响应。我们发现，这些二维材料的极限强度和整体应力响应受其化学组成和加载方向的强烈影响。我们证明，观察到的力学行为差异可归因于过渡金属原子与硫族原子之间区域中占据的杂化电子态的空间重新分布。尽管过渡金属与硫族元素之间存在强共价键合，我们发现仍可建立一个简单的线性关系来描述力学强度对过渡金属原子向硫族原子电荷转移的依赖关系。
> > **Density Functional Theory (DFT)**: 密度泛函理论，一种基于电子密度研究多电子体系电子结构的量子力学方法。
> > **Ultimate Strength**: 极限强度，材料在无缺陷情况下所能承受的最大应力。
> > **Hybridized Electronic States**: 杂化电子态，不同原子轨道组合形成的新的等价轨道状态。

> > I. INTRODUCTION
> > 第一章 引言
> > 
> > Since its first mechanical exfoliation from graphite, graphene — an allotrope of carbon with a twodimensional (2D) structure — has attracted tremendous scientific and technological attention due to novel electronic, mechanical, and chemical properties.1,2 The intense interest in graphene has also stimulated an active search for possible inorganic 2D or quasi-2D materials with unique characteristics. Examples of such materials include hexagonal boron nitride, transition metal dichalcogenides (TMDs), and transition metal oxides.3–5 Earlier attempts to obtain monolayer and a few-layer thick samples of these materials were based on mechanical exfoliation from their bulk counterparts. Recent advances in fabrication techniques, such as liquid exfoliation, lithium intercalation, epitaxy, laser thinning, and chemical vapor deposition, have successfully resulted in a scalable production of large 2D samples containing only a single layer to a few layers.6–11
> > 自从首次从石墨中机械剥离出石墨烯——一种具有二维结构的碳的同素异形体——以来，它便因其新颖的电子、力学和化学性质而引起了巨大的科学和技术关注1,2。对石墨烯的浓厚兴趣也激发了人们积极寻找具有独特特性的可能无机二维或准二维材料。这类材料的例子包括六方氮化硼、过渡金属二硫族化合物（TMDs）和过渡金属氧化物3–5。早期获取这些材料单层和少层厚样品的方法是基于从其块体对应物上的机械剥离。制备技术的最新进展，如液相剥离、锂插层、外延生长、激光减薄和化学气相沉积，已成功实现了仅含单层至数层的大面积二维样品的可规模化生产6–11。
> > **Graphene**: 石墨烯，由碳原子以sp2杂化形成的蜂窝状二维薄膜。
> > **Mechanical Exfoliation**: 机械剥离，一种通过胶带反复撕扯块状晶体以获得薄层材料的方法。

> > Among the non-carbon 2D materials that are being explored, dichalcogenides of group IV, V and VI transition metals are of particular interest. Similar to graphene and its bulk counterpart graphite, these materials have strong in-plane bonding while the individual layers are bonded by weak van der Waals interactions. Several TMDs, unlike graphene, have an intrinsic band gap in the range of 1 - 2 eV, making them attractive for field effect transistors and optoelectronic devices.12,13 Many singlelayer TMDs also demonstrate novel electronic properties that are otherwise not seen in their bulk or few-layer thick counterparts, for example, direct to indirect band gap transition, enhanced photoluminescence, and valley polarization.14–21
> > 在正在探索的非碳二维材料中，第IV、V和VI族过渡金属的二硫族化合物尤其引人关注。与石墨烯及其块体对应物石墨类似，这些材料具有强的面内键合，而各层之间则由弱的范德瓦尔斯相互作用结合。与石墨烯不同，一些TMDs具有1至2 eV的本征带隙，这使得它们在场效应晶体管和光电器件中颇具吸引力12,13。许多单层TMDs还展现出其块体或少层对应物中未见的新颖电子性质，例如直接带隙到间接带隙的转变、增强的光致发光以及谷极化14–21。

In this article, using first-principles density functional theory simulations, we report on the fundamental mechanical behavior of monolayer TMDs when subjected to a large deformation. Here we consider TMDs with chemical composition MX2, where M (= Mo, W) is a group VI transition metal and X (= S, Se, Te) is a chalcogen. Among the range of naturally occurring TMDs, group VI TMDS are semiconductors and, therefore, are good candidates for flexible and strain-engineered optoelectronic and photonic devices. Here we have determined the entire stress response of monolayer TMDs to large applied elastic strains — the intrinsic materials characteristics that are crucial in designing the strain-engineered and flexible devices. We find that the stress response and the ultimate strength of monolayer TMDs strongly depends on the chemical composition as well as the loading direction. For the same transition metal M, the sulfides (MS2) are the strongest and selenides (MSe2) are stronger than the tellurides (MTe2). The chalcogens of W (WX2) can accommodate larger stresses than that of Mo (MoX2). For all compositions, the ultimate strength of the monolayer along the armchair direction far exceeds the strength in zigzag direction, typically by a factor of 1.5 - 2.2. Our electronic structure analysis reveals that the origin of the observed mechanical behavior can be attributed to the hybridization between M-d and X-p orbitals and the charge transfer from the transition metal to the chalcogens.

> > III. CONCLUSION
> > 第三章 结论
> > 
> > In summary, we have investigated the relationship between the intrinsic mechanical response of the monolayer group VI TMDs to large elastic deformation, the atomiclevel structure, and chemical composition. Our calculations demonstrate that the chemical composition of a monolayer TMD strongly influences its mechanical response to large elastic deformation, with the chalcogens of W exhibiting much greater ultimate strength than the chalcogens of Mo. The stress response of monolayer TMDs also depends on the crystal symmetry, with the armchair directions being consistently stronger across all chemical compositions. The origin of the observed mechanical behavior can be attributed to a strong hybridization between the outermost p orbitals of the chalcogens and the d orbitals of the transition metal. This hybridization leads to a redistribution of the electronic charge to the shared region between the transition metal and chalcogen atoms. Our study clearly highlights the interplay between the atomic structure and the composition of monolayer TMDs that lends them a great strength to sustain large reversible deformations — deformations large enough to modify their electronic structure as envisioned for novel optoelectronic and photonics applications. It should be emphasized that our first-principles investigation provides a realistic estimate of the intrinsic mechanical response of monolayer TMDs as measured by the nano-indentation experiments. In such experiments, the stressed region of the monolayer under the atomic force microscope tip can be expected to be defect-free and, therefore, the measured stresses can approach the ultimate strength. Therefore, our investigation of the stress-strain relation of monolayer TMDs presented here not only yields crucial insights into mechanical behavior of these materials but also can be compared with the experimental measurements.
> > 总之，我们研究了单层VI族TMDs对大弹性变形的本征力学响应与原子级结构和化学组成之间的关系。我们的计算表明，单层TMD的化学组成强烈影响其对大弹性变形的力学响应，其中W的硫族化合物表现出比Mo的硫族化合物高得多的极限强度。单层TMDs的应力响应也依赖于晶体对称性，在所有化学组分中扶手椅方向始终更强。观察到的力学行为的根源可归因于硫族元素最外层p轨道与过渡金属d轨道之间的强杂化。这种杂化导致电子电荷重新分布到过渡金属和硫族原子之间的共享区域。我们的研究清楚地揭示了单层TMDs原子结构与组成之间的相互作用，这种相互作用赋予了它们承受大可逆变形的卓越强度——这些变形足以改变其电子结构，正如新型光电子和光子学应用所设想的那样。需要强调的是，我们的第一性原理研究为纳米压痕实验所测量的单层TMDs本征力学响应提供了现实的估计。在此类实验中，原子力显微镜探针下单层的受应力区域可望是无缺陷的，因此测得的应力可接近极限强度。因此，我们在此提出的单层TMDs应力-应变关系研究，不仅为这些材料的力学行为提供了关键见解，而且可与实验测量进行比较。
