> # A two-dimensional cellular automaton model was developed for the simulation of nucleation and growth of ferrite grains at various cooling rates in low-carbon steels.
> 
> # 用于模拟低碳钢中铁素体晶粒形核与长大的二维元胞自动机模型
> 
> The model calculates the diffusion of the solute and temperature fields in an explicit finite method and incorporates local temperature and concentration changes into a nucleation or growth function, which is utilized by the automaton in a probabilistic fashion. 该模型采用显式有限方法计算溶质场和温度场的扩散，并将局部温度和浓度变化纳入形核或生长函数中，由元胞自动机以概率方式使用。
> 
> The modeling provides an efficient way to understand how those physical processes dynamically progress and affect nucleation and growth of ferrite grains. 该建模为理解这些物理过程如何动态进行并影响铁素体晶粒的形核与长大提供了有效途径。
> 
> **Cellular Automaton (CA)**: 元胞自动机，一种离散的计算模型，由规则的网格单元组成，每个单元的状态根据与相邻单元相关的确定性规则随时间演化。

> # I. INTRODUCTION
> 
> # I. 引言
> 
> Because microstructures of low-carbon steels, especially their grain sizes of ferrite, have strong influence on their mechanical properties, considerable industrial importance is placed on predicting γ – α phase transformation of steels during heat treatment. 由于低碳钢的微观组织，特别是铁素体晶粒尺寸，对其力学性能有很强的影响，因此预测钢在热处理过程中的γ – α相变具有相当大的工业重要性。
> 
> In controlled rolling, which is an optimized reheating, hot rolling, and cooling process, the chief aim is to obtain the microstructures consisting of small and uniform ferrite grains, which have favorable mechanical properties in general, and in particular yield preferable strength and toughness. 在控制轧制（一种优化的再加热、热轧和冷却过程）中，主要目标是获得由细小且均匀的铁素体晶粒组成的微观组织，这种组织通常具有良好的力学性能，特别是能产生优选的强度和韧性。
> 
> In recent years, many efforts have been undertaken to understand how to obtain the refinement microstructure of ferrite grains.1–6 近年来，已经进行了许多努力来理解如何获得铁素体晶粒的细化微观组织。1–6
> 
> However, the microstructure formation in steels is not yet fully understood because of the complexity of the solid-state transformation involving the combined complex behavior of heat–mass transfer, nucleation and growth etc., in the liquid and/or solid states and their interfaces during the γ – α phase transformation. 然而，钢中微观组织的形成尚未完全被理解，因为在γ – α相变过程中，固态相变涉及液态和/或固态及其界面中传热传质、形核与长大等的联合复杂行为，具有复杂性。
> 
> Although experimental observations could qualitatively explain the microstructure formation without using modeling, quantitative understanding of this combined behavior and its dynamic dependence on temperature and composition of steels is required to control the microstructure formation and in turn, the mechanical and physical properties of products by modeling. 尽管实验观察可以在不使用建模的情况下定性地解释微观组织的形成，但需要通过建模对这种联合行为及其对钢的温度和成分的动态依赖性进行定量理解，以控制微观组织的形成，从而控制产品的力学和物理性能。
> 
> In these modeling works, Umemoto et al. studied the effect of cooling rate and austenite grain size on the resultant ferrite grain size.1,2 在这些建模工作中，Umemoto等人研究了冷却速率和奥氏体晶粒尺寸对最终铁素体晶粒尺寸的影响。1,2
> 
> **Austenite**: 奥氏体，碳溶于γ-Fe中形成的间隙固溶体，具有面心立方结构。
> 
> Jacot and Rappaz provided a two-phase model for the prediction of microstructural evolution in an Fe–C alloy, which undergoes a diffusive phase transformation.3 Jacot和Rappaz提供了一个两相模型，用于预测经历扩散型相变的Fe–C合金中的微观组织演变。3
> 
> However, their works could not provide an complete understanding of nucleation and growth of ferrite grains. 然而，他们的工作无法提供对铁素体晶粒形核与长大的完整理解。
> 
> In fact, nucleation and growth of grains has been simulated in solidification by using a method called cellular automation (CA).7–10 事实上，晶粒的形核与长大已在凝固过程中通过使用一种称为元胞自动机（CA）的方法进行了模拟。7–10
> 
> According to Wolfram,11 CA could be an alternative model for solving the problems for systems with small degrees of freedom, typically handled by differential equations, or complex systems with large degrees of freedom. 根据Wolfram的说法，11 CA可以作为解决小自由度系统（通常由微分方程处理）或大自由度复杂系统问题的替代模型。
> 
> There are five distinct characteristics of CA: (i) There exists a grid of uniform cells, (ii) which are updated every time step (iii) with a finite value or state (iv) using deterministic rules, and (v) every cell is connected to a neighborhood of surrounding cells that control its evolution in time. CA有五个显著特征：(i) 存在一个均匀单元的网格，(ii) 每个时间步都会更新，(iii) 具有有限的值或状态，(iv) 使用确定性规则，以及(v) 每个单元都连接到控制其随时间演化的周围单元邻域。
> 
> Thus, if nucleation and growth of grains could be simulated by the CA technique in solidification, why could it not be used to predict the phase transformation, from austenite to ferrite, involved with nucleation and growth of ferrite grains in low-carbon steels? 因此，如果晶粒的形核与长大可以在凝固过程中通过CA技术进行模拟，为什么它不能用于预测低碳钢中涉及铁素体晶粒形核与长大的从奥氏体到铁素体的相变呢？
> 
> The aim of the present work is to establish a two-dimensional CA model that predicts nucleation and growth of ferrite grains for low carbon steels Fe– Xi–C (here Xi = Si, Mn, Ni, Cu, Cr). 本工作的目的是建立一个二维CA模型，用于预测低碳钢Fe–Xi–C（此处Xi = Si, Mn, Ni, Cu, Cr）中铁素体晶粒的形核与长大。
> 
> The model uses an explicit finite volume technique to calculate the solutal and thermal fields. 该模型使用显式有限体积技术来计算溶质场和温度场。
> 
> The local temperature and concentration changes are incorporated into a nucleation or growth function, which is used by the automaton in a probabilistic fashion within a small representative specimen. 局部温度和浓度变化被纳入形核或生长函数中，由元胞自动机在一个小的代表性试样中以概率方式使用。
> 
> In addition, the simulated results are verified by the experimental results.2 此外，模拟结果通过实验结果进行了验证。2
> 
> # II. MATHEMATICAL DESCRIPTION
> 
> # II. 数学描述
> 
> With respect to an Fe– Xi–C multicomponent alloy with a given low carbon concentration c, Fe- Xi could be regarded as a super-element S;12 thus the multicomponent S–C alloy is similar to a low-carbon Fe–C alloy. 对于给定低碳浓度c的Fe–Xi–C多组分合金，Fe-Xi可被视为超元素S；12因此，多组分S–C合金类似于低碳Fe–C合金。
> 
> **Super-element**: 超元素，在此模型中，将铁与合金元素（Xi）的组合视为一个单一的等效元素，以简化多组分系统的热力学计算。
> 
> As a temperature in a local zone of the S–C alloy specimen is decreased at a given cooling rate, the phase transformations would take place: 当S–C合金试样局部区域的温度以给定冷却速率降低时，将发生相变：
> 
> (γ –Fe) → (α –Fe) + (γ + Fe) → (α + Fe) + cementite . (1) (γ –Fe) → (α –Fe) + (γ + Fe) → (α + Fe) + 渗碳体 . (1)
> 
> For the Fe–C alloy with the concentration c, it is assumed that the equilibrium temperature beginning at γ → α phase transformation Ae3 is a temperature on the γ-phase line of an Fe–C phase diagram corresponding to the concentration c as shown in Fig. 1. 对于浓度为c的Fe–C合金，假设γ → α相变开始的平衡温度Ae3是Fe–C相图上对应于浓度c的γ相线上的温度，如图1所示。
> 
> **Ae3**: 在Fe-C相图中，表示奥氏体(γ)开始向铁素体(α)转变的平衡临界温度线。

> ## A. The γ → α phase transformation under continuous cooling
> 
> ## A. 连续冷却下的γ → α相变
> 
> The γ → α phase transformation under continuous cooling could be considered as the sum of short-time isothermal holdings at successive temperatures as shown in Fig. 2. 连续冷却下的γ → α相变可被视为如图2所示的一系列连续温度下短时等温保持的总和。
> 
> It could be assumed, as observed in experiments, that ferrite grains nucleate preferentially on grain boundaries of austenite. 如实验中所观察到的，可以假设铁素体晶粒优先在奥氏体晶界上形核。
> 
> In such cases, ferrite grains will initially nucleate on the grain boundaries of austenite, then the nucleated ferrite grains grow into the inner part of the austenite grains (as shown in Fig. 3). 在这种情况下，铁素体晶粒将首先在奥氏体晶界上形核，然后已形核的铁素体晶粒向奥氏体晶粒内部生长（如图3所示）。
> 
> During continuous cooling from Te to T, the number of newly formed ferrite grains n1 is 在从Te到T的连续冷却过程中，新形成的铁素体晶粒数量n1为
> 
> nT = T Te I T 1 − f Q dT . (2) nT = ∫ T Te (I(T)(1 − f) / Q) dT . (2)
> 
> Here I(T ) is ferrite nucleation rate per unit area of austenite grain boundary at a temperature T , f is a grain boundary area fraction occupied by the ferrite grains, and Q is a cooling rate. 这里，I(T)是在温度T下单位面积奥氏体晶界的铁素体形核率，f是被铁素体晶粒占据的晶界面积分数，Q是冷却速率。
> 
> At the temperature T , the ferrite nucleation rate I is expressed as 在温度T下，铁素体形核率I表示为
> 
> I = K1(kT) −1/2 D exp − K2 RT ΔGN γ→α+γ1/2 , (3) I = K1(kT)^(−1/2) D exp(− K2/(RT ΔGN γ→α+γ1/2)) , (3)
> 
> where D is the carbon diffusivity in austenite, K1 is a constant related to the nucleation site density, K2 is a constant related to the austenite/ferrite interfacial energy, k is Boltzmann’s constant, and ΔGN γ→α+γ1 is a driving force of ferrite nucleation. 其中，D是碳在奥氏体中的扩散系数，K1是与形核位置密度相关的常数，K2是与奥氏体/铁素体界面能相关的常数，k是玻尔兹曼常数，ΔGN γ→α+γ1是铁素体形核的驱动力。
> 
> It will be described in Sec. II.B. 这将在第II.B节中描述。
> 
> ## B. Driving force of ferrite nucleation
> 
> ## B. 铁素体形核驱动力
> 
> The driving force of ferrite nucleation ΔGN(Fe) γ→α+γ1 of the Fe–C alloy is calculated by ΔGN(Fe) γ→α+γ1 = ΔGFe γ→α − RT ln aFe, where ΔGFe γ→α is a free energy change of Fe in the γ → α phase transformation, and aFe is an activity coefficient of Fe in austernite. Fe–C合金的铁素体形核驱动力ΔGN(Fe) γ→α+γ1通过ΔGN(Fe) γ→α+γ1 = ΔGFe γ→α − RT ln aFe计算，其中ΔGFe γ→α是Fe在γ → α相变中的自由能变化，aFe是Fe在奥氏体中的活度系数。
> 
> For the Fe– Xi–C alloy presented in the simulation, because Fe– Xi has been regarded as the super-element S, which is similar to a calculation of the driving force of ferrite nucleation for the Fe–C alloy, the driving force of ferrite nucleation ΔGN(S) γ→α+γ1 to the multicomponent alloy could be written as 对于模拟中呈现的Fe–Xi–C合金，由于Fe–Xi已被视为超元素S，类似于Fe–C合金的铁素体形核驱动力计算，多组分合金的铁素体形核驱动力ΔGN(S) γ→α+γ1可写为
> 
> ΔGN S γ→α+γ1 = ΔGS γ→α − RT ln aS , (4) ΔGN S γ→α+γ1 = ΔGS γ→α − RT ln aS , (4)
> 
> where ΔGS γ→α is the free energy change of the superelement S from the γ → α phase transformation, and it is given by 其中，ΔGS γ→α是超元素S从γ → α相变的自由能变化，由下式给出
> 
> ΔGS γ→α = 141 Σ xi T i M − T i NM + ΔGFe S γ→α . (5) ΔGS γ→α = 141 Σ xi(T i M − T i NM) + ΔGFe(S) γ→α . (5)
> 
> Here xi is a mole fraction of a alloy element Xi, T i M and T i NM are Zener parameters, which are listed in Table I.13 这里，xi是合金元素Xi的摩尔分数，T i M和T i NM是Zener参数，列于表I中。13
> 
> ΔGFe(S) γ→α is the free energy change of Fe in the γ → α phase transformation, and it is given by ΔGFe(S) γ→α是Fe在γ → α相变中的自由能变化，由下式给出
> 
> ΔGFe S γ→α = 20853.06 − 466.35T − 0.046304T2 + 71.147T lnT . (6) ΔGFe(S) γ→α = 20853.06 − 466.35T − 0.046304T^2 + 71.147T lnT . (6)
> 
> The activity coefficient of the super-element S in austenite aS can be expressed as 超元素S在奥氏体中的活度系数aS可表示为
> 
> ln aS = ln 1 − Z xC 1 − xC Z − 1 . (7) ln aS = (1/(Z − 1)) ln((1 − Z xC)/(1 − xC)) . (7)
> 
> Here xC is a initial mole fraction of carbon in austenite, and Z is given by 这里，xC是碳在奥氏体中的初始摩尔分数，Z由下式给出
> 
> Z = 14 − 12exp(W /RT ) . (8) Z = 14 − 12exp(W/RT) . (8)
> 
> Here R is a gas constant, and W ( = 1250 J/mol) is the exchange energy among carbon atoms in austenite. 这里，R是气体常数，W ( = 1250 J/mol)是奥氏体中碳原子之间的交换能。
> 
> ## C. Diffusion of the solute and temperature fields
> 
> ## C. 溶质场和温度场的扩散
> 
> The diffusion of the solute and temperature fields has a large influence on the γ → α phase transformation, or the phase transformation is controlled by the diffusion. 溶质场和温度场的扩散对γ → α相变有很大影响，或者说相变是由扩散控制的。
> 
> Then diffusion kinetics is described as follows 那么扩散动力学描述如下
> 
> ∂u/∂t = Du ∇^2 u , (9) ∂u/∂t = Du ∇^2 u , (9)
> 
> ∂c/∂t = D ∇^2 c , (10) ∂c/∂t = D ∇^2 c , (10)
> 
> Dγ (∂cγ/∂n) − Dα (∂cα/∂n) = vn (cγ − cα) . (11) Dγ (∂cγ/∂n) − Dα (∂cα/∂n) = vn (cγ − cα) . (11)
> 
> Here, t is time, c is a carbon concentration in phase v ( γ or α), and D is the associated diffusion coefficient (here Dγ and Dα are assumed to be two constants), cγ| and cα| are two concentrations of the γ and α domains at the α/γ interface, vn is the normal velocity of the α/γ interface movement resulting from a concentration difference between α and γ sides at the α/γ interface, and n means the normal to this interface pointing to the γ side. 这里，t是时间，c是相v（γ或α）中的碳浓度，D是相关的扩散系数（这里假设Dγ和Dα为两个常数），cγ|和cα|是α/γ界面处γ和α域的两个浓度，vn是由于α/γ界面处α和γ侧之间的浓度差引起的α/γ界面移动的法向速度，n表示指向γ侧的该界面的法线。
>

> 
> u = (T − T∞)/(Lat /Cp) , (12) u = (T − T∞)/(Lat/Cp) , (12)

> where Lat is the latent heat of the phase transformation, Cp is the heat capacity of the multicomponent alloy, and T∞ is the liquid temperature on the boundary B far from the studied regions as shown in Fig. 3. 其中，Lat是相变潜热，Cp是多组分合金的热容，T∞是如图3所示远离研究区域的边界B上的液相温度。

> # III. CELLULAR AUTOMATON MODELING
> 
> # III. 元胞自动机建模
> 
> The cells of the cellular automaton are characterized by certain attributes that determine the states of one cell, and the CA evolution is defined by rules for changing the attributes. 元胞自动机的单元由决定一个单元状态的某些属性来表征，CA的演化由改变这些属性的规则来定义。
> 
> Four key features must be specified in the model: the neighborhood of a cell, boundary conditions, nucleation rules, and transition rules. 模型中必须指定四个关键特征：单元的邻域、边界条件、形核规则和转变规则。
> 
> The neighborhood determines which cells will be “seen” by any cell in the CA lattice. 邻域决定了CA格子中任何单元将“看到”哪些单元。
> 
> In this work, the CA is a two-dimensional hexagonal lattice with periodic boundary condition at each boundary face, and the neighborhood is defined as the nearest neighbors as seen in Fig. 4. 在这项工作中，CA是一个二维六边形格子，每个边界面具有周期性边界条件，邻域定义为最近邻，如图4所示。
> 
> The present simulation is two-dimensional, and the simulated results could be interpreted as what happens in a cross section of the simulated austenite or ferrite grains. 当前的模拟是二维的，模拟结果可以解释为在所模拟的奥氏体或铁素体晶粒的横截面中发生的情况。
> 
> Each cell in the CA lattice takes a state: α, γ, or “α/γ interface” as shown in Fig. 5, and an integer in a class of integer numbers from 1 to 100 is used to represent one crystallized orientation of each cell. CA格子中的每个单元采取一种状态：α、γ或“α/γ界面”，如图5所示，并使用1到100整数类中的一个整数来表示每个单元的一个结晶取向。
> 
> Then the description of the modeling procedure will be given as follows. 然后，建模过程的描述如下。
> 
> In the beginning of the simulation, all cells in the CA lattice are set as the γ phase with the same initial concentration, i.e., the composition of the alloy, and the same initial temperature Tini, which is higher than Ae3, corresponding to the alloy composition. 在模拟开始时，CA格子中的所有单元都被设置为具有相同初始浓度（即合金成分）和相同初始温度Tini（高于对应于合金成分的Ae3）的γ相。
> 
> As the temperature is decreased at a given cooling rate Q, the temperature change of every cell is written as T → T − Q Δt, where Δt is a time step. 随着温度以给定的冷却速率Q降低，每个单元的温度变化写为T → T − Q Δt，其中Δt是时间步长。
> 
> Step 1: To a cell (i, j) on the grain boundaries of austenite with a carbon concentration c(i,j), its Ae3,(i,j) corresponding to its c(i,j) is capable of being obtained from the Fe–C phase diagram. 步骤1：对于奥氏体晶界上具有碳浓度c(i,j)的单元(i, j)，其对应于c(i,j)的Ae3,(i,j)可以从Fe–C相图中获得。
> 
> If the cell's temperature T(i,j) is decreased below Ae3,(i,j), T(i,j) < Ae3,(i,j), a nucleation probability pnuc of that cell becoming ferrite is calculated as detailed in Sec. III.A. 如果单元的温度T(i,j)降至Ae3,(i,j)以下，T(i,j) < Ae3,(i,j)，则按照第III.A节中的详细说明计算该单元变为铁素体的形核概率pnuc。
> 
> To that cell, a random number rs (0 ≤ r < 1) is generated in advance. 对于该单元，预先产生一个随机数rs (0 ≤ rs < 1)。
> 
> If rs ≤ pnuc is satisfied for it, the cell will nucleate. 如果满足rs ≤ pnuc，该单元将形核。
> 
> In the meantime, it will release an amount of latent heat from the phase transformation and precipitate an amount of solutes c(i,j) precipitate into its neighboring austenite cells, c(i,j) precipitate = c(i,j) − c* α(i,j), where c* α(i,j) is the concentration on ferrite phase line of the phase diagram at the temperature T(i,j). 同时，它将从相变中释放一定量的潜热，并向其相邻的奥氏体单元中析出一定量的溶质c(i,j) precipitate，c(i,j) precipitate = c(i,j) − c* α(i,j)，其中c* α(i,j)是在温度T(i,j)下相图的铁素体相线上的浓度。
> 
> Each cell on the grain boundaries of austenite will also be scanned following the same steps as above. 奥氏体晶界上的每个单元也将按照上述相同的步骤进行扫描。
> 
> Step 2: The cells remaining at the α/γ interface are identified. 步骤2：识别留在α/γ界面处的单元。
> 
> Each α/γ interface cell will be scanned again, based on its carbon concentration, temperature, and the states of its neighboring cells, to determine whether or not the phase transformation (nucleation or growth of its neighbor ferrite cells) can take place. 每个α/γ界面单元将根据其碳浓度、温度及其相邻单元的状态再次被扫描，以确定是否可以进行相变（其相邻铁素体单元的形核或长大）。
> 
> In this model, the phase transformation from ferrite to austenite is not considered. 在该模型中，不考虑从铁素体到奥氏体的相变。
> 
> Step 3: Each cell transformed to ferrite from austenite, such as one cell (i, j), will precipitate an amount of solutes into its neighboring austenite cells, and the cell (i, j) has a concentration determined by the ferrite line of the phase diagram corresponding to its temperature. 步骤3：每个从奥氏体转变为铁素体的单元，例如单元(i, j)，将向相邻的奥氏体单元中析出一定量的溶质，并且单元(i, j)具有由对应于其温度的相图铁素体线确定的浓度。
> 
> Meanwhile, the cell will also release an amount of latent heat of phase transformation, and its temperature field is increased according to u(i,j) → u(i,j) + latent, which “latent” is dimensionless latent heat. 同时，该单元还将释放一定量的相变潜热，其温度场根据u(i,j) → u(i,j) + latent增加，其中“latent”是无量纲潜热。
> 
> Step 4: The diffusion of the solute and temperature fields takes place. 步骤4：发生溶质场和温度场的扩散。
> 
> The concentration c(i,j) ( γ or α/γ ) and the temperature field u(i,j) of each cell in the CA lattice, such as one cell (i, j), are calculated as detailed in Sec. III.B. CA格子中每个单元（例如单元(i, j)）的浓度c(i,j)（γ或α/γ）和温度场u(i,j)按照第III.B节中的详细说明进行计算。
> 
> The temperature T(i,j) of the cell is given by T∞ + u(i,j) Cp/Lat → T(i,j). 单元的温度T(i,j)由T∞ + u(i,j) Cp/Lat → T(i,j)给出。
> 
> Step 5: The temperature of every cell in the CA lattice, such as one cell (i, j), is decreased continuously, T(i,j) − Δt Q → T(i,j). 步骤5：CA格子中每个单元的温度，例如单元(i, j)，连续降低，T(i,j) − Δt Q → T(i,j)。
> 
> If the temperature of one cell remaining austenite is a little lower than the temperature of cementite formation, cementite will form. 如果剩余奥氏体的一个单元的温度略低于渗碳体形成温度，渗碳体将形成。

> According to Eq. (2), as there is an undercooling temperature ΔT = Ae3 − T to one cell on the grain boundaries of austenite, a new nucleus density n is given by 根据公式(2)，当奥氏体晶界上的一个单元存在过冷度ΔT = Ae3 − T时，新晶核密度n由下式给出

> ## B. Growth
> 
> ## B. 生长
> 
> Growth of ferrite is controlled by the diffusion of the solute and temperature fields, and it continues until Ae3 of the α/γ interface cell, such as a cell (i, j) becomes lower than its temperature T(i,j) or when cementite phase forms in front of the growing ferrite grains and prevents their further growth. 铁素体的生长受溶质场和温度场的扩散控制，并持续到α/γ界面单元（例如单元(i, j)）的Ae3变得低于其温度T(i,j)，或者当渗碳体相在生长的铁素体晶粒前沿形成并阻止其进一步生长时。

> Step 1: The diffusion of the solute and temperature fields among those cells of the CA lattice is described by an explicit finite approach. 步骤1：CA格子中那些单元之间的溶质场和温度场的扩散通过显式有限方法进行描述。

> 
> pcap (i,j)→nei = l(i,j) (i,j)→nei / LCA . (24) pcap (i,j)→nei = l(i,j) (i,j)→nei / LCA . (24)

> ## C. Cementite formation
> 
> ## C. 渗碳体形成
> 
> As the temperature decreases, more and more ferrite forms, and carbon solutes build up in front of the growing ferrite grains. 随着温度降低，越来越多的铁素体形成，碳溶质在生长的铁素体晶粒前沿积聚。
> 
> Thus, many cells become undercooled with respect to cementite formation, and the formed cementite will stop growth of the ferrite grains. 因此，许多单元相对于渗碳体形成变得过冷，形成的渗碳体将阻止铁素体晶粒的生长。

> Then, as the temperature is decreased, the nucleated ferrite grains begin to grow, and more and more ferrite grains nucleate [Fig. 6(b)]. 然后，随着温度降低，已形核的铁素体晶粒开始生长，并且越来越多的铁素体晶粒形核[图6(b)]。
> 
> Finally, Fig. 6(c) displays the morphology with many large and small ferrite grains. 最后，图6(c)展示了具有许多大小铁素体晶粒的形貌。
> 
> As described in Sec. II.A., the model assumes that ferrite grains nucleate on the boundaries of austenite grains, thus each cell on the boundaries of austenite grains is a potential nucleation site. 如第II.A节所述，模型假设铁素体晶粒在奥氏体晶界上形核，因此奥氏体晶界上的每个单元都是潜在的形核位置。
> 
> Under cooling, the nucleation probability of ferrite becomes large for each potential nucleation site, and it means that many cells may nucleate. 在冷却过程中，每个潜在形核位置的铁素体形核概率变大，这意味着许多单元可能形核。
> 
> Nevertheless, the nucleated ferrite cells will precipitate an amount of solutes into their neighboring austenite grains and release certain latent heat. 然而，已形核的铁素体单元将向其相邻的奥氏体晶粒中析出一定量的溶质并释放一定的潜热。
> 
> Therefore, the concentrations of the austenite cells neighboring upon the ferrite grains become enriched. 因此，邻近铁素体晶粒的奥氏体单元的浓度变得富集。
> 
> In addition, those of the other austenite cells (inner austenite grains and on the boundaries of austenite grains), which do not neighbor upon the ferrite grains, may also be increased for the solute diffusion. 此外，其他不邻近铁素体晶粒的奥氏体单元（内部奥氏体晶粒和奥氏体晶界上的单元）的浓度也可能因溶质扩散而增加。
> 
> In the meantime, the temperature of the austenite cells will become high, resulting from the temperature field diffusion. 同时，由于温度场扩散，奥氏体单元的温度将升高。
> 
> For those austenite cells, equilibrium temperatures beginning at the phase transformation Aes could be decreased because their concentrations are increased from the solutal diffusion or receiving precipitated solutes, but their actual temperatures are increased from the thermal diffusion. 对于这些奥氏体单元，由于溶质扩散或接收析出的溶质，其浓度增加，因此相变开始的平衡温度Aes可能降低，但它们的实际温度因热扩散而升高。
> 
> At a given time, the equilibrium temperatures beginning at the phase transformation of some austenite cells may be lower than their actual temperatures. 在给定时间，一些奥氏体单元相变开始的平衡温度可能低于其实际温度。
> 
> Therefore, because undercooling temperatures (ΔT = Aes − T) of the cells remaining austenite are decreased, the nucleation probabilities of the potential nucleation cells on the grain boundaries of austenite are also decreased. 因此，由于剩余奥氏体单元的过冷度(ΔT = Aes − T)降低，奥氏体晶界上潜在形核单元的形核概率也降低。
> 
> Simply stated, diffusion affects nucleation of ferrite. 简而言之，扩散影响铁素体的形核。
> 
> Meanwhile, although the austenite cells may not nucleate, they will be able to become ferrite, resulting from growth of their neighboring ferrite cells. 同时，尽管奥氏体单元可能不形核，但它们将能够由于相邻铁素体单元的生长而变成铁素体。
> 
> Growth of ferrite grains can proceed if undercooling temperatures exist in their neighboring austenite cells and cementite does not form in front of the growing ferrite grains. 如果其相邻的奥氏体单元中存在过冷度，并且渗碳体未在生长的铁素体晶粒前沿形成，则铁素体晶粒的生长可以进行。
> 
> Certainly, growth of the ferrite grains is also affected by the diffusion of the solute and temperature fields for which uniform distribution of the solute and temperature fields is a great help to sufficient growth of the ferrite grains. 当然，铁素体晶粒的生长也受溶质场和温度场扩散的影响，溶质场和温度场的均匀分布对铁素体晶粒的充分生长有很大帮助。
> 
> If the given cooling rate is small enough, the uniformity structure of ferrite grains could be obtained. 如果给定的冷却速率足够小，可以获得铁素体晶粒的均匀组织。
> 
> However, because the cooling rate could not be small enough in the model, distribution of the solute or temperature fields is not uniform in the modeled specimen. 然而，由于模型中的冷却速率不可能足够小，模拟试样中的溶质场或温度场分布不均匀。
> 
> Then the number of the nucleated ferrite grains may be large in some zones of the specimen, or the ferrite grains might grow more quickly in the other zones. 然后，试样某些区域中形核的铁素体晶粒数量可能很多，或者铁素体晶粒在其他区域可能生长得更快。
> 
> Thus, the final structure as shown in Fig. 6(c) is obtained, where the size of some grains is larger than that of others, and the resultant ferrite grain size is not uniform. 因此，获得了如图6(c)所示的最终组织，其中一些晶粒的尺寸大于其他晶粒，并且最终铁素体晶粒尺寸不均匀。
> 
> In general, the diffusion of the solute and temperature fields plays an important role in nucleation and growth, or nucleation and growth is diffusion-controlled. 总的来说，溶质场和温度场的扩散在形核和生长中起着重要作用，或者说形核和生长是扩散控制的。
> 
> Besides these grains there are some small deep black regions inside ferrite grains or on the grain boundaries of ferrite, which are cementite. 除了这些晶粒之外，在铁素体晶粒内部或铁素体晶界上还有一些小的深黑色区域，这些是渗碳体。
> 
> The formation of cementite could be understood by the following fact: The small regions are enriched in solutes while the ferrite phase forms around them. 渗碳体的形成可以通过以下事实来理解：当铁素体相在它们周围形成时，这些小的区域富含溶质。
> 
> Before the enriched solutes can diffuse, the temperatures of the regions remaining austenite decrease down the temperature of cementite formation; then cementite phase forms in the regions. 在富集的溶质能够扩散之前，剩余奥氏体区域的温度降低到渗碳体形成温度；然后渗碳体相在这些区域中形成。

> From the figures, we can clearly see that a large cooling rate makes the ferrite grain size finer than a small cooling rate does. 从图中，我们可以清楚地看到，大的冷却速率使铁素体晶粒尺寸比小的冷却速率更细小。
> 
> The phenomenon can be explained by the following. 这种现象可以解释如下。
> 
> To make the ferrite grain size uniform and fine, increasing nucleation number and controlling growth of ferrite are two efficient ways. 为了使铁素体晶粒尺寸均匀且细小，增加形核数量和控制铁素体生长是两种有效途径。
> 
> If the cooling rate is small, the diffusion of the solute field could go on a quite long time. 如果冷却速率小，溶质场的扩散可以进行相当长的时间。
> 
> The average concentrations of the cells remaining austenite become high because they receive the precipitated solutes from the ferrite grains. 剩余奥氏体单元的平均浓度变高，因为它们接收了来自铁素体晶粒的析出溶质。
> 
> Then their equilibrium temperatures beginning at the phase transformation become lower. 然后，它们相变开始的平衡温度变低。
> 
> In some cases, because their temperatures are higher than their equilibrium temperatures beginning at the phase transformation, they cannot nucleate. 在某些情况下，由于它们的温度高于相变开始的平衡温度，它们不能形核。
> 
> Nevertheless, under rapid cooling, the temperature is decreased down to quite a low level (which is much lower than the equilibrium temperature beginning at phase transformation corresponding to the alloy composition). 然而，在快速冷却下，温度降低到相当低的水平（远低于对应于合金成分的相变开始平衡温度）。
> 
> At this time, many potential nucleation sites on the grain boundaries of austenite could become ferrite as there exist undercooling temperatures for them, and the large undercooling temperatures also make nucleation probabilities of those potential sites high. 此时，奥氏体晶界上的许多潜在形核位置可以变成铁素体，因为它们存在过冷度，并且大的过冷度也使这些潜在位置的形核概率很高。
> 
> Thus, the final nucleation sum of ferrite increases because the sum of the potential nucleation cells and the nucleation probabilities of them are both improved. 因此，铁素体的最终形核总数增加，因为潜在形核单元的总数及其形核概率都得到了提高。
> 
> In addition to the nucleation number increased, the diffusion of the solute and temperature fields also has a great influence on growth of the ferrite grains. 除了形核数量增加之外，溶质场和温度场的扩散也对铁素体晶粒的生长有很大影响。
> 
> Because the diffusion of the temperature field is much quicker than that of the solute field and the temperature distribution becomes quickly uniform to the small modeled specimen, the diffusion of the solute field has larger influence on nucleation and growth of ferrite. 由于温度场的扩散比溶质场的扩散快得多，并且对于小的模拟试样，温度分布迅速变得均匀，因此溶质场的扩散对铁素体的形核和生长有更大的影响。
> 
> At a small enough cooling rate, the concentration distribution could be uniform due to enough diffusion of the solute field. 在足够小的冷却速率下，由于溶质场的充分扩散，浓度分布可以均匀。

> At a large cooling rate, before diffusion of solute field could take place, the growth of the ferrite grains toward their neighboring austenite cells could have finished. 在大的冷却速率下，在溶质场扩散能够发生之前，铁素体晶粒向其相邻奥氏体单元的生长可能已经完成。

> 
> Thus, the grain size of ferrite is not able to become very large, and the uniformity and fine grain size of ferrite is formed. 因此，铁素体的晶粒尺寸不能变得非常大，从而形成了铁素体的均匀性和细小晶粒尺寸。

> # V. 结论
> 
> A two-dimensional cellular automaton model has been developed to model the combined complex behavior of heat–mass transfer, nucleation of ferrite, and growth of nucleated grains during the γ – α phase transformation of low-carbon steels. 已开发了一个二维元胞自动机模型，用于模拟低碳钢γ – α相变过程中传热传质、铁素体形核以及已形核晶粒生长的联合复杂行为。
> 
> This modeling gives us physical insight into how dynamically they interact and constrain each other and what their combined effects on the microstructural evolution are. 该建模为我们提供了物理洞察，以了解它们如何动态地相互作用和相互约束，以及它们对微观组织演变的联合效应是什么。
> 
> The simulated results are compared with the optical micrographs showing ferrite grains formed at various cooling rates, and they are in good agreement with those micrographs. 将模拟结果与显示在不同冷却速率下形成的铁素体晶粒的光学显微照片进行了比较，它们与这些显微照片非常吻合。
> 
> Moreover, CA modeling can demonstrate the microstructural evolution of multigrains involving nucleation and growth in nearly reality, and it can, also give the microstructure at any time. 此外，CA建模可以近乎真实地展示涉及形核和长大的多晶粒微观组织演变，并且还可以给出任何时刻的微观组织。
> 
> Nevertheless, it is investigated only through experiments, The phase transformation of the low carbon steel Fe– Xi–C is proven to be a mass–heat diffusion-controlled process, and the diffusion processes have an important influence on the nucleation and growth, and in turn, the microstructure formation of ferrite grains. 然而，这仅通过实验进行研究。低碳钢Fe–Xi–C的相变被证明是一个传质-传热扩散控制的过程，扩散过程对形核和生长以及进而对铁素体晶粒的微观组织形成具有重要影响。
> 
> With the same initial grain size, the final morphology of ferrite depends mainly on the cooling rate during the phase transformation. 在相同的初始晶粒尺寸下，铁素体的最终形貌主要取决于相变过程中的冷却速率。
> 
> A larger cooling rate is a great help to obtain finer ferrite grains in the CA model, which is in agreement with experiment. 在CA模型中，较大的冷却速率有助于获得更细小的铁素体晶粒，这与实验一致。
> 
> Thus, it is concluded that the present model can evaluate the γ – α phase transformation of low-carbon steels. 因此，得出的结论是，本模型可以评估低碳钢的γ – α相变。
