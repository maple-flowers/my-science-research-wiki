> [!quote]- <center>Abstract</center>
> 
> Two-dimensional (2D) magnetic crystals have been extensively explored thanks to their potential applications in spintronics, valleytronics, and topological superconductivity. Here we report a novel monolayer magnet, namely puckered pentagonal VTe2 (PP-VTe2), intriguing atomic and electronic structures of which were firmly validated from first-principles calculations. The PP-VTe2 exhibits strong intrinsic ferromagnetism and semiconducting property distinct from the half-metallic bulk pyrite VTe2 (BP-VTe2) phase. An unusual magnetic anisotropy with large magnetic exchange energies is found. More interestingly, the multiferroic coupling between its 2D ferroelasticity and in-plane magnetization is further identified in PP-VTe2, lending it unprecedented controllability with external strains and electric fields. Serving as an emergent 2D ferromagnetic semiconductor with a novel crystal structure, monolayer PP-VTe2 provides an ideal platform for exploring exotic crystalline and spin configurations in low-dimensional systems.
>
>【摘要翻译】二维磁性晶体由于其在自旋电子学、价电子学和拓扑超导电性等方面的潜在应用而得到了广泛的研究。本文报道了一种新颖的单层磁体，即折叠五边形VTe2（PP-VTe2），其有趣的原子和电子结构通过第一性原理计算得到了证实。PP-VTe2具有与半金属块体黄铁矿VTe2（BP-VTe2）相不同的本征铁磁性和半导体性质。发现了一种具有较大磁交换能的反常磁各向异性。更有趣的是，在PP-VTe2中进一步发现了其二维铁弹性和面内磁化之间的多铁性耦合，使其在外加应变和电场下具有前所未有的可控性。单分子膜PP-VTe2是一种新兴的二维铁磁半导体材料，具有新颖的晶体结构，为探索低维体系中奇异的晶体结构和自旋结构提供了理想的平台。

> <span class="highlight" style="background-color: #5fb236">Two-dimensional (2D) magnetic crystals have been extensively explored thanks to their potential applications in spintronics, valleytronics, and topological superconductivity. > <span class="highlight" style="background-color: #2ea8e5">Recently, the 2D magnetism had been widely investigated or predicted in vanadium dichalcogenides (VSe2 and VTe2) theoretically and experimentally [18–22]. For example, the FM ordering of monolayer VSe2 on highly oriented pyrolytic graphite and MoS2 substrates had been reported [18]. However, these results remain debated and the agreement initially reached is that monolayer VSe2 is not an itinerant magnet since its FM ordering is suppressed by charge density wave (CDW) [21]. These ambiguous results declare an indecisive conclusion of ferromagnetism in monolayer VSe2. On the other hand, another vanadium dichalcogenide VTe2, which was known for the Kondo effect in transport measurements as multilayer nanoplates [23] and a novel (4 × 4) CDW pattern as T-phase monolayer thin films [24–27], haven’t been proved to hold any long-range magnetism in previous experiments. With the expectation of magnetism in VTe2 systems, much effort should be devoted to more phases and their magnetic properties. Besides the traditional T and H phases, there is another unique puckered pentagonal structure for transition metal dichalcogenides, which could be considered as a pyrite structure when thinned to 2D limits represented by layered PdSe2 [28–30]. To the best of our knowledge, however, the puckered pentagonal phase of VTe2 (PP-VTe2) has not been reported, which could have developed magnetism in VTe2 monolayers.</span>
> 
> 最近，二维磁性在理论和实验上得到了广泛的研究或预测[18-22]。例如，单层VSe2在高取向热解石墨和MoS2衬底上的调频排序已经被报道[18]。然而，这些结果仍有争议，最初达成的一致意见是单层VSe2不是流动磁体，因为它的调频排序被电荷密度波（CDW）抑制[21]。这些模棱两可的结果宣布了单层VSe2中铁磁性的一个不确定的结论。  
> 另一方面，另一种二硫化物钒VTe2，以多层纳米板[23]和新型（4×4）CDW图案作为T相单层薄膜[24-27]的输运测量中的近藤效应而闻名，在以前的实验中没有被证明具有任何长程磁性。随着VTe2系统中磁性的预期，应该在更多相及其磁性能上投入大量精力。除了传统的T和H相之外，过渡金属二硫化物还有另一种独特的褶皱五边形结构，当减薄到由层状PdSe2[28-30]表示的2D极限时，它可以被认为是黄铁矿结构。

> <span class="highlight" style="background-color: #2ea8e5">Here, we report a monolayer puckered pentagonal VTe2, which is a brand-new phase of vanadium dichalcogenides composed of pentagonal motifs. The structural stability of PPVTe2 is dynamically and mechanically confirmed by phonon dispersions and Born–Huang criteria, respectively [31]. The electronic structure calculations indicate an emergent spinpolarized semiconducting band with a 0.33 eV narrow band gap. In-plane FM orderings and high Curie temperature are obtained by magnetic exchange energies calculations and Monte Carlo (MC) simulations. Besides the magnetic properties, 2D ferroelastic phase transitions are realized due to the crystal anisotropy induced by lattice distortions and atomic displacements, which further lead to a direct multiferroic coupling. Furthermore, electrical control of magnetism [15] is conceived because of the entire magnetic density of states (DOS) around the Fermi level. Upon that, we proposed that PP-VTe2 may provide a favorable platform to study the spin-polarized charge carriers’ properties and our work will accordingly extend our understanding of 2D semiconducting magnets.</span>
> 
> 在这里，我们报告了一个单层褶皱的五边形VTe2，它是由五边形基元组成的钒二硫化物的全新相。PPVTe2的结构稳定性分别由声子色散和Boin-Wong准则[31]动态和机械地证实。电子结构计算表明出现了具有0.33 eV窄带隙的自旋极化半导体带。通过磁交换能计算和蒙特卡罗（MC）模拟获得了面内FM有序和高居里温度。除了磁性之外，由于晶格畸变和原子位移引起的晶体各向异性，从而进一步导致直接的多铁耦合，实现了2D铁弹性相变。  
> 此外，磁的电控制[15]是由于费米能级周围的整个磁态密度（DOS）而构思的。在此基础上，我们提出PP-VTe2可能为研究自旋极化电荷载流子的性质提供一个有利的平台，我们的工作将相应地扩展我们对二维半导体磁体的理解。【👈 译】 ([p1](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/GMMS86V5?page=1&annotation=QE63US53))

> <span class="highlight" style="background-color: #2ea8e5">To understand the crystal structures and electronic properties of PP-VTe2, the bulk VTe2 with pyrite structure should be firstly investigated. Puckered pentagonal structural motif extending along two dimensions within the vertical height of 2 Å can be considered as one sub-layer, which is illustrated in Fig. 1(a) with V2Te4 per unit cell. The calculated spin-polarized band structure of BP-VTe2 is shown in Fig. 1(b) with the Brillion zone and high-symmetry momentum paths shown in Fig. 1(d). Each V has 6 nearest Te neighbors forming an octahedral crystal field, further leading to the split of eg and t2g levels, where the three low t2g levels are occupied by the 3 unpaired electrons indicating a large magnetic moment of 3 B per V atom. > ## 四、正文核心内容剖析
> 
> ### 1. 研究背景与动机
> 
> 研究人员首先点明了二维磁性半导体在纳米尺度下的巨大研究价值，并回顾了CrI₃、Cr₂Ge₂Te₆等标志性成果。他们特别指出，钒基二硫族化物（VSe₂和VTe₂）虽然备受关注，但实验结果存在争议：单层VSe₂的磁性可能被电荷密度波（CDW）抑制，而1T相VTe₂则未被证实有长程磁性。因此，探索VTe₂的新结构相，以开发其潜在的磁性，成为一个明确的研究突破口。
> 
> ### 2. 方法模型与核心创新
> 
> 本研究的核心创新在于成功预测了一种全新的PP-VTe₂结构。该结构的灵感源于块体黄铁矿相VTe₂（BP-VTe₂）的（100）面。研究人员通过高精度的**第一性原理计算（DFT）**，使用VASP软件包，系统地研究了该结构。
> 
> -   **稳定性验证**：通过**声子谱**（无虚频）和**弹性常数**（满足波恩-黄昆判据，Born-Huang criteria）的计算，确证了PP-VTe₂的动力学和力学稳定性。计算不同子层滑移下的总能量，证明了当前结构是全局能量最低点。
> -   **磁性研究**：采用DFT+U方法处理V原子3d电子的强关联效应，对比了非磁、铁磁和三种反铁磁态的能量，确定了铁磁基态。通过**磁各向异性能**计算确定了面内易磁化轴，并利用**蒙特卡洛（MC）模拟**基于海森堡模型估算了居里温度（~110 K）。
> -   **多铁性耦合**：基于PP-VTe₂矩形晶胞的固有各向异性，提出了其二维铁弹性的概念。利用**微动弹性带（NEB）**方法计算了铁弹翻转路径和能垒（258 meV/atom），并明确指出铁弹翻转会驱动易磁化轴同步旋转90°，这是多铁性耦合的直接证据。
> 
> ### 3. 实验结果与讨论
> 
> 由于是纯计算工作，此处的“实验”指计算模拟结果。
> 
> -   **电子结构**：计算发现PP-VTe₂的带隙为0.33 eV，远小于CrI₃（~1.07 eV）等常见二维磁性半导体，是一种**窄带隙半导体**，在红外光电子学领域有应用前景。其带隙可通过层数进行调控，从0.33 eV（单层）到0.11 eV（8层），并伴随间接-直接带隙转变。
> -   **磁性机理**：磁性主要来源于V³⁺离子的3d轨道。在八面体晶体场中，3个未配对电子占据低能级的t₂g轨道，提供了约3 μB的磁矩。自旋极化态密度图显示，费米能级附近的态几乎全部由自旋极化的V 3d电子贡献，这为**电控磁性**提供了可能：通过栅极电压注入电子或空穴，改变自旋向上和向下能带的占据数，从而调控体系的净磁矩。
> 
> ## 五、结论与贡献
> 
> 本研究通过系统的第一性原理计算，预测并验证了一种新型二维多功能材料——单层褶皱五边形VTe₂（PP-VTe₂）。其核心结论与贡献如下：
> 
> 1.  **发现新结构**：在VTe₂体系中首次提出了稳定的PP-VTe₂结构，拓展了VTe₂的相图。
> 2.  **确立新物性**：证实PP-VTe₂是一种本征的**铁磁半导体**，区别于其母体BP-VTe₂的半金属性，填补了窄带隙二维磁性半导体的空白。
> 3.  **揭示多铁性耦合**：首次在2D体系中揭示了铁弹性与面内铁磁性之间直接的多铁性耦合，为通过机械应变调控磁性提供了全新范例。
> 4.  **提出电控磁性方案**：基于其独特的电子结构，提出了通过电场调控载流子浓度来改变磁性的可行方案，为低功耗磁电存储和逻辑器件设计提供了理论指导。
>

> ## 七、学术思考与批判性分析
> 
> 在肯定该工作重要价值的同时，我们也应保持批判性思维，深入思考以下几个问题：
> 
> 1.  **稳定性与可合成性挑战**：尽管计算证明了PP-VTe₂的动力学和力学稳定性，但其化学形成能（-0.265 eV/atom）略高于已知的1T相（-0.307 eV/atom），这表明它是一个亚稳态结构。在实验合成中，如何克服热力学倾向、精准控制生长条件以获得亚稳态的PP相而非稳定的1T相，将是一个巨大的挑战。剥离能的计算为物理剥离提供了可能，但大面积、高质量单层的制备仍是难题。
> 2.  **居里温度与实际应用**：110 K的居里温度远低于室温，这严重限制了其在实际电子器件中的应用。尽管作者与其它早期发现的二维磁体进行了比较，但寻找具有室温以上居里温度的铁磁半导体仍是该领域的核心目标。未来如何通过掺杂、应变、缺陷工程等手段将Tc提升至室温，是走向应用的关键。
> 3.  **多铁性耦合模型的精确性**：文中揭示的多铁性耦合是基于静态结构计算，即铁弹翻转前后基态磁矩方向的对比。对于铁弹翻转这一动态过程中的磁矩演化路径，以及晶格自由度与自旋自由度的实时耦合强度，文中并未进行深入地动力学模拟（如自旋-晶格动力学）。这部分研究对于理解耦合的微观机制至关重要。
> 4.  **电控磁性的量化分析**：文中对电控磁性的论述是定性的。虽然指出通过栅压改变载流子浓度可以调控磁矩，但缺乏一个定量的、包含栅压效应的电化学模型。例如，在多大栅压范围、多高载流子浓度下，磁矩会如何变化？这种变化是否可逆？介电击穿或量子隧穿等实际效应是否会掩盖这种调控效应？这些都是需要进一步量化研究的问题。
> 5.  **激子效应与光学性质**：作为一个直接带隙半导体（多层时），其光学性质会受到**激子（exciton）**效应的显著影响，特别是对于单层材料，库仑屏蔽效应较弱，激子束缚能很大。文中未讨论激子效应，这对于其作为红外光电子学材料的潜力评估是一个重要的缺失环节。

> ## 八、用户问题预测与解答
> 
> 基于对文献的理解，以下预测并解答了学习者可能提出的5个延伸问题：
> 
> **1. 问：什么是“多铁性”和“多铁性耦合”？文中是如何实现二维多铁性耦合的？** **答**：**多铁性（multiferroicity）**是指材料在同一个相中同时存在两种或两种以上的铁序（ferroic orders），如铁磁性、铁电性、铁弹性等。**多铁性耦合**则是指这些铁序之间能够相互调控，例如，用电场控制磁化，或用磁场控制电极化。本文在二维PP-VTe₂中发现的是**铁弹性**和**铁磁性**的耦合。PP-VTe₂的矩形晶胞导致了结构各向异性，使其具有了铁弹性（可在两个等价取向间切换）。计算表明，当铁弹性翻转发生时（即晶格旋转90°），其面内易磁化轴也同步旋转了90°。这就实现了通过机械应变（驱动铁弹翻转）来控制磁化方向，是一种直接的二维多铁性耦合。
> 
> **2. 问：为什么作者要强调PP-VTe₂是一种“窄带隙”半导体？这有什么特殊意义？** **答**：二维磁性半导体的带隙大小决定了其潜在的应用领域。例如，带隙约1.0 eV的CrI₃适用于可见光波段。而PP-VTe₂的带隙仅为0.33 eV，远小于CrI₃，属于**窄带隙半导体**。这使其能够吸收和发射能量较低的光子，对应**红外波段**。因此，PP-VTe₂有望应用于红外光探测、红外激光器、热成像等红外光电子学领域。此外，窄带隙也意味着其电子更容易被热激发或电场调控，对于实现电控磁性器件也更为有利。
> 
> **3. 问：文中是如何证明PP-VTe₂是稳定的？声子谱和弹性常数分别说明了什么？** **答**：作者从两个关键角度论证了PP-VTe₂单层可以稳定存在：
> 
> -   **动力学稳定性**：通过计算**声子色散谱**来证明。声子是晶格振动的量子化描述。如果声子谱中所有频率都是正值（即没有虚频），说明原子在平衡位置附近的微小振动会恢复，而不是无限偏离，体系是动力学稳定的。这就像一个小球处在碗底，轻轻推一下，它会来回摆动后回到原位。
> -   **力学稳定性**：通过计算**弹性常数**并检验其是否满足**波恩-黄昆（Born-Huang）判据**来证明。这些判据是描述材料抵抗弹性形变能力的数学条件。计算得到的C₁₁、C₂₂、C₁₂、C₄₄等参数满足这些不等式，意味着该结构的晶格在受到微小应力时不会自发塌缩或发生形变，是力学稳定的。
> 
> **4. 问：什么是“蒙特卡洛模拟”？它在本文中起什么作用？** **答**：**蒙特卡洛（Monte Carlo, MC）模拟**是一种基于随机抽样和统计规律的数值计算方法。在本文中，它被用来估算PP-VTe₂的**居里温度（Tc）**。作者首先构建了一个描述磁原子间相互作用的“海森堡模型”，模型中的交换耦合参数（J₀）和磁各向异性参数（λ）是从第一性原理计算中提取的。然后，在一个包含大量原子磁矩的二维网格上，MC模拟通过随机扰动磁矩方向，并根据能量变化和温度概率（Metropolis算法）决定是否接受新状态。通过模拟不同温度下体系总磁矩和比热的涨落，就可以找到磁矩急剧降为零的温度点，即Tc。这是一种将微观电子结构计算的结果，桥接到宏观物性（相变温度）的重要方法。
> 
> **5. 问：除了PP-VTe₂，文中是否还提到了其他类似材料？这暗示了什么研究趋势？** **答**：是的，在文末的讨论和补充材料中，作者提到了**PP-VSe₂**和**PP-VS₂**。计算表明，这两种材料在费米能级附近具有与PP-VTe₂相似的能带结构，因此也有望实现电控磁性。这暗示了一个更宏大的研究趋势：即**从发现单一材料，走向系统性地预测和设计一类具有特定功能的新材料**。通过将PP-VTe₂的成功经验推广到整个钒基二硫族化物家族，甚至其他过渡金属硫族化物中，研究者们正在努力构建一个庞大的二维多功能材料数据库，为未来的实验筛选和器件应用提供丰富的候选者。

> ## ABSTRACT
> 
> ## 摘要
> 
> Two-dimensional (2D) magnetic crystals have been extensively explored thanks to their potential applications in spintronics, valleytronics, and topological superconductivity. Here we report a novel monolayer magnet, namely puckered pentagonal VTe2 (PP-VTe2), intriguing atomic and electronic structures of which were firmly validated from first-principles calculations. The PP-VTe2 exhibits strong intrinsic ferromagnetism and semiconducting property distinct from the half-metallic bulk pyrite VTe2 (BP-VTe2) phase. An unusual magnetic anisotropy with large magnetic exchange energies is found. More interestingly, the multiferroic coupling between its 2D ferroelasticity and in-plane magnetization is further identified in PP-VTe2, lending it unprecedented controllability with external strains and electric fields. Serving as an emergent 2D ferromagnetic semiconductor with a novel crystal structure, monolayer PP-VTe2 provides an ideal platform for exploring exotic crystalline and spin configurations in low-dimensional systems. 二维（2D）磁性晶体因其在自旋电子学、谷电子学和拓扑超导中的潜在应用而受到广泛研究。本文报道了一种新型单层磁体，即褶皱五边形VTe2（PP-VTe2），其奇特的原子和电子结构通过第一性原理计算得到了有力验证。PP-VTe2表现出强的本征铁磁性和半导体特性，与半金属体相黄铁矿型VTe2（BP-VTe2）相截然不同。发现其具有大的磁交换能且磁各向异性异常。更有趣的是，在PP-VTe2中进一步确认了二维铁弹性与面内磁化之间的多铁耦合，使其可通过外部应变和电场实现前所未有的调控能力。作为一种具有新颖晶体结构的新型二维铁磁半导体，单层PP-VTe2为探索低维体系中奇异的晶体和自旋构型提供了理想平台。 **2D**: 二维 **ferromagnetism**: 铁磁性 **first-principles**: 第一性原理，基于量子力学从头计算的方法 **ferroelasticity**: 铁弹性

> ## 1 Introduction
> 
> ## 1 引言
> 
> Since the discovery of van der Waals magnetic crystals CrI3 [1], much research interest has been devoted to exploring the significant potential of two-dimensional (2D) magnets in various fields, such as electronics, spintronics, sensors, information storage, and computing technologies [2–7]. Tremendous studies have been contributed by two aspects of 2D magnetic semiconductors in nanoscale: discovering novel magnetic materials through epitaxial growth, and probing unique magnetic mechanisms through regulating magnetic interactions [8, 9]. Several kinds of 2D materials have been reported to preserve intrinsic 2D ferromagnetism such as Cr2Ge2Te6, Fe3GeTe2, CrSe2, and CrTe2 [10–13], and both ferromagnetic (FM) and antiferromagnetic (AFM) interlayer interactions, as well as tunable magnetism, have been confirmed in semiconducting CrI3 and CrBr3 bilayers [14–17]. Recently, the 2D magnetism had been widely investigated or predicted in vanadium dichalcogenides (VSe2 and VTe2) theoretically and experimentally [18–22]. For example, the FM ordering of monolayer VSe2 on highly oriented pyrolytic graphite and MoS2 substrates had been reported [18]. However, these results remain debated and the agreement initially reached is that monolayer VSe2 is not an itinerant magnet since its FM ordering is suppressed by charge density wave (CDW) [21]. These ambiguous results declare an indecisive conclusion of ferromagnetism in monolayer VSe2. On the other hand, another vanadium dichalcogenide VTe2, which was known for the Kondo effect in transport measurements as multilayer nanoplates [23] and a novel (4 × 4) CDW pattern as T-phase monolayer thin films [24–27], haven’t been proved to hold any long-range magnetism in previous experiments. With the expectation of magnetism in VTe2 systems, much effort should be devoted to more phases and their magnetic properties. 自发现范德华磁性晶体CrI3 [1]以来，大量研究致力于探索二维（2D）磁体在电子学、自旋电子学、传感器、信息存储和计算技术等领域的巨大潜力[2–7]。在纳米尺度上，2D磁性半导体的研究主要集中于两个方面：通过外延生长发现新型磁性材料，以及通过调控磁相互作用探索独特的磁机制[8, 9]。已有数种2D材料被报道具有本征二维铁磁性，如Cr2Ge2Te6、Fe3GeTe2、CrSe2和CrTe2 [10–13]，并且铁磁（FM）和反铁磁（AFM）层间相互作用以及可调磁性也已在半导体性CrI3和CrBr3双层中得到了证实[14–17]。近期，二维磁性在钒的二硫族化物（VSe2和VTe2）中得到了广泛的理论和实验研究或预测[18–22]。例如，曾报道单层VSe2在高定向热解石墨和MoS2衬底上具有FM有序[18]。然而，这些结果仍存在争议，最初达成的共识是单层VSe2并非巡游磁体，因为其FM有序会被电荷密度波（CDW）压制[21]。这些模糊的结果表明单层VSe2的铁磁性尚无定论。另一方面，另一种钒的二硫族化物VTe2，此前已知在多层纳米片的输运测量中表现出近藤效应[23]，并在T相单层薄膜中呈现新颖的(4×4) CDW图样[24–27]，但尚未在实验中证实具有任何长程磁性。期待在VTe2体系中存在磁性，因此需要对其更多相及其磁性质投入大量研究。 **CrI3**: 碘化铬，一种层状范德华磁性材料 **FM**: 铁磁 **AFM**: 反铁磁 **CDW**: 电荷密度波
> 
> Besides the traditional T and H phases, there is another unique puckered pentagonal structure for transition metal dichalcogenides, which could be considered as a pyrite structure when thinned to 2D limits represented by layered PdSe2 [28–30]. To the best of our knowledge, however, the puckered pentagonal phase of VTe2 (PP-VTe2) has not been reported, which could have developed magnetism in VTe2 monolayers. 除了传统的T相和H相，过渡金属二硫族化物还有一种独特的褶皱五边形结构，当减薄至二维极限时，可以视为黄铁矿结构，以层状PdSe2为代表[28–30]。然而，据我们所知，尚未报道过VTe2的褶皱五边形相（PP-VTe2），该相可能在VTe2单层中产生磁性。
> 
> Here, we report a monolayer puckered pentagonal VTe2, which is a brand-new phase of vanadium dichalcogenides composed of pentagonal motifs. The structural stability of PPVTe2 is dynamically and mechanically confirmed by phonon dispersions and Born–Huang criteria, respectively [31]. The electronic structure calculations indicate an emergent spinpolarized semiconducting band with a 0.33 eV narrow band gap. In-plane FM orderings and high Curie temperature are obtained by magnetic exchange energies calculations and Monte Carlo (MC) simulations. Besides the magnetic properties, 2D ferroelastic phase transitions are realized due to the crystal anisotropy induced by lattice distortions and atomic displacements, which further lead to a direct multiferroic coupling. Furthermore, electrical control of magnetism [15] is conceived because of the entire magnetic density of states (DOS) around the Fermi level. > Due to the lack of four-fold rotational symmetry, the monolayer PP-VTe2 exhibits the crystal anisotropy of 1.8% between in-plane lattice parameters a and b promising for 2D ferroelasticity. Then the relative energies as a function of the phase transition pathway were calculated, and the energy barriers of ferroelastic switching were estimated by using the nudged elastic band (NEB) theory [45]. To constrain the unit cell and reduce the influence of lattice deformation during the switching procedures, we firstly applied a compressive strain along the x-axis and a tensile strain along the y-axis on the initial state (IS) in Fig. 4(a) to transform the rectangular lattice into a square one. By rotating the IS for 90°, the lattice of the final state (FS) was obtained and shown in Figs. 4(c) and 4(e). Considering that atomic positions between the IS and FS are different, an intermediate state (MS) is proposed as shown in Fig. 4(b). Comparing the MS with the IS, all Te atoms in the lower sub-layer move horizontally maintaining the puckered pentagonal motif intact. > Based on the calculated spin-polarized band structure (Fig. S2(a) in the ESM) and projected DOS (Fig. S2(b) in the ESM) of the monolayer PP-VTe2, we find out that the total magnetic moments completely arise from the spin-polarized states ranging from −1 to −0.1 eV, which are almost donated by the V 3d electrons as indicated by the blue shadows. > ## 3 Conclusions
> 
> ## 3 结论
> 
> We have predicted an emergent ferromagnetic semiconductor of monolayer VTe2 with a 2D puckered pentagonal configuration that is dynamically stable. Distinct from the half-metallic BP-VTe2 and other conventional 2D semiconducting magnets, we show that PP-VTe2 is a narrow-gap semiconductor proposed for infrared optoelectronics. The FM ground state with unusual in-plane magnetic anisotropy and the estimated Curie temperature as 110 K of PP-VTe2 are demonstrated. The 2D multiferroic coupling that ferroelastic phase transitions will lead to the switching of the easy-magnetization axis in PP-VTe2 has also been explained by its exotic crystal structures. Moreover, we notice a feasible electrical control of magnetism in PP-VTe2 since its magnetic DOS completely arises from the spin-polarized states near the Fermi level. In a word, as a 2D semiconductor with a unique crystal structure and strong ferromagnetism, monolayer PP-VTe2 is an ideal 2D material for studying magnetic and ferroelastic properties, as well as tunable states by external strains and electric fields. 我们预测了一种具有动力学稳定性的二维褶皱五边形构型的单层VTe2新兴铁磁半导体。与半金属BP-VTe2和其他传统二维半导体磁体不同，我们证明PP-VTe2是一种面向红外光电子学的窄带隙半导体。展示了PP-VTe2的FM基态，具有不寻常的面内磁各向异性，以及估算的居里温度110 K。还通过其奇特的晶体结构解释了二维多铁耦合，即铁弹性相变将导致PP-VTe2中易磁化轴的翻转。此外，我们注意到PP-VTe2中磁学DOS完全来自费米能级附近的自旋极化态，可实现电控磁性。总之，作为一种具有独特晶体结构和强铁磁性的二维半导体，单层PP-VTe2是研究磁性和铁弹性性质以及通过外部应变和电场调控状态的理想二维材料。

> ## 4 Methods
> 
> ## 4 方法
> 
> The DFT calculations were implemented by Vienna ab initio simulation package (VASP) [48]. Ion cores and wave functions were described by the projector augmented wave (PAW) [49] method. All structural relaxations were conducted by using the generalized gradient approximation (GGA) proposed by Perdew, Burke, and Ernzerhof (PBE) [50], with a 9 × 9 × 1 k-point grid generated by Monkhost-Pack. High-precision geometry relaxation would stop only when the total energy difference was less than 10−8 eV and Hellmann–Feynman forces were less than 10−6 eV/Å, which is critical for annihilating the imaginary phonon modes. The plane-wave cutoff energy was set to be 500 eV. A vacuum space of 20 Å was adopted. The phonon dispersions were obtained by density functional perturbation theory (DFPT). The finite distortion method [51] was used when we calculated the linear elastic constants. Additionally, SOC was included in self-consistent calculations when calculating the MAE.
