> Abstract
>            
>              Molecular dynamics simulations are performed to explore the coalescence of the two hetero magic Ag
>              55
>              and Cu
>              55
>              clusters having icosahedral packing at room temperature, and the structural changes of the alloying Ag–Cu clusters at elevated temperatures. The simulation results reveal the influence of the contacting distances and relative orientations between the two clusters on the coalescing processes as well as the packing patterns of the coalesced binary clusters on heating. The activation energy is used to describe the energy required to initiate the coalescence. The Lode–Nadai parameter's distributions of the coalesced clusters provide more insight into the loading states on the atoms during different stages. The results show that the two clusters appear to form the neck involving twist and “slip” in the initial stage of the coalescence, and the Ag atoms present significant diffusion along the facets of the Cu cluster. As the temperature increases, different packing structures including Icosahedron, core/shell metastable, core@partial‐shell, and half Cu‐half Ag patterns occur.
>
>【摘要翻译】摘要用分子动力学模拟方法研究了两种异魔术银的结合55和Cu55室温下具有二十面体堆积的团簇，以及合金化Ag–Cu团簇在高温下的结构变化。模拟结果揭示了两团簇之间的接触距离和相对取向对聚结过程的影响，以及聚结二元团簇的堆积方式对加热的影响。活化能用于描述引发聚结所需的能量。凝聚团簇的Lode–Nadai参数分布提供了对原子在不同阶段的加载状态的更多了解。结果表明，两个团簇在聚并初期形成了扭曲和滑移的颈部，Ag原子沿团簇表面扩散。随着温度的升高，不同的堆积结构包括二十面体、核/壳亚稳结构、核/壳亚稳结构等，core@partial外壳和半Cu‐半Ag图案出现。
