> ABSTRACT 摘要
> 
> The atomic arrangements and electrical properties of SiGe nanomaterials are of significance when used in novel nanoelectronic devices and optoelectronic devices. Atomic arrangements, bond energies, and charge distribution of a Ge dimer adsorbed on Si(0 0 1) surfaces with different initial heights and inclination angles are investigated by using the DFTB algorithm. Eight different stable adsorption modes of the Ge dimer on the Si slab surface are demonstrated. The variations of the structural and electrical properties of the eight modes are elucidated. Different initial conditions caused stable adsorption of Ge dimer on the surface or caused two single Ge atoms from the Ge dimer to fragment. The Ge dimer, with different inclination angles, is adsorbed on the top site, bridge site, or hollow site of the Si dimer on the surface. The bond length of the Ge dimer is stretched, and the two Ge atoms lose charges. The further the Ge atom is from the surface, the more charges it loses. The different adsorption sites of the Ge dimer affect the properties of the Si dimer on the surface. The top adsorption site mode exerts a greater influence on the bond length and bond energy of the Si dimer, while the bridge adsorption site mode exerts a greater influence on the electron distribution of the Si dimer. The properties of the Ge monoatomic adsorption are akin to the case of a Ge dimer being adsorbed on the bridge site. The different adsorption sites and inclination angles cause the EGap values of the systems to differ, and the effect of the inclination angle on different sites also differs. The Ge dimer and Ge monoatom not only have similar effects on the properties of the Si dimer, but also exert the similar effects on EGap, suggesting that changes in the Si surface are the main contributors to the change in EGap. SiGe纳米材料的原子排列和电学性质在新型纳米电子器件和光电器件中具有重要意义。本文采用DFTB算法研究了不同初始高度和倾角下Ge二聚体吸附在Si(0 0 1)表面的原子排列、键能和电荷分布。展示了Ge二聚体在Si板表面上的八种不同稳定吸附模式，并阐明了八种模式的结构和电学性质变化。不同的初始条件导致Ge二聚体稳定吸附在表面，或导致Ge二聚体分裂成两个单个Ge原子。不同倾角的Ge二聚体吸附在表面Si二聚体的顶位、桥位或空位上。Ge二聚体的键长被拉伸，且两个Ge原子失去电荷。Ge原子离表面越远，失去的电荷越多。Ge二聚体的不同吸附位点影响表面Si二聚体的性质。顶位吸附模式对Si二聚体的键长和键能影响更大，而桥位吸附模式对Si二聚体的电子分布影响更大。Ge单原子吸附的性质类似于Ge二聚体吸附在桥位的情况。不同的吸附位点和倾角导致系统的EGap值不同，且倾角对不同位点的影响也不同。Ge二聚体和Ge单原子不仅对Si二聚体的性质具有相似影响，而且对EGap也有相似影响，表明Si表面的变化是EGap变化的主要贡献者。
> 
> **EGap**: 能隙，即最高占据分子轨道(HOMO)与最低未占分子轨道(LUMO)之间的能量差，是决定材料电子性质的关键参数。

> 1.  Introduction
> 2.  引言
> 
> SiGe nanomaterials have been studied for applications in novel nanoelectronic devices and optoelectronic devices due to their low cost and ease of fusion with silicon bases in the preparation process [1–6]. Recently, a new hexagonal SiGe alloy proposed by Fadaly, et al. has been used as a laser light source and is based on silicon [1]. The emission wavelength of the new materials can be continuously tuned in a broad range by controlling its composition. Additionally, controlling the Ge content of the SiGe micro or nano-films allows tuning of the conversion efficiency, absorption and luminescence spectral width of films used in ultrathin-film solar cells [7], integrated optical devices [8], thin-film transistors [3], etc. Cao et al. [7] designed ultrathin Si/Ge thin-film solar cells with a thickness of 2 mm having advantages including high efficiency, ultrathin absorber layers, low cost, and long-term stability. Monajjemi et al. [8] proposed that the nanocrystalline silicon–germanium films had low band gaps and high carrier mobility as compared with an Si thin film, therefore these materials can be used as optical materials in production of integrated optical devices. Torres et al. [3] investigated the structural, optical, and electrical properties of hydrogenated nanocrystalline silicon–germanium (nc-SiGe:H) thin films obtained by plasma enhanced chemical vapour deposition with thicknesses ranging between 30 and 100 nm. They proposed that the peak of the GeGe bonds increased with the deposition RF-power, showing the crystalline fraction in the films by observing the Raman spectra. For SiGe nano-film, as the thickness is less than 10 nm, the quantum confinement effect becomes more obvious, where the energy gap of the system changes significantly, and the Fermi energy level shifts. These will significantly affect the barrier of the metal–semiconductor contact. In device applications, changes in energy level play an important role in affecting occupancies of the carriers, transition between bands, electron density participating in conduction, and their mobility. Li et al. proposed that the ultrathin layers or monolayer Si/Ge films could improve the high responsivity of the vertical-illumination Si/Ge uni-travelling carrier (UTC) devices because of the nanoscale effect [9]. For ultrathin germanium adsorbed on Si nano-films, Krüger et al. found that the surface of a Si substrate exhibited metallic properties as the Ge atoms were adsorbed on special sites in the Si substrate [10]. Therefore, one of the key problems arising is how to control sedimentary position of Ge atoms on Si substrates in the epitaxial growth process. In the laboratory, the initial conditions pertaining to the Ge source, including position, height, etc., as well as reconstructed surface structures of the substrate affect the structures and properties of the Si/Ge films during heterogeneous epitaxial growth. When the film thickness is reduced to sub-nanometre size, the structure of Ge atoms adsorbed on the film surface is hardly observed experimentally. Thus, the theoretical research becomes particularly necessary for us to understand the properties of Si nano-film with Ge atoms absorbed thereon and it is meaningful for the experimental work to modulate the Si/Ge nano-film and optimise its performance further. In the present work, Density Functional Tight Binding (DFTB) simulations are carried out to study the effects of adsorption sites on the structure stability, bond energies, and charge distribution for the systems of the adsorbed Ge dimer on the Si(0 0 1) surface with asymmetric dimers present in a c(4 × 2) pattern. The influence of the Ge dimer adsorption on the energy gap of the systems is also studied. SiGe纳米材料因其低成本和在制备过程中易于与硅基底融合，在新型纳米电子器件和光电器件中的应用得到了广泛研究[1–6]。最近，Fadaly等人提出的一种新型六方SiGe合金已被用作基于硅的激光光源[1]。通过控制其组分，新材料的发射波长可在宽范围内连续调谐。此外，控制SiGe微米或纳米薄膜的Ge含量可以调节超薄薄膜太阳能电池[7]、集成光学器件[8]、薄膜晶体管[3]等应用中的转换效率、吸收和发光光谱宽度。Cao等人[7]设计了厚度为2 μm的超薄Si/Ge薄膜太阳能电池，具有高效率、超薄吸收层、低成本和长期稳定性等优点。Monajjemi等人[8]提出，与Si薄膜相比，纳米晶硅锗薄膜具有较低的带隙和较高的载流子迁移率，因此这些材料可用作集成光学器件生产中的光学材料。Torres等人[3]研究了通过等离子体增强化学气相沉积获得的厚度在30至100 nm之间的氢化纳米晶硅锗（nc-SiGe:H）薄膜的结构、光学和电学性质。他们提出，GeGe键的峰值随沉积RF功率增加而增强，通过观察拉曼光谱显示了薄膜中的晶相分数。对于SiGe纳米薄膜，当厚度小于10 nm时，量子限域效应变得更加明显，系统的能隙显著变化，费米能级发生移动。这些将显著影响金属-半导体接触的势垒。在器件应用中，能级的变化对载流子占据、带间跃迁、参与导电的电子密度及其迁移率起着重要作用。Li等人提出，由于纳米尺度效应，超薄层或单层Si/Ge薄膜可以提高垂直照射Si/Ge单行载流子（UTC）器件的高响应度[9]。对于吸附在Si纳米薄膜上的超薄锗，Krüger等人发现，当Ge原子吸附在Si衬底的特殊位点时，Si衬底表面表现出金属性质[10]。因此，一个关键问题是如何在外延生长过程中控制Ge原子在Si衬底上的沉积位置。在实验室中，Ge源的初始条件，包括位置、高度等，以及衬底的重构表面结构，会影响异质外延生长过程中Si/Ge薄膜的结构和性质。当薄膜厚度减小到亚纳米尺寸时，实验上难以观察到吸附在薄膜表面的Ge原子结构。因此，理论研究对于我们理解Ge原子吸附在Si纳米薄膜上的性质尤为必要，并对实验工作调制Si/Ge纳米薄膜和进一步优化其性能具有重要意义。在本工作中，采用密度泛函紧束缚（DFTB）模拟研究了吸附位点对具有c(4 × 2)图案的非对称二聚体的Si(0 0 1)表面上吸附Ge二聚体体系的结构稳定性、键能和电荷分布的影响。同时研究了Ge二聚体吸附对体系能隙的影响。
> 
> **c(4 × 2)**: 一种表面重构图案，表示表面晶胞相对于体相晶胞的周期为4倍和2倍。 **Fermi energy level**: 费米能级，电子占据的最高能级，决定材料的导电性质。 **Quantum confinement effect**: 量子限域效应，当材料尺寸减小到纳米尺度时，电子运动受限导致能级分立的效应。 **Epitaxial growth**: 外延生长，一种在单晶衬底上生长与其晶格匹配的单晶薄膜的技术。
> 
> 2.  Computational details
> 3.  计算细节
> 
> Within the DFTB [11–13] formalism, the total energy of the studied system consists of the orbital energy and the short-ranged pair potential between the atoms. The source code and Slater-Koster files including SiSi, Ge-Ge, Si-Ge, and Ge-Si are provided by Dr. Yi Dong and Prof. Michael Springborg of the University of Saarland in Germany. Springborg and co-workers proved this algorithm suitable for infinite, periodic, crystalline systems and found optimised lattice constants for crystalline Si and Ge of 5.46 Å and 5.71 Å, respectively: these values are within less than 1% of the experimental values [14–16]. In present calculations, the energy threshold value is 0.0002 eV. When the difference between the values of the present step and the last step is smaller than the threshold value, the calculations are stopped. Mülliken electronic populations were calculated from Linear Combination of Atomic Orbitals (LCAO) coefficients, which are based on Roothaan’s SCF LCAO-MO (Molecular Orbital) wave function method [17], showing that the results can be taken as measures of bonding and antibonding strengths [18,19]. > Fig. 4(a) and (b) show seven different configurations (B1–B7) appearing in area B. Among the seven configurations, three cases are found: the first one is that in configurations B1–B5 (Fig. 4(aI)–(aV)), the two Ge atoms are respectively bonded to different Si atoms and are adsorbed on the hollow sites on both sides above the Si dimer. The distance between the two Ge atoms is between 3.49 Å and 4.16 Å, and the BE between atoms is zero (Table 2), indicating that there is no interaction between the two atoms. Here, we called this adsorption mode Ge monoatomic hollow site adsorption, referred to as MHS. For the Si dimers in configurations B1–B3 (Fig. 4(bI)–(bIII)), the BLs are 2.3 Å. Compared to the initial structure, the BLs are shortened by 0.3 Å, and the BE values are increased by 2.883 eV–2.938 eV, indicating that this adsorption enhances the interaction between the two atoms of Si dimer while the buckling angles of Si dimers do not change. The Si1 atom lost charges, and the Si2 atom lost fewer charges (Fig. 4(cI)–(cIII)). For the Ge atoms in these configurations, the heights of Ge atoms from the surface are similar, and the lost charges from the two Ge atoms are nearly equal. In configurations B4–B5 (Fig. 4(bIV)–(bV)), the BL of the Si dimer is 2.63 Å, compared with the initial value, the BL is stretched by 0.03 Å, and the bond energy is reduced by 0.87 eV–1.061 eV, indicating that this adsorption weakens the interaction between the two atoms of the Si dimer. Its buckling angles and the surface change significantly, where Si1 atoms are upward atoms, Si2 atoms are downward atoms, and the buckling direction is reversed. It can be seen from Fig. 4(cIV)–(cV) that both Si1 and Si2 atoms obtain charges, and Si1 atoms have more charges than Si2 atoms, indicating that this adsorption mode affects the electron distribution on the Si surface. The second corresponds to configuration B6 (Fig. 4(aVI) and (bVI)), where the Ge dimer is adsorbed in a near-parallel format at the bridge site of Si dimer. Here, we called this adsorption mode Ge dimers parallel bridge site adsorption, referred to as DPBS. The third corresponds to configuration B7 (Fig. 4(aVII) and (bVII)), where the Ge dimer is adsorbed at a lower angle on the top site of Si2 atom. Here, we called this adsorption mode Ge dimer inclined top site adsorption, referred to as DITS. For these two modes, the buckling angles between the Ge dimer and Si surfaces are significantly different, and the angle of DITS is larger than that of DPBS. The BLs of the Ge dimer are 2.92 Å and 2.82 Å, respectively, and the difference between them is 0.10 Å; however, the corresponding BE values of them are quite different, and the latter is 2.04 eV more (Table 2), so the interaction between the two Ge atoms in the DITS adsorption is stronger. In the DPBS mode (B6), the Ge1 atom is far from the Si dimer compared with the Ge2 atom, and there is a relatively weak interaction between the Ge1 atom and the Si dimer. The BL of the Ge2-Si1 bond is close to that of the Ge2-Si2 bond (it is only 0.01 Å shorter), and its BE is 0.489 eV being larger than that of the Ge2Si2 bond, therefore the interaction of the Ge2-Si1 bond is stronger. As compared with B6, the BL is 0.22 Å, and the BE increases by 2.04 eV. > 4.  Conclusion
> 5.  结论
> 
> The adsorption behaviours of a Ge dimer on the c(4 × 2) reconstructed surface of an Si slab with different heights and inclination angles were studied by using the DFTB algorithm. The Ge dimer can be stably adsorbed on the Si slab surface with some specific heights and inclination angles. When the initial height exceeds 3.5 Å, the Ge dimer prefers to be vertically adsorbed on the slab surface. Different initial inclination angles cause the Ge dimer to be adsorbed on the top site and bridge site of the Si dimer. When the initial height is less than 3.5 Å, two situations arise: the first is that the Ge dimer is adsorbed parallel to, or inclined at an angle to, the slab surface. The Ge dimer with different initial angles appears on the top site, bridge site, or hollow site of the Si dimer. The other is that two single Ge atoms from the fragmentation of the Ge dimer are adsorbed on two hollow sites or one hollow site combined with a bridge site on the Si dimer. The structural and electrical properties of the adsorbed Ge dimer change to significant extents: the bond length of the Ge dimer is stretched, and the two Ge atoms lose charges. The further the Ge atom is from the surface, the more charges it loses. The different adsorption sites of the Ge dimer thus greatly affect the properties of the Si dimer in the surface. For the top site, the bond length of the Si dimer decreases significantly, and the bond energy increases greatly, whereas the electronic population distribution is less affected. For the bridge site, the bond length of the Si dimer becomes slightly longer, and the bond energy is slightly reduced, but the population distribution changes greatly. The Si atom changes from losing charge to gaining it. For the hollow sites accompanied by the broken bond of the Si dimer, both Ge atoms lose fewer charges. For Ge monoatomic adsorption, the different adsorbing sites of the two Ge atoms form bonds with different Si atoms, which affect the bond length, bond energy, and electron distribution of the Si dimer. These properties are akin to the case of the Ge dimer adsorbing an Si atom on a bridge site. The different adsorption sites and inclination angles cause the EGap values of the systems to differ, and the effect of the inclination angle on different sites is also different. The EGap value can be adjusted by changing the inclination angle. The biggest adjustment occurs in the top site adsorption mode. The angle effects of top site and bridge site adsorption present opposite pictures: the EGap values of Ge monoatomic adsorption are always lower than those of Ge dimer adsorptions. When the Ge dimer and Ge monoatom have similar effects on the properties of the Si dimer, the effects on the EGap value are also similar, so the changes in the Si surface make the principal contribution to the change in EGap. 采用DFTB算法研究了不同高度和倾角下Ge二聚体在Si板c(4 × 2)重构表面上的吸附行为。Ge二聚体可以在某些特定的高度和倾角下稳定吸附在Si板表面。当初始高度超过3.5 Å时，Ge二聚体倾向于垂直吸附在板表面。不同的初始倾角导致Ge二聚体吸附在Si二聚体的顶位和桥位。当初始高度小于3.5 Å时，出现两种情况：第一种是Ge二聚体平行于板表面或与板表面成一定角度吸附。不同初始角度的Ge二聚体出现在Si二聚体的顶位、桥位或空位上。另一种是Ge二聚体分裂成的两个单个Ge原子吸附在两个空位或一个空位与一个桥位联合的Si二聚体上。吸附的Ge二聚体的结构和电学性质发生显著变化：Ge二聚体的键长被拉伸，两个Ge原子失去电荷。Ge原子离表面越远，失去的电荷越多。因此，Ge二聚体的不同吸附位点极大地影响了表面中Si二聚体的性质。对于顶位，Si二聚体的键长显著减小，键能大幅增加，而电子布居分布受影响较小。对于桥位，Si二聚体的键长略微变长，键能略微降低，但布居分布变化很大。Si原子从失去电荷变为获得电荷。对于伴随Si二聚体键断裂的空位，两个Ge原子失去的电荷较少。对于Ge单原子吸附，两个Ge原子的不同吸附位点与不同的Si原子形成键，这影响了Si二聚体的键长、键能和电子分布。这些性质类似于Ge二聚体在桥位吸附Si原子的情况。不同的吸附位点和倾角导致体系的EGap值不同，且倾角对不同位点的影响也不同。可以通过改变倾角来调节EGap值。最大的调节发生在顶位吸附模式中。顶位和桥位吸附的角度效应呈现相反的图像：Ge单原子吸附的EGap值始终低于Ge二聚体吸附的EGap值。当Ge二聚体和Ge单原子对Si二聚体的性质具有相似影响时，对EGap值的影响也相似，因此Si表面的变化是EGap变化的主要贡献。
