> ## 一、引言
> 
> 这篇论文讨论的是一个材料物理与器件工程中的经典问题：**铁电性（ferroelectricity）是否会在材料尺寸缩小到足够薄时消失？**
> 
> ### 1. 研究背景
> 
> **铁电材料（ferroelectric materials）**是一类具有**自发极化（spontaneous polarization）**、且这种极化可以被外电场翻转的材料。它们在**非易失存储器（FERAM）**、场效应器件、传感器和执行器中都很重要。随着微电子器件不断缩小，铁电薄膜也必须做得越来越薄，于是一个关键问题出现了：
> 
> > 铁电薄膜是否存在一个“**临界厚度（critical thickness）**”，低于这个厚度，铁电性就不再稳定？
> 
> 在这篇论文之前，实验已经在约 **40 Å（约10个晶胞）** 厚度的钙钛矿薄膜中观测到铁电性；一些理论工作甚至暗示，在理想条件下铁电性可以一直保持到极低厚度，似乎**没有明确的临界尺寸**。
> 
> ### 2. 论文要解决的核心问题
> 
> 作者指出，先前很多理论工作有一个共同局限：  
> 它们通常假设内部电场为零，或者没有真实处理**金属电极—铁电体界面（metal–ferroelectric interface）**，尤其忽略了：
> 
> -   电极的**有限屏蔽长度（finite screening length）**
> -   界面化学结构
> -   衬底应变条件
> 
> 而真实器件中，这些因素恰恰决定超薄铁电膜能否保持极化。
> 
> ### 3. 论文核心贡献
> 
> 作者通过**第一性原理计算（first-principles calculations）**研究一个更真实的结构：  
> **BaTiO3 薄膜夹在两个金属 SrRuO3 电极之间，并处于短路条件下。**
> 
> 他们发现：
> 
> -   该体系**确实存在临界厚度**
> -   当 BaTiO3 厚度**低于约 6 个晶胞（约24–26 Å）**时，铁电性消失
> -   原因不是简单的“尺寸太小”，而是**界面偶极导致的退极化电场（depolarizing field）**压制了铁电不稳定性
> 
> 这篇论文的重要性在于，它把“是否有临界厚度”这个问题，从理想模型推进到了**真实器件界面条件**下的定量回答。
> 
> * * *
> 
> ## 二、论文结构与逻辑概览
> 
> 这篇论文的逻辑非常清晰，可以概括为一条四步论证链：
> 
> ### 1. 提出问题
> 
> 已有实验和部分理论暗示超薄铁电膜仍可能保持铁电性，但这些结论多建立在理想边界条件上，不足以代表真实器件。
> 
> ### 2. 建立真实模型
> 
> 作者构建了一个典型铁电电容器模型：
> 
> -   铁电层：**BaTiO3**
> -   电极：**SrRuO3**
> -   衬底约束：等效采用 **SrTiO3** 的面内晶格常数
> -   电边界条件：电极**短路**
> 
> ### 3. 用第一性原理计算验证是否存在铁电不稳定性
> 
> 作者分别计算不同厚度下，沿软模位移方向施加畸变时体系能量如何变化。  
> 结果表明：
> 
> -   厚膜：能量呈双阱结构，铁电态稳定
> -   薄膜：能量在零位移处最小，顺电态稳定
> 
> 这意味着存在厚度阈值。
> 
> ### 4. 用电静力学模型解释机制
> 
> 作者进一步证明，铁电消失的根本原因是：
> 
> -   薄膜极化会在界面产生表面束缚电荷
> -   金属电极虽然会屏蔽，但由于屏蔽长度有限，不能完全补偿
> -   因此薄膜内部产生**退极化场**
> -   该电场抬高极化态能量，尤其在薄膜中更明显
> -   最终在足够薄时彻底压制铁电相
> 
> ### 可视化建议
> 
> _可画成“问题—模型—计算结果—物理机制解释”的四段流程图。_

> 
> -   **n = 5**：电极厚度固定，足够厚以避免界面相互影响

> 在铁电极化形成后：
> 
> -   铁电层两端出现极化束缚电荷
> -   金属电极中的自由电子发生重排进行屏蔽
> -   但这种屏蔽不是“无限快、无限强、零长度”的理想行为

>

> ## 四、正文核心内容剖析
> 
> * * *
> 
> ### ### 4.1 研究对象与材料选择
> 
> 作者选择 **BaTiO3（钛酸钡）** 作为铁电层，因为它是最典型的钙钛矿铁电体之一。  
> 电极选择 **SrRuO3（钌酸锶）**，原因包括：
> 
> -   金属性良好
> -   与钙钛矿氧化物外延匹配好
> -   在实验上是常见的铁电电容器电极材料
> 
> 这个材料组合使得研究结果具有较高现实相关性。
> 
> * * *
> 
> ### ### 4.2 计算方法
> 
> 作者采用的是：
> 
> -   **密度泛函理论（DFT, Density Functional Theory）**
> -   **局域密度近似（LDA, Local Density Approximation）**
> -   **SIESTA** 数值原子轨道方法实现
> 
> #### 边界条件设置
> 
> 1.  **周期性超胞**
> 2.  **短路条件**由周期性边界自然实现
> 3.  **衬底效应**通过固定面内晶格常数为 SrTiO3 的体晶格常数来模拟
> 
> 这意味着作者没有显式放入厚的 SrTiO3 衬底，而是用“约束应变”的方式间接纳入其影响。
> 
> * * *
> 
> ### ### 4.3 应变对 BaTiO3 的影响
> 
> 在体材料层面，BaTiO3 在面内受到约 **2% 压缩应变**。  
> 结果是：
> 
> -   面内铁电不稳定性被抑制
> -   垂直方向极化增强
> -   计算得到的自发极化从 **24 μC/cm²** 增加到 **31 μC/cm²**
> 
> 这一步很重要，因为它说明：  
> 如果没有界面退极化场，从“晶格/应变”角度看，薄膜其实更倾向于沿法向保持铁电性。
> 
> 换句话说，后续铁电性的消失并不是因为应变把它毁掉了，而是因为**电边界条件**带来了额外惩罚。
> 
> * * *
> 
> ### ### 4.4 如何判断是否有铁电性？
> 
> 作者采用两步策略：
> 
> #### 第一步：构造顺电参考态
> 
> 在中央 TiO2 层强加镜面对称，使体系不能自发出现极化，然后完全弛豫得到顺电构型。
> 
> #### 第二步：沿体相软模方向逐步施加位移
> 
> 所谓**软模（soft mode）**，是指与铁电结构转变相关、能量代价很低甚至导致不稳定的晶格振动模式。  
> 作者把 BaTiO3 原子沿软模方向位移，参数化为 y，计算总能量变化。
> 
> 如果能量随小位移降低，说明铁电畸变稳定；  
> 如果能量随位移升高，说明顺电态更稳定。
> 
> * * *
> 
> ### ### 4.5 核心结果：存在临界厚度
> 
> 通过图2可得：
> 
> -   **m < 6**：铁电性消失
> -   **m ≈ 6**：处于临界附近
> -   **m > 6**：铁电性恢复
> 
> 这里的临界厚度约为 **6 个晶胞**，相当于 **24–26 Å**。
> 
> 作者还做了一个额外验证：  
> 对 **m = 2** 的体系，从一个铁电构型出发进行完全原子弛豫，最终原子又回到顺电位置。  
> 这说明不是“你只检查了某条位移路径所以错过真正铁电态”，而是真的没有稳定铁电解。
> 
> * * *
> 
> ### ### 4.6 物理机制：退极化场为何致命？
> 
> 这是本文最重要的思想。
> 
> #### 极化产生界面束缚电荷
> 
> 当铁电层出现极化 (P) 时，在界面会出现表面极化电荷：
> 
> [ \sigma_{pol} = P \cdot \hat{n} ]
> 
> 其中 (\hat{n}) 是界面法向单位矢量。
> 
> #### 金属电极并非完美屏蔽
> 
> 理想导体模型常假定电极可瞬时、完全、零长度地抵消这些电荷。  
> 但真实金属有**有限屏蔽长度**，因此补偿电荷会分布在一段有限距离内，而不是压缩在几何界面上一条面上。
>

> # Critical thickness for ferroelectricity in perovskite ultrathin films
> 
> # 钙钛矿超薄膜中铁电性的临界厚度
> 
> **Ferroelectricity**: 铁电性，指某些电介质材料具有自发极化，且自发极化方向可随外加电场方向改变的性质。 **Perovskite**: 钙钛矿，一种具有特定晶体结构的材料，化学式通式为ABO₃。
> 
> Javier Junquera & Philippe Ghosez Javier Junquera 和 Philippe Ghosez
> 
> Département de Physique, Université de Liège, Bâtiment B-5, B-4000 Sart-Tilman, Belgium 比利时列日大学物理系，B-5楼，B-4000 萨尔蒂尔曼，比利时

> The integration of ferroelectric oxide films into microelectronic devices1,2, combined with the size reduction constraints imposed by the semiconductor industry, have revived interest in the old question concerning the possible existence of a critical thickness for ferroelectricity. Current experimental techniques have allowed the detection of ferroelectricity in perovskite films down to a thickness of 40 Å (ten unit cells), ref. 3. Recent atomistic simulations4,5 have confirmed the possibility of retaining the ferroelectric ground state at ultralow thicknesses, and suggest the absence of a critical size. Here we report first-principles calculations on a realistic ferroelectric–electrode interface. We show that, contrary to current thought, BaTiO3 thin films between two metallic SrRuO3 electrodes in short circuit lose their ferroelectric properties below a critical thickness of about six unit cells (∼24 Å). A depolarizing electrostatic field, caused by dipoles at the ferroelectric–metal interfaces, is the reason for the disappearance of the ferroelectric instability. Our results suggest the existence of a lower limit for the thickness of useful ferroelectric layers in electronic devices. 将铁电氧化物薄膜集成到微电子器件中1,2，加上半导体行业对尺寸缩小的限制要求，重新激发了人们对一个老问题的兴趣，即是否存在铁电性的临界厚度。当前的实验技术已经能够在厚度低至40 Å（十个晶胞）的钙钛矿薄膜中检测到铁电性3。最近的原子级模拟4,5证实了在超低厚度下保持铁电基态的可能性，并暗示不存在临界尺寸。在这里，我们报告了对一个现实的铁电体-电极界面的第一性原理计算。我们表明，与当前观点相反，处于短路状态的两个金属SrRuO3电极之间的BaTiO3薄膜，在约六个晶胞（∼24 Å）的临界厚度以下会失去其铁电性质。由铁电体-金属界面处的偶极子引起的去极化静电场是铁电不稳定性消失的原因。我们的结果表明，电子器件中有用的铁电层存在一个厚度下限。 **First-principles calculations**: 第一性原理计算，指基于量子力学基本原理，不依赖经验参数，从头计算材料性质的方法。 **Depolarizing electrostatic field**: 去极化静电场，指与铁电体内部极化方向相反的电场，会削弱或消除自发极化。 **Ferroelectric instability**: 铁电不稳定性，指材料结构倾向于发生离子位移，从而产生自发极化的内在特性。

> Epitaxial growth of high-quality thin films of ABO3 perovskite oxides on semiconductors—Si (ref. 6) and Ge (ref. 1)—and metallic7 substrates has opened the door to the use of alternative materials in metal-oxide-semiconductor field-effect transistors (MOSFETs)1,8 and non-volatile ferroelectric random access memories (FERAMs)9,10. Recently, 30 Gbit cm−2 data storage densities have been demonstrated for Pb(Zr0.2Ti0.8)O3 films on a metallic oxide electrode2. As the size of high-quality perovskite layers of interest for technological applications decreases, the fundamental questions of the thickness dependence of the ferroelectric properties, and of their possible disappearance at a finite critical thickness, become crucial. It was thought that ferroelectricity was suppressed in small particles and thin films11, but this situation changed after experiments that identified ferroelectric ground states in 40-Å-thick perovskite oxide films3 and in 10-Å-thick crystalline copolymer films12. In support of these experimental observations, theoretical investigations using a phenomenological approach13, first-principles effective hamiltonian calculations4 and full first-principles simulations5 predicted ferroelectric ground states for various ABO3 slabs under the condition of vanishing internal electric field. However, in all these approaches, the influence of a real metal–perovskite interface (including the finite screening length of the electrode, the interface chemistry and the strain conditions imposed by the substrate) on both the atomic relaxation and polarization was missing—or was only empirically included through the choice of specific electromechanical boundary conditions. The only more-fundamental study of metal–BaTiO3 interfaces is due to Rao et al.14, but does not address the question of the ferroelectric instability. Here we simulate from first principles the behaviour of a typical ferroelectric capacitor structure epitaxially grown on a thick SrTiO3 substrate, and made of an ultrathin film of BaTiO3 (a prototypical ferroelectric oxide) between two metallic SrRuO3 electrodes15 (Fig. 1). The electrical boundary conditions are fixed by putting the electrodes in short-circuit. Calculations were done within density functional theory and the local density approximation. 在半导体——Si6和Ge1——以及金属7衬底上外延生长高质量ABO3钙钛矿氧化物薄膜，为在金属-氧化物-半导体场效应晶体管（MOSFETs）1,8和非易失性铁电随机存取存储器（FERAMs）9,10中使用替代材料打开了大门。最近，在金属氧化物电极上的Pb(Zr0.2Ti0.8)O3薄膜已展示了30 Gbit cm−2的数据存储密度2。随着技术应用所关注的高质量钙钛矿层尺寸的减小，关于铁电性质的厚度依赖性及其在有限临界厚度下可能消失的基本问题变得至关重要。人们曾认为铁电性在小颗粒和薄膜中会受到抑制11，但在实验中发现40-Å厚的钙钛矿氧化物薄膜3和10-Å厚的结晶共聚物薄膜12中存在铁电基态后，这种情况发生了改变。为了支持这些实验观察，使用唯象方法13、第一性原理有效哈密顿量计算4和完整的第一性原理模拟5进行的理论研究，预测了在内部电场为零的条件下，各种ABO₃平板的铁电基态。然而，在所有这些方法中，都缺少真实的金属-钙钛矿界面（包括电极的有限屏蔽长度、界面化学以及衬底施加的应变条件）对原子弛豫和极化的影响——或者只是通过选择特定的机电边界条件经验性地包含在内。唯一对金属-BaTiO₃界面进行更基础研究的是Rao等人14，但并未涉及铁电不稳定性问题。在这里，我们从第一性原理出发，模拟了一个典型铁电电容器结构的行为，该结构外延生长在厚的SrTiO₃衬底上，由两个金属SrRuO₃电极之间的BaTiO₃（一种原型铁电氧化物）超薄膜构成15（图1）。通过将电极短路来固定电学边界条件。计算是在密度泛函理论和局域密度近似内完成的。 **Epitaxial growth**: 外延生长，指在单晶衬底上，沿衬底晶向生长一层具有特定晶格取向的单晶薄膜的技术。 **Screening length**: 屏蔽长度，指金属中电荷扰动能够被屏蔽的特征距离。 **Density functional theory**: 密度泛函理论，一种研究多电子体系电子结构的量子力学方法。

> We used a numerical atomic orbital method, as implemented in the SIESTA code16. SrRuO3 is an excellent electrode material for ferroelectric device structures17, because of the high crystalline quality and coherent interfaces obtained in heteroepitaxial growth with perovskite oxides18,19. A SrO/TiO2 interface with BaTiO3 is expected experimentally (owing to the volatility of Ru) and assumed in our calculations. The simulations were carried out using a supercell technique. The basic unit, periodically repeated in space, is illustrated in Fig. 1 and corresponds to the generic formula [SrO-(RuO2-SrO)n/TiO2-(BaO-TiO2)m], where n was fixed to five (a thickness large enough to avoid interaction between the two interfaces) and m ranged from two to ten. The periodic boundary conditions naturally impose the required short-circuit condition between the electrodes. The SrTiO3 substrate was not explicitly treated, but was implicitly included by constraining the in-plane lattice constant of the whole structure to the bulk cell parameter of SrTiO3 (ref. 20). Strain relaxations of bulk SrRuO3 and BaTiO3 were done under this constraint, and the resulting tetragonal unit cells were used as the building blocks for the supercell. At the bulk level, a compressive strain identical to that imposed by the substrate (∼2%) is sufficient to suppress the in-plane ferroelectric instability of BaTiO3, while the concomitant relaxation in the perpendicular direction amplifies21 the computed spontaneous polarization from 24 to 31 μC cm−2. The different heterostructures were first relaxed imposing a mirror symmetry plane on the central TiO2 layer. The relaxation was continued until the maximum force on the atoms was smaller than 40 meV Å−1. The symmetry constraint prevents the appearance of ferroelectricity, and the resulting structures correspond to the optimized paraelectric states. Starting from the previous relaxed configurations taken as reference, we then searched for the existence of a ferroelectric instability, moving BaTiO3 atoms continuously following the displacement pattern of the bulk tetragonal soft mode22, y, determined for the same tetragonal cell geometry as in the supercell. Only the monodomain case was considered, corresponding to a situation that can be achieved experimentally9. In Fig. 2, the symbols show the evolution of the energy (with respect to the reference paraelectric phase) when freezing-in atomic displacements y of increasing amplitudes. Calculations have been done for different thicknesses of the BaTiO3 layer. Figure 2 points out a change of behaviour at a critical thickness around m = 6 (∼26 Å). For larger thicknesses, the energy decreases when the atoms follow the bulk soft-mode path, clearly demonstrating that the thin film has a ferroelectric ground state (in agreement with experimental results for the same thickness3). In contrast, below the critical value, the energy is minimized for the paraelectric configuration. Only a small region of the whole configuration space is analysed in Fig. 2, so this observation alone is not sufficient to make conclusions about the absence of ferroelectricity. However, as will become clear below, the mechanism responsible for the increase of energy when freezing-in the displacements depends only on the induced polarization and not on the details of the atomic displacement pattern. > The polarization, P, generated in the ferroelectric thin film by a polar pattern of atomic displacements, leads to surface charges, σpol = P·nˆ (where nˆ is a unitary vector normal to the interface that points out of the ferroelectric film) of opposite sign at the interfaces with the electrodes. Electrons in the metal tend to screen these charges, and consequently two dipoles of the same sign arise at the two metal–ferroelectric interfaces of the sample. All these features are illustrated in Fig. 3a, where the difference in the macroscopic23 total charge density distribution (ionic plus electronic) between the ferroelectric and the paraelectric phases is plotted. The induced deformation of the charge density in the metal has a complex structure, and decays exponentially in the metallic region beyond the first RuO2 layer. It spreads over a characteristic distance that is related to the screening length of the electrode. Each dipole is responsible for electrostatic potential drops in the same direction, so that the only way to preserve short-circuit boundary conditions along the structure will be the appearance of a depolarizing electric field inside the ferroelectric film, illustrated in Fig. 3b for different values of the induced polarization. The amplitude of this field, Ed, depends on (1) the screening length of the metal and the polarization of the thin film, which both monitor the potential drop ΔV across the interface, and (2) the film thickness l (Ed = 2ΔV/l). The effect of the depolarizing field on the energy of the system can be extrapolated from the following model. In the presence of the field Ed, the energy E of the thin film can be expressed as24 E = U − P·Ed; where U is the internal energy under zero field. Assuming that the modification of the bonding at the interface only plays a minor role, the evolution of U can be approximated from the bulk soft-mode double-well energy22. To calculate P, contributions from the atomic displacements (yia, where i is an atomic index and a is a cartesian direction) and from the electronic polarization in the presence of the field are taken into account (Pb = (1/Ω)Σi,a Z_ab,i(T) yia + χ∞Edb, where Ω is the volume of the ferroelectric thin film). Again, neglecting deviations in the surface region, bulk values can be assumed for the different quantities. The transverse effective charges Z_ab,i(T) were calculated with the Berry phase approach25, whereas the electronic susceptibility χ∞ was obtained from a supercell technique26. Finally, the amplitude of the field Ed can be deduced from the slope of the macroscopic average of the electrostatic potential in the ferroelectric (Fig. 3b). With this simplified model (lines in Fig. 2), all the first-principles results are reproduced. > In the case of zero internal field (assuming perfect screening4 or compensating for the depolarizing field5), E reduces to U and the behaviour of the film is similar to that of the bulk. However, in the case of a real metal–ferroelectric interface, perfect screening is not achieved under short-circuit boundary conditions, resulting in a sizeable depolarizing field. The existence of such a field was previously suggested by Mehta et al.27, and was recently invoked to explain experimental changes of the coercive fields with thickness28. Here, we quantify the effect of the depolarizing field for a realistic system and demonstrate its crucial role. First, it is responsible for the suppression of ferroelectricity at a finite size. Second, even well above the critical thickness, the depolarizing field reduces the amplitude of the spontaneous polarization, which only slowly approaches the bulk value for films of increasing size (Fig. 2 inset). The results reported here have been obtained for a defect-free perfectly insulating BaTiO3 crystal, whereas typical ferroelectric capacitors are well-known to exhibit high leakage currents (mainly monitored by the Poole–Frenkel mechanism in the case of metal oxide electrodes29). It might therefore be questioned whether charge redistribution associated with the finite conductivity of the ferroelectric could further screen the depolarizing field and affect our conclusions. For usual current densities30 (J < 10−5 A cm−2, at 300 K and for fields of the order of those of Fig. 3b), screening of the uncompensated surface polarization charges (σpol < 10−6 C cm−2) would require a timescale of the order of 0.1 s. Compared to the switching of the polarization (typically ∼10−9 s), this is a very slow process. We can therefore conclude that this process cannot prevent the film switching back to the paraelectric state below the critical thickness. For m larger than the critical value and after a sufficiently long time, the finite conductivity could allow the appearance of additional screening charges. However, as it happens for charges coming from the electrodes, the finite spread of this extra charge distribution will probably provide an incomplete screening. A non-vanishing depolarizing field is therefore still expected, and might have marked effects on fundamental properties important for technological applications, such as the coercive fields28 (and hence the switching voltage) or fatigue of the samples. We believe that our results will stimulate further work on growing ultrathin perovskite films, and on measuring the thickness dependence of the polarization within such films. > Recent ice core studies have raised the disturbing possibility that glacial–interglacial climate changes may be non-uniform across Antarctica1,2. These findings have been confined to records from the Ross Sea sector of the continent, but significant deviations in other areas would call into question the widely assumed validity of the climate record obtained from Vostok, East Antarctica, on large spatial scales3. Here we present an isotopic profile from a core drilled at Dome Fuji4,5, situated 1,500 km from Vostok in a different sector of East Antarctica. The two records show remarkable similarities over the past three glacial cycles (the extent of the Dome Fuji record) in both large-amplitude changes, such as terminations, interglacials and interstadials and more subtle glacial events, even when the origin of precipitation is accounted for. Our results indicate that Antarctic climate is essentially homogeneous at the scale of the East Antarctic Plateau, possibly as a consequence of the symmetry of the plateau and the adjacent ocean. 最近的冰芯研究提出了一个令人不安的可能性，即冰期-间冰期的气候变化在整个南极洲可能是不一致的1,2。这些发现仅限于该大陆罗斯海区域的记录，但其他区域的显著偏差将质疑目前广泛认为的来自东南极洲Vostok站的气候记录在大空间尺度上的有效性3。在这里，我们展示了在Dome Fuji（富士穹顶）4,5钻取的冰芯同位素剖面，该地点位于东南极洲不同区域，距离Vostok站1,500公里。这两份记录在过去三个冰期旋回中（Dome Fuji记录的长度）显示出显著的相似性，无论是在大振幅变化（例如终止期、间冰期和间冰阶）还是在更细微的冰期事件中，即使考虑了降水来源也是如此。我们的结果表明，南极洲的气候在东南极高原的尺度上本质上是均一的，这可能是该高原及其毗邻海洋的对称性导致的结果。 **Isotopic profile**: 同位素剖面，指沿冰芯深度或时间序列测量的氧或氢同位素比率（如δ¹⁸O, δD）的变化曲线，常用于重建古温度。 **Terminations**: 终止期，指冰期旋回中从冰期到间冰期的快速过渡阶段。 **Interstadials**: 间冰阶，指冰期内相对温暖的短暂气候阶段。

> Climate records extending at least back to the Last Glacial Maximum, 20 kyr BP (thousands of years before present), have now been recovered from deep ice cores drilled at seven different Antarctic sites (Fig. 1). The inland East Antarctic records (Dome C, Dome B and Vostok, with two published series at Dome C and Vostok) are very similar where they overlap, with a deglaciation history characterized by a long gradual warming interrupted by the Antarctic Cold Reversal, clearly preceding the North Atlantic Younger Dryas6. These characteristics are shared by the records from Byrd7 (inland West Antarctica) and Law Dome (coastal East Antarctica)8. Although the exact timing of the Antarctic Cold Reversal is still debated8, all these records show a deglacial pattern very different from that registered in Greenland cores. In contrast, the records from Taylor Dome1 and Siple Dome2, two near-coastal sites in the Ross Sea sector (Fig. 1), show Greenland-like features with indication of either climate changes synchronous with the North Atlantic1 or of very rapid changes typical of the Greenland record2. Here we present a climate isotopic record from a core successfully recovered by Japanese drillers in 1995 and 1996 at Dome Fuji in East Antarctica4, which, in this context, is important for at least two reasons. First, Dome Fuji and other inland East Antarctic sites are located in completely different sectors of East Antarctica (Fig. 1). Second, the Dome Fuji core extends back to about 330 kyr BP (ref. 9). It provides the second-longest climate time series after the Vostok cores, allowing a detailed comparison over the last three glacial–interglacial cycles, including three successive deglaciations (Fig. 2). Ongoing Dome Fuji studies provide a wealth of new and detailed information in various fields (climate, atmospheric chemistry, atmospheric composition, ice properties and ice dynamics). We focus on the continuous 18O profile in ice (δ18O) and on its comparison with the Vostok deuterium record3 (δD). We also shortly discuss implications of the two deuterium-excess profiles (d = δD − 8 × δ18O) in terms of local temperature interpretation. 至少可追溯到末次冰盛期（20 kyr BP，距今千年）的气候记录，现已从南极洲七个不同地点钻取的深冰芯中获得（图1）。内陆东南极记录（Dome C, Dome B 和 Vostok，其中Dome C和Vostok各有两个已发表的系列）在重叠部分非常相似，其冰消期历史特征是长期逐渐变暖，并被南极冷反转所中断，明显早于北大西洋的新仙女木期6。这些特征与Byrd7（西南极内陆）和Law Dome（东南极海岸）8的记录相同。尽管南极冷反转的确切时间仍有争议8，所有这些记录都显示出与格陵兰冰芯记录的冰消期模式非常不同。相比之下，来自Taylor Dome1和Siple Dome2的记录——这两个位于罗斯海扇区的近海岸地点（图1）——显示出类似格陵兰的特征，表明其气候变化要么与北大西洋同步1，要么具有格陵兰记录中典型的非常快速的变化2。在此，我们展示了一个由日本钻探人员于1995年和1996年在东南极洲Dome Fuji成功获取的冰芯的气候同位素记录4，在此背景下，这一记录的重要性至少有两个原因。首先，Dome Fuji和其他内陆东南极地点位于东南极洲截然不同的区域（图1）。其次，Dome Fuji冰芯可追溯到约330 kyr BP（参考文献9）。它提供了继Vostok冰芯之后第二长的时间序列，允许对过去三个冰期-间冰期旋回，包括三个连续的冰消期，进行详细比较（图2）。正在进行的Dome Fuji研究在多个领域（气候、大气化学、大气成分、冰的性质和冰的动力学）提供了丰富的新详细信息。我们重点关注冰中连续的18O剖面（δ18O）及其与Vostok氘记录3（δD）的比较。我们还简要讨论了两个氘盈余剖面（d = δD − 8 × δ18O）在局地温度解释方面的意义。 **Last Glacial Maximum**: 末次冰盛期，指最近一次冰期中，冰盖范围达到最大、全球气温最低的时期，距今约2万年。 **Antarctic Cold Reversal**: 南极冷反转，指南极洲在末次冰消期变暖过程中的一个短暂降温时期。 **Younger Dryas**: 新仙女木期，指末次冰消期向全新世过渡期间，北半球发生的一次急剧降温事件。 **δ18O**: δ18O，氧-18同位素比率，是氧的同位素组成的一种度量，通常用于冰芯研究中作为温度的替代指标。 **δD**: δD，氘/氢同位素比率，是氢的同位素组成的一种度量，同样用于古气候重建。 **Deuterium-excess**: 氘盈余，定义为d = δD − 8 × δ18O，反映水汽来源区的物理条件（如温度、湿度），而不是凝结地点。
