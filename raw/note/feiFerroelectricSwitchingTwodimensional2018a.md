> [!quote]- <center>Abstract</center>

> A ferroelectric is a material with a polar structure whose polarity can be reversed (switched) by applying an electric field1,2. In metals, itinerant electrons screen electrostatic forces between ions, which explains in part why polar metals are very rare3–7. Screening also excludes external electric fields, apparently ruling out the possibility of ferroelectric switching. However, in principle, a thin enough polar metal could be sufficiently penetrated by an electric field to have its polarity switched. Here we show that the topological semimetal WTe2 provides an embodiment of this principle. Although monolayer WTe2 is centro-symmetric and thus non-polar, the stacked bulk structure is polar. We find that two- or three-layer WTe2 exhibits spontaneous out-of-plane electric polarization that can be switched using gate electrodes. We directly detect and quantify the polarization using graphene as an electric-field sensor8. Moreover, the polarization states can be differentiated by conductivity and the carrier density can be varied to modify the properties. The temperature at which polarization vanishes is above 350 kelvin, and even when WTe2 is sandwiched between graphene layers it retains its switching capability at room temperature, demonstrating a robustness suitable for applications in combination with other two-dimensional materials9–12. Two- and three-layer WTe2 exhibits spontaneous out-of-plane electric polarization that can be switched electrically at room temperature and is sufficiently robust for use in applications with other two-dimensional materials.
>

> ## 一、引言
> 
> 提到“铁电性”，我们通常会想到一些绝缘体材料，比如广泛用于存储器的 **钛酸钡（BaTiO₃）**。这类材料内部具有自发的电极化，并且这个极化方向可以被外加电场翻转。一个至关重要的问题是：金属，这种内部充满自由电子的材料，能否也是铁电体？
> 
> 直觉上，答案是否定的。因为金属中的自由电子会像“静电场屏蔽罩”一样，抵消掉离子之间的静电力，不仅使内部难以形成稳定的电极化，也让外部电场难以穿透，从而无法翻转极化。然而，1965年，Anderson和Blount提出了“**铁电金属（Ferroelectric metal）**”的理论猜想，认为某些金属冷却时可能出现极性结构，但他们也认为这种极性不可翻转。
> 
> 本论文正是针对这一长达半个世纪的难题，首次在实验上实现了二维金属的铁电性翻转。作者团队发现，层状拓扑半金属 **二碲化钨（WTe₂）** 在减薄至两层或三层时，会表现出一种自发的、可被电场翻转的面外电极化。这项研究不仅打破了“铁电性与金属性不兼容”的传统认知，也为新一代多功能电子器件提供了全新的材料平台。
> 
> ## 二、论文结构与逻辑概览
> 
> 这篇论文的论证逻辑严密，形成了一个完整的闭环，我们可以将其核心框架概括为：
> 
> 1.  **提出问题与假设**：金属中的自由电子会屏蔽外部电场，导致铁电翻转看似不可能。但一个足够薄的极性金属，理论上可能被电场穿透并实现极化翻转。
> 2.  **寻找候选材料与实验设计**：作者选择层状极性金属WTe₂作为研究对象。通过设计一种“三明治”结构的双栅极器件，在原子级厚度的WTe₂薄片上施加垂直于平面的电场。
> 3.  **核心发现与验证**：
>     -   **现象观察**：在两层和三层WTe₂器件中，观察到电阻随电场变化的双稳态（**bistability**）回滞曲线，这是铁电翻转的典型特征。单层和更厚的器件则无此现象。
>     -   **直接证据**：将顶栅极替换为单层石墨烯作为电场传感器，直接探测到了由WTe₂电极化翻转所引起的电场变化，从而定量估算出极化强度。
>     -   **性质调控**：通过调节栅压，可以改变WTe₂的载流子浓度，进而调控其铁电和导电特性，实现了铁电态与金属态的共存。
> 4.  **理论解释与展望**：提出一种新的物理机制，即极化翻转可能源于电子-空穴关联效应，而非传统的晶格畸变。这为理解WTe₂中丰富的量子现象（如拓扑、超导）提供了新的联系。
>

> ## 四、正文核心内容剖析
> 
> ### 1. 研究背景与动机
> 
> 传统铁电物理学认为，金属中的自由电子会屏蔽一切静电场，因此不可能存在可切换的铁电性。尽管近年来发现了少数具有极性结构的“极性金属”，但它们的极性方向从未被证实可以被电场翻转。本研究挑战了这一传统认知，提出了一个核心假设：**如果金属足够薄，外部电场可能无法被完全屏蔽，从而能够穿透并翻转其内部极化。**
> 
> ### 2. 核心方法与模型
> 
> 实验成功的关键在于以下三个要素的巧妙结合：
> 
> -   **材料选择**：选择**WTe₂**（二碲化钨）作为研究对象。其块体是一种极性半金属，具有天然的极性轴，但层数减少时性质会发生剧变。
> -   **器件设计**：构建了**双栅极场效应晶体管**。将2-3层WTe₂封装在两层**hBN**（六方氮化硼，一种完美的二维绝缘体）之间，外部再用石墨烯作为栅极。这构成了一个理想的“原子级电容器”，可以对WTe₂施加均匀的垂直电场**E⊥**并同时调节其载流子浓度**ne**。
>     -   _可视化提示：这个器件结构可类比为一个汉堡，中间好几层，每层的作用都不同。_
> -   **探测手段**：采用**石墨烯电场传感器**。石墨烯的电阻对电场极其敏感，将其置于WTe₂上方，可以像“听诊器”一样，直接探测到WTe₂极化翻转时产生的微小电场变化，从而绕过了传统方法因极化强度太小而无法探测的难题。
> 
> ### 3. 实验结果与讨论
> 
> -   **极化翻转的直接证据**：
>     
>     1.  **电学双稳态**：在双层和三层WTe₂中，电导-电场扫描曲线出现回滞窗口，且这种双稳态可持续到室温以上。单层（有对称中心）和更厚的WTe₂（电场被屏蔽）则无此现象，完美验证了初始假设。
>     2.  **电场直接探测**：石墨烯传感器清晰地探测到两种极化状态（P↑和P↓）对应的不同电场，并据此估算出极化强度**P ≈ 1 × 10⁴ e·cm⁻¹**，这比传统铁电体BaTiO₃的极化强度低三个数量级，解释了为何传统方法难以测量。
>     3.  **温度依赖性**：极化强度随温度升高而减弱，在约340K以上变得不稳定，预示着存在一个向非极性态的相变。
> -   **铁电态的调控**： 通过调节栅压，作者发现：
>     
>     -   铁电性和金属性可以共存。在电子掺杂浓度超过**nc ~ 2 × 10¹² cm⁻²**时，材料呈现金属性，但铁电翻转依然存在。
>     -   极化翻转对电导的巨大影响可能源于电荷在两层间的重新分布。当极化翻转时，施加的电位差克服了极化自身产生的电位差，导致载流子在不同层间转移，从而显著改变电导。
> -   **新颖的物理机制探讨**： 传统的铁电性源于离子晶格位移（晶格畸变）。但本文的观测结果提出了一个引人入胜的新可能性：WTe₂的铁电性可能源于**电子-空穴关联效应**。在补偿点（ne=0，电子和空穴浓度相等）附近，电子和空穴的强关联作用可能驱动了自发极化，极化翻转可能主要涉及电子云相对于离子骨架的集体运动。这种电子驱动的铁电性，其翻转速度在理论上可能极快，远超传统声子介导的翻转。
>     
> 
> ## 五、结论与贡献
> 
> 本研究首次在实验上实现了二维金属（双层和三层WTe₂）的铁电性翻转，并对其进行了定量表征和调控。其核心贡献在于：
> 
> 1.  **概念验证**：打破了“铁电性仅存在于绝缘体”的传统认知，为寻找和设计新型铁电金属提供了指导原则——即利用超薄层状极性金属。
> 2.  **方法论创新**：开发了利用范德华异质结和石墨烯作为电场传感器的超灵敏探测技术，为研究微小极化强度的二维材料开辟了新路径。
> 3.  **新物理探索**：揭示了WTe₂中铁电性的可能新机制（电子关联驱动），并将其与材料中已知的拓扑量子自旋霍尔效应和超导性等其他新奇量子现象联系起来，为未来研究提供了丰富的物理内涵。
> 4.  **应用潜力**：该铁电性在室温下稳定存在，且与二维材料平台兼容，为开发集成铁电、拓扑、超导等特性的多功能纳米电子器件提供了可能。
> 
> ## 六、未来发展方向展望
> 
> 作者在文中指出了未来研究的几个方向：
> 
> 1.  **微观机理澄清**：需要深入探究极化翻转的具体微观机制，即到底是电子-空穴关联驱动，还是存在难以探测的微小晶格畸变。如果证实是电子驱动，其超快翻转速度将成为一个重要的研究课题。
> 2.  **畴结构研究**：在更大尺寸器件中，研究极化畴的形成、畴壁的动力学行为及其对电学输运的影响。
> 3.  **关联现象探索**：深入研究WTe₂中铁电性、量子自旋霍尔效应、超导性等不同量子基态之间的相互作用和耦合关系，这对于理解三维WTe₂中极端磁阻等奇特性质可能至关重要。
> 
> ## 七、学术思考与批判性分析
> 
> 在赞赏这项工作的同时，我们需要进行一些批判性思考：
> 
> 1.  **极化机制的直接证据**：论文提出了一个非常诱人的“电子-空穴关联驱动”的假设，但直接的实验证据仍然缺乏。如何设计实验来区分电子驱动和微弱的晶格驱动，是下一个关键挑战。例如，能否通过超快光谱技术直接观测极化翻转过程中晶格和电子的响应时间差？
>     
> 2.  **极化强度的定量准确性**：估算出的极化强度P依赖于一个高度简化的静电模型，该模型假设了无限大的电子压缩率和被完美夹在中间的极化电荷层。实际器件中的非理想因素（如hBN的有限厚度、电荷分布范围）会引入误差。这个估算值更应被视为一个数量级参考，而非精确值。
>     
> 3.  **畴结构和非均匀性**：论文指出，在切换时存在随机跳跃，以及在接触附近存在屏蔽效应，这意味着器件的极化翻转可能不是单一畴的均匀翻转，而是涉及多畴的成核和运动。当前基于整体电导的测量，难以解析这些微观畴动力学过程，而这对于理解其翻转机制和应用潜力至关重要。
>     
> 4.  **“铁电金属”的定义边界**：Anderson和Blount最初定义的“铁电金属”是指具有可翻转的极性结构。对于WTe₂，其面内是金属性，但面外是否具有金属性？文中提到极化翻转会改变面间的电位差，这暗示了面外方向更接近一个弱导电或绝缘态。那么，它的面外极化翻转是否与传统铁电绝缘体在机制上并无本质区别？这引出了一个问题：我们是否正在重新定义“铁电金属”？
>     
> 5.  **与其它二维铁电体的比较**：文中未与其他近期发现的二维铁电体（如CuInP₂S₆、α-In₂Se₃）进行深入比较。这些材料同样是范德华层状材料，它们中的铁电性是传统离子位移机制。将WTe₂的电子型铁电性与这些材料进行对比，将有助于凸显其独特性和普适性。
>

> ## 八、用户问题预测与解答
> 
> **问题1：什么是铁电性？能用比喻解释一下吗？** **答**：可以。想象一个指南针，它具有一个天然的指向（从南极到北极）。铁电材料就像是一个“电指南针”，它内部有一个自发的、从正电荷中心指向负电荷中心的“箭头”（电极化）。这个“电箭头”的方向可以被外加电压（电场）来回翻转，就像你可以用一块磁铁强行翻转指南针的方向一样。本论文就是发现了一种金属，虽然是电的良导体，但内部也有这样一个可以被翻转的“电箭头”。
> 
> **问题2：为什么说在金属中发现铁电性很困难？** **答**：金属就像一个充满“自由电子”的海洋。这些电子会迅速移动，中和掉任何试图建立的电场。这产生了两个后果：1）内部很难形成稳定的正负电荷中心分离（极化）；2）外部电场很难穿透金属内部去翻转那个极化，就像水很难渗入一块密实的海绵。本研究的核心突破在于，他们发现，如果这块金属“海绵”被压得足够薄（只有两三层原子厚），电场就可以“挤”过去，触及并翻转其内部的极化。
> 
> **问题3：实验中为什么使用单层WTe₂作为对照组？** **答**：这是科学实验中的“对照原则”。单层WTe₂的结构是中心对称的，就像一个完美的圆，没有任何天然的方向性，因此不可能有自发极化。实验发现单层器件没有回滞现象，这有力地证明了在双层和三层中观察到的效应确实是源自其非中心对称的堆叠结构所产生的铁电性，而不是其他无关的干扰，比如电荷注入到绝缘层中。
> 
> **问题4：为什么用石墨烯来探测极化，而不是直接测量电流？** **答**：这是实验设计中的神来之笔。传统的铁电测量是直接测量极化翻转时产生的位移电流。但这里估算的WTe₂极化强度非常小，比传统铁电体小一千倍，这个微弱信号在微米尺度的器件上很容易被噪声淹没。石墨烯是一种对电场极度敏感的材料，其电阻变化可以直接反映周围电场的变化。用它作为“电场传感器”，相当于把一个微弱的电信号放大并转换成了一个更容易测量的电阻信号，从而巧妙地探测到了极化翻转。
> 
> **问题5：这项研究有什么实际应用前景？** **答**：这项研究开辟了“铁电金属”这个全新的应用领域，其潜力巨大：
> 
> 1.  **超高密度、非破坏性读取存储器**：传统铁电存储器（FeRAM）需反复翻转极化来读取，存在疲劳问题。而这里，极化状态（P↑或P↓）直接对应不同的电导（电阻），只需测量电阻就能“读取”信息，完全不破坏极化状态，这在原理上是一种更优的存储方式。
> 2.  **多功能集成器件**：WTe₂本身还集成了拓扑边缘态（可用于低功耗导电）和超导性。在一个单一材料中集成铁电、拓扑、超导等多种功能，是构建未来多功能、超低功耗器件的理想平台。
> 3.  **超快开关**：如果极化翻转确如文中所推测的那样是电子驱动的，那么其翻转速度可能比传统铁电体快得多，有望用于制造皮秒级甚至飞秒级的高速开关。
> 
> ## 九、专区：便于 Obsidian Dataview 插件调用
>

> # Ferroelectric switching of a two-dimensional metal
> 
> 二维金属中的铁电翻转
> 
> ## Abstract:
> 
> 摘要：
> 
> A ferroelectric is a material with a polar structure whose polarity can be reversed by applying an electric field1,2. In metals, the itinerant electrons tend to screen electrostatic forces between ions, helping to explain why polar metals are very rare3-7. Screening also excludes external electric fields, apparently ruling out the possibility of polarity reversal and thus ferroelectric switching. In principle, however, a thin enough polar metal could be penetrated by an electric field sufficiently to be switched. Here we show that the layered topological semimetal WTe2 provides the first embodiment of this principle. Although monolayer WTe2 is centrosymmetric and thus nonpolar, the stacked bulk structure is polar. We find that two- or three-layer WTe2 exhibits a spontaneous out-of-plane electric polarization which can be switched using gate electrodes. We directly detect and quantify the polarization using graphene as an electric field sensor8. Moreover, the polarization states can be differentiated by conductivity, and the carrier density can be varied to modify the properties. The critical temperature is above 350 K, and even when WTe2 is sandwiched in graphene it retains its switching capability at room temperature, demonstrating a robustness suitable for applications in combination with other two-dimensional materials9-12.
> 
> 铁电体是一种具有极性结构的材料，其极性可以通过施加电场来反转1,2。在金属中，巡游电子倾向于屏蔽离子间的静电力，这有助于解释为何极性金属极为罕见3-7。屏蔽作用也排斥外部电场，表面上看排除了极性反转从而发生铁电翻转的可能性。然而，原则上足够薄的极性金属可以被电场穿透到足以实现翻转。我们在此展示层状拓扑半金属WTe2提供了这一原理的首个实例。尽管单层WTe2是中心对称因而是非极性的，但其堆叠的体结构是极性的。我们发现两层或三层WTe2表现出可被栅电极翻转的自发面外电极化。我们利用石墨烯作为电场传感器直接检测并量化了这种极化8。此外，极化态可通过电导率区分，且载流子密度可调以改变其性质。临界温度高于350 K，即使WTe2被夹在石墨烯中，它也能在室温下保持其翻转能力，展现出适用于与其他二维材料结合的鲁棒性9-12。
> 
> **Ferroelectric**: 铁电体。一种具有自发极化且极化方向可被外加电场反转的材料。 **Topological semimetal**: 拓扑半金属。一类具有非平庸拓扑电子能带结构的半金属材料。 **Gate electrode**: 栅电极。用于施加电场以调控材料中载流子浓度或电状态的电极。 **Graphene**: 石墨烯。一种由单层碳原子构成的二维材料。 **Carrier density**: 载流子密度。单位面积或体积中导电电子或空穴的数量。

> ## Main text:

> 正文：
> 
> The structure of a polar material effectively contains an arrow, that is, an axis along which the two directions are physically distinguishable. This is necessary for the existence of a spontaneous electric polarization. Of the 32 three-dimensional crystal classes, the ten having a polar axis are known as the pyroelectrics, because heating them changes any electric polarization along this axis to produce a voltage. When Anderson and Blount introduced3 the term ferroelectric metal in 1965, they were referring to the possibility of polar structure appearing in certain metallic crystals on cooling. They naturally assumed that, even if such polar metals exist, the polarity would not be switchable. The first definite cases of metals with polar structure have only very recently been identified4-7. Several ferroelectric insulators have been found to maintain ferroelectric characteristics in ultrathin films13-17. However, when materials with a layered structure are thinned down towards the monolayer limit their properties often change qualitatively. This is amply illustrated by graphene, which becomes a two-dimensional Dirac metal11,12; by MoS2, which changes from indirect to direct gap semiconductor10; and by CrI3, which varies between antiferromagnetic and ferromagnetic9. Another example is the topological semimetal WTe218, which becomes either a two-dimensional topological insulator19-22 or a superconductor at low temperatures in the monolayer limit, depending on the electrostatic doping level. Here we focus on another aspect of WTe2: it is a polar metal. The three-dimensional (1T′) structure has a polar space group7, Pnm21, and it remains metallic down to three layers when undoped23 or even as a monolayer when electrostatically doped20. We show here that as it approaches this limit the polarity can be switched, making it effectively ferroelectric even while it is metallic in the plane.
> 
> 极性材料的结构实际上包含一个箭头，即一个物理上两个方向可区分的轴。这对于自发电极化的存在是必要的。在32种三维晶体类别中，十种具有极轴的称为热释电体，因为加热它们会改变沿该轴的任何电极化，从而产生电压。当Anderson和Blount在1965年引入3"铁电金属"这一术语时，他们指的是某些金属晶体在冷却时可能出现极性结构。他们自然假设，即使这种极性金属存在，极性也无法被翻转。第一批确切的具有极性结构的金属实例直到最近才被确认4-7。几种铁电绝缘体已被发现在超薄膜中保持铁电特性13-17。然而，当具有层状结构的材料减薄到接近单层极限时，其性质通常会发生质变。石墨烯，它成为二维狄拉克金属11,12；MoS2，它从间接带隙半导体变为直接带隙半导体10；以及CrI3，它在反铁磁性和铁磁性之间变化9，都充分说明了这一点。另一个例子是拓扑半金属WTe218，它在单层极限下根据静电掺杂水平，要么成为二维拓扑绝缘体19-22，要么在低温下成为超导体。这里我们关注WTe2的另一个方面：它是一种极性金属。其三维(1T′)结构具有极性空间群7 Pnm21，并且在未掺杂时直至三层23，甚至在静电掺杂时作为单层20，它仍保持金属性。我们在此展示，当它接近这一极限时，极性可以被翻转，使其即使在面内呈金属性的同时，实际上也是铁电性的。
> 
> **Polar material**: 极性材料。结构上存在唯一极轴，沿该轴正反方向物理性质可区分的材料。 **Pyroelectrics**: 热释电体。指具有极性结构，且温度变化会导致其极化强度改变并产生电压的一类材料。 **Ferroelectric metal**: 铁电金属。一种理论上可能存在极性结构，但其极性是否可翻转存在争议的金属。 **Space group**: 空间群。描述晶体内部微观结构对称性的数学组合。
> 
> The 1T′ structure (Fig. 1a) contains b-c mirror (M) and a-c glide (G) planes, so that the polar axis, which must be parallel to both of them, is the c-axis, perpendicular to the layers7,18. We can apply an electric field along this axis using the device geometry indicated in Fig. 1b. An electrically contacted thin WTe2 flake is sandwiched between two hexagonal boron nitride (hBN) dielectric sheets, with thicknesses dt (top) and db (bottom). Above and below are gate electrodes, usually of few-layer graphene, to which voltages Vt and Vb are applied relative to the grounded WTe2 (see Methods and Extended Data Fig. 1 for device fabrication and characterization). We define E⊥ = (−Vt/dt + Vb/db)/2, the applied electric field passing upwards through the layer, which will couple to out-of-plane polarization. When E⊥ is swept up and down the conductance of 3-layer (Fig. 1c) and 2-layer (Fig. 1d) devices shows bistability near E⊥ = 0 characteristic of ferroelectric switching, at all temperatures T from 1.6 K up to above room temperature. No bistability is seen in monolayer WTe2 (Fig. 1e), consistent with its structure having a center of symmetry (Fig. 1e inset, red dots) and hence being non-polar; this also rules out instabilities involving charge injection into the hBN. Nor is bistability seen in thicker crystals, including in the situation where one is employed as a gate electrode (see Extended Data Fig. 2). This, and the larger field required to switch the trilayer than the bilayer, can be explained by screening of E⊥ on a length scale of nanometers. Similar bistability was seen in all bilayer devices (see Extended Data Fig. 3). To prove that it is associated with out-of-plane electric polarization, we made devices in which the top gate is replaced by monolayer graphene, whose conductivity is sensitive to the precise electric field Et in the upper hBN.
> 
> 1T′结构（图1a）包含b-c镜面（M）和a-c滑移面（G），因此极轴（必须平行于这两个面）是垂直于各层的c轴7,18。我们可以使用图1b所示的器件几何结构沿该轴施加电场。一片电接触的薄WTe2薄片夹在两层六方氮化硼（hBN）介电片之间，其厚度分别为dt（顶部）和db（底部）。上方和下方是栅电极，通常由少层石墨烯制成，相对于接地的WTe2施加电压Vt和Vb（器件制备和表征见方法和扩展数据图1）。我们定义E⊥ = (−Vt/dt + Vb/db)/2，即向上穿过该层的施加电场，它将与面外极化耦合。当E⊥上下扫描时，3层（图1c）和2层（图1d）器件的电导在E⊥ = 0附近显示出铁电翻转特征的双稳态，温度范围从1.6 K直至室温以上。在单层WTe2中未观察到双稳态（图1e），这与其结构具有对称中心（图1e插图，红点）因而非极性一致；这也排除了涉及电荷注入hBN的不稳定性。在较厚的晶体中，包括被用作栅电极的情况下，也未观察到双稳态（见扩展数据图2）。这一点，以及翻转三层所需电场比双层更大，可以通过E⊥在纳米尺度上的屏蔽来解释。在所有双层器件中都观察到了类似的双稳态（见扩展数据图3）。为了证明它与面外电极化有关，我们制备了用单层石墨烯替代顶栅的器件，其电导率对上hBN中的精确电场Et敏感。
> 
> **Hexagonal boron nitride (hBN)**: 六方氮化硼。一种层状绝缘体，原子级平整，常用作二维材料器件的介电衬底或封装层。 **Dielectric**: 电介质。不导电但能被电场极化的材料。 **Bistability**: 双稳态。系统在相同外部条件下存在两种稳定状态的现象。 **Center of symmetry**: 对称中心。晶体结构中的一个点，经过该点反演操作后结构不变，具有对称中心的材料是非极性的。
> 
> Figure 2 presents measurements at a series of temperatures on such a bilayer WTe2 device (B2) with four gold contacts to the top graphene, depicted in Fig. 2a (see Extended Data Fig. 4). If the WTe2 acts as a conducting sheet it will screen out any electric field due to a voltage applied to the bottom gate. Indeed, Fig. 2b shows that the conductance Ggr of the graphene depends only very weakly on Vb, except in a certain interval where it jumps between two states. The conductance of the WTe2 is bistable in precisely the same interval (see Extended Data Fig. 4). The two states must correspond to different values of Et that can occur for exactly the same set of applied bias voltages. This implies the existence of two different vertical distributions of charge in the bilayer WTe2. We deduce that sweeping the bottom gate changes E⊥ (here E⊥ = Vb/2db since Vt = 0), which at the ends of the hysteresis loop flips the polarization state (henceforth denoted by P↑ or P↓), changing Et by an amount δEt, and so changing Ggr. We can infer δEt by applying a bias VW to the WTe2 and measuring the change δV = dtδEt required to produce the same change in Ggr, as shown in Fig. 2c. For the simplified case dt = db and all voltages at zero, the electrostatic potential profile is inverted between P↑ (red) and P↓ (green), as sketched in Fig. 2d, and the areal polarization density is given by P ≈ ε0δV (see Methods). At 20 K this gives P ≈ 1 × 104 e·cm-1, equivalent to transferring ~2 × 1011 e·cm-2 between the two layers, a distance of ~0.7 nm. This is three orders of magnitude lower than the volume polarization density ~0.2 C·m-2 ~ 1014 e·cm-2 in the classic ferroelectric1 BaTiO3. Combined with the micron-scale device size, such a small polarization makes it very hard to detect the ferroelectricity by standard displacement current measurements.
> 
> 图2展示了在一个具有四个金触点连接顶部石墨烯的双层WTe2器件（B2）上，于一系列温度下进行的测量，如图2a所示（见扩展数据图4）。如果WTe2充当导电片，它将屏蔽掉由施加到底栅的电压所引起的任何电场。确实，图2b显示石墨烯的电导Ggr仅非常微弱地依赖于Vb，除了在某个特定区间内它在两个状态之间跳跃。WTe2的电导在完全相同的区间内是双稳态的（见扩展数据图4）。这两个状态必定对应于在完全相同的一组施加偏压下可能出现的不同Et值。这意味着双层WTe2中存在两种不同的垂向电荷分布。我们推断，扫描底栅改变E⊥（此处E⊥ = Vb/2db，因为Vt = 0），在磁滞回线的两端翻转极化状态（此后记为P↑或P↓），使Et改变量δEt，从而改变Ggr。我们可以通过向WTe2施加偏压VW，并测量产生相同Ggr变化所需的改变量δV = dtδEt来推断δEt，如图2c所示。对于简化的情形dt = db且所有电压为零，静电势分布在P↑（红色）和P↓（绿色）之间反转，如图2d所绘，面极化密度由P ≈ ε0δV给出（见方法）。在20 K时，这给出P ≈ 1 × 104 e·cm-1，相当于在两层之间转移了~2 × 1011 e·cm-2，距离为~0.7 nm。这比经典铁电体1 BaTiO3中的体积极化密度~0.2 C·m-2 ~ 1014 e·cm-2低三个数量级。结合微米级的器件尺寸，如此小的极化使得通过标准位移电流测量来检测铁电性变得非常困难。
> 
> **Hysteresis loop**: 磁滞回线。在铁电或铁磁材料中，物理量（如极化强度或磁化强度）随外场变化呈现的滞后环状曲线，是翻转行为的特征。 **Displacement current**: 位移电流。电位移矢量随时间变化率所产生的电流，在铁电体翻转过程中会产生可测量的瞬态电流峰。
> 
> In Fig. 2e we plot δV as a function of temperature. Between ~60 K and 300 K it decreases roughly linearly with T, extrapolating to zero at roughly 450 K. However, above ~340 K the signal becomes unstable and we can no longer identify a hysteresis loop, suggesting that a transition to a nonpolar state occurs in this temperature range. We also made a simpler device with no top hBN and monolayer graphene directly encapsulating bilayer WTe2. It exhibited highly reproducible hysteresis in the conductance, visible up to 300 K (see Extended Data Fig. 5), showing that the ferroelectric switching is robust enough for applications in combination with other 2D materials at room temperature.
> 
> 在图2e中，我们绘制了δV作为温度的函数。在~60 K到300 K之间，它随T大致线性下降，外推至约450 K时为零。然而，在~340 K以上，信号变得不稳定，我们无法再识别出磁滞回线，这表明在此温度范围内发生了向非极性态的转变。我们还制备了一个更简单的器件，没有顶部hBN，单层石墨烯直接封装双层WTe2。它在电导中表现出高度可重复的磁滞，在300 K下仍可见（见扩展数据图5），表明铁电翻转具有足够的鲁棒性，可在室温下与其他二维材料结合应用。
> 
> We also investigated the effect of the gate-induced charge doping, defined by ene = εhBNε0(Vt/dt + Vb/db). The quantity ne would be the areal density of added electrons if the material were a simple metal. In Figs. 3a and b we plot the conductance at 7 K for device B1 (the same bilayer as in Fig. 1d), as a joint function of Vt and Vb, measured with Vt stepped and Vb swept up or down. Each sweep was started in the same fully polarized state. The black dashed line denotes E⊥ = 0 and the white dashed line ne = 0. The two plots differ only in the central hysteretic region, as is made clearer by plotting the difference between them (Fig. 3c). Similar behavior is seen at higher temperature (Fig. 3d, at 200 K). At E⊥ = 0, G is a similar function of ne for both P↑ and P↓ (Fig. 3e), with temperature dependence that is insulating near ne = 0 and metallic for ne > nc~2 × 1012 cm-2, as reported previously20. Figure 3f shows traces obtained by sweeping E⊥ repeatedly up and down for selected values of ne at 7 K. In each case the single conductance level at large E⊥ evolves smoothly and reproducibly into one of the two stable levels as E⊥ is reduced to zero, implying that the state remains uniformly polarized, without domain structure, at E⊥ = 0. For small or negative ne, the effect of E⊥ is large and of opposite sign for P↑ and P↓, producing butterfly-shaped hysteresis loops. For ne well above nc, E⊥ has less effect on the conductance and the hysteresis is smaller but it is still present. Hence the doped bilayer, like the trilayer, is simultaneously ferroelectric and metallic.
> 
> 我们还研究了栅诱导电荷掺杂的效应，其定义为ene = εhBNε0(Vt/dt + Vb/db)。如果该材料是简单金属，量ne将是添加电子的面密度。在图3a和b中，我们绘制了器件B1（与图1d相同的双层）在7 K时的电导，作为Vt和Vb的联合函数，测量时Vt步进，Vb向上或向下扫描。每次扫描都从相同的完全极化状态开始。黑色虚线表示E⊥ = 0，白色虚线表示ne = 0。两幅图仅在中心磁滞区域有所不同，通过绘制它们之间的差异（图3c）可以更清楚地看到这一点。在更高温度下观察到类似行为（图3d，200 K）。在E⊥ = 0时，对于P↑和P↓，G是ne的相似函数（图3e），其温度依赖性在ne = 0附近表现为绝缘性，在ne > nc~2 × 1012 cm-2时表现为金属性，正如先前报道的20。图3f显示了在7 K下，针对选定的ne值反复上下扫描E⊥所获得的曲线。在每种情况下，大E⊥处的单一电导水平随着E⊥减小到零，平滑且可重复地演变为两个稳定水平之一，这意味着在E⊥ = 0时，状态保持均匀极化，没有畴结构。对于小的或负的ne，E⊥的影响很大，并且对P↑和P↓符号相反，产生蝴蝶形磁滞回线。对于远高于nc的ne，E⊥对电导的影响较小，磁滞也较小，但仍然存在。因此，掺杂的双层，如同三层一样，同时是铁电性和金属性的。
> 
> **Domain structure**: 畴结构。铁电体中，为了降低静电能，极化方向一致的区域形成畴，不同畴之间极化方向不同，被畴壁分隔。
> 
> At low temperatures (Fig. 3c) we observe an increase of the hysteresis loop width for increasingly negative ne, while at 200 K (Fig. 3d) it is almost independent of ne. When the conductance jumps there is some stochastic variation in the positions and substructure of the jumps suggesting domain dynamics. If the surrounding gates were not present to screen the depolarization field, domains would inevitably form to limit the electrostatic energy24, as observed in other ultrathin ferroelectrics13,16,25,26. In our devices, defects such as rips, bubbles and folds could nucleate domains or pin domain walls. In addition, E⊥ is not completely uniform, because above and near the Pt contacts it will be reduced by screening. Indeed, the pattern of switching depends on choice of measurement contacts within a given device (see Extended Data Fig. 6). We also observe that in some bilayer devices, such as B2 (see Fig. 2), the switching field is not symmetric about E⊥ = 0. A possible explanation for this is that sometimes, despite all precautions, during device fabrication one side of the WTe2 flake is exposed to mild oxidation, producing asymmetric trapped charge.
> 
> 在低温下（图3c），我们观察到随ne负向增加，磁滞回线宽度增加，而在200 K（图3d）时，它几乎与ne无关。当电导跳跃时，跳跃的位置和亚结构存在一些随机变化，暗示存在畴动力学。如果周围没有栅极来屏蔽去极化场，畴将不可避免地形成以限制静电能24，正如在其他超薄铁电体中所观察到的13,16,25,26。在我们的器件中，诸如撕裂、气泡和褶皱等缺陷可能促使畴成核或钉扎畴壁。此外，E⊥并非完全均匀，因为在Pt接触上方和附近，它会因屏蔽而减弱。确实，翻转的模式取决于给定器件内测量接触的选择（见扩展数据图6）。我们还观察到，在某些双层器件中，例如B2（见图2），翻转场并非关于E⊥ = 0对称。对此一个可能的解释是，有时尽管采取了一切预防措施，在器件制备过程中，WTe2薄片的一侧暴露于轻微氧化，产生了不对称的俘获电荷。
> 
> **Depolarization field**: 去极化场。铁电体中，由极化电荷产生的与极化方向相反的电场，若不加以屏蔽，会导致极化不稳定或形成畴。 **Domain wall**: 畴壁。分隔不同极化方向畴的界面区域。
> 
> The fact that the conductance is sensitive to the polarization is consistent with the expectation that the polarization redistributes charge between the layers, which are inequivalent when E⊥ is nonzero. While the specific mechanisms for the sensitivity to ne, E⊥, and P are still under investigation, a few remarks are in order. First, the monolayer conductance at 4 K in Fig. 1e, which is due to edge conduction since this is the established QSH regime, is almost independent of E⊥. Second, in bilayers at large positive or negative ne the reversal of P has a similar effect on the conductance to changing E⊥, by an amount ~0.15 V/nm at 7 K (indicated by dotted horizontal line in Fig. 3f). This corresponds to a change in the electrostatic potential difference between the two WTe2 layers by ~100 mV. This is of the same order as the estimated change in the potential difference associated with the polarization reverse, 2δV ~ 40 mV, suggesting that the potential imbalance between the layers governs the sensitivity of the conductance to both E⊥ and P. It is also roughly the same as the width of the hysteresis loop; that is, the polarization flips roughly when the applied potential difference exceeds the potential due to the spontaneous polarization. This is another indicator that electron transfer between the layers may be involved. Third, the very sharp minimum seen in G close to ne = 0 in bilayers, visible in Fig. 3e, presumably marks the compensation point where electron and hole densities are exactly equal, suggesting that electron-hole correlation may be important. Taken together, the above observations raise the intriguing possibility that electron-hole correlation effects, rather than a lattice instability27, drive the spontaneous polarization in this layered semimetal. If this is the case, the polarization could principally involve a relative motion of the electron cloud relative to the ion cores, rather than a lattice distortion, in which case the switching would be intrinsically very fast.
> 
> 电导对极化敏感这一事实，与极化在层间重新分配电荷的预期一致，当E⊥非零时这些层是不等价的。虽然对ne、E⊥和P的敏感性具体机制仍在研究中，但有几点值得提及。首先，图1e中单层在4 K下的电导（由于这是已确立的量子自旋霍尔(QSH)区域，源自边缘传导）几乎与E⊥无关。其次，在双层中，在大正或大负ne时，P的反转对电导的影响与改变E⊥相似，在7 K时约为~0.15 V/nm（如图3f中虚线水平线所示）。这对应于两个WTe2层之间静电势差变化约~100 mV。这与极化反转相关的势差估计变化2δV ~ 40 mV处于同一数量级，表明层间势不平衡决定了电导对E⊥和P的敏感性。它也大致与磁滞回线的宽度相同；也就是说，极化大致在施加的势差超过自发极化势时翻转。这是层间可能涉及电子转移的另一个指标。第三，在双层中，在ne = 0附近观察到的G的非常尖锐的极小值，可在图3e中看到，大概标志着电子和空穴密度恰好相等的补偿点，表明电子-空穴关联可能很重要。综上所述，上述观察结果提出了一个引人入胜的可能性：电子-空穴关联效应，而非晶格不稳定性27，驱动了这种层状半金属中的自发极化。如果是这种情况，极化可能主要涉及电子云相对于离子实的相对运动，而非晶格畸变，在这种情况下，翻转本质上是极快的。
> 
> **Quantum Spin Hall (QSH)**: 量子自旋霍尔效应。一种拓扑物态，材料体态绝缘，但边缘存在受拓扑保护的无耗散自旋流。 **Compensation point**: 补偿点。半导体或半金属中，电子和空穴浓度相等，净载流子浓度为零的点。
> 
> Ferroelectricity adds another ingredient to the intriguing combination of QSH edges, correlation effects and superconductivity already seen in this atomically thin topological semimetal. Although the QSH behavior and superconductivity are restricted to the centrosymmetric monolayer while the ferroelectricity occurs only for two or more layers, it is possible that these diverse phenomena are connected in ways which may also be relevant to understanding the properties that emerge in the 3D limit, including extreme and anisotropic magnetoresistance28,29, a polar axis, and Weyl points18,30.
> 
> 铁电性为这种原子级薄的拓扑半金属中已经看到的QSH边缘态、关联效应和超导电性的迷人组合增添了另一种要素。尽管QSH行为和超导电性仅限于中心对称的单层，而铁电性仅发生在两层或更多层时，但这些多样的现象可能以某种方式相互关联，这可能也与理解在三维极限下出现的性质有关，包括极大的各向异性磁阻28,29、极轴和外尔点18,30。
> 
> **Weyl points**: 外尔点。三维动量空间中能带结构中的二重简并点，其低能激发可用外尔方程描述，是拓扑半金属的特征。

> ## Methods
> 
> 方法
> 
> **Preparation and characterization of WTe2 devices.** We measured devices with four different layouts: (1) WTe2 with graphite gates above and below (M1, B1, B4, T1); (2) bilayer WTe2 with monolayer graphene as top gate (B2); (3) a bilayer WTe2/graphene heterostructure (B3); and (4) a monolayer graphene device gated by few-layer WTe2 (F1). In the following, we describe fabrication of the first type; the others are similar. First, graphite and hBN crystals were mechanically exfoliated under ambient conditions onto substrates consisting of 285 nm thermal SiO2 on highly p-doped silicon. Graphite flakes 2-6 nm thick were chosen for top and bottom gates and 5-30 nm thick hBN flakes (a layered electrical insulator free of trapped charges and dangling bonds) were chosen for the top and bottom dielectric31. The top and bottom parts were prepared separately using a polymer-based dry transfer technique32. For the bottom part, an hBN flake was picked up on a polymer stamp and placed on the bottom graphite. After dissolving the polymer, Pt metal contacts (~8 nm) were patterned on the hBN by standard e-beam lithography, e-beam evaporation and liftoff. For the top part, the top graphite was picked up first, then the top hBN. Both stacks were then transferred to an oxygen/water-free glovebox. WTe2 crystals were exfoliated inside the glovebox and flakes from monolayer to trilayer thickness were optically identified and quickly picked up with the top part; the stack was then completed by transferring onto the lower contacts/hBN/graphite stack before taking out of the glovebox. Finally, after dissolving the polymer, another step of e-beam lithography and metallization was used to define electrical bonding pads (Au/V) connecting to the metal contacts and the top and bottom gates. Extended Data Fig. 1 shows schematics of the fabrication processes, optical and AFM images of a typical bilayer WTe2 device (B4).
> 
> **WTe2器件的制备与表征。** 我们测量了四种不同布局的器件：（1）上下均有石墨栅极的WTe2（M1, B1, B4, T1）；（2）以单层石墨烯为顶栅的双层WTe2（B2）；（3）双层WTe2/石墨烯异质结构（B3）；以及（4）由少层WTe2栅控的单层石墨烯器件（F1）。下面，我们描述第一种类型的制备；其他类似。首先，在环境条件下，将石墨和hBN晶体机械剥离到由高p掺杂硅上285 nm热SiO2组成的衬底上。选择2-6 nm厚的石墨薄片作为顶栅和底栅，选择5-30 nm厚的hBN薄片（一种无俘获电荷和悬挂键的层状电绝缘体）作为顶部和底部电介质31。顶部和底部部分使用基于聚合物的干法转移技术32分别制备。对于底部部分，用聚合物印模拾取一片hBN薄片并放置在底部石墨上。溶解聚合物后，通过标准电子束光刻、电子束蒸发和剥离工艺，在hBN上图案化Pt金属接触（~8 nm）。对于顶部部分，先拾取顶部石墨，然后拾取顶部hBN。然后将两个堆叠转移到无氧/无水的手套箱中。WTe2晶体在手套箱内剥离，从单层到三层厚度的薄片通过光学识别，并迅速用顶部部分拾取；然后在取出手套箱之前，通过转移到下部接触/hBN/石墨堆叠上完成堆叠。最后，溶解聚合物后，使用另一步电子束光刻和金属化来定义连接到金属接触以及顶栅和底栅的电键合垫（Au/V）。扩展数据图1显示了制备过程的示意图、典型双层WTe2器件（B4）的光学和AFM图像。
> 
> **Estimation of the electric polarization.** We use the following simplified model to estimate the spontaneous polarization of the bilayer WTe2 from the measurements in Fig. 2b. We assume that dt = db ≫ d, where d is the thickness of the WTe2; that all conductors (bottom graphite gate, top graphene and bilayer WTe2) are grounded and have infinite electronic compressibility; and that the areal polarization density P is associated with two thin sheets of areal charge density ±P/d separated by d. Under these assumptions, when the polarization reverses there is no net flow of charge between the conductors and the WTe2 remains neutral, and the potential profile between the gates is simply reversed when the polarization flips (Fig. 2d). By Gauss’s law,
> 
> **电极化的估算。** 我们使用以下简化模型，从图2b的测量中估算双层WTe2的自发极化。我们假设dt = db ≫ d，其中d是WTe2的厚度；所有导体（底部石墨栅极、顶部石墨烯和双层WTe2）均接地且具有无限电子压缩性；面极化密度P与两个相距d、面电荷密度为±P/d的薄层相关。在这些假设下，当极化反转时，导体之间没有净电荷流动，WTe2保持中性，并且当极化翻转时，栅极之间的势分布简单地反转（图2d）。根据高斯定律，
> 
> εoεhBNEt = ε0Ei + P/d , (1)
> 
> where Et is the electric field in the hBN (equal on both sides because the bilayer is neutral) and Ei is the field between the two charge sheets. Since the top graphene and the center of the bilayer are both at zero potential, 其中Et是hBN中的电场（两侧相等，因为双层是电中性的），Ei是两个电荷层之间的电场。由于顶部石墨烯和双层中心都处于零电位，
> 
> 2Etdt + Eid = 0 . (2)
> 
> From (1) and (2), 由(1)和(2)可得，
> 
> Et = P / ε0(2dt+εhBNd) . (3)
> 
> The change in Et when the polarization reverses is then δEt = 2Et = 2P / ε0(2dt+εhBNd). With dt ≈ 10 nm and d ≈ 1 nm, the first term in the denominator dominates so δEt ≈ P/(ε0dt) and thus 极化反转时Et的变化为δEt = 2Et = 2P / ε0(2dt+εhBNd)。因dt ≈ 10 nm和d ≈ 1 nm，分母中第一项占主导，因此δEt ≈ P/(ε0dt)，从而
> 
> P ≈ ε0dtδEt = ε0δV .
> 
> In reality, the ratio of dt to db could be up to about 1:3, the conductors have finite compressibility, and the polarization charge is more spread out, which taken together will introduce an extra numerical coefficient of order unity. 实际上，dt与db的比率可能高达约1:3，导体具有有限的压缩性，且极化电荷分布更分散，这些因素综合起来将引入一个数量级为1的额外数值系数。
> 
> **Removing parallel (parasitic) conduction through the graphene in device B2.** In device B2 the graphene extends over regions with no WTe2 underneath so that it acts as a uniform gate for the entire WTe2 sheet. The quantity that we call Ggr is the result of the following measurement, which maximizes sensitivity to just a central region of graphene above the WTe2. First, we ground two opposing contacts to the graphene and measure only the current that flows from the biased contact to the one opposite, as shown in Fig. 2a. However, because of finite contact resistance, a small portion of this current still flows through graphene not above the WTe2. To remove this parasitic current component, we set the WTe2 voltage VW such that the graphene is at its Dirac-point minimum in the region over the WTe2. Since the minimum is quite broad, the graphene over the WTe2 is then insensitive to Vb and the measured dependence on Vb comes only from the parasitic component which can then be subtracted out. Note that removing it has no effect on the magnitude of the hysteresis. In Extended Data Fig. 4b, we illustrate this procedure at 220 K.
