> ## 一、引言
> 
> 在凝聚态物理与材料科学中，**从头算分子动力学**（ab initio molecular dynamics, AIMD）是一种将**密度泛函理论**（Density Functional Theory, DFT）与经典分子动力学相结合的计算方法，可在无经验参数的情况下模拟原子运动与电子结构的实时演化。1985年，Car与Parrinello开创性地引入**虚拟电子动力学**，使电子自由度与离子自由度在同一时间标度上演化，极大推动了AIMD的发展。然而，该方案在金属体系中面临严峻挑战：电子与离子运动频率的共振以及能级交叉会导致能量在子系统间不可控转移，使系统偏离**绝热基态**（Born-Oppenheimer surface），即所谓的**非绝热性问题**。
> 
> 本文《Ab initio molecular dynamics for liquid metals》由Kresse与Hafner于1993年发表在《Physical Review B》，针对Car-Parrinello方法在金属中的核心困难，提出了一种替代方案：在每一个分子动力学步长中，利用**共轭梯度**（conjugate-gradient）技术精确求解电子基态，并借助**子空间对齐**（subspace alignment）高效预测新离子构型下的波函数，从而完全避免虚构电子动力学。该方法展示了极佳的能量守恒与热力学控制，成功模拟了液态Na和液态Ge的静态结构、扩散性质及电子态密度，与实验高度吻合。本文不仅是计算方法学的重要突破，也为液态金属、半导体乃至非晶态材料的第一性原理模拟奠定了坚实基础。
> 
> ## 二、论文结构与逻辑概览
> 
> 论文按照“问题—方法—验证—讨论”的经典逻辑展开，可概括为以下链条：
> 
> 1.  **背景与问题**：指出Car-Parrinello方法在金属体系中因电子-离子共振和能级交叉导致的非绝热性问题，迫使研究者要么周期性进行能量最小化，要么引入Nosé热浴对电子系统进行恒温控制，而作者认为直接在每个MD步做基态能量最小化是更根本的解决方案。
> 2.  **方法构建**：详细描述基于**预条件共轭梯度**（preconditioned conjugate-gradient）的电子能量最小化流程，包括内层波函数优化、子空间对角化、Fermi能量计算、电荷密度混合（Kerker方案）等；同时给出离子运动的Nosé热浴与预测-校正积分算法，以及利用子空间对齐预测新波函数的方法。
> 3.  **液态Na验证**：在54原子体系上测试，展示总能量漂移＜1 meV/atom，结构因子（配对相关函数）与扩散系数与实验一致，键角分布显示近二十面体短程有序。
> 4.  **液态Ge验证**：在64原子体系上进行更严苛的测试，证明能量守恒在4 meV内，结构显示配位数仅6.2，与普通金属的10–12截然不同，键角分布呈现四面体特征（~109°）和60°峰，电子态密度与光电子能谱高度吻合，并揭示sp³杂化破坏与赝能隙的形成机制。
> 5.  **结论与展望**：论证该方法无需虚构电子动力学即可实现液态金属的从头算MD，时间步长可达3 fs，计算成本可接受，但更长模拟时间和更高精度仍是挑战。
>

> ## 一、引言
> 
> 在凝聚态物理与材料科学中，通过第一性原理模拟液态金属的原子与电子结构是一个长期挑战。传统的**Car-Parrinello分子动力学（Car-Parrinello Molecular Dynamics, CPMD）** 方法通过引入电子自由度的虚构动力学，将电子基态计算与离子运动统一起来，在绝缘体与半导体中取得了巨大成功。然而，对于金属体系，由于电子能带结构中不存在带隙，电子与离子运动的特征频率极易发生共振，导致系统偏离绝热面（即**Born-Oppenheimer面**），产生严重的非绝热效应，使得模拟失稳。
> 
> 本文发表于1993年，由G. Kresse和J. Hafner提出了一种根本性的解决方案：**放弃电子的虚构动力学，转而在每个分子动力学步通过共轭梯度（Conjugate-Gradient）方法精确求解电子基态及Hellmann-Feynman力**，并利用子空间对齐（Subspace Alignment）技术外推波函数初值，从而实现了对液态金属长达数皮秒的稳定从头算模拟。文章以液态Na（自由电子金属）和液态Ge（“坏金属”）为实例，展示了该方法在结构因子、扩散系数和电子态密度等方面与实验的高度吻合，奠定了现代第一性原理分子动力学模拟液态金属与合金的重要基础。

> ## 四、正文核心内容剖析
> 
> ### 4.1 研究背景与问题
> 
> CPMD方法（Car和Parrinello, 1985）的电子虚构动力学在绝缘体中因带隙存在而天然解耦，但在金属中，离子运动频率与电子激发频率重叠，导致能量从离子子系统流向电子子系统，使其偏离基态。为解决此问题，论文提出了一种新的ab initio MD方案，完全摒弃电子的虚构牛顿动力学，转而采用**共轭梯度直接极小化Kohn-Sham总能泛函**。
> 
> ### 4.2 方法模型
> 
> 方法的计算流程如下：
> 
> 1.  **内层循环**：对每个k点、每个能带，用预条件共轭梯度法优化波函数，直至能量特征值变化小于10⁻⁵ eV或小于第一步变化的30%。
> 2.  **子空间对角化**：遍历所有带（包括部分空带）后，进行子空间对角化，用高斯展宽计算分数占据数和新的费米能级。
> 3.  **电荷密度更新**：采用Kerker混合方案抑制低q分量电荷振荡，避免电荷“晃动”。
> 4.  **外层终止**：当每原子能量变化小于1.5×10⁻⁵ eV时，电子极小化完成。
> 5.  **离子运动**：采用Nose热浴产生正则系综，用四阶预报-校正积分器，时间步长可达3×10⁻¹⁵ s（3 fs）。
> 6.  **波函数预测**：离子移动后，用子空间对齐技术（Arias, Payne, Joannopoulos）估计新波函数，使初态能量与基态能量之差不超过5×10⁻⁵ eV/atom。
> 
> 这一方法保证了每一步离子运动都在Born-Oppenheimer面上，绝热性完全可控。
> 
> ### 4.3 液态Na模拟
> 
> 体系：54原子，T=400 K，数密度0.02436 Å⁻³，平面波截断能6 Ry，仅Γ点，时间步长3 fs。
> 
> -   能量守恒：全程2.5 ps内总能量变化<2 meV，预测波函数误差<3×10⁻⁵ eV/atom。
> -   结构：对关联函数与实验重合，配位数13，扩散系数约6×10⁻⁵ cm²/s（实验5.3×10⁻⁵ cm²/s）。
> -   键角分布：二十面体特征，峰在63.5°和116.5°。
> 
> ### 4.4 液态Ge模拟
> 
> 赝势：Vanderbilt型，芯半径1.5 a.u.，s和d非局域通道用Kleinman-Bylander因子化。  
> 体系：64原子，T=1250 K，截断能12 Ry，7000个平面波，138个带（256个价电子，10个空带），高斯展宽0.2 eV。
> 
> -   能量守恒：2.7 ps内总能量变化<4 meV。
> -   结构：配位数6.2，对关联函数第一峰尖锐，远距离振荡弱，与实验一致。
> -   键角分布：短键（<2.80 Å）角分布以109°为中心，反映sp³四面体残存；长键则在60°和109°均有分布，表明存在复杂的多面体连接。
> -   电子结构：赝能隙位于E_F以下约5 eV，与光电子谱吻合，解释为s-p分裂增强，部分源于4s电子钻穿3d芯区，导致更强的电子-离子耦合。
> -   与经典MD对比：基于同一赝势的微扰理论对势能较好描述对关联函数，但三体关联和局部结构涨落存在差异，而这些差异在低温下（接近液-非晶半导体转变）会变得重要。

> ## 七、学术思考与批判性分析
> 
> 1.  **共轭梯度极小化的效率问题**：该方法每一步都需精确收敛至基态，虽然避免了非绝热效应，但计算量远大于CPMD的每步仅一次力计算。尽管文中展示了良好的时间步长（3 fs），但未讨论不同体系大小下迭代收敛的标度行为，这是否会限制其在大规模体系上的应用？

We present ab initio quantum-mechanical molecular-dynamics calculations based on the calculation of the electronic ground state and of the Hellmann-Feynman forces in the local-density approximation at each molecular-dynamics step. This is possible using conjugate-gradient techniques for energy minimization, and predicting the wave functions for new ionic positions using sub-space alignment. This approach avoids the instabilities inherent in quantum-mechanical molecular-dynamics calculations for metals based on the use of a fictitious Newtonian dynamics for the electronic degrees of freedom. This method gives perfect control of the adiabaticity and allows us to perform simulations over several picoseconds.
A few years ago Car and Parrinello introduced an
approach that unifies molecular-dynamics techniques for the calculation of the atomic structure with the local-density approximation2 (LDA) for electronic struc
ture calculations, with the complete set of quantummechanical many-body forces calculated using the Hellmann-Feynman theorem. The basic idea is the introduction of a fictitious dynamics for the electronic degrees of freedom. Since the electronic wave functions of the LDA are meaningful only if the electrons are in their ground state for the instantaneous ionic configuration, an essential condition for the practicability of the Car-Parrinello method is that the transfer of energy between the atomic and electronic subsystems is small in order to prevent the electron states to drift away from the ground states. This transfer of energy is difBcult to
control in metallic systems. In insulators, the width of the electronic band gap divided by the fictitious mass of
the electronic degrees of freedom defines the separation in the characteristic frequencies of the atomic and electronic motions. In metals, this separation is absent and there are essentially two mechanisms that drive metallic systems into nonadiabaticity: a resonance between the atomic and electronic frequencies opening a channel for energy transfer and a level crossing between occupied
and empty electron states. s The operational solution of these nonadiabaticity problems is (a) to perform peri
odic energy minimizations to bring the system "back
to the Born-Oppenheimer surface" or (b) to attach the
electronic subsystem to a Nose thermostat that prevents
the heating up of the electron system. s Clearly the al
ternative is to perform the minimization of the KohnSham functional for the electronic total energy at any
time step of the molecular-dynamics simulation, so that the problem of the nonadiabaticity does not arise at all.
An efficient way to perform the minimization process are conjugate-gradient methods. These techniques have now been developed to a point were it is possible to perform canonical molecular-dynamics simulations for liquid metals over periods of several picoseconds, with complete control over deviations from adiabaticity and good en
ergy conservation. Applications for liquid metals ranging

measured atomic structure factors and electronic spectra.
Our technique for performing a complete calculation of the LDA ground state after each molecular-dynamics
step is based on the conjugate-gradient techniques de
veloped by Payne and co-workers. s s and used in self

> A few years ago Car and Parrinello introduced an approach that unifies molecular-dynamics techniques for the calculation of the atomic structure with the local-density approximation2 (LDA) for electronic struc ture calculations, with the complete set of quantummechanical many-body forces calculated using the Hellmann-Feynman theorem. The basic idea is the introduction of a fictitious dynamics for the electronic degrees of freedom. Since the electronic wave functions of the LDA are meaningful only if the electrons are in their ground state for the instantaneous ionic configuration, an essential condition for the practicability of the Car-Parrinello method is that the transfer of energy between the atomic and electronic subsystems is small in order to prevent the electron states to drift away from the ground states. This transfer of energy is difBcult to control in metallic systems. In insulators, the width of the electronic band gap divided by the fictitious mass of the electronic degrees of freedom defines the separation in the characteristic frequencies of the atomic and electronic motions. In metals, this separation is absent and there are essentially two mechanisms that drive metallic systems into nonadiabaticity: a resonance between the atomic and electronic frequencies opening a channel for energy transfer and a level crossing between occupied and empty electron states. s The operational solution of these nonadiabaticity problems is (a) to perform peri odic energy minimizations to bring the system "back to the Born-Oppenheimer surface" or (b) to attach the electronic subsystem to a Nose thermostat that prevents the heating up of the electron system. s Clearly the al ternative is to perform the minimization of the KohnSham functional for the electronic total energy at any time step of the molecular-dynamics simulation, so that the problem of the nonadiabaticity does not arise at all. An efficient way to perform the minimization process are conjugate-gradient methods. These techniques have now been developed to a point were it is possible to perform canonical molecular-dynamics simulations for liquid metals over periods of several picoseconds, with complete control over deviations from adiabaticity and good en ergy conservation. Applications for liquid metals ranging from Na to Ge demonstrate very good agreement with the measured atomic structure factors and electronic spectra. 几年前，Car和Parrinello引入了一种方法，将用于计算原子结构的分子动力学技术与用于电子结构计算的局域密度近似（LDA）相结合，并利用Hellmann-Feynman定理计算完整的量子力学多体力。其基本思想是引入电子自由度的虚拟动力学。由于LDA的电子波函数仅在电子处于瞬时离子构型的基态时才有意义，Car-Parrinello方法实际可行的关键条件是原子与电子子系统之间的能量转移必须很小，以防止电子态偏离基态。在金属体系中，这种能量转移难以控制。在绝缘体中，电子能带隙宽度除以电子自由度的虚拟质量定义了原子和电子运动特征频率的分离。在金属中，这种分离不存在，主要有两种机制导致金属体系非绝热化：原子与电子频率之间的共振，为能量转移开辟通道；以及占据态和空态之间的能级交叉。这些非绝热问题的操作性解决方案是：(a) 定期进行能量最小化，使系统“回到玻恩-奥本海默面”；或 (b) 将电子子系统耦合到一个Nose恒温器，以防止电子系统升温。显然，另一种选择是在分子动力学模拟的每个时间步进行Kohn-Sham泛函的电子总能量最小化，从而彻底避免非绝热问题。高效的最小化方法之一是共轭梯度法。这些技术现已发展到能够对液态金属进行长达几皮秒的正则分子动力学模拟，并完全控制绝热性偏差和良好的能量守恒。从Na到Ge的液态金属应用表明，与实验测量的原子结构因子和电子谱非常吻合。 **Car-Parrinello method**: 一种将分子动力学与密度泛函理论结合的方法，通过引入电子自由度的虚拟动力学实现同时演化。 **Hellmann-Feynman theorem**: 表明能量对参数的导数等于哈密顿量对该参数导数的期望值，用于计算原子受力。 **Nonadiabaticity**: 非绝热性，指电子态偏离基态，与原子核运动发生能量交换。 **Born-Oppenheimer surface**: 玻恩-奥本海默面，在绝热近似下，电子能量作为原子坐标函数的势能面。 **Nose thermostat**: Nose恒温器，一种用于分子动力学模拟中生成正则系综的热浴方法。 **Kohn-Sham functional**: Kohn-Sham泛函，密度泛函理论中用于求解多电子系统基态能量的泛函。

> Our technique for performing a complete calculation of the LDA ground state after each molecular-dynamics step is based on the conjugate-gradient techniques de veloped by Payne and co-workers. s s and used in self consistent electronic structure calculations by Bylander, Kleinman, and Lee.s io The method is a doubly iterative one: in the inner loop the wave functions for each k point in the Brillouin zone and each band are improved by a preconditioned conjugate-gradient method as described in Ref. 9 until the change in the energy eigenvalue is smaller than 10 s eV (or smaller than 30% of the change in the first step). After running over all bands (includ ing some empty bands), a subspace diagonalization is performed, the new Fermi energy is calculated using a Gaussian broadening of the energy levels, and the charge density is updated. The problems arising from using fractional occupation numbers and their consequences for ab initio molecular-dynamics simulations have been discussed by Weinert and Davenportii and by Wentzcov itch, Martins, and Allen. i2 It has been shown that the variational quantity is not the internal energy but a gen eralized free energy. To prevent charge sloshing, the mix ing scheme proposed by Kerkeris is used. This scheme has the advantage of damping the oscillations in the low-q components of the charge density. The electronic-energy minimization is terminated when the change in the en ergy per atom becomes smaller than 1.5 x 10 s eV. The atomic motion is described by Nosh dynamicsi4 generating a canonical ensemble at prefixed temperature.
