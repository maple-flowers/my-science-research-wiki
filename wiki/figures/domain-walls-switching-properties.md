![图6 螺旋((Eu,Y)MnO₃)与非螺旋(YMn₂O₅交换伸缩)磁致电电性对比及P∥e₃×Q定则](../../raw/figures/cheongMultiferroicsMagneticTwist2007a/fig_6_7B4LX9VV.png)
*   **来源**：[[../papers/cheongMultiferroicsMagneticTwist2007a]]
