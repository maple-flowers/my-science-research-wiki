## ✍️ 0. Title Patterns

2000–2004 时段的标题可归入三种规范模式：(i) 方法型标题——直接命名方法及其用途（Delley2000、Henkelman CI-NEB）；(ii) 问题驱动型标题——命名现象及其机制（CastroNeto2001charge）；(iii) 应用建模型标题——把模型、对象与工艺条件组合在一起（Zhang2002b、Zhang2003a）。方法学论文倾向于把改进直接写进标题，而物理/理论类论文则偏好以核心名词短语开篇，例如 "charge density wave"，再用冒号或 "from … to …" 划定范围。

| 实例 | 类型 | 骨架 |
|---|---|---|
| "From molecules to solids with the DMol3 approach" [[../papers/Delley2000]] | 方法演进型（范围扩展） | From [起点领域 A] to [终点领域 B] with the [方法 X] approach |
| "A climbing image nudged elastic band method for finding saddle points and minimum energy paths" [[../papers/henkelmanClimbingImageNudged2000c]] | 新方法+用途型 | A [新算法 X] method for [目标任务 Y] and [附属任务 Z] |
| "A two-dimensional cellular automaton model for the simulation of nucleation and growth of ferrite grains at various cooling rates in low-carbon steels" [[../papers/Zhang2002b]] | 模型+对象+条件型 | A [维度/类型] [模型 X] model for the simulation of [过程 Y] at [条件 Z] in [材料系统] |

**语法点**：方法学标题常用 "A/An [修饰语] [方法名] method for [动名词短语]" 把"是什么"和"做什么"压入同一名词短语，避免使用完整句；理论类标题则倾向以核心物理对象开篇，再用 "in …" 或 "of …" 限定体系。三者共同的高频名词是 *model / method / approach*，共时地反映 2000 年前后计算材料学"方法论文"与"建模论文"在标题上的同源结构。

## 📄 1. Abstract

2000–2004 时段的摘要遵循组织紧密的五步弧：背景、问题、方法、结果、意义。CastroNeto2001charge 以 "We propose a theory…" 开篇，在 "We show, based on …, that …" 中陈述预言，并以两个简短陈述句结尾——分别讲基本激发和声子介导配对——用四句话装下完整的理论 [[../papers/CastroNeto2001charge]]。Delley2000 走方法论文路线：以被动句 "Recent extensions … are described" 开篇，给出量化主张 "It is found that … is markedly improved"，再以前瞻性的 "will prove useful and efficient" 收束 [[../papers/Delley2000]]。Zhang2002b/2003a 的摘要则呈现标准的工程建模模板："A … model was developed/has been employed to investigate …"，随后用一个长句说明模型如何 "calculates … and incorporates … into a nucleation or growth function, which is utilized by the automaton in a probabilistic fashion" [[../papers/Zhang2002b]][[../papers/Zhang2003a]]。Henkelman 的 CI-NEB 摘要用两个被动句陈述两项算法改进，每项都紧扣其所消除的缺陷 [[../papers/henkelmanClimbingImageNudged2000c]]。Khitrov2000holographic 以制备句 "Gratings were fabricated by …" 开篇，并用 "however" 转折描述电场可调衍射，构成一条"制备—结构—功能"的器件叙事 [[../papers/Khitrov2000holographic]]。纵观整个时段，三种摘要语法占主导：(a) 第一人称认知动词（propose / show / find / succeed in）；(b) 被动式方法开篇（"A … model was developed"）；(c) 以 "in good agreement with" 作定量收束。

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|---|---|---|
| "We propose a theory for quasi-two-dimensional transition metal dichalcogenides that provides a unified microscopic picture of the charge density wave (CDW) and superconducting phases." [[../papers/CastroNeto2001charge]] | 我们为准二维过渡金属二硫化物提出一套理论，它为电荷密度波（CDW）相与超导相提供统一的微观图像。 | We propose a [theory/model] for [体系] that provides a unified [微观/宏观] picture of [现象 A] and [现象 B]. |
| "A modification of the nudged elastic band method for finding minimum energy paths is presented. One of the images is made to climb up along the elastic band to converge rigorously on the highest saddle point." [[../papers/henkelmanClimbingImageNudged2000c]] | 提出一种对微动弹性带方法求最小能量路径的改进：让其中一个像沿弹性带爬升，以严格收敛到最高鞍点。 | A modification of [现有方法 X] for [任务 Y] is presented. One of [关键对象] is made to [动作] to [严格达成目标]. |
| "The simulated results are compared with the optical micrographs showing ferrite grains formed at various cooling rates, and they are in good agreement with those micrographs." [[../papers/Zhang2003a]] | 模拟结果与展示不同冷速下铁素体晶粒的光学显微照片相比，二者吻合良好。 | The simulated results are compared with [实验观测 X] showing [现象 Y] at [条件 Z], and they are in good agreement with those [观测]. |

**语法点**：(1) "We propose/show/find + that 从句"是摘要主张句的黄金开头，比被动句更直接、比"it is shown that"更有作者在场感；(2) "A modification/extension of … for … is presented" 是方法改进类摘要的经典被动开篇，后接一句以"One of … is made to …"详述关键机制，形成"总—分"两句组；(3) 以"they are in good agreement with those micrographs"收尾时，用代词 *they* 回指 *results*、用 *those* 限定对比对象，避免重复名词，是英文摘要经济的指代策略。

### 🧩 经典案例逐句拆解

> "A two-dimensional cellular automaton model was developed for the simulation of nucleation and growth of ferrite grains at various cooling rates in low-carbon steels. The model calculates the diffusion of the solute and temperature fields in an explicit finite method and incorporates local temperature and concentration changes into a nucleation or growth function, which is utilized by the automaton in a probabilistic fashion."
> ——摘自 [[../papers/Zhang2002b]] 摘要。

① "A two-dimensional cellular automaton model was developed for the simulation of nucleation and growth of ferrite grains at various cooling rates in low-carbon steels."
② "The model calculates the diffusion of the solute and temperature fields in an explicit finite method and incorporates local temperature and concentration changes into a nucleation or growth function, which is utilized by the automaton in a probabilistic fashion."

**中文翻译**：
① 开发了一种二维元胞自动机模型，用于模拟低碳钢在不同冷速下铁素体晶粒的形核与长大。
② 该模型以显式有限元方法计算溶质场与温度场的扩散，并将局部温度与浓度变化纳入一个形核/长大函数，由元胞自动机以概率方式调用。

| 成分 | 句① | 句② |
|---|---|---|
| 主语 | A two-dimensional cellular automaton model | The model |
| 谓语 | was developed | calculates … and incorporates … |
| 目的/补充 | for the simulation of … at … in … | into a … function, which is utilized by … |
| 信息功能 | 交代"什么模型、做什么、在什么条件下" | 交代"如何做、耦合了哪些物理量、以什么方式调用" |

**核心教训**：摘要前两句是"模型论文"的黄金双句——第一句用被动语态把模型类型、模拟对象、工况条件压入一个名词短语；第二句立即切换到主动语态（The model calculates … and incorporates …），以并列动词把多物理场耦合讲清楚，最后用一个非限定性定语从句", which is utilized by … in a probabilistic fashion"点明算法性质。这种"被动开篇＋主动展开＋定语从句收束"的三拍结构，可直接套用到任何"建模＋模拟"类论文摘要，只要把 *cellular automaton* 替换为相场、有限元、KMC 等具体方法即可。

### 🧩 经典案例逐句拆解（第二例）

> "A modification of the nudged elastic band method for finding minimum energy paths is presented. One of the images is made to climb up along the elastic band to converge rigorously on the highest saddle point. Also, variable spring constants are used to increase the density of images near the top of the energy barrier to get an improved estimate of the reaction coordinate near the saddle point."
> ——摘自 [[../papers/henkelmanClimbingImageNudged2000c]] 摘要。

① "A modification of the nudged elastic band method for finding minimum energy paths is presented."
② "One of the images is made to climb up along the elastic band to converge rigorously on the highest saddle point."
③ "Also, variable spring constants are used to increase the density of images near the top of the energy barrier to get an improved estimate of the reaction coordinate near the saddle point."

**中文翻译**：
① 提出了对用于求最小能量路径的微动弹性带方法的一项改进。
② 让其中一个像沿弹性带爬升，以严格收敛到最高鞍点。
③ 此外使用可变弹簧常数来增加能垒顶部附近像的密度，从而更好地估计鞍点附近的反应坐标。

| 句 | 功能 | 语态与衔接 |
|---|---|---|
| ① | 总述改进对象与用途 | 被动 "is presented"，无作者主语 |
| ② | 第一项改进：climbing image | "One of the images is made to …" 使役被动 + to 不定式表目的 |
| ③ | 第二项改进：variable springs | "Also, … are used to increase … to get …" 双 to 短语层叠 |

**核心教训**：方法改进型摘要可在三句话内讲清"原方法+两项改进"。第一句总述（A modification of X for Y is presented），第二、三句分别以"One of … is made to …"与"Also, … are used to … to get …"介绍两项改进。注意两项目的的层次：句②用单 to 短语（to converge rigorously on …），句③用双 to 短语（to increase … to get …），后者体现"手段—中间目标—最终目标"的三层递进，非常适合描述数值算法。

## 🚪 2. Introduction & Scientific Background

本时段的引言遵循四步弧——重要性、前人进展、未解决问题、本研究目标——并通常用一个 "Although …, …" 从句在承认既有图景的同时点出缺口。CastroNeto2001charge 给出了模板："Although the phenomenological description of the CDW transition is well understood due to the work of McMillan, the current understanding of the microscopic origin of the CDW state is not complete," 一句话就把读者从 McMillan 平均场理论带到尚未解决的微观问题 [[../papers/CastroNeto2001charge]]。接下来两句制造核心张力："Fermi surface nesting, which is the main mechanism for CDW formation in 1D systems, leads to a lattice deformation, to the gapping of the Fermi surface and therefore to insulating behavior. Band structure calculations do not show, however, strong nesting at the Fermi surface of TMD," 谜题再被 "What is more striking is that TMD become superconductors at low temperatures." 叠加一层 [[../papers/CastroNeto2001charge]]。这种"教科书机制—however 翻转—第二重谜题叠加"的三拍钩子，是该时期物理论文中最高效的引言模式。

Zhang2002b 与 Zhang2003a 代表工业材料方向的对应写法。Zhang2002b 以 "Because microstructures of low-carbon steels, especially their grain sizes of ferrite, have strong influence on their mechanical properties, considerable industrial importance is placed on predicting γ–α phase transformation of steels during heat treatment" 开篇，用一个 because 引导的长句陈述工业驱动力 [[../papers/Zhang2002b]]。Zhang2003a 则用 "The problems dealing with phase transformations in low carbon steels, especially the transformation from austenite (γ) to ferrite (α), have attracted greater attention due to their industrial importance." 完成同样的工作 [[../papers/Zhang2003a]]。两篇随后都转向实验的局限——"Nevertheless, the complexity and difficulty of real time observation … limit the use of those techniques" 或 "However, the microstructure formation in steels is not yet fully understood because of …"——从而让建模成为自然出路 [[../papers/Zhang2002b]][[../papers/Zhang2003a]]。尤为醒目的是那句反问："If nucleation and growth of grains could be simulated by the CA technique in solidification, why could it not be used to predict the phase transformation, from austenite to ferrite, involved with nucleation and growth of ferrite grains in low-carbon steels?"——一句话就论证了方法迁移的合法性 [[../papers/Zhang2002b]]。

Delley2000 沿方法演进线索组织引言："This article describes the local orbital density functional (DF) method DMol3 which evolved from a gas phase molecular method to a method, which with its recent advances, encompasses possibilities for state of the art calculations on insulating and metallic solids." 句中 "evolved from … to …" 结构用一句话同时承载了历史与当前能力 [[../papers/Delley2000]]。Unknown2003charge 以具体的化学目标开篇："In this study, we prepared (BEDT-TTF)1.5CuX2 (X = Cl, Br) compounds in acetonitrile and examined the oxidation state of the copper metal ion since the complete reduction from copper(II) to copper(I) leads to a high electrical conductivity," 用 since 把行动与动机压缩在一起 [[../papers/Unknown2003charge]]。

纵观整个时段，缺口陈述集中在 "… is not complete / not yet fully understood / cannot describe …"，而目标陈述集中在 "The aim of the present work is to …" 与 "We propose that, due to …, …"。CastroNeto2001charge 的假设句——"We propose that, due to strong variations in the electron-phonon coupling and to imperfect nesting of the Fermi surface, the CDW gap is six-fold symmetric and has nodes (f-wave)."——把物理原因（due to …）与可证伪的主张（… is six-fold symmetric and has nodes）耦合在一起，是本语料库中最精巧的目标句 [[../papers/CastroNeto2001charge]]。

**语法点**：(1) "Although [已解决], [未解决]"是引言 gap 句的最高频模板，主句务必落在"未解决"那一端；(2) "Because …, considerable … importance is placed on …"用被动语态把重要性判断客观化，比直接说"this is important"更学术；(3) 反问句 "If X could …, why could it not …?" 在引言中能瞬间建立"方法迁移"的合理性，但全文只用一次，否则显得情绪化；(4) "due to"与"because of"后接名词短语，而"since/because"后接从句——CastroNeto2001charge 的 "due to the work of McMillan" 与 Unknown2003charge 的 "since the complete reduction … leads to …" 正是这两种结构的范例。

### 🧩 经典案例逐句拆解

> "Fermi surface nesting, which is the main mechanism for CDW formation in 1D systems, leads to a lattice deformation, to the gapping of the Fermi surface and therefore to insulating behavior. Band structure calculations do not show, however, strong nesting at the Fermi surface of TMD. What is more striking is that TMD become superconductors at low temperatures."
> ——摘自 [[../papers/CastroNeto2001charge]] 引言。

① "Fermi surface nesting, which is the main mechanism for CDW formation in 1D systems, leads to a lattice deformation, to the gapping of the Fermi surface and therefore to insulating behavior."
② "Band structure calculations do not show, however, strong nesting at the Fermi surface of TMD."
③ "What is more striking is that TMD become superconductors at low temperatures."

**中文翻译**：
① 费米面嵌套是一维体系中 CDW 形成的主要机制，它导致晶格畸变、费米面打开能隙，并因此产生绝缘行为。
② 然而，能带结构计算并未在 TMD 的费米面上显示出强嵌套。
③ 更引人注目的是，TMD 在低温下会变成超导体。

| 句 | 功能 | 句法要点 |
|---|---|---|
| ① | 陈述教科书机制 | 主语 + which 非限定定语从句 + leads to A, to B and therefore to C 三段式后果链 |
| ② | 用 however 翻转 | 否定句中插入 "however"（do not show, however, strong nesting） |
| ③ | 叠加第二重矛盾 | What is more striking is that …（主语从句作主语） |

**核心教训**：引言的"问题钩子"不必是一整段，三句话即可完成。先以一句"教科书机制 + 三段式后果链（leads to A, to B, and therefore to C）"建立读者预期；再以简短的否定句配插入式 however 直接戳破该机制在新体系中的失效；最后以"What is more striking is that …"叠加第二个看似矛盾的现象。三句之内，两个谜题同时立住，后续的"We propose that …"假设就显得是必然回应。写引言时若觉得铺垫过长，可优先检查是否能用这种"机制句＋however 否定句＋what is more striking 叠加句"三连击压缩前置篇幅。

### 🧩 经典案例逐句拆解（第二例）

> "Because microstructures of low-carbon steels, especially their grain sizes of ferrite, have strong influence on their mechanical properties, considerable industrial importance is placed on predicting γ – α phase transformation of steels during heat treatment. However, the microstructure formation in steels is not yet fully understood because of the complexity of the solid-state transformation involving the combined complex behavior of heat–mass transfer, nucleation and growth etc., in the liquid and/or solid states and their interfaces during the γ – α phase transformation."
> ——摘自 [[../papers/Zhang2002b]] 引言。

① "Because microstructures of low-carbon steels, especially their grain sizes of ferrite, have strong influence on their mechanical properties, considerable industrial importance is placed on predicting γ–α phase transformation of steels during heat treatment."
② "However, the microstructure formation in steels is not yet fully understood because of the complexity of the solid-state transformation involving the combined complex behavior of heat–mass transfer, nucleation and growth etc., in the liquid and/or solid states and their interfaces during the γ–α phase transformation."

**中文翻译**：
① 由于低碳钢的微观组织、尤其是铁素体晶粒尺寸对其力学性能有强烈影响，因此预测热处理过程中钢的 γ–α 相变具有重要的工业价值。
② 然而，由于固态转变的复杂性——涉及 γ–α 相变过程中液相和/或固相及其界面处传热传质、形核与长大等耦合的复杂行为——钢中微观组织的形成尚未被充分理解。

| 句 | 语篇功能 | 句法要点 |
|---|---|---|
| ① | 工业重要性 | Because + 因果从句 + 主句被动（importance is placed on -ing） |
| ② | 科学缺口 | However + 否定主句 + because of + 长名词短语 + involving … |

**核心教训**：工程类论文引言的"重要性—缺口"双句组可以用"Because …, considerable … importance is placed on … However, … is not yet fully understood because of the complexity of … involving …"一气呵成。句②的长度来自"because of + 名词短语 + involving + 多相/多过程并列"——把 A、B、C 三个耦合过程全部塞进一个 involving 短语，避免拆成多个短句而损失紧凑感。写引言时只要把"heat–mass transfer, nucleation and growth"替换为自己领域的耦合过程，即可复用。

### 🧩 经典案例逐句拆解（第三例）

> "The aim of the present work is to establish a two-dimensional CA model that predicts nucleation and growth of ferrite grains for low carbon steels Fe–Xi–C. If nucleation and growth of grains could be simulated by the CA technique in solidification, why could it not be used to predict the phase transformation, from austenite to ferrite, involved with nucleation and growth of ferrite grains in low-carbon steels?"
> ——摘自 [[../papers/Zhang2002b]] 引言末尾。

① "The aim of the present work is to establish a two-dimensional CA model that predicts nucleation and growth of ferrite grains for low carbon steels Fe–Xi–C."
② "If nucleation and growth of grains could be simulated by the CA technique in solidification, why could it not be used to predict the phase transformation, from austenite to ferrite, involved with nucleation and growth of ferrite grains in low-carbon steels?"

**中文翻译**：
① 本工作旨在建立一个二维 CA 模型，用于预测低碳钢 Fe–Xi–C 中铁素体晶粒的形核与长大。
② 既然 CA 技术可以模拟凝固过程中晶粒的形核长大，为何不能用来预测低碳钢中同样涉及铁素体形核长大的奥氏体—铁素体相变？

| 句 | 功能 | 语气 |
|---|---|---|
| ① | 明示目标 | 陈述句，"The aim … is to …" |
| ② | 方法迁移的合法性论证 | 反问句，"If X could …, why could it not …?" |

**核心教训**：引言结尾陈述目标之后，可跟一个反问句为方法迁移正名。陈述句承担"我要做什么"的契约功能，反问句则承担"为什么这样做合理"的说服功能，二者形成"声明—辩护"的节奏。但反问句在整篇论文中只用一次，多则显得情绪化。

## ⚙️ 3. Key Methodological Developments

本时段的方法类文字可分为四组：计算框架、模型假设、参数设定与实验表征。Delley2000 是框架陈述的范本。"The DMol method relies fully on numerical integration in 3D for all matrix elements" 用一句话点明方法论基石 [[../papers/Delley2000]]。"The present method uses basis functions from numerical atoms or more general central field systems" 与 "Localized basis functions almost automatically lead to sparse one-electron Hamiltonian matrices for large systems" 随后说明基组来源及其标度优势；"The Brillouin-zone integrations over all occupied orbitals are done basically with equispaced Fourier meshes similar to the ones proposed by Monkhorst and Pack" 则把数值选择锚定到一个经典文献 [[../papers/Delley2000]]。CastroNeto2001charge 把解析与数值交织在一起："Numerical studies have shown that gλ(k, q) can vanish at certain points in the Brillouin zone" 为节结构提供动机，"A simple tight-binding calculation gives, gλ(k, q) = …" 给出解析形式 [[../papers/CastroNeto2001charge]]。

Zhang2003a 提供了假设段的完整模板。"For the CA algorithm, it must preliminarily be determined that: (1) the boundary conditions …, (2) the neighborhood …, (3) the states …, (4) the initial configuration …, (5) the nucleation rules, (6) the growth rules, and (7) the time step" 在一个编号列表中枚举了七个设计维度 [[../papers/Zhang2003a]]。"It could be assumed, as observed in experiments, that ferrite grains nucleate preferentially on grain boundaries of austenite" 把一项假设锚定到实验观察 [[../papers/Zhang2002b]]。"With respect to an Fe–Xi–C multicomponent alloy with a given low carbon concentration c, Fe–Xi could be regarded as a super-element S; thus the multicomponent S–C alloy is similar to a low-carbon Fe–C alloy" 展示了如何用 "regarded as / thus / similar to" 把多元体系化简为伪二元体系 [[../papers/Zhang2002b]]。在参数设定方面，"In the present model, the CA lattice is chosen as a two-dimensional 200 × 200 hexagonal one, representing a 125 × 125 μm² sample, with the periodic boundary conditions" 用一个名词短语同时给出维度、分辨率、几何、物理尺寸与边界条件 [[../papers/Zhang2003a]]；"The temperature change of every cell will be T→T − Q·Δt, where Δt is the time step" 则用 where 从句就地定义符号 [[../papers/Zhang2003a]]。

表征类句子来自 Unknown2003charge。"EPR spectra measurements were carried out for the solution samples (DMF/CH2Cl2 = 50/50) at 77 K using a ESP-300S EPR spectrometer at the X-band frequency" 把样品、温度、仪器与波段压缩进一个被动句 [[../papers/Unknown2003charge]]。"Magnetic susceptibility data was collected from 4 K to 300 K using the MPMS7 … by the SQUID method" 与 "Infrared (IR) spectra (400–4000 cm⁻¹) were obtained using KBr pellets with a Polaris FT-IR spectrophotometer …" 复用同一模板，使之成为方法语料中最可直接迁移的骨架 [[../papers/Unknown2003charge]]。Henkelman 的 CI-NEB 方法描述采用"一般主张—具体机制"配对——"A modification … is presented. One of the images is made to climb up …"——同时服务于摘要与方法 [[../papers/henkelmanClimbingImageNudged2000c]]。全息聚合工作中的制备方法则被有意与更具选择性的路线对照："The technique does not rely on the selectivity of particular etching processes or on the necessity of growing fibers by means of chemical vapor deposition," 这是一句以"所避免之物"来界定方法的否定式能力句 [[../papers/Khitrov2002internal]]。

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|---|---|---|
| "The DMol method relies fully on numerical integration in 3D for all matrix elements." [[../papers/Delley2000]] | DMol 方法在所有矩阵元上完全依赖三维数值积分。 | The [方法 X] relies fully on [核心技术 Y] for [对象 Z]. |
| "For the CA algorithm, it must preliminarily be determined that: (1) the boundary conditions …, (2) the neighborhood …, (3) the states …, (4) the initial configuration …, (5) the nucleation rules, (6) the growth rules, and (7) the time step." [[../papers/Zhang2003a]] | 对于 CA 算法，必须预先确定：(1) 边界条件，(2) 邻域，(3) 元胞状态，(4) 初始构型，(5) 形核规则，(6) 长大规则，(7) 时间步长。 | For the [算法 X], it must preliminarily be determined that: (1) …, (2) …, [七要素编号列表]. |
| "EPR spectra measurements were carried out for the solution samples (DMF/CH2Cl2 = 50/50) at 77 K using a ESP-300S EPR spectrometer at the X-band frequency." [[../papers/Unknown2003charge]] | 在 77 K 下，用 ESP-300S EPR 谱仪在 X 波段对溶液样品（DMF/CH2Cl2=50/50）进行 EPR 谱测量。 | [表征 X] measurements were carried out for [样品] (条件) at [温度] using [仪器] at [波段/参数]. |

**语法点**：(1) "rely fully on / be based on / use"三词轮换可以避免方法段中"uses"的重复；(2) "it must preliminarily be determined that …"是正式体的无人称要求句，比"we must determine"更客观；(3) 表征段的"measurements were carried out for [样品] at [温度] using [仪器] at [参数]"是英文论文方法部分最稳定的被动模板，几乎可逐词替换；(4) "where Δt is the time step"这种就地符号定义的 where 从句，应紧跟在首次出现公式之后，不要把符号定义集中到文末。

### 🧩 经典案例逐句拆解

> "In the present model, the CA lattice is chosen as a two-dimensional 200 × 200 hexagonal one, representing a 125 × 125 μm² sample, with the periodic boundary conditions. The choice of this kind of lattice could reduce the anisotropy caused by a CA lattice in the modeling."
> ——摘自 [[../papers/Zhang2003a]] 方法部分。

① "In the present model, the CA lattice is chosen as a two-dimensional 200 × 200 hexagonal one, representing a 125 × 125 μm² sample, with the periodic boundary conditions."
② "The choice of this kind of lattice could reduce the anisotropy caused by a CA lattice in the modeling."

**中文翻译**：
① 在本模型中，CA 网格被选为 200×200 的二维六角网格，代表一个 125×125 μm² 的样品，并采用周期性边界条件。
② 选择这种网格可以减小建模中由 CA 网格引起的各向异性。

| 成分 | 句① | 句② |
|---|---|---|
| 句首状语 | In the present model | The choice of this kind of lattice |
| 被动/主动 | is chosen as …, representing …, with … | could reduce … caused by … |
| 信息负载 | 维度+分辨率+形状+物理尺寸+边界条件 | 解释"为什么是六角网格" |

**核心教训**：方法段的参数规格句不要只给数字，要紧接着一句解释"为什么这样选"。句①用"chosen as + 维度 + 分辨率 + 几何 + representing + 尺寸 + with + 边界条件"六段式把数值交代完毕；句②以"The choice of this kind of lattice could reduce … caused by …"补一句因果辩护，把"数值选择"升级为"有物理依据的设计决策"。这一"参数句+辩护句"的双句组在所有建模/计算论文中均可复用。

### 🧩 经典案例逐句拆解（第二例）

> "The present method uses basis functions from numerical atoms or more general central field systems. Localized basis functions almost automatically lead to sparse one-electron Hamiltonian matrices for large systems. To calculate the DFT Hamiltonian and overlap matrices for the k representations in a lattice there are two basic approaches. The Brillouin-zone integrations over all occupied orbitals are done basically with equispaced Fourier meshes similar to the ones proposed by Monkhorst and Pack."
> ——摘自 [[../papers/Delley2000]] 方法部分。

① "The present method uses basis functions from numerical atoms or more general central field systems."
② "Localized basis functions almost automatically lead to sparse one-electron Hamiltonian matrices for large systems."
③ "To calculate the DFT Hamiltonian and overlap matrices for the k representations in a lattice there are two basic approaches."
④ "The Brillouin-zone integrations over all occupied orbitals are done basically with equispaced Fourier meshes similar to the ones proposed by Monkhorst and Pack."

**中文翻译**：
① 本方法使用来自数值原子或更一般中心力场系统的基函数。
② 对大体系而言，局域基函数几乎自然地导致稀疏的单电子哈密顿矩阵。
③ 为计算晶格中 k 表象下的 DFT 哈密顿矩阵与重叠矩阵，存在两种基本途径。
④ 对所有占据轨道的布里渊区积分，基本采用等间距 Fourier 网格完成，类似于 Monkhorst 与 Pack 所提出的方案。

| 句 | 方法要素 | 句法标记 |
|---|---|---|
| ① | 基函数来源 | uses X from Y or Z |
| ② | 局域基的优势 | almost automatically lead to [有利性质] for large systems |
| ③ | 算法分支预告 | To [任务] there are two basic approaches. |
| ④ | 具体选择与文献锚定 | are done basically with [方案], similar to the ones proposed by [文献] |

**核心教训**：计算方法段的四步走是"基函数—优势—分支—具体选择并锚定文献"。句④特别值得模仿：在描述自己的数值方案时，用"similar to the ones proposed by [经典文献]"既表达了对前人的尊重，也让审稿人无需追问出处。注意"the ones"替代重复出现的"meshes"，是英文避免重复名词的常用指代。

### 🧩 经典案例逐句拆解（第三例）

> "The model involves the following assumptions. It could be assumed, as observed in experiments, that ferrite grains nucleate preferentially on grain boundaries of austenite. With respect to an Fe–Xi–C multicomponent alloy with a given low carbon concentration c, Fe–Xi could be regarded as a super-element S; thus the multicomponent S–C alloy is similar to a low-carbon Fe–C alloy. Each cell in the CA lattice takes a state: α, γ, or 'α/γ interface'."
> ——综合自 [[../papers/Zhang2003a]] 与 [[../papers/Zhang2002b]] 方法部分。

① "The model involves the following assumptions."
② "It could be assumed, as observed in experiments, that ferrite grains nucleate preferentially on grain boundaries of austenite."
③ "With respect to an Fe–Xi–C multicomponent alloy with a given low carbon concentration c, Fe–Xi could be regarded as a super-element S; thus the multicomponent S–C alloy is similar to a low-carbon Fe–C alloy."
④ "Each cell in the CA lattice takes a state: α, γ, or 'α/γ interface'."

**中文翻译**：
① 该模型包含如下假设。
② 正如实验所观察到的，可以假设铁素体晶粒优先在奥氏体晶界上形核。
③ 对于具有给定低碳浓度 c 的 Fe–Xi–C 多元合金，可将 Fe–Xi 视为一个超级组元 S；因而该多元 S–C 合金与低碳 Fe–C 合金相似。
④ CA 网格中的每个元胞取 α、γ 或"α/γ 界面"三种状态之一。

| 句 | 假设类型 | 辩护方式 |
|---|---|---|
| ① | 假设段总起 | "involves the following assumptions" |
| ② | 形核位置假设 | 以"as observed in experiments"求实验支持 |
| ③ | 多元体系化简假设 | 以"With respect to …, X could be regarded as Y; thus …"逻辑推导 |
| ④ | 元胞状态枚举 | 以冒号直接列出离散状态 |

**核心教训**：模型假设段的开篇"The model involves the following assumptions."几乎是所有计算建模论文的通用句。每条假设都应附带一句辩护：要么引用实验观察（as observed in experiments），要么给出逻辑推导（thus … similar to …），从而把"假设"提升为"有依据的简化"。假设枚举完毕后，应紧接一句状态/变量定义句（如"Each cell takes a state: A, B, or C"），让读者明确离散变量空间。

## 📊 4. Core Results & Physical Mechanisms

本时段的结果类文字主要使用三种句型：图件引导句（"In Fig. X we plot …"）、定量比较句（"within X % / by about X mHa"）与机制解释句（"This can be explained on the basis of …"）。CastroNeto2001charge 把三者交织在一起。"It is easy to see that gλ(k, Q1) vanishes along the nodal lines in Fig.2" 以轻巧的指示语引导读者读图 [[../papers/CastroNeto2001charge]]。"The conclusion is that the CDW gap will change sign in six points along the Fermi surface leading to the situation depicted on Fig.2. This is the f-wave CDW state" 随后为物理态命名 [[../papers/CastroNeto2001charge]]。"In Fig.3 we plot our results against the experimental data. The agreement is remarkable given the simplicity of the model" 用 "given + 名词短语" 在承认模型简化的同时宣称吻合 [[../papers/CastroNeto2001charge]]。在定量关系方面，"As we can see the relevant parameter which controls the superconducting transition is a/c" 之后接 "In Fig.1 we plot the transition temperatures as a function of this parameter. We can clearly see the anti-correlation between Tcdw and Tc"，用三个连续句子排列控制参数、坐标轴与结论；"These results are valid for ω ≪ Max[|Δcdw|] ≈ 60 meV" 则坦诚地给出有效性能量窗口 [[../papers/CastroNeto2001charge]]。

Delley2000 是 DFT 基准文字的范本。"The calculated equilibrium lattice constant is fixed within 0.1% for Rc > 9 a.u." 给出收敛精度 [[../papers/Delley2000]]。"It is gratifying to see that two different all electron implementations of DFT like DMol3 and FLAPW come to agree to within a fraction of a mHartree for energies, 0.1% for lattice constants, and 3% for the bulk modulus, when using the same density functional" 在一句话中枚举了两种方法在三个可观测量上的偏差 [[../papers/Delley2000]]。"The agreement with experiment is by no means as good as the different density functional results among themselves" 坦诚地把方法间一致性与实验一致性区分开 [[../papers/Delley2000]]；"An upshot of these benchmark calculations is that the larger part of uncertainty in the results is due to limitations in the presently available functionals, and not with limitations of the present method of calculation" 则把基准提升为对整个领域的判断 [[../papers/Delley2000]]。

Zhang2003a 追踪微观组织演化。"The computed results shows that a larger cooling rate is helpful for obtaining finer ferrite grains" 与 "The figure indicates that the quicker the cooling rate is, the finer the final grain size of ferrite is after the phase transformation" 用"越……越……"比较结构强化冷速—晶粒尺寸关系 [[../papers/Zhang2003a]]。"Each curve undergoes a rapid increase of n_nuc with ΔT in zone I, and becomes saturated in zone II" 用单一主语加两个动词描述两阶段曲线 [[../papers/Zhang2003a]]。"Physically, it means that the final nucleation number is a function of the cooling rate, and the nucleation procedure has already finished in zone I at the early stage of undercooling" 以 "Physically, it means that …" 把曲线翻译为物理命题 [[../papers/Zhang2003a]]。机制句呈现两种语气：CastroNeto 的链式推进——"Besides leading to damping, the phonons generate a retarded interaction. This retarded interaction, like in an ordinary superconductor, leads to pairing in the singlet channel"；以及 Zhang 的一句话归约——"Simply stated, diffusion affects nucleation of ferrite"，其后接 "In general, the diffusion of the solute and temperature fields plays an important role in nucleation and growth, or nucleation and growth is diffusion-controlled" [[../papers/CastroNeto2001charge]][[../papers/Zhang2002b]]。Unknown2003charge 构筑了一条证据链："This result can be explained on the basis of the fact that the existence of copper(II) metal decreases the conductivity due to the Coulomb interaction between …" 与 "The observation that the Curie-Weiss law fit the data well indicates that the localized electrons in the copper(II) ions dominate the magnetic properties at low temperatures" 用 "the fact that …" 与 "the observation that …" 把证据绑定到结论 [[../papers/Unknown2003charge]]。

## 💬 5. Discussion

本时段的讨论段做三类工作：向外比较、向内深挖、向外展望。向外比较以 CastroNeto2001charge 为代表："As we have shown there are many similarities between the problem discussed here and HTC. Perhaps one of the most interesting is the anti-correlation between the Tcdw and Tc," 把该体系与高温铜氧化物超导体并列，使工作的意义漫溢到更大的领域 [[../papers/CastroNeto2001charge]]。"The growth of Tc under pressure is also consistent with our picture" 引入一项独立的外部观察 [[../papers/CastroNeto2001charge]]；"The main difference, of course, is that at Tcdw there is a true second order phase transition while the pseudo-gap temperature seems to be a crossover energy scale" 用 "of course" 承认类比的边界 [[../papers/CastroNeto2001charge]]。Delley2000 采取了罕见的坦率语气："This result is in contradiction with the GGA result from Ref. 28. However, the author is confident that qualitative confirmations of the present result could be found in a number of waste-paper baskets already" 用黑色幽默回应文献矛盾而不放弃主张 [[../papers/Delley2000]]；"It is interesting to compare total energies of graphite and diamond" 与 "The present result is not documented in the literature yet, it is not a complete surprise" 则把缺乏先例转化为有分寸的主张 [[../papers/Delley2000]]。Zhang2003a 把结果提升为机制判断："In this transformation, there exists competition between nucleation and the growth of ferrite" 与 "This reveals that at the beginning of this transformation, the competition is drastic at a large cooling rate" 在"竞争"这一单一概念下统合冷速、形核与长大 [[../papers/Zhang2003a]]。"To make the ferrite grain size uniform and fine, increasing nucleation number and controlling growth of ferrite are two efficient ways" 随后引出工程启示 [[../papers/Zhang2002b]]；"Because the diffusion of the temperature field is much quicker than that of the solute field and the temperature distribution becomes quickly uniform to the small modeled specimen, the diffusion of the solute field has larger influence on nucleation and growth of ferrite" 在一个长因果句中确定溶质扩散（而非热扩散）为速率控制步骤 [[../papers/Zhang2002b]]。Unknown2003charge 把讨论组织为证据链："This observation leads to the conclusion that the BEDT-TTF is partially ionized and that the copper metals are in the mixed valence Cu(II/I) states" 用两个并列的 that 从句承载复合结论 [[../papers/Unknown2003charge]]，并由 "This low magnetic moment is additional evidence for the mixed valence copper(II/I) states" 加以强化 [[../papers/Unknown2003charge]]。三种讨论语法反复出现：类比（"similarities between … and …"）、因果归因（"Because …, … has larger influence on …"）、证据叠加（"This observation leads to … / additional evidence for …"）。

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|---|---|---|
| "As we have shown there are many similarities between the problem discussed here and HTC. Perhaps one of the most interesting is the anti-correlation between the Tcdw and Tc." [[../papers/CastroNeto2001charge]] | 正如我们所示，这里讨论的问题与 HTC 之间存在许多相似之处。也许最有趣的一点是 Tcdw 与 Tc 之间的反相关。 | As we have shown there are many similarities between [问题 A] and [问题 B]. Perhaps one of the most interesting is [具体相似点]. |
| "An upshot of these benchmark calculations is that the larger part of uncertainty in the results is due to limitations in the presently available functionals, and not with limitations of the present method of calculation." [[../papers/Delley2000]] | 这些基准计算的一个结论是，结果中大部分不确定性来自当前可用泛函的局限，而非本计算方法的局限。 | An upshot of these [计算/实验] is that the larger part of [不确定性/误差] is due to [原因 A], and not with [原因 B]. |
| "Because the diffusion of the temperature field is much quicker than that of the solute field and the temperature distribution becomes quickly uniform to the small modeled specimen, the diffusion of the solute field has larger influence on nucleation and growth of ferrite." [[../papers/Zhang2002b]] | 由于温度场的扩散比溶质场快得多，且在小尺寸模型试样中温度分布迅速均匀化，因此溶质场扩散对铁素体形核长大的影响更大。 | Because [过程 A] is much quicker than [过程 B] and [附加条件], [过程 B] has larger influence on [结果]. |

**语法点**：(1) "An upshot of … is that …"是"the result shows that"的高级替代，暗含"经过一番分析后浮现的结论"之意；(2) "A is due to X, and not with Y"这种"肯定一项＋否定一项"的并列结构能清晰归因，注意否定端的介词不必与肯定端相同（due to / with），但读起来要自然；(3) "Perhaps one of the most interesting is …"是讨论段引出重点的礼貌方式，比"the most important is"更克制；(4) 在长因果句中，若原因从句含两个并列命题（A and B），主句前应加逗号以划定层次。

|---|---|---|
| ① | 建立类比 | As we have shown, there are many similarities between A and B. |

### 🧩 经典案例逐句拆解（第二例）

> "This result is in contradiction with the GGA result from Ref. 28. However, the author is confident that qualitative confirmations of the present result could be found in a number of waste-paper baskets already. The present result is not documented in the literature yet, it is not a complete surprise."
> ——摘自 [[../papers/Delley2000]] 讨论段。

① "This result is in contradiction with the GGA result from Ref. 28."
② "However, the author is confident that qualitative confirmations of the present result could be found in a number of waste-paper baskets already."
③ "The present result is not documented in the literature yet, it is not a complete surprise."

**中文翻译**：
① 该结果与文献 28 中的 GGA 结果相矛盾。
② 然而，作者相信在许多废纸篓里已经能找到对本文结果的定性印证。
③ 本结果尚未见诸文献，但也并非完全出人意料。

| 句 | 语篇动作 | 语气 |
|---|---|---|
| ① | 坦承矛盾 | "is in contradiction with" |
| ② | 自信回应 | "the author is confident that … could be found in waste-paper baskets" |
| ③ | 克制收束 | "not documented …, it is not a complete surprise" |

**核心教训**：当自己的结果与已发表文献矛盾时，最糟糕的做法是回避。Delley 给出了一个高级模板：先直接点名矛盾（in contradiction with Ref. N），再以一句带幽默的自信句回应（"could be found in waste-paper baskets"暗指他人算错后丢弃的结果），最后以"not documented …, not a complete surprise"克制收束。这种"承认矛盾—不失风度地辩护—留有余地"的三句组既不冒犯审稿人，也不放弃自己的判断。

### 🧩 经典案例逐句拆解（第三例）

> "In this transformation, there exists competition between nucleation and the growth of ferrite. This reveals that at the beginning of this transformation, the competition is drastic at a large cooling rate. To make the ferrite grain size uniform and fine, increasing nucleation number and controlling growth of ferrite are two efficient ways."
> ——摘自 [[../papers/Zhang2003a]] 与 [[../papers/Zhang2002b]] 讨论段。

① "In this transformation, there exists competition between nucleation and the growth of ferrite."
② "This reveals that at the beginning of this transformation, the competition is drastic at a large cooling rate."
③ "To make the ferrite grain size uniform and fine, increasing nucleation number and controlling growth of ferrite are two efficient ways."

**中文翻译**：
① 在该转变中，铁素体的形核与长大之间存在竞争。
② 这表明在转变开始阶段，冷速较大时竞争尤为激烈。
③ 为使铁素体晶粒尺寸均匀细小，增加形核数与控制铁素体长大是两条有效途径。

| 句 | 层次 | 关键结构 |
|---|---|---|
| ① | 提出竞争概念 | "there exists competition between A and B" |
| ② | 条件化竞争 | "This reveals that at [阶段], the competition is [形容词] at [条件]" |
| ③ | 给出工程对策 | "To make [目标状态], [手段 A] and [手段 B] are two efficient ways" |

**核心教训**：工程类论文讨论段在揭示机制后应给出可操作建议。句①以"there exists competition between A and B"提出核心概念，句②以"This reveals that …"把概念条件化（何时、何种条件下激烈），句③以"To make …, [手段] are two efficient ways"把科学发现翻译成工程对策。这一"概念—条件—对策"三步结构让讨论不止于解释，更具有指导意义。

## 🏁 6. Conclusion

本时段的结论几乎一致遵循三步收束：总结发现、投射意义、给出预言或工程建议。CastroNeto2001charge 以 "In summary, we have proposed a microscopic theory for the CDW and superconducting phases of TMD that involve the formation of a gapless CDW state," 开篇，用 in summary 加现在完成时标记全局性主张 [[../papers/CastroNeto2001charge]]。随后以 "We show that our theory can explain the available experimental data including ARPES" 陈述解释范围 [[../papers/CastroNeto2001charge]]，以 "The similarities between the phenomena described here and the one observed in other layered systems (such as HTC) is striking and the study of such exotic CDW might illuminate the way for the understanding of the complex physics in transition metal systems" 投射意义 [[../papers/CastroNeto2001charge]]，最后以可证伪的预言 "We predict that high-resolution ARPES should be able to measure the modulation of the CDW gap around the Fermi surface and the opening of a superconducting gap at the Dirac points" 收束 [[../papers/CastroNeto2001charge]]。这四句话构成"理论—解释—意义—预言"的闭环，是理论物理论文的范本结论。

Delley2000 沿方法论文路线收束："It is expected that the present method will prove useful and efficient in finding density functional results for molecules, solids, and in particular, surfaces with molecules," 用 "It is expected that … will prove useful and efficient in …" 作出有分寸的前瞻 [[../papers/Delley2000]]。Zhang2003a 给出标准的建模论文收束："We have employed a two-dimensional cellular automaton model to investigate the transformation from austenite to ferrite in low carbon steels during continuous cooling"（重述方法）[[../papers/Zhang2003a]]；"Based on this model, we quantitatively demonstrate how the microstructural evolution depends dynamically on the combined behavior of nucleation and the growth of ferrite in this transformation"（重述核心定量贡献）[[../papers/Zhang2003a]]；"Moreover, the way the cooling conditions affect the competition between nucleation and growth of ferrite is also quantified"（以 Moreover 标记一项附加贡献）[[../papers/Zhang2003a]]；以及 "By these simulations, we believe that this modeling based on CA algorithm could be of great help to effectively control this transformation from austenite to ferrite in low carbon steels"（以 "we believe that … could be of great help" 给出工程价值判断）[[../papers/Zhang2003a]]。一个被频繁复用的通用收束是："With the same initial grain size, the final morphology of ferrite depends mainly on the cooling rate during the phase transformation. A larger cooling rate is a great help to obtain finer ferrite grains in the CA model, which is in agreement with experiment," 把控制变量、主要结果与实验吻合压缩进两句话 [[../papers/Zhang2003a]]。Unknown2003charge 与 Zhang 系列还使用了断言式收束："The phase transformation of the low carbon steel Fe–Xi–C is proven to be a mass–heat diffusion-controlled process, and the diffusion processes have an important influence on the nucleation and growth, and in turn, the microstructure formation of ferrite grains," 其中 "is proven to be" 把全部论证结晶为一个定性判断 [[../papers/Zhang2003a]]；以及化学特有的收束："Furthermore, it can be concluded that the ratio of BEDT-TTF to CuX2 should be more than 2 per formula unit to reduce copper(II) completely to copper(I), leading to a high electrical conductivity" 给出具体的化学计量建议 [[../papers/Unknown2003charge]]。本时段没有任何结论引入新数据或新图；每一句结论都在重铸、提升或外推正文中已有的结果。

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|---|---|---|
| "In summary, we have proposed a microscopic theory for the CDW and superconducting phases of TMD that involve the formation of a gapless CDW state." [[../papers/CastroNeto2001charge]] | 总之，我们为 TMD 的 CDW 相与超导相提出了一套微观理论，其中涉及无禁带 CDW 态的形成。 | In summary, we have [过去分词：proposed/shown/demonstrated] a [理论/模型] for [体系/现象] that involve(s) [关键机制]. |
| "We predict that high-resolution ARPES should be able to measure the modulation of the CDW gap around the Fermi surface and the opening of a superconducting gap at the Dirac points." [[../papers/CastroNeto2001charge]] | 我们预测高分辨 ARPES 应能测量 CDW 能隙沿费米面的调制，以及 Dirac 点处超导能隙的打开。 | We predict that [实验手段 X] should be able to measure [可观测量 A] and [可观测量 B]. |
| "By these simulations, we believe that this modeling based on CA algorithm could be of great help to effectively control this transformation from austenite to ferrite in low carbon steels." [[../papers/Zhang2003a]] | 通过这些模拟，我们相信这种基于 CA 算法的建模能极大地帮助有效控制低碳钢中奥氏体—铁素体相变。 | By these [模拟/实验], we believe that this [方法] based on [算法] could be of great help to [工程目标]. |

**语法点**：(1) "In summary, we have proposed …"用现在完成时回顾全文贡献，比"We propose"更有"已完成工作"的仪式感；(2) "We predict that X should be able to measure Y and Z"中 should 不是义务情态，而是"it is reasonable to expect"的预测用法，比 will 更克制；(3) "could be of great help to …"是"can greatly help"的名词化正式写法，在工程类论文结论中常见；(4) "It is expected that … will prove useful"是被动式的温和预言，适合方法学论文，避免与商业宣传语气混淆。

### 🧩 经典案例逐句拆解

> "In summary, we have proposed a microscopic theory for the CDW and superconducting phases of TMD that involve the formation of a gapless CDW state. We show that our theory can explain the available experimental data including ARPES. The similarities between the phenomena described here and the one observed in other layered systems (such as HTC) is striking and the study of such exotic CDW might illuminate the way for the understanding of the complex physics in transition metal systems. We predict that high-resolution ARPES should be able to measure the modulation of the CDW gap around the Fermi surface and the opening of a superconducting gap at the Dirac points."
> ——摘自 [[../papers/CastroNeto2001charge]] 结论段。

① "In summary, we have proposed a microscopic theory for the CDW and superconducting phases of TMD that involve the formation of a gapless CDW state."
② "We show that our theory can explain the available experimental data including ARPES."
③ "The similarities between the phenomena described here and the one observed in other layered systems (such as HTC) is striking and the study of such exotic CDW might illuminate the way for the understanding of the complex physics in transition metal systems."
④ "We predict that high-resolution ARPES should be able to measure the modulation of the CDW gap around the Fermi surface and the opening of a superconducting gap at the Dirac points."

**中文翻译**：
① 总之，我们为 TMD 的 CDW 相与超导相提出了一套涉及无禁带 CDW 态形成的微观理论。
② 我们表明该理论能解释包括 ARPES 在内的现有实验数据。
③ 这里描述的现象与其他层状体系（如 HTC）中观察到的现象之间的相似性令人瞩目，对此类奇异 CDW 的研究或许能为理解过渡金属体系中的复杂物理照亮道路。
④ 我们预测高分辨 ARPES 应能测量 CDW 能隙沿费米面的调制以及 Dirac 点处超导能隙的打开。

| 句 | 结论动作 | 句法标记 |
|---|---|---|
| ① | 重述主张 | In summary, we have proposed … |
| ② | 交代解释力 | We show that … can explain … including … |
| ③ | 意义外推 | The similarities … is striking and … might illuminate the way for … |
| ④ | 给出可证伪预言 | We predict that … should be able to measure … and … |

**核心教训**：一个完整的理论物理论文结论应当包含四个动作——"主张（proposed）—解释力（explain）—意义（illuminate the way）—预言（predict）"。这四句话每一句都对应一个可识别的语篇动作，且动词不重复（proposed / explain / illuminate / predict）。写结论时最忌讳只重复结果而不给出可检验预言或意义外推；CastroNeto2001charge 提供了一个可直接套用的四句话模板，任何理论/计算论文都可以把"microscopic theory"替换为自己的核心贡献、把"ARPES"替换为自己领域的实验手段即可。

## 🧬 7. Evolution（综述专属）

2000–2004 时间段内本笔记库收录的严格意义上的综述文章数量有限。CastroNeto2001charge 虽以研究论文形式呈现，却在引言与讨论中承担了部分综述功能：它回顾了自 McMillan 以来的 CDW 唯象理论、对比了一维费米面嵌套机制与二维 TMD 能带结构的差异，并把 TMD 中的 CDW–超导共存与高温超导体的赝能隙–超导相图并列考察，形成了一段事实上的小型演进综述 [[../papers/CastroNeto2001charge]]。其典型综述句式是："The quasi-two-dimensional (2D) transition metal dichalcogenides (TMD) have been extensively studied since their discovery."以"have been extensively studied since + 时间起点"承担领域史叙述功能 [[../papers/CastroNeto2001charge]]；以及"The main difference, of course, is that at Tcdw there is a true second order phase transition while the pseudo-gap temperature seems to be a crossover energy scale."通过跨体系比较勾勒概念演化 [[../papers/CastroNeto2001charge]]。

Delley2000 则在方法学层面带有综述色彩："This article describes the local orbital density functional (DF) method DMol3 which evolved from a gas phase molecular method to a method, which with its recent advances, encompasses possibilities for state of the art calculations on insulating and metallic solids."一句"evolved from … to …"勾勒了 DMol3 从分子到固体的演进路径 [[../papers/Delley2000]]，并通过与 FLAPW 的基准比较隐式综述了当时全电子 DFT 方法的格局 [[../papers/Delley2000]]。Zhang2002b 与 Zhang2003a 的引言段也部分承担了微观组织模拟领域的综述职责："In recent years, modeling of microstructural formation in material processing has markedly progressed and can be computed fairly readily and with some degree of reliability based on cellular automaton (CA) and Monte Carlo."一句话归纳了 CA 与 Monte Carlo 两条路线的近期进展 [[../papers/Zhang2003a]]，随后以"If nucleation and growth of grains could be simulated by the CA technique in solidification, why could it not be used to predict the phase transformation …"指出从凝固到固态相变的方法迁移趋势 [[../papers/Zhang2002b]]。

由于该时段缺乏专门的长篇综述，这里仅记录三类可复用的"准综述"骨架：(1) 领域史开篇——"[体系] have been extensively studied since [时间/事件]"；(2) 方法演进——"[方法] evolved from [起点] to [当前能力], which with its recent advances, encompasses …"；(3) 跨体系比较——"The main difference, of course, is that [体系 A] … while [体系 B] seems to …"。待后续时间段（2005 年以后）收录真正的综述文章后，本节将补充"概念演进树"与"主题年表"两类综述专属结构。
