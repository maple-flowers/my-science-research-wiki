# Cross-Cutting Writing Patterns

> [!INFO] 跨段高频表达索引
> 本页从全部 187 篇带双语转写的论文中，横向聚合反复出现的英文用词、短语与句式，按**修辞功能**（不是按 IMRaD 章节）组织。每个模式给出**骨架**、**真实例句**与**来源**。来源链接一律指向 `[[../papers/<citekey>]]`。
>
> 频率标记：**HIGH** = 在 5 个以上五年段反复出现；**MED** = 在 3–4 个五年段出现；**LOW** = 集中在某一时期或某一子领域。

---

## 1. 开场与定位 (Opening & Positioning)

### 1.1 领域重要性开场

**骨架**：`<Phenomenon> has attracted considerable/great/significant attention/interest in recent years <due to/because of> <reason>.`

| 实例 | 直译 | 来源 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| "Two-dimensional multiferroic materials have garnered significant attention due to their exceptional electronic properties and promising potential for magnetoelectric sensors, non-volatile data storages, and ultrafast switches." | 二维多铁材料因其卓越的电子性质以及在磁电传感器、非易失性存储和超快开关方面的应用潜力而备受关注。 | [[../papers/zhongHighthroughputExfoliationMultiferroic2025]] |
| "The problems dealing with phase transformations in low carbon steels, especially the transformation from austenite (γ) to ferrite (α), have attracted greater attention due to their industrial importance." | 涉及低碳钢相变——尤其是奥氏体向铁素体转变——的问题因其工业重要性而受到更多关注。 | [[../papers/Zhang2003a]] |
| "Since its first observation in WTe₂ in 2017, two-dimensional (2D) sliding ferroelectricity has emerged as a paradigm shift in the pursuit of ultra-thin ferroelectrics." | 自 2017 年在 WTe₂ 中首次观察到以来，二维滑移铁电性已成为追求超薄铁电体过程中的一次范式转变。 | [[../papers/zhangEmergingFrontiersTwodimensional2025]] |

**语法点**：三例分别采用"现在完成时 + due to + NP"、"现在完成时 + due to + NP（更早期）"、"Since + 时间点 + 现在完成时 + as a paradigm shift"三种递进。第一、二例把"为什么重要"压缩进原因状语；第三例用 `since` 锚定发现年份，把论文定位在该发现的延长线上。当代论文越来越倾向用 `has emerged as` / `has garnered significant attention` 替代平淡的 `is important`。

### 1.2 工具/方法奠基开场

**骨架**：`<Method/Framework> is widely used / has been widely applied for <purpose>.`

- "Kohn–Sham density functional theory is widely used for..." [[../papers/perdewGeneralizedGradientApproximation1996a]]
- "In recent years substantial progress has been achieved in..." [[../papers/dudarevElectronenergylossSpectraStructural1998a]]
- "The quasi-two-dimensional (2D) transition metal dichalcogenides (TMD) have been extensively studied since their discovery." [[../papers/CastroNeto2001charge]]

### 1.3 In-this-paper 宣告

**骨架**：`In this <paper/work/Letter/study>, we <verb> that...`

| 实例 | 直译 | 来源 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| "In this study, we propose a bond density and binding strength criterion to identify exfoliable crystallographic planes in ABO₃ compounds." | 在本研究中，我们提出了一种键密度与结合强度判据，用以识别 ABO₃ 化合物中可剥离的晶面。 | [[../papers/zhongHighthroughputExfoliationMultiferroic2025]] |
| "This article describes the local orbital density functional (DF) method DMol3 which evolved from a gas phase molecular method to a method, which with its recent advances, encompasses possibilities for state of the art calculations on insulating and metallic solids." | 本文介绍局域轨道密度泛函方法 DMol3，它从气相分子方法发展而来，经近期拓展已能对绝缘与金属固体进行最先进水平的计算。 | [[../papers/Delley2000]] |
| "Here we report first-principles predictions of conventional phonon-mediated superconductivity in the two-dimensional kagome metal–organic framework Cu₃(CO)₆ with a critical temperature T_c = 16.5 K." | 在此我们报道对二维 kagome 金属有机框架 Cu₃(CO)₆ 中常规声子介导超导的第一性原理预测，其临界温度 T_c = 16.5 K。 | [[../papers/zhengAnisotropicSuperconductivityTwodimensional2025]] |

**语法点**：`In this study, we propose...` 是方法/准则类论文的标准开场；`This article describes...` 偏软件/方法论文（2000 年代风格）；`Here we report...` 把发现与定量结果在一句之内完成，是当代 Letter/Nature 系列摘要最常见的首句模式。

## 2. 问题与缺口 (Identifying the Gap)

### 2.1 Although-Contrast（高频）

**骨架**：`Although <established fact/previous success>, <unsolved problem/limitation>.`

| 实例 | 直译 | 来源 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| "Although the phenomenological description of the CDW transition is well understood due to the work of McMillan, the current understanding of the microscopic origin of the CDW state is not complete." | 尽管 McMillan 的工作已使 CDW 相变的唯象描述相当清楚，但目前对 CDW 态微观起源的理解仍不完整。 | [[../papers/CastroNeto2001charge]] |
| "While the impurity-induced nanoscale electronic disorder has been extensively reported, its microscopic origins remain elusive." | 尽管杂质诱导的纳米尺度电子无序已被广泛报道，其微观起源仍不清楚。 | [[../papers/Kang2012dimer]] |
| "Although experimental observations could qualitatively explain the microstructure formation without using modeling, quantitative understanding of this combined behavior and its dynamic dependence on temperature and composition of steels is required to control the microstructure formation." | 实验观察虽能在不建模的情况下定性解释微结构形成，但要控制微结构形成，就必须定量理解这一耦合行为及其对钢温度和成分的动态依赖。 | [[../papers/Zhang2002b]] |

**语法点**：`Although/While + 完成时被动（has been extensively reported/well understood）+ 主句（remains elusive/is not complete/is required）` 几乎是过去二十年引言"建立缺口"的模板。核心张力：**已知什么** vs. **还不知道什么**。写作时把"已知"压缩到从句，把"未知/需要"放在主句以突出本研究的位置。

### 2.2 However 转折

**骨架**：`<Established claim>. However, <new observation/challenge>.`

- "Band structure calculations do not show, however, strong nesting at the Fermi surface of TMD." [[../papers/CastroNeto2001charge]]
- "However, the question of whether the ferroelectric instability survives in the ultrathin limit has not been addressed." [[../papers/Goswami2011multiferroic]]
- "However, there is one remarkable difference between the two phases." [[../papers/Li2013bonding]]

### 2.3 "Yet / nevertheless" 让步

- "Nevertheless, the complexity and difficulty of real time observation of the solid-state transformation limit the use of those techniques." [[../papers/Zhang2003a]]
- "Nevertheless, all isostructural doping systems displayed sustained orange luminescence." [[../papers/aminiAtomicscaleVisualizationMultiferroicity2024]]

## 3. 方法描述 (Describing Methods)

### 3.1 被动方法句

**骨架**：`<Method/Calculation> was/were performed/carried out using <software> with <key settings>.`

| 实例 | 直译 | 来源 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| "First-principles calculations were performed with the VASP package using projector augmented-wave (PAW) pseudopotentials and the PBE generalized-gradient approximation with a Hubbard U_eff of 3.0 eV on Cr 3d states." | 第一性原理计算使用 VASP 软件包，采用投影缀加波（PAW）赝势与 PBE 广义梯度近似，对 Cr 3d 态使用 Hubbard U_eff = 3.0 eV。 | [[../papers/zhangNonvolatileControlTopological2025]] |
| "Electronic structure and phonon calculations were carried out with Quantum ESPRESSO using norm-conserving pseudopotentials, 80/320 Ry wavefunction/charge cutoffs, and a 40 × 40 × 1 electron–phonon grid via the EPW package." | 电子结构与声子计算使用 Quantum ESPRESSO，采用模守恒赝势，波函数/电荷截断为 80/320 Ry，并通过 EPW 软件包使用 40×40×1 的电子-声子网格。 | [[../papers/zhengAnisotropicSuperconductivityTwodimensional2025]] |
| "All DFT calculations used VASP with PAW pseudopotentials, the GGA-PBE functional, a 520 eV cutoff, 0.02 Å⁻¹ k-spacing, 10⁻⁷ eV energy convergence, 0.001 eV Å⁻¹ force threshold, >15 Å vacuum, and HSE06 for converged magnetic states." | 所有 DFT 计算使用 VASP，采用 PAW 赝势、GGA-PBE 泛函、520 eV 截断、0.02 Å⁻¹ k 点间距、10⁻⁷ eV 能量收敛、0.001 eV/Å 力阈值、>15 Å 真空层，磁性收敛使用 HSE06。 | [[../papers/zhongHighthroughputExfoliationMultiferroic2025]] |

**语法点**：方法段的句子普遍是"主语（计算/实验）+ 被动动词 + using + 工具/参数"。当代 DFT 论文倾向在一句内堆叠完整参数（cutoff、k-mesh、vacuum、convergence threshold），避免方法段碎片化。

### 3.2 公式/定义引入

**骨架**：`<Quantity> is defined/can be expressed as/is given by <equation>.`

- "The final expression for the Hamilton operator is remarkably elegant and simple:" [[../papers/kresseUltrasoftPseudopotentialsProjector1999c]]
- "Physically, Eqs. (8a) and (10) state that the change in polarization of the solid is proportional to the displacement of the center of charge of the Wannier functions." [[../papers/king-smithTheoryPolarizationCrystalline1993]]
- "It is straightforward to derive the required equations by rearranging the total energy functional." [[../papers/kresseUltrasoftPseudopotentialsProjector1999c]]

### 3.3 工作流描述

**骨架**：`<Step A>. This loop/procedure is repeated until <convergence criterion>.`

- "This loop is repeated until self-consistency is reached, for a non-selfconsistent calculation no charge density update is done." [[../papers/kresseEfficientIterativeSchemes1996d]]
- "We usually start with a set of random trial vectors and perform three sweeps over all bands." [[../papers/kresseEfficiencyAbinitioTotal1996a]]
- "In each iteration the initial trial set is equivalent to the final set of the previous iteration." [[../papers/kresseEfficientIterativeSchemes1996d]]

## 4. 报告结果 (Reporting Results)

### 4.1 We-find / We-show

**骨架**：`We find/show/observe/demonstrate that <result>.`

| 实例 | 直译 | 来源 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| "We find that the present numerical localized basis sets yield improved results as compared to previous results for the same functionals." | 我们发现，与使用相同泛函的先前结果相比，当前的数值局域基组给出了更好的结果。 | [[../papers/Delley2000]] |
| "We observe the emergence of ferroelectric forces..." | 我们观察到铁电力的出现…… | [[../papers/wuNonvolatileSwitchableHalfmetallicity2024]] |
| "We demonstrate that ferroelectric polarization reversal in In₂Se₃ can reversibly tune the Dzyaloshinskii–Moriya interaction (DMI) and magnetic anisotropy energy (MAE) in adjacent CrInTe₂." | 我们证明 In₂Se₃ 中的铁电极化反转可以可逆地调控相邻 CrInTe₂ 中的 Dzyaloshinskii–Moriya 相互作用（DMI）与磁各向异性能（MAE）。 | [[../papers/zhangNonvolatileControlTopological2025]] |

### 4.2 It-is-found 无人称观察

**骨架**：`It is found/observed/confirmed that <result>.`

- "It is found that the performance in predicting molecular enthalpies of formation is markedly improved for the Perdew–Burke–Ernzerhof functional." [[../papers/Delley2000]]
- "It was found from the XRD patterns that..." [[../papers/aminiAtomicscaleVisualizationMultiferroicity2024]]
- "It was confirmed that..." [[../papers/gaoGiantChiralMagnetoelectric2024a]]

### 4.3 Figure- / Table-driven 叙述

**骨架**：`Figure/Table X shows/presents/summarizes <observable>.` / `As shown in Fig. X, ...`

- "As shown in Fig. ..." HIGH，各段均高频出现。
- "Table ... presents ..." [[../papers/gajdosLinearOpticalProperties2006]]
- "Figure X shows..." 标准图引导句。

### 4.4 数据-机制并置

**骨架**：`<Quantitative result>, indicating/suggesting/implying that <interpretation>.`

- "The topological Hall angle reaches 11.68° for skyrmions but only 2.44° for bimerons, an approximately 80% reduction directly attributable to the polarization-controlled DMI." [[../papers/zhangNonvolatileControlTopological2025]]
- "Low-frequency Cu–O in-plane phonons below 15 meV contribute more than 70% of the total λ, with the mode at the q = K wavevector showing the strongest linewidth broadening." [[../papers/zhengAnisotropicSuperconductivityTwodimensional2025]]

**语法点**：现在分词短语 `indicating/suggesting/implying that` 是结果段最紧凑的"数据 + 解释"写法；当解释较长时，改用同位语名词短语（"an approximately 80% reduction directly attributable to..."）把结论压缩成一个名词短语，避免另起一句。

## 5. 因果归因 (Causal Attribution)

这是材料物理论文出现频率最高的修辞功能，各段都发展出一套近义表达梯度。

### 5.1 因果强弱梯度

| 强度 | 模式 | 实例 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| 最弱（相关性） | `is associated with / is related to` | "A is associated with B" [[../papers/CastroNeto2001charge]] |
| 弱 | `can be attributed to / can be ascribed to` | "This can be attributed to..." [[../papers/XiaokangZhang2013calibrating]] |
| 中 | `is attributed to / is ascribed to` | "This is attributed to the decreased FWHM" |
| 中强 | `arises from / originates from / stems from / derives from` | "The shift originates from the interplay..." |
| 强 | `is due to / is a consequence of / results from` | "This is due to..." |
| 最强（因果机制） | `gives rise to / leads to / results in / is responsible for / accounts for` | "This gives rise to net out-of-plane polarity" |

**语法点**：当你只想说"这与 X 有关"时用 `is associated with`；当已能定位机制时用 `arises from/originates from`；当要反向说"X 造成了 Y"时用 `gives rise to/leads to/results in`。注意 `is responsible for` 与 `accounts for` 在物理论文中常带"是主要原因"的语气，比 `causes` 更柔和但比 `is related to` 强得多。

### 5.2 经典因果句式

- "The origin of ... is / lies in / is associated with..." MED，各段均出现。
- "The reason for ... is that..." MED。
- "This can be rationalized by..." LOW-MED（2020 年代兴起，常用于解释与预期不符的结果）。
- "...which explains why..." MED。
- "X, because/since Y." HIGH，常见于方法/讨论段。

## 6. 对比与比较 (Contrast & Comparison)

### 6.1 对比连接词

| 模式 | 频率 | 用法 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| `However,` | HIGH | 句首，最强对比 |
| `In contrast (to N)` | HIGH | 后接名词短语，常用于对照实验/对照材料 |
| `By contrast,` | MED | 句首，比 in contrast 书面 |
| `On the other hand,` | HIGH | 必须有 on the one hand 或隐含对照面 |
| `Unlike N,` | HIGH-MED | 句首，直接拿对照物作主语参照 |
| `Compared with N,` | HIGH | 后接主句陈述本体的行为 |
| `Whereas / while` | HIGH | 句中，连接两个并列从句 |
| `Nevertheless,` | MED | 句首，让步后强调本体仍成立 |
| `In spite of / Despite N,` | MED | 后接名词短语 |

### 6.2 比较级与倍数

**骨架**：`<A> is <comparative> than <B> by a factor of N / by N% / by N orders of magnitude.`

- "an approximately 80% reduction directly attributable to..." [[../papers/zhangNonvolatileControlTopological2025]]
- "an increase of over 90-fold" [[../papers/Perugu2024morphology]]
- "an order of magnitude higher than..." [[../papers/rameshMultiferroicsProgressProspects2007]]
- "approximately 3 orders of magnitude shorter" [[../papers/KumarChoubey2011mechanoluminescence]]

**语法点**：`by a factor of N` = 乘 N 倍；`by N%` = 变化 N 个百分点；`by N orders of magnitude` = 数量级变化；`N-fold` 是复合形容词。这四种结构在结果段混用，但不要在一句中堆叠两种。

## 7. 定量表达 (Quantitative Language)

### 7.1 近似与范围

| 模式 | 频率 | 实例 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| `on the order of N` | HIGH（全部五年段） | "on the order of 10⁵ V cm⁻¹" |
| `of the order of N` | MED | 同义，英式风格 |
| `approximately / roughly / about N` | HIGH | "about 0.2 μm" |
| `~N` (tilde) | HIGH（当代） | "~0.06 Å", "T_c ~ 16.5 K" |
| `ca. / approx. N` | MED（早期） | 老派论文 |
| `in the range of X to Y` / `ranging from X to Y` | HIGH | "particle size in the range of 10 nm to 30 nm" |
| `up to N` / `as high as N` / `as low as N` | HIGH | "up to 100 nm" |
| `exceeding N` | MED | "lifetimes exceeding 100 ms" |
| `within X%` | MED | "with an accuracy of..." |

### 7.2 函数依赖

| 模式 | 实例 |
|:---|:---|
| `as a function of N` | HIGH，各段均出现 |
| `increases/decreases with N` | HIGH |
| `is proportional to N` | MED |
| `scales linearly with N` | MED-HIGH（物理） |
| `is inversely proportional to N` | LOW-MED |
| `follows the Arrhenius law` | MED（动力学） |
| `with increasing N` | HIGH-MED |
| `varies non-monotonically with N` | LOW |

### 7.3 验证一致性

| 模式 | 语气 |
|:---|:---|
| `in good/excellent/close agreement with` | 最强一致 |
| `consistent with` | 中性一致 |
| `comparable to` | 量级相当 |
| `in line with` | 较口语化的一致 |
| `deviates from / in contrast to` | 不一致 |

## 8. 主张与谦逊 (Hedging & Claim Strength)

### 8.1 We-claim 动词梯度

| 强度 | 动词 | 用法 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| 最强 | `demonstrate` | 实验/计算已直接证明 |
| 强 | `show` | 数据直接支持 |
| 中 | `find / observe` | 报告发现 |
| 中 | `report` | 陈述作者做了什么 |
| 中弱 | `propose / suggest` | 提出假设/解释 |
| 弱 | `argue` | 带论证的主张 |
| 最弱 | `speculate / conjecture` | 推测 |

### 8.2 Hedging（让主张可辩护）

- `may be / might be / could be` HIGH，各段均出现。
- `is expected to / it appears that / it seems that` MED。
- `in some cases / under certain conditions` LOW-MED。
- `It is tempting to (identify/interpret) ...` LOW（经典 hedge，见于 King-Smith & Vanderbilt 极化理论论文）。
- `Strictly speaking, ...` LOW。
- `To the best of our knowledge, ...` MED（综述/引言）。
- `It should be noted that / It must be remembered that` MED（方法段）。

**语法点**：`demonstrate` 用于实验直接可证；`show` 用于图表直接呈现；`find` 用于发现但不预设机制；`propose/suggest` 用于提出模型或解释；`argue` 带有"我将给出理由"的语气。摘要中通常用 `demonstrate/show/report`，讨论中降为 `suggest/propose`。

## 9. 结论与展望 (Conclusion & Outlook)

### 9.1 总结信号词

- `In summary, / In conclusion, / To conclude, / To summarize,` HIGH。
- `We have shown/demonstrated that...` 现在完成时，HIGH。
- `Our findings/Our results establish...` MED。
- `Taken together, these results suggest...` MED（2010 后兴起）。

### 9.2 意义与展望

**骨架**：`<Our finding> paves the way for / sheds light on / provides a platform for / opens a route to <future impact>.`

| 实例 | 直译 | 来源 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| "Our work paves the way for the design and discovery of 2D non-vdW materials with exceptional phase characteristics." | 我们的工作为设计与发现具有特殊相特征的二维非 vdW 材料铺平了道路。 | [[../papers/zhongHighthroughputExfoliationMultiferroic2025]] |
| "Our results establish CrInTe₂/In₂Se₃ as a candidate platform for voltage-controlled, nonvolatile topological spintronic devices." | 我们的结果确立 CrInTe₂/In₂Se₃ 作为电压控制、非易失性拓扑自旋电子器件的候选平台。 | [[../papers/zhangNonvolatileControlTopological2025]] |
| "Sliding ferroelectricity has matured from a single-material curiosity into a vibrant subfield at the intersection of ferroelectrics, 2D materials, and device physics." | 滑移铁电性已从单一材料的新奇现象发展为一个横跨铁电体、二维材料与器件物理的活跃子领域。 | [[../papers/zhangEmergingFrontiersTwodimensional2025]] |

**语法点**：当代论文的结论段普遍采用"现在完成时回顾（we have demonstrated）+ 现在时定位（establishes X as Y）+ 展望短语（paves the way for / opens a route to）"的三段式。`establish X as Y` 是把工作"写进领域地图"的最强定位动词；`pave the way for / shed light on` 已成为模板化展望用语，需节制使用以免空泛。

## 10. 演进叙事 (Evolution Narrative — Reviews)

综述类论文（2005–2009 Spaldin/Ramesh/Cheong，2020 后 Bhowal、Zhang Emerging Frontiers 等）有一套独特的历史叙事句式：

### 10.1 发现-范式转变

**骨架**：`Since <year/pioneering work>, <field> has evolved/emerged/matured from <earlier state> into <current state>.`

- "Since its first observation in WTe₂ in 2017, two-dimensional (2D) sliding ferroelectricity has emerged as a paradigm shift..." [[../papers/zhangEmergingFrontiersTwodimensional2025]]
- "The discovery of sliding ferroelectricity in bilayer WTe₂ by Wu and colleagues in 2017 opened a new route to atomically thin ferroelectrics." [[../papers/zhangEmergingFrontiersTwodimensional2025]]
- "In 2004, Geim and Novoselov achieved a significant milestone by discovering 2D graphene, marking the commencement of a new epoch in the realm of 2D materials." [[../papers/zahraCriticalAnalysisFerroelectric2025]]

### 10.2 挑战-展望并列

- "Three principal hurdles must be overcome: ..., ..., and ..." [[../papers/zhangEmergingFrontiersTwodimensional2025]]
- "Despite possessing these remarkable properties, several challenges still limit the full potential of MXene in practical applications." [[../papers/zahraCriticalAnalysisFerroelectric2025]]
- "The interplay between X, Y, and Z remains largely unexplored and promises rich emergent physics." [[../papers/zhangEmergingFrontiersTwodimensional2025]]

## 11. 形容词-名词高频搭配 (Adjective-Noun Collocations)

跨段反复出现、值得直接背诵的搭配：

| 搭配 | 频率 | 典型用法 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| `good / excellent / remarkable agreement` | HIGH | 与实验/前人工作一致 |
| `crucial / key / critical role` | HIGH | 机制中起关键作用 |
| `strong coupling / interaction / hybridization` | HIGH | 电子-声子/界面耦合 |
| `significant / considerable / substantial enhancement` | HIGH | 性能提升 |
| `underlying mechanism / physics` | HIGH | 深层机制 |
| `intrinsic property / mechanism` | MED | 本征性质 |

## 12. 模板句骨架总表 (Template Frames)

把全库最高频的可复用骨架集中于此，写作时直接替换占位符。

1. **Abstract 五句骨架**：`<Phenomenon> has attracted attention due to <reason>. However, <gap>. Here we <verb> that <key finding>. Specifically, <quantitative result>. Our results <establish/pave the way for> <impact>.`

2. **Method 句**：`<Calculation/Experiment> was performed using <tool> with <functional/pseudopotential/cutoff/k-mesh/convergence>.`

3. **Result + interpretation**：`<Observable> <verb> from X to Y, indicating/suggesting that <mechanism>.`

4. **Attribution**：`This <behavior/enhancement/suppression> can be attributed to / arises from / originates from <cause>.`

5. **Comparison**：`In contrast to / Unlike <reference>, <our system> exhibits <distinctive behavior>.`

6. **Quantitative claim**：`<Quantity> is on the order of / in the range of / as high as <value>, a factor of N <larger/smaller> than <reference>.`

7. **Implication**：`These findings <suggest/provide/establish> <broader interpretation>, paving the way for / shedding light on <future direction>.`

8. **Review evolution**：`Since <year>, <field> has evolved from <early state> to <current state>. Yet <remaining challenge> must be addressed.`

9. **Hedged claim**：`It is <tempting/reasonable/plausible> to <interpret/identify> X as Y, although <caveat>.`

10. **Formula intro**：`<Quantity> is given by / can be expressed as Eq. (N), where <variables> denote...`
