## ✍️ 0. Title Patterns

2015–2019 年这一段落的标题呈现出三种典型模板，正好对应该时期实验论文、理论/计算论文与综述论文的分野。

1. **"动名词 + 研究对象 + by/through + 表征手段"**：把方法直接写进标题，是 Scientific Reports 类实验论文的常见做法。
   - *Studying the Polarization Switching in Polycrystalline BiFeO₃ Films by 2D Piezoresponse Force Microscopy* — [[../papers/Jin2015studying|Jin et al., 2015]]
2. **"现象 A 与现象 B of/in 体系"**：用并列结构突出双机制或双控制手段，常出现在把"电写/力写"并置的铁电论文里。
   - *Electrical and mechanical switching of ferroelectric polarization in the 70 nm BiFeO₃ film* — [[../papers/Chen2016electrical|Chen et al., 2016]]
3. **"Progress and prospects in + 领域"**：Wiley WIREs 系列综述的标志性框架，"progress"回顾历史，"prospects"抛出展望，天然对应综述的"过去—未来"双段式结构。
   - *Progress and prospects in low-dimensional multiferroic materials* — [[../papers/huProgressProspectsLowdimensional2019|Hu & Kan, 2019]]

> **写作启示**：这一时期的标题信息量普遍偏大，倾向于把"体系 + 尺寸/厚度 + 现象 + 手段"一次交代清楚；相比 2020 年后更短、更概念化的标题，2015–2019 风格更接近"一句话摘要"。

## 📄 1. Abstract

### Synthesis

本语料库中 2015–2019 年的摘要遵循一种极为稳定的四步节奏：点明宏观利害的**缺口开场**、**方法陈述**、带定量数据的**本研究发现**、以及**意义收束**。首句几乎总是先把工作锚定在器件或现象层面的问题上，再收窄到具体材料。对于铁电工作，该问题即宏观功能行为："Ferroelectric polarization switching and its domain evolution play a key role on the macroscopic electric properties of ferroelectric or piezoelectric devices"（[[../papers/Chen2016electrical|Chen2016electrical]]），紧接着一个对比——机械翻转已在约 5 nm 膜中被证实，但 "it is still a challenge for a mechanical force to switch polarization of a slightly thicker film in the same way as an electric field."。在多铁 BiFeO₃ 工作中，开场转而突出耦合序参量："non-180° ferroelectric domain switching may induce ferroelastic and/or (anti-)ferromagnetic effect. So the determination and control of ferroelectric domain switching angles is crucial"（[[../papers/Jin2015studying|Jin2015studying]]）。相邻材料论文套用同一模板，只是替换了利害所在：双光子探针论文以调控光物理性质的需求开场，这种调控需 "in response to external stimuli, such as polarity, pH, ion concentration, and temperature"（[[../papers/H2017fluorescence|H2017fluorescence]]）。

方法步骤被压缩为一个陈述句，通常用被动语态："We try to study the intrinsic characters of polarization switching in BiFeO₃ by introducing a special data processing method"（[[../papers/Jin2015studying|Jin2015studying]]），或 "Atomic simulations by using an embedded-atom-method potential were used to study changes of packing patterns in melting, freezing, and coalescence of titanium particles"（[[../papers/Zhang2019b|Zhang2019b]]）。随后 "Here, we report that…" 的转折以一两个定量锚点抛出核心结果：70 nm 膜的极化 "completely switched by a mechanical force, and its domain evolution is similar to that observed with electrical switching"（[[../papers/Chen2016electrical|Chen2016electrical]]）；翻转角统计被拆解为 71°、109°、180° 事件分别 "about 42%, 29% and 29%, respectively"（[[../papers/Jin2015studying|Jin2015studying]]）。收束句重新上升到应用层面——前瞻性存储器件、磁电耦合，或 "a brilliant prototype for the developments of ideal two-photon fluorescence probes"（[[../papers/Huang2019solvatochromic|Huang2019solvatochromic]]）——闭合宏观—微观—宏观的弧线。

### 例句表

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|---|---|---|
| "Ferroelectric polarization switching and its domain evolution play a key role on the macroscopic electric properties of ferroelectric or piezoelectric devices." | 铁电极化翻转及其畴演化对铁电器件或压电器件的宏观电学性质起关键作用。 | 现象 A 与 B + **play a key role on** + 宏观性质 + of + 器件类别。 |
| "Here, we report that the polarization of a 70 nm BiFeO₃ epitaxial film can be completely switched by a mechanical force, and its domain evolution is similar to that observed with electrical switching." | 在此，我们报道 70 nm BiFeO₃ 外延膜的极化可被机械力完全翻转，且其畴演化与电致翻转所观察到的相似。 | **Here, we report that** + 具体体系 + **can be completely switched by** + 手段, and + 对比从句。 |
| "71° polarization switching is more favorable, with the area percentages of 71°, 109° and 180° domain switching being about 42%, 29% and 29%, respectively." | 71° 极化翻转更为有利，71°、109°、180° 畴翻转的面积百分比分别约为 42%、29%、29%。 | 结论 + **with** + 名词 + **being about** + 数值 1/2/3 + **respectively**。 |

**语法点**：(1) "play a key role on/in" 是高频但偏口语化的搭配，正式写作可替换为 "govern" / "dictate"；(2) "Here, we report that…" 后接被动语态 "can be switched by" 比主动 "we switched" 更显客观；(3) 三连数值必须以 "respectively" 收束，且数值顺序与前文名词顺序严格对应。

### 🧩 经典案例逐句拆解

> **来源**：截取自 [[../papers/Chen2016electrical|Chen et al.]], *Scientific Reports* (2016)

> "Ferroelectric polarization switching and its domain evolution play a key role on the macroscopic electric properties of ferroelectric or piezoelectric devices. Mechanical switching has been reported recently in ~5 nm BaTiO₃ and PbZr₀.₂Ti₀.₈O₃ epitaxial films; however it is still a challenge for a mechanical force to switch polarization of a slightly thicker film in the same way as an electric field. Here, we report that the polarization of a 70 nm BiFeO₃ epitaxial film can be completely switched by a mechanical force, and its domain evolution is similar to that observed with electrical switching."

逐句标号：

① "Ferroelectric polarization switching and its domain evolution play a key role on the macroscopic electric properties of ferroelectric or piezoelectric devices."
② "Mechanical switching has been reported recently in ~5 nm BaTiO₃ and PbZr₀.₂Ti₀.₈O₃ epitaxial films; however it is still a challenge for a mechanical force to switch polarization of a slightly thicker film in the same way as an electric field."
③ "Here, we report that the polarization of a 70 nm BiFeO₃ epitaxial film can be completely switched by a mechanical force, and its domain evolution is similar to that observed with electrical switching."

中文翻译：

① 铁电极化翻转及其畴演化在铁电或压电器件的宏观电学性质中起关键作用。
② 近期已有报道在约 5 nm 的 BaTiO₃ 与 PbZr₀.₂Ti₀.₈O₃ 外延膜中实现机械翻转；然而，要让机械力以与电场相同的方式翻转稍厚膜的极化仍是一项挑战。
③ 在此我们报道，70 nm BiFeO₃ 外延膜的极化可被机械力完全翻转，且其畴演化与电致翻转中观察到的相似。

| 句子 | 功能 | 句法要点 |
|---|---|---|
| ① | 大背景定位 | 并列主语 + play a key role on + 宏观性质 |
| ② | 已有进展 + gap | 现在完成时 "has been reported" + 分号 + however 转折 + "it is a challenge for X to do Y in the same way as Z" |
| ③ | 本研究主张 | Here, we report that + 被动式定量结果 + and 并列从句呼应前句 gap |

**核心教训**：摘要前三句构成"宏观背景 → 已知边界 → 我们把边界向外推了多少"的三明治。注意 ② 用分号连接两个强相关分句，比拆成两句更紧凑；"in the same way as an electric field" 这一比较状语把"机械 vs 电"的对照写得一目了然，是 ③ 中 "similar to that observed with electrical switching" 得以回响的前提。

## 🚪 2. Introduction & Scientific Background

### Synthesis

这一时段的引言偏好**漏斗形**：一到两句的领域定义、"前人已表明 X，但 Y 仍待解决"的桥接，以及 "Herein, we report…" 的落点。开场刻意平实——近乎教科书式——以便后续的缺口凸显出来。湿度传感器论文以功用性定义开头："Optical fiber is medium to carrying information from one point to another in the form of light"（[[../papers/2019optical|2019optical]]）；纳米科学论文以衔接尺度的句子开头："As a bridge between isolated atoms and bulk materials, metallic nanoparticles have attracted significant interest over several decades"（[[../papers/Zhang2019b|Zhang2019b]]）；应用化学论文则枚举利益相关领域："Humidity quantity is considered as one of the most important scientific topics in different branches of fields such as food industry, biology, chemistry, medicine, drug delivery, agriculture, automobiles, etc."（[[../papers/Doroodmand2017conjugated|Doroodmand2017conjugated]]）。这些句子在文体上并不优雅；它们的任务是*定位*。

下一步建立**比较优势**。聚合物光纤工作以链式列举优点："its flexibility, resistance to electromagnetic interference, resistance to effects caused by vibration, and good coupling of light from light sources,"，并将其与制造前景挂钩——"the relatively simple preparation and fabrication process … provide the hope of creating an inexpensive humidity sensor"（[[../papers/2019optical|2019optical]]）。对比步骤随后将缺口锐化。双光子探针工作是最干净的例子："Although a small number of TPF probes for polarity were reported, and two among them were used for lipid rafts imaging, their applications can't be extended from polarity detection to viscosities and temperature. Furthermore, in them, small molecule-size always is accompanied by narrow solvatochromic range and small δ. So they are not ideal candidates"（[[../papers/H2017fluorescence|H2017fluorescence]]）。这一模式——Although X, and Y, their applications can't be extended to Z; furthermore W; so not ideal——在三句话中堆叠了三层局限。

引言中段通常按时间顺序**叙述前人工作**，并附作者—年份引用："Zhang et al in 2008 tested… Aneesh et al in 2009 characterized… Recently in 2017 David ddk characterized…"（[[../papers/2019optical|2019optical]]）。综述式引言把这压缩为单句比较性论断："In contrast to PET-based fluorescence probes, which only show increasing or decreasing emission intensity at fixed wavelength, solvatochromic probes may indicate the presence of an analyte by a significant change of emission wavelength, which is unequivocally detectable"（[[../papers/H2017fluorescence|H2017fluorescence]]）。落点句几乎总有标志词引导："Herein, we report two TP solvatochromic probes … which has been successfully employed in the design of TPF probes for metal ions"（[[../papers/H2017fluorescence|H2017fluorescence]]）。对于 Hu & Kan 2019 年综述——本时段的概念封顶之作——引言则落在一个领域定义性主张上：基于二维材料的铁电或多铁性功能材料 "have been a fast developing field which attracts intensive investigations due to their potential applications and underlying new science"（[[../papers/huProgressProspectsLowdimensional2019|huProgressProspectsLowdimensional2019]]）。该句同时完成三件事——点名平台（二维材料）、点名序参量（铁电/多铁）、并以应用和"新科学"双重理由为工作正名。

纵观整个语料库，这一时期的科学背景段落更重**枚举**而非机理推导；它们列举候选材料、候选刺激、候选应用，让列表本身传达广度。机理基础——退极化场、Mermin–Wagner 涨落、二阶 Jahn–Teller 畸变——大多推迟到后续章节，这一选择反映该领域自我定位仍处于发现/探索阶段，而非成熟的理论检验阶段。

### 例句表

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|---|---|---|
| "Although a small number of TPF probes for polarity were reported, and two among them were used for lipid rafts imaging, their applications can't be extended from polarity detection to viscosities and temperature." | 尽管已有少量用于极性的 TPF 探针被报道，其中两种被应用于脂筏成像，但它们的应用无法从极性检测拓展到黏度和温度。 | **Although** + 从句 1, and + 从句 2, + 主句 "can't be extended from A to B and C"。 |
| "As a bridge between isolated atoms and bulk materials, metallic nanoparticles have attracted significant interest over several decades." | 作为孤立原子与块体材料之间的桥梁，金属纳米颗粒几十年来引起了广泛关注。 | **As a bridge between** A and B, + 主语 + **have attracted significant interest over** + 时段。 |
| "Herein, we report two TP solvatochromic probes (P1 and P2) derived from 4-methyl-2,5-dicyano-4'-amino stilbene (DCS) with remarkably large δ, which has been successfully employed in the design of TPF probes for metal ions." | 在此，我们报道两种源自 4-甲基-2,5-二氰基-4′-氨基二苯乙烯（DCS）的 TP 溶剂化变色探针（P1、P2），它们具有显著大的 δ，该母核已被成功用于金属离子 TPF 探针的设计。 | **Herein, we report** + 对象 + 过去分词短语作定语 + **with** + 定量属性 + which 非限定从句。 |

**语法点**：(1) "Although …, and …, + 主句" 可在让步从句内部叠加一个并列从句，使逻辑层次更密；(2) "can't" 在正式摘要/引言中应写为 "cannot"；(3) "Herein" 是引言收束句的标志词，比 "In this paper" 更书面化，但 2020 年后部分期刊认为其偏陈旧，可酌情替换。

### 🧩 经典案例逐句拆解

> **来源**：截取自 [[../papers/H2017fluorescence|Huang & Qu]], *Biomarkers Journal* (2017)

> "Although a small number of TPF probes for polarity were reported, and two among them were used for lipid rafts imaging, their applications can't be extended from polarity detection to viscosities and temperature. Furthermore, in them, small molecule-size always is accompanied by narrow solvatochromic range and small δ. So they are not ideal candidates for TP solvatochromic probes. In contrast to PET-based fluorescence probes, which only show increasing or decreasing emission intensity at fixed wavelength, solvatochromic probes may indicate the presence of an analyte by a significant change of emission wavelength, which is unequivocally detectable. Herein, we report two TP solvatochromic probes (P1 and P2) derived from 4-methyl-2,5-dicyano-4'-amino stilbene (DCS) with remarkably large δ."

逐句标号：

① 让步从句 + 并列：已有 TPF 极性探针，但仅用于极性，未拓展到黏度和温度。
② Furthermore 追加：小分子尺寸伴随窄变色范围和小 δ。
③ So 收束：因此它们不是理想候选。
④ In contrast to 横向比较：PET 探针只变强度，溶剂化变色探针以波长位移给出"无歧义"信号。
⑤ Herein 落点：本研究报道两种具有大 δ 的 TP 溶剂化变色探针。

中文翻译：

① 尽管已有少量用于极性的 TPF 探针被报道，其中两种也被用于脂筏成像，但它们的应用无法从极性检测拓展到黏度和温度。
② 此外，在这些探针中，小分子尺寸往往伴随着狭窄的溶剂化变色范围和较小的 δ。
③ 因此，它们并非 TP 溶剂化变色探针的理想候选。
④ 与仅在固定波长表现为发射强度增减的 PET 荧光探针相反，溶剂化变色探针可通过发射波长的显著变化来指示被分析物的存在，这种变化是明确可检的。
⑤ 在此，我们报道两种源自 4-甲基-2,5-二氰基-4′-氨基二苯乙烯（DCS）的 TP 溶剂化变色探针（P1、P2），具有显著大的 δ。

| 句子 | 功能 | 衔接手段 |
|---|---|---|
| ① | 承认前人贡献 + 指出能力边界 | Although …, and …, can't be extended |
| ② | 追加第二层局限 | Furthermore |
| ③ | 小结前人不足 | So |
| ④ | 改换评价坐标系 | In contrast to |
| ⑤ | 提出本工作 | Herein, we report |

**核心教训**：引言的 gap 段不应只说"前人没做过 X"，而要让读者看到前人为何 *难以* 做 X（机理层面：分子尺寸 ↔ 变色范围 ↔ δ 三者互相牵制），再用一句 "In contrast to…" 把本工作所用的范式（波长位移 vs 强度调制）抬出来。这样 "Herein, we report…" 才不是凭空冒出，而是从前文逻辑中长出来的。

## ⚙️ 3. Key Methodological Developments

### Synthesis

本时段的方法写作由**三种原型**主导：薄膜生长后接扫描探针表征（BiFeO₃ 工作）、原子级或半经验模拟（Ti 纳米颗粒、Si/Ge 表面）、以及湿化学合成加光谱验证（双光子探针、共轭聚合物）。生长句把众多工艺参数压缩进单个从句，几乎总用被动语态和过去时："BiFeO₃ (~70 nm) epitaxial films and La₀.₆₇Sr₀.₃₃MnO₃ (~70 nm) buffer layers were grown on commercial (0 0 1) SrTiO₃ substrates using pulsed laser deposition (PLD) at 630 °C and 16 Pa oxygen pressure inside the chamber"（[[../papers/Chen2016electrical|Chen2016electrical]]）；多晶变体则 "fabricated on Pt/Ti/SiO₂/Si substrates via Metal Organic Decomposition method, and their rhombohedral structure was confirmed by X-ray Diffraction patterns"（[[../papers/Jin2015studying|Jin2015studying]]）。其模式为 **膜 + 厚度 + 衬底 + by/using + 方法 + at + 温度/压力**，结构确认作为第二从句附加。

仪器句一口气交代厂商、型号和模式："A commercial SPM (Nanoscope IV, DI) connected with two additional lock-in amplifiers (7280 DSP) was used to study the polarization switching"（[[../papers/Jin2015studying|Jin2015studying]]）；"surface morphology, out-of-plane (OP) phase, in-plane (IP) phase and amplitude images were studied by using the commercial atomic force microscope (AFM, Bruker multimode 8 or Asylum Research Cypher) with a piezoelectric force microscopy (PFM) mode"（[[../papers/Chen2016electrical|Chen2016electrical]]）。注意这四通道清单——形貌、OP 相位、IP 相位、振幅——在这一时期成为 PFM 论文的标准配置。

对于**多步流程**，作者使用显式枚举："Our data processing includes the following four steps: Step 1: Normalize PFM signals before and after switching. Step 2: Obtain theoretical piezoelectric displacements before switching. Step 3: Provide all the possible theoretical piezoelectric displacements after switching. Step 4: Determine the domain orientation and switching angles"（[[../papers/Jin2015studying|Jin2015studying]]）。计算类论文如法炮制："Firstly, two reconstructed Si(0 0 1) surfaces including c(4×2) and p(2×2) are obtained. Then, the Ge atom is placed on these two reconstructed surfaces. The calculations show the following results"（[[../papers/Wu2018|Wu2018]]）。最后，**参数优化**段落使用 "Effect of X has been evaluated according to Y"：溶剂效应依据循环伏安图评估（[[../papers/Doroodmand2017conjugated|Doroodmand2017conjugated]]）；阳离子效应逐一筛查（K⁺、Na⁺、Li⁺、Ca²⁺、Ba²⁺、Mg²⁺、Al³⁺）。该时期的方法学标志是**穷尽性**：列出每一个通道、每一步、每一个筛查变量。

### 🧩 经典案例逐句拆解

> **来源**：截取自 [[../papers/Jin2015studying|Jin et al.]], *Scientific Reports* (2015)

> "Polycrystalline BFO films with thickness of about 300 nm and average grain size of about 130 nm were fabricated on Pt/Ti/SiO₂/Si substrates via Metal Organic Decomposition method, and their rhombohedral structure was confirmed by X-ray Diffraction patterns. A commercial SPM (Nanoscope IV, DI) connected with two additional lock-in amplifiers (7280 DSP) was used to study the polarization switching. Our data processing includes the following four steps: Step 1: Normalize PFM signals before and after switching. Step 2: Obtain theoretical piezoelectric displacements before switching. Step 3: Provide all the possible theoretical piezoelectric displacements after switching. Step 4: Determine the domain orientation and switching angles."

逐句标号：

① 样品制备：膜厚、晶粒尺寸、衬底、方法、结构确认。
② 仪器：SPM 主机 + 外接锁相放大器，用途。
③ 数据处理总括句 + 四步清单。
④–⑦ 四步依次为：归一化 → 翻转前理论位移 → 翻转后全部可能位移 → 判定畴向与翻转角。

中文翻译：

① 厚度约 300 nm、平均晶粒尺寸约 130 nm 的多晶 BFO 膜通过金属有机分解法制备于 Pt/Ti/SiO₂/Si 衬底上，其菱方结构由 X 射线衍射谱确认。
② 一台外接两台锁相放大器（7280 DSP）的商用 SPM（Nanoscope IV, DI）用于研究极化翻转。
③ 我们的数据处理包含以下四步：
④ 第一步：归一化翻转前后的 PFM 信号；
⑤ 第二步：获得翻转前的理论压电位移；
⑥ 第三步：给出翻转后所有可能的理论压电位移；
⑦ 第四步：确定畴取向与翻转角。

| 句子 | 信息密度 | 写作作用 |
|---|---|---|
| ① | 5 个参数 | 让读者可独立复现样品 |
| ② | 3 个硬件标识 | 锁相放大器是 2D-PFM 反演所需，刻意点明 |
| ③–⑦ | 4 步算法 | 把"我们如何得到 71°/109°/180° 统计"透明化 |

**核心教训**：方法学段落最忌只写"用 PFM 研究了翻转"。这一段把样品—仪器—算法三段写得几乎可以照着复刻，尤其是四步数据处理，把抽象的"2D PFM 反演翻转角"拆成可验证的子任务。写方法时假设读者要 *复现* 而非仅 *理解*，句子自然会落到正确的粒度。

## 📊 4. Core Results & Physical Mechanisms

### Synthesis

这一时期的结果章节通常在任何解读之前先以**以图导观察**开场。PFM 工作具有代表性："Figure 2b gives the surface morphology of the sample before polar scan, which actually didn't change after poling. While from the PFM images before (Fig. 2c,d) and after (Fig. 2e,f) polar scan, it can be seen clearly that the piezoresponse signals of the polar scanned region dramatically changes after poling"（[[../papers/Jin2015studying|Jin2015studying]]）。其逻辑是形貌控制优先：证明形貌未变，以便把相位/振幅衬度归因于极化而非粗糙度。传感器论文使用等效的控制："The characterization of optical fibers can also be seen through the linearity and sensor hysteresis curves. The data is taken by looking at the output voltage graph against the humidity of the air when the humidity value rises and the humidity falls"（[[../papers/2019optical|2019optical]]）。

与前人工作的比较是显式且定量的。DFTB 吸附工作将其二聚体屈曲角与早前计算对比："In Ref. [20], Miwa studied the structural and electronic properties of Si-Si, Ge-Ge, and Si-Ge mixed dimers on Si(0 0 1) surfaces by first principle calculations. For the case of buckling Si-Si dimer on Si(0 0 1) surface, he obtains the dimer bond length is 2.27 Å, and the buckling angle is 15.7°. Our calculations indicate a buckling angle of 9.81° for the short bonds…"（[[../papers/Wu2018|Wu2018]]）。该模板——引用前人数值、报告我们的数值、让差异可见而非掩饰——是审慎计算报告的标志。BiFeO₃ 工作同样有自我意识："Up to now, most of the works concerning switching angles of BFO are about epitaxial films, with which we can compare in the following three aspects"（[[../papers/Jin2015studying|Jin2015studying]]）。

定量报告偏好密集句："The intensity of the blue component was linearly increased over a wide range of RH between 5–80%. The fabricated optical sensor had improved detection limit (0.17% RH), standard saturated limit (>80% RH), accepted relative standard deviation (RSD = 3.6%, n = 3), short rise time (~9.5 s compared to the commercial RH sensor), good linearity (R = 0.9971) and maximum ~7.5 % RH hysteresis"（[[../papers/Doroodmand2017conjugated|Doroodmand2017conjugated]]）。六项品质因数堆叠于单句中，每个括号数值紧随其标签。因果链随后拆解观察："The smaller light intensity is caused by evanescent waves that penetrate the deeper cladding, because as the humidity rises, the TiO₂-SiO₂ [refractive index] decreases. Based on equation (1) if the index density of cladding bias is smaller then the depth of penetration … of the evanescent wave is smaller"（[[../papers/2019optical|2019optical]]）。机理归因常带保留："This phenomenon might be related to BFO's special crystal structure. Even if the original OPP is along the poling field, there are still switching possibilities (71° or 109°) as long as the component of polarization along –z axis is not the maximum among all eight equivalent polarization directions"（[[../papers/Jin2015studying|Jin2015studying]]）；"The reduction in emission intensity for P1 in acetone and other more polar solvents together with a near lack of photoreactivity is certainly consistent with decay of the emissive state to one or more of the possible noncoplanar and nonemissive TICT which subsequently decay rapidly to the ground state"（[[../papers/H2017fluorescence|H2017fluorescence]]）。这些保留性词汇——"might be related to"、"is consistent with"、"as long as"、"certainly consistent with"——使机理主张与证据相称。

### 例句表

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|---|---|---|
| "The intensity of the blue component was linearly increased over a wide range of RH between 5–80%." | 蓝色分量的强度在 5%–80% RH 的宽区间内线性增加。 | 观测量 + **was linearly increased over a wide range of** + 变量 + **between** A–B。 |
| "The smaller light intensity is caused by evanescent waves that penetrate the deeper cladding, because as the humidity rises, the TiO₂-SiO₂ [refractive index] decreases." | 较小的光强由渗透进更深包层的倏逝波引起，因为随着湿度上升，TiO₂-SiO₂ 的折射率下降。 | 现象 + **is caused by** + 机制主体 + that 从句 + **because as** + 条件变化。 |
| "This phenomenon might be related to BFO's special crystal structure." | 这一现象可能与 BFO 特殊的晶体结构有关。 | 现象 + **might be related to** + 机理候选（弱断言）。 |

**语法点**：(1) "linearly increased over a wide range of X between A–B%" 是结果段描写线性区间的模板；(2) 因果链中 "is caused by X that …, because as Y rises, Z …" 允许在一句中嵌套物理主体 + 变化条件 + 后果，但应避免超过三层；(3) "might be related to" 比 "is caused by" 弱一档，用于证据不足以确证机理时。

### 🧩 经典案例逐句拆解

> **来源**：截取自 [[../papers/Jin2015studying|Jin et al.]], *Scientific Reports* (2015)

> "The understanding of the experimental results bases on the following three points: (a) Electric energy and stress energy are comparable during polarization switching, and they compete with each other. (b) Electric energy increases with switching angle, while the 180° switching has the maximum electric energy. (c) Stress energy varies for different switching. This phenomenon might be related to BFO's special crystal structure. Even if the original OPP is along the poling field, there are still switching possibilities (71° or 109°) as long as the component of polarization along –z axis is not the maximum among all eight equivalent polarization directions."

逐句标号：

① 总括：实验结果可从三点理解。
② (a) 电能与应力能可比、相互竞争。
③ (b) 电能随翻转角增大，180° 时最大。
④ (c) 不同翻转的应力能不同。
⑤ 弱机理归因：可能与 BFO 特殊晶体结构有关。
⑥ 进一步细化：即使原 OPP 沿极化场方向，只要沿 –z 分量不是八个等效方向中最大的，仍可能发生 71°/109° 翻转。

中文翻译：

① 对实验结果的理解基于以下三点：
② (a) 极化翻转过程中电能与应力能可比，二者相互竞争；
③ (b) 电能随翻转角增大，180° 翻转具有最大电能；
④ (c) 应力能因翻转类型而异。
⑤ 这一现象可能与 BFO 特殊的晶体结构有关。
⑥ 即使原始面外极化沿极化场方向，只要沿 –z 轴的极化分量不是八个等效极化方向中最大的，仍存在翻转可能（71° 或 109°）。

| 标号 | 论证角色 | 证据强度 |
|---|---|---|
| ②–④ | 三因素能量学框架 | 中（理论模型） |
| ⑤ | 弱归因 | 低（might be related to） |
| ⑥ | 晶体学约束的具体推论 | 中—高（八方向对称性可验证） |

**核心教训**：解释多畴翻转统计时，作者没有直接抛出"因为晶体结构特殊"，而是先给出 (a)(b)(c) 三条能量学命题，再用 "might be related to" 作软着陆，最后用八等效方向的几何论证把软归因收紧为可证伪的推论。"软归因 + 硬约束" 是机理性讨论中既诚实又有说服力的标准写法。

## 💬 5. Discussion

### Synthesis

本语料库中的讨论段落倾向于把三条线索编织在一起：**与既有文献的比较**、**物理解读**、以及**方法或发现的普适性**。在 BiFeO₃ 工作中，比较是结构性的："Up to now, most of the works concerning switching angles of BFO are about epitaxial films, with which we can compare in the following three aspects"（[[../papers/Jin2015studying|Jin2015studying]]），随后作者枚举取向分布、统计权重和矫顽行为。机械翻转论文触及更宽广的意涵：铁电功能 "in the absence of an electric field, such as memory devices and other micro-electromechanical systems"（[[../papers/Chen2016electrical|Chen2016electrical]]），把力驱动写入定位为绕开电击穿和漏电的一条路径。双光子探针工作引出另一类普适性——化学设计的可迁移性："The successful application of P1 provides a brilliant prototype for the developments of ideal two-photon fluorescence probes"（[[../papers/Huang2019solvatochromic|Huang2019solvatochromic]]），主张二氰基-给体架构是可复用的骨架。

湿度与光学传感器论文**同时讨论误差预算与物理机制**："The average error obtained from the processing of the formula in equation 1 shows the discrepancy between the readings of the device designed with the standard gauge. Many factors cause this because the designed sensors are still very susceptible to systematic errors"（[[../papers/2019optical|2019optical]]）；"The result states that the higher the air humidity the higher the voltage generated by the photodiode output. This is due to the smaller intensity of light traveling on optical fibers"（[[../papers/2019optical|2019optical]]）。其风格直白到近乎未经雕琢，但逻辑顺序——观察、物理原因、误差来源——是正确的。计算类讨论依托结构比较："Our calculations indicate a buckling angle of 9.81° for the short bonds…"（[[../papers/Wu2018|Wu2018]]），随后显式对比 Miwa 的 15.7°，并讨论重构选择（c(4×2) vs p(2×2)）如何改变该值。

Hu & Kan 2019 年综述讨论的独特之处在于其**领域尺度的反思性**：它不把单个二维铁电体视为孤立发现，而是视为设计分类学中的条目——本征范德华铁电体、通过功能化/应变/电场诱导的铁电性、以及超薄钙钛矿中的反常铁电性。这类综述中的讨论本质上是一幅*地图*：指出哪些区域密布预言、哪些已获实验确认、哪些仍为空白。综述还刻意点名*机制*——二阶 Jahn–Teller 畸变、八面体旋转与 A 位位移的三线性耦合、II 型多铁体中自旋驱动的非本征铁电性——以便未来工作能在地图上自我定位。正是这一概念动作，到 2019 年标志着低维铁电性成为一个连贯领域，而非零散材料的拼凑。

### 例句表

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|---|---|---|
| "Our study sheds light on prospective applications of ferroelectrics in the absence of an electric field, such as memory devices and other micro-electromechanical systems." | 我们的研究为铁电体在无电场条件下的潜在应用（如存储器及其他微机电系统）提供了启示。 | **Our study sheds light on** + 应用域 + **in the absence of** + 传统手段 + **such as** + 举例。 |
| "This work helps exploring a route to control polarization switching in BiFeO₃, so as to realize desirable magnetoelectric coupling." | 本工作有助于探索一条控制 BiFeO₃ 极化翻转的路径，以实现理想的磁电耦合。 | **This work helps exploring a route to** + 控制对象 + **so as to realize** + 终极目标。 |
| "In contrast to PET-based fluorescence probes, which only show increasing or decreasing emission intensity at fixed wavelength, solvatochromic probes may indicate the presence of an analyte by a significant change of emission wavelength." | 与仅在固定波长表现为发射强度增减的 PET 探针不同，溶剂化变色探针可通过发射波长的显著变化来指示被分析物的存在。 | **In contrast to** A + which 从句描述局限, + B + **may indicate by** + 新机制。 |

**语法点**：(1) "shed light on" 是讨论段高频短语，正式场合可替换为 "clarifies / elucidates"；(2) "so as to realize" 表目的，比 "in order to" 更书面；(3) "In contrast to A, which …, B …" 用一个非限定从句把 A 的局限嵌入对比，避免另起一句。

### 🧩 经典案例逐句拆解

> **来源**：截取自 [[../papers/Chen2016electrical|Chen et al.]], *Scientific Reports* (2016)

> "In summary, the electrical switching of polarization in the ~70 nm epitaxial BiFeO₃ film is similar to that of mechanical switching. The 180° polarization switching is commonly completed with two steps. Our study sheds light on prospective applications of ferroelectrics in the absence of an electric field, such as memory devices and other micro-electromechanical systems."

逐句标号：

① 结论性比较：电写与力写在 70 nm 膜中相似。
② 机理性细节：180° 翻转通常分两步完成。
③ 推广：为无电场条件下的铁电应用提供启示。

中文翻译：

① 总之，约 70 nm 外延 BiFeO₃ 膜中的电极化翻转与机械翻转相似。
② 180° 极化翻转通常通过两步完成。
③ 我们的研究为铁电体在无电场条件下的潜在应用（如存储器及其他微机电系统）提供了启示。

| 句子 | 讨论层次 | 对应前文 |
|---|---|---|
| ① | 现象层面的等价性 | PFM 畴演化图 |
| ② | 动力学细节 | 两步翻转的中间态观测 |
| ③ | 器件含义 | 力写可绕过漏电/击穿 |

**核心教训**：讨论段不必长，但要让每句话落在不同的抽象层——现象、机制、应用——且三句之间有"升维"关系。把 ①②③ 压成一段三句话，比写三段冗长复述结果更有力。

## 🏁 6. Conclusion

### Synthesis

本时段的结论偏好**三元组**：一句概括核心发现，一个否则会被遗漏的定量或机理性细节，以及一句关于方法可迁移性或应用的前瞻性陈述。BiFeO₃ 工作再次提供最干净的模板："In summary, the electrical switching of polarization in the ~70 nm epitaxial BiFeO₃ film is similar to that of mechanical switching. The 180° polarization switching is commonly completed with two steps. Our study sheds light on prospective applications of ferroelectrics in the absence of an electric field, such as memory devices and other micro-electromechanical systems"（[[../papers/Chen2016electrical|Chen2016electrical]]）。三句话，三个抽象高度。

双光子探针的结论更长、更具庆贺色彩，但遵循同一弧线："In conclusion, we have shown that the covalent attachment of two cyano groups in the single aromatic ring and dimethylamino group to stilbene is a considerably ingenious strategy, which makes the molecule a highly sensitive two-photon thermo-solvatochromic probe with a remarkably large δ and a wide solvatochromic range capable of being used to detect the analytes with high π*, polarities, viscosities, and temperature. It was the first time that TP triple fluorescence was sighted"（[[../papers/H2017fluorescence|H2017fluorescence]]）。"首次"主张即定量/新颖性锚点。工程类结论把性能压缩为若干品质因数："The polymer fiber optic sensor system with TiO₂-SiO₂ cladding has been able to respond to air humidity with the most optimum length of stripping cladding obtained at 2 cm stripping length. It can be seen from the coefficient of determination that is R² = 0.982 the most optimum sensitivity is 0.0376 V/%"（[[../papers/2019optical|2019optical]]）。

最具领域意识的结论来自方法学论文和综述。PFM 翻转角工作显式推广其数据处理流程："More broadly, our data processing method could be extended to other ferroelectric materials with random orientation. With this method, we can avoid the time-consuming sample-rotating procedure and make it possible to timely investigate the relaxation process of switched domains"（[[../papers/Jin2015studying|Jin2015studying]]）；"This work also provides a framework for exploring an approach about how to control switching mode, so as to achieve remarkable magnetoelectric coupling"（[[../papers/Jin2015studying|Jin2015studying]]）。综述结论承担领域定位工作："There are lots of papers reviewing on humidity sensor and most of them focusing on general types of humidity sensor which are relative humidity sensor and absolute humidity sensor. In this paper, we discuss detail on the ZnO as metal oxide humidity sensor"（[[../papers/Ismail2015humidity|Ismail2015humidity]]）。所有这些的共同模式是 **主张 → 范围 → 遗产**。

### 例句表

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|---|---|---|
| "More broadly, our data processing method could be extended to other ferroelectric materials with random orientation." | 更广泛地说，我们的数据处理方法可推广到其他具有随机取向的铁电材料。 | **More broadly**, + 方法主语 + **could be extended to** + 更广泛的对象。 |
| "With this method, we can avoid the time-consuming sample-rotating procedure and make it possible to timely investigate the relaxation process of switched domains." | 借助该方法，我们可避免耗时的样品旋转流程，并使及时研究翻转畴的弛豫过程成为可能。 | **With this method**, + we can avoid + 旧弊端 + and **make it possible to** + 新能力。 |
| "In conclusion, we have shown that the covalent attachment of two cyano groups in the single aromatic ring and dimethylamino group to stilbene is a considerably ingenious strategy." | 总之，我们表明在单芳环上共价连接两个氰基并在二苯乙烯上连接二甲氨基是一种相当巧妙的策略。 | **In conclusion, we have shown that** + 化学设计 + **is a considerably ingenious strategy**. |

**语法点**：(1) "More broadly" 是结论段从具体结果迈向一般性陈述的标准转场；(2) "make it possible to + 动词" 是表达方法学贡献的高频句式，比 "can" 更显突破；(3) "we have shown that" 用现在完成时呼应全文工作已完成，比 "we showed" 更适合作为结论收束。

### 🧩 经典案例逐句拆解

> **来源**：截取自 [[../papers/Jin2015studying|Jin et al.]], *Scientific Reports* (2015)

> "More broadly, our data processing method could be extended to other ferroelectric materials with random orientation. With this method, we can avoid the time-consuming sample-rotating procedure and make it possible to timely investigate the relaxation process of switched domains. This work also provides a framework for exploring an approach about how to control switching mode, so as to achieve remarkable magnetoelectric coupling."

逐句标号：

① 方法可推广性：从 BFO 推广到任意随机取向铁电体。
② 方法学收益：省去样品旋转，使畴弛豫的时间分辨研究成为可能。
③ 科学目标延伸：为控制翻转模式、实现磁电耦合提供框架。

中文翻译：

① 更广泛地说，我们的数据处理方法可推广至其他具有随机取向的铁电材料。
② 借助该方法，我们可以避免耗时的样品旋转程序，并使及时研究翻转畴的弛豫过程成为可能。
③ 本工作也为探索控制翻转模式的途径提供了框架，从而实现显著的磁电耦合。

| 句子 | 维度 | 关键动词短语 |
|---|---|---|
| ① | 适用对象的广度 | could be extended to |
| ② | 实验效率与新能力 | avoid … and make it possible to |
| ③ | 终极科学目标 | provides a framework for … so as to |

**核心教训**：结论段的最后三句话应构成一个"外推阶梯"：从本文体系 → 同类体系 → 学科目标。许多论文的结论只停在第①层（"我们在 BFO 上做了 X"），本文示范了如何用 "More broadly … With this method … This work also provides a framework …" 三个衔接词把读者一路送到学科意义层面。

## 🧬 7. Evolution（综述专属）

### Synthesis

2015–2019 时段包含若干综述式作品，合在一起可追溯低维铁电性如何从零散预言结晶为一个被命名的子领域。概念上最重要的是 [[../papers/huProgressProspectsLowdimensional2019|Hu & Kan (2019)]]，其摘要已完成领域定义的姿态："Functional materials with ferroelectricity or multiferroicity based on two-dimensional (2D) materials have been a fast developing field which attracts intensive investigations due to their potential applications and underlying new science. In this study, we review recent progress in theoretical simulation and experimental observation of low-dimensional multiferroic materials, including exploring 2D van der Waals ferroelectrics, designing ferroelectrics or multiferroics by various manipulations such as chemical functionalization or external strain. The reported new physical mechanisms are also provided to explain the low-dimensional multiferroics. We also discuss several exciting possibilities for design of devices based on these materials in novel applications." 这四个分句——领域身份、范围（理论+实验）、机制、器件——即是新领域的目录。

综述的内部组织反映一种演化逻辑。首先，**本征范德华铁电体**被编目：通过三聚化的 1T-MoS₂、具有面内极化并与铁弹性耦合的 IV 族单硫族化物（SnS、GeSe）、V 族元素单层（As、Sb、Bi）、面内/面外极化互锁的 α-In₂Se₃，以及半金属 WTe₂ 中反直觉的面外极化。其次，**诱导铁电性**被作为一门设计学科处理：石墨烯/锗烯/MoS₂ 的化学功能化、通过二阶 Jahn–Teller 效应对 PbTe 施加双轴应变、以及对磷烯纳米带施加垂直电场。第三，**超薄钙钛矿膜**透过容差因子透镜被重新审视，区分表面驱动的 FE 100-S、三线性耦合的 FE 110-IP 与常规 FE 110-P 态——前两者是"越薄越强"的相，其 d 轨道独立性开辟了与磁性共存的道路。第四，**多铁耦合**通过卤素功能化磷烯、配体功能化锗烯、Jahn–Teller 畸变的 CrBr₃ 以及 Hf₂VC₂F₂ MXene 中自旋驱动的非本征铁电性来追求。

同一窗口内的相邻综述在更窄领域展现同样的演化模式。[[../papers/Ismail2015humidity|Ismail et al. (2015)]] 显式将自己与既有综述文献区分："There are lots of papers reviewing on humidity sensor and most of them focusing on general types of humidity sensor which are relative humidity sensor and absolute humidity sensor. In this paper, we discuss detail on the ZnO as metal oxide humidity sensor"——这一从通用到材料专属的移动，映照出二维铁电综述从广义多铁体下移到具体材料族的过程。方法学教训是：快速增长领域中的综述必须**同时制图与收窄**：绘制整个图景以使读者理解分类学，再收窄到综述独特贡献所在的子区域。到 2019 年，低维铁电体的分类学已足够稳定，Hu & Kan 得以呈现表 1（16 种铁电体及极化值、势垒、Tc）和表 2（11 种多铁体及磁矩），而 "T"（理论）与 "E"（实验）标记的分布本身讲述了演化故事：预言密布、实验确认稀疏，In₂Se₃ 与 SnTe 被单列为从理论到实验的成功案例。

### 例句表（综述三例）

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|---|---|---|
| "Functional materials with ferroelectricity or multiferroicity based on two-dimensional (2D) materials have been a fast developing field which attracts intensive investigations due to their potential applications and underlying new science." | 基于二维材料的铁电或多铁性功能材料已成为一个快速发展的领域，因其潜在应用与背后的新科学而吸引了密集研究。 | **X based on Y have been a fast developing field which attracts intensive investigations due to** A and B. |
| "In this study, we review recent progress in theoretical simulation and experimental observation of low-dimensional multiferroic materials, including exploring 2D van der Waals ferroelectrics, designing ferroelectrics or multiferroics by various manipulations such as chemical functionalization or external strain." | 在本研究中，我们综述了低维多铁材料在理论模拟与实验观察方面的最新进展，包括探索二维范德华铁电体，以及通过化学功能化或外应变等手段设计铁电体或多铁体。 | **we review recent progress in** A and B of X, **including** V-ing …, V-ing … **by** … **such as** … |
| "There are lots of papers reviewing on humidity sensor and most of them focusing on general types of humidity sensor which are relative humidity sensor and absolute humidity sensor. In this paper, we discuss detail on the ZnO as metal oxide humidity sensor." | 已有大量湿度传感器综述，多数聚焦于相对湿度传感器与绝对湿度传感器等通用类型。本文则详细讨论作为金属氧化物湿度传感器的 ZnO。 | **There are lots of papers reviewing on** X, **and most of them focusing on** Y. **In this paper, we discuss** Z. |

**语法点**：(1) 综述摘要常用 "have been a fast developing field" 来定义领域，但该短语偏口语，正式写作可替换为 "have emerged as a rapidly maturing field"；(2) "In this study, we review recent progress in A and B of X, including V-ing …, V-ing …" 是综述摘要的核心模板，可一次交代理论/实验双轨与具体子方向；(3) "There are lots of papers… In this paper, we discuss…" 用"There be"句把自己与既有综述区分开，是综述定位的常用手法。

### 🧩 经典案例逐句拆解

> **来源**：截取自 [[../papers/huProgressProspectsLowdimensional2019|Hu & Kan]], *WIREs Computational Molecular Science* (2019)

> "Functional materials with ferroelectricity or multiferroicity based on two-dimensional (2D) materials have been a fast developing field which attracts intensive investigations due to their potential applications and underlying new science. In this study, we review recent progress in theoretical simulation and experimental observation of low-dimensional multiferroic materials, including exploring 2D van der Waals ferroelectrics, designing ferroelectrics or multiferroics by various manipulations such as chemical functionalization or external strain. The reported new physical mechanisms are also provided to explain the low-dimensional multiferroics. We also discuss several exciting possibilities for design of devices based on these materials in novel applications."

逐句标号：

① 领域定位：二维材料上的铁电/多铁已成为快速发展的领域，双驱动力为应用与新科学。
② 综述范围：理论模拟与实验观察双轨；具体子方向为范德华铁电体与功能化/应变设计。
③ 机制承诺：提供解释低维多铁性的新物理机制。
④ 应用展望：讨论基于这些材料的新型器件可能性。

中文翻译：

① 基于二维（2D）材料的铁电性或多铁性功能材料，已成为一个快速发展的领域，因其潜在应用与背后的新科学而吸引了密集研究。
② 在本研究中，我们综述了低维多铁材料在理论模拟与实验观察方面的最新进展，包括探索二维范德华铁电体，以及通过化学功能化或外应变等各种操纵手段设计铁电体或多铁体。
③ 所报道的新物理机制也被用于解释低维多铁性。
④ 我们还讨论了在新颖应用中基于这些材料设计器件的若干令人兴奋的可能性。

| 句子 | 综述职能 | 对应正文板块 |
|---|---|---|
| ① | 领域正当性 | Introduction |
| ② | 内容地图（理论 + 实验 + 设计手段） | §2 低维铁电体 + §3 多铁体 |
| ③ | 机制综合 | 各小节的机制讨论 |
| ④ | 展望 | Devices/Outlook |

**核心教训**：综述摘要四句话分别回答"Why a field / What we cover / What is new mechanistically / Where it is going"。这四个问题缺其一，摘要就会退化为目录罗列。Hu & Kan 这段之所以能成为 2019 年低维铁电领域的"宣言式"摘要，正是因为它在四句话内同时完成了**领域命名、范围界定、机制承诺和应用展望**。后来者写同类综述，可直接以这四问为自检清单。

> **段落小结**：2015–2019 是低维铁电/多铁性从"BiFeO₃ 畴翻转精细化"过渡到"二维范德华铁电作为独立领域"的转折期。写作上，IMRaD 骨架高度稳定，方法段追求可复现的高密度枚举，结果段以图引文、以数立论，讨论段把能量学/对称性论证与器件含义分层陈述；综述则通过"领域命名—分类地图—机制综合—器件展望"四段式完成对新方向的定型。
