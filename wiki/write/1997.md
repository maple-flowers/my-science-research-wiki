- Another interesting feature for the phase boundary is that it tends to have a characteristic angle of 60°, as shown in Fig. 4, which corresponds to the lattice angle of the triangular lattice.
- In summary, we have successfully fabricated the H-phase crystal sheet on the surface of a T-phase TaS2 crystal by applying a high bias voltage to a STM tip. The highresolution STM image near the phase boundary shows a mismatch of S atomic rows at the interface, suggesting a sliding of the surface S sheet in a collective way. The phase boundary turns out to have a characteristic angle of 60°. Near the triangular phase boundary we have also observed the local distortions of the CDW superlattice originating from CDW twins.
