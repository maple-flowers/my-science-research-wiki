## ✍️ 0. Title Patterns

> **本节目标**：拆解 1945–1999 年奠基性论文的标题句式。与当代"形容词 + in + 材料"的高密度标题不同，这一时代的标题更倾向于宣告方法、命名对象或直陈论题，冠词与介词使用更为完整。

### 句式一：方法宣告式 — `[方法名] [过去分词/名词化]`

| 实例 | 类型 | 骨架拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| *Special points for Brillouin-zone integrations* [[../papers/monkhorstSpecialPointsBrillouinzone1976]] | 方法宣告 | `[名词] for [计算任务]` |
| *Projector augmented-wave method* [[../papers/blochlProjectorAugmentedwaveMethod1994b]] | 方法命名 | `[方法专名] method` |
| *Efficient iterative schemes for ab initio total-energy calculations using a plane-wave basis set* [[../papers/kresseEfficientIterativeSchemes1996d]] | 方法 + 适用范围 | `[形容词] [方法] for [任务] using [工具]` |

**语法点**：三例均为名词短语，无谓语动词——这是方法学论文标题的典型形态。`for` 引向该方法所解决的计算任务（Brillouin-zone integrations / total-energy calculations），`using` 引向所依赖的数值工具（plane-wave basis set）。与当代标题相比，形容词只用一个（`Special` / `Efficient`），且不使用 `Giant`、`Robust` 这类性能宣告词；方法本身的命名就是卖点。

---

### 句式二：论题直陈式 — `[理论对象] of [体系]`

| 实例 | 类型 | 骨架拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| *Theory of polarization of crystalline solids* [[../papers/king-smithTheoryPolarizationCrystalline1993]] | 理论命题 | `Theory of [物理量] of [体系]` |
| *Electron-energy-loss spectra and the structural stability of nickel oxide: An LSDA+U study* [[../papers/dudarevElectronenergylossSpectraStructural1998a]] | 现象 + 方法（冒号双段） | `[现象 A] and [现象 B] of [材料]: [方法] study` |
| *Generalized Gradient Approximation Made Simple* [[../papers/perdewGeneralizedGradientApproximation1996a]] | 概念 + 评价（短句宣告） | `[概念] Made [形容词]` |

**语法点**：`Theory of X of Y` 用双层 `of` 嵌套，是这一时代理论论文的庄重句式；冒号双段式在 90 年代后期开始流行，前半段交代物理对象，后半段用 `An LSDA+U study` 标注方法学定位；`Made Simple` 则是罕见的口语化宣告——用过去分词作补语，暗示此前的 GGA（如 PW91）"过于复杂"，是一种克制的竞争性修辞。

---

### 句式三：从 X 到 Y — 演进/桥梁式

| 实例 | 类型 | 骨架拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| *From ultrasoft pseudopotentials to the projector augmented-wave method* [[../papers/kresseUltrasoftPseudopotentialsProjector1999c]] | 方法桥梁 | `From [旧方法] to [新方法]` |
| *Ab initio molecular dynamics for liquid metals* [[../papers/kresseInitiomolecularDynamicsLiquid1993]] | 方法 + 适用体系 | `[方法] for [难处理体系]` |
| *Efficiency of ab-initio total energy calculations for metals and semiconductors using a plane-wave basis set* [[../papers/kresseEfficiencyAbinitioTotal1996a]] | 性能 + 任务 + 工具 | `Efficiency of [任务] for [体系] using [工具]` |

**语法点**：`From A to B` 在标题中暗示"统一、桥梁、推广"——Kresse & Joubert 用它宣告 PAW 是 US-PP 的自然延伸而非断裂；`for liquid metals` 把方法的"难处理体系"直接写进标题，等于宣告方法的适用边界被推进；`Efficiency of...` 则以性能名词开头，是数值方法论文的自信姿态——不宣称新物理，只宣称"做得更快"。

## 📄 1. Abstract

> **本节目标**：掌握奠基时代摘要的"背景—缺口—方法—结果—意义"五段式。与当代高密度摘要相比，这一时代的摘要句子更长、从句嵌套更深，且倾向于在背景句中就完成对整个方法学谱系的回顾。

### ① 背景句 — 确立方法学坐标

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| "Kohn-Sham density functional theory is widely used for self-consistent-field electronic structure calculations of the ground-state properties of atoms, molecules, and solids." [[../papers/perdewGeneralizedGradientApproximation1996a]] | Kohn-Sham 密度泛函理论被广泛用于原子、分子与固体基态性质的自洽场电子结构计算。 | `[理论] is widely used for [任务] of [对象 A, B, and C]` |
| "Many calculations in crystals involve integrating periodic functions of a Bloch wave vector over either the entire Brillouin zone (BZ) or over specified portions." [[../papers/monkhorstSpecialPointsBrillouinzone1976]] | 晶体中的许多计算涉及对 Bloch 波矢的周期函数在整个布里渊区（BZ）或其指定部分上积分。 | `Many [任务] involve [动名词] [对象] over either [范围 A] or [范围 B]` |
| "For many metals, semiconductors and insulators the local spin density approximation (LSDA) to the density functional theory (DFT) is known to provide a reliable variational description of the ground state of the electronic structure of the solid." [[../papers/dudarevElectronenergylossSpectraStructural1998a]] | 对许多金属、半导体和绝缘体，密度泛函理论（DFT）的局域自旋密度近似（LSDA）被认为能对固体电子结构的基态提供可靠的变分描述。 | `For many [体系类别], [方法] is known to provide [评价] description of [对象]` |

**语法点**：三例都以一般现在时开头，把"已知共识"当作永恒事实陈述。`is widely used`、`involve`、`is known to provide` 三个谓语分别从"使用广度""任务本质""可靠性评价"三个角度切入背景。三重并列 `atoms, molecules, and solids` 与 `metals, semiconductors and insulators` 是这一时代摘要的典型铺陈——用穷尽式列举划定方法的适用版图。`is known to` 比 `is` 多一层"学界公认"的背书语气。

---

### ② 缺口句 — 指出失败案例

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| "At the same time there are cases where applications of the DFT-LSDA have been far less successful." [[../papers/dudarevElectronenergylossSpectraStructural1998a]] | 与此同时，DFT-LSDA 的应用在某些情形中远不那么成功。 | `At the same time there are cases where [方法] have been far less successful` |
| "The best choice for f(n↑, n↓, ∇n↑, ∇n↓) is still a matter of debate." [[../papers/perdewGeneralizedGradientApproximation1996a]] | f(n↑, n↓, ∇n↑, ∇n↓) 的最佳选择仍是一个有争议的问题。 | `The best choice for [数学对象] is still a matter of debate` |
| "This transfer of energy is difficult to control in metallic systems." [[../papers/kresseInitiomolecularDynamicsLiquid1993]] | 这种能量转移在金属体系中难以控制。 | `This [现象] is difficult to [动词] in [难处理体系]` |

**语法点**：三种缺口策略——`At the same time there are cases where...`（以让步副词开头，先承认成功再指出失败，语气最温和）；`is still a matter of debate`（把缺口包装成"学界争议"，避免直接批评前人）；`is difficult to control in...`（直接陈述技术障碍）。`far less successful` 用比较级 `far` 强化程度但不点名失败对象，是学术礼貌；`a matter of debate` 是奠基时代摘要中极高频的名词化表达。

---

### ③ 方法句 — 我们构造/提出

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| "We present ab initio quantum-mechanical molecular-dynamics calculations based on the calculation of the electronic ground state and of the Hellmann-Feynman forces in the local-density approximation at each molecular-dynamics step." [[../papers/kresseInitiomolecularDynamicsLiquid1993]] | 我们提出基于在每个分子动力学步中于局域密度近似下计算电子基态与 Hellmann-Feynman 力的第一性原理量子力学分子动力学计算。 | `We present [方法] based on [核心计算] at each [循环步]` |
| "A method is given for generating sets of special points in the Brillouin zone which provides an efficient means of integrating periodic functions of the wave vector." [[../papers/monkhorstSpecialPointsBrillouinzone1976]] | 给出了一种在布里渊区中生成特殊点集的方法，它为积分波矢的周期函数提供了高效手段。 | `A method is given for [动名词] [对象] which provides [评价] means of [任务]` |
| "A first-principles numerical GGA can be constructed by starting from the second-order density-gradient expansion for the exchange-correlation hole surrounding the electron in a system of slowly varying density, then cutting off its spurious long-range parts to satisfy sum rules on the exact hole." [[../papers/perdewGeneralizedGradientApproximation1996a]] | 一个第一性原理数值 GGA 可以这样构造：从缓变密度体系中电子周围交换关联空穴的二阶梯度展开出发，然后截去其虚假的长程部分以满足精确空穴的求和规则。 | `[方法] can be constructed by starting from [起点], then cutting off [部分] to satisfy [约束]` |

**语法点**：三例分别采用主动（`We present`）、被动（`A method is given`）、情态被动（`can be constructed by`）三种语态。奠基时代的方法句偏好被动——把"方法"本身置于句首主语位置，弱化作者存在感；`by starting from..., then cutting off...to satisfy...` 用两个动名词短语串联"起点—操作—约束"三步，是构造性方法描述的黄金骨架。

### ④ 结果句 — 验证与数据

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| "Applications for liquid metals ranging from Na to Ge demonstrate very good agreement with the measured atomic structure factors and electronic spectra." [[../papers/kresseInitiomolecularDynamicsLiquid1993]] | 对从 Na 到 Ge 的液态金属的应用展示了与实测原子结构因子和电子谱的极好吻合。 | `Applications for [体系] ranging from A to B demonstrate [程度] agreement with [实验数据]` |
| "We have found that by using the same value of the effective Hubbard U characterizing on-site Coulomb repulsion between 3d electrons, it is possible to give a reasonably good description of the measured electron energy loss spectra and values of the lattice constant, cohesive energy and elastic moduli." [[../papers/dudarevElectronenergylossSpectraStructural1998a]] | 我们发现，通过使用刻画 3d 电子间在位库仑排斥的有效 Hubbard U 的同一个值，可以对实测电子能量损失谱以及晶格常数、结合能和弹性模量给出相当好的描述。 | `We have found that by using [参数], it is possible to give [评价] description of [数据 A, B, C, and D]` |
| "The measured detectivity of the film was of the order of 10⁸ cm Hz¹/² W⁻¹ for 500 K black body temperature with 800 Hz chopping frequency and 10 per cent electrical bandwidth at 77 K." [[../papers/Srinivasan1989lead]] | 在 77 K、800 Hz 斩波频率与 10% 电带宽下、针对 500 K 黑体温度，所测得的薄膜探测率约为 10⁸ cm Hz¹/² W⁻¹。 | `The measured [量] was of the order of [数值] for [条件 A] with [条件 B] at [条件 C]` |

**语法点**：`demonstrate very good agreement with`、`give a reasonably good description of`、`was of the order of` 是三种不同强度的结果陈述——`demonstrate` 最强（以应用证明），`give a description` 居中（描述性吻合），`of the order of` 最弱（仅量级吻合）。多重实验条件用 `for / with / at` 三个介词分层挂接，是实验论文结果句的标准挂接法。

### ⑤ 意义句 — 克制的展望

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| "The relationships to the Chadi-Cohen and Gilat-Raubenheimer methods are indicated." [[../papers/monkhorstSpecialPointsBrillouinzone1976]] | 指出了与 Chadi-Cohen 及 Gilat-Raubenheimer 方法的关系。 | `The relationships to [前人方法 A and B] are indicated` |
| "However, its simpler form and derivation make it easier to understand, apply, and perhaps improve." [[../papers/perdewGeneralizedGradientApproximation1996a]] | 然而，其更简单的形式与推导使其更易于理解、应用，并或许易于改进。 | `However, its [优点] make it easier to [动词 A, B, and perhaps C]` |
| "To further improve the energy conservation and to extend the length of the simulation remains a challenge." [[../papers/kresseInitiomolecularDynamicsLiquid1993]] | 进一步改善能量守恒并延长模拟时长仍是一项挑战。 | `To further improve A and to extend B remains a challenge` |

**语法点**：与当代摘要的 `pave the way toward`、`establish a new paradigm` 相比，奠基时代的意义句极度克制。`are indicated`（被指出）几乎是最弱的意义陈述；`easier to understand, apply, and perhaps improve` 用三个并列动词加一个 `perhaps`，把"可改进性"谦逊地留作可能性；`remains a challenge` 则把局限公开化——这一时代的作者不惮于在摘要中承认未解决的问题。

### 🧩 经典案例逐句拆解：一篇完整摘要

以下是 Dudarev et al. (1998) 关于 LSDA+U 方法的摘要前半部分，逐句标注功能与语法：

> **来源**：截取自 [[../papers/dudarevElectronenergylossSpectraStructural1998a|Dudarev et al. (1998)]], *Phys. Rev. B*

> **①** For many metals, semiconductors and insulators the local spin density approximation (LSDA) to the density functional theory (DFT) is known to provide a reliable variational description of the ground state of the electronic structure of the solid. **②** At the same time there are cases where applications of the DFT-LSDA have been far less successful. **③** The main feature distinguishing transition metal oxides from more conventional oxides like MgO or TiO2 is that in the former case ions of metal contain partly filled d shells. **④** Since the strength of the effective on-site Coulomb interaction between d electrons (characterized by the Hubbard U) is comparable with the valence bandwidth, the processes associated with electron transfer between two metal ions or resulting from addition or removal of d electrons give rise to large fluctuations of the energy of the system, leading to the localization of carriers and to the formation of band gaps. **⑤** We demonstrate how by taking better account of electron correlations in the 3d shell of metal ions in nickel oxide it is possible to improve the description of both electron energy loss spectra and parameters characterizing the structural stability of the material compared with local spin density functional theory.
>
> **中文翻译**：**①** 对许多金属、半导体和绝缘体，密度泛函理论（DFT）的局域自旋密度近似（LSDA）被认为能对固体电子结构的基态提供可靠的变分描述。**②** 与此同时，DFT-LSDA 的应用在某些情形中远不那么成功。**③** 过渡金属氧化物区别于 MgO、TiO2 等更常规氧化物的主要特征在于，前者的金属离子含有部分填充的 d 壳层。**④** 由于 d 电子之间有效在位库仑相互作用（以 Hubbard U 表征）的强度与价带带宽相当，与两个金属离子间电子转移或 d 电子增减相关的过程会引起系统能量的大幅涨落，导致载流子局域化并形成带隙。**⑤** 我们展示了如何通过更好地考虑氧化镍中金属离子 3d 壳层的电子关联，相较于局域自旋密度泛函理论，改进对电子能量损失谱以及表征材料结构稳定性参数的描述。

| 句 | 功能 | 逐句拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **①** | **背景（共识）** | `For many [三类体系], [方法] is known to provide [评价] description`（介词短语划定适用范围→被动语态锚定共识）。**三重并列 `metals, semiconductors and insulators` 是穷尽式铺陈，等于在说"该方法几乎处处成功"，为句②的转折蓄势。** |
| **②** | **缺口（转折）** | `At the same time there are cases where...have been far less successful`（让步副词 + there be 句型 + 现在完成时）。**`At the same time` 比 `However` 更温和——不否定前句，只是补充"还有别的情形"；`far less successful` 用比较级而非 `failed`，是学术礼貌。** |
| **③** | **收窄（对象定位）** | `The main feature distinguishing A from B is that...`（现在分词后置定语 + is that 表语从句）。**一句话把"失败案例"收窄到"过渡金属氧化物"这一具体类别，并点出其物理根源（partly filled d shells），是漏斗式摘要的典型收窄句。** |
| **④** | **机制（因果展开）** | `Since [原因], [过程] give rise to [结果], leading to [进一步结果]`（Since 原因状语从句 + 主句 + 现在分词伴随状语）。**这是全摘要最长的一句，用 `Since...give rise to...leading to...` 串起"原因→直接后果→进一步后果"的三级因果链，一个句子完成物理机制交代。** |
| **⑤** | **方法 + 结果** | `We demonstrate how by taking [措施] it is possible to improve [对象 A and B] compared with [旧方法]`（主动主句 + how 引导宾语从句 + 被动形式主语 + compared with 比较状语）。**`how by taking...it is possible` 是一个嵌入式倒装——正常语序应为 `it is possible to improve...by taking...`，把 `by taking` 提前到 `how` 之后是为了强调"措施"是新贡献。** |

**核心教训**：这篇摘要的结构是"共识→例外→收窄→机制→贡献"五步漏斗，比当代摘要的"背景+问题"两句合并更为从容。句④ 的长因果链是这一时代摘要的标志性句法——作者信任读者能跟随一个 60 词以上的复合句，不急于拆成短句。

## 🚪 2. Introduction & Scientific Background

> **本节目标**：理解奠基时代引言的"谱系回顾—失败定位—本文承诺"三段式。这一时代的引言不像当代论文那样在首段就用 `The past decade has witnessed` 制造热度，而是倾向于从一个稳固的共识出发，逐段收紧至一个尚未解决的具体矛盾。

### 第一段：谱系式背景开场

奠基时代的引言首句几乎不用"热度"类动词（attract / spark / witness），而是直接陈述一个理论或方法的当前地位，仿佛在为读者绘制一张方法学地图。Perdew et al. 以 "Kohn-Sham density functional theory is widely used for self-consistent-field electronic structure calculations..." 开场 [[../papers/perdewGeneralizedGradientApproximation1996a]]，一句话把理论名称、任务类型、适用对象（atoms, molecules, and solids）全部交代清楚；Kresse & Hafner 则以历史回顾开场："A few years ago Car and Parrinello introduced an approach that unifies molecular-dynamics techniques for the calculation of the atomic structure with the local-density approximation (LDA) for electronic structure calculations." [[../papers/kresseInitiomolecularDynamicsLiquid1993]]——用 `A few years ago [Author] introduced` 把当前工作锚定在一条可追溯的方法学谱系上。实验论文同样遵循这一逻辑，Srinivasan 以 "Infrared imaging and monitoring systems have gained strategic importance in recent years both for civilian and military applications." [[../papers/Srinivasan1989lead]] 开场，虽然用了现在完成时 `have gained`，但 `strategic importance` 与 `both for civilian and military applications` 的双重铺陈仍是"领域地位 + 应用版图"的图谱式写法。

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| "A few years ago Car and Parrinello introduced an approach that unifies molecular-dynamics techniques for the calculation of the atomic structure with the local-density approximation (LDA) for electronic structure calculations." [[../papers/kresseInitiomolecularDynamicsLiquid1993]] | 几年前，Car 与 Parrinello 提出了一种将原子结构计算的分子动力学技术与电子结构计算的局域密度近似（LDA）统一起来的方法。 | `A few years ago [作者] introduced an approach that unifies [方法 A] for [任务 A] with [方法 B] for [任务 B]` |
| "Infrared imaging and monitoring systems have gained strategic importance in recent years both for civilian and military applications." [[../papers/Srinivasan1989lead]] | 红外成像与监测系统近年来在民用和军事应用中都获得了战略重要性。 | `[领域] have gained [评价] importance in recent years both for A and B applications` |
| "The most popular functionals have a form appropriate for slowly varying densities: the local spin density (LSD) approximation." [[../papers/perdewGeneralizedGradientApproximation1996a]] | 最流行的泛函具有适合缓变密度的形式：局域自旋密度（LSD）近似。 | `The most popular [对象] have a form appropriate for [条件]: [具体名称]` |

**语法点**：三例分别采用"历史锚点（过去时 introduced）""地位上升（现在完成时 have gained）""当前分类（一般现在时 have）"三种时态策略。`unifies A with B` 比 `combines A and B` 更强调"原本分立的两者被统一"；`both for A and B` 是穷尽式应用列举；冒号后直接给出专有名词（LSD），是"先描述性质、后命名"的教学式写法。

### 第二段：失败的精确定位

当代论文的缺口句常用 `Despite extensive efforts, ... remains elusive`，而奠基时代的作者更倾向于用一整段精确刻画失败的边界——在哪些体系失败、失败的物理表征是什么、前人尝试过什么补救。Dudarev et al. 的引言是这一写法的典范："At the same time there are cases where applications of the DFT-LSDA have been far less successful. In particular, difficulties arise when a conventional DFT-LSDA approach is applied to the treatment of the electronic structure of the material where some of the ions contain partly filled valence d or f shells." [[../papers/dudarevElectronenergylossSpectraStructural1998a]] 先用 `At the same time` 温和转折，再用 `In particular` 把失败情形从泛泛的"cases"收紧到"partly filled d or f shells"这一具体结构特征。紧接着，作者引用前人工作："It was shown by Terakura et al. that for many of the transition metal oxides the DFT-LSDA predicts metallic ground states instead of experimentally observed insulating ones."——用 `It was shown by [Author] that` 的被动句式把他人的负面结果摆出来，避免自己直接批评方法。再进一步："If antiferromagnetic order is taken into account, the DFT-LSDA treatment may lead to an insulating state but the forbidden gap still turns out to be an order of magnitude smaller than that observed using electron spectroscopy."——条件句 + `but` 转折 + `an order of magnitude smaller` 定量比较，三层递进把"部分补救仍不够"说透。最后："In addition to making incorrect predictions regarding the nature of the ground state of transition metal and actinide oxides, the DFT-LSDA systematically underestimates their equilibrium lattice constants and overestimates binding energies." [[../papers/dudarevElectronenergylossSpectraStructural1998a]] 用 `In addition to...the DFT-LSDA systematically underestimates...and overestimates...` 把失败从"基态性质"扩展到"结构参数"，完成对旧方法缺陷的全维度列举。

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| "It was shown by Terakura et al. that for many of the transition metal oxides the DFT-LSDA predicts metallic ground states instead of experimentally observed insulating ones." [[../papers/dudarevElectronenergylossSpectraStructural1998a]] | Terakura 等人表明，对许多过渡金属氧化物，DFT-LSDA 预言了金属基态，而非实验观测到的绝缘基态。 | `It was shown by [作者] that for [体系], [旧方法] predicts [错误结果] instead of [实验结果]` |
| "PW91 incorporates some inhomogeneity effects while retaining many of the best features of LSD, but has its own problems." [[../papers/perdewGeneralizedGradientApproximation1996a]] | PW91 在保留 LSD 许多最佳特性的同时纳入了一些非均匀性效应，但也有其自身的问题。 | `[旧方法] incorporates [改进] while retaining [优点], but has its own problems` |
| "In metals, this separation is absent and there are essentially two mechanisms that drive metallic systems into nonadiabaticity." [[../papers/kresseInitiomolecularDynamicsLiquid1993]] | 在金属中，这种分离不存在，且基本上有两种机制驱使金属体系进入非绝热性。 | `In [体系], this [性质] is absent and there are essentially [数量] mechanisms that drive [体系] into [状态]` |

**语法点**：`It was shown by X that` 是把批评"外包"给前人的经典被动句式；`incorporates A while retaining B, but has its own problems` 用三个谓语（纳入/保留/但有问题）在一句内完成"承认优点—指出缺陷"的平衡评价；`this separation is absent` 中的 `this` 回指上文（绝缘体中电子与离子自由度的分离），是引言段内保持衔接的高频指代词。

### 第三段：本文承诺

在精确划定缺口之后，奠基时代的引言用极为直接的句式宣告本文贡献，常用 "This paper presents..."、"Below we address..."、"We solve..." 等。Monkhorst & Pack 写道："This paper presents an alternate approach which yields sets of points identical to those given by CC and additional sets with the same properties." [[../papers/monkhorstSpecialPointsBrillouinzone1976]]——一句话说明"替代方法 + 与前人关系 + 额外贡献"。Dudarev et al. 的承诺句更长："Below we address this question and, by using nickel oxide as an example, show how it can be answered within the framework of a recently proposed computational approach bridging the DFT-LSDA with the unrestricted Hartree-Fock (UHF) approximation (the LSDA+U method)." [[../papers/dudarevElectronenergylossSpectraStructural1998a]]——`Below we address this question and...show how...` 用两个并列动词承诺"提出问题 + 给出答案"，`by using nickel oxide as an example` 交代验证体系，`bridging A with B` 则给新方法一个"桥梁"定位。Perdew et al. 的承诺最自信："We solve the 6 problems above with a simple new derivation of a simple new GGA functional in which all parameters [other than those in ε_XC^{unif}(n↑, n↓)] are fundamental constants." [[../papers/perdewGeneralizedGradientApproximation1996a]]——`We solve the 6 problems above` 直接回指前文列举的六个缺陷，数量词 `6` 让承诺可核查，`simple new...simple new` 的重复则是刻意的修辞（简单性是本文卖点）。

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| "This paper presents an alternate approach which yields sets of points identical to those given by CC and additional sets with the same properties." [[../papers/monkhorstSpecialPointsBrillouinzone1976]] | 本文提出一种替代方法，它产生与 CC 所给相同的点集以及具有相同性质的额外点集。 | `This paper presents an alternate approach which yields [与前人相同的结果] and [额外结果]` |
| "Below we address this question and, by using [体系] as an example, show how it can be answered within the framework of [新方法]." [[../papers/dudarevElectronenergylossSpectraStructural1998a]] | 在下文中我们处理这一问题，并以[体系]为例，展示如何能在[新方法]的框架内回答它。 | `Below we address this question and, by using X as an example, show how it can be answered within the framework of Y` |
| "In contrast to the construction of the PW91 functional, which was designed to satisfy as many exact conditions as possible, the GGA presented here satisfies only those which are energetically significant." [[../papers/perdewGeneralizedGradientApproximation1996a]] | 与被设计为满足尽可能多精确条件的 PW91 泛函构造不同，这里提出的 GGA 仅满足那些在能量上重要的条件。 | `In contrast to [旧方法], which was designed to [旧目标], the [新方法] presented here satisfies only those which are [新准则]` |

**语法点**：`This paper presents`（论文作主语，最非人格化）、`Below we address`（作者作主语，与读者直接对话）、`In contrast to [旧方法]...the GGA presented here...`（新旧对比作主语，最有竞争性）三种承诺句式分别对应三种作者姿态：谦逊、对话、进取。`In contrast to A, which was designed to..., B presented here satisfies only...` 中的 `only` 是关键——把"少做"重新定义为"精做"，是奠基时代克制但有力的差异化修辞。

### 🧩 经典案例逐句拆解：引言的缺口定位段

以下是 Dudarev et al. (1998) 引言中连续的缺口定位段，展示"失败类型学"的写法：

> **来源**：截取自 [[../papers/dudarevElectronenergylossSpectraStructural1998a|Dudarev et al. (1998)]], *Phys. Rev. B*

> **①** At the same time there are cases where applications of the DFT-LSDA have been far less successful. **②** In particular, difficulties arise when a conventional DFT-LSDA approach is applied to the treatment of the electronic structure of the material where some of the ions contain partly filled valence d or f shells. **③** The origin of the failure of the DFT-LSDA in transition metal oxides is known to be associated with an inadequate description of the strong Coulomb repulsion between 3d electrons localized on metal ions. **④** It was shown by Terakura et al. that for many of the transition metal oxides the DFT-LSDA predicts metallic ground states instead of experimentally observed insulating ones. **⑤** If antiferromagnetic order is taken into account, the DFT-LSDA treatment may lead to an insulating state but the forbidden gap still turns out to be an order of magnitude smaller than that observed using electron spectroscopy. **⑥** In addition to making incorrect predictions regarding the nature of the ground state of transition metal and actinide oxides, the DFT-LSDA systematically underestimates their equilibrium lattice constants and overestimates binding energies.
>
> **中文翻译**：**①** 与此同时，DFT-LSDA 的应用在某些情形中远不那么成功。**②** 特别是，当常规 DFT-LSDA 方法被用于处理某些离子含有部分填充价 d 或 f 壳层的材料的电子结构时，困难就出现了。**③** DFT-LSDA 在过渡金属氧化物中失败的起源被认为与对定域在金属离子上的 3d 电子间强库仑排斥描述不充分有关。**④** Terakura 等人表明，对许多过渡金属氧化物，DFT-LSDA 预言金属基态而非实验观测到的绝缘基态。**⑤** 如果考虑反铁磁序，DFT-LSDA 处理可能导致绝缘态，但禁带仍比电子能谱观测到的小一个数量级。**⑥** 除了对过渡金属和锕系氧化物基态性质做出错误预言外，DFT-LSDA 还系统性地低估其平衡晶格常数并高估结合能。

| 句 | 功能 | 逐句拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **①** | **总起缺口** | `At the same time there are cases where...have been far less successful`（让步副词 + there be + 现在完成时）。**这是整段的"软启动"——不直接说"失败"，而说"远不那么成功"，留足学术礼貌。** |
| **②** | **收窄失败情形** | `In particular, difficulties arise when...is applied to...where...`（`In particular` 收窄 + when 时间状语 + where 关系从句嵌套）。**一句话完成两次收窄：从 cases → partly filled d/f shells，用两个从句把"失败发生在什么结构特征下"说清楚。** |
| **③** | **诊断物理根源** | `The origin of the failure...is known to be associated with [物理原因]`（名词化主语 + 被动 + be associated with）。**`The origin of...is associated with` 是因果诊断的黄金句式，比直接说 `because` 更名词化、更正式。** |
| **④** | **引用前人负面结果** | `It was shown by [作者] that for [体系], [方法] predicts [错误结果] instead of [实验结果]`（被动外置主语 + instead of 对比）。**把批评外包给文献，自己只做陈述者，是引言中规避"直接攻击方法"的安全策略。** |
| **⑤** | **承认部分补救但仍不足** | `If [补救措施] is taken into account, [方法] may lead to [部分正确结果] but [关键量] still turns out to be [定量偏差]`（If 条件句 + may lead to + but 转折 + turns out to be）。**`may lead to` 用情态动词承认可能性，`still turns out to be` 用 `turn out` 暗示"即使如此仍被证明不足"，`an order of magnitude smaller` 给出定量严重性。** |
| **⑥** | **扩展失败维度** | `In addition to making incorrect predictions regarding [维度 A], [方法] systematically underestimates [量 B] and overestimates [量 C]`（介词短语承上 + 两个对称谓语）。**`In addition to` 把失败从"基态性质"扩展到"结构参数"；`systematically underestimates...and overestimates...` 用两个反义动词对称列举，形成"方法有偏"的完整画像。** |

**核心教训**：奠基时代的缺口段不是一句话（`X remains elusive`），而是一整段"失败类型学"——先说有失败，再说失败在什么情形下发生，再诊断原因，再引用他人证据，再承认部分补救仍不足，最后扩展到更多失败维度。这种"层层收紧"的写法让审稿人无法说"你对旧方法的批评不公允"，因为每一层都有条件、有引用、有定量。

## ⚙️ 3. Key Methodological Developments

> **本节目标**：掌握奠基时代方法段的"数学推导—算法描述—实现细节—收敛判据"四层结构。这一时代的方法论文（尤其是 Kresse、Blöchl、Monkhorst、Perdew）几乎都遵循这一次序，且每一层都有特定的句式标记。

### 第一层：数学推导

推导段的句子以 "It is straightforward to..."、"It can now easily be seen that..."、"Substituting Eq. (X)..." 等标记开头，呈现一种"与读者一同推导"的教学语气。Monkhorst & Pack 的推导段是典范："If we now impose the restriction |R_i| < q/2, |R'_i| < q/2 (i = 1, 2, 3), then it follows that θ^{mn}(q) = δ_{mn}." [[../papers/monkhorstSpecialPointsBrillouinzone1976]]——条件句 + `it follows that` 是数学推导中最常见的结论引出句式。紧接着："In other words, all those functions A_m(k) for which Eq. (10) is satisfied are orthonormal on the discrete set of BZ points k_{prs}."——用 `In other words` 把数学符号翻译成物理语言。Perdew et al. 的推导则采用构造式语气："We begin with the GGA for correlation in the form E_C^{GGA}[n↑, n↓] = ∫ d³r n [ε_C^{unif}(r_s, ζ) + H(r_s, ζ, t)]." [[../papers/perdewGeneralizedGradientApproximation1996a]] 以及 "We construct the gradient contribution H from three conditions."——`We begin with...`、`We construct...from N conditions` 是构造性推导的标准开场。

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| "If we now impose the restriction [条件], then it follows that [结论]." [[../papers/monkhorstSpecialPointsBrillouinzone1976]] | 如果我们现在施加[条件]这一限制，则有[结论]。 | `If we now impose the restriction [条件], then it follows that [结论]` |
| "Substituting Eq. (5) for A_m and A_n we can reduce S_{mn}(q) to [表达式]." [[../papers/monkhorstSpecialPointsBrillouinzone1976]] | 将 A_m 和 A_n 代入式 (5)，我们可以将 S_{mn}(q) 化简为[表达式]。 | `Substituting Eq. (X) for [变量], we can reduce [表达式] to [结果]` |
| "It is straightforward to confirm that the product over j in Eq. (15) is independent of how the phases of the wave functions are chosen." [[../papers/king-smithTheoryPolarizationCrystalline1993]] | 容易确认式 (15) 中对 j 的乘积与波函数相位的选择方式无关。 | `It is straightforward to confirm that [数学对象] is independent of how [选择]` |

**语法点**：`If we now impose...then it follows that...` 是推导中"施加约束→得出结论"的标准双句结构，`now` 在口语中多余但在推导段中起"步骤推进"作用；`Substituting...we can reduce...to...` 用现在分词开头，是代数操作的高频句式；`It is straightforward to confirm that` 比 `It is easy to show that` 更正式，且 `confirm` 暗示"读者可自行验证"，是教学式推导的信任姿态。

### 第二层：算法描述

推导完成后，方法段转向"算法如何在计算机上执行"。Kresse & Hafner 对其双重迭代算法的描述是经典样本："The method is a doubly iterative one: in the inner loop the wave functions for each k point in the Brillouin zone and each band are improved by a preconditioned conjugate-gradient method." [[../papers/kresseInitiomolecularDynamicsLiquid1993]]——先用 `The method is a doubly iterative one` 给出算法的总体结构标签，再用冒号展开内层循环。紧接其后："After running over all bands (including some empty bands), a subspace diagonalization is performed, the new Fermi energy is calculated using a Gaussian broadening of the energy levels, and the charge density is updated."——`After [步骤 A], [步骤 B] is performed, [步骤 C] is calculated using..., and [步骤 D] is updated` 用被动语态串联四个串行操作。收敛判据也用被动："The electronic-energy minimization is terminated when the change in the energy per atom becomes smaller than 1.5 × 10⁻⁵ eV." [[../papers/kresseInitiomolecularDynamicsLiquid1993]] 而原子运动则另起一句："The atomic motion is described by Nose dynamics generating a canonical ensemble at prefixed temperature."

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| "After running over all bands (including some empty bands), a subspace diagonalization is performed, the new Fermi energy is calculated using a Gaussian broadening of the energy levels, and the charge density is updated." [[../papers/kresseInitiomolecularDynamicsLiquid1993]] | 在遍历所有能带（包括部分空带）后，执行子空间对角化，用能级的高斯展宽计算新的费米能，并更新电荷密度。 | `After [步骤 A], [步骤 B] is performed, [步骤 C] is calculated using..., and [步骤 D] is updated` |
| "Complete self-consistency loop consists of several steps: subspace rotation, CG or RMM-DIIS minimization, orthonormalization, update of partial occupancies and charge density." [[../papers/kresseEfficientIterativeSchemes1996d]] | 完整的自洽循环由若干步骤组成：子空间旋转、CG 或 RMM-DIIS 最小化、正交化、部分占据数与电荷密度更新。 | `The complete loop consists of several steps: [步骤 A], [B], [C], [D and E]` |
| "In each iteration the initial trial set is equivalent to the final set of the previous iteration, initialization is done with a random number generator." [[../papers/kresseEfficientIterativeSchemes1996d]] | 在每次迭代中，初始试探集等价于上一次迭代的最终集，初始化由随机数生成器完成。 | `In each iteration [状态 A] is equivalent to [状态 B], initialization is done with [工具]` |

**语法点**：算法段偏好被动语态与名词并列——`is performed / is calculated / is updated` 三个被动谓语并列，避免了 "we do A, then we do B" 的流水账；`consists of several steps: A, B, C, D and E` 用冒号加名词清单，是交代算法组成的最紧凑方式；`is done with` 是 "is performed using" 的口语化简写，在 90 年代代码论文中常见。

### 第三层：实现细节

算法之下是实现层面的选择——部分波如何构造、投影子如何选取、截断半径如何设定。Blöchl 的 PAW 论文是这一层的范本："The AE partial waves are obtained by radially integrating the Schrodinger equation outward for the self-consistent atomic AE potential and a set of energies." [[../papers/blochlProjectorAugmentedwaveMethod1994b]] 以及 "The AE partial waves are chosen to describe the physically relevant states, i.e., those from the valence band region, reasonably well." [[../papers/blochlProjectorAugmentedwaveMethod1994b]]——`are obtained by [操作] for [条件]` 与 `are chosen to [目的], i.e., [同位语解释]` 是实现细节的两个标准句式。参数选择则用主动："Often we choose A = 6 and r_g as three-quarters of the covalent radius." [[../papers/blochlProjectorAugmentedwaveMethod1994b]]——`Often we choose A = N and B as [分数] of [物理量]` 用主动语态加频率副词 `Often`，暗示这是经验性默认值而非严格推导。

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| "The AE partial waves are obtained by radially integrating the Schrodinger equation outward for the self-consistent atomic AE potential and a set of energies." [[../papers/blochlProjectorAugmentedwaveMethod1994b]] | AE 部分波通过对自洽原子 AE 势和一组能量向外径向积分薛定谔方程而获得。 | `[对象] are obtained by [副词+动名词] [方程] outward for [条件 A and B]` |
| "The values of the cutoff radius and the exponent are chosen such that this potential is virtually identical to the AE atomic potential outside the augmentation region." [[../papers/blochlProjectorAugmentedwaveMethod1994b]] | 截断半径与指数的值被选得使该势在增广区外实际上与 AE 原子势相同。 | `The values of [参数 A and B] are chosen such that [判据]` |
| "To prevent charge sloshing, the mixing scheme proposed by Kerker is used." [[../papers/kresseInitiomolecularDynamicsLiquid1993]] | 为防止电荷振荡，采用了 Kerker 提出的混合方案。 | `To prevent [问题], the scheme proposed by [作者] is used` |

**语法点**：实现细节段高度依赖被动语态（`are obtained`、`are chosen`、`is used`），因为焦点是"对象如何被构造"而非"谁做了选择"；`such that` 引导的结果状语从句是参数选择的标准判据引出方式；`To prevent [问题], [方案] is used` 用不定式目的状语 + 被动主句，先交代"为什么需要这个技巧"，再交代"用了什么"。

### 第四层：收敛与预处理

最后一层是数值稳定性——预处理矩阵、混合方案、收敛监控。Kresse & Furthmüller 写道："Nevertheless, the inversion of H - ε_app S is not easier than the diagonalization of the matrix H and definitely intractable for large matrices. This makes a more approximate treatment necessary." [[../papers/kresseEfficiencyAbinitioTotal1996a]]——先用 `Nevertheless` 承认精确方案不可行，再用 `This makes...necessary` 引出近似处理的必要性。预处理函数的选择则用主动 + 文献引用："Therefore, we actually adopted the preconditioning function proposed by Teter et al." [[../papers/kresseEfficiencyAbinitioTotal1996a]] 收敛监控采用建议式被动："In practice one simply monitors the convergence of both the actual value of f as q increases, and the rapidity with which their magnitudes fall off for increasing m values." [[../papers/monkhorstSpecialPointsBrillouinzone1976]]

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| "The key point of quasi-Newton methods is the assumption that the residual vector can be linearized near the minimum." [[../papers/kresseEfficiencyAbinitioTotal1996a]] | 拟牛顿法的要点是残差向量可在极小值附近被线性化这一假设。 | `The key point of [方法] is the assumption that [数学命题]` |
| "This approach is flexible and for a special parameter setting the charge density mixing schemes of Pulay and that proposed by Srivastava and Blügel are obtained." [[../papers/kresseEfficiencyAbinitioTotal1996a]] | 这一方法是灵活的，在特殊参数设置下可得到 Pulay 的电荷密度混合方案以及 Srivastava 和 Blügel 提出的方案。 | `This approach is flexible and for [条件] the schemes of [作者 A] and that proposed by [作者 B] are obtained` |
| "Generally it does not improve the convergence speed in comparison to the 'smoother' preconditioning function (53)." [[../papers/kresseEfficiencyAbinitioTotal1996a]] | 一般而言，与"更光滑"的预处理函数 (53) 相比，它并不能改善收敛速度。 | `Generally it does not improve [指标] in comparison to [替代方案]` |

**语法点**：`The key point of X is the assumption that Y` 是提炼方法核心假设的句式；`for a special parameter setting...are obtained` 用被动展示"本方法包含前人方案作为特例"，是一种兼容性宣示；`Generally it does not improve...in comparison to...` 则是诚实地报告负面比较结果，`Generally` 暗示"大多数情况下"但保留例外可能。

### 🧩 经典案例逐句拆解：算法循环段

以下是 Kresse & Hafner (1993) 对其第一性原理分子动力学算法核心循环的连续描述，展示"内层循环—外层更新—收敛判据"的完整链条：

> **来源**：截取自 [[../papers/kresseInitiomolecularDynamicsLiquid1993|Kresse & Hafner (1993)]], *Phys. Rev. B*

> **①** The method is a doubly iterative one: in the inner loop the wave functions for each k point in the Brillouin zone and each band are improved by a preconditioned conjugate-gradient method. **②** After running over all bands (including some empty bands), a subspace diagonalization is performed, the new Fermi energy is calculated using a Gaussian broadening of the energy levels, and the charge density is updated. **③** The electronic-energy minimization is terminated when the change in the energy per atom becomes smaller than 1.5 × 10⁻⁵ eV. **④** The atomic motion is described by Nose dynamics generating a canonical ensemble at prefixed temperature. **⑤** After moving the atoms, the new wave functions are estimated by using the subspace alignment. **⑥** To prevent charge sloshing, the mixing scheme proposed by Kerker is used. **⑦** This scheme has the advantage of damping the oscillations in the low-q components of the charge density.
>
> **中文翻译**：**①** 该方法是双重迭代的：在内层循环中，布里渊区中每个 k 点与每条能带的波函数由预处理共轭梯度法改进。**②** 在遍历所有能带（包括部分空带）后，执行子空间对角化，用能级的高斯展宽计算新的费米能，并更新电荷密度。**③** 当每原子能量变化小于 1.5 × 10⁻⁵ eV 时，电子能量最小化终止。**④** 原子运动由 Nose 动力学描述，在预设温度下产生正则系综。**⑤** 移动原子后，通过子空间对齐估计新的波函数。**⑥** 为防止电荷振荡，采用 Kerker 提出的混合方案。**⑦** 该方案具有抑制电荷密度低 q 分量振荡的优点。

| 句 | 功能 | 逐句拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **①** | **算法总标签 + 内层** | `The method is a doubly iterative one: in the inner loop [对象] are improved by [方法]`（判断句 + 冒号展开）。**先用 `doubly iterative` 给算法贴结构标签，再用冒号展开内层——读者立刻知道"有两层，先讲里面这层"。** |
| **②** | **内层后处理** | `After [步骤 A], [B] is performed, [C] is calculated using..., and [D] is updated`（介词 + 三个被动并列）。**三个被动谓语串行，无主语（we），让算法本身成为叙述主角——这是方法段去人格化的典型写法。** |
| **③** | **收敛判据** | `[最小化] is terminated when [指标] becomes smaller than [阈值]`（被动 + when 条件从句）。**`is terminated when` 比 `stops when` 更正式；给出具体阈值 1.5 × 10⁻⁵ eV 是可重复性的锚点。** |
| **④** | **外层（离子）** | `The atomic motion is described by [方法] generating [系综] at [条件]`（被动 + 现在分词后置定语）。**从电子转到离子用一句独立的被动句切换，`generating a canonical ensemble` 是现在分词补充物理含义。** |
| **⑤** | **内外层衔接** | `After moving the atoms, the new wave functions are estimated by using [技巧]`（介词 + 被动）。**`After moving the atoms` 省略了逻辑主语（we），是方法段常见的悬垂分词——因主语明确而可接受。** |
| **⑥** | **数值稳定技巧** | `To prevent [问题], the scheme proposed by [作者] is used`（不定式目的 + 被动）。**先讲"要防止什么"，再讲"用了什么"，目的状语前置让读者理解该技巧的必要性。** |
| **⑦** | **技巧的优点** | `This scheme has the advantage of [动名词] in [分量]`（主动 + 名词化优点）。**`has the advantage of` 是解释"为什么选这个方案"的标准句式，比 `because it damps...` 更名词化。** |

**核心教训**：方法段的每一句都在回答一个隐含问题——"算法结构是什么？"（①）、"内层结束后做什么？"（②）、"何时停止？"（③）、"离子怎么动？"（④）、"新旧波函数如何衔接？"（⑤）、"如何防止数值不稳定？"（⑥）、"为什么选这个方案？"（⑦）。七个问题环环相扣，整段没有一句是"我们做了 X"的流水账，全部以算法对象为主语。

## 📊 4. Core Results & Physical Mechanisms

> **本节目标**：掌握奠基时代结果段的"验证—比较—机制归因"三层写法。这一时代的结果段不追求破折号夹注的高密度数据展示，而是用完整的复合句把"算得准不准""与谁比""为什么"说透。

### 第一层：与实验的吻合

结果段最常见的开场是报告计算值与实验值的吻合程度，且使用一套分级的形容词：`excellent` > `very good` > `good` > `reasonable` > `satisfactory`。Kresse & Hafner 写道："The pair correlation function is in excellent agreement with experiment." [[../papers/kresseInitiomolecularDynamicsLiquid1993]]——最短的吻合报告句，主语是物理量，谓语是 `is in excellent agreement with`。更详细的："The diffusion constant is approximately D = 6 × 10⁻⁵ cm²/s, again in reasonable agreement with experiment."——数值 + `again`（暗示这是第二个吻合）+ `reasonable`（比 excellent 低一档）。King-Smith & Vanderbilt 的压电常数验证则给出百分比："Our calculations on the strained crystal yielded a (a/e)γ'₁₄ of -1.352. The value agrees to better than 5% with the result obtained from linear-response methods." [[../papers/king-smithTheoryPolarizationCrystalline1993]] 以及 "Our overall value for the piezoelectric constant γ₁₄ was -0.28, compared with an experimental value of -0.32."

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| "The pair correlation function is in excellent agreement with experiment." [[../papers/kresseInitiomolecularDynamicsLiquid1993]] | 对关联函数与实验极为吻合。 | `[物理量] is in [程度] agreement with experiment` |
| "The value agrees to better than 5% with the result obtained from linear-response methods." [[../papers/king-smithTheoryPolarizationCrystalline1993]] | 该值与线性响应方法所得结果吻合至 5% 以内。 | `The value agrees to better than [X%] with the result obtained from [对比方法]` |
| "Our overall value for [量] was [数值], compared with an experimental value of [数值]." [[../papers/king-smithTheoryPolarizationCrystalline1993]] | 我们对[量]的总值为[数值]，相比实验值为[数值]。 | `Our overall value for X was Y, compared with an experimental value of Z` |

**语法点**：`in [程度] agreement with` 是名词化的吻合表达，比 `agrees well with` 更正式；`to better than 5%` 用介词 `to` 引向精度，比 `within 5%` 更强调"达到了某个精度"；`compared with` 置于句末作状语，比 `while the experimental value was` 更紧凑。

### 第二层：结构特征与异常

在报告整体吻合之后，结果段转向"有趣的结构性特征"——那些与已知晶相不同、或与简单预期不符的发现。Kresse & Hafner 对液态 Ge 的分析是典范："The detailed analysis of the atomic structure shows that the atomic arrangement in liquid Ge is very different from that in normal liquid metals." [[../papers/kresseInitiomolecularDynamicsLiquid1993]]——`The detailed analysis shows that` 引出一个对比性结论。紧接着给定量："The coordination number, obtained by integrating the radial distribution function up to the first minimum at R = 3.35 Å, is N_c = 6.2, i.e., considerably lower than the value N_c = 10-12 characteristic for normal metals, but in good agreement with experiment."——主语（coordination number）+ 过去分词短语说明如何获得（`obtained by integrating...`）+ 谓语给出数值 + `i.e.` 解释意义 + `but` 转折回到实验吻合。一个句子同时完成了"方法—数值—对比—验证"四件事。态密度层面："It is interesting that the calculated DOS of liquid Ge is very different from that of any of the crystalline phases." [[../papers/kresseInitiomolecularDynamicsLiquid1993]] 以及 "The characteristic feature is the deep pseudogap about 5 eV below the Fermi level, which is independent of the k space sampling."

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| "The coordination number, obtained by integrating the radial distribution function up to the first minimum at R = 3.35 Å, is N_c = 6.2, i.e., considerably lower than the value N_c = 10-12 characteristic for normal metals, but in good agreement with experiment." [[../papers/kresseInitiomolecularDynamicsLiquid1993]] | 配位数——通过将径向分布函数积分至 R = 3.35 Å 处第一极小值获得——为 N_c = 6.2，即显著低于正常金属特征值 N_c = 10–12，但与实验吻合良好。 | `[量], obtained by [方法] up to [位置], is [数值], i.e., considerably lower than [对比值] characteristic for [常规体系], but in good agreement with experiment` |
| "The characteristic feature is the deep pseudogap about 5 eV below the Fermi level, which is independent of the k space sampling." [[../papers/kresseInitiomolecularDynamicsLiquid1993]] | 特征性标记是费米能级以下约 5 eV 处的深赝能隙，它与 k 空间采样无关。 | `The characteristic feature is [特征] at [位置], which is independent of [数值细节]` |
| "It is interesting that the calculated DOS of liquid Ge is very different from that of any of the crystalline phases." [[../papers/kresseInitiomolecularDynamicsLiquid1993]] | 有趣的是，所计算的液态 Ge 的态密度与任何晶相的态密度都非常不同。 | `It is interesting that the calculated [量] of [体系] is very different from that of [对比体系]` |

**语法点**：长句中插入过去分词短语（`obtained by integrating...`）是交代"数值如何得出"的标准方式，用逗号隔开使主句不被淹没；`i.e.` 比 `that is` 更紧凑，用于把数值翻译成物理判断；`which is independent of` 用非限制性定语从句补充数值的稳健性，等于在说"这个特征不是数值噪声"。

### 第三层：物理机制归因

结果段的最高层是解释"为什么"。奠基时代的机制句不使用 `driven by` 这类高度压缩的过去分词，而偏好完整的因果从句：`The breakdown of...is a consequence of...`、`It has been shown that the formation of...is due to...`、`This is most likely due to...`。Kresse & Hafner 解释液态 Ge 的配位数下降："The breakdown of the sp³ hybridization is a consequence of the profound change of the atomic short-range order." [[../papers/kresseInitiomolecularDynamicsLiquid1993]] 以及 "On melting the coordination number increases from N_c = 4 to N_c = 6.5, and the angular correlation characteristics for tetrahedral bonding are reduced significantly."——用 `On melting`（一融化就……）这一介词短语触发因果叙述。对赝能隙的解释更长："It has been shown that the formation of the pseudogap is due to an increasing s-p splitting arising from relativistic effects. In Ge it is enhanced by a partial penetration of the 4s electrons into the 3d core, leading to a stronger electron-ion interaction." [[../papers/kresseInitiomolecularDynamicsLiquid1993]]——第一句用 `It has been shown that...is due to...arising from...` 串起"现象—原因—原因的原因"三级链；第二句用 `In Ge it is enhanced by...leading to...` 在具体体系中强化机制。Monkhorst & Pack 则用更谨慎的归因："This is most likely due to the periodic behavior of f(k), which causes the Taylor expansion to be slowly convergent for k close to the BZ boundaries." [[../papers/monkhorstSpecialPointsBrillouinzone1976]]

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| "The breakdown of the sp³ hybridization is a consequence of the profound change of the atomic short-range order." [[../papers/kresseInitiomolecularDynamicsLiquid1993]] | sp³ 杂化的破缺是原子短程序深刻变化的结果。 | `The breakdown of [现象] is a consequence of [原因]` |
| "It has been shown that the formation of the pseudogap is due to an increasing s-p splitting arising from relativistic effects." [[../papers/kresseInitiomolecularDynamicsLiquid1993]] | 已表明赝能隙的形成是源于相对论效应的 s-p 分裂增大所致。 | `It has been shown that the formation of [现象] is due to [原因] arising from [深层原因]` |
| "This is most likely due to the periodic behavior of f(k), which causes the Taylor expansion to be slowly convergent for k close to the BZ boundaries." [[../papers/monkhorstSpecialPointsBrillouinzone1976]] | 这极可能是由于 f(k) 的周期性行为，它导致泰勒展开在 k 接近 BZ 边界时收敛缓慢。 | `This is most likely due to [原因], which causes [后果] for [条件]` |

**语法点**：`is a consequence of`、`is due to`、`is most likely due to` 是三个强度递进的因果归因——前者最确定（直接后果），中间客观陈述，后者加 `most likely` 表示推测。`arising from` 是现在分句作后置定语，把"原因的原因"挂接到名词后；`which causes...to be...for...` 用非限制性定语从句把因果链延伸到具体条件。

### 🧩 经典案例逐句拆解：液态 Ge 的结果与机制段

以下是 Kresse & Hafner (1993) 报告液态 Ge 结构的连续结果段，展示"异常发现—定量—机制"的完整链条：

> **来源**：截取自 [[../papers/kresseInitiomolecularDynamicsLiquid1993|Kresse & Hafner (1993)]], *Phys. Rev. B*

> **①** The detailed analysis of the atomic structure shows that the atomic arrangement in liquid Ge is very different from that in normal liquid metals. **②** The coordination number, obtained by integrating the radial distribution function up to the first minimum at R = 3.35 Å, is N_c = 6.2, i.e., considerably lower than the value N_c = 10-12 characteristic for normal metals, but in good agreement with experiment. **③** The pair correlation function is in excellent agreement with experiment. **④** On melting the coordination number increases from N_c = 4 to N_c = 6.5, and the angular correlation characteristics for tetrahedral bonding are reduced significantly. **⑤** The breakdown of the sp³ hybridization is a consequence of the profound change of the atomic short-range order. **⑥** It has been shown that the formation of the pseudogap is due to an increasing s-p splitting arising from relativistic effects. In Ge it is enhanced by a partial penetration of the 4s electrons into the 3d core, leading to a stronger electron-ion interaction.
>
> **中文翻译**：**①** 对原子结构的详细分析表明，液态 Ge 中的原子排列与正常液态金属非常不同。**②** 配位数——通过将径向分布函数积分至 R = 3.35 Å 处第一极小值获得——为 N_c = 6.2，即显著低于正常金属特征值 N_c = 10–12，但与实验吻合良好。**③** 对关联函数与实验极为吻合。**④** 一融化，配位数便从 N_c = 4 增至 N_c = 6.5，四面体键合的角关联特征显著减弱。**⑤** sp³ 杂化的破缺是原子短程序深刻变化的结果。**⑥** 已表明赝能隙的形成是源于相对论效应的 s-p 分裂增大所致；在 Ge 中，它因 4s 电子部分穿入 3d 芯而增强，导致更强的电子-离子相互作用。

| 句 | 功能 | 逐句拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **①** | **异常发现总起** | `The detailed analysis shows that [体系 A] is very different from [体系 B]`（名词化主语 + 宾语从句）。**`detailed analysis` 比 `calculation` 更强调"经过细致分析"，`very different from that in normal liquid metals` 用 `that` 指代 arrangement，避免重复。** |
| **②** | **定量 + 对比 + 验证** | `[量], obtained by [方法] up to [位置], is [数值], i.e., considerably lower than [常规值], but in good agreement with experiment`（主语 + 过去分词插入 + 谓语 + i.e. 解释 + but 转折）。**这是全段信息密度最高的一句：方法、数值、与常规对比、与实验对比四件事压缩在一句内，但靠 `i.e.` 和 `but` 两个路标让读者不迷路。** |
| **③** | **补充验证** | `The pair correlation function is in excellent agreement with experiment.`（最短句）。**在长句之后放一个短句，是节奏控制——让读者喘一口气，同时用 `excellent`（比上句 `good` 更高一档）强化可信度。** |
| **④** | **熔化前后对比** | `On melting [量] increases from A to B, and [另一量] are reduced significantly`（On + 动名词触发 + 两个并列谓语）。**`On melting` 是"一融化就……"的紧凑因果触发，比 `When it melts` 更书面；`increases...reduced` 一升一降形成对称。** |
| **⑤** | **机制第一层归因** | `The breakdown of [现象] is a consequence of [原因]`（名词化 + be + 名词短语）。**全用名词（breakdown / consequence / change），无动词因果——这是最正式的归因句式，把因果关系固化为"X 是 Y 的后果"这一命题。** |
| **⑥** | **机制深层归因** | `It has been shown that the formation of [现象] is due to [原因] arising from [深层原因]. In [体系] it is enhanced by [机制], leading to [后果]`（被动外置 + 现在分句 + 第二句被动 + 现在分词结果状语）。**第一句给出普遍机制，第二句用 `In Ge it is enhanced by` 在具体体系中强化，`leading to` 收束到最终物理后果——三级因果链用两个句子完成。** |

**核心教训**：这段结果叙述的节奏是"长—短—长—长—短—长"——句②的高密度长句之后用句③的短句缓解，句⑤的短判断句之后用句⑥的长因果句收束。机制归因不在第一时间抛出，而是在定量对比（句②③④）充分建立"确实有异常"之后才给出（句⑤⑥），这种"先让数据说话，再给解释"的次序比当代论文中"数据与机制同句"的写法更有耐心。

## 💬 5. Discussion

> **本节目标**：理解奠基时代讨论段的"差异归因—方法学比较—计算意义—遗留问题"写法。这一时代的讨论不追求"重新定义评价标准"的宏大姿态，而更倾向于诚实地定位差异来源、指出方法的适用边界。

### 第一步：差异归因

当计算与实验或其他计算存在差异时，讨论段首先定位差异的可能来源。King-Smith & Vanderbilt 写道："The agreement between our calculation and experiment is reasonable, given that the two terms in Eq. (18) show a strong tendency to cancel." [[../papers/king-smithTheoryPolarizationCrystalline1993]]——用 `given that`（考虑到）把"合理但不完美的吻合"归因于一个内在的数值困难（两项强抵消）。紧接着更具体地："We attribute the small differences between our results and those of Gironcoli, Baroni, and Resta to the use of different pseudopotentials and parametrizations of the exchange and correlation potential." [[../papers/king-smithTheoryPolarizationCrystalline1993]]——`We attribute A to B` 是主动归因句式，差异被归给方法选择（赝势、泛函参数化）而非物理模型本身。Monkhorst & Pack 对其方法误差的讨论则更坦白："A satisfactory analysis of the errors incurred in the above two cases is hard to give. This obviously depends critically on the function f(k)." [[../papers/monkhorstSpecialPointsBrillouinzone1976]]——`A satisfactory analysis...is hard to give` 直接承认无法给出令人满意的误差分析，`depends critically on` 指出误差对被积函数的敏感性。

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| "The agreement between our calculation and experiment is reasonable, given that the two terms in Eq. (18) show a strong tendency to cancel." [[../papers/king-smithTheoryPolarizationCrystalline1993]] | 考虑到式 (18) 中两项表现出强烈的抵消倾向，我们的计算与实验之间的吻合是合理的。 | `The agreement between A and B is reasonable, given that [内在困难]` |
| "We attribute the small differences between our results and those of [其他作者] to the use of different [方法选择]." [[../papers/king-smithTheoryPolarizationCrystalline1993]] | 我们把我们的结果与[其他作者]结果之间的微小差异归因于使用了不同的[方法选择]。 | `We attribute the small differences between A and B to the use of different C` |
| "A satisfactory analysis of the errors incurred in [情形] is hard to give. This obviously depends critically on [因素]." [[../papers/monkhorstSpecialPointsBrillouinzone1976]] | 对[情形]中产生的误差给出令人满意的分析是困难的。这显然关键地依赖于[因素]。 | `A satisfactory analysis of...is hard to give. This depends critically on X` |

**语法点**：`given that` 是介词化的连词，引导"考虑到某个不利条件"，比 `because` 更委婉；`attribute A to B` 是主动归因，主语必须是人（we）；`is hard to give` 用不定式补语主动形式表达被动意义（"难以被给出"），是学术英语的常见习惯。

---

### 第二步：方法学定位与比较

讨论段的第二层是把新方法放在已有方法的版图中定位。Monkhorst & Pack 写道："In contrast to the local interpolation and integration methods reviewed by Gilat, the above procedure is based on a global BZ fit, but nevertheless it belongs to the class of 'hybrid methods' which Gilat shows to be highly successful." [[../papers/monkhorstSpecialPointsBrillouinzone1976]]——`In contrast to A, B is based on C, but nevertheless it belongs to the class of D which A shows to be successful`：一句话同时完成"与谁不同""基于什么""仍属于哪一类"三件事，既差异化又承认传承。Perdew et al. 对 PBE 与 PW91 的比较则是竞争性定位："Except for its additional satisfaction of conditions (c) and (f) and its smoother potential, the new GGA is close to PW91." [[../papers/perdewGeneralizedGradientApproximation1996a]] 以及 "Calculations of atomization energies for small molecules also show that our simple new E_XC^{GGA} yields essentially the same results as the more Byzantine PW91."——`more Byzantine PW91` 是罕见的带贬义形容词（"拜占庭式的"即"过于复杂的"），用文化典故暗示 PW91 的繁复，是这一时代克制修辞中少有的锋芒。

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| "In contrast to the local interpolation and integration methods reviewed by [作者], the above procedure is based on a global BZ fit, but nevertheless it belongs to the class of 'hybrid methods' which [作者] shows to be highly successful." [[../papers/monkhorstSpecialPointsBrillouinzone1976]] | 与[作者]综述的局域插值和积分方法不同，上述程序基于全局 BZ 拟合，但尽管如此它仍属于[作者]所展示的非常成功的"混合方法"类别。 | `In contrast to [旧类], the new procedure is based on [新基础], but nevertheless it belongs to the class of [共同类] which [作者] shows to be successful` |
| "Except for its additional satisfaction of [条件] and its [优点], the new [方法] is close to [旧方法]." [[../papers/perdewGeneralizedGradientApproximation1996a]] | 除了额外满足[条件]及其[优点]外，新[方法]接近[旧方法]。 | `Except for its [改进点], the new X is close to Y` |
| "From a computational point of view, however, the advantages of the RMM-DIIS method become apparent." [[../papers/kresseEfficientIterativeSchemes1996d]] | 然而，从计算的角度看，RMM-DIIS 方法的优点变得明显。 | `From a computational point of view, however, the advantages of X become apparent` |

**语法点**：`In contrast to A, B...but nevertheless it belongs to C` 是"差异化但不断裂"的定位句式——`but nevertheless` 是双重转折，先承认差异再承认归属；`Except for X, Y is close to Z` 把"新增优点"作为例外前置，主句强调"本质相同"，是宣称"我们改进而非颠覆"的修辞；`From a computational point of view` 把评价限定在特定视角，暗示在其他视角下可能未必更优。

### 第三步：计算意义与实用价值

讨论段常把结果的意义落在"计算可行性"上。Kresse & Hafner 写道："The good agreement between the classical and the quantum-mechanical simulations is also important from a computational point of view: it allows one to restrict the time-consuming Hellmann-Feynman-type simulation to states close to the equilibrium and to approach equilibrium in the classical mode." [[../papers/kresseInitiomolecularDynamicsLiquid1993]]——冒号后用 `it allows one to restrict...and to approach...` 给出实际计算策略。Kresse & Furthmüller 报告算法复杂度："Overall the scaling of the CPU-time requirements of our iterative matrix diagonalization scheme is nearly quadratic with respect to the system size, i.e., O(N²), for systems containing up to 1000 electrons." [[../papers/kresseEfficientIterativeSchemes1996d]]

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| "The good agreement between the classical and the quantum-mechanical simulations is also important from a computational point of view: it allows one to restrict [昂贵操作] to [条件] and to approach [目标] in [廉价模式]." [[../papers/kresseInitiomolecularDynamicsLiquid1993]] | 经典模拟与量子力学模拟之间的良好吻合从计算角度看也很重要：它允许人们把耗时的 Hellmann-Feynman 型模拟限制在接近平衡的状态，并以经典模式趋近平衡。 | `The agreement between A and B is important from a computational point of view: it allows one to restrict [昂贵操作] to [条件] and to approach [目标] in [廉价模式]` |
| "Overall the scaling of the CPU-time requirements of our scheme is nearly quadratic with respect to the system size, i.e., O(N²), for systems containing up to 1000 electrons." [[../papers/kresseEfficientIterativeSchemes1996d]] | 总体而言，对于含多至 1000 个电子的体系，我们方案的 CPU 时间需求相对于体系大小近似为二次标度，即 O(N²)。 | `Overall the scaling of [资源] is nearly [阶] with respect to [规模], i.e., O(N^k), for systems containing up to N` |
| "For all cases treated up to now these parameters resulted in a very good convergence during the selfconsistent procedure, and optimizing the parameters never improved convergence speed by more than 10%." [[../papers/kresseEfficiencyAbinitioTotal1996a]] | 对迄今为止处理的所有情形，这些参数在自洽过程中产生了非常好的收敛，而优化参数从未使收敛速度改善超过 10%。 | `For all cases treated up to now these parameters resulted in [结果], and optimizing them never improved [指标] by more than X%` |

**语法点**：冒号在讨论段中频繁用于"先给结论，后给实际含义"；`it allows one to` 用非人称 `one` 而非 `us`，把方法的益处推广到任何使用者；`never improved...by more than 10%` 是用负面比较来论证"默认参数已足够好，无需调参"，是一种实用主义的结论。

### 第四步：遗留问题与未来工作

奠基时代的讨论不惮于在结尾列出未解决的问题。Kresse & Hafner："To further improve the energy conservation and to extend the length of the simulation remains a challenge." [[../papers/kresseInitiomolecularDynamicsLiquid1993]]——两个并列不定式作主语，`remains a challenge` 是最简洁的遗留问题陈述。Kresse & Furthmüller 更具体："Two problems remain, first the choice of the initial matrix G1, and second whether an optimized metric for evaluating the scalar products can be found." [[../papers/kresseEfficiencyAbinitioTotal1996a]]——`Two problems remain, first..., and second...` 用序号明确列出。King-Smith & Vanderbilt 则用更试探的语气："The conditions under which such an identification is useful will be the subject of a future communication." [[../papers/king-smithTheoryPolarizationCrystalline1993]]——`will be the subject of a future communication` 是"留待后续文章"的经典承诺句式。

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| "Two problems remain, first [问题 A], and second [问题 B]." [[../papers/kresseEfficiencyAbinitioTotal1996a]] | 仍有两个问题，第一是[问题 A]，第二是[问题 B]。 | `Two problems remain, first X, and second Y` |
| "The conditions under which such an identification is useful will be the subject of a future communication." [[../papers/king-smithTheoryPolarizationCrystalline1993]] | 这种识别在何种条件下有用，将是未来一篇通讯的主题。 | `The conditions under which [命题] is useful will be the subject of a future communication` |
| "A remaining drawback of the RMM-DIIS method is that it always finds the vector which is closest to the initial trial vector." [[../papers/kresseEfficientIterativeSchemes1996d]] | RMM-DIIS 方法的一个残留缺点是它总是找到最接近初始试探向量的向量。 | `A remaining drawback of X is that it always [行为]` |

**语法点**：`Two problems remain` 是不及物动词的存在式表达，比 `There are two problems` 更正式；`will be the subject of a future communication` 用将来时 + 名词化（subject）把"未做"包装成"计划中"；`A remaining drawback...is that...` 用 `remaining` 暗示"虽然整体成功，但仍有一点残留"，是最克制的自我批评。

### 🧩 经典案例逐句拆解：压电常数讨论段

以下是 King-Smith & Vanderbilt (1993) 对压电常数计算结果的讨论，展示"吻合报告—内在困难—差异归因"的完整链条：

> **来源**：截取自 [[../papers/king-smithTheoryPolarizationCrystalline1993|King-Smith & Vanderbilt (1993)]], *Phys. Rev. B*

> **①** Our calculations on the strained crystal yielded a (a/e)γ'₁₄ of -1.352. **②** The value agrees to better than 5% with the result obtained from linear-response methods. **③** Our overall value for the piezoelectric constant γ₁₄ was -0.28, compared with an experimental value of -0.32. **④** The agreement between our calculation and experiment is reasonable, given that the two terms in Eq. (18) show a strong tendency to cancel. **⑤** We attribute the small differences between our results and those of Gironcoli, Baroni, and Resta to the use of different pseudopotentials and parametrizations of the exchange and correlation potential.
>
> **中文翻译**：**①** 我们对受应变晶体的计算给出 (a/e)γ'₁₄ 为 -1.352。**②** 该值与线性响应方法所得结果吻合至 5% 以内。**③** 我们对压电常数 γ₁₄ 的总值为 -0.28，而实验值为 -0.32。**④** 考虑到式 (18) 中两项表现出强烈的抵消倾向，我们的计算与实验之间的吻合是合理的。**⑤** 我们把我们的结果与 Gironcoli、Baroni 和 Resta 结果之间的微小差异归因于使用了不同的赝势以及交换关联势的参数化。

| 句 | 功能 | 逐句拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **①** | **报告计算值** | `Our calculations on [体系] yielded [量] of [数值]`（主动 + 过去时）。**最短的结果开场，`yielded` 比 `gave` 更正式，暗示"经过计算产生"。** |
| **②** | **与其他理论比较** | `The value agrees to better than 5% with the result obtained from [方法]`（主语 + 谓语 + 精度 + 对比对象）。**`agrees to better than 5% with` 是精度比较的紧凑句式；`obtained from linear-response methods` 用过去分词后置，避免再加一个从句。** |
| **③** | **与实验比较** | `Our overall value for [量] was [数值], compared with an experimental value of [数值]`（主句 + compared with 状语）。**`overall` 暗示这是综合多个分量后的总值；用 `compared with` 而非 `while` 让比较成为附加信息而非并列命题。** |
| **④** | **解释为何只是 reasonable** | `The agreement...is reasonable, given that [内在困难]`（判断 + given that 让步解释）。**`given that` 是这句的核心——它预判了读者的疑问"为什么只是 reasonable 而不是 excellent"，并给出一个技术性理由（两项强抵消导致数值敏感）。** |
| **⑤** | **差异归因** | `We attribute the small differences between A and B to the use of different C and D`（主动归因 + 两个并列原因）。**`small differences` 先定性（差异很小，不影响结论），再用 `attribute...to` 归因于方法选择（赝势、参数化）而非物理模型错误——这是把差异"降级"为技术细节的标准策略。** |

**核心教训**：这段讨论只有五句，但完成了"自报数值—与理论比—与实验比—解释吻合为何只是 reasonable—解释与他人理论差异的来源"五步。关键技巧是 `given that`（句④）——它把"不够完美的吻合"从一个潜在弱点转化为一个"可以理解的数值困难"，而 `attribute...to the use of different...`（句⑤）则把"与他人的差异"从"谁对谁错"转化为"方法选择不同"。两个技巧合起来，让讨论段在不夸大结果的前提下守住了结论的可信度。

## 🏁 6. Conclusion

> **本节目标**：掌握奠基时代结论段的"成就总结—方法优势—遗留挑战"三段式。与当代结论的"范式转变 + 金句收尾"不同，这一时代的结论更像一份技术交付报告：做了什么、好在哪里、还差什么。

### 第一句：成就总结

结论开场几乎都用现在完成时 `We have shown / demonstrated that...`，把全文贡献压缩为一句。Kresse & Hafner 写道："We have shown that on the basis of improved conjugate-gradient techniques and the extrapolation of wave functions using subspace-alignment ab initio molecular dynamics for liquid metals are feasible without the introduction of a fictitious pseudo-Newtonian dynamics of the electrons." [[../papers/kresseInitiomolecularDynamicsLiquid1993]]——一个长句，主语 `We have shown` + that 从句，从句中用 `on the basis of A and B` 交代方法基础，用 `are feasible without C` 宣告可行性并排除旧方案的弊端。实验论文的总结更朴素："The prepared films of PbSnTe by MBE technique were checked for IR band-pass filter and photoconductive detector applications and satisfactory results were obtained." [[../papers/Srinivasan1989lead]]——两个被动谓语并列（`were checked` / `were obtained`），是实验论文结论的标准报告体。

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| "We have shown that on the basis of [技术 A and B] [方法] for [任务] are feasible without the introduction of [旧弊端]." [[../papers/kresseInitiomolecularDynamicsLiquid1993]] | 我们已表明，基于[技术 A 与 B]，用于[任务]的[方法]是可行的，无需引入[旧弊端]。 | `We have shown that on the basis of A and B, [方法] for [任务] are feasible without C` |
| "The GGA proposed here retains correct features of LSD, and combines them with the most energetically important features of gradient-corrected nonlocality." [[../papers/perdewGeneralizedGradientApproximation1996a]] | 这里提出的 GGA 保留了 LSD 的正确特性，并将其与梯度校正非局域性中在能量上最重要的特性相结合。 | `The [方法] proposed here retains [优点 A] and combines them with [优点 B]` |
| "The prepared [样品] by [制备技术] were checked for [应用 A and B] and satisfactory results were obtained." [[../papers/Srinivasan1989lead]] | 通过[制备技术]制备的[样品]被检验用于[应用 A 与 B]，并获得了满意结果。 | `The prepared [样品] by [技术] were checked for [应用] and satisfactory results were obtained` |

**语法点**：`We have shown that...are feasible without...` 用 `without` 引导的否定介词短语强调"避免了旧方法的某个特征"，是方法学论文结论的常见差异化修辞；`retains A and combines them with B` 用两个主动动词描述新方法的"继承+整合"双重特征；实验论文则偏好被动并列，让样品与结果而非作者充当主语。

---

### 第二句：方法优势或性能数据

总结之后，结论段常给出一两个关键性能数据或方法优势作为支撑。Kresse & Hafner 强调时间步长："The calculation of the exact Hellmann-Feynman forces at each time step and the use of an accurate predictor-corrector algorithm for the ionic equations of motion allow the use of time steps which are of the same order as those used in classical molecular-dynamics routines." [[../papers/kresseInitiomolecularDynamicsLiquid1993]]——`A and B allow the use of C which are of the same order as D`，用"与经典 MD 同量级的时间步长"作为方法可行性的定量证据。Perdew et al. 则强调简单性："However, its simpler form and derivation make it easier to understand, apply, and perhaps improve." [[../papers/perdewGeneralizedGradientApproximation1996a]]——`make it easier to [三个并列动词]`，把简单性转化为可理解性、可应用性、可改进性三重优势。Kresse & Furthmüller 的迭代方案结论则给出收敛优化的定量边界："For all cases treated up to now these parameters resulted in a very good convergence during the selfconsistent procedure, and optimizing the parameters never improved convergence speed by more than 10%." [[../papers/kresseEfficiencyAbinitioTotal1996a]]

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| "The calculation of [A] and the use of [B] allow the use of [C] which are of the same order as those used in [参照方法]." [[../papers/kresseInitiomolecularDynamicsLiquid1993]] | [A]的计算与[B]的使用允许使用与[参照方法]中所用同量级的[C]。 | `The calculation of A and the use of B allow the use of C which are of the same order as those in D` |
| "However, its simpler form and derivation make it easier to understand, apply, and perhaps improve." [[../papers/perdewGeneralizedGradientApproximation1996a]] | 然而，其更简单的形式与推导使其更易于理解、应用，并或许易于改进。 | `However, its [优点] make it easier to [动词 A, B, and perhaps C]` |
| "We have also checked that the metric and the initial approximation for the inverse of the Jacobian matrix improve the convergence independently." [[../papers/kresseEfficientIterativeSchemes1996d]] | 我们还检验了度量与 Jacobian 矩阵逆的初始近似各自独立地改善收敛。 | `We have also checked that A and B improve the convergence independently` |

**语法点**：`allow the use of C which are of the same order as those in D` 用 `those` 指代 time steps，避免重复；`of the same order as` 是"同量级"的正式表达；`easier to understand, apply, and perhaps improve` 三个动词并列，`perhaps` 修饰最后一个，暗示"可改进"是一种开放性而非承诺；`independently` 置于句末，强调两个因素的贡献可分离。

---

### 第三句：遗留挑战

奠基时代结论的最后一句常是一个坦诚的遗留问题，而非雄心勃勃的展望。Kresse & Hafner："To further improve the energy conservation and to extend the length of the simulation remains a challenge." [[../papers/kresseInitiomolecularDynamicsLiquid1993]]——两个并列不定式作主语，`remains a challenge` 单数谓语（把两件事视为一个统一挑战）。King-Smith & Vanderbilt 则用试探性的物理识别收尾："Before closing we note that it is tempting to physically identify the quantity P^(λ) defined in Eq. (8b) as the absolute polarization of the perturbed crystal." [[../papers/king-smithTheoryPolarizationCrystalline1993]]——`Before closing we note that` 是结论结尾的经典标记，`it is tempting to` 表示"诱人但需谨慎"，把一个可能的过度解读留作开放问题。Perdew et al. 的收尾相对自信但仍留有余地："However, its simpler form and derivation make it easier to understand, apply, and perhaps improve."——`perhaps improve` 既暗示自身方法可被未来改进，也邀请同行在此基础上工作。

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| "To further improve [A] and to extend [B] remains a challenge." [[../papers/kresseInitiomolecularDynamicsLiquid1993]] | 进一步改善[A]并延长[B]仍是一项挑战。 | `To further improve A and to extend B remains a challenge` |
| "Before closing we note that it is tempting to physically identify [量] as [解释]." [[../papers/king-smithTheoryPolarizationCrystalline1993]] | 在结束前我们注意到，把[量]在物理上识别为[解释]是诱人的。 | `Before closing we note that it is tempting to identify X as Y` |
| "The conditions under which such an identification is useful will be the subject of a future communication." [[../papers/king-smithTheoryPolarizationCrystalline1993]] | 这种识别在何种条件下有用，将是未来一篇通讯的主题。 | `The conditions under which X is useful will be the subject of a future communication` |

**语法点**：两个不定式并列作主语时，若被视为一个统一概念，谓语用单数（`remains` 而非 `remain`）；`Before closing we note that` 是结论结尾的元话语标记，提醒读者"这是最后一点"；`it is tempting to` 是一种"自我克制"的修辞——承认某种解读很自然，但用 `tempting`（诱人的）暗示它可能超出了证据所能支持的范围。

---

### 🧩 经典案例逐句拆解：PBE 泛函结论段

以下是 Perdew et al. (1996) 提出 PBE 泛函论文的结论，展示"成就—简单性优势—开放改进"的完整收束：

> **来源**：截取自 [[../papers/perdewGeneralizedGradientApproximation1996a|Perdew, Burke & Ernzerhof (1996)]], *Phys. Rev. Lett.*

> **①** The GGA proposed here retains correct features of LSD, and combines them with the most energetically important features of gradient-corrected nonlocality. **②** Except for its additional satisfaction of conditions (c) and (f) and its smoother potential, the new GGA is close to PW91. **③** Calculations of atomization energies for small molecules also show that our simple new E_XC^{GGA} yields essentially the same results as the more Byzantine PW91. **④** However, its simpler form and derivation make it easier to understand, apply, and perhaps improve.
>
> **中文翻译**：**①** 这里提出的 GGA 保留了 LSD 的正确特性，并将其与梯度校正非局域性中在能量上最重要的特性相结合。**②** 除了额外满足条件 (c) 和 (f) 及其更光滑的势外，新 GGA 接近 PW91。**③** 对小分子原子化能的计算也表明，我们简单的新 E_XC^{GGA} 给出与更繁复的 PW91 基本相同的结果。**④** 然而，其更简单的形式与推导使其更易于理解、应用，并或许易于改进。

| 句 | 功能 | 逐句拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **①** | **成就总结** | `The GGA proposed here retains [优点 A] and combines them with [优点 B]`（过去分词后置 + 两个并列主动谓语）。**`retains...combines...with` 用"继承+整合"两个动词定义新泛函的定位——它不是革命性替代，而是对 LSD 正确部分的保留与对梯度校正精华的吸收。** |
| **②** | **与直接竞争者比较** | `Except for its additional satisfaction of [条件] and its [优点], the new GGA is close to PW91`（Except for 例外短语 + 主句）。**`Except for` 把差异点作为例外前置，主句 `is close to PW91` 强调本质相同——这是"我们不夸大差异"的诚实姿态，也让 PBE 更容易被 PW91 用户接受。** |
| **③** | **性能对等验证** | `Calculations of [基准] also show that our simple new [方法] yields essentially the same results as the more Byzantine PW91`（主语 + show + 宾语从句 + as 比较）。**`essentially the same`（基本相同）是关键——PBE 不宣称比 PW91 更准，只宣称"同样准但更简单"；`more Byzantine` 是全文唯一的锋芒，用文化典故暗示 PW91 的繁复已成为负担。** |
| **④** | **简单性优势收尾** | `However, its simpler form and derivation make it easier to understand, apply, and perhaps improve`（转折 + 主语 + make + 形式宾语 + 三个并列不定式）。**结尾不展望应用、不宣告范式转变，只落在"更易懂、易用、也许易改进"三个朴素优点上。`perhaps improve` 把未来改进的可能性开放给整个社区——这是奠基时代方法论自信与克制的完美结合。** |

**核心教训**：PBE 的结论只有四句，却完成了"自我定位—与竞争者关系—性能对等—优势收束"四步。最值得注意的是它不做什么：不宣称"开辟新途径"、不预测"将被广泛采用"、不给应用画饼。它只是说"我们做了一个更简单的东西，它和复杂的那个一样准，因此更易用"。这种克制恰恰是 PBE 成为有史以来引用最高的泛函论文之一的修辞基础——作者把"简单"本身当作足够的卖点。

## 🧬 7. Evolution（综述专属）

> **本节目标**：本时期（1945–1999）综述性文献较少，且该时代的"演进叙事"主要内嵌于原创论文的引言之中，而非独立成篇的 Review。本节基于笔记中实际存在的材料，说明奠基性论文如何在引言内完成对领域起源的叙述。

### 奠基论文如何叙述领域起源

1945–1999 年的计算凝聚态物理领域尚处于"方法建立期"，综述（Review）作为一种文体在 Phys. Rev. B、PRL 等期刊中并不像 21 世纪那样普遍。领域的"演进叙事"主要由原创论文的引言段承担——作者在报告新方法之前，先回顾从 Kohn-Sham 方程到 Car-Parrinello 方法、从 LSD 到 GGA、从规范保持赝势到 PAW 的方法学谱系。Kresse & Hafner 在引言首句写道："A few years ago Car and Parrinello introduced an approach that unifies molecular-dynamics techniques for the calculation of the atomic structure with the local-density approximation (LDA) for electronic structure calculations." [[../papers/kresseInitiomolecularDynamicsLiquid1993]]——这是一个典型的"起源锚点"句：用 `A few years ago [Author] introduced` 把当前工作置于一条可追溯的谱系上。Perdew et al. 则在引言中用一句话完成了从 LSD 到 PW91 的演进定位："The most popular functionals have a form appropriate for slowly varying densities: the local spin density (LSD) approximation." 随后指出 "PW91 incorporates some inhomogeneity effects while retaining many of the best features of LSD, but has its own problems." [[../papers/perdewGeneralizedGradientApproximation1996a]]——`incorporates...while retaining...but has...` 三个谓语在一句之内完成了对前人方法"改进—继承—缺陷"的三段式评价，这正是后来综述论文中"某方法解决了什么、又留下什么"的标准叙事单元。

Dudarev et al. 的引言提供了一个更完整的内嵌式演进叙事：先承认 LSDA 的成功（"For many metals, semiconductors and insulators the local spin density approximation...is known to provide a reliable variational description" [[../papers/dudarevElectronenergylossSpectraStructural1998a]]），再指出失败案例（过渡金属氧化物），再引用 Terakura 等人的负面结果，最后定位 LSDA+U 作为 "bridging the DFT-LSDA with the unrestricted Hartree-Fock (UHF) approximation" 的桥梁方法。这种"共识—失败—前人补救—本文方法"的四段式，本质上就是一篇微型综述。King-Smith & Vanderbilt 则在 Berry phase 极化理论的论文中引用 Zak 等人的近期工作来定位自己的贡献："The integral on the right-hand side of Eq. (8b) is closely related to the Berry phase of band n, a quantity which has been recently introduced by Zak and co-workers." [[../papers/king-smithTheoryPolarizationCrystalline1993]]——用 `closely related to...recently introduced by` 把新理论锚定在一个正在形成的概念谱系中。

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| "A few years ago Car and Parrinello introduced an approach that unifies [方法 A] for [任务 A] with [方法 B] for [任务 B]." [[../papers/kresseInitiomolecularDynamicsLiquid1993]] | 几年前 Car 与 Parrinello 提出了一种统一[方法 A]与[方法 B]的途径。 | `A few years ago [作者] introduced an approach that unifies A for X with B for Y` |
| "PW91 incorporates [改进] while retaining [继承], but has its own problems." [[../papers/perdewGeneralizedGradientApproximation1996a]] | PW91 纳入[改进]同时保留[继承]，但有其自身问题。 | `[旧方法] incorporates A while retaining B, but has its own problems` |
| "The integral...is closely related to the Berry phase of band n, a quantity which has been recently introduced by Zak and co-workers." [[../papers/king-smithTheoryPolarizationCrystalline1993]] | 该积分与第 n 条能带的 Berry 相密切相关，后者是 Zak 等人近期引入的量。 | `[本工作中的对象] is closely related to [已有概念], a quantity recently introduced by [作者]` |

**语法点**：奠基时代的内嵌式演进叙事偏好三种句式——历史锚点（`A few years ago [Author] introduced`，过去时）、方法评价（`incorporates A while retaining B, but has C`，一般现在时的三重谓语）、概念定位（`is closely related to...recently introduced by`，被动 + 过去分词后置）。三种句式分别对应"从哪里来""前人方法的利弊""本工作在谱系中的位置"三个演进叙事维度。

### 从内嵌谱系到独立综述的过渡信号

虽然笔记中没有完整的综述论文，但若干句子已经显示出"综述式"自我意识的萌芽。Perdew et al. 在比较多个 GGA 时写道："Judging the derivations and formal properties of various GGA's can guide a rational choice among them." [[../papers/perdewGeneralizedGradientApproximation1996a]]——`Judging...can guide a rational choice among them` 是一种站在多个方法之上的综述者视角，而非单一方法的倡导者视角。Monkhorst & Pack 在结论中明确指出与其他方法类别的关系："The relationships to the Chadi-Cohen and Gilat-Raubenheimer methods are indicated." [[../papers/monkhorstSpecialPointsBrillouinzone1976]]——`The relationships to A and B are indicated` 虽然简短，却是"在方法版图中定位本文"的综述性行为。Blöchl 在 PAW 论文中也展现出对方法家族的整体观："Even though the individual partial waves and projectors will differ, they represent an almost equivalent choice." [[../papers/blochlProjectorAugmentedwaveMethod1994b]]——承认不同具体实现之间的等价性，是综述性判断而非单一实现的辩护。

这些句子预示了 2000 年之后综述文体的成熟：当一个领域积累了足够多的竞争性方法（多种 GGA、多种赝势、多种 BZ 积分方案），就需要有论文站在更高视角对它们进行分类、比较与选择建议。1945–1999 年的原创论文在引言与讨论中反复进行的"方法定位"工作，正是后来 Review 论文的种子。

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| "Judging the derivations and formal properties of various GGA's can guide a rational choice among them." [[../papers/perdewGeneralizedGradientApproximation1996a]] | 判断各种 GGA 的推导与形式性质可指导在它们之间做出理性选择。 | `Judging [标准] of various [方法] can guide a rational choice among them` |
| "The relationships to the Chadi-Cohen and Gilat-Raubenheimer methods are indicated." [[../papers/monkhorstSpecialPointsBrillouinzone1976]] | 指出了与 Chadi-Cohen 及 Gilat-Raubenheimer 方法的关系。 | `The relationships to [方法 A and B] are indicated` |
| "Even though the individual partial waves and projectors will differ, they represent an almost equivalent choice." [[../papers/blochlProjectorAugmentedwaveMethod1994b]] | 尽管各个部分波与投影子会有所不同，它们代表了几乎等价的选择。 | `Even though the individual [实现] will differ, they represent an almost equivalent choice` |

**语法点**：`Judging...can guide a rational choice` 用动名词作主语，采取"裁判者"视角；`The relationships...are indicated` 是极简的被动定位句，把方法间关系作为事实陈述；`Even though A will differ, they represent B` 用让步从句承认差异、主句强调等价，是综述中"多种实现本质相同"的标准判断句式。

---

**核心教训**：在综述文体尚未成熟的奠基时代，"领域演进"的叙事分散在每篇原创论文的引言与讨论中。学习这一时代的写作，不是寻找独立的 Review 范本，而是学会如何在一篇原创论文的引言内用三五句话完成"从哪里来、前人做了什么、还差什么、本文在哪里"的微型演进叙事。这种内嵌式谱系写作比独立综述更考验简洁性——作者必须在不展开文献综述的前提下，让读者迅速定位本文的方法学坐标。
