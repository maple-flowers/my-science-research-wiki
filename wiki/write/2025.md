### 1. Introduction & Scientific Background
*   **The Paradigm of Two-Dimensionalization**: Two-dimensionalization unlocks the incredible potential of materials to exhibit unique and superior physical properties, yet extending this to non-layered crystalline materials presents both challenges and excitement [[../papers/yanDecipheringStabilityTwodimensional2025]].
*   **Challenges in Non-vdW Oxides**: While 2D multiferroic oxides hold great potential for next-generation electronics, the mainstream $ABO_3$ compounds are typically non-van der Waals (non-vdW) materials, lacking natural cleavage planes [[../papers/zhongHighthroughputExfoliationMultiferroic2025]].
*   **Sliding Ferroelectricity as a Solution**: Traditional ferroelectrics face severe size limitations due to depolarization fields; however, **sliding ferroelectricity** (first conceptualized in [[../papers/wuSlidingFerroelectricity2D2021a]]) emerges as a robust mechanism where interlayer sliding produces out-of-plane polarization, overcoming scaling bottlenecks [[../papers/sunSlidingFerroelectricityTwodimensional2025]].
*   **The Quest for Room-Temperature Multiferroicity**: A central challenge remains finding a stable material platform that exhibits strong magnetoelectric coupling at room temperature, particularly in the form of **multiferroic heterostructures** [[../papers/tianRoomtemperatureTwodimensionalMultiferroic2026]].

### 2. Key Methodological Developments
*   **Rational Design Criteria**: Researchers have moved beyond intuition-based search to establish universal rules, such as identifying exfoliable crystallographic planes through **bond density ($\rho$)** and **binding strength ($\xi$)** criteria [[../papers/zhongHighthroughputExfoliationMultiferroic2025]].
*   **LEGO-like Assembly Rules**: Structural stability can be deciphered by treating materials as assemblies of "building blocks" (tetrahedrons and triangles), where the total energy is a linear superposition of individual block contributions [[../papers/yanDecipheringStabilityTwodimensional2025]].
*   **Intercalation-Induced Symmetry Breaking**: A novel design paradigm involves inserting non-metallic ions (e.g., $F^-$) into vdW gaps to break inversion symmetry and integrate ferroelectricity with magnetic order [[../papers/yuFerroelectricControlMagnetism2026]].
*   **Strain-Mediated Coupling Platforms**: The implementation of dual-gate heterostructures allows for the clear distinction between field-effect and inverse-piezoelectric strain-mediated magnetoelectric coupling [[../papers/caiFerroelectricitydrivenStrainmediatedMagnetoelectric2025]].

### 3. Core Results & Physical Mechanisms
*   **Giant Spin Splitting**: Systematic screening identified 35 stable $ABO_3$ monolayers harboring **giant spin splitting** (up to 0.606 eV), primarily arising from $p-d$ orbital interactions and broken inversion symmetry [[../papers/zhongHighthroughputExfoliationMultiferroic2025]].
*   **Electron-Filling Driven Multiferroicity**: In bilayer $CrTe_2$ multiferroic metals, the ferroelectricity originates from interlayer charge transfer driven by electron-filling differences, rather than simple structural sliding [[../papers/tianRoomtemperatureTwodimensionalMultiferroic2026]].
*   **Tunable Sliding Polarization**: Sliding ferroelectricity in materials like $RuX_2$ exhibits ultra-low switching barriers (~7.16 meV/f.u.) and is highly sensitive to layer-stacking engineering and compressive strain [[../papers/hanTunableSlidingFerroelectricity2025]].
*   **Non-volatile Control of Topology**: Reversing ferroelectric polarization in multiferroic heterostructures can induce a full reversal of spin polarization, spin texture, and the **gap Chern number** [[../papers/yuFerroelectricControlMagnetism2026]].

### 4. Research Logic & Evolution
*   **From Intuition to Rational Rules**: The field is evolving from "shooting in the dark" toward a systematic understanding of stability through electronic counting rules and building-block assembly [[../papers/yanDecipheringStabilityTwodimensional2025]].
*   **Integration of Functional Orders**: Modern research seeks to interlock multiple ferroic orders, achieving **phase-interlocked** responses where structural switching affords a reversible route between semiconducting and half-metallic states [[../papers/zhongHighthroughputExfoliationMultiferroic2025]].
*   **Advancing to Room-Temperature Applications**: Recent breakthroughs in $CrTe_2$ and $Fe_3GaTe_2$ heterostructures demonstrate the feasibility of room-temperature, air-stable multiferroic devices with ultra-low power consumption (0.5 aJ) [[../papers/tianRoomtemperatureTwodimensionalMultiferroic2026]] [[../papers/caiFerroelectricitydrivenStrainmediatedMagnetoelectric2025]].
