# 2022 Annual Synthesis: Two-Dimensional Multiferroicity and Phase Transitions

1. **Introduction & Scientific Background**
The year 2022 marked a paradigm shift in two-dimensional (2D) condensed matter physics, characterized by the successful bypass of the traditional "d0 rule" in multiferroic design and the discovery of unconventional phase transitions in van der Waals (vdW) systems. Historically, the coexistence of ferroelectricity and ferromagnetism in a single-phase material was considered scarce due to the mutual exclusivity of empty d-orbitals (required for polarization) and partially filled d-orbitals (required for magnetism). However, the exploration of p-orbital magnetism in oxynitrides [[../papers/aiFerroelectricityCoexistedPorbital2022]] and the identification of type-II multiferroicity in transition metal halides [[../papers/songEvidenceSinglelayerVan2022]] have opened new avenues for high-temperature, low-power spintronics and non-volatile memory.

2. **Key Methodological Developments**
The advancement of 2022 research relied heavily on multi-scale and multi-modal methodologies:
- **Theoretical Multi-scale Frameworks**: A robust pipeline integrating Density Functional Theory (DFT), Machine Learning Interatomic Potentials (DeepMD), and Finite Element Method (FEM) was established to design strain-induced topological polar structures in PbX systems [[../papers/xuTunableFerroelectricTopological2022]].
- **Advanced Optical Probing**: The use of Second Harmonic Generation (SHG) as a gold standard for inversion symmetry breaking, combined with Raman Optical Activity (ROA) for detecting electromagnons, proved decisive in confirming multiferroicity in the monolayer limit [[../papers/songEvidenceSinglelayerVan2022]].
- **Soft-Chemistry and Control**: Electrostatic gating and (de-)intercalation emerged as powerful tools for achieving extreme carrier concentrations ($>10^{14}\text{ cm}^{-2}$) and inducing structural phase transitions (e.g., 2H to 1T' in MoS₂) without destroying the covalent skeleton [[../papers/wuElectrostaticGatingIntercalation2022]].
- **Micro-nanofabrication**: Two-photon polymerization (2PP) demonstrated sub-100 nm precision in creating monolithic 3D optical sensors on non-standard substrates like optical fibers [[../papers/Unknown2022polymerization]].

3. **Core Results & Physical Mechanisms**
The research output centered on several breakthrough mechanisms:
- **P-orbital Multiferroicity**: In Tl₂NO₂, ferromagnetism is driven by itinerant N-2p electrons rather than d-electrons, allowing it to coexist with N-ion displacement-induced ferroelectricity, potentially realizing the "ferroelectric metal" state [[../papers/aiFerroelectricityCoexistedPorbital2022]].
- **Type-II and Helical Magnetism**: Monolayer NiI₂ was confirmed as the first 2D type-II multiferroic, where a proper-screw spin helix breaks inversion symmetry and induces electric polarization via the inverse Dzyaloshinskii-Moriya mechanism [[../papers/songEvidenceSinglelayerVan2022]].
- **Phase-Change Ferroelectricity**: Unlike BaTiO₃'s displacement type, α-In₂Se₃ utilizes a "re-bonding" mechanism where Se atoms undergo significant lateral shifts (~100 pm), enabling robust ferroelectricity at the atomic limit despite depolarization fields [[../papers/huangTwodimensionalIn2Se3Rising2022]].
- **Magnetic CDW Transitions**: Two distinct mechanisms for ferromagnetic Charge Density Wave (FM-CDW) states were identified in TMDs: the superexchange transition in CrX₂/VTe₂ and dimerization-induced magnetism in MnX₂ [[../papers/chenFerromagneticNonmagnetic1T2022]].
- **Interlayer Phase Switching**: The record-breaking thermal hysteresis ($>400\text{ K}$) in EuTe₄ was attributed to the delayed switching of the relative phase (0 or π) between CDW orders in single and double Te layers [[../papers/lvUnconventionalHystereticTransition2022]].

4. **Research Logic & Evolution**
The logic of 2022 research moved from static material prediction to dynamic control and complex heterostructures. This period laid the foundation for the subsequent explosion of **sliding ferroelectricity** (refined in 2021/2025), where the relative displacement of layers provides a new degree of freedom for polarization. The strong spin-orbit coupling (SOC) inherent in these polar systems (e.g., Tl₂NO₂) paved the way for the discovery of **giant spin splitting** in 2025. Furthermore, the successful demonstration of magnetoelectric coupling in Tl₂NO₂/WTe₂ heterostructures heralded the current 2026 trend of designing **multiferroic heterostructures** for active electric-field control of spin textures. The transition from bulk-like behaviors to purely 2D topological polar defects, such as vortices and skyrmions in PbTe [[../papers/xuTunableFerroelectricTopological2022]], continues to drive the field toward high-density topological electronics.
