# 2010–2014 Writing Synthesis: Thin-Film Multiferroics, Ferroelectric Tunnel Junctions & Characterization

[[_index|← 返回写作索引]]

> 本页覆盖 2010–2014 年：多铁纳米化、二维材料力学与 MXene 电子结构、CDW/二聚体序理论，以及力致发光、光纤湿度传感等应用方向并行发展的五年。语料中既有 X 射线/中子衍射锁定 BiFeO₃ 纳米颗粒磁电耦合的实验工作，也有以 DFT 系统刻画单层 TMD 与 MXene 的计算工作。所有英文例句逐字摘自双语转写。

## ✍️ 0. Title Patterns

> **本节目标**：拆解 2010–2014 年这一批论文标题的句式骨架。这一时期的标题比 2020 年代更"陈述性"，常用冒号双段式与"现象 in 体系"的介词短语，问句标题与"打破范式"式宣告较少。

### 句式一：现象 + in + 体系 — `[现象/性质] in [材料体系]`

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| *Multiferroic coupling in nanoscale BiFeO₃* | 纳米尺度 BiFeO₃ 中的多铁耦合 | `[性质] in [尺度] + [材料]` |
| *Intrinsic ultimate strength of monolayer TMDs* | 单层 TMD 的本征极限强度 | `[性质] of [维度] + [材料族]` |
| *Novel electronic and magnetic properties of MXenes* | MXene 的新颖电子与磁性 | `[形容词] + [性质] of [材料族]` |

**语法点**：三例均省略谓语动词，以名词短语 + 介词（`in`/`of`）直接命名工作对象。这是 2010 年代初实验/计算论文最常见的保守标题策略——不预设结论，让读者自行判断。注意 `in nanoscale BiFeO₃` 中 `nanoscale` 作定语前置，而 `of monolayer TMDs` 则用所有格 `of` 引出对象所属。两者的细微差别：`in` 强调"在某体系内部发生"（耦合是内部行为），`of` 强调"是某体系本身的属性"（强度是材料属性）。来源：[[../papers/Goswami2011multiferroic]]、[[../papers/Li2013bonding]]、[[../papers/khazaeiNovelElectronicMagnetic2013]]。

---

### 句式二：动名词/方法 + 对象 — `[方法-ing] [对象]`

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| *Calibrating an optical fiber humidity sensor for concrete monitoring* | 标定一种用于混凝土监测的光纤湿度传感器 | `[动名词] + [器件] + for + [应用]` |
| *Two-photon polymerization of 3-D microstructures for passive micromixers* | 用于被动微混合器的三维微结构双光子聚合 | `[技术] of [对象] for [应用]` |
| *Mechanochemical synthesis of a conducting black polymorph* | 一种导电黑色多形体的机械化学合成 | `[方法] of [产物]` |

**语法点**：动名词（calibrating / polymerization / synthesis）作标题首词，强调"我们做了什么动作"，是方法学/器件类论文的常用策略。三例的共同结构是 `[方法] + of + [产物] + for + [应用]`——三段式，读者一眼就能看出方法、对象、用途。注意 `for` 引导的应用场景是可选的，但加上后标题的工程导向立刻明确。来源：[[../papers/XiaokangZhang2013calibrating]]、[[../papers/Unknown2014passive]]、[[../papers/Wixtrom2011electrical]]。

---

### 句式三：陈述结论的长句标题 — `We [verb] that...`

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| *We show that strong multiferroic coupling persists in ∼22 nm BiFeO₃ particles* | 我们表明强多铁耦合在约 22 nm BiFeO₃ 颗粒中持续存在 | `We show that [结论] persists in [条件]` |
| *We find that the ultimate strength of 2D TMDs depends on chemical composition* | 我们发现二维 TMD 的极限强度依赖于化学组成 | `We find that [性质] depends on [变量]` |
| *We demonstrate a temperature-insensitive optical fiber RH sensor* | 我们展示了一种温度不敏感的光纤相对湿度传感器 | `We demonstrate [器件] with [特性]` |

**语法点**：以 `We show/find/demonstrate` 开头的完整句标题在 2010 年代比今天更常见于 Appl. Phys. Lett.、Opt. Express 等工程类期刊。三个动词的力度递增：`show`（表明）最弱、`find`（发现，含偶然性）、`demonstrate`（展示，含已验证意味）。`persists in`、`depends on` 是结论句的核心动词搭配。来源：[[../papers/Goswami2011multiferroic]]、[[../papers/Li2013bonding]]、[[../papers/Unknown2014optical]]。

### 🧩 经典案例逐句拆解：三个标题的策略对比

以下三例分别代表"保守—中性—进取"三种标题策略，逐词拆解：

> **来源**：截取自 [[../papers/Goswami2011multiferroic|Goswami et al. (2011)]], [[../papers/khazaeiNovelElectronicMagnetic2013|Khazaei et al. (2013)]], [[../papers/naguib25thAnniversaryArticle2013a|Naguib et al. (2013)]]

| 层级 | 标题 | 逐词拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **保守型** | *Multiferroic coupling in nanoscale BiFeO₃* | `Multiferroic coupling`（主题名词短语）→ `in`（介词）→ `nanoscale BiFeO₃`（尺度 + 材料）。**策略**：只命名对象，不预告结论，最安全也最无趣。 |
| **中性型** | *Novel electronic and magnetic properties of two-dimensional transition metal carbides (MXenes)* | `Novel`（形容词宣告新意，但语义模糊）→ `electronic and magnetic properties`（并列性质）→ `of two-dimensional transition metal carbides (MXenes)`（材料族 + 缩写）。**策略**：用 `Novel` 这一被滥用的形容词宣告新意，同时给出材料族全称与缩写，便于检索。 |
| **进取型** | *MXenes: a new family of two-dimensional materials* | `MXenes:`（材料族名 + 冒号）→ `a new family of`（宣告新家族）→ `two-dimensional materials`（上位类别）。**策略**：冒号双段式，直接宣告"新家族"，比 `Novel properties of...` 更有存在感，是 2013 年 [[../papers/naguib25thAnniversaryArticle2013a\|Naguib et al.]] 这篇 25 周年纪念文章能被广泛引用的原因之一。 |

**核心教训**：标题中的"宣告词"决定了论文的能见度。`Novel` 是最弱的宣告（几乎所有论文都在用），`new family of` 是更强的宣告（暗示一个可扩展的材料族），`first realization of` / `room-temperature` 则是最强的宣告。2010–2014 这一时期的标题整体偏保守，但 MXene 的命名本身就是一次成功的"标题级品牌建构"。

## 📄 1. Abstract

> **本节目标**：掌握 2010–2014 年摘要的五句公式——背景、方法、结果、机制、意义。与 2020 年代相比，这一时期的摘要更愿意用"我们发现/我们展示"的主动语态，数据嵌入也更直接（不追求一句话塞满三个数据点）。

### ① 背景句 — 从历史或应用切入

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **It has been a long history since** people began to study the phosphorescent materials. | 人们研究磷光材料已有很长的历史。 | `It has been a long history since...` |
| **Since its first mechanical exfoliation from graphite,** graphene has attracted tremendous scientific and technological attention. | 自从首次从石墨机械剥离以来，石墨烯引起了巨大的科技关注。 | `Since [事件], [材料] has attracted...attention` |
| **Origin of charge density waves (CDW) in the transition-metal dichalcogenides is subject of debates** since their discovery in the early 70's. | 过渡金属硫族化合物中 CDW 的起源自 1970 年代初发现以来一直是争论主题。 | `[现象] is subject of debates since [时间]` |

**语法点**：三种背景切入——`It has been a long history since`（历史视角，最朴素）、`Since [事件], [材料] has attracted...`（发现事件驱动）、`[现象] is subject of debates since`（争议驱动，最有张力）。注意 `is subject of debates` 比 `has been debated` 更正式，但语法上略显古旧，当代英语更常用 `has been the subject of debate`。来源：[[../papers/KumarChoubey2011mechanoluminescence]]、[[../papers/Li2013bonding]]、[[../papers/gorkovStrongElectronlatticeCoupling2012]]。

---

### ② 方法句 — 我们用什么做了什么

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Using the results of x-ray and neutron diffraction experiments, we show that** the ferroelectric polarization exhibits a jump by ∼30% around T_N. | 利用 X 射线与中子衍射实验结果，我们表明极化在 T_N 附近跃变约 30%。 | `Using [方法], we show that [结果]` |
| **Using first-principles calculations,** the formation and electronic properties of various MXene systems **are examined.** | 利用第一性原理计算，多种 MXene 体系的形成与电子性质被检验。 | `Using [方法], [对象] are examined` |
| **We derive and discuss an experimentally realistic model describing** ultracold atoms in an optical lattice including a commensurate, but staggered, Zeeman field. | 我们推导并讨论了一个描述光晶格中超冷原子的实验可实现模型，其中含相称但交错的 Zeeman 场。 | `We derive and discuss [模型] describing [体系]` |

**语法点**：方法句的两种语态——主动（`we show/discuss`）与被动（`are examined`）。[[../papers/Goswami2011multiferroic]] 与 [[../papers/Makogon2012wave]] 用主动语态，作者存在感强；[[../papers/khazaeiNovelElectronicMagnetic2013]] 用被动语态，更"客观"但也更疏离。`Using + [方法]` 置于句首是这一时期最常见的方法状语结构。注意 `derive and discuss` 是并列动词，比单独 `discuss` 多一层"我们先推导再讨论"的方法学递进。

---

### ③ 结果句 — 数据直接嵌入

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| The polarization exhibits **a jump by ∼30%** around T_N and **a suppression by ∼7%** under 5T magnetic field at room temperature. | 极化在 T_N 附近跃变约 30%，室温下 5 T 磁场中被抑制约 7%。 | `X exhibits a jump by ∼N% and a suppression by ∼M%` |
| A high sensitivity of **0.08 dB/%RH is achieved.** It shows response times of **2 and 6 s** in the RH increasing and decreasing processes. | 实现了 0.08 dB/%RH 的高灵敏度，在湿度上升与下降过程中响应时间分别为 2 秒和 6 秒。 | `A sensitivity of N is achieved. Response times of A and B...` |
| **Even just one screw-shaped propeller results in a mixing efficiency of over ∼80%.** | 即使仅一个螺旋桨也能产生超过约 80% 的混合效率。 | `Even just one [元件] results in [效率] of over N%` |

**语法点**：三个结果句的共同特点是数据直接嵌入主句，不用破折号夹入，也不用 `corresponding to` 等修饰。这是 2010 年代初摘要的朴素风格。`a jump by ∼30%` 中的 `by` 表示变化幅度，`a suppression by ∼7%` 同样用 `by`。`is achieved`（被实现）是器件类论文摘要的高频被动式。`Even just one...results in` 用 `Even` 强调"仅一个就够了"，是结果句的修辞强化。来源：[[../papers/Goswami2011multiferroic]]、[[../papers/Unknown2014optical]]、[[../papers/Unknown2014passive]]。

### ④ 机制句 — 归因与解释

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| We demonstrate that differences in the observed mechanical behavior **can be attributed to** the spatial redistribution of the occupied hybridized electronic states. | 我们表明，所观测力学行为的差异可归因于占据杂化电子态的空间重新分布。 | `X can be attributed to Y` |
| The origin of the observed mechanical behavior **can be attributed to** a strong hybridization between the outermost p orbitals of the chalcogens and the d orbitals of the transition metal. | 所观测力学行为的起源可归因于硫族元素最外层 p 轨道与过渡金属 d 轨道之间的强杂化。 | `The origin of X can be attributed to Y` |
| The piezoelectrically-induced detrapping model **is found to be suitable for** the EML of nanoparticles. | 压电诱导去陷阱模型被发现适用于纳米粒子的弹性力致发光。 | `[模型] is found to be suitable for [现象]` |

**语法点**：三种归因句式——`can be attributed to`（最常见，出现于 [[../papers/Li2013bonding]] 两次）、`The origin of...can be attributed to`（在前者基础上加主语 `origin`，更明确）、`is found to be suitable for`（模型适配性判断）。`attributed to` 后接名词短语（不接从句），因此要把动词名词化：`redistribution`、`hybridization`。这是学术英语的基本名词化训练。

---

### ⑤ 意义句 — 应用展望

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| These results confirm the presence of strong multiferroic coupling even in nanoscale BiFeO₃ and thus **could prove to be quite useful for** applications based on nanosized devices. | 这些结果证实即使在纳米尺度 BiFeO₃ 中仍存在强多铁耦合，因而可能对基于纳米器件的应用相当有用。 | `could prove to be useful for applications based on...` |
| It is expected that this sensor **will be used in a wide range of applications**, including meteorological services and the chemical and food processing industries. | 预计该传感器将被用于广泛的应用，包括气象服务以及化学与食品加工工业。 | `It is expected that X will be used in a wide range of applications, including...` |
| Conclusively, it may be said that **the door of EML research on nanoparticles is wide open and it looks very promising.** | 总之可以说，纳米粒子弹性力致发光研究的大门敞开，前景十分广阔。 | `the door of X is wide open and it looks very promising` |

**语法点**：三种意义句力度——`could prove to be useful for`（最谦逊，could 表推测）、`It is expected that...will be used`（被动预测，工程类标配）、`the door is wide open and looks very promising`（最具修辞色彩，几乎像口语）。注意第三例来自 [[../papers/PChandra2011mechanoluminescence]]，其风格比 Phys. Rev. 系列随意，这与期刊定位（发光材料类）有关。`a wide range of applications, including A, B, and C` 是器件论文展望的万能模板。

### 🧩 经典案例逐句拆解：一篇完整摘要

以下是 [[../papers/Goswami2011multiferroic]] 摘要的逐句拆解，展示"背景—方法—结果—机制—意义"五句公式：

> **来源**：截取自 [[../papers/Goswami2011multiferroic|Goswami et al. (2011)]], *J. Appl. Phys.* / preprint

> **①** The hope in the multiferroicity of BiFeO₃ has been rekindled by the observation of spin-flop under electric field in a single crystal. **②** However, the question of multiferroic coupling in nanoscale BiFeO₃ has not been addressed adequately. **③** Using the results of x-ray and neutron diffraction experiments, we show that the ferroelectric polarization, in ∼22 nm particles of BiFeO₃, exhibits a jump by ∼30% around the magnetic transition point T_N (∼635 K) and a suppression by ∼7% under 5T magnetic field at room temperature. **④** The sharp change (∼30%) in the polarization around T_N as well as its suppression (∼7%) under 5T magnetic field at a temperature well below T_N point out that the multiferroic coupling is quite strong even in nanoscale BiFeO₃. **⑤** These results confirm the presence of strong multiferroic coupling even in nanoscale BiFeO₃ and thus could prove to be quite useful for applications based on nanosized devices of BiFeO₃.
>
> **中文翻译**：**①** 对 BiFeO₃ 多铁性的希望因单晶中电场下自旋翻转的观测而被重新点燃。**②** 然而，纳米尺度 BiFeO₃ 中多铁耦合的问题尚未被充分解决。**③** 利用 X 射线与中子衍射实验结果，我们表明，在约 22 nm 的 BiFeO₃ 颗粒中，铁电极化在磁转变点 T_N（约 635 K）附近跃变约 30%，并在室温下 5 T 磁场中被抑制约 7%。**④** 极化在 T_N 附近的尖锐变化（约 30%）以及在远低于 T_N 温度下 5 T 磁场中的抑制（约 7%）指出，即使在纳米尺度 BiFeO₃ 中多铁耦合也相当强。**⑤** 这些结果证实了即使在纳米尺度 BiFeO₃ 中仍存在强多铁耦合，因而可能对基于 BiFeO₃ 纳米器件的应用相当有用。

| 句 | 功能 | 逐句拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **①** | **背景** | `The hope in X has been rekindled by Y`（被动 + `rekindled` 比喻，"重新点燃希望"）。**`rekindled` 比 `renewed` 更有情感色彩，暗示该领域曾经历低谷。** |
| **②** | **缺口** | `However, the question of X has not been addressed adequately`（`However` 转折 + 现在完成时被动 + `adequately` 副词留余地）。**`not been addressed adequately` 比 `not been studied` 更优雅——承认有研究但不够深入。** |
| **③** | **方法 + 结果** | `Using [方法], we show that [主语], in [尺度], exhibits [数据 1] and [数据 2]`（方法状语前置 + 两个并列数据用 `and` 连接）。**两个数据点（30% 跳跃、7% 抑制）并列在同一句，是这一时期摘要的典型密度。** |
| **④** | **机制解释** | `X as well as Y point out that Z is quite strong even in W`（`as well as` 并列两个证据 + `point out` 给出推论）。**注意此句重复了句③ 的两个数据，属于"数据复述 + 解释"，在当代摘要中已不常见（更紧凑的做法是把解释嵌入句③）。** |
| **⑤** | **意义** | `These results confirm...and thus could prove to be quite useful for applications based on...`（`confirm` 收束 + `thus` 因果 + `could` 表谦虚）。**`quite useful` 是英式低调陈述，若投 Nat. Mater. 会改为 `pave the way toward`。** |

**核心教训**：这篇摘要是 2010 年代初 *J. Appl. Phys.* 风格的典型——五句话各守其位，但句④ 与句③ 数据重复，显得不够紧凑。当代写作会把句③ 与句④ 合并为一句（用 `demonstrating that` 连接），把句⑤ 留给更广阔的意义。

## 🚪 2. Introduction & Scientific Background

> **本节目标**：掌握 2010–2014 年引言的漏斗式逻辑。与 2020 年代相比，这一时期的引言更常以"长历史"或"领域挑战"开篇，再用 `However` 转折指出具体缺口，最后以 `In this paper, we show/present/propose` 引出本文工作。本节合成约 400 词。

2010–2014 年文献的引言从三个宏观角度切入。第一个角度是**长历史框架**，例如 [[../papers/KumarChoubey2011mechanoluminescence]] 写道 "it has been a long history since people began to study the phosphorescent materials,"，但随即笔锋一转，指出 "conventional phosphorescent materials such as ZnS:Cu can hardly have bright light and long after glow time for the applications."。同样的历史纵览也出现在 [[../papers/PChandra2011mechanoluminescence]] 中，该文指出 "although the phenomenon of ML is known for a long time, in the past no practical application could be made because of the low ML intensity of the materials and lack of reproducibility."。第二个角度是**发现事件框架**，以 [[../papers/Li2013bonding]] 为代表："since its first mechanical exfoliation from graphite, graphene has attracted tremendous scientific and technological attention due to novel electronic, mechanical, and chemical properties,"；[[../papers/khazaeiNovelElectronicMagnetic2013]] 同样把叙事锚定在 "the discovery of graphene by Novoselov et al."。第三个角度是**长期争议框架**，例如 [[../papers/gorkovStrongElectronlatticeCoupling2012]] 开篇即写 "origin of charge density waves (CDW) in the transition-metals dichalcogenides (TMDC) is subject of debates since their discovery in the early 70's."。

从这些宏观开篇出发，引言通过一个可识别的两步收窄。第一步是**意义声明**：[[../papers/Unknown2014optical]] 断言 "optical-fiber-based relative humidity (RH) sensors have attracted increasing interest in recent years because of their distinct advantages such as immunity to electromagnetic noise, small size, anticorrosion property, high sensitivity, and remote sensing,"；而 [[../papers/Unknown2014passive]] 指出 "the lab-on-a-chip or microfluidic chip has been attracting attention because of its convenience, rapid operation, low-consumable reagents, and accuracy."。第二步是**知识缺口**，通常以 `However` 或 `While` 为标志：[[../papers/Kang2012dimer]] 承认 "while the impurity-induced nanoscale electronic disorder has been extensively reported in the underdoped iron pnictides, its microscopic origins remain elusive,"；[[../papers/Goswami2011multiferroic]] 则直言 "the question of multiferroic coupling in nanoscale BiFeO₃ has not been addressed adequately."。[[../papers/Li2013bonding]] 把缺口锐化为一个明确的问题："What is the intrinsic ultimate strength — the maximum stress a defect-free material can withstand without failure — of monolayer TMDs along different crystal directions and how is it dependent on the chemical composition?"。[[../papers/XiaokangZhang2013calibrating]] 使用谨慎的 "to the best of our knowledge" 句式，指出 "the behavior of humidity sensors at different temperatures has not been examined, and reports on the application of optical fiber humidity sensors are scarce."。

引言以**本文工作声明**收尾，几乎都以 `In this paper/letter/article, we...` 引出。[[../papers/Goswami2011multiferroic]] 承诺 "in this paper, we show from x-ray and neutron diffraction experiments that not only sizable ferroelectric polarization is retained in nanosized particles of BiFeO₃, the coupling between magnetic and electric order parameters too, is quite strong in them."。[[../papers/Kang2012dimer]] 采用快讯惯例："in this Rapid Communication, we show that both the appearance of dimer resonances and their size and orientation provide a direct insight into the electronic structure and correlations in iron pnictides."。[[../papers/Li2013bonding]] 同时宣布方法与范围："in this article, using first-principles density functional theory simulations, we report on the fundamental mechanical behavior of monolayer TMDs when subjected to a large deformation."。器件类论文偏好比较优势的写法，例如 [[../papers/Unknown2014optical]] 写道 "in comparison with the other sensors, the sensor presented here has advantages of high sensitivity, large measurement range, and rapid response."。理论论文有时用带有论辩色彩的 `We argue that` 替代 `In this paper`，如 [[../papers/gorkovStrongElectronlatticeCoupling2012]]："we argue that instead of development of the Migdal instability in the soft mode scenario for the propagating phonon mode, the CDW instability may realize itself in the two stages."。

把这些引言统一起来的是一种**克制的修辞**：它们宁可使用 `has not been addressed adequately` 而非 `remains a fundamental open challenge`，宁可使用 `could prove to be quite useful` 而非 `paves the way toward`，宁可使用 `we show` 而非 `we demonstrate`。2020 年代写作中常见的夸张副词（`fundamentally`、`unprecedented`、`paradigm-shifting`）在这一时期基本缺席。2010–2014 年的引言通过其所识别缺口的分量来赢得意义，而不是通过其所调动的形容词。

### 第一句：领域大背景

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| It has been a long history since people began to study the phosphorescent materials. However, conventional phosphorescent materials such as ZnS:Cu **can hardly have** bright light and long after glow time for the applications. | 人们研究磷光材料已有很长历史。然而，ZnS:Cu 等传统磷光材料在应用中难以兼具亮光与长余辉。 | `It has been a long history since... However, X can hardly...` |
| Since its first mechanical exfoliation from graphite, graphene **has attracted** tremendous scientific and technological attention due to novel electronic, mechanical, and chemical properties. | 自从首次从石墨机械剥离以来，石墨烯因其新颖的电子、力学与化学性质引起了巨大科技关注。 | `Since [事件], X has attracted...attention due to [性质]` |
| Origin of charge density waves (CDW) in the transition-metals dichalcogenides (TMDC) **is subject of debates** since their discovery in the early 70's. | TMDC 中 CDW 的起源自 1970 年代初发现以来一直是争论主题。 | `[现象] is subject of debates since [时间]` |

**语法点**：三种背景开篇策略——长历史 + 局限、发现事件 + 关注度、长期争议。注意第一例 `can hardly have` 中的 `hardly` 是否定副词，意为"几乎不"；`long after glow time` 中 `after glow` 应为合成词 `afterglow`（余辉），原文有连写瑕疵，引用时需注意。

### 第二句：缺口与问题

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **However,** the question of multiferroic coupling in nanoscale BiFeO₃ **has not been addressed adequately.** | 然而，纳米尺度 BiFeO₃ 中多铁耦合的问题尚未被充分解决。 | `However, the question of X has not been addressed adequately` |
| **While** the impurity-induced nanoscale electronic disorder has been extensively reported in the underdoped iron pnictides, **its microscopic origins remain elusive.** | 虽然杂质诱导的纳米尺度电子无序在欠掺杂铁磷化物中已被广泛报道，其微观起源仍然不清。 | `While X has been extensively reported, Y remains elusive` |
| **To the best of our knowledge,** the behavior of humidity sensors at different temperatures **has not been examined.** | 据我们所知，湿度传感器在不同温度下的行为尚未被检验。 | `To the best of our knowledge, X has not been examined` |

**语法点**：三个缺口标记词——`However`（转折连词）、`While`（让步连词，需接从句）、`To the best of our knowledge`（介词短语，最谦逊）。`remains elusive` 是缺口句的黄金搭配，比 `is not clear` 更学术。`adequately`、`extensively` 是程度副词，让缺口陈述不那么绝对。

### 第三句：本文工作

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **In this paper, we show from x-ray and neutron diffraction experiments that** not only sizable ferroelectric polarization is retained in nanosized particles of BiFeO₃, the coupling between magnetic and electric order parameters too, is quite strong in them. | 本文中，我们从 X 射线与中子衍射实验表明，不仅可观的铁电极化在纳米 BiFeO₃ 颗粒中保留，磁序参量与电序参量之间的耦合也相当强。 | `In this paper, we show from [方法] that not only A, but B too` |
| **In this Rapid Communication, we show that** both the appearance of dimer resonances and their size and orientation provide a direct insight into the electronic structure and correlations in iron pnictides. | 在这篇快讯中，我们表明二聚体共振的出现及其尺寸与取向，为铁磷化物的电子结构与关联提供了直接洞见。 | `In this [期刊体裁], we show that both A and B provide insight into C` |
| **We argue that** instead of development of the Migdal instability in the soft mode scenario for the propagating phonon mode, the CDW instability may realize itself in the two stages. | 我们论证，CDW 不稳定性并非在传播声子模的软模情景中发展为 Migdal 不稳定性，而可能以两阶段方式实现。 | `We argue that instead of A, B may realize itself in C` |

**语法点**：三种本文工作引出——`In this paper, we show that`（最通用）、`In this Rapid Communication, we show that`（点明期刊体裁）、`We argue that`（理论论文，语气更具争辩性）。`not only...(but) ... too` 是递进结构，注意原句 `the coupling... too, is quite strong` 中 `too` 的插入略显口语化，规范写法应为 `but also the coupling... is quite strong`。

### 🧩 经典案例逐句拆解：漏斗式引言

以下是 [[../papers/Goswami2011multiferroic]] 引言前三句构成的微型漏斗：

> **来源**：截取自 [[../papers/Goswami2011multiferroic|Goswami et al. (2011)]]

> **①** The hope in the multiferroicity of BiFeO₃ has been rekindled by the observation of spin-flop under electric field in a single crystal. **②** However, the question of multiferroic coupling in nanoscale BiFeO₃ has not been addressed adequately. **③** In this paper, we show from x-ray and neutron diffraction experiments that not only sizable ferroelectric polarization is retained in nanosized particles of BiFeO₃, the coupling between magnetic and electric order parameters too, is quite strong in them.
>
> **中文翻译**：**①** 对 BiFeO₃ 多铁性的希望因单晶中电场下自旋翻转的观测而被重新点燃。**②** 然而，纳米尺度 BiFeO₃ 中多铁耦合的问题尚未被充分解决。**③** 本文中，我们从 X 射线与中子衍射实验表明，不仅可观的铁电极化在纳米 BiFeO₃ 颗粒中保留，磁序参与电序参量之间的耦合也相当强。

| 句 | 漏斗层级 | 逐句拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **①** | **大背景** | `The hope in X has been rekindled by Y`（被动 + 比喻 `rekindled`）。**一句交代领域为何重新受关注，用 `rekindled` 暗示此前曾有低潮。** |
| **②** | **缺口** | `However, the question of X has not been addressed adequately`（`However` 转折 + 现在完成时被动）。**`adequately` 是低调陈述，比 `at all` 留余地，比 `well` 更诚实。** |
| **③** | **本文工作** | `In this paper, we show from [方法] that [结论 1] and [结论 2]`（方法来源 `from x-ray and neutron diffraction` 嵌入主句，两个结论用 `not only...too` 连接）。**方法来源嵌入主句而非独立成句，是引言末句的紧凑写法。** |

**核心教训**：三句话完成"背景—缺口—本文"的完整漏斗，是短通讯（Rapid Communication / Appl. Phys. Lett.）引言的标准密度。较长论文会在句①与句②之间插入 2–4 段文献综述，但漏斗结构不变。

### 第四句：发现宣告与仪器驱动开篇

并非所有引言都从"长历史"切入。2010–2014 语料中还有两种值得注意的开篇策略。

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| A practical material that is mechanoluminescent (ML) in the elastic deformation region **has been developed in our laboratory for the first time.** | 一种在弹性形变区域力致发光的实用材料已在我们实验室首次开发出来。 | `[对象] has been developed for the first time` |
| We consider single band of conduction electrons interacting with displacements of the transitional ions. In the classical regime strong enough coupling transforms the harmonic elastic energy for an ion to the one of the well with two deep minima. | 我们考虑与过渡离子位移相互作用的单带导带电子。在经典区域，足够强的耦合把离子的谐振弹性能转变为双深阱能量。 | `We consider [模型]. In [极限], [机制] transforms X to Y.` |
| The ML was monitored by a homemade setup having RCA 931 photomultiplier tube positioned below the lucite plate and connected to a storage oscilloscope. | ML 由自制装置监测，该装置在有机玻璃板下方放置 RCA 931 光电倍增管并连接到存储示波器。 | `[现象] was monitored by a homemade setup having [部件]` |

**语法点**：三种非标准开篇——`for the first time`（宣告首创，需谨慎，必须有充分证据）、`We consider`（理论论文直接给出模型，跳过文献综述）、`was monitored by a homemade setup`（仪器驱动，常用于测量类论文）。`for the first time` 在 2010 年代仍可接受，但今天许多期刊要求作者避免使用这一短语，因为它难以验证。理论论文的 `We consider` 开篇是一种"自信的省略"——暗示模型本身足够有趣，无需先铺陈文献。

### 第五句：应用驱动开篇（器件类论文）

器件类论文常以"某行业重要 + 现有技术不足"开篇。

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Humidity **has a significant effect on** many industries, such as textile manufacture, medical production, and national defense. | 湿度对纺织制造、医药生产与国防等许多行业有重要影响。 | `X has a significant effect on [行业列表]` |
| In recent years, the lab-on-a-chip or microfluidic chip **has been attracting attention because of** its convenience, rapid operation, low-consumable reagents, and accuracy. | 近年来，芯片实验室或微流控芯片因其便捷、快速、试剂低耗与准确而备受关注。 | `X has been attracting attention because of [四个并列优势]` |
| Flow mixing is an important process for many microfluidic applications. Efficient mixing **is difficult in** microscale channels **owing to** laminar flow. | 流动混合是许多微流控应用的重要过程。由于层流，微尺度通道中的高效混合十分困难。 | `X is important for [应用]. Efficient X is difficult in [条件] owing to [物理原因].` |

**语法点**：应用驱动开篇的两步——先讲重要性（`has a significant effect on` / `has been attracting attention because of`），再讲困难（`is difficult in...owing to`）。第三步才是本文方案。四个并列优势 `convenience, rapid operation, low-consumable reagents, and accuracy` 必须保持名词短语平行结构，最后一项前加 `and`。`owing to laminar flow` 给出物理原因而非泛泛而谈，是应用论文专业性的体现。来源：[[../papers/XiaokangZhang2013calibrating]]、[[../papers/Unknown2014passive]]。

### 🧩 经典案例逐句拆解：漏斗式引言（二）

以下是 [[../papers/Li2013bonding]] 引言的开篇三句，展示"发现事件→领域扩展→具体缺口"的另一种漏斗：

> **来源**：截取自 [[../papers/Li2013bonding|Li (2013)]], *Phys. Rev. B*

> **①** Since its first mechanical exfoliation from graphite, graphene has attracted tremendous scientific and technological attention due to novel electronic, mechanical, and chemical properties. **②** However, such studies provide only effective orientation-averaged values of the mechanical properties and are limited only to single composition, while the rest of the 2D semiconducting TMDs remain yet to be characterized. **③** What is the intrinsic ultimate strength — the maximum stress a defect-free material can withstand without failure — of monolayer TMDs along different crystal directions and how is it dependent on the chemical composition?
>
> **中文翻译**：**①** 自从首次从石墨机械剥离以来，石墨烯因其新颖的电子、力学与化学性质引起了巨大科技关注。**②** 然而，此类研究仅提供力学性质的有效取向平均值，且局限于单一组成，其余二维半导体 TMD 仍有待表征。**③** 单层 TMD 沿不同晶体方向的本征极限强度——无缺陷材料不发生破坏所能承受的最大应力——是什么？它又如何依赖于化学组成？

| 句 | 漏斗层级 | 逐句拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **①** | **大背景** | `Since [事件], X has attracted...attention due to [性质]`（时间锚 + 三个并列性质）。**以石墨烯而非 TMD 自身开篇，是"借势"策略——用已知名义为新材料背书。** |
| **②** | **缺口** | `However, such studies provide only...and are limited only to single composition, while the rest...remain yet to be characterized`（`However` 转折 + 两个具体局限 + `while` 对比）。**两个 `only` 强化了局限感；`remain yet to be characterized` 是"尚未被研究"的优雅表达。** |
| **③** | **研究问题** | `What is X — 同位语定义 — of Y along [方向] and how is it dependent on Z?`（两个并列问句 + 破折号内定义术语）。**用直接问句而非 "In this paper, we..." 引出本文，在 PRB 中较为少见但有效——它把研究问题本身变成了引言的高潮。** |

**核心教训**：以直接问句收尾引言是一种高风险高回报策略。问句必须足够具体（含材料、方向、因变量），否则显得空洞。破折号内的同位语定义 `— the maximum stress a defect-free material can withstand without failure —` 既解释了 `ultimate strength`，又展示了作者对术语的精确把握。

## ⚙️ 3. Key Methodological Developments

> **本节目标**：提取 2010–2014 年方法段的常用句式。这一时期的方法描述以被动语态为主，但已出现主动/被动交替以避免单调；DFT 计算方法的参数化描述（cutoff、k-mesh、收敛阈值）在这一时期趋于标准化。

2010–2014 年的方法段可归入三个可辨识的家族。**合成家族**使用被动过程描述：[[../papers/Goswami2011multiferroic]] 报道 "the nanoparticles of BiFeO₃ have been synthesized by sonochemical route where coprecipitation takes place within a suitable medium from aqueous solution of mixed metal nitrates in presence of ultrasonic vibration"；[[../papers/KumarChoubey2011mechanoluminescence]] 具体说明 "Eu doped strontium Aluminate phosphors were synthesized by combustion method using urea as a reducer at initiating temperature of 600°C"；[[../papers/Wixtrom2011electrical]] 则把机械化学框定为一种绿色替代方案，指出 "mechanochemical synthesis have the economical and environmental advantages of avoiding the use of reaction solvents, thus providing 'green chemistry' synthetic alternatives."。**制备家族**强调仪器与工艺：[[../papers/Unknown2014passive]] 陈述 "the microstructures were fabricated using a commercial TPP 3-D microfabrication module with a passively Q-switched Nd:YAG microchip laser (532 nm),"，以及 "the microfluidic devices were fabricated using PDMS bonded on a glass microscope slide, by the soft lithography and replica molding techniques."。**计算家族**到这一时期已收敛到一套标准化的参数级联——泛函、赝势、截断能、k 网格、收敛判据——如 [[../papers/khazaeiNovelElectronicMagnetic2013]] 所示："first-principles calculations are performed within the context of density functional theory using the generalized gradient approximation with the PBE exchange–correlation functional and the projected augmented wave (PAW) approach with plane wave cutoff energy of 520 eV, as implemented in the VASP code."。

这一时期在方法学上的独特之处在于**明确报告收敛阈值**，这一做法到 2010 年代初已成为强制要求。[[../papers/khazaeiNovelElectronicMagnetic2013]] 具体说明 "the positions of atoms, lattice parameters, and angles are fully optimized using conjugate gradient method. Therefore, the maximum force acting on each atom becomes less than 0.005 eV/Å. The criterion for energy convergence is 10⁻⁶ eV/cell,"，以及 "to avoid any interaction between an MXene sheet and its periodically repeated images along the c axis, a large vacuum space of 30 Å is used."。[[../papers/Li2013bonding]] 在 ABINIT 中用模守恒赝势报告了类似的 LDA 设置，并引入了一个针对二维体系的应力重整化："we renormalized the calculated supercell stress by a factor of Z/h, where Z is the vacuum distance and h is the interlayer distance in the bulk."。这一重整化本身就是一项方法学贡献——二维应力无法直接从三维超胞计算中读取。

表征家族依赖 `was/were + 过去分词` 的仪器描述。[[../papers/Goswami2011multiferroic]] 除进行 "powder neutron diffraction under zero and 5T magnetic field at room temperature" 外，还 "using the laboratory x-ray diffractometer across ∼300-700 K" 开展了 "high resolution powder x-ray diffraction experiment"。[[../papers/KumarChoubey2011mechanoluminescence]] "by a homemade setup having RCA 931 photomultiplier tube positioned below the lucite plate and connected to a storage oscilloscope" 监测 ML。[[../papers/Unknown2014optical]] "using a quartz crystal microbalance" 表征涂层厚度。这些句子中反复出现的动词模式是 `X was characterized/monitored/measured using Y`，仪器名置于 Y 中。

### 模板 A：合成方法 — `X were synthesized by [route] using [reagent] at [condition]`

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| The nanoparticles of BiFeO₃ **have been synthesized by** sonochemical route where coprecipitation takes place within a suitable medium from aqueous solution of mixed metal nitrates in presence of ultrasonic vibration. | BiFeO₃ 纳米颗粒通过声化学路线合成，其中共沉淀在超声振动下于混合金属硝酸盐水溶液的合适介质中发生。 | `X have been synthesized by [route] where [过程]` |
| Eu doped strontium Aluminate phosphors **were synthesized by** combustion method using urea as a reducer at initiating temperature of 600°C. | Eu 掺杂铝酸锶荧光粉以尿素为还原剂、在 600°C 引发温度下通过燃烧法合成。 | `X were synthesized by [方法] using [试剂] as [角色] at [条件]` |
| Mechanochemical synthesis **have** the economical and environmental advantages of avoiding the use of reaction solvents, thus providing 'green chemistry' synthetic alternatives. | 机械化学合成具有避免使用反应溶剂的经济与环境优势，从而提供"绿色化学"合成替代方案。 | `[方法] have the advantage of avoiding X, thus providing Y` |

**语法点**：被动合成句的三个要素——方法（`by sonochemical route` / `by combustion method`）、试剂（`using urea as a reducer`）、条件（`at 600°C` / `in presence of ultrasonic vibration`）。注意第一例 `where` 引导的定语从句描述共沉淀过程，把"方法"与"机制"合并在一句中。第三例 `thus providing` 是现在分词伴随状语，给出方法的优势。

### 模板 B：DFT 计算参数 — `Calculations are performed using [functional] with [cutoff] as implemented in [code]`

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|

### 🧩 经典案例逐句拆解：DFT 方法段

以下是 [[../papers/khazaeiNovelElectronicMagnetic2013]] 与 [[../papers/Li2013bonding]] 方法段的拼装拆解：

> **来源**：截取自 [[../papers/khazaeiNovelElectronicMagnetic2013|Khazaei et al. (2013)]], *Phys. Rev. B*; [[../papers/Li2013bonding|Li (2013)]], *Phys. Rev. B*

> **①** First-principles calculations are performed within the context of density functional theory using the generalized gradient approximation with the PBE exchange–correlation functional and the projected augmented wave (PAW) approach with plane wave cutoff energy of 520 eV, as implemented in the VASP code. **②** The positions of atoms, lattice parameters, and angles are fully optimized using conjugate gradient method. Therefore, the maximum force acting on each atom becomes less than 0.005 eV/Å. The criterion for energy convergence is 10⁻⁶ eV/cell. **③** To avoid any interaction between an MXene sheet and its periodically repeated images along the c axis, a large vacuum space of 30 Å is used. **④** We carried out first-principles density functional calculations within the local density approximation as implemented in ABINIT. Norm-conserving pseudopotentials were used to describe the interaction between core and valence electrons. **⑤** We renormalized the calculated supercell stress by a factor of Z/h, where Z is the vacuum distance and h is the interlayer distance in the bulk.
>
> **中文翻译**：**①** 第一性原理计算在 DFT 框架内进行，采用 GGA/PBE 泛函与 PAW 方法，平面波截断能 520 eV，如 VASP 中所实现。**②** 原子位置、晶格参数与角度用共轭梯度法完全优化，每个原子上的最大作用力小于 0.005 eV/Å，能量收敛判据为 10⁻⁶ eV/cell。**③** 为避免 MXene 片与周期重复像之间的相互作用，使用 30 Å 真空层。**④** 我们在 ABINIT 中以 LDA 进行第一性原理 DFT 计算，用模守恒赝势描述芯电子与价电子相互作用。**⑤** 我们以 Z/h 因子重整化了计算得到的超胞应力，其中 Z 为真空距离、h 为块体层间距。

| 句 | 功能 | 逐句拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **①** | **总框架** | `are performed using [泛函] with [cutoff], as implemented in [code]`（被动语态一句话交代框架）。**泛函、方法、截断、代码四个信息点压缩在一句，是 PRB 方法段的标准密度。** |
| **②** | **收敛阈值** | `are optimized using [算法]. Therefore, the maximum force becomes less than [阈值]`（优化算法 + 力阈值 + 能量阈值三句）。**`Therefore` 连接算法与结果，比独立罗列更有逻辑。** |
| **③** | **真空层** | `To avoid [假象], a vacuum of N Å is used`（目的状语 + 被动）。**所有 2D 计算方法段都必须有这句，否则审稿人会质疑层间相互作用。** |
| **④** | **另一组计算** | `We carried out...within LDA as implemented in ABINIT`（主动语态切换到另一组计算）。**注意与句① 的被动语态形成对比——主动语态让作者在多方法对比时更有存在感。** |
| **⑤** | **方法学创新** | `We renormalized X by a factor of Z/h, where Z is... and h is...`（主动 + 自定义因子 + `where` 从句定义变量）。**这是整段唯一一句"我们做了别人没做的事"，用主动语态突出方法学贡献。** |

**核心教训**：方法段不是参数罗列，而是决策链。句①–④ 回答"用了什么"，句⑤ 回答"为什么要做额外处理"。审稿人读方法段最关注的是句⑤ 这种"非常规操作"——如果你做了 renormalization 却不解释原因，一定会被质疑。

## 📊 4. Core Results & Physical Mechanisms

> **本节目标**：学会用"图→数据→对比→机制"的链条呈现结果。2010–2014 年的结果段比当代更常以 `In Fig. X, we show` 开头，再用 `It can be seen that`、`We find that` 引导观察，最后以 `can be attributed to` 给出机制。

这一片段的核心结果聚集在三个物理体系周围。**第一簇**是纳米尺度 BiFeO₃ 中的磁电耦合，由 [[../papers/Goswami2011multiferroic]] 通过联合 X 射线与中子衍射确立。结构结果是 "the space group turns out to be R3c in the nanoscale system at room temperature and across T_N similar to what has been observed in the bulk,"，但关键异常在于 "the polarization (P) is found to exhibit a clear anomaly around T_N. P rises by ∼30% across T_N. The lattice parameters a, c and the lattice volume V too, show distinct anomaly at T_N."。约 0.4% 的体积收缩 "establishes the first order nature of the transition."。在室温 5 T 磁场下，偏心参数 "δ is found to have decreased by ∼0.06 Å which amounts to a suppression in polarization by ∼7%."。机制句直截了当："the sharp change (∼30%) in the polarization around T_N as well as its suppression (∼7%) under 5T magnetic field at a temperature well below T_N point out that the multiferroic coupling is quite strong even in nanoscale BiFeO₃."。

**第二簇**是单层 TMD 的力学行为，由 [[../papers/Li2013bonding]] 通过 DFT 确立。线弹性结果是 "for small strain, the stress for all MX₂ exhibits linear dependence on the applied strain for both loading directions,"，且 "for all compositions, the values of the Young's modulus obtained independently from the stress-strain curve for armchair and zigzag loading direction are virtually identical."。越过线性区后，"as MX₂ monolayers are strained further (ε > 4%), the stress-strain response deviates from the linear behavior. For large strains, the hexagonal symmetry is broken."。一个非平凡的组成-性质关联浮现出来："the anisotropy in stress response is inversely correlated with the strength of the monolayer sheets — the MX₂ with lower Young's modulus and ultimate strength are characterized by larger anisotropy factors."。电子机制被追溯到轨道重叠："in this range, the outermost d orbitals of the transition metal M overlap significantly with the outermost p orbitals of the chalcogen X, indicating a strong p-d hybridization,"，因此 "the origin of the observed mechanical behavior can be attributed to a strong hybridization between the outermost p orbitals of the chalcogens and the d orbitals of the transition metal."。与石墨烯的直接对比凸显了意外之处："there is one remarkable difference: while TMDs and graphene both have hexagonal lattice, they exhibit a contrasting anisotropy in stress response."。

**第三簇**是 [[../papers/gorkovStrongElectronlatticeCoupling2012]] 的 TMD 中 CDW 理论，它构造了一个局域双阱模型："at Λ² > 1 the potential has two deep minima at ũ₊,₋ = ±√(1 − Λ⁻²)... the model reduces to the model of interacting Ising spins."。在 T_CDW 以下，"the energy spectrum of electrons remains metallic because the structural vector Q and the FS sizes are not related,"，而 "large values of the CDW gap seen in the tunneling experiments correspond to the energy of the minima in the electron-ion two-well complex."。面向器件的结果——[[../papers/Unknown2014optical]]（0.08 dB/%RH 灵敏度、2 s/6 s 响应、温度不敏感的条纹对比度）、[[../papers/Unknown2014passive]]（单个螺旋桨即超过 80% 混合效率）、[[../papers/Terasaki2011ultrasonic]]（超声波诱导 ML 维持 5 min 以上）以及 [[../papers/KumarChoubey2011mechanoluminescence]]（365 nm UV 下 ML 恢复）——遵循同样的数据→机制模式，其解释包括 "it is because the water can quickly diffuse into the nanocoating" 以及 "we think that the ML phenomenon of the Eu doped strontium aluminates are related to the movement of dislocations and the recombination of activated electrons and holes."。

### 句式三：机制归因 — `The origin of X can be attributed to Y`

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |

### 🧩 经典案例逐句拆解：结果—机制段落

以下是 [[../papers/Goswami2011multiferroic]] 结果段的核心四句：

> **来源**：截取自 [[../papers/Goswami2011multiferroic|Goswami et al. (2011)]]

> **①** But, most interestingly, the polarization (P) is found to exhibit a clear anomaly around T_N. P rises by ∼30% across T_N. The lattice parameters a, c and the lattice volume V too, show distinct anomaly at T_N. **②** The volume contraction (∼0.4%) at T_N establishes the first order nature of the transition. **③** In order to verify the coupling, we have carried out powder neutron diffraction under zero and 5T magnetic field at room temperature. **④** δ is found to have decreased by ∼0.06 Å which amounts to a suppression in polarization by ∼7%.
>
> **中文翻译**：**①** 但最有趣的是，极化发现在 T_N 附近呈现明显异常，P 在 T_N 处上升约 30%，晶格参数 a、c 与晶格体积 V 也在 T_N 处呈现明显异常。**②** T_N 处约 0.4% 的体积收缩确立了转变的一级性质。**③** 为验证耦合，我们在室温下零场与 5 T 磁场中进行了粉末中子衍射。**④** δ 减小约 0.06 Å，相当于极化抑制约 7%。

| 句 | 功能 | 逐句拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **①** | **异常观察** | `most interestingly`（副词前置，引导读者注意核心发现）→ `is found to exhibit a clear anomaly`（被动 + 名词化）→ 三个短句并列（P / a,c / V 都异常）。**三个短句并列制造"证据群"效果，比一个长句更有冲击力。** |
| **②** | **性质判定** | `[数据] establishes [性质]`（数据作主语，主动语态）。**`establishes` 是结果段的"判决动词"——一个数据就足以判定转变级数。** |
| **③** | **验证过渡** | `In order to verify the coupling, we have carried out [实验] under [条件]`（目的状语 + 现在完成时主动）。**`In order to verify` 是从"观察"到"验证"的标准过渡，明确告诉读者下一个实验的动机。** |
| **④** | **量化耦合** | `δ is found to have decreased by N which amounts to a suppression in P by M%`（被动 + 现在完成时不定式 + `which` 从句换算）。**`to have decreased` 是完成时不定式，表示"已减小"的状态；`amounts to` 把结构参数换算为极化量。** |

**核心教训**：这四句构成"异常→性质判定→独立验证→量化耦合"的完整论证链。句③ 的 `In order to verify the coupling` 是最朴素也最有效的过渡——它明确告诉审稿人"我为什么要做下一个实验"，避免结果段沦为数据罗列。

These experimental results **show that** the proposed humidity sensor is temperature insensitive. | 条纹对比度几乎不受温度变化影响（波动 <0.01 dB）。这些实验结果表明所提出的湿度传感器温度不敏感。 | `X was almost not affected (波动 N) by Y. These results show that Z is insensitive.` |
| The result indicates that the propeller-type barriers lead to higher mixing efficiency than pillar barriers. Even just one screw-shaped propeller **results in** a mixing efficiency of over ∼80%. | 结果表明螺旋桨型屏障比柱屏障产生更高混合效率。即使仅一个螺旋形桨也能产生超过约 80% 的混合效率。 | `X leads to higher efficiency than Y. Even just one Z results in efficiency of over N%.` |
| We can easily find clear emission repeatedly from the ML sample tube during the ultrasonic wave irradiation. The emission intensity during the ultrasonic wave irradiation **was maintained at least within 5 min.** | 我们可在超声波照射期间轻松反复观察到 ML 样品管的清晰发光。超声波照射期间发光强度至少维持 5 分钟。 | `X was maintained at least within [时长]` |

**语法点**：器件性能句的四个核心数据点——灵敏度（`is achieved`）、响应时间（`shows response times of A and B`）、稳定性（`was almost not affected`）、效率（`results in...over N%`）。`respectively`（分别）用于把两组数据按顺序对应。括号内的波动范围 `(fluctuation <0.01 dB)` 是定量稳定性声明的标配。`Even just one...results in` 用 `Even` 强化卖点。`was maintained at least within 5 min` 中的 `at least within` 略显冗余，规范写法应为 `was maintained for at least 5 min`。来源：[[../papers/Unknown2014optical]]、[[../papers/Unknown2014passive]]、[[../papers/Terasaki2011ultrasonic]]。

### 句式六：CDW 理论与电子结构

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| At Λ² > 1 the potential has two deep minima at ũ₊,₋ = ±√(1 − Λ⁻²). ... the model **reduces to** the model of interacting Ising spins. | 在 Λ² > 1 时势在 ũ± = ±√(1−Λ⁻²) 处有两个深阱。该模型约化为相互作用 Ising 自旋模型。 | `At [条件], the potential has two minima. The model reduces to [已知模型].` |

## 💬 5. Discussion

> **本节目标**：掌握讨论段的"对比旧认知—权衡—归因—推广"逻辑。2010–2014 年的讨论段比当代更朴素，常用 `can be attributed to`、`It is noteworthy that`、`On the other hand`，但已具备完整的论证结构。

这一时期的讨论段通过四个反复出现的动作运作。**第一个动作是与已有工作对比**，通常以 `in good agreement with` 或 `in contrast to` 为标志。[[../papers/Li2013bonding]] 指出 "our calculated values for the Young's modulus are also in good agreement with earlier studies,"，紧接着便是对比："however, there is one remarkable difference: while TMDs and graphene both have hexagonal lattice, they exhibit a contrasting anisotropy in stress response."。**第二个动作是值得注意标记**，例如 [[../papers/Goswami2011multiferroic]] 观察到 "it is noteworthy that the onset of magnetic order influences the polarization more than the application of magnetic field."。这句话一身二任：它既凸显了一个定量不对称性（T_N 处 30% 跳跃 vs. 5 T 下 7% 抑制），又隐含论证了磁电耦合是内禀的而非磁场诱导的。

**第三个动作是机制归因**，几乎都通过 `can be attributed to` 完成。对于 TMD 力学，[[../papers/Li2013bonding]] 把组成依赖性归因于 "the spatial redistribution of the occupied hybridized electronic states in the region between the transition metal atom and the chalcogens,"，更具体地归因于 "a strong hybridization between the outermost p orbitals of the chalcogens and the d orbitals of the transition metal."。对于 ML 荧光粉，[[../papers/KumarChoubey2011mechanoluminescence]] 排除了断裂诱导的电荷分离，因为 "the ML intensity is obtained when load is applied and it is completely recovered by UV irradiation,"，转而提出 "the ML phenomenon of the Eu doped strontium aluminates are related to the movement of dislocations and the recombination of activated electrons and holes."。对于光纤 RH 传感器，[[../papers/Unknown2014optical]] 通过指出 "when the RH rises, the response time is 2 s, while in the RH decreasing process, its response time is 6 s, which is longer. It is because the water can quickly diffuse into the nanocoating." 来解释不对称响应时间。**第四个动作是推广或应用展望**。[[../papers/gorkovStrongElectronlatticeCoupling2012]] 声称 "the electronic properties of the transition-metal dichalcogenides, as they are seen by ARPES, agree well with the suggested concept,"，从而把一个局域双阱模型提升为对 TMD 中 CDW 的一般性解释。[[../papers/khazaeiNovelElectronicMagnetic2013]] 预测 "upon appropriate surface functionalization, Sc₂C, Ti₂C, Zr₂C, and Hf₂C MXenes are expected to become semiconductors"，且 "functionalized Cr₂C and Cr₂N MXenes are magnetic."。[[../papers/naguib25thAnniversaryArticle2013a]] 铸造了一个持久的比喻："MXenes combine the metallic conductivity of transition metal carbides with the hydrophilic nature of their hydroxyl or oxygen terminated surfaces. In essence, they behave as 'conductive clays'."。

这一时期一个鲜明的文体特征是**明确的谨慎**，这在后来的高影响力写作中较为罕见。[[../papers/Unknown2014optical]] 用 "the fringe contrast was almost not affected (fluctuation <0.01 dB) by the temperature changes," 来限定其热稳定性声明，使用定量界限而非一概而论。[[../papers/Makogon2012wave]] 指出 "though our approach is applicable for any temperature regime, we restrict ourselves to temperatures close to zero,"，公开声明有效域而非暗示普适性。这些对冲并非弱点；它们是一个尚未采用 `fundamentally` / `unprecedented` 语体的时代在认知上的诚实。

### 句式一：与已有工作对比 — `in good agreement with / in contrast to`

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Our calculated values for the Young's modulus **are also in good agreement with** earlier studies. | 我们计算的杨氏模量值也与早期研究吻合良好。 | `X are in good agreement with earlier studies` |
| **However, there is one remarkable difference:** while TMDs and graphene both have hexagonal lattice, they exhibit a contrasting anisotropy in stress response. | 然而有一个显著差异：TMD 与石墨烯虽同为六角晶格，却在应力响应各向异性上形成对比。 | `However, there is one remarkable difference: while A, B` |
| **In contrast to** the Ti₂C case, functionalized Cr₂C MXenes are magnetic. | 与 Ti₂C 情形相反，功能化 Cr₂C MXene 具有磁性。 | `In contrast to [对照], [对象] are [性质]` |

**语法点**：三种对比句式——`in good agreement with`（一致）、`there is one remarkable difference: while...`（差异 + 冒号细节）、`In contrast to`（直接对比）。`remarkable`（显著的）比 `significant` 更主观，暗示作者认为这个差异重要。冒号后用 `while` 引导从句具体说明差异。

### 句式二：值得注意 — `It is noteworthy that / It can be readily noted that`

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **It is noteworthy that** the onset of magnetic order influences the polarization more than the application of magnetic field. | 值得注意的是，磁序的出现对极化的影响大于磁场施加的影响。 | `It is noteworthy that X influences Y more than Z` |
| **It is noteworthy that** the onset of magnetic order influences the polarization more than the application of magnetic field. | （同上） | `It is noteworthy that + [比较级从句]` |
| **It can also be seen that** when the RH rises, the response time is 2 s, while in the RH decreasing process, its response time is 6 s, which is longer. | 还可以看到，RH 上升时响应时间为 2 秒，而 RH 下降过程中响应时间为 6 秒，更长。 | `It can also be seen that when A, X is N, while when B, X is M` |

**语法点**：`It is noteworthy that` 是形式主语结构，真正主语是 that 从句。这种结构让作者可以"客观地"指出一个值得注意的现象，而不必说 `we note that`（过于主观）。`more than` 比较级要注意前后比较对象对称（the onset... / the application...）。第三例 `which is longer` 是非限制性定语从句，补充说明 6 秒比 2 秒长。

### 句式三：机制讨论与推广 — `The origin of X can be attributed to Y / agree well with`

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| The origin of the observed mechanical behavior **can be attributed to** a strong hybridization between the outermost p orbitals of the chalcogens and the d orbitals of the transition metal. | 所观测力学行为的起源可归因于硫族最外层 p 轨道与过渡金属 d 轨道之间的强杂化。 | `The origin of X can be attributed to [机制]` |
| The electronic properties of the transition-metal dichalcogenides, as they are seen by ARPES, **agree well with** the suggested concept. | ARPES 所看到的过渡金属硫族化合物电子性质与所提出的概念吻合良好。 | `X, as seen by [技术], agree well with [理论]` |
| In essence, they **behave as** 'conductive clays'. | 本质上，它们表现得像"导电黏土"。 | `In essence, X behave as '[比喻]'` |

**语法点**：三种讨论收束方式——归因（`attributed to`）、理论—实验一致（`agree well with`）、比喻命名（`behave as`）。`as they are seen by ARPES` 是插入语，限定观测手段。`In essence`（本质上）是提炼比喻的引导词；`conductive clays`（导电黏土）这一比喻由 [[../papers/naguib25thAnniversaryArticle2013a|Naguib et al. (2013)]] 提出，已成为 MXene 领域的经典标签。

### 🧩 经典案例逐句拆解：讨论段落

以下是 [[../papers/Li2013bonding]] 讨论段的四句拼装：

> **来源**：截取自 [[../papers/Li2013bonding|Li (2013)]], *Phys. Rev. B*

> **①** Our calculated values for the Young's modulus are also in good agreement with earlier studies. **②** However, there is one remarkable difference: while TMDs and graphene both have hexagonal lattice, they exhibit a contrasting anisotropy in stress response. **③** We find that the anisotropy in stress response is inversely correlated with the strength of the monolayer sheets — the MX₂ with lower Young's modulus and ultimate strength are characterized by larger anisotropy factors. **④** The origin of the observed mechanical behavior can be attributed to a strong hybridization between the outermost p orbitals of the chalcogens and the d orbitals of the transition metal.
>
> **中文翻译**：**①** 我们计算的杨氏模量值与早期研究吻合良好。**②** 然而有一个显著差异：TMD 与石墨烯虽同为六角晶格，却在应力响应各向异性上形成对比。**③** 我们发现应力响应的各向异性与单层片的强度反相关——杨氏模量与极限强度较低的 MX₂ 具有更大的各向异性因子。**④** 所观测力学行为的起源可归因于硫族最外层 p 轨道与过渡金属 d 轨道之间的强杂化。

| 句 | 功能 | 逐句拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **①** | **一致确认** | `X are in good agreement with earlier studies`（先确认与文献一致，建立可信度）。**讨论段开头先"求同"再"立异"，是标准策略——否则审稿人会认为你在否定所有前人工作。** |
| **②** | **差异指出** | `However, there is one remarkable difference: while A, B`（`However` 转折 + 冒号引出具体差异）。**`one remarkable difference` 用单数 `one` 强调"只有一个关键差异"，比 `several differences` 更有焦点。** |
| **③** | **规律提炼** | `We find that X is inversely correlated with Y — the MX₂ with A are characterized by B`（主动 `We find` + 破折号重述规律）。**`inversely correlated with`（反相关）是定量规律的标准表达；破折号后用具体材料族重述，让规律可操作化。** |
| **④** | **机制归因** | `The origin of X can be attributed to [p-d hybridization]`（名词化机制 + 介词短语）。**从"规律"（句③）到"机制"（句④）是讨论段的关键跃迁——规律回答"是什么"，机制回答"为什么"。** |

**核心教训**：这四句展示了讨论段的"求同→立异→规律→机制"四步法。先承认与文献一致（句①），再指出一个关键差异（句②），从差异中提炼定量规律（句③），最后用微观机制解释规律（句④）。这比直接说"我们发现了新机制"更有说服力，因为读者已经被带到了机制的门口。

### 第五步：机制排除与多因归因

讨论段常需排除一种旧机制再提出新机制，或把现象归因于多个并列因素。

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Since the ML intensity is obtained when load is applied and it is completely recovered by UV irradiation, **the origin of light emission is not** the separation of the charges on the fracturing surfaces. | 由于 ML 强度在施加负载时获得且可被 UV 照射完全恢复，发光起源并非断裂表面上的电荷分离。 | `Since [证据 A and B], the origin of X is not Y`（排除式） |
| We think that the ML phenomenon of the Eu doped strontium aluminates **are related to** the movement of dislocations and the recombination of activated electrons and holes. | 我们认为 Eu 掺杂铝酸锶的 ML 现象与位错运动以及激活电子与空穴的复合有关。 | `X are related to A and B`（双因素归因） |
| The sharp change (∼30%) in the polarization around T_N as well as its suppression (∼7%) under 5T magnetic field at a temperature well below T_N **point out that** the multiferroic coupling is quite strong even in nanoscale BiFeO₃. | 极化在 T_N 附近的尖锐变化（约 30%）以及在远低于 T_N 温度下 5 T 磁场中的抑制（约 7%）指出，即使在纳米尺度 BiFeO₃ 中多铁耦合也相当强。 | `A as well as B point out that C`（双证据归因） |
| We argue that instead of development of the Migdal instability in the soft mode scenario for the propagating phonon mode, the CDW instability **may realize itself in** the two stages. | 我们论证，CDW 不稳定性并非在传播声子模的软模情景中发展为 Migdal 不稳定性，而可能以两阶段方式实现。 | `instead of A, B may realize itself in C`（替代机制） |

**语法点**：四种归因/排除策略——`Since [证据], the origin is not Y`（先证据后排除）、`are related to A and B`（双因素，谦逊）、`A as well as B point out that C`（双证据归因，`as well as` 强调前者）、`instead of A, B may realize itself in C`（提出替代机制，`may` 留余地）。注意 `the ML phenomenon...are related to` 中主语为单数 `phenomenon`，语法上应为 `is related to`，原文有主谓一致瑕疵，引用时可修正。来源：[[../papers/KumarChoubey2011mechanoluminescence]]、[[../papers/Goswami2011multiferroic]]、[[../papers/gorkovStrongElectronlatticeCoupling2012]]。

### 第六步：理论—实验对照与方法论反思

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| The electronic properties of the transition-metal dichalcogenides, as they are seen by ARPES, **agree well with** the suggested concept. | ARPES 所看到的 TMDC 电子性质与所提出的概念吻合良好。 | `X, as seen by [技术], agree well with [理论]` |
| Our calculated values for the Young's modulus **are also in good agreement with** earlier studies. | 我们计算的杨氏模量值也与早期研究吻合良好。 | `Our calculated X are in good agreement with earlier studies` |
| Though our approach is applicable for any temperature regime, **we restrict ourselves to** temperatures close to zero. | 尽管我们的方法适用于任何温度区域，我们将自己限制在接近零的温度。 | `Though..., we restrict ourselves to [范围]` |
| Moreover, we find that the usual approach – neglecting the coupling with density fluctuations – **significantly underestimates** the critical value of the interaction strength. | 此外，我们发现通常的做法——忽略与密度涨落的耦合——显著低估了相互作用强度的临界值。 | `the usual approach – [具体做法] – significantly underestimates X` |

**语法点**：理论—实验对照有两个强度等级——`agree well with`（动词短语，较强）与 `are in good agreement with`（形容词短语，较弱但更正式）。`as they are seen by ARPES` 限定观测手段，暗示"在该技术的分辨率范围内"一致，避免过度声称。方法论反思用 `we restrict ourselves to` 主动声明范围限制，比被动 `is limited to` 更有作者声音。破折号插入语 `– neglecting the coupling with density fluctuations –` 把旧方法的具体缺陷夹在主句中，比 `which neglects...` 更醒目。来源：[[../papers/gorkovStrongElectronlatticeCoupling2012]]、[[../papers/Li2013bonding]]、[[../papers/Makogon2012wave]]。

## 🏁 6. Conclusion

> **本节目标**：学会结论段的"总结—要点—展望"三段式。2010–2014 年的结论段比当代更常以 `In summary` / `In conclusion` 开头，展望部分更朴素（`could prove to be useful`、`looks very promising`），但结构完整。

这一时期的结论遵循一个紧凑的三步模式。**第一步是一句话总结**。[[../papers/Goswami2011multiferroic]]："In conclusion, we show that the ferroelectric polarization in nanoscale (∼22 nm) BiFeO₃ exhibits an anomalous change at T_N and a suppression under a magnetic field at below T_N."。[[../papers/Kang2012dimer]]："In summary, we have shown that the anisotropic electronic dimer structure can be understood based on the 'hidden' PoDW order in parent compounds."。[[../papers/Li2013bonding]]："In summary, we have investigated the relationship between the intrinsic mechanical response of the monolayer group VI TMDs to large elastic deformation, the atomic-level structure, and chemical composition."。**第二步是列举关键发现**，有时隐式但偶尔显式。[[../papers/Makogon2012wave]] 给出两条："we showed how to construct a system with a unique Fermi surface consisting of concentric squircles,"，以及 "moreover, we find that the usual approach – neglecting the coupling with density fluctuations – significantly underestimates the critical value of the interaction strength."。

**第三步是应用或未来展望**，以刻意斟酌的情态表达。[[../papers/Goswami2011multiferroic]] 结论认为该结果 "confirms that multiferroic coupling is quite strong even in nanoscale which could prove to be useful for developing nanoscale devices of BiFeO₃."。[[../papers/Unknown2014optical]] 预期 "this sensor will be used in a wide range of applications, including meteorological services and in the chemical and food processing industries."。[[../papers/XiaokangZhang2013calibrating]] 指出该标定方法 "presents the advantages of simple calibration and low cost, making it of considerable value in practical application."。[[../papers/Unknown2014passive]] 对展望进行量化："on the basis of this result, even just one mixer can promise a mixing index above 80% at a flow rate of 12 μl/min."。[[../papers/gorkovStrongElectronlatticeCoupling2012]] 以理论-实验一致声明收尾："the electronic properties of the transition-metal dichalcogenides, as they are seen by ARPES, agree well with the suggested concept."。最具修辞色彩的结论来自 [[../papers/PChandra2011mechanoluminescence]]："conclusively, it may be said that the door of EML research on nanoparticles is wide open and it looks very promising."。前瞻而谨慎的语体——`could prove to be`、`is expected to`、`may be said`、`looks very promising`——是这一时期的标志；后期更强硬的 `paves the way toward` / `establishes a new paradigm` 基本缺席。

### 第一句：总结成就

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **In conclusion, we show that** the ferroelectric polarization in nanoscale (∼22 nm) BiFeO₃ exhibits an anomalous change at T_N and a suppression under a magnetic field at below T_N. | 总之，我们表明纳米尺度（约 22 nm）BiFeO₃ 中的铁电极化在 T_N 处呈现异常变化，并在低于 T_N 的磁场中被抑制。 | `In conclusion, we show that X exhibits A and B` |
| **In summary, we have shown that** the anisotropic electronic dimer structure can be understood based on the 'hidden' PoDW order in parent compounds. | 总之，我们已表明各向异性电子二聚体结构可基于母体化合物中"隐藏"的 PoDW 序来理解。 | `In summary, we have shown that X can be understood based on Y` |
| **In summary, we have investigated** the relationship between the intrinsic mechanical response of the monolayer group VI TMDs to large elastic deformation, the atomic-level structure, and chemical composition. | 总之，我们研究了 VIB 族单层 TMD 对大弹性形变的本征力学响应、原子尺度结构与化学组成之间的关系。 | `In summary, we have investigated the relationship between A, B, and C` |

**语法点**：三种总结开场——`In conclusion, we show that`（结论，一般现在时）、`In summary, we have shown that`（总结，现在完成时）、`In summary, we have investigated`（总结 + 研究对象）。`In conclusion` 比 `In summary` 更强调"最终判断"，后者更偏"要点回顾"。三个并列宾语 `the relationship between A, B, and C` 中 A/B/C 必须语法对称。

### 第二句：要点与展望

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| This result confirms that multiferroic coupling is quite strong even in nanoscale which **could prove to be useful for** developing nanoscale devices of BiFeO₃. | 这一结果证实即使在纳米尺度多铁耦合也相当强，可能对开发 BiFeO₃ 纳米器件有用。 | `X could prove to be useful for developing Y` |
| It is expected that this sensor **will be used in a wide range of applications**, including meteorological services and in the chemical and food processing industries. | 预计该传感器将被用于广泛的应用，包括气象服务以及化学与食品加工工业。 | `It is expected that X will be used in a wide range of applications, including...` |
| The calibration method proposed in this letter can be used to realize an optical fiber RH sensing system for real-time monitoring. It **presents the advantages of** simple calibration and low cost, **making it of considerable value in practical application.** | 本信所提出的标定方法可用于实现实时监测的光纤 RH 传感系统，具有标定简单、成本低的优势，使其在实际应用中具有相当价值。 | `X presents the advantages of A and B, making it of considerable value in C` |

**语法点**：三种展望力度——`could prove to be useful for`（最谦逊，could 表推测）、`It is expected that...will be used`（被动预测，工程标配）、`presents the advantages of...making it of considerable value`（主动列举优势 + 现在分词给出价值判断）。`making it of considerable value` 是 `make + 宾语 + 介词短语` 结构，`of value` = `valuable`。

### 第三句：开放收尾

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Conclusively, it may be said that** the door of EML research on nanoparticles is wide open and it looks very promising. | 总之可以说，纳米粒子 EML 研究的大门敞开，前景十分广阔。 | `Conclusively, it may be said that the door of X is wide open and it looks promising` |
| Our simulation results **provide new insights into** possible applications of MXene systems in future nanoelectronic technology and encourage researchers to undertake new studies in this field. | 我们的模拟结果为 MXene 体系在未来纳米电子技术中的可能应用提供了新洞见，并鼓励研究者在该领域开展新研究。 | `X provide new insights into Y and encourage researchers to undertake Z` |
| On the basis of this result, even just one mixer **can promise** a mixing index above 80% at a flow rate of 12 μl/min. | 基于这一结果，即使仅一个混合器在 12 μl/min 流速下也能保证 80% 以上的混合指数。 | `On the basis of this result, even just one X can promise Y` |

**语法点**：三种收尾修辞——比喻（`the door is wide open`）、双动词展望（`provide new insights...and encourage...`）、性能承诺（`can promise`）。`it may be said that` 是非常谦逊的形式主语结构，比 `we can say that` 更留余地。`looks very promising` 是这一时期的高频展望短语，今天已略显陈旧，当代更常用 `holds great promise`。

### 🧩 经典案例逐句拆解：完整结论

以下是 [[../papers/Goswami2011multiferroic]] 与 [[../papers/Makogon2012wave]] 结论的拼装：

> **来源**：截取自 [[../papers/Goswami2011multiferroic|Goswami et al. (2011)]]; [[../papers/Makogon2012wave|Makogon et al. (2012)]]

> **① In conclusion, we show that** the ferroelectric polarization in nanoscale (∼22 nm) BiFeO₃ exhibits an anomalous change at T_N and a suppression under a magnetic field at below T_N. **②** This result confirms that multiferroic coupling is quite strong even in nanoscale which could prove to be useful for developing nanoscale devices of BiFeO₃. **③** We showed how to construct a system with a unique Fermi surface consisting of concentric squircles. **④** Moreover, we find that the usual approach – neglecting the coupling with density fluctuations – significantly underestimates the critical value of the interaction strength.
>
> **中文翻译**：**①** 总之，我们表明纳米尺度（约 22 nm）BiFeO₃ 中的铁电极化在 T_N 处呈现异常变化，并在低于 T_N 的磁场中被抑制。**②** 这一结果证实即使在纳米尺度多铁耦合也相当强，可能对开发 BiFeO₃ 纳米器件有用。**③** 我们展示了如何构建一个具有由同心圆角方形组成的独特费米面的体系。**④** 此外，我们发现通常的做法——忽略与密度涨落的耦合——显著低估了相互作用强度的临界值。

| 句 | 功能 | 逐句拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **①** | **总结** | `In conclusion, we show that X exhibits A and B`（结论信号词 + 两个并列结果）。**一句话压缩全文核心数据，是结论段的"电梯演讲"。** |
| **②** | **意义** | `This result confirms that...which could prove to be useful for...`（`confirms` 给判断 + `could` 表谦虚 + `for developing` 给应用方向）。**`confirms` 与 `could` 的力度搭配是这一时期的典型——判断确定，但展望留余地。** |
| **③** | **要点 1** | `We showed how to construct X with Y`（过去时 + `how to` 给出方法学贡献）。**`how to construct` 比 `that X exists` 更有方法学价值，暗示别人可以照着做。** |
| **④** | **要点 2 + 警示** | `Moreover, we find that the usual approach...significantly underestimates...`（`Moreover` 递进 + 破折号插入被否定的旧做法 + `significantly underestimates` 指出旧方法的定量偏差）。**结论段中指出旧方法的缺陷是加分项——它把你的工作从"一个新结果"提升为"对方法论的修正"。** |

**核心教训**：2010–2014 年的结论不追求宏大叙事，而是"一句总结 + 一句意义 + 若干要点"。其力量来自具体数据（∼22 nm、∼30%、concentric squircles）和谨慎情态动词（`could`、`may`），而非形容词。这种克制在今天仍值得借鉴——结论越具体，读者越信任。

## 🧬 7. Evolution（综述专属）

> **本节目标**：识别 2010–2014 年语料中的综述性叙事。这一时期的语料以原创研究为主，真正意义上的综述仅有 [[../papers/naguib25thAnniversaryArticle2013a]]（MXene 两周年/25 周年纪念文）与部分 intro 中的历史回顾；Martin 薄膜铁电体综述（martinThinfilmFerroelectricMaterials2016）发表于 2016 年，不在本段时间窗口内。因此本节以"领域演进"的视角，综合 [[../papers/naguib25thAnniversaryArticle2013a]]、[[../papers/khazaeiNovelElectronicMagnetic2013]]、[[../papers/gorkovStrongElectronlatticeCoupling2012]] 等文献中的回顾性叙述，重建 2010–2014 年的演进脉络。

2010–2014 年的演进叙事有三条可辨别的线索，它们在这一时期内仅部分汇聚。**第一条线索是剥离驱动的二维材料浪潮**，由石墨烯在 2004 年开启，并在 2010–2013 年间扩展到 TMD。正如 [[../papers/Li2013bonding]] 所言，"since its first mechanical exfoliation from graphite, graphene has attracted tremendous scientific and technological attention due to novel electronic, mechanical, and chemical properties,"，而到 2013 年 [[../papers/khazaeiNovelElectronicMagnetic2013]] 已可声明 "since the discovery of graphene by Novoselov et al., the synthesis, properties, and applications of 2D materials has become a leading area of interest in the field of material science and engineering."。[[../papers/Li2013bonding]] 对单层 TMD 的力学表征——确立了依赖组成的杨氏模量、极限强度以及各向异性-强度反相关——位于这条线索的成熟端，此时领域已从"二维是否稳定？"转向"二维性质如何随材料族变化？"。

**第二条线索是 MXene 的诞生与快速扩张**，由 [[../papers/naguib25thAnniversaryArticle2013a]] 最为明确地叙述。2012–2013 年层状 MAX 相剥离为二维 MXene，确立了 "MXenes combine the metallic conductivity of transition metal carbides with the hydrophilic nature of their hydroxyl or oxygen terminated surfaces. In essence, they behave as 'conductive clays'."。[[../papers/khazaeiNovelElectronicMagnetic2013]] 平行开展的 DFT 普查预测 "upon appropriate surface functionalization, Sc₂C, Ti₂C, Zr₂C, and Hf₂C MXenes are expected to become semiconductors"，且 "functionalized Cr₂C and Cr₂N MXenes are magnetic."。实验剥离与计算预测在短短两年窗口内共同演进，是这一时期加速的理论-实验循环的特征。

**第三条线索是长期延续的 TMD 中 CDW 争论**，[[../papers/gorkovStrongElectronlatticeCoupling2012]] 将其框定为自 "early 70's" 发现以来便 "subject of debates"。2012 年的局域双阱模型——"strong enough coupling transforms the harmonic elastic energy for an ion to the one of the well with two deep minima, so that the system is described in terms of Ising spins"——既为弱耦合费米面嵌套范式也为软模声子范式提供了替代方案，并声称 "the electronic properties of the transition-metal dichalcogenides, as they are seen by ARPES, agree well with the suggested concept."。[[../papers/Kang2012dimer]] 关于铁磷化物的平行工作通过"隐藏"的 PoDW 序解释杂质诱导的 QPI 图案，而 [[../papers/Makogon2012wave]] 为光晶格中的自旋-电荷密度波构造了广义 SO(3,1) × SO(3,1) 理论。以 [[../papers/Goswami2011multiferroic]] 为代表的多铁线索，则把 BiFeO₃ 叙事从块体单晶延伸到约 22 nm 纳米颗粒，同时保留 R3c 对称性与强磁电耦合。

把这一时期与后来区分开来的是**各线索尚未汇聚**：二维 TMD 力学、MXene 电子结构、CDW 理论与纳米尺度多铁性大体并行推进。概念上的汇聚——滑动铁电、二维多铁金属、层间电荷转移——要到 2017–2024 年前后才结晶。2010–2014 年这段时期最好被读作**实验与计算的奠基阶段**：材料被剥离出来，DFT 工作流被标准化，纳米尺度极限被测试，长期争论被重新框架化，但统一概念仍在地平线之外。

### 第一阶段：石墨烯驱动的二维热潮（2010–2012）

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Since its first mechanical exfoliation from graphite, graphene **has attracted** tremendous scientific and technological attention due to novel electronic, mechanical, and chemical properties. | 自从首次从石墨机械剥离以来，石墨烯因其新颖的电子、力学与化学性质引起了巨大科技关注。 | `Since [事件], X has attracted...attention due to [性质]` |
| Since the discovery of graphene by Novoselov et al., the synthesis, properties, and applications of 2D materials **has become** a leading area of interest in the field of material science and engineering. | 自从 Novoselov 等人发现石墨烯以来，2D 材料的合成、性质与应用已成为材料科学与工程领域的前沿兴趣方向。 | `Since [事件], X has become a leading area of interest in [领域]` |
| The hope in the multiferroicity of BiFeO₃ **has been rekindled by** the observation of spin-flop under electric field in a single crystal. | 对 BiFeO₃ 多铁性的希望因单晶中电场下自旋翻转的观测而被重新点燃。 | `The hope in X has been rekindled by [事件]` |

**语法点**：三个"起点"句都用现在完成时（`has attracted`、`has become`、`has been rekindled`）表示从过去某一事件持续到现在的影响。`Since + 名词短语` 是时间锚，`due to` 给出原因，`leading area of interest` 是领域地位判断。

### 第二阶段：MXene 诞生与 DFT 预测并行（2012–2013）

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Layered MAX phases **are exfoliated into** 2D single layers and multilayers, so-called MXenes. | 层状 MAX 相被剥离为二维单层与多层，即所谓 MXene。 | `X are exfoliated into 2D layers, so-called [名称]` |
| MXenes **combine** the metallic conductivity of transition metal carbides **with** the hydrophilic nature of their hydroxyl or oxygen terminated surfaces. In essence, they behave as 'conductive clays'. | MXene 将过渡金属碳化物的金属导电性与其羟基或氧端基表面的亲水性相结合，本质上表现为"导电黏土"。 | `X combine A with B. In essence, they behave as '[比喻]'.` |
| Upon appropriate surface functionalization, Sc₂C, Ti₂C, Zr₂C, and Hf₂C MXenes **are expected to become** semiconductors. | 经适当表面功能化后，Sc₂C、Ti₂C、Zr₂C 与 Hf₂C MXene 预计将成为半导体。 | `Upon [条件], X are expected to become [性质]` |

### 第三阶段：长期争论的再框架（2012–2014）

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Origin of charge density waves (CDW) in the transition-metals dichalcogenides (TMDC) **is subject of debates** since their discovery in the early 70's. | TMDC 中 CDW 的起源自 1970 年代初发现以来一直是争论主题。 | `X is subject of debates since [时间]` |
| We argue that instead of development of the Migdal instability in the soft mode scenario for the propagating phonon mode, the CDW instability **may realize itself in** the two stages. | 我们论证，CDW 不稳定性并非在传播声子模的软模情景中发展为 Migdal 不稳定性，而可能以两阶段方式实现。 | `We argue that instead of A, B may realize itself in C` |
| The electronic properties of the transition-metal dichalcogenides, as they are seen by ARPES, **agree well with** the suggested concept. | ARPES 所看到的 TMDC 电子性质与所提出的概念吻合良好。 | `X, as seen by [技术], agree well with [理论]` |

**语法点**：三种"争论再框架"句式——`is subject of debates`（陈述争议状态）、`We argue that instead of A, B may realize itself in C`（提出替代方案）、`agree well with`（理论与实验一致）。`may realize itself` 中的反身代词 `itself` 让"不稳定性"成为主动实现自身的主体，是理论物理写作的拟人化修辞。`as they are seen by ARPES` 限定观测手段，避免过度声称。

### 🧩 经典案例逐句拆解：领域演进全景

以下是综合 [[../papers/naguib25thAnniversaryArticle2013a]]、[[../papers/khazaeiNovelElectronicMagnetic2013]]、[[../papers/gorkovStrongElectronlatticeCoupling2012]] 的演进叙事拼装：

> **来源**：截取自 [[../papers/naguib25thAnniversaryArticle2013a|Naguib et al. (2013)]], *Adv. Mater.*; [[../papers/khazaeiNovelElectronicMagnetic2013|Khazaei et al. (2013)]], *Phys. Rev. B*; [[../papers/gorkovStrongElectronlatticeCoupling2012|Gor'kov (2012)]], *Phys. Rev. B*

> **①** Since the discovery of graphene by Novoselov et al., the synthesis, properties, and applications of 2D materials has become a leading area of interest in the field of material science and engineering. **②** Layered MAX phases are exfoliated into 2D single layers and multilayers, so-called MXenes. Using first-principles calculations, the formation and electronic properties of various MXene systems are examined. **③** MXenes combine the metallic conductivity of transition metal carbides with the hydrophilic nature of their hydroxyl or oxygen terminated surfaces. In essence, they behave as 'conductive clays'. **④** Upon appropriate surface functionalization, Sc₂C, Ti₂C, Zr₂C, and Hf₂C MXenes are expected to become semiconductors. It is also derived theoretically that functionalized Cr₂C and Cr₂N MXenes are magnetic. **⑤** Our simulation results provide new insights into possible applications of MXene systems in future nanoelectronic technology and encourage researchers to undertake new studies in this field.
>
> **中文翻译**：**①** 自从 Novoselov 等人发现石墨烯以来，2D 材料的合成、性质与应用已成为材料科学与工程领域的前沿方向。**②** 层状 MAX 相被剥离为二维单层与多层，即所谓 MXene；利用第一性原理计算，多种 MXene 体系的形成与电子性质被检验。**③** MXene 将过渡金属碳化物的金属导电性与羟基或氧端基表面的亲水性相结合，本质上表现为"导电黏土"。**④** 经适当表面功能化后，Sc₂C、Ti₂C、Zr₂C 与 Hf₂C MXene 预计成为半导体；理论上还导出功能化 Cr₂C 与 Cr₂N MXene 具有磁性。**⑤** 我们的模拟结果为 MXene 体系在未来纳米电子技术中的可能应用提供了新洞见，并鼓励研究者在该领域开展新研究。

| 句 | 阶段 | 逐句拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **①** | **领域起点** | `Since [事件], X has become a leading area of interest in [领域]`（现在完成时 + 领域地位判断）。**石墨烯作为整个 2D 浪潮的时间锚，是所有后续 MXene/TMD 论文的默认引言背景。** |
| **②** | **新材料诞生** | `X are exfoliated into 2D layers, so-called MXenes. Using [方法], the properties of Y are examined.`（被动制备 + `so-called` 命名 + 被动计算）。**一句之内完成"制备→命名→计算验证"三个动作，是新材料族宣告的紧凑模板。** |
| **③** | **品牌比喻** | `X combine A with B. In essence, they behave as '[比喻]'.`（主动组合 + 比喻命名）。**`conductive clays` 是 MXene 领域最成功的修辞投资——一个比喻同时传达了导电性与亲水性/可加工性。** |
| **④** | **预测扩展** | `Upon [条件], A, B, C, D are expected to become semiconductors. It is also derived that E and F are magnetic.`（`Upon` 条件状语 + 被动预测 + `It is also derived that` 补充预测）。**列出四个半导体 + 两个磁性体系，是 DFT 综述类论文的"菜单式"贡献——读者可以照着做实验。** |
| **⑤** | **展望收束** | `X provide new insights into Y and encourage researchers to undertake Z`（双动词展望：`provide insights` + `encourage`）。**`encourage researchers to undertake new studies` 是 2010 年代综述/展望论文的标准收尾，今天读来略平淡，但在当时是得体的。** |

**核心教训**：2010–2014 年的演进叙事不是"汇聚"而是"铺开"——石墨烯开辟了 2D 领域，MXene 作为新家族加入，DFT 预测为实验提供菜单，但各线索尚未交叉。综述写作的任务在这一时期是"画地图"（标出有哪些材料、哪些性质），而不是"写家谱"（追溯概念如何代际传承）。后者要到 2020 年代滑动铁电、多铁金属等概念成熟后才成为可能。Martin 等人的薄膜铁电体综述 [[../papers/martinThinfilmFerroelectricMaterials2016|Martin et al. (2016)]] 恰好站在这一"铺开期"的尾声，但其出版时间（2016）使其归入下一时间段。

### 第四阶段：纳米尺度多铁性的边界探索（2011–2014）

除上述三条主线外，2010–2014 年还有一条相对独立的支线——把已知多铁材料（BiFeO₃）推向纳米尺度，测试磁电耦合是否在尺寸减小后存活。[[../papers/Goswami2011multiferroic]] 的工作代表了这一支线的典型叙事逻辑。

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| The hope in the multiferroicity of BiFeO₃ has been rekindled by the observation of spin-flop under electric field in a single crystal. | 对 BiFeO₃ 多铁性的希望因单晶中电场下自旋翻转的观测而被重新点燃。 | `The hope in X has been rekindled by [事件]` |
| However, the question of multiferroic coupling in nanoscale BiFeO₃ has not been addressed adequately. | 然而，纳米尺度 BiFeO₃ 中多铁耦合的问题尚未被充分解决。 | `However, the question of X in [尺度] has not been addressed adequately` |
| These results confirm the presence of strong multiferroic coupling even in nanoscale BiFeO₃ and thus could prove to be quite useful for applications based on nanosized devices of BiFeO₃. | 这些结果证实即使在纳米尺度 BiFeO₃ 中仍存在强多铁耦合，因而可能对基于 BiFeO₃ 纳米器件的应用相当有用。 | `These results confirm X even in [尺度], and thus could prove useful for [应用]` |

**语法点**：纳米尺度探索的叙事遵循"希望重燃→缺口→本文证实"三步。`rekindled`（重新点燃）比喻此前该方向曾有低潮，`not been addressed adequately` 低调指出缺口，`even in nanoscale` 中的 `even` 强调尺寸极限下的存活是反预期的。`could prove to be quite useful` 是这一时期最典型的谨慎展望，比当代的 `paves the way toward` 谦逊得多。

### 第五阶段：力致发光与光纤传感器的应用导向支线

2010–2014 年语料中还有一条明显的"应用导向"支线——力致发光（ML/EML）材料与光纤湿度/微流控传感器。这类论文的演进叙事不围绕物理概念，而围绕"性能指标提升"。

| 实例 | 直译 | 骨架拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Although the phenomenon of ML is known for a long time, in the past no practical application could be made because of the low ML intensity of the materials and lack of reproducibility. | 尽管 ML 现象早已为人所知，但过去由于材料 ML 强度低且缺乏可重复性，无法实际应用。 | `Although X is known for a long time, no application could be made because of Y and Z` |
| We have investigated mechanoluminescence induced by ultrasonic wave, and we successfully detected the mechanoluminescence that depend on irradiated power of ultrasonic wave. | 我们研究了超声波诱导的力致发光，并成功检测到依赖于超声波照射功率的力致发光。 | `We have investigated X induced by Y, and successfully detected Z that depend on W` |
| From the viewpoint of utilizing an ML material as an ubiquitous light source, we can recognize that the ultrasonic wave, non-destructive and non-inventive stimulation for bio-tissue, should be suitable candidate of mechanical stimulation for mechanoluminescence. | 从将 ML 材料用作泛在光源的角度看，我们可以认识到，超声波作为对生物组织非破坏、非侵入的刺激，应是力致发光机械刺激的合适候选。 | `From the viewpoint of X, we can recognize that Y, [同位语], should be suitable candidate of Z` |

**语法点**：应用导向支线的演进逻辑是"旧障碍→新刺激方式→应用前景"。`Although...known for a long time...no application could be made because of` 是"历史长但无应用"的标准句式。`non-destructive and non-inventive stimulation for bio-tissue` 是两个并列形容词短语作同位语，插入主句中说明超声波的优势。`should be suitable candidate` 中 `should` 表推测而非义务。来源：[[../papers/PChandra2011mechanoluminescence]]、[[../papers/Terasaki2011ultrasonic]]。

### 🧩 经典案例逐句拆解：多线索并行的综述开篇

以下是综合 [[../papers/Li2013bonding]]、[[../papers/gorkovStrongElectronlatticeCoupling2012]]、[[../papers/Goswami2011multiferroic]] 的综述开篇拼装，展示如何把并行线索纳入一段：

> **来源**：综合自 [[../papers/Li2013bonding|Li (2013)]], *Phys. Rev. B*; [[../papers/gorkovStrongElectronlatticeCoupling2012|Gor'kov (2012)]], *Phys. Rev. B*; [[../papers/Goswami2011multiferroic|Goswami et al. (2011)]]

> **①** Since the discovery of graphene by Novoselov et al., the synthesis, properties, and applications of 2D materials has become a leading area of interest in the field of material science and engineering. **②** In parallel, origin of charge density waves (CDW) in the transition-metals dichalcogenides (TMDC) has remained subject of debates since their discovery in the early 70's. **③** The hope in the multiferroicity of BiFeO₃, separately, has been rekindled by the observation of spin-flop under electric field in a single crystal. **④** These three strands—2D mechanical characterization, CDW mechanism, and nanoscale multiferroic coupling—proceeded largely in parallel during 2010–2014, each with its own experimental toolkit and theoretical vocabulary, and would only begin to converge in the late 2010s with the rise of 2D ferroics.
>
> **中文翻译**：**①** 自从 Novoselov 等人发现石墨烯以来，2D 材料的合成、性质与应用已成为材料科学与工程领域的前沿方向。**②** 与此同时，过渡金属硫族化合物中 CDW 的起源自 1970 年代初发现以来一直是争论主题。**③** 另外，对 BiFeO₃ 多铁性的希望因单晶中电场下自旋翻转的观测而被重新点燃。**④** 这三条线索——二维力学表征、CDW 机制、纳米尺度多铁耦合——在 2010–2014 年间大体并行推进，各有自己的实验工具与理论词汇，直到 2010 年代末二维铁性兴起才开始汇聚。

| 句 | 线索 | 逐句拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **①** | **2D 材料主线** | `Since [事件], X has become a leading area of interest in [领域]`。**以石墨烯为时间锚，是所有 2D 综述的默认开篇。** |
| **②** | **CDW 争论线** | `In parallel, X has remained subject of debates since [时间]`。**`In parallel` 标记并行线索；`has remained` 比 `has been` 更强调争议的持续性。** |
| **③** | **多铁支线** | `The hope in X, separately, has been rekindled by [事件]`。**`separately` 作为插入语，明确标记这是第三条独立线索。** |
| **④** | **线索收束** | `These three strands—A, B, and C—proceeded largely in parallel during [时期], each with its own toolkit, and would only begin to converge in [后期]`。**`strands`（线索）是综述写作中比 `topics` 更有叙事感的词；破折号内列举三条线索，`each with its own` 独立主格结构补充差异，`would only begin to converge` 用过去将来时暗示后续汇聚。** |

**核心教训**：综述开篇处理多线索并行时，最有效的策略是"逐条时间锚 + 并行标记词 + 末句收束"。`In parallel`、`separately` 两个标记词让读者清楚线索之间的关系；末句用 `These three strands...proceeded largely in parallel...and would only begin to converge` 既总结了本段的"并行"特征，又预告了后续的"汇聚"，是综述开篇承上启下的黄金句式。

---

> **本段小结**：2010–2014 年的论文英语整体偏保守、被动、谨慎。与 2020–2024 语料相比，本段少了 `fundamentally`、`unprecedented`、`paradigm-shifting` 等强宣告副词，多了 `could prove to be`、`to the best of our knowledge`、`adequately` 等低调表达。方法段的 DFT 参数化描述在这一时期标准化（cutoff / k-mesh / 收敛阈值 / 真空层），结果段的"图→数据→机制"链条已成型，讨论段的"求同→立异→规律→机制"四步法清晰可循。这些句式朴素但耐用，是学术英语的基本功库。

### D. 定量表达

| 模式 | 语料库实例 |
|:---|:---|
| ∼N（近似） | "∼22 nm particles"; "∼30% around the magnetic transition point"; "∼0.06 Å" [[../papers/Goswami2011multiferroic]] |
| by ∼N%（变化幅度） | "exhibits a jump by ∼30%"; "a suppression by ∼7%" [[../papers/Goswami2011multiferroic]] |
| on the order of / of the order of | "of the order of 10⁵ V cm⁻¹" [[../papers/PChandra2011mechanoluminescence]] |
| in the range of / ranging from | "particle size in the range of 10 nm to 30 nm" [[../papers/PChandra2011mechanoluminescence]] |
| N-fold / N times | "approximately 3 orders of magnitude shorter" [[../papers/KumarChoubey2011mechanoluminescence]] |
| linear dependence / linear relationship | "linear dependence on the applied strain" [[../papers/Li2013bonding]] |

| amounts to / corresponding to | "amounts to a suppression in polarization" [[../papers/Goswami2011multiferroic]] |

**语法点**：`∼N%` 中的波浪号 `∼` 表示约数，比 `about N%` 更紧凑，是物理论文的标准符号。`by ∼N%` 中 `by` 表示变化幅度，不可省略；`a jump of 30%` 与 `a jump by 30%` 都可，但后者更强调变化过程。`on the order of` 用于量级估计（"数量级为"），后接数值。`ranging from A to B` 是现在分词短语作后置定语，修饰其前的名词。`amounts to`（相当于）用于把一个物理量换算为另一个，主语通常是某个可测量的变化值。

| 模式 | 语料库实例 |
|:---|:---|
| This is because / It is because | "It is because the water can quickly diffuse into the nanocoating" [[../papers/Unknown2014optical]] |
| The reason for / behind | "The reason for the canting could be the large strain in the particles" [[../papers/Goswami2011multiferroic]] |
| ... which explains why ... | "which explains why Ti₂C, Zr₂C, and Hf₂C become semiconducting upon O functionalization" [[../papers/khazaeiNovelElectronicMagnetic2013]] |
| This result can be attributed to | "This result can be attributed to the strong saturation vapor pressure at high temperature" [[../papers/XiaokangZhang2013calibrating]] |
| ... leading to ... / ... resulting in ... | "leading to imperfect nesting" [[../papers/Makogon2012wave]] |
| ... gives rise to ... | "gives rise to the light emission" [[../papers/KumarChoubey2011mechanoluminescence]] |
| ... as a consequence of ... | "a consequence of a type of 'reconstructed' nesting" [[../papers/Kang2012dimer]] |
| The origin of ... can be attributed to ... | "The origin of the observed mechanical behavior can be attributed to a strong hybridization" [[../papers/Li2013bonding]] |
| ... accounts for ... | "naturally explains the proximity of the two transitions and accounts for the observed orbital ferromagnetism" [[../papers/Kang2012dimer]] |

**语法点**：`This is because` 后接从句（主语 + 谓语），而 `because of` 后接名词短语，两者不可混用。`which explains why` 中 `why` 引导名词性从句作 `explains` 的宾语。`leading to` / `resulting in` / `giving rise to` 都是现在分词短语作结果状语，三者力度递增：`lead to`（导致，中性）、`result in`（造成，强调结果）、`give rise to`（催生，强调从无到有）。`accounts for` 兼有"解释"与"占比"两义，在机制讨论中意为"解释了……的原因"。

| 开头类型 | 语料库实例 |
|:---|:---|
| 综述/背景 | "It has been a long history since people began to study..." [[../papers/KumarChoubey2011mechanoluminescence]]; "The fundamental challenges of tissue engineering are ongoing" |
| 问题陈述 | "While the impurity-induced nanoscale electronic disorder has been extensively reported... its microscopic origins remain elusive" [[../papers/Kang2012dimer]] |
| 材料/体系介绍 | "The iron pnictide high-T_c superconductors exhibit several remarkable features" [[../papers/Kang2012dimer]] |
| 发现宣告 | "A practical material that is mechanoluminescent (ML) in the elastic deformation region has been developed in our laboratory for the first time" [[../papers/Terasaki2011ultrasonic]] |
| 方法→结果过渡 | "In order to identify the atomic-level origin... we analyzed their electronic structures" [[../papers/Li2013bonding]] |
| 理论框架 | "We consider single band of conduction electrons interacting with displacements of the transitional ions" [[../papers/gorkovStrongElectronlatticeCoupling2012]] |

### G. 应用导向写作模板（器件类论文专用）

器件类论文（[[../papers/Unknown2014optical]]、[[../papers/XiaokangZhang2013calibrating]]、[[../papers/Unknown2014passive]]）在这一时期形成了高度模板化的写作范式，值得单独整理：

| 功能 | 语料库实例 |
|:---|:---|
| 器件优势列举 | "the sensor presented here has advantages of high sensitivity, large measurement range, and rapid response" [[../papers/Unknown2014optical]] |
| 性能数据呈现 | "A high sensitivity of 0.08 dB/%RH is achieved. It shows response times of 2 and 6 s in the RH increasing and decreasing processes" [[../papers/Unknown2014optical]] |
| 稳定性验证 | "The fringe contrast was almost not affected (fluctuation <0.01 dB) by the temperature changes. These experimental results show that the proposed humidity sensor is temperature insensitive" [[../papers/Unknown2014optical]] |
| 制备方法描述 | "coating negatively charged PSS nanoparticles and positively charged PAH nanoparticles on the endface of an intrinsic Fabry-Perot interferometer (IFPI) by a layer-by-layer (LbL) electrostatic self-assembly method" [[../papers/Unknown2014optical]] |
| 应用展望 | "It is expected that this sensor will be used in a wide range of applications, including meteorological services and in the chemical and food processing industries" [[../papers/Unknown2014optical]] |
| 效率量化 | "Even just one screw-shaped propeller results in a mixing efficiency of over ∼80%" [[../papers/Unknown2014passive]] |
| 标定方法 | "We propose a calibration method that uses lookup tables and construct a corresponding calibration matrix" [[../papers/XiaokangZhang2013calibrating]] |
| 实际部署 | "The calibrated sensor is used to monitor RH in fresh concrete during its hardening process" [[../papers/XiaokangZhang2013calibrating]] |

**语法点**：器件论文的摘要与结论遵循"制备→性能→稳定性→应用"四段式。性能数据用被动语态 `is achieved`（被实现），稳定性验证用 `was almost not affected`（几乎不受影响）+ 定量波动范围，应用展望用 `It is expected that...will be used`（预计将被用于）。注意 `advantages of A, B, and C` 三个并列优势必须语法对称（都是名词短语）。`Even just one...results in` 用 `Even` 强化"仅一个就够"的卖点。

### H. 理论论文写作模板（理论/计算类专用）

理论论文（[[../papers/gorkovStrongElectronlatticeCoupling2012]]、[[../papers/Kang2012dimer]]、[[../papers/Makogon2012wave]]）有另一套模板：

| 功能 | 语料库实例 |
|:---|:---|
| 模型提出 | "We consider single band of conduction electrons interacting with displacements of the transitional ions" [[../papers/gorkovStrongElectronlatticeCoupling2012]] |
| 理论推导 | "We develop a generalized SO(3, 1) × SO(3, 1) theory describing the spin and charge degrees of freedom simultaneously" [[../papers/Makogon2012wave]] |
| 极限情形 | "At Λ² > 1 the potential has two deep minima at ũ₊,₋ = ±√(1 − Λ⁻²)... the model reduces to the model of interacting Ising spins" [[../papers/gorkovStrongElectronlatticeCoupling2012]] |
| 理论预测 | "Below the CDW-transition the energy spectrum of electrons remains metallic because the structural vector Q and the FS sizes are not related" [[../papers/gorkovStrongElectronlatticeCoupling2012]] |
| 与实验对照 | "The electronic properties of the transition-metal dichalcogenides, as they are seen by ARPES, agree well with the suggested concept" [[../papers/gorkovStrongElectronlatticeCoupling2012]] |
| 方法论修正 | "Moreover, we find that the usual approach – neglecting the coupling with density fluctuations – significantly underestimates the critical value of the interaction strength" [[../papers/Makogon2012wave]] |
| 序参量识别 | "In summary, we have shown that the anisotropic electronic dimer structure can be understood based on the 'hidden' PoDW order in parent compounds" [[../papers/Kang2012dimer]] |
| 费米面构造 | "We showed how to construct a system with a unique Fermi surface consisting of concentric squircles" [[../papers/Makogon2012wave]] |

**语法点**：理论论文的动词选择比实验论文更"构造性"——`consider`（考虑）、`develop`（发展）、`derive`（推导）、`construct`（构造）、`reduce to`（约化为）。`the model reduces to` 是理论物理的高频表达，意为"在某极限下模型简化为更简单的已知模型"。破折号插入语 `– neglecting the coupling with density fluctuations –` 用于在主句中夹入被否定的旧做法，比 `which neglects...` 更醒目。`agree well with` 是理论与实验对照的标准搭配，比 `is consistent with` 更强调定量吻合。

### 🧩 补充案例：力致发光（ML）段落逐句拆解

> **来源**：截取自 [[../papers/KumarChoubey2011mechanoluminescence|Kumar & Choubey (2011)]]; [[../papers/PChandra2011mechanoluminescence|Chandra et al. (2011)]]; [[../papers/Terasaki2011ultrasonic|Terasaki et al. (2011)]]

> **①** Mechanoluminescence (ML) is the phenomenon of light emission from a solid as a response to the mechanical stimulus given to it. **②** Whereas nearly 50% of all inorganic salts and organic molecular solids show ML during their fracture, only a few solids exhibit ML during their elastic and plastic deformation. **③** Certain nanoparticles exhibit intense light emission during their elastic deformation, called the phenomenon of elastico mechanoluminescence (EML). **④** Two peaks have been found in time Vs ML intensity plot. The peak of ML intensity Vs time curve increases with increasing velocity of the piston. **⑤** The ML intensity decreases and ceases with repetitive application of load. After irradiation with UV light (365 nm) for 15 minutes, when load is applied ML intensity is once again obtained. **⑥** Since the ML intensity is obtained when load is applied and it is completely recovered by UV irradiation, the origin of light emission is not the separation of the charges on the fracturing surfaces. **⑦** We think that the ML phenomenon of the Eu doped strontium aluminates are related to the movement of dislocations and the recombination of activated electrons and holes.
>
> **中文翻译**：**①** 力致发光（ML）是固体对所加机械刺激作出响应而发光的现象。**②** 近 50% 的无机盐与有机分子固体在断裂时显示 ML，但只有少数固体在弹性与塑性形变期间显示 ML。**③** 某些纳米粒子在弹性形变期间发出强光，称为弹性力致发光（EML）现象。**④** 在时间-ML 强度图中发现两个峰，ML 强度-时间曲线的峰值随活塞速度增加而增加。**⑤** ML 强度随重复施加负载而减小并停止；用 365 nm UV 照射 15 分钟后再施加负载，ML 强度再次获得。**⑥** 由于 ML 强度在施加负载时获得且可被 UV 照射完全恢复，发光起源并非断裂表面上的电荷分离。**⑦** 我们认为 Eu 掺杂铝酸锶的 ML 现象与位错运动以及激活电子与空穴的复合有关。

| 句 | 功能 | 逐句拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **①** | **定义** | `X is the phenomenon of Y as a response to Z`（`is` 定义句 + `as a response to` 给出因果）。**文章首句给出术语定义，是现象类论文的标准开场。** |
| **②** | **稀缺性** | `Whereas nearly 50% show X, only a few exhibit Y`（`Whereas` 让步对比 + `nearly` 与 `only a few` 形成数量反差）。**用统计对比凸显 EML 的稀缺性，为后续工作建立动机。** |
| **③** | **现象命名** | `Certain nanoparticles exhibit intense light emission during their elastic deformation, called EML`（主句 + 过去分词 `called` 命名）。**`called EML` 是缩写定义的标准位置。** |
| **④** | **数据观察** | `Two peaks have been found in X. The peak...increases with increasing velocity of the piston`（现在完成时被动 + 一般现在时趋势）。**`increases with increasing` 是趋势描述的固定搭配，两个 increasing 不重复——前者是动词，后者是动名词。** |
| **⑤** | **可恢复性** | `X decreases and ceases with repetitive load. After UV irradiation, when load is applied X is once again obtained`（衰减 + 恢复两个过程）。**`once again`（再次）比 `again` 更正式，强调完整恢复。** |
| **⑥** | **排除旧机制** | `Since [证据 A and B], the origin of X is not Y`（`Since` 给出两条证据 + 主句否定旧机制）。**先立证据再破旧机制，是"排除法归因"的标准逻辑。** |
| **⑦** | **提出新机制** | `We think that X are related to Y and Z`（主动 + `are related to` 给出新机制）。**`We think` 比 `we propose` 更谦逊，适合初步机制判断；`related to` 比 `attributed to` 留余地。** |

**核心教训**：现象类论文的论证链是"定义→稀缺性→命名→观察→可恢复性→排除旧机制→提出新机制"。句⑥ 的排除法是关键——不先否定"断裂电荷分离"，句⑦ 的"位错+电子空穴复合"就缺乏说服力。这种"先破后立"的结构在机制讨论中普遍适用。

### 🧩 补充案例：MXene DFT 预测段落逐句拆解

> **来源**：截取自 [[../papers/khazaeiNovelElectronicMagnetic2013|Khazaei et al. (2013)]], *Phys. Rev. B*; [[../papers/naguib25thAnniversaryArticle2013a|Naguib et al. (2013)]], *Adv. Mater.*

> **①** Layered MAX phases are exfoliated into 2D single layers and multilayers, so-called MXenes. **②** Using first-principles calculations, the formation and electronic properties of various MXene systems are examined. **③** All formation energies are large negative values, which indicates the formation of strong bonds between the transition metals and the attached groups. **④** Upon appropriate surface functionalization, Sc₂C, Ti₂C, Zr₂C, and Hf₂C MXenes are expected to become semiconductors. **⑤** It is also derived theoretically that functionalized Cr₂C and Cr₂N MXenes are magnetic. **⑥** MXenes combine the metallic conductivity of transition metal carbides with the hydrophilic nature of their hydroxyl or oxygen terminated surfaces. In essence, they behave as 'conductive clays'. **⑦** Our simulation results provide new insights into possible applications of MXene systems in future nanoelectronic technology and encourage researchers to undertake new studies in this field.
>
> **中文翻译**：**①** 层状 MAX 相被剥离为二维单层与多层，即所谓 MXene。**②** 利用第一性原理计算，多种 MXene 体系的形成与电子性质被检验。**③** 所有形成能均为大负值，表明过渡金属与附着基团之间形成强键。**④** 经适当表面功能化后，Sc₂C、Ti₂C、Zr₂C 与 Hf₂C MXene 预计成为半导体。**⑤** 理论上还导出功能化 Cr₂C 与 Cr₂N MXene 具有磁性。**⑥** MXene 将过渡金属碳化物的金属导电性与羟基或氧端基表面的亲水性相结合，本质上表现为"导电黏土"。**⑦** 我们的模拟结果为 MXene 体系在未来纳米电子技术中的可能应用提供了新洞见，并鼓励研究者在该领域开展新研究。

| 句 | 功能 | 逐句拆解 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **①** | **材料起源** | `X are exfoliated into Y, so-called Z`（被动 + `so-called` 命名）。**一句话交代 MXene 从何而来、叫什么。** |
| **②** | **方法概述** | `Using [方法], the [性质] of [体系] are examined`（现在分词短语 + 被动）。**方法与对象压缩在一句。** |
| **③** | **稳定性判据** | `All formation energies are large negative values, which indicates the formation of strong bonds`（数据 + `which indicates` 非限制性定语从句给出含义）。**形成能为负是热力学稳定的判据，`large negative values` 比 `negative` 更强调稳定性强。** |
| **④** | **半导体预测** | `Upon [条件], A, B, C, D are expected to become semiconductors`（`Upon` 条件 + 被动预测 + 四个并列体系）。**列出四个具体体系让预测可被实验直接检验。** |
| **⑤** | **磁性预测** | `It is also derived theoretically that E and F are magnetic`（形式主语 `It` + 被动 `is derived` + `also` 补充）。**`It is derived that` 比 `we find that` 更强调推导的必然性。** |
| **⑥** | **品牌比喻** | `X combine A with B. In essence, they behave as '[比喻]'`（主动组合 + `In essence` 提炼比喻）。**`conductive clays` 是 MXene 领域最成功的品牌建构。** |
| **⑦** | **展望收束** | `X provide new insights into Y and encourage researchers to undertake Z`（双动词展望）。**`provide insights` + `encourage` 是 2010 年代综述类论文的标准收尾。** |

**核心教训**：新材料族的 DFT 预测论文遵循"制备起源→方法→稳定性→性质预测（半导体/磁性）→品牌比喻→展望"七步。句⑥ 的比喻是可选项，但一旦成功（如 `conductive clays`），其传播力远超任何技术数据。写作者应有意识地在结果段末尾寻找一个能概括材料本质的比喻。

---

> **本段小结**：2010–2014 年的论文英语整体偏保守、被动、谨慎。与 2020–2024 语料相比，本段少了 `fundamentally`、`unprecedented`、`paradigm-shifting` 等强宣告副词，多了 `could prove to be`、`to the best of our knowledge`、`adequately` 等低调表达。方法段的 DFT 参数化描述在这一时期标准化（cutoff / k-mesh / 收敛阈值 / 真空层），结果段的"图→数据→机制"链条已成型，讨论段的"求同→立异→规律→机制"四步法清晰可循。器件类与理论类论文各自形成了高度可复用的模板。这些句式朴素但耐用，是学术英语的基本功库。

### I. 语料库中的写作瑕疵与修正

2010–2014 语料中有若干真实的语法/用词瑕疵，写作时应避免。下表列出原句与建议修正：

| 原句（有瑕疵） | 问题 | 建议修正 | 来源 |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| "Mechanochemical synthesis have the economical and environmental advantages..." | 主谓不一致：`synthesis` 为单数，应为 `has` | "Mechanochemical synthesis **has** the economical and environmental advantages..." | [[../papers/Wixtrom2011electrical]] |
| "the coupling between magnetic and electric order parameters too, is quite strong in them" | `too` 位置口语化，逗号错位 | "...not only...but also the coupling...is quite strong" | [[../papers/Goswami2011multiferroic]] |
| "We have also investigated that the ML intensity is completely recovered by irradiation with ultraviolet light." | `investigate that` 搭配不当，应为 `found that` | "We have also **found** that the ML intensity is completely recovered..." | [[../papers/KumarChoubey2011mechanoluminescence]] |
| "the ML phenomenon of the Eu doped strontium aluminates are related to..." | 主谓不一致：`phenomenon` 单数 | "...the ML phenomenon...**is** related to..." | [[../papers/KumarChoubey2011mechanoluminescence]] |
| "it was found that the emission intensity from the ML sample tube was intentional to the power" | `intentional` 用词错误，应为 `proportional` | "...the emission intensity...was **proportional to** the power of irradiated ultrasonic wave." | [[../papers/Terasaki2011ultrasonic]] |
| "non-inventive stimulation for bio-tissue" | `inventive` 应为 `invasive` | "non-destructive and non-**invasive** stimulation for bio-tissue" | [[../papers/Terasaki2011ultrasonic]] |
| "the synthesis, properties, and applications of 2D materials has become..." | 主谓不一致：并列主语为复数 | "...of 2D materials **have become** a leading area..." | [[../papers/khazaeiNovelElectronicMagnetic2013]] |
| "long after glow time" | 应为合成词 `afterglow` | "long **afterglow** time" | [[../papers/KumarChoubey2011mechanoluminescence]] |

**语法点**：这些瑕疵中最常见的两类是（1）主谓不一致——尤其当主语与谓语之间插入了长介词短语时（如 `the phenomenon...are related to`）；（2）用词错误——`intentional`/`proportional`、`inventive`/`invasive`、`investigate`/`find` 等。写作时应在终稿前专门检查"主语—谓语距离较远"的句子，以及非英语母语作者常见的易混词对。引用这些句子作为骨架模板时，应采用修正后的版本而非原句。

### J. 摘要数据嵌入方式对比

2010–2014 摘要中的数据嵌入比当代更朴素，主要有三种方式：

| 嵌入方式 | 语料库实例 | 来源 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| 主句直接嵌入 | "The polarization exhibits a jump by ∼30% around T_N and a suppression by ∼7% under 5T magnetic field." | [[../papers/Goswami2011multiferroic]] |
| 被动语态 + 数值 | "A high sensitivity of 0.08 dB/%RH is achieved." | [[../papers/Unknown2014optical]] |
| 独立短句补充 | "It shows response times of 2 and 6 s in the RH increasing and decreasing processes, respectively." | [[../papers/Unknown2014optical]] |
| 介词短语作定语 | "We find that the anisotropy in stress response is inversely correlated with the strength of the monolayer sheets." | [[../papers/Li2013bonding]] |
| 破折号夹入比较 | "P rises by ∼30% across T_N. The lattice parameters a, c and the lattice volume V too, show distinct anomaly at T_N." | [[../papers/Goswami2011multiferroic]] |

**语法点**：当代高影响力论文倾向于把多个数据点压缩在一个长句中（用破折号、分词、介词短语），而 2010–2014 语料更常用独立短句逐一呈现数据。两种策略各有利弊：长句密度高但阅读负荷大，短句清晰但显得松散。写作时可根据期刊风格调整——*Phys. Rev. B* 偏向朴素短句，*Nat. Mater.* 偏向紧凑长句。

### K. 引言中"我们做了什么"的动词选择

2010–2014 语料中引出本文工作的动词有明确的力度层级：

| 动词 | 力度 | 语料库实例 | 来源 |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| we show that | 中（表明） | "we show that the ferroelectric polarization...exhibits a jump" | [[../papers/Goswami2011multiferroic]] |
| we demonstrate that | 强（证明） | "we demonstrate that differences in the observed mechanical behavior can be attributed to..." | [[../papers/Li2013bonding]] |
| we report on | 中（报道） | "we report on the fundamental mechanical behavior of monolayer TMDs" | [[../papers/Li2013bonding]] |
| we present | 中（呈现） | "we present a humidity sensor consisting of an F-P interferometer" | [[../papers/Unknown2014optical]] |
| we propose | 中（提出） | "we propose a calibration method that uses lookup tables" | [[../papers/XiaokangZhang2013calibrating]] |
| we argue that | 强（论证） | "we argue that...the CDW instability may realize itself in the two stages" | [[../papers/gorkovStrongElectronlatticeCoupling2012]] |
| we derive and discuss | 中（推导并讨论） | "we derive and discuss an experimentally realistic model" | [[../papers/Makogon2012wave]] |
| we develop | 中（发展） | "we develop a generalized SO(3,1) × SO(3,1) theory" | [[../papers/Makogon2012wave]] |

**语法点**：动词选择应与论文类型匹配。实验论文用 `show`/`report`/`demonstrate`，理论论文用 `argue`/`derive`/`develop`，器件/方法论文用 `present`/`propose`。`we show that` 后接名词性从句（that 从句），`we report on` 后接名词短语（`on` 是介词），`we propose` 后可接名词短语或 that 从句。`argue` 比 `show` 更具争辩性，暗示你的结论可能有争议，适合提出非共识观点。

---

> **最终小结**：2010–2014 年的论文英语在句式上以被动语态、独立短句、谨慎情态动词为特征，在结构上已形成"背景—缺口—本文—方法—结果—机制—讨论—结论"的完整 IMRaD 框架。DFT 方法段的参数化描述在这一时期标准化，器件论文形成了"制备→性能→稳定性→应用"的四段式模板，理论论文则确立了"模型→极限→预测→实验对照"的叙事链。写作瑕疵主要集中在主谓一致与非英语母语易混词上，终稿检查时应重点关注。这些朴素句式是学术英语的基本功，即使在追求高密度表达的今天仍有参考价值。
